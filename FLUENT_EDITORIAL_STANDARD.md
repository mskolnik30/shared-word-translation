# Fluent editorial standard

Implementation standard · 17 September 2026 · editorial review pending

Fluent is an original-language translation for sustained reading aloud and silently. Its English should be natural, clear, and reverent while preserving the source's meaning, force, literary shape, and consequential uncertainties. TSW is a sibling translation and a comparator, never the English base text for Fluent.

This standard implements `FLUENT_TRANSLATION_PHILOSOPHY.md` alongside the supplied Translator's Preface, How to Read This Translation, Translation Rules and Conventions, and formatting instructions. The supplied documents remain the historical criteria. Where their form-conscious guidance would discourage routine smoothing, Fluent's established philosophy permits restructuring for natural English. That permission changes English form, not content, emphasis, ambiguity, or the obligation to account for every source clause. It does not authorize paraphrase, theological explanation, or smoothing away difficult events. Other unresolved conflicts must be logged rather than silently resolved in Fluent's favor.

## Criteria and evidence

| ID | Requirement | Evidence needed to close the criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F01 | Account for every source clause using the bound Hebrew, Aramaic, or Greek text. | Verse-level comparison of participants, actions, negation, conditions, quantities, time, relationships, and discourse links; documented treatment of textual differences. A valid source hash alone is not a linguistic review. |
| F02 | Use idiomatic contemporary English without added explanation. | Continuous reading plus source comparison for every substantive rewrite. Split a sentence only if logical relationships and emphasis survive. Readability scores may locate problems but cannot certify fidelity or understanding. |
| F03 | Preserve significant ambiguity and distinguish variants from interpretation. | A register of consequential alternatives, the chosen rendering, and the reason for preserving an alternative in text or a concise note. Do not give conjectures the status of manuscript readings. |
| F04 | Preserve voice, genre, emphasis, and functional repetition. | Whole-book review of narrative, speech, law, poetry, argument, genealogy, and formulae. Record justified departures from recurring wording. |
| F05 | Use recurring terms consistently in context. | A book-level term register with occurrences and reasons for variation. Preserve meaningful terms such as covenant, righteousness, kingdom, grace, and resurrection. Do not import a covenant into every occurrence of a word for kindness. |
| F06 | Handle gender, names, divine titles, and capitalization consistently. | Contextual checks on generic versus specific reference, number, metaphor, kinship, titles, and divine-name treatment. Inclusive wording must not erase a significant male referent or image; masculine grammar must not suppress maternal imagery. |
| F07 | Provide selective, useful apparatus. | Every note and vocabulary entry classified and reviewed. Retain significant variants, alternatives, idioms, wordplay, and necessary cultural or grammatical help. Remove production language, routine plot summaries, defensive explanations, and application. |
| F08 | Make paragraphs, headings, and poetry serve the text. | Read each chapter in sequence and at chapter boundaries. Do not separate a dependent clause from its completion. Headings must not decide a disputed interpretation. Verify the rendered reader as well as the source markup. |
| F09 | Maintain Fluent's independent English voice. | Source-first drafting/revision records and contextual review of substantial overlap with TSW. No minimum difference quota. Familiar phrases, lists, quotations, and the best concise rendering may properly coincide. Similarity statistics do not prove independent authorship. |
| F10 | Preserve traceability and complete coverage. | Exact before/after chapter hashes, source and comparator bindings, change reasons, current ledgers, complete verse mapping, and a reproducible checkpoint. Structural success is not editorial approval. |
| F11 | Obtain independent language review and reader evidence. | Named qualified reviewers' findings and their resolution, plus observed reader difficulties from representative passages and whole-book reading. Author self-review and model review do not count as independent review. |
| F12 | Complete reconciliation and final signoff. | Resolve material discrepancies with the Companion, rerun final checks, document remaining limitations, and obtain Matt's editorial approval. Do not automatically rewrite TSW or the Companion to match Fluent. |

## Editorial order

For each 50-chapter block, read the current English in order; compare every passage against its bound source; resolve terminology and ambiguity questions; revise the apparatus; then reread the resulting book or continuous unit. Review must cross batch boundaries where a book, speech, poem, or argument continues. A final short block may contain fewer than 50 chapters. Routine batches need no new user approval.

A pass can be preserved before the entire block is finished, but its coverage must be named precisely: for example, “all Genesis apparatus reviewed for editorial scope,” not “Genesis meets all criteria.” Work through unresolved items in the same block before calling the block complete. Keep independent review, reader testing, and final approval open until their evidence exists.

For each correction, record the location, prior wording, revised wording, criterion, rationale, source evidence where meaning is affected, and verification outcome. Preserve removed apparatus in the editorial record. Potential Companion material is a proposal, not an authorized insertion into the Companion.

## Apparatus decisions

- **Keep** a note when it supplies relevant information readers cannot readily recover from the English alone.
- **Rewrite** a useful note when production language, duplication, or an application obscures its textual purpose. Use direct language such as “The Hebrew can also mean …”.
- **Remove** a note that only repeats the passage, describes routine translation choices, or argues an application. Record its full prior wording and the reason.
- **Defer** a contested linguistic, historical, or textual claim when the available evidence is insufficient. Put the question in the editorial register; do not disguise uncertainty as a completed check.

Notes and vocabulary have separate purposes. A note explains a consequential choice or feature at a passage; a vocabulary entry gives a useful contextual sense of a source word. Avoid duplicating the same explanation in both. Not every chapter needs an equal number of entries.

## House style

Retain the existing Markdown chapter structure: front matter, descriptive `##` headings, verse labels such as `v01:`, paragraph blocks, `## Notes`, and `## Vocabulary`. Use two-digit minimum verse labels in the apparatus as well as the text, with an en dash for ranges. Use standard double quotation marks consistently in new or revised English notes. Preserve source scripts and do not normalize Hebrew or Greek in ways that change the bound source evidence.

Use LORD for the divine name according to the established convention. The corpus's inconsistent bold treatment of LORD/GOD is an open presentation decision until applied and rendered consistently. Do not mark a criterion complete merely by declaring a preference here. Similarly, varying scholarly transliterations must receive a documented corpus-wide convention before global normalization.

Use contemporary spelling and punctuation without casual slang or archaism. Preserve direct speech, meaningful numerical detail, distinct names, and source differences across related accounts. Poetry should have purposeful lineation; prose paragraphs should reflect connected units of thought. Section headings and apparatus are editorial helps, not Scripture.

## Completion rule

Use explicit pass states: **not started**, **in progress**, **self-review complete with open issues**, **ready for independent review**, and **independently reviewed with findings resolved**. A mechanical check may be **passed**, but that does not advance a human review state by itself.

Full editorial compliance requires closure evidence for every applicable criterion, resolution of all substantive findings, representative reader testing, and final editorial approval. No numerical overlap target, script, or single authoring pass can confer that status.
