## Tola and Jair

<p>
v01: After Abimelech, Tola son of Puah, son of Dodo, an Issacharite, arose to rescue Israel. He lived at Shamir in Ephraim’s hill country.
v02: He judged Israel for twenty-three years. Then he died and was buried at Shamir.
</p>

<p>
v03: After him arose Jair the Gileadite, who judged Israel for twenty-two years.
v04: He had thirty sons who rode thirty donkeys. They had thirty towns in Gilead, called Havvoth-jair to this day.
v05: Jair died and was buried at Kamon.
</p>

## Israel abandons the LORD again

<p>
v06: Again the Israelites did what was evil in the LORD’s eyes. They served the Baals and the Ashtaroth, the gods of Aram, Sidon, Moab, the Ammonites, and the Philistines. They abandoned the LORD and did not serve him.
v07: The LORD’s anger burned against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of the Philistines and the Ammonites.
v08: That year they shattered and crushed the Israelites. For eighteen years they oppressed all the Israelites east of the Jordan, in the Amorite land of Gilead.
v09: The Ammonites crossed the Jordan to fight Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim’s household as well. Israel was in deep distress.
</p>

## A cry and a refusal

<p>
v10: The Israelites cried out to the LORD, “We have sinned against you. We abandoned our God and served the Baals.”
v11: The LORD answered the Israelites, “Did I not rescue you from Egypt, the Amorites, the Ammonites, and the Philistines?
v12: The Sidonians, Amalek, and Maon oppressed you too. You cried out to me, and I rescued you from their hand.
v13: Yet you abandoned me and served other gods. So I will not rescue you again.
v14: Go and cry out to the gods you chose! Let them rescue you in your time of distress.”
</p>

<p>
v15: The Israelites said to the LORD, “We have sinned. Do to us whatever seems good in your eyes. Only rescue us today, please.”
v16: They removed the foreign gods from among them and served the LORD. He could no longer bear Israel’s misery.
</p>

## Who will lead Gilead?

<p>
v17: The Ammonites were called to arms and camped in Gilead. The Israelites gathered and camped at Mizpah.
v18: The people, the leaders of Gilead, asked one another, “Who will begin the fight against the Ammonites? He will be head over everyone living in Gilead.”
</p>

## Notes

v04: The words for donkeys and towns sound alike in Hebrew. The threefold thirty is retained.

v08: The account speaks both of that year and of eighteen years. The translation preserves both time expressions.

v12: The Hebrew names Maon. Some ancient witnesses instead name Midian; the pinned Hebrew reading is retained.

v16: He could no longer bear renders an idiom about his soul becoming short. The text holds the LORD’s refusal beside his response to Israel’s misery.

## Vocabulary

v02: judged
Led or governed Israel; the title includes more than courtroom decisions.

v04: Havvoth-jair
The settlements of Jair.

v18: head
A leader over the community, the role discussed in the next chapter.
