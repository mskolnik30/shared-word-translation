## Waiting for the answer

<p>
v01: I will stand at my watchpost
and take my place on the rampart.
I will watch to see what he says to me
and how I should answer about my complaint.
</p>

## The vision and the faithful

<p>
v02: The LORD answered me:
“Write the vision;
make it plain on tablets,
so the one who reads it can run.
v03: The vision is still for its appointed time.
It presses toward the end and will not lie.
If it delays, wait for it;
it will surely come. It will not be late.
v04: Look at the swollen one—
his life within him is not upright.
But the righteous will live by their faithfulness.
</p>

<p>
v05: Wine, too, betrays.
The arrogant man will not stay at rest.
He opens his appetite as wide as Sheol;
like death, he is never satisfied.
He gathers all nations to himself
and collects all peoples.
</p>

## Woes against the plunderer

<p>
v06: Will not all of them take up a taunt against him,
a mocking riddle, and say:
‘Woe to the one who piles up what is not his—
how long?—
and loads himself with pledges!’
v07: Will not your creditors suddenly rise?
Will not those who make you tremble wake up?
Then you will become their plunder.
v08: Because you plundered many nations,
all the peoples left will plunder you—
for human bloodshed and violence against the land,
the city, and all who live in it.
</p>

<p>
v09: Woe to the one who makes evil profit for his house,
setting his nest high
to escape the reach of harm!
v10: You have planned shame for your own house
by cutting off many peoples;
you have sinned against your own life.
v11: A stone will cry out from the wall,
and a timber beam will answer it.
</p>

<p>
v12: Woe to the one who builds a town with bloodshed
and establishes a city through injustice!
v13: Is this not from the LORD of Armies:
peoples labor only to feed the fire,
and nations exhaust themselves for nothing?
v14: For the earth will be filled
with knowledge of the LORD’s glory,
as waters cover the sea.
</p>

<p>
v15: Woe to the one who makes his neighbor drink,
pouring out your fury and making them drunk
so you can look at their nakedness!
v16: You are filled with disgrace instead of glory.
Drink, you too, and expose your uncircumcision!
The cup in the LORD’s right hand will come around to you,
and filthy shame will cover your glory.
v17: For the violence done to Lebanon will engulf you,
as will the destruction of animals that terrified them,
because of human bloodshed and violence against the land,
the city, and all who live in it.
</p>

## Silent before the LORD

<p>
v18: What use is a carved image that its maker carved,
a cast image that teaches lies?
Its maker trusts his own creation,
making idols that cannot speak.
v19: Woe to the one who tells wood, ‘Wake up!’
and a silent stone, ‘Get up!’
Can it teach?
Look, it is coated with gold and silver,
but there is no breath at all inside it.
v20: But the LORD is in his holy temple.
Let all the earth be silent before him.”
</p>

## Notes

v04: “Faithfulness” can also be rendered “faith.” The Hebrew describes the righteous person’s faithful trust; the swollen or puffed-up person stands in contrast.

v05: The pinned Hebrew reads “wine.” The difficult opening is not silently changed to “wealth.”

v15–16: The drink image joins intoxication, exposure, and judgment. The term rendered “fury” can also suggest venom or poison; the command about uncircumcision follows the written wording.

v17: “Them” retains the Hebrew pronoun referring back to the animals rather than changing it to “you.”

## Vocabulary

v02: **Vision**: A received prophetic revelation, here to be written and awaited.

v20: **Holy temple**: The LORD’s sacred dwelling, before which the earth is summoned to silence.
