## Wind and deceit

<p>
v01: Ephraim feeds on wind
and chases the east wind all day.
He multiplies lies and violence.
They make a covenant with Assyria
and carry oil to Egypt.
v02: The LORD has a case against Judah.
He will call Jacob to account for his ways
and repay him for his deeds.
</p>

## Remembering Jacob

<p>
v03: In the womb he grasped his brother’s heel;
in his strength he struggled with God.
v04: He struggled with an angel and prevailed;
he wept and pleaded with him.
He found him at Bethel,
and there he spoke with us—
v05: the LORD, the God of Armies;
the LORD is the name by which he is remembered.
v06: So return with your God’s help.
Keep faithful love and justice,
and wait continually for your God.
</p>

## Wealth and remembrance

<p>
v07: A merchant has dishonest scales in his hand;
he loves to exploit.
v08: Ephraim says, ‘I have become rich;
I have gained wealth for myself.
In all my earnings they will find no guilt in me,
nothing that is sin.’
v09: But I am the LORD your God
from the land of Egypt.
I will make you live in tents again,
as in the days of the appointed festival.
v10: I spoke to the prophets;
I multiplied visions.
Through the prophets I gave comparisons.
v11: Is Gilead wickedness?
Surely they have become worthless!
At Gilgal they sacrifice bulls;
their altars too will be stone heaps
in the furrows of a field.
v12: Jacob fled to the countryside of Aram;
Israel served for a wife,
and for a wife he tended flocks.
v13: Through a prophet the LORD brought Israel up from Egypt;
through a prophet Israel was guarded.
v14: Ephraim has provoked bitter anger.
His Lord will leave his bloodguilt upon him
and repay him for his contempt.
</p>

## Notes

v03-05: The Jacob sayings echo heel-grasping, struggle, and Bethel, but the subjects of some “he/him” clauses are uncertain. The source says God spoke “with us,” not only with Jacob.

v07,09,11: The word for merchant is also “Canaan.” The tents evoke an appointed festival but may also suggest renewed wilderness life. Gilgal and “heaps” sound alike in Hebrew.

## Vocabulary

v05: **God of Armies**: A title of divine command over hosts or armies.

v12-13: **Guard**: Jacob guards flocks; a prophet guards Israel. The repeated root links the ancestral and national stories.
