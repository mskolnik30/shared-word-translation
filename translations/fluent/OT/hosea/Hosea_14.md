## Return with words

<p>
v01: Return, Israel, to the LORD your God,
for you have stumbled in your guilt.
v02: Take words with you and return to the LORD.
Say to him, ‘Take away all guilt and accept what is good.
We will offer the bulls of our lips.
v03: Assyria will not save us;
we will not ride warhorses.
We will no longer call the work of our hands “Our God,”
for with you the fatherless finds compassion.’
</p>

## Healing and growth

<p>
v04: “I will heal their turning away;
I will love them freely,
for my anger has turned away from him.
v05: I will be like dew to Israel.
He will blossom like a lily
and send down roots like Lebanon.
v06: His shoots will spread;
his splendor will be like an olive tree,
his fragrance like Lebanon.
v07: Those who live in his shade will return.
They will bring grain to life and blossom like a vine.
His renown will be like the wine of Lebanon.
v08: Ephraim, what more have I to do with idols?
I am the one who answers and watches over him.
I am like a flourishing cypress;
your fruit comes from me.”
</p>

## The ways of the LORD

<p>
v09: Who is wise enough to understand these things,
discerning enough to know them?
The LORD’s ways are straight:
the righteous walk in them,
but rebels stumble in them.
</p>

## Notes

v02: The pinned Hebrew says “bulls” and “our lips.” The image treats spoken response as an offering. “Fruit of our lips” reflects a different textual understanding and is not silently substituted.

v07-08: The shade’s referent and the speakers in verse 8 are not fully marked. This draft reads verse 8 as God’s speech; it can also be understood as a dialogue with Ephraim.

## Vocabulary

v04: **Freely**: Willingly, without compulsion; the word describes the love promised here.

v09: **Ways**: Paths of life and conduct; the same paths are walked by the righteous and stumbled over by rebels.
