## Evil exposed

<p>
v01: when I heal Israel,
Ephraim’s guilt and Samaria’s evil are exposed.
They practice deceit;
a thief breaks in,
and a raiding band plunders outside.
v02: They do not take it to heart
that I remember all their evil.
Now their deeds surround them;
they are right before my face.
v03: Their evil makes a king glad;
their lies delight rulers.
v04: They are all adulterers,
like an oven heated by a baker,
who stops stirring the fire
from the kneading of the dough until it rises.
v05: On the day of our king,
the rulers became sick with the heat of wine.
He stretched out his hand with scoffers.
v06: They bring their hearts near like an oven as they lie in wait.
Their baker sleeps all night;
in the morning it burns like a blazing fire.
v07: All of them burn hot as an oven
and consume their judges.
All their kings have fallen;
none of them calls to me.
</p>

## Ephraim among the nations

<p>
v08: Ephraim mixes himself among the peoples.
Ephraim is a flatbread never turned over.
v09: Foreigners consume his strength,
but he does not know it.
Gray hairs are scattered over him,
but he does not know it.
v10: Israel’s pride testifies against him,
yet they have not returned to the LORD their God
or sought him through all this.
v11: Ephraim is like a gullible dove without sense.
They call to Egypt;
they go to Assyria.
v12: As they go, I will spread my net over them;
I will bring them down like birds of the sky.
I will discipline them
as their congregation has been told.
v13: Woe to them, for they have wandered away from me!
Devastation to them, for they have rebelled against me!
I would redeem them,
but they speak lies about me.
v14: They do not cry to me from their hearts;
they wail on their beds.
They gather for grain and new wine
and turn against me.
v15: I trained them and strengthened their arms,
but they plot evil against me.
v16: They turn, but not upward;
they have become an unreliable bow.
Their rulers will fall by the sword
because of their raging tongues.
This will make them a laughingstock in Egypt.
</p>

## Notes

v04-06: The oven and baker form an extended image of heated plotting. Verse 6 preserves “their baker,” though the wording and some pronoun references are difficult.

v12,14,16: The congregation’s hearing in verse 12 is obscure. Verse 14 follows gathering rather than an emendation to self-cutting. “Not upward” in verse 16 preserves the terse wording; “not to the Most High” is an interpretation of it.

## Vocabulary

v08: **Unturned flatbread**: Bread left cooked on one side and uncooked on the other; the image exposes Ephraim’s condition without an added abstract label.

v13: **Redeem**: Secure release or rescue from another’s power.
