## Drought and a plea for help

<p>
v01: The **LORD**’s word that came to Jeremiah concerning the droughts:
v02: Judah mourns;
her gates languish.
They sink to the ground in mourning,
and Jerusalem’s cry rises.
v03: Her nobles send their servants for water.
They come to the cisterns and find none.
They return with empty jars,
ashamed and humiliated,
their heads covered.
v04: The ground is cracked,
for no rain has fallen on the land.
The farmers are ashamed;
they cover their heads.
v05: Even a doe in the field gives birth
and abandons her fawn,
because there is no grass.
v06: Wild donkeys stand on the bare heights,
panting for air like jackals.
Their eyes fail
because there is no vegetation.
v07: Though our wrongs testify against us,
**LORD**, act for your name’s sake.
Our acts of turning away are many;
we have sinned against you.
v08: Hope of Israel,
its savior in time of trouble,
why are you like a resident foreigner in the land,
a traveler who turns aside for the night?
v09: Why are you like a stunned man,
a warrior unable to save?
Yet you are among us, **LORD**;
your name is called over us.
Do not abandon us!
</p>

## Promises the LORD did not send

<p>
v10: This is what the **LORD** says about this people: “They love to wander like this; they have not restrained their feet. So the **LORD** does not accept them. Now he will remember their guilt and call their sins to account.”
</p>

<p>
v11: The **LORD** said to me, “Do not pray for this people’s good.
</p>

<p>
v12: When they fast, I will not hear their cry. When they offer burnt offerings and grain offerings, I will not accept them. Instead, I will bring them to an end by sword, famine, and plague.”
</p>

<p>
v13: I said, “Ah, Lord **GOD**! The prophets keep telling them, ‘You will not see the sword, and famine will not come upon you. I will give you lasting peace in this place.’”
</p>

<p>
v14: The **LORD** said to me, “Those prophets are prophesying lies in my name. I did not send them, command them, or speak to them. What they prophesy to you is a lying vision, divination, emptiness, and the deceit of their own hearts.
</p>

<p>
v15: So this is what the **LORD** says about the prophets who prophesy in my name though I did not send them, who say, ‘Sword and famine will not come to this land’: By sword and famine those prophets will meet their end.
</p>

<p>
v16: The people to whom they prophesy will be thrown out in Jerusalem’s streets because of famine and sword. No one will bury them—them, their wives, their sons, or their daughters. I will pour their own evil upon them.
</p>

## A lament and a plea

<p>
v17: Tell them this:
‘Let my eyes stream with tears
night and day, without stopping.
For my virgin daughter, my people,
is broken with a great fracture,
a terribly severe wound.
v18: If I go out into the field—look, those slain by the sword!
If I enter the city—look, those sick with hunger!
Prophet and priest alike
wander into a land they do not know.’”
v19: Have you utterly rejected Judah?
Does your soul loathe Zion?
Why have you struck us
so that there is no healing for us?
We hoped for peace, but no good came;
for a time of healing, but look—terror!
v20: We acknowledge our wickedness, **LORD**,
and the guilt of our ancestors;
we have sinned against you.
v21: For your name’s sake, do not spurn us;
do not dishonor your glorious throne.
Remember; do not break
your covenant with us.
v22: Can the nations’ empty idols bring rain?
Can the heavens give showers?
Is it not you, **LORD** our God?
We wait in hope for you,
for you have made all these things.
</p>

## Notes

v2–4: The gates and ground are personified as sharing Judah’s distress. The word rendered “cracked” can also describe being shattered or dismayed.

v8–9: The prayer compares God’s apparent absence to a temporary resident and God’s apparent inability to help to a stunned man. These are the lament’s questions, not a narrator’s explanation.

v17–18: The lamenting voice is not fully identified. The last line of verse 18 may describe going into an unfamiliar land or moving about without understanding what is happening.

## Vocabulary

v3: **Cisterns**: Reservoirs that store water, often rainwater.

v21: **Glorious throne**: An image associated with God’s sanctuary and rule.
