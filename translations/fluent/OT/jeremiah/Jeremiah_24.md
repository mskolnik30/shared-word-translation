## Two baskets of figs

<p>
v01: The **LORD** showed me two baskets of figs set before the temple of the **LORD**. This was after Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had exiled Jeconiah son of Jehoiakim, king of Judah, along with Judah's officials, craftworkers, and smiths, from Jerusalem and brought them to Babylon.
v02: One basket held very good figs, like the first ripe figs. The other held very bad figs, so bad they could not be eaten.
v03: The **LORD** asked me, “What do you see, Jeremiah?” I answered, “Figs. The good ones are very good, and the bad ones are very bad—so bad they cannot be eaten.”
</p>

## The meaning of the figs

<p>
v04: Then the word of the **LORD** came to me:
v05: “The **LORD**, the God of Israel, says: Like these good figs, I will regard with favor the exiles from Judah whom I sent from this place to the land of the Chaldeans.
v06: I will set my eyes on them for good and bring them back to this land. I will build them up and not tear them down; I will plant them and not uproot them.
v07: I will give them a heart to know me, that I am the **LORD**. They will be my people, and I will be their God, for they will return to me with their whole heart.
</p>

<p>
v08: “But like the bad figs, so bad they cannot be eaten, says the **LORD**, so I will treat Zedekiah king of Judah, his officials, and the remnant of Jerusalem—those left in this land and those living in the land of Egypt.
v09: I will make them an object of horror and harm among all the kingdoms of the earth, a reproach, a byword, a taunt, and a curse in every place where I drive them.
v10: I will send sword, famine, and plague against them until they are gone from the land I gave to them and their ancestors.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: The second group of skilled workers is traditionally called smiths or locksmiths; the precise occupational range is uncertain.

v05–08: The favorable figs represent people already deported. The bad figs represent Zedekiah and those remaining in the land or living in Egypt. The comparison does not equate remaining at home with divine approval.

## Vocabulary

v06: **Build and plant**: Recurring images of restoration, paired here with the reversal of tearing down and uprooting.
