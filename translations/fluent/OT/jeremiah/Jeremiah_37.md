## A request for prayer

<p>
v01: Zedekiah son of Josiah became king in place of Coniah son of Jehoiakim. Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon had made him king over the land of Judah.
v02: Neither he nor his servants nor the people of the land listened to the words the **LORD** spoke through Jeremiah the prophet.
v03: King Zedekiah sent Jehucal son of Shelemiah and the priest Zephaniah son of Maaseiah to Jeremiah the prophet with this request: “Please pray to the **LORD** our God for us.”
v04: Jeremiah was still coming and going among the people; they had not yet put him in prison.
v05: Pharaoh's army had marched out of Egypt. When the Chaldeans besieging Jerusalem heard the news, they withdrew from Jerusalem.
</p>

## The army will return

<p>
v06: Then the word of the **LORD** came to Jeremiah the prophet:
v07: “The **LORD**, the God of Israel, says: Tell the king of Judah who sent you to inquire of me: Pharaoh's army, which marched out to help you, is going back to its own land, Egypt.
v08: The Chaldeans will return, fight this city, capture it, and burn it down.
v09: The **LORD** says: Do not deceive yourselves by saying, ‘The Chaldeans will surely leave us.’ They will not leave.
v10: Even if you defeated the whole Chaldean army fighting against you, leaving only wounded men, each would rise from his tent and burn this city down.”
</p>

## Jeremiah arrested

<p>
v11: When the Chaldean army withdrew from Jerusalem because of Pharaoh's army,
v12: Jeremiah set out from Jerusalem for the land of Benjamin to receive his share there among the people.
v13: At the Benjamin Gate, a guard officer named Irijah son of Shelemiah, son of Hananiah, seized Jeremiah the prophet. “You are deserting to the Chaldeans!” he said.
v14: “That is a lie!” Jeremiah answered. “I am not deserting to the Chaldeans.” But Irijah would not listen. He arrested Jeremiah and brought him to the officials.
v15: The officials were angry with Jeremiah. They beat him and imprisoned him in the house of Jonathan the scribe, which they had made into a prison.
v16: Jeremiah was put into the dungeon, into its cells, and remained there many days.
</p>

## A private question and a plea

<p>
v17: Then King Zedekiah sent for him. In his palace the king secretly asked, “Is there a word from the **LORD**?” Jeremiah answered, “There is. You will be handed over to the king of Babylon.”
v18: Jeremiah also said to King Zedekiah, “What wrong have I done to you, your servants, or this people, that you have put me in prison?
v19: Where are your prophets who prophesied to you, ‘The king of Babylon will not come against you or against this land’?
v20: Now, my lord the king, please listen. Let my plea fall before you: do not send me back to Jonathan the scribe's house, or I will die there.”
v21: King Zedekiah ordered Jeremiah to be held in the guard courtyard and given a loaf of bread each day from the bakers' street, until all the bread in the city was gone. So Jeremiah remained in the guard courtyard.
</p>

## Notes

v12,16: The purpose of Jeremiah's journey is uncertain; the verb can concern receiving a share or withdrawing. The term translated “cells” in verse 16 is rare, so the exact prison layout is uncertain.

v17–21: The king seeks a private word but Jeremiah repeats the warning. The resulting change is a less severe place of confinement and a daily ration, not acquittal or freedom.

## Vocabulary

v21: **Guard courtyard**: An enclosed area associated with the royal guard, used here to hold Jeremiah.
