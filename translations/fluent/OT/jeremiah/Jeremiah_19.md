## The shattered jar

<p>
v01: This is what the **LORD** said: “Go and buy an earthenware jar from a potter. Take some of the elders of the people and some of the senior priests,
v02: and go out to the Valley of Ben-hinnom, at the entrance of the Potsherd Gate. There proclaim the words I tell you.
</p>

<p>
v03: Say: Hear the **LORD**’s word, kings of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem. This is what the **LORD** of hosts, Israel’s God, says: Look, I am bringing disaster on this place that will make the ears of everyone who hears it ring.
</p>

<p>
v04: They have abandoned me and made this place foreign. Here they have burned offerings to other gods, gods neither they nor their ancestors nor Judah’s kings knew. They have filled this place with innocent blood.
</p>

<p>
v05: They have built Baal’s high places to burn their sons in the fire as burnt offerings to Baal—something I never commanded, never spoke of, and that never entered my mind.
</p>

<p>
v06: So look, days are coming,” declares the **LORD**, “when this place will no longer be called Topheth or the Valley of Ben-hinnom, but the Valley of Slaughter.
</p>

<p>
v07: I will empty out Judah’s and Jerusalem’s plans in this place. I will make them fall by the sword before their enemies, by the hands of those seeking their lives. I will give their corpses as food to the birds of the sky and the animals of the earth.
</p>

<p>
v08: I will make this city a horror and an object of hissing. Everyone who passes will be appalled and hiss at all its wounds.
</p>

<p>
v09: I will make them eat the flesh of their sons and daughters. They will eat one another’s flesh in the siege and distress their enemies, those seeking their lives, inflict on them.
</p>

<p>
v10: “Then break the jar in front of the men who went with you.
</p>

<p>
v11: Tell them: This is what the **LORD** of hosts says: In the same way, I will break this people and this city, as someone breaks a potter’s vessel that can never be repaired. People will bury the dead at Topheth for lack of room to bury them elsewhere.
</p>

<p>
v12: This is what I will do to this place and its inhabitants,” declares the **LORD**. “I will make this city like Topheth.
</p>

<p>
v13: Jerusalem’s houses and the houses of Judah’s kings will be unclean like Topheth—all those houses whose roofs were used to burn offerings to all the host of heaven and pour drink offerings to other gods.”
</p>

## The warning in the temple court

<p>
v14: Jeremiah returned from Topheth, where the **LORD** had sent him to prophesy. He stood in the courtyard of the **LORD**’s house and said to all the people,
</p>

<p>
v15: “This is what the **LORD** of hosts, Israel’s God, says: Look, I am bringing on this city and all its towns every disaster I pronounced against it, because they stiffened their necks and would not listen to my words.”
</p>

## Notes

v2,7: The Potsherd Gate’s precise location is uncertain. “Empty out” in verse 7 echoes the sound of the Hebrew word for the jar.

v9,11: The siege’s cannibalism is stated directly. Unlike the workable clay of chapter 18, the broken vessel here cannot be repaired. Burial at Topheth follows the lack of space elsewhere.

## Vocabulary

v1: **Earthenware**: Fired clay pottery, brittle enough to shatter.

v13: **Host of heaven**: The heavenly bodies worshiped on the rooftops in this accusation.
