## Years of unheard warnings

<p>
v01: This word came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah. That was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.
v02: Jeremiah the prophet spoke it to all the people of Judah and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem:
v03: “For twenty-three years—from the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, until today—the word of the **LORD** has come to me. I have spoken to you again and again, but you have not listened.
v04: The **LORD** has sent all his servants the prophets to you again and again, but you have not listened or inclined your ear to hear.
v05: They said, ‘Turn back, each of you, from your evil way and your evil deeds. Then live on the soil the **LORD** gave you and your ancestors from long ago and for all time.
v06: Do not follow other gods to serve them and bow down to them. Do not provoke me with what your hands have made, and I will not bring harm on you.’
v07: ‘But you did not listen to me, declares the **LORD**. You provoked me with what your hands made, bringing harm on yourselves.’
</p>

## Seventy years and Babylon’s reckoning

<p>
v08: “So the **LORD** of hosts says: ‘Because you have not listened to my words,
v09: I will send for all the clans of the north, declares the **LORD**, and for my servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon. I will bring them against this land, its inhabitants, and all these surrounding nations. I will devote them to destruction and make them a horror, something to hiss at, and lasting ruins.
v10: I will put an end among them to the sound of joy and gladness, the voice of bridegroom and bride, the sound of the millstones, and the light of the lamp.
v11: This whole land will become a ruin and a horror, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon for seventy years.
v12: When seventy years are complete, I will call the king of Babylon, that nation, and the land of the Chaldeans to account for their guilt, declares the **LORD**. I will make it a lasting waste.
v13: I will bring upon that land all the words I have spoken against it, everything written in this scroll that Jeremiah prophesied against all the nations.
v14: For many nations and great kings will enslave them in turn. I will repay them according to their deeds and the work of their hands.’”
</p>

## The nations drink the cup

<p>
v15: This is what the **LORD**, the God of Israel, said to me: “Take from my hand this cup of wine, the wine of wrath, and make all the nations to whom I send you drink it.
v16: They will drink, stagger, and go mad because of the sword I am sending among them.”
v17: So I took the cup from the hand of the **LORD** and made all the nations to whom the **LORD** sent me drink it:
v18: Jerusalem and the towns of Judah, its kings and officials—to make them a ruin, a horror, something to hiss at, and a curse, as they are today;
v19: Pharaoh king of Egypt, his servants, his officials, and all his people;
v20: all the mixed peoples; all the kings of the land of Uz; all the kings of the land of the Philistines—Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and what remains of Ashdod;
v21: Edom, Moab, and the Ammonites;
v22: all the kings of Tyre, all the kings of Sidon, and the kings of the coastlands across the sea;
v23: Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who clip the edges of their hair;
v24: all the kings of Arabia and all the kings of the mixed peoples living in the wilderness;
v25: all the kings of Zimri, all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of Media;
v26: all the kings of the north, near and far, one after another; and all the kingdoms on the face of the earth. After them the king of Sheshach will drink.
</p>

<p>
v27: “Tell them: ‘The **LORD** of hosts, the God of Israel, says: Drink, get drunk, vomit, fall, and never rise again, because of the sword I am sending among you.’
v28: If they refuse to take the cup from your hand and drink, tell them: ‘The **LORD** of hosts says: You will certainly drink!
v29: For look, I am beginning to bring disaster on the city that bears my name. Do you expect to go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for I am summoning a sword against everyone who lives on the earth, declares the **LORD** of hosts.’
</p>

## The storm and the shepherds

<p>
v30: “You must prophesy all these words to them. Tell them:
The **LORD** roars from on high;
from his holy dwelling he raises his voice.
He roars mightily over his pasture.
Like those treading grapes, he shouts
against all the inhabitants of the earth.
v31: The uproar reaches the ends of the earth,
for the **LORD** has a case against the nations.
He brings all flesh to judgment;
he hands the wicked over to the sword,
declares the **LORD**.
v32: The **LORD** of hosts says:
Look! Disaster spreads from nation to nation;
a mighty storm rises
from the farthest reaches of the earth.
v33: “On that day, those slain by the **LORD** will lie from one end of the earth to the other. They will not be mourned, gathered, or buried. They will be dung on the surface of the ground.
</p>

<p>
v34: Wail and cry out, shepherds!
Roll in the dust, leaders of the flock!
Your days for slaughter and scattering have come;
you will fall like a precious vessel.
v35: The shepherds will have nowhere to flee;
the leaders of the flock will have no escape.
v36: Listen to the shepherds' cry,
the wailing of the leaders of the flock!
The **LORD** is devastating their pasture.
v37: The peaceful pastures lie silent
because of the **LORD**'s burning anger.
v38: Like a young lion he has left his lair.
Their land has become a horror
because of the fury of the oppressor
and because of his burning anger.”
</p>

## Notes

v03,11–12: The twenty-three years describe Jeremiah's speaking up to this address. The seventy years describe the nations' service to Babylon and the time before Babylon is called to account.

v23–26: The list retains the hair-clipping practice and the name Zimri without assigning uncertain modern identities. Sheshach represents Babylon by substituting letters paired from opposite ends of the Hebrew alphabet.

v34: The phrase translated “slaughter and scattering” is difficult. The text places the shepherds' downfall beside a precious vessel falling; it does not explain every part of the comparison.

v38: “The fury of the oppressor” follows the pointed Hebrew. A reading involving a destroying sword is also possible with a different textual understanding; the final reference to burning anger is clear.

## Vocabulary

v09: **Devote to destruction**: A severe expression of setting people or things apart for destruction; the violence is not merely loss of influence.

v15: **Cup of wrath**: A cup whose drinking represents undergoing the announced judgment.
