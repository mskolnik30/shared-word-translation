## A word to Baruch

<p>
v01: This is the word Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah when Baruch wrote these words on a scroll as Jeremiah dictated them, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah:
v02: “This is what the **LORD**, the God of Israel, says to you, Baruch:
v03: ‘You said, “Woe to me!
The **LORD** has added grief to my pain.
I am worn out with groaning
and can find no rest.”’
v04: “This is what you are to tell him: ‘The **LORD** says:
What I built, I am tearing down;
what I planted, I am uprooting—
and that means the whole land.
v05: And you seek great things for yourself?
Do not seek them.
I am bringing disaster on all flesh, declares the **LORD**.
But wherever you go, I will give you your life as plunder.’”
</p>

## Notes

v1: This word is dated to Jehoiakim's fourth year, earlier than the preceding events in Egypt. The book has not arranged every oracle chronologically.

v4–5: “The whole land” can also be understood as “the whole earth.” “Your life as plunder” repeats the image of surviving with one's life as the only gain; compare 21:9 and 39:18.

## Vocabulary

v5: **All flesh**: A broad expression for living humanity; the scope is deliberately sweeping.
