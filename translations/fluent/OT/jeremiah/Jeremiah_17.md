## Sin engraved on the heart

<p>
v01: Judah’s sin is written with an iron pen,
engraved with a hard stone point
on the tablet of their heart
and on the horns of your altars.
v02: Their children remember their altars and Asherah poles
beside flourishing trees
on the high hills.
v03: My mountain in the open country,
I will give your wealth and all your treasures as plunder,
your high places because of sin
throughout your territory.
v04: Through your own doing, you will let go of the inheritance I gave you. I will make you serve your enemies in a land you do not know. For you have kindled a fire in my anger; it will burn forever.
</p>

## Two kinds of trust

<p>
v05: This is what the **LORD** says:
“Cursed is the one who trusts in human beings,
makes flesh an arm of strength,
and turns their heart away from the **LORD**.
v06: That person will be like a shrub in the Arabah,
never seeing good when it comes,
living in scorched wilderness places,
a salt land where no one lives.
v07: Blessed is the one who trusts in the **LORD**,
whose confidence is the **LORD**.
v08: That person will be like a tree planted by water,
sending roots toward a stream.
It does not notice when heat comes;
its leaves stay green.
In a year of drought it is not anxious
and does not stop bearing fruit.”
v09: The heart is more deceitful than anything,
and desperately sick.
Who can understand it?
v10: “I, the **LORD**, search the heart
and test the innermost being,
to give each person according to their ways,
according to the fruit of their deeds.”
v11: Like a partridge that broods over eggs it did not lay
is one who gains wealth unjustly.
In the middle of life it will leave them;
in the end they will prove a fool.
v12: A glorious throne, exalted from the beginning,
is the place of our sanctuary.
v13: **LORD**, hope of Israel,
all who abandon you will be ashamed.
“Those who turn away from me will be written in the dust,”
for they have abandoned the **LORD**,
the spring of living water.
</p>

## The prophet’s appeal

<p>
v14: Heal me, **LORD**, and I will be healed;
save me, and I will be saved,
for you are my praise.
v15: They keep saying to me,
“Where is the **LORD**’s word?
Let it come!”
v16: But I have not rushed away from shepherding in your service,
nor have I longed for the dreadful day.
You know;
what left my lips was before you.
v17: Do not become a terror to me;
you are my refuge in the day of disaster.
v18: Let my persecutors be ashamed, not me.
Let them be terrified, not me.
Bring the day of disaster on them;
break them with a double breaking.
</p>

## At the gates on the Sabbath

<p>
v19: This is what the **LORD** said to me: “Go and stand at the People’s Gate, through which Judah’s kings enter and leave, and at all Jerusalem’s gates.
</p>

<p>
v20: Say to them: Hear the **LORD**’s word, kings of Judah, all Judah, and all Jerusalem’s inhabitants who enter through these gates.
</p>

<p>
v21: This is what the **LORD** says: Take care for your lives. Do not carry a load on the Sabbath day or bring one through Jerusalem’s gates.
</p>

<p>
v22: Do not take a load out of your houses on the Sabbath day or do any work. Keep the Sabbath day holy, as I commanded your ancestors.
</p>

<p>
v23: But they did not listen or incline their ears. They stiffened their necks, refusing to listen or accept correction.
</p>

<p>
v24: “If you truly listen to me,” declares the **LORD**, “bringing no load through this city’s gates on the Sabbath day and keeping the Sabbath holy by doing no work on it,
</p>

<p>
v25: then kings and officials sitting on David’s throne will enter this city’s gates, riding in chariots and on horses—they and their officials, the people of Judah and Jerusalem’s inhabitants. This city will be inhabited forever.
</p>

<p>
v26: People will come from Judah’s cities and the country around Jerusalem, from Benjamin’s territory, the Shephelah, the hill country, and the Negeb. They will bring burnt offerings, sacrifices, grain offerings, and frankincense, and bring thank offerings into the **LORD**’s house.
</p>

<p>
v27: But if you do not listen to me by keeping the Sabbath day holy and not carrying loads through Jerusalem’s gates on the Sabbath day, I will kindle a fire in her gates. It will consume Jerusalem’s palaces and will not be put out.”
</p>

## Notes

v1–3: The engraving point is made of a very hard material, traditionally “diamond”; “hard stone” avoids a precise mineral claim. Verse 2’s relationship to the surrounding lines is uncertain: it may concern children remembering the altars or attachment to altars compared with attachment to children. Verse 3 preserves the abrupt address to “my mountain.”

v8: The recorded reading says the tree does not “see” the heat, rendered “notice.” The written form can be read “fear,” as many translations do.

v10–11: “Innermost being” renders “kidneys.” The partridge saying uses an ancient image of brooding over another’s eggs to portray unjustly acquired wealth; it is not a modern zoological description.

v13,16: Verse 13 retains the source’s shift from “you” to “me.” Verse 16’s “shepherding in your service” renders a difficult phrase, literally “being a shepherd after you”; another proposed reading concerns not urging God to bring disaster.

## Vocabulary

v6: **Arabah**: A dry region; here the image emphasizes a barren, salty place.

v26: **Shephelah**: The low foothills west of Judah’s hill country.
