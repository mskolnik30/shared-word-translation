## Hunger in the wilderness

<p>
v01: They traveled on from Elim, and the whole community of Israelites came to the wilderness of Sin, between Elim and Sinai. It was the fifteenth day of the second month after they left Egypt.
v02: There in the wilderness, the whole community of Israelites grumbled against Moses and Aaron.
v03: The Israelites said to them, “If only we had died by the LORD’s hand in Egypt, where we sat by pots of meat and ate bread until we were full! You have brought us into this wilderness to starve this whole assembly to death.”
</p>

## Bread from heaven

<p>
v04: The LORD said to Moses, “I am going to rain bread from heaven for you. Each day the people are to go out and gather that day’s portion. In this way I will test whether they will walk in my instruction or not.
v05: On the sixth day, when they prepare what they bring in, it will be twice as much as they gather each day.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Moses and Aaron told all the Israelites, “This evening you will know that it was the LORD who brought you out of Egypt.
v07: In the morning you will see the LORD’s glory, because he has heard your grumbling against him. Who are we, that you grumble against us?”
v08: Moses added, “You will know this when the LORD gives you meat to eat in the evening and all the bread you need in the morning, because he has heard the grumbling you direct at him. Who are we? Your grumbling is against the LORD, not against us.”
</p>

<p>
v09: Moses told Aaron, “Say to the whole community of Israelites, ‘Come near before the LORD, because he has heard your grumbling.’”
v10: As Aaron spoke to the whole community of Israelites, they turned toward the wilderness, and there was the LORD’s glory, appearing in the cloud.
</p>

<p>
v11: The LORD spoke to Moses.
v12: “I have heard the Israelites’ grumbling. Tell them, ‘At twilight you will eat meat, and in the morning you will have your fill of bread. Then you will know that I am the LORD your God.’”
</p>

## Gathering each day’s portion

<p>
v13: That evening quail came up and covered the camp. In the morning a layer of dew lay around the camp.
v14: When the layer of dew lifted, there on the wilderness surface was something fine and flaky, fine as frost on the ground.
v15: When the Israelites saw it, they asked one another, “What is it?” They did not know what it was. Moses told them, “This is the bread the LORD has given you to eat.
v16: This is what the LORD has commanded: Each of you is to gather as much as you need to eat—an omer for each person. Count the people in your tent and gather for them.”
</p>

<p>
v17: The Israelites did so. Some gathered more, some less.
v18: But when they measured it by the omer, those who gathered more had nothing left over, and those who gathered less had no shortage. Each had gathered what they needed to eat.
</p>

<p>
v19: Moses told them, “No one is to leave any of it until morning.”
v20: But they did not listen to Moses. Some men left part of it until morning, and it bred worms and stank. Moses was angry with them.
</p>

<p>
v21: Morning by morning they gathered it, each as much as they needed to eat. When the sun grew hot, it melted.
</p>

## Rest on the seventh day

<p>
v22: On the sixth day they gathered twice as much bread, two omers for each person. All the community’s leaders came and reported this to Moses.
v23: He told them, “This is what the LORD said: Tomorrow is a complete rest, a holy Sabbath to the LORD. Bake what you want to bake and boil what you want to boil. Set aside whatever remains and keep it until morning.”
v24: They kept it until morning, as Moses had commanded. It did not stink, and there were no worms in it.
</p>

<p>
v25: Moses said, “Eat it today, because today is a Sabbath to the LORD. Today you will not find it in the field.
v26: Gather it for six days. On the seventh day, the Sabbath, there will be none.”
</p>

<p>
v27: On the seventh day some of the people went out to gather, but they found nothing.
</p>

<p>
v28: The LORD said to Moses, “How long will you all refuse to keep my commandments and instructions?
v29: See, the LORD has given you the Sabbath. That is why he gives you bread for two days on the sixth day. Each of you must stay where you are; no one is to leave their place on the seventh day.”
</p>

<p>
v30: So the people rested on the seventh day.
</p>

## Manna kept for later generations

<p>
v31: The house of Israel called it manna. It resembled coriander seed. It was white and tasted like wafers made with honey.
v32: Moses said, “This is what the LORD has commanded: ‘Keep an omer of it for your generations to come, so they can see the bread I fed you in the wilderness when I brought you out of Egypt.’”
</p>

<p>
v33: Moses told Aaron, “Take a jar, put an omer of manna in it, and place it before the LORD to be kept for your generations to come.”
v34: Aaron placed it before the testimony for safekeeping, as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v35: The Israelites ate manna for forty years, until they came to an inhabited land. They ate manna until they reached the edge of Canaan.
v36: An omer is one-tenth of an ephah.
</p>

## Notes

v1: Sin is the name of this wilderness, distinct from the English word ‘sin.’ The verse locates it between Elim and Sinai.

v8: The Hebrew begins with a clause about the LORD giving food, without an expressed main clause. ‘You will know this’ carries forward the statement in verses 6–7.

v18: The text reports that no one had an excess or a shortage after measuring. It does not explain how the differing amounts gathered came to meet each person’s need.

v23–29: The command to keep food overnight here is a stated exception to verse 19. The double portion accompanies the Sabbath rest.

v34–35: The testimony and the forty years look ahead beyond the present camp. The translation preserves those forward-looking references rather than moving them to a later point in the story.

## Vocabulary

v15, 31: Manna — man
The food’s name echoes man hu, the people’s question, ‘What is it?’

v16, 36: Omer — ‘omer
A dry measure. The chapter itself defines it as one-tenth of an ephah.

v23: Sabbath — shabbat
A day of stopping and rest, here the seventh day.

v34: Testimony — ‘edut
The covenant testimony, later specified as the tablets placed in the ark. The ark itself is not named in this verse.
