## The ark and its cover

<p>
v01: Bezalel made the ark from acacia wood, two and a half cubits long, a cubit and a half wide, and a cubit and a half high.
v02: He overlaid it with pure gold inside and out, and made a gold molding around it.
v03: He cast four gold rings for its four feet, two rings on one side and two on the other.
v04: He made poles of acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.
v05: He put the poles through the rings on the ark’s sides to carry the ark.
</p>

<p>
v06: He made an atonement cover of pure gold, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.
v07: He made two gold cherubim, hammering them into shape at the two ends of the cover,
v08: one cherub at one end and one at the other. He made the cherubim of one piece with the cover at its two ends.
v09: The cherubim spread their wings upward, sheltering the cover with their wings. They faced one another, with their faces turned toward the cover.
</p>

## The table

<p>
v10: He made the table from acacia wood, two cubits long, one cubit wide, and a cubit and a half high.
v11: He overlaid it with pure gold and made a gold molding around it.
</p>

<p>
v12: He made a rim a handbreadth wide around it, with a gold molding around the rim.
v13: He cast four gold rings for it and attached them at the four corners where its four legs were.
v14: The rings were close to the rim, to hold the poles for carrying the table.
v15: He made the poles for carrying the table from acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.
v16: He made the table’s utensils from pure gold: its dishes and ladles, and its bowls and pitchers for pouring offerings.
</p>

## The lampstand

<p>
v17: He made the lampstand from pure gold, hammering it into shape. Its base, shaft, cups, buds, and flowers were all of one piece with it.
</p>

<p>
v18: Six branches extended from its sides, three branches from one side and three from the other.
v19: One branch had three cups shaped like almond blossoms, with a bud and a flower, and another branch had three cups shaped like almond blossoms, with a bud and a flower. All six branches extending from the lampstand were made this way.
v20: The lampstand itself had four cups shaped like almond blossoms, with its buds and flowers.
v21: There was a bud beneath the first pair of branches, a bud beneath the next pair, and a bud beneath the last pair, for all six branches extending from it.
v22: The buds and branches were of one piece with it, the whole lampstand hammered from a single piece of pure gold.
</p>

<p>
v23: He made its seven lamps, its tongs, and its firepans from pure gold.
v24: He made the lampstand and all its utensils from one talent of pure gold.
</p>

## The incense altar and aromatics

<p>
v25: He made the incense altar from acacia wood, a cubit long and a cubit wide, forming a square, and two cubits high. Its horns were of one piece with it.
v26: He overlaid its top, all its sides, and its horns with pure gold, and made a gold molding around it.
v27: He made two gold rings for it beneath the molding, on its two opposite sides, to hold the poles used to carry it.
v28: He made the poles from acacia wood and overlaid them with gold.
</p>

<p>
v29: He made the holy anointing oil and the pure fragrant incense, the work of a perfumer.
</p>

## Notes

v3: “Feet” can also be understood as corners, as in 25:12.

v6–9: The cover is the atonement cover described in 25:17–22. Its two cherubim are part of the same work, facing each other and the cover.

v12, 16: The table’s exact rim construction and vessel shapes remain uncertain. The four vessel terms are preserved; the bowls and pitchers are named in the reverse order from 25:29.

v19–24: The repeated ornament descriptions distinguish three cups on each side branch from four on the central lampstand. The one talent of gold includes the lampstand and all its utensils.

## Vocabulary

v1: Ark — aron
A chest; here the chest made to hold the testimony.

v6: Atonement cover — kapporet
The sacred gold cover over the ark, traditionally called the mercy seat.

v7: Cherubim — keruvim
Winged beings represented here at the ends of the cover.

v12: Handbreadth — tofaḥ
A length based on the width of a hand.

v24: Talent — kikkar
A large ancient unit of weight; the original unit is retained.
