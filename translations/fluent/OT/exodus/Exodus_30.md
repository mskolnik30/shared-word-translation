## The Altar of Incense

<p>
v01: “You will make an altar for burning incense. You will make it of acacia wood.

v02: It will be one cubit long and one cubit wide; it will be square, and two cubits high. Its horns will be of one piece with it.

v03: You will overlay it with pure gold—its top, its sides all around, and its horns—and make a gold molding around it.

v04: You will make two gold rings for it below its molding, on two opposite sides. They will be holders for poles with which to carry it.

v05: You will make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.

v06: You will place it before the veil that is by the ark of the testimony, before the cover that is over the testimony, where I will meet with you.

v07: Aaron will burn fragrant incense on it; morning by morning, when he tends the lamps, he will burn it.

v08: When Aaron sets up the lamps at twilight, he will burn it, a continual incense before the LORD throughout your generations.

v09: You will not offer strange incense upon it, or burnt offering or grain offering, and you will not pour a drink offering upon it.

v10: Aaron will make atonement on its horns once a year. With the blood of the sin offering of atonement he will make atonement for it once a year throughout your generations. It is most holy to the LORD.”
</p>

## The Ransom at the Census

<p>
v11: The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

v12: “When you take a census of the children of Israel according to people who are counted, each will give a ransom for his life to the LORD when they are counted, so that there will be no plague among them when they are counted.

v13: This is what everyone who passes over to people who are counted will give: half a shekel according to the sanctuary shekel—the shekel is twenty gerahs—half a shekel as a contribution to the LORD.

v14: Everyone who passes over to people who are counted, from twenty years old and upward, will give the contribution to the LORD.

v15: The rich will not give more, and the poor will not give less than half a shekel when you give the contribution to the LORD to make atonement for your lives.

v16: You will take the atonement silver from the children of Israel and give it for the service of the tent of meeting. It will be for the children of Israel as a remembrance before the LORD, to make atonement for your lives.”
</p>

## The Bronze Basin

<p>
v17: The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

v18: “You will make a bronze basin with a bronze stand for washing. You will put it between the tent of meeting and the altar and put water in it.

v19: Aaron and his sons will wash their hands and their feet from it.

v20: When they enter the tent of meeting, they will wash with water so that they do not die. Or when they approach the altar to minister, to burn an offering by fire to the LORD,

v21: they will wash their hands and feet so that they do not die. It will be a perpetual statute for them, for him and his offspring throughout their generations.”
</p>

## The Holy Anointing Oil

<p>
v22: The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

v23: “Take for yourself the finest spices: five hundred shekels of flowing myrrh, half as much—two hundred fifty—of fragrant cinnamon, two hundred fifty of fragrant cane,

v24: five hundred of cassia, according to the sanctuary shekel, and a hin of olive oil.

v25: You will make from these a holy anointing oil, a fragrant mixture, the work of a perfumer. It will be a holy anointing oil.

v26: With it you will anoint the tent of meeting and the ark of the testimony,

v27: the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, the altar of incense,

v28: the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin and its stand.

v29: You will consecrate them, and they will be most holy. Whatever touches them will become holy.

v30: You will anoint Aaron and his sons and consecrate them to serve as priests to me.

v31: You will speak to the children of Israel, saying, ‘This will be my holy anointing oil throughout your generations.

v32: It will not be poured on ordinary human flesh, and you will not make any like it according to its composition. It is holy; it will be holy to you.

v33: Whoever mixes anything like it or whoever puts any of it on an outsider will be cut off from his people.’”
</p>

## The Holy Incense

<p>
v34: The LORD said to Moses, “Take fragrant spices: stacte, onycha, galbanum, and fragrant spices with pure frankincense, equal parts of each.

v35: Make from them incense, a fragrant mixture, the work of a perfumer, salted, pure, and holy.

v36: You will beat some of it very fine and put some of it before the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet with you. It will be most holy to you.

v37: The incense that you make according to its composition you will not make for yourselves. It will be holy to you for the LORD.

v38: Whoever makes any like it to smell it will be cut off from his people.”
</p>

## Notes

v01–10: The incense altar is distinct from the bronze altar of burnt offering. Its regular service is fragrant incense morning and evening.

v06: The altar stands before the veil, oriented toward the ark and the place of divine meeting beyond it.

v09: “strange incense” means incense unauthorized for this altar, not merely incense from a foreign culture.

v10: Annual atonement on the altar’s horns anticipates the later Day of Atonement ritual.

v12: The census payment is called a “ransom for his life.” Counting the people is thus accompanied by acknowledgment that Israel’s lives belong to the LORD.

v15: Rich and poor give the same half-shekel, creating equality in the census atonement contribution.

v18–21: Priestly washing is required under threat of death, showing that bodily cleansing is part of approaching sacred space.

v23–25: Identification of ancient aromatic substances is not always certain. TSW uses conventional names for the spices while retaining the given proportions.

v29: “whatever touches them shall become holy” marks the furnishings as intensely sacred rather than available for ordinary use.

v32–33: The anointing oil’s formula is restricted. Holiness includes protection of sacred use from imitation.

v34: Stacte, onycha, and galbanum are traditional identifications of rare Hebrew aromatic terms; precise botanical or mineral equivalence remains uncertain.

v35: “salted” may describe the incense as seasoned with salt or otherwise ritually prepared. TSW retains the concrete term.

## Vocabulary

v01: קְטֹרֶת (qetoret)
“Incense.” Fragrant material burned before the LORD.

v06: פָּרֹכֶת (parokhet)
“Veil.” The curtain before the Most Holy Place.

v12: כֹּפֶר נַפְשׁוֹ (kofer nafsho)
“Ransom for his life.” Payment associated with census atonement.

v13: גֵּרָה (gerah)
“Gerah.” A small weight unit; twenty gerahs make a shekel.

v18: כִּיּוֹר (kiyyor)
“Basin.” The bronze vessel used for priestly washing.

v23: מֹר דְּרוֹר (mor deror)
“Flowing myrrh.” A fragrant resin used in the anointing oil.

v25: רֹקֵחַ (roqeach)
“Perfumer.” A skilled compounder of aromatic mixtures.

v29: קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים (qodesh qodashim)
“Most holy.” The highest degree of sanctuary consecration.

v34: לְבוֹנָה (levonah)
“Frankincense.” A fragrant resin used in sacred incense.

v35: מְמֻלָּח (memullach)
“Salted” or “seasoned.” A preparation term for the incense mixture.
