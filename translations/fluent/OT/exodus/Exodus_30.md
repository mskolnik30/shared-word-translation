## The incense altar

<p>
v01: “Make an altar for burning incense, using acacia wood.
v02: It is to be a cubit long and a cubit wide, forming a square, and two cubits high. Its horns must be of one piece with it.
v03: Overlay its top, all its sides, and its horns with pure gold. Make a gold molding all around it.
v04: Make two gold rings beneath the molding, on its two opposite sides, to hold the poles used to carry it.
v05: Make the poles of acacia wood and overlay them with gold.
v06: Put it in front of the curtain that is before the ark of the testimony, facing the atonement cover over the testimony, where I will meet with you.
</p>

<p>
v07: Aaron is to burn fragrant incense on it every morning when he tends the lamps.
v08: He is also to burn it when he lights the lamps at twilight—a regular incense offering before the LORD throughout your generations.
v09: Do not offer unauthorized incense, a burnt offering, or a grain offering on it, and do not pour a drink offering on it.
v10: Once a year Aaron is to make atonement on its horns with blood from the sin offering for atonement. Once a year he is to make atonement for it throughout your generations. It is most holy to the LORD.”
</p>

## The census contribution

<p>
v11: The LORD spoke to Moses:
v12: “When you take a census of the Israelites, each man must give the LORD a ransom for his life when he is counted, so that no plague strikes them during the count.
</p>

<p>
v13: Everyone passing into the count must give half a shekel by the sanctuary standard—the shekel is twenty gerahs—half a shekel as a contribution to the LORD.
v14: Everyone counted who is twenty years old or older must give the LORD’s contribution.
v15: The rich must not give more than half a shekel, and the poor must not give less, when you give the LORD’s contribution to make atonement for your lives.
v16: Receive the atonement money from the Israelites and use it for the service of the tent of meeting. It will be a memorial for the Israelites before the LORD, to make atonement for your lives.”
</p>

## The bronze basin

<p>
v17: The LORD spoke to Moses:
v18: “Make a bronze basin with a bronze stand for washing. Put it between the tent of meeting and the altar, and fill it with water.
</p>

<p>
v19: Aaron and his sons are to wash their hands and feet with water from it.
v20: When they enter the tent of meeting, they must wash with water so they do not die. They must also wash when they approach the altar to serve by burning an offering by fire to the LORD.
v21: They must wash their hands and feet so they do not die. This is a lasting ordinance for Aaron and his descendants throughout their generations.”
</p>

## The anointing oil

<p>
v22: The LORD spoke to Moses:
v23: “Take the finest spices: five hundred shekels of flowing myrrh, half that amount—two hundred fifty—of fragrant cinnamon, and two hundred fifty of fragrant cane,
v24: five hundred of cassia, all by the sanctuary shekel, and a hin of olive oil.
v25: Blend them into holy anointing oil, a perfumer’s mixture. It is to be holy anointing oil.
</p>

<p>
v26: Use it to anoint the tent of meeting and the ark of the testimony,
v27: the table and all its utensils, the lampstand and its utensils, the incense altar,
v28: the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand.
v29: Consecrate them so they become most holy. Whatever touches them will become holy.
v30: Anoint Aaron and his sons and consecrate them to serve me as priests.
</p>

<p>
v31: Tell the Israelites, ‘This is to be my holy anointing oil throughout your generations.
v32: Do not pour it on human flesh, and do not make another mixture with the same proportions. It is holy; you must treat it as holy.
v33: Anyone who makes a mixture like it or applies any of it to an outsider will be cut off from their people.’”
</p>

## The incense

<p>
v34: The LORD said to Moses, “Take fragrant ingredients: stacte, onycha, and galbanum, along with pure frankincense, in equal quantities.
v35: Blend them into incense, the work of a perfumer, seasoned with salt, pure and holy.
v36: Grind some of it very fine and put it before the testimony in the tent of meeting, where I will meet with you. It is to be most holy to you.
v37: Do not make incense in these proportions for yourselves. You must treat it as holy to the LORD.
v38: Anyone who makes incense like it to enjoy its fragrance will be cut off from their people.”
</p>

## Notes

v6: The incense altar stands in front of the curtain, outside the innermost space containing the ark and its cover. The repeated “in front of” language relates the altar to that place of meeting.

v10: The repeated “once a year” concerns atonement with blood on the altar, distinct from the daily incense service in verses 7–8.

v12–16: The census payment is a ransom for each counted person’s life. The rich and poor owe the same half-shekel; the stated age is twenty and older. The text links the payment to protection from plague during the count.

v29: As in 29:37, the contact clause may describe becoming holy or require prior holiness. It concerns sacred status, not an automatic moral change.

v32–33: The ban on human flesh stands alongside the command to anoint Aaron and his sons in verse 30. In context it restricts ordinary or unauthorized use of this oil. “Outsider” refers to someone outside the authorized group.

v34–35: Stacte, onycha, and galbanum are traditional names for aromatic substances; their exact identification is uncertain. “Seasoned with salt” follows one understanding of the preparation term, which has also been understood as blending. [Translator discussion](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+30&version=NET).

v33, 38: “Cut off” names a severe sanction affecting a person’s place among the people; these verses do not specify how it is carried out.

## Vocabulary

v9: Unauthorized — zar
Foreign to what is prescribed or permitted here; the term need not identify an ethnic origin.

v12: Ransom — kofer
A payment associated here with the preservation of a person’s life.

v13: Shekel; gerah
Units of weight. This passage defines the sanctuary shekel as twenty gerahs.

v18: Basin — kiyyor
A vessel for the priests’ washing before service.

v25: Anointing oil — shemen mishḥah
The specially compounded oil used to set apart the sanctuary objects and priests.
