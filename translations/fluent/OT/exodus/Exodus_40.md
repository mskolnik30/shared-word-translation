## Setting up the sanctuary

<p>
v01: The LORD spoke to Moses:
v02: “On the first day of the first month, set up the tabernacle, the tent of meeting.
v03: Place the ark of the testimony inside and screen it with the dividing curtain.
v04: Bring in the table and arrange what belongs on it. Bring in the lampstand and light its lamps.
v05: Put the gold incense altar in front of the ark of the testimony, and hang the entrance screen for the tabernacle.
</p>

<p>
v06: Place the altar of burnt offering in front of the entrance to the tabernacle, the tent of meeting.
v07: Put the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and fill it with water.
v08: Set up the courtyard all around and hang the screen at its entrance.
</p>

## Consecrating the sanctuary and priests

<p>
v09: Take the anointing oil and anoint the tabernacle and everything in it. Consecrate it and all its utensils, so it becomes holy.
v10: Anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils. Consecrate the altar, so it becomes most holy.
v11: Anoint the basin and its stand, and consecrate it.
</p>

<p>
v12: Bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.
v13: Dress Aaron in the holy garments, anoint him, and consecrate him to serve me as priest.
v14: Bring his sons forward and dress them in tunics.
v15: Anoint them as you anointed their father, so they may serve me as priests. Their anointing will establish a lasting priesthood for them throughout their generations.”
</p>

<p>
v16: Moses did everything just as the LORD had commanded him; that is what he did.
</p>

## Moses sets everything in place

<p>
v17: The tabernacle was set up on the first day of the first month in the second year.
v18: Moses set up the tabernacle. He put its bases in place, raised its frames, inserted its crossbars, and erected its pillars.
v19: He spread the tent over the tabernacle and put the tent’s covering over it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v20: He took the testimony and placed it inside the ark, fitted the poles to the ark, and put the atonement cover on top.
v21: He brought the ark into the tabernacle and hung the screening curtain, screening off the ark of the testimony, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v22: He placed the table in the tent of meeting on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the dividing curtain.
v23: He arranged the bread on it before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v24: He placed the lampstand in the tent of meeting opposite the table, on the south side of the tabernacle.
v25: He lit the lamps before the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v26: He placed the gold altar in the tent of meeting in front of the dividing curtain.
v27: He burned fragrant incense on it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v28: He hung the entrance screen for the tabernacle.
v29: He placed the altar of burnt offering at the entrance to the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, and offered the burnt offering and grain offering on it, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v30: He placed the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar, and put water in it for washing.
v31: Moses, Aaron, and Aaron’s sons would wash their hands and feet with water from it.
v32: Whenever they entered the tent of meeting or approached the altar, they would wash, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v33: He set up the courtyard around the tabernacle and the altar, and hung the screen at the courtyard entrance. So Moses finished the work.
</p>

## The cloud and the glory

<p>
v34: Then the cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the LORD’s glory filled the tabernacle.
v35: Moses could not enter the tent of meeting because the cloud had settled on it and the LORD’s glory filled the tabernacle.
</p>

<p>
v36: Whenever the cloud lifted from the tabernacle, the Israelites would set out on their journeys.
v37: But if the cloud did not lift, they would not set out until the day it lifted.
v38: For the LORD’s cloud was over the tabernacle by day, and fire was in it by night, in the sight of the whole house of Israel throughout all their journeys.
</p>

## Notes

v5, 22–26: The table and lampstand stand north and south, outside the inner curtain but inside the tent. The incense altar is in front of that curtain; the ark is behind it.

v17: The installation takes place on the first day of the first month in the second year, following the departure from Egypt.

v31–32: Moses is included with Aaron and his sons among those who wash. The verbs describe repeated washing for service. [Translator discussion](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+40&version=NET).

v34–35: The cloud covers the tent and the glory fills the tabernacle. The repeated description includes Moses’ inability to enter.

v38: “In it” can refer to the cloud or the tabernacle; the Hebrew does not repeat the noun. The whole house of Israel sees the cloud by day and fire by night throughout the journeys.

## Vocabulary

v2: Tabernacle — mishkan
A dwelling place; the name connects the sanctuary with the promise to dwell among Israel in 25:8 and 29:45–46.

v10: Most holy — qodesh qodashim
Literally “holy of holies,” an intensified expression of sacred status, here applied to the altar.

v34: Glory — kavod
Honor, weight, or splendor; here the LORD’s glory fills the completed sanctuary.

v38: House of Israel — beit yisraʾel
Israel understood as one extended household or people.
