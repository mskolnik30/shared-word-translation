## The inner curtains

<p>
v01: “Make the tabernacle with ten curtains of finely twisted linen and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. Work cherubim into them with a skilled design.
v02: Each curtain is to be twenty-eight cubits long and four cubits wide. All the curtains must have the same dimensions.
v03: Join five curtains together, and join the other five together.
</p>

<p>
v04: Make blue loops along the edge of the end curtain in the first set. Do the same along the edge of the end curtain in the second set.
v05: Make fifty loops on the first curtain and fifty loops on the end curtain of the second set, so the loops face one another.
v06: Make fifty gold clasps and join the curtains together with them, so the tabernacle becomes one whole.
</p>

## The tent and outer coverings

<p>
v07: Make curtains of goat hair as a tent over the tabernacle. Make eleven curtains.
v08: Each curtain is to be thirty cubits long and four cubits wide. All eleven must have the same dimensions.
v09: Join five curtains into one set and six into another. Fold the sixth curtain double at the front of the tent.
</p>

<p>
v10: Make fifty loops along the edge of the end curtain in the first set and fifty loops along the edge of the curtain in the second set.
v11: Make fifty bronze clasps, insert them into the loops, and join the tent together so it becomes one whole.
v12: The extra length of the tent curtains—the remaining half-curtain—is to hang down over the back of the tabernacle.
v13: The extra cubit on each side along the length of the tent curtains is to hang down over the two sides of the tabernacle to cover it.
</p>

<p>
v14: Make a covering for the tent of rams’ skins dyed red, with a covering of taḥash skins on top.
</p>

## The supporting frames

<p>
v15: Make upright frames of acacia wood for the tabernacle.
v16: Each frame is to be ten cubits long and a cubit and a half wide.
v17: Each frame is to have two tenons aligned with one another. Make all the tabernacle’s frames this way.
</p>

<p>
v18: Make twenty frames for the south side of the tabernacle.
v19: Make forty silver bases beneath the twenty frames: two bases under one frame for its two tenons, and two bases under the next frame for its two tenons.
v20: For the tabernacle’s other side, facing north, make twenty frames,
v21: with forty silver bases—two bases under one frame and two under the next.
</p>

<p>
v22: For the rear of the tabernacle, facing west, make six frames.
v23: Make two more frames for the corners at the rear of the tabernacle.
v24: They are to be paired at the bottom and joined together at the top into one ring. Make both corner frames this way, one for each corner.
v25: There will be eight frames with sixteen silver bases, two bases under one frame and two under the next.
</p>

## The crossbars

<p>
v26: Make crossbars of acacia wood: five for the frames on one side of the tabernacle,
v27: five for the frames on the other side, and five for the frames at the rear, facing west.
v28: The middle crossbar is to run through the middle of the frames from end to end.
</p>

<p>
v29: Overlay the frames with gold and make gold rings on them to hold the crossbars. Overlay the crossbars with gold too.
v30: Set up the tabernacle according to the design you were shown on the mountain.
</p>

## The dividing curtain

<p>
v31: Make a curtain of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen, with cherubim worked into it by a skilled designer.
v32: Hang it on four acacia pillars overlaid with gold, with gold hooks, standing on four silver bases.
</p>

<p>
v33: Hang the curtain beneath the clasps and bring the ark of the testimony inside, behind the curtain. The curtain will separate the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place for you.
v34: Put the atonement cover on the ark of the testimony in the Most Holy Place.
v35: Place the table outside the curtain. Put the lampstand opposite the table on the south side of the tabernacle, and put the table on the north side.
</p>

## The entrance screen

<p>
v36: Make a screen for the tent’s entrance of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen, worked in embroidery.
v37: Make five acacia pillars for the screen and overlay them with gold. Their hooks are to be gold; cast five bronze bases for them.”
</p>

## Notes

v1–14: The inner layer has ten curtains in two groups of five, joined by gold clasps. The goat-hair tent has eleven in groups of five and six, joined by bronze clasps. Its extra length covers the sides and rear. These layers and their repeated measurements remain distinct.

v14: Taḥash is the uncertain skin or leather material named in 25:5.

v15–17, 24: The uprights may be solid boards or open frames. The projecting “hands” are understood as tenons fitted into bases. The corner-joining language is difficult and does not establish a complete modern construction plan. [Translator discussion](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+26&version=NET).

v31–37: The inner dividing curtain and the entrance screen differ: the inner curtain has cherubim and four pillars on silver bases; the entrance screen has embroidery and five pillars on bronze bases. The table and lampstand are outside the inner curtain but still inside the tabernacle.

## Vocabulary

v6: Clasp — qeres
A fastener linking the facing loops of curtain sets.

v17: Tenon — yad
A projecting part used to join wood to a supporting socket; the Hebrew literally says “hand.”

v31: Dividing curtain — parokhet
Also called the veil; it separates the two sacred areas inside the tabernacle.

v33: Most Holy Place — qodesh haqqodashim
Literally “holy of holies,” an intensified expression for the innermost sacred space.
