## The priestly garments

<p>
v01: From blue, purple, and scarlet yarn they made the garments for service in the sanctuary. They made Aaron’s holy garments, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## The ephod

<p>
v02: He made the ephod from gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen.
v03: They hammered the gold into thin sheets, and he cut it into threads to work into the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine linen as part of the skilled design.
v04: They made shoulder pieces for the ephod, joining it together at its two ends.
v05: Its woven waistband was of one piece with it and made the same way: gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v06: They prepared the onyx stones in gold settings, engraved like seals with the names of Israel’s sons.
v07: He put them on the ephod’s shoulder pieces as memorial stones for Israel’s sons, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## The breastpiece

<p>
v08: He made the breastpiece with a skilled design, like the ephod, from gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and finely twisted linen.
v09: They made the breastpiece square and folded double, a span long and a span wide when doubled.
v10: They set four rows of stones in it. The first row was carnelian, topaz, and emerald;
v11: the second row, turquoise, sapphire, and moonstone;
v12: the third row, jacinth, agate, and amethyst;
v13: and the fourth row, beryl, onyx, and jasper. The stones were mounted in gold settings.
v14: The twelve stones bore the names of Israel’s sons. Each was engraved like a seal with one name, for the twelve tribes.
</p>

<p>
v15: They made chains of pure gold for the breastpiece, twisted like cords.
v16: They made two gold settings and two gold rings, and attached the two rings to the two ends of the breastpiece.
v17: They attached the two gold cords to the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece.
v18: They attached the other ends of the two cords to the two settings, fastening them to the shoulder pieces on the front of the ephod.
</p>

<p>
v19: They made two more gold rings and put them at the two ends of the breastpiece, on its inner edge facing the ephod.
v20: They made two gold rings and attached them low on the front of the ephod’s two shoulder pieces, near the seam and above its waistband.
v21: They tied the breastpiece’s rings to the ephod’s rings with a blue cord, so it sat above the waistband and did not come loose from the ephod, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## The robe

<p>
v22: He wove the robe worn with the ephod entirely from blue yarn.
v23: The robe had an opening in the middle like that of a coat of armor, with an edge around the opening so it would not tear.
v24: They made pomegranates around the robe’s hem from twisted blue, purple, and scarlet yarn.
</p>

<p>
v25: They made bells of pure gold and placed them between the pomegranates all around the robe’s hem, between the pomegranates.
v26: A bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, all around the robe’s hem for service, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## Tunics and other garments

<p>
v27: They wove fine linen tunics for Aaron and his sons,
v28: the fine linen turban and ornate head coverings of fine linen, and linen undergarments of finely twisted linen,
v29: and the sash, embroidered in finely twisted linen and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## The holy diadem

<p>
v30: They made the plate of the holy diadem from pure gold and engraved on it, like a seal, “Holy to the LORD.”
v31: They fastened a blue cord to it to attach it high on the turban, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## The completed work brought to Moses

<p>
v32: So all the work on the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, was finished. The Israelites had done everything just as the LORD had commanded Moses; that is what they did.
</p>

<p>
v33: They brought the tabernacle to Moses: the tent and all its equipment—clasps, frames, crossbars, pillars, and bases;
v34: the covering of rams’ skins dyed red, the covering of taḥash skins, and the screening curtain;
v35: the ark of the testimony with its poles and the atonement cover;
v36: the table with all its utensils and the bread of the Presence;
v37: the pure lampstand, its lamps arranged in order, all its utensils, and the oil for the light;
v38: the gold altar, the anointing oil, the fragrant incense, and the screen for the tent’s entrance;
v39: the bronze altar with its bronze grating, its poles and all its utensils, and the basin with its stand;
v40: the courtyard hangings with their pillars and bases, the screen for its entrance, its ropes and pegs, and all the equipment for the service of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting;
v41: and the garments for service in the sanctuary, Aaron the priest’s holy garments, and his sons’ garments for serving as priests.
</p>

<p>
v42: The Israelites did all the work just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
v43: Moses inspected all the work and saw that they had done it just as the LORD had commanded. That is what they had done. Then Moses blessed them.
</p>

## Notes

v2–3: The account alternates between singular and plural makers. Verse 3 describes gold hammered into sheets and cut into threads for the textile work.

v10–13: The gemstone names are provisional traditional identifications, matching the same Hebrew list in 28:17–20. Ancient stone names do not correspond securely to modern mineral names.

v23: The comparison with a coat of armor concerns the reinforced opening; the rare term and exact construction remain uncertain. See 28:32.

v24: The Hebrew names twisted blue, purple, and scarlet yarn. Some understand “twisted” to imply linen, though linen is not explicitly named here; this rendering takes it as describing the colored yarn. [Translator discussion](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+39&version=NET).

v30–31: The plate is called the holy diadem, linking the inscription “Holy to the LORD” with the description in 28:36–38.

v32, 42–43: The repeated statements of completion and obedience lead to Moses’ inspection and blessing. They recall the earlier commands without replacing the detailed inventory.

## Vocabulary

v2: Ephod — efod
The priestly garment with shoulder pieces and an attached waistband.

v7: Memorial — zikkaron
Something that brings to remembrance; here the stones bear the names of Israel’s sons.

v9: Span — zeret
A length based on the outstretched hand, from thumb to little finger.

v30: Diadem — nezer
A sacred crown or mark of consecration; here the inscribed gold plate.
