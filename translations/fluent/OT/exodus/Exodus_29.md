## Consecrating Aaron and His Sons

<p>
v01: “And this is what you will do to them to consecrate them, so that they may serve as priests to me: take one young bull and two rams without blemish,

v02: unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers spread with oil. You will make them of fine wheat flour.

v03: You will put them in one basket and bring them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.

v04: You will bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.

v05: Then you will take the garments and clothe Aaron with the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastpiece, and bind the ephod upon him with its woven band.

v06: You will set the turban on his head and put the holy crown upon the turban.

v07: You will take the anointing oil, pour it on his head, and anoint him.

v08: Then you will bring his sons and clothe them with tunics.

v09: You will gird them with sashes, Aaron and his sons, and bind head coverings on them. The priesthood will be theirs by a perpetual statute. Thus you will fill the hands of Aaron and his sons.
</p>

## The Bull for the Sin Offering

<p>
v10: “You will bring the bull before the tent of meeting, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the bull.

v11: You will slaughter the bull before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

v12: You will take some of the blood of the bull and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger, and pour all the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.

v13: You will take all the fat that covers the entrails, the appendage of the liver, and the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, and burn them on the altar.

v14: But the flesh of the bull, its skin, and its dung you will burn with fire outside the camp. It is a sin offering.
</p>

## The First Ram

<p>
v15: “Then you will take one ram, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the ram.

v16: You will slaughter the ram, take its blood, and throw it against the altar on all sides.

v17: You will cut the ram into its pieces, wash its entrails and legs, and put them with its pieces and its head.

v18: You will burn the whole ram on the altar. It is a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.
</p>

## The Ram of Ordination

<p>
v19: “Then you will take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons will lay their hands on the head of the ram.

v20: You will slaughter the ram, take some of its blood, and put it on the lobe of Aaron’s right ear, on the lobes of his sons’ right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet. Then throw the rest of the blood against the altar on all sides.

v21: You will take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil and sprinkle it on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and the garments of his sons with him. Then he and his garments will be holy, and his sons and their garments with him.
</p>

## The Portion Placed in Their Hands

<p>
v22: “You will take from the ram the fat, the fat tail, the fat that covers the entrails, the appendage of the liver, the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, and the right thigh, for it is a ram of ordination,

v23: and one loaf of bread, one cake of bread with oil, and one wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that is before the LORD.

v24: You will put all these in the palms of Aaron and in the palms of his sons, and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD.

v25: Then you will take them from their hands and burn them on the altar with the burnt offering as a pleasing aroma before the LORD. It is an offering by fire to the LORD.

v26: You will take the breast from the ram of Aaron’s ordination and wave it as a wave offering before the LORD, and it will be your portion.

v27: You will consecrate the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution that was waved and lifted up from the ram of ordination, from what belongs to Aaron and his sons.

v28: It will belong to Aaron and his sons as a perpetual portion from the children of Israel, for it is a contribution. It will be a contribution from the children of Israel from their offerings of well-being, their contribution to the LORD.
</p>

## The Priestly Garments Continue through the Generations

<p>
v29: “The holy garments of Aaron will belong to his sons after him, to be anointed in them and to have their hands filled in them.

v30: Seven days the son who becomes priest in his place will wear them when he enters the tent of meeting to minister in the Holy Place.
</p>

## Eating the Ram of Ordination

<p>
v31: “You will take the ram of ordination and boil its flesh in a holy place.

v32: Aaron and his sons will eat the flesh of the ram and the bread that is in the basket at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

v33: They will eat those things by which atonement was made to fill their hands and consecrate them, but an outsider will not eat them, because they are holy.

v34: If any of the flesh of the ordination offering or any of the bread remains until morning, you will burn the remainder with fire. It will not be eaten, because it is holy.
</p>

## Seven Days of Ordination

<p>
v35: “Thus you will do to Aaron and his sons according to all that I have commanded you. Seven days you will fill their hands.

v36: Each day you will offer a bull as a sin offering for atonement. You will purify the altar when you make atonement for it, and you will anoint it to consecrate it.

v37: Seven days you will make atonement for the altar and consecrate it. Then the altar will be most holy; whatever touches the altar will become holy.
</p>

## The Continual Burnt Offering

<p>
v38: “And this is what you will offer upon the altar: two year-old lambs each day, continually.

v39: The one lamb you will offer in the morning, and the second lamb you will offer at twilight.

v40: With the first lamb will be a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a quarter hin of beaten oil and a quarter hin of wine as a drink offering.

v41: The second lamb you will offer at twilight. You will offer with it a grain offering and drink offering like those of the morning, as a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.

v42: It will be a continual burnt offering throughout your generations at the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD, where I will meet with you to speak to you there.
</p>

## I Will Dwell among the Children of Israel

<p>
v43: “There I will meet with the children of Israel, and the place will be consecrated by my glory.

v44: I will consecrate the tent of meeting and the altar. Aaron and his sons I will consecrate to serve as priests to me.

v45: I will dwell among the children of Israel and will be their God.

v46: They will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out from the land of Egypt so that I might dwell in their midst. I am the LORD their God.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Priesthood is established through washing, clothing, anointing, sacrifice, blood, food, and a seven-day rite. No single action alone constitutes the whole consecration.

v09: “fill the hands” is the Hebrew idiom for ordination. The priest’s hands are literally filled with sacred portions later in the ritual.

v10, v15, v19: Laying hands on the animal identifies Aaron and his sons with the sacrifice in distinct rites of purification, burnt offering, and ordination.

v20: Blood is placed on right ear, thumb, and big toe, symbolically marking hearing, action, and walking within priestly service.

v21: Blood from the altar and anointing oil are applied to priests and garments together, joining sacrificial and anointing holiness.

v22: The “fat tail” refers to the large fatty tail of ancient Near Eastern sheep breeds.

v24: “wave offering” describes a ritual presentation before the LORD. The exact physical motion is not fully described by the text.

v33: The priests eat the food “by which atonement was made,” joining sacred meal and ordination. The meal is restricted because the food is holy.

v37: “whatever touches the altar shall become holy” expresses the altar’s intensified sacred status and the danger of casual contact with holy space.

v38–42: The daily lambs establish a continual rhythm of worship morning and evening, independent of special festivals.

v46: The exodus reaches its theological purpose in divine dwelling: Israel is brought out of Egypt so that the LORD may dwell in their midst.

## Vocabulary

v01: קָדַשׁ (qadash)
“To consecrate.” To set apart for sacred service.

v07: שֶׁמֶן הַמִּשְׁחָה (shemen ha-mishchah)
“Anointing oil.” Oil used to install and consecrate priests.

v09: מִלֵּא יָד (mille yad)
“To fill the hand.” The Hebrew idiom for ordination.

v14: חַטָּאת (chatta't)
“Sin offering” or “purification offering.” A sacrifice addressing impurity and guilt.

v18: רֵיחַ נִיחוֹחַ (reach nichoach)
“Pleasing aroma.” Formula describing an offering accepted before the LORD.

v22: אֵיל מִלֻּאִים (eil millu'im)
“Ram of ordination.” The ram used in filling the priests’ hands.

v24: תְּנוּפָה (tenufah)
“Wave offering.” A sacred portion presented before the LORD.

v28: שְׁלָמִים (shelamim)
“Offerings of well-being.” Sacrifices associated with peace, wholeness, and shared eating.

v36: כִּפֶּר (kipper)
“To make atonement.” To address guilt or impurity and restore sacred order.

v38: תָּמִיד (tamid)
“Continually.” Regular, ongoing sanctuary service.
