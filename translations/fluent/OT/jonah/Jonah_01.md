## Jonah goes the other way

<p>
v01: The word of the LORD came to Jonah son of Amittai:
v02: “Get up and go to Nineveh, the great city, and proclaim against it, because their evil has come up before me.”
v03: Jonah got up to flee to Tarshish, away from the LORD’s presence. He went down to Joppa, found a ship bound for Tarshish, paid its fare, and went down aboard to travel with them to Tarshish, away from the LORD’s presence.
</p>

## The storm

<p>
v04: But the LORD hurled a great wind onto the sea. A great storm rose on the sea, and the ship was on the point of breaking apart.
v05: The sailors were afraid, and each cried out to his god. They hurled the cargo into the sea to lighten the ship. Meanwhile Jonah had gone down into the hold, lain down, and fallen fast asleep.
v06: The captain came to him and said, “What are you doing asleep? Get up and call on your god! Perhaps the god will take notice of us, and we will not perish.”
v07: The sailors said to one another, “Come, let us cast lots to find out who has brought this disaster on us.” They cast lots, and the lot fell on Jonah.
v08: They asked him, “Tell us, please, who is responsible for this disaster? What is your work? Where do you come from? What is your country? What people do you belong to?”
v09: He answered, “I am a Hebrew. I fear the LORD, the God of heaven, who made the sea and the dry land.”
v10: The men became terribly afraid and asked, “What have you done?” They knew he was fleeing from the LORD’s presence, because he had told them.
</p>

## Into the sea

<p>
v11: They asked, “What should we do with you so the sea will calm down for us?” The sea was growing more and more stormy.
v12: He told them, “Pick me up and hurl me into the sea. Then the sea will calm down for you. I know this great storm has come upon you because of me.”
v13: Instead, the men rowed hard to get back to dry land, but they could not. The sea kept growing stormier against them.
v14: Then they called to the LORD: “Please, LORD, do not let us perish for this man’s life, and do not charge us with innocent blood. For you, LORD, have done as you pleased.”
v15: They picked Jonah up and hurled him into the sea, and the sea stopped raging.
v16: Then the men feared the LORD greatly. They offered a sacrifice to the LORD and made vows.
v17: The LORD appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah, and Jonah was inside the fish for three days and three nights.
</p>

## Notes

v03-05: The repeated going down, Tarshish, and flight from the LORD’s presence are deliberate narrative features. “Hurled” links the wind, cargo, and Jonah’s later casting into the sea.

v17: The creature is a “great fish,” without a species identification. The three days and three nights are retained as stated. This verse is 2:1 in the Hebrew source.

## Vocabulary

v07: **Lots**: Objects used to make a selection; the sailors use them here to seek the cause of the disaster.

v09,16: **Fear**: The repeated word moves from terror in the storm to fear directed toward the LORD.
