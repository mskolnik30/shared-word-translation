## A prayer from the depths

<p>
v01: From inside the fish, Jonah prayed to the LORD his God.
v02: He said:
“In my distress I called to the LORD, and he answered me.
From the belly of Sheol I cried for help,
and you heard my voice.
v03: You cast me into the depths, into the heart of the seas.
The current surrounded me;
all your breakers and waves swept over me.
v04: I said, ‘I have been driven out of your sight,
yet I will look again toward your holy temple.’
v05: Water closed over me to the throat;
the deep surrounded me.
Seaweed was wrapped around my head.
v06: I went down to the roots of the mountains;
the earth’s bars closed behind me forever.
But you brought my life up from the pit,
LORD my God.
v07: As my life was ebbing away, I remembered the LORD.
My prayer came to you, into your holy temple.
v08: Those who cling to worthless vanities
abandon their faithful love.
v09: But I will sacrifice to you with a voice of thanksgiving.
What I have vowed, I will fulfill.
Deliverance belongs to the LORD!”
v10: The LORD spoke to the fish, and it vomited Jonah onto dry land.
</p>

## Notes

v04,08: Verse 4 can be heard as confidence about looking again or as a question. “Their faithful love” in verse 8 may mean loyalty they abandon or kindness available to them; the possessive is retained.

## Vocabulary

v02: **Sheol**: The realm of the dead; the prayer describes the threat of death through its belly and the earth’s bars.

v09: **Deliverance**: Rescue; the closing line assigns it to the LORD.
