## Jonah’s anger

<p>
v01: But to Jonah this seemed a great wrong, and he burned with anger.
v02: He prayed to the LORD: “Please, LORD, isn’t this what I said while I was still in my own country? That is why I fled toward Tarshish in the first place. I knew you were a gracious and compassionate God, slow to anger and rich in faithful love, who relents from bringing disaster.
v03: Now, LORD, please take my life from me. I would be better off dead than alive.”
v04: The LORD asked, “Is it right for you to be angry?”
</p>

## The plant and the question

<p>
v05: Jonah went out of the city and sat to its east. He made himself a shelter there and sat in its shade to see what would happen to the city.
v06: The LORD God appointed a plant, which grew up over Jonah to shade his head and relieve his distress. Jonah was greatly delighted with the plant.
v07: At dawn the next day, God appointed a worm. It attacked the plant, and the plant withered.
v08: When the sun rose, God appointed a scorching east wind. The sun beat down on Jonah’s head until he grew faint. He asked to die, saying, “I would be better off dead than alive.”
v09: God asked Jonah, “Is it right for you to be angry about the plant?” He answered, “It is right for me to be angry—angry enough to die!”
v10: The LORD said, “You have compassion for the plant, though you did not labor over it or make it grow. It came into being in a night and perished in a night.
v11: Should I not have compassion for Nineveh, the great city, with more than 120,000 people who cannot distinguish their right hand from their left—and many animals?”
</p>

## Notes

v01-02: Jonah regards the spared city as a “great wrong.” The wording echoes the book’s evil/disaster vocabulary; it expresses Jonah’s complaint, not the narrator’s endorsement.

v04,06,08-09: The anger question may concern its rightness or intensity. The plant’s species and the rare adjective for the east wind are uncertain; the draft does not specify a castor plant or gourd as settled fact.

v11: The right-hand/left-hand description may concern children or a broader lack of discernment; the text says people, not explicitly infants. It gives more than 120,000 and ends with many animals and an unanswered question.

## Vocabulary

v06-08: **Appointed**: The same verb links fish, plant, worm, and wind as agents in the story.

v10-11: **Compassion**: The repeated concern for plant and city carries the final comparison.
