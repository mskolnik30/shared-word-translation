# Ecclesiastes 1

<p>
v01: The words of Qoheleth, son of David, king in Jerusalem.
</p>

<p>
v02: Vapor of vapors, says Qoheleth,
vapor of vapors—everything is vapor.

v03: What gain does a person have from all their labor
in which they labor under the sun?

v04: A generation goes, and a generation comes,
but the earth stands forever.

v05: The sun rises, and the sun sets;
it hurries to its place, and there it rises.

v06: Going toward the south, and turning toward the north,
turning, turning goes the wind;
and upon its circuits the wind returns.

v07: All the streams go to the sea,
yet the sea is not full;
to the place where the streams go,
there they return to go again.

v08: Everything are weary;
no one can speak of them.
The eye is not satisfied with seeing,
nor is the ear filled with hearing.

v09: What has been is what will be,
and what has been done is what will be done;
there is nothing new under the sun.

v10: Is there anything of which one says, “Look—this is new”?
It has already been
in ages before us.

v11: There is no remembrance of the former ones,
nor will there be remembrance of the later ones to come
among people who come afterward.
</p>

<p>
v12: I, Qoheleth, was king over Israel in Jerusalem.
</p>

<p>
v13: I gave my heart to seek and to explore by wisdom all that is done under heaven. It is a grievous task that God has given to human beings to occupy them.
</p>

<p>
v14: I have seen all the works that are done under the sun, and see: everything is vapor and a chasing after wind.
</p>

<p>
v15: What is crooked cannot be made straight, and what is lacking cannot be counted.
</p>

<p>
v16: I spoke with my heart, saying, “Look, I have grown great and increased in wisdom beyond everyone who were before me over Jerusalem, and my heart has seen much wisdom and knowledge.”
</p>

<p>
v17: I gave my heart to know wisdom and to know madness and folly. I knew that this too is a pursuit of wind.
</p>

<p>
v18: For with much wisdom comes much vexation, and one who increases knowledge increases pain.
</p>

## Notes

v08: “All things are weary.” The Hebrew word can also mean “words” or “matters.” The line may speak both of the weariness of all things and of the inability of speech to exhaustively express them.

## Vocabulary

v01: קֹהֶלֶת (qohelet)
“Qoheleth; Teacher; Preacher.” The speaker’s title is grammatically feminine in form, while the surrounding description presents a Davidic king.

v02: הֶבֶל (hevel)
“Vapor,” “breath,” or mist. In this chapter it names the passing and ungraspable quality of everything pursued under the sun.

v03: יִתְרוֹן (yitron)
“Gain,” “advantage,” or what remains over. Qoheleth uses it to ask whether labor yields anything lasting.

v14: רְעוּת רוּחַ (re'ut ruach)
“Wind; Spirit; A chasing after wind.”
