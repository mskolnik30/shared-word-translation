## Bildad: God does not twist justice

<p>
v01: Then Bildad the Shuhite answered:
v02: “How long will you say these things?
Your words are a mighty wind!
v03: Does God twist justice?
Does the Almighty twist what is right?
v04: If your children sinned against him,
he handed them over to their rebellion.
v05: But if you seek God earnestly
and plead with the Almighty,
v06: if you are pure and upright,
then he will rouse himself for you
and restore your rightful dwelling.
v07: Your beginning will seem small;
your future will grow very great.
</p>

## Learn from earlier generations

<p>
v08: Ask the former generation,
and attend to what their ancestors discovered.
v09: We are only of yesterday and know nothing;
our days on earth are a shadow.
v10: Will they not teach you and tell you,
bring out words from their hearts?
</p>

<p>
v11: Can papyrus grow tall without a marsh?
Can reeds flourish without water?
v12: Still green and uncut,
it withers before any other grass.
v13: Such are the paths of all who forget God;
the hope of the godless will perish.
v14: His confidence is fragile;
what he trusts is a spider’s house.
v15: He leans on his house, but it does not stand;
he clings to it, but it does not hold.
v16: He is like a lush plant in the sunlight,
its shoots spreading over the garden.
v17: Its roots wind around a heap of stones;
it searches out a home among the rocks.
v18: If it is swallowed up from its place,
that place denies it: ‘I never saw you.’
v19: Such is the joy of its way,
and others spring up from the dust.
</p>

<p>
v20: God will not reject a person of integrity,
nor take evildoers by the hand.
v21: He will yet fill your mouth with laughter
and your lips with shouts of joy.
v22: Those who hate you will be clothed with shame,
and the tent of the wicked will be gone.”
</p>

## Notes

v04: Bildad attributes the children’s death to their sin. This is his accusation, not a statement by the narrator.

v14: The wording describing confidence as fragile or cut off is rare. The spider’s house makes its insecurity vivid.

v17: The roots are pictured seeing or reaching toward a house of stones. The ancient image is retained.

v19: “Joy” can be heard ironically: the plant’s apparent flourishing ends in replacement by another.

## Vocabulary

v13: Godless
Here, a person estranged from or disregarding God; Bildad applies the category within his argument.
