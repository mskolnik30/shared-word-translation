## Elihu becomes angry

<p>
v01: These three men stopped answering Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.
v02: Then Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, became angry. He was angry with Job for declaring himself right rather than God.
v03: He was also angry with Job’s three friends, because they had found no answer and yet had condemned Job.
v04: Elihu had waited while Job spoke, because the others were older than he was.
v05: But when Elihu saw that the three men had no answer left, his anger flared.
</p>

## Elihu: Age alone does not make wisdom

<p>
v06: Elihu son of Barachel the Buzite said:
“I am young, and you are aged;
that is why I held back,
afraid to tell you what I know.
v07: I thought, ‘Let age speak;
let many years make wisdom known.’
v08: But it is the spirit within a mortal,
the breath of the Almighty, that gives understanding.
v09: Many years do not make people wise,
nor do the aged alone understand justice.
v10: So I say, ‘Listen to me;
I too will tell you what I know.’
v11: I waited for your words,
listened for your insights
as you searched for what to say.
v12: I gave you my attention,
but none of you refuted Job
or answered his words.
v13: Do not say, ‘We have found wisdom;
God must defeat him, not a human.’
v14: Job has not directed his words against me,
and I will not answer him with your arguments.
</p>

## I too will speak

<p>
v15: They are dismayed and answer no more;
words have deserted them.
v16: Must I wait because they do not speak,
because they stand there without another answer?
v17: I too will give my answer;
I too will tell what I know.
v18: For I am full of words;
the spirit within me presses hard.
v19: My belly is like wine with no vent,
like new wineskins ready to burst.
v20: I must speak to find relief;
I must open my lips and answer.
v21: I will show no one favoritism
and flatter no human being.
v22: For I do not know how to flatter;
my Maker would soon carry me away.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The pinned text says the three friends condemned Job despite finding no answer. It is not silently changed to condemning God.

v09: “Many years” interprets a word that can name the great or the aged. The next clause explicitly names elders.

v19: The image names new wineskins. The draft preserves this rather than importing the different wineskin saying from the Gospels.

## Vocabulary

The son of Barachel the Buzite, from the family of Ram.

v08: Breath of the Almighty
