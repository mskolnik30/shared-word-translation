## Bildad: Who can be pure before God?

<p>
v01: Then Bildad the Shuhite answered:
v02: “Dominion and dread belong to him;
he makes peace in his heights.
v03: Can his troops be numbered?
On whom does his light not rise?
v04: How can a mortal be righteous before God?
How can one born of woman be pure?
v05: Even the moon is not bright,
and the stars are not pure in his eyes.
v06: How much less a mortal, a maggot,
a human being, a worm!”
</p>

## Notes

v06: Maggot and worm are Bildad’s images for human frailty and uncleanness within his argument. They are not detached statements by the narrator.

## Vocabulary

v02: Dominion
The authority to rule.
