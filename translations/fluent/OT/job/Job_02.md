## A second challenge

<p>
v01: Another day the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD. The accuser also came among them to present himself before the LORD.
v02: The LORD asked the accuser, “Where have you come from?” He answered, “From roaming the earth and walking about on it.”
v03: The LORD said, “Have you considered my servant Job? There is no one like him on earth: a man of integrity, upright, fearing God and turning away from evil. He still holds firmly to his integrity, though you incited me against him to destroy him without cause.”
v04: The accuser answered the LORD, “Skin for skin! A man will give everything he has for his own life.
v05: But stretch out your hand and touch his bone and flesh. He will surely curse you to your face!”
v06: The LORD said to the accuser, “He is in your hands. Only spare his life.”
</p>

## Job is struck

<p>
v07: The accuser left the LORD’s presence and struck Job with terrible sores from the soles of his feet to the crown of his head.
v08: Job took a piece of broken pottery to scrape himself as he sat among the ashes.
v09: His wife said to him, “Are you still holding firmly to your integrity? Curse God and die!”
v10: He answered, “You are speaking like one of the foolish women. Should we accept good from God and not accept adversity?” Through all this, Job did not sin with his lips.
</p>

## Three friends arrive

<p>
v11: Three of Job’s friends heard about all the adversity that had come upon him. Each set out from his own home: Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite. They agreed to come together to mourn with him and comfort him.
v12: When they saw him from a distance, they did not recognize him. They wept aloud, tore their robes, and threw dust into the air over their heads.
v13: They sat with him on the ground for seven days and seven nights. No one spoke a word to him, because they saw how great his pain was.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The words “without cause” explicitly disconnect Job’s suffering from a prior offense that would justify it. The divine speech also retains the accuser’s role in inciting the attack.

v09: “Curse” translates the same euphemistic use of “bless” found in chapter 1.

v10: The narrator says Job did not sin “with his lips.” The qualification is part of the text.

## Vocabulary

v03: Integrity
The wholeness of character that Job continues to hold to.

v11: Comfort
The friends first come to grieve with Job and offer consolation.
