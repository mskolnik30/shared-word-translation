## Job holds to his integrity

<p>
v01: Job continued his discourse:
v02: “As God lives, who has denied me justice,
and the Almighty, who has made my soul bitter,
v03: as long as breath remains in me
and God’s breath is in my nostrils,
v04: my lips will speak no injustice,
and my tongue will utter no deceit.
v05: I will never admit that you are right!
Until I die, I will not let go of my integrity.
v06: I hold firmly to my righteousness and will not release it;
my heart reproaches me for none of my days.
</p>

## The fate of the wicked

<p>
v07: Let my enemy fare like the wicked,
and anyone who rises against me like the unjust.
v08: What hope has the godless when he is cut off,
when God takes away his life?
v09: Will God hear his cry
when trouble comes upon him?
v10: Will he delight in the Almighty
and call on God at all times?
v11: I will teach you about God’s hand;
I will not conceal what is with the Almighty.
v12: You have all seen this yourselves.
Why, then, do you keep talking such emptiness?
v13: This is the wicked person’s portion from God,
the inheritance the ruthless receive from the Almighty:
v14: however many his children, they are destined for the sword;
his descendants will not have enough bread.
v15: Those who survive him will be buried by death,
and his widows will not weep.
v16: Though he heaps up silver like dust
and piles up clothing like clay,
v17: he may pile it up, but the righteous will wear it;
the innocent will divide the silver.
v18: He builds his house like a moth’s,
like a shelter a watchman makes.
v19: He lies down wealthy, but will not be gathered;
he opens his eyes, and it is gone.
v20: Terrors overtake him like a flood;
a storm carries him off in the night.
v21: The east wind lifts him away, and he is gone;
it sweeps him out of his place.
v22: It hurls itself at him without pity;
he struggles to flee its grasp.
v23: It claps its hands at him
and whistles him out of his place.
</p>

## Notes

v08: The difficult verb can concern being cut off or making gain. Either way, Job asks what hope remains when God takes life.

v13–23: These lines resemble the friends’ descriptions of the wicked. The received text gives no new speaker; the draft keeps them in Job’s speech.

v19: “Will not be gathered” is a difficult expression. It may concern burial, gathering wealth, or no longer doing so. The draft leaves the image unresolved.

v22–23: The east wind can remain the subject of these personified actions; God or other people have also been understood as the agents.

## Vocabulary

v18: Moth’s house
An image of a fragile dwelling.

v21: East wind
A destructive wind that carries the man away.
