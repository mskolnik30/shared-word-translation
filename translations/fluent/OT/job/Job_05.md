## Eliphaz: Seek God

<p>
v01: Call out now! Is anyone answering you?
To which of the holy ones will you turn?
v02: Resentment kills the fool,
and jealousy brings death to the simple.
v03: I have seen a fool taking root,
but suddenly I cursed his dwelling.
v04: His children are far from safety;
they are crushed at the city gate, with no one to rescue them.
v05: The hungry eat his harvest,
taking it even from among thorns,
and the thirsty pant after his wealth.
v06: For wrongdoing does not sprout from the dust,
nor does trouble grow from the ground.
v07: A human being is born to trouble
as surely as sparks fly upward.
</p>

<p>
v08: But I would seek God;
I would lay my case before him.
v09: He does great things beyond searching out,
wonders beyond counting.
v10: He gives rain to the earth
and sends water over the fields.
v11: He lifts the lowly high
and raises mourners to safety.
v12: He frustrates the schemes of the shrewd,
so their hands achieve no success.
v13: He catches the wise in their own cunning,
and the plans of the devious are swept away.
v14: They meet darkness in the daytime
and grope at noon as though it were night.
v15: But he saves the poor from the sword of their mouths
and from the hand of the strong.
v16: So the poor have hope,
and injustice shuts its mouth.
</p>

## The blessing of correction

<p>
v17: Happy is the person God corrects!
Do not reject the discipline of the Almighty.
v18: For he wounds, but he binds up;
he strikes, but his hands heal.
v19: He will rescue you from six troubles;
in seven, harm will not touch you.
v20: In famine he will redeem you from death,
and in war from the power of the sword.
v21: You will be sheltered from the scourge of the tongue;
you will not fear destruction when it comes.
v22: You will laugh at destruction and famine;
you need not fear the earth’s wild animals.
v23: For you will have a covenant with the stones of the field,
and the wild animals will be at peace with you.
v24: You will know that your tent is secure;
you will inspect your dwelling and find nothing missing.
v25: You will know that your descendants are many,
your offspring like the grass of the earth.
v26: You will come to the grave in full vigor,
like a sheaf brought in at its proper season.
v27: We have examined this, and so it is.
Listen to it and know it for yourself.”
</p>

## Notes

v05: The rare word rendered “the thirsty” has also been understood as robbers.

v07: “Sparks” renders “sons of flame,” an expression that may also evoke birds or the name Resheph. The upward-flying image is retained.

v17: “Almighty” is the traditional rendering of Shaddai; the title’s precise derivation is uncertain.

v19: Six troubles followed by seven is an ascending number pattern, not a limit on the troubles named.

v26: The rare expression for vigor pictures maturity at death; it does not supply a numerical age.

## Vocabulary

v23: Covenant with the stones
A poetic picture of harmony with the land, followed by peace with wild animals.
