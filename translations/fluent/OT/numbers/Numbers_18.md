## Responsibility for the sanctuary

<p>
v01: The LORD said to Aaron, “You, your sons and your ancestral house with you must bear responsibility for offenses against the sanctuary. You and your sons must bear responsibility for offenses against your priesthood.
v02: Bring your fellow Levites, your ancestral tribe, near with you, so they may join you and serve you while you and your sons serve before the tent of the testimony.
v03: They must attend to your duties and the duties of the whole tent. But they must not approach the sanctuary’s furnishings or the altar, or both they and you will die.
</p>

<p>
v04: They are to join you and take care of the tent of meeting, doing all the work of the tent. No unauthorized person may approach you.
v05: You must attend to the sanctuary and the altar, so that wrath will not fall on the Israelites again.
</p>

<p>
v06: I myself have taken your fellow Levites from among the Israelites. They are a gift to you, given to the LORD to do the work of the tent of meeting.
</p>

<p>
v07: You and your sons must attend to your priesthood in everything concerning the altar and what lies inside the curtain. You must carry out that service. I give you the priesthood as a gift of service. Any unauthorized person who approaches must be put to death.”
</p>

## The priests’ share

<p>
v08: The LORD told Aaron, “I have put you in charge of the contributions made to me. All the Israelites’ holy gifts I have given to you and your sons as your allotted portion, a lasting provision.
</p>

<p>
v09: This will be yours from the most holy offerings, the part kept back from the fire: all their gifts to me from grain offerings, sin offerings and guilt offerings. These are most holy for you and your sons.
v10: Eat it as something most holy. Every male may eat it; you must treat it as holy.
</p>

<p>
v11: These are yours too: the contributions from their gifts, including all the Israelites’ wave offerings. I give them to you, your sons and your daughters with you as a lasting provision. Everyone in your household who is clean may eat them.
</p>

<p>
v12: I give you all the finest oil, all the finest new wine and grain—the first of these that they give to the LORD.
v13: The first ripe produce of everything in their land that they bring to the LORD belongs to you. Everyone in your household who is clean may eat it.
</p>

<p>
v14: Everything in Israel devoted irrevocably to God belongs to you.
</p>

<p>
v15: The first to open the womb among all flesh, human or animal, that they present to the LORD belongs to you. But you must redeem the firstborn humans, and you must redeem the firstborn of unclean animals.
v16: For those who must be redeemed, from one month old, the redemption price is your valuation of five shekels of silver, using the sanctuary shekel, which is twenty gerahs.
</p>

<p>
v17: But do not redeem the firstborn of cattle, sheep or goats; they are holy. Splash their blood against the altar and burn their fat as an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma for the LORD.
v18: Their meat belongs to you, just as the breast of the wave offering and the right thigh belong to you.
</p>

<p>
v19: I give all the contributions from the holy gifts that the Israelites set aside for the LORD to you, your sons and your daughters with you as a lasting provision. It is an everlasting covenant of salt before the LORD for you and your descendants with you.”
</p>

## The Levites’ inheritance

<p>
v20: The LORD said to Aaron, “You will receive no inheritance in their land and have no share among them. I am your share and your inheritance among the Israelites.
</p>

<p>
v21: To the Levites I give every tithe in Israel as their inheritance in return for the service they perform, the work of the tent of meeting.
v22: The Israelites must no longer approach the tent of meeting, incurring guilt and dying.
</p>

<p>
v23: The Levites are to do the work of the tent of meeting and bear responsibility for their offenses. This is a lasting rule throughout your generations. They will receive no inheritance among the Israelites.
v24: I have given the Levites as their inheritance the Israelites’ tithes, which they set aside as a contribution for the LORD. That is why I have told them they will receive no inheritance among the Israelites.”
</p>

## A tithe from the tithe

<p>
v25: The LORD gave Moses this instruction:
v26: “Tell the Levites: When you receive from the Israelites the tithe I have given you as your inheritance, set aside a contribution from it for the LORD—a tithe of the tithe.
v27: Your contribution will be counted as though it were grain from the threshing floor or produce from the winepress.
</p>

<p>
v28: So you too must set aside a contribution for the LORD from all the tithes you receive from the Israelites. Give this contribution for the LORD to Aaron the priest.
v29: From every gift you receive, set aside the LORD’s full contribution, its holy portion from the finest of it.
</p>

<p>
v30: Tell them: When you have set aside the finest part, what remains will count for the Levites as produce from the threshing floor or the winepress.
v31: You and your households may eat it anywhere, because it is your payment for service at the tent of meeting.
v32: You will incur no guilt on its account when you have set aside the finest part. Do not profane the Israelites’ holy gifts, and you will not die.”
</p>

## Notes

v1–7: “Bear responsibility” renders the language of bearing guilt. Aaron’s ancestral house has sanctuary responsibilities, while Aaron and his sons have distinct priestly duties. The consequences of crossing these boundaries remain explicit.

v6–8: The Levites are given to the priests for service and dedicated to the LORD. The priesthood is a “gift of service,” an expression that can emphasize either service given as a gift or service connected with gifts. The allotted portion in verse 8 uses a word also associated with anointing.

v10–11: “As something most holy” is also read “in a most holy place.” The context concerns food for priestly males, not a general invitation to eat in the innermost sanctuary. Verse 11 explicitly includes daughters for a different category of gifts, subject to cleanness.

v15–17: Human firstborn must be redeemed, as must firstborn unclean animals. Firstborn cattle, sheep and goats are not redeemed here; their blood and fat go to the altar. The five-shekel valuation follows the human redemption practice in Numbers 3:46–48.

v19: The covenant of salt is an image of an enduring bond. The verse preserves that image without explaining a particular salt ritual.

v20–24: The denial of inheritance concerns a territorial share among Israel’s tribes. It does not erase the later provision of towns in Numbers 35. The Levites’ tithes support their service.

v23: “Their offenses” follows the Hebrew pronoun. It may refer to the Levites’ own responsibility or to guilt connected with the Israelites’ access to the tent. The wording does not decide every aspect of that responsibility.

v26–32: The Levites contribute a tenth of the tithe they receive, using its finest portion. The remaining food may be eaten by their households anywhere; this permission does not apply indiscriminately to every holy offering.

## Vocabulary

v2: Join
The Hebrew verb lāwāh echoes the name Levi; compare Genesis 29:34.

v9: Guilt offering
Also called a reparation offering; distinct from the sin offering and grain offering.

v11: Wave offering
A presentation before the LORD, also called an elevation offering. The term is retained consistently with earlier chapters.

v14: ḥērem — devoted
Something placed irrevocably under a sacred claim. Context determines what happens to it; the word does not always mean destruction.

v16: Shekel and gerah
Weights used for silver: one sanctuary shekel equals twenty gerahs. These are not modern coins.

v21, 26: Tithe
A tenth. The Levites receive Israel’s tithes and set aside a tenth of those tithes for the LORD.
