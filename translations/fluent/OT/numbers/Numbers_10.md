## The two silver trumpets

<p>
v01: The LORD gave Moses this instruction:
v02: “Make two trumpets of hammered silver for yourself. Use them to call the community together and to signal the camps to move.
v03: When both trumpets are blown, the whole community is to gather before you at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
v04: When just one is blown, the leaders—the heads of Israel’s thousands—are to gather before you.
</p>

<p>
v05: When you sound the alarm signal, the camps on the east are to set out.
v06: At the second alarm signal, the camps on the south are to set out. The alarm signal is to be sounded for their departures.
v07: To gather the assembly, blow the trumpets, but do not sound the alarm signal.
</p>

<p>
v08: Aaron’s sons, the priests, are to blow the trumpets. This is a lasting statute for you through all your generations.
</p>

<p>
v09: When you go to war in your own land against an enemy who oppresses you, sound the alarm on the trumpets. You will be remembered before the LORD your God and rescued from your enemies.
v10: On your days of rejoicing, at your appointed festivals and at the beginnings of your months, blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings and peace-offering sacrifices. They will be a reminder on your behalf before your God. I am the LORD your God.”
</p>

## Leaving Sinai

<p>
v11: In the second year, on the twentieth day of the second month, the cloud rose from above the tabernacle of the testimony.
v12: The Israelites set out from the wilderness of Sinai, traveling by stages, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran.
v13: This was their first departure at the LORD’s command given through Moses.
</p>

<p>
v14: The camp of Judah set out first under its banner, arranged in its divisions. Nahshon son of Amminadab led Judah’s division.
v15: Nethanel son of Zuar led the division of the tribe of Issachar.
v16: Eliab son of Helon led the division of the tribe of Zebulun.
</p>

<p>
v17: The tabernacle was taken down, and the Gershonites and Merarites set out carrying it.
</p>

<p>
v18: The camp of Reuben set out under its banner, arranged in its divisions. Elizur son of Shedeur led Reuben’s division.
v19: Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai led the division of the tribe of Simeon.
v20: Eliasaph son of Deuel led the division of the tribe of Gad.
</p>

<p>
v21: The Kohathites set out carrying the holy things. By the time they arrived, the tabernacle would already be set up.
</p>

<p>
v22: The camp of Ephraim set out under its banner, arranged in its divisions. Elishama son of Ammihud led Ephraim’s division.
v23: Gamaliel son of Pedahzur led the division of the tribe of Manasseh.
v24: Abidan son of Gideoni led the division of the tribe of Benjamin.
</p>

<p>
v25: The camp of Dan set out under its banner, arranged in its divisions, as the rear guard for all the camps. Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai led Dan’s division.
v26: Pagiel son of Ochran led the division of the tribe of Asher.
v27: Ahira son of Enan led the division of the tribe of Naphtali.
</p>

<p>
v28: This was the Israelites’ marching order by divisions when they set out.
</p>

## Moses asks Hobab to come

<p>
v29: Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses’ relative by marriage, “We are setting out for the place the LORD said, ‘I will give it to you.’ Come with us, and we will do good to you, because the LORD has promised good for Israel.”
v30: Hobab answered, “I will not go with you. I am going back to my own land and my own people.”
</p>

<p>
v31: Moses said, “Please do not leave us. You know where we can camp in the wilderness. You can be our eyes.
v32: If you come with us, whatever good the LORD does for us, we will do for you.”
</p>

## The ark goes ahead

<p>
v33: They traveled from the mountain of the LORD for three days. The ark of the LORD’s covenant went ahead of them on that three-day journey to seek out a resting place for them.
v34: The LORD’s cloud was over them by day whenever they set out from camp.
</p>

<p>
v35: Whenever the ark set out, Moses would say:
“Rise, LORD!
Let your enemies scatter;
let those who hate you flee before you!”
</p>

<p>
v36: When the ark came to rest, he would say:
“Return, LORD,
to Israel’s myriads of thousands!”
</p>

## Notes

v4: “Thousands” can name organized groups as well as a numerical size. The term is retained here without assigning an exact membership to each group.

v5–7: The departure alarm and the assembly call are distinct signals. Their exact sound is not described. The Hebrew specifies east and south here; it does not give additional numbered signals for the other camps.

v9–10: The trumpets accompany both danger and celebration. “Remembered” and “reminder” share a Hebrew root; both concern the people being remembered before God.

v21: “The holy things” is literally “the sanctuary,” here referring to what the Kohathites carried. Those carrying the tabernacle structure in verse 17 could set it up before the Kohathites arrived.

v29: The Hebrew relationship term is usually rendered “father-in-law,” but the names and relationships are difficult to align with Exodus 2:18; 3:1 and Judges 4:11. “Relative by marriage” leaves the precise relationship open. The description can also be attached to Reuel rather than Hobab; neither man’s name is changed to resolve the question.

v31–33: Hobab’s knowledge of the wilderness and the guidance of the cloud and ark stand alongside each other. The account does not record his answer to Moses’ renewed invitation. The ark goes ahead during the three-day journey; the wording does not require a three-day distance between it and the people.

v35–36: The Hebrew text marks these two invocations with inverted nun signs before and after them. Their precise function is debated; both verses are retained in place. “Myriads of thousands” preserves the expansive expression in verse 36, whose compressed wording requires supplying the relation “to” in English.

## Vocabulary

v2: Trumpet — ḥaṣoṣerah
The silver instrument named here, distinct from the ram’s-horn shofar.

v5: Alarm signal — teruah
A trumpet signal used here for setting out and for war, distinguished from the assembly call.

v33: Covenant — berit
The ark is named in relation to the LORD’s covenant, also called the ark of the testimony elsewhere.

v36: Myriad — revavah
Ten thousand, also used in expressions of a vast multitude. The verse does not give a new census count.
