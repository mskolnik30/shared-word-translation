## Aaron’s staff sprouts

<p>
v01: The LORD gave Moses these instructions:
v02: “Speak to the Israelites and collect a staff from the leader of each ancestral house, twelve staffs in all. Write each man’s name on his staff.
v03: Write Aaron’s name on Levi’s staff, because there must be one staff for the head of each ancestral house.
v04: Place them in the tent of meeting, in front of the testimony, where I meet with you.
v05: The staff of the man I choose will sprout. In this way I will silence the Israelites’ complaints against me, the complaints they make against you.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Moses spoke to the Israelites, and all their leaders gave him staffs, one for each leader of an ancestral house—twelve staffs, with Aaron’s staff among them.
</p>

<p>
v07: Moses placed the staffs before the LORD in the tent of the testimony.
</p>

<p>
v08: The next day Moses entered the tent of the testimony. Aaron’s staff, representing Levi’s house, had sprouted! It had put out buds, produced blossoms and ripened almonds.
</p>

<p>
v09: Moses brought all the staffs out from before the LORD to all the Israelites. They looked, and each leader took his staff.
</p>

<p>
v10: The LORD told Moses, “Put Aaron’s staff back in front of the testimony. Keep it there as a sign for the rebellious, so that you may end their complaints against me and they will not die.”
</p>

<p>
v11: Moses did exactly as the LORD commanded him.
</p>

## The people fear approaching

<p>
v12: The Israelites said to Moses, “We are dying! We are lost! All of us are lost!
v13: Anyone who comes near—who comes near the LORD’s tabernacle—dies. Must we all die?”
</p>

## Notes

v2–6: The passage names twelve staffs and places Aaron’s staff among them. It does not list the other tribal names here; the draft does not invent a thirteenth staff or resolve the representation by adding a list.

v8: Buds, blossoms and ripe almonds are all named. The translation keeps this full progression rather than summarizing it as a flowering staff.

v10: The staff is kept in front of the testimony. This verse does not explicitly say it was put inside the ark.

v12–13: The repeated cries express the people’s fear. Their claim that everyone who approaches dies leads into the distinctions of responsibility and access in chapter 18.

v1–13: Public Numbers 17:1–13 corresponds to Hebrew 17:16–28.

## Vocabulary

v2: maṭṭeh — staff
The word also means tribe, linking the physical staffs and the groups represented.

v4: Testimony
The covenant witness, associated with the tablets placed in the ark; see Exodus 25:16. The phrase names the meeting place in relation to that witness.

v10: Sign
A visible reminder. Here it is intended to end the complaints and prevent further deaths.
