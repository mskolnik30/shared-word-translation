## Keeping the Camp Clean

<p>
v01: The LORD said to Moses,
v02: “Command the Israelites to send out of the camp everyone with a defiling skin condition, everyone with a bodily discharge, and everyone made unclean by a dead person.
v03: Send them out, male and female alike. Send them outside the camp so that they do not make their camps unclean, for I dwell among them.”
v04: The Israelites did this and sent them outside the camp. They did just as the LORD had told Moses.
</p>

## Confession and Restitution

<p>
v05: The LORD said to Moses,
v06: “Tell the Israelites: When a man or woman commits any of the sins people commit, breaking faith with the LORD, that person incurs guilt.
v07: They must confess the sin they committed, make full restitution, add one-fifth to it, and give it to the person they wronged.
v08: If the wronged person has no family redeemer to receive the restitution, it is to be paid to the LORD for the priest. This is in addition to the ram used to make atonement for the guilty person.
v09: Every contribution from the Israelites’ holy gifts that they bring to the priest belongs to him.
v10: Each person’s holy gifts are their own; whatever they give to the priest belongs to the priest.”
</p>

## The Jealousy Ordeal

<p>
v11: The LORD said to Moses,
v12: “Tell the Israelites: If a man’s wife goes astray and breaks faith with him,
v13: and another man has sexual intercourse with her without her husband knowing, so that her defilement remains hidden, with no witness against her and without her being caught,
v14: and a spirit of jealousy comes over him and he becomes jealous of his wife when she has defiled herself—or a spirit of jealousy comes over him and he becomes jealous of her when she has not defiled herself—
v15: then the husband is to bring his wife to the priest and bring an offering for her: a tenth of an ephah of barley flour. He must not pour oil on it or put frankincense on it, because it is a grain offering for jealousy, an offering of remembrance that brings guilt to mind.
</p>

<p>
v16: The priest is to bring her forward and have her stand before the LORD.
v17: He is to take holy water in a clay vessel, take some dust from the tabernacle floor, and put it into the water.
v18: The priest is to have the woman stand before the LORD, loosen her hair, and place the offering of remembrance—the grain offering for jealousy—in her hands. He is to hold the bitter water that brings a curse.
</p>

<p>
v19: The priest is to put her under oath and say, ‘If no other man has slept with you, and you have not gone astray into defilement while under your husband’s authority, may you be unharmed by this bitter water that brings a curse.
v20: But if you have gone astray while under your husband’s authority, if you have defiled yourself and a man other than your husband has had sexual intercourse with you—’
v21: The priest is to put the woman under this oath of cursing and say to her, ‘May the LORD make you a curse and an oath among your people, when the LORD makes your thigh waste away and your belly swell.
v22: May this water that brings a curse enter your body, making your belly swell and your thigh waste away.’ Then the woman is to say, ‘Amen, amen.’
</p>

<p>
v23: The priest is to write these curses on a scroll and wash them off into the bitter water.
v24: He is to make the woman drink the bitter water that brings a curse, and the curse-bearing water will enter her with bitter effects.
v25: The priest is to take the grain offering for jealousy from the woman’s hands, wave it before the LORD, and bring it to the altar.
v26: He is to take a handful of the grain offering as its memorial portion and burn it on the altar. After that he is to make the woman drink the water.
</p>

<p>
v27: When he makes her drink the water, if she has defiled herself and broken faith with her husband, the curse-bearing water will enter her with bitter effects. Her belly will swell, her thigh will waste away, and she will become a curse among her people.
v28: But if the woman has not defiled herself and is clean, she will be unharmed and able to conceive.
</p>

<p>
v29: This is the instruction for jealousy: when a wife goes astray while under her husband’s authority and defiles herself,
v30: or when a spirit of jealousy comes over a man and he becomes jealous of his wife. He is to have the woman stand before the LORD, and the priest is to carry out this entire instruction for her.
v31: The husband will be free from guilt, but the woman will bear her guilt.”
</p>

## Notes

v02: The skin condition is tsaraat, discussed in Leviticus 13–14; it is not a precise modern diagnosis. These exclusions concern ritual uncleanness, which is not itself a statement of moral guilt.

v06: The Hebrew can mean sins people commit or sins committed against another person. The following instructions require confession and restitution to the person wronged.

v08: The family redeemer is a relative entitled to act for the wronged person. The verse provides for restitution when there is no such recipient.

v10: The first “their own” may instead refer to the priest. The second clause explicitly concerns what is given to the priest.

v14: The husband’s jealousy can arise whether or not his wife has been unfaithful. Suspicion and guilt are not equated.

v18: The action on the woman’s head can be understood as loosening her hair or uncovering her head. The priest directs the procedure.

v19–20: “Under your husband’s authority” renders an ancient expression literally meaning “under your husband.” It refers to her married status within the relationship described here.

v21–22: “Thigh waste away” renders the difficult expression “thigh fall.” “Thigh” may refer indirectly to reproductive organs; the precise bodily outcome is uncertain. The text does not explicitly state that the woman is pregnant. Becoming “a curse and an oath” means being invoked as an example in such speech.

v24–27: Drinking is stated in verse 24 and taken up again after the offering in verses 26–27. The repeated instructions are retained without adding a required number of separate drinks.

v28–31: “Able to conceive” renders the image “be sown with seed.” The innocent outcome remains explicit. The closing statement assigns different consequences to husband and wife; no matching procedure against a suspected husband is given here.

## Vocabulary

v06: מָעַל (maal)
Break faith: act unfaithfully within a relationship of obligation. The same root appears in the marriage case later in this chapter.

v08: גֹּאֵל (goel)
Family redeemer: a relative who acts to restore or protect a family member’s interests.

v15: קִנְאָה (qinah)
Jealousy or zeal. Here the word concerns a husband’s jealousy and suspicion.

v22: אָמֵן (amen)
An affirmation accepting the words spoken. The woman’s response is repeated: “Amen, amen.”
