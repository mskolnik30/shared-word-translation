## The clan’s inheritance question

<p>
v01: The heads of the ancestral houses in the clan of Gilead son of Machir, son of Manasseh, from the clans of Joseph’s descendants, came forward. They spoke before Moses and the leaders, the heads of the Israelites’ ancestral houses.
</p>

<p>
v02: They said, “The LORD commanded my lord to give the Israelites the land as an inheritance by lot. My lord was also commanded by the LORD to give our brother Zelophehad’s inheritance to his daughters.
v03: If they marry men from another Israelite tribe, their inheritance will be taken away from our ancestors’ inheritance and added to the inheritance of the tribe they marry into. It will be taken away from our allotted inheritance.
v04: Even when the Israelites’ Jubilee comes, the women’s inheritance will belong to the tribe they marry into. Their inheritance will be taken away from the inheritance of our ancestral tribe.”
</p>

## Marriage and inherited land

<p>
v05: Then, at the LORD’s command, Moses instructed the Israelites: “The tribe of Joseph’s descendants is right in what it says.
v06: This is what the LORD commands concerning Zelophehad’s daughters: They may marry whomever they think best, but only within a clan of their father’s tribe.
</p>

<p>
v07: An inheritance must not pass from tribe to tribe among the Israelites. Each Israelite must hold on to the inheritance of their ancestral tribe.
</p>

<p>
v08: Every daughter who inherits land in an Israelite tribe must marry someone from a clan of her father’s tribe, so that each Israelite may retain their ancestral inheritance.
</p>

<p>
v09: No inheritance may pass from one tribe to another. Each Israelite tribe must hold on to its own inheritance.”
</p>

## Zelophehad’s daughters marry

<p>
v10: Zelophehad’s daughters did as the LORD had commanded Moses.
v11: Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah and Noah, Zelophehad’s daughters, married sons of their father’s brothers.
v12: They married within the clans of Manasseh son of Joseph, and their inheritance remained with the tribe of their father’s clan.
</p>

<p>
v13: These are the commands and rulings that the LORD gave the Israelites through Moses on the plains of Moab, by the Jordan across from Jericho.
</p>

## Notes

v2–5: The clan’s request follows the daughters’ successful claim in Numbers 27. The LORD affirms the clan’s concern here, just as the daughters’ claim was affirmed there.

v4: The clan argues that Jubilee would not restore land transferred through the daughters’ marriages to another tribe. Compare the land-restoration provisions in Leviticus 25.

v6–8: The daughters may choose their husbands within the stated ancestral-tribal limit. The general rule in verse 8 concerns daughters who inherit land; it is not expressed as a ban on every marriage across tribal lines.

v11–12: The women marry their paternal cousins. Their names appear here in the order Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah and Noah, differing from the order in Numbers 26–27. The draft preserves this order.

v13: The closing statement returns to the plains of Moab, by the Jordan across from Jericho, the setting for the final instructions.

## Vocabulary

v2: Inheritance
The land assigned within Israel’s families and tribes. The daughters retain their inheritance while the marriage rule keeps it within the ancestral tribe.

v4: Jubilee
The restoration year described in Leviticus 25, when ancestral landholdings return under that chapter’s rules.

v7, 9: Hold on to
The Hebrew uses the language of clinging or remaining attached, here to an ancestral inheritance.
