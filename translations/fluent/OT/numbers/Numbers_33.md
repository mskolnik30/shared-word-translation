## The stages of the journey

<p>
v01: These are the stages of the Israelites’ journey when they came out of Egypt in their divisions under the leadership of Moses and Aaron.
v02: At the LORD’s command, Moses recorded their departures, stage by stage. These are the stages of their journey, listed by departure point.
</p>

<p>
v03: They left Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of that month. On the day after Passover, the Israelites went out with a raised hand, in full view of all the Egyptians.
v04: The Egyptians were burying all their firstborn whom the LORD had struck down among them. The LORD had also carried out judgments against their gods.
</p>

## From Egypt to Sinai

<p>
v05: The Israelites left Rameses and camped at Succoth.
v06: They left Succoth and camped at Etham, on the edge of the wilderness.
v07: They left Etham and turned back toward Pi-hahiroth, opposite Baal-zephon, and camped near Migdol.
</p>

<p>
v08: They left their camp opposite Hahiroth and passed through the middle of the sea into the wilderness. They traveled for three days in the wilderness of Etham and camped at Marah.
</p>

<p>
v09: They left Marah and came to Elim. At Elim there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they camped there.
</p>

<p>
v10: They left Elim and camped by the Sea of Reeds.
v11: They left the Sea of Reeds and camped in the wilderness of Sin.
v12: They left the wilderness of Sin and camped at Dophkah.
v13: They left Dophkah and camped at Alush.
</p>

<p>
v14: They left Alush and camped at Rephidim, where there was no water for the people to drink.
v15: They left Rephidim and camped in the wilderness of Sinai.
</p>

## From Sinai to the plains of Moab

<p>
v16: They left the wilderness of Sinai and camped at Kibroth-hattaavah.
v17: They left Kibroth-hattaavah and camped at Hazeroth.
</p>

<p>
v18: They left Hazeroth and camped at Rithmah.
v19: They left Rithmah and camped at Rimmon-perez.
v20: They left Rimmon-perez and camped at Libnah.
v21: They left Libnah and camped at Rissah.
v22: They left Rissah and camped at Kehelathah.
</p>

<p>
v23: They left Kehelathah and camped at Mount Shepher.
v24: They left Mount Shepher and camped at Haradah.
v25: They left Haradah and camped at Makheloth.
v26: They left Makheloth and camped at Tahath.
v27: They left Tahath and camped at Terah.
</p>

<p>
v28: They left Terah and camped at Mithkah.
v29: They left Mithkah and camped at Hashmonah.
v30: They left Hashmonah and camped at Moseroth.
v31: They left Moseroth and camped at Bene-jaakan.
v32: They left Bene-jaakan and camped at Hor-haggidgad.
</p>

<p>
v33: They left Hor-haggidgad and camped at Jotbathah.
v34: They left Jotbathah and camped at Abronah.
v35: They left Abronah and camped at Ezion-geber.
</p>

<p>
v36: They left Ezion-geber and camped in the wilderness of Zin, that is, Kadesh.
v37: They left Kadesh and camped at Mount Hor, on the edge of the land of Edom.
</p>

<p>
v38: At the LORD’s command, Aaron the priest went up Mount Hor and died there. It was the first day of the fifth month in the fortieth year after the Israelites came out of Egypt.
v39: Aaron was one hundred and twenty-three years old when he died on Mount Hor.
</p>

<p>
v40: The Canaanite king of Arad, who lived in the Negev in the land of Canaan, heard that the Israelites were coming.
</p>

<p>
v41: They left Mount Hor and camped at Zalmonah.
v42: They left Zalmonah and camped at Punon.
v43: They left Punon and camped at Oboth.
</p>

<p>
v44: They left Oboth and camped at Iye-abarim, on the border of Moab.
v45: They left Iyim and camped at Dibon-gad.
v46: They left Dibon-gad and camped at Almon-diblathaim.
</p>

<p>
v47: They left Almon-diblathaim and camped in the mountains of Abarim, opposite Nebo.
v48: They left the mountains of Abarim and camped on the plains of Moab by the Jordan across from Jericho.
</p>

<p>
v49: Their camp stretched along the Jordan from Beth-jeshimoth to Abel-shittim, on the plains of Moab.
</p>

## Instructions for the land

<p>
v50: There on the plains of Moab, by the Jordan across from Jericho, the LORD spoke to Moses.
</p>

<p>
v51: “Tell the Israelites: When you cross the Jordan into the land of Canaan,
v52: you must drive out all the land’s inhabitants before you. Destroy all their figured stones and all their cast images, and demolish all their high places.
</p>

<p>
v53: Take possession of the land and settle in it, for I have given you the land to possess.
</p>

<p>
v54: Divide the land by lot among your clans. Give a larger inheritance to a larger group and a smaller inheritance to a smaller group. Each group will have the place its lot falls on; take your inheritance according to your ancestral tribes.
</p>

<p>
v55: But if you do not drive out the land’s inhabitants before you, those you leave will be barbs in your eyes and thorns in your sides. They will harass you in the land where you settle.
v56: And what I planned to do to them, I will do to you.”
</p>

## Notes

v2: “At the LORD’s command” may qualify Moses’ recording of the route, as here, or the journeys being recorded. The repeated departure-and-camp pattern is retained throughout the itinerary.

v8, 11, 36: Verse 8 uses Hahiroth after Pi-hahiroth in verse 7. It names the wilderness of Etham, while Exodus 15:22 names Shur in its account. The draft preserves each passage’s wording. Sin and Zin are also distinct names in this itinerary.

v30–39: The route and Aaron’s death are preserved in the sequence given here rather than rearranged to match other travel notices. Aaron’s death is dated to the first day of the fifth month in year forty, at the age of 123.

v44–45: Iyim is the shortened form used here after Iye-abarim in verse 44.

v52: “Figured stones” refers to carved, decorated or image-bearing stones in this cultic setting; the exact objects are uncertain. Cast images and high places are named separately.

v54–56: The instructions combine allocation by lot with shares proportionate to group size. The closing warning turns the threatened treatment of the land’s inhabitants back on Israel.

## Vocabulary

v3: With a raised hand
An image of bold, open departure, also used in Exodus 14:8. It need not describe every traveler’s physical posture.

v10: Sea of Reeds
The Hebrew name traditionally rendered “Red Sea.” The ancient name is retained without identifying every campsite on a modern map.

v16: Kibroth-hattaavah
“Graves of craving,” recalling Numbers 11:34.

v38: Mount Hor
Here the mountain on Edom’s border where Aaron died. The northern boundary mountain in Numbers 34:7 occupies a different setting.

v52: High places
Elevated or designated places of worship. Here they are among the cultic sites to be destroyed.

v55: Barbs and thorns
Images of piercing pain in the eyes and sides, describing the harassment in the warning.
