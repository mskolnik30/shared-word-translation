## The Kohathites’ Service

<p>
v01: The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,
v02: “Take a census of the descendants of Kohath from among the Levites, by clan and ancestral household,
v03: from thirty years old up to fifty—all who enter the service to work at the tent of meeting.
v04: The Kohathites’ work at the tent of meeting concerns the most holy things.
</p>

## Preparing the Sacred Objects

<p>
v05: When the camp is ready to move, Aaron and his sons are to go in, take down the dividing curtain and cover the ark of the testimony with it.
v06: They are to put a covering of taḥash skin over it, spread a cloth entirely of blue over that, and put its carrying poles in place.
</p>

<p>
v07: They are to spread a blue cloth over the table of the Presence and place on it the dishes, ladles, bowls and pitchers for poured offerings. The regular bread is to remain on it.
v08: They are to spread a scarlet cloth over these things, cover it with taḥash skin and put its carrying poles in place.
</p>

<p>
v09: They are to take a blue cloth and cover the lampstand for lighting, its lamps, tongs, firepans and all the oil containers used in its service.
v10: They are to wrap the lampstand and all its equipment in a covering of taḥash skin and place it on a carrying bar.
</p>

<p>
v11: They are to spread a blue cloth over the gold altar, cover it with taḥash skin and put its carrying poles in place.
v12: They are to gather all the utensils used for service in the sanctuary, put them in a blue cloth, cover them with taḥash skin and place them on a carrying bar.
</p>

<p>
v13: They are to remove the ashes from the altar and spread a purple cloth over it.
v14: They are to put on it all the equipment used in its service: the firepans, forks, shovels and basins—all the altar’s equipment. Then they are to spread a covering of taḥash skin over it and put its carrying poles in place.
</p>

<p>
v15: Once Aaron and his sons have finished covering the holy things and all their sacred equipment when the camp moves, the Kohathites may come to carry them. They must not touch the holy things, or they will die. These are the loads the Kohathites are to carry for the tent of meeting.
v16: Eleazar son of Aaron the priest is responsible for the lighting oil, fragrant incense, regular grain offering and anointing oil. He has oversight of the whole tabernacle and everything in it, its holy things and equipment.”
</p>

## Protecting the Kohathites

<p>
v17: The LORD said to Moses and Aaron,
v18: “Do not let the Kohathite clans be cut off from among the Levites.
v19: Do this for them so that they may live and not die when they approach the most holy things: Aaron and his sons are to go in and assign each man his task and his load.
v20: The Kohathites must not go in to look at the holy things, even for a moment, or they will die.”
</p>

## The Gershonites’ Service

<p>
v21: The LORD said to Moses,
v22: “Take a census of Gershon’s descendants as well, by ancestral household and clan.
v23: Register those from thirty years old up to fifty, all who enter the service to do the work of the tent of meeting.
</p>

<p>
v24: This is the work assigned to the Gershonite clans, both their service and what they are to carry:
v25: They are to carry the tabernacle curtains and the tent of meeting, its covering and the taḥash covering above it, and the screen at the entrance of the tent of meeting,
v26: as well as the courtyard hangings and the screen at the gate of the courtyard around the tabernacle and altar, their ropes and all the equipment for their service. They are to do all the work needed for these things.
</p>

<p>
v27: All the Gershonites’ work, everything they carry and every task they perform, is to be directed by Aaron and his sons. You are to assign them responsibility for everything they carry.
v28: This is the service of the Gershonite clans at the tent of meeting. Their responsibilities are under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.
</p>

## The Merarites’ Service

<p>
v29: Register Merari’s descendants by clan and ancestral household.
v30: Register those from thirty years old up to fifty, all who enter the service to do the work of the tent of meeting.
</p>

<p>
v31: These are the things they are responsible for carrying in all their service at the tent of meeting: the tabernacle’s frames, crossbars, pillars and bases,
v32: and the pillars around the courtyard, with their bases, pegs, ropes and all their equipment for every task. Assign by name the items they are responsible for carrying.
v33: This is the service of the Merarite clans, all their work at the tent of meeting, under the direction of Ithamar son of Aaron the priest.”
</p>

## The Census for Service

<p>
v34: Moses, Aaron and the community’s leaders registered the Kohathites by clan and ancestral household,
v35: from thirty years old up to fifty, all who entered the service to work at the tent of meeting.
v36: Those registered by clan numbered 2,750.
v37: These were the registered members of the Kohathite clans, all who served at the tent of meeting. Moses and Aaron registered them at the LORD’s command given through Moses.
</p>

<p>
v38: The descendants of Gershon were registered by clan and ancestral household,
v39: from thirty years old up to fifty, all who entered the service to work at the tent of meeting.
v40: Those registered by clan and ancestral household numbered 2,630.
v41: These were the registered members of the Gershonite clans, all who served at the tent of meeting. Moses and Aaron registered them at the LORD’s command.
</p>

<p>
v42: The Merarite clans were registered by clan and ancestral household,
v43: from thirty years old up to fifty, all who entered the service to work at the tent of meeting.
v44: Those registered by clan numbered 3,200.
v45: These were the registered members of the Merarite clans. Moses and Aaron registered them at the LORD’s command given through Moses.
</p>

<p>
v46: Moses, Aaron and Israel’s leaders registered all these Levites by clan and ancestral household,
v47: from thirty years old up to fifty, all who entered the service to do the work and carry the loads of the tent of meeting.
v48: The total number registered was 8,580.
v49: At the LORD’s command through Moses, each man was registered with his assigned task and load. Their registration followed what the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The thirty-to-fifty service range is retained here. Numbers 8:24 gives a starting age of twenty-five; the translation does not silently make the passages agree.

v06: Taḥash is an uncertain skin or leather material, also named in Exodus 25–26. The text does not securely identify an animal species. “Put its carrying poles in place” preserves the instruction here; Exodus 25:15 says the ark’s poles are not to be removed. The precise action is not explained.

v07: The bread is to stay with the table. The shapes of the named vessels are uncertain; their use for poured offerings is retained.

v10: The Hebrew mot denotes a carrying bar or support, distinct from the fitted poles named elsewhere. Its construction and how the load was attached are not described.

v20: The Hebrew expression can be understood as “even for a moment” or as “while the holy things are being covered.” Both readings restrict the Kohathites’ access; the preceding instructions assign the covering to Aaron and his sons.

v32: “By name” applies grammatically to the items to be carried. Verse 19 separately speaks of assigning each man his work and load.

v49: The final Hebrew clause is compressed and difficult: roughly, “and his registered ones, whom the LORD commanded Moses.” The draft supplies the connection needed for a registration summary; the wording does not spell out another assignment procedure.

## Vocabulary

v03: צָבָא (tsava)
Organized service. In chapter 1 it concerns the army; here it concerns sanctuary work.

v04: קֹדֶשׁ הַקֳּדָשִׁים (qodesh haqqodashim)
Most holy things: the sacred objects described in the following instructions. The same expression can name the innermost sanctuary elsewhere.

v15: מַשָּׂא (massa)
Load or burden: what someone must carry. Here the loads are assigned along with other tasks.

v16: פְּקֻדָּה (pequddah)
Oversight or responsibility entrusted to someone. Eleazar’s oversight is distinguished from Ithamar’s duties later in the chapter.
