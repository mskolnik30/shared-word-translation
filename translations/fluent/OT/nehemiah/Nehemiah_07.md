## Guarding the rebuilt city

<p>
v01: When the wall was rebuilt and I had installed the doors, the gatekeepers, singers, and Levites were appointed.
v02: I put my brother Hanani and Hananiah, commander of the citadel, in charge of Jerusalem. Hananiah was a trustworthy man who feared God more than most.
v03: I told them, “Jerusalem’s gates must not be opened until the sun is hot. While the guards are still standing there, shut the doors and secure them. Appoint Jerusalem’s residents as guards, some at their posts and others opposite their homes.”
</p>

<p>
v61: The following came up from Tel-melah, Tel-harsha, Cherub, Addon, and Immer, but could not establish their ancestral houses or descent—whether they belonged to Israel:

## Notes

v03: The reading tradition has “I said,” which keeps Nehemiah as the speaker. The instruction to delay opening until the sun is hot is unusual and is retained.

v06–65: This register parallels Ezra 2 but differs in several names and counts. Each list is translated as it stands; the figures are not harmonized.

v52: Nephishesim follows the reading tradition; the corresponding name in Ezra 2:50 is Nephusim.

v67: The pinned Hebrew source has no horse-and-mule verse corresponding to the usual 7:68. This edition therefore proceeds from v67 to v69, as the existing verse labels do. Its public 7:69–73 binds Hebrew 7:68–72. No line is imported from Ezra 2:66.

v70: The Hebrew gives “thirty and five hundred” priestly robes, rendered 530. The wording is not altered to create a separate silver contribution.

## Vocabulary

v65: Urim and Thummim
Sacred means of priestly decision. The disputed priestly families must wait for an authorized decision.

v70: Daric
A monetary unit used for the gold gifts. Ancient coin identifications and weights are not fully certain.
