## Priests and Levites

<p>
v01: These are the priests and Levites who came up with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and Jeshua: Seraiah, Jeremiah, Ezra,
v02: Amariah, Malluch, Hattush,
v03: Shecaniah, Rehum, Meremoth,
v04: Iddo, Ginnethoi, Abijah,
v05: Mijamin, Maadiah, Bilgah,
v06: Shemaiah, Joiarib, Jedaiah,
v07: Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, and Jedaiah. These were the heads of the priests and their relatives in Jeshua’s days.
</p>

<p>
v08: The Levites were Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel, Sherebiah, Judah, and Mattaniah. Mattaniah and his relatives were responsible for the thanksgiving songs.
v09: Their relatives Bakbukiah and Unni stood opposite them in their service groups.
</p>

<p>
v10: Jeshua was the father of Joiakim, Joiakim of Eliashib, and Eliashib of Joiada.
v11: Joiada was the father of Jonathan, and Jonathan of Jaddua.
</p>

## Priestly family heads

<p>
v12: In Joiakim’s days, the priests who headed ancestral houses were: for Seraiah, Meraiah; for Jeremiah, Hananiah;
v13: for Ezra, Meshullam; for Amariah, Jehohanan;
v14: for Melichu, Jonathan; for Shebaniah, Joseph;
v15: for Harim, Adna; for Meraioth, Helkai;
v16: for Iddo, Zechariah; for Ginnethon, Meshullam;
v17: for Abijah, Zichri; for Miniamin; for Moadiah, Piltai;
v18: for Bilgah, Shammua; for Shemaiah, Jehonathan;
v19: for Joiarib, Mattenai; for Jedaiah, Uzzi;
v20: for Sallai, Kallai; for Amok, Eber;
v21: for Hilkiah, Hashabiah; for Jedaiah, Nethanel.
</p>

<p>
v22: In the days of Eliashib, Joiada, Johanan, and Jaddua, the Levite family heads were recorded, as were the priests, during the reign of Darius the Persian.
v23: The Levite family heads were recorded in the Book of the Annals up to the days of Johanan son of Eliashib.
</p>

<p>
v24: The Levite leaders were Hashabiah, Sherebiah, and Jeshua son of Kadmiel. They and their relatives stood opposite one another, group facing group, to praise and give thanks as David, the man of God, had commanded.
v25: Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam, Talmon, and Akkub were gatekeepers guarding the storerooms at the gates.
v26: They served in the days of Joiakim son of Jeshua, son of Jozadak, and in the days of Nehemiah the governor and Ezra the priest and scribe.
</p>

## Dedicating Jerusalem’s wall

<p>
v27: For the dedication of Jerusalem’s wall, the Levites were sought out wherever they lived and brought to Jerusalem, to celebrate joyfully with thanksgiving and singing, with cymbals, harps, and lyres.
v28: The singers gathered from the region around Jerusalem, from the Netophathite villages,
v29: from Beth-gilgal, and from the fields of Geba and Azmaveth. The singers had built villages for themselves around Jerusalem.
v30: The priests and Levites purified themselves, then purified the people, the gates, and the wall.
</p>

<p>
v31: I brought Judah’s leaders up onto the wall and appointed two large thanksgiving processions. One went to the right along the wall toward the Dung Gate.
v32: Hoshaiah and half Judah’s leaders followed them,
v33: along with Azariah, Ezra, Meshullam,
v34: Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, and Jeremiah,
v35: and some priests with trumpets. Zechariah son of Jonathan, son of Shemaiah, son of Mattaniah, son of Micaiah, son of Zaccur, son of Asaph,
v36: and his relatives Shemaiah, Azarel, Milalai, Gilalai, Maai, Nethanel, Judah, and Hanani carried the musical instruments of David, the man of God. Ezra the scribe went ahead of them.
v37: At the Fountain Gate they went straight up the steps of the City of David, by the ascent to the wall, above David’s house, as far as the Water Gate on the east.
</p>

<p>
v38: The second thanksgiving procession went the other way. I followed it with half the people along the wall, past the Tower of the Ovens to the Broad Wall,
v39: then past the Ephraim Gate, the Old Gate, the Fish Gate, the Tower of Hananel, and the Tower of the Hundred, as far as the Sheep Gate. They stopped at the Gate of the Guard.
</p>

<p>
v40: The two thanksgiving processions took their places in God’s house. I was there with half the officials,
v41: and the priests Eliakim, Maaseiah, Miniamin, Micaiah, Elioenai, Zechariah, and Hananiah with trumpets,
v42: along with Maaseiah, Shemaiah, Eleazar, Uzzi, Jehohanan, Malchijah, Elam, and Ezer. The singers sang out, with Jezrahiah directing them.
v43: They offered great sacrifices that day and rejoiced, for God had given them great joy. The women and children rejoiced too. Jerusalem’s joy could be heard far away.
</p>

## Support for the temple service

<p>
v44: That day men were appointed over the storerooms for contributions, firstfruits, and tithes. They were to gather into them from the towns’ fields the portions prescribed by the instruction for the priests and Levites. Judah rejoiced over the priests and Levites who served.
v45: They carried out the service of their God and the work of purification. The singers and gatekeepers also served as David and his son Solomon had commanded.
v46: Long ago, in the days of David and Asaph, there had been leaders of the singers and songs of praise and thanksgiving to God.
v47: In the days of Zerubbabel and Nehemiah, all Israel supplied the singers’ and gatekeepers’ daily portions. They set apart the Levites’ portions, and the Levites set apart portions for Aaron’s descendants.
</p>

## Notes

v11: Jonathan here and Johanan in verse 22 are retained as distinct written forms; the genealogy is not silently harmonized.

v14: Melichu follows the reading tradition beside the written form Malluchi.

v17: No family head is supplied after Miniamin in the Hebrew. The incomplete pair is left visible rather than assigning Piltai to both families.

v24: The Hebrew reads “Jeshua son of Kadmiel.” It is not changed to “Jeshua and Kadmiel” to match another list.

v25: The compact list can be grouped in different ways; some interpreters distinguish singers among its first three names from the following gatekeepers.

v38: The second procession goes the other way around the wall. The wording does not require an exact modern compass direction.

## Vocabulary

v27: Dedication
Setting the rebuilt wall apart with celebration, thanksgiving, and purification.

v31: Thanksgiving choirs
Groups whose songs of thanks accompany the two processions along the wall.
