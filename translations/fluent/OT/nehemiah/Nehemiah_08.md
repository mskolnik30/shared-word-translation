## The instruction is read and explained

<p>
v01: All the people gathered as one in the square before the Water Gate. They asked Ezra the scribe to bring the Book of the Instruction of Moses, which the LORD had commanded Israel to follow.
v02: On the first day of the seventh month, Ezra the priest brought the instruction before the assembly—men, women, and everyone able to understand what they heard.
v03: He read from it in the square before the Water Gate from dawn until midday, facing the men, women, and all who could understand. Everyone listened attentively to the Book of the Instruction.
</p>

<p>
v04: Ezra the scribe stood on a wooden platform made for the occasion. Beside him on his right stood Mattithiah, Shema, Anaiah, Uriah, Hilkiah, and Maaseiah. On his left stood Pedaiah, Mishael, Malchijah, Hashum, Hashbaddanah, Zechariah, and Meshullam.
v05: Ezra opened the book where everyone could see it, for he stood above them. As he opened it, all the people stood up.
v06: Ezra blessed the LORD, the great God, and all the people raised their hands and answered, “Amen! Amen!” Then they bowed down and worshiped the LORD with their faces to the ground.
</p>

<p>
v07: Jeshua, Bani, Sherebiah, Jamin, Akkub, Shabbethai, Hodiah, Maaseiah, Kelita, Azariah, Jozabad, Hanan, Pelaiah, and the Levites helped the people understand the instruction while the people remained in their places.
v08: They read clearly from the book, from God’s instruction, and gave its meaning so the people could understand what was read.
</p>

## A holy day for shared joy

<p>
v09: Nehemiah the governor, Ezra the priest and scribe, and the Levites who were teaching the people told everyone, “This day is holy to the LORD your God. Do not mourn or weep.” All the people had been weeping as they heard the words of the instruction.
v10: He told them, “Go and eat rich food, drink sweet drinks, and send portions to anyone who has nothing prepared. This day is holy to our Lord. Do not grieve, for the joy of the LORD is your stronghold.”
v11: The Levites quieted all the people, saying, “Be still. This day is holy. Do not grieve.”
v12: All the people went off to eat and drink, send portions to others, and celebrate with great joy, because they understood the words that had been explained to them.
</p>

## Living in shelters

<p>
v13: On the second day, the family heads of all the people, the priests, and the Levites gathered around Ezra the scribe to study the words of the instruction.
v14: They found written in the instruction that the LORD had commanded through Moses that the Israelites were to live in shelters during the festival in the seventh month,
v15: and that this announcement should be made throughout their towns and in Jerusalem: “Go out to the hills and bring branches of olive, oil tree, myrtle, palm, and leafy trees to make shelters, as written.”
</p>

<p>
v16: The people went out, brought branches, and made shelters on their roofs, in their courtyards, in the courts of God’s house, and in the squares at the Water Gate and the Ephraim Gate.
v17: The whole assembly that had returned from captivity made shelters and lived in them. From the days of Joshua son of Nun until that day, the Israelites had not done it like this. Their joy was very great.
v18: Day by day, from the first day to the last, Ezra read from the Book of God’s Instruction. They celebrated the festival for seven days and held a solemn assembly on the eighth, as prescribed.
</p>

## Notes

v08: The reading is made clear and its meaning given so the people understand. The wording may include interpretation or translation, but it does not name a second language.

v10: “The joy of the LORD” may mean joy found in the LORD or joy associated with him. “Stronghold” retains the protective image.

v15: The identification of the “oil tree” is uncertain; wild olive and other resinous or oily trees have been proposed. It is listed separately from the olive tree.

v17: The Hebrew uses the name Jeshua for Joshua son of Nun. “Like this” qualifies the claim about the celebration; the verse need not mean that no observance at all had occurred since Joshua.

## Vocabulary

v01: Instruction
Hebrew torah: teaching or instruction, including the written law of Moses.

v14: Festival of Shelters
The festival also called Booths or Tabernacles, marked by living in temporary shelters.
