## The People Bind Themselves to the Torah

<p>
v28: The rest of the people—the priests, Levites, gatekeepers, singers, temple servants, and everyone who had separated themselves from the peoples of the lands to the Torah of God, along with their wives, sons, and daughters, everyone with knowledge and understanding—
v29: joined with their relatives, their nobles, and entered into a curse and an oath to walk in the Torah of God given through Moses the servant of God, and to observe and do all the commandments of the LORD our Lord, along with his judgments and statutes.
v30: We would not give our daughters to the peoples of the land or take their daughters for our sons.
v31: If the peoples of the land bring merchandise or any grain to sell on the Sabbath day, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or on a holy day. We will also let the seventh year rest and release every debt.
</p>

## Provision for the House of God

<p>
v32: We also placed ourselves under an obligation to give one-third of a shekel each year for the service of the house of our God:
v33: for the bread of the Presence, the regular grain offering, the regular burnt offering, the Sabbaths, new moons, appointed festivals, holy things, sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and all the work of the house of our God.
v34: We—the priests, Levites, and people—cast lots concerning the wood offering, to bring it to the house of our God by ancestral houses at appointed times year by year, to burn on the altar of the LORD our God, as written in the Torah.
v35: We will bring the firstfruits of our ground and the firstfruits of every kind of fruit tree, year by year, to the house of the LORD;
v36: also the firstborn of our sons and livestock, as written in the Torah, and the firstborn of our herds and flocks, to bring them to the house of our God, to the priests who minister in the house of our God.
</p>

<p>
v37: We will bring the first of our dough, our contributions, the fruit of every kind of tree, the new wine, and the oil to the priests, to the chambers of the house of our God; and we will bring the tithes of our ground to the Levites, for the Levites are the ones who receive the tithes in all our agricultural towns.
v38: A priest, a descendant of Aaron, will be with the Levites when the Levites receive the tithes, and the Levites will bring up a tenth of the tithe to the house of our God, to the chambers of the storehouse.
v39: For the Israelites and the descendants of Levi will bring the contribution of grain, new wine, and oil to the chambers where the vessels of the sanctuary are, and where the priests who minister, the gatekeepers, and the singers are. We will not forsake the house of our God.
</p>

## Notes

v01–27: The sealed names represent priests, Levites, and lay leaders, giving the written agreement broad communal representation.

v28: Separation is described specifically in relation to adherence to the Torah of God; the verse frames communal identity around covenantal practice.

v29: “curse and oath” — The agreement includes solemn sanctions as well as promises, making covenantal responsibility concrete.

v30: The concern with marriage is framed by covenant loyalty rather than ethnicity alone; later chapters show the issue in terms of households, language, and faithfulness.

v31: Sabbath observance is linked directly with economic practice, and the seventh year with release from debt.

v32: The one-third-shekel contribution differs from the half-shekel amount known from Exodus 30, suggesting a specific postexilic communal obligation rather than silent harmonization.

v34–38: Wood, firstfruits, firstborn, contributions, and tithes are organized so that temple worship can function materially.

v39: The covenantal section culminates in the concise commitment, “We will not forsake the house of our God.”

## Vocabulary

v01: חָתַם (chatam)
“To seal.” To authenticate a written agreement with an identifying seal.

v29: אָלָה (alah)
“Curse.” A solemn sanction attached to covenant-breaking.

v29: שְׁבוּעָה (shevu'ah)
“Oath.” A binding sworn commitment.

v31: שַׁבָּת (shabbat)
“Sabbath.” The seventh day set apart for rest and holiness.

v32: שֶׁקֶל (sheqel)
“Shekel.” A standard unit of weight used for monetary assessment.

v35: רֵאשִׁית (reshit)
“First” or “firstfruits.” The beginning portion set apart for God.

v37: מַעֲשֵׂר (ma'aser)
“Tithe.” A tenth portion given for Levitical and sacred service.
