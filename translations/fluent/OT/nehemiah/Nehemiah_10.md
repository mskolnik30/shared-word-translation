## The people’s commitments

<p>
v28: The rest of the people—the priests, Levites, gatekeepers, singers, temple servants, and everyone who had separated from the peoples of the lands to follow God’s instruction, with their wives, sons, and daughters, all who could know and understand—
v29: joined their noble brothers in a curse and an oath: to walk in God’s instruction given through Moses, God’s servant, and to keep and carry out all the commands, judgments, and statutes of the LORD our Lord.
</p>

<p>
v30: “We will not give our daughters in marriage to the peoples of the land or take their daughters for our sons.
v31: If the peoples of the land bring merchandise or any grain to sell on the Sabbath, we will not buy from them on the Sabbath or a holy day. In the seventh year we will let the land rest and release every debt.
</p>

## Supporting the house of God

<p>
v32: We accept the obligation to give a third of a shekel each year for the service of our God’s house:
v33: for the bread set out in rows, the regular grain offering and burnt offering, the Sabbaths, new moons, appointed festivals, holy offerings, sin offerings to make atonement for Israel, and all the work of our God’s house.
v34: We—the priests, Levites, and people—have cast lots by ancestral house for the wood offering, to bring it to our God’s house at appointed times each year, to burn on the altar of the LORD our God, as written in the instruction.
</p>

<p>
v35: We will bring the firstfruits of our soil and the first fruit of every kind of tree to the LORD’s house year after year.
v36: We will also bring the firstborn of our sons and livestock, as written in the instruction, and the firstborn of our cattle and flocks, to the priests serving in our God’s house.
</p>

<p>
v37: We will bring the first of our dough, our contributions, fruit from every kind of tree, new wine, and oil to the priests in the chambers of our God’s house. We will bring a tenth of our soil’s produce to the Levites, who collect the tithes in all our farming towns.
v38: An Aaronite priest must be with the Levites when they collect the tithes. The Levites will bring a tenth of the tithes to the treasury chambers of our God’s house.
v39: The Israelites and Levites must bring the contributions of grain, new wine, and oil to the chambers, where the sanctuary vessels are kept and the serving priests, gatekeepers, and singers are stationed. We will not abandon the house of our God.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: Public 10:1–39 corresponds to Hebrew 10:2–40; the preceding Hebrew verse appears as public 9:38.

v19: Nebai follows the recorded reading tradition beside the written form Nobai.

v32: The annual contribution is one-third of a shekel here. It is not changed to the half-shekel amount found in other passages.

v36: Bringing firstborn sons under the instruction does not mean sacrificing them. The law distinguishes redemption of human firstborn from animal offerings (Exodus 13:13; Numbers 18:15–16).

## Vocabulary

v29: A curse and an oath
A sworn commitment that invokes a penalty for breaking it.

v38: Tithe of the tithe
The Levites give a tenth of the tithes they receive to support the priests.
