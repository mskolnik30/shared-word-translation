## The builders and their sections

<p>
v01: Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests began rebuilding the Sheep Gate. They consecrated it and set up its doors. They consecrated the wall as far as the Tower of the Hundred and the Tower of Hananel.
v02: The men of Jericho built the next section, and beside them Zaccur son of Imri built.
v03: The sons of Hassenaah rebuilt the Fish Gate. They laid its beams and installed its doors, bolts, and bars.
v04: Beside them Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, made repairs. Next came Meshullam son of Berechiah, son of Meshezabel, and then Zadok son of Baanah.
v05: The men of Tekoa repaired the next section, but their nobles would not bend their necks to the work under their masters.
</p>

<p>
v06: Joiada son of Paseah and Meshullam son of Besodeiah repaired the Old Gate. They laid its beams and installed its doors, bolts, and bars.
v07: Beside them Melatiah the Gibeonite and Jadon the Meronothite made repairs, with the men of Gibeon and Mizpah, under the authority of the governor of Beyond the River.
v08: Uzziel son of Harhaiah, one of the goldsmiths, repaired the next section, and beside him Hananiah, one of the perfume makers. They restored Jerusalem as far as the Broad Wall.
v09: Beside them Rephaiah son of Hur, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, made repairs.
v10: Next, Jedaiah son of Harumaph repaired the section opposite his house. Beside him Hattush son of Hashabneiah made repairs.
v11: Malchijah son of Harim and Hasshub son of Pahath-moab repaired another section and the Tower of the Ovens.
v12: Beside him Shallum son of Hallohesh, ruler of half the district of Jerusalem, made repairs with his daughters.
</p>

<p>
v13: Hanun and the people of Zanoah repaired the Valley Gate. They rebuilt it, installed its doors, bolts, and bars, and repaired a thousand cubits of wall as far as the Dung Gate.
v14: Malchijah son of Rechab, ruler of the district of Beth-haccherem, repaired the Dung Gate. He rebuilt it and installed its doors, bolts, and bars.
</p>

<p>
v15: Shallun son of Col-hozeh, ruler of the district of Mizpah, repaired the Fountain Gate. He rebuilt it, roofed it, and installed its doors, bolts, and bars. He also repaired the wall of the Pool of Shelah by the king’s garden, as far as the steps descending from the City of David.
v16: After him Nehemiah son of Azbuk, ruler of half the district of Beth-zur, repaired as far as the place opposite David’s tombs, the artificial pool, and the House of the Warriors.
v17: After him the Levites repaired, led by Rehum son of Bani. Beside him Hashabiah, ruler of half the district of Keilah, made repairs for his district.
v18: After him their fellow Levites repaired, led by Bavvai son of Henadad, ruler of the other half of the district of Keilah.
v19: Beside him Ezer son of Jeshua, ruler of Mizpah, repaired another section opposite the ascent to the armory at the Angle.
v20: After him Baruch son of Zaccai worked zealously on another section, from the Angle to the entrance of the house of Eliashib the high priest.
v21: After him Meremoth son of Uriah, son of Hakkoz, repaired another section, from the entrance of Eliashib’s house to the end of the house.
</p>

<p>
v22: After him the priests from the surrounding plain made repairs.
v23: After them Benjamin and Hasshub repaired opposite their house. After them Azariah son of Maaseiah, son of Ananiah, repaired beside his house.
v24: After him Binnui son of Henadad repaired another section, from Azariah’s house to the Angle and the Corner.
v25: Palal son of Uzai repaired opposite the Angle and the projecting tower of the upper royal house, by the court of the guard. After him came Pedaiah son of Parosh.
v26: The temple servants lived on the Ophel, as far as the area opposite the Water Gate on the east and the projecting tower.
v27: After him the men of Tekoa repaired another section, from opposite the great projecting tower to the wall of the Ophel.
</p>

<p>
v28: Above the Horse Gate, the priests made repairs, each opposite his own house.
v29: After them Zadok son of Immer repaired opposite his house. After him came Shemaiah son of Shecaniah, keeper of the East Gate.
v30: After him Hananiah son of Shelemiah and Hanun, the sixth son of Zalaph, repaired another section. After them Meshullam son of Berechiah repaired opposite his room.
v31: After him Malchijah, one of the goldsmiths, repaired as far as the house of the temple servants and merchants, opposite the Muster Gate, and as far as the upper room at the Corner.
v32: Between the upper room at the Corner and the Sheep Gate, the goldsmiths and merchants made repairs.
</p>

## Notes

v05: The Hebrew says the nobles would not bring their necks into the work of “their masters.” The reference may be to supervisors or to their Lord.

v06: “Old Gate” may instead be the proper name “Jeshanah Gate.”

v07: The phrase involving the governor’s throne can indicate his jurisdiction or a location associated with his seat of authority.

v08: The verb rendered “restored” is difficult here; “left untouched” is another proposed sense.

v12: Shallum’s daughters are explicitly included among those doing the repairs.

v16: This Nehemiah, son of Azbuk, is distinct from the narrator, Nehemiah son of Hacaliah.

v20: Zaccai follows the Hebrew reading tradition recorded beside the written form Zabbai.

v25: “Upper” may describe the royal house or the projecting tower; the compact wording leaves the attachment uncertain.

## Vocabulary

v01: Consecrated
Set apart for sacred use. The priests consecrate the gate and their section.

v31: Muster Gate
A gate name associated with inspection or appointment; its exact location is uncertain.
