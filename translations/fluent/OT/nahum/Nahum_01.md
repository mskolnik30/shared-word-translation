## An oracle concerning Nineveh

<p>
v01: An oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum of Elkosh.
</p>

## The LORD’s power and refuge

<p>
v02: The LORD is a jealous and avenging God;
the LORD avenges and is full of wrath.
The LORD takes vengeance on his adversaries
and reserves wrath for his enemies.
v03: The LORD is slow to anger and great in power,
but he will certainly not leave guilt unpunished.
The LORD’s way is in whirlwind and storm;
clouds are the dust of his feet.
v04: He rebukes the sea and dries it up;
he makes all the rivers run dry.
Bashan and Carmel wither;
Lebanon’s blossoms wither.
v05: Mountains quake before him,
and hills melt away.
The earth heaves before him—
the world and everyone living in it.
v06: Who can stand before his indignation?
Who can endure his burning anger?
His fury pours out like fire;
rocks break apart before him.
v07: The LORD is good,
a stronghold on the day of distress.
He knows those who take refuge in him.
v08: But with an overwhelming flood
he will make a complete end of her place;
darkness will pursue his enemies.
</p>

## The oppressor’s end

<p>
v09: What are you plotting against the LORD?
He is making a complete end;
distress will not rise a second time.
v10: Though entangled like thorns
and drunk with their drink,
they will be consumed like fully dried stubble.
v11: From you came one who plotted evil against the LORD,
a counselor of wickedness.
v12: This is what the LORD says:
“Though they are at full strength and so numerous,
they will be cut down and pass away.
Though I have afflicted you,
I will afflict you no more.
v13: Now I will break his yoke from your neck
and tear your bonds apart.”
v14: The LORD has commanded concerning you:
“No more descendants will carry your name.
From the house of your gods
I will cut off carved and cast images.
I will prepare your grave, for you are worthless.”
v15: Look, on the mountains,
the feet of one bringing good news, announcing peace!
Celebrate your festivals, Judah; fulfill your vows.
The wicked one will never again pass through you;
he is utterly cut off.
</p>

## Notes

v02-08: The opening poem has partial alphabetic features, but the transmitted lines do not form an unbroken acrostic. The draft does not reconstruct missing lines. “Her place” in verse 8 most naturally looks toward Nineveh, though the name is not repeated there.

v10-14: The thorn and drunkenness wording is difficult. The address shifts between the enemy and afflicted Judah; feminine and masculine Hebrew forms help distinguish them. The plotter is not named.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Oracle**: A prophetic pronouncement, here concerning Nineveh.

v15: **Good news**: News of release from the oppressor, expressed through the arriving messenger’s feet.
