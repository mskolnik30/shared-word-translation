## The LORD Is Jealous and Avenging

<p>
v01: An oracle concerning Nineveh. The book of the vision of Nahum the Elkoshite.
</p>

<p>
v02: A jealous and avenging God is the LORD;
the LORD avenges and is master of wrath.
The LORD takes vengeance on adversaries
and keeps wrath for enemies.

v03: The LORD is slow to anger
and great in power,
but the LORD will certainly not leave the guilty unpunished.
The LORD's way is in whirlwind and storm,
and clouds are the dust of the LORD's feet.

v04: The LORD rebukes the sea and dries it up
and makes all the rivers dry.
Bashan and Carmel wither,
and the blossom of Lebanon withers.

v05: Mountains quake before the LORD,
and hills melt.
The earth heaves before the LORD,
the world and everyone who dwell in it.

v06: Before the LORD's indignation, who can stand?
Who can endure the burning of the LORD's anger?
The LORD's wrath is poured out like fire,
and rocks are broken apart before the LORD.
</p>

## The LORD Is a Stronghold

<p>
v07: The LORD is good,
a stronghold in the day of trouble,
and knows those
who take refuge in the LORD.

v08: But with an overwhelming flood
the LORD will make a complete end of her place,
and will pursue enemies
into darkness.
</p>

## Trouble Will Not Rise Twice

<p>
v09: What are you plotting against the LORD?
The LORD will make a complete end;
trouble will not rise
a second time.

v10: For though they are tangled like thorns
and soaked as with their drink,
they will be consumed
like completely dry stubble.

v11: From you came one
who plotted evil against the LORD,
one who counseled
what is worthless.
</p>

## The Yoke Will Be Broken

<p>
v12: Thus says the LORD:

“Though they are strong and numerous,
even so they will be cut down
and pass away.
Though I have afflicted you,
I will afflict you no more.

v13: And now I will break their yoke from upon you
and tear apart your bonds.”
</p>

## Good News upon the Mountains

<p>
v14: The LORD has commanded concerning you:

“No offspring will continue your name.
From the house of your gods
I will cut off carved image and cast image.
I will prepare your grave,
for you are contemptible.”

v15: Look upon the mountains—
the feet of one bringing good news,
proclaiming peace!

Celebrate your festivals, O Judah;
fulfill your vows.
For the worthless one
will no longer pass through you;
that one is completely cut off.
</p>

## Notes

v01: “oracle” — The Hebrew *massa* can mean an oracle or burden. It introduces a weighty prophetic word directed here against Nineveh, capital of Assyria.

v02: “jealous and avenging” — The language presents the LORD's jealousy as intense commitment and the LORD's vengeance as judicial response to adversaries. The translation retains the force of the paired terms without reducing them to ordinary human emotion.

v03: “will certainly not leave the guilty unpunished” — The Hebrew intensifies the verb through repetition. The wording holds the LORD's patience together with the certainty that wrongdoing is not simply ignored.

v05: “The earth heaves” — The Hebrew verb is difficult in this context and can carry the sense of lifting, trembling, or being devastated. TSW preserves the image of creation convulsing before the LORD.

v07: “knows those who take refuge” — “Know” is relational rather than merely informational. The LORD's knowledge of those seeking refuge is placed beside the LORD's power in judgment.

v08: “her place” — The Hebrew pronoun most naturally points toward Nineveh, though the city is not named again in the immediate line. The reference is preserved without expansion.

v10: “soaked as with their drink” — The Hebrew is compressed and difficult. The line seems to combine tangled thorns, drunkenness or saturation, and dry stubble as images of a people who appear resistant yet are easily consumed.

v11: “what is worthless” — The Hebrew *beliyya'al* can describe worthlessness, wickedness, or destructive rebellion. The term later developed additional associations, but TSW retains its immediate moral force here.

v12: The addressee shifts from Nineveh's strength to the afflicted people whom the LORD promises not to afflict again. The prophetic speech moves rapidly between judgment and consolation.

v14: “No offspring will continue your name” — The judgment concerns the end of dynastic or national continuity, not merely the death of one individual.

v15: In the Masoretic chapter division this verse begins chapter 2. TSW follows standard public English chapter numbering, where the proclamation of good news concludes Nahum 1.

## Vocabulary

v01: מַשָּׂא (massa)
“Oracle” or “burden.” A weighty prophetic pronouncement.

v02: קַנּוֹא (qanno)
“Jealous” or “zealous.” Intense exclusive commitment.

v02: נָקַם (naqam)
“To avenge” or “take vengeance.” To act in response to wrong or hostility.

v03: אָרֵךְ אַפַּיִם (arekh appayim)
“Slow to anger.” Literally “long of nostrils,” an idiom for patience.

v07: מָעוֹז (maoz)
“Stronghold” or “refuge.” A place of safety in danger.

v07: חָסָה (chasah)
“To take refuge.” To seek protection or shelter.

v11: בְּלִיַּעַל (beliyya'al)
“Worthlessness,” “wickedness,” or “destructive rebellion.” A term for what is corrupt and without good purpose.

v15: שָׁלוֹם (shalom)
“Peace,” “wholeness,” or “well-being.” Here the content of the good news proclaimed to Judah.
