## The dogs return at evening

<p>
v01: For the music leader. “Do Not Destroy.” Of David. A miktam, when Saul sent men to watch the house and kill him.
Rescue me from my enemies, my God;
set me high beyond those rising against me.
v02: Rescue me from evildoers;
save me from men of bloodshed.
</p>

<p>
v03: Look! They lie in wait for my life;
fierce men gather against me—
not because I rebelled or sinned, LORD.
v04: Though I have done no wrong, they run and take their positions.
Wake up! Come to meet me and see!
v05: You, LORD God of Armies, God of Israel,
wake up to call all the nations to account!
Show no favor to any treacherous evildoer. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v06: They return at evening, growling like dogs,
and circle the city.
v07: Listen to what pours from their mouths!
Swords are on their lips: “Who can hear us?”
</p>

<p>
v08: But you, LORD, laugh at them;
you mock all the nations.
v09: Against his strength, I watch for you,
for God is my stronghold.
v10: My God of faithful love will come to meet me;
God will let me look upon those watching for my fall.
</p>

<p>
v11: Do not kill them, or my people may forget.
Make them wander by your might; bring them low,
Lord, our shield.
v12: For the sin of their mouths, the words on their lips,
let them be caught in their pride,
for the curses and lies they speak.
v13: Consume them in wrath; consume them until they are gone!
Then they will know that God rules over Jacob,
to the ends of the earth. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v14: They return at evening, growling like dogs,
and circle the city.
v15: They wander in search of food;
if they are not satisfied, they stay the night.
</p>

<p>
v16: But I will sing of your strength;
at morning I will sing aloud of your faithful love.
You have been my stronghold,
a place to flee on my day of distress.
v17: My strength, I will make music to you,
for God is my stronghold,
my God of faithful love.
</p>

## Notes

v09–10,17: Verse 9 has “his strength,” unlike “my strength” in verse 17. “Against” supplies the sense of the abrupt opening. Verse 10 follows the traditional reading “my faithful love” rather than the written “his faithful love.”

v11–15: “Do not kill them” and “consume them” remain side by side. The dog refrain returns; the final verb in verse 15 can describe lodging all night, though some understand it as growling.

## Vocabulary

v09: **Stronghold — misgav**: A high, secure place. The word echoes the request to be set beyond reach in verse 1.
