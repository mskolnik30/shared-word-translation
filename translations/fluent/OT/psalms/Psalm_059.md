For the leader. Do not destroy. Of David. A Miktam. When Saul sent and they watched the house to kill him.

<p>
v01: Deliver me from my enemies, my God;
    protect me from people who rise against me.
v02: Deliver me from workers of wickedness,
    and save me from men of blood.
v03: For, look, they lie in wait for my soul;
    the mighty gather against me,
    not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O LORD.
v04: Without fault they run and prepare themselves.
    Rouse yourself to help me, and see!
</p>

<p>
v05: You, LORD God of Hosts,
    God of Israel, arise to punish all the nations.
    Do not be merciful to all treacherous workers of evil. Selah.

v06: They return at evening;
    they growl like a dog, and go around the city.
v07: Look, they spew with their mouths;
    swords are on their lips, for who hears?
v08: But you, LORD, laugh at them;
    you mock all the nations.

v09: O, my strength, I will wait for you;
    for God is my fortress.

v10: My God of steadfast love will come to meet me;
    God will let me look upon my enemies.
v11: Do not kill them, lest my people forget;
    make them wander by your power and bring them down, O Lord, our shield.
v12: By the sin of their mouth, and the words of their lips,
    let them be caught in their pride,
    for the curses and lies they utter.
v13: Consume them in wrath;
    consume them till they are no more.
    Then it will be known that God rules over Jacob
    to the ends of the earth. Selah.

v14: At evening let them return;
    let them howl like a dog, and go around the city.
v15: They wander for food;
    if they are not satisfied, they remain all night.
v16: Yet I will sing of your strength;
    in the morning I will sing of your steadfast love.
    For you have been my fortress,
    and a refuge in the day of my distress.
</p>

<p>
v17: O my strength, I will sing praises to you,
    for God is my fortress, the God of steadfast love.
</p>

## Notes

v11: *lest my people forget* — Implies that the destruction of the enemies should serve as a memorial of God's deliverance and protection.

## Vocabulary

v02: אֱלֹהִים (*e.lo.him*) — "God" here denotes the deity as powerful and supreme, emphasizing his role as protector and deliverer.

v11: חֶ֫סֶד (*che.sed*) — Usually translating as "steadfast love" or "lovingkindness," this denotes God's persistent and unconditional mercy and loyalty towards his people.

v13: גָּאוֹן (*ga.on*) — "Pride" in this context refers to the arrogance of the enemies whom God is asked to judge.

v17: מִשְׂגָּב (*mis.gav*) — "Fortress" conveys the idea of God being a strong and secure refuge for those who seek protection.
