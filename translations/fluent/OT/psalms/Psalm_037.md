## Wait for the LORD

<p>
v01: Of David.
Do not burn with anger over evildoers
or envy those who do wrong.
v02: They will soon wither like grass
and fade like green plants.
</p>

<p>
v03: Trust the LORD and do good.
Live in the land and feed on faithfulness.
v04: Take delight in the LORD,
and he will give you your heart’s desires.
</p>

<p>
v05: Entrust your way to the LORD;
trust him, and he will act.
v06: He will bring your righteousness out like light,
your vindication like the midday sun.
</p>

<p>
v07: Be still before the LORD and wait for him.
Do not burn with anger over someone who prospers
by carrying out evil schemes.
</p>

<p>
v08: Let go of anger; leave fury behind.
Do not burn with resentment—it only leads to harm.
v09: Evildoers will be cut off,
but those who wait for the LORD will inherit the land.
</p>

<p>
v10: A little while, and the wicked will be gone.
Look closely at their place: they are no longer there.
v11: The humble will inherit the land
and delight in abundant peace.
</p>

<p>
v12: The wicked plot against the righteous
and gnash their teeth at them.
v13: The Lord laughs at the wicked,
for he sees their day approaching.
</p>

<p>
v14: The wicked draw their swords and bend their bows
to bring down the afflicted and needy,
to slaughter those who walk uprightly.
v15: Their swords will enter their own hearts,
and their bows will break.
</p>

<p>
v16: Better the little a righteous person has
than the wealth of many wicked people.
v17: The arms of the wicked will be broken,
but the LORD supports the righteous.
</p>

<p>
v18: The LORD knows the days of the blameless;
their inheritance will last forever.
v19: They will not be put to shame in bad times;
in days of famine they will have enough.
</p>

<p>
v20: But the wicked will perish.
The LORD’s enemies are like the splendor of pastures:
they vanish—vanish in smoke.
</p>

<p>
v21: The wicked borrow and do not repay;
the righteous give generously.
v22: Those he blesses will inherit the land;
those he curses will be cut off.
</p>

<p>
v23: The LORD makes a person’s steps secure
and delights in that person’s way.
v24: Though they stumble, they will not be hurled down,
for the LORD holds their hand.
</p>

<p>
v25: I was young, and now I am old,
yet I have not seen the righteous abandoned
or their offspring searching for bread.
v26: They are always generous and lend freely;
their offspring become a blessing.
</p>

<p>
v27: Turn from evil and do good,
and dwell securely forever.
v28: The LORD loves justice and will not forsake his faithful ones.
They are guarded forever,
but the offspring of the wicked will be cut off.
</p>

<p>
v29: The righteous will inherit the land
and live there forever.
</p>

<p>
v30: The righteous speak wisdom;
their tongues speak what is just.
v31: Their God’s instruction is in their hearts;
their steps do not slip.
</p>

<p>
v32: The wicked watch the righteous,
looking for a way to kill them.
v33: The LORD will not abandon them to the wicked
or let them be condemned when brought to trial.
</p>

<p>
v34: Wait for the LORD and keep his way.
He will raise you up to inherit the land;
you will see the wicked cut off.
</p>

<p>
v35: I saw a wicked tyrant
spreading like a flourishing tree in its native soil.
v36: Then he passed away—he was gone!
I searched for him, but he could not be found.
</p>

<p>
v37: Watch the blameless; look at the upright.
There is a future for the person of peace.
v38: But rebels will be destroyed together;
the future of the wicked will be cut off.
</p>

<p>
v39: The salvation of the righteous comes from the LORD,
their stronghold in times of distress.
v40: The LORD helps and rescues them;
he rescues them from the wicked and saves them,
because they take refuge in him.
</p>

## Notes

v01–40: The poem is alphabetically arranged, mostly in paired verses. Repeated land and inheritance language connects its counsel with life in a shared place.

v03,20: “Feed on faithfulness” may also mean cultivate faithfulness or graze in safety. “Splendor of pastures” is uncertain; the same consonants have been read as the fat of lambs.

v23,25,28: The delight in verse 23 may be God’s in the person’s way or the person’s in God’s way. Verse 25 is the speaker’s life testimony. Verse 28 retains the Hebrew statement that the faithful are guarded forever.

v35–38: The Hebrew pictures a flourishing native tree and says “he passed away,” though other readings differ. The “future” of a person may include descendants as well as what lies ahead.

## Vocabulary

v05: **Entrust — galal**: Literally, roll your way onto the LORD. The image transfers a burden.

v31: **Instruction — torah**: Teaching that directs life, here carried within the heart.
