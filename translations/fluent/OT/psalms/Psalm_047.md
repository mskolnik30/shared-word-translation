## King over all the earth

<p>
v01: For the music leader. Of the sons of Korah. A psalm.
Clap your hands, all peoples;
shout to God with joyful cries!
v02: The LORD Most High is awe-inspiring,
a great king over all the earth.
v03: He subdues peoples beneath us,
nations beneath our feet.
v04: He chooses our inheritance for us,
the pride of Jacob, whom he loves. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v05: God goes up amid shouts,
the LORD amid the sound of the ram’s horn.
v06: Sing praise to God, sing praise!
Sing praise to our king, sing praise!
v07: God is king of all the earth;
sing a song of understanding.
</p>

<p>
v08: God reigns over the nations;
God sits on his holy throne.
v09: The nobles of the peoples gather
as the people of Abraham’s God.
The shields of the earth belong to God;
he is greatly exalted.
</p>

## Notes

v07,09: “Song of understanding” translates maskil, also an uncertain psalm title. The nobles may gather as the people of Abraham’s God or with them. The shields picture earthly power or its protectors belonging to God.

## Vocabulary

v05: **Ram’s horn — shofar**: An animal-horn instrument used for loud signals and ceremonial sound.
