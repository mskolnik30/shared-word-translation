## Praise and a watered earth

<p>
v01: For the music leader. A psalm of David. A song.
God, praise waits in silence for you in Zion;
to you the vow will be fulfilled.
v02: You who hear prayer,
all flesh will come to you.
v03: Wrongdoings have overwhelmed me,
but you atone for our rebellions.
v04: Happy is the one you choose and bring near
to live in your courts!
Let us be filled with the goodness of your house,
your holy temple.
</p>

<p>
v05: With awesome deeds of righteousness you answer us,
God who saves us,
the trust of all the earth’s farthest reaches
and the distant seas.
v06: You establish mountains by your power,
clothed with might.
v07: You still the roar of seas, the roar of their waves,
and the tumult of peoples.
v08: Those who live at earth’s edges stand in awe of your signs.
You make the places of sunrise and sunset shout for joy.
</p>

<p>
v09: You attend to the earth and water it, enriching it greatly.
God’s stream is full of water.
You prepare their grain, for this is how you prepare the earth.
v10: You soak its furrows and settle its ridges,
soften it with showers and bless its growth.
v11: You crown the year with your goodness;
your tracks drip with abundance.
v12: The wilderness pastures drip with it;
the hills wrap themselves in joy.
v13: The meadows put on flocks;
the valleys clothe themselves in grain.
They shout for joy—yes, they sing!
</p>

## Notes

v01: The opening joins silence and praise: it can suggest praise waiting in silence, or praise being fitting for God. The Hebrew does not explain the relationship.

v09–13: Water, tracks dripping abundance, clothed hills, and singing fields form one extended picture of the land receiving God's care.

## Vocabulary

v03: **Atonement**: The Hebrew verb kipper concerns removing or dealing with wrongdoing so the barrier it creates is overcome.

v11: **Tracks**: The image is of God's passing leaving rich abundance behind.
