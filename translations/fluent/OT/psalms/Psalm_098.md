## Creation sings before the judge

<p>
v01: A psalm.
Sing a new song to the LORD,
for he has worked wonders.
His right hand and his holy arm
have won victory for him.
v02: The LORD has made his salvation known;
he has revealed his righteousness before the nations' eyes.
v03: He has remembered his faithful love and faithfulness
toward the house of Israel.
All the ends of the earth have seen
the salvation of our God.
</p>

<p>
v04: Shout to the LORD, all the earth!
Break into joyful song and make music.
v05: Make music to the LORD with the lyre,
with the lyre and the sound of song.
v06: With trumpets and the sound of the ram's horn,
shout before the king, the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v07: Let the sea thunder with everything in it,
the world and everyone who lives in it.
v08: Let the rivers clap their hands;
let the mountains sing for joy together
v09: before the LORD, for he is coming to judge the earth.
He will judge the world with righteousness
and the peoples fairly.
</p>

## Notes

v01–03,09: The poem holds Israel's particular experience and the nations' witness together. Its ending resembles Psalm 96 but has its own wording, retained here.

## Vocabulary

v06: **Trumpets and ram's horn**: Distinct instruments: metal trumpets and an animal horn.
