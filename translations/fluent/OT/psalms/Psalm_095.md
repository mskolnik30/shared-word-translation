## Worship and a warning

<p>
v01: Come, let us sing for joy to the LORD;
let us shout to the rock of our salvation.
v02: Let us come before him with thanksgiving
and shout to him with songs.
</p>

<p>
v03: For the LORD is a great God,
a great king above all gods.
v04: The depths of the earth are in his hand;
the mountain heights belong to him.
v05: The sea is his, for he made it;
his hands formed the dry land.
</p>

<p>
v06: Come, let us bow down in worship;
let us kneel before the LORD who made us.
v07: For he is our God,
and we are the people of his pasture,
the flock in his hand.
Today, if you hear his voice,
</p>

<p>
v08: “Do not harden your hearts as at Meribah,
as on the day of Massah in the wilderness,
v09: where your fathers tested me
and put me to the proof, though they had seen my work.
</p>

<p>
v10: For forty years I loathed that generation.
I said, ‘Their hearts keep straying;
they do not know my ways.’
v11: So I swore in my anger,
‘They will never enter my rest.’”
</p>

## Notes

v07–11: The invitation to worship turns into a warning in God's voice. The final Hebrew oath has the force of a firm denial: they will not enter his rest.

## Vocabulary

v08: **Meribah and Massah**: Names associated with quarreling and testing in Israel's wilderness story.
