## A community protests its defeat

<p>
v01: For the music leader. Of the sons of Korah. A maskil.
God, we have heard with our own ears;
our ancestors told us what you did in their days, long ago.
v02: With your hand you drove out nations and planted our ancestors.
You afflicted peoples and spread our ancestors abroad.
v03: They did not gain the land by their own sword;
their own arm did not save them.
It was your right hand, your arm, and the light of your face,
because you favored them.
</p>

<p>
v04: You are my king, God;
command victories for Jacob.
v05: Through you we thrust back our foes;
through your name we trample those who rise against us.
v06: I do not trust in my bow;
my sword cannot save me.
v07: You saved us from our foes
and put those who hate us to shame.
v08: In God we boast all day long;
we will thank your name forever. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v09: Yet you have rejected and disgraced us;
you no longer march out with our armies.
v10: You make us retreat before the foe;
those who hate us plunder us for themselves.
v11: You hand us over like sheep for food
and scatter us among the nations.
v12: You sell your people for nothing
and gain no wealth from their price.
</p>

<p>
v13: You make us a reproach to our neighbors,
a mockery and a jeer to those around us.
v14: You make us a byword among the nations,
a people at whom others shake their heads.
v15: My disgrace is before me all day;
shame covers my face,
v16: at the voice of the one who taunts and reviles,
at the sight of the enemy bent on revenge.
</p>

<p>
v17: All this has come upon us, yet we have not forgotten you
or been false to your covenant.
v18: Our hearts have not turned back;
our steps have not strayed from your path,
v19: though you crushed us in a place of jackals
and covered us with deepest darkness.
</p>

<p>
v20: If we had forgotten our God’s name
or spread our hands to a foreign god,
v21: would not God search this out?
He knows the secrets of the heart.
v22: Yet because of you we are killed all day long,
counted as sheep for slaughter.
</p>

<p>
v23: Wake up! Why do you sleep, Lord?
Rise! Do not reject us forever.
v24: Why do you hide your face
and forget our affliction and oppression?
v25: We have sunk down into the dust;
our bellies cling to the ground.
v26: Rise to help us;
redeem us for the sake of your faithful love.
</p>

## Notes

v02–03: The Hebrew contrasts the nations driven out with the ancestors planted in the land. The ancestors are named explicitly here to keep the two groups clear.

v17–23: The community insists that it has not abandoned the covenant. Its suffering is not explained away as concealed guilt; it even asks why God sleeps.

## Vocabulary

v14: **Byword**: A people’s humiliation becomes a saying on others’ lips; shaking the head is another gesture of scorn.

v19: **Deepest darkness — tsalmavet**: A word also rendered “shadow of death.” Here it covers a devastated people.
