## Brothers dwelling together

<p>
v01: A song of ascents. Of David.
Look how good and pleasant it is
when brothers dwell together!
</p>

<p>
v02: It is like fine oil on the head,
running down the beard, Aaron’s beard,
running down to the collar of his robes.
</p>

<p>
v03: It is like the dew of Hermon
running down on the mountains of Zion.
For there the LORD commanded the blessing:
life forever.
</p>

## Notes

v02–03: The repeated downward flow connects oil and dew. Hermon’s dew on Zion is poetic imagery; the translation does not replace it with a geographical explanation.

## Vocabulary

v02: **Aaron**: Israel’s priestly ancestor, named in the image of oil flowing over head, beard, and robes.
