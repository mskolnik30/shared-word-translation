## A prayer of integrity

<p>
v01: Of David.
Vindicate me, LORD, for I have walked with integrity.
I have trusted in the LORD without wavering.
v02: Examine me, LORD, and test me;
refine my kidneys and my heart.
v03: Your faithful love is before my eyes,
and I walk in your truth.
</p>

<p>
v04: I do not sit with deceitful people
or go along with those who hide what they are.
v05: I hate the gathering of evildoers
and will not sit with the wicked.
</p>

<p>
v06: I wash my hands in innocence
and walk around your altar, LORD,
v07: to raise a voice of thanksgiving
and recount all your wonders.
v08: LORD, I love the house where you dwell,
the place where your glory makes its home.
</p>

<p>
v09: Do not sweep my life away with sinners,
my life with men who shed blood,
v10: whose hands are full of schemes
and whose right hands overflow with bribes.
</p>

<p>
v11: But I will walk with integrity.
Redeem me and be gracious to me.
v12: My feet stand on level ground;
in the assemblies I will bless the LORD.
</p>

## Notes

v02: Kidneys and heart picture the hidden inner person—desires, thought, and conscience. The speaker invites a searching test.

v06–11: Washing hands and approaching the altar join worship with a claim of integrity. The prayer still asks for redemption and grace.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Integrity — tom**: Wholeness or soundness of conduct; a life that holds together rather than concealing deceit.
