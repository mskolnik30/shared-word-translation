## Brief life and enduring God

<p>
v01: A prayer of an afflicted person who grows faint and pours out a complaint before the LORD.
LORD, hear my prayer;
let my cry for help reach you.
v02: Do not hide your face from me when I am in distress.
Lean down and listen;
answer quickly when I call.
</p>

<p>
v03: My days vanish like smoke;
my bones burn like embers.
v04: My heart is struck down and withered like grass;
I forget even to eat my bread.
v05: Because of my loud groaning,
my bones cling to my flesh.
</p>

<p>
v06: I am like a desert owl,
like an owl among the ruins.
v07: I lie awake,
like a lone bird on a rooftop.
v08: All day my enemies taunt me;
those who mock me use my name in curses.
</p>

<p>
v09: I eat ashes like bread
and mix my drink with tears,
v10: because of your indignation and fury;
you have lifted me up and thrown me away.
v11: My days are like a lengthening shadow;
I wither like grass.
</p>

<p>
v12: But you, LORD, sit enthroned forever;
your remembrance lasts through every generation.
v13: You will rise and have compassion on Zion,
for it is time to show her favor;
the appointed time has come.
v14: Your servants cherish her stones
and show tenderness even to her dust.
</p>

<p>
v15: The nations will fear the LORD's name,
and all the earth's kings your glory.
v16: For the LORD will rebuild Zion
and appear in his glory.
v17: He will turn toward the prayer of the destitute;
he will not despise their prayer.
</p>

<p>
v18: Let this be written for the generation to come,
so a people yet to be created may praise YAH:
v19: he looked down from his holy height;
from heaven the LORD looked at the earth,
v20: to hear the prisoner's groan
and release those marked for death,
v21: so the LORD's name may be recounted in Zion
and his praise in Jerusalem,
v22: when peoples gather together,
and kingdoms, to serve the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v23: He has broken my strength along the way;
he has cut my days short.
v24: I say, “My God, do not take me away halfway through my days,
you whose years last through all generations.
</p>

<p>
v25: Long ago you founded the earth;
the heavens are the work of your hands.
v26: They will perish, but you will remain.
They will all wear out like a garment;
you will change them like clothing, and they will pass away.
v27: But you remain the same,
and your years will never end.
</p>

<p>
v28: Your servants' children will dwell securely;
their offspring will be established before you.”
</p>

## Notes

v03,06,08: The terms rendered embers and desert owl are uncertain; exact bird species cannot be established. Using the sufferer's name in curses means invoking a fate like his.

v23–24: The reading tradition has “my strength,” while the written form has “his strength.” The Hebrew's personal complaint is retained; later quotations do not replace its speaker structure.

## Vocabulary

v18: **YAH**: The short form of the divine name.

v20: **Marked for death**: Literally “sons of death,” people facing death who are pictured here as captives.
