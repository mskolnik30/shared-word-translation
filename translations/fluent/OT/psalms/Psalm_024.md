## The earth belongs to the LORD

<p>
v01: Of David. A psalm.
The earth belongs to the LORD, with everything in it—
the world and all who live in it.
v02: For he founded it upon the seas
and established it upon the rivers.
</p>

## Who may stand in his holy place?

<p>
v03: Who may climb the LORD’s mountain?
Who may stand in his holy place?
v04: One with clean hands and a pure heart,
who has not lifted up my life to falsehood
and has not sworn deceitfully.
v05: Such a person will receive blessing from the LORD
and vindication from the God who saves them.
v06: This is the generation of those who seek him,
who seek your face—Jacob. Selah.
</p>

## The King of glory

<p>
v07: Lift your heads, you gates!
Rise up, you ancient doors,
so the King of glory may come in!
v08: Who is this King of glory?
The LORD, strong and mighty,
the LORD, mighty in battle.
v09: Lift your heads, you gates!
Lift yourselves up, you ancient doors,
so the King of glory may come in!
v10: Who is he, this King of glory?
The LORD of hosts—
he is the King of glory. Selah.
</p>

## Notes

v04: The pinned text reads “my life,” an abrupt first-person form. Many translations follow “his life” from another reading; others connect the phrase with invoking God falsely. The draft keeps the received difficulty.

v06: The read form is plural, “those who seek him.” “Jacob” may name the seeking people; “God of Jacob” is a familiar alternative that adds words not expressed in this pinned text.

## Vocabulary

v02: Upon the seas
An ancient image of the earth established over waters.

v10: LORD of hosts
The LORD associated with heavenly armies or multitudes.
