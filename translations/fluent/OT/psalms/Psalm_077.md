## Remembering through the sleepless night

<p>
v01: For the music leader. According to Jeduthun. Of Asaph. A psalm.
I cry aloud to God;
aloud I call to God, and he listens to me.
v02: In my distress I sought the Lord.
All night my hand stayed stretched out without tiring;
I refused to be comforted.
v03: I remember God and groan;
I reflect, and my spirit grows faint. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v04: You hold my eyelids open.
I am so troubled that I cannot speak.
v05: I think about the days long past,
the years of ancient times.
v06: At night I remember my song;
I ponder in my heart, and my spirit searches:
</p>

<p>
v07: “Will the Lord reject us forever?
Will he never show favor again?
v08: Has his faithful love vanished for good?
Has his promise failed for every generation?
v09: Has God forgotten to be gracious?
Has anger shut away his compassion?” Selah.
</p>

<p>
v10: Then I said, “This is what wounds me:
the right hand of the Most High has changed.”
</p>

<p>
v11: I will remember the deeds of YAH;
yes, I will remember your wonders of long ago.
v12: I will ponder everything you have done
and reflect on your great deeds.
</p>

<p>
v13: God, your way is holy.
What god is as great as God?
v14: You are the God who works wonders;
you have made your strength known among the peoples.
v15: With your arm you redeemed your people,
the children of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v16: The waters saw you, God;
the waters saw you and writhed.
Even the depths trembled.
v17: Clouds poured down water;
the skies gave their thunderous cry,
and your arrows flashed back and forth.
v18: Your thunder rolled in the whirlwind;
lightning lit the world.
The earth trembled and shook.
</p>

<p>
v19: Your way ran through the sea,
your path through mighty waters,
but your footprints could not be found.
v20: You led your people like a flock
by the hands of Moses and Aaron.
</p>

## Notes

v10: This difficult line can be read as grief that the Most High's hand has changed, or as an appeal recalling the years of that hand's action. The draft follows the former, leaving the subsequent remembering to make the turn.

v19–20: The path through water evokes the exodus. Invisible footprints are followed by the ordinary human agency of Moses and Aaron.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Jeduthun**: A name associated with temple music; the exact force of “according to” in the title is uncertain.

v11: **YAH**: The short form of the divine name; it is retained where the Hebrew uses it.
