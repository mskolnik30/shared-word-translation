## Come and see, come and hear

<p>
v01: For the music leader. A song. A psalm.
Shout to God, all the earth!
v02: Make music to the glory of his name;
make his praise glorious.
v03: Say to God, “How awesome are your deeds!
Before your great strength, your enemies cringe.
v04: All the earth bows before you and makes music to you;
they make music to your name.” Selah.
</p>

<p>
v05: Come and see what God has done,
his awesome dealings with humanity.
v06: He turned the sea into dry ground;
they crossed the river on foot.
There let us rejoice in him!
v07: He rules forever by his might;
his eyes watch the nations.
Let the rebellious not exalt themselves. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v08: Bless our God, you peoples;
let the sound of his praise be heard.
v09: He has kept us among the living
and has not let our feet slip.
v10: You tested us, God;
you refined us as silver is refined.
v11: You brought us into a net;
you laid a crushing load on our backs.
v12: You let people ride over our heads.
We went through fire and water,
but you brought us out into abundance.
</p>

<p>
v13: I will enter your house with burnt offerings
and fulfill my vows to you—
v14: the vows my lips uttered
and my mouth spoke when I was in distress.
v15: I will offer you fat animals as burnt offerings,
with the smoke of rams;
I will sacrifice cattle and male goats. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v16: Come and hear, all who revere God;
I will tell what he has done for me.
v17: My mouth called to him;
high praise was ready on my tongue.
v18: Had I cherished wrongdoing in my heart,
the Lord would not have listened.
v19: But God did listen;
he paid attention to my prayer’s voice.
v20: Blessed be God, who has not turned away my prayer
or withdrawn his faithful love from me.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The verb can describe cringing submission or feigned obedience; the enemies' inward loyalty is not asserted.

v06: Sea and river evoke Israel's passages through water. “There” draws the worshiping community into the remembered rescue.

v17: The Hebrew locates praise “under my tongue,” a compact image rendered here as praise ready on the tongue.

## Vocabulary

v12: **Ride over our heads**: An image of brutal domination; the suffering is not merely embarrassment.

v15: **Burnt offerings**: Sacrifices consumed on the altar; the listed animals remain distinct.
