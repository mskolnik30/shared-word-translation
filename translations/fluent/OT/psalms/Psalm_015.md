## Who may live in your tent?

<p>
v01: A psalm of David.
LORD, who may be a guest in your tent?
Who may dwell on your holy mountain?
v02: One who walks with integrity, does what is right,
and speaks truth from the heart;
v03: who does not slander with the tongue,
harm a neighbor,
or take up a reproach against a friend;
v04: who rejects the contemptible
but honors those who revere the LORD;
who keeps an oath even when it hurts;
v05: who does not lend money at interest
or take a bribe against the innocent.
One who does these things will never be shaken.
</p>

## Notes

v04: The oath is understood as one kept even at personal cost. The compressed wording can also be read as swearing to do harm.

v05: The text names interest without adding the qualifier excessive.

## Vocabulary

v01: Tent
The sanctuary pictured as the LORD’s dwelling.
