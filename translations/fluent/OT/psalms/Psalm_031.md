## My times are in your hand

<p>
v01: For the music leader. A psalm of David.
In you, LORD, I take refuge; never let me be put to shame.
Rescue me in your righteousness.
v02: Bend your ear toward me; hurry to rescue me.
Be my rock of refuge, a fortress to save me.
v03: You are my crag and my fortress.
For your name’s sake, lead me and guide me.
v04: Free me from the net they hid for me,
for you are my stronghold.
</p>

<p>
v05: Into your hand I entrust my spirit.
You have redeemed me, LORD, faithful God.
v06: I hate those who cling to empty idols;
my trust is in the LORD.
v07: I will rejoice and be glad in your faithful love.
You have seen my affliction; you know my life’s distress.
v08: You have not shut me into the enemy’s hand;
you have set my feet in a broad place.
</p>

<p>
v09: Be gracious to me, LORD, for I am in distress.
My eyes waste away with grief, my whole being and my body too.
v10: My life drains away in sorrow, my years in sighing.
My strength fails because of my wrongdoing; my bones waste away.
v11: Because of all my foes I have become an object of scorn,
especially to my neighbors, a terror to my acquaintances.
Those who see me outside flee from me.
v12: I have slipped from memory like someone dead;
I am like a broken vessel.
v13: I hear the whispering of many—terror on every side!
They gather against me and plot to take my life.
</p>

<p>
v14: But I trust in you, LORD.
I say, “You are my God.”
v15: My times are in your hand.
Rescue me from the hand of my enemies and pursuers.
v16: Let your face shine on your servant;
save me in your faithful love.
v17: LORD, do not let me be put to shame, for I call on you.
Let the wicked be ashamed and fall silent in Sheol.
v18: Silence lying lips
that speak insolently against the righteous with pride and contempt.
</p>

<p>
v19: How abundant is the goodness you have stored up for those who revere you,
which you show to those who take refuge in you, before all people!
v20: You hide them in the shelter of your presence from human plots;
you keep them safe in a shelter from quarreling tongues.
v21: Blessed be the LORD!
He showed me wondrous faithful love in a besieged city.
v22: In my panic I said, “I am cut off from your sight!”
Yet you heard my plea when I cried to you for help.
</p>

<p>
v23: Love the LORD, all you faithful ones.
The LORD guards the faithful and repays the proud in full.
v24: Be strong, and let your hearts take courage,
all you who hope in the LORD.
</p>

## Notes

v06,10: The Hebrew says “I hate” and “my wrongdoing.” These are retained rather than the alternative readings “you hate” and “my affliction.”

v20–21: The word translated “plots” is rare. The city may be besieged or strongly fortified; the verse does not identify it.

## Vocabulary

v05: **Spirit — ruaḥ**: Breath or life entrusted to God. Here the prayer belongs within an appeal for rescue.

v15: **My times**: The changing circumstances and course of a life, placed in God’s hand.
