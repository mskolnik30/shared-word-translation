## The king rejoices

<p>
v01: For the director. A psalm of David.
LORD, the king rejoices in your strength;
how greatly he celebrates your saving help!
v02: You have given him his heart’s desire
and have not withheld what his lips requested. Selah.
v03: You meet him with blessings of goodness;
you place a crown of fine gold on his head.
v04: He asked you for life, and you gave it—
length of days, forever and ever.
v05: His glory is great through your saving help;
you lay splendor and majesty upon him.
v06: You make him a blessing forever;
you gladden him with the joy of your presence.
v07: For the king trusts in the LORD;
through the Most High’s faithful love he will not be shaken.
</p>

## Enemies will not prevail

<p>
v08: Your hand will find all your enemies;
your right hand will find those who hate you.
v09: You will make them like a fiery furnace when you appear;
the LORD will swallow them in his anger,
and fire will consume them.
v10: You will destroy their fruit from the earth,
their offspring from the human race.
v11: Though they intend evil against you
and devise a plot, they will not succeed.
v12: For you will make them turn their backs
when you aim your bowstrings at their faces.
</p>

<p>
v13: Rise high, LORD, in your strength!
We will sing and make music of your might.
</p>

## Notes

v04: The royal prayer speaks expansively of life forever and ever. The draft preserves the expression.

v08–12: The addressee can be understood as God or as the king empowered by God. The text itself shifts to naming the LORD in verse 9.

v10: The destruction of offspring is part of the poem’s severe vision of victory and is not softened.

## Vocabulary

v07: Faithful love
Steadfast loyalty that sustains the king.
