## The LORD is my shepherd

<p>
v01: A psalm of David.
The LORD is my shepherd; I will lack nothing.
v02: He lets me lie down in green pastures;
he leads me beside restful waters.
v03: He restores my life;
he guides me along right paths for his name’s sake.
</p>

<p>
v04: Even when I walk through a valley of deepest darkness,
I will fear no harm, because you are with me.
Your rod and your staff give me comfort.
</p>

<p>
v05: You spread a table before me in the presence of my enemies;
you lavish oil on my head; my cup is brimming.
v06: Surely goodness and faithful love will pursue me all my life,
and I will return to the LORD’s house for length of days.
</p>

## Notes

v04: The word can mean deepest darkness or the shadow of death. The draft retains danger without restricting it to the moment of death.

v06: The pointed verb means return; dwelling reflects another reading or understanding. “Length of days” is retained without deciding between a long life and an unlimited future.

## Vocabulary

v04: Rod and staff
Tools of the shepherd’s protection and guidance.

v06: Pursue
Goodness and faithful love pursue the speaker, reversing an image often used of enemies.
