## God calls his people to account

<p>
v01: A psalm of Asaph.
The Mighty One, God, the LORD, speaks and summons the earth
from the sun’s rising to its setting.
v02: From Zion, the perfection of beauty,
God shines forth.
v03: Our God comes and will not stay silent.
Fire devours before him; a fierce storm rages around him.
</p>

<p>
v04: He summons heaven above and the earth
to judge his people:
v05: “Gather my faithful ones to me,
those who made my covenant by sacrifice.”
v06: The heavens declare his righteousness,
for God himself is judge. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v07: “Hear, my people, and I will speak.
Israel, I will testify against you.
I am God, your God.
v08: I do not rebuke you for your sacrifices;
your burnt offerings are always before me.
v09: I need no bull from your house
or male goats from your pens,
v10: for every forest animal is mine,
the cattle on a thousand hills.
v11: I know every bird of the mountains;
the creatures of the fields are mine.
v12: If I were hungry, I would not tell you,
for the world and all it holds belong to me.
v13: Do I eat the flesh of bulls
or drink the blood of goats?
</p>

<p>
v14: Offer thanksgiving as your sacrifice to God;
fulfill your vows to the Most High.
v15: Call on me on a day of distress.
I will rescue you, and you will honor me.”
</p>

<p>
v16: But to the wicked God says,
“What right have you to recite my statutes
and take my covenant on your lips,
v17: when you hate correction
and throw my words behind you?
v18: When you see a thief, you are pleased to join him;
you throw in your lot with adulterers.
v19: You let your mouth loose for evil;
your tongue weaves deceit.
v20: You sit and speak against your brother,
slandering your own mother’s son.
v21: You did these things, and I stayed silent.
You thought I was just like you.
But I will rebuke you and lay it all before your eyes.
</p>

<p>
v22: Understand this, you who forget God,
or I will tear you apart, with no one to rescue you.
v23: The one who offers thanksgiving honors me.
To the one who orders their way rightly
I will show God’s salvation.”
</p>

## Notes

v01,08–15: Three divine designations—El, Elohim, and YHWH—open the summons. The charge is not a shortage of sacrifices: the speech rejects treating offerings as food God needs.

v18,23: Joining the thief expresses approval or participation. “Rightly” in the final line makes explicit the moral sense of ordering one’s way; it is not a separate Hebrew word.

## Vocabulary

v14: **Thanksgiving — todah**: Thanks expressed to God, also the name of a thank offering. The line can emphasize both gratitude and sacrifice.
