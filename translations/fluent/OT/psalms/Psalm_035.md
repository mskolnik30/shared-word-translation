## Contend for me

<p>
v01: Of David.
Contend, LORD, with those who contend with me;
fight those who fight me.
v02: Take up shield and great shield;
rise to help me.
v03: Draw your spear and block the way of my pursuers.
Say to me, “I am your salvation.”
</p>

<p>
v04: Let those seeking my life be ashamed and disgraced;
let those plotting my harm retreat in humiliation.
v05: Let them be chaff before the wind,
with the LORD’s messenger driving them away.
v06: Let their path be dark and slippery,
with the LORD’s messenger pursuing them.
v07: Without cause they hid their net in a pit for me;
without cause they dug a trap for my life.
v08: Let ruin catch the enemy unaware;
let the net he hid catch him;
let him fall into it, into ruin.
</p>

<p>
v09: Then I will rejoice in the LORD,
delighting in his salvation.
v10: All my bones will say, “LORD, who is like you?
You rescue the afflicted from those too strong for them,
the afflicted and needy from the one who robs them.”
</p>

<p>
v11: Violent witnesses rise up;
they question me about things I know nothing of.
v12: They repay my good with evil,
leaving my life bereaved.
v13: Yet when they were ill, I wore sackcloth
and humbled myself with fasting;
my prayer returned to my bosom.
v14: I went about as though grieving for a friend or brother;
bowed in mourning, as for my mother.
</p>

<p>
v15: But when I stumbled, they rejoiced and gathered;
attackers I did not know gathered against me,
tearing at me without stopping.
v16: Like godless mockers at a feast,
they gnashed their teeth at me.
</p>

<p>
v17: Lord, how long will you look on?
Rescue my life from their ravages,
my only life from these young lions.
v18: I will thank you in the great assembly;
among a mighty people I will praise you.
</p>

<p>
v19: Do not let my treacherous enemies rejoice over me
or those who hate me without cause wink at one another.
v20: They do not speak peace;
they devise deceitful words against those living quietly in the land.
v21: They open their mouths wide against me,
saying, “Aha! Aha! Our own eyes saw it!”
</p>

<p>
v22: You have seen, LORD; do not stay silent.
Lord, do not remain far from me.
v23: Wake up! Rise to defend my cause,
my God and my Lord, to contend for me.
v24: Vindicate me according to your righteousness, LORD my God;
do not let them rejoice over me.
v25: Do not let them think, “Aha! Just what we wanted!”
Do not let them say, “We have swallowed him!”
</p>

<p>
v26: Let all who rejoice at my harm be ashamed and humiliated;
let those who exalt themselves over me wear shame and disgrace.
v27: Let those who desire my vindication shout for joy and be glad.
Let them always say, “Great is the LORD,
who delights in his servant’s well-being!”
v28: My tongue will speak of your righteousness
and praise you all day long.
</p>

## Notes

v03,13: The command after “spear” may mean block the way or name another weapon. “My prayer returned to my bosom” is also uncertain: it may picture repeated prayer, a bowed posture, or prayer returning to its speaker.

v15–16: The description of the gathering and its mockers is difficult. “Attackers” may instead refer to afflicted or disabled people; “mockers at a feast” renders a rare phrase associated with mocking and a cake. The uncertainty concerns the wording, not the hostility described.

## Vocabulary

v10: **All my bones**: The whole embodied person joins the praise; the poem repeatedly gives distress and rescue bodily form.

v24: **Vindicate**: To judge in someone’s favor. The petitioner asks God to settle a contested cause.
