## The trap broke and we escaped

<p>
v01: A song of ascents. Of David.
If the LORD had not been for us—
let Israel say it—
v02: if the LORD had not been for us
when people rose against us,
v03: they would have swallowed us alive
when their anger burned against us.
v04: The waters would have swept us away;
the torrent would have passed over our lives.
v05: The raging waters
would have passed over our lives.
</p>

<p>
v06: Blessed be the LORD,
who did not give us as prey to their teeth.
v07: We escaped like a bird from the hunters’ trap.
The trap broke, and we escaped.
v08: Our help is in the name of the LORD,
the maker of heaven and earth.
</p>

## Notes

v01–05: The repeated “if” clauses describe what would have happened without help. The flood and swallowing images are not turned into a literal event report.

## Vocabulary

v07: **Hunters’ trap**: A snare for birds; its breaking pictures the community’s escape.
