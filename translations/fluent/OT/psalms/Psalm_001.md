## Two ways

<p>
v01: Happy is the person
who does not walk by the counsel of the wicked,
stand in the path of sinners,
or sit among scoffers,
v02: but delights in the LORD’s instruction
and murmurs it over day and night.
v03: That person is like a tree planted beside streams of water,
bearing fruit in season,
its leaves never withering.
Whatever that person does flourishes.
v04: The wicked are not like this;
they are chaff the wind drives away.
v05: So the wicked will not stand in judgment,
nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous.
v06: For the LORD knows the way of the righteous,
but the way of the wicked comes to ruin.
</p>

## Notes

v02: Instruction translates torah, teaching that directs life. Meditating can include murmuring or reciting it.

## Vocabulary

v01: Walk, stand, sit
A sequence of involvement with the counsel, path, and company of the wicked.

v06: Knows
Here knowing a way includes attentive concern for those who follow it.
