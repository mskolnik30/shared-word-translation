## My refuge is the LORD

<p>
v01: For the director. Of David.
In the LORD I take refuge.
How can you say to me,
“Fly like a bird to your mountain!
v02: Look, the wicked bend their bows;
they set arrows on the string
to shoot from darkness at the upright in heart.
v03: When the foundations are torn down,
what can the righteous do?”
</p>

<p>
v04: The LORD is in his holy temple;
the LORD’s throne is in heaven.
His eyes see; his gaze tests humanity.
v05: The LORD tests the righteous,
but his whole being hates the wicked and those who love violence.
v06: He rains snares on the wicked;
fire, sulfur, and a scorching wind are their cup’s portion.
v07: For the LORD is righteous and loves righteous deeds;
the upright will see his face.
</p>

## Notes

v01–03: The extent of the advisers’ quotation is editorially supplied; the Hebrew does not mark its ending. Verse 1 has different written and read forms for fleeing.

v06: The pinned word is “snares,” although some translations read “coals.”

v07: The final clause can mean that the upright see God’s face or that his face beholds the upright.

## Vocabulary

v04: Eyelids
A vivid image of God examining human beings.
