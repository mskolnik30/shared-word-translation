## I Will Extol You, My God and King

<p>
v01: A song of praise. Of David.
I will exalt you, my God and King,
and bless your name forever and ever.
v02: Every day I will bless you
and praise your name forever and ever.
</p>

<p>
v03: The LORD is great and worthy of abundant praise;
his greatness is beyond searching out.
</p>

## Generational Praise

<p>
v04: One generation commends your works to another;
they tell of your mighty deeds.
v05: I will ponder the glorious splendor of your majesty
and the accounts of your wonders.
</p>

<p>
v06: They speak of the power of your fearsome acts;
I will recount your greatness.
v07: They pour out remembrance of your abundant goodness
and sing joyfully of your righteousness.
</p>

## The LORD’s Character

<p>
v08: The LORD is gracious and compassionate,
slow to anger and great in faithful love.
v09: The LORD is good to all;
his compassion reaches all he has made.
</p>

## Universal Praise

<p>
v10: All you have made will thank you, LORD;
your faithful ones will bless you.
v11: They will speak of your kingdom’s glory
and tell of your might,
</p>

<p>
v12: to make his mighty deeds known to humanity
and the glorious splendor of his kingdom.
</p>

<p>
v13: Your kingdom is a kingdom for all ages;
your rule lasts through every generation.
</p>

## God’s Care for Creation

<p>
v14: The LORD supports all who fall
and raises all who are bowed down.
v15: The eyes of all look to you in hope;
you give them their food at the right time.
</p>

<p>
v16: You open your hand
and satisfy the desire of every living thing.
</p>

## The LORD’s Righteousness and Nearness

<p>
v17: The LORD is righteous in all his ways
and faithful in all he does.
v18: The LORD is near to all who call on him,
to all who call on him in truth.
</p>

<p>
v19: He fulfills the desire of those who fear him;
he hears their cry and saves them.
v20: The LORD guards all who love him,
but he destroys all the wicked.
</p>

## Closing Praise

<p>
v21: My mouth will speak the LORD’s praise;
let all flesh bless his holy name forever and ever.
</p>

## Notes

v01–21: This is an alphabetic poem in Hebrew. The pinned text moves from the mem line in verse 13 to the samekh line in verse 14; it has no separate nun line. The translation does not supply one.

v06,12: Verse 6 follows the read form, “your greatness,” rather than the written plural. Verse 12 changes from addressing God to speaking of “his” deeds and kingdom; that shift is retained.

## Vocabulary

v13: **Kingdom**: God’s rule is pictured as a kingship lasting through every generation.
