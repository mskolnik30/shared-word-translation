## Peace within Jerusalem

<p>
v01: A song of ascents. Of David.
I rejoiced when they said to me,
“Let us go to the house of the LORD.”
v02: Our feet are standing
inside your gates, Jerusalem.
</p>

<p>
v03: Jerusalem, built as a city
joined firmly together,
v04: there the tribes go up, the tribes of YAH,
as a testimony ordained for Israel,
to thank the name of the LORD.
v05: For there stand thrones of judgment,
the thrones of the house of David.
</p>

<p>
v06: Ask for Jerusalem’s peace:
“May those who love you be secure.
v07: May peace be within your ramparts,
security within your palaces.”
</p>

<p>
v08: For the sake of my brothers and companions,
I will say, “Peace be within you.”
v09: For the sake of the house of the LORD our God,
I will seek your good.
</p>

## Notes

v03–07: The poem links Jerusalem’s joined construction, gathered tribes, royal judgment, and peace. Its Hebrew plays on the sounds of Jerusalem, peace, and security.

## Vocabulary

v04: **Testimony**: An established obligation or attestation; here connected with Israel’s going up to give thanks.
