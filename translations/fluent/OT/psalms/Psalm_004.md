## An evening appeal

<p>
v01: For the director. With stringed instruments. A psalm of David.
Answer when I call, God who vindicates me!
You gave me room when I was hemmed in.
Be gracious to me and hear my prayer.
v02: You men of standing, how long will you turn my honor to shame,
love emptiness, and chase a lie? Selah
v03: Know that the LORD has set apart the faithful for himself;
the LORD hears when I call to him.
v04: Tremble, and do not sin;
speak within your hearts on your beds,
and be still. Selah
v05: Offer righteous sacrifices
and put your trust in the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v06: Many ask, “Who will show us good?”
Let the light of your face shine on us, LORD.
v07: You have put joy in my heart,
more than when their grain and new wine abound.
v08: In peace I will lie down and sleep,
for you alone, LORD, let me live in safety.
</p>

## Notes

v01: “Of David” can also mean “for David”; the title does not by itself settle the psalm’s history of composition.

v04: The verb can mean trembling or being agitated, including anger.

v08: “Alone” can emphasize the LORD as the sole giver of safety or the speaker lying down alone in safety.

## Vocabulary

v02: Men of standing
Literally, sons of man; here an address to those dishonoring the speaker.
