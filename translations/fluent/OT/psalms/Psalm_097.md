## The earth rejoices at its king

<p>
v01: The LORD reigns! Let the earth rejoice;
let the many coastlands be glad.
</p>

<p>
v02: Cloud and thick darkness surround him;
righteousness and justice are his throne's foundation.
v03: Fire goes before him
and burns up his enemies on every side.
v04: His lightning lights the world;
the earth sees it and trembles.
v05: Mountains melt like wax before the LORD,
before the Lord of all the earth.
</p>

<p>
v06: The heavens proclaim his righteousness;
all the peoples see his glory.
v07: Let all who serve carved images be ashamed,
all who boast in worthless idols.
Bow before him, all you gods!
</p>

<p>
v08: Zion hears and is glad;
the daughters of Judah rejoice
because of your judgments, LORD.
v09: For you, LORD, are Most High over all the earth,
exalted far above all gods.
</p>

<p>
v10: You who love the LORD, hate evil.
He guards the lives of his faithful ones
and rescues them from the hand of the wicked.
v11: Light is sown for the righteous,
and joy for the upright in heart.
</p>

<p>
v12: Rejoice in the LORD, you righteous people,
and give thanks as you remember his holiness.
</p>

## Notes

v07,10–11: The command addresses “all you gods,” not a substituted term for angels. Verse 10 retains the command to those who love the LORD. Verse 11 follows “light is sown,” rather than a variant reading in which light dawns.

## Vocabulary

v08: **Daughters of Judah**: A poetic way of speaking of Judah's towns and their communities.
