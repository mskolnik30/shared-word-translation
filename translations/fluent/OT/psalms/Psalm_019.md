## The heavens tell

<p>
v01: For the director. A psalm of David.
The heavens tell of God’s glory;
the expanse announces what his hands have made.
v02: Day pours out speech to day;
night shares knowledge with night.
v03: There is no speech, there are no words;
their voice is not heard.
v04: Yet their measuring line goes out through all the earth,
their words to the ends of the world.
In the heavens he has pitched a tent for the sun.
v05: The sun comes out like a bridegroom from his chamber;
like a warrior, it rejoices to run its course.
v06: It rises at one end of the heavens
and circles to the other;
nothing is hidden from its heat.
</p>

## The LORD’s instruction

<p>
v07: The LORD’s instruction is complete, restoring life;
the LORD’s testimony is trustworthy, making the inexperienced wise.
v08: The LORD’s precepts are right, bringing joy to the heart;
the LORD’s command is clear, giving light to the eyes.
v09: Fear of the LORD is pure, enduring forever;
the LORD’s judgments are true, altogether righteous.
v10: They are more desirable than gold, than much fine gold;
sweeter than honey, than honey dripping from the comb.
v11: Your servant is also warned by them;
there is great reward in keeping them.
</p>

## Clear me of hidden faults

<p>
v12: Who can recognize every error?
Clear me of faults hidden from me.
v13: Keep your servant back from presumptuous sins too;
do not let them rule me.
Then I will be blameless,
cleared of great transgression.
v14: May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart
be welcome before you,
LORD, my rock and my redeemer.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The draft reads the heavens’ testimony as wordless. Another understanding is that there is no speech or language where their voice is unheard.

v04: The pinned word means line, often a measuring line. “Voice” or “sound” reflects a different reading familiar from another textual tradition.

v13: The plural can concern presumptuous sins or arrogant people. The draft follows the former.

## Vocabulary

v01: Expanse
The spread-out sky in the poem’s ancient imagery.

v07: Instruction
Torah: teaching that guides life.

v14: Redeemer
One who acts to reclaim or deliver another.
