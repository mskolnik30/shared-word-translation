## Why have you abandoned me?

<p>
v01: For the director. To “The Doe of the Dawn.” A psalm of David.
My God, my God, why have you abandoned me?
Why are you so far from saving me, from the words of my groaning?
v02: My God, I call by day, but you do not answer;
by night too, and I find no rest.
v03: Yet you are holy,
enthroned on Israel’s praises.
v04: Our ancestors trusted in you;
they trusted, and you rescued them.
v05: They cried to you and escaped;
they trusted in you and were not put to shame.
</p>

<p>
v06: But I am a worm, not a man,
scorned by people and despised by the crowd.
v07: Everyone who sees me mocks me;
they sneer and shake their heads:
v08: “Commit yourself to the LORD! Let the LORD rescue him;
let him save this man, since he delights in him!”
</p>

<p>
v09: But you brought me out of the womb;
you gave me security at my mother’s breasts.
v10: I was cast upon you from the womb;
from my mother’s belly you have been my God.
v11: Do not stay far from me.
Trouble is near, and there is no one to help.
</p>

## Surrounded and poured out

<p>
v12: Many bulls surround me;
strong bulls of Bashan close me in.
v13: They open their mouths against me
like a lion tearing prey and roaring.
v14: I am poured out like water;
all my bones are pulled apart.
My heart has become wax,
melting inside me.
v15: My strength is dry as a potsherd;
my tongue sticks to my jaws.
You lay me in the dust of death.
v16: Dogs surround me;
a band of evildoers closes in.
Like a lion—my hands and feet!
v17: I can count all my bones;
they look on and stare at me.
v18: They divide my clothes among themselves
and cast lots for my garment.
</p>

## Do not stay far away

<p>
v19: But you, LORD, do not stay far away!
My strength, hurry to help me!
v20: Rescue my life from the sword,
my only life from the power of the dog.
v21: Save me from the lion’s mouth,
from the horns of wild oxen—
you have answered me!
</p>

## Praise in the assembly

<p>
v22: I will tell my brothers and sisters of your name;
in the assembly I will praise you.
v23: You who fear the LORD, praise him!
All Jacob’s offspring, honor him!
All Israel’s offspring, stand in awe of him!
v24: For he has not despised or recoiled from the afflicted one’s suffering.
He has not hidden his face from him;
when he cried to him, he heard.
v25: From you comes my praise in the great assembly;
I will fulfill my vows before those who fear him.
v26: The humble will eat and be satisfied;
those who seek the LORD will praise him.
May your hearts live forever!
</p>

<p>
v27: All the ends of the earth will remember and return to the LORD;
all the families of the nations will bow before you.
v28: For kingship belongs to the LORD;
he rules over the nations.
v29: All the prosperous of the earth will eat and bow down;
all who go down to the dust will kneel before him,
even those who cannot keep themselves alive.
v30: Offspring will serve him;
the coming generation will be told of the Lord.
v31: They will come and tell of his righteousness
to a people yet to be born:
“He has done it!”
</p>

## Notes

v01: “The Doe of the Dawn” appears to be a performance direction or tune title; its exact function is uncertain.

v03: The phrase can picture God enthroned on Israel’s praises or dwelling among them.

v08: The mockers’ taunt shifts from addressing the sufferer to speaking about him. The change is retained.

v16: The pinned Hebrew reads “like a lion—my hands and feet,” with no expressed verb. Readings rendered “dug” or “pierced” belong to alternative textual interpretations or witnesses. The draft preserves the pinned wording and its difficulty instead of silently harmonizing a later application.

v21: “You have answered me” is a completed-action form in the pinned text. Its abrupt appearance marks the turn toward praise.

v29: The final clause is difficult and can mean that a person has not kept, or cannot keep, life alive. The draft understands it as mortal inability.

## Vocabulary

v12: Bashan
A region associated with rich pasture and powerful cattle.

v15: Potsherd
A dry fragment of broken pottery.

v20: My only life
The speaker’s one irreplaceable life.
