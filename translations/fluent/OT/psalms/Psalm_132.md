## A dwelling, a covenant, and a lamp

<p>
v01: A song of ascents.
LORD, remember David
and all the hardship he endured,
v02: how he swore to the LORD
and vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob:
</p>

<p>
v03: “I will not enter my house
or climb into my bed;
v04: I will not give sleep to my eyes
or slumber to my eyelids,
v05: until I find a place for the LORD,
a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob.”
</p>

<p>
v06: We heard of it in Ephrathah;
we found it in the fields of Jaar.
v07: Let us enter his dwelling;
let us bow down at his footstool.
</p>

<p>
v08: Rise, LORD, to your resting place,
you and the ark of your strength.
v09: Let your priests be clothed with righteousness;
let your faithful ones shout for joy.
v10: For the sake of David your servant,
do not turn away the face of your anointed one.
</p>

<p>
v11: The LORD swore a true oath to David
and will not turn back from it:
“I will set one of your own offspring on your throne.
v12: If your sons keep my covenant
and the testimonies I teach them,
their sons too will sit on your throne forever.”
</p>

<p>
v13: For the LORD has chosen Zion;
he has desired it as his dwelling:
</p>

<p>
v14: “This is my resting place forever;
here I will dwell, for I have desired it.
v15: I will richly bless its provisions;
I will satisfy its needy people with bread.
v16: I will clothe its priests with salvation,
and its faithful ones will shout aloud for joy.
</p>

<p>
v17: There I will make a horn sprout for David;
I have prepared a lamp for my anointed one.
v18: I will clothe his enemies with shame,
but his crown will blossom upon him.”
</p>

## Notes

v06: “It” is not explicitly named in this verse; the ark in verse 8 supplies the likely context. Ephrathah and the fields of Jaar remain named without expanding an uncertain itinerary.

v11–18: The promise about descendants includes an explicit covenant condition in verse 12. Horn, lamp, clothing, and blossoming crown carry the royal imagery.

## Vocabulary

v08: **Ark**: The sacred chest associated with God’s presence and strength; not a boat in this context.

v17: **Horn and lamp**: Images of royal strength and continuing dynasty.
