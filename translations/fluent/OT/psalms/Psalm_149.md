## Sing to the LORD a New Song

<p>
v01: Praise **YAH**!
Sing a new song to the LORD,
his praise in the assembly of his faithful ones.
</p>

<p>
v02: Let Israel rejoice in its maker;
let Zion’s children celebrate their king.
v03: Let them praise his name with dancing
and make music for him with tambourine and lyre.
</p>

## The LORD Delights in His People

<p>
v04: For the LORD delights in his people;
he adorns the humble with salvation.
v05: Let his faithful ones exult in honor
and shout for joy on their beds.
</p>

## Praise and Judgment

<p>
v06: Let high praises of God be in their throats
and a double-edged sword in their hands,
v07: to carry out vengeance on the nations
and punishment on the peoples,
</p>

<p>
v08: to bind their kings with chains
and their nobles with iron shackles,
v09: to carry out on them the written judgment.
This is honor for all his faithful ones.
Praise **YAH**!
</p>

## Notes

v06–09: The poem joins praise with armed vengeance and the binding of rulers. Those actions are retained; the text does not explain the written judgment more precisely here.

## Vocabulary

v01,05,09: **Faithful ones**: People marked by loyalty within their relationship with God.
