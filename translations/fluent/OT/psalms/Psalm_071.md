## From youth to gray hair

<p>
v01: In you, LORD, I take refuge;
never let me be put to shame.
v02: In your righteousness rescue me and bring me to safety.
Bend your ear toward me and save me.
v03: Be my rock, a dwelling I can always enter.
You have commanded my rescue,
for you are my crag and my fortress.
v04: My God, free me from the wicked man’s hand,
from the grip of the unjust and ruthless.
</p>

<p>
v05: You are my hope, Lord, LORD,
my trust since youth.
v06: I have leaned on you since birth;
you brought me out of my mother’s womb.
My praise is always of you.
v07: I have become a sign to many,
but you are my strong refuge.
v08: Let my mouth be filled with your praise,
with your splendor all day long.
</p>

<p>
v09: Do not cast me aside in old age;
do not forsake me when my strength is spent.
v10: My enemies speak against me;
those watching for my life take counsel together,
v11: saying, “God has forsaken him.
Pursue him and seize him; no one will rescue him.”
v12: God, do not stay far from me;
my God, hurry to help me!
v13: Let my accusers be ashamed and consumed;
let those seeking my harm wrap themselves in reproach and disgrace.
</p>

<p>
v14: But I will keep hoping
and add still more to all your praise.
v15: My mouth will recount your righteousness,
your salvation all day long,
though I cannot count its deeds.
v16: I will come with the mighty deeds of the Lord, the LORD;
I will tell of your righteousness, yours alone.
</p>

<p>
v17: God, you have taught me since youth;
until now I have told of your wonders.
v18: Even when I am old and gray, God, do not forsake me,
until I tell the next generation of your arm,
and all who are to come of your might.
v19: Your righteousness, God, reaches the heights.
You have done great things; God, who is like you?
v20: You have made me see many bitter troubles;
you will revive me again,
bring me up again from the earth’s depths.
v21: Increase my honor;
turn and comfort me.
</p>

<p>
v22: I too will thank you with the harp for your faithfulness, my God;
I will make music to you with the lyre, Holy One of Israel.
v23: My lips will shout for joy when I make music to you,
and so will my life, which you redeemed.
v24: My tongue will ponder your righteousness all day,
for those seeking my harm are ashamed and disgraced.
</p>

## Notes

v03,06: Verse 3 retains the Hebrew image of a dwelling rock. The verb in verse 6 can suggest being brought out from the womb or sustained from birth.

v20: Both reading notes give singular “me,” while the written forms have plural “us.” The draft follows the reading tradition and preserves the written forms in the source record.

## Vocabulary

v07: **Portent**: An unsettling sign or object of wonder; it need not imply that observers admire the speaker.

v15: **Count**: The abundance of God's saving acts exceeds the speaker's reckoning.
