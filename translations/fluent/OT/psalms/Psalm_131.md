## Like a weaned child

<p>
v01: A song of ascents. Of David.
LORD, my heart is not proud and my eyes are not haughty.
I do not occupy myself with things too great
or with wonders beyond me.
</p>

<p>
v02: Surely I have calmed and quieted my soul,
like a weaned child against its mother;
like a weaned child, my soul rests against me.
</p>

<p>
v03: Israel, hope in the LORD
from now and forever.
</p>

## Notes

v02: The child is weaned, not nursing. The repeated comparison portrays a quieted self resting with its mother, while the final line turns the image inward.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Haughty eyes**: An image of pride or looking down on others.
