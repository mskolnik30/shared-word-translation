## A song of rescue

<p>
v01: For the director. Of the LORD’s servant David, who spoke the words of this song to the LORD on the day the LORD rescued him from all his enemies and from Saul. He said:
I love you, LORD, my strength.
v02: The LORD is my crag, my fortress, my rescuer;
my God is my rock, where I take refuge,
my shield, the horn of my salvation, my stronghold.
v03: I call on the LORD, who is worthy of praise,
and I am saved from my enemies.
v04: Death’s cords wound around me;
torrents of destruction terrified me.
v05: Sheol’s cords surrounded me;
death’s traps lay ahead of me.
v06: In my distress I called on the LORD;
I cried to my God for help.
From his temple he heard my voice;
my cry came before him, into his ears.
</p>

## The LORD descends

<p>
v07: Then the earth heaved and shook;
the mountains’ foundations trembled.
They heaved because he was angry.
v08: Smoke rose from his nostrils;
consuming fire came from his mouth,
and blazing coals flared from him.
v09: He bent the heavens down and descended,
with thick darkness beneath his feet.
v10: He rode a cherub and flew;
he soared on the wings of the wind.
v11: He made darkness his hiding place,
his shelter all around him—
dark waters and dense clouds.
v12: Out of the brightness before him, his clouds swept past,
with hail and burning coals.
v13: The LORD thundered in the heavens;
the Most High gave his voice—
hail and burning coals.
v14: He sent his arrows and scattered them;
he hurled lightning and threw them into confusion.
v15: The channels of water appeared;
the foundations of the world lay bare
at your rebuke, LORD,
at the blast of breath from your nostrils.
</p>

<p>
v16: He reached down from on high and took hold of me;
he drew me out of deep waters.
v17: He rescued me from my powerful enemy,
from those who hated me, for they were too strong for me.
v18: They confronted me on the day of my disaster,
but the LORD was my support.
v19: He brought me out into a broad place;
he rescued me because he delighted in me.
</p>

## He repaid my righteousness

<p>
v20: The LORD dealt with me according to my righteousness;
he repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands.
v21: For I have kept the LORD’s ways;
I have not wickedly turned away from my God.
v22: All his judgments were before me;
I did not put his statutes away from me.
v23: I was blameless toward him;
I guarded myself against my own wrongdoing.
v24: So the LORD repaid me according to my righteousness,
according to the cleanness of my hands in his sight.
</p>

<p>
v25: With the faithful you show yourself faithful;
with the blameless you show yourself blameless.
v26: With the pure you show yourself pure,
but with the crooked you show yourself shrewd.
v27: You save an afflicted people,
but you bring haughty eyes down.
v28: You are the one who lights my lamp;
the LORD my God brightens my darkness.
v29: With you I can charge a troop;
with my God I can leap a wall.
v30: God’s way is blameless;
the LORD’s word has been refined.
He is a shield to everyone who takes refuge in him.
</p>

## Strength for battle

<p>
v31: Who is God besides the LORD?
Who is a rock except our God?
v32: It is God who belts me with strength
and makes my way blameless.
v33: He makes my feet like a deer’s
and sets me on my heights.
v34: He trains my hands for battle;
my arms can bend a bronze bow.
v35: You give me the shield of your salvation;
your right hand supports me,
and your gentleness makes me great.
v36: You give my steps room beneath me,
and my ankles do not give way.
</p>

<p>
v37: I pursue my enemies and overtake them;
I do not turn back until they are finished.
v38: I crush them so they cannot rise;
they fall beneath my feet.
v39: You belt me with strength for battle;
you bring those who rise against me down beneath me.
v40: You make my enemies turn their backs to me;
I destroy those who hate me.
v41: They cry for help, but no one saves them;
they cry to the LORD, but he does not answer.
v42: I grind them like dust before the wind;
I pour them out like mud in the streets.
v43: You rescue me from the people’s disputes;
you make me head of nations.
A people I did not know serves me.
v44: As soon as they hear of me, they obey;
foreigners come cringing before me.
v45: Foreigners lose heart
and come trembling out of their strongholds.
</p>

## The LORD lives

<p>
v46: The LORD lives! Blessed be my rock!
Let the God who saves me be exalted!
v47: He is the God who gives me vengeance
and brings peoples under me.
v48: He rescues me from my enemies.
Yes, you lift me above those who rise against me;
you rescue me from violent men.
v49: That is why I will thank you among the nations, LORD,
and sing praise to your name.
v50: He gives his king great victories
and shows faithful love to his anointed—
to David and his offspring forever.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The complete title connects the song with rescue from enemies and Saul. It precedes the opening confession in the Hebrew. Compare the related song in 2 Samuel 22; this draft follows the pinned Psalm rather than harmonizing its differences.

v12–13: Hail and burning coals are repeated in the pinned text and retained.

v26: The response to the crooked uses a verb of twisting or dealing shrewdly, not the same virtue named in the preceding lines.

v35: The word translated “gentleness” can concern humility or condescension.

v37–42: The king’s crushing and destroying of enemies is explicit military violence, not recast as a struggle against abstract faults.

v50: The draft follows the read form, giving great victories, rather than treating the unpointed written form as a tower.

## Vocabulary

v02: Horn of salvation
A horn symbolizes powerful protection or victory.

v10: Cherub
A heavenly creature associated with the divine presence.
