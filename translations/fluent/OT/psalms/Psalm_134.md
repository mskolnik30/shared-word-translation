## A blessing in the night

<p>
v01: A song of ascents.
Come, bless the LORD, all you servants of the LORD
who stand in the house of the LORD through the nights.
</p>

<p>
v02: Lift your hands toward the sanctuary
and bless the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v03: May the LORD, the maker of heaven and earth,
bless you from Zion.
</p>

## Notes

v01–03: The call addresses temple servants at night; the final blessing addresses a singular “you.” “Toward the sanctuary” in verse 2 can also be understood as “in holiness.”

## Vocabulary

v02: **Sanctuary**: The holy place toward which worshipers lift their hands in this reading.
