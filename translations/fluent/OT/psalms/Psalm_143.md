## Teach me the way to walk

<p>
v01: A psalm of David.
LORD, hear my prayer; listen to my pleas.
In your faithfulness answer me, in your righteousness.
v02: Do not enter into judgment with your servant,
for no living person is righteous before you.
</p>

<p>
v03: The enemy pursues my life;
he crushes me to the ground
and makes me dwell in darkness like those long dead.
v04: My spirit grows faint within me;
my heart is appalled inside me.
</p>

<p>
v05: I remember the days of old;
I meditate on all you have done
and ponder the work of your hands.
v06: I spread my hands toward you;
my whole being thirsts for you like parched land. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v07: Answer me quickly, LORD; my spirit is failing.
Do not hide your face from me,
or I will become like those who go down to the Pit.
v08: Let me hear your faithful love in the morning, for I trust in you.
Make known the way I should walk,
for to you I lift my whole being.
</p>

<p>
v09: Rescue me from my enemies, LORD;
to you I flee for shelter.
v10: Teach me to do your will, for you are my God.
Let your good spirit lead me on level ground.
</p>

<p>
v11: For your name’s sake, LORD, keep me alive;
in your righteousness bring my life out of trouble.
v12: In your faithful love, put an end to my enemies;
destroy all who afflict my life,
for I am your servant.
</p>

## Notes

v09–10: Verse 9’s final expression is unusual, literally involving covering “to you”; the draft understands seeking shelter. The good spirit leading on level ground retains a concrete path image.

## Vocabulary

v07: **Pit**: A poetic name or image for the realm of death.
