## Remember your ruined sanctuary

<p>
v01: A maskil of Asaph.
God, why have you rejected us forever?
Why does your anger smoke against the sheep of your pasture?
v02: Remember the community you acquired long ago,
the tribe you redeemed as your own possession—
Mount Zion, where you made your home.
v03: Walk among these lasting ruins;
see all the damage the enemy has done in the sanctuary.
</p>

<p>
v04: Your enemies roared inside your meeting place;
they set up their own signs as signs of victory.
v05: They looked like people swinging axes
through a thicket of trees.
v06: And now they smash all its carved woodwork
with hatchets and hammers.
v07: They set your sanctuary on fire;
they defiled the dwelling of your name, leveling it to the ground.
v08: They said in their hearts, “We will crush them all.”
They burned every meeting place of God in the land.
</p>

<p>
v09: We see none of our signs.
There is no prophet left,
and no one among us knows how long this will last.
v10: God, how long will the foe mock you?
Will the enemy scorn your name forever?
v11: Why do you hold back your hand, your right hand?
Pull it from the fold of your robe and finish them!
</p>

<p>
v12: Yet God has been my king from long ago,
working acts of rescue throughout the earth.
v13: You broke the sea apart by your strength;
you shattered the heads of the sea monsters on the waters.
v14: You crushed Leviathan's heads
and gave him as food to creatures of the desert.
v15: You split open springs and streams;
you dried up rivers that never ran dry.
v16: The day is yours, and so is the night;
you established the light and the sun.
v17: You set all the earth's boundaries;
you formed summer and winter.
</p>

<p>
v18: Remember this, LORD: the enemy has mocked you;
a foolish people has treated your name with contempt.
v19: Do not hand the life of your dove over to wild beasts;
do not forget the lives of your afflicted people forever.
v20: Look to your covenant,
for the land's dark places are full of dens of violence.
v21: Do not let the crushed turn back in shame;
let the poor and needy praise your name.
</p>

<p>
v22: Rise, God, and plead your own cause!
Remember how fools mock you all day long.
v23: Do not forget the voices of your enemies,
the uproar of those who rise against you, always growing louder.
</p>

## Notes

v04–08: Enemy signs replace the community's signs. “Meeting places” does not by itself identify later synagogues. The damaged woodwork and burning remain concrete.

v13–14: Sea monsters and many-headed Leviathan draw on ancient images of threatening waters. The recipients of Leviathan as food are difficult to identify; the Hebrew can describe desert creatures or desert dwellers as a people.

v19: God's community is pictured as a vulnerable dove. The repeated Hebrew word hayyah connects wild creatures with the life of the afflicted.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Maskil**: A psalm designation, perhaps related to insight or instruction; its precise musical function is uncertain.

v20: **Covenant**: A binding relationship and commitment invoked as the ground for God's response.
