## The guardian who does not sleep

<p>
v01: A song for ascents.
I lift my eyes toward the mountains.
Where will my help come from?
v02: My help comes from the LORD,
the maker of heaven and earth.
</p>

<p>
v03: May he not let your foot slip;
may your guardian not doze.
v04: Look: Israel’s guardian
neither dozes nor sleeps.
</p>

<p>
v05: The LORD is your guardian;
the LORD is your shade at your right hand.
v06: The sun will not strike you by day,
nor the moon by night.
</p>

<p>
v07: The LORD will guard you from all harm;
he will guard your life.
v08: The LORD will guard your going out and coming in,
from now and forever.
</p>

## Notes

v01–04: The title says “for ascents,” a slight variation on the collection’s usual title. The mountain question is answered by naming the maker of heaven and earth. Verse 3 is phrased as a wish; verse 4 gives an assurance.

## Vocabulary

v03–08: **Guardian**: The repeated Hebrew root means keep, watch, or guard. Its recurrence holds the psalm together.
