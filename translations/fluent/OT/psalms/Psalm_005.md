## A morning appeal

<p>
v01: For the director. For the Nehiloth. A psalm of David.
Listen to my words, LORD;
attend to my murmuring.
v02: Hear my cry for help, my King and my God,
for it is to you that I pray.
v03: LORD, in the morning you hear my voice;
in the morning I lay my prayer before you and watch.
</p>

<p>
v04: You are no God who delights in wickedness;
evil cannot lodge with you.
v05: The boastful cannot stand before your eyes;
you hate everyone who does wrong.
v06: You destroy those who tell lies;
the LORD abhors the bloodthirsty and deceitful.
</p>

<p>
v07: But through your abundant faithful love I enter your house;
in reverence I bow toward your holy temple.
v08: LORD, lead me in your righteousness because of my enemies;
make your way straight before me.
</p>

<p>
v09: Nothing in their mouths is reliable;
within them is destruction.
Their throats are open graves;
their tongues speak smoothly.
v10: Declare them guilty, God!
Let their own schemes bring them down.
Drive them out for their many rebellions,
for they have rebelled against you.
</p>

<p>
v11: Let all who take refuge in you rejoice;
let them sing for joy forever.
Spread your shelter over them,
so those who love your name may exult in you.
v12: For you bless the righteous, LORD;
you surround them with favor like a shield.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Nehiloth is an uncertain musical term; it is often associated with wind instruments.

v03: The speaker arranges something before God: the prayer is understood here, though arranging an offering is another possibility.

v05–06: The hatred of evildoers is directed at people in the poem, not only at abstract wrongdoing.

## Vocabulary

v07: Faithful love
Steadfast care within a relationship.
