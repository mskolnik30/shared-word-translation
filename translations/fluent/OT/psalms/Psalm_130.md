## Waiting from the depths

<p>
v01: A song of ascents.
From the depths I call to you, LORD.
v02: Lord, hear my voice;
let your ears attend to my pleas for mercy.
</p>

<p>
v03: If you kept account of wrongs, YAH,
Lord, who could stand?
v04: But forgiveness is with you,
so that you may be feared.
</p>

<p>
v05: I wait for the LORD; my whole being waits,
and I hope in his word.
v06: My whole being waits for the Lord
more than watchmen for morning,
more than watchmen for morning.
</p>

<p>
v07: Israel, hope in the LORD,
for with the LORD is faithful love,
and with him is abundant redemption.
v08: He himself will redeem Israel
from all its wrongs.
</p>

## Notes

v03–08: The poem moves from individual guilt and waiting to Israel’s hope for redemption. Forgiveness leads to fear of God in verse 4; the connection is retained.

## Vocabulary

v07–08: **Redemption**: Release or rescue; here explicitly from Israel’s wrongs.
