## The living God and powerless idols

<p>
v01: Praise YAH!
Praise the name of the LORD;
praise him, servants of the LORD,
v02: you who stand in the house of the LORD,
in the courts of the house of our God.
v03: Praise YAH, for the LORD is good;
make music for his name, for it is pleasant.
v04: For YAH has chosen Jacob for himself,
Israel as his treasured possession.
</p>

<p>
v05: I know that the LORD is great,
that our Lord is above all gods.
v06: Whatever the LORD pleases, he does,
in heaven and on earth,
in the seas and all the depths.
v07: He raises clouds from the ends of the earth;
he makes lightning for the rain
and brings wind out of his storehouses.
</p>

<p>
v08: He struck down Egypt’s firstborn,
both human and animal.
v09: He sent signs and wonders into your midst, Egypt,
against Pharaoh and all his servants.
v10: He struck many nations
and killed mighty kings:
v11: Sihon king of the Amorites,
Og king of Bashan,
and all the kingdoms of Canaan.
v12: He gave their land as an inheritance,
an inheritance to Israel his people.
</p>

<p>
v13: LORD, your name lasts forever;
LORD, your remembrance lasts through every generation.
v14: For the LORD will vindicate his people
and relent toward his servants.
</p>

<p>
v15: The nations’ idols are silver and gold,
the work of human hands.
v16: They have mouths but cannot speak;
they have eyes but cannot see.
v17: They have ears but cannot listen;
there is not even breath in their mouths.
v18: Those who make them become like them,
as does everyone who trusts in them.
</p>

<p>
v19: House of Israel, bless the LORD!
House of Aaron, bless the LORD!
v20: House of Levi, bless the LORD!
You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD!
v21: Blessed from Zion be the LORD,
who dwells in Jerusalem.
Praise YAH!
</p>

## Notes

v15–18: The idol description resembles Psalm 115 but has its own wording, especially the absence of breath in verse 17. Those differences remain.

## Vocabulary

v04: **Treasured possession**: Something specially chosen and valued; here Israel in relation to God.
