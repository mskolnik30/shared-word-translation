## He is holy

<p>
v01: The LORD reigns! Let the peoples tremble.
He sits enthroned above the cherubim; let the earth shake.
v02: The LORD is great in Zion;
he is exalted above all the peoples.
v03: Let them thank your great and awesome name.
He is holy.
</p>

<p>
v04: The mighty king loves justice.
You have established fairness;
you have brought justice and righteousness to Jacob.
v05: Exalt the LORD our God;
bow down at his footstool.
He is holy.
</p>

<p>
v06: Moses and Aaron were among his priests,
Samuel among those who called on his name.
They called to the LORD, and he answered them.
v07: He spoke to them from the pillar of cloud;
they kept his testimonies and the statute he gave them.
v08: LORD our God, you answered them.
You were a forgiving God to them,
yet you took vengeance for their deeds.
</p>

<p>
v09: Exalt the LORD our God;
bow down at his holy mountain,
for the LORD our God is holy.
</p>

## Notes

v04,06,08: Verse 4 compresses royal strength and love of justice. Moses and Aaron are both included among priests. Forgiveness and vengeance for deeds stand together in verse 8; neither is omitted.

## Vocabulary

v05: **Footstool**: A royal image for the place beneath God's throne, associated with his earthly sanctuary.
