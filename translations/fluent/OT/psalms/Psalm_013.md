## How long will you forget me?

<p>
v01: For the director. A psalm of David.
How long, LORD? Will you forget me forever?
How long will you hide your face from me?
v02: How long must I wrestle with plans within me,
with sorrow in my heart all day?
How long will my enemy rise over me?
</p>

<p>
v03: Look at me and answer, LORD my God!
Give light to my eyes, lest I sleep in death,
v04: lest my enemy say, “I have overcome him,”
and my foes rejoice when I fall.
</p>

<p>
v05: But I have trusted in your faithful love;
my heart will rejoice in your rescue.
v06: I will sing to the LORD,
for he has dealt generously with me.
</p>

## Notes

v01,05–06: The Hebrew title appears before the poem. The final two English verses form one verse in Hebrew.

## Vocabulary

v03: Light to my eyes
An appeal for renewed life before the sleep of death.
