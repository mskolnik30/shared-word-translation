## Words turned into arrows

<p>
v01: For the music leader. A psalm of David.
God, hear my voice as I complain;
protect my life from the enemy’s terror.
v02: Hide me from the secret counsel of evildoers,
from the tumult of those who do harm.
</p>

<p>
v03: They sharpen their tongues like swords
and aim bitter words like arrows,
v04: to shoot at the blameless from hiding.
They shoot him suddenly, without fear.
</p>

<p>
v05: They encourage each other in an evil plan;
they talk of hiding snares, saying,
“Who will see them?”
v06: They search out ways to do wrong:
“We have perfected a thoroughly searched-out plan!”
A person’s inward thought and heart are deep.
</p>

<p>
v07: But God shoots them with an arrow;
suddenly they are wounded.
v08: Their own tongues bring them down;
everyone who sees them shakes their head.
</p>

<p>
v09: Then all people will fear;
they will tell what God has done
and ponder his work.
v10: Let the righteous rejoice in the LORD and take refuge in him;
let all the upright in heart glory.
</p>

## Notes

v05–08: The hidden-snares question leaves its pronoun open. Verse 6 piles up words for searching and has difficult syntax; the draft hears the plotters boasting. Verse 8’s mixed number is rendered as their own tongues bringing them down. Those watching may shake their heads or withdraw.

## Vocabulary

v03,07: **Arrows and words**: The attackers aim speech as arrows; God’s arrow then turns their sudden attack back on them.
