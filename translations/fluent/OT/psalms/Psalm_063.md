## Thirst in the wilderness

<p>
v01: A psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judah.
God, you are my God; I seek you eagerly.
My whole being thirsts for you; my flesh longs for you
in a dry, weary land with no water.
v02: So I have gazed on you in the sanctuary,
seeing your strength and glory.
</p>

<p>
v03: Your faithful love is better than life;
my lips will praise you.
v04: So I will bless you as long as I live;
in your name I will lift my hands.
</p>

<p>
v05: I will be satisfied as with fat and rich food;
my mouth will praise you with joyful lips.
v06: When I remember you on my bed,
I ponder you through the night watches.
</p>

<p>
v07: You have been my help;
in the shadow of your wings I sing aloud.
v08: My whole being clings to you;
your right hand holds me fast.
</p>

<p>
v09: But those who seek to destroy my life
will go into the depths of the earth.
v10: They will be handed over to the sword
and become a portion for jackals.
</p>

<p>
v11: But the king will rejoice in God;
everyone who swears by him will glory,
for the mouths that speak lies will be stopped.
</p>

## Notes

v01,05–06,11: Seeking can suggest early or eager pursuit. Fat and rich food picture satisfaction in an ancient feast. Night watches are divisions of the night. In verse 11, “him” most naturally points to God but can be heard in relation to the king.

## Vocabulary

v08: **Cling — davaq**: To hold closely or stick fast. The next line answers the speaker’s clinging with God’s supporting hand.
