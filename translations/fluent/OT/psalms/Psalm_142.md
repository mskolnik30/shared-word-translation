## A prayer from the cave

<p>
v01: A maskil of David, when he was in the cave. A prayer.
With my voice I cry to the LORD;
with my voice I plead with the LORD.
v02: I pour out my complaint before him;
I tell my trouble before him.
</p>

<p>
v03: When my spirit grows faint within me,
you yourself know my path.
On the road I walk, they have hidden a trap for me.
v04: Look to my right and see:
no one acknowledges me.
No refuge remains for me; no one cares for my life.
</p>

<p>
v05: I cry to you, LORD;
I say, “You are my refuge,
my portion in the land of the living.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Attend to my cry, for I am brought very low.
Rescue me from my pursuers,
for they are stronger than I am.
v07: Bring my life out of confinement so I may thank your name.
The righteous will gather around me,
for you will deal generously with me.
</p>

## Notes

v01–07: The separate title names a cave but does not identify which episode of David’s life. It is included in verse 1; later Hebrew labels are one higher. “Confinement” in verse 7 need not mean a literal jail.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Maskil**: A traditional psalm designation, possibly connected with skill or instruction; its precise musical or literary force is uncertain.
