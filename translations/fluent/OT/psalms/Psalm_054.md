## Save me by your name

<p>
v01: For the music leader. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David, when the Ziphites came and said to Saul, “Is not David hiding among us?”
God, save me by your name;
vindicate me by your power.
v02: God, hear my prayer;
listen to the words of my mouth.
</p>

<p>
v03: Strangers have risen against me;
ruthless men seek my life.
They do not keep God before them. Selah.
</p>

<p>
v04: Look, God is my helper;
the Lord is among those who sustain my life.
v05: He will turn evil back on those watching for my fall.
In your faithfulness, destroy them.
</p>

<p>
v06: I will freely offer you a sacrifice;
I will thank your name, LORD, for it is good.
v07: He has rescued me from every distress;
my eyes have looked upon my enemies.
</p>

## Notes

v01,03–05: The title names the Ziphites’ report to Saul. Verse 3 follows “strangers,” not an alternative reading “arrogant.” “Among those who sustain my life” retains the unusual construction in verse 4. Verse 5 follows the traditional read form, “he will turn evil back.”

## Vocabulary

v01: **Name**: God’s identity and power as invoked in prayer, not merely a spoken label.
