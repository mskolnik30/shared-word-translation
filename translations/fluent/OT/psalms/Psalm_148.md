## Praise the LORD from the Heavens

<p>
v01: Praise **YAH**!
Praise the LORD from the heavens;
praise him in the heights.
v02: Praise him, all his messengers;
praise him, all his armies.
v03: Praise him, sun and moon;
praise him, all you shining stars.
v04: Praise him, highest heavens,
and you waters above the heavens.
v05: Let them praise the name of the LORD,
for he commanded, and they were created.
v06: He established them forever and ever;
he gave a decree that will not pass away.
</p>

## Praise the LORD from the Earth

<p>
v07: Praise the LORD from the earth,
you sea monsters and all the depths,
v08: fire and hail, snow and mist,
storm wind carrying out his word,
v09: mountains and all hills,
fruit trees and all cedars,
v10: wild animals and all livestock,
creeping creatures and winged birds,
v11: earth’s kings and all peoples,
rulers and all the earth’s judges,
v12: young men and young women together,
old people and children.
</p>

## The Name of the LORD Alone Is Exalted

<p>
v13: Let them praise the name of the LORD,
for his name alone is exalted;
his majesty is above earth and heaven.
v14: He has raised a horn for his people,
praise for all his faithful ones,
for the people of Israel, the people close to him.
Praise **YAH**!
</p>

## Notes

v06: The last clause may describe a decree that will not pass away, or a boundary the created things will not cross.

## Vocabulary

v14: **Horn**: An image of strength and raised standing, drawn from an animal’s horn.
