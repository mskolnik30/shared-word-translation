## An appeal for judgment

<p>
v01: A Shiggaion of David, which he sang to the LORD concerning Cush, a Benjaminite.
LORD my God, I take refuge in you.
Save me from all who pursue me; deliver me,
v02: lest an enemy tear me apart like a lion,
ripping me to pieces with no one to rescue me.
</p>

<p>
v03: LORD my God, if I have done this,
if injustice is on my hands,
v04: if I repaid a peaceful ally with evil—
I even rescued one who opposed me without cause—
v05: then let an enemy pursue and overtake me,
trample my life into the ground,
and lay my honor in the dust. Selah
</p>

<p>
v06: Rise in anger, LORD;
stand against my enemies’ fury.
Awake for me; you have commanded justice.
v07: Let the assembly of peoples surround you;
return to your high place above them.
v08: The LORD judges the peoples.
Judge me, LORD, according to my righteousness,
according to the integrity within me.
v09: Bring the evil of the wicked to an end
and establish the righteous,
you righteous God who tests hearts and kidneys.
</p>

<p>
v10: My shield is with God,
who saves the upright in heart.
v11: God is a righteous judge,
a God who expresses indignation every day.
v12: If a person does not turn back, God sharpens his sword;
he bends his bow and makes it ready.
v13: He prepares deadly weapons
and makes his arrows burn.
</p>

<p>
v14: See how the wicked labor with wrongdoing,
conceive trouble, and give birth to falsehood.
v15: They dig a pit and deepen it,
then fall into the hole they made.
v16: Their trouble returns on their own heads;
their violence falls on their own crowns.
v17: I will thank the LORD for his righteousness
and sing to the name of the LORD Most High.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Shiggaion is an uncertain designation for the poem or its performance. The title refers to Cush the Benjaminite, whose circumstances are not explained here.

v04: The difficult second clause can concern rescuing a causeless enemy or plundering an adversary.

v12: The draft takes the one who does not turn back as the wicked person, followed by God preparing weapons. The sequence of subjects is not explicit.

## Vocabulary

v09: Kidneys
A bodily image for inward motives and feelings.
