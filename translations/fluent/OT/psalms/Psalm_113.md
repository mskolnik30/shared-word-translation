## From the heights to the refuse heap

<p>
v01: Praise YAH!
Praise, you servants of the LORD;
praise the name of the LORD.
v02: Let the name of the LORD be blessed
from now and forever.
v03: From the rising of the sun to its setting,
the name of the LORD is to be praised.
</p>

<p>
v04: The LORD is high above all nations;
his glory is above the heavens.
v05: Who is like the LORD our God,
enthroned on high,
v06: who stoops to look
on the heavens and the earth?
</p>

<p>
v07: He raises the poor from the dust
and lifts the needy from the refuse heap,
v08: to seat them with nobles,
with the nobles of his people.
v09: He settles the childless woman in a home
as a joyful mother of children.
Praise YAH!
</p>

## Notes

v05–09: The repeated downward and upward movements connect divine height with care for people in low places. Verse 9 portrays a particular reversal of childlessness, not a timetable attached to every reader’s life.

## Vocabulary

v07: **Refuse heap**: A place of discarded waste, marking extreme poverty in the image.
