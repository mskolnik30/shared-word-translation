## Building, guarding, and receiving

<p>
v01: A song of ascents. Of Solomon.
Unless the LORD builds the house,
its builders labor in vain.
Unless the LORD guards the city,
the guard keeps watch in vain.
</p>

<p>
v02: In vain you rise early and stay up late,
eating the bread of painful toil;
he gives sleep to the one he loves.
</p>

<p>
v03: Look: sons are an inheritance from the LORD;
the fruit of the womb is a reward.
v04: Like arrows in a warrior’s hand
are sons born in one’s youth.
v05: Happy is the man whose quiver is full of them.
They will not be ashamed
when they speak with enemies at the gate.
</p>

## Notes

v02: The last clause may mean God gives his beloved sleep, or gives to his beloved while they sleep. The draft takes sleep as the gift and retains the alternative here.

v03–05: The household is portrayed through sons, arrows, a quiver, and speaking with enemies at the city gate. The masculine family and public setting remain visible.

## Vocabulary

v05: **Gate**: The city’s public place for legal and civic dealings as well as entry and defense.
