## Betrayed by a close friend

<p>
v01: For the music leader. With stringed instruments. A maskil of David.
God, listen to my prayer;
do not turn away from my plea.
v02: Pay attention to me and answer.
I am restless in my complaint, moaning
v03: at the enemy’s voice,
under the wicked man’s pressure.
They heap trouble on me
and pursue me in anger.
</p>

<p>
v04: My heart writhes inside me;
the terrors of death have fallen on me.
v05: Fear and trembling come over me;
horror covers me.
</p>

<p>
v06: I said, “If only I had wings like a dove!
I would fly away and settle somewhere.
v07: I would wander far away
and spend the night in the wilderness. Selah.
v08: I would hurry to find shelter
from the rushing wind and storm.”
</p>

<p>
v09: Swallow them up, Lord; divide their tongues!
I see violence and conflict in the city.
v10: Day and night they circle it on its walls;
trouble and misery are inside it.
v11: Destruction is within it;
oppression and deceit never leave its square.
</p>

<p>
v12: If an enemy were insulting me, I could bear it.
If someone who hated me rose against me, I could hide from him.
v13: But it is you—a man my equal,
my companion, my close friend.
v14: We shared the sweetness of private counsel
and walked with the crowd in God’s house.
</p>

<p>
v15: Let death take them by surprise;
let them go down alive to Sheol!
Evil lives in their homes and within them.
</p>

<p>
v16: As for me, I call to God,
and the LORD will save me.
v17: Evening, morning, and noon, I complain and moan;
he hears my voice.
v18: He has ransomed me safely from the battle against me,
though there were many opposing me.
v19: God will hear and humble them—
the one enthroned from long ago. Selah.
They do not change,
and they do not fear God.
</p>

<p>
v20: He reaches out against those at peace with him;
he has profaned his covenant.
v21: His mouth is smoother than butter,
but war is in his heart.
His words are softer than oil,
but they are drawn swords.
</p>

<p>
v22: Throw your burden onto the LORD;
he will sustain you.
He will never let the righteous be shaken.
v23: But you, God, will bring them down to the pit of destruction.
Men of bloodshed and deceit will not live out half their days.
As for me, I will trust you.
</p>

## Notes

v15,18–20: Verse 15 follows the traditional two-word reading naming death. The many people in verse 18 may be fighting against the speaker or standing alongside him. “They do not change” may refer to their conduct or to their unchanging circumstances. The singular betrayer reappears without being named.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Maskil**: A psalm designation of uncertain meaning, possibly relating to insight or skill.

v22: **Burden — yehav**: What is laid upon or assigned to someone. The image asks the hearer to put that weight on the LORD.
