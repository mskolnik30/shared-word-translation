## Rescue and a flourishing people

<p>
v01: Of David.
Blessed be the LORD, my rock,
who trains my hands for battle, my fingers for war.
v02: He is my faithful love and my fortress,
my stronghold and my deliverer,
my shield, in whom I take refuge,
who subdues my people under me.
</p>

<p>
v03: LORD, what is a human being that you know him,
a mortal that you take account of him?
v04: A human being is like a breath;
his days are like a passing shadow.
</p>

<p>
v05: LORD, bend your heavens and come down;
touch the mountains so they smoke.
v06: Flash lightning and scatter them;
send your arrows and throw them into confusion.
v07: Stretch your hands down from on high;
free me and rescue me from mighty waters,
from the hand of foreigners,
v08: whose mouths speak falsehood
and whose right hand is a hand of lies.
</p>

<p>
v09: God, I will sing you a new song;
on a ten-stringed harp I will make music for you,
v10: you who give kings victory,
who free David your servant from the deadly sword.
</p>

<p>
v11: Free me and rescue me from the hand of foreigners,
whose mouths speak falsehood
and whose right hand is a hand of lies.
</p>

<p>
v12: May our sons be like plants grown tall in their youth,
our daughters like corner pillars carved for a palace.
v13: May our storehouses be full, supplying every kind of produce;
may our flocks multiply by thousands and tens of thousands in our fields.
v14: May our cattle be laden with increase;
may there be no breach, no going out,
no cry of distress in our squares.
</p>

<p>
v15: Happy is the people for whom it is so;
happy is the people whose God is the LORD.
</p>

## Notes

v02,12–14: Verse 2 follows “my people,” not the variant-like rendering “peoples.” Verses 12–14 are taken as wishes for the community. Their connection to verse 11 is compressed, and other constructions are possible. The cattle may be laden, pregnant, or strong for carrying; “no breach” and “no going out” can describe civic security or livestock loss. The draft keeps the difficult images and records these alternatives.

## Vocabulary

v08: **Right hand of lies**: The right hand evokes pledged trust or an oath, here marked as false.
