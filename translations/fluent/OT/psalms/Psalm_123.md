## Eyes lifted for mercy

<p>
v01: A song of ascents.
To you I lift my eyes,
you who are enthroned in the heavens.
v02: As the eyes of slaves look to their masters’ hand,
as a female slave looks to her mistress’s hand,
so our eyes look to the LORD our God
until he shows us favor.
</p>

<p>
v03: Show us favor, LORD, show us favor,
for we have had more than enough contempt.
v04: We have had more than enough
of the mockery of the complacent, the contempt of the proud.
</p>

## Notes

v02: The comparison uses male and female slaves and their masters’ hands. It depicts dependence while retaining the unequal social relationships in the source.

## Vocabulary

v03: **Favor**: Gracious attention or mercy, sought repeatedly by a community worn down by contempt.
