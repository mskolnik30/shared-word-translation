## Send your light and truth

<p>
v01: Vindicate me, God; defend my cause against a faithless nation.
Rescue me from deceitful and unjust people.
v02: You are the God of my refuge. Why have you rejected me?
Why must I go about mourning under the enemy’s oppression?
</p>

<p>
v03: Send your light and your truth; let them lead me.
Let them bring me to your holy mountain, to your dwelling places.
v04: Then I will come to God’s altar, to God, my overflowing joy.
I will thank you with the lyre, God, my God.
</p>

<p>
v05: Why are you bowed down, my soul? Why do you roar within me?
Hope in God, for I will yet thank him,
my saving help, my God.
</p>

## Notes

v01,05: “Faithless” describes the hostile nation’s disloyal conduct. The closing refrain repeats Psalm 42:11, including the Hebrew image “the saving help of my face.”

## Vocabulary

v03: **Light and truth**: God’s guidance is pictured as two companions sent to lead the speaker home to worship.
