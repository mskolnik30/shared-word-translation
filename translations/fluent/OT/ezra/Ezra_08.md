## Those who traveled with Ezra

<p>
v01: These are the family heads who came up with me from Babylon during King Artaxerxes’s reign, listed by descent:
</p>

<p>
v02: from the family of Phinehas, Gershom; from the family of Ithamar, Daniel; from the family of David, Hattush,
v03: of the family of Shecaniah; from the family of Parosh, Zechariah, with 150 males registered with him;
v04: from the family of Pahath-moab, Eliehoenai son of Zerahiah, with 200 males;
v05: from the family of Shecaniah, the son of Jahaziel, with 300 males;
v06: from the family of Adin, Ebed son of Jonathan, with 50 males;
v07: from the family of Elam, Jeshaiah son of Athaliah, with 70 males;
v08: from the family of Shephatiah, Zebadiah son of Michael, with 80 males;
v09: from the family of Joab, Obadiah son of Jehiel, with 218 males;
v10: from the family of Shelomith, the son of Josiphiah, with 160 males;
v11: from the family of Bebai, Zechariah son of Bebai, with 28 males;
v12: from the family of Azgad, Johanan son of Hakkatan, with 110 males;
v13: from the family of Adonikam, the last to come, whose names were Eliphelet, Jeiel, and Shemaiah, with 60 males;
v14: from the family of Bigvai, Uthai and Zaccur, with 70 males.
</p>

## Levites recruited

<p>
v15: I gathered them at the river that runs toward Ahava, and we camped there for three days. I inspected the people and the priests but found no Levites there.
v16: I sent for the leaders Eliezer, Ariel, Shemaiah, Elnathan, Jarib, another Elnathan, Nathan, Zechariah, and Meshullam, and for the teachers Joiarib and Elnathan.
v17: I instructed them to go to Iddo, the leader at Casiphia, and gave them a message for Iddo and his fellow temple servants there: bring us ministers for the house of our God.
</p>

<p>
v18: Because the good hand of our God was on us, they brought us a discerning man from the descendants of Mahli son of Levi, son of Israel: Sherebiah, with his sons and relatives, eighteen in all.
v19: They also brought Hashabiah and, with him, Jeshaiah from the descendants of Merari, with their relatives and sons, twenty in all,
v20: and 220 temple servants, from those David and the officials had assigned to assist the Levites. All were recorded by name.
</p>

## Seeking protection for the journey

<p>
v21: There by the Ahava River I proclaimed a fast, so we could humble ourselves before our God and ask him for a safe journey for ourselves, our little ones, and all our possessions.
v22: I was ashamed to ask the king for soldiers and horsemen to protect us from enemies along the way, because we had told him, “Our God’s hand brings good to all who seek him, but his power and anger are against all who abandon him.”
v23: So we fasted and sought our God about this, and he answered our plea.
</p>

## The gifts entrusted to their keepers

<p>
v24: I set apart twelve of the leading priests, along with Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and ten of their relatives.
v25: I weighed out to them the silver, gold, and vessels contributed for our God’s house by the king, his advisers and officials, and all the Israelites who were present.
v26: I weighed into their hands 650 talents of silver, silver vessels weighing a hundred talents, a hundred talents of gold,
v27: twenty gold bowls worth a thousand darics, and two vessels of fine, gleaming bronze, as precious as gold.
</p>

<p>
v28: I told them, “You are holy to the LORD, and the vessels are holy. The silver and gold are a voluntary offering to the LORD, the God of your ancestors.
v29: Keep careful watch over them until you weigh them out before the leading priests, Levites, and family heads of Israel in Jerusalem, in the chambers of the LORD’s house.”
</p>

<p>
v30: The priests and Levites received the weighed silver, gold, and vessels to take to Jerusalem, to our God’s house.
</p>

## Arrival in Jerusalem

<p>
v31: We left the Ahava River for Jerusalem on the twelfth day of the first month. Our God’s hand was on us, and he rescued us from enemies and ambushes along the way.
v32: We reached Jerusalem and stayed there for three days.
</p>

<p>
v33: On the fourth day, the silver, gold, and vessels were weighed out in our God’s house into the care of Meremoth son of Uriah the priest. Eleazar son of Phinehas was with him, along with the Levites Jozabad son of Jeshua and Noadiah son of Binnui.
v34: Everything was checked by number and weight, and the full weight was recorded at that time.
</p>

<p>
v35: The exiles who had returned from captivity offered burnt offerings to the God of Israel: twelve bulls for all Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, and twelve male goats as a sin offering. All of it was a burnt offering to the LORD.
v36: They delivered the king’s decrees to the royal satraps and the governors of Beyond the River, who supported the people and God’s house.
</p>

## Notes

v02–03: “Of the family of Shecaniah” is connected here with Hattush in verse 2. The Hebrew verse boundary falls between them.

v05, 10: These entries are difficult and appear incomplete: a family is named and then an unnamed “son of” Jahaziel or Josiphiah. The draft does not supply names from other textual traditions.

v13: “The last to come” may mean the later members or remaining descendants of Adonikam.

v14: Zaccur follows the recorded read form; the written form is Zabbud.

v18: Sherebiah is taken here as the discerning man. The Hebrew can also be read as introducing him in addition to that man.

v24: The draft takes Sherebiah, Hashabiah, and their ten relatives as Levites accompanying twelve leading priests, in light of verses 18–19 and 30. The compact Hebrew can also be read as a single group of twelve.

v35: The verse names a sin offering and then calls the whole offering a burnt offering. Both statements are retained.

## Vocabulary

v20: Temple servants
The Nethinim, assigned here to assist the Levites.

v21: Fast
Abstaining from food as part of humbling oneself and seeking God.

v36: Satraps
Senior officials in the Persian imperial administration.
