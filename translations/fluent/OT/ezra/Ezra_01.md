## Cyrus authorizes the return

<p>
v01: In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, the LORD stirred Cyrus’s spirit to fulfill the word the LORD had spoken through Jeremiah. Cyrus issued a proclamation throughout his kingdom and also put it in writing:
v02: “This is what Cyrus king of Persia says: ‘The LORD, the God of heaven, has given me all the kingdoms of the earth. He has appointed me to build him a house in Jerusalem, in Judah.
v03: Whoever among you belongs to his people—may his God be with him! Let him go up to Jerusalem in Judah and rebuild the house of the LORD, the God of Israel. He is the God who is in Jerusalem.
v04: Wherever any survivor is living, the people of that place are to support him with silver, gold, goods, and livestock, besides the voluntary offering for God’s house in Jerusalem.’”
</p>

<p>
v05: Then the family heads of Judah and Benjamin, the priests, and the Levites prepared to go up and rebuild the LORD’s house in Jerusalem—everyone whose spirit God had stirred.
v06: All their neighbors helped them with silver vessels, gold, goods, livestock, and valuable gifts, besides all the voluntary offerings.
</p>

## The temple vessels returned

<p>
v07: King Cyrus brought out the vessels of the LORD’s house that Nebuchadnezzar had taken from Jerusalem and placed in the house of his gods.
v08: Cyrus king of Persia had Mithredath the treasurer bring them out and count them over to Sheshbazzar, the leader of Judah.
</p>

<p>
v09: Here was the inventory: thirty gold dishes, a thousand silver dishes, twenty-nine knives,
v10: thirty gold bowls, four hundred ten silver bowls of a second kind, and a thousand other vessels.
v11: The gold and silver vessels totaled five thousand four hundred. Sheshbazzar brought them all up when the exiles were brought from Babylon to Jerusalem.
</p>

## Notes

v01–03: The opening repeats the end of 2 Chronicles, but Ezra continues the proclamation. Its wording is retained where shared.

v03: The final Jerusalem phrase can describe God here, or be connected back to his house.

v09–11: Some vessel names are uncertain. The itemized figures total 2,499; verse 11 gives 5,400. Both are retained. “Of a second kind” in verse 10 is also uncertain.

## Vocabulary

v01: Spirit
The inner life and resolve of a person; here God stirs someone to act.

v04: Voluntary offering
A gift freely contributed.
