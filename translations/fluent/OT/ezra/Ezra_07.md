## Ezra comes to Jerusalem

<p>
v01: After these events, during the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, came Ezra. His line ran through Seraiah, Azariah, Hilkiah,
v02: Shallum, Zadok, Ahitub,
v03: Amariah, Azariah, Meraioth,
v04: Zerahiah, Uzzi, Bukki,
v05: Abishua, Phinehas, Eleazar, and Aaron the chief priest.
</p>

<p>
v06: This Ezra came up from Babylon. He was a scribe skilled in the instruction of Moses, which the LORD, the God of Israel, had given. The king granted every request he made, for the hand of the LORD his God was on him.
v07: Some of the Israelites, priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and temple servants also went up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.
v08: Ezra arrived in Jerusalem in the fifth month of that seventh year.
</p>

<p>
v09: He had begun the journey up from Babylon on the first day of the first month. He reached Jerusalem on the first day of the fifth month, for the good hand of his God was on him.
v10: Ezra had set his heart on studying the LORD’s instruction, putting it into practice, and teaching its statutes and judgments in Israel.
</p>

## Artaxerxes’s letter

<p>
v11: This is a copy of the letter King Artaxerxes gave Ezra the priest and scribe, a scribe learned in the LORD’s commands and statutes for Israel:
v12: “Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of heaven: Complete greetings. And now:
</p>

<p>
v13: “I decree that anyone in my kingdom from the people of Israel, including their priests and Levites, who wishes to go with you to Jerusalem may go.
v14: You are sent by the king and his seven advisers to investigate Judah and Jerusalem according to the law of your God that is in your hand.
v15: You are also to carry the silver and gold that the king and his advisers have voluntarily given to the God of Israel, whose dwelling is in Jerusalem,
v16: together with all the silver and gold you obtain throughout the province of Babylon, and the voluntary gifts that the people and priests give for their God’s house in Jerusalem.
</p>

<p>
v17: Use this money diligently to buy bulls, rams, and lambs, with their grain offerings and drink offerings. Offer them on the altar of your God’s house in Jerusalem.
v18: Whatever you and your fellow Israelites consider best to do with the remaining silver and gold, do it according to your God’s will.
v19: Deliver the vessels entrusted to you for the service of your God’s house into the presence of the God of Jerusalem.
v20: Any further needs of your God’s house that you must provide for are to be supplied from the royal treasury.
</p>

<p>
v21: “I, King Artaxerxes, also decree to all the treasurers in Beyond the River: Whatever Ezra the priest, scribe of the law of the God of heaven, asks of you must be supplied diligently,
v22: up to a hundred talents of silver, a hundred cors of wheat, a hundred baths of wine, and a hundred baths of oil, with no fixed limit on salt.
v23: Whatever the God of heaven commands is to be carried out carefully for his house. Why should wrath fall on the kingdom of the king and his sons?
v24: You are also informed that no tribute, tax, or toll may be imposed on any of the priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, temple servants, or other workers in this house of God.
</p>

<p>
v25: “And you, Ezra, according to the wisdom of your God that is in your hand, appoint magistrates and judges to judge all the people in Beyond the River who know your God’s laws. Teach those who do not know them.
v26: Anyone who does not obey the law of your God and the law of the king must be judged promptly, whether by death, banishment, confiscation of property, or imprisonment.”
</p>

## Ezra gives thanks

<p>
v27: Blessed be the LORD, the God of our ancestors! He put it into the king’s heart to bring honor to the LORD’s house in Jerusalem,
v28: and extended his faithful love to me before the king, his advisers, and all the king’s powerful officials. With the hand of the LORD my God on me, I took courage and gathered leaders from Israel to go up with me.
</p>

## Notes

v01–05: This ancestry connects Ezra to Aaron. Biblical genealogies can omit generations; the chain is not expanded with names from other passages.

v09: The initial departure date is followed in 8:31 by the departure from the Ahava River on the twelfth day.

v12–26: The royal letter is in Aramaic; Hebrew resumes at verse 27. The greeting in verse 12 is elliptical; “Complete greetings” supplies the implied greeting.

v26: The word rendered “banishment” refers to removal or uprooting; its precise penalty is uncertain. The other named penalties are death, loss of property, and imprisonment.

## Vocabulary

v06: Scribe
A trained writer and interpreter of written instruction, not only a copyist.

v22: Cor; bath; talent
A cor measured dry goods, a bath measured liquids, and a talent measured weight.

v27–28: Heart; hand
The king’s heart describes intention; God’s hand describes active help.
