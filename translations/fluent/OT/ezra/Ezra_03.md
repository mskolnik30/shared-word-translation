## The altar is rebuilt

<p>
v01: When the seventh month arrived, the Israelites were in their towns. The people gathered as one in Jerusalem.
v02: Jeshua son of Jozadak and his fellow priests, together with Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel and his relatives, began rebuilding the altar of the God of Israel. They would offer burnt offerings on it, as written in the instruction of Moses, the man of God.
v03: They set the altar on its foundations, for they were afraid of the peoples of the surrounding lands. On it they offered burnt offerings to the LORD, morning and evening.
</p>

<p>
v04: They observed the Festival of Shelters as written, offering the required number of burnt offerings day by day, each day’s offerings according to its rule.
v05: After that came the regular burnt offerings, those for the new moons and all the LORD’s appointed holy festivals, and the offerings of everyone who gave a voluntary gift to the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v06: They began offering burnt offerings to the LORD on the first day of the seventh month, although the foundation of the LORD’s temple had not yet been laid.
v07: They paid the stonecutters and craftsmen in silver. They gave food, drink, and oil to the Sidonians and Tyrians to bring cedar timber from Lebanon by sea to Joppa, under the authorization Cyrus king of Persia had granted them.
</p>

## Laying the temple foundation

<p>
v08: In the second month of the second year after their arrival at God’s house in Jerusalem, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Jeshua son of Jozadak, and the rest of their fellow priests and Levites, with everyone who had returned to Jerusalem from captivity, began the work. They appointed Levites twenty years old and above to oversee the work on the LORD’s house.
v09: Jeshua, his sons and relatives, and Kadmiel and his sons, descendants of Judah, stood together to oversee those working on God’s house. With them were the sons of Henadad, their sons, and their fellow Levites.
</p>

<p>
v10: When the builders laid the foundation of the LORD’s temple, the priests took their places in their robes with trumpets, and the Levites descended from Asaph with cymbals, to praise the LORD as David king of Israel had directed.
v11: They sang to one another in praise and thanksgiving to the LORD:
“He is good;
his faithful love for Israel lasts forever.”
Then all the people raised a great shout of praise to the LORD because the foundation of his house had been laid.
</p>

<p>
v12: Many of the older priests, Levites, and family heads who had seen the first house wept aloud as they watched the foundation of this house being laid. Many others lifted their voices in joyful shouts.
v13: No one could distinguish the joyful shouts from the people’s weeping. The people were shouting so loudly that the sound could be heard far away.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The Hebrew links building the altar with fear of surrounding peoples. It can also be understood as building despite that fear.

v09: The Hebrew says “Judah” here. The related family designation in 2:40 is “Hodaviah.” The difference is retained.

v12–13: The text describes weeping and joy together but does not explain every person’s reason for weeping.

## Vocabulary

v02: Burnt offering
An offering burned on the altar for God.

v04: Festival of Shelters
The autumn festival involving temporary shelters, also called Booths or Tabernacles.

v11: Faithful love
Hebrew hesed: enduring loyalty and kindness in a relationship.
