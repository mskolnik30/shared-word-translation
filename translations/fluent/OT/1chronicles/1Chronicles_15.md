## Preparing to carry the ark

<p>
v01: David built houses for himself in the City of David. He prepared a place for God’s ark and pitched a tent for it.
v02: Then David said, “Only the Levites may carry God’s ark. The LORD chose them to carry the ark of the LORD and to serve him forever.”
v03: David assembled all Israel at Jerusalem to bring the ark of the LORD up to the place he had prepared for it.
</p>

<p>
v04: He gathered Aaron’s descendants and the Levites:
v05: from Kohath’s descendants, Uriel the leader and 120 of his relatives;
v06: from Merari’s descendants, Asaiah the leader and 220 of his relatives;
v07: from Gershom’s descendants, Joel the leader and 130 of his relatives;
v08: from Elizaphan’s descendants, Shemaiah the leader and 200 of his relatives;
v09: from Hebron’s descendants, Eliel the leader and 80 of his relatives;
v10: from Uzziel’s descendants, Amminadab the leader and 112 of his relatives.
</p>

<p>
v11: David summoned the priests Zadok and Abiathar, and the Levites Uriel, Asaiah, Joel, Shemaiah, Eliel, and Amminadab.
v12: He told them, “You are the heads of the Levite ancestral houses. Consecrate yourselves and your relatives, and bring the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel, up to the place I have prepared for it.
v13: Because you did not carry it the first time, the LORD our God broke out against us. We did not seek him in the prescribed way.”
v14: So the priests and Levites consecrated themselves to bring up the ark of the LORD, the God of Israel.
v15: The Levites carried God’s ark on their shoulders with its poles, as Moses had commanded according to the word of the LORD.
</p>

## Singers and gatekeepers

<p>
v16: David told the Levite leaders to appoint their relatives as singers, with harps, lyres, and cymbals, to raise a loud sound of joy.
v17: The Levites appointed Heman son of Joel and his relative Asaph son of Berechiah, and, from their Merarite relatives, Ethan son of Kushaiah.
v18: With them were their relatives of the second rank: Zechariah, Ben, Jaaziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, and the gatekeepers Obed-edom and Jeiel.
v19: The singers Heman, Asaph, and Ethan were to sound bronze cymbals.
v20: Zechariah, Aziel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Unni, Eliab, Maaseiah, and Benaiah were to play harps according to alamoth.
v21: Mattithiah, Eliphelehu, Mikneiah, Obed-edom, Jeiel, and Azaziah were to lead with lyres according to sheminith.
v22: Chenaniah, the Levites’ leader in music, directed the music because he understood it.
</p>

<p>
v23: Berechiah and Elkanah were gatekeepers for the ark.
v24: The priests Shebaniah, Joshaphat, Nethanel, Amasai, Zechariah, Benaiah, and Eliezer were to sound trumpets before God’s ark. Obed-edom and Jehiah were also gatekeepers for the ark.
</p>

## The procession

<p>
v25: David, the elders of Israel, and the commanders of thousands went with joy to bring the ark of the LORD’s covenant up from Obed-edom’s house.
v26: Because God helped the Levites who carried the ark of the LORD’s covenant, they sacrificed seven bulls and seven rams.
v27: David wore a robe of fine linen, as did all the Levites carrying the ark, the singers, and Chenaniah, the leader of the singers’ music. David also wore a linen ephod.
v28: All Israel brought up the ark of the LORD’s covenant with shouts, the sound of the ram’s horn, trumpets and cymbals, and music from harps and lyres.
</p>

<p>
v29: As the ark of the LORD’s covenant entered the City of David, Michal, Saul’s daughter, looked down through a window. She saw King David leaping and celebrating, and she despised him in her heart.
</p>

## Notes

v13: “You did not carry it” supplies the action implied by the Hebrew’s incomplete opening clause.

v18: Ben is present in the Hebrew list. It can also mean “son”; its place here is uncertain.

v20–21: Alamoth and sheminith are musical directions whose exact performance meanings are lost. Alamoth is related to young women; sheminith to “eighth.”

v22,27: The Hebrew massa can mean carrying or burden and can also be associated with an utterance. The draft understands Chenaniah’s role musically in this context; directing the carrying is another reading.

## Vocabulary

v12: Consecrate
Set apart for sacred service, including the required preparation.

v27: Ephod
A garment associated with priestly service; David’s is described as linen.
