## David addresses the assembly

<p>
v01: David assembled all Israel’s officials in Jerusalem: the tribal leaders, the commanders of the divisions serving the king, the commanders of thousands and hundreds, the officials over all the property and livestock of the king and his sons, together with the court officials, the warriors, and all the capable fighting men.
v02: King David rose to his feet and said, “Listen to me, my brothers and my people. I had it in my heart to build a house of rest for the ark of the LORD’s covenant, for the footstool of our God, and I made preparations to build it.
v03: But God told me, ‘You must not build a house for my name, because you are a man of wars and have shed blood.’
v04: Yet the LORD, the God of Israel, chose me from my whole ancestral house to be king over Israel forever. He chose Judah as ruler, and from the house of Judah he chose my father’s house. Among my father’s sons, he was pleased to make me king over all Israel.
v05: Of all my sons—and the LORD has given me many sons—he chose my son Solomon to sit on the throne of the LORD’s kingdom over Israel.
v06: He told me, ‘Your son Solomon will build my house and my courtyards, for I have chosen him as my son, and I will be his father.
v07: I will establish his kingdom forever, if he remains firm in carrying out my commands and judgments, as he does today.’
</p>

<p>
v08: Now, before the eyes of all Israel, the LORD’s assembly, and in the hearing of our God, keep and seek out all the commands of the LORD your God. Then you may possess this good land and leave it as an inheritance to your children after you forever.
</p>

<p>
v09: And you, my son Solomon, know your father’s God and serve him with a whole heart and a willing spirit. The LORD searches every heart and understands every purpose behind our thoughts. If you seek him, he will let you find him; if you abandon him, he will reject you forever.
v10: Consider this now: the LORD has chosen you to build a house as a sanctuary. Be strong and do it.”
</p>

## Plans for the temple

<p>
v11: David gave his son Solomon the plan for the entrance hall, its buildings, storerooms, upper rooms, inner rooms, and the room for the atonement cover.
v12: He gave him the plan for everything he had received through the Spirit: the courtyards of the LORD’s house, all the surrounding rooms, the treasuries of God’s house, and the treasuries of dedicated gifts.
v13: He also gave directions for the divisions of priests and Levites, all the work of serving the LORD’s house, and all the utensils used in its service.
</p>

<p>
v14: He specified the weight of gold for every gold utensil used in each kind of service, and the weight of silver for every silver utensil used in each kind of service.
v15: He specified the gold weight for the gold lampstands and their lamps, each lampstand with its lamps, and the silver weight for the silver lampstands and their lamps, according to the use of each lampstand.
v16: He specified the gold weight for each table of arranged bread, and the silver for the silver tables;
v17: the pure gold for the forks, sprinkling bowls, and pitchers; the gold weight for each gold basin; and the silver weight for each silver basin;
v18: and the weight of refined gold for the incense altar. He also gave the plan for the chariot—the gold cherubim with wings spread out to cover the ark of the LORD’s covenant.
</p>

<p>
v19: “All this is in writing,” David said. “The LORD’s hand upon me gave me understanding of every detail of the plan.”
</p>

<p>
v20: David said to his son Solomon, “Be strong and courageous, and do the work. Do not be afraid or discouraged, for the LORD God, my God, is with you. He will not fail you or abandon you until all the work for the service of the LORD’s house is finished.
v21: The divisions of priests and Levites are ready for all the service of God’s house. Every willing person skilled in any craft will be with you for all the work. The officials and all the people are at your command.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: The word for court officials can also mean eunuchs; their bodily status is not specified here.

v12: The Hebrew ruach can mean spirit or mind. The draft understands the plan as received through the Spirit; “what he had in mind” is another possible reading.

v18: The cherubim are described as a chariot. The throne image is retained rather than reduced to decoration.

v19: The verse connects written plans with the LORD’s hand and David’s understanding, without spelling out the process of transmission.

## Vocabulary

v02: Footstool
An image linking the ark with God’s kingship.

v11: Atonement cover
The sacred cover of the ark, traditionally also called the mercy seat.
