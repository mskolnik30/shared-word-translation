## Worship before the ark

<p>
v01: They brought God’s ark in and set it inside the tent David had pitched for it. Then they presented burnt offerings and well-being offerings before God.
v02: When David finished presenting the burnt offerings and well-being offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the LORD.
v03: He distributed food to every Israelite, man and woman alike: a loaf of bread, a portion of meat, and a raisin cake for each.
</p>

<p>
v04: He appointed some Levites to serve before the ark of the LORD, to commemorate, thank, and praise the LORD, the God of Israel.
v05: Asaph was the leader, with Zechariah second, then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, Obed-edom, and Jeiel. They played harps and lyres, while Asaph sounded the cymbals.
v06: The priests Benaiah and Jahaziel sounded trumpets regularly before the ark of God’s covenant.
v07: That day David first charged Asaph and his relatives with giving thanks to the LORD:
</p>

## A song of thanksgiving

<p>
v08: Give thanks to the LORD; call on his name.
Make his deeds known among the peoples.
v09: Sing to him; make music to him.
Tell of all his wonders.
v10: Boast in his holy name.
Let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice.
v11: Seek the LORD and his strength.
Seek his face continually.
v12: Remember the wonders he has done,
his signs and the judgments from his mouth,
v13: you offspring of Israel his servant,
you children of Jacob, his chosen ones.
</p>

<p>
v14: He is the LORD our God.
His judgments reach all the earth.
v15: Remember his covenant forever,
the word he commanded for a thousand generations,
v16: the covenant he made with Abraham,
and his oath to Isaac.
v17: He established it for Jacob as a decree,
for Israel as an everlasting covenant,
v18: saying, “To you I will give the land of Canaan,
your allotted inheritance.”
v19: When you were few in number,
very few, and living there as foreigners,
v20: they wandered from nation to nation,
from one kingdom to another people.
v21: He allowed no one to oppress them;
for their sake he rebuked kings:
v22: “Do not touch my anointed ones;
do my prophets no harm.”
</p>

<p>
v23: Sing to the LORD, all the earth.
Tell the good news of his salvation day after day.
v24: Declare his glory among the nations,
his wonders among all the peoples.
v25: For the LORD is great and greatly to be praised;
he is to be feared above all gods.
v26: All the gods of the peoples are idols,
but the LORD made the heavens.
v27: Splendor and majesty are before him;
strength and joy are in his place.
</p>

<p>
v28: Ascribe to the LORD, families of the peoples,
ascribe to the LORD glory and strength.
v29: Ascribe to the LORD the glory of his name.
Bring an offering and come before him.
Bow before the LORD in holy splendor.
v30: Tremble before him, all the earth.
The world stands firm; it will not be shaken.
v31: Let the heavens rejoice and the earth be glad.
Let them say among the nations, “The LORD reigns!”
v32: Let the sea roar, and everything in it.
Let the fields rejoice, and everything in them.
v33: Then the trees of the forest will sing for joy
before the LORD, for he comes to judge the earth.
</p>

<p>
v34: Give thanks to the LORD, for he is good;
his faithful love lasts forever.
v35: Say, “Save us, God of our salvation.
Gather us and rescue us from the nations,
so we may thank your holy name
and glory in praising you.”
</p>

<p>
v36: Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel,
from everlasting to everlasting!
Then all the people said, “Amen,” and praised the LORD.
</p>

## Daily service

<p>
v37: David left Asaph and his relatives there before the ark of the LORD’s covenant to serve regularly before the ark, attending to each day’s needs.
v38: He also left Obed-edom and their sixty-eight relatives. Obed-edom son of Jedithun and Hosah were gatekeepers.
</p>

<p>
v39: He left Zadok the priest and his fellow priests before the LORD’s tabernacle at the high place in Gibeon,
v40: to present burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering regularly, morning and evening, following everything written in the instruction the LORD had commanded Israel.
</p>

<p>
v41: With them were Heman, Jeduthun, and the rest who had been chosen and designated by name to give thanks to the LORD, because his faithful love lasts forever.
v42: Heman and Jeduthun had trumpets and cymbals to sound, and instruments for the songs of God. Jeduthun’s sons were stationed at the gate.
</p>

<p>
v43: Then all the people went home, and David returned to bless his household.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The food term rendered “portion of meat” is uncertain; a date cake or another food portion has also been proposed.

v05: Jeiel appears twice in the list, as in the Hebrew.

v08–36: The song has close parallels in Psalms 105, 96, and 106. Its own wording is retained, including Israel in verse 13, the command “Remember” in verse 15, and joy in verse 27.

v29: “In holy splendor” may refer to sacred beauty or to holy attire.

v38: The Hebrew shifts to “their relatives” and names Obed-edom twice. It is unclear whether two men of that name are meant. Jedithun is the form here; Jeduthun occurs nearby.

## Vocabulary

v15: Covenant
A binding relationship expressed through commitments; the song connects it with Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and the land.

v34: Faithful love
Hebrew hesed: enduring loyalty and kindness within a relationship.
