## Priestly divisions

<p>
v01: These were the divisions of Aaron’s descendants. Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
v02: Nadab and Abihu died before their father and had no sons, so Eleazar and Ithamar served as priests.
v03: David, with Zadok from Eleazar’s descendants and Ahimelech from Ithamar’s descendants, organized them according to their duties in the service.
v04: Eleazar’s descendants had more leading men than Ithamar’s, so they divided them accordingly: sixteen heads of ancestral houses from Eleazar’s descendants and eight from Ithamar’s.
v05: They assigned both groups by lot, for leaders of the sanctuary and leaders in God’s service came from both Eleazar’s and Ithamar’s descendants.
v06: The Levitical scribe Shemaiah son of Nethanel recorded them before the king and the officials, Zadok the priest, Ahimelech son of Abiathar, and the heads of the priestly and Levitical ancestral houses. One ancestral house was selected for Eleazar, and one was selected for Ithamar.
</p>

<p>
v07: The first lot fell to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah,
v08: the third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim,
v09: the fifth to Malchijah, the sixth to Mijamin,
v10: the seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah,
v11: the ninth to Jeshua, the tenth to Shecaniah,
v12: the eleventh to Eliashib, the twelfth to Jakim,
v13: the thirteenth to Huppah, the fourteenth to Jeshebeab,
v14: the fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer,
v15: the seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Happizzez,
v16: the nineteenth to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezkel,
v17: the twenty-first to Jachin, the twenty-second to Gamul,
v18: the twenty-third to Delaiah, and the twenty-fourth to Maaziah.
</p>

<p>
v19: These were their assigned duties when they entered the LORD’s house to serve, following the rule given through their ancestor Aaron, as the LORD, the God of Israel, had commanded him.
</p>

## Other Levitical families

<p>
v20: As for the remaining descendants of Levi: from Amram’s descendants, Shubael; from Shubael’s descendants, Jehdeiah.
v21: From Rehabiah: Isshiah, the leader of Rehabiah’s descendants.
v22: From the Izharites, Shelomoth; from Shelomoth’s descendants, Jahath.
v23: The sons were Jeriah, Amariah second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth.
v24: From Uzziel’s descendants, Micah; from Micah’s descendants, Shamir.
v25: Micah’s brother was Isshiah; from Isshiah’s descendants, Zechariah.
</p>

<p>
v26: Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi. Jaaziah’s descendant was Beno.
v27: Merari’s descendants through Jaaziah were Beno, Shoham, Zaccur, and Ibri.
v28: From Mahli, Eleazar, who had no sons.
v29: From Kish: Jerahmeel, a descendant of Kish.
v30: Mushi’s sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the Levites’ descendants by their ancestral houses.
</p>

<p>
v31: They too cast lots, just as their relatives, Aaron’s descendants, had done, before King David, Zadok, Ahimelech, and the heads of the priestly and Levitical ancestral houses. The head of the senior house stood on equal terms with his younger relative.
</p>

## Notes

v06: The Hebrew repeats “selected” in the Ithamar clause. The precise drawing procedure is uncertain; a two-to-one alternating mechanism is not added.

v23: The opening is incomplete. The parallel at 23:19 identifies this group as Hebron’s sons, with Jeriah first; the draft does not make Jeriah the father of the other three.

v26–27: Beno can be read as a name or as “his son.” The relationship of Jaaziah to the surrounding Merarite list is unclear.

## Vocabulary

v05: Lot
An object or procedure used to assign a choice without an individual selecting the outcome.

v10: Abijah
The eighth priestly division; the same division name appears in Luke 1:5.
