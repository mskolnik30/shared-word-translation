## A house for David

<p>
v01: After David had settled into his house, he said to the prophet Nathan, “Here I am living in a cedar house, while the ark of the LORD’s covenant is under tent curtains.”
v02: Nathan told David, “Do whatever is in your heart, for God is with you.”
v03: But that night the word of God came to Nathan:
v04: “Go and tell my servant David: This is what the LORD says: You are not the one to build me a house to live in.
v05: I have not lived in a house from the day I brought Israel up until today. I have moved from tent to tent, from one dwelling place to another.
v06: Wherever I traveled with all Israel, did I ever ask any of Israel’s judges, whom I commanded to shepherd my people, ‘Why have you not built me a cedar house?’
</p>

<p>
v07: Now tell my servant David: This is what the LORD of Armies says: I took you from the pasture, from following the flock, to be ruler over my people Israel.
v08: I have been with you wherever you went and have cut off all your enemies before you. I will make your name like the names of the great ones of the earth.
v09: I will provide a place for my people Israel and plant them there. They will live in their own place and be disturbed no more. Violent people will no longer wear them down as they did before,
v10: from the time I appointed judges over my people Israel. I will subdue all your enemies. I tell you: the LORD will build you a house.
</p>

<p>
v11: When your days are complete and you go to be with your ancestors, I will raise up your offspring after you, one of your sons, and establish his kingdom.
v12: He will build me a house, and I will establish his throne forever.
v13: I will be a father to him, and he will be a son to me. I will not take my faithful love away from him as I took it from the one who was before you.
v14: I will set him in my house and my kingdom forever, and his throne will stand firm forever.”
v15: Nathan told David all these words and the whole vision.
</p>

## David’s prayer

<p>
v16: Then King David went in and sat before the LORD. He said, “Who am I, LORD God, and what is my house, that you have brought me this far?
v17: Yet this was a small thing in your eyes, God. You have spoken about your servant’s house far into the future. You have regarded me as a man of high standing, LORD God.
v18: What more can David say to you about the honor you have given your servant? You know your servant.
v19: LORD, for your servant’s sake and according to your own heart, you have done all this greatness and made all these great things known.
</p>

<p>
v20: LORD, there is no one like you. There is no God besides you, according to everything we have heard with our own ears.
v21: And who is like your people Israel, the one nation on earth whose God went out to redeem a people for himself? You made a name for yourself through great and fearsome deeds, driving out nations before your people whom you redeemed from Egypt.
v22: You made your people Israel your own people forever, and you, LORD, became their God.
</p>

<p>
v23: Now, LORD, let the word you have spoken about your servant and his house stand firm forever. Do as you have said.
v24: Let it stand firm, and let your name be great forever, so people will say, ‘The LORD of Armies, the God of Israel, is God over Israel!’ And let the house of David your servant stand firm before you.
v25: For you, my God, have revealed to your servant that you will build him a house. That is why your servant has found the courage to pray before you.
v26: Now, LORD, you are God, and you have promised this good thing to your servant.
v27: You have been pleased to bless your servant’s house, so that it may remain before you forever. For you, LORD, have blessed it, and it is blessed forever.”
</p>

## Notes

v04: Chronicles gives a direct statement that David will not build the house; the parallel in 2 Samuel 7:5 is framed as a question.

v10–14: House moves from a building to a lasting family or dynasty. Verse 14 says “my house” and “my kingdom,” emphasizing God’s claim.

v17: The final Hebrew phrase is difficult. “A man of high standing” is one reading; the wording may concern an elevated human order or station.

v25: “Revealed” renders the image of uncovering someone’s ear.

## Vocabulary

v07: LORD of Armies
A divine title presenting the LORD as commander of heavenly and earthly forces.

v13: Father and son
The relational language of the royal promise; its wording is retained without adding a later doctrinal interpretation.
