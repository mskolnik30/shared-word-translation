## Levites assigned to service

<p>
v01: When David was old and full of days, he made his son Solomon king over Israel.
v02: He gathered all Israel’s leaders, the priests, and the Levites.
v03: The Levites thirty years old and upward were counted. Their total, counting the men individually, was thirty-eight thousand.
v04: Of these, twenty-four thousand were to oversee the work of the LORD’s house, and six thousand were officials and judges.
v05: Four thousand were gatekeepers, and four thousand praised the LORD with the instruments David said, “I made for praise.”
v06: David organized them into divisions according to Levi’s sons: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.
</p>

## The Levitical families

<p>
v07: From the Gershonites came Ladan and Shimei.
v08: Ladan’s sons were Jehiel the leader, Zetham, and Joel—three in all.
v09: Shimei’s sons were Shelomith, Haziel, and Haran—three in all. These were the heads of Ladan’s ancestral houses.
v10: Shimei’s sons were Jahath, Zina, Jeush, and Beriah. These four were Shimei’s sons.
v11: Jahath was the leader, and Zizah was second. Jeush and Beriah did not have many sons, so they formed one ancestral house for their assignment.
</p>

<p>
v12: Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel—four in all.
v13: Amram’s sons were Aaron and Moses. Aaron and his sons were set apart forever to consecrate the most holy things, to burn offerings before the LORD, serve him, and bless in his name forever.
</p>

<p>
v14: As for Moses, the man of God, his sons were counted with the tribe of Levi.
v15: Moses’ sons were Gershom and Eliezer.
v16: Gershom’s descendant was Shebuel the leader.
v17: Eliezer’s descendant was Rehabiah the leader. Eliezer had no other sons, but Rehabiah’s descendants became very numerous.
</p>

<p>
v18: Izhar’s descendant was Shelomith the leader.
v19: Hebron’s sons were Jeriah the leader, Amariah second, Jahaziel third, and Jekameam fourth.
v20: Uzziel’s sons were Micah the leader and Isshiah second.
</p>

<p>
v21: Merari’s sons were Mahli and Mushi. Mahli’s sons were Eleazar and Kish.
v22: Eleazar died without sons; he had only daughters. Their relatives, the sons of Kish, married them.
v23: Mushi’s sons were Mahli, Eder, and Jeremoth—three in all.
</p>

## Duties in the LORD’s house

<p>
v24: These were Levi’s descendants by their ancestral houses, the family heads as registered, counted individually by name. They carried out the work of serving the LORD’s house from twenty years old and upward.
v25: For David had said, “The LORD, the God of Israel, has given his people rest and will dwell in Jerusalem forever.
v26: The Levites no longer need to carry the tabernacle or any of the utensils used in its service.”
v27: Under David’s final instructions, the Levites were counted from twenty years old and upward.
</p>

<p>
v28: Their station was alongside Aaron’s descendants in the service of the LORD’s house: caring for the courtyards and rooms, purifying all holy things, and performing the work of serving God’s house.
v29: They were responsible for the arranged bread, the fine flour for grain offerings, the unleavened wafers, the griddle offerings, the mixed offerings, and every quantity and measure.
v30: They were to stand every morning to thank and praise the LORD, and likewise every evening,
v31: and whenever burnt offerings were presented to the LORD on Sabbaths, new moons, and appointed festivals, in the prescribed number, serving regularly before the LORD.
v32: They were to keep charge of the tent of meeting, the holy place, and the duties of their relatives, Aaron’s descendants, in the service of the LORD’s house.
</p>

## Notes

v03,24,27: The chapter first counts men thirty and older, then gives twenty and older under David’s final instructions. Both thresholds are preserved.

v09–11: Shelomith follows the read form in verse 9. Zina in verse 10 and Zizah in verse 11 are both retained.

v13: The Hebrew may mean Aaron was consecrated as most holy, or that he was set apart to consecrate the most holy things. The draft takes the latter reading.

v29: The term for the “mixed offerings” refers to a prepared grain dish, but the precise cooking method is uncertain.

## Vocabulary

v05: Gatekeeper
A person responsible for controlled access and guarding sanctuary property.

v31: New moon
The beginning of a lunar month, marked by prescribed offerings.
