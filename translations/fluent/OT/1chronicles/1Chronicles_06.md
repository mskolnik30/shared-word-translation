## The priestly line

<p>
v01: Levi’s sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.
v02: Kohath’s sons were Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel.
v03: Amram’s children were Aaron, Moses, and Miriam. Aaron’s sons were Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
v04: Eleazar fathered Phinehas, and Phinehas fathered Abishua.
v05: Abishua fathered Bukki, and Bukki fathered Uzzi.
v06: Uzzi fathered Zerahiah, and Zerahiah fathered Meraioth.
v07: Meraioth fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub.
v08: Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Ahimaaz.
v09: Ahimaaz fathered Azariah, and Azariah fathered Johanan.
v10: Johanan fathered Azariah, who served as priest in the house Solomon built in Jerusalem.
v11: Azariah fathered Amariah, and Amariah fathered Ahitub.
v12: Ahitub fathered Zadok, and Zadok fathered Shallum.
v13: Shallum fathered Hilkiah, and Hilkiah fathered Azariah.
v14: Azariah fathered Seraiah, and Seraiah fathered Jehozadak.
v15: Jehozadak went into exile when the LORD deported Judah and Jerusalem through Nebuchadnezzar.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Public 6:1–15 corresponds to Hebrew 5:27–41; public 6:16–81 to Hebrew 6:1–66.

v22: Amminadab is retained here; verse 38 names Izhar in the corresponding ancestry. These lists have not been silently harmonized.

v26: The Hebrew repeats Elkanah and has a written/read difference between “his son” and “sons of.” The difficult sequence is retained.

v28: The Hebrew reads “the firstborn, Vashni, and Abijah.” Many understand a damaged phrase meaning “the firstborn [Joel], and the second, Abijah”; Joel is named in verse 33. The draft retains Vashni and leaves the textual question open.

v54–81: The list preserves its own town names and totals. Some names and the number of towns actually listed differ from Joshua 21; thirteen in verse 60 is not silently reduced to the eleven named here.

v57,67: The Hebrew uses the plural “cities of refuge” before each list. Not every following town is identified as a refuge city elsewhere.

v61: The Hebrew wording about tribal families and half of Manasseh is compressed and difficult. Additional tribes have not been inserted.

## Vocabulary

v49: Atonement
Ritual action addressing sin and impurity so that the relationship with God may be restored.

v55: Pasturelands
Open land attached to a town for its animals and other needs.
