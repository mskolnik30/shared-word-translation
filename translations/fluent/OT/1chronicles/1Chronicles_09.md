## Jerusalem’s inhabitants

<p>
v01: All Israel was enrolled in genealogies, recorded in the Book of the Kings of Israel. Judah was deported to Babylon for its unfaithfulness.
v02: The first to settle in their holdings in their towns were Israelites, priests, Levites, and temple servants.
v03: People from Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and Manasseh settled in Jerusalem.
v04: Uthai son of Ammihud, son of Omri, son of Imri, son of Bani, belonged to the descendants of Perez son of Judah.
v05: From the Shilonites came Asaiah the firstborn and his sons.
v06: From Zerah’s descendants came Jeuel. Their relatives numbered six hundred ninety.
</p>

<p>
v07: From Benjamin came Sallu son of Meshullam, son of Hodaviah, son of Hassenuah;
v08: Ibneiah son of Jeroham; Elah son of Uzzi, son of Michri; and Meshullam son of Shephatiah, son of Reuel, son of Ibnijah.
v09: Their relatives, listed by their generations, numbered nine hundred fifty-six. All these men headed their ancestral households.
</p>

## Priests and Levites

<p>
v10: From the priests came Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, and Jachin;
v11: Azariah son of Hilkiah, son of Meshullam, son of Zadok, son of Meraioth, son of Ahitub, a leader of God’s house;
v12: Adaiah son of Jeroham, son of Pashhur, son of Malchijah; and Maasai son of Adiel, son of Jahzerah, son of Meshullam, son of Meshillemith, son of Immer.
v13: Their relatives, heads of ancestral households, numbered 1,760, capable men for the work of serving in God’s house.
</p>

<p>
v14: From the Levites came Shemaiah son of Hasshub, son of Azrikam, son of Hashabiah, a descendant of Merari;
v15: Bakbakkar, Heresh, Galal, and Mattaniah son of Mica, son of Zichri, son of Asaph;
v16: Obadiah son of Shemaiah, son of Galal, son of Jeduthun; and Berechiah son of Asa, son of Elkanah, who lived in the Netophathite villages.
</p>

## Gatekeepers and temple service

<p>
v17: The gatekeepers were Shallum, Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their relatives. Shallum was the head.
v18: They still served at the King’s Gate on the east, the gatekeepers for the Levites’ camps.
v19: Shallum son of Kore, son of Ebiasaph, son of Korah, and his fellow Korahites from his ancestral household were responsible for guarding the tent’s thresholds. Their ancestors had guarded the entrance to the LORD’s camp.
v20: Phinehas son of Eleazar had been their leader in earlier times. The LORD was with him.
v21: Zechariah son of Meshelemiah guarded the entrance to the tent of meeting.
</p>

<p>
v22: In all, two hundred twelve were chosen to guard the thresholds. They were registered by genealogy in their villages. David and Samuel the seer had appointed them to these positions of trust.
v23: They and their sons took turns guarding the gates of the LORD’s house, the tent sanctuary.
v24: The gatekeepers served on all four sides: east, west, north, and south.
v25: Their relatives in the villages came at appointed times to work with them for seven days.
v26: The four chief gatekeepers, who were Levites, held positions of trust. They oversaw the chambers and treasuries of God’s house.
v27: They spent the night around God’s house, because they were responsible for guarding it and opening it every morning.
</p>

<p>
v28: Some were responsible for the articles used in worship. They counted them when bringing them in and when taking them out.
v29: Others were appointed over the furnishings and all the sacred utensils, as well as the fine flour, wine, oil, frankincense, and spices.
</p>

<p>
v30: Some of the priests blended the spices.
v31: Mattithiah, a Levite and the firstborn of Shallum the Korahite, was entrusted with preparing the griddle cakes.
v32: Some of their Kohathite relatives were responsible for preparing the arranged bread every Sabbath.
</p>

<p>
v33: The singers, heads of Levitical households, stayed in the chambers and were free from other duties, because their work occupied them day and night.
v34: These were the heads of Levitical ancestral households, leaders listed by their generations. They lived in Jerusalem.
</p>

## Saul’s ancestry

<p>
v35: Jeiel, the father of Gibeon, lived in Gibeon. His wife was named Maacah.
v36: His firstborn son was Abdon; then Zur, Kish, Baal, Ner, Nadab,
v37: Gedor, Ahio, Zechariah, and Mikloth.
v38: Mikloth fathered Shimeam. They too lived in Jerusalem with their relatives, opposite their relatives’ homes.
</p>

<p>
v39: Ner fathered Kish, and Kish fathered Saul. Saul fathered Jonathan, Malchi-shua, Abinadab, and Esh-baal.
v40: Jonathan’s son was Merib-baal, and Merib-baal fathered Micah.
v41: Micah’s sons were Pithon, Melech, and Tahrea.
v42: Ahaz fathered Jarah. Jarah fathered Alemeth, Azmaveth, and Zimri, and Zimri fathered Moza.
v43: Moza fathered Binea. His line continued through Rephaiah, then Eleasah, then Azel.
v44: Azel had six sons: Azrikam, Bocheru, Ishmael, Sheariah, Obadiah, and Hanan. These were Azel’s sons.
</p>

## Notes

v02: The settlement notice follows exile in verse 1 and is rendered in that setting.

v05: Shilonites is the Hebrew designation; it is often related to the descendants of Shelah, but the wording has not been replaced.

v23: The unusual expression “house of the tent” connects this gatekeeping description with the tent sanctuary.

v35–44: This repeated genealogy has its own forms and details, including Ner, Zechariah, Shimeam, Jarah, and Rephaiah. They have not been made identical to chapter 8.

## Vocabulary

v02: Temple servants
A group assigned to support sanctuary service, distinct here from priests and Levites.

v32: Arranged bread
Bread laid out before God and renewed each Sabbath; also called the bread of the Presence.
