## From Adam to Noah

<p>
v01: Adam, Seth, Enosh,
v02: Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,
v03: Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,
v04: Noah; Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
</p>

## The nations

<p>
v05: Japheth’s sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.
v06: Gomer’s sons were Ashkenaz, Diphath, and Togarmah.
v07: Javan’s sons were Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim.
</p>

<p>
v08: Ham’s sons were Cush, Egypt, Put, and Canaan.
v09: Cush’s sons were Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raama, and Sabteca. Raama’s sons were Sheba and Dedan.
v10: Cush fathered Nimrod, who was the first to become a mighty man on earth.
</p>

<p>
v11: Egypt fathered the Ludites, Anamites, Lehabites, Naphtuhites,
v12: Pathrusites, Casluhites, from whom the Philistines came, and Caphtorites.
</p>

<p>
v13: Canaan fathered Sidon his firstborn and Heth,
v14: the Jebusites, Amorites, Girgashites,
v15: Hivites, Arkites, Sinites,
v16: Arvadites, Zemarites, and Hamathites.
</p>

<p>
v17: Shem’s descendants were Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.
v18: Arpachshad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber.
v19: Eber had two sons. One was named Peleg, because in his days the earth was divided; his brother’s name was Joktan.
</p>

<p>
v20: Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,
v21: Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,
v22: Ebal, Abimael, Sheba,
v23: Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were Joktan’s sons.
</p>

## From Shem to Abraham

<p>
v24: Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah,
v25: Eber, Peleg, Reu,
v26: Serug, Nahor, Terah,
v27: Abram—that is, Abraham.
</p>

## Abraham’s family

<p>
v28: Abraham’s sons were Isaac and Ishmael.
</p>

<p>
v29: These are their family lines: Ishmael’s firstborn was Nebaioth, followed by Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,
v30: Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema,
v31: Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These were Ishmael’s sons.
</p>

<p>
v32: Keturah, Abraham’s concubine, bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. Jokshan’s sons were Sheba and Dedan.
v33: Midian’s sons were Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were Keturah’s descendants.
</p>

<p>
v34: Abraham fathered Isaac. Isaac’s sons were Esau and Israel.
</p>

## Esau and Seir

<p>
v35: Esau’s sons were Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.
v36: Eliphaz’s descendants were Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek.
v37: Reuel’s sons were Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.
</p>

<p>
v38: Seir’s sons were Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan.
v39: Lotan’s sons were Hori and Homam. Lotan’s sister was Timna.
v40: Shobal’s sons were Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. Zibeon’s sons were Aiah and Anah.
v41: Anah’s son was Dishon. Dishon’s sons were Hamran, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.
v42: Ezer’s sons were Bilhan, Zaavan, and Jaakan. Dishon’s sons were Uz and Aran.
</p>

## Edom’s kings and chiefs

<p>
v43: These were the kings who reigned in Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites: Bela son of Beor, whose city was named Dinhabah.
v44: Bela died, and Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah became king in his place.
v45: Jobab died, and Husham from the land of the Temanites became king in his place.
v46: Husham died, and Hadad son of Bedad became king in his place. He defeated Midian in the territory of Moab. His city was named Avith.
v47: Hadad died, and Samlah from Masrekah became king in his place.
v48: Samlah died, and Shaul from Rehoboth on the River became king in his place.
v49: Shaul died, and Baal-hanan son of Achbor became king in his place.
v50: Baal-hanan died, and Hadad became king in his place. His city was named Pai. His wife’s name was Mehetabel, daughter of Matred, daughter of Me-zahab.
</p>

<p>
v51: Hadad died. These were Edom’s chiefs: Chief Timna, Chief Alvah, Chief Jetheth,
v52: Chief Oholibamah, Chief Elah, Chief Pinon,
v53: Chief Kenaz, Chief Teman, Chief Mibzar,
v54: Chief Magdiel, and Chief Iram. These were Edom’s chiefs.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The opening names trace successive generations. At Noah the list branches into Shem, Ham, and Japheth. Later “sons” can refer to peoples or more distant descendants.

v06: Chronicles has Diphath, Rodanim, Meshech, and Ebal in places where Genesis’ family table has some different forms. The local names are preserved.

v19: Peleg’s name is connected with division. The verse does not specify a geological event or explain exactly how the earth was divided.

v36: Timna appears in this compressed list; Genesis 36:12 identifies her as Eliphaz’s concubine and Amalek’s mother. Chronicles’ wording is not expanded to reproduce that account.

v42: The Hebrew here has Dishon, though the corresponding branch is called Dishan in verse 38 and Genesis 36:28.

## Vocabulary

v11: Egypt
The Hebrew name Mizraim is also the usual name for Egypt.

v32: Concubine
A woman in a recognized household sexual relationship with status distinct from a principal wife.

v51: Chief
A leader of an Edomite clan or territorial group.
