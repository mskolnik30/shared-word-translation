## Materials for the temple

<p>
v01: David said, “Here is the house of the LORD God, and here is the altar of burnt offering for Israel.”
v02: David ordered the resident foreigners in the land of Israel to be gathered. He appointed stonecutters to prepare dressed stones for building God’s house.
v03: David prepared large quantities of iron for the nails of the gate doors and for the fittings, and more bronze than could be weighed.
v04: He also prepared cedar timber beyond counting, for the Sidonians and Tyrians brought him large quantities of cedar.
</p>

<p>
v05: David said, “My son Solomon is young and inexperienced, and the house to be built for the LORD must be exceedingly great, famous and splendid throughout all lands. I will make preparations for him.” So David prepared materials in abundance before his death.
</p>

## David’s charge to Solomon

<p>
v06: He summoned his son Solomon and charged him to build a house for the LORD, the God of Israel.
v07: David said to Solomon, “My son, I had it in my heart to build a house for the name of the LORD my God.
v08: But the word of the LORD came to me: ‘You have shed much blood and fought great wars. You must not build a house for my name, because you have shed so much blood on the earth before me.
v09: Look, a son will be born to you. He will be a man of rest, and I will give him rest from all his enemies around him. His name will be Solomon, and I will give Israel peace and quiet in his days.
v10: He will build a house for my name. He will be a son to me, and I will be a father to him. I will establish the throne of his kingdom over Israel forever.’
</p>

<p>
v11: Now, my son, may the LORD be with you. May you succeed and build the house of the LORD your God, as he has said about you.
v12: Only may the LORD give you insight and understanding when he places you over Israel, so that you keep the instruction of the LORD your God.
v13: Then you will succeed, if you take care to carry out the statutes and judgments the LORD commanded Moses for Israel. Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or discouraged.
</p>

<p>
v14: Through hardship I have prepared for the LORD’s house a hundred thousand talents of gold and a million talents of silver, with bronze and iron too abundant to weigh. I have also prepared timber and stone, and you are to add to them.
v15: You have many workers: stonecutters, workers in stone and wood, and people skilled in every kind of craft.
v16: The gold, silver, bronze, and iron are beyond counting. Get up and act, and may the LORD be with you!”
</p>

## A charge to Israel’s leaders

<p>
v17: David commanded all Israel’s leaders to help his son Solomon.
v18: “Isn’t the LORD your God with you?” he said. “Hasn’t he given you rest on every side? He has given the land’s inhabitants into my hand, and the land has been subdued before the LORD and his people.
v19: Now set your heart and your whole being on seeking the LORD your God. Get up and build the sanctuary of the LORD God, so that the ark of the LORD’s covenant and God’s holy utensils may be brought into the house built for the LORD’s name.”
</p>

## Notes

v02: Resident foreigners are gathered and assigned work. The account does not describe this labor as a voluntary gift.

v09: Solomon, Hebrew Shelomoh, echoes shalom, “peace.” The promise also repeats the word rest.

v14: The Hebrew gives a hundred thousand talents of gold and a million talents of silver. These very large quantities are retained without reduction.

## Vocabulary

v08: For my name
The house is dedicated to the LORD’s identity and presence.

v19: Sanctuary
A place set apart as holy for worship and service.
