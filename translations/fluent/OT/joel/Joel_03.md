## The nations brought to judgment

<p>
v01: “In those days and at that time,
when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem,
v02: I will gather all the nations
and bring them down to the Valley of Jehoshaphat.
There I will enter into judgment with them
for my people, my inheritance Israel,
whom they scattered among the nations.
They divided up my land.
v03: They cast lots for my people,
traded a boy for a prostitute,
and sold a girl for wine and drank it.
v04: And what have you against me, Tyre and Sidon
and all the regions of Philistia?
Are you repaying me for something?
If you are paying me back,
I will swiftly bring your repayment down on your own heads.
v05: You took my silver and gold
and carried my finest treasures into your temples.
v06: You sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks
to send them far from their own territory.
v07: Look, I will rouse them from the place where you sold them
and bring your repayment down on your own heads.
v08: I will sell your sons and daughters
into the hands of the people of Judah,
and they will sell them to the Sabeans, a distant nation.”
For the LORD has spoken.
</p>

## The summons to battle

<p>
v09: Proclaim this among the nations:
“Consecrate a war! Rouse the warriors!
Let all the soldiers approach and advance.
v10: Beat your plowshares into swords
and your pruning hooks into spears.
Let the weak say, ‘I am a warrior!’
v11: Hurry and come, all you surrounding nations;
gather there!”
Bring down your warriors, LORD!
v12: “Let the nations rouse themselves
and come up to the Valley of Jehoshaphat,
for there I will sit to judge
all the surrounding nations.
v13: Swing the sickle, for the harvest is ripe.
Come, tread, for the winepress is full;
the vats overflow,
for their evil is great.”
v14: Multitudes, multitudes in the valley of decision!
The day of the LORD is near
in the valley of decision.
v15: Sun and moon grow dark;
the stars withdraw their light.
</p>

## A refuge in Zion

<p>
v16: The LORD roars from Zion
and raises his voice from Jerusalem;
heaven and earth shake.
But the LORD is a refuge for his people,
a stronghold for the people of Israel.
v17: “You will know that I am the LORD your God,
dwelling in Zion, my holy mountain.
Jerusalem will be holy,
and strangers will no longer pass through her.
v18: On that day the mountains will drip sweet wine,
the hills will flow with milk,
and all Judah’s streambeds will flow with water.
A spring will flow from the house of the LORD
and water the Valley of Acacias.
v19: Egypt will become a desolation,
and Edom a desolate wilderness,
for violence against the people of Judah,
whose innocent blood they shed in their land.
v20: But Judah will be inhabited forever,
and Jerusalem through every generation.
v21: I will clear their bloodguilt that I have not cleared.”
The LORD dwells in Zion.
</p>

## Notes

v02,14: Jehoshaphat means “the LORD judges.” The valley of decision is the setting of divine judgment in this passage; the phrase does not itself describe people choosing a response at a meeting.

v03,08: The trafficking of boys and girls and the threatened retaliatory sale of sons and daughters are explicit. The translation preserves both without endorsing enslavement.

v17-19,21: “Strangers” occurs in a setting of hostile incursions. The Valley of Acacias is not securely located here. The final “clear their blood” clause can be understood as clearing bloodguilt or vindicating blood by requital; its referent and force remain difficult.

## Vocabulary

v10: **Plowshares; pruning hooks**: Farming tools remade as weapons here; Micah 4:3 describes the reverse transformation.

v13: **Winepress**: A place for treading grapes, used here as an image of judgment on abundant evil.
