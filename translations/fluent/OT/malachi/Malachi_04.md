## The coming day

<p>
v01: “Look, the day is coming, blazing like an oven. All the arrogant and everyone who does evil will be stubble. The coming day will set them ablaze,” says the LORD of Armies, “leaving them neither root nor branch.
</p>

<p>
v02: But for you who fear my name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings. You will go out and leap like calves released from the stall.
v03: You will tread down the wicked; they will be ashes beneath the soles of your feet on the day I act,” says the LORD of Armies.
</p>

## Remember Moses; Elijah will come

<p>
v04: “Remember the instruction of Moses my servant, the statutes and judgments I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel.
</p>

<p>
v05: Look, I am sending you Elijah the prophet before the great and fearsome day of the LORD comes.
v06: He will turn fathers’ hearts toward their children and children’s hearts toward their fathers, so that I do not come and strike the land with a ban of destruction.”
</p>

## Notes

v01–06: These public verses are Hebrew 3:19–24. The original source records are bound individually despite the chapter-number difference.

v02,06: “Wings” belongs to the sun’s healing image. The final destruction term carries the force of a ban; the family reconciliation names fathers and children specifically.

## Vocabulary

v04: **Horeb**: The mountain name used when recalling Moses’s law.

v05: **Elijah**: The prophet whose promised coming returns as a question in Matthew 11 and 17.
