## The messenger and the refiner

<p>
v01: “Look, I am sending my messenger to clear a way before me. Suddenly the Lord you seek will come to his temple—the messenger of the covenant in whom you delight. Look, he is coming,” says the LORD of Armies.
</p>

<p>
v02: But who can endure the day of his coming? Who can stand when he appears? For he is like a refiner’s fire and launderers’ soap.
v03: He will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver. He will purify Levi’s descendants and refine them like gold and silver, so they may present offerings to the LORD in righteousness.
v04: Then Judah’s and Jerusalem’s offerings will please the LORD, as in days long ago, in former years.
</p>

<p>
v05: “I will draw near to you for judgment and be a swift witness against sorcerers, adulterers, those who swear falsely, those who cheat hired workers of wages, oppress widows and the fatherless, and turn aside the resident foreigner, and who do not fear me,” says the LORD of Armies.
</p>

## Return to me

<p>
v06: “For I, the LORD, have not changed, and you, Jacob’s descendants, have not come to an end.
v07: Ever since your ancestors’ days you have turned away from my statutes and failed to keep them. Return to me, and I will return to you,” says the LORD of Armies. “But you ask, ‘How should we return?’
</p>

<p>
v08: Will a human rob God? Yet you are robbing me. You ask, ‘How have we robbed you?’ In tithes and contributions.
v09: You are under a curse, yet you rob me—the whole nation of you.
</p>

<p>
v10: Bring the whole tithe into the storehouse, so there may be food in my house. Test me in this,” says the LORD of Armies, “and see whether I will not open heaven’s windows for you and pour out blessing beyond measure.
v11: I will rebuke the devourer for you, so it will not destroy the ground’s produce, and your vine in the field will not lose its fruit,” says the LORD of Armies.
v12: “All the nations will call you fortunate, for you will be a land of delight,” says the LORD of Armies.
</p>

## Words against the LORD

<p>
v13: “Your words against me have been harsh,” says the LORD. “Yet you ask, ‘What have we said against you?’
v14: You have said, ‘Serving God is useless. What profit is there in keeping his charge or walking in mourning before the LORD of Armies?
v15: Now we call the arrogant fortunate. Those who do evil prosper; they even test God and escape.’”
</p>

## A book of remembrance

<p>
v16: Then those who feared the LORD spoke with one another. The LORD paid attention and listened, and a book of remembrance was written before him for those who feared the LORD and honored his name.
v17: “They will be mine,” says the LORD of Armies, “my treasured possession on the day I act. I will spare them as a man spares his son who serves him.
v18: Then you will again see the difference between the righteous and the wicked, between the one who serves God and the one who does not.
</p>

## Notes

v01: “My messenger” echoes the name or title at 1:1. The Lord and messenger-of-the-covenant language is retained without assigning each phrase an extra identity.

v06: The relationship between the LORD’s not changing and Jacob’s sons not coming to an end is preserved.

## Vocabulary

v02: **Refiner’s fire; launderer’s soap**: Two images of cleansing that introduce the testing of the priests.

v16: **Book of remembrance**: A record image for those who fear the LORD and honor his name.
