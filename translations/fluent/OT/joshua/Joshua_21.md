## The Levites request towns

<p>
v01: The heads of the Levites’ ancestral houses approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of Israel’s tribal houses.
v02: At Shiloh in Canaan they said, “The LORD commanded through Moses that we receive towns to live in, with pasturelands for our livestock.”
v03: So, at the LORD’s command, the Israelites gave the Levites the following towns and pasturelands out of their own inheritances.
</p>

## The lots for Levi’s clans

<p>
v04: The lot came out for the Kohathite clans. The descendants of Aaron the priest, who were among the Levites, received thirteen towns by lot from Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin.
v05: The remaining descendants of Kohath received ten towns by lot from the clans of Ephraim, Dan, and half the tribe of Manasseh.
v06: Gershon’s descendants received thirteen towns by lot from the clans of Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and half the tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.
v07: Merari’s descendants, clan by clan, received twelve towns from Reuben, Gad, and Zebulun.
v08: The Israelites gave these towns and their pasturelands to the Levites by lot, as the LORD had commanded through Moses.
</p>

## Towns for Aaron’s descendants

<p>
v09: From Judah and Simeon they gave the towns named below.
v10: They went to Aaron’s descendants, from the Kohathite clans of Levi, because the first lot was theirs.
v11: They received Kiriath-arba, that is, Hebron, in Judah’s hill country, with the pasturelands around it. Arba was the father of Anak.
v12: But the town’s fields and villages were given to Caleb son of Jephunneh as his possession.
</p>

<p>
v13: To Aaron the priest’s descendants they gave Hebron, the city of refuge for a person who has killed someone, with its pasturelands; Libnah with its pasturelands;
v14: Jattir with its pasturelands; Eshtemoa with its pasturelands;
v15: Holon with its pasturelands; Debir with its pasturelands;
v16: Ain with its pasturelands; Juttah with its pasturelands; and Beth-shemesh with its pasturelands—nine towns from these two tribes.
</p>

<p>
v17: From Benjamin they received Gibeon with its pasturelands; Geba with its pasturelands;
v18: Anathoth with its pasturelands; and Almon with its pasturelands—four towns.
v19: The priestly descendants of Aaron received thirteen towns in all, with their pasturelands.
</p>

## Towns for the remaining Kohathites

<p>
v20: The clans of the remaining Kohathite Levites received towns by lot from Ephraim.
v21: They received Shechem, the city of refuge for a person who has killed someone, with its pasturelands in Ephraim’s hill country; Gezer with its pasturelands;
v22: Kibzaim with its pasturelands; and Beth-horon with its pasturelands—four towns.
</p>

<p>
v23: From Dan they received Elteke with its pasturelands; Gibbethon with its pasturelands;
v24: Aijalon with its pasturelands; and Gath-rimmon with its pasturelands—four towns.
</p>

<p>
v25: From half the tribe of Manasseh they received Taanach with its pasturelands; and Gath-rimmon with its pasturelands—two towns.
v26: The clans of Kohath’s remaining descendants received ten towns in all, with their pasturelands.
</p>

## Towns for the Gershonites

<p>
v27: The Gershonite clans of Levi received from half the tribe of Manasseh: Golan in Bashan, the city of refuge for a person who has killed someone, with its pasturelands; and Beeshterah with its pasturelands—two towns.
</p>

<p>
v28: From Issachar they received Kishion with its pasturelands; Daberath with its pasturelands;
v29: Jarmuth with its pasturelands; and En-gannim with its pasturelands—four towns.
</p>

<p>
v30: From Asher they received Mishal with its pasturelands; Abdon with its pasturelands;
v31: Helkath with its pasturelands; and Rehob with its pasturelands—four towns.
</p>

<p>
v32: From Naphtali they received Kedesh in Galilee, the city of refuge for a person who has killed someone, with its pasturelands; Hammoth-dor with its pasturelands; and Kartan with its pasturelands—three towns.
v33: The Gershonite clans received thirteen towns in all, with their pasturelands.
</p>

## Towns for the Merarites

<p>
v34: The clans of Merari’s descendants, the remaining Levites, received from Zebulun: Jokneam with its pasturelands; Kartah with its pasturelands;
v35: Dimnah with its pasturelands; and Nahalal with its pasturelands—four towns.
</p>

<p>
v36: From Reuben they received Bezer with its pasturelands; Jahaz with its pasturelands;
v37: Kedemoth with its pasturelands; and Mephaath with its pasturelands—four towns.
</p>

<p>
v38: From Gad they received Ramoth in Gilead, the city of refuge for a person who has killed someone, with its pasturelands; Mahanaim with its pasturelands;
v39: Heshbon with its pasturelands; and Jazer with its pasturelands—four towns in all.
v40: The clans of Merari’s descendants, the remaining Levitical clans, received twelve towns by lot in all.
</p>

## Forty-eight towns

<p>
v41: All the Levites’ towns within the Israelites’ possessions numbered forty-eight, with their pasturelands.
v42: Every one of these towns had pasturelands around it. This was true of all these towns.
</p>

## The promised land and rest

<p>
v43: The LORD gave Israel all the land he had sworn to give their ancestors. They took possession of it and settled there.
v44: The LORD gave them rest on every side, just as he had sworn to their ancestors. Not one of their enemies stood against them; the LORD gave all their enemies into their hand.
v45: Not one word failed of all the good the LORD had spoken to Israel’s household. It all came to pass.
</p>

## Notes

v11–12: The grant of Hebron and pasturelands is distinguished from Caleb’s possession of the fields and villages. The text retains both recipients.

v25: Gath-rimmon is named here as well as in Dan’s allotment in verse 24. The repeated name is not replaced from a parallel list.

v36–37: These two verses occur in the pinned source used here. They are absent from some significant Hebrew manuscripts but present in others and in the Greek and Latin versions.

v41: The four group totals are thirteen, ten, thirteen, and twelve, totaling forty-eight towns.

v43–45: The comprehensive fulfillment summary is retained alongside the remaining-nations notices elsewhere, including Joshua 23:4–5. It is not rewritten to settle their relationship.

## Vocabulary

v02: מִגְרָשׁ (migrash)
Open land around a town, here rendered pasturelands. It is repeatedly included with the town grants.

v12: אֲחֻזָּה (achuzzah)
Possession or holding. Caleb’s grant is distinguished from the Levites’ use of the town.

v45: דָּבָר (davar)
Word, matter, or thing. Not one word fails; the same assurance returns in 23:14.
