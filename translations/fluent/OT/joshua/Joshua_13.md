## Land still to be possessed

<p>
v01: Joshua was old, advanced in years. The LORD told him, “You are old, advanced in years, and a great deal of land still remains to be possessed.
v02: This is the land that remains: all the districts of the Philistines and all the Geshurite territory,
v03: from the Shihor east of Egypt to the border of Ekron in the north, counted as Canaanite land; the territory of the five Philistine rulers in Gaza, Ashdod, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron; and the Avvim
v04: in the south; all the Canaanite land, and Mearah, which belongs to the Sidonians, as far as Aphek, as far as the Amorite border;
v05: the land of the Gebalites; and all Lebanon to the east, from Baal-gad below Mount Hermon to Lebo-hamath.
v06: I myself will drive out before the Israelites all the people of the hill country from Lebanon to Misrephoth-maim, all the Sidonians. Only assign the land to Israel as an inheritance, as I have commanded you.
v07: Now divide this land as an inheritance among the nine tribes and the half-tribe of Manasseh.”
</p>

## The eastern inheritances

<p>
v08: With the other half of Manasseh, the Reubenites and Gadites had received their inheritance east of the Jordan, the land Moses, the LORD’s servant, had given them.
v09: It extended from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley, including the town in the middle of the valley, and all the plateau from Medeba to Dibon,
v10: and all the towns of Sihon king of the Amorites, who had ruled in Heshbon, as far as the Ammonite border.
v11: It included Gilead, the territory of the Geshurites and Maacathites, all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan as far as Salecah—
v12: the whole kingdom of Og in Bashan, who had ruled in Ashtaroth and Edrei. He was one of the remaining Rephaim. Moses had defeated them and driven them out.
v13: But the Israelites did not drive out the Geshurites and Maacathites. Geshur and Maacath still live among Israel to this day.
</p>

<p>
v14: To the tribe of Levi alone he gave no inheritance. The offerings made by fire to the LORD, the God of Israel, are their inheritance, as he had promised them.
</p>

## Reuben’s inheritance

<p>
v15: Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of Reuben, clan by clan.
v16: Their territory extended from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley, including the town in the middle of the valley and the whole plateau around Medeba.
v17: It included Heshbon and all its towns on the plateau: Dibon, Bamoth-baal, Beth-baal-meon,
v18: Jahaz, Kedemoth, Mephaath,
v19: Kiriathaim, Sibmah, Zereth-shahar on the hill in the valley,
v20: Beth-peor, the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jeshimoth—
v21: all the towns of the plateau and the entire kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites, who had ruled in Heshbon. Moses defeated him and the Midianite leaders Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba, Sihon’s princes who lived in the land.
v22: Among those they killed with the sword, the Israelites also killed Balaam son of Beor, the diviner.
v23: The Jordan formed Reuben’s boundary. This was the Reubenites’ inheritance, clan by clan, with its towns and villages.
</p>

## Gad’s inheritance

<p>
v24: Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of Gad, to the Gadites clan by clan.
v25: Their territory included Jazer, all the towns of Gilead, and half the land of the Ammonites as far as Aroer, opposite Rabbah.
v26: It stretched from Heshbon to Ramath-mizpeh and Betonim, and from Mahanaim to the territory of Lidbir.
v27: In the valley it included Beth-haram, Beth-nimrah, Succoth, and Zaphon, the rest of the kingdom of Sihon king of Heshbon. The Jordan formed its boundary as far as the edge of the Sea of Chinnereth, on the Jordan’s eastern side.
v28: This was the Gadites’ inheritance, clan by clan, with its towns and villages.
</p>

## Half of Manasseh’s inheritance

<p>
v29: Moses also gave an inheritance to the half-tribe of Manasseh; it belonged to that half-tribe’s clans.
v30: Their territory began at Mahanaim and included all Bashan, the whole kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all the settlements of Jair in Bashan—sixty towns.
v31: Half of Gilead, together with Ashtaroth and Edrei, the royal towns of Og in Bashan, belonged to the descendants of Machir son of Manasseh—to half of Machir’s descendants, clan by clan.
</p>

<p>
v32: These were the inheritances Moses assigned on the plains of Moab, east of the Jordan opposite Jericho.
v33: But Moses gave no inheritance to the tribe of Levi. The LORD, the God of Israel, is their inheritance, as he had promised them.
</p>

## Notes

v03–04: “In the south” is attached here to the Avvim at the end of verse 3. It can also be taken with the following Canaanite territory.

v08: The Hebrew begins “with him.” The other half of Manasseh is supplied from the eastern allotment described in verses 29–31.

v14,33: Verse 14 calls the LORD’s offerings Levi’s inheritance; verse 33 calls the LORD himself their inheritance. Both descriptions remain.

v23,27: The compressed Hebrew expression is “the Jordan and boundary.” It is rendered here as the Jordan forming the boundary.

v26: Lidbir retains the form in the pinned Hebrew. Debir is another rendering of this difficult place-name.

## Vocabulary

v06: נַחֲלָה (nachalah)
Inheritance. A received, enduring share; allocation is distinguished here from land already possessed.

v09: מִישׁוֹר (mishor)
Level land or plateau. Here the upland tableland east of the Jordan.

v22: קֹסֵם (qosem)
Diviner. A person who practices divination; this is the designation given to Balaam here.
