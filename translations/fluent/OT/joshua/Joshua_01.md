## The LORD commissions Joshua

<p>
v01: After Moses, the LORD’s servant, died, the LORD said to Joshua son of Nun, Moses’ assistant:
v02: “My servant Moses is dead. Now get up and cross this Jordan, you and all these people, into the land I am giving them—the Israelites.
v03: I have given you every place the sole of your foot steps on, just as I promised Moses.
v04: Your territory will stretch from the wilderness and this Lebanon to the great river, the Euphrates—all the land of the Hittites—and to the Great Sea in the west.
v05: No one will be able to stand against you as long as you live. As I was with Moses, I will be with you. I will not let you go or abandon you.
v06: Be strong and courageous, for you will lead these people into possession of the land I swore to their ancestors to give them.
</p>

## Keep the instruction

<p>
v07: Only be strong and very courageous. Carefully carry out all the instruction my servant Moses commanded you. Do not turn from it to the right or the left, so that you may act wisely wherever you go.
v08: Keep this scroll of instruction on your lips. Recite it and reflect on it day and night, so that you carefully do everything written in it. Then you will make your way prosper; then you will act wisely.
v09: Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be terrified or discouraged, for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”
</p>

## Prepare to cross the Jordan

<p>
v10: Joshua gave the people’s officers these orders:
v11: “Go through the camp and tell the people, ‘Get your provisions ready. In three days you will cross this Jordan to take possession of the land the LORD your God is giving you to possess.’”
</p>

## The eastern tribes promise to help

<p>
v12: To the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh, Joshua said:
v13: “Remember what Moses, the LORD’s servant, commanded you: ‘The LORD your God is giving you rest and has given you this land.’
v14: Your wives, your little ones, and your livestock may stay in the land Moses gave you on this side of the Jordan. But all your mighty warriors must cross over armed ahead of your fellow Israelites and help them,
v15: until the LORD gives them rest as he has given you, and they too possess the land the LORD your God is giving them. Then you may return to your own land and possess it—the land Moses, the LORD’s servant, gave you east of the Jordan.”
</p>

<p>
v16: They answered Joshua, “We will do everything you have commanded us and go wherever you send us.
v17: We will obey you just as we obeyed Moses in everything. Only may the LORD your God be with you as he was with Moses!
v18: Anyone who rebels against your orders and refuses to obey whatever you command is to be put to death. Only be strong and courageous!”
</p>

## Notes

v03: “I have given” presents the gift as assured before the crossing. The promise addresses the people through Joshua.

v07–08: “Act wisely” can also mean succeed. The passage connects Joshua’s way with carrying out the instruction; “recite it and reflect on it” unfolds a Hebrew verb that can include murmuring or speaking as one meditates.

v13–15: The eastern tribes have received land, but their warriors must help the other tribes obtain rest before returning. See Deuteronomy 3:18–20.

## Vocabulary

v07: תּוֹרָה (torah)
Instruction or law. The teaching given through Moses; the same word returns at the public reading in 8:34–35.

v08: הָגָה (hagah)
Murmur, recite, or meditate. Here the scroll stays on Joshua’s lips as he attends to it day and night.

v13: נוּחַ (nuach)
Rest. Here the causative form means give rest; the land and the obligation to help others remain connected.
