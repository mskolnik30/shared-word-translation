## The people follow the ark

<p>
v01: Joshua rose early in the morning. He and all the Israelites left Shittim and came to the Jordan, where they spent the night before crossing.
v02: At the end of three days, the officers went through the camp
v03: and gave the people these orders: “When you see the ark of the covenant of the LORD your God being carried by the Levitical priests, leave your places and follow it.
v04: Keep a distance of about two thousand cubits between you and the ark. Do not go near it. Then you will know which way to go, for you have never traveled this way before.”
</p>

<p>
v05: Joshua told the people, “Consecrate yourselves, for tomorrow the LORD will do wonders among you.”
v06: Then he told the priests, “Lift the ark of the covenant and go ahead of the people.” They lifted it and went ahead of the people.
</p>

## The living God among Israel

<p>
v07: The LORD said to Joshua, “Today I will begin to make you great in the eyes of all Israel, so they will know that I am with you as I was with Moses.
v08: Give this command to the priests carrying the ark of the covenant: ‘When you reach the edge of the Jordan’s water, stand still in the Jordan.’”
</p>

<p>
v09: Joshua called to the Israelites, “Come here and listen to the words of the LORD your God.”
v10: “This is how you will know that the living God is among you,” he said, “and that he will certainly drive out before you the Canaanites, Hittites, Hivites, Perizzites, Girgashites, Amorites, and Jebusites.
v11: Look! The ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth is crossing the Jordan ahead of you.
v12: Now choose twelve men from Israel’s tribes, one from each tribe.
v13: When the soles of the priests’ feet rest in the Jordan’s water—the priests carrying the ark of the LORD, the Lord of all the earth—the water flowing from upstream will be cut off and stand in a single heap.”
</p>

## Israel crosses on dry ground

<p>
v14: The people left their tents to cross the Jordan, with the priests carrying the ark of the covenant ahead of them.
v15: The Jordan overflows all its banks throughout the harvest season. As the ark’s carriers reached the river and the priests’ feet dipped into the water’s edge,
v16: the water coming downstream stopped and rose in a single heap far away at Adam, the town beside Zarethan. The water flowing toward the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, was completely cut off. The people crossed opposite Jericho.
v17: The priests carrying the ark of the LORD’s covenant stood firmly on dry ground in the middle of the Jordan. All Israel crossed on dry ground until the whole nation had finished crossing the river.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The ark is the sacred chest associated with the LORD’s covenant. “Levitical priests” identifies the carriers as priests of Levi.

v04: Two thousand cubits is roughly nine hundred meters, depending on the cubit used. The Hebrew itself gives an approximate distance.

v16: The main text follows the written Hebrew “at Adam.” Its marginal reading tradition has “from Adam.” Both locate the stopped water in relation to Adam near Zarethan.

v16: The Sea of the Arabah and the Salt Sea name the same sea, now commonly called the Dead Sea.

## Vocabulary

v05: קָדַשׁ (qadash)
Be holy or consecrate. Here the people are to prepare themselves as set apart for the coming event.

v10: יָרַשׁ (yarash)
Possess or dispossess. In the causative form here, drive out; all seven named peoples remain the objects.

v13: נֵד (ned)
Heap. The stopped river water is pictured as standing in a single heap.
