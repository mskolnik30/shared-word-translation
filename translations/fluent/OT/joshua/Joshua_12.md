## Kings east of the Jordan

<p>
v01: These are the kings the Israelites defeated east of the Jordan, toward the sunrise, taking possession of their lands from the Arnon Valley to Mount Hermon, including the whole eastern Arabah:
v02: Sihon king of the Amorites, who ruled in Heshbon. His rule extended from Aroer on the edge of the Arnon Valley, including the middle of the valley and half of Gilead as far as the Jabbok River, the Ammonite border.
v03: It also covered the eastern Arabah as far as the Sea of Chinneroth, and southward along the east side of the Sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, toward Beth-jeshimoth and below the slopes of Pisgah.
</p>

<p>
v04: There was also the territory of Og king of Bashan, one of the remaining Rephaim, who ruled in Ashtaroth and Edrei.
v05: He ruled Mount Hermon, Salecah, and all Bashan to the border of the Geshurites and Maacathites, and half of Gilead to the border of Sihon king of Heshbon.
</p>

<p>
v06: Moses, the LORD’s servant, and the Israelites defeated them. Moses, the LORD’s servant, gave their land as a possession to the Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh.
</p>

## Kings west of the Jordan

<p>
v07: These are the kings Joshua and the Israelites defeated west of the Jordan, from Baal-gad in the Lebanon Valley to Mount Halak, which rises toward Seir. Joshua gave their land as a possession to Israel’s tribes, each with its allotted share:
v08: the hill country, lowlands, Arabah, slopes, wilderness, and Negev—the land of the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites.
</p>

<p>
v09: The king of Jericho, one; the king of Ai beside Bethel, one;

v11: the king of Jarmuth, one; the king of Lachish, one;
v12: the king of Eglon, one; the king of Gezer, one;

## Notes

v02: The Hebrew names “the middle of the valley” without naming a town here. Joshua 13:9 explicitly mentions a town in that position; the wording of this verse is retained.

v03: The Sea of Chinneroth is also known as the Sea of Galilee. The Sea of the Arabah and the Salt Sea name the same southern sea.

v18: Lasharon can also be read with an initial preposition, “of Sharon.” The name form is retained here.

v23: Goiim means nations and may be a collective designation. The pinned Hebrew has Gilgal; the translation does not substitute a different region.

## Vocabulary

v01: יָרַשׁ (yarash)
Take possession or dispossess. The catalogue names lands taken from the defeated kings.

v04: רְפָאִים (Rephaim)
A people named in accounts of the earlier inhabitants. Og is identified as one of their remnant.

v09–24: אֶחָד (echad)
One. Repeated for each king, making a tally that ends at thirty-one.
