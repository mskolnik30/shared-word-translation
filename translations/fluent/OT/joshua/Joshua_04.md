## Twelve stones for remembrance

<p>
v01: When the whole nation had finished crossing the Jordan, the LORD said to Joshua:
v02: “Choose twelve men from the people, one from each tribe,
v03: and tell them, ‘Take twelve stones from here, from the middle of the Jordan where the priests’ feet stood firmly. Carry them across with you and set them down where you spend tonight.’”
</p>

<p>
v04: Joshua called the twelve men he had appointed from the Israelites, one from each tribe.
v05: He told them, “Go ahead of the ark of the LORD your God into the middle of the Jordan. Each of you is to lift a stone onto his shoulder, one for each tribe of Israel.
v06: These will be a sign among you. In days to come, when your children ask, ‘What do these stones mean to you?’
v07: tell them that the Jordan’s water was cut off before the ark of the LORD’s covenant. When the ark crossed the Jordan, its water was cut off. These stones will be a memorial for the Israelites forever.”
v08: The Israelites did what Joshua commanded. They took twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan, one for each tribe of Israel, as the LORD had told Joshua. They carried them to the camp and set them down there.
</p>

<p>
v09: Joshua also set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, where the priests carrying the ark of the covenant had stood. They are there to this day.
</p>

## The crossing is completed

<p>
v10: The priests carrying the ark remained in the middle of the Jordan until everything the LORD had ordered Joshua to tell the people was done, in keeping with all Moses had commanded Joshua. The people hurried across.
v11: Once all the people had finished crossing, the ark of the LORD and the priests crossed in full view of the people.
v12: The Reubenites, Gadites, and half-tribe of Manasseh crossed over armed ahead of the Israelites, just as Moses had told them.
v13: About forty thousand, equipped for battle, crossed before the LORD to the plains of Jericho for war.
v14: That day the LORD made Joshua great in the eyes of all Israel. They held him in awe all his life, just as they had held Moses in awe.
</p>

<p>
v15: The LORD said to Joshua:
v16: “Command the priests carrying the ark of the testimony to come up out of the Jordan.”
v17: So Joshua commanded the priests, “Come up out of the Jordan.”
v18: The priests carrying the ark of the LORD’s covenant came up from the middle of the Jordan. As soon as the soles of their feet reached dry ground, the Jordan’s water returned to its place and overflowed all its banks as before.
</p>

## Teaching the children at Gilgal

<p>
v19: The people came up from the Jordan on the tenth day of the first month and camped at Gilgal, on Jericho’s eastern edge.
v20: At Gilgal Joshua set up the twelve stones they had taken from the Jordan.
v21: He told the Israelites, “In days to come, when your children ask their fathers, ‘What are these stones?’
v22: tell your children, ‘Israel crossed this Jordan on dry ground.’
v23: For the LORD your God dried up the Jordan’s water before you until you crossed, just as he did to the Sea of Reeds, which he dried up before us until we crossed.
v24: He did this so that all the peoples of the earth would know how strong the LORD’s hand is, and so that you would always fear the LORD your God.”
</p>

## Notes

v08–09: Verse 8 describes stones carried out to the camp; verse 9 describes stones set up in the river. Both notices are retained.

v16: “Ark of the testimony” uses a different title from “ark of the covenant.” The testimony recalls the covenant tablets kept in the ark.

v23: The speech shifts from the Jordan crossing “before you” to the Sea of Reeds crossing “before us.” The change of pronoun is preserved.

## Vocabulary

v07: זִכָּרוֹן (zikkaron)
Memorial or reminder. The stones prompt a question and an account of the crossing.

v14: יָרֵא (yare)
Fear or hold in awe. Here Israel regards Joshua with the awe previously shown to Moses; in verse 24 the object is the LORD.

v24: יָד (yad)
Hand. The LORD’s strong hand pictures his power in the crossings.
