## Summer fruit and the end

<p>
v01: This is what the Lord GOD showed me: a basket of summer fruit.
v02: He asked, “What do you see, Amos?” I answered, “A basket of summer fruit.” Then the LORD said to me, “The end has come for my people Israel. I will no longer pass them by.
v03: The temple songs will turn to wailing on that day,” declares the Lord GOD. “So many bodies, thrown everywhere! Hush!”
</p>

## Crooked measures

<p>
v04: Hear this, you who pant after the needy
to make the poor of the land cease to exist,
v05: saying, “When will the new moon be over so we can sell grain,
and the Sabbath, so we can open the grain bins?
We will shrink the ephah, increase the shekel,
and cheat with crooked scales.
v06: We will buy the poor for silver
and the needy for a pair of sandals,
and even sell the grain sweepings.”
v07: The LORD has sworn by the pride of Jacob:
“I will never forget any of their deeds.
v08: Will not the land shake for this,
and everyone living in it mourn?
The whole land will rise like the Nile,
toss and sink like Egypt’s Nile.
v09: On that day,” declares the Lord GOD,
“I will make the sun go down at noon
and darken the earth in broad daylight.
v10: I will turn your festivals into mourning
and all your songs into funeral laments.
I will put sackcloth on every waist
and baldness on every head.
I will make it like mourning for an only child,
and its end like a bitter day.
</p>

## A famine of hearing

<p>
v11: Days are coming,” declares the Lord GOD,
“when I will send a famine through the land—
not hunger for bread or thirst for water,
but for hearing the words of the LORD.
v12: They will stagger from sea to sea,
from north to east.
They will roam in search of the word of the LORD
but will not find it.
v13: On that day beautiful young women
and young men will faint from thirst.
v14: Those who swear by Samaria’s Guilt,
who say, ‘As your god lives, Dan!’
and ‘As the way of Beer-sheba lives!’—
they will fall and never rise again.”
</p>

## Notes

v01-03: Summer fruit, qayits, sounds like “end,” qets. The building in verse 3 can be a temple or palace; the terse final “Hush!” is retained.

v07,14: “Pride of Jacob” is an ambiguous oath expression. “Samaria’s Guilt” and “the way of Beer-sheba” are difficult religious references; proposed deity names or pilgrimage interpretations should not be mistaken for explicit wording.

## Vocabulary

v05: **Ephah; shekel**: A measure of grain and a weight used for payment. Shrinking one and enlarging the other cheats the buyer twice.

v11: **Famine of hearing**: The announced scarcity is of hearing the LORD’s words, not merely of food or possession of texts.
