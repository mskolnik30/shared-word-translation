## Locusts and fire

<p>
v01: This is what the Lord GOD showed me: he was forming a swarm of locusts just as the late crop began to grow—the crop after the king’s mowing.
v02: When they had finished eating the land’s vegetation, I said, “Lord GOD, please forgive! How can Jacob stand? He is so small.”
v03: The LORD relented about this. “It will not happen,” the LORD said.
v04: This is what the Lord GOD showed me: the Lord GOD was calling for judgment by fire. It consumed the great deep and was consuming the land.
v05: I said, “Lord GOD, please stop! How can Jacob stand? He is so small.”
v06: The LORD relented about this. “This too will not happen,” the Lord GOD said.

<p>

## Amos and Amaziah

<p>
v10: Then Amaziah, the priest of Bethel, sent word to Jeroboam king of Israel: “Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of Israel. The land cannot bear all his words.
v11: For this is what Amos says: ‘Jeroboam will die by the sword, and Israel will certainly go into exile away from its land.’”
v12: Amaziah told Amos, “Seer, go! Flee to the land of Judah. Earn your bread there, and prophesy there.
v13: But do not prophesy at Bethel anymore. It is the king’s sanctuary, the kingdom’s temple.”
v14: Amos answered Amaziah, “I was no prophet, nor a prophet’s son. I was a herdsman and a tender of sycamore figs.
v15: But the LORD took me from following the flock, and the LORD told me, ‘Go, prophesy to my people Israel.’
v16: Now hear the word of the LORD. You say, ‘Do not prophesy against Israel; do not pour out words against the house of Isaac.’
v17: So this is what the LORD says: ‘Your wife will become a prostitute in the city; your sons and daughters will fall by the sword; your land will be divided by a measuring line. You will die on unclean ground, and Israel will certainly go into exile away from its land.’”
</p>

## Notes

v07-08: Anak is traditionally rendered “plumb line,” but its exact material and meaning are uncertain. The draft keeps that established image while marking the uncertainty.

v09-11,14: Amaziah reports a threat against Jeroboam himself, whereas verse 9 names his house. Amos’s verbless denial can be read “I am” or “I was” no prophet. “Prophet’s son” can refer to membership in a prophetic group rather than biological parentage.

v17: The oracle explicitly includes sexual humiliation, the deaths of sons and daughters, land seizure, and exile. It does not explain the wife’s circumstances or imply freely chosen prostitution.

## Vocabulary

v01: **King’s mowing**: A harvest taken before the later growth; the vision specifies this vulnerable agricultural moment.

v14: **Sycamore figs**: Fruit of the sycamore-fig tree, distinct from the tree commonly called sycamore in some English-speaking regions.
