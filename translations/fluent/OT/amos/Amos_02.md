## Against Moab

<p>
v01: This is what the LORD says:
“For three rebellions of Moab, and for four,
I will not turn it back:
he burned the bones of Edom’s king into lime.
v02: I will send fire into Moab,
and it will consume Kerioth’s citadels.
Moab will die amid uproar,
with battle cries and the sound of a ram’s horn.
v03: I will cut off the judge from her midst
and kill all her rulers with him,” says the LORD.
</p>

## Against Judah

<p>
v04: This is what the LORD says:
“For three rebellions of Judah, and for four,
I will not turn it back:
they rejected the LORD’s instruction
and did not keep his decrees.
Their lies led them astray,
the lies their ancestors followed.
v05: I will send fire into Judah,
and it will consume Jerusalem’s citadels.”
</p>

## Against Israel

<p>
v06: This is what the LORD says:
“For three rebellions of Israel, and for four,
I will not turn it back:
they sell the innocent for silver
and the needy for a pair of sandals.
v07: They crave even the dust of the ground on the heads of the poor
and twist the path of the afflicted.
A man and his father go to the same young woman,
profaning my holy name.
v08: They stretch out beside every altar
on clothes taken as pledges,
and in the house of their God
they drink wine taken in fines.
v09: Yet I destroyed the Amorite before them,
tall as cedars and strong as oaks.
I destroyed his fruit above
and his roots below.
v10: I brought you up from the land of Egypt
and led you through the wilderness for forty years
to take possession of the Amorite’s land.
v11: I raised up some of your sons as prophets
and some of your young men as Nazirites.
Is this not so, people of Israel?” declares the LORD.
v12: “But you made the Nazirites drink wine
and commanded the prophets, ‘Do not prophesy!’
v13: Now I will press down beneath you
as a cart loaded with sheaves presses down.
v14: The swift will have no escape;
the strong will find no strength;
the warrior will not save his life.
v15: The archer will not stand his ground;
the fleet-footed will not escape;
the horseman will not save his life.
v16: Even the bravest of warriors
will flee naked on that day,” declares the LORD.
</p>

## Notes

v07: The first line’s Hebrew is difficult and may evoke craving the dust on the poor or trampling them into it. The woman in the next line is called a young woman, not explicitly a prostitute; the shared access by father and son remains unsoftened.

v08,13: Pledged garments are collateral taken from debtors; fines supply the wine. The loaded-cart verb in verse 13 is rare and may describe pressing down or creaking under weight.

## Vocabulary

v11-12: **Nazirite**: A person under a special vow of dedication, which includes abstaining from wine.

v09: **Fruit and roots**: The tree image spans above and below to portray complete destruction.
