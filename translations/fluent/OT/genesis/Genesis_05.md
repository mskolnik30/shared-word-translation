## The Family of Adam

<p>
v01: This is the written account of Adam's family. When God created humanity, God made them in God's likeness.
v02: God created them male and female, blessed them, and named them Humanity on the day they were created.
</p>

<p>
v03: At 130 years old, Adam became the father of a son in his likeness, bearing his image. He named him Seth.
v04: After Seth's birth, Adam lived another 800 years and had other sons and daughters.
v05: Adam lived 930 years in all. Then he died.
</p>

## The Generations Continue

<p>
v06: Seth was 105 years old when he became the father of Enosh.
v07: After Enosh's birth, Seth lived another 807 years and had other sons and daughters.
v08: Seth lived 912 years in all. Then he died.
</p>

<p>
v09: Enosh was 90 years old when he became the father of Kenan.
v10: After Kenan's birth, Enosh lived another 815 years and had other sons and daughters.
v11: Enosh lived 905 years in all. Then he died.
</p>

<p>
v12: Kenan was 70 years old when he became the father of Mahalalel.
v13: After Mahalalel's birth, Kenan lived another 840 years and had other sons and daughters.
v14: Kenan lived 910 years in all. Then he died.
</p>

<p>
v15: Mahalalel was 65 years old when he became the father of Jared.
v16: After Jared's birth, Mahalalel lived another 830 years and had other sons and daughters.
v17: Mahalalel lived 895 years in all. Then he died.
</p>

<p>
v18: Jared was 162 years old when he became the father of Enoch.
v19: After Enoch's birth, Jared lived another 800 years and had other sons and daughters.
v20: Jared lived 962 years in all. Then he died.
</p>

## Enoch Walks with God

<p>
v21: Enoch was 65 years old when he became the father of Methuselah.
v22: After Methuselah's birth, Enoch walked with God for 300 years and had other sons and daughters.
v23: Enoch lived 365 years in all.
v24: Enoch walked with God. Then he was no longer there, because God took him.
</p>

## From Methuselah to Noah

<p>
v25: Methuselah was 187 years old when he became the father of Lamech.
v26: After Lamech's birth, Methuselah lived another 782 years and had other sons and daughters.
v27: Methuselah lived 969 years in all. Then he died.
</p>

<p>
v28: Lamech was 182 years old when he became the father of a son.
v29: He named him Noah, saying, “He will bring us relief from our work and the painful labor of our hands on the ground the LORD has cursed.”
v30: After Noah's birth, Lamech lived another 595 years and had other sons and daughters.
v31: Lamech lived 777 years in all. Then he died.
</p>

<p>
v32: Noah was 500 years old, and he became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
</p>

## Notes

v01–05: Adam can name a person or humanity. The image and likeness language recalls 1:26–27. The ages follow the Masoretic Text.

v06–24: The repeated pattern gives an age at fatherhood, later years and other children, a total lifespan, and death. Enoch's entry changes that pattern: he walks with God and is taken by God. The passage does not supply a destination or a mechanism.

v29–32: Noah's name is associated in the saying with relief from painful labor, though the name and the verb are not the same Hebrew root. Verse 32 introduces three sons after an age statement; it need not mean all three were born at the same time.

## Vocabulary

v01: סֵפֶר (sefer)
Written record or book.

v22: הִתְהַלֵּךְ (hithallekh)
Walk or walk about; here used in the phrase walked with God.

v29: נָחַם (nacham)
Comfort, bring relief, or regret, depending on form and context; the regret language returns in 6:6–7.
