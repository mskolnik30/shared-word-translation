## Returning to the Land

<p>
v01: Abram went up from Egypt to the Negev with his wife and everything he had. Lot went with him.
v02: Abram was very wealthy in livestock, silver, and gold.
</p>

<p>
v03: He traveled in stages from the Negev to Bethel, back to the place between Bethel and Ai where he had first pitched his tent,
v04: where he had built the altar before. There Abram called on the LORD's name.
</p>

## Abram and Lot Separate

<p>
v05: Lot, who traveled with Abram, also had flocks, herds, and tents.
v06: The land could not support them living together. Their possessions were so great that they could no longer stay together.
v07: Quarrels broke out between Abram's herders and Lot's herders. The Canaanites and Perizzites were also living in the land then.
</p>

<p>
v08: Abram said to Lot, “Please, let there be no quarrel between us or between our herders. We are family.
v09: The whole land is before you. Please separate from me. If you go left, I will go right; if you go right, I will go left.”
</p>

<p>
v10: Lot looked out over the whole Jordan plain. It was well watered all the way to Zoar, like the LORD's garden, like the land of Egypt. This was before the LORD destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah.
v11: Lot chose the whole Jordan plain for himself and traveled east. So the two separated.
</p>

<p>
v12: Abram settled in Canaan, while Lot settled among the towns of the plain and pitched his tents as far as Sodom.
v13: The people of Sodom were wicked and sinned greatly against the LORD.
</p>

## The Land and the Descendants

<p>
v14: After Lot had separated from Abram, the LORD said to Abram, “Look from where you are: north, south, east, and west.
v15: All the land you see I will give to you and your descendants forever.
</p>

<p>
v16: I will make your descendants like the dust of the earth. Only if someone could count the earth's dust could your descendants be counted.
v17: Get up and walk through the land, its length and its breadth, because I will give it to you.”
</p>

<p>
v18: Abram moved his tents and settled by the oaks of Mamre at Hebron. There he built an altar to the LORD.
</p>

## Notes

v08–11: “Family” renders brothers in its broader kinship sense: Abram is Lot’s uncle. The eastward phrase in v11 can also be read as movement from the east. Verse 9 uses left and right, not compass directions.

## Vocabulary

v08: אָח (ach)
Brother or relative; context determines the relationship.

v16: עָפָר (afar)
Dust; here an image of descendants too numerous to count.

v17: הִתְהַלֵּךְ (hithallekh)
Walk about; here through the land.
