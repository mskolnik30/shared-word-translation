## Judah’s Family

<p>
v01: About that time Judah left his brothers and settled near an Adullamite named Hirah.
v02: There Judah saw the daughter of a Canaanite man named Shua. He married her and slept with her.
v03: She became pregnant and gave birth to a son, and Judah named him Er.
v04: She became pregnant again and gave birth to a son, and she named him Onan.
v05: She gave birth to another son and named him Shelah. Judah was in Chezib when she gave birth to him.
</p>

## Tamar Is Widowed

<p>
v06: Judah chose a wife named Tamar for Er, his firstborn son.
v07: But Er, Judah’s firstborn, did what was evil in the LORD’s sight, so the LORD put him to death.
v08: Then Judah told Onan, “Sleep with your brother’s widow. Fulfill your duty as her brother-in-law and provide offspring for your brother.”
v09: Onan knew that the offspring would not be counted as his. So whenever he slept with his brother’s wife, he spilled his semen on the ground to keep from providing offspring for his brother.
v10: What he did was evil in the LORD’s sight, so the LORD put him to death as well.
</p>

<p>
v11: Judah said to his daughter-in-law Tamar, “Remain a widow in your father’s house until my son Shelah grows up.” He was thinking, “He might die too, like his brothers.” So Tamar went and lived in her father’s house.
</p>

## Tamar Confronts Judah’s Failure

<p>
v12: A long time passed, and Judah’s wife—the daughter of Shua—died. After Judah’s mourning ended, he went up to his sheep shearers at Timnah with his friend Hirah the Adullamite.
v13: Tamar was told, “Your father-in-law is going up to Timnah to shear his sheep.”
v14: She took off her widow’s clothes, covered herself with a veil, and wrapped herself up. Then she sat at the entrance to Enaim on the road to Timnah, because she saw that Shelah had grown up but she had not been given to him as a wife.
</p>

<p>
v15: When Judah saw her, he assumed she was a prostitute because she had covered her face.
v16: Not realizing she was his daughter-in-law, he turned toward her beside the road and said, “Come, let me sleep with you.” She asked, “What will you give me to sleep with you?”
v17: “I’ll send you a young goat from my flock,” he answered. She said, “Will you give me something as a pledge until you send it?”
v18: “What pledge should I give you?” he asked. She answered, “Your seal, its cord, and the staff in your hand.” He gave them to her and slept with her, and she became pregnant by him.
v19: Then she got up and left. She removed her veil and put her widow’s clothes back on.
</p>

## The Missing Pledge

<p>
v20: Judah sent the young goat with his Adullamite friend to retrieve the pledge from the woman, but Hirah could not find her.
v21: He asked the local men, “Where is the shrine prostitute who was beside the road at Enaim?” They answered, “There has been no shrine prostitute here.”
v22: Hirah returned to Judah and said, “I could not find her. The local men also said, ‘There has been no shrine prostitute here.’”
v23: Judah said, “Let her keep the pledge, or we will become a laughingstock. I did send this young goat, but you could not find her.”
</p>

## Tamar Vindicated

<p>
v24: About three months later Judah was told, “Your daughter-in-law Tamar has acted as a prostitute, and now she is pregnant from prostitution.” Judah said, “Bring her out and have her burned.”
v25: As she was being brought out, Tamar sent this message to her father-in-law: “I am pregnant by the man who owns these.” Then she said, “Please recognize whose seal, cord, and staff these are.”
v26: Judah recognized them and said, “She is more in the right than I am, because I did not give her to my son Shelah.” He never slept with her again.
</p>

## Perez and Zerah

<p>
v27: When the time came for Tamar to give birth, there were twins in her womb.
v28: As she was giving birth, one baby put out a hand. The midwife tied a scarlet thread around his wrist and said, “This one came out first.”
v29: But he pulled his hand back, and his brother came out. The midwife exclaimed, “What a breach you have broken through for yourself!” So he was named Perez.
v30: Afterward his brother came out with the scarlet thread around his wrist, and he was named Zerah.
</p>

## Notes

v02: Judah’s wife is not named. She is the daughter of a Canaanite man named Shua.

v08–10: The repeated action describes Onan preventing conception because the offspring would count as his brother’s.

v21–22: The word here, qedeshah, differs from the ordinary word for prostitute in verses 15 and 24. It has often been rendered “cult prostitute” or “shrine prostitute,” though the precise institutional connection is debated.

v25: Tamar’s “Please recognize” repeats the words used when Joseph’s robe was presented to Jacob in 37:32.

v26: “More in the right than I” compares Tamar’s conduct with Judah’s failure to give her to Shelah. “He did not know her again” means Judah had no further sexual relations with her.

v29: Perez sounds like the Hebrew word for a breach or breakthrough.

## Vocabulary

v08: יָבַם (yavam)
To fulfill a brother-in-law’s duty by providing an heir for a deceased brother.

v17: עֵרָבוֹן (ʿeravon)
A pledge held as security.

v18: חוֹתָם (ḥotam)
A personal seal used to authenticate identity or authority.

v21: קְדֵשָׁה (qedeshah)
A term for a prostitute possibly associated with a shrine; its exact nuance is uncertain.

v26: צָדַק (tsadaq)
To be right, just, or in the right.

v29: פֶּרֶץ (Perets)
Perez; associated here with a breach or breakthrough.
