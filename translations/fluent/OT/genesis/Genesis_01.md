## The Beginning

<p>
v01: In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.
v02: The earth was unformed and empty. Darkness lay over the deep, and God's Spirit hovered over the waters.
</p>

## The First Day

<p>
v03: Then God said, “Let there be light,” and light came to be.
v04: God saw that the light was good and separated the light from the darkness.
v05: God named the light Day and the darkness Night. Evening came, and morning came: day one.
</p>

## The Second Day

<p>
v06: God said, “Let there be a dome between the waters, separating water from water.”
v07: God made the dome and separated the water beneath it from the water above it. And so it was.
v08: God named the dome Sky. Evening came, and morning came: the second day.
</p>

## The Third Day

<p>
v09: God said, “Let the water beneath the sky gather in one place, and let dry ground appear.” And so it was.
v10: God named the dry ground Land and the gathered waters Seas. God saw that it was good.
</p>

<p>
v11: God said, “Let the land grow vegetation: plants that produce seed and fruit trees that bear fruit with seed in it, each of its own kind, on the land.” And so it was.
v12: The land grew vegetation: plants producing seed of their own kinds and trees bearing fruit with seed in it, each of its own kind. God saw that it was good.
v13: Evening came, and morning came: the third day.
</p>

## The Fourth Day

<p>
v14: God said, “Let lights appear in the dome of the sky to separate day from night. Let them mark signs, appointed times, days, and years.
v15: Let them shine in the dome of the sky to give light to the earth.” And so it was.
</p>

<p>
v16: God made the two great lights: the larger light to rule the day and the smaller light to rule the night. God made the stars too.
v17: God placed them in the dome of the sky to shine on the earth,
v18: to rule day and night, and to separate light from darkness. God saw that it was good.
v19: Evening came, and morning came: the fourth day.
</p>

## The Fifth Day

<p>
v20: God said, “Let the waters teem with living creatures, and let birds fly above the earth across the dome of the sky.”
v21: God created the great sea creatures and all the living things that move and teem in the waters, each of its own kind, and every kind of winged bird. God saw that it was good.
v22: God blessed them: “Be fruitful, multiply, and fill the waters of the seas. Let the birds multiply on the earth.”
v23: Evening came, and morning came: the fifth day.
</p>

## The Sixth Day

<p>
v24: God said, “Let the land produce living creatures of every kind: livestock, creatures that crawl, and wild animals, each of its own kind.” And so it was.
v25: God made every kind of wild animal, every kind of livestock, and every kind of creature that crawls on the ground. God saw that it was good.
</p>

<p>
v26: God said, “Let us make humanity in our image, to bear our likeness. Let them rule the fish in the sea, the birds in the sky, the livestock, the whole earth, and every creature that crawls on it.”
</p>

<p>
v27: God created humanity in his image;
in God's image he created them;
male and female he created them.
</p>

<p>
v28: God blessed them and said, “Be fruitful, multiply, fill the earth, and subdue it. Rule the fish in the sea, the birds in the sky, and every living creature that moves on the earth.”
</p>

<p>
v29: God said, “Look, I give you every seed-producing plant on all the earth and every tree with seed-bearing fruit. These will be your food.
v30: To every wild animal, every bird in the sky, and every creature that crawls on the earth—everything that has life in it—I give green plants for food.” And so it was.
</p>

<p>
v31: God looked at everything that God had made: it was very good. Evening came, and morning came: the sixth day.
</p>

## Notes

v01–02: The opening can also be understood as “When God began to create …” leading into the following scene. “The deep” names the waters already present; the wording does not explain their origin.

v02: “God's Spirit” translates ruach elohim. Ruach can mean spirit, wind, or breath, and the phrase has also been understood as a wind from God or a mighty wind. The capital letter is an English editorial choice.

v05: The Hebrew says “day one”; the later days are numbered second, third, and so on. The evening-and-morning refrain is intentionally repeated.

v06–08: “Dome” renders raqia, also translated “expanse” or “vault.” It separates waters above from waters below.

v14–16: “Appointed times” can include seasons and set occasions. The larger and smaller lights are described by their roles; the text does not name them sun and moon here.

v26–28: The speaker says “us” and “our” without identifying the plural referents. “Humanity” renders the collective adam. Verse 27 moves from a singular human referent to “them”; the English uses “them” for the collective while keeping male and female explicit. “Rule” and “subdue” retain the force of the source verbs.

v30: The phrase is literally “in which there is a living being/life.” It uses nephesh chayyah, also found in 2:7; the separate Hebrew word neshama, “breath,” does not occur in this verse.

## Vocabulary

v02: רוּחַ (ruach)
Spirit, wind, or breath; context determines the sense.

v06: רָקִיעַ (raqia)
Dome, vault, or expanse; the divider between the waters in this scene.

v26: אָדָם (adam)
Humanity or a human; also used as the name Adam.

v26: צֶלֶם (tselem)
Image or representation.

v28: רָדָה (radah)
Rule or exercise dominion.

v28: כָּבַשׁ (kavash)
Subdue or bring under control.
