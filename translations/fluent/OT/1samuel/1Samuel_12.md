## Samuel calls for testimony

<p>
v01: Samuel said to all Israel, “I have listened to everything you asked of me and appointed a king over you.
v02: Now the king walks before you. I have grown old and gray, and my sons are here among you. I have walked before you from my youth until this day.
</p>

<p>
v03: Here I am. Testify against me before the LORD and his anointed: whose ox have I taken? Whose donkey have I taken? Whom have I exploited or oppressed? From whose hand have I taken a bribe to shut my eyes? I will repay you.”
</p>

<p>
v04: They answered, “You have not exploited or oppressed us. You have taken nothing from anyone.”
</p>

<p>
v05: He said, “The LORD is witness against you today, and his anointed is witness, that you have found nothing in my hand.” “He is witness,” came the answer.
</p>

## Israel’s history before the LORD

<p>
v06: Samuel continued, “It was the LORD who appointed Moses and Aaron and brought your ancestors up from Egypt.
</p>

<p>
v07: Now stand here while I lay the case before you in the LORD’s presence: all the righteous acts the LORD has done for you and your ancestors.
v08: After Jacob went to Egypt, your ancestors cried out to the LORD. He sent Moses and Aaron, who brought your ancestors out of Egypt and settled them in this place.
v09: But they forgot the LORD their God. He sold them into the hand of Sisera, commander of Hazor’s army, into the Philistines’ hand, and into the hand of Moab’s king. These fought against them.
</p>

<p>
v10: They cried out to the LORD: ‘We have sinned. We abandoned the LORD and served the Baals and Ashtoreths. Now rescue us from our enemies’ hand, and we will serve you.’
</p>

<p>
v11: The LORD sent Jerubbaal, Bedan, Jephthah, and Samuel. He rescued you from the enemies around you, and you lived securely.
</p>

<p>
v12: Yet when you saw Nahash king of the Ammonites coming against you, you told me, ‘No! A king must reign over us,’ although the LORD your God was your king.
</p>

<p>
v13: Now here is the king you chose, the one you asked for. The LORD has given you a king.
</p>

<p>
v14: If you fear the LORD, serve him, listen to his voice, and do not rebel against his command, then both you and the king who reigns over you will be following the LORD your God.
</p>

<p>
v15: But if you do not listen to the LORD’s voice and rebel against his command, his hand will be against you as it was against your ancestors.
</p>

## Thunder at harvest

<p>
v16: Now stand and watch this great thing the LORD is about to do before your eyes.
</p>

<p>
v17: Is it not wheat harvest today? I will call to the LORD, and he will send thunder and rain. Then you will see and know how great an evil you have done in the LORD’s sight by asking for a king.”
</p>

<p>
v18: Samuel called to the LORD, and the LORD sent thunder and rain that day. All the people stood in great fear of the LORD and Samuel.
</p>

<p>
v19: They all said to Samuel, “Pray to the LORD your God for your servants, so we do not die. We have added to all our sins this evil of asking for a king.”
</p>

## Serve with all your heart

<p>
v20: Samuel said, “Do not be afraid. You have done all this evil, but do not turn away from following the LORD. Serve him with all your heart.
</p>

<p>
v21: Do not turn aside to follow empty things that cannot help or rescue you. They are emptiness.
</p>

<p>
v22: For the sake of his great name, the LORD will not abandon his people. He was pleased to make you his own people.
</p>

<p>
v23: As for me, far be it from me to sin against the LORD by ceasing to pray for you. I will teach you the good and upright way.
</p>

<p>
v24: Only fear the LORD and serve him faithfully with all your heart. Consider the great things he has done for you.
v25: But if you persist in doing evil, both you and your king will be swept away.”
</p>

## Notes

v11: Bedan is the name in the pinned Hebrew. The list is not silently changed to match a familiar list of judges. Samuel also refers to himself by name.

v14: The Hebrew’s conditional sentence is compressed. This rendering takes following the LORD as the outcome of the stated obedience; it does not add an unexpressed promise of prosperity.

v15: Literally, “against you and against your ancestors.” “As it was” makes the comparison intelligible. The draft does not replace “ancestors” with “king.”

## Vocabulary

v07: Righteous acts — tsidqot
Acts displaying what is right, here recounted as the LORD’s saving dealings with Israel.

v21: Empty things — tohu
What is insubstantial or futile; the repeated word stresses their inability to help.
