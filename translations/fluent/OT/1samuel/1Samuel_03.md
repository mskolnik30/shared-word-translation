## Samuel learns whose voice he hears

<p>
v01: The boy Samuel served the LORD under Eli. In those days a word from the LORD was rare; visions were not widespread.
v02: One night Eli was lying in his usual place. His eyes had begun to fail, and he could no longer see well.
v03: The lamp of God had not yet gone out. Samuel was lying in the LORD’s sanctuary, where the ark of God was.
</p>

<p>
v04: The LORD called Samuel, and he answered, “Here I am.”
</p>

<p>
v05: He ran to Eli. “Here I am. You called me.” But Eli said, “I did not call. Go back and lie down.” So he went and lay down.
</p>

<p>
v06: The LORD called again, “Samuel!” Samuel got up and went to Eli. “Here I am. You called me.” “I did not call, my son,” Eli said. “Go back and lie down.”
</p>

<p>
v07: Samuel did not yet know the LORD; the LORD’s word had not yet been revealed to him.
</p>

<p>
v08: A third time the LORD called Samuel. He got up and went to Eli. “Here I am. You called me.” Then Eli understood that the LORD was calling the boy.
</p>

<p>
v09: Eli told Samuel, “Go and lie down. If he calls you, say, ‘Speak, LORD. Your servant is listening.’” Samuel went and lay down in his place.
</p>

<p>
v10: The LORD came and stood there, calling as before, “Samuel, Samuel!” Samuel answered, “Speak. Your servant is listening.”
</p>

## The word against Eli

<p>
v11: The LORD said to Samuel, “I am about to do something in Israel that will make both ears of everyone who hears it ring.
v12: On that day I will carry out everything I have spoken against Eli’s house, from beginning to end.
v13: I told him that I would judge his house forever because of the guilt he knew about: his sons were bringing a curse on themselves, and he did not restrain them.
v14: Therefore I have sworn concerning Eli’s house: its guilt will never be atoned for by sacrifice or offering.”
</p>

<p>
v15: Samuel lay there until morning, then opened the doors of the LORD’s house. He was afraid to tell Eli about the vision.
</p>

<p>
v16: Eli called, “Samuel, my son!” “Here I am,” he answered.
</p>

<p>
v17: Eli asked, “What did he tell you? Please do not hide it from me. May God do this to you, and more besides, if you hide a single part of what he told you.”
</p>

<p>
v18: So Samuel told him everything, hiding nothing. Eli said, “He is the LORD. Let him do what seems good to him.”
</p>

## A prophet recognized

<p>
v19: Samuel grew, and the LORD was with him. He let none of Samuel’s words fall to the ground.
v20: All Israel, from Dan to Beersheba, recognized Samuel as a trustworthy prophet of the LORD.
v21: The LORD continued to appear at Shiloh, revealing himself there to Samuel through the LORD’s word.
</p>

## Notes

v13: The pinned Hebrew reads “bringing a curse on themselves.” Another textual understanding is “blaspheming God.” The wording is not silently changed here.

v19: To let words “fall to the ground” is to let them fail or go unfulfilled.

## Vocabulary

v01: Word — davar
Can mean a word, message, or matter. Hearing and carrying the LORD’s word shape Samuel’s calling.
