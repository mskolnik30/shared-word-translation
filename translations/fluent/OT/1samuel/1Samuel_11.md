## Jabesh under siege

<p>
v01: Nahash the Ammonite came up and laid siege to Jabesh-gilead. All the men of Jabesh said to him, “Make a covenant with us, and we will serve you.”
</p>

<p>
v02: Nahash the Ammonite answered, “I will make it on this condition: I gouge out every one of your right eyes and bring disgrace on all Israel.”
</p>

<p>
v03: The elders of Jabesh said, “Give us seven days to send messengers throughout Israel. If no one saves us, we will come out to you.”
</p>

## Saul summons Israel

<p>
v04: The messengers reached Gibeah of Saul and reported this in the people’s hearing. Everyone wept aloud.
</p>

<p>
v05: Just then Saul came in from the field behind the cattle. “What is wrong with the people? Why are they crying?” he asked. They told him what the men of Jabesh had said.
</p>

<p>
v06: When Saul heard these words, God’s Spirit rushed upon him, and he burned with anger.
</p>

<p>
v07: He took a pair of oxen, cut them in pieces, and sent the pieces by messengers throughout Israel: “This is what will be done to the cattle of anyone who does not come out after Saul and Samuel.” Terror from the LORD fell on the people, and they came out as one man.
</p>

<p>
v08: He mustered them at Bezek: three hundred thousand Israelites and thirty thousand men of Judah.
</p>

<p>
v09: They told the messengers who had come, “Tell the men of Jabesh-gilead: ‘Tomorrow, by the heat of the sun, you will be rescued.’” The messengers returned with the news, and the men of Jabesh rejoiced.
</p>

<p>
v10: The men of Jabesh told the Ammonites, “Tomorrow we will come out to you, and you may do whatever seems good to you.”
</p>

<p>
v11: The next day Saul divided the troops into three companies. They entered the Ammonite camp during the morning watch and struck them down until the heat of the day. The survivors scattered so completely that no two remained together.
</p>

## The kingship renewed

<p>
v12: The people said to Samuel, “Who was it that said, ‘Will Saul reign over us?’ Hand those men over so we can put them to death.”
</p>

<p>
v13: But Saul said, “No one will be put to death today. Today the LORD has brought deliverance to Israel.”
</p>

<p>
v14: Samuel told the people, “Come, let us go to Gilgal and renew the kingship there.”
</p>

<p>
v15: Everyone went to Gilgal. There, before the LORD, they made Saul king and offered sacrifices of well-being. Saul and all the men of Israel rejoiced greatly there.
</p>

## Notes

v02: Nahash’s demand names physical mutilation and the disgrace he intends to impose on all Israel.

v07: The dismembered cattle and threat belong to Saul’s summons. The narrative also attributes the people’s terror to the LORD.

## Vocabulary

v01: Covenant — berit
Here a binding agreement of political submission, as the offer to serve Nahash makes clear.
