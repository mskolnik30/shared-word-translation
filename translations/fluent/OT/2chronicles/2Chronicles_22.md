## Ahaziah and Ahab’s house

<p>
v01: Jerusalem's inhabitants made his youngest son Ahaziah king in his place, because the raiding band that came into the camp with the Arabs had killed all the older sons. So Ahaziah son of Jehoram became king of Judah.
v02: Ahaziah was forty-two when he became king, and he ruled in Jerusalem for one year. His mother's name was Athaliah, a daughter of Omri.
</p>

<p>
v03: He too followed the ways of Ahab's house, for his mother counseled him to do wrong.
v04: He did what was evil in the LORD's eyes, just as Ahab's house had done. After his father's death, they became his advisers, bringing about his ruin.
</p>

<p>
v05: Following their advice, he joined Jehoram son of Ahab, king of Israel, in a war against Hazael king of Aram at Ramoth-gilead. The Arameans wounded Joram.
v06: He returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds they had given him at Ramah when he fought Hazael king of Aram. Azariah son of Jehoram, king of Judah, went down to Jezreel to see Jehoram son of Ahab because he was wounded.
</p>

<p>
v07: God brought about Ahaziah's downfall through his visit to Joram. When he arrived, he went out with Jehoram to meet Jehu son of Nimshi, whom the LORD had anointed to cut off Ahab's house.
v08: As Jehu was executing judgment on Ahab's house, he found Judah's officials and the sons of Ahaziah's brothers who attended Ahaziah, and he killed them.
v09: He searched for Ahaziah, and they captured him hiding in Samaria. They brought him to Jehu and put him to death. They buried him, saying, “He was a descendant of Jehoshaphat, who sought the LORD with all his heart.” No one in Ahaziah's house had the strength to hold the kingdom.
</p>

## Joash is hidden

<p>
v10: When Athaliah, Ahaziah's mother, saw that her son was dead, she rose up and destroyed all the royal offspring of Judah's house.
</p>

<p>
v11: But Jehoshabeath, the king's daughter, took Joash son of Ahaziah and stole him away from among the king's sons who were being killed. She put him and his nurse in a bedroom. Jehoshabeath was King Jehoram's daughter, the wife of Jehoiada the priest, and Ahaziah's sister. She hid Joash from Athaliah so that Athaliah did not kill him.
v12: He remained hidden with them in God's house for six years, while Athaliah ruled the land.
</p>

## Notes

v02: The pinned Hebrew reads forty-two. This conflicts with the preceding account of his father’s age; 2 Kings 8:26 reads twenty-two. The discrepancy is retained, not resolved silently. Daughter of Omri can indicate a later-generation descendant.

v05–06: Jehoram and Joram are forms of the same name here. Verse 6 calls Judah’s king Azariah, although the surrounding account calls him Ahaziah; compare Jehoahaz in 21:17.

v09: The Hebrew says son of Jehoshaphat, using son for a descendant. The capture in Samaria and subsequent killing are retained as this account gives them.

## Vocabulary

v07: Anointed
Set apart for a task by anointing; Jehu’s commission concerns the destruction of Ahab’s house.

v11: Jehoshabeath
The rescuer is both Ahaziah’s sister and the priest Jehoiada’s wife. Her actions preserve Joash’s life.
