## Building the temple

<p>
v01: Solomon began building the LORD’s house in Jerusalem on Mount Moriah, where the LORD had appeared to his father David, at the place David had prepared on the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite.
v02: He began building on the second day of the second month in the fourth year of his reign.
v03: These were the foundation dimensions Solomon used for building God’s house, measured in cubits of the older standard: sixty cubits long and twenty cubits wide.
v04: The entrance hall across the front was twenty cubits long, matching the width of the house, and a hundred twenty cubits high. He overlaid its interior with pure gold.
</p>

<p>
v05: He paneled the main hall with cypress, overlaid it with fine gold, and decorated it with palm trees and chains.
v06: He adorned the house with precious stones for beauty. The gold was gold from Parvaim.
v07: He overlaid the house—its beams, thresholds, walls, and doors—with gold, and carved cherubim on the walls.
</p>

## The Most Holy Place

<p>
v08: He made the Most Holy Place twenty cubits long, across the width of the house, and twenty cubits wide. He overlaid it with six hundred talents of fine gold.
v09: The gold nails weighed fifty shekels. He also overlaid the upper rooms with gold.
</p>

<p>
v10: In the Most Holy Place he made two sculpted cherubim and overlaid them with gold.
v11: Together the cherubim’s wings spanned twenty cubits. One wing of the first cherub was five cubits long and touched the wall of the house; its other wing was five cubits long and touched the other cherub’s wing.
v12: One wing of the other cherub was five cubits long and touched the other wall of the house; its other wing was five cubits long and joined the first cherub’s wing.
v13: The wings of these cherubim spread out twenty cubits. The cherubim stood on their feet, facing the main hall.
</p>

<p>
v14: He made the curtain of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and worked cherubim into it.
</p>

## The pillars

<p>
v15: He made two pillars in front of the house, thirty-five cubits in length, with a capital five cubits high on top of each.
v16: He made chains in the inner sanctuary and placed them on top of the pillars. He made a hundred pomegranates and put them on the chains.
v17: He erected the pillars in front of the temple, one on the right and one on the left. He named the right one Jachin and the left one Boaz.
</p>

## Notes

v02: “Day” is supplied in the phrase “on the second”; the Hebrew does not repeat the noun.

v04: The pinned Hebrew gives a height of 120 cubits. A reading of twenty cubits occurs in some textual witnesses; the draft retains the pinned figure and flags the difficulty.

v15: The Hebrew gives thirty-five cubits without clearly saying whether this is for each pillar or both. First Kings 7:15 gives eighteen cubits for a pillar.

v16: “In the inner sanctuary” follows the Hebrew; a necklace-like chain is another proposed reading of this difficult phrase.

## Vocabulary

v03: Cubit
An ancient length measure based on the forearm. This verse explicitly refers to an older standard.

v17: Jachin and Boaz
The pillar names suggest “he establishes” and “in him is strength.”
