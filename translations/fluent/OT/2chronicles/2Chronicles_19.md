## Return and rebuke

<p>
v01: Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned safely to his house in Jerusalem.
v02: Jehu son of Hanani the seer went out to meet him. He told King Jehoshaphat, “Should you help the wicked and love those who hate the LORD? Because of this, wrath from the LORD is upon you.
v03: Still, good things have been found in you: you removed the Asherah poles from the land and set your heart on seeking God.”
</p>

## Judges throughout Judah

<p>
v04: Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem. He went out among the people again, from Beer-sheba to Ephraim’s hill country, and brought them back to the LORD, their ancestors’ God.
v05: He appointed judges throughout the land, in every fortified town of Judah, town by town.
</p>

<p>
v06: He told the judges, “Consider what you are doing. You judge on the LORD’s behalf, not on behalf of human beings, and he is with you when you give judgment.
v07: Now let dread of the LORD be upon you. Act with care, for the LORD our God shows no injustice or favoritism and takes no bribes.”
</p>

<p>
v08: In Jerusalem too, Jehoshaphat appointed some of the Levites, priests, and heads of Israel’s ancestral houses to give the LORD’s judgments and settle disputes. They returned to Jerusalem.
</p>

<p>
v09: He charged them, “This is how you must act: with fear of the LORD, faithfully, and with a whole heart.
v10: Whenever a dispute comes to you from your fellow Israelites living in their towns—whether a case of bloodshed or a question about instruction, commandment, statutes, or judgments—you must warn them, so they do not incur guilt before the LORD and bring wrath upon you and your people. Do this, and you will not incur guilt.
</p>

<p>
v11: Amariah the chief priest is over you in every matter concerning the LORD, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael, leader of Judah’s house, in every matter concerning the king. The Levites will serve you as officers. Be strong and act, and may the LORD be with those who do good.”
</p>

## Notes

v8: The Hebrew concludes they returned to Jerusalem. The referent is not specified; some translations instead understand the judges as residing there.

v10: Bloodshed renders the compressed expression between blood and blood, concerning legal distinctions in cases involving death or bloodguilt.

## Vocabulary

v7: Favoritism
The Hebrew image is lifting faces: treating people according to status rather than justice.

v11: Officers
Shoterim are administrative officials; the term does not specify modern police roles.
