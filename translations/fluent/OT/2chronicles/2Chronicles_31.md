## Worship and its support

<p>
v01: When all this was over, all the Israelites who were present went out to Judah's cities. They smashed the sacred pillars, cut down the Asherah poles, and demolished the high places and altars throughout Judah and Benjamin, Ephraim and Manasseh, until they had finished. Then all the Israelites returned to their own property in their cities.
</p>

<p>
v02: Hezekiah organized the priests and Levites in their divisions, each according to his service: offering burnt offerings and well-being offerings, ministering, giving thanks, and praising at the gates of the LORD's camps.
</p>

<p>
v03: The king supplied from his own property the portion for burnt offerings: morning and evening offerings, and those for Sabbaths, new moons, and appointed festivals, as written in the LORD's law.
</p>

<p>
v04: He ordered the people living in Jerusalem to give the priests and Levites their allotted portion, so they could devote themselves firmly to the LORD's law.
</p>

<p>
v05: As the order spread, the Israelites brought abundant firstfruits of grain, new wine, oil, honey, and every kind of field produce. They brought a tenth of everything in great abundance.
v06: The people of Israel and Judah living in Judah's cities also brought a tenth of their cattle and flocks, and a tenth of the sacred things consecrated to the LORD their God. They piled them in heap after heap.
</p>

<p>
v07: They began laying the heaps in the third month and finished in the seventh month.
</p>

<p>
v08: Hezekiah and the officials came and saw the heaps. They blessed the LORD and his people Israel.
v09: Hezekiah asked the priests and Levites about the heaps.
</p>

<p>
v10: Azariah the chief priest, from Zadok's house, answered him: “Since people began bringing the contribution to the LORD's house, we have eaten our fill and had plenty left over. The LORD has blessed his people, and this great abundance is what remains.”
</p>

## Storing and distributing the gifts

<p>
v11: Hezekiah ordered storerooms to be prepared in the LORD's house, and they prepared them.
</p>

<p>
v12: They faithfully brought in the contributions, tithes, and sacred gifts. Cananiah the Levite supervised them, with his brother Shimei as second in command.
v13: Jehiel, Azaziah, Nahath, Asahel, Jerimoth, Jozabad, Eliel, Ismachiah, Mahath, and Benaiah were overseers under Cananiah and his brother Shimei. King Hezekiah and Azariah, the official in charge of God's house, appointed them.
</p>

<p>
v14: Kore son of Imnah, the Levite who guarded the east gate, was responsible for the freewill offerings to God. He distributed the LORD's contributions and the most holy gifts.
</p>

<p>
v15: Under him were Eden, Miniamin, Jeshua, Shemaiah, Amariah, and Shecaniah in the priests' cities. They faithfully distributed portions to their fellow priests by divisions, to great and small alike,
v16: apart from the males registered by genealogy from three years old upward, all who entered the LORD's house for their daily duties in the service of their divisions.
</p>

<p>
v17: The priests were registered by ancestral families, and the Levites from twenty years old upward by their duties and divisions.
v18: The registration included all their little children, wives, sons, and daughters—the whole community—for they faithfully kept themselves holy.
</p>

<p>
v19: For Aaron's descendants, the priests living in the pasturelands around their cities, men were designated by name in each city to give portions to every male among the priests and everyone registered among the Levites.
</p>

<p>
v20: This is what Hezekiah did throughout Judah. He did what was good, right, and faithful before the LORD his God.
v21: In everything he undertook for the service of God's house and for the law and commands, he sought his God with all his heart. He did the work and prospered.
</p>

## Notes

v06: The tithe of sacred things is a compressed expression. The translation retains it rather than replacing it with another kind of offering.

v12–13: Cananiah follows the source’s read form. The written form is commonly represented as Conaniah. The same choice is used in 35:9.

v16–18: The distribution and registration instructions are compressed and difficult. Three years old is the Hebrew reading in verse 16; it is not changed to thirty. Verse 17 separately gives twenty as the Levite registration threshold. Household dependents are explicitly included in verse 18.

## Vocabulary

v02: The LORD’s camps
The sanctuary precincts are pictured as the LORD’s encampment.

v05: Firstfruits and tithe
The first yield offered to God and a tenth set apart from the produce. They are related but distinct terms.

v14: Most holy gifts
Gifts subject to particular restrictions on their handling and use.
