# 2 Chronicles 35

## Josiah Celebrates Passover

<p>
v01: Josiah made a Passover to the LORD in Jerusalem, and they slaughtered the Passover on the fourteenth day of the first month.

v02: He appointed the priests to their duties and strengthened them for the service of the house of the LORD.

v03: He said to the Levites who taught all Israel, who were holy to the LORD, “Put the holy ark in the house that Solomon son of David king of Israel built. You need not carry it upon your shoulders. Now serve the LORD your God and his people Israel.

v04: Prepare yourselves according to your ancestral houses, according to your divisions, by the writing of David king of Israel and by the writing of Solomon his son.

v05: Stand in the Holy Place according to the divisions of the ancestral houses of your brothers, the people, and according to the division of a Levitical ancestral house.

v06: Slaughter the Passover, consecrate yourselves, and prepare for your brothers, to do according to the word of the LORD by the hand of Moses.”
</p>

## Josiah and the Officials Provide Offerings

<p>
v07: Josiah contributed to the people from the flock lambs and young goats, all for the Passover offerings for everyone who were present, thirty thousand in number, and three thousand cattle. These were from the king’s possessions.

v08: His officials also contributed willingly to the people, to the priests, and to the Levites. Hilkiah, Zechariah, and Jehiel, rulers of the house of God, gave to the priests for Passover offerings two thousand six hundred and three hundred cattle.

v09: Conaniah and Shemaiah and Nethanel his brothers, and Hashabiah, Jeiel, and Jozabad, rulers of the Levites, contributed to the Levites five thousand Passover offerings and five hundred cattle.
</p>

## The Passover Is Prepared

<p>
v10: So the service was prepared, and the priests stood at their station and the Levites by their divisions according to the king’s command.

v11: They slaughtered the Passover, and the priests threw the blood received from their hand, while the Levites skinned the animals.

v12: They removed the burnt offerings to give them according to the divisions of the ancestral houses of the people, to offer to the LORD, as written in the book of Moses. And so with the cattle.

v13: They roasted the Passover with fire according to the ordinance, and they boiled the holy offerings in pots, cauldrons, and pans and quickly brought them to all the people.

v14: Afterward they prepared for themselves and for the priests, because the priests, the sons of Aaron, were offering the burnt offerings and the fat until night. So the Levites prepared for themselves and for the priests, the sons of Aaron.

v15: The singers, the sons of Asaph, were at their station according to the command of David, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun the king’s seer, and the gatekeepers were at each gate. They did not need to leave their service, for their brothers the Levites prepared for them.

v16: So all the service of the LORD was prepared that day to make the Passover and offer burnt offerings upon the altar of the LORD, according to the command of King Josiah.

v17: The children of Israel who were present made the Passover at that time and the Festival of Unleavened Bread seven days.
</p>

## No Passover Like It since Samuel

<p>
v18: No Passover like it had been made in Israel from the days of Samuel the prophet, and none of the kings of Israel had made a Passover like what Josiah made, with the priests, Levites, all Judah and Israel who were present, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

v19: In the eighteenth year of Josiah’s reign this Passover was made.
</p>

## Josiah Goes against Pharaoh Neco

<p>
v20: After all this, when Josiah had prepared the house, Neco king of Egypt went up to fight at Carchemish by the Euphrates, and Josiah went out to meet him.

v21: Yet Neco sent messengers to him, saying, “What have I to do with you, king of Judah? I am not coming against you this day but against the house with which I am at war, and God has commanded me to hurry. Stop opposing God, who is with me, so that he does not destroy you.”

v22: Yet Josiah did not turn his face from him. Instead he disguised himself to fight with him and did not listen to the words of Neco from the mouth of God. He came to fight in the Valley of Megiddo.
</p>

## Josiah Dies

<p>
v23: The archers shot King Josiah, and the king said to his servants, “Take me away, for I am severely wounded.”

v24: His servants removed him from the chariot, put him in the second chariot that he had, and brought him to Jerusalem. He died and was buried in the tombs of his ancestors. All Judah and Jerusalem mourned for Josiah.

v25: Jeremiah lamented for Josiah, and all the male and female singers spoke about Josiah in their laments to this day. They made them an ordinance in Israel. Look, they are written in the Laments.

v26: Now the rest of the acts of Josiah and his steadfast deeds, according to what is written in the instruction of the LORD,

v27: and his acts, first and last, look, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The command to place the ark in the temple is difficult because it should already have been there. It may imply that the ark had been moved during earlier apostasy or that Levites are being told to cease carrying it.

v18: Chronicles compares Josiah’s Passover with the days of Samuel, emphasizing its extraordinary scope and order.

v21–22: The surprising claim that Neco’s warning came “from the mouth of God” means Josiah’s failure is refusal to hear divine speech through a foreign king.

v25: The “Laments” mentioned here are not necessarily the canonical book of Lamentations; the cited collection is otherwise unknown.
