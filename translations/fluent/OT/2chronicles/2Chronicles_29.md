## Hezekiah reopens the house

<p>
v01: Hezekiah was twenty-five when he became king, and he ruled in Jerusalem for twenty-nine years. His mother's name was Abijah daughter of Zechariah.
v02: He did what was right in the LORD's eyes, following all that his ancestor David had done.
</p>

<p>
v03: In the first month of the first year of his reign, he opened the doors of the LORD's house and repaired them.
v04: He brought in the priests and Levites and assembled them in the eastern square.
</p>

<p>
v05: He told them, “Listen to me, Levites. Consecrate yourselves now, and consecrate the house of the LORD, the God of your ancestors. Remove the defilement from the sanctuary.
v06: Our ancestors were unfaithful and did what was evil in the LORD our God's eyes. They abandoned him, turned their faces away from the LORD's dwelling, and turned their backs on it.
v07: They even shut the porch doors and extinguished the lamps. They neither burned incense nor offered burnt offerings in the sanctuary to Israel's God.
</p>

<p>
v08: So the LORD's wrath came upon Judah and Jerusalem. He made them an object of horror, desolation, and hissing, as you can see with your own eyes.
v09: Our fathers have fallen by the sword, and our sons, daughters, and wives are in captivity because of this.
</p>

<p>
v10: Now I have set my heart on making a covenant with the LORD, Israel's God, so that his burning anger may turn away from us.
v11: My sons, do not be negligent now. The LORD has chosen you to stand before him, to serve him as his ministers and to burn offerings.”
</p>

## The sanctuary is cleansed

<p>
v12: These Levites arose: from the Kohathites, Mahath son of Amasai and Joel son of Azariah; from Merari's descendants, Kish son of Abdi and Azariah son of Jehallelel; from the Gershonites, Joah son of Zimmah and Eden son of Joah;
v13: from Elizaphan's descendants, Shimri and Jeiel; from Asaph's descendants, Zechariah and Mattaniah;
v14: from Heman's descendants, Jehiel and Shimei; and from Jeduthun's descendants, Shemaiah and Uzziel.
</p>

<p>
v15: They gathered their fellow Levites, consecrated themselves, and went in to cleanse the LORD's house, following the king's command in accordance with the LORD's words.
v16: The priests entered the inner part of the LORD's house to cleanse it. They brought everything unclean they found in the LORD's temple out into its court, and the Levites took it away to the Kidron Valley.
</p>

<p>
v17: They began the consecration on the first day of the first month and reached the LORD's porch on the eighth day. They consecrated the LORD's house for eight more days and finished on the sixteenth day of the first month.
</p>

<p>
v18: Then they went in to King Hezekiah and said, “We have cleansed the entire house of the LORD, the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils, and the table for the arranged bread and all its utensils.
v19: We have prepared and consecrated all the utensils King Ahaz cast aside during his unfaithful reign. They are now before the LORD's altar.”
</p>

## Offerings and praise

<p>
v20: King Hezekiah rose early, gathered the city's officials, and went up to the LORD's house.
</p>

<p>
v21: They brought seven bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats as a sin offering for the kingdom, the sanctuary, and Judah. He ordered the priests, Aaron's descendants, to offer them on the LORD's altar.
v22: They slaughtered the cattle, and the priests received the blood and splashed it against the altar. They slaughtered the rams and splashed their blood against the altar. They slaughtered the lambs and splashed their blood against the altar.
</p>

<p>
v23: They brought the male goats for the sin offering before the king and the assembly, who laid their hands on them.
v24: The priests slaughtered them and applied their blood to the altar as a sin offering, to make atonement for all Israel. The king had ordered both the burnt offering and the sin offering for all Israel.
</p>

<p>
v25: He stationed the Levites in the LORD's house with cymbals, harps, and lyres, following the command of David, Gad the king's seer, and Nathan the prophet. The command came from the LORD through his prophets.
v26: The Levites stood with David's instruments, and the priests with the trumpets.
</p>

<p>
v27: Hezekiah ordered the burnt offering to be offered on the altar. As it began, the song to the LORD began too, with trumpets accompanied by the instruments of David king of Israel.
v28: The whole assembly bowed in worship, the singers sang, and the trumpets sounded. All this continued until the burnt offering was finished.
</p>

<p>
v29: When the offering was finished, the king and everyone with him knelt and bowed in worship.
v30: King Hezekiah and the officials told the Levites to praise the LORD with the words of David and Asaph the seer. They praised him joyfully, then bowed their heads and worshiped.
</p>

## The assembly brings gifts

<p>
v31: Then Hezekiah said, “Now you have consecrated yourselves to the LORD. Come forward and bring sacrifices and thank offerings to the LORD's house.” The assembly brought sacrifices and thank offerings, and everyone with a willing heart brought burnt offerings.
</p>

<p>
v32: The assembly brought seventy cattle, one hundred rams, and two hundred lambs, all as burnt offerings to the LORD.
v33: The consecrated offerings amounted to six hundred cattle and three thousand sheep and goats.
</p>

<p>
v34: But there were too few priests to skin all the burnt offerings. Their fellow Levites helped them until the work was finished and the priests had consecrated themselves, for the Levites were more wholehearted about consecrating themselves than the priests.
v35: There were many burnt offerings, along with the fat of the well-being offerings and the drink offerings accompanying the burnt offerings. So service in the LORD's house was reestablished.
</p>

<p>
v36: Hezekiah and all the people rejoiced at what God had prepared for the people, because it had all happened so suddenly.
</p>

## Notes

v12–14: The list names fourteen Levites in seven pairs. Jeiel in verse 13 and Jehiel in verse 14 follow the read forms in the source.

v17: The work runs from the first through the sixteenth day of the first month. The following chapter explains the later Passover date.

v21–24: Verse 21 groups four sets of seven animals together. The following actions distinguish the goats as the sin offering and repeat that the offerings are for all Israel.

v36: The Hebrew says that God prepared for the people, without explicitly naming what he prepared.

## Vocabulary

v05: Consecrate
Set apart for sacred service. Consecration of people and cleansing of the house prepare for renewed worship.

v24: Atonement
The ritual addresses sin and restores the community’s relation to God; the repeated scope is all Israel.

v31: Consecrated yourselves
Literally, filled your hands, an expression for dedication to service.

v35: Well-being offerings
Sacrifices associated with fellowship, thanksgiving, and shared meals; the fat is offered on the altar.
