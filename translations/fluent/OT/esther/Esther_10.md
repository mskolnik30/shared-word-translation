## Mordecai’s standing

<p>
v01: King Ahasuerus imposed tribute on the mainland and the islands of the sea.
v02: All his acts of power and might, and the full account of Mordecai’s greatness to which the king raised him—are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Media and Persia?
v03: Mordecai the Jew ranked second to King Ahasuerus. He was great among the Jews and esteemed by the many among his brothers, seeking his people’s good and speaking for the well-being of all his people.
</p>

## Notes

v03: “The many among his brothers” does not explicitly say everyone. The final phrase, literally “all his offspring,” can refer more broadly to his people or to their descendants.

## Vocabulary

v01: Tribute
Payment exacted by an imperial ruler from subject territories.
