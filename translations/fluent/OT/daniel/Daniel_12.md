## Deliverance and awakening

<p>
v01: “At that time Michael will arise, the great prince who stands over your people. There will be a time of distress unlike any since a nation first existed. But at that time your people will escape—everyone found written in the book.
v02: Many who sleep in the dusty ground will wake, some to everlasting life and others to shame and everlasting contempt.
v03: The discerning will shine like the brightness of the expanse, and those who lead many to righteousness like the stars forever and ever.
v04: But you, Daniel, close up the words and seal the book until the time of the end. Many will go to and fro, and knowledge will increase.”
</p>

## The final questions

<p>
v05: Then I, Daniel, looked and saw two others standing, one on each bank of the river.
v06: One asked the man dressed in linen, who was above the river’s waters, “How long until these astonishing things come to an end?”
v07: I heard the man dressed in linen, above the river’s waters. He raised his right and left hands to heaven and swore by the one who lives forever: “For a time, times, and half a time. When the shattering of the holy people’s power is finished, all these things will be completed.”
v08: I heard but did not understand. So I asked, “My lord, what will be the outcome of these things?”
v09: He said, “Go your way, Daniel, for the words are closed up and sealed until the time of the end.
v10: Many will be purified, cleansed, and refined, but the wicked will act wickedly. None of the wicked will understand; the discerning will understand.
v11: From the time the regular offering is removed and the abomination that devastates is set up, there will be 1,290 days.
v12: Happy is the one who waits and reaches the 1,335 days.
v13: But you, go on to the end. You will rest, then stand in your allotted place at the end of the days.”
</p>

## Notes

v02: The source says “many,” and describes waking to two enduring outcomes. The translation does not replace that wording with a comprehensive scheme of resurrection.

v07,11-12: The expressions “a time, times, and half a time,” 1,290 days, and 1,335 days are preserved separately. The text’s figures are not harmonized into one calculation.

v13: Daniel is told to rest and then stand in his allotted place at the end of the days. The wording leaves the manner of that fulfillment unstated.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Michael**: The great prince described as standing over Daniel’s people.

v04: **Seal**: Secure a document or vision; the command concerns its preservation until the stated time.
