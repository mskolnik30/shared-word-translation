## This very day

<p>
v01: In the ninth year, on the tenth day of the tenth month, the word of the LORD came to me:
v02: “Son of man, write down this date, this very day. On this very day the king of Babylon has laid siege to Jerusalem.
</p>

## The pot

<p>
v03: Tell the rebellious household a parable. Say: This is what the Lord GOD says:
Put the pot on, put it on!
Pour water into it.
v04: Gather the pieces into it,
every good piece, thigh and shoulder.
Fill it with the choicest bones.
v05: Take the choicest of the flock.
Pile bones beneath it too.
Bring it to a rolling boil;
let its bones cook within it.
v06: “Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says:
Woe to the city of bloodshed,
the pot with corrosion inside it,
whose corrosion has not come out!
Empty it piece by piece;
cast no lot for it.
v07: Her blood is within her.
She set it on bare rock;
she did not pour it on the ground
to cover it with dust.
v08: To stir up fury and exact vengeance,
I have set her blood on bare rock,
so it cannot be covered.
v09: “Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says:
Woe to the city of bloodshed!
I too will make the pile high.
v10: Heap up the wood,
kindle the fire,
cook the meat thoroughly,
season the mixture,
and let the bones be charred.
v11: Set the empty pot on its coals,
so its copper grows hot and burns,
its uncleanness melts within it,
and its corrosion is consumed.
v12: The effort has wearied me,
yet its heavy corrosion will not come out.
Into the fire with its corrosion!
v13: Your uncleanness is lewdness. I tried to cleanse you, but you would not become clean. You will not be cleansed of your uncleanness again until I have let my fury rest upon you.
v14: I, the LORD, have spoken. It is coming, and I will act. I will not hold back, spare, or relent. They will judge you according to your ways and your deeds, declares the Lord GOD.”
</p>

## The delight of Ezekiel’s eyes

<p>
v15: The word of the LORD came to me:
v16: “Son of man, I am taking away the delight of your eyes with a sudden blow. You must not mourn or weep or let your tears flow.
v17: Groan in silence. Perform no mourning rites for the dead. Keep your turban bound on and your sandals on your feet. Do not cover your upper lip or eat the bread people bring.”
v18: I spoke to the people in the morning. In the evening my wife died. The next morning I did as I had been commanded.
v19: The people asked me, “Will you not tell us what these things you are doing mean for us?”
v20: I answered, “The word of the LORD came to me:
v21: ‘Tell the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am about to profane my sanctuary, the pride of your strength, the delight of your eyes, what your hearts cherish. The sons and daughters you left behind will fall by the sword.
v22: You will do as I have done. You will not cover your upper lips or eat the bread people bring.
v23: Your turbans will remain on your heads and your sandals on your feet. You will not mourn or weep, but waste away in your guilt and groan to one another.
v24: Ezekiel will be a sign to you. You will do everything he has done. When this happens, you will know that I am the Lord GOD.’”
</p>

## The fugitive and the opened mouth

<p>
v25: “And you, son of man, on the day I take away their stronghold, their joy and splendor, the delight of their eyes and the longing of their hearts, their sons and daughters,
v26: on that day a fugitive will come to you and bring the news to your ears.
v27: On that day your mouth will open when the fugitive comes. You will speak and no longer be silent. You will be a sign to them, and they will know that I am the LORD.”
</p>

## Notes

v05,10,12: Verse 5 says “bones” beneath the pot; “wood” is a proposed correction, not the pinned wording. The cooking command in verse 10 and the wearying effort in verse 12 are difficult; the broader image is of cooking followed by an unsuccessful attempt to remove corrosion.

v16-18,22: The death of Ezekiel’s wife and the restrictions on mourning are stated starkly. The text does not give her name or describe her response. Verse 22’s “as I have done” refers to Ezekiel’s actions within his report of the divine message.

## Vocabulary

v17: **Mourning customs**: The listed headwear, footwear, lip covering, and food concern outward practices of mourning. The command to groan silently does not say that Ezekiel feels no grief.
