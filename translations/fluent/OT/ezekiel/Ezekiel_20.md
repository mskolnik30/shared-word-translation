## The elders’ inquiry

<p>
v01: In the seventh year, on the tenth day of the fifth month, some of Israel’s elders came to consult the LORD and sat down before me.
v02: The word of the LORD came to me:
v03: “Son of man, speak to the elders of Israel. Tell them, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says: Have you come to consult me? As I live, I will not let you consult me, declares the Lord GOD.’
v04: Will you judge them? Will you judge them, son of man? Confront them with their ancestors’ abominations.
</p>

## In Egypt

<p>
v05: Tell them: This is what the Lord GOD says: On the day I chose Israel, I raised my hand in an oath to the descendants of the house of Jacob. I made myself known to them in the land of Egypt and raised my hand to them, saying, ‘I am the LORD your God.’
v06: On that day I raised my hand to them, promising to bring them out of Egypt into a land I had searched out for them, flowing with milk and honey, the glory of all lands.
v07: I told them, ‘Each of you, throw away the detestable things your eyes are drawn to. Do not defile yourselves with Egypt’s idols. I am the LORD your God.’
v08: But they rebelled against me and refused to listen. None of them threw away the detestable things their eyes were drawn to, and they did not abandon Egypt’s idols. So I said I would pour out my fury on them and spend my anger against them in the land of Egypt.
v09: But I acted for the sake of my name, so it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations among whom they lived. In those nations’ sight I had made myself known to Israel by bringing them out of Egypt.
</p>

## In the wilderness

<p>
v10: I brought them out of Egypt and led them into the wilderness.
v11: I gave them my statutes and made my judgments known to them: whoever carries them out will live by them.
v12: I also gave them my Sabbaths as a sign between me and them, so they would know that I am the LORD who makes them holy.
v13: But the house of Israel rebelled against me in the wilderness. They did not live by my statutes and rejected my judgments, by which a person who carries them out will live. They greatly profaned my Sabbaths. So I said I would pour out my fury on them in the wilderness and destroy them.
v14: But I acted for the sake of my name, so it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations who had seen me bring them out.
v15: I also raised my hand in an oath to them in the wilderness that I would not bring them into the land I had given them, flowing with milk and honey, the glory of all lands.
v16: They rejected my judgments, did not live by my statutes, and profaned my Sabbaths, because their hearts followed their idols.
v17: Yet my eye spared them from destruction, and I did not make an end of them in the wilderness.
</p>

## The next generation

<p>
v18: I told their children in the wilderness, ‘Do not live by your ancestors’ statutes or keep their judgments. Do not defile yourselves with their idols.
v19: I am the LORD your God. Live by my statutes, keep my judgments, and carry them out.
v20: Keep my Sabbaths holy. They will be a sign between me and you, so you will know that I am the LORD your God.’
v21: But the children rebelled against me. They did not live by my statutes or keep my judgments by carrying them out, though a person who carries them out will live by them. They profaned my Sabbaths. So I said I would pour out my fury on them and spend my anger against them in the wilderness.
v22: But I drew back my hand and acted for the sake of my name, so it would not be profaned in the sight of the nations who had seen me bring them out.
v23: I also raised my hand in an oath to them in the wilderness that I would disperse them among the nations and scatter them through other lands,
v24: because they did not carry out my judgments, rejected my statutes, and profaned my Sabbaths. Their eyes followed their ancestors’ idols.
v25: I also gave them statutes that were not good and judgments by which they could not live.
v26: I defiled them through their gifts, as they made every firstborn pass through the fire, so that I might devastate them, so that they would know that I am the LORD.
</p>

## In the land

<p>
v27: “Therefore, son of man, speak to the house of Israel. Tell them: This is what the Lord GOD says: In this too your ancestors insulted me by acting unfaithfully toward me.
v28: I brought them into the land I had raised my hand to give them. Yet whenever they saw a high hill or a leafy tree, there they offered their sacrifices, there they presented their provoking offerings, there they placed their pleasing aromas, and there they poured out their drink offerings.
v29: I asked them, ‘What is this high place you go to?’ So it has been called Bamah to this day.
v30: Therefore tell the house of Israel: This is what the Lord GOD says: Are you defiling yourselves in the ways of your ancestors, whoring after their detestable things?
v31: When you bring your gifts and make your children pass through the fire, you defile yourselves with all your idols to this day. Should I let you consult me, house of Israel? As I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will not let you consult me.
v32: What rises in your minds will never happen. You say, ‘We will be like the nations, like the peoples of other lands, serving wood and stone.’
</p>

## Gathering and judgment

<p>
v33: “As I live, declares the Lord GOD, I will surely reign over you with a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and poured-out fury.
v34: I will bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands where you have been scattered, with a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and poured-out fury.
v35: I will bring you into the wilderness of the peoples and enter into judgment with you there, face to face.
v36: As I entered into judgment with your ancestors in the wilderness of Egypt, so I will enter into judgment with you, declares the Lord GOD.
v37: I will make you pass under the rod and bring you into the bond of the covenant.
v38: I will purge from among you those who rebel and transgress against me. I will bring them out of the land where they reside, but they will not enter the land of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
v39: “As for you, house of Israel, this is what the Lord GOD says: Go, each of you, and serve your idols! But afterward you will surely listen to me, and you will no longer profane my holy name with your gifts and your idols.
v40: For on my holy mountain, on Israel’s high mountain, declares the Lord GOD, the whole house of Israel, all of them, will serve me in the land. There I will accept them, and there I will require your contributions, your choicest gifts, and all your holy offerings.
v41: I will accept you as a pleasing aroma when I bring you out from the peoples and gather you from the lands where you have been scattered. Through you I will show my holiness in the sight of the nations.
v42: Then you will know that I am the LORD when I bring you into the land of Israel, the land I raised my hand to give your ancestors.
v43: There you will remember your ways and all the deeds by which you defiled yourselves. You will loathe yourselves for all the evil you have done.
v44: You will know that I am the LORD when I deal with you for the sake of my name, rather than according to your evil ways and corrupt deeds, house of Israel, declares the Lord GOD.”
</p>

## Fire in the south

<p>
v45: The word of the LORD came to me:
v46: “Son of man, turn your face southward. Speak out against the south and prophesy against the forest of the Negeb.
v47: Tell the forest of the Negeb: Hear the word of the LORD! This is what the Lord GOD says: I am kindling a fire in you that will consume every green tree and every dry tree. Its blazing flame will not be quenched, and every face from south to north will be scorched by it.
v48: All flesh will see that I, the LORD, have kindled it. It will not be quenched.”
v49: I said, “Ah, Lord GOD! They say about me, ‘Is he not just a teller of parables?’”
</p>

## Notes

v25-26: The text directly attributes not-good statutes and defilement through offerings to God. These claims remain alongside the accusations of rebellion. “Through the fire” in verse 26 is supplied from the explicit wording in verse 31.

v29,39: Bamah means “high place”; the question plays on its sound. Verse 39 can be read as an assurance of future listening, as here, or as a conditional refusal to listen; the Hebrew is compressed.

v45-49: These verses correspond to 21:1–5 in the pinned Hebrew numbering. Public chapter 21 begins at Hebrew 21:6.

## Vocabulary

v05-06: **Raised my hand**: A repeated oath gesture. Retaining the hand language links the promises and judgments across the chapter.
