## Against Tyre

<p>
v01: In the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, the word of the LORD came to me:
v02: “Son of man, Tyre said about Jerusalem, ‘Aha! The gateway of the peoples is broken! It has turned toward me. Now that she is ruined, I will be filled.’
v03: Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I am against you, Tyre. I will bring many nations up against you, as the sea brings up its waves.
v04: They will destroy Tyre’s walls and tear down her towers. I will scrape away her soil and make her a bare rock.
v05: She will be a place for spreading nets out in the sea, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD. She will become plunder for the nations.
v06: Her daughter towns on the mainland will be killed by the sword. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
</p>

## The siege

<p>
v07: “For this is what the Lord GOD says: I am bringing Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon against Tyre from the north, a king of kings, with horses, chariots, cavalry, and a great mass of troops.
v08: He will kill your daughter towns on the mainland by the sword. He will build siegeworks against you, heap up a ramp, and raise a shield against you.
v09: He will direct the blows of his battering ram against your walls and tear down your towers with his weapons.
v10: His many horses will cover you with dust. Your walls will shake at the sound of cavalry, wheels, and chariots as he enters your gates, as men enter a breached city.
v11: His horses’ hooves will trample all your streets. He will kill your people by the sword, and your mighty pillars will fall to the ground.
v12: They will plunder your wealth and loot your goods. They will tear down your walls and demolish your fine houses, throwing your stones, timber, and soil into the water.
v13: I will silence the sound of your songs; the music of your lyres will be heard no more.
v14: I will make you a bare rock, a place for spreading nets. You will never be rebuilt, for I, the LORD, have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.
</p>

## The coastlands tremble

<p>
v15: “This is what the Lord GOD says to Tyre: Will the coastlands not shake at the sound of your fall, as the wounded groan and slaughter takes place within you?
v16: All the princes of the sea will step down from their thrones, remove their robes, and strip off their embroidered clothes. Clothed in trembling, they will sit on the ground, trembling every moment, appalled at you.
v17: They will take up a lament over you and say:
‘How you have perished,
you who were settled from the seas,
the celebrated city,
mighty on the sea—
you and your inhabitants,
who spread terror to all who lived there!’
v18: ‘Now the coastlands tremble
on the day you fall;
the islands in the sea
are dismayed at your passing.’
v19: “For this is what the Lord GOD says: When I make you a ruined city, like cities no one inhabits, when I bring the deep up over you and the great waters cover you,
v20: I will bring you down with those who descend to the pit, to the people of long ago. I will settle you in the world below, among ancient ruins, with those who descend to the pit, so you will not be inhabited. But I will set splendor in the land of the living.
v21: I will make you a terror, and you will be no more. People will search for you, but you will never again be found, declares the Lord GOD.”
</p>

## Notes

v1: The Hebrew gives the year and day but does not name the month.

v6,8: A city’s “daughters” are its dependent settlements. The language of killing these daughters represents attacks on those communities.

v9: The siege equipment is described with rare terms; the exact forms of the weapons are uncertain.

v11: The pillars are monuments of the city’s strength; the wording does not identify them more precisely.

v12: The Hebrew moves from a singular attacker to plural attackers. The shift is retained.

v17: “Settled from the seas” renders a difficult expression that may describe the city’s seafaring population.

v20: The final clause in the pinned Hebrew is positive: God will set splendor in the land of the living. Readings that make it another denial of Tyre’s return require a different understanding or alteration of the text.

## Vocabulary

v14: **Bare rock**: The image connects the ruined city with a place for spreading fishing nets.

v20: **The pit**: The realm of the dead, pictured below the land of the living.
