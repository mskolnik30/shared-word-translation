## The ruler of Tyre

<p>
v01: The word of the LORD came to me:
v02: “Son of man, tell the ruler of Tyre: This is what the Lord GOD says: Your heart grew proud, and you said, ‘I am a god. I sit on a divine throne in the heart of the seas.’ Yet you are human, not a god, though you consider your mind equal to a god’s.
v03: So you are wiser than Daniel! No secret is hidden from you!
v04: By your wisdom and understanding you acquired wealth, gathering gold and silver into your treasuries.
v05: Through your great skill in trading you increased your wealth, and your wealth made your heart proud.
v06: “Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you consider your mind equal to a god’s,
v07: I am bringing foreigners against you, the most ruthless of nations. They will draw their swords against the beauty of your wisdom and profane your splendor.
v08: They will bring you down to the pit. You will die the death of the slain in the heart of the seas.
v09: Will you still say, ‘I am a god,’ before the one killing you? You are human, not a god, in the hands of those who wound you.
v10: You will die the death of the uncircumcised at the hands of foreigners, for I have spoken, declares the Lord GOD.”
</p>

## A lament for the king

<p>
v11: The word of the LORD came to me:
v12: “Son of man, take up a lament over the king of Tyre. Tell him: This is what the Lord GOD says:
‘You were the seal of perfection,
full of wisdom and perfect in beauty.
v13: You were in Eden, the garden of God.
Every precious stone covered you:
carnelian, topaz, and diamond,
beryl, onyx, and jasper,
sapphire, turquoise, and emerald, with gold.
Your settings and mountings were prepared
on the day you were created.
v14: You were an anointed guardian cherub,
and I placed you there.
You were on God’s holy mountain,
walking among stones of fire.
v15: You were blameless in your ways
from the day you were created
until wrongdoing was found in you.
v16: Through your abundant trade
you were filled with violence, and you sinned.
So I expelled you in disgrace from God’s mountain;
I destroyed you, guardian cherub,
from among the stones of fire.
v17: Your beauty made your heart proud;
you ruined your wisdom for the sake of your splendor.
I threw you to the ground
and set you before kings to stare at you.
v18: By your many wrongs and dishonest trade
you profaned your sanctuaries.
So I brought fire out from within you,
and it consumed you.
I turned you to ashes on the ground
in the sight of everyone watching.
v19: All who know you among the peoples
are appalled at you.
You have become a terror,
and you will be no more forever.’”
</p>

## Against Sidon

<p>
v20: The word of the LORD came to me:
v21: “Son of man, turn your face toward Sidon and prophesy against her.
v22: Say: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am against you, Sidon. I will display my glory within you. They will know that I am the LORD when I carry out judgments in her and show myself holy within her.
v23: I will send pestilence into her and blood into her streets. The slain will fall within her as the sword strikes from every side. Then they will know that I am the LORD.
v24: “The house of Israel will no longer have a pricking brier or a painful thorn among all the neighbors who despise them. Then they will know that I am the Lord GOD.
</p>

## Israel gathered home

<p>
v25: “This is what the Lord GOD says: When I gather the house of Israel from the peoples among whom they were scattered, I will show myself holy through them in the sight of the nations. They will live on their own soil, which I gave to my servant Jacob.
v26: They will live there securely, building houses and planting vineyards. They will live securely when I carry out judgments against all their neighbors who despise them. Then they will know that I am the LORD their God.”
</p>

## Notes

v3: The Hebrew writes Danel and supplies Daniel as its reading. The identity of the wise figure invoked in this taunt is disputed.

v12: This lament is explicitly addressed to the king of Tyre. Its Eden and cherub imagery is retained without supplying another named character.

v13: The gemstone names are approximate. The terms rendered as settings and mountings are difficult and can elsewhere name musical instruments.

v14,16: The pinned pointing addresses the ruler as a guardian cherub. Another interpretation places him with a cherub. Verse 16 retains the divine speaker’s action and address to the cherub.

## Vocabulary

v2: **Heart of the seas**: An image of Tyre’s offshore position and claimed security.

v14: **Cherub**: A heavenly guardian figure, also associated with God’s throne elsewhere in Ezekiel.
