## A day for the nations

<p>
v01: The word of the LORD came to me:
v02: “Son of man, prophesy. Say: This is what the Lord GOD says:
‘Wail, “Alas for the day!”
v03: For a day is near,
the day of the LORD is near—
a day of clouds,
a time for the nations.
v04: A sword will come upon Egypt,
and anguish will seize Cush
when the slain fall in Egypt,
its wealth is carried off,
and its foundations are torn down.
v05: Cush, Put, Lud, all the mixed peoples, Cub, and the people of the covenant land will fall with them by the sword.
v06: “‘This is what the LORD says: Egypt’s supporters will fall, and the pride of its strength will sink. From Migdol to Syene they will fall within it by the sword, declares the Lord GOD.
v07: They will lie desolate among desolate lands, and its cities will stand among ruined cities.
v08: Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I set fire to Egypt and all its helpers are broken.
v09: On that day messengers will go out from me in ships to terrify unsuspecting Cush. Anguish will seize them on Egypt’s day. Look, it is coming!
</p>

## Egypt’s cities fall

<p>
v10: “‘This is what the Lord GOD says: I will put an end to Egypt’s multitude through Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.
v11: He and his people, the most ruthless of nations, will be brought in to destroy the land. They will draw their swords against Egypt and fill the land with the slain.
v12: I will dry up the Nile channels and sell the land into the hands of evil men. Through foreigners I will lay waste the land and everything in it. I, the LORD, have spoken.
v13: “‘This is what the Lord GOD says: I will destroy the idols and put an end to the worthless gods of Memphis. There will no longer be a prince from Egypt, and I will spread fear through Egypt.
v14: I will lay Pathros waste, set fire to Zoan, and carry out judgments in Thebes.
v15: I will pour out my fury on Pelusium, Egypt’s stronghold, and cut off the multitude of Thebes.
v16: I will set fire to Egypt. Pelusium will writhe in anguish; Thebes will be breached, and Memphis will face enemies in broad daylight.
v17: The young men of On and Pi-beseth will fall by the sword, and the cities will go into captivity.
v18: At Tahpanhes daylight will darken when I break Egypt’s yoke bars there and put an end to its proud strength. A cloud will cover the city, and its daughters will go into captivity.
v19: I will carry out judgments against Egypt. Then they will know that I am the LORD.’”
</p>

## The broken arms

<p>
v20: In the eleventh year, on the seventh day of the first month, the word of the LORD came to me:
v21: “Son of man, I have broken Pharaoh king of Egypt’s arm. It has not been bound up for healing or wrapped in a bandage to give it strength to grip a sword.
v22: Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt. I will break both his arms, the strong one and the broken one, and make the sword fall from his hand.
v23: I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the lands.
v24: I will strengthen the arms of Babylon’s king and put my sword in his hand. I will break Pharaoh’s arms, and he will groan before him like a mortally wounded man.
v25: I will strengthen the arms of Babylon’s king, while Pharaoh’s arms fall limp. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I put my sword into Babylon’s king’s hand and he stretches it out against Egypt.
v26: I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations and disperse them through the lands. Then they will know that I am the LORD.”
</p>

## Notes

v5: Cub is not securely identified. “People of the covenant land” is also uncertain; the phrase does not explicitly identify the group.

v13–18: The Egyptian place names follow familiar English forms: Memphis for Noph, Thebes for No, Pelusium for Sin, and On for Aven. Aven may also carry the sense of trouble or wickedness. Pi-beseth and Tahpanhes are retained.

v16: The final expression about Memphis and daytime enemies is difficult; the draft preserves that image rather than supplying a reconstructed battle scene.

v17–18: The feminine language can represent the cities or their people. A city’s daughters can refer to its dependent settlements.

## Vocabulary

v3: **Day of the LORD**: A time of the LORD’s decisive action, described here as darkness and judgment upon nations.

v21: **Broken arm**: An image of Pharaoh’s defeated military power.
