## The city of bloodshed

<p>
v01: The word of the LORD came to me:
v02: “And you, son of man, will you judge? Will you judge the city of bloodshed? Confront her with all her abominations.
v03: Say, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says: City that sheds blood within herself, bringing her time near, and makes idols to defile herself!
v04: You have become guilty through the blood you shed and unclean through the idols you made. You have brought your days near and reached your appointed years. So I have made you an object of scorn to the nations and ridicule to every land.
v05: Those near and far will mock you, you of defiled name, full of turmoil.
v06: “‘Look at Israel’s princes among you, each using his power to shed blood.
v07: Among you father and mother are treated with contempt. Within you the resident foreigner is extorted; among you the orphan and widow are oppressed.
v08: You despise my holy things and profane my Sabbaths.
v09: Among you are slanderers who cause bloodshed. Among you people eat on the mountains and practice lewdness.
v10: Among you men uncover their fathers’ nakedness and violate women during their menstrual uncleanness.
v11: One commits an abomination with his neighbor’s wife; another lewdly defiles his daughter-in-law; another violates his sister, his father’s daughter, among you.
v12: Among you bribes are taken to shed blood. You take interest and profit, extort your neighbors for gain, and forget me, declares the Lord GOD.
v13: “‘I strike my hands together at the dishonest gain you have made and the bloodshed within you.
v14: Will your heart endure? Will your hands remain strong in the days when I deal with you? I, the LORD, have spoken, and I will do it.
v15: I will disperse you among the nations, scatter you through other lands, and put an end to your uncleanness.
v16: You will be profaned through your own doing in the sight of the nations. Then you will know that I am the LORD.’”
</p>

## Dross in the furnace

<p>
v17: The word of the LORD came to me:
v18: “Son of man, the house of Israel has become dross to me. All of them are copper, tin, iron, and lead in a furnace; they have become the dross of silver.
v19: Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: Because you have all become dross, I am gathering you into Jerusalem.
v20: As silver, copper, iron, lead, and tin are gathered into a furnace and fire is blown upon them to melt them, so I will gather you in my anger and fury. I will put you in and melt you.
v21: I will gather you and blow upon you with the fire of my fury, and you will be melted within the city.
v22: As silver is melted in a furnace, so you will be melted within it. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have poured out my fury upon you.”
</p>

## No one in the breach

<p>
v23: The word of the LORD came to me:
v24: “Son of man, tell her, ‘You are a land not cleansed, not rained upon on the day of indignation.’
v25: The conspiracy of her prophets within her is like a roaring lion tearing prey. They devour lives, seize wealth and precious things, and multiply widows within her.
v26: Her priests violate my instruction and profane my holy things. They make no distinction between holy and common, and teach no distinction between unclean and clean. They shut their eyes to my Sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.
v27: Her officials within her are like wolves tearing prey, shedding blood and destroying lives for dishonest gain.
v28: Her prophets coat things with whitewash for them, seeing empty visions and giving them lying divinations. They say, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says,’ when the LORD has not spoken.
v29: The people of the land practice extortion and robbery. They oppress the poor and needy and extort the resident foreigner, denying justice.
v30: I looked among them for someone to build up the wall and stand in the breach before me on behalf of the land, so that I would not destroy it. But I found no one.
v31: So I poured out my indignation on them and consumed them with the fire of my fury. I brought their ways down on their own heads, declares the Lord GOD.”
</p>

## Notes

v10-11: “Uncovering a father’s nakedness” is a sexual kinship idiom. The verbs describing treatment of the menstruating woman and the sister convey violation or humiliation; the translation does not neutralize them as ordinary consensual relations.

v16,25: Verse 16 is difficult; “be profaned” is followed here rather than an interpretation involving possession. Verse 25 reads “prophets” in the pinned text; “princes” is a proposed alternative not adopted here.

## Vocabulary

v26: **Holy/common; unclean/clean**: These are two distinct contrasts in the priestly instruction, not interchangeable pairs.
