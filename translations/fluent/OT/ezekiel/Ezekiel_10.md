## Fire from among the cherubim

<p>
v01: I looked: above the expanse over the cherubim’s heads appeared something like sapphire, resembling the likeness of a throne.
v02: He told the man dressed in linen, “Go in among the whirling wheels beneath the cherub. Fill your hands with burning coals from between the cherubim and scatter them over the city.” He went in as I watched.
v03: The cherubim stood on the right side of the house when the man went in, and the cloud filled the inner court.
v04: The glory of the LORD rose from above the cherub to the threshold of the house. The cloud filled the house, and the brightness of the LORD’s glory filled the court.
v05: The sound of the cherubim’s wings could be heard as far as the outer court, like the voice of God Almighty when he speaks.
v06: When he commanded the man dressed in linen, “Take fire from among the whirling wheels, from between the cherubim,” the man went in and stood beside a wheel.
v07: A cherub reached out from among the cherubim toward the fire between them, took some, and placed it in the hands of the man dressed in linen. He took it and went out.
v08: Beneath the cherubim’s wings there appeared the form of human hands.
</p>

## Wheels and living creatures

<p>
v09: I looked and saw four wheels beside the cherubim: one wheel beside one cherub, another beside another. The wheels gleamed like beryl.
v10: All four had the same likeness, as though one wheel were set within another.
v11: When they moved, they went in any of their four directions without turning. They followed wherever the head faced, without turning as they went.
v12: Their whole bodies, their backs, hands, wings, and wheels were full of eyes all around—the wheels belonging to all four.
v13: I heard the wheels being called “the whirling wheels.”
v14: Each had four faces: the first was a cherub’s face, the second a human face, the third a lion’s face, and the fourth an eagle’s face.
v15: The cherubim rose. These were the living creatures I had seen beside the Kebar River.
v16: When the cherubim moved, the wheels moved beside them. When the cherubim lifted their wings to rise from the ground, the wheels did not turn away from their side.
v17: When the creatures stood still, the wheels stood still; when the creatures rose, the wheels rose with them, because the spirit of the living creatures was in them.
</p>

## The glory at the east gate

<p>
v18: Then the glory of the LORD left the threshold of the house and stood above the cherubim.
v19: As I watched, the cherubim lifted their wings and rose from the ground, with the wheels beside them. They stopped at the entrance of the east gate of the LORD’s house, with the glory of the God of Israel above them.
v20: These were the living creatures I had seen beneath the God of Israel beside the Kebar River. I now knew they were cherubim.
v21: Each had four faces and four wings, with the likeness of human hands beneath its wings.
v22: Their faces were the same faces I had seen beside the Kebar River—the same appearance, the same creatures. Each went straight ahead.
</p>

## Notes

v14: The first face is called a cherub’s face here, while 1:10 names an ox’s face. The translation keeps the difference even though verses 20–22 identify the creatures as the same ones.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Cherubim**: Plural of cherub. These are the living creatures Ezekiel identifies in verse 20, not the small winged children familiar from later art.

v13: **Whirling wheels**: Hebrew galgal distinguishes the revolving wheelwork from the ordinary word for a wheel, ofan.
