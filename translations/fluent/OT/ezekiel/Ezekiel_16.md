## Jerusalem’s beginnings

<p>
v01: The word of the LORD came to me:
v02: “Son of man, confront Jerusalem with her abominations.
v03: Say, ‘This is what the Lord GOD says to Jerusalem: Your origins and your birth were in the land of the Canaanites. Your father was an Amorite and your mother a Hittite.
v04: On the day you were born, your cord was not cut. You were not washed with water to clean you, rubbed with salt, or wrapped in cloths.
v05: No eye pitied you enough to do even one of these things for you. Instead, you were thrown into the open field because your life was despised on the day you were born.
v06: “‘I passed by and saw you kicking in your blood. I said to you in your blood, “Live!” I said to you in your blood, “Live!”
v07: I made you flourish like the growth of the field. You grew and became tall and reached full womanhood. Your breasts developed and your hair grew, but you were naked and bare.
v08: I passed by and saw that your time had come, the time for love. I spread the edge of my garment over you and covered your nakedness. I swore an oath to you and entered into a covenant with you, declares the Lord GOD, and you became mine.
v09: I washed you with water, rinsed your blood from you, and anointed you with oil.
v10: I clothed you in embroidered fabric and put sandals of fine leather on your feet. I wrapped you in fine linen and covered you in costly cloth.
v11: I adorned you with ornaments, putting bracelets on your wrists and a necklace around your neck.
v12: I put a ring in your nose, earrings on your ears, and a splendid crown on your head.
v13: You were adorned with gold and silver; your clothing was fine linen, costly cloth, and embroidery. You ate fine flour, honey, and oil. You became exceedingly beautiful and rose to royalty.
v14: Your beauty made your name known among the nations. It was perfect because of the splendor I had placed upon you, declares the Lord GOD.
</p>

<p>

## Mother and sisters

<p>
v44: “‘Everyone who uses proverbs will use this one about you: “Like mother, like daughter.”
v45: You are your mother’s daughter: she loathed her husband and children. You are your sisters’ sister: they loathed their husbands and children. Your mother was a Hittite and your father an Amorite.
v46: Your older sister is Samaria, who lives to your left with her daughters. Your younger sister, who lives to your right, is Sodom with her daughters.
v47: You did not merely follow their ways or practice their abominations. As though that were too little, you became more corrupt than they were in all your ways.
v48: As I live, declares the Lord GOD, your sister Sodom and her daughters never did what you and your daughters have done.
v49: This was the guilt of your sister Sodom: she and her daughters had pride, plenty of food, and untroubled ease, but they did not strengthen the hand of the poor and needy.
v50: They grew haughty and committed an abomination before me, so I removed them when I saw it.
v51: Samaria did not commit half your sins. You multiplied your abominations beyond theirs and made your sisters appear righteous by all the abominations you committed.
v52: Bear your disgrace, you who have passed judgment on your sisters. Your sins are more abominable than theirs, so they appear more righteous than you. Be ashamed and bear your disgrace, for you have made your sisters appear righteous.
v53: “‘I will restore their fortunes—the fortunes of Sodom and her daughters and the fortunes of Samaria and her daughters—and restore your fortunes among them,
v54: so that you may bear your disgrace and be ashamed of all you have done, bringing them consolation.
v55: Your sisters, Sodom and her daughters, will return to their former state; Samaria and her daughters will return to their former state; and you and your daughters will return to yours.
v56: You would not even mention your sister Sodom in the days of your pride,
v57: before your own wickedness was exposed. Now you are scorned by the daughters of Aram and all her neighbors, the daughters of the Philistines, who despise you on every side.
v58: You must bear your lewdness and your abominations, declares the LORD.
</p>

## The remembered covenant

<p>
v59: “‘For this is what the Lord GOD says: I will deal with you as you have dealt, you who despised the oath by breaking the covenant.
v60: Yet I will remember my covenant with you in the days of your youth and establish an everlasting covenant with you.
v61: Then you will remember your ways and be ashamed when you receive your sisters, both older and younger. I will give them to you as daughters, though not on the basis of your covenant.
v62: I myself will establish my covenant with you, and you will know that I am the LORD,
v63: so that you may remember and be ashamed, unable to open your mouth again because of your disgrace, when I make atonement for you for everything you have done, declares the Lord GOD.’”
</p>

## Notes

v03,45-46: Jerusalem, Samaria, and Sodom are personified as female relatives. Their “daughters” are associated communities. Left and right correspond to north and south when facing east; the parentage belongs to the allegory.

v07,10: “Full womanhood” renders the difficult phrase literally meaning “ornament of ornaments” in a context of bodily maturation. The animal skin and costly textile in verse 10 are also uncertain; no precise species or fabric is asserted.

v21: “Through the fire” supplies the setting of the passing-over expression from the child-sacrifice context; “fire” is not explicit in this verse, though it is in 20:31.

v26,30,36: Verse 26 literally calls the Egyptians “great of flesh,” understood sexually here. The heart expression in verse 30 is uncertain, variously understood as weak, feverish, or impetuous. The noun in verse 36 ordinarily means copper; “lust” is a contextual interpretation, while expenditure of wealth is another possibility.

v43,47: The last clause of verse 43 is rendered as a rhetorical question; it can instead be read as a prohibition against further lewdness. Verse 47’s short phrase about “little” is understood to intensify the comparison with the sisters.

## Vocabulary

v08,60-63: **Covenant**: The relationship sworn in verse 8 is remembered and reestablished in verses 60–63. The translation retains both the covenant and the difficult statements about shame and atonement.
