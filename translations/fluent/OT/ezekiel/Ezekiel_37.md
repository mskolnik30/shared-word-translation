## The valley of bones

<p>
v01: The hand of the LORD came upon me. He brought me out by the spirit of the LORD and set me down in the middle of a valley full of bones.
v02: He led me all around them. There were very many bones across the valley, and they were very dry.
v03: He asked me, “Son of man, can these bones live?” I answered, “Lord GOD, you know.”
v04: Then he told me, “Prophesy over these bones. Say to them, ‘Dry bones, hear the word of the LORD!
v05: This is what the Lord GOD says to these bones: I am bringing breath into you, and you will live.
v06: I will put sinews on you, bring flesh upon you, cover you with skin, and put breath into you. You will live, and you will know that I am the LORD.’”
v07: So I prophesied as I was commanded. As I prophesied, there was a sound—a rattling—and the bones came together, bone to its bone.
v08: I looked: sinews were on them, flesh had grown, and skin covered them. But there was no breath in them.
v09: He told me, “Prophesy to the breath. Prophesy, son of man, and say to the breath: This is what the Lord GOD says: Come from the four winds, breath, and breathe into these slain, so they may live.”
v10: I prophesied as he commanded me. Breath entered them; they lived and stood on their feet, an immense army.
v11: He told me, “Son of man, these bones are the whole house of Israel. They say, ‘Our bones have dried up, our hope is lost, and we are cut off.’
v12: Therefore prophesy and tell them: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am opening your graves and bringing you up from them, my people. I will bring you to Israel’s soil.
v13: Then you will know that I am the LORD, when I open your graves and bring you up from them, my people.
v14: I will put my spirit within you, and you will live. I will settle you on your own soil. Then you will know that I, the LORD, have spoken and done it, declares the LORD.”
</p>

## Two sticks become one

<p>
v15: The word of the LORD came to me:
v16: “Son of man, take a stick and write on it, ‘For Judah and the Israelites joined to him.’ Then take another stick and write on it, ‘For Joseph—the stick of Ephraim—and all the house of Israel joined to him.’
v17: Bring them together into one stick, so they become one in your hand.
v18: When your people ask, ‘Will you not tell us what these mean?’
v19: tell them: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am taking Joseph’s stick, which is in Ephraim’s hand, and the tribes of Israel joined to him. I will join them to Judah’s stick and make them one stick. They will be one in my hand.
v20: Hold the sticks you have written on before their eyes.
v21: Tell them: This is what the Lord GOD says: I am taking the Israelites from the nations where they have gone. I will gather them from all around and bring them to their own soil.
v22: I will make them one nation in the land, on Israel’s mountains. One king will rule them all. They will no longer be two nations, never again divided into two kingdoms.
v23: They will no longer defile themselves with their idols, detestable things, or any of their rebellions. I will save them from all the places where they have lived and sinned, and I will cleanse them. They will be my people, and I will be their God.
v24: My servant David will be king over them, and they will all have one shepherd. They will follow my judgments, keep my statutes, and carry them out.
v25: They will live in the land I gave my servant Jacob, where your ancestors lived. They, their children, and their children’s children will live there forever, and my servant David will be their prince forever.
v26: I will make a covenant of peace with them, an everlasting covenant. I will establish them and make them numerous, and I will put my sanctuary among them forever.
v27: My dwelling will be over them. I will be their God, and they will be my people.
v28: Then the nations will know that I, the LORD, make Israel holy, when my sanctuary is among them forever.”
</p>

## Notes

v1–14: The Hebrew word ruach can mean spirit, breath, or wind. The movement among these senses connects the vision’s images; English uses different words where the immediate setting calls for them.

v9,11: The bones belong to people described as slain. Verse 11 identifies the bones with the whole house of Israel; the explanation is part of the vision itself.

v23: “Dwellings” follows the pinned text. Other readings understand the phrase in terms of backsliding or unfaithfulness.

v27: The Hebrew places God’s dwelling “over them,” also understood as being among them.

## Vocabulary

v16: **Joseph and Ephraim**: Names representing the northern tribes, contrasted with Judah and then joined with it.

v24: **David**: The name used for the one shepherd and ruler of the reunited people.
