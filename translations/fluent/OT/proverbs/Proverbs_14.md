## Wisdom Builds

<p>
v01: A wise woman builds her house,
but Folly tears it down with her own hands.
v02: Whoever walks uprightly fears the LORD;
whoever twists his ways despises him.
v03: In a fool’s mouth is a rod of pride,
but the lips of the wise protect them.
v04: Where there are no oxen, the feeding trough is empty,
but an ox’s strength brings a plentiful harvest.
v05: A faithful witness does not lie;
a false witness breathes out lies.
</p>

<p>
v06: A scoffer seeks wisdom and finds none,
but knowledge comes readily to the discerning.
v07: Leave the presence of a fool;
you will not find knowledgeable speech there.
v08: The wisdom of the prudent is to understand their way;
the folly of fools is deceit.
v09: Fools mock at guilt,
but among the upright there is goodwill.
v10: The heart knows its own bitterness,
and no outsider can share its joy.
</p>

## The Way That Seems Right

<p>
v11: The house of the wicked will be destroyed,
but the tent of the upright will flourish.
v12: There is a way that seems right to a person,
but its end is the ways of death.
v13: Even in laughter the heart can ache,
and joy can end in sorrow.
v14: The wayward in heart have their fill of their own ways;
a good person has his fill from his own deeds.
v15: The inexperienced believe every word;
the prudent consider their steps.
</p>

<p>
v16: The wise fear and turn away from evil;
a fool rages on in confidence.
v17: A quick-tempered person acts foolishly;
a schemer is hated.
v18: The inexperienced inherit folly;
the prudent are crowned with knowledge.
v19: The evil bow before the good,
and the wicked at the gates of the righteous.
v20: The poor are hated even by their neighbors,
but the rich have many who love them.
</p>

## Compassion for the Poor

<p>
v21: Whoever despises his neighbor sins;
happy is the one who shows mercy to the lowly.
v22: Do not those who plot evil go astray?
Faithful love and faithfulness meet those who plan good.
v23: All hard work brings some gain;
mere talk leads only to need.
v24: The crown of the wise is their wealth;
the folly of fools is still folly.
v25: A truthful witness rescues lives,
but one who breathes out lies is deceitful.
</p>

## The Fear of the LORD

<p>
v26: In the fear of the LORD is strong confidence,
and his children will have a refuge.
v27: The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life,
turning people away from death’s snares.
v28: A large population is a king’s splendor;
without a people, a ruler is ruined.
v29: One slow to anger has great understanding;
a short temper raises folly high.
v30: A tranquil heart gives life to the body,
but jealousy rots the bones.
</p>

<p>
v31: Whoever oppresses the poor insults their Maker;
whoever shows mercy to the needy honors him.
v32: The wicked are brought down by their evil,
but the righteous find refuge even in death.
v33: Wisdom rests in a discerning heart;
even among fools she makes herself known.
v34: Righteousness raises a nation up,
but sin is a disgrace to peoples.
v35: A king favors a servant who acts wisely,
but his fury falls on one who brings shame.
</p>

## Notes

v09,14,26,33: Verse 9 may concern guilt or a guilt offering. Verse 14 leaves the good person’s return compressed; the draft supplies deeds in parallel with ways. “His children” in verse 26 has an uncertain possessor. Verse 33 retains the affirmative statement that wisdom is known even among fools.

## Vocabulary

v31: **Maker**: The Creator of the poor person; treatment of that person is presented as treatment of the Maker.
