## A Father's Instruction

<p>
v01: Sons, listen to a father’s discipline;
pay attention and learn understanding.
v02: For I give you good teaching;
do not abandon my instruction.
v03: When I was a son with my father,
tender and the only one in my mother’s sight,
v04: he taught me and said,
“Let your heart hold fast to my words;
keep my commands and live.
v05: Get wisdom; get understanding.
Do not forget or turn away from what I say.
</p>

<p>
v06: Do not abandon her, and she will guard you;
love her, and she will protect you.
v07: The beginning of wisdom is this: get wisdom!
Whatever else you acquire, get understanding.
v08: Cherish her, and she will raise you up;
embrace her, and she will honor you.
v09: She will place a lovely garland on your head
and present you with a beautiful crown.”
</p>

## The Path of Wisdom

<p>
v10: Listen, my son, and receive my words;
then your years of life will multiply.
v11: I have taught you the way of wisdom;
I have led you on straight paths.
v12: As you walk, your steps will not be cramped;
when you run, you will not stumble.
v13: Hold tightly to discipline; do not let go.
Guard her, for she is your life.
</p>

## Do Not Enter the Path of the Wicked

<p>
v14: Do not enter the path of the wicked;
do not proceed along the way of evildoers.
v15: Avoid it; do not pass along it.
Turn away from it and move on.
v16: For they cannot sleep unless they do evil;
they lose sleep unless they make someone fall.
v17: They eat the bread of wickedness
and drink the wine of violence.
</p>

<p>
v18: But the path of the righteous is like dawn’s light,
shining ever brighter until full day.
v19: The way of the wicked is deep darkness;
they do not know what makes them stumble.
</p>

## Guard Your Heart

<p>
v20: My son, pay attention to my words;
turn your ear to what I say.
v21: Do not let them leave your sight;
keep them within your heart.
v22: For they are life to those who find them
and healing to their whole body.
v23: Above all that you guard, guard your heart,
for from it flow the springs of life.
</p>

<p>
v24: Put twisted speech away from you;
keep devious talk far from your lips.
v25: Let your eyes look straight ahead,
your gaze directly before you.
v26: Make level the path for your feet,
and let all your ways be firm.
v27: Do not turn to the right or the left;
turn your foot away from evil.
</p>

## Notes

v03,07: “The only one” describes the speaker’s place in his mother’s sight. Verse 7 can mean the beginning or the chief requirement of wisdom; its repeated command is to acquire wisdom and understanding.

v16,26: Verse 16 follows the read form “make someone fall.” In verse 26 the verb can mean making a path level or weighing it carefully.

## Vocabulary

v23: **Heart**: The inner center of thought, desire, and decision, not feelings alone.
