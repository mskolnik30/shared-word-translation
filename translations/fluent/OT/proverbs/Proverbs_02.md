## Seek Wisdom as Hidden Treasure

<p>
v01: My son, if you receive my words
and store up my commands within you,
v02: making your ear attentive to wisdom
and turning your heart toward understanding,
v03: if you call out for insight
and raise your voice for understanding,
v04: if you seek it like silver
and search for it like buried treasure,
v05: then you will understand the fear of the LORD
and find the knowledge of God.
</p>

## The LORD Gives Wisdom

<p>
v06: For the LORD gives wisdom;
knowledge and understanding come from his mouth.
v07: He stores up sound wisdom for the upright;
he is a shield for those who walk with integrity,
v08: guarding the paths of justice
and protecting the way of his faithful ones.
v09: Then you will understand righteousness, justice, and fairness—
every good path.
</p>

## Wisdom Will Guard You

<p>
v10: For wisdom will enter your heart,
and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.
v11: Discretion will watch over you;
understanding will guard you,
v12: rescuing you from the way of evil,
from the man whose speech is twisted,
v13: from those who leave straight paths
to walk along dark ways,
v14: who rejoice in doing evil
and delight in evil’s perversity,
v15: whose paths are crooked
and whose courses are devious.
</p>

## Rescue from the Strange Woman

<p>
v16: Wisdom will also rescue you from the forbidden woman,
from the outsider whose words are smooth,
v17: who abandons the companion of her youth
and forgets the covenant of her God.
v18: Her house sinks toward death,
and her paths lead to the shades of the dead.
v19: None who go to her return
or reach the paths of life.
</p>

## Walk in the Way of the Good

<p>
v20: So you will walk in the way of the good
and keep to the paths of the righteous.
v21: For the upright will dwell in the land,
and those of integrity will remain in it;
v22: but the wicked will be cut off from the land,
and the faithless will be uprooted from it.
</p>

## Notes

v16–17: The “forbidden woman” is literally a strange or outsider woman. The companion and covenant language makes sexual unfaithfulness central; the wording does not require that she be ethnically foreign.

## Vocabulary

v18: **Shades of the dead**: The Hebrew rephaim names the dead as inhabitants of the underworld.
