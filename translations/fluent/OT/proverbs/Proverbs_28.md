## The Righteous Are Bold

<p>
v01: The wicked flee when no one pursues,
but the righteous are confident as a young lion.
v02: When a land rebels, its rulers multiply;
with an understanding, knowledgeable person, order endures.
v03: A poor man who oppresses the needy
is like a sweeping rain that leaves no bread.
v04: Those who abandon instruction praise the wicked;
those who keep instruction contend with them.
v05: Evil men do not understand justice;
those who seek the LORD understand it fully.
</p>

<p>
v06: Better a poor person who walks with integrity
than a rich person whose ways are crooked.
v07: A son who keeps instruction has understanding;
a companion of gluttons brings his father shame.
v08: Whoever increases wealth through interest and extra charges
gathers it for someone who shows mercy to the poor.
v09: Whoever turns his ear away from hearing instruction—
even his prayer is detestable.
v10: Whoever leads the upright astray onto an evil road
will fall into his own pit,
but those of integrity will inherit good.
</p>

## Confession and Mercy

<p>
v11: A rich man is wise in his own eyes,
but a poor person with understanding sees through him.
v12: When the righteous triumph, there is great glory;
when the wicked rise, people hide.
v13: Whoever covers his offenses will not prosper;
whoever confesses and abandons them will receive mercy.
v14: Happy is the person who remains reverent,
but one who hardens his heart falls into trouble.
v15: A roaring lion, a charging bear—
such is a wicked ruler over a poor people.
</p>

## Faithfulness and Greed

<p>
v16: A ruler without understanding is a great oppressor;
one who hates unjust gain will prolong his days.
v17: A person burdened with another’s lifeblood
will flee to the Pit; let no one support him.
v18: Whoever walks with integrity will be delivered;
one whose ways are crooked will fall suddenly.
v19: Whoever works his land has enough bread;
whoever chases empty pursuits has enough poverty.
v20: A trustworthy person has abundant blessings;
one who hurries to grow rich will not go unpunished.
</p>

<p>
v21: Showing partiality is not good,
yet a man may do wrong for a piece of bread.
v22: A stingy man hurries after wealth,
unaware that need is coming upon him.
v23: Whoever corrects a person will afterward find more favor
than one who flatters with his tongue.
v24: Whoever robs his father or mother and says, “It is no offense,”
is companion to a destroyer.
</p>

## Trust the LORD, Not Yourself

<p>
v25: A greedy person stirs up conflict,
but one who trusts in the LORD will flourish.
v26: Whoever trusts his own heart is a fool;
whoever walks in wisdom will escape.
v27: Whoever gives to the poor will lack nothing;
whoever closes his eyes will have many curses.
v28: When the wicked rise, people hide;
when they perish, the righteous increase.
</p>

## Notes

v03,14,18: Verse 3 retains “poor man” as the oppressor. Verse 14 describes continual fear without naming its object; “reverent” reads it in the book’s wisdom setting. Verse 18 may mean falling suddenly or falling in one of the crooked ways.

## Vocabulary

v04,09: **Instruction**: Torah: authoritative teaching that is heard and kept. Here it is the measure of conduct and even of prayer.
