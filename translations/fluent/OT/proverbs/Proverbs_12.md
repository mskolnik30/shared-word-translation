## Whoever Loves Discipline Loves Knowledge

<p>
v01: Whoever loves discipline loves knowledge;
whoever hates correction is brutish.
v02: A good person obtains favor from the LORD,
but he condemns a schemer.
v03: No one is established through wickedness,
but the root of the righteous will not be shaken.
v04: A capable wife is her husband’s crown;
one who brings shame is rot in his bones.
</p>

<p>
v05: The plans of the righteous are just;
the schemes of the wicked are deceitful.
v06: The words of the wicked lie in wait for blood,
but the mouth of the upright rescues them.
v07: The wicked are overthrown and are no more,
but the house of the righteous stands.
v08: A man is praised according to his good judgment,
but one with a twisted heart is despised.
</p>

## Diligence and Care

<p>
v09: Better to be lightly esteemed and have a servant
than to put on importance and lack bread.
v10: The righteous know the needs of their animals,
but even the compassion of the wicked is cruel.
v11: Whoever works his land has enough bread;
whoever chases empty pursuits lacks sense.
v12: The wicked covet the catch of evildoers,
but the root of the righteous yields fruit.
</p>

<p>
v13: An evil person is snared by sinful speech,
but the righteous come out of trouble.
v14: From the fruit of his mouth a person is filled with good,
and the work of his hands is repaid to him.
v15: A fool’s way is right in his own eyes,
but the wise listen to counsel.
v16: A fool’s anger is known at once;
a prudent person overlooks an insult.
</p>

## Truthful Speech

<p>
v17: One who speaks faithfully tells what is right;
a false witness tells deceit.
v18: Reckless speech stabs like a sword,
but the tongue of the wise brings healing.
v19: Truthful lips endure forever;
a lying tongue lasts only a moment.
v20: Deceit is in the hearts of those who plot evil;
joy belongs to those who counsel peace.
</p>

<p>
v21: No harm befalls the righteous,
but the wicked are filled with trouble.
v22: Lying lips are detestable to the LORD;
those who act faithfully delight him.
v23: A prudent person keeps knowledge to himself;
the heart of fools proclaims folly.
v24: Diligent hands will rule,
but slack hands will face forced labor.
</p>

## Anxiety and Encouragement

<p>
v25: Anxiety weighs down a person’s heart,
but a good word makes it glad.
v26: The righteous give guidance to their neighbors,
but the way of the wicked leads them astray.
v27: The lazy do not roast their game,
but diligence is a person’s precious possession.
v28: On the path of righteousness is life;
along its trail there is no death.
</p>

## Notes

v12,26–28: Several compact clauses are difficult: verse 12 may refer to an evil catch, net, or stronghold; verse 26 to guiding or examining a neighbor; verse 27 to roasting game or obtaining it. Verse 28 retains the pinned text’s “no death.” These choices remain open for editorial review.

## Vocabulary

v04: **Capable**: The Hebrew chayil includes strength, ability, and worth; it reappears prominently in 31:10–31.
