## A Good Name

<p>
v01: A good name is more desirable than great wealth;
favor is better than silver and gold.
v02: Rich and poor meet together;
the LORD is the Maker of them all.
v03: The prudent see danger and hide,
but the inexperienced go on and suffer for it.
v04: The reward of humility and the fear of the LORD
is wealth, honor, and life.
v05: Thorns and snares lie on the crooked person’s way;
whoever guards his life stays far from them.
</p>

## Form the Young in Wisdom

<p>
v06: Train a youth according to his way;
even when old, he will not turn from it.
v07: The rich rule over the poor,
and the borrower is a servant to the lender.
v08: Whoever sows injustice reaps trouble,
and the rod of his fury will fail.
v09: The generous will be blessed,
for they share their bread with the poor.
v10: Drive out a scoffer, and conflict leaves;
disputes and dishonor cease.
</p>

<p>
v11: Whoever loves purity of heart and speaks graciously
will have the king as a friend.
v12: The LORD’s eyes guard knowledge,
but he overturns the words of the faithless.
v13: A sluggard says, “There is a lion outside!
I will be killed in the public square!”
v14: The mouth of forbidden women is a deep pit;
one under the LORD’s anger falls into it.
v15: Folly is bound to a youth’s heart;
the rod of discipline drives it far away.
v16: Oppressing the poor to increase one’s wealth
and giving to the rich lead only to need.
</p>

## Words of the Wise

<p>
v17: Turn your ear and hear the words of the wise;
apply your heart to my knowledge.
v18: It is pleasant when you keep them within you,
when they are all ready on your lips.
v19: So that your trust may be in the LORD,
I make them known to you today—to you yourself.
v20: Have I not written excellent sayings for you,
with counsel and knowledge,
v21: to make you know the reliability of true words,
so you can bring back truthful words to those who sent you?
</p>

## Do Not Exploit the Vulnerable

<p>
v22: Do not rob the poor because they are poor;
do not crush the afflicted at the gate.
v23: For the LORD will plead their case
and rob their robbers of life.
v24: Do not befriend a hot-tempered person
or associate with a furious man,
v25: or you may learn his ways
and take a snare for your own life.
v26: Do not be among those who clasp hands in a pledge,
those who guarantee debts.
v27: If you have nothing to pay,
why should your bed be taken from under you?
v28: Do not move the ancient boundary marker
that your ancestors set in place.
v29: Have you seen someone skilled at his work?
He will stand before kings,
not before obscure people.
</p>

## Notes

v04,06,16: Verse 4 can link humility and fear as two qualities or make fear follow humility. Verse 6 says “according to his way,” leaving the precise relation of training and the youth’s way open. Verse 16 compresses the relationship between oppression, gain, giving, and poverty.

v20: The written form can mean “formerly.” The read form is understood here as “excellent sayings”; another interpretation yields “thirty sayings.” The draft does not impose a numbered scheme on the following units.

## Vocabulary

v22: **Gate**: The city’s public place for legal and civic business; crushing the poor there includes denying justice.
