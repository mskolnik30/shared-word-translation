## Better a Dry Morsel with Peace

<p>
v01: Better a dry crust with quiet
than a house full of sacrificial feasts with quarrels.
v02: A servant who acts wisely will rule over a shameful son
and share the inheritance among the brothers.
v03: A crucible is for silver and a furnace for gold,
but the LORD tests hearts.
v04: An evildoer listens to wicked lips;
a liar pays attention to a destructive tongue.
v05: Whoever mocks the poor insults their Maker;
whoever rejoices at disaster will not go unpunished.
</p>

<p>
v06: Grandchildren are the crown of the old,
and fathers are the glory of their children.
v07: Fine speech does not suit a fool;
how much less do lying lips suit a noble!
v08: A bribe is a precious stone in its owner’s eyes;
wherever he turns, he succeeds.
v09: Whoever covers an offense seeks love;
whoever repeats a matter separates close friends.
v10: A rebuke sinks deeper into one who understands
than a hundred blows into a fool.
</p>

## Friendship and Conflict

<p>
v11: An evil person seeks only rebellion;
a cruel messenger will be sent against him.
v12: Better to meet a bear robbed of her cubs
than a fool in his folly.
v13: Whoever repays good with evil—
evil will not leave his house.
v14: Starting a quarrel is like letting water loose;
leave the dispute before it breaks out.
v15: Acquitting the guilty and condemning the innocent—
both are detestable to the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v16: Why is there a price in a fool’s hand to buy wisdom
when he has no sense?
v17: A friend loves at all times,
and a brother is born for trouble.
v18: A person without sense clasps hands in a pledge,
guaranteeing a debt in his neighbor’s presence.
v19: Whoever loves quarrels loves wrongdoing;
whoever raises his doorway high seeks destruction.
v20: A twisted heart finds no good;
a deceitful tongue falls into trouble.
</p>

## A Joyful Heart

<p>
v21: The one who fathers a fool has sorrow;
a fool’s father has no joy.
v22: A joyful heart brings good healing;
a crushed spirit dries up the bones.
v23: A wicked person takes a concealed bribe
to bend the paths of justice.
v24: Wisdom is before the face of the discerning,
but a fool’s eyes roam to the ends of the earth.
</p>

<p>
v25: A foolish son is grief to his father
and bitterness to the woman who bore him.
v26: It is not good to fine the innocent
or to strike nobles for doing right.
v27: One who knows restrains his words;
a person of understanding has a noble spirit.
v28: Even a fool is considered wise when silent,
discerning when he closes his lips.
</p>

## Notes

v08,19,27: The bribe’s apparent power is described from its owner’s viewpoint. The high doorway in verse 19 may suggest proud display, but the image is retained. Verse 27 follows the read form “noble/precious spirit”; the written form is “cool spirit.”

## Vocabulary

v18: **Pledge**: A binding undertaking to pay another person’s debt, symbolized by clasped hands.
