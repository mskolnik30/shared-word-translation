## The Fool and Folly

<p>
v01: Like snow in summer and rain at harvest,
honor does not suit a fool.
v02: Like a fluttering bird, like a flying swallow,
a groundless curse does not come to rest.
v03: A whip for the horse, a bridle for the donkey,
and a rod for the backs of fools.
v04: Do not answer a fool according to his folly,
or you too will become like him.
v05: Answer a fool according to his folly,
or he will become wise in his own eyes.
</p>

<p>
v06: Sending a message by a fool
is cutting off your feet and drinking violence.
v07: Like the dangling legs of someone unable to walk
is a proverb in the mouth of fools.
v08: Like binding a stone in a sling
is giving honor to a fool.
v09: Like a thorn in a drunkard’s hand
is a proverb in the mouth of fools.
v10: Like an archer who wounds everyone
is one who hires a fool or hires passersby.
</p>

<p>
v11: Like a dog returning to its vomit,
a fool repeats his folly.
v12: Have you seen someone wise in his own eyes?
There is more hope for a fool than for him.
</p>

## The Lazy Person

<p>
v13: A sluggard says, “A lion is on the road!
A lion is in the streets!”
v14: A door turns on its hinge,
and a sluggard on his bed.
v15: A sluggard buries his hand in the dish;
he is too weary to bring it back to his mouth.
v16: A sluggard is wiser in his own eyes
than seven people who answer sensibly.
</p>

## Conflict and Deception

<p>
v17: Like grabbing a dog by the ears
is a passerby who gets angry over someone else’s quarrel.
v18: Like a madman shooting firebrands,
arrows, and death,
v19: so is someone who deceives his neighbor
and says, “I was only playing!”
v20: Without wood a fire goes out;
without a whisperer a quarrel grows quiet.
v21: Charcoal for embers, wood for fire,
and a quarrelsome person for kindling conflict.
v22: A whisperer’s words are like tasty morsels;
they go down into the innermost belly.
</p>

## Hatred Hidden by Speech

<p>
v23: Silver dross coating earthenware—
such are burning lips with an evil heart.
v24: A hater disguises himself with his lips
while storing up deceit within.
v25: When his voice grows gracious, do not trust him,
for seven detestable things are in his heart.
v26: Though hatred is covered by deception,
his evil will be exposed in the assembly.
v27: Whoever digs a pit falls into it;
a stone rolls back on the one who rolls it.
v28: A lying tongue hates those it crushes,
and a smooth mouth brings ruin.
</p>

## Notes

v02,04–05: Verse 2 follows the written “not”; the read form is “to him,” which can suggest a curse returning to its source. Verses 4–5 deliberately give opposite instructions with different consequences. Both are retained for discernment, not merged into one rule.

v10,23: Verse 10 is exceptionally difficult; “archer” and “wounds” are selected, but readings involving a master or great person are possible. Verse 23 retains the pinned silver dross and burning lips, rather than silently substituting smooth lips.

## Vocabulary

v22: **Whisperer**: A person whose private talk spreads suspicion or gossip; the morsel image shows its appeal.
