## Wisdom Has Built Her House

<p>
v01: Wisdom has built her house;
she has carved out its seven pillars.
v02: She has slaughtered her animals, mixed her wine,
and set her table.
v03: She has sent out her young women;
she calls from the city’s highest places,
v04: “Whoever is inexperienced, turn in here!”
To one who lacks sense she says,
v05: “Come, eat my bread
and drink the wine I have mixed.
v06: Leave inexperience behind and live;
walk straight along the way of understanding.”
</p>

## The Wise Receive Correction

<p>
v07: Whoever disciplines a scoffer brings dishonor on himself;
whoever corrects the wicked receives abuse.
v08: Do not correct a scoffer, or he will hate you;
correct a wise person, and he will love you.
v09: Give instruction to the wise, and they become wiser;
teach the righteous, and they add to their learning.
</p>

## The Beginning of Wisdom

<p>
v10: The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom,
and knowledge of the Holy One is understanding.
v11: For through me your days will multiply,
and years will be added to your life.
v12: If you are wise, you are wise for your own benefit;
if you scoff, you alone will bear it.
</p>

## Folly Calls from Her Door

<p>
v13: Folly is a noisy woman,
all inexperience, knowing nothing.
v14: She sits at the door of her house,
on a seat in the city’s high places,
v15: calling to those passing by
who are going straight on their way,
v16: “Whoever is inexperienced, turn in here!”
To one who lacks sense she says,
v17: “Stolen water is sweet,
and bread eaten in secret is pleasant.”
v18: But he does not know that the shades of the dead are there,
that her guests are in the depths of Sheol.
</p>

## Notes

v04,16: Wisdom and Folly give the same invitation. The repeated wording asks the reader to distinguish their offerings and destinations.

v10: “The Holy One” reads the plural Hebrew “holy ones” as a designation of God. A reference to holy beings is also possible.

## Vocabulary

v18: **Sheol**: The realm of the dead, the destination hidden behind Folly’s invitation.
