## Proverbs of Solomon

<p>
v01: The proverbs of Solomon.
A wise son brings his father joy;
a foolish son brings his mother sorrow.
v02: Treasures gained by wickedness bring no benefit,
but righteousness rescues from death.
v03: The LORD does not let the righteous go hungry,
but he thwarts the craving of the wicked.
v04: A slack hand brings poverty;
a diligent hand brings wealth.
v05: A son who gathers in summer acts wisely;
a son who sleeps at harvest brings shame.
</p>

<p>
v06: Blessings rest on the head of the righteous,
but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence.
v07: The memory of the righteous becomes a blessing;
the name of the wicked rots away.
v08: The wise in heart accept commands,
but a babbling fool comes to ruin.
v09: Whoever walks with integrity walks securely;
whoever twists his ways will be found out.
v10: A wink of the eye brings sorrow,
and a babbling fool comes to ruin.
</p>

<p>
v11: The mouth of the righteous is a fountain of life,
but the mouth of the wicked conceals violence.
v12: Hatred stirs up conflicts,
but love covers every offense.
v13: Wisdom is found on discerning lips,
but a rod is for the back of one who lacks sense.
v14: The wise store up knowledge,
but a fool’s mouth brings ruin near.
v15: The wealth of the rich is their fortified city;
the poverty of the poor is their ruin.
</p>

<p>
v16: The wages of the righteous lead to life;
the income of the wicked leads to sin.
v17: Whoever keeps discipline is a path to life,
but whoever abandons correction leads others astray.
v18: Whoever conceals hatred has lying lips,
and whoever spreads slander is a fool.
v19: Where words multiply, wrongdoing does not cease;
whoever restrains his lips acts wisely.
v20: The tongue of the righteous is choice silver;
the heart of the wicked is worth little.
</p>

<p>
v21: The lips of the righteous feed many,
but fools die for lack of sense.
v22: The LORD’s blessing brings wealth,
and he adds no sorrow with it.
v23: Doing wrong is sport to a fool;
so is wisdom to a person of understanding.
v24: What the wicked dread will come upon them;
what the righteous desire will be granted.
v25: The whirlwind passes, and the wicked are gone,
but the righteous have an enduring foundation.
</p>

<p>
v26: Like vinegar to teeth and smoke to eyes,
so is a sluggard to those who send him.
v27: The fear of the LORD adds days,
but the years of the wicked are cut short.
v28: The hope of the righteous brings joy,
but the expectation of the wicked perishes.
v29: The LORD’s way is a stronghold for the upright,
but ruin for those who do wrong.
v30: The righteous will never be shaken,
but the wicked will not dwell in the land.
</p>

<p>
v31: The mouth of the righteous bears wisdom,
but the twisted tongue will be cut off.
v32: The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable;
the mouth of the wicked knows perversity.
</p>

## Notes

v06,11,22: “The mouth of the wicked conceals violence” can also be read “violence covers the mouth of the wicked.” Verse 22 may say God adds no sorrow to his blessing, or that toil adds nothing to it.

## Vocabulary

v17: **Discipline**: Training that includes correction. The person who receives it can guide others; rejecting it can mislead them.
