## The Purpose of Proverbs

<p>
v01: The proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel.
v02: For learning wisdom and discipline,
for understanding words of insight;
v03: for receiving discipline that teaches good judgment,
righteousness, justice, and fairness;
v04: for giving the inexperienced good sense,
and a young man knowledge and discretion.
v05: Let the wise listen and add to their learning;
let the discerning gain guidance,
v06: to understand proverbs and figures of speech,
the words of the wise and their riddles.
</p>

## The Beginning of Knowledge

<p>
v07: The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge;
fools despise wisdom and discipline.
</p>

## Do Not Join Those Who Shed Blood

<p>
v08: My son, listen to your father’s discipline,
and do not abandon your mother’s instruction.
v09: They are a lovely garland for your head
and ornaments for your neck.
</p>

<p>
v10: My son, if sinners entice you,
do not agree to go.
v11: They may say, “Come with us!
Let’s lie in wait to spill blood;
let’s ambush someone innocent for no reason.
v12: Let’s swallow them alive, like Sheol,
whole, like those going down to the Pit.
v13: We will find all kinds of precious wealth
and fill our houses with plunder.
v14: Throw in your lot with us;
we will all share one purse.”
v15: My son, do not walk their road;
keep your feet off their path.
v16: Their feet run toward evil;
they hurry to spill blood.
</p>

<p>
v17: A net is spread in vain
in full view of any bird.
v18: But these men lie in wait for their own blood;
they ambush their own lives.
v19: Such are the paths of everyone greedy for gain:
it takes the life of those who possess it.
</p>

## Wisdom Calls in the Streets

<p>
v20: Wisdom cries out in the street;
she raises her voice in the public squares.
v21: At the head of the noisy streets she calls;
at the city gates she speaks her words:
v22: “How long, you inexperienced ones, will you love inexperience?
How long will scoffers delight in scoffing
and fools hate knowledge?
v23: Turn when I rebuke you!
Then I will pour out my spirit to you
and make my words known to you.
</p>

<p>
v24: I called, but you refused;
I stretched out my hand, but no one paid attention.
v25: You dismissed all my counsel
and would have none of my rebuke.
v26: So I too will laugh at your disaster;
I will mock when terror comes upon you,
v27: when terror sweeps over you like devastation
and disaster arrives like a whirlwind,
when distress and anguish come upon you.
</p>

<p>
v28: Then they will call me, but I will not answer;
they will search for me, but will not find me.
v29: Because they hated knowledge
and did not choose the fear of the LORD,
v30: because they wanted none of my counsel
and rejected every rebuke I gave,
v31: they will eat the fruit of their own way
and have their fill of their own schemes.
v32: The turning away of the inexperienced kills them;
the complacency of fools destroys them.
v33: But whoever listens to me will live securely,
at ease, without fear of disaster.”
</p>

## Notes

v01–07: Proverbs teaches through compact sayings, extended speeches, questions, and images. Its sayings invite judgment about situations; the book also sets sayings beside one another that pull in different directions, as in 26:4–5.

v08,20–33: The father addresses a son, and Wisdom speaks as a woman. These voices remain distinct. Wisdom’s laughter at disaster is part of her severe warning, not a softened narrator’s comment.

## Vocabulary

v07: **Fear of the LORD**: Reverent fear that shapes how a person learns and acts; a recurring starting point for wisdom in this book.
