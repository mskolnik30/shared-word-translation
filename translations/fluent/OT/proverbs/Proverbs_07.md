## Keep My Words

<p>
v01: My son, keep my words;
store up my commands within you.
v02: Keep my commands and live;
guard my instruction like the pupil of your eye.
v03: Bind them to your fingers;
write them on the tablet of your heart.
v04: Say to Wisdom, “You are my sister,”
and call Understanding your close relative,
v05: so they may guard you from the forbidden woman,
from the outsider whose words are smooth.
</p>

## A Young Man Without Sense

<p>
v06: At the window of my house
I looked out through the lattice.
v07: Among the inexperienced I saw,
among the youths I noticed, a young man without sense.
v08: He crossed the street near her corner
and headed along the road to her house,
v09: at twilight, as the day faded,
in the dark heart of the night.
</p>

## She Came Out to Meet Him

<p>
v10: Then a woman came to meet him,
dressed like a prostitute, her intentions concealed.
v11: She is loud and defiant;
her feet never stay at home.
v12: Now in the street, now in the squares,
she lies in wait at every corner.
v13: She seized him and kissed him;
with a bold face she said,
v14: “I had fellowship offerings to make;
today I fulfilled my vows.
v15: That is why I came out to meet you,
to search eagerly for you—and I found you!
</p>

<p>
v16: I have spread coverings on my couch,
patterned linen from Egypt.
v17: I have perfumed my bed
with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.
v18: Come, let us drink our fill of love until morning;
let us delight in lovemaking.
v19: The man is not at home;
he has gone on a long journey.
v20: He took a bag of silver with him;
he will not return home until the full moon.”
</p>

## He Followed Her

<p>
v21: With much persuasion she turned him;
with smooth lips she drew him away.
v22: All at once he follows her,
like an ox going to slaughter,
like a fool in fetters going to correction,
v23: until an arrow pierces his liver;
like a bird darting into a trap,
he does not know that his life is at stake.
</p>

## Do Not Wander into Her Ways

<p>
v24: Now, sons, listen to me;
pay attention to the words of my mouth.
v25: Do not let your heart turn toward her ways;
do not stray onto her paths.
v26: For many are the wounded she has brought down;
her slain are a mighty host.
v27: Her house is a road to Sheol,
going down to the chambers of death.
</p>

## Notes

v22–23: The middle comparison in verse 22 is difficult. This draft retains the Hebrew’s fetters, correction, and fool rather than replacing them with another animal. The arrow pierces the liver in verse 23.

v26: The last line can emphasize the great number or the strength of those killed. “A mighty host” retains both possibilities without specifying individuals.

## Vocabulary

v14: **Fellowship offerings**: Sacrifices associated with a shared meal. The speaker invokes fulfilled vows while enticing the young man.
