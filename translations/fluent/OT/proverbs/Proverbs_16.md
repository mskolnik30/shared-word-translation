## The LORD Establishes the Way

<p>
v01: The arrangements of the heart belong to a person,
but the tongue’s answer comes from the LORD.
v02: All a person’s ways seem pure in his own eyes,
but the LORD weighs spirits.
v03: Roll your works onto the LORD,
and your plans will be established.
v04: The LORD has made everything for its purpose,
even the wicked for a day of disaster.
v05: Every proud heart is detestable to the LORD;
be assured, it will not go unpunished.
</p>

<p>
v06: Through faithful love and faithfulness guilt is atoned for;
through the fear of the LORD one turns from evil.
v07: When a person’s ways please the LORD,
he makes even his enemies be at peace with him.
v08: Better a little with righteousness
than abundant income without justice.
v09: A human heart plans its way,
but the LORD establishes its steps.
v10: An oracle is on the lips of a king;
his mouth must not betray justice.
</p>

## Honest Measures and Wise Rule

<p>
v11: Honest balances and scales belong to the LORD;
all the weights in the pouch are his work.
v12: Wicked action is detestable to kings,
for a throne is established through righteousness.
v13: Righteous lips delight kings;
they love one who speaks uprightly.
v14: A king’s fury sends messengers of death,
but a wise person appeases it.
v15: In the light of a king’s face is life;
his favor is like a cloud of spring rain.
</p>

## Wisdom Is Better Than Gold

<p>
v16: How much better to acquire wisdom than gold!
To acquire understanding is more desirable than silver.
v17: The highway of the upright turns away from evil;
whoever guards his way protects his life.
v18: Pride comes before destruction,
a haughty spirit before a fall.
v19: Better a lowly spirit among the humble
than a share of plunder with the proud.
v20: Whoever attends to a matter wisely finds good;
happy is the one who trusts in the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v21: The wise in heart are called discerning;
sweetness of speech adds persuasive power.
v22: Good judgment is a fountain of life to its possessor,
but the discipline of fools is folly.
v23: A wise heart gives insight to its mouth
and adds persuasive power to its lips.
v24: Pleasant words are a honeycomb,
sweet to the soul and healing to the bones.
v25: There is a way that seems right to a person,
but its end is the ways of death.
</p>

## The Power of Speech and Self-Control

<p>
v26: A worker’s appetite works for him;
his mouth drives him on.
v27: A worthless man digs up evil;
on his lip is a scorching fire.
v28: A twisted man stirs up conflict;
a whisperer separates close friends.
v29: A violent man entices his neighbor
and leads him on a way that is not good.
v30: One who narrows his eyes plans perversity;
one who presses his lips together brings evil to completion.
</p>

<p>
v31: Gray hair is a beautiful crown;
it is found on the way of righteousness.
v32: Better one slow to anger than a warrior,
one who rules his spirit than one who captures a city.
v33: The lot is cast into the lap,
but every decision it gives comes from the LORD.
</p>

## Notes

v04,06,10: The poem attributes purpose even to the wicked’s day of disaster. Verse 6 does not specify whose faithful love and faithfulness atone for guilt. Verse 10 is rendered as a royal obligation to judge faithfully, not a claim that every king always does so.

## Vocabulary

v03: **Roll your works onto the LORD**: A concrete image for entrusting one’s undertakings to God.
