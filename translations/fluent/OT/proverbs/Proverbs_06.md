## If You Have Pledged Yourself for Another

<p>
v01: My son, if you have guaranteed your neighbor’s debt,
if you have clasped hands in a pledge to a stranger,
v02: you are snared by the words of your mouth,
caught by the words of your mouth.
v03: Then do this, my son, and free yourself,
for you have fallen into your neighbor’s hands:
go, humble yourself, and press your plea with your neighbor.
v04: Give your eyes no sleep
and your eyelids no rest.
v05: Free yourself like a gazelle from the hunter’s hand,
like a bird from the trapper’s hand.
</p>

## Go to the Ant

<p>
v06: Go to the ant, you sluggard;
study her ways and become wise.
v07: She has no commander,
no overseer or ruler,
v08: yet she prepares her food in summer
and gathers her provisions at harvest.
v09: How long will you lie there, you sluggard?
When will you rise from sleep?
v10: “A little sleep, a little rest,
a little folding of the hands to lie down”—
v11: and poverty will come upon you like a prowler,
need like a man with a shield.
</p>

## A Worthless Person

<p>
v12: A worthless person, a man of wrongdoing,
goes about with twisted speech,
v13: winking his eyes, signaling with his feet,
pointing with his fingers.
v14: With perversity in his heart he plots evil continually;
he stirs up conflict.
v15: Therefore disaster will come upon him suddenly;
in an instant he will be broken beyond healing.
</p>

## Seven Things the LORD Hates

<p>
v16: There are six things the LORD hates,
seven that are detestable to him:
v17: haughty eyes, a lying tongue,
hands that spill innocent blood,
v18: a heart that devises wicked plans,
feet that hurry to run toward evil,
v19: a false witness who breathes out lies,
and someone who stirs up conflict among brothers.
</p>

## Keep the Commandment

<p>
v20: My son, keep your father’s command
and do not abandon your mother’s instruction.
v21: Bind them always to your heart;
fasten them around your neck.
v22: When you walk, instruction will lead you;
when you lie down, it will guard you;
when you wake, it will speak with you.
v23: For the command is a lamp, and instruction is light;
corrective discipline is the way of life,
</p>

## Do Not Desire Another's Spouse

<p>
v24: keeping you from the evil woman,
from the smooth tongue of the outsider.
v25: Do not desire her beauty in your heart
or let her capture you with her eyes.
v26: For a prostitute’s price is a loaf of bread,
but another man’s wife hunts a precious life.
v27: Can a man scoop fire into his lap
without burning his clothes?
v28: Can a man walk on hot coals
without scorching his feet?
v29: So it is with one who goes to his neighbor’s wife;
no one who touches her will go unpunished.
</p>

<p>
v30: People do not despise a thief who steals
to satisfy his hunger,
v31: but if caught, he must repay sevenfold,
even giving all the wealth of his house.
v32: A man who commits adultery lacks sense;
he who does it destroys his own life.
v33: He will find wounds and dishonor;
his disgrace will not be wiped away.
v34: For jealousy fuels a husband’s fury,
and he will show no pity on the day of vengeance.
v35: He will accept no compensation;
he will refuse, however large your gift.
</p>

## Notes

v26: The first line is compressed: it may state a prostitute’s price as a loaf of bread, or say that involvement with her reduces someone to a loaf. The second line warns that adultery imperils a precious life.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Guarantee a debt**: Accept responsibility for another person’s payment. The clasped hands mark a binding pledge.
