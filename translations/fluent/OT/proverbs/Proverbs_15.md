## A Gentle Answer

<p>
v01: A gentle answer turns back fury;
a hurtful word stirs up anger.
v02: The tongue of the wise makes knowledge welcome;
the mouth of fools gushes folly.
v03: The LORD’s eyes are everywhere,
watching the evil and the good.
v04: A healing tongue is a tree of life;
a crooked tongue breaks the spirit.
v05: A fool despises his father’s discipline;
whoever keeps correction becomes prudent.
</p>

## The LORD Knows the Heart

<p>
v06: The house of the righteous holds great wealth;
the income of the wicked brings trouble.
v07: The lips of the wise scatter knowledge;
the heart of fools does not.
v08: The sacrifice of the wicked is detestable to the LORD,
but the prayer of the upright delights him.
v09: The way of the wicked is detestable to the LORD,
but he loves those who pursue righteousness.
v10: Harsh discipline awaits one who leaves the path;
whoever hates correction will die.
</p>

<p>
v11: Sheol and Abaddon lie open before the LORD—
how much more human hearts!
v12: A scoffer does not love being corrected;
he will not go to the wise.
v13: A joyful heart brightens the face;
a sorrowful heart crushes the spirit.
v14: A discerning heart seeks knowledge;
the mouth of fools feeds on folly.
v15: All the days of the afflicted are hard,
but a cheerful heart has a continual feast.
</p>

## Better with the Fear of the LORD

<p>
v16: Better a little with the fear of the LORD
than great treasure with turmoil.
v17: Better a meal of vegetables where there is love
than a fattened ox with hatred.
v18: A furious man stirs up conflict;
one slow to anger quiets a quarrel.
v19: A sluggard’s way is like a hedge of thorns;
the path of the upright is a raised highway.
v20: A wise son brings his father joy;
a foolish man despises his mother.
</p>

## The Path of Life

<p>
v21: Folly is joy to one who lacks sense;
a person of understanding walks straight.
v22: Plans fail without consultation;
with many counselors they stand.
v23: A fitting answer brings a person joy;
how good is a word at the right time!
v24: For the discerning, the path of life leads upward,
away from Sheol below.
v25: The LORD tears down the house of the proud,
but secures the widow’s boundary.
</p>

<p>
v26: Evil plans are detestable to the LORD,
but pleasant words are pure.
v27: Whoever is greedy for gain troubles his household;
whoever hates bribes will live.
v28: The heart of the righteous weighs an answer;
the mouth of the wicked gushes evil.
v29: The LORD is far from the wicked,
but he hears the prayer of the righteous.
v30: Bright eyes bring joy to the heart;
good news nourishes the bones.
v31: An ear that listens to life-giving correction
will dwell among the wise.
v32: Whoever rejects discipline despises his own life;
whoever listens to correction gains understanding.
v33: The fear of the LORD teaches wisdom,
and humility comes before honor.
</p>

## Notes

v25: The widow’s boundary is a property boundary. The contrast concerns both the proud person’s house and a vulnerable person’s protected land.

## Vocabulary

v11: **Abaddon**: “Destruction,” paired with Sheol as a name for the realm of the dead.
