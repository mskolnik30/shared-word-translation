## Wisdom Receives Discipline

<p>
v01: A wise son accepts his father’s discipline;
a scoffer does not listen to rebuke.
v02: From the fruit of his mouth a person eats good things,
but the appetite of the faithless is for violence.
v03: Whoever guards his mouth protects his life;
whoever opens his lips wide invites ruin.
v04: The sluggard craves and has nothing;
the diligent are richly satisfied.
</p>

<p>
v05: The righteous hate falsehood,
but the wicked bring a stench and shame.
v06: Righteousness guards those whose way is blameless;
wickedness overturns the sinner.
v07: One person pretends to be rich and has nothing;
another pretends to be poor and owns great wealth.
v08: A person’s wealth can ransom his life,
but the poor hear no threat.
</p>

## The Light of the Righteous

<p>
v09: The light of the righteous shines joyfully,
but the lamp of the wicked goes out.
v10: Pride produces only quarrels,
but wisdom is with those who take counsel.
v11: Wealth gained from emptiness dwindles,
but whoever gathers little by little increases it.
v12: Hope long delayed makes the heart sick;
a desire fulfilled is a tree of life.
</p>

<p>
v13: Whoever despises a word will be held liable for it;
whoever respects a command will be repaid.
v14: The instruction of the wise is a fountain of life,
turning people away from death’s snares.
v15: Good judgment wins favor,
but the way of the faithless is hard.
v16: Every prudent person acts with knowledge;
a fool spreads his folly abroad.
</p>

## Faithful Messengers and Wise Companions

<p>
v17: A wicked messenger falls into trouble,
but a faithful envoy brings healing.
v18: Poverty and dishonor come to one who rejects discipline;
whoever keeps correction will be honored.
v19: A desire fulfilled is sweet to the soul,
but turning from evil is detestable to fools.
v20: Whoever walks with the wise becomes wise;
a companion of fools suffers harm.
</p>

## A Good Inheritance

<p>
v21: Trouble pursues sinners,
but good repays the righteous.
v22: A good person leaves an inheritance to grandchildren,
but a sinner’s wealth is stored up for the righteous.
v23: The unplowed field of the poor has abundant food,
but it is swept away by injustice.
v24: Whoever holds back his rod hates his son;
whoever loves him is diligent in discipline.
v25: The righteous eat until satisfied,
but the belly of the wicked goes hungry.
</p>

## Notes

v11,15: Wealth “from emptiness” may be insubstantial or dishonestly obtained wealth. The “hard” way in verse 15 uses a word that can also mean enduring or firm; the contrast favors difficulty here.

v23–24: The first saying names injustice that takes the poor’s food. The next retains a rod in parental discipline; the physical image has not been replaced with a general term for teaching.

## Vocabulary

v12: **Tree of life**: An image for the restoring force of a desire fulfilled, contrasting a sick heart.
