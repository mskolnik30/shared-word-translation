## Justice Establishes a People

<p>
v01: One often corrected who stiffens his neck
will suddenly be broken beyond healing.
v02: When the righteous increase, the people rejoice;
when a wicked person rules, the people groan.
v03: A man who loves wisdom brings his father joy;
a companion of prostitutes wastes wealth.
v04: Through justice a king makes a land stand firm;
a man who exacts contributions tears it down.
v05: A man who flatters his neighbor
spreads a net for his feet.
</p>

<p>
v06: An evil man’s offense is a snare,
but the righteous sing and rejoice.
v07: The righteous know the cause of the poor;
the wicked have no understanding of such knowledge.
v08: Scoffers set a city ablaze;
the wise turn anger away.
v09: When a wise man goes to court with a fool,
there is raging and laughing, but no quiet.
v10: Bloodthirsty men hate one of integrity,
but the upright seek to preserve his life.
</p>

## Discipline and Restraint

<p>
v11: A fool lets out all his anger;
a wise person holds it back and quiets it.
v12: When a ruler listens to falsehood,
all his attendants become wicked.
v13: The poor and the oppressor meet together;
the LORD gives light to the eyes of both.
v14: A king who judges the poor faithfully
will have his throne established forever.
v15: The rod and correction give wisdom;
a youth left to himself brings his mother shame.
</p>

<p>
v16: When the wicked increase, offenses increase,
but the righteous will see their downfall.
v17: Discipline your son, and he will give you rest;
he will bring delight to your soul.
v18: Without prophetic vision, a people casts off restraint;
happy is the one who keeps instruction.
v19: A servant is not disciplined by words alone;
though he understands, there is no response.
v20: Have you seen someone hasty in speech?
There is more hope for a fool than for him.
</p>

## Pride, Fear, and Justice

<p>
v21: One who pampers his servant from youth
will find him acting as a son in the end.
v22: An angry man stirs up conflict;
a furious man commits many offenses.
v23: A person’s pride brings him low,
but a lowly spirit holds on to honor.
v24: A thief’s partner hates his own life;
he hears the oath of testimony but says nothing.
v25: Fear of people lays a snare,
but whoever trusts in the LORD is safe on high.
</p>

<p>
v26: Many seek a ruler’s favor,
but a person’s justice comes from the LORD.
v27: An unjust man is detestable to the righteous,
and one whose way is upright is detestable to the wicked.
</p>

## Notes

v10,21: Verse 10’s “seek his life” is read as protective care by the upright; the syntax is difficult. The rare word manon in verse 21 is read as son-like status, but its exact meaning is uncertain.

## Vocabulary

v18: **Prophetic vision**: Revelatory guidance, not simply a personal ambition or an organization’s plan.
