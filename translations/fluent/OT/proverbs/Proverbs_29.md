## Justice Establishes a People

<p>
v01: One who is often corrected
yet stiffens the neck
will suddenly be broken
beyond healing.

v02: When the righteous increase,
the people rejoice,
but when the wicked rule,
the people groan.

v03: Whoever loves wisdom
makes a parent glad,
but one who keeps company with prostitutes
squanders wealth.

v04: By justice a king
establishes the land,
but one who demands gifts
tears it down.

v05: A person who flatters a neighbor
spreads a net
for their feet.
</p>

<p>
v06: An evil person
is caught by their transgression,
but the righteous
sing and rejoice.

v07: The righteous know
the rights of the poor;
the wicked
do not understand such knowledge.

v08: Scoffers inflame a city,
but the wise
turn away anger.

v09: If a wise person
enters a dispute with a fool,
the fool rages and laughs,
and there is no rest.

v10: Bloodthirsty people
hate the blameless,
but the upright
seek to preserve their life.
</p>

## Discipline and Restraint

<p>
v11: A fool gives full vent
to the spirit,
but the wise
calmly hold it back.

v12: If a ruler listens to falsehood,
all the ruler’s servants
become wicked.

v13: The poor person and the oppressor
meet together;
the LORD gives light
to the eyes of both.

v14: If a king judges the poor
with faithfulness,
the throne
will be established forever.

v15: The rod and correction
give wisdom,
but a child left without guidance
brings shame to a mother.
</p>

<p>
v16: When the wicked increase,
transgression increases,
but the righteous
will see their downfall.

v17: Discipline your child,
and they will give you rest
and bring delight
to your life.

v18: Where there is no vision,
a people cast off restraint,
but blessed is the one
who keeps instruction.

v19: A servant
will not be corrected by words alone;
though understanding,
there may be no response.

v20: Do you see someone
hasty in speech?
There is more hope
for a fool than for them.
</p>

## Pride, Fear, and Justice

<p>
v21: Whoever pampers a servant
from youth
may find in the end
that the servant claims the household.

v22: An angry person
stirs up conflict,
and one given to rage
commits many transgressions.

v23: A person’s pride
will bring them low,
but one humble in spirit
will obtain honor.

v24: Whoever shares with a thief
hates their own life;
they hear the oath
but disclose nothing.

v25: Fear of another person
sets a snare,
but whoever trusts in the LORD
is set securely.
</p>

<p>
v26: Many seek
the face of a ruler,
but a person’s justice
comes from the LORD.

v27: An unjust person
is an abomination to the righteous,
and one whose way is upright
is an abomination to the wicked.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Repeated correction can cease to be effective when resistance itself becomes habitual.

v04: Justice stabilizes political life, while rule organized around gifts or exactions consumes the land.

v07: Righteousness includes understanding the legal and material claims of the poor.

v11: Wisdom is not emotional absence but the capacity to govern expression rather than being governed by it.

v18: “Vision” refers to prophetic revelation, not simply personal ambition or strategic planning. When divine instruction is absent or ignored, social restraint breaks down.

v21: The Hebrew final noun is rare and uncertain. It may refer to the servant becoming an heir or otherwise assuming an inappropriate status within the household.

v24: The person associated with a thief becomes trapped: speaking exposes complicity, while silence under oath carries guilt.

v25: Fear of human approval or retaliation can become captivity; trust in the LORD provides a different security.

## Vocabulary

v01: קָשָׁה־עֹרֶף (qesheh oref)
“Stiff-necked.” An image of stubborn resistance.

v04: מִשְׁפָּט (mishpat)
“Justice” or “judgment.” Right rule establishing the land.

v07: דִּין (din)
“Legal claim” or “right.” The cause belonging to the poor.

v11: רוּחַ (ruach)
“Spirit,” “breath,” or “temper.” Here what a fool releases without restraint.

v18: חָזוֹן (chazon)
“Vision.” Prophetic revelation.

v23: שְׁפַל־רוּחַ (shefal ruach)
“Humble in spirit.” Literally low in spirit.

v25: חֶרְדַּת אָדָם (cherdat adam)
“Fear of a person.” Anxiety regarding human power or opinion.

v26: פָּנִים (panim)
“Face.” To seek a ruler’s face is to seek favorable access or decision.
