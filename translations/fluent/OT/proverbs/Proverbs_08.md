## Wisdom Calls

<p>
v01: Does not Wisdom call out?
Does not Understanding raise her voice?
v02: On the heights beside the road,
at the crossroads, she takes her stand.
v03: Beside the city gates, at the entrance,
at the open doorways, she cries:
v04: “To you, people, I call;
my voice goes out to all humankind.
v05: You inexperienced ones, learn good sense;
you fools, learn understanding.
</p>

## My Mouth Speaks Truth

<p>
v06: Listen, for I speak worthy things;
what comes from my lips is right.
v07: My mouth speaks truth;
wickedness is detestable to my lips.
v08: All the words of my mouth are righteous;
there is nothing twisted or crooked in them.
v09: They are all straightforward to the discerning
and right to those who find knowledge.
v10: Receive my discipline rather than silver,
knowledge rather than choice gold.
v11: For wisdom is better than jewels;
no desirable thing can compare with her.
</p>

## Wisdom and Prudence

<p>
v12: I, Wisdom, dwell with good sense;
I find knowledge and discretion.
v13: The fear of the LORD is hatred of evil.
I hate pride, arrogance, the evil way,
and twisted speech.
v14: Counsel and sound wisdom are mine;
I am understanding; strength is mine.
v15: Through me kings reign
and rulers decree what is just.
v16: Through me princes govern,
and nobles—all who judge rightly.
</p>

<p>
v17: I love those who love me,
and those who seek me will find me.
v18: Wealth and honor are with me,
enduring riches and righteousness.
v19: My fruit is better than gold, even fine gold;
my yield surpasses choice silver.
v20: I walk on the path of righteousness,
along the ways of justice,
v21: giving substance as an inheritance to those who love me
and filling their treasuries.
</p>

## Wisdom Before the World

<p>
v22: The LORD acquired me at the beginning of his way,
before his works of long ago.
v23: From ages past I was established,
from the beginning, before the earth.
v24: When there were no deeps, I was brought forth,
when there were no springs heavy with water.
v25: Before the mountains were set in place,
before the hills, I was brought forth,
v26: before he made earth and open country
or the first dust of the world.
</p>

<p>
v27: When he established the heavens, I was there;
when he traced a circle on the face of the deep,
v28: when he made firm the clouds above,
when the springs of the deep grew strong,
v29: when he set the sea its boundary
so the waters would not cross his command,
when he marked out the earth’s foundations,
v30: I was beside him as a master worker.
I was his delight day after day,
playing before him at all times,
v31: playing in his inhabited world,
delighting in humankind.
</p>

## Whoever Finds Me Finds Life

<p>
v32: Now, sons, listen to me;
happy are those who keep my ways.
v33: Listen to discipline and become wise;
do not reject it.
v34: Happy is the person who listens to me,
watching at my doors day after day,
waiting at my doorposts.
v35: For whoever finds me finds life
and obtains favor from the LORD.
v36: But whoever misses me harms his own life;
all who hate me love death.”
</p>

## Notes

v22–25: “Acquired” renders qanah, which can express acquiring or possessing and can carry a creating sense. The poem next speaks of Wisdom being established and brought forth. This personified speech does not itself settle later theological debates.

v30: “Master worker” renders the uncertain amon. Another reading pictures Wisdom as a child beside God; the following lines explicitly describe playing and delight.

## Vocabulary

v36: **Misses me**: The verb can mean missing a mark or sinning. Here it contrasts finding Wisdom and life with harming oneself.
