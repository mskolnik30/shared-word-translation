## The cedars fall

<p>
v01: Open your doors, Lebanon,
so fire may consume your cedars!
v02: Wail, cypress, for the cedar has fallen;
the majestic trees are ruined.
Wail, oaks of Bashan,
for the dense forest has been brought down!
v03: Listen—the shepherds wail,
for their splendor is ruined.
Listen—the young lions roar,
for the pride of the Jordan is ruined.
</p>

## Shepherding the doomed flock

<p>
v04: The LORD my God says, “Shepherd the flock marked for slaughter.
v05: Their buyers kill them and go unpunished. Their sellers say, ‘Blessed be the LORD! I am rich!’ Their own shepherds have no pity on them.
v06: For I will no longer pity the people of the land,” declares the LORD. “I will hand each person over to his neighbor and his king. They will crush the land, and I will not rescue anyone from their hands.”
</p>

<p>
v07: So I shepherded the flock marked for slaughter, especially the afflicted of the flock. I took two staffs, naming one Favor and the other Bonds, and shepherded the flock.
v08: I removed the three shepherds in one month. I lost patience with them, and they too loathed me.
v09: I said, “I will no longer shepherd you. Let the dying die and the perishing perish. Let those left eat one another’s flesh.”
</p>

<p>
v10: I took my staff Favor and broke it, annulling the covenant I had made with all the peoples.
v11: It was annulled that day. Then the afflicted of the flock who were watching me knew that this was the word of the LORD.
v12: I told them, “If you think it right, give me my wages. If not, keep them.” They weighed out my wages: thirty pieces of silver.
v13: The LORD said to me, “Throw it to the potter—that splendid price at which they valued me!” So I took the thirty pieces of silver and threw them into the LORD’s house, to the potter.
v14: Then I broke my second staff, Bonds, annulling the brotherhood between Judah and Israel.
</p>

## The worthless shepherd

<p>
v15: The LORD said to me, “Take up the equipment of a foolish shepherd again.
v16: For I am raising up a shepherd in the land who will not attend to the perishing, seek the young, heal the broken, or feed those still standing. Instead, he will eat the flesh of the fat sheep and tear off their hooves.
</p>

<p>
v17: Woe to the worthless shepherd
who abandons the flock!
May a sword strike his arm and his right eye!
May his arm wither completely
and his right eye go utterly blind!”
</p>

## Notes

v07: “Afflicted” follows the pinned Hebrew rather than a reconstruction referring to sheep merchants.

v13: “Potter” follows the Hebrew wording. The thirty pieces of silver and the house of the LORD remain explicit.

## Vocabulary

v07: **Favor; Bonds**: Names of the two staffs; their breaking gives the action its symbolic force.

v17: **Worthless shepherd**: A leader condemned for abandoning the flock.
