## A burden concerning Israel

<p>
v01: An oracle: the word of the LORD concerning Israel. The LORD, who stretches out the heavens, lays the earth’s foundation, and forms the human spirit within, declares:
</p>

## Jerusalem and the surrounding peoples

<p>
v02: “I am making Jerusalem a bowl that sends all the surrounding peoples reeling. Judah too will be caught up in the siege of Jerusalem.
v03: On that day I will make Jerusalem a heavy stone for all the peoples. Everyone who lifts it will badly injure themselves, and all the earth’s nations will gather against her.
</p>

<p>
v04: On that day,” declares the LORD, “I will strike every horse with panic and its rider with madness. I will keep my eyes open over the house of Judah, but strike every horse of the peoples with blindness.
v05: Then Judah’s clan leaders will say in their hearts, ‘Jerusalem’s people are my strength through the LORD of Armies, their God.’
v06: On that day I will make Judah’s clan leaders like a firepot among wood, like a blazing torch among sheaves. They will consume all the surrounding peoples to the right and left, while Jerusalem will again be inhabited in her own place, in Jerusalem.
</p>

<p>
v07: The LORD will save Judah’s tents first, so the glory of David’s house and Jerusalem’s people will not exceed Judah’s.
v08: On that day the LORD will shield Jerusalem’s people. The one who stumbles among them will be like David, and David’s house like God, like the angel of the LORD going before them.
v09: On that day I will set out to destroy all the nations coming against Jerusalem.
</p>

## Mourning for the pierced one

<p>
v10: I will pour out a spirit of grace and supplication on David’s house and Jerusalem’s people. They will look to me, the one they pierced, and mourn over him as one mourns an only child; they will grieve bitterly over him as over a firstborn.
v11: On that day the mourning in Jerusalem will be great, like the mourning for Hadad-rimmon in the plain of Megiddo.
</p>

<p>
v12: The land will mourn, clan by clan, each apart: David’s clan apart and their wives apart; Nathan’s clan apart and their wives apart;
v13: Levi’s clan apart and their wives apart; the Shimeites’ clan apart and their wives apart;
v14: all the remaining clans, each clan apart and their wives apart.
</p>

## Notes

v10: The Hebrew shifts from “me, whom they pierced” to mourning “for him.” Both pronouns are retained; the draft does not remove the tension.

v11: Hadad-rimmon and Megiddo locate a comparison whose precise historical reference is not explained here.

## Vocabulary

v02: **Cup of reeling**: An image of destabilizing intoxication for those surrounding Jerusalem.

v10: **Only child; firstborn**: Family losses used to express the intensity of mourning.
