## A question about fasting

<p>
v01: In King Darius’s fourth year, on the fourth day of the ninth month, Kislev, the word of the LORD came to Zechariah.
v02: Bethel had sent Sharezer, Regem-melech, and his men to seek the LORD’s favor
v03: and to ask the priests of the house of the LORD of Armies and the prophets, “Should I mourn and abstain in the fifth month, as I have done for so many years?”
</p>

<p>
v04: The word of the LORD of Armies came to me:
v05: “Tell all the people of the land and the priests: When you fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh months for these seventy years, was it really for me that you fasted?
v06: And when you eat and drink, are you not eating and drinking for yourselves?
v07: Are these not the words the LORD proclaimed through the earlier prophets, when Jerusalem and its surrounding towns were inhabited and at ease, and the Negev and the lowlands were inhabited?”
</p>

## Justice and a refusal to listen

<p>
v08: The word of the LORD came to Zechariah:
v09: “The LORD of Armies says: Judge with true justice. Show faithful kindness and compassion to one another.
v10: Do not oppress the widow, the fatherless, the resident foreigner, or the poor. Do not plan harm against one another in your hearts.”
</p>

<p>
v11: But they refused to pay attention. They turned a stubborn shoulder and stopped their ears from hearing.
v12: They made their hearts hard as flint, refusing to hear the instruction and words the LORD of Armies had sent by his spirit through the earlier prophets. So great anger came from the LORD of Armies.
v13: “Just as he called and they would not listen, so they called and I would not listen,” says the LORD of Armies.
v14: “I scattered them like a storm among nations they had not known. The land was left desolate behind them, with no one going out or returning. They made a desirable land into a wasteland.”
</p>

## Notes

v02: Bethel is understood here as the community sending the delegation; the Hebrew can also be construed with Bethel as part of a personal name.

v09–10: The commands pair faithful love with concrete treatment of the widow, orphan, foreigner, and poor person.

## Vocabulary

v03: **Fast**: Abstaining from food as part of mourning or religious observance.

v12: **Flint**: An image of extreme hardness in the refusal to hear.
