## The flying scroll

<p>
v01: Again I looked up and saw a flying scroll.
v02: He asked me, “What do you see?” I answered, “A flying scroll, twenty cubits long and ten cubits wide.”
v03: He said, “This is the curse going out over the whole land. Every thief will be swept away according to what is on one side, and everyone who swears will be swept away according to what is on the other.
v04: I have sent it out,” declares the LORD of Armies. “It will enter the thief’s house and the house of anyone who swears falsely by my name. It will stay inside that house and consume it, timbers and stones alike.”
</p>

## The woman in the basket

<p>
v05: The angel speaking with me came forward and said, “Look up and see what is coming out.”
v06: I asked, “What is it?” He answered, “An ephah basket coming out.” He added, “This is their appearance throughout the land.”
v07: A lead cover was lifted, and there was a woman sitting inside the ephah basket.
v08: He said, “This is Wickedness.” He pushed her back into the ephah basket and threw the lead weight over its opening.
</p>

<p>
v09: I looked up and saw two women coming forward, with wind in their wings. Their wings were like a stork’s. They lifted the ephah basket between earth and heaven.
v10: I asked the angel speaking with me, “Where are they taking the ephah basket?”
v11: He answered, “To build a house for it in the land of Shinar. When it is ready, the basket will be set there on its base.”
</p>

## Notes

v03: The clauses about the two offenses and the scroll’s sides are difficult. Their relation to the curse is clearer than the exact construction.

v06: “Appearance” follows the pinned Hebrew; it is not changed to “iniquity.”

## Vocabulary

v02: **Cubit**: An ancient length measure based on the forearm; the scroll is twenty by ten cubits.

v06: **Ephah**: A grain measure and its measuring container, which becomes the vision’s basket.
