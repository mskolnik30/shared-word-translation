## The day of the LORD

<p>
v01: Look, a day belonging to the LORD is coming, when your plunder will be divided within you.
v02: “I will gather all the nations against Jerusalem for battle. The city will be captured, its houses plundered, and its women raped. Half the city will go into exile, but the rest of the people will not be cut off from the city.”
</p>

<p>
v03: Then the LORD will go out and fight those nations, as he fights on a day of battle.
v04: On that day his feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem. The Mount of Olives will split in two from east to west, making a very great valley. Half the mountain will move north and half south.
v05: You will flee through the valley of my mountains, for the valley will reach Azel. You will flee as you fled from the earthquake in the days of Uzziah king of Judah. Then the LORD my God will come, and all the holy ones with you.
</p>

## Living water and one king

<p>
v06: On that day there will be no light; the precious lights will congeal.
v07: It will be a unique day, known to the LORD, neither day nor night. At evening there will be light.
v08: On that day living water will flow from Jerusalem, half toward the eastern sea and half toward the western sea, in summer and winter alike.
v09: The LORD will be king over all the earth. On that day the LORD will be one, and his name one.
</p>

<p>
v10: The whole land will become like the Arabah, from Geba to Rimmon south of Jerusalem. Jerusalem will be raised up and remain in her place, from the Benjamin Gate to the site of the First Gate, to the Corner Gate, and from the Tower of Hananel to the king’s winepresses.
v11: People will live in her. There will be no more devotion to destruction; Jerusalem will live securely.
</p>

## Judgment on the attackers

<p>
v12: This is the plague with which the LORD will strike all the peoples who fought against Jerusalem: their flesh will rot while they stand on their feet, their eyes will rot in their sockets, and their tongues will rot in their mouths.
v13: On that day a great panic from the LORD will fall on them. Each will seize his neighbor’s hand and raise his own hand against his neighbor’s.
v14: Judah too will fight at Jerusalem. The wealth of all the surrounding nations will be gathered—gold, silver, and clothing in great abundance.
v15: The same plague will fall on the horses, mules, camels, donkeys, and all the animals in those camps.
</p>

## The nations worship

<p>
v16: Everyone left from all the nations that came against Jerusalem will go up year after year to bow before the King, the LORD of Armies, and celebrate the Festival of Booths.
v17: If any of the earth’s families do not go up to Jerusalem to bow before the King, the LORD of Armies, no rain will fall on them.
v18: If Egypt’s family does not go up and come, they too will receive no rain. The plague will strike them, as the LORD strikes the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Festival of Booths.
v19: This will be Egypt’s punishment and the punishment of all the nations that do not go up to celebrate the Festival of Booths.
</p>

<p>
v20: On that day the horses’ bells will bear the words “Holy to the LORD.” The cooking pots in the LORD’s house will be like the sacrificial bowls before the altar.
v21: Every cooking pot in Jerusalem and Judah will be holy to the LORD of Armies. All who sacrifice will come, take them, and cook in them. No Canaanite will remain in the house of the LORD of Armies on that day.
</p>

## Notes

v02: The capture of the city includes rape. The draft names the harm plainly.

v05: The pinned wording describes fleeing and ends “with you.” These are not silently changed to an alternative verb or to “with him.”

v06: The written form is rendered with the congealing verb; the source also records a reading tradition involving frost. The whole light-and-cold clause is difficult.

v14,18,21: Verse 14 can mean fighting at or against Jerusalem. Verse 18 has an elliptical negative; “rain” is supplied from verse 17. “Canaanite” in verse 21 may also mean a merchant.

## Vocabulary

v08: **Living waters**: Flowing water, here issuing from Jerusalem in both seasons.

v16: **Festival of Booths**: The festival named in the vision of the nations’ worship.
