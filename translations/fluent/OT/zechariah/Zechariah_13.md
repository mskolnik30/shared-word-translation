## A fountain for cleansing

<p>
v01: On that day a fountain will be opened for David’s house and Jerusalem’s people, for sin and impurity.
</p>

## False prophecy removed

<p>
v02: On that day,” declares the LORD of Armies, “I will cut off the idols’ names from the land, and they will be remembered no more. I will also remove the prophets and the spirit of impurity from the land.
v03: If anyone still prophesies, his father and mother who gave him life will tell him, ‘You must not live, because you have spoken lies in the LORD’s name.’ His father and mother who gave him life will pierce him when he prophesies.
</p>

<p>
v04: On that day every prophet will be ashamed of his vision when he prophesies. They will no longer put on a hairy cloak to deceive.
v05: He will say, ‘I am no prophet. I work the ground; someone bought me when I was young.’
v06: If someone asks him, ‘What are these wounds between your arms?’ he will answer, ‘The wounds I received in the house of those who love me.’
</p>

## The shepherd struck

<p>
v07: Sword, wake against my shepherd,
against the man who is my associate,”
declares the LORD of Armies.
“Strike the shepherd, and the sheep will scatter;
I will turn my hand against the little ones.
v08: Throughout the land,” declares the LORD,
“two-thirds will be cut off and die,
but one-third will remain.
v09: I will bring that third through fire,
refine them as silver is refined,
and test them as gold is tested.
They will call on my name, and I will answer.
I will say, ‘They are my people,’
and they will say, ‘The LORD is my God.’”
</p>

## Notes

v05: The statement about being bought from youth is difficult; the draft preserves it without inventing an occupation history.

v06: The wounds are literally “between your arms,” potentially the chest or back area. “Those who love me” is retained without assigning this exchange to a later figure.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Fountain**: The opened water source is an image of cleansing from sin and impurity.

v09: **Refine**: Purifying metal by fire becomes an image for testing the surviving people.
