## Joshua before the angel

<p>
v01: He showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the LORD, with the accuser at his right hand to accuse him.
v02: The LORD said to the accuser, “The LORD rebuke you, accuser! The LORD who chooses Jerusalem rebuke you! Is this not a burning stick snatched from the fire?”
v03: Joshua stood before the angel wearing filthy clothes.
v04: The angel told those standing before him, “Take off his filthy clothes.” Then he said to Joshua, “See, I have removed your guilt from you, and I will dress you in fine robes.”
v05: I said, “Let them put a clean turban on his head.” So they placed a clean turban on his head and dressed him, while the angel of the LORD stood nearby.
</p>

## A charge and a promise

<p>
v06: The angel of the LORD solemnly charged Joshua:
v07: “The LORD of Armies says, ‘If you walk in my ways and keep my charge, you will govern my house and guard my courts. I will give you access among these who stand here.
</p>

<p>
v08: Listen, Joshua the high priest, you and your companions seated before you—for they are men who serve as signs. I am bringing my servant, the Branch.
v09: Look at the stone I have set before Joshua: on that one stone are seven eyes. I will engrave its inscription,’ declares the LORD of Armies, ‘and I will remove the guilt of that land in a single day.
v10: On that day,’ declares the LORD of Armies, ‘each of you will invite his neighbor under his vine and fig tree.’”
</p>

## Notes

v01: “The accuser” renders the role expressed by the Hebrew satan in this courtroom scene.

v05: The Hebrew says “I said.” The prophet’s intervention is retained rather than reassigned to the angel.

v09: The one stone and seven eyes are preserved as the vision presents them; their full symbolism is not specified.

## Vocabulary

v02: **Burning stick**: A piece of burning wood rescued from a fire.

v08: **Branch**: A growth image used as a title of the coming servant.
