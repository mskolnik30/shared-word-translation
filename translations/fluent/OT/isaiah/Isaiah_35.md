## The Holy Way

<p>
v01: The wilderness and dry land will rejoice;
the desert plain will celebrate and bloom like a meadow flower.
v02: It will bloom abundantly,
celebrate with joy and singing.
Lebanon’s glory will be given to it,
Carmel’s and Sharon’s splendor.
They will see the **LORD**’s glory, our God’s splendor.
v03: Strengthen slack hands;
make trembling knees firm.
v04: Tell those with anxious hearts,
“Be strong! Do not fear!
Look, your God will come with vengeance,
with God’s repayment.
He himself will come and save you.”
</p>

<p>
v05: Then blind eyes will open,
and deaf ears will be unstopped.
v06: Then the lame will leap like a deer,
and the mute tongue will sing for joy.
For waters will burst out in the wilderness,
streams in the desert plain.
v07: The mirage will become a pool,
thirsty ground springs of water.
In the jackals’ former resting place,
grass will become reeds and papyrus.
v08: A highway, a road, will be there,
called the Holy Way.
No unclean person will travel it;
it will be for those who walk that way.
Even fools will not go astray.
</p>

<p>
v09: No lion will be there;
no ravenous beast will climb onto it
or be found there.
The redeemed will walk there.
v10: Those ransomed by the **LORD** will return
and come to Zion with singing,
everlasting joy upon their heads.
Gladness and joy will overtake them;
sorrow and sighing will flee.
</p>

## Notes

v08: The final clause can mean that even fools will not lose their way, or that fools will not travel on this road. The draft takes it as an assurance about the way.

## Vocabulary

v10: **Ransomed**: Released at a cost. The return song recurs almost word for word in Isaiah 51:11.
