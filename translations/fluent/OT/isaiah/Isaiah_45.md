## Cyrus and the LORD Who Creates

<p>
v01: The **LORD** says to his anointed, to Cyrus,
whose right hand I have grasped
to subdue nations before him,
to loosen kings’ belts,
to open doors before him so gates cannot be shut:
v02: “I will go ahead of you and straighten the rough places.
I will shatter bronze doors and cut through iron bars.
v03: I will give you treasures kept in darkness,
wealth hidden in secret places,
so you may know that I am the **LORD**,
Israel’s God, who calls you by name.
v04: For the sake of Jacob my servant, Israel my chosen one,
I call you by name and give you a title,
though you do not know me.
</p>

<p>
v05: I am the **LORD**, and there is no other;
apart from me there is no God.
I equip you, though you do not know me,
v06: so people from the sun’s rising to its setting
may know there is none besides me.
I am the **LORD**, and there is no other.
v07: I form light and create darkness,
make well-being and create disaster.
I, the **LORD**, do all these things.
v08: Drip down, heavens above;
let the skies rain righteousness.
Let the earth open and salvation bear fruit,
and let righteousness sprout with it.
I, the **LORD**, have created it.”
</p>

<p>
v09: Woe to one who quarrels with his Maker,
a potsherd among earth’s potsherds!
Does clay ask its potter, “What are you making?”
Does your work say, “He has no hands”?
v10: Woe to one who says to a father, “What are you begetting?”
or to a woman, “What are you laboring to bring forth?”
v11: The **LORD**, Israel’s Holy One and Maker, says:
“Ask me about things to come;
would you command me about my children
and the work of my hands?
v12: I made the earth and created humanity upon it.
My own hands stretched out the heavens,
and I commanded all their host.
</p>

<p>
v13: I stirred him up in righteousness
and will make all his roads straight.
He will build my city and release my exiles,
without payment or bribe,”
says the **LORD** of hosts.
v14: The **LORD** says:
“Egypt’s labor and Cush’s trade,
and the tall Sabeans, will come over to you and be yours.
They will follow you, coming in chains,
bowing before you and pleading with you:
‘Surely God is in you, and there is no other;
there is no other God.’”
v15: Truly, you are a God who hides himself,
Israel’s God, Savior.
v16: All of them are ashamed and disgraced;
the makers of images go away in disgrace together.
</p>

<p>
v17: But Israel is saved by the **LORD**
with everlasting salvation.
You will not be ashamed or disgraced
forever and ever.
v18: For the **LORD** says,
the Creator of the heavens—he is God—
the one who formed and made the earth, who established it,
who did not create it as emptiness but formed it to be inhabited:
“I am the **LORD**, and there is no other.
v19: I did not speak in secret, in a dark land;
I did not tell Jacob’s offspring, ‘Seek me in emptiness.’
I, the **LORD**, speak righteousness,
declare what is straight.
v20: Gather and come, draw near together,
you survivors of the nations!
Those who carry their wooden idols know nothing;
they pray to a god who cannot save.
</p>

<p>
v21: Declare your case; present it! Let them consult together.
Who made this heard long ago, announced it in advance?
Was it not I, the **LORD**?
There is no other God beside me,
a righteous God and Savior; there is none except me.
v22: Turn to me and be saved, all earth’s ends,
for I am God, and there is no other.
v23: By myself I have sworn;
a righteous word has gone from my mouth and will not return:
to me every knee will bend,
every tongue will swear allegiance.
v24: ‘Only in the **LORD**,’ people will say of me,
‘are righteousness and strength.’
To him all who rage against him will come and be ashamed.
</p>

<p>
v25: In the **LORD** all Israel’s offspring
will be vindicated and glory.”
</p>

## Notes

v01,07: “Anointed” explicitly describes Cyrus. The word rendered “disaster” can also mean evil; the verse attributes both well-being and calamity to the LORD.

v11,14: Verse 11 can be punctuated as commands or as a challenge to those issuing commands to God. Verse 14 explicitly describes people arriving in chains.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Anointed**: Marked out for a task or office, using the language of consecration with oil.
