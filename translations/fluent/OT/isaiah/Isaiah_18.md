## An Oracle Concerning Cush

<p>
v01: Ah, land of whirring wings,
beyond the rivers of Cush,
v02: sending envoys by water,
in papyrus boats over the waters!
Go, swift messengers,
to a nation tall and smooth-skinned,
to a people feared near and far,
a powerful, trampling nation
whose land the rivers divide.
v03: All who inhabit the world,
all who dwell on earth:
when a signal is raised on the mountains, look!
When a horn is sounded, listen!
v04: For the **LORD** told me:
“I will remain still and watch from my dwelling,
like clear heat in sunlight,
like a cloud of dew in harvest heat.”
</p>

<p>
v05: Before harvest, when flowering ends
and blossoms become ripening grapes,
he will cut off the shoots with pruning hooks,
remove and slash the spreading branches.
v06: They will all be left
for mountain birds of prey and wild animals.
The birds will summer on them;
the wild animals will winter on them.
v07: At that time tribute will be brought to the **LORD** of hosts from a people tall and smooth-skinned, a people feared near and far, a powerful, trampling nation whose land the rivers divide—to Mount Zion, the place of the name of the **LORD** of hosts.
</p>

## Notes

v02,07: The nation’s physical description and the phrases about measuring or trampling are difficult. The draft keeps their ancient descriptive force without identifying a modern ethnic group.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Cush**: A region south of Egypt in the ancient geographic horizon; it should not simply be equated with one modern country.
