## Judah Called to Justice

<p>
v01: The vision Isaiah son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem, during the reigns of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah.
v02: Hear, heavens! Listen, earth!
The **LORD** has spoken:
“I raised children and brought them up,
but they rebelled against me.
v03: An ox knows its owner,
a donkey its master’s feeding trough,
but Israel does not know;
my people do not understand.”
v04: Alas, sinful nation,
people weighed down with guilt,
offspring of evildoers, corrupt children!
They have abandoned the **LORD**,
scorned the Holy One of Israel,
and turned their backs.
</p>

<p>
v05: Why invite more blows?
Why keep rebelling?
The whole head is sick,
the whole heart faint.
v06: From the sole of the foot to the head,
no part is sound—
wounds, bruises, raw sores,
not cleaned, not bandaged,
not soothed with oil.
v07: Your country is desolate;
your cities have burned.
Foreigners devour your land before your eyes;
it is desolate, overthrown by strangers.
v08: Daughter Zion is left
like a shelter in a vineyard,
a hut in a cucumber field,
a city under siege.
</p>

<p>
v09: Had the **LORD** of hosts not left us a few survivors,
we would have become like Sodom,
like Gomorrah.
v10: Hear the **LORD**’s word, rulers of Sodom!
Listen to our God’s instruction, people of Gomorrah!
v11: “What do I want with all your sacrifices?” says the **LORD**.
“I have had enough burnt offerings of rams
and the fat of well-fed animals.
I take no pleasure in the blood
of bulls, lambs, or male goats.
v12: When you come to appear before me,
who asked you to trample my courts?
</p>

<p>
v13: Bring no more empty offerings!
Your incense disgusts me.
New moons, Sabbaths, summoned assemblies—
I cannot bear wrongdoing joined to solemn gatherings.
v14: I hate your new moons and appointed festivals.
They have become a burden;
I am tired of carrying them.
v15: When you spread out your hands,
I will hide my eyes from you.
Even if you multiply prayers, I will not listen.
Your hands are full of blood.
v16: Wash yourselves; make yourselves clean.
Remove your evil deeds from my sight.
Stop doing evil.
</p>

<p>
v17: Learn to do good. Seek justice.
Set oppression right.
Defend the fatherless;
plead the widow’s case.
v18: “Come now, let us settle the matter,” says the **LORD**.
“Though your sins are scarlet,
they shall be white as snow;
though red as crimson,
they shall become like wool.
v19: If you are willing and listen,
you will eat the land’s good produce.
v20: But if you refuse and rebel,
the sword will eat you.”
The mouth of the **LORD** has spoken.
</p>

<p>
v21: How the faithful city has become a prostitute!
Once she was full of justice;
righteousness lodged in her—
but now, murderers.
v22: Your silver has become dross;
your drink is diluted with water.
v23: Your rulers are rebels, partners of thieves.
Every one loves a bribe and chases rewards.
They do not defend the fatherless;
the widow’s case never reaches them.
v24: Therefore the Lord, the **LORD** of hosts,
the Mighty One of Israel, declares:
“Ah, I will find relief from my adversaries
and take vengeance on my enemies.
</p>

<p>
v25: I will turn my hand against you,
smelt away your dross as with lye,
and remove all your alloy.
v26: I will restore your judges as at first,
your counselors as in the beginning.
Afterward you will be called
City of Righteousness, Faithful City.”
v27: Zion will be redeemed through justice,
and those who return to her through righteousness.
v28: But rebels and sinners will be shattered together;
those who abandon the **LORD** will come to an end.
</p>

<p>
v29: They will be ashamed of the mighty trees you desired;
you will blush for the gardens you chose.
v30: You will be like a terebinth with withering leaves,
like a garden without water.
v31: The strong will become tinder,
and their work a spark.
Both will burn together,
with no one to put them out.
</p>

## Notes

v17: The difficult command can mean setting oppression right, correcting an oppressor, or helping the oppressed. All readings place justice at the center of the surrounding commands.

v18: The invitation is language of settling a dispute, not simply a request for abstract reasoning. The stained cloth imagery follows it.

## Vocabulary

v25: **Lye**: A cleansing substance used here in the refining image; the exact comparison can also be heard as thorough purification.
