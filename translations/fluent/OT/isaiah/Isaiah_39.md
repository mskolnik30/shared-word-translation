## Envoys from Babylon

<p>
v01: At that time Merodach-baladan son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a gift to Hezekiah, having heard that he had been ill and had recovered.
v02: Hezekiah welcomed them gladly and showed them his treasury—the silver, gold, spices, fine oil, his whole armory, and everything in his storehouses. There was nothing in his palace or in his entire domain that Hezekiah did not show them.
v03: The prophet Isaiah came to King Hezekiah and asked, “What did these men say, and where did they come from?” Hezekiah answered, “They came to me from a distant land, from Babylon.”
v04: Isaiah asked, “What did they see in your palace?” Hezekiah said, “They saw everything in my palace. There is nothing in my storehouses I did not show them.”
v05: Then Isaiah said to Hezekiah, “Hear the word of the **LORD** of hosts:
</p>

<p>
v06: ‘Days are coming when everything in your palace, everything your ancestors have stored up until today, will be carried to Babylon. Nothing will remain,’ says the **LORD**.
v07: ‘Some of your own sons, born to you, will be taken away and become eunuchs in the king of Babylon’s palace.’”
v08: Hezekiah said to Isaiah, “The **LORD**’s word you have spoken is good.” For he said, “There will be peace and stability in my days.”
</p>

## Notes

v07: Saris can describe a eunuch or a court official. The prospect is service in the Babylonian king’s palace; the bodily implication is not excluded.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Merodach-baladan**: The Babylonian king who sends the delegation in this account.
