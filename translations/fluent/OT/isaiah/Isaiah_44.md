## Israel Chosen and Idols Exposed

<p>
v01: “But now listen, Jacob my servant,
Israel whom I have chosen.
v02: The **LORD**, who made you and formed you from the womb,
who will help you, says:
Do not fear, Jacob my servant,
Jeshurun whom I have chosen.
v03: I will pour water on thirsty ground,
streams on dry land.
I will pour my Spirit on your offspring,
my blessing on your descendants.
v04: They will sprout among the grass,
like willows beside flowing water.
</p>

<p>
v05: One will say, ‘I belong to the **LORD**’;
another will call himself by Jacob’s name.
Another will write on his hand, ‘The **LORD**’s,’
and take the name Israel.
v06: The **LORD**, Israel’s King and Redeemer,
the **LORD** of hosts, says:
I am first and I am last;
apart from me there is no God.
v07: Who is like me? Let him call out,
declare it and set it before me,
since I established the ancient people.
Let them declare what is coming and what will happen.
v08: Do not be frightened or afraid.
Have I not long ago made you hear and told you?
You are my witnesses.
Is there any God besides me?
There is no other Rock; I know of none.”
</p>

<p>
v09: All who form idols are emptiness; their treasured objects bring no benefit. Their witnesses neither see nor know, and so they are put to shame.
v10: Who would form a god or cast an idol that brings no benefit?
v11: Look, all its companions will be ashamed; its craftsmen are only human. Let them all gather and stand forward: they will tremble and be ashamed together.
v12: The smith works with a cutting tool over the coals, shaping it with hammers and working it with his strong arm. But he grows hungry and loses strength; without water to drink he becomes faint.
</p>

<p>
v13: The woodworker stretches a line, marks the shape with a stylus, works it with planes, and outlines it with a compass. He makes it in a man’s shape, with human beauty, to live in a house.
v14: He cuts cedars, takes a holm oak or an oak, and lets it grow strong among the forest’s trees. He plants a pine, and the rain makes it grow.
v15: It becomes fuel for someone. He takes some and warms himself; he lights a fire and bakes bread. He also makes a god and worships it; he makes an idol and bows before it.
v16: Half he burns in the fire. Over that half he cooks meat, roasts it and eats his fill. He also warms himself and says, “Ah! I am warm; I can see the fire!”
</p>

<p>
v17: With the rest he makes a god, his idol. He bows and worships it, prays to it and says, “Rescue me, for you are my god!”
v18: They do not know or understand, for their eyes are smeared shut so they cannot see, and their hearts so they cannot discern.
v19: No one takes it to heart or has the knowledge and understanding to say, “Half I burned in the fire; I baked bread on its coals, roasted meat and ate it. Shall I make the rest an abomination? Shall I bow before a block of wood?”
v20: He feeds on ashes. A deceived heart has led him astray; he cannot rescue himself or say, “Is there not a lie in my right hand?”
</p>

<p>
v21: “Remember these things, Jacob,
Israel, for you are my servant.
I formed you; you are my servant.
Israel, you will not be forgotten by me.
v22: I have wiped away your rebellions like a thick cloud,
your sins like a cloud.
Return to me, for I have redeemed you.”
v23: Sing, heavens, for the **LORD** has acted!
Shout, depths of the earth!
Burst into song, mountains, forest, and every tree in it!
For the **LORD** has redeemed Jacob
and displays his glory in Israel.
v24: The **LORD**, your Redeemer, who formed you from the womb, says:
“I am the **LORD**, maker of everything,
stretching out the heavens alone,
spreading the earth by myself,
</p>

<p>
v25: frustrating liars’ signs and making fools of diviners,
turning the wise back
and making their knowledge foolish,
v26: confirming my servant’s word
and fulfilling my messengers’ counsel;
saying of Jerusalem, ‘She will be inhabited,’
and of Judah’s cities, ‘They will be rebuilt;
I will raise her ruins,’
v27: saying to the deep, ‘Dry up!
I will dry your rivers,’
v28: saying of Cyrus, ‘My shepherd!
He will fulfill all my purpose,’
and saying of Jerusalem, ‘She will be rebuilt,’
and of the temple, ‘Your foundation will be laid.’”
</p>

## Notes

v05,24: Writing “LORD’s” on the hand can also be understood as signing oneself over to the LORD. In verse 24 the recorded reading is “by myself”; the written form asks, “Who was with me?”

## Vocabulary

v02: **Jeshurun**: A poetic name for Israel associated with being upright.
