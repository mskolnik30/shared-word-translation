## Awake, Zion

<p>
v01: Awake, awake! Put on your strength, Zion!
Put on your splendid clothes,
Jerusalem, holy city.
The uncircumcised and the unclean
will no longer enter you.
</p>

<p>
v02: Shake off the dust; rise and take your seat, Jerusalem.
Free yourself from the bonds around your neck,
captive Daughter Zion.
</p>

<p>
v03: For this is what the **LORD** says:
“You were sold for nothing,
and you will be redeemed without silver.”
</p>

<p>
v04: For this is what the Lord **GOD** says:
“My people first went down to Egypt to live there as foreigners;
then Assyria oppressed them for nothing.
</p>

<p>
v05: Now what do I have here?” declares the **LORD**.
“My people have been taken for nothing;
their rulers howl,” declares the **LORD**,
“and my name is despised continually, all day long.
</p>

<p>
v06: So my people will know my name;
on that day they will know
that I am the one speaking.
Here I am!”
</p>

## Good News of Deliverance

<p>
v07: How beautiful on the mountains
are the feet of the messenger
who announces peace, who brings good news,
who announces salvation,
who says to Zion, “Your God reigns!”
</p>

<p>
v08: Listen! Your watchmen raise their voices;
together they sing for joy.
With their own eyes they see
the **LORD** returning to Zion.
</p>

<p>
v09: Break into joyful song together,
ruins of Jerusalem!
For the **LORD** has comforted his people;
he has redeemed Jerusalem.
</p>

<p>
v10: The **LORD** has bared his holy arm
before all the nations.
All the ends of the earth will see
the salvation of our God.
</p>

## Depart, Be Clean

<p>
v11: Leave, leave! Go out from there!
Touch nothing unclean.
Go out from her midst; purify yourselves,
you who carry the **LORD**’s vessels.
</p>

<p>
v12: You will not leave in haste
or go out in flight,
for the **LORD** goes before you,
and Israel’s God guards your rear.
</p>

## The Servant Will Be Exalted

<p>
v13: Look, my servant will act wisely;
he will rise, be lifted up, and be very high.
</p>

<p>
v14: Just as many were appalled at you—
so disfigured was his appearance beyond that of a man,
his form beyond that of human beings—
</p>

<p>
v15: so he will sprinkle many nations.
Kings will shut their mouths because of him,
for they will see what they had not been told
and understand what they had not heard.
</p>

## Notes

v13–15: The servant’s wise action can also mean success. The poem shifts from “you” to “his.” “Sprinkle” follows the usual sense of the pinned verb; “startle” is a debated alternative.

## Vocabulary

v11: **Vessels**: Objects used in sanctuary worship; their carriers are called to purity.
