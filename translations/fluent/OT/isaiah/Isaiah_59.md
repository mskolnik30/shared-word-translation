## Sin, Justice, and the Redeemer

<p>
v01: Look, the **LORD**’s hand is not too short to save,
nor his ear too dull to hear.
v02: Rather, your offenses have separated you from your God;
your sins have hidden his face from you,
so he does not hear.
v03: Your palms are stained with blood,
your fingers with guilt.
Your lips speak lies;
your tongue mutters injustice.
v04: No one brings a case justly;
no one pleads honestly.
They trust in emptiness and speak falsehood,
conceive trouble and give birth to wrongdoing.
</p>

<p>
v05: They hatch vipers’ eggs
and weave spiders’ webs.
Whoever eats their eggs dies;
when one is crushed, a snake hatches.
v06: Their webs cannot become clothing;
they cannot cover themselves with what they make.
Their works are works of wrongdoing;
acts of violence are in their hands.
v07: Their feet run to evil;
they hurry to shed innocent blood.
Their thoughts are thoughts of wrongdoing;
devastation and destruction mark their highways.
v08: They do not know the way of peace;
there is no justice in their tracks.
They have made their paths crooked;
no one walking them knows peace.
</p>

<p>
v09: So justice is far from us,
and righteousness does not reach us.
We wait for light, but there is darkness;
for brightness, but we walk in gloom.
v10: We grope along a wall like the blind,
groping like people without eyes.
We stumble at noon as at dusk;
among the vigorous we are like the dead.
v11: We all growl like bears
and moan and moan like doves.
We wait for justice, but there is none;
for salvation, but it is far from us.
v12: For our rebellions before you are many,
and our sins testify against us.
Our rebellions are with us;
we know our offenses:
</p>

<p>
v13: rebelling and denying the **LORD**,
turning back from following our God,
speaking oppression and revolt,
conceiving and uttering lying words from the heart.
v14: Justice is driven backward,
and righteousness stands far away.
Truth stumbles in the public square,
and honesty cannot enter.
v15: Truth is missing,
and whoever turns from evil becomes prey.
The **LORD** saw it, and it displeased him
that there was no justice.
v16: He saw that there was no one
and was appalled that no one intervened.
So his own arm brought him salvation,
and his righteousness sustained him.
</p>

<p>
v17: He put on righteousness as a breastplate
and a helmet of salvation on his head.
He dressed in garments of vengeance
and wrapped himself in zeal like a cloak.
v18: He will repay according to their deeds:
fury to his adversaries, repayment to his enemies;
to the coastlands he will repay what is due.
v19: From the west they will fear the **LORD**’s name,
and from the sunrise his glory.
For he will come like a rushing river
driven by the **LORD**’s wind.
v20: “A Redeemer will come to Zion,
to those in Jacob who turn from rebellion,”
declares the **LORD**.
</p>

<p>
v21: “As for me, this is my covenant with them,” says the **LORD**. “My Spirit upon you, and my words that I have put in your mouth, will not depart from your mouth, from your offspring’s mouths, or from the mouths of their offspring,” says the **LORD**, “from now and forever.”
</p>

## Notes

v10,19: The rare word rendered “vigorous” in verse 10 is uncertain. Verse 19 can also be read as an enemy coming like a flood and the LORD’s Spirit acting against him; the draft follows the rushing-river image of divine arrival.

## Vocabulary

v17: **Breastplate**: Armor protecting the torso; righteousness, salvation, vengeance and zeal become the divine warrior’s clothing.
