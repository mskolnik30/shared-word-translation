## Moab and the Throne of Steadfast Love

<p>
v01: Send a lamb to the ruler of the land,
from Sela through the wilderness
to Daughter Zion’s mountain.
v02: Like wandering birds,
like a scattered nest,
Moab’s daughters will be
at the Arnon crossings.
v03: “Give counsel; render judgment.
At noon make your shade like night.
Hide the outcasts;
do not betray the fugitive.
v04: Let my outcasts live with you, Moab;
be a hiding place for them from the destroyer.
For the extortioner is gone, destruction has ended,
the trampler has vanished from the land.
</p>

<p>
v05: A throne will be established in steadfast love,
and on it, in David’s tent,
one will sit faithfully,
judging, seeking justice,
and swift to do right.”
v06: We have heard of Moab’s pride—so very proud—
its arrogance, pride, and fury.
Its boasts are empty.
v07: So Moab will wail for Moab;
everyone will wail.
For Kir-hareseth’s raisin cakes
you will moan, utterly stricken.
v08: Heshbon’s fields have withered,
and Sibmah’s vine.
Rulers of nations have struck down its choice branches,
which reached Jazer and wandered into the wilderness;
its shoots spread out and crossed the sea.
</p>

<p>
v09: So I weep with Jazer over Sibmah’s vine.
I drench you with my tears, Heshbon and Elealeh,
for a shout has fallen
on your summer fruit and harvest.
v10: Joy and gladness are taken from fruitful fields;
no singing, no shouting rings through the vineyards.
No one treads wine in the presses.
I have ended the harvest shout.
v11: So my inward parts sound like a lyre for Moab,
my very heart for Kir-heres.
v12: When Moab appears at the high place and wears itself out, when it enters its sanctuary to pray, it will achieve nothing.
</p>

<p>
v13: This is the word the **LORD** spoke concerning Moab formerly.
v14: Now the **LORD** says, “Within three years, counted like a hired worker’s years, Moab’s glory and all its great multitude will be brought low. The remnant will be very small and powerless.”
</p>

## Notes

v04: The pointed Hebrew reads “my outcasts,” followed by an address to Moab. Reading “Moab’s outcasts” changes that relationship; the draft follows the pointed text.

v09–11: The speaker joins Moab’s grief. The lament is preserved alongside the judgment without supplying a new speaker whenever its emotional direction changes.

## Vocabulary

v14: **Years of a hired worker**: A carefully counted term of service; the image gives the three-year limit precision.
