## Judgment upon the Nations

<p>
v01: Come near, nations, and hear; peoples, pay attention!
Let the earth and all that fills it listen,
the world and all that springs from it.
v02: The **LORD** is angry with all nations,
furious with all their armies.
He has devoted them to destruction,
handed them over for slaughter.
v03: Their slain will be thrown out;
the stench of their corpses will rise,
and mountains will melt with their blood.
v04: All heaven’s host will decay,
and the heavens roll up like a scroll.
All their host will wither,
like a leaf withering on a vine,
like one withering on a fig tree.
</p>

<p>
v05: “My sword has drunk its fill in heaven.
Look! It descends on Edom,
on the people I have devoted to destruction, for judgment.”
v06: The **LORD** has a sword filled with blood,
smeared with fat—
the blood of lambs and goats, the fat of rams’ kidneys.
For the **LORD** has a sacrifice in Bozrah,
a great slaughter in Edom’s land.
v07: Wild oxen will fall with them,
young bulls with mighty bulls.
Their land will drink its fill of blood,
and their dust be enriched with fat.
v08: For the **LORD** has a day of vengeance,
a year of repayment for Zion’s cause.
</p>

<p>
v09: Edom’s streams will turn to pitch,
its dust to sulfur;
its land will become burning pitch.
v10: Night and day it will not go out;
its smoke will rise forever.
From generation to generation it will lie waste;
no one will pass through it forever and ever.
v11: Pelican and hedgehog will possess it;
owl and raven will live there.
He will stretch over it the measuring line of emptiness
and the plumb stones of waste.
v12: Its nobles—there is no one there to proclaim king;
all its officials will be nothing.
</p>

<p>
v13: Thorns will rise in its palaces,
nettles and brambles in its strongholds.
It will become a home for jackals,
an enclosure for ostriches.
v14: Desert creatures will meet hyenas;
a goat-demon will call to its fellow.
There Lilith will settle
and find herself a resting place.
v15: There an owl will nest and lay eggs,
hatch them and gather her young in her shade.
There the kites will gather,
each with its mate.
v16: Seek in the **LORD**’s scroll and read:
not one of these will be missing;
none will lack its mate.
For my mouth has commanded it,
and his spirit has gathered them.
</p>

<p>
v17: He has cast the lot for them;
his hand has divided it for them with a measuring line.
They will possess it forever,
live there from generation to generation.
</p>

## Notes

v11,14–15: Several animal names are uncertain. Lilith is retained as the name of a night being; translating it as an ordinary owl would settle a disputed identification.

v16: The Hebrew changes from “my mouth” to “his spirit.” The draft retains that shift rather than making both pronouns third person.

## Vocabulary

v11: **Emptiness and waste**: The measuring imagery uses terms also found in the creation account’s description of the unformed earth.
