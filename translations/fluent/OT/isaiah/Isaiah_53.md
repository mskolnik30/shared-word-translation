## The Suffering Servant

<p>
v01: Who has believed what we have heard?
To whom has the **LORD**’s arm been revealed?
</p>

<p>
v02: He grew before him like a tender shoot,
like a root from dry ground.
He had no fine form or splendor to draw our gaze,
no appearance to make us desire him.
</p>

<p>
v03: Despised and abandoned by people,
a man of pains, acquainted with sickness,
like someone from whom faces turn away—
he was despised, and we gave him no regard.
</p>

<p>
v04: Yet it was our sicknesses he carried,
our pains he bore.
We thought him stricken,
struck by God and afflicted.
</p>

<p>
v05: But he was pierced because of our rebellions,
crushed because of our offenses.
The discipline that brought us peace was upon him;
by his wound we were healed.
</p>

<p>
v06: All of us wandered like sheep;
each of us turned to our own way.
The **LORD** made the guilt of us all
fall upon him.
</p>

<p>
v07: He was oppressed and afflicted,
yet he did not open his mouth.
Like a lamb led to slaughter,
like a ewe silent before her shearers,
he did not open his mouth.
</p>

<p>
v08: Through oppression and judgment he was taken away.
Who considered his generation?
For he was cut off from the land of the living;
because of my people’s rebellion, the blow fell on him.
</p>

<p>
v09: They assigned his grave with the wicked,
and with a rich man in his death,
though he had done no violence
and no deceit was in his mouth.
</p>

<p>
v10: Yet the **LORD** desired to crush him and make him sick.
If you make his life a reparation offering,
he will see offspring and prolong his days,
and the **LORD**’s purpose will prosper in his hand.
</p>

<p>
v11: After his life’s anguish he will see and be satisfied.
By his knowledge my righteous servant
will bring righteousness to many,
and he will bear their offenses.
</p>

<p>
v12: So I will give him a share among the many,
and he will divide plunder with the strong,
because he poured out his life to death
and was counted among rebels.
Yet he carried the sin of many
and interceded for rebels.
</p>

## Notes

v08–09: “His generation” and the removal through oppression and judgment are disputed expressions. The suffix rendered “on him” can be plural, “on them.” Verse 9 has an unusual plural form, literally “in his deaths.”

v10–11: The offering clause can mean “if you make his life” or “if his life makes.” “By his knowledge” may mean the servant’s knowledge or knowledge of him. The pinned text does not include “light” after “see,” though another textual reading does.

## Vocabulary

v10: **Reparation offering**: Asham names guilt or an offering associated with addressing an offense. Its use here does not by itself settle every aspect of the servant’s identity or suffering.
