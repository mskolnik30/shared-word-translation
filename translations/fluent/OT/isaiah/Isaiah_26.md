## A Song of Trust

<p>
v01: On that day this song will be sung in Judah:
“We have a strong city!
He makes salvation its walls and rampart.
v02: Open the gates,
so a righteous nation may enter,
a nation that keeps faith.
v03: You keep in peace—peace—
the mind that rests on you,
because it trusts in you.
v04: Trust in the **LORD** forever,
for **YAH**, the **LORD**, is an everlasting rock.
</p>

<p>
v05: He has brought down those who live on high;
the lofty city he lays low,
lays it low to the ground, brings it to the dust.
v06: Feet trample it—
the feet of the oppressed, the steps of the poor.”
v07: The path of the righteous is level.
Upright One, you make the righteous person’s road smooth.
v08: Along the path of your judgments, **LORD**, we wait for you.
Your name, your remembrance, is our deepest desire.
</p>

<p>
v09: With my whole being I desire you at night;
with the spirit within me I seek you.
When your judgments are on the earth,
the world’s inhabitants learn righteousness.
v10: Show favor to the wicked, and they still do not learn righteousness.
In a land of uprightness they do wrong
and fail to see the **LORD**’s majesty.
v11: **LORD**, your hand is raised, but they do not see.
Let them see your zeal for your people and be ashamed;
let the fire for your enemies consume them.
v12: **LORD**, you establish peace for us;
everything we have accomplished, you have done for us.
</p>

<p>
v13: **LORD** our God, other lords have ruled us;
but through you alone we proclaim your name.
v14: They are dead; they will not live.
They are shades; they will not rise.
You visited them and destroyed them,
wiping away every memory of them.
v15: You have enlarged the nation, **LORD**,
you have enlarged the nation; you are glorified.
You have extended all the land’s boundaries.
v16: **LORD**, in distress they sought you;
they poured out a whispered prayer
when your discipline was upon them.
</p>

<p>
v17: As a pregnant woman near delivery
writhes and cries in labor,
so we were before you, **LORD**.
v18: We conceived, we writhed,
but gave birth only to wind.
We brought no salvation to the earth;
no inhabitants of the world were born.
v19: Your dead will live; my dead body will rise.
Wake and sing, you who dwell in dust!
Your dew is a dew of light,
and the earth will bring forth the shades.
v20: Go, my people, enter your rooms
and shut your door behind you.
Hide for a little while,
until the wrath passes.
</p>

<p>
v21: Look! The **LORD** comes out from his place
to call earth’s inhabitants to account for their guilt.
The earth will uncover its bloodshed
and no longer conceal its slain.
</p>

## Notes

v18–19: Verse 18’s “were born” can also be read “fell.” Verse 19 shifts from “your dead” to “my dead body,” with a plural verb; the corporate image is grammatically unusual. Its “dew of lights” is also rendered “dew of herbs.”

v14,19: The statement that the dead will not rise in verse 14 and the promise that “your dead” will live in verse 19 address different groups. Their contrast is retained.

## Vocabulary

v04: **YAH**: A shortened form of the divine name. The Hebrew places it alongside the full name rendered LORD.
