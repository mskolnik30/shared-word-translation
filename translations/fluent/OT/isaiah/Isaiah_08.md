## The Sign of Maher-Shalal-Hash-Baz

<p>
v01: The **LORD** said to me, “Take a large tablet and write on it with an ordinary stylus: ‘For Maher-shalal-hash-baz.’”
v02: “I will call reliable witnesses for myself: Uriah the priest and Zechariah son of Jeberechiah.”
v03: I went to the prophetess, and she conceived and bore a son. The **LORD** said to me, “Name him Maher-shalal-hash-baz.
v04: Before the boy knows how to say ‘My father’ or ‘My mother,’ Damascus’s wealth and Samaria’s plunder will be carried before the king of Assyria.”
</p>

<p>
v05: The **LORD** spoke to me again:
v06: “Because this people has rejected
the gently flowing waters of Shiloah
and rejoices over Rezin and Remaliah’s son,
v07: the Lord is bringing against them
the River’s mighty, surging waters—
the king of Assyria in all his glory.
It will rise over every channel
and overflow every bank.
v08: It will sweep into Judah, flooding through,
reaching up to the neck.
Its outspread wings will fill your land’s breadth,
Immanuel.”
</p>

<p>
v09: Rage, peoples, and be shattered!
Listen, all distant lands!
Prepare for battle—and be shattered!
Prepare for battle—and be shattered!
v10: Devise a plan—it will be broken.
Speak a word—it will not stand,
for God is with us.
v11: The **LORD** spoke to me with his hand strong upon me, warning me not to walk in this people’s way:
v12: “Do not call everything a conspiracy
that this people calls a conspiracy.
Do not fear what they fear
or be terrified.
</p>

<p>
v13: The **LORD** of hosts—regard him as holy.
Let him be your fear,
let him be your terror.
v14: He will become a sanctuary,
yet a stone to strike against,
a rock to stumble over,
for both houses of Israel;
a trap and snare for Jerusalem’s inhabitants.
v15: Many will stumble over them,
fall and break,
be snared and caught.”
v16: Bind up the testimony;
seal the instruction among my disciples.
</p>

<p>
v17: I will wait for the **LORD**,
who hides his face from the house of Jacob.
I will hope in him.
v18: Here I am, with the children the **LORD** has given me, as signs and wonders in Israel from the **LORD** of hosts, who dwells on Mount Zion.
v19: When they tell you, “Consult the mediums and spiritists who chirp and mutter,” should not a people consult its God? Should the dead be consulted for the living?
v20: To the instruction and the testimony! If they do not speak according to this word, there is no dawn for them.
</p>

<p>
v21: They will pass through the land distressed and hungry. When hunger overtakes them, they will rage and curse their king and their God. They will turn their faces upward,
v22: then look toward the earth: distress and darkness, oppressive gloom! They will be driven into deep darkness.
</p>

## Notes

v06: The Hebrew text used here speaks of rejoicing with Rezin and Remaliah’s son. Readings about melting in fear use a different understanding or alteration of the text.

v14: The source includes both a sanctuary and a stone or rock that causes stumbling, followed by a trap. All those images are retained.

v20: The relation of the final clause to the preceding instruction is compressed. The draft retains the image of having no dawn rather than turning it into a general verdict about ignorance.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Maher-shalal-hash-baz**: A sign-name meaning roughly “quick spoil, speedy plunder,” explained by the coming removal of Damascus’s and Samaria’s wealth.
