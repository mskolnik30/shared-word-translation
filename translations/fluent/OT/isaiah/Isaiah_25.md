## The LORD's Feast and Salvation

<p>
v01: **LORD**, you are my God.
I will exalt you and praise your name,
for you have done wonders—
plans from long ago, faithfully fulfilled.
v02: You have made a city a heap,
a fortified town a ruin.
The foreigners’ palace is a city no more;
it will never be rebuilt.
v03: So a strong people will honor you;
the city of ruthless nations will fear you.
v04: You have been a refuge for the poor,
a refuge for the needy in distress,
a shelter from the storm, a shade from the heat.
For the breath of the ruthless is like a storm against a wall,
</p>

<p>
v05: like heat in a dry place.
You subdue the foreigners’ uproar;
as heat is subdued by a cloud’s shade,
the song of the ruthless is brought low.
v06: On this mountain the **LORD** of hosts will prepare
a feast for all peoples:
rich food, aged wine,
rich food full of marrow, aged wine well strained.
v07: On this mountain he will swallow up
the covering spread over all peoples,
the veil laid over all nations.
v08: He will swallow up death forever.
The Lord **GOD** will wipe tears from every face
and remove his people’s disgrace from all the earth,
for the **LORD** has spoken.
</p>

<p>
v09: On that day people will say,
“Look, this is our God! We waited for him, and he saved us.
This is the **LORD**; we waited for him.
Let us rejoice and be glad in his salvation!”
v10: The **LORD**’s hand will rest on this mountain,
but Moab will be trampled where it stands,
as straw is trampled in a dung pit.
v11: Moab will spread its hands in it
as a swimmer spreads his hands to swim.
But he will bring down Moab’s pride,
despite the skill of its hands.
v12: Your high, fortified walls
he has brought down, laid low,
struck to the ground, to the dust.
</p>

## Notes

v07–08: The same Hebrew verb describes swallowing up the covering and swallowing up death. “All peoples” and “all faces” give the promise its breadth.

v10–11: The recorded reading has straw trampled “in” a dung pit; the written form mentions its waters. Moab’s spreading hands are compared to swimming, but the final expression about their skill is uncertain.

## Vocabulary

v06: **Aged wine**: Wine left to mature on its sediment and then strained; part of the rich feast imagery.
