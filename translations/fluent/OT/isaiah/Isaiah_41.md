## Do Not Fear, for I Am with You

<p>
v01: “Islands, be silent before me!
Let peoples renew their strength.
Let them approach, then speak;
let us come together for judgment.
v02: Who stirred up one from the east,
whom righteousness summons to its feet?
He hands nations over to him and brings kings low;
he makes them dust before his sword,
driven stubble before his bow.
v03: He pursues them and passes safely on,
by a path his feet have never traveled.
v04: Who has worked and done this,
calling generations from the beginning?
I, the **LORD**, am first,
and with the last I am he.”
</p>

<p>
v05: The islands see and fear; earth’s ends tremble.
They draw near and come.
v06: Each helps his neighbor,
saying to his brother, “Be strong!”
v07: The craftsman encourages the goldsmith,
the one who smooths with a hammer the one who strikes the anvil.
He says of the soldering, “It is good,”
and secures the idol with nails so it will not wobble.
v08: “But you, Israel, my servant,
Jacob, whom I chose,
offspring of Abraham who loved me—
</p>

<p>
v09: I took hold of you from earth’s ends,
called you from its farthest corners.
I said, ‘You are my servant;
I have chosen you and have not rejected you.’
v10: Do not fear, for I am with you.
Do not look around in dismay, for I am your God.
I strengthen you; I help you;
I uphold you with my righteous right hand.
v11: Look, all who rage against you will be ashamed and disgraced.
Those who contend with you will become nothing and perish.
v12: You will look for your opponents and not find them.
Those who fight you will become nothing, nothing at all.
</p>

<p>
v13: For I, the **LORD** your God, hold your right hand
and tell you, ‘Do not fear; I help you.’
v14: Do not fear, worm Jacob, people of Israel.
I help you,” declares the **LORD**;
your Redeemer is Israel’s Holy One.
v15: “Look, I make you a new threshing sledge, sharp and double-edged.
You will thresh mountains and crush them,
make hills like chaff.
v16: You will winnow them; wind will carry them off,
and a tempest scatter them.
But you will rejoice in the **LORD**,
and glory in Israel’s Holy One.
</p>

<p>
v17: The poor and needy seek water, but there is none;
their tongues are parched with thirst.
I, the **LORD**, will answer them;
I, Israel’s God, will not abandon them.
v18: I will open rivers on bare heights, springs within valleys.
I will make the wilderness a pool of water,
dry land sources of water.
v19: I will put cedar, acacia, myrtle, and oil tree in the wilderness;
in the desert I will set cypress, plane, and pine together,
v20: so they may see and know, consider and understand together
that the **LORD**’s hand has done this,
that Israel’s Holy One has created it.
</p>

<p>
v21: Present your case,” says the **LORD**.
“Bring forward your strongest arguments,” says Jacob’s King.
v22: Let them come forward and tell us what will happen.
Tell us what the former things were,
so we may consider and know their outcome;
or announce to us what is coming.
v23: Tell us what will happen afterward,
so we may know you are gods.
Do good or do harm,
so we may be dismayed and see it together!
v24: Look, you are less than nothing,
and your work is worthless.
Whoever chooses you is an abomination.
</p>

<p>
v25: “I stirred up one from the north, and he came;
from the rising sun he calls on my name.
He comes upon rulers like clay,
like a potter trampling mud.
v26: Who announced it from the beginning so we could know,
in advance so we could say, ‘He is right’?
No one announced it, no one made it heard;
no one heard your words.
v27: I was first to tell Zion, ‘Look, here they are!’
I give Jerusalem a bearer of good news.
v28: I look, but there is no one;
among these there is no counselor
who can answer a word when I ask.
</p>

<p>
v29: Look, all of them are emptiness;
their deeds are nothing,
their cast images wind and waste.
</p>

## Notes

v02,25: The victorious figure is not named in these verses. The north and the east are both retained; Cyrus is named later in 44:28.

v19: The seven tree names are retained as a list. Several ancient plant identifications, especially the oil tree and the final three species, remain uncertain.

## Vocabulary

v14: **Redeemer**: One who acts to recover or release another; the title draws on the language of family obligation.
