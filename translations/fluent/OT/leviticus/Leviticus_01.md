## The Burnt Offering from the Herd

<p>
v01: The LORD called to Moses and spoke to him from the Tent of Meeting, saying,
v02: “Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When any one of you brings an offering to the LORD from the livestock, you may bring your offering from the herd or from the flock.
</p>

<p>
v03: If the offering is a burnt offering from the herd, the person will present a male without defect. It will be presented at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting so that it may be accepted before the LORD.
v04: The person will lay a hand on the head of the burnt offering, and it will be accepted on that person’s behalf to make atonement.
v05: The bull will be slaughtered before the LORD. Aaron’s sons, the priests, will bring forward the blood and dash the blood against all sides of the altar at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.
v06: The burnt offering will be skinned and cut into its pieces.
v07: The sons of Aaron the priest will put fire on the altar and arrange wood on the fire.
v08: Aaron’s sons, the priests, will arrange the pieces, the head, and the fat upon the wood that is on the fire on the altar.
v09: The entrails and legs will be washed with water. Then the priest will turn all of it into smoke on the altar as a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.
</p>

## The Burnt Offering from the Flock

<p>
v10: If the offering is from the flock—from the sheep or from the goats—as a burnt offering, the person will present a male without defect.
v11: It will be slaughtered on the north side of the altar before the LORD, and Aaron’s sons, the priests, will dash its blood against all sides of the altar.
v12: It will be cut into its pieces, with its head and its fat, and the priest will arrange them upon the wood that is on the fire on the altar.
v13: The entrails and the legs will be washed with water. The priest will present all of it and turn it into smoke on the altar. It is a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.
</p>

## The Burnt Offering of Birds

<p>
v14: If the offering to the LORD is a burnt offering of birds, the person will bring the offering from turtledoves or young pigeons.
v15: The priest will bring it to the altar, pinch off its head, and turn the head into smoke on the altar. Its blood will be drained against the side of the altar.
v16: The priest will remove its crop with its contents and throw it beside the altar on the east side, in the place for ashes.
v17: The priest will tear it open by its wings without dividing it completely. Then the priest will turn it into smoke on the altar, upon the wood that is on the fire. It is a burnt offering, an offering by fire, a pleasing aroma to the LORD.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Leviticus begins with the LORD speaking from the Tent of Meeting, continuing directly from the completion of the tabernacle at the end of Exodus.

v03: “burnt offering” — The Hebrew *olah* refers to an offering that “goes up,” both in its presentation and in the smoke rising from the altar.

v04: “lay a hand” — The gesture establishes identification between the offerer and the animal without the text fully explaining every dimension of that relationship.

v04: “make atonement” — The Hebrew *kipper* has a broad ritual range involving atonement, purgation, or restoration. TSW retains the traditional term “atonement.”

v09: “pleasing aroma” — The phrase describes the offering’s acceptance before the LORD and is repeated throughout the sacrificial instructions.

## Vocabulary

v02: קָרְבָּן (qorban)
“Offering.” Something brought near and presented to God.

v03: עֹלָה (olah)
“Burnt offering.” An offering wholly turned into smoke on the altar.

v04: כִּפֶּר (kipper)
“To make atonement.” To deal ritually with guilt, impurity, or disrupted relationship.

v05: זָרַק (zaraq)
“To dash” or “throw.” Used for applying blood against the altar.

v09: רֵיחַ נִיחֹחַ (reiach nichoach)
“Pleasing aroma.” A recurring expression for an offering received favorably before the LORD.
