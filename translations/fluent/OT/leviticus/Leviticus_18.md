## Living by the LORD's statutes

<p>
v01: The LORD said to Moses,
v02: “Speak to the Israelites. Tell them: ‘I am the LORD your God.
v03: Do not follow the practices of Egypt, where you lived, or the practices of Canaan, where I am bringing you. Do not live by their statutes.
v04: Carry out my ordinances and keep my statutes, living by them. I am the LORD your God.
</p>

<p>
v05: Keep my statutes and my ordinances. Whoever carries them out will live by them. I am the LORD.
</p>

## Sexual relations and kinship

<p>
v06: No man among you may approach a close relative for sexual relations. I am the LORD.
v07: Do not uncover your father's nakedness or your mother's nakedness. She is your mother; you must not have sexual relations with her.
v08: Do not have sexual relations with your father's wife. Her nakedness is your father's nakedness.
</p>

<p>
v09: Your sister may be your father's daughter or your mother's daughter, born within the household or outside it. You must not have sexual relations with either.
v10: Do not have sexual relations with your son's daughter or your daughter's daughter. Their nakedness is your own nakedness.
v11: The daughter of your father's wife, born to your father, is your sister. You must not have sexual relations with her.
</p>

<p>
v12: Do not have sexual relations with your father's sister. She is your father's close relative.
v13: Do not have sexual relations with your mother's sister, because she is your mother's close relative.
v14: Do not uncover your father's brother's nakedness. Do not approach his wife for sexual relations; she is your aunt.
</p>

<p>
v15: Do not have sexual relations with your daughter-in-law. She is your son's wife; you must not have sexual relations with her.
v16: Do not have sexual relations with your brother's wife. Her nakedness is your brother's nakedness.
</p>

<p>
v17: Do not have sexual relations with both a woman and her daughter. Do not marry her son's daughter or her daughter's daughter for sexual relations. They are the woman's close relatives. This is depravity.
v18: While your wife is alive, do not marry her sister as a rival wife and have sexual relations with her.
</p>

## Further prohibitions

<p>
v19: Do not approach a woman for sexual relations during her menstrual uncleanness.
v20: Do not have sexual intercourse with your neighbor's wife and become unclean through her.
</p>

<p>
v21: Do not give any of your offspring to be handed over to Molech. Do not profane the name of your God. I am the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v22: Do not lie with a male as with a woman. It is an abomination.
v23: Do not have intercourse with any animal and become unclean through it. A woman must not present herself to an animal for it to mate with her. This is a perversion.
</p>

## The land and its inhabitants

<p>
v24: Do not make yourselves unclean by any of these things. All these things have made the nations unclean, the nations I am driving out before you.
v25: The land became unclean, so I punished it for its wrongdoing, and the land vomited out its inhabitants.
</p>

<p>
v26: But you must keep my statutes and my ordinances. Neither the native-born nor the foreigner living among you may do any of these abominations.
v27: The people who lived in the land before you did all these abominations, and the land became unclean.
v28: Do not defile the land and have it vomit you out, as it vomited out the nation before you.
</p>

<p>
v29: Anyone who does any of these abominations will be cut off from among their people.
v30: Keep the charge I have given you: do not practice the abominable customs followed before you, and do not make yourselves unclean by them. I am the LORD your God.’”
</p>

## Notes

v5: “Will live by them” leaves the character and extent of that life unstated. The verse does not add “eternally.”

v6–17: “Uncover nakedness” is the repeated Hebrew expression for sexual relations. Some clauses identify a woman's nakedness with that of her husband, father, or grandfather. Those relational images remain where they carry the explanation.

v7: The opening can also be read as one connected prohibition: uncovering the father's nakedness through sexual relations with the mother.

v9: “Within the household or outside it” describes birth. The precise family circumstances are not explained.

v17–18: “Marry” renders “take,” used here with women as its objects. Verse 18 retains the sister relationship and the rivalry between wives, with the restriction “while your wife is alive.”

v21: The Hebrew says to give offspring “to cause to pass over to Molech.” Whether Molech names a divine recipient or a kind of offering is debated. Fire is not named in this verse; compare 2 Kings 23:10.

v22: The phrase is literally “the lyings of a woman.” The verse describes an act with a male; it does not use a term for sexual orientation.

v25–28: The land is pictured as becoming unclean and vomiting out its inhabitants. The warning applies this same image to Israel.

v29: “Cut off” names a severe penalty without specifying its precise form here.

## Vocabulary

v4–5: חֻקָּה / מִשְׁפָּט (huqqah / mishpat)
Statute / ordinance: recurring terms for the LORD's instructions and judgments.

v6–17: עֶרְוָה (ervah)
Nakedness. Its repeated use connects the sexual prohibitions with the family relationships named in them.

v17: זִמָּה (zimmah)
Depravity; also used in 19:29 and 20:14.

v22, 26–30: תּוֹעֵבָה (toevah)
Abomination: something treated as abhorrent. Here the word names forbidden acts.

v26: גֵּר (ger)
A foreigner residing among the people, distinguished here from the native-born.
