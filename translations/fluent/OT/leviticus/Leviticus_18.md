## Do Not Follow the Practices of Egypt and Canaan

<p>
v01: The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,
v02: “Speak to the Israelites and say to them: I am the LORD your God.
v03: You will not do as they do in the land of Egypt, where you lived, and you will not do as they do in the land of Canaan, where I am bringing you. You will not walk in their statutes.
v04: You will do my ordinances and keep my statutes, walking in them. I am the LORD your God.
v05: You will keep my statutes and my ordinances; the person who does them will live by them. I am the LORD.
</p>

## Forbidden Kinship Relations

<p>
v06: None of you will approach a close relative to uncover nakedness. I am the LORD.
v07: You will not uncover the nakedness of your father or the nakedness of your mother. She is your mother; you will not uncover her nakedness.
v08: You will not uncover the nakedness of your father’s wife; it is your father’s nakedness.
v09: You will not uncover the nakedness of your sister, your father’s daughter or your mother’s daughter, whether born in the household or born elsewhere.
v10: You will not uncover the nakedness of your son’s daughter or your daughter’s daughter, for their nakedness is your own nakedness.
v11: You will not uncover the nakedness of your father’s wife’s daughter, born to your father; she is your sister.
v12: You will not uncover the nakedness of your father’s sister; she is your father’s close relative.
v13: You will not uncover the nakedness of your mother’s sister, for she is your mother’s close relative.
v14: You will not uncover the nakedness of your father’s brother; you will not approach his wife. She is your aunt.
v15: You will not uncover the nakedness of your daughter-in-law. She is your son’s wife; you will not uncover her nakedness.
v16: You will not uncover the nakedness of your brother’s wife; it is your brother’s nakedness.
v17: You will not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter. You will not take her son’s daughter or her daughter’s daughter to uncover her nakedness; they are close relatives. It is depravity.
v18: You will not take a woman together with her sister as a rival, uncovering her nakedness while her sister is still alive.
</p>

## Other Forbidden Relations

<p>
v19: You will not approach a woman to uncover her nakedness while she is in the uncleanness of her menstrual period.
v20: You will not have sexual relations with your neighbor’s wife, becoming unclean with her.
v21: You will not give any of your offspring to be passed over to Molech, and you will not profane the name of your God. I am the LORD.
v22: You will not lie with a male as one lies with a woman; it is a detestable thing.
v23: You will not have sexual relations with any animal, becoming unclean with it. A woman will not stand before an animal to mate with it; it is a perversion.
</p>

## Do Not Defile the Land

<p>
v24: Do not make yourselves unclean by any of these things, for by all these things the nations I am driving out before you became unclean.
v25: The land became unclean, and I visited its guilt upon it, and the land vomited out its inhabitants.
v26: But you will keep my statutes and my ordinances and will not do any of these detestable things, neither the native-born nor the resident alien who lives among you,
v27: for the people of the land who were before you did all these detestable things, and the land became unclean,
v28: so that the land does not vomit you out when you make it unclean, as it vomited out the nation that was before you.
v29: For anyone who does any of these detestable things—the persons who do them will be cut off from among their people.
v30: You will keep my charge, so that you do not practice any of the detestable customs practiced before you and do not make yourselves unclean by them. I am the LORD your God.”
</p>

## Notes

v05: “shall live by them” — The statement joins obedience and life without explaining whether the language is primarily communal, temporal, covenantal, or more expansive.

v06: “uncover nakedness” is a Hebrew idiom for sexual relations and is retained to preserve the repeated legal language of the chapter.

v18: The Hebrew can be read as forbidding marriage to two sisters while both are alive, emphasizing rivalry; TSW retains that relational wording rather than generalizing the prohibition.

v18: The Hebrew can be read as forbidding marriage to two sisters while both are alive; the syntax centers on taking one woman “as a rival” to her sister.

v21: “Molech” — The precise nature of the practice implied by “pass over” and the identity of Molech remain debated. The text clearly prohibits giving offspring within this cultic practice.

v25: The land is personified as vomiting out its inhabitants, portraying moral and ritual defilement as affecting the land itself.

## Vocabulary

v03: חֻקָּה (chuqqah)
“Statute.” An established prescription or practice.

v06: גִּלָּה עֶרְוָה (gillah ervah)
“To uncover nakedness.” A repeated legal idiom for prohibited sexual relations.

v17: זִמָּה (zimmah)
“Depravity” or “wicked design.” A strong term for prohibited sexual wrongdoing.

v21: מֹלֶךְ (Molekh)
“Molech.” A name or cultic designation associated with prohibited child offering.

v23: תֶּבֶל (tevel)
“Perversion” or “confusion.” A term for a prohibited mixing of categories.
