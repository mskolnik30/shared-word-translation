## A man with a discharge

<p>
v01: The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron:
v02: “Speak to the Israelites and tell them: ‘When any man has a discharge from his genitals, his discharge makes him unclean.
v03: This is his uncleanness because of the discharge: whether his body lets the discharge flow or is blocked by it, he is unclean.
v04: Every bed the man with the discharge lies on becomes unclean, and anything he sits on becomes unclean.
v05: Anyone who touches his bed must wash their clothes and bathe in water, and they remain unclean until evening.
v06: Anyone who sits on something the man with the discharge has sat on must wash their clothes and bathe in water, and they remain unclean until evening.
v07: Anyone who touches the body of the man with the discharge must wash their clothes and bathe in water, and they remain unclean until evening.
v08: If the man with the discharge spits on someone who is clean, that person must wash their clothes and bathe in water, and they remain unclean until evening.
</p>

<p>
v09: Anything the man with the discharge rides on becomes unclean.
v10: Anyone who touches anything that was under him becomes unclean until evening. Anyone who carries those things must wash their clothes and bathe in water, and they remain unclean until evening.
</p>

<p>
v11: If the man with the discharge touches anyone without first rinsing his hands in water, the person he touches must wash their clothes and bathe in water, and they remain unclean until evening.
v12: An earthenware vessel touched by the man with the discharge must be broken. Any wooden utensil he touches must be rinsed in water.
</p>

## Cleansing after a discharge

<p>
v13: When the man is free of his discharge, he must count seven days for his cleansing. He must wash his clothes and bathe his body in fresh water. Then he will be clean.
v14: On the eighth day he must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons, come before the LORD at the entrance to the tent of meeting, and give the birds to the priest.
v15: The priest must offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. He must make atonement for the man before the LORD because of his discharge.
</p>

## Semen

<p>
v16: When a man has an emission of semen, he must bathe his whole body in water, and he remains unclean until evening.
v17: Any clothing or leather with semen on it must be washed in water, and it remains unclean until evening.
v18: When a man has intercourse with a woman and emits semen, both must bathe in water, and both remain unclean until evening.
</p>

## Menstrual impurity

<p>
v19: When a woman has a flow of blood from her body, she remains in her menstrual impurity for seven days. Anyone who touches her becomes unclean until evening.
v20: Anything she lies on during her menstrual impurity becomes unclean, and anything she sits on becomes unclean.
v21: Anyone who touches her bed must wash their clothes and bathe in water, and they remain unclean until evening.
v22: Anyone who touches something she sits on must wash their clothes and bathe in water, and they remain unclean until evening.
</p>

<p>
v23: If something is on her bed or on what she sits on, anyone who touches it becomes unclean until evening.
</p>

<p>
v24: If a man has intercourse with her and her menstrual impurity comes upon him, he becomes unclean for seven days. Every bed he lies on becomes unclean.
</p>

## Prolonged bleeding

<p>
v25: If a woman bleeds for many days outside the time of her menstrual period, or her bleeding continues beyond her period, she remains unclean throughout the discharge, just as during her menstrual impurity.
v26: Every bed she lies on while the discharge lasts is unclean for her, just as her bed is during her menstrual impurity. Anything she sits on is also unclean, just as during her menstrual impurity.
v27: Anyone who touches these things becomes unclean. They must wash their clothes and bathe in water, and they remain unclean until evening.
</p>

<p>
v28: When she is free of her discharge, she must count seven days. After that she will be clean.
v29: On the eighth day she must take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest at the entrance to the tent of meeting.
v30: The priest must offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. He must make atonement for her before the LORD because of her unclean discharge.’
</p>

## Protecting the tabernacle

<p>
v31: You must keep the Israelites separate from their impurity, so they do not die in their impurity by defiling my tabernacle, which is among them.
</p>

<p>
v32: These are the instructions for a man with a discharge, for a man who emits semen and becomes unclean by it,
v33: for a woman during her menstrual impurity, for anyone with a discharge, male or female, and for a man who has intercourse with an unclean woman.”
</p>

## Notes

v2–3: “Genitals” makes explicit the contextual use of Hebrew “flesh.” Verse 3 includes both flowing and blockage. Some ancient witnesses have a longer text repeating the duration of these conditions; this translation follows the Masoretic text.

v10–11: The rules distinguish touching from carrying and identify the unwashed hands as those of the man with the discharge. Rinsing his hands is not the full cleansing process, which follows in verses 13–15.

v13: “Fresh water” is literally “living water,” from a flowing source, as in 14:5. Being free of the discharge begins the seven-day count.

v23: The Hebrew pronoun is unclear. This translation reads it as something lying on the bed or seat; another reading takes it to refer to the bed or seat itself.

v24: The text gives the impurity consequences of intercourse during menstruation. It does not say here that the menstrual flow began unexpectedly. See also the differently framed instructions in 18:19 and 20:18.

v28–30: These offerings follow the prolonged or out-of-time bleeding of verse 25. They are not prescribed here for every menstrual period.

v31: “You” is plural, continuing the address to Moses and Aaron. The warning concerns defiling the LORD’s dwelling among the Israelites.

## Vocabulary

v2: זוֹב (zov)
Flow or discharge. The chapter distinguishes the male discharge, semen, menstrual blood, and prolonged bleeding.

v16: שִׁכְבַת זֶרַע (shikhvat zera)
An emission of semen, literally an emission or lying of “seed.”

v19: נִדָּה (niddah)
Menstrual impurity here; the word also has a wider use for impurity or separation.

v31: מִשְׁכָּן (mishkan)
Dwelling or tabernacle: the LORD’s dwelling among the people.

v32–33: תּוֹרָה (torah)
Instructions. The final list keeps the chapter’s several bodily conditions distinct.
