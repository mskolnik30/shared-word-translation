## Gathering for the Priests’ Ordination

<p>
v01: The LORD spoke to Moses:
v02: “Bring Aaron and his sons with him, the garments and anointing oil, the bull for the sin offering, the two rams, and the basket of unleavened bread.
v03: Gather the whole community at the entrance of the tent of meeting.”
</p>

<p>
v04: Moses did as the LORD commanded him, and the community gathered at the entrance of the tent of meeting.
v05: Moses told the community, “This is what the LORD has commanded us to do.”
</p>

## Washing, Clothing, and Anointing

<p>
v06: Then Moses brought Aaron and his sons forward and washed them with water.
v07: He put the tunic on Aaron, tied the sash around him, and dressed him in the robe. Then he put the ephod on him and secured it with its woven waistband.
v08: He placed the breastpiece on him and put the Urim and Thummim inside it.
v09: He set the turban on Aaron’s head and attached the gold plate, the holy crown, to its front, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

<p>
v10: Moses took the anointing oil and anointed the tabernacle and everything in it, consecrating them.
v11: He sprinkled some oil on the altar seven times, then anointed the altar and all its utensils, the basin and its stand, to consecrate them.
v12: He poured some of the anointing oil on Aaron’s head and anointed him to consecrate him.
</p>

<p>
v13: Moses brought Aaron’s sons forward, dressed them in tunics, tied sashes around them, and put head coverings on them, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## The Sin Offering

<p>
v14: He brought forward the bull for the sin offering. Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head.
v15: The bull was slaughtered, and Moses took the blood. With his finger he put some on the altar’s horns all around, purifying the altar. He poured the remaining blood at its base and consecrated it so that atonement could be made on it.
v16: All the fat on the entrails, the lobe of the liver, and both kidneys with their fat were taken, and Moses burned them on the altar as smoke.
v17: But the bull—its skin, flesh, and dung—was burned with fire outside the camp, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## The Burnt Offering

<p>
v18: He brought forward the ram for the burnt offering. Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head.
v19: The ram was slaughtered, and Moses splashed its blood against all sides of the altar.
v20: The ram was cut into pieces, and Moses burned the head, the pieces, and the fat on the altar as smoke.
v21: The entrails and legs were washed with water, and Moses burned the whole ram on the altar as smoke. It was a burnt offering, a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## The Ordination Ram

<p>
v22: Then he brought forward the second ram, the ram for ordination. Aaron and his sons laid their hands on its head.
v23: The ram was slaughtered, and Moses took some of its blood and put it on Aaron’s right earlobe, his right thumb, and the big toe of his right foot.
v24: He brought Aaron’s sons forward, and Moses put some blood on their right earlobes, their right thumbs, and the big toes of their right feet. Then Moses splashed the blood against all sides of the altar.
</p>

<p>
v25: He took the fat—the fat tail, all the fat on the entrails, the lobe of the liver, both kidneys with their fat—and the right thigh.
v26: From the basket of unleavened bread before the LORD, he took one unleavened cake, one cake made with oil, and one wafer, and placed them on the fat and right thigh.
v27: He placed all of these in Aaron’s hands and his sons’ hands. Then they were waved as a wave offering before the LORD.
v28: Moses took them from their hands and burned them on the altar as smoke, on top of the burnt offering. They were ordination offerings, a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v29: Moses took the breast and waved it as a wave offering before the LORD. It was Moses’ share of the ordination ram, just as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## Consecration and Seven Days of Duty

<p>
v30: Moses took some anointing oil and some blood from the altar and sprinkled Aaron and his garments, his sons and their garments. In this way he consecrated Aaron and his garments, his sons and their garments.
</p>

<p>
v31: Moses told Aaron and his sons, “Boil the meat at the entrance of the tent of meeting. Eat it there with the bread in the ordination basket, as I commanded when I said, ‘Aaron and his sons are to eat it.’
v32: Burn whatever meat and bread remain.
</p>

<p>
v33: Do not leave the entrance of the tent of meeting for seven days, until your days of ordination are complete, for your ordination takes seven days.
v34: What has been done today is what the LORD commanded to be done to make atonement for you.
v35: Stay at the entrance of the tent of meeting day and night for seven days. Keep the LORD’s charge so that you do not die, for this is what I was commanded.”
v36: Aaron and his sons did everything the LORD had commanded through Moses.
</p>

## Notes

v06–09: The garments and installation actions follow the instructions in Exodus 28–29. The repeated command formulas connect what Moses does here with those earlier instructions.

v08: The Urim and Thummim are placed in the breastpiece. Their physical form and method of use are not explained here.

v15: The Hebrew does not name the slaughterer; interpreters identify him as Moses or Aaron. The same uncertainty recurs in verses 19 and 23. The altar is purified and consecrated; the final phrase may mean atonement “on it” or “for it.”

v23–24: Each of the three blood applications is on the right side, first for Aaron and then for his sons. The narrative does not explain the symbolism.

v27: The Hebrew says “he waved them.” This may refer to Moses or to Aaron acting with his sons. The draft leaves the person performing the movement unspecified.

v31: The pinned Hebrew reads “I commanded.” Some ancient versions read “I was commanded,” as in verse 35.

v33: The Hebrew idiom for ordaining the priests is “filling your hands.” The seven-day requirement is repeated in verses 33 and 35.

## Vocabulary

v07: אֵפוֹד (ʾephod)
“Ephod.” A priestly garment worn over the robe, secured with its own waistband.

v10: קִדֵּשׁ (qiddesh)
“Consecrate.” Set a person or object apart as holy.

v22: מִלֻּאִים (milluʾim)
“Ordination.” Installation in priestly service, related to the idiom of filling the hands.

v35: מִשְׁמֶרֶת (mishmeret)
“Charge.” A duty entrusted to someone to keep.
