## David’s Song of Deliverance

<p>
v01: David spoke the words of this song to the LORD when the LORD delivered him from the hand of all his enemies and from Saul’s hand.
</p>

<p>
v02: He said:
“The LORD is my cliff, my fortress,
and the one who delivers me.
v03: My God is my rock; in him I take refuge,
my shield and the horn of my salvation,
my stronghold and my place of escape.
My savior, you save me from violence.
v04: I call to the LORD, who is worthy of praise,
and I am saved from my enemies.
</p>

<p>
v05: The breakers of death surrounded me;
torrents of destruction terrified me.
v06: The cords of Sheol coiled around me;
the snares of death confronted me.
v07: In my distress I called to the LORD;
to my God I called.
From his temple he heard my voice;
my cry for help reached his ears.
</p>

<p>
v08: Then the earth shook and quaked;
the foundations of heaven trembled.
They shook because he was angry.
v09: Smoke rose from his nostrils;
devouring fire came from his mouth.
Burning coals blazed from him.
v10: He bent the heavens and came down,
thick darkness beneath his feet.
v11: He rode a cherub and flew;
he appeared on the wings of the wind.
v12: He made darkness shelters around him,
piled-up waters, thick clouds of the skies.
v13: From the brightness before him
burning coals blazed.
v14: The LORD thundered from heaven;
the Most High gave his voice.
v15: He sent arrows and scattered them,
lightning, and threw them into confusion.
v16: The channels of the sea appeared;
the world’s foundations lay bare
at the LORD’s rebuke,
at the blast of breath from his nostrils.
</p>

<p>
v17: He reached down from on high and took me;
he drew me out of many waters.
v18: He rescued me from my strong enemy,
from those who hated me, for they were stronger than I.
v19: They confronted me on my day of disaster,
but the LORD became my support.
v20: He brought me out into a spacious place;
he rescued me because he delighted in me.
</p>

<p>
v21: The LORD rewarded me according to my righteousness;
he repaid me according to the cleanness of my hands.
v22: For I have kept the LORD’s ways
and have not wickedly turned from my God.
v23: All his judgments are before me;
I do not turn aside from his statutes.
v24: I have been blameless before him
and have guarded myself from my own guilt.
v25: The LORD repaid me according to my righteousness,
according to my cleanness before his eyes.
</p>

<p>
v26: With the faithful you show yourself faithful;
with the blameless warrior you show yourself blameless.
v27: With the pure you show yourself pure;
with the crooked you show yourself shrewd.
v28: You save an afflicted people,
but your eyes are on the proud to bring them low.
</p>

<p>
v29: For you are my lamp, LORD;
the LORD lights up my darkness.
v30: With you I can charge a raiding band;
with my God I can leap a wall.
</p>

<p>
v31: God’s way is complete;
the LORD’s word is refined.
He is a shield to all who take refuge in him.
v32: Who is God apart from the LORD?
Who is a rock apart from our God?
v33: God is my strong refuge;
he clears my way completely.
v34: He makes my feet like a doe’s
and sets me on my heights.
v35: He trains my hands for war,
so my arms can bend a bronze bow.
v36: You give me the shield of your salvation;
your answering me makes me great.
v37: You give my steps room beneath me,
and my ankles do not give way.
</p>

<p>
v38: I pursue my enemies and destroy them;
I do not turn back until I finish them.
v39: I finish them and crush them;
they cannot rise.
They fall beneath my feet.
v40: You gird me with strength for battle;
you make those who rise against me bow beneath me.
v41: You give me my enemies’ backs;
I wipe out those who hate me.
v42: They look for help, but no one saves;
they look to the LORD, but he does not answer.
v43: I grind them like the dust of the earth;
like mud in the streets, I crush and trample them.
</p>

<p>
v44: You deliver me from my people’s disputes;
you preserve me as head of nations.
A people I did not know serves me.
v45: Foreigners cringe before me;
at the mere hearing of me they obey.
v46: Foreigners lose heart
and come limping from their strongholds.
</p>

<p>
v47: The LORD lives! Blessed be my rock!
Exalted be God, the rock of my salvation!
v48: He is the God who gives me vengeance,
who brings peoples down beneath me,
v49: who brings me out from my enemies.
You lift me above those who rise against me;
you rescue me from violent men.
v50: So I will thank you among the nations, LORD,
and sing praise to your name.
v51: He is a tower of salvation for his king,
showing steadfast love to his anointed,
to David and his offspring forever.”
</p>

## Notes

v01–51: The poem retains its own wording and images. Similarity to another biblical song does not authorize replacing this source’s lines. Its violent victories and claims of righteousness remain David’s sung words.

v11: The Hebrew says “he appeared” or “was seen,” not “he soared.”

v21–25: The speaker claims righteousness, clean hands, and blamelessness. These statements are retained alongside the narrative’s accounts of his wrongdoing.

v33–36: Verse 33’s verb is difficult; “clears my way completely” retains a sense of release and an unobstructed way. Verses 33–34 follow the read “my way” and “my feet.” In verse 36, “your answering me” is one understanding of a difficult expression also associated with gentleness or condescension.

v46: The verb is unusual. “Come limping” expresses one possible sense; the form is also associated with girding oneself.

v51: The read tradition has “tower of salvation.” The written form can be read as magnifying or giving great victories.

## Vocabulary

v03: Horn of salvation
A horn is an image of strength; the phrase pictures effective saving power.

v06: Sheol
The realm of the dead, pictured here with cords that entangle.

v31: Refined
An image of metal tested and purified by fire, applied to the LORD’s word.

v51: Anointed
The king designated by anointing, here identified as David with his continuing offspring.
