## Samaria falls

<p>
v01: In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah, Hoshea son of Elah became king over Israel in Samaria. He reigned for nine years.
v02: He did what was evil in the LORD’s eyes, though not like the kings of Israel before him.
</p>

<p>
v03: Shalmaneser king of Assyria marched against him. Hoshea became his servant and paid him tribute.
v04: But Assyria’s king discovered a conspiracy in Hoshea: he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt and had stopped paying his yearly tribute to Assyria. So Assyria’s king arrested him and confined him in prison.
</p>

<p>
v05: The king of Assyria invaded the whole land, advanced on Samaria, and besieged it for three years.
v06: In Hoshea’s ninth year, the king of Assyria captured Samaria and deported Israel to Assyria. He settled them in Halah, along the Habor, the river of Gozan, and in the cities of Media.
</p>

## Why Israel went into exile

<p>
v07: This happened because the Israelites had sinned against the LORD their God, who had brought them up from Egypt, out from under the power of Pharaoh king of Egypt. They had feared other gods
v08: and followed the practices of the nations the LORD had driven out before them, as well as those introduced by Israel’s kings.
</p>

<p>
v09: The Israelites secretly did things against the LORD their God that were not right. They built high places in all their settlements, from watchtower to fortified city.
v10: They set up sacred pillars and Asherah poles on every high hill and under every leafy tree.
v11: At every high place they burned offerings, like the nations the LORD had driven away before them. They did evil things that provoked the LORD to anger.
v12: They served idols, though the LORD had told them, “You must not do this.”
</p>

<p>
v13: The LORD warned Israel and Judah through every prophet and seer: “Turn from your evil ways. Keep my commandments and decrees, following all the instruction I commanded your ancestors and sent to you through my servants the prophets.”
v14: But they would not listen. They stiffened their necks like their ancestors, who had not trusted the LORD their God.
</p>

<p>
v15: They rejected his decrees, the covenant he had made with their ancestors, and the warnings he had given them. They followed what was empty and became empty themselves. They followed the surrounding nations, though the LORD had commanded them not to act as those nations did.
v16: They abandoned all the commandments of the LORD their God. They made two cast-metal calves and an Asherah, bowed to all the army of heaven, and served Baal.
v17: They made their sons and daughters pass through the fire, practiced divination and read omens, and sold themselves to do what was evil in the LORD’s eyes, provoking him to anger.
</p>

<p>
v18: The LORD was very angry with Israel and removed them from his presence. Only the tribe of Judah remained.
v19: But Judah too failed to keep the commandments of the LORD their God. They followed the practices Israel had introduced.
v20: The LORD rejected all Israel’s descendants, afflicted them, and handed them over to plunderers until he cast them out of his presence.
</p>

<p>
v21: When he tore Israel away from David’s house, they made Jeroboam son of Nebat king. Jeroboam drove Israel away from following the LORD and led them into great sin.
v22: The Israelites followed all the sins Jeroboam committed and did not turn away from them,
v23: until the LORD removed Israel from his presence, as he had said through all his servants the prophets. Israel was deported from its own land to Assyria, where they remain to this day.
</p>

## New settlers in Samaria

<p>
v24: The king of Assyria brought people from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim and settled them in Samaria’s towns in place of the Israelites. They took possession of Samaria and lived in its towns.
v25: When they first settled there, they did not fear the LORD. So the LORD sent lions among them, and the lions killed some of them.
</p>

<p>
v26: The king of Assyria was told, “The nations you deported and settled in Samaria’s towns do not know the requirements of the god of the land. He has sent lions among them, and the lions are killing them because they do not know the requirements of the god of the land.”
</p>

<p>
v27: The king of Assyria commanded, “Send back one of the priests you deported from there. Let them go and settle there, and let him teach them the requirements of the god of the land.”
v28: So one of the priests deported from Samaria came and settled in Bethel. He taught them how to fear the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v29: Yet each nation made its own gods and put them in the shrines at the high places the people of Samaria had built, each nation in the towns where it lived.
v30: The people of Babylon made Succoth-benoth; the people of Cuth made Nergal; the people of Hamath made Ashima.
v31: The Avvites made Nibhaz and Tartak. The Sepharvites burned their children in the fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.
</p>

<p>
v32: They also feared the LORD and appointed people from among themselves as priests of the high places, who served for them in those shrines.
v33: They feared the LORD, yet served their own gods according to the customs of the nations from which they had been deported.
</p>

<p>
v34: To this day they continue their former practices. They do not fear the LORD or follow the decrees, judgments, instruction, and commandment the LORD gave the descendants of Jacob, whom he named Israel.
</p>

<p>
v35: The LORD made a covenant with them and commanded them, “You must not fear other gods, bow to them, serve them, or sacrifice to them.
v36: You must fear the LORD, who brought you up from Egypt with great power and an outstretched arm. Bow to him and sacrifice to him.
v37: Always be careful to carry out the decrees, judgments, instruction, and commandment he wrote for you. Do not fear other gods.
v38: Do not forget the covenant I made with you. Do not fear other gods.
v39: Fear the LORD your God, and he will rescue you from the hands of all your enemies.”
</p>

<p>
v40: But they would not listen. They continued their former practices.
v41: So these nations feared the LORD while also serving their carved images. Their children and grandchildren still do as their ancestors did, to this day.
</p>

## Notes

v15: “They followed what was empty and became empty” preserves a repeated Hebrew root. The object of misplaced worship shapes the description of the worshipers.

v17: Both sons and daughters are named in the fire rites.

v24: The account concerns peoples forcibly relocated into Samaria. It should not be turned into a claim about the character of all later Samaritans.

v33: Verses 33 and 41 say the settlers feared the LORD while serving other gods; verse 34 says they did not fear the LORD. The shifts in description and evaluation are left visible.

## Vocabulary

v06: Exile
Forced displacement from the homeland.

v10: Asherah poles
Cult objects associated with Asherah worship.

v13: Seer
A designation for a person who receives prophetic insight.

v35: Fear
In these worship statements, reverence and religious allegiance, not only an emotion.
