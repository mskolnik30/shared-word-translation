## Jehu is anointed

<p>
v01: Elisha the prophet called one of the company of prophets and said, “Tuck your robe into your belt, take this flask of oil with you, and go to Ramoth-gilead.
v02: When you arrive, find Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi. Go in, have him rise from among his fellow officers, and take him into an inner room.
v03: Take the flask of oil, pour it on his head, and say, ‘This is what the LORD says: I anoint you king over Israel.’ Then open the door and flee. Don’t wait.”
</p>

<p>
v04: So the young prophet went to Ramoth-gilead.
v05: When he arrived, the army commanders were sitting together. He said, “Commander, I have a message for you.” Jehu asked, “For which of us?” He answered, “For you, commander.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Jehu got up and went into the house. The prophet poured the oil on his head and said, “This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, says: I anoint you king over the LORD’s people, over Israel.
v07: You are to strike down the house of Ahab your master, so that I may avenge the blood of my servants the prophets, and the blood of all the LORD’s servants, at Jezebel’s hand.
v08: Ahab’s whole house will perish. I will cut off from Ahab every male who urinates against a wall, bound or free in Israel.
v09: I will make Ahab’s house like the house of Jeroboam son of Nebat and the house of Baasha son of Ahijah.
v10: Dogs will eat Jezebel on the plot of land at Jezreel, and no one will bury her.” Then he opened the door and fled.
</p>

## The commanders proclaim him king

<p>
v11: Jehu came out to his master’s officers. One asked him, “Is everything all right? Why did that madman come to you?” He answered, “You know the man and his chatter.”
v12: They said, “That’s a lie! Tell us.” So he said, “He told me this and that, including, ‘This is what the LORD says: I anoint you king over Israel.’”
</p>

<p>
v13: At once each of them took his garment and spread it beneath Jehu on the bare steps. They blew the ram’s horn and proclaimed, “Jehu is king!”
</p>

<p>
v14: So Jehu son of Jehoshaphat, son of Nimshi, conspired against Joram. Joram and all Israel had been defending Ramoth-gilead against Hazael king of Aram.
v15: But King Joram had returned to Jezreel to recover from the wounds the Arameans had inflicted when he fought Hazael king of Aram. Jehu said, “If this is what you want, let no one escape from the city to take word to Jezreel.”
</p>

## The kings meet Jehu

<p>
v16: Jehu mounted his chariot and went to Jezreel, where Joram lay recovering. Ahaziah king of Judah had come down to visit Joram.
v17: The lookout standing on Jezreel’s tower saw Jehu’s company approaching and said, “I see a company of men.” Joram said, “Send a horseman to meet them and ask, ‘Is it peace?’”
</p>

<p>
v18: The horseman rode out to meet him and said, “The king asks, ‘Is it peace?’” Jehu answered, “What have you to do with peace? Fall in behind me.” The lookout reported, “The messenger reached them, but he isn’t coming back.”
</p>

<p>
v19: The king sent a second horseman. He reached them and said, “The king asks, ‘Is it peace?’” Jehu answered, “What have you to do with peace? Fall in behind me.”
v20: The lookout reported, “He reached them, but he isn’t coming back. The driving looks like Jehu son of Nimshi’s driving—he drives like a madman.”
</p>

<p>
v21: Joram said, “Hitch up!” They hitched his chariot, and Joram king of Israel and Ahaziah king of Judah went out, each in his own chariot. They met Jehu on Naboth the Jezreelite’s plot of land.
v22: When Joram saw Jehu, he asked, “Is it peace, Jehu?” Jehu answered, “What peace can there be while your mother Jezebel’s whoring and her many sorceries go on?”
</p>

<p>
v23: Joram wheeled around and fled, calling to Ahaziah, “Treachery, Ahaziah!”
v24: Jehu drew his bow with all his strength and struck Joram between the shoulders. The arrow came out through his heart, and he slumped in his chariot.
</p>

<p>
v25: Jehu said to Bidkar his officer, “Pick him up and throw him onto Naboth the Jezreelite’s plot. Remember how you and I rode together behind his father Ahab when the LORD pronounced this burden against him:
v26: ‘Yesterday I saw the blood of Naboth and the blood of his sons, declares the LORD. I will repay you on this plot, declares the LORD.’ So pick him up and throw him onto the plot, according to the LORD’s word.”
</p>

<p>
v27: When Ahaziah king of Judah saw this, he fled toward Beth-haggan. Jehu pursued him, saying, “Strike him too!” They struck him in his chariot at the ascent of Gur near Ibleam. He fled to Megiddo and died there.
v28: His servants carried him in a chariot to Jerusalem and buried him in his tomb with his ancestors in the City of David.
v29: Ahaziah had become king over Judah in the eleventh year of Joram son of Ahab.
</p>

## Jezebel’s death

<p>
v30: Jehu came to Jezreel. When Jezebel heard, she painted her eyes, arranged her hair, and looked out of the window.
v31: As Jehu entered the gate, she said, “Is it peace, Zimri, murderer of your master?”
</p>

<p>
v32: He looked up at the window and said, “Who is on my side? Who?” Two or three eunuchs looked down at him.
v33: He said, “Throw her down!” They threw her down, and some of her blood splashed onto the wall and the horses, and Jehu trampled her.
</p>

<p>
v34: Then he went in and ate and drank. “See to that cursed woman,” he said. “Bury her, for she was a king’s daughter.”
v35: But when they went to bury her, they found only her skull, her feet, and the palms of her hands.
</p>

<p>
v36: They returned and told him. He said, “This is the LORD’s word, which he spoke through his servant Elijah the Tishbite: ‘On the plot at Jezreel, dogs will eat Jezebel’s flesh.
v37: Jezebel’s body will be like dung on the face of the field, on the plot at Jezreel, so that no one will be able to say, “This is Jezebel.”’”
</p>

## Notes

v08: The Hebrew uses a coarse expression for males, literally those who urinate against a wall. The paired terms rendered “bound or free” are uncertain social categories.

v13: The precise meaning of the expression for the bare or upper steps is uncertain.

v22: “Whoring” is an accusation associated with religious infidelity. This speech does not establish a separate sexual biography of Jezebel.

v26: This recalled oracle mentions Naboth’s sons as well as Naboth. The earlier account in 1 Kings 21 does not narrate their deaths.

v29: This verse gives the eleventh year of Joram; 8:25 gives the twelfth. Both source notices are preserved.

v31: Zimri also killed his royal master and briefly took the throne; Jezebel’s address recalls that earlier coup.

## Vocabulary

v01: Anoint
Pour oil on someone as a sign of appointment.

v25: Burden
A weighty prophetic pronouncement.

v32: Eunuchs
Male court attendants; the Hebrew title can also be used more broadly for officials.
