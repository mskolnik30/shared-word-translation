## Elijah is taken away

<p>
v01: When the LORD was about to take Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind, Elijah and Elisha set out from Gilgal.
v02: Elijah said to Elisha, “Stay here, please. The LORD has sent me to Bethel.” Elisha answered, “As the LORD lives, and as you live, I will not leave you.” So they went down to Bethel.
</p>

<p>
v03: The company of prophets at Bethel came out to Elisha. “Do you know the LORD is taking your master away from over you today?” they asked. “Yes, I know,” he said. “Be quiet.”
</p>

<p>
v04: Elijah said, “Elisha, stay here, please. The LORD has sent me to Jericho.” He answered, “As the LORD lives, and as you live, I will not leave you.” So they went to Jericho.
v05: The company of prophets at Jericho approached Elisha. “Do you know the LORD is taking your master away from over you today?” they asked. “Yes, I know,” he said. “Be quiet.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Elijah said, “Stay here, please. The LORD has sent me to the Jordan.” He answered, “As the LORD lives, and as you live, I will not leave you.” So the two went on.
v07: Fifty men from the company of prophets followed and stood some distance away, while the two stood beside the Jordan.
</p>

<p>
v08: Elijah took his cloak, rolled it up, and struck the water. It divided to either side, and the two crossed on dry ground.
</p>

<p>
v09: After they crossed, Elijah said, “Ask what I can do for you before I am taken from you.” Elisha answered, “Please let me receive a double share of your spirit.”
v10: “You have asked a difficult thing,” Elijah said. “If you see me being taken from you, it will be yours. If not, it will not.”
</p>

<p>
v11: As they walked along talking, a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated them. Elijah went up to heaven in the whirlwind.
v12: Elisha watched and cried, “My father, my father! Israel’s chariot and its horsemen!” When he could no longer see him, he grasped his clothes and tore them in two.
</p>

<p>
v13: He picked up Elijah’s cloak, which had fallen from him, and returned to stand on the Jordan’s bank.
v14: He took the fallen cloak and struck the water. “Where is the LORD, Elijah’s God?” he asked. He too struck the water, and it divided to either side. Elisha crossed.
</p>

## Elisha returns

<p>
v15: The company of prophets from Jericho saw him from a distance. “Elijah’s spirit rests on Elisha,” they said. They came to meet him and bowed to the ground before him.
v16: They said, “Your servants have fifty strong men. Please let them search for your master. Perhaps the LORD’s spirit lifted him up and dropped him on a mountain or in a valley.” “Do not send them,” he said.
</p>

<p>
v17: But they pressed him until he was embarrassed, and he said, “Send them.” They sent fifty men, who searched for three days but did not find Elijah.
v18: They returned to Elisha, who was staying in Jericho. “Didn’t I tell you not to go?” he said.
</p>

## Water and a curse

<p>
v19: The town’s men told Elisha, “As my lord can see, the town is well situated. But the water is bad, and the land causes loss of life.”
v20: “Bring me a new bowl with salt in it,” he said. They brought it.
v21: He went to the spring, threw the salt into it, and said, “The LORD says: I have healed this water. No more death or loss of life will come from it.”
v22: The water has remained healed to this day, just as Elisha said.
</p>

<p>
v23: From there he went up toward Bethel. As he walked up the road, young boys came out of the town and mocked him: “Go up, baldhead! Go up, baldhead!”
v24: He turned, looked at them, and cursed them in the LORD’s name. Two female bears came out of the woods and tore forty-two of the children.
v25: From there he went to Mount Carmel and then returned to Samaria.
</p>

## Notes

v09: A “double share” evokes the firstborn’s inheritance portion. It need not mean twice Elijah’s miraculous power.

v11: The fiery chariot separates the men; the text says Elijah ascends in the whirlwind.

v14: The Hebrew words rendered “he too” are difficult to place. They can be joined to Elisha’s action or to the preceding question.

v19: The word describing the land can mean causing miscarriage, bereavement, or barrenness. The related wording in verse 21 is kept broad as loss of life.

v23: The Hebrew calls the mockers young boys and then children. It gives no basis for turning them into an armed adult gang. The bears tear forty-two of them; the text does not explicitly say all forty-two die.

## Vocabulary

v03: Company of prophets
Literally “sons of the prophets”: a prophetic group, not necessarily biological sons.

v08: Cloak
Elijah’s outer garment, carried into Elisha’s ministry.

v23: Go up
The taunt uses an ascent verb also prominent in Elijah’s departure.
