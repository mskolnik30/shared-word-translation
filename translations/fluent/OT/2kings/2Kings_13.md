## Jehoahaz of Israel

<p>
v01: In the twenty-third year of Joash son of Ahaziah, king of Judah, Jehoahaz son of Jehu became king over Israel in Samaria. He reigned for seventeen years.
v02: He did what was evil in the LORD’s eyes and followed the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had caused Israel to commit. He did not turn away from them.
v03: The LORD’s anger burned against Israel, and he kept giving them into the hands of Hazael king of Aram and Ben-hadad son of Hazael.
</p>

<p>
v04: Then Jehoahaz sought the LORD’s favor, and the LORD listened to him, for he saw how the king of Aram was oppressing Israel.
v05: The LORD gave Israel a deliverer, and they escaped Aram’s power. The Israelites lived in their tents as they had before.
</p>

<p>
v06: Yet they did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam’s house, which he had caused Israel to commit. They continued in them, and the Asherah also remained standing in Samaria.
v07: Jehoahaz was left with only fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand foot soldiers. The king of Aram had destroyed the rest, making them like dust at threshing.
</p>

<p>
v08: The rest of Jehoahaz’s acts, everything he did, and his might are written in the annals of the kings of Israel.
v09: Jehoahaz rested with his ancestors, and they buried him in Samaria. His son Joash became king in his place.
</p>

## Jehoash of Israel

<p>
v10: In the thirty-seventh year of Joash king of Judah, Jehoash son of Jehoahaz became king over Israel in Samaria. He reigned for sixteen years.
v11: He did what was evil in the LORD’s eyes. He did not turn away from any of the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had caused Israel to commit, but continued in them.
</p>

<p>
v12: The rest of Joash’s acts, everything he did, and his might in fighting Amaziah king of Judah are written in the annals of the kings of Israel.
v13: Joash rested with his ancestors, and Jeroboam sat on his throne. Joash was buried in Samaria with the kings of Israel.
</p>

## Elisha’s last sign

<p>
v14: When Elisha was suffering from the illness that would kill him, Joash king of Israel came down to him and wept over him. “My father, my father!” he said. “Israel’s chariot and its horsemen!”
v15: Elisha said to him, “Take a bow and arrows.” So he took a bow and arrows.
v16: He told the king of Israel, “Put your hand on the bow.” When he did, Elisha laid his hands on the king’s hands.
</p>

<p>
v17: “Open the window toward the east,” he said, and the king opened it. Elisha said, “Shoot!” He shot. Then Elisha said, “The LORD’s arrow of victory, an arrow of victory over Aram! You will strike Aram at Aphek until you have finished them.”
</p>

<p>
v18: Then he said, “Take the arrows.” He took them. Elisha told the king of Israel, “Strike the ground.” He struck three times and stopped.
v19: The man of God was angry with him. “You should have struck five or six times!” he said. “Then you would have struck Aram until you had finished them. Now you will strike Aram only three times.”
</p>

## Elisha’s death

<p>
v20: Elisha died, and they buried him. Moabite raiding bands used to enter the land at the turn of the year.
v21: Once, while people were burying a man, they saw a raiding band. They threw the man into Elisha’s tomb. When his body touched Elisha’s bones, he came to life and stood on his feet.
</p>

<p>
v22: Hazael king of Aram oppressed Israel throughout Jehoahaz’s reign.
v23: But the LORD was gracious to them, had compassion on them, and turned toward them because of his covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. He was unwilling to destroy them, and until then he had not cast them out of his presence.
</p>

<p>
v24: Hazael king of Aram died, and his son Ben-hadad became king in his place.
v25: Jehoash son of Jehoahaz took back from Ben-hadad son of Hazael the towns Hazael had taken from his father Jehoahaz in war. Joash defeated him three times and recovered Israel’s towns.
</p>

## Notes

v05: The deliverer is not named in this verse.

v10: Joash and Jehoash are forms of the same name. The text distinguishes the kings of Israel and Judah by their fathers and kingdoms.

v18: The instruction to strike the ground does not state whether the arrows are shot into it or struck against it by hand.

v21: The dead man revives on contact with Elisha’s bones. The narrative gives no extended explanation or instruction about later practices.

## Vocabulary

v14: Israel’s chariot and its horsemen
The cry recalls Elisha’s words at Elijah’s departure in 2:12.

v23: Covenant
The promise relationship with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob remains a reason for compassion.
