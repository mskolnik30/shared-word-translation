## Ahab’s sons killed

<p>
v01: Ahab had seventy sons in Samaria. Jehu wrote letters and sent them to Samaria, to Jezreel’s leaders, the elders, and the guardians of Ahab’s sons. He wrote:
v02: “Your master’s sons are with you, and you have chariots, horses, a fortified city, and weapons. As soon as this letter reaches you,
v03: choose the best and most suitable of your master’s sons, set him on his father’s throne, and fight for your master’s house.”
</p>

<p>
v04: They were utterly terrified. “Two kings couldn’t stand against him,” they said. “How can we?”
v05: The palace administrator, the city governor, the elders, and the guardians sent word to Jehu: “We are your servants. We will do whatever you tell us. We won’t make anyone king. Do what seems good to you.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Jehu wrote them a second letter: “If you are on my side and will obey me, take the heads of your master’s sons and come to me at Jezreel by this time tomorrow.” The seventy royal sons were with the leading men of the city, who were raising them.
v07: When the letter reached them, they took the royal sons and slaughtered all seventy. They put their heads in baskets and sent them to Jehu at Jezreel.
</p>

<p>
v08: A messenger came and told him, “They have brought the royal sons’ heads.” He said, “Put them in two heaps at the entrance to the gate until morning.”
</p>

<p>
v09: In the morning he went out, stood before all the people, and said, “You are innocent. I conspired against my master and killed him. But who struck down all these?
v10: Know, then, that not one word the LORD has spoken against Ahab’s house will fall to the ground. The LORD has done what he said through his servant Elijah.”
v11: Jehu struck down everyone left of Ahab’s house in Jezreel—all his leading men, his close associates, and his priests. He left him no survivor.
</p>

## Jehu travels to Samaria

<p>
v12: Then he set out for Samaria. On the way, at Beth-eked of the Shepherds,
v13: Jehu met relatives of Ahaziah king of Judah. “Who are you?” he asked. They answered, “We are Ahaziah’s relatives. We’re going down to greet the king’s sons and the queen mother’s sons.”
v14: He said, “Take them alive!” They took them alive, then slaughtered them at the cistern of Beth-eked—all forty-two men. He left none of them alive.
</p>

<p>
v15: He went on from there and met Jehonadab son of Rechab coming toward him. Jehu greeted him and asked, “Is your heart true to mine, as mine is to yours?” Jehonadab answered, “It is.” “If so, give me your hand.” He gave him his hand, and Jehu brought him up into the chariot.
v16: “Come with me,” Jehu said, “and see my zeal for the LORD.” So he had him ride in his chariot.
</p>

<p>
v17: When he came to Samaria, he struck down everyone left to Ahab there, wiping out his house, according to the LORD’s word spoken to Elijah.
</p>

## The trap at Baal’s temple

<p>
v18: Jehu gathered all the people and said, “Ahab served Baal a little; Jehu will serve him much.
v19: Now call all Baal’s prophets, all his worshipers, and all his priests to me. Let no one be absent, because I have a great sacrifice for Baal. Anyone absent will not live.” Jehu was acting deceitfully, to destroy Baal’s worshipers.
</p>

<p>
v20: Jehu said, “Proclaim a sacred assembly for Baal.” So they proclaimed it.
v21: Jehu sent word throughout Israel, and all Baal’s worshipers came. Not one stayed away. They entered Baal’s temple, and it was filled from end to end.
</p>

<p>
v22: He told the keeper of the wardrobe, “Bring out garments for all Baal’s worshipers.” So he brought out garments for them.
v23: Jehu and Jehonadab son of Rechab went into Baal’s temple. Jehu said to Baal’s worshipers, “Search carefully. Make sure no servants of the LORD are here with you, only worshipers of Baal.”
</p>

<p>
v24: They went in to offer sacrifices and burnt offerings. Jehu had stationed eighty men outside and said, “If any of the men I place in your hands escapes, your life will pay for his.”
</p>

<p>
v25: As soon as he finished offering the burnt offering, Jehu said to the guards and officers, “Go in and strike them down. Let no one get out.” They struck them down with the sword. The guards and officers threw the bodies out, then went into the inner part of Baal’s temple.
v26: They brought out the sacred pillars from Baal’s temple and burned them.
v27: They demolished Baal’s pillar, tore down Baal’s temple, and made it a latrine, as it remains to this day.
</p>

<p>
v28: So Jehu destroyed Baal worship in Israel.
</p>

## Jehu’s reign assessed

<p>
v29: Yet Jehu did not turn away from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat, which he had caused Israel to commit—the golden calves at Bethel and Dan.
v30: The LORD said to Jehu, “Because you have done well in carrying out what is right in my eyes, and have done to Ahab’s house all that was in my heart, your sons will sit on Israel’s throne to the fourth generation.”
v31: But Jehu did not take care to walk in the instruction of the LORD, the God of Israel, with all his heart. He did not turn away from the sins Jeroboam had caused Israel to commit.
</p>

<p>
v32: In those days the LORD began cutting away parts of Israel. Hazael struck them throughout Israel’s territory:
v33: east of the Jordan, all the land of Gilead—the Gadites, Reubenites, and Manassites—from Aroer by the Arnon Valley, including Gilead and Bashan.
</p>

<p>
v34: The rest of Jehu’s acts, everything he did, and all his might are written in the annals of the kings of Israel.
v35: Jehu rested with his ancestors and was buried in Samaria. His son Jehoahaz became king in his place.
v36: Jehu reigned over Israel in Samaria for twenty-eight years.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The Hebrew addresses Jezreel’s leaders while locating the recipients in Samaria. The place name is retained rather than emended.

v09: The declaration “You are innocent” belongs to Jehu’s public defense, not to the narrator’s verdict.

v13: The Hebrew “brothers” can refer to a wider kin group; the draft uses “relatives.”

v25: The final expression is literally something like “the city of the house of Baal.” Its architectural meaning is uncertain; “inner part” is a contextual rendering.

v30: The approval concerns action against Ahab’s house. Verses 29 and 31 explicitly qualify Jehu’s overall conduct.

## Vocabulary

v15: Heart
The center of intention and loyalty, used here in a pledge of alliance.

v27: Latrine
The cult site is deliberately degraded for ordinary waste use.

v31: Instruction
Hebrew torah: teaching or law that is to guide conduct.
