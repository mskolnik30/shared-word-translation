## Return and restoration

<p>
v01: “When all these things come upon you—the blessing and the curse I have set before you—and you take them to heart among all the nations where the LORD your God has driven you,
v02: and you return to the LORD your God and listen to him, you and your children, with all your heart and all your soul, according to everything I command you today,
v03: then the LORD your God will restore your fortunes and have compassion on you. He will return and gather you from all the peoples where the LORD your God has scattered you.
</p>

<p>
v04: Even if your scattered people are at the farthest edge of the heavens, the LORD your God will gather you from there and bring you back from there.
v05: The LORD your God will bring you into the land your ancestors possessed, and you will possess it. He will do you good and make you more numerous than your ancestors.
</p>

<p>
v06: The LORD your God will circumcise your heart and the heart of your descendants, so that you may love the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul, and live.
v07: The LORD your God will put all these curses on your enemies, on those who hate you and persecute you.
</p>

<p>
v08: You will return, listen to the LORD, and carry out all his commandments that I am giving you today.
v09: The LORD your God will give you abundance in all the work of your hands, in the fruit of your womb, the fruit of your livestock, and the fruit of your ground. The LORD will again rejoice over you for good, as he rejoiced over your ancestors,
v10: if you listen to the LORD your God, keeping his commandments and statutes written in this scroll of instruction, and return to the LORD your God with all your heart and all your soul.
</p>

## The word is near

<p>
v11: This commandment I am giving you today is not too difficult for you or too far away.
v12: It is not in the heavens, so that you must ask, ‘Who will go up to the heavens for us and bring it to us, so that we can hear it and carry it out?’
v13: Nor is it beyond the sea, so that you must ask, ‘Who will cross the sea for us and bring it to us, so that we can hear it and carry it out?’
v14: The word is very near you, in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can carry it out.
</p>

## Choose life

<p>
v15: Look: today I have set before you life and good, death and evil.
v16: I command you today to love the LORD your God, to walk in his ways, and to keep his commandments, statutes, and judgments. Then you will live and increase, and the LORD your God will bless you in the land you are entering to possess.
</p>

<p>
v17: But if your heart turns away and you do not listen, if you are drawn away to bow down to other gods and serve them,
v18: I tell you today that you will certainly perish. You will not live long on the ground you are crossing the Jordan to enter and possess.
</p>

<p>
v19: I call the heavens and the earth as witnesses against you today: I have set before you life and death, blessing and curse. Choose life, so that you and your descendants may live,
v20: loving the LORD your God, listening to him, and holding fast to him. For he is your life and the length of your days, so that you may live on the ground the LORD swore to give your ancestors Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”
</p>

## Notes

v1–8: The Hebrew repeatedly uses the root “return”: taking things to heart, returning to the LORD, restoration, and gathering. The repeated language connects the people’s return with God’s restoring action.

v3: “Restore your fortunes” can also be understood as restoring captives. The following lines explicitly describe gathering the scattered people.

v6: Compare the command to circumcise the heart in 10:16. Here God performs the action, enabling love and life; the bodily image remains in the text.

v12–14: The questions about heaven and sea both end with hearing and doing. The nearby word is likewise given to be carried out.

v20: “He is your life” takes the pronoun as referring to the LORD. The preceding actions—loving, listening, and holding fast—describe the relationship.

## Vocabulary

v6: Circumcise your heart
An image of inward change and covenant devotion, using the bodily covenant sign.

v20: Hold fast
Cling to or remain closely attached to someone.
