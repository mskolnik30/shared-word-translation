## Blessings that overtake you

<p>
v01: “If you listen carefully to the LORD your God and faithfully carry out all his commandments that I am giving you today, the LORD your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth.
v02: All these blessings will come upon you and overtake you, if you listen to the LORD your God.
</p>

<p>
v03: You will be blessed in the town
and blessed in the fields.
v04: Blessed will be the fruit of your womb,
the fruit of your ground,
and the fruit of your livestock—
the calves of your herds and the young of your flocks.
v05: Your basket and your kneading bowl will be blessed.
v06: You will be blessed when you come in
and blessed when you go out.
</p>

<p>
v07: The LORD will cause the enemies who rise against you to be defeated before you. They will come against you along one road and flee from you along seven.
v08: The LORD will command blessing upon your storehouses and everything you undertake. He will bless you in the land the LORD your God is giving you.
v09: The LORD will establish you as a people holy to himself, as he swore to you, if you keep the commandments of the LORD your God and walk in his ways.
v10: All the peoples of the earth will see that the LORD’s name is called over you, and they will fear you.
</p>

<p>
v11: The LORD will give you abundance—in the fruit of your womb, the fruit of your livestock, and the fruit of your ground—in the land the LORD swore to your ancestors to give you.
v12: The LORD will open his good storehouse, the heavens, to give your land rain in its season and bless all the work of your hands. You will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow.
v13: The LORD will make you the head, not the tail. You will be above, never below, if you listen to the commandments of the LORD your God that I am giving you today and faithfully carry them out.
v14: Do not turn aside, to the right or to the left, from any of the words I command you today by following other gods and serving them.
</p>

## Curses that overtake you

<p>
v15: But if you do not listen to the LORD your God and faithfully carry out all his commandments and statutes that I am giving you today, all these curses will come upon you and overtake you.
</p>

<p>
v16: You will be cursed in the town
and cursed in the fields.
v17: Your basket and your kneading bowl will be cursed.
v18: Cursed will be the fruit of your womb
and the fruit of your ground,
the calves of your herds and the young of your flocks.
v19: You will be cursed when you come in
and cursed when you go out.
</p>

<p>
v20: The LORD will send curse, confusion, and rebuke upon everything you undertake, until you are destroyed and quickly perish because of your evil deeds in forsaking me.
v21: The LORD will make pestilence cling to you until he has consumed you from the land you are entering to possess.
v22: The LORD will strike you with wasting disease, fever, inflammation, scorching heat, sword, blight, and mildew. They will pursue you until you perish.
v23: The sky over your head will be bronze, and the earth beneath you will be iron.
v24: The LORD will turn your land’s rain into dust and powder. It will fall on you from the heavens until you are destroyed.
v25: The LORD will cause you to be defeated before your enemies. You will go against them along one road and flee from them along seven. You will become an object of horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.
v26: Your corpses will become food for all the birds of the heavens and the animals of the earth, with no one to frighten them away.
</p>

<p>
v27: The LORD will strike you with the boils of Egypt, hemorrhoids, scabs, and itching, from which you cannot be healed.
v28: The LORD will strike you with madness, blindness, and a bewildered heart.
v29: At noon you will grope about like a blind person groping in darkness, and you will have no success in your ways. You will be oppressed and robbed continually, with no one to rescue you.
</p>

<p>
v30: You will be engaged to a woman, but another man will rape her. You will build a house but never live in it. You will plant a vineyard but never enjoy its fruit.
v31: Your ox will be slaughtered before your eyes, but you will eat none of it. Your donkey will be taken from you, and it will not return. Your flock will be handed to your enemies, with no one to rescue you.
v32: Your sons and daughters will be handed over to another people while you watch. All day your eyes will strain for them and fail, and your hands will have no power to help.
v33: A people you do not know will eat the fruit of your ground and all you have labored for. You will be continually oppressed and crushed.
v34: You will be driven mad by what your eyes see.
v35: The LORD will strike your knees and legs with severe boils from which you cannot be healed, from the sole of your foot to the crown of your head.
</p>

## Loss, exile, and reversal

<p>
v36: The LORD will lead you and the king you set over you to a nation neither you nor your ancestors have known. There you will serve other gods, gods of wood and stone.
v37: You will become a horror, a proverb, and a taunt among all the peoples where the LORD drives you.
</p>

<p>
v38: You will take much seed out to the field but gather little, because locusts will consume it.
v39: You will plant and tend vineyards, but you will neither drink the wine nor gather the grapes, because worms will eat them.
v40: You will have olive trees throughout your territory, but you will not anoint yourself with oil, because your olives will drop off.
v41: You will have sons and daughters, but they will not remain yours, because they will go into captivity.
v42: Swarming insects will take possession of all your trees and the fruit of your ground.
</p>

<p>
v43: The resident foreigner among you will rise higher and higher above you, while you sink lower and lower.
v44: He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him. He will be the head, and you will be the tail.
</p>

<p>
v45: All these curses will come upon you, pursue you, and overtake you until you are destroyed, because you did not listen to the LORD your God by keeping the commandments and statutes he gave you.
v46: They will be a sign and a wonder upon you and your descendants forever.
</p>

<p>
v47: Because you did not serve the LORD your God with joy and a glad heart when you had an abundance of everything,
v48: you will serve the enemies the LORD sends against you, in hunger, thirst, nakedness, and need of everything. He will put an iron yoke on your neck until he has destroyed you.
</p>

## The horrors of siege

<p>
v49: The LORD will bring a nation against you from far away, from the end of the earth, swooping like an eagle—a nation whose language you do not understand.
v50: It will be a fierce-looking nation, showing no respect for the old and no mercy to the young.
v51: It will eat the fruit of your livestock and the fruit of your ground until you are destroyed. It will leave you no grain, new wine, oil, calves from your herds, or young from your flocks, until it has made you perish.
v52: It will besiege you in all your towns until the high, fortified walls you trust come down throughout your land. It will besiege you in all your towns throughout the land the LORD your God has given you.
</p>

<p>
v53: You will eat the fruit of your womb, the flesh of the sons and daughters the LORD your God has given you, in the siege and distress your enemy brings upon you.
</p>

<p>
v54: Even the gentlest, most delicate man among you will look with hostility at his brother, the wife he embraces, and the children he still has,
v55: refusing to give any of them the flesh of his children that he is eating. Nothing else will remain to him in the siege and distress your enemy brings upon you in all your towns.
</p>

<p>
v56: Even the gentlest, most delicate woman among you, who would not venture to set her foot on the ground because she was so delicate and gentle, will look with hostility at the husband she embraces, her son, and her daughter,
v57: withholding the afterbirth that comes out between her legs and the children she bears. She will eat them secretly for lack of everything, in the siege and distress your enemy brings upon you in your towns.
</p>

## Scattered among the nations

<p>
v58: If you do not faithfully carry out all the words of this instruction written in this scroll, revering this glorious and fearsome name—the LORD your God—
v59: the LORD will make your plagues and your descendants’ plagues extraordinary: great, lasting plagues and severe, lasting diseases.
</p>

<p>
v60: He will bring back upon you all the diseases of Egypt that you dreaded, and they will cling to you.
v61: The LORD will also bring upon you every disease and plague not written in this scroll of instruction, until you are destroyed.
v62: You will be left few in number, though you were as numerous as the stars of the heavens, because you did not listen to the LORD your God.
</p>

<p>
v63: Just as the LORD rejoiced over you in doing you good and multiplying you, so the LORD will rejoice over you in making you perish and destroying you. You will be torn from the ground you are entering to possess.
v64: The LORD will scatter you among all peoples, from one end of the earth to the other. There you will serve other gods neither you nor your ancestors have known, gods of wood and stone.
</p>

<p>
v65: Among those nations you will find no rest, no resting place for the sole of your foot. There the LORD will give you a trembling heart, failing eyes, and a languishing soul.
v66: Your life will hang in doubt before you. Night and day you will be afraid, with no assurance of your life.
v67: In the morning you will say, ‘If only it were evening!’ And in the evening you will say, ‘If only it were morning!’ Such will be the dread in your heart and the sights before your eyes.
v68: The LORD will take you back to Egypt in ships, along the route I told you that you would never see again. There you will offer yourselves for sale to your enemies as male and female slaves, but there will be no buyer.”
</p>

## Notes

v2, 15: The blessings and curses both “overtake” Israel. The paired lists repeat town and field, basket and kneading bowl, coming and going, and head and tail.

v10: Having the LORD’s name “called over” the people marks their public association with him.

v20: “Me” is the Hebrew wording: the voice shifts into God’s first person without an introductory formula. Similar shifts occur in 29:5–6.

v22, 27, 42: Some ancient disease and pest names cannot be identified precisely. Verse 22 follows the pinned reading “sword”; “drought” is another possible vocalization. In verse 27 the written text has a term for tumors, while its recorded reading uses a term commonly understood as hemorrhoids.

v30: The written verb is a blunt term for sexual intercourse; the recorded reading substitutes “lie with.” The threatened seizure of another man’s fiancée is rendered as rape, preserving the violence of the situation.

v53–57: The siege warning describes people eating their children and withholding the flesh from other family members. “Fruit of your womb” repeats the earlier blessing language in a horrifying reversal.

v63: The same verb for rejoicing governs doing good and bringing destruction. The translation preserves that disturbing parallel.

v68: The final image reverses the departure from Egyptian slavery. The people offer themselves for sale, but no one buys them.

## Vocabulary

v5: Kneading bowl
A container used to prepare dough.

v48: Yoke
A bar laid across the necks of working animals; here an iron yoke pictures harsh subjection.
