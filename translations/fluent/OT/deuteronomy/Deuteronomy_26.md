## Firstfruits and the Story of Deliverance

<p>
v01: “When you enter the land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance, possess it, and settle there,
v02: take some of the first of every crop you harvest from the ground of the land the LORD your God gives you. Put it in a basket and go to the place the LORD your God chooses for his name to dwell.
v03: Go to the priest serving at that time and say, ‘Today I declare to the LORD your God that I have entered the land the LORD swore to our ancestors to give us.’
v04: The priest must take the basket from your hand and set it before the altar of the LORD your God.
</p>

<p>
v05: Then declare before the LORD your God, ‘My father was a wandering Aramean. He went down to Egypt and lived there as a foreigner with only a few people. There he became a great nation, mighty and numerous.
v06: The Egyptians treated us harshly, afflicted us, and imposed hard labor on us.
v07: We cried out to the LORD, the God of our ancestors. The LORD heard our voice and saw our affliction, our toil, and our oppression.
v08: The LORD brought us out of Egypt with a mighty hand and an outstretched arm, with great terror, signs, and wonders.
v09: He brought us to this place and gave us this land, a land flowing with milk and honey.
v10: Now, LORD, I bring the first of the fruit of the ground you have given me.’ Set it before the LORD your God and bow down before the LORD your God.
</p>

<p>
v11: Rejoice in all the good the LORD your God has given you and your household—you, the Levite, and the resident foreigner among you.
</p>

## The Third-Year Tithe

<p>
v12: When you finish setting aside the whole tithe of your produce in the third year, the year of the tithe, give it to the Levite, the resident foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, so that they may eat their fill in your towns.
v13: Then say before the LORD your God, ‘I have removed the sacred portion from my house and given it to the Levite, the resident foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, just as you commanded me. I have not transgressed your commands or forgotten them.
v14: I have eaten none of it while mourning, removed none while unclean, and given none to the dead. I have listened to the LORD my God and done everything you commanded me.
v15: Look down from your holy dwelling, from heaven, and bless your people Israel and the ground you have given us, as you swore to our ancestors—a land flowing with milk and honey.’
</p>

## A People Bound to the LORD

<p>
v16: Today the LORD your God commands you to carry out these statutes and judgments. Take care to practice them with all your heart and all your soul.
v17: Today you have declared the LORD to be your God, promising to walk in his ways, keep his statutes, commands, and judgments, and listen to his voice.
v18: And today the LORD has declared that you are to be his treasured people, as he promised you, and that you are to keep all his commands.
v19: He will set you high above all the nations he has made, in praise, renown, and honor, so that you may be a people holy to the LORD your God, as he promised.”
</p>

## Notes

v3: The pinned Hebrew has “the LORD your God,” spoken to the priest. Some witnesses read “my God”; the pinned wording is retained.

v5: “Wandering” translates oved, which can also mean “perishing” or “lost.” The father is commonly understood as Jacob. The declaration moves from one vulnerable ancestor and a small group to a numerous people.

v5–10: The speaker moves from “my father” to “us,” then back to “I” as the firstfruits are presented. These shifts draw the present speaker into the remembered deliverance and are retained.

v14: The speaker denies eating the sacred portion in mourning, removing any while unclean, or giving any to the dead. The exact dead-related use is not explained in the verse.

v17–18: The rare forms heemarta and heemirkha are rendered as reciprocal declarations. Other renderings emphasize obtaining an affirmation or acknowledgment. The obligations remain attached to both statements.

## Vocabulary

v2: Firstfruits
The first portion of the harvest brought in acknowledgment of the giver of the land.

v12: Tithe
A tenth; this third-year provision is given to the named recipients in the towns.

v18: Treasured people
A people belonging especially to the LORD; the phrase is linked here with keeping his commands.
