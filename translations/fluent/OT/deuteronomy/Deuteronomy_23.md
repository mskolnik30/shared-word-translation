<p>
v01: “No man whose testicles have been crushed or whose penis has been cut off may enter the assembly of the LORD.
v02: No one born of a forbidden union may enter the assembly of the LORD. Even to the tenth generation, none of their descendants may enter the assembly of the LORD.
v03: No Ammonite or Moabite may enter the assembly of the LORD. Even to the tenth generation, their descendants may never enter the assembly of the LORD,
v04: because they did not meet you with bread and water on the road when you came out of Egypt, and because Balaam son of Beor was hired against you from Pethor in Aram-naharaim to curse you.
v05: But the LORD your God refused to listen to Balaam. The LORD your God turned the curse into a blessing for you, because the LORD your God loved you.

## Notes

v1–25: These public verses correspond to Hebrew 23:2–26. Hebrew 23:1 is translated at public 22:30.

v1–8: “Assembly of the LORD” names the body from which these categories are excluded. The precise scope of admission—cultic participation, communal standing, or related rights—is debated. The translation keeps “assembly” rather than substituting “nation” or “worship service.”

v2: “Born of a forbidden union” renders mamzer, a term whose precise scope is uncertain. It should not be assumed to cover every child born outside marriage.

v10: “An emission during the night” renders the euphemism “a happening of the night.” The resulting uncleanness is a ritual status.

v15–16: The escaped slave is not to be returned and may choose where to live. The Hebrew does not expressly specify his nationality or the location of his former master.

v17–18: “Cult prostitute” is the traditional rendering of qedeshah and qadesh, female and male terms connected with being set apart. Their precise cultic roles are disputed; the verse does not describe their rituals. “Dog” in verse 18 is literal Hebrew and has also been understood as a disparaging term for a male prostitute.

## Vocabulary

v4: Aram-naharaim
The named region associated here with Pethor, Balaam’s home.

v19: Interest
An additional amount charged on a loan; the rule is not limited to excessive rates.

v21: Vow
A voluntary promise to God that becomes binding when made.
