## Moses blesses Israel

<p>
v01: This is the blessing with which Moses, the man of God, blessed the Israelites before his death.
v02: He said:
“The LORD came from Sinai
and dawned upon them from Seir.
He shone from Mount Paran
and came from myriads of holy ones;
from his right hand came a fiery law for them.
v03: Indeed, he cherishes the peoples;
all his holy ones are in your hand.
They bow at your feet;
each receives your words.
v04: Moses commanded us instruction,
an inheritance for the assembly of Jacob.
v05: He was king in Jeshurun
when the heads of the people gathered,
when Israel’s tribes came together.
</p>

<p>
v06: May Reuben live and not die,
though his men are few.”
</p>

## Judah

<p>
v07: He said this of Judah:
“Hear Judah’s voice, LORD,
and bring him to his people.
His hands contend for him;
be his help against his foes.”
</p>

## Levi

<p>
v08: Of Levi he said:
“Let your Thummim and Urim belong to your faithful man,
whom you tested at Massah,
with whom you contended at the waters of Meribah.
v09: He said of his father and mother, ‘I do not regard them.’
He did not acknowledge his brothers
or recognize his own sons.
For they kept your word
and guarded your covenant.
v10: They will teach your judgments to Jacob
and your instruction to Israel.
They will place incense before your nostrils
and whole offerings on your altar.
v11: Bless his strength, LORD,
and accept the work of his hands.
Shatter the loins of those who rise against him,
of those who hate him, so they cannot rise again.”
</p>

## Benjamin

<p>
v12: Of Benjamin he said:
“The LORD’s beloved rests securely beside him.
He shelters him all day long,
and he dwells between his shoulders.”
</p>

## Joseph

<p>
v13: Of Joseph he said:
“May the LORD bless his land
with heaven’s precious gifts, with dew,
and with the deep crouching below,
v14: with the precious produce of the sun
and the precious yield of the moons,
v15: with the best from ancient mountains
and precious gifts from everlasting hills,
v16: with the precious gifts of the earth and all that fills it,
and the favor of the one who dwelt in the bush.
Let these come upon Joseph’s head,
upon the crown of the one set apart from his brothers.
</p>

<p>
v17: His firstborn bull has majesty;
his horns are the horns of a wild ox.
With them he gores peoples together
to the ends of the earth.
They are Ephraim’s ten thousands;
they are Manasseh’s thousands.”
</p>

## Zebulun and Issachar

<p>
v18: Of Zebulun he said:
“Rejoice, Zebulun, in your going out,
and Issachar, in your tents.
v19: They will call peoples to the mountain
and offer righteous sacrifices there.
For they suck the abundance of the seas
and the treasures hidden in the sand.”
</p>

## Gad

<p>
v20: Of Gad he said:
“Blessed is the one who makes room for Gad!
He lies down like a lion
and tears off an arm, even a scalp.
v21: He chose the best portion for himself,
for there a ruler’s portion was reserved.
He came with the heads of the people;
he carried out the LORD’s righteousness
and his judgments with Israel.”
</p>

## Dan

<p>
v22: Of Dan he said:
“Dan is a lion’s cub,
leaping from Bashan.”
</p>

## Naphtali

<p>
v23: Of Naphtali he said:
“Naphtali, satisfied with favor
and full of the LORD’s blessing,
take possession of the west and the south.”
</p>

## Asher

<p>
v24: Of Asher he said:
“May Asher be blessed among sons;
may he be favored by his brothers
and dip his foot in oil.
v25: May your bars be iron and bronze,
and your strength last as long as your days.”
</p>

## Israel’s helper

<p>
v26: “There is no one like God, Jeshurun,
riding the heavens to help you,
the clouds in his majesty.
</p>

<p>
v27: The God of old is your dwelling place;
beneath you are everlasting arms.
He drove the enemy from before you
and said, ‘Destroy!’
v28: Israel dwells securely,
the spring of Jacob alone,
in a land of grain and new wine,
where the heavens drip dew.
v29: Happy are you, Israel! Who is like you,
a people saved by the LORD?
He is the shield that helps you,
the sword of your majesty.
Your enemies will cringe before you,
and you will tread their high places.”
</p>

## Notes

v2–5: These opening lines are difficult. “Fiery law” follows the recorded two-word reading; the underlying expression is obscure. The shifts from “his” to “your” and from Moses’ speech to “Moses commanded us” remain visible. The “he” called king in verse 5 is not explicitly named.

v6: “Though his men are few” supplies the relationship between two compact clauses. Another reading carries “not” from the preceding line into this one: “may his men not be few.” No separate blessing for Simeon appears in this chapter.

v8–9: Massah is related to “testing,” and Meribah to “contention.” The faithful man is unnamed. Verse 9 describes loyalty that overrides family recognition and then shifts to the plural “they.”

v12: The final “he” and “his” are not explicitly identified. The shoulders image can be understood in different ways depending on whether the LORD or Benjamin is the one dwelling.

v16: The one who dwelt in the bush recalls the encounter in Exodus 3. “Set apart” describes Joseph’s distinction from his brothers; it need not designate a Nazirite vow.

v21, 23, 25: The ruler’s reserved portion in verse 21 is obscure. “West” in verse 23 can also mean “sea.” In verse 25 both “bars” (also rendered “sandals”) and “strength” are uncertain terms.

v28–29: “The spring of Jacob” is kept as an image; it may picture Jacob’s people as a source or flow. “High places” follows the pinned wording at the close.

## Vocabulary

v8: Thummim and Urim
Sacred objects associated with priestly inquiry; see Exodus 28:30. The order here is Thummim, then Urim.

v10: Whole offerings
Offerings burned entirely on the altar.

v26: Jeshurun
The poetic name for Israel also used in 32:15.
