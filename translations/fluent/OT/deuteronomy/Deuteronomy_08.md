## Remember the wilderness

<p>
v01: “Be careful to practice this whole commandment that I give you today, so that you may live, become numerous, and enter and possess the land the LORD swore to your fathers.
v02: Remember the whole way the LORD your God led you through the wilderness these forty years, to humble you and test you, to know what was in your heart—whether you would keep his commands or not.
</p>

<p>
v03: He humbled you, let you go hungry, and fed you manna, which neither you nor your fathers had known. He did this to teach you that a human being does not live on bread alone, but on everything that comes from the LORD's mouth.
v04: Your clothing did not wear out on you, and your feet did not swell during these forty years.
</p>

<p>
v05: Know in your heart that the LORD your God disciplines you as a man disciplines his son.
v06: Keep the commands of the LORD your God, walking in his ways and fearing him.
</p>

## The good land

<p>
v07: For the LORD your God is bringing you into a good land, a land of flowing streams, springs, and deep waters welling up in valleys and hills,
v08: a land of wheat and barley, vines, fig trees, and pomegranates, a land of olive oil and honey,
v09: a land where you will not eat bread in scarcity and will lack nothing, a land whose stones contain iron and from whose hills you can mine copper.
</p>

<p>
v10: When you have eaten your fill, bless the LORD your God for the good land he has given you.
</p>

## Do not forget amid abundance

<p>
v11: Be careful not to forget the LORD your God by failing to keep his commands, rulings, and statutes that I give you today.
</p>

<p>
v12: When you eat your fill, build good houses and live in them,
v13: and your herds and flocks increase, your silver and gold increase, and everything you have increases,
v14: do not let your heart grow proud and forget the LORD your God, who brought you out of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.
</p>

<p>
v15: He led you through the vast, terrifying wilderness, with its fiery snakes and scorpions, its thirsty ground where there was no water. He brought water out of flint rock for you.
v16: He fed you manna in the wilderness, something your fathers had not known, to humble and test you, and in the end to do you good.
</p>

## The source of your strength

<p>
v17: Do not say in your heart, ‘My power and the strength of my own hand have made this wealth for me.’
v18: Remember the LORD your God, because he is the one who gives you the power to gain wealth, fulfilling his covenant that he swore to your fathers, as he is doing today.
</p>

<p>
v19: If you forget the LORD your God and follow other gods, serving them and bowing down to them, I testify against you today that you will surely perish.
v20: Like the nations the LORD is destroying before you, so you will perish, because you would not listen to the voice of the LORD your God.”
</p>

## Notes

v2: The written/read annotation concerns the spelling of “his commands.” It does not require repeating the phrase. The passage states that the testing was to know what was in the people's heart; the translation leaves that claim intact.

v3: The Hebrew says “everything that comes from the LORD's mouth.” This includes what God commands or provides without inserting a narrower object into the main text.

v5: The comparison is specifically a man disciplining his son, connecting wilderness hardship with instruction.

v15: “Fiery snakes” retains the ancient description, commonly associated with the burning effect of a venomous bite. The wording does not say that the snakes literally breathe fire.

v17–20: The warning against self-credit follows the list of gifts. Its final consequence turns the destruction of the nations back upon Israel if Israel abandons the LORD.

## Vocabulary

v3: Manna
The wilderness food described in Exodus 16; here unfamiliarity with it serves the teaching about dependence.

v8: Honey
The Hebrew word can denote bee honey or a sweet fruit product. The verse does not identify the source.

v14: A proud heart
Literally a heart lifted up, an image of self-exaltation.

v18: Covenant
A binding relationship and commitment, here the oath to the ancestors that the LORD fulfills.
