## Hear and practice the instruction

<p>
v01: “Now, Israel, listen to the statutes and rulings I am teaching you to practice. Then you may live, enter the land, and take possession of what the LORD, the God of your fathers, is giving you.
v02: Do not add to what I command you or take anything away from it. Keep the commands of the LORD your God that I am giving you.
</p>

<p>
v03: Your own eyes saw what the LORD did at Baal-peor. The LORD your God destroyed from among you everyone who followed Baal of Peor.
v04: But all of you who cling to the LORD your God are alive today.
</p>

<p>
v05: See, I have taught you statutes and rulings as the LORD my God commanded me, so that you may practice them in the land you are entering to possess.
v06: Keep them and put them into practice. This will be your wisdom and understanding in the eyes of the peoples, who will hear all these statutes and say, ‘Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people!’
</p>

<p>
v07: For what great nation has a god so near to it as the LORD our God is to us whenever we call on him?
v08: And what great nation has statutes and rulings as righteous as this whole instruction I am setting before you today?
</p>

## Remember the voice at Horeb

<p>
v09: Only be careful. Watch yourself closely, so that you do not forget what your eyes have seen or let it leave your heart all the days of your life. Make it known to your children and grandchildren.
</p>

<p>
v10: Remember the day you stood before the LORD your God at Horeb, when the LORD told me, ‘Gather the people to me so I can let them hear my words. Then they may learn to fear me all the days they live on earth and teach their children.’
v11: You came near and stood at the foot of the mountain. The mountain blazed with fire to the heart of the heavens, amid darkness, cloud, and thick gloom.
v12: The LORD spoke to you from the fire. You heard the sound of words, but saw no form—only a voice.
</p>

<p>
v13: He declared his covenant to you, commanding you to keep the Ten Words, and he wrote them on two stone tablets.
v14: At that time the LORD commanded me to teach you statutes and rulings, so that you would practice them in the land you are crossing over to possess.
</p>

## You saw no form

<p>
v15: So watch yourselves closely. You saw no form on the day the LORD spoke to you at Horeb from the fire.
v16: Do not corrupt yourselves by making a carved image in the shape of any figure, whether male or female,
v17: or in the shape of any animal on earth, any winged bird flying in the sky,
v18: anything that creeps along the ground, or any fish in the waters below the earth.
</p>

<p>
v19: When you look up to the heavens and see the sun, moon, and stars, all the host of heaven, do not be drawn away to bow down to them and serve them. The LORD your God has allotted them to all the peoples everywhere under heaven.
</p>

<p>
v20: But the LORD took you and brought you out of the iron furnace, out of Egypt, to be the people of his own inheritance, as you are today.
</p>

<p>
v21: The LORD was angry with me because of you. He swore that I would not cross the Jordan or enter the good land the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance.
v22: I will die in this land. I will not cross the Jordan, but you will cross and take possession of that good land.
</p>

<p>
v23: Watch yourselves, so that you do not forget the covenant the LORD your God made with you and make yourselves a carved image in any form the LORD your God has forbidden.
v24: For the LORD your God is a consuming fire, a jealous God.
</p>

## Scattering and return

<p>
v25: When you have children and grandchildren and have lived long in the land, if you corrupt yourselves by making a carved image of any kind and do what is evil in the eyes of the LORD your God, provoking him to anger,
v26: I call heaven and earth as witnesses against you today: you will quickly perish from the land you are crossing the Jordan to possess. You will not live long in it. You will be utterly destroyed.
</p>

<p>
v27: The LORD will scatter you among the peoples. Only a few of you will remain among the nations where the LORD drives you.
v28: There you will serve gods made by human hands, wood and stone, which cannot see, hear, eat, or smell.
</p>

<p>
v29: But from there you will seek the LORD your God and find him, if you search for him with all your heart and all your soul.
v30: When you are in distress and all these things come upon you in the days ahead, you will return to the LORD your God and listen to his voice.
</p>

<p>
v31: For the LORD your God is a merciful God. He will not abandon you or destroy you or forget the covenant he swore to your fathers.
</p>

## Has anything like this happened?

<p>
v32: Ask about the days before you, from the day God created humanity on the earth. Ask from one end of the heavens to the other: Has anything this great ever happened, or has anything like it ever been heard?
v33: Has any people heard the voice of a god speaking from the fire, as you have heard, and lived?
v34: Or has a god ever attempted to take a nation for himself from within another nation, through trials, signs, wonders, war, a strong hand, an outstretched arm, and great terrors, as the LORD your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?
</p>

<p>
v35: You were shown these things so that you would know that the LORD is God. There is no other besides him.
v36: From heaven he let you hear his voice to instruct you. On earth he let you see his great fire, and you heard his words from the fire.
</p>

<p>
v37: Because he loved your fathers and chose their descendants after them, he brought you out of Egypt by his presence and his great power,
v38: to drive out before you nations greater and stronger than you, bring you in, and give you their land as an inheritance, as he has now done.
</p>

<p>
v39: Know this today and take it to heart: the LORD is God in heaven above and on earth below. There is no other.
v40: Keep his statutes and commands that I am giving you today, so that it may go well with you and your children after you, and so that you may live long on the land the LORD your God is giving you for all time.”
</p>

## Three towns of refuge

<p>
v41: Then Moses set apart three towns beyond the Jordan, toward the sunrise,
v42: where someone could flee after killing a neighbor unintentionally, without having hated that neighbor beforehand. By fleeing to one of these towns, the killer could stay alive.
</p>

<p>
v43: The towns were Bezer in the wilderness on the plateau for the Reubenites, Ramoth in Gilead for the Gadites, and Golan in Bashan for the Manassites.
</p>

## The setting of the instruction

<p>
v44: This is the instruction Moses set before the Israelites.
</p>

<p>
v45: These are the testimonies, statutes, and rulings Moses gave the Israelites when they came out of Egypt,
v46: beyond the Jordan, in the valley opposite Beth-peor, in the land of Sihon, the Amorite king who lived in Heshbon. Moses and the Israelites defeated him when they came out of Egypt.
</p>

<p>
v47: They took possession of his land and the land of Og king of Bashan, the two Amorite kings beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise.
v48: Their territory stretched from Aroer on the edge of the Wadi Arnon to Mount Sion, that is, Hermon,
v49: and included all the Arabah east of the Jordan, as far as the Sea of the Arabah below the slopes of Pisgah.
</p>

## Notes

v7: The Hebrew expression can be rendered “a god so near” or “gods so near.” The comparison emphasizes the LORD's nearness whenever Israel calls.

v9–14: Remembering includes teaching later generations. The passage distinguishes the voice heard from the form not seen, then connects the covenant with the Ten Words and further instruction.

v19–20: The heavenly bodies are said to be allotted to all peoples, while Israel is taken as the LORD's inheritance. The text here does not explicitly authorize the other peoples to worship the heavenly bodies.

v21: “Because of you” translates an expression that can also mean “because of your words” or “on your account.” Moses again connects his exclusion with the people.

v24–31: The consuming fire and jealous God language belongs to the warning. The later appeal names the LORD as merciful and promises that he will not abandon, destroy, or forget the ancestral covenant. Both parts are retained.

v30: “In the days ahead” renders “in the latter days.” The phrase points to a future horizon without specifying a timetable.

v37: After the plural “your fathers,” the Hebrew shifts to singular forms, “his descendants after him.” The draft takes these collectively with the fathers. A reference to a particular ancestor is also possible. “By his presence” preserves the wording without inserting a named intermediary.

v41–43: The refuge towns are for someone who kills unintentionally without prior hatred. Compare the fuller procedures in Numbers 35; refuge is not a declaration that no killing occurred.

v48: Sion is the name given here for Hermon. It is distinct from Zion and differs from the form Sirion in 3:9.

## Vocabulary

v13: Ten Words
The Hebrew designation for the commands commonly called the Ten Commandments.

v19: Host of heaven
The array of heavenly bodies, here the sun, moon, and stars.

v20: Iron furnace
An image of Egypt as a place of severe affliction, using the heat of a metalworking furnace.

v24: Jealous
Describes the LORD's demand for exclusive allegiance within the covenant.

v45: Testimonies, statutes, and rulings
Related terms for the covenant's authoritative teaching, prescribed requirements, and judgments.
