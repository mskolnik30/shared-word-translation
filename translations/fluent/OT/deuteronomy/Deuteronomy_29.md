## The covenant in Moab

<p>
v01: These are the words of the covenant the LORD commanded Moses to make with the Israelites in the land of Moab, in addition to the covenant he made with them at Horeb.
</p>

## Remember what you have seen

<p>
v02: Moses called all Israel together and said to them, “You yourselves have seen everything the LORD did before your eyes in Egypt to Pharaoh, to all his servants, and to all his land.
v03: Your eyes saw the great trials, those great signs and wonders.
v04: Yet to this day the LORD has not given you a heart to understand, eyes to see, or ears to hear.
</p>

<p>
v05: I led you through the wilderness for forty years. The clothes on you did not wear out, and the sandals on your feet did not wear out.
v06: You ate no bread and drank no wine or strong drink, so that you would know that I am the LORD your God.
</p>

<p>
v07: When you came to this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out to fight us, and we defeated them.
v08: We took their land and gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and half the tribe of Manasseh.
v09: So keep the words of this covenant and carry them out, so that you may succeed in everything you do.
</p>

## All stand within the covenant

<p>
v10: All of you stand today before the LORD your God: your heads, your tribes, your elders and officers, all the men of Israel,
v11: your children, your wives, and the resident foreigner within your camp, from the one who cuts your wood to the one who draws your water.
</p>

<p>
v12: You are here to enter the covenant of the LORD your God, with its binding oath and curse, which the LORD your God is making with you today,
v13: so that he may establish you today as his people and be your God, as he promised you and swore to your ancestors Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
</p>

<p>
v14: I am making this covenant, with its oath and curse, not with you alone,
v15: but both with those standing here with us today before the LORD our God and with those who are not here with us today.
</p>

## The danger of a turning heart

<p>
v16: You know how we lived in Egypt and how we passed through the nations along the way.
v17: You saw their detestable things and their filthy idols of wood and stone, silver and gold, there among them.
</p>

<p>
v18: Make sure there is no man or woman, clan or tribe among you whose heart turns away today from the LORD our God to go and serve the gods of those nations. Make sure no root among you produces poison and wormwood.
v19: Someone may hear the words of this oath and curse, yet bless himself in his heart, saying, ‘I will have peace, even though I follow my stubborn heart.’ That would sweep away the watered with the thirsty.
</p>

<p>
v20: The LORD will not be willing to forgive him. Instead, the LORD’s anger and jealousy will smoke against that person. Every curse written in this scroll will settle on him, and the LORD will wipe out his name from under the heavens.
v21: The LORD will single him out from all the tribes of Israel for calamity, in keeping with all the covenant curses written in this scroll of instruction.
</p>

## What later generations will ask

<p>
v22: The next generation—your children who rise after you—and the foreigner who comes from a distant land will see the plagues on this land and the diseases the LORD has inflicted on it, and they will speak about it.
v23: All its land will be sulfur, salt, and scorched earth, unsown and unproductive, with no grass growing on it—like the overthrow of Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim, which the LORD overthrew in his anger and wrath.
</p>

<p>
v24: All the nations will ask, ‘Why has the LORD done this to this land? What caused this great blaze of anger?’
v25: People will answer, ‘Because they abandoned the covenant of the LORD, the God of their ancestors, which he made with them when he brought them out of Egypt.
v26: They went and served other gods and bowed down to them—gods they had not known and that he had not allotted to them.
v27: So the LORD’s anger burned against this land, bringing upon it every curse written in this scroll.
v28: In anger, wrath, and great rage, the LORD uprooted them from their ground and threw them into another land, where they are today.’
</p>

<p>
v29: The hidden things belong to the LORD our God, but the revealed things belong to us and our children forever, so that we may carry out all the words of this instruction.”
</p>

## Notes

v1: This verse is Hebrew 28:69. Public 29:2–29 corresponds to Hebrew 29:1–28; each verse is bound to its complete original source record.

v4–6: The speech distinguishes witnessing events from receiving understanding. In verses 5–6 Moses speaks in God’s first person, ending with “I am the LORD your God.”

v12–15: The oath carries a curse for covenant violation. Its reach includes those standing there and those absent; the text does not limit the latter phrase to one category.

v19: “The watered with the thirsty” is an obscure saying. It appears to warn of destruction that spreads beyond the speaker. The image is retained because identifying its two parts more precisely would settle an uncertain interpretation.

v29: The hidden/revealed contrast ends with the purpose of carrying out the instruction. The pinned XML also preserves special scribal dots over part of this verse.

## Vocabulary

v18: Wormwood
A bitter plant, paired here with poison as an image of harmful growth.

v29: Revealed things
What has been made known, contrasted here with what remains hidden with God.
