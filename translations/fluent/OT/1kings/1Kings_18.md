## Elijah meets Obadiah and Ahab

<p>
v01: After a long time, in the third year, the word of the LORD came to Elijah: “Go, show yourself to Ahab. I will send rain on the ground.”
v02: Elijah went to show himself to Ahab. The famine in Samaria was severe.
</p>

<p>
v03: Ahab summoned Obadiah, who managed the palace. Obadiah deeply feared the LORD.
v04: When Jezebel was cutting down the LORD’s prophets, Obadiah had hidden a hundred of them in caves, fifty to a cave, and supplied them with bread and water.
</p>

<p>
v05: Ahab told Obadiah, “Go through the land to every spring and every wadi. Perhaps we can find grass and keep the horses and mules alive, so we will not have to destroy any of the animals.”
v06: They divided the land between them to search it. Ahab went one way by himself, and Obadiah another.
</p>

<p>
v07: As Obadiah went along, Elijah met him. He recognized him, fell facedown, and said, “Is it really you, my lord Elijah?”
v08: “It is,” he replied. “Go, tell your lord, ‘Elijah is here.’”
</p>

<p>
v09: Obadiah said, “What sin have I committed that you would hand your servant over to Ahab to kill me?
v10: As the LORD your God lives, there is no nation or kingdom where my lord has not sent to look for you. Whenever they said, ‘He is not here,’ he made that kingdom or nation swear they could not find you.
v11: And now you say, ‘Go, tell your lord, “Elijah is here.”’
</p>

<p>
v12: As soon as I leave you, the LORD’s Spirit may carry you somewhere I do not know. If I tell Ahab and he cannot find you, he will kill me. Yet your servant has feared the LORD since my youth.
v13: Has my lord not been told what I did when Jezebel killed the LORD’s prophets? I hid a hundred of the LORD’s prophets in caves, fifty to a cave, and supplied them with bread and water.
v14: And now you say, ‘Go, tell your lord, “Elijah is here.”’ He will kill me!”
</p>

<p>
v15: Elijah said, “As the LORD of Armies lives, whom I serve, I will show myself to Ahab today.”
v16: Obadiah went to meet Ahab and told him, and Ahab went to meet Elijah.
</p>

<p>
v17: When Ahab saw Elijah, he said, “Is that you, the one bringing trouble on Israel?”
v18: Elijah answered, “I have not brought trouble on Israel. You and your father’s house have, by abandoning the LORD’s commands and following the Baals.
</p>

<p>
v19: Now summon all Israel to meet me on Mount Carmel, along with Baal’s four hundred fifty prophets and Asherah’s four hundred prophets, who eat at Jezebel’s table.”
</p>

## The test on Mount Carmel

<p>
v20: Ahab sent throughout Israel and assembled the prophets on Mount Carmel.
v21: Elijah approached all the people and said, “How long will you limp between two sides? If the LORD is God, follow him. If Baal is, follow him.” The people answered him not a word.
</p>

<p>
v22: Then Elijah said, “I alone remain as a prophet of the LORD, but Baal has four hundred fifty prophets.
v23: Let two bulls be brought to us. Let them choose one, cut it up, and place it on the wood, but light no fire. I will prepare the other bull and place it on the wood, and I will light no fire.
v24: You call on your god’s name, and I will call on the LORD’s name. The God who answers with fire is God.” All the people answered, “That is good.”
</p>

<p>
v25: Elijah told Baal’s prophets, “Choose one bull and prepare it first, since there are so many of you. Call on your god’s name, but light no fire.”
v26: They took the bull given to them and prepared it. From morning until noon they called on Baal’s name: “Baal, answer us!” But there was no voice, no answer. They limped around the altar that had been made.
</p>

<p>
v27: At noon Elijah mocked them: “Call louder! He is a god, after all. Perhaps he is thinking, or relieving himself, or away on a journey. Perhaps he is asleep and must wake up.”
v28: They shouted louder and cut themselves with swords and spears, as was their custom, until blood poured over them.
v29: After noon they continued prophesying until the time of the offering. But there was no voice, no answer, no one paying attention.
</p>

<p>
v30: Elijah told all the people, “Come closer to me.” They came, and he repaired the LORD’s altar, which had been torn down.
v31: Elijah took twelve stones, one for each tribe of Jacob’s sons—Jacob, to whom the LORD had said, “Your name will be Israel.”
v32: With the stones he built an altar in the LORD’s name. Around it he made a trench large enough to hold two seahs of seed.
</p>

<p>
v33: He arranged the wood, cut up the bull, and laid it on the wood.
v34: He said, “Fill four jars with water and pour it over the burnt offering and the wood.” Then he said, “Do it again.” They did it again. “A third time,” he said, and they did it a third time.
v35: The water ran around the altar, and he filled the trench with water too.
</p>

<p>
v36: At the time of the offering, Elijah the prophet approached and said, “LORD, God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, let it be known today that you are God in Israel, that I am your servant, and that I have done all this at your word.
v37: Answer me, LORD, answer me, so these people may know that you, LORD, are God, and that you have turned their hearts back.”
</p>

<p>
v38: Then the LORD’s fire fell. It consumed the burnt offering, the wood, the stones, and the dust, and licked up the water in the trench.
v39: When all the people saw it, they fell facedown and said, “The LORD is God! The LORD is God!”
</p>

<p>
v40: Elijah told them, “Seize Baal’s prophets! Do not let one escape.” They seized them, and Elijah brought them down to the Wadi Kishon and slaughtered them there.
</p>

## Rain returns

<p>
v41: Then Elijah told Ahab, “Go up, eat and drink. There is the sound of heavy rain.”
v42: Ahab went up to eat and drink. Elijah climbed to Carmel’s summit, bent down to the ground, and put his face between his knees.
</p>

<p>
v43: He told his servant, “Go up and look toward the sea.” The servant looked and said, “There is nothing.” Seven times Elijah told him, “Go back.”
v44: On the seventh time the servant said, “A cloud as small as a man’s hand is rising from the sea.” Elijah said, “Go, tell Ahab, ‘Harness your chariot and go down before the rain stops you.’”
</p>

<p>
v45: In a little while the sky grew dark with clouds and wind, and heavy rain fell. Ahab rode away to Jezreel.
v46: The LORD’s hand came upon Elijah. He tucked his clothing into his belt and ran ahead of Ahab all the way to the entrance of Jezreel.
</p>

## Notes

v19–22: Elijah requests 450 prophets of Baal and 400 of Asherah. The ensuing contest specifically counts Baal’s prophets. It does not separately narrate the fate of all 400 prophets of Asherah.

v21,26: “Limp” connects the people’s divided allegiance with the prophets’ movement around the altar. The first phrase can picture two branches or two alternatives.

v22: Elijah’s claim to be the only remaining prophet stands alongside the account of Obadiah hiding a hundred. It is left as Elijah’s speech.

v27: “Relieving himself” takes a possible euphemistic sense of the Hebrew. “Occupied” or “gone aside” are less specific possibilities.

v32: The trench is described by two seahs of seed. This may indicate its capacity or the area that amount of seed would sow.

v33–34: The verse division follows the pinned Hebrew: preparing the bull in verse 33, then all three water instructions in verse 34.

v37: The Hebrew says “you have turned their hearts back.” The direction can be heard as restoration, though turning backward or away is also possible. The perfect form is retained.

v40: “Slaughtered” preserves the force of the verb. The account names Elijah as carrying out the killing.

## Vocabulary

v15: LORD of Armies
A title presenting the LORD as commander of hosts or armies.

v36: Grain offering
An offering of grain or flour; its customary time locates the prayer within the day.
