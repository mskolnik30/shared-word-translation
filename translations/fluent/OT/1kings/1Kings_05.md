## Timber from Hiram

<p>
v01: Hiram king of Tyre sent his servants to Solomon when he heard that Solomon had been anointed king in his father’s place. Hiram had always loved David.
</p>

<p>
v02: Solomon sent this message to Hiram:
v03: “You know that my father David could not build a house for the name of the LORD his God because of the warfare surrounding him, until the LORD put his enemies under the soles of my feet.
v04: But now the LORD my God has given me rest on every side. There is no adversary and no evil happening.
v05: So I intend to build a house for the name of the LORD my God, just as the LORD told my father David: ‘Your son, whom I will put on your throne in your place, will build the house for my name.’
v06: Now give orders for cedars to be cut for me in Lebanon. My servants will work with yours, and I will pay your servants whatever wages you set. You know that none of us knows how to cut timber like the Sidonians.”
</p>

<p>
v07: When Hiram heard Solomon’s words, he was delighted. “Blessed be the LORD today,” he said, “who has given David a wise son to rule this great people!”
</p>

<p>
v08: Hiram sent word to Solomon: “I have heard your message. I will meet all your needs for cedar and cypress timber.
v09: My servants will bring it down from Lebanon to the sea. I will make it into rafts and float it to the place you designate. I will break the rafts apart there, and you can take the timber away. In return, meet my needs by supplying food for my household.”
</p>

<p>
v10: Hiram supplied Solomon with all the cedar and cypress timber he wanted.
v11: Solomon supplied Hiram with twenty thousand cors of wheat as food for his household and twenty cors of beaten olive oil. He gave Hiram this amount year after year.
</p>

<p>
v12: The LORD gave Solomon wisdom as he had promised. Hiram and Solomon were at peace, and the two made a covenant.
</p>

## Labor for the temple

<p>
v13: King Solomon conscripted a forced-labor levy from all Israel: thirty thousand men.
v14: He sent them to Lebanon in shifts of ten thousand a month. They spent one month in Lebanon and two months at home. Adoniram was over the forced labor.
</p>

<p>
v15: Solomon also had seventy thousand burden bearers and eighty thousand stonecutters in the hills,
v16: besides Solomon’s thirty-three hundred chief overseers, who supervised the people doing the work.
</p>

<p>
v17: At the king’s command they quarried great, costly stones, dressed stones for the house’s foundations.
v18: Solomon’s builders, Hiram’s builders, and the Gebalites dressed the stone and prepared the timber and stone for building the house.
</p>

## Notes

v01–18: This chapter corresponds to Hebrew 5:15–32 in the pinned source.

v03: “My feet” follows the source’s read form. Its written form has “his feet,” referring back to David.

v11: The source specifies twenty thousand cors of wheat and twenty cors of beaten olive oil each year.

v13–14: Israel’s thirty thousand men serve in rotating forced-labor shifts. Chapter 9:22 distinguishes Israelites from those Solomon made slaves; the two descriptions are both retained.

v16: This passage gives 3,300 supervisors. Chapter 9:23 gives 550 chief officials; the translation does not replace either count.

## Vocabulary

v08: Cypress
A conventional identification of the Hebrew timber name; the exact tree species is uncertain.

v12: Covenant
A binding agreement, here between Solomon and Hiram.

v18: Gebalites
People of Gebal, named alongside Solomon’s and Hiram’s builders.
