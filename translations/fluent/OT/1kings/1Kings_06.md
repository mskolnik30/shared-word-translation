## Building the LORD’s house

<p>
v01: In the four hundred eightieth year after the Israelites left Egypt, in the fourth year of Solomon’s reign over Israel, in Ziv, the second month, he began building the LORD’s house.
v02: The house King Solomon built for the LORD was sixty cubits long, twenty cubits wide, and thirty cubits high.
</p>

<p>
v03: The porch in front of the house’s main hall extended twenty cubits across the width of the house and projected ten cubits from its front.
v04: He made windows for the house with recessed frames.
</p>

<p>
v05: He built a structure against the outside walls of the house, around the main hall and the inner sanctuary, with side rooms all around.
v06: The lowest story was five cubits wide, the middle one six, and the third seven. He made ledges around the outside of the house so the beams would not be inserted into its walls.
</p>

<p>
v07: The house was built with stone finished at the quarry. No hammer, axe, or other iron tool was heard in the house while it was being built.
v08: The entrance to the middle side story was on the right side of the house. Winding stairs led up to the middle story and from there to the third.
v09: He built the house and finished it, roofing it with cedar beams and boards.
v10: He built the side structure around the whole house, each story five cubits high, and attached it to the house with cedar timbers.
</p>

## The LORD’s promise

<p>
v11: The word of the LORD came to Solomon:
v12: “As for this house you are building: if you walk in my statutes, carry out my judgments, and keep all my commands by following them, I will fulfill with you the promise I made to your father David.
v13: I will dwell among the Israelites and will not abandon my people Israel.”
</p>

## The inner rooms and their decoration

<p>
v14: So Solomon built the house and finished it.
v15: He lined the inside walls with cedar boards, covering them with wood from the floor to the ceiling, and laid cypress boards over the floor.
v16: He partitioned off twenty cubits at the rear of the house with cedar boards from the floor up to the walls. Inside, he made this the inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place.
v17: The main hall in front of it was forty cubits long.
v18: The cedar inside the house was carved with gourds and open flowers. Everything was cedar; no stone could be seen.
</p>

<p>
v19: He prepared the inner sanctuary deep within the house to hold the ark of the LORD’s covenant.
v20: The inner sanctuary was twenty cubits long, twenty wide, and twenty high. He overlaid it with pure gold and covered the altar with cedar.
v21: Solomon overlaid the inside of the house with pure gold. He stretched gold chains across the front of the inner sanctuary and overlaid it with gold.
v22: He covered the entire house with gold, until the whole house was finished. He also covered the entire altar belonging to the inner sanctuary with gold.
</p>

<p>
v23: In the inner sanctuary he made two cherubim of olive wood, each ten cubits high.
v24: One wing of the first cherub was five cubits long, and the other was five cubits: ten cubits from wingtip to wingtip.
v25: The second cherub also measured ten cubits. Both cherubim had the same dimensions and shape.
v26: The first cherub was ten cubits high, and so was the second.
</p>

<p>
v27: He placed the cherubim inside the innermost room. Their wings were spread out: one cherub’s wing touched one wall, the other’s wing touched the opposite wall, and their wings met in the middle of the house.
v28: He overlaid the cherubim with gold.
</p>

<p>
v29: All around the house’s walls, in both the inner and outer rooms, he carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers.
v30: He covered the floor with gold in both the inner and outer rooms.
</p>

<p>
v31: For the entrance to the inner sanctuary he made olive-wood doors, with a five-sided doorframe.
v32: On the two olive-wood doors he carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers. He overlaid the doors with gold, spreading it over the cherubim and palm trees.
v33: For the entrance to the main hall he likewise made olive-wood doorposts, with a four-sided frame,
v34: and two cypress doors. Each door had two folding panels.
v35: He carved cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers on them and covered them with gold fitted to the carvings.
</p>

<p>
v36: He built the inner court with three courses of dressed stone and one course of cedar beams.
v37: The foundation of the LORD’s house was laid in the fourth year, in the month of Ziv.
v38: In the eleventh year, in Bul, the eighth month, the house was finished in all its details and according to all its specifications. He spent seven years building it.
</p>

## Notes

v04: The compressed description of the windows is uncertain. “Recessed frames” is an architectural interpretation, not a recoverable construction drawing.

v08: The pinned Hebrew calls this the entrance to the middle story, then describes going up to the middle story. Both references are retained.

v16: The Hebrew here reads “from the floor up to the walls.” Unlike verse 15, it does not explicitly name the ceiling.

v31–33: “Five-sided” and “four-sided” interpret the numbered doorframes. The phrases can also be taken as proportions of an opening; their precise architecture is uncertain.

v37–38: The dates run from Ziv in Solomon’s fourth year to Bul in his eleventh. The narrative itself describes the building as taking seven years.

## Vocabulary

v02: Cubit
A length based on the forearm. The original measurements stay visible so readers can compare the building’s proportions.

v16: Most Holy Place
The innermost sanctuary, where the ark would be placed.

v23: Cherubim
Winged beings represented here by large wooden figures covered with gold; the two figures’ wings reach across the sanctuary.
