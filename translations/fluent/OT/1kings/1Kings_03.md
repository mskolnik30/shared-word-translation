## Solomon asks for wisdom

<p>
v01: Solomon made a marriage alliance with Pharaoh king of Egypt. He married Pharaoh’s daughter and brought her to the City of David until he finished building his own house, the LORD’s house, and the wall around Jerusalem.
v02: The people were still sacrificing at the high places, because no house for the LORD’s name had yet been built.
</p>

<p>
v03: Solomon loved the LORD and followed the statutes of his father David. Still, he sacrificed and burned offerings at the high places.
v04: The king went to Gibeon to sacrifice, because it was the great high place. Solomon offered a thousand burnt offerings on that altar.
</p>

<p>
v05: At Gibeon the LORD appeared to Solomon in a dream at night. God said, “Ask what I should give you.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Solomon answered, “You showed great steadfast love to your servant, my father David, because he walked before you in faithfulness, righteousness, and an upright heart toward you. You have kept this great steadfast love for him by giving him a son to sit on his throne today.
v07: Now, LORD my God, you have made your servant king in place of my father David. But I am only a young boy; I do not know how to go out or come in.
v08: Your servant stands among the people you chose, a people so numerous they cannot be counted or numbered.
v09: Give your servant a listening heart to judge your people and distinguish good from evil. For who can judge this great people of yours?”
v10: The request pleased the Lord: this was what Solomon had asked for.
</p>

<p>
v11: God said to him, “Because you asked for this, and did not ask for a long life, riches, or the lives of your enemies, but asked for discernment to hear a case justly,
v12: I will do as you asked. I give you a wise and discerning heart. No one like you has come before you, and no one like you will arise after you.
v13: I also give you what you did not ask for: both riches and honor. Among the kings, no one will be like you throughout your life.
v14: And if you walk in my ways, keeping my statutes and commands as your father David did, I will lengthen your life.”
</p>

<p>
v15: Solomon woke up. It had been a dream. He went to Jerusalem and stood before the ark of the Lord’s covenant. He offered burnt offerings and fellowship offerings, then gave a feast for all his servants.
</p>

## Two women and one living child

<p>
v16: Then two women who were prostitutes came to the king and stood before him.
v17: One woman said, “Please, my lord, this woman and I live in the same house. I gave birth while she was there with me.
v18: On the third day after I gave birth, she gave birth too. We were alone; no one else was in the house. Just the two of us.
</p>

<p>
v19: During the night this woman’s son died because she lay on him.
v20: She got up in the middle of the night and took my son from beside me while your servant was asleep. She laid him at her breast and laid her dead son at mine.
v21: When I got up in the morning to nurse my son, he was dead! But when I looked closely at him in the morning, he was not the son I had borne.”
</p>

<p>
v22: The other woman said, “No! The living son is mine, and the dead one is yours.” The first said, “No! The dead son is yours, and the living one is mine.” So they argued before the king.
v23: The king said, “This one says, ‘The living son is mine, and the dead one is yours.’ And that one says, ‘No! The dead son is yours, and the living one is mine.’”
</p>

<p>
v24: Then the king said, “Bring me a sword.” They brought a sword before the king.
v25: “Cut the living child in two,” the king said. “Give half to one and half to the other.”
</p>

<p>
v26: The woman whose son was alive was overwhelmed with compassion for him. She said to the king, “Please, my lord, give her the living child! Whatever you do, do not kill him!” But the other said, “He will belong to neither of us. Cut him in two.”
v27: The king answered, “Give the living child to the first woman. Do not kill him. She is his mother.”
</p>

<p>
v28: All Israel heard about the judgment the king had given. They stood in awe of him, because they saw that God’s wisdom was in him to do justice.
</p>

## Notes

v07: “A young boy” is Solomon’s self-description. “Go out or come in” can describe conducting one’s responsibilities; it does not supply his age.

v09: A “listening heart” joins attention, discernment, and judgment. In biblical Hebrew the heart includes thought and decision, not only feeling.

v19: The account that the other woman lay on her son is the first woman’s testimony. It is not a separate statement by the narrator.

v27: “The first woman” identifies the woman who asked that the living child be spared in verse 26; the Hebrew says “her.”

## Vocabulary

v02: High places
Worship sites, often on heights. The account distinguishes the lack of a temple from its assessment of Solomon’s worship.

v15: Fellowship offerings
Sacrifices associated with shared well-being and a meal; the Hebrew term is related to shalom, peace or wholeness.
