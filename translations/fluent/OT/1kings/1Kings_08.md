</p>

</p>

<p>
v06: Then the priests brought the ark of the covenant of the LORD into its place, into the inner sanctuary of the house, the most holy place, beneath the wings of the cherubim.
</p>

v07: For the cherubim spread out their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubim covered the ark and its poles from above.
</p>

<p>
v08: The poles were so long that the ends of the poles could be seen from the holy place before the inner sanctuary, but they could not be seen outside; and they are there to this day.
</p>

v09: There was nothing in the ark except the two stone tablets that Moses had placed there at Horeb, where the LORD made a covenant with the people of Israel when they came out from the land of Egypt.
</p>

v10: When the priests came out from the holy place, the cloud filled the house of the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v11: The priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the glory of the LORD filled the house of the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v12: Then Solomon said,
“The LORD said that he would dwell in deep darkness.
</p>

<p>
v13: I have indeed built you an exalted house,
a settled place for you to dwell forever.”
</p>

<p>
v14: Then the king turned around and blessed the whole assembly of Israel, while the whole assembly of Israel stood.
</p>

<p>
v15: He said, “Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who spoke with his mouth to David my father and has fulfilled it by his hand, saying,
</p>

<p>
v16: ‘Since the day I brought my people Israel out of Egypt, I chose no city from among all the tribes of Israel in which to build a house for my name to be there; but I chose David to be over my people Israel.’
</p>

<p>
v17: “It was in the heart of David my father to build a house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.
</p>

<p>
v18: But the LORD said to David my father, ‘Because it was in your heart to build a house for my name, you did well that it was in your heart.
</p>

<p>

</p>

v20: “The LORD has fulfilled his word that he spoke. I have risen in place of David my father and sit on the throne of Israel, as the LORD spoke; and I have built the house for the name of the LORD, the God of Israel.
</p>

<p>
v21: There I have made a place for the ark, in which is the covenant of the LORD that he made with our ancestors when he brought them out from the land of Egypt.”
</p>

## Notes

v56: “Not one word has failed” literally says that not one word has “fallen.”

## Vocabulary

v01: אֲרוֹן (ʾaron)
“ark.” The ark is called “the ark of the covenant of the LORD.” Its movement from Zion into the temple centers the dedication on the covenant rather than on the building alone.

v23: חֶסֶד (ḥesed)
“steadfast love.” This word describes God’s enduring covenant faithfulness toward servants who walk before God wholeheartedly.

v27: שְׁמֵי הַשָּׁמַיִם (šĕmê haššāmayim)
“the highest heaven.” Literally “heaven of the heavens,” the phrase heightens Solomon’s confession that God exceeds every created space.

v30: סָלַח (sālaḥ)
“forgive.” Repeated throughout the prayer, this verb frames forgiveness as God’s response to confession, repentance, and pleading.

v39: לֵבָב (lēvav)
“heart.” In this chapter the heart is the inner center of intention, knowledge, loyalty, and moral response. God alone knows the heart fully.
