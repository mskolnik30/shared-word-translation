## The ark enters the temple

<p>
v01: Then Solomon assembled Israel’s elders, all the tribal heads and leaders of the ancestral families of the Israelites, before King Solomon in Jerusalem. They were to bring up the ark of the LORD’s covenant from the City of David, which is Zion.
v02: All the men of Israel gathered before King Solomon at the festival in Ethanim, the seventh month.
v03: When all Israel’s elders had arrived, the priests lifted the ark.
v04: They brought up the LORD’s ark, the tent of meeting, and all the holy utensils in the tent. The priests and Levites brought them up.
v05: King Solomon and the whole assembly of Israel gathered with him stood before the ark, sacrificing so many sheep and cattle that they could not be counted or numbered.
</p>

<p>
v06: The priests brought the ark of the LORD’s covenant to its place in the house’s inner sanctuary, the Most Holy Place, beneath the cherubim’s wings.
v07: The cherubim spread their wings over the ark’s place, sheltering the ark and its poles from above.
v08: The poles were so long that their ends could be seen from the Holy Place in front of the inner sanctuary, but not from outside. They are there to this day.
</p>

<p>
v09: Nothing was in the ark except the two stone tablets Moses had placed there at Horeb, where the LORD made a covenant with the Israelites when they left Egypt.
v10: When the priests came out of the Holy Place, the cloud filled the LORD’s house.
v11: The priests could not stand to minister because of the cloud, for the LORD’s glory filled the LORD’s house.
</p>

## Solomon blesses the assembly

<p>
v12: Then Solomon said, “The LORD said he would dwell in thick darkness.
v13: I have indeed built you a lofty house,
a settled place for you to dwell forever.”
</p>

<p>
v14: The king turned and blessed the whole assembly of Israel while they stood.
v15: He said, “Blessed be the LORD, Israel’s God! With his own mouth he spoke to my father David, and with his own hand he fulfilled it. He said:
v16: ‘From the day I brought my people Israel out of Egypt, I chose no city among all Israel’s tribes in which to build a house for my name. But I chose David to be over my people Israel.’
</p>

<p>
v17: My father David had it in his heart to build a house for the name of the LORD, Israel’s God.
v18: But the LORD told my father David, ‘You did well to have it in your heart to build a house for my name.
v19: Still, you will not build the house. Your son, who comes from your own body, will build the house for my name.’
</p>

<p>
v20: The LORD has fulfilled the word he spoke. I have risen in my father David’s place and sit on Israel’s throne, as the LORD promised. I have built the house for the name of the LORD, Israel’s God,
v21: and made a place there for the ark containing the LORD’s covenant, which he made with our ancestors when he brought them out of Egypt.”
</p>

## Solomon’s prayer

<p>
v22: Solomon stood before the LORD’s altar in front of the whole assembly of Israel and spread his hands toward heaven.
v23: He said, “LORD, Israel’s God, there is no God like you in heaven above or on earth below. You keep covenant and steadfast love with your servants who walk before you with all their heart.
v24: You kept what you promised your servant, my father David. Your mouth spoke it; your hand fulfilled it, as we see today.
</p>

<p>
v25: Now, LORD, Israel’s God, keep what you promised your servant, my father David: ‘You will never lack a man to sit before me on Israel’s throne, if only your sons watch their ways and walk before me as you have walked before me.’
v26: And now, God of Israel, please confirm the word you spoke to your servant, my father David.
</p>

<p>
v27: “But will God really dwell on earth? Heaven, even the highest heavens, cannot contain you—how much less this house I have built!
v28: Yet turn toward your servant’s prayer and plea, LORD my God. Listen to the cry and prayer your servant offers before you today.
v29: May your eyes be open toward this house night and day, toward the place where you said, ‘My name will be there.’ Hear the prayer your servant prays toward this place.
v30: Hear the plea of your servant and your people Israel when they pray toward this place. Hear from heaven, your dwelling place; hear and forgive.
</p>

<p>
v31: “When someone sins against a neighbor and is required to take an oath under a curse, and the oath comes before your altar in this house,
v32: hear in heaven, act, and judge your servants. Condemn the guilty, bringing their conduct back on their own heads, and vindicate the righteous, giving them what their righteousness deserves.
</p>

<p>
v33: “When your people Israel are defeated by an enemy because they have sinned against you, and they turn back to you, acknowledge your name, and pray and plead with you in this house,
v34: hear in heaven and forgive the sin of your people Israel. Bring them back to the land you gave their ancestors.
</p>

<p>
v35: “When heaven is shut and no rain falls because they have sinned against you, and they pray toward this place, acknowledge your name, and turn from their sin because you have afflicted them,
v36: hear in heaven and forgive the sin of your servants, your people Israel. Teach them the good way to walk, and send rain on the land you gave your people as an inheritance.
</p>

<p>
v37: “When famine or plague comes on the land, when there is blight, mildew, locusts or devouring locusts, when an enemy besieges them in the land at their gates—whatever plague or sickness there may be—
v38: whatever prayer or plea is offered by any person or by all your people Israel, each knowing the affliction of their own heart and spreading their hands toward this house,
v39: hear in heaven, your dwelling place. Forgive and act. Give to each according to all their ways, for you know their hearts—you alone know the hearts of all humanity—
v40: so they may fear you all the days they live on the land you gave our ancestors.
</p>

<p>
v41: “And as for the foreigner who does not belong to your people Israel but comes from a distant land because of your name—
v42: for people will hear of your great name, your strong hand, and your outstretched arm—when that foreigner comes and prays toward this house,
v43: hear in heaven, your dwelling place, and do all the foreigner asks of you. Then all the peoples of the earth may know your name and fear you as your people Israel do, and know that this house I have built bears your name.
</p>

<p>
v44: “When your people go to war against their enemy along the way you send them, and pray to the LORD toward the city you chose and the house I built for your name,
v45: hear their prayer and plea in heaven, and uphold their cause.
</p>

<p>
v46: “When they sin against you—for there is no one who does not sin—and you become angry with them and give them over to an enemy, so their captors take them to an enemy land, far away or near,
v47: and they come to their senses in the land where they are held captive, turn back, and plead with you there, saying, ‘We have sinned, done wrong, and acted wickedly,’
v48: and they return to you with all their heart and all their being in the land of the enemies who captured them, and pray to you toward their own land, which you gave their ancestors, toward the city you chose and the house I built for your name,
v49: hear their prayer and plea in heaven, your dwelling place, and uphold their cause.
v50: Forgive your people who sinned against you, and all their rebellions against you. Grant them compassion in the eyes of their captors, so their captors will show them compassion.
</p>

<p>
v51: For they are your people and your inheritance, whom you brought out of Egypt, out of the iron furnace.
v52: May your eyes be open to your servant’s plea and the plea of your people Israel. Listen whenever they call to you.
v53: For you set them apart as your inheritance from all the peoples of the earth, just as you said through your servant Moses when you brought our ancestors out of Egypt, Lord GOD.”
</p>

## Blessing and dedication

<p>
v54: When Solomon finished all this prayer and pleading to the LORD, he rose from before the LORD’s altar, where he had been kneeling with his hands spread toward heaven.
v55: He stood and blessed the whole assembly of Israel in a loud voice:
v56: “Blessed be the LORD, who has given rest to his people Israel just as he promised! Not one word has failed of all the good he promised through his servant Moses.
</p>

<p>
v57: May the LORD our God be with us as he was with our ancestors. May he never leave us or abandon us.
v58: May he turn our hearts toward him, so we walk in all his ways and keep the commands, statutes, and judgments he gave our ancestors.
</p>

<p>
v59: May these words I have pleaded before the LORD remain close to the LORD our God day and night. May he uphold his servant’s cause and his people Israel’s cause, each day as it requires,
v60: so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the LORD is God. There is no other.
v61: Let your heart be wholly with the LORD our God, walking in his statutes and keeping his commands, as you do today.”
</p>

<p>
v62: Then the king and all Israel with him offered sacrifices before the LORD.
v63: Solomon offered fellowship sacrifices to the LORD: twenty-two thousand cattle and a hundred twenty thousand sheep. So the king and all the Israelites dedicated the LORD’s house.
v64: That day the king consecrated the middle of the court in front of the LORD’s house. There he offered burnt offerings, grain offerings, and the fat of fellowship offerings, because the bronze altar before the LORD was too small to hold them all.
</p>

<p>
v65: At that time Solomon and all Israel with him celebrated the festival before the LORD our God—a great assembly from Lebo-hamath to the Wadi of Egypt—for seven days and another seven days, fourteen days in all.
v66: On the eighth day he sent the people away. They blessed the king and went home to their tents, joyful and glad of heart for all the good the LORD had done for his servant David and his people Israel.
</p>

## Notes

v09: This account says the ark contained only the two stone tablets. No other objects are added.

v12–13: Solomon’s short poetic declaration retains both thick darkness and a permanent dwelling. Verse 27 then asks how any house could contain God.

v31–32: The oath invokes a curse in a dispute. Solomon asks God to judge the guilty and the innocent; the translation keeps that legal setting.

v41–43: The prayer explicitly includes a foreigner from a distant land, so that all peoples may know the LORD’s name.

v46–50: The prayer anticipates wrongdoing, exile, return of heart, forgiveness, and compassion from captors. It does not explicitly promise release in verse 50.

v65–66: Verse 65 says seven days plus seven days, fourteen in all; verse 66 says “the eighth day.” Their relation is left as the account presents it.

## Vocabulary

v11: Glory
The LORD’s overwhelming presence, here filling the house in a cloud.

v21: Covenant
The binding relationship with Israel, represented here by the tablets in the ark.

v51: Iron furnace
An image for Egypt as a place of intense affliction.
