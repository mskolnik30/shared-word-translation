## The neglected house

<p>
v01: In the second year of King Darius, on the first day of the sixth month, the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet to Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, and to Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest:
</p>

<p>
v02: The LORD of Armies says, “These people say, ‘The time has not come—the time to rebuild the LORD’s house.’”
</p>

<p>
v03: Then the word of the LORD came through Haggai the prophet:
v04: “Is it time for you yourselves to live in paneled houses while this house lies in ruins?”
</p>

<p>
v05: Now the LORD of Armies says, “Think carefully about your ways.
v06: You sow much but bring in little. You eat but never have enough. You drink but are not filled. You put on clothes but are not warm. Those who earn wages put them into a bag full of holes.”
</p>

## Consider your ways

<p>
v07: The LORD of Armies says, “Think carefully about your ways.
v08: Go up into the hills, bring timber, and rebuild the house. Then I will take pleasure in it and be honored,” says the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v09: “You expected much, but look—there was little. You brought it home, and I blew it away. Why?” declares the LORD of Armies. “Because my house lies in ruins while each of you runs to his own house.
v10: That is why the heavens above you have withheld dew and the earth has withheld its produce.
v11: I called for drought on the land and the hills, on grain, new wine, and oil, on everything the ground produces, on humans and animals, and on all the work of your hands.”
</p>

## The people begin work

<p>
v12: Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Joshua son of Jehozadak the high priest, and all the remnant of the people listened to the voice of the LORD their God and to the words of Haggai the prophet, whom the LORD their God had sent. The people feared the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v13: Haggai, the LORD’s messenger, delivered the LORD’s message to the people: “I am with you,” declares the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v14: The LORD stirred the spirit of Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, governor of Judah, the spirit of Joshua son of Jehozadak, the high priest, and the spirit of all the remnant of the people. They came and worked on the house of the LORD of Armies, their God,
v15: on the twenty-fourth day of the sixth month, in the second year of King Darius.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The date names Darius’s second year, the sixth month, and its first day. Zerubbabel’s governorship and Joshua’s priesthood remain distinct.

v05–07: The repeated call to consider their ways links daily frustration with the neglected house; the repetition is intentional.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Governor**: Zerubbabel’s civil office, distinct from the high priest’s role.

v14: **Stirred the spirit**: An expression for awakening resolve to act.
