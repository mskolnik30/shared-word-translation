## The rebellious city

<p>
v01: Woe to the rebellious, polluted city,
the city that oppresses!
</p>

<p>
v02: She has not listened to a voice
or accepted correction.
She has not trusted the LORD
or drawn near to her God.
</p>

<p>
v03: Her officials within her are roaring lions;
her judges are wolves at dusk,
leaving nothing to gnaw by morning.
</p>

<p>
v04: Her prophets are reckless, treacherous men;
her priests profane what is holy
and do violence to instruction.
</p>

<p>
v05: The LORD within her is righteous;
he does no injustice.
Morning after morning he brings his judgment to light;
he never fails.
But the unjust know no shame.
</p>

<p>
v06: “I have cut off nations;
their corner towers are deserted.
I have laid waste their streets, with no one passing through.
Their cities are destroyed, without a person,
without an inhabitant.
</p>

<p>
v07: I said, ‘Surely you will fear me
and accept correction.’
Then her dwelling would not be cut off,
whatever I had appointed against her.
But they rose early to corrupt all their deeds.
</p>

## Judgment and a purified people

<p>
v08: So wait for me,” declares the LORD,
“for the day I rise to seize prey.
For my decision is to gather nations,
to assemble kingdoms,
to pour my indignation on them,
all my burning anger.
The whole earth will be consumed
by the fire of my jealousy.
</p>

<p>
v09: Then I will give the peoples purified lips,
so they may all call on the name of the LORD
and serve him shoulder to shoulder.
</p>

<p>
v10: From beyond the rivers of Cush,
my worshipers, my scattered people,
will bring my offering.
</p>

<p>
v11: On that day you will no longer be ashamed
of all the deeds by which you rebelled against me.
For then I will remove from your midst
those who revel in your pride;
you will no longer be arrogant on my holy mountain.
</p>

<p>
v12: I will leave among you a people poor and lowly,
and they will take refuge in the name of the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v13: The remnant of Israel will do no injustice
and speak no lies;
no deceitful tongue will be found in their mouths.
They will graze and lie down,
with no one to terrify them.”
</p>

## Rejoice, daughter Zion

<p>
v14: Sing out, daughter Zion!
Raise a shout, Israel!
Rejoice and celebrate with all your heart,
daughter Jerusalem!
</p>

<p>
v15: The LORD has removed the judgments against you
and cleared your enemy away.
The king of Israel, the LORD, is among you;
you will no longer fear harm.
</p>

<p>
v16: On that day Jerusalem will be told:
“Do not be afraid, Zion;
do not let your hands fall limp.
</p>

<p>
v17: The LORD your God is among you,
a warrior who saves.
He will rejoice over you with delight;
he will be silent in his love;
he will celebrate over you with singing.”
</p>

<p>
v18: “I will gather those grieving over the appointed festival;
they were from you,
a burden of reproach upon her.
</p>

<p>
v19: Look, at that time I will deal with all who oppress you.
I will save the lame and gather the outcast.
I will give them praise and renown
in every land where they were shamed.
</p>

<p>
v20: At that time I will bring you in,
at the time I gather you.
I will give you renown and praise
among all the peoples of the earth
when I restore your fortunes before your eyes,”
says the LORD.
</p>

## Notes

v08: “Prey” follows the pinned vocalization; another interpretation of the same consonants is “witness.”

v10: “Daughter of my dispersed ones” is collective language, not a newly introduced individual woman.

v17: The Hebrew says he will be silent in his love. The draft retains this unusual expression rather than substituting “renew you.”

v18: This verse is textually and syntactically difficult. The draft retains festival grief, gathering, and the shifting pronouns; their exact connection remains uncertain.

## Vocabulary

v09: **Pure lip**: An image for cleansed speech, linked here to calling on the LORD together.

v14: **Daughter Zion**: Jerusalem addressed as a woman, with her people included in the address.
