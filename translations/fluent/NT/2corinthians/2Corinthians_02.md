## Paul’s Change of Plans

<p>
v01: So I decided this for myself: I would not come to you again in sorrow.
v02: For if I cause you sorrow, who is left to make me glad except the one I have caused sorrow?
v03: I wrote as I did so that when I came I would not be grieved by those who should have made me glad. I was confident about all of you, that my joy belongs to all of you.
</p>

<p>
v04: For I wrote to you through much affliction and anguish of heart, with many tears—not to cause you sorrow, but so that you might know the abundant love I have especially for you.
</p>

## Forgiveness for the Offender

<p>
v05: But if anyone has caused sorrow, he has not caused sorrow to me alone, but—to avoid overstating it—to some extent to all of you.
v06: The punishment imposed by the majority is enough for such a person.
v07: So now, instead, you should forgive and comfort him, so that he is not overwhelmed by excessive sorrow.
</p>

<p>
v08: Therefore I urge you to reaffirm your love for him.
v09: This is also why I wrote: to learn by testing whether you are obedient in everything.
v10: Anyone you forgive, I also forgive. Indeed, whatever I have forgiven—if I had anything to forgive—I have forgiven for your sake in the presence of Christ,
v11: so that Satan might not take advantage of us, for we are not unaware of his schemes.
</p>

## Paul’s Anxiety at Troas

<p>
v12: When I came to Troas for the gospel of Christ and a door was opened for me in the Lord,
v13: I had no relief in my spirit because I did not find my brother Titus there. So I said goodbye to them and left for Macedonia.
</p>

## Triumph in Christ

<p>
v14: But thanks be to God, who always leads us in Christ's triumphal procession and through us spreads everywhere the fragrance of knowing him.
v15: For to God we are Christ's fragrance among those being saved and among those perishing:
v16: to some, an aroma from death leading to death; to others, an aroma from life leading to life. And who is sufficient for these things?
</p>

<p>
v17: For we are not like the many who peddle God's word. Instead, we speak in Christ with sincerity, as people sent from God and standing before God.
</p>

## Notes

v05–11: After a majority punishment, Paul calls the community to forgiveness, comfort, and reaffirmed love so the person is not overwhelmed by grief.

v14: The triumphal procession can include captives being led; the image is not reduced to a generic victory parade.

v17: The commercial verb depicts people who peddle or adulterate God's word for gain.

## Vocabulary

v07: **καταπίνω (katapinō)**: Swallow up, overwhelm, or devour.

v14: **θριαμβεύω (thriambeuō)**: Lead in a triumphal procession.
