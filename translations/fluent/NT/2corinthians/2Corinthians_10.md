## Paul Defends His Ministry

<p>
v01: Now I, Paul, personally appeal to you by Christ's gentleness and graciousness—I who am “humble” when face-to-face with you, but “bold” toward you when absent.
v02: I ask that, when present, I will not have to be bold with the confidence I expect to show against some who think we walk according to the flesh.
</p>

<p>
v03: For though we walk in the flesh, we do not wage war according to the flesh.
v04: For the weapons of our warfare are not fleshly but powerful through God for tearing down strongholds. We tear down arguments
v05: and every proud obstacle raised against the knowledge of God, and we take every thought captive to obey Christ.
v06: And we are ready to punish every disobedience once your obedience is complete.
</p>

<p>
v07: Look at what is right in front of you. If anyone is confident that he belongs to Christ, he should consider this again about himself: just as he belongs to Christ, so do we.
v08: For even if I boast somewhat more about our authority, which the Lord gave for building you up and not tearing you down, I will not be ashamed.
</p>

<p>
v09: I do not want to seem as though I am trying to frighten you through my letters.
v10: For someone says, “His letters are weighty and forceful, but his bodily presence is weak and his speech amounts to nothing.”
v11: Such a person should consider this: what we are in words through letters when absent, we will also be in action when present.
</p>

<p>
v12: For we do not dare to place or compare ourselves with some who recommend themselves. But when they measure themselves by themselves and compare themselves with themselves, they lack understanding.
</p>

<p>
v13: We, however, will not boast beyond measure, but only according to the boundary God assigned to us—a boundary that extends even as far as you.
v14: For we are not overextending ourselves, as if we had not reached you, because we did reach as far as you with Christ's gospel.
</p>

<p>
v15: We do not boast beyond measure in the labors of others. But we hope that as your faith grows, our work among you will be greatly enlarged within our boundary,
v16: so that we may proclaim the gospel in the regions beyond you, without boasting about work already prepared in someone else's field.
</p>

<p>
v17: “Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord.”
v18: For it is not the one who recommends himself who is approved, but the one whom the Lord recommends.
</p>

## Notes

v01–2: Paul quotes or echoes charges that he is timid in person and bold at a distance before answering them.

v03–6: The military imagery targets arguments, proud obstacles, and disobedience in this dispute; it is not a mandate for physical violence.

v08: Paul says the Lord gave authority for building up the community and not for tearing it down.

## Vocabulary

v04: **ὀχύρωμα (ochyrōma)**: Stronghold or fortified place.

v13: **κανών (kanōn)**: Measured boundary, field, or rule.
