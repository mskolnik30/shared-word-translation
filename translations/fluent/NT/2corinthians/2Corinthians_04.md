## Treasure in Clay Jars

<p>
v01: Therefore, since we have this ministry because we received mercy, we do not lose heart.
v02: Instead, we have renounced secret, shameful things. We do not walk in deceit or distort God's word, but by openly presenting the truth we commend ourselves to every human conscience before God.
</p>

<p>
v03: Even if our gospel is veiled, it is veiled among those who are perishing.
v04: In their case, the god of this age has blinded the minds of unbelievers so that the light of the gospel of Christ's glory, who is God's image, would not shine on them.
</p>

<p>
v05: For we do not proclaim ourselves, but Jesus Christ as Lord, and ourselves as your servants because of Jesus.
v06: For the God who said, “Light will shine out of darkness,” is the one who shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of God's glory in the face of Jesus Christ.
</p>

<p>
v07: But we have this treasure in clay jars so that the extraordinary power may be God's and not from us.
v08: We are afflicted in every way but not crushed, perplexed but not driven to despair,
v09: persecuted but not abandoned, struck down but not destroyed.
v10: We always carry around in the body the death of Jesus, so that the life of Jesus may also be revealed in our body.
</p>

<p>
v11: For we who live are continually being handed over to death because of Jesus, so that Jesus' life may also be revealed in our mortal flesh.
v12: So death is at work in us, but life in you.
</p>

## Living by Faith

<p>
v13: But since we have the same spirit of faith, according to what is written, “I believed; therefore I spoke,” we also believe, and therefore we speak.
v14: We know that the one who raised the Lord Jesus will also raise us with Jesus and present us together with you.
</p>

<p>
v15: For all this is for your sake, so that grace, as it extends through more and more people, may cause thanksgiving to overflow to God's glory.
</p>

<p>
v16: Therefore we do not lose heart. Even though our outer person is wasting away, our inner person is being renewed day by day.
v17: For our momentary light affliction is producing for us an eternal weight of glory beyond all measure,
v18: as we look not at what is seen but at what is unseen. For what is seen is temporary, but what is unseen is eternal.
</p>

## Notes

v07: The clay-jar image holds human fragility together with power that belongs to God.

v08–12: Affliction, persecution, striking down, and exposure to death are translated as real harms, not merely inward states.

v17: Calling affliction ‘light’ is Paul's contrast with an immeasurable weight of glory; it should not be used to minimize another person's suffering.

## Vocabulary

v07: **ὀστράκινος (ostrakinos)**: Made of earthenware or clay.

v17: **βάρος (baros)**: Weight, burden, or heaviness.
