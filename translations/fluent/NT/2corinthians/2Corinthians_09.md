## The Collection and Readiness

<p>
v01: Now concerning the service for the saints, it is unnecessary for me to write to you.
v02: For I know your eagerness, which I boast about to the Macedonians: “Achaia has been ready since last year.” And your zeal has stirred up most of them.
</p>

<p>
v03: But I sent the brothers so that our boasting about you in this matter would not prove empty, but that you would be ready, just as I said.
v04: Otherwise, if Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we—to say nothing of you—would be ashamed in this confidence.
</p>

<p>
v05: So I thought it necessary to urge the brothers to go to you in advance and prepare beforehand the generous gift you had already promised, so that it would be ready as a gift and not as something extorted.
</p>

## The Measure of Generosity

<p>
v06: The point is this: the one who sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and the one who sows with blessings will also reap with blessings.
v07: Each person should give as decided in the heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.
</p>

<p>
v08: And God is able to make every grace overflow to you, so that in everything, always having everything you need, you may overflow in every good work.
v09: As it is written, “He scattered; he gave to the poor. His righteousness remains forever.”
</p>

<p>
v10: The one who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will supply and multiply your seed and increase the harvest of your righteousness.
v11: You will be enriched in every way for every kind of generosity, which through us produces thanksgiving to God.
</p>

## Thanksgiving to God

<p>
v12: For the ministry of this service not only supplies what the saints lack, but also overflows in many thanksgivings to God.
v13: Because this ministry has proved your character, they will glorify God for the obedience of your confession of Christ's gospel and for the generosity of your sharing with them and with everyone.
</p>

<p>
v14: And in their prayer for you they will long for you because of God's surpassing grace upon you.
v15: Thanks be to God for his indescribable gift!
</p>

## Notes

v05–7: The gift is prepared in advance so it is generous rather than extracted; each person decides in the heart, not under pressure.

v06: ‘With blessings’ can describe generous sowing and a resulting harvest of blessing.

v08: The repeated forms of ‘all’ and ‘every’ emphasize sufficiency for good work rather than luxury for its own sake.

## Vocabulary

v05: **πλεονεξία (pleonexia)**: Greed, exploitation, or something extracted.

v07: **ἱλαρός (hilaros)**: Cheerful or glad.
