## Final Warnings

<p>
v01: This will be my third visit to you. “Every matter must be established by the testimony of two or three witnesses.”
v02: I warned those who sinned earlier and everyone else, and now I warn them in advance while absent, just as I did when present the second time: if I come again, I will not spare them,
v03: since you seek proof that Christ is speaking in me. He is not weak toward you, but is powerful among you.
v04: For he was crucified in weakness, yet he lives by God's power. For we too are weak in him, yet we will live with him by God's power in our dealings with you.
</p>

<p>
v05: Examine yourselves to see whether you are in the faith; test yourselves. Or do you not recognize about yourselves that Jesus Christ is among you—unless, indeed, you fail the test?
v06: But I hope you will recognize that we have not failed the test.
v07: Now we pray to God that you do nothing wrong—not so that we may appear to have passed the test, but so that you may do what is right, even if we appear to have failed.
v08: For we cannot do anything against the truth, but only for the truth.
v09: For we rejoice whenever we are weak but you are strong. We also pray for this: your restoration.
v10: This is why I write these things while absent, so that when present I will not have to act harshly according to the authority the Lord gave me for building up and not tearing down.
</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>
v11: Finally, brothers and sisters, rejoice. Be restored, be encouraged, think in harmony, live in peace, and the God of love and peace will be with you.
v12: Greet one another with a holy kiss.
v13: All the saints greet you.
</p>

<p>
v14: The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with all of you.
</p>

## Notes

v01–2: Paul invokes the legal rule of two or three witnesses while warning that his third visit will not spare persistent wrongdoing.

v05: The commands are plural and directed to the whole community: they are to examine and test themselves.

v07–10: Paul values the Corinthians doing good and being restored above appearing personally successful; his authority is for building up.

## Vocabulary

v05: **δοκιμάζω (dokimazō)**: Test, examine, or prove genuine.

v09: **κατάρτισις (katartisis)**: Restoration, mending, or full preparation.
