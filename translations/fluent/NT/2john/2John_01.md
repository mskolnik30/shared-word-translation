## Greeting in Truth and Love

<p>
v01: The elder, to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth—and not I alone, but also everyone who knows the truth—
v02: because of the truth that remains in us and will be with us forever.
v03: Grace, mercy and peace will be with us from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Father's Son, in truth and love.
</p>

## Walk in Truth and Love

<p>
v04: I rejoiced greatly to find some of your children walking in truth, just as we received a command from the Father.
v05: And now I ask you, lady—not as though writing you a new command, but the one we have had from the beginning—that we love one another.
v06: And this is love: that we walk according to his commands. This is the command, just as you heard from the beginning: that you walk in it.
</p>

## Watch for Deceivers

<p>
v07: For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess Jesus Christ coming in the flesh. Such a person is the deceiver and the antichrist.
v08: Watch yourselves, so that you do not lose what we have worked for, but receive a full reward.
v09: Everyone who goes ahead and does not remain in the teaching of Christ does not have God. Whoever remains in the teaching has both the Father and the Son.
v10: If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive that person into your house or offer a greeting,
v11: because whoever offers that person a greeting shares in their evil works.
</p>

## Face to Face

<p>
v12: I have much to write to you, but I do not want to do it with paper and ink. Instead, I hope to come to you and speak face to face, so that your joy may be complete.
v13: The children of your chosen sister greet you.
</p>

## Notes

v01,13: The chosen lady and her children may be a woman and her family or a church addressed as a woman; the chosen sister has the same ambiguity.

v07: “Coming in the flesh” retains the present participle. It may characterize Christ’s embodied coming without specifying a separate future event.

v08: The pinned Greek reads “you” losing what “we” worked for. Other witnesses vary these pronouns.

v09: “Teaching of Christ” may mean teaching from Christ or teaching about him.

v12: The pinned reading is “your joy”; other witnesses read “our joy.”

## Vocabulary

v09: **προάγω (proagō)**: Go ahead or advance.

v12: **στόμα πρὸς στόμα**: Literally “mouth to mouth,” rendered face to face.
