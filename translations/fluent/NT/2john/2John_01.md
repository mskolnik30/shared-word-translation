## Greeting in Truth and Love

<p>
v01: The elder, to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth—and not I alone, but also everyone who knows the truth—
v02: because of the truth that remains in us and will be with us forever.
v03: Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us from God the Father and from Jesus Christ, the Father’s Son, in truth and love.
</p>

## Walk in Truth and Love

<p>
v04: I rejoiced greatly because I found some of your children walking in truth, just as we received a command from the Father.
v05: And now I ask you, lady—not as though writing a new command to you, but one we have had from the beginning—that we love one another.
v06: And this is love: that we walk according to his commands. This is the command, just as you heard from the beginning, that you should walk in it.
</p>

## Watch for Deceivers

<p>
v07: For many deceivers have gone out into the world, people who do not confess Jesus Christ coming in flesh. Such a person is the deceiver and the antichrist.
v08: Watch yourselves so that you do not lose what we have worked for but may receive a full reward.
v09: Everyone who goes beyond and does not remain in the teaching of Christ does not have God. The one who remains in the teaching has both the Father and the Son.
v10: If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive that person into your house or give them a greeting,
v11: because the one who gives such a greeting shares in that person’s evil works.
</p>

## Face to Face

<p>
v12: Although I have many things to write to you, I do not want to do so with paper and ink. Instead, I hope to come to you and speak face to face, so that our joy may be made complete.
v13: The children of your chosen sister greet you.
</p>

## Notes

v01: “The chosen lady and her children” may refer to a particular woman and family or, metaphorically, to a local church and its members. The closing reference to a “chosen sister” keeps the ecclesial reading plausible. Fluent does not decide the referent by paraphrase.

v01–03: Truth and love are not competing values. The letter introduces them as mutually defining realities.

v02: “Remain” continues the major Johannine `menō` network already established in John and 1 John.

v04: The elder says “some” of her children are walking in truth. The wording need not imply that others are apostate; it may simply refer to those he has encountered.

v05–06: The love command is not new in time; its continuing newness comes through practiced obedience and the community’s received beginning.

v07: The participle “coming in flesh” can describe the confession of Jesus Christ as the one who came and whose identity remains embodied. Fluent keeps the present participle rather than reducing it to a past-event formula.

v07: “Antichrist” is a theological label for deceiving christological opposition in the Johannine letters, not a prediction identifying one modern political figure.

v09: `proagō`, “goes beyond,” can sound like progress, but here it describes moving beyond the received teaching and thereby leaving it behind.

v10–11: House reception in the first-century mission context provided material support and public endorsement to traveling teachers. The command concerns participation in false teaching, not ordinary kindness to every non-Christian neighbor.

v12: The elder prefers embodied presence and face-to-face speech over written communication, even while using a letter to preserve truth and love.
