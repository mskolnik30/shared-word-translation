## Bear with the Weak

<p>
v01: Now we who are strong ought to bear with the weaknesses of those who are not strong and not please ourselves.
v02: Each of us should please our neighbor for that person's good, in order to build them up.
v03: For even Christ did not please himself, but as it is written: “The insults of those who insult you have fallen on me.”
v04: For whatever was written beforehand was written for our instruction, so that through endurance and through the encouragement of the Scriptures we might have hope.
v05: May the God of endurance and encouragement grant you to live in harmony with one another according to Christ Jesus,
v06: so that together, with one voice, you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
v07: Therefore, welcome one another, just as Christ also welcomed you, for God's glory.
</p>

## Christ Serves Israel and Brings Mercy to the Nations

<p>
v08: For I say that Christ became a servant of the circumcised on behalf of God's truth, in order to confirm the promises given to the patriarchs,
v09: and so that the Gentiles might glorify God for his mercy, as it is written: “Therefore I will praise you among the Gentiles and sing praises to your name.”
v10: And again it says, “Rejoice, Gentiles, with his people.”
v11: And again, “Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles, and let all the peoples praise him.”
v12: And again Isaiah says, “The root of Jesse will come, the one who rises to rule the Gentiles; in him the Gentiles will hope.”
v13: May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace as you believe, so that you may overflow with hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.
</p>

## Paul’s Priest-like Ministry of the Gospel

<p>
v14: I myself am convinced about you, my brothers and sisters, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, and able to instruct one another.
v15: Yet I have written to you rather boldly on some points, to remind you again, because of the grace given to me by God
v16: to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, performing the priestly service of God's good news, so that the Gentiles might become an acceptable offering, made holy by the Holy Spirit.
v17: Therefore, in Christ Jesus I have reason to boast about my service to God.
v18: For I will not dare to speak of anything except what Christ accomplished through me to bring the Gentiles to obedience, by word and deed,
v19: by the power of signs and wonders, by the power of the Spirit. Thus, from Jerusalem and all the way around to Illyricum, I have fully proclaimed the good news of Christ.
v20: And so I make it my ambition to proclaim the good news where Christ has not been named, so that I will not build on someone else's foundation,
v21: but as it is written: “Those who were not told about him will see, and those who have not heard will understand.”
</p>

## Paul Plans to Visit Rome

<p>
v22: This is also why I have often been hindered from coming to you.
v23: But now, since I no longer have a place for work in these regions and have longed for many years to come to you,
v24: I hope to see you when I travel to Spain and to be sent on my way there by you, after I have first enjoyed your company for a while.
v25: But now I am going to Jerusalem to serve the saints.
v26: For Macedonia and Achaia were pleased to make a contribution for the poor among the saints in Jerusalem.
v27: They were pleased to do so, and indeed they are indebted to them. For if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual blessings, they also owe it to them to serve them with material blessings.
v28: Therefore, after I have completed this task and securely delivered this fruit to them, I will leave for Spain by way of you.
v29: And I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of Christ's blessing.
</p>

<p>
v30: Now I urge you, brothers and sisters, through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Spirit, to struggle together with me in prayers to God on my behalf,
v31: that I may be rescued from those in Judea who are disobedient, and that my service for Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints,
v32: so that by God's will I may come to you with joy and be refreshed together with you.
v33: May the God of peace be with all of you. Amen.
</p>

## Notes

v01–7: The strong are told to bear the weaknesses of others and follow Christ's self-denial; welcome is mutual rather than assimilation into one group's preferences.

v16: Paul uses priestly language for his Gentile mission: he performs sacred service so that the Gentiles themselves become an offering made holy by the Spirit.

v24: Spain is Paul's intended destination, with Rome as a visit and support point; the verse does not say the journey was completed.

v27: The material contribution is described both as voluntary delight and as a debt arising from shared spiritual blessings.

v31: Paul names danger from disobedient people in Judea and uncertainty about whether the Jerusalem saints will accept his service.

## Vocabulary

v01: **βαστάζω (bastazō)**: Bear, carry, or support a burden.

v16: **ἱερουργέω (hierourgeō)**: Perform sacred or priestly service.

v26: **κοινωνία (koinōnia)**: Sharing, participation, fellowship, or contribution.
