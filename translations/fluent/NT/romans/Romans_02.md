## God’s Impartial Judgment

<p>
v01: Therefore you are without excuse, whoever you are who judges. For in judging someone else, you condemn yourself, because you who judge practice the same things.
v02: We know that God's judgment rightly falls on those who practice such things.
v03: Do you suppose, you who judge those who practice such things while doing them yourself, that you will escape God's judgment?
v04: Or do you despise the riches of his kindness, restraint, and patience, not realizing that God's kindness is leading you to repentance?
v05: But because of your hardness and unrepentant heart, you are storing up wrath for yourself on the day of wrath, when God's righteous judgment is revealed.
v06: He will repay each person according to their works:
v07: eternal life to those who, by perseverance in doing good, seek glory, honor, and immortality;
v08: but wrath and fury for those who are self-seeking, disobey the truth, and obey injustice.
v09: There will be affliction and distress for every human being who does evil—for the Jewish person first and also for the Greek—
v10: but glory, honor, and peace for everyone who does good—for the Jewish person first and also for the Greek.
v11: For God shows no partiality.
</p>

## Hearers and Doers of the Law

<p>
v12: All who sinned without the Law will also perish without the Law, and all who sinned under the Law will be judged by the Law.
v13: For it is not the hearers of the Law who are righteous before God, but the doers of the Law who will be declared righteous.
v14: For whenever Gentiles who do not have the Law do instinctively what the Law requires, these people, though not having the Law, are a law to themselves.
v15: They show that the work required by the Law is written on their hearts, while their conscience bears witness and their competing thoughts accuse or even defend them.
v16: This will be evident on the day when, according to my good news, God judges people's secrets through Christ Jesus.
</p>

## Possessing the Law Is Not Enough

<p>
v17: But if you call yourself Jewish, rely on the Law, boast in God,
v18: know his will, and approve what is excellent because you are instructed from the Law;
v19: if you are confident that you yourself are a guide for the blind, a light for those in darkness,
v20: an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of infants, possessing in the Law the embodiment of knowledge and truth—
v21: you, then, who teach someone else, do you not teach yourself? You who preach against stealing, do you steal?
v22: You who say people must not commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who detest idols, do you rob temples?
v23: You who boast in the Law, do you dishonor God by breaking the Law?
v24: For, as it is written, “God's name is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you.”
</p>

## Circumcision of the Heart

<p>
v25: Circumcision is indeed beneficial if you practice the Law; but if you break the Law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.
v26: So if an uncircumcised person keeps the Law's righteous requirements, will not that person's uncircumcision be counted as circumcision?
v27: And the person who is physically uncircumcised yet fulfills the Law will judge you who, despite having the written code and circumcision, break the Law.
v28: For a person is not truly Jewish merely outwardly, nor is circumcision merely outward and physical.
v29: Rather, a person is Jewish inwardly, and circumcision is of the heart—by the Spirit, not by the written code. That person's praise comes not from people but from God.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The direct address turns the charge back on anyone who condemns others while practicing the same things.

v14–16: Gentiles without Torah are described as sometimes doing its requirements, with conscience and competing thoughts bearing witness.

v25–29: The argument contrasts merely outward circumcision with inward, Spirit-worked faithfulness; it does not erase Jewish identity or the Law’s prior value.

## Vocabulary

v04: **μετάνοια (metanoia)**: Repentance, a changed mind and direction.

v15: **συνείδησις (syneidēsis)**: Conscience or moral awareness.

v29: **πνεῦμα (pneuma)**: Spirit, wind, or breath.
