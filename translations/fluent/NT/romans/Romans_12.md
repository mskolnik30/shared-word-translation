## A Living Sacrifice

<p>
v01: Therefore, I urge you, brothers and sisters, by God's mercies, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God—this is your reasonable worship.
v02: Do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you may discern what God's will is—what is good, pleasing, and perfect.
</p>

## Many Members, One Body

<p>
v03: For by the grace given to me, I say to everyone among you: do not think of yourself more highly than you ought to think, but think with sound judgment, according to the measure of faith God has assigned to each person.
v04: For just as we have many members in one body, and all the members do not have the same function,
v05: so we who are many are one body in Christ, and individually we are members of one another.
v06: We have different gifts according to the grace given to us: if prophecy, use it according to the proportion of faith;
v07: if service, in serving; if one teaches, in teaching;
v08: if one encourages, in encouragement; the one who gives, with generosity; the one who leads, with diligence; the one who shows mercy, with cheerfulness.
</p>

## Love Without Hypocrisy

<p>
v09: Let love be without hypocrisy. Abhor what is evil; cling to what is good.
v10: Love one another deeply with family affection. Outdo one another in showing honor.
v11: Do not be lazy in diligence; be fervent in spirit; serve the Lord.
v12: Rejoice in hope; endure in affliction; persist in prayer.
v13: Share in the needs of the saints; pursue hospitality.
</p>

<p>
v14: Bless those who persecute you; bless and do not curse.
v15: Rejoice with those who rejoice; weep with those who weep.
v16: Live in harmony with one another. Do not set your minds on lofty things, but associate with the lowly. Do not become wise in your own estimation.
v17: Repay no one evil for evil. Give careful thought to what is honorable in the sight of everyone.
v18: If possible, as far as it depends on you, live at peace with everyone.
v19: Do not avenge yourselves, beloved, but leave room for God's wrath, for it is written: “Vengeance is mine; I will repay,” says the Lord.
v20: Rather, “If your enemy is hungry, feed him; if he is thirsty, give him something to drink. For by doing this you will heap burning coals on his head.”
v21: Do not be conquered by evil, but conquer evil with good.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The whole embodied life is pictured as a living sacrifice; the image is not reduced to inward devotion.

v16: ‘The lowly’ may refer to humble people or humble tasks. The main text leaves the referent open.

v19–21: The reader is forbidden personal vengeance and commanded to feed an enemy. The burning-coals image is retained without turning care into disguised retaliation.

## Vocabulary

v01: **λογικός (logikos)**: Reasonable, rational, or fittingly spiritual.

v02: **μεταμορφόω (metamorphoō)**: Transform in form or character.

v13: **φιλοξενία (philoxenia)**: Hospitality, literally love of strangers.
