## Governing Authorities

<p>
v01: Let every person submit to the governing authorities, for there is no authority except from God, and the authorities that exist have been appointed by God.
v02: Therefore, whoever resists the authority has opposed what God has appointed, and those who oppose it will bring judgment on themselves.
v03: For rulers are not a cause of fear for good conduct but for bad. Do you want to have no fear of the authority? Do what is good, and you will receive its praise,
v04: for it is God's servant for your good. But if you do what is bad, be afraid, for it does not carry the sword for nothing. It is God's servant, an avenger who brings wrath on the one who practices evil.
v05: Therefore, it is necessary to submit, not only because of wrath but also because of conscience.
v06: This is also why you pay taxes, for the authorities are God's public servants, devoting themselves to this very task.
v07: Pay everyone what is owed: tax to whom tax is due, revenue to whom revenue, respect to whom respect, honor to whom honor.
</p>

## Love Fulfills the Law

<p>
v08: Owe nothing to anyone except to love one another, for the one who loves another has fulfilled the law.
v09: For the commandments “Do not commit adultery,” “Do not murder,” “Do not steal,” “Do not covet,” and any other commandment are summed up in this word: “Love your neighbor as yourself.”
v10: Love does no harm to a neighbor. Therefore, love is the fulfillment of the law.
</p>

## The Day Has Drawn Near

<p>
v11: And do this because you know the time: the hour has already come for you to wake from sleep, for our salvation is nearer now than when we first believed.
v12: The night is nearly over, and the day has drawn near. So let us cast off the works of darkness and put on the weapons of light.
v13: Let us walk honorably as in the daytime, not in revelry and drunkenness, not in sexual immorality and debauchery, not in quarreling and jealousy.
v14: Instead, put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh to gratify its desires.
</p>

## Notes

v01–7: Paul's statements about governing authority, judgment, taxation, and the sword are translated as written. The main text does not insert a modern political theory or claim that every ruler's conduct is good.

v04: The sword represents real coercive and potentially lethal force; it is not softened to a vague symbol.

v08–10: Love fulfills the law by refusing harm to the neighbor; the continuing debt of love remains owed.

v12: ‘Weapons of light’ preserves the military term also translated ‘instruments’ in Romans 6.

## Vocabulary

v01: **ἐξουσία (exousia)**: Authority, ruling power, or right to act.

v04: **μάχαιρα (machaira)**: Sword or large knife.

v07: **φόρος (phoros)**: Direct tax or tribute.
