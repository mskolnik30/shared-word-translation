## Paul’s Desire for Israel’s Salvation

<p>
v01: Brothers and sisters, my heart's desire and my prayer to God for them is for their salvation.
v02: For I testify about them that they have zeal for God, but not according to true knowledge.
v03: For because they did not know God's righteousness and sought to establish their own, they did not submit to God's righteousness.
v04: For Christ is the culmination of the law for righteousness to everyone who believes.
</p>

## The Word Is Near You

<p>
v05: For Moses writes about the righteousness that comes from the law: “The person who does these things will live by them.”
v06: But the righteousness that comes from faith speaks this way: “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who will ascend into heaven?’”—that is, to bring Christ down—
v07: “or, ‘Who will descend into the abyss?’”—that is, to bring Christ up from the dead.
v08: But what does it say? “The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart.” This is the word of faith that we proclaim:
v09: If you confess with your mouth, “Jesus is Lord,” and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved.
v10: For with the heart one believes and is declared righteous, and with the mouth one confesses and is saved.
</p>

<p>
v11: For Scripture says, “Everyone who believes in him will not be put to shame.”
v12: For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek, because the same Lord is Lord of all and richly blesses all who call on him.
v13: For “everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.”
</p>

## Hearing and Proclamation

<p>
v14: How then can they call on one in whom they have not believed? And how can they believe in one whom they have not heard? And how can they hear without someone proclaiming?
v15: And how can they proclaim unless they are sent? As it is written: “How beautiful are the feet of those who announce good things!”
v16: But not everyone obeyed the good news. For Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our message?”
v17: So faith comes from hearing, and hearing through the word of Christ.
</p>

## Israel Has Heard

<p>
v18: But I ask: Did they not hear? Indeed they did: “Their voice has gone out to all the earth, and their words to the ends of the inhabited world.”
v19: But I ask: Did Israel not understand? First Moses says, “I will make you jealous by those who are not a nation; I will make you angry by a foolish nation.”
v20: And Isaiah is so bold as to say, “I was found by those who were not seeking me; I revealed myself to those who were not asking for me.”
v21: But concerning Israel he says, “All day long I stretched out my hands to a disobedient and contradicting people.”
</p>

## Notes

v04: τέλος can mean end, goal, result, or culmination. ‘Culmination’ preserves both fulfillment and decisive endpoint without claiming the law was pointless.

v09–13: Mouth and heart belong to one confession-and-belief description; the sequence is not presented as a mechanical formula.

v14: The Greek can ask how people believe in one ‘whom they have not heard,’ allowing Christ to be the heard speaker, not merely the topic of a message.

v17: The pinned source reads ‘the word of Christ’; some manuscripts read ‘the word of God.’

## Vocabulary

v04: **τέλος (telos)**: End, goal, outcome, or culmination.

v09: **ὁμολογέω (homologeō)**: Confess, acknowledge, or openly agree.

v15: **εὐαγγελίζω (euangelizō)**: Announce good news.
