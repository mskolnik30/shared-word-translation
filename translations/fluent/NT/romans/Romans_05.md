## Peace with God

<p>
v01: Therefore, since we have been declared righteous by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ.
v02: Through him we have also obtained access by faith into this grace in which we stand, and we boast in the hope of God's glory.
v03: And not only that, but we also boast in our afflictions, knowing that affliction produces perseverance,
v04: perseverance produces proven character, and proven character produces hope.
v05: And hope does not put us to shame, because God's love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit who was given to us.
</p>

<p>
v06: For while we were still powerless, at the appointed time Christ died for the ungodly.
v07: For rarely will someone die for a righteous person—though perhaps someone might even dare to die for a good person.
v08: But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.
v09: How much more, then, since we have now been declared righteous by his blood, will we be saved through him from wrath.
v10: For if, while we were enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of his Son, how much more, having been reconciled, will we be saved by his life.
v11: And not only that, but we also boast in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received reconciliation.
</p>

## Adam and Christ

<p>
v12: Therefore, just as sin entered the world through one man, and death through sin, so death spread to all people, because all sinned—
v13: for sin was in the world before the Law, but sin is not charged to one's account when there is no Law.
v14: Nevertheless, death reigned from Adam to Moses, even over those who did not sin in the likeness of Adam's transgression. Adam is a pattern of the one to come.
</p>

<p>
v15: But the gracious gift is not like the trespass. For if the many died through one man's trespass, how much more did God's grace and the gift in the grace of the one man, Jesus Christ, overflow to the many.
v16: Nor is the gift like the result of the one man's sin. The judgment from one trespass led to condemnation, but the gracious gift from many trespasses led to a verdict of righteousness.
v17: For if death reigned through the one man's trespass, how much more will those who receive the overflowing grace and gift of righteousness reign in life through the one man, Jesus Christ.
v18: So then, just as one trespass led to condemnation for all people, so also one righteous act led to justification and life for all people.
v19: For just as through the one man's disobedience the many were made sinners, so also through the one man's obedience the many will be made righteous.
</p>

<p>
v20: Now the Law came in alongside so that the trespass might increase. But where sin increased, grace overflowed all the more,
v21: so that, just as sin reigned in death, grace might also reign through righteousness, leading to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The pinned Greek reads ‘we have peace,’ an indicative statement. Some ancient witnesses support ‘let us have peace.’

v12: The final phrase is rendered ‘because all sinned.’ It has also been interpreted ‘in whom all sinned’; that alternative materially affects inherited-sin readings.

v14: Adam is called a pattern of the coming one; the following verses develop both correspondence and greater contrast.

v18–19: Paul’s parallel uses ‘all’ and ‘the many’ within the Adam/Christ comparison. The translation leaves the scope visible without supplying a doctrinal system.

## Vocabulary

v01: **εἰρήνη (eirēnē)**: Peace, reconciliation, or wholeness.

v10: **καταλλαγή (katallagē)**: Reconciliation, restoration from hostility.

v15: **χάρισμα (charisma)**: Gracious gift.
