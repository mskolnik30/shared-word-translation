## Teach What Fits Sound Teaching

<p>
v01: But you must speak what is consistent with sound teaching.
v02: Older men are to be sober, dignified, self-controlled and sound in faith, love and endurance.
v03: Older women likewise are to be reverent in conduct, not slanderers or enslaved to much wine, but teachers of what is good,
v04: so that they may train the young women to love their husbands and children,
v05: to be self-controlled, pure, workers at home, good and submissive to their own husbands, so that God's word may not be slandered.
v06: Likewise, urge the younger men to be self-controlled.
v07: In everything, present yourself as an example of good works. In your teaching show integrity and dignity,
v08: with sound speech that cannot be condemned, so that an opponent may be ashamed, having nothing bad to say about us.
</p>

## Slaves Adorn the Teaching of God Our Savior

<p>
v09: Slaves are to submit to their own masters in everything, to be pleasing and not talk back,
v10: not stealing, but demonstrating complete good faith, so that in everything they may adorn the teaching about God our Savior.
</p>

## Grace Trains Us

<p>
v11: For God's grace has appeared, bringing salvation to all people.
v12: It trains us to renounce ungodliness and worldly desires and to live self-controlled, just and godly lives in the present age,
v13: while we wait for the blessed hope and appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ.
v14: He gave himself for us to redeem us from all lawlessness and cleanse for himself a people of his own, zealous for good works.
v15: Speak these things, encourage and correct with complete authority. Let no one disregard you.
</p>

## Notes

v09–10: The passage addresses enslaved people inside slavery and demands submission; its coercive social setting is not softened.
