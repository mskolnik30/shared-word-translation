## Ready for Every Good Work

<p>
v01: Remind them to submit to rulers and authorities, to obey and to be ready for every good work,
v02: to slander no one, to be peaceable and gentle, showing complete humility toward everyone.
v03: For we too were once foolish, disobedient and deceived, enslaved to various desires and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful and hating one another.
</p>

## Saved by Mercy, Renewed by the Spirit

<p>
v04: But when the kindness and love for humanity of God our Savior appeared,
v05: he saved us—not because of righteous works we had done, but according to his mercy—through the washing of rebirth and renewal by the Holy Spirit,
v06: whom he poured out richly on us through Jesus Christ our Savior,
v07: so that, justified by his grace, we might become heirs according to the hope of eternal life.
v08: This saying is trustworthy, and I want you to insist on these things so that those who have believed God may be careful to devote themselves to good works. These things are good and beneficial for people.
</p>

## Avoid Foolish Controversies

<p>
v09: But avoid foolish controversies, genealogies, quarrels and fights about the law, because they are unprofitable and empty.
v10: After a first and second warning, reject a divisive person,
v11: knowing that such a person is warped and sins, being self-condemned.
</p>

## Final Instructions

<p>
v12: When I send Artemas or Tychicus to you, make every effort to come to me at Nicopolis, because I have decided to spend the winter there.
v13: Diligently help Zenas the lawyer and Apollos on their way, so that they lack nothing.
v14: Our people too must learn to devote themselves to good works for urgent needs, so that they will not be unfruitful.
v15: Everyone with me greets you. Greet those who love us in faith. Grace be with all of you.
</p>

## Notes

v10: The term describes a divisive or faction-making person; later technical meanings should not be assumed automatically.
