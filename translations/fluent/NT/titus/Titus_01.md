## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, a slave of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, for the faith of God's chosen people and their knowledge of the truth that accords with godliness,
v02: in the hope of eternal life, which God, who does not lie, promised before eternal times
v03: and revealed at the proper time in his word through the proclamation entrusted to me by the command of God our Savior,
v04: to Titus, my true child in our common faith: Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.
</p>

## Appoint Elders in Every Town

<p>
v05: I left you in Crete for this reason: to set right what remained and appoint elders in every town, as I directed you.
v06: An elder must be beyond accusation, a one-woman man, with faithful children not accused of wild living or rebellion.
v07: For an overseer, as God's steward, must be beyond accusation—not self-willed, quick-tempered, addicted to wine, violent or greedy for dishonest gain,
v08: but hospitable, loving what is good, self-controlled, just, holy and disciplined,
v09: holding firmly to the trustworthy word according to the teaching, so that he may be able both to encourage with sound teaching and to correct those who contradict it.
</p>

## Silence Empty Talkers

<p>
v10: For there are many rebellious people, empty talkers and deceivers, especially those from the circumcision group.
v11: They must be silenced, because they overturn whole households by teaching what they should not for shameful gain.
v12: One of their own, a prophet of theirs, said, “Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy bellies.”
v13: This testimony is true. Therefore, rebuke them sharply so that they may become sound in the faith,
v14: not paying attention to Jewish myths and commands of people who turn away from the truth.
v15: To the pure, everything is pure; but to those who are defiled and unbelieving, nothing is pure. Both their mind and conscience are defiled.
v16: They profess to know God, but deny him by their actions. They are detestable, disobedient and unfit for any good work.
</p>

## Notes

v06: The household qualification describes a ‘one-woman man’ with believing or faithful children; both expressions require contextual review.

v12: The ethnic insult is quoted as a Cretan prophet’s saying and then endorsed in the next verse; its harmful rhetoric is retained, not universalized beyond the text.
