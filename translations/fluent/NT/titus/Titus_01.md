## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, a slave of God and apostle of Jesus Christ, for the trust of God’s chosen people and the knowledge of the truth that accords with godliness,
v02: in hope of eternal life, which God, who does not lie, promised before eternal times
v03: and revealed at the proper time in his word through the proclamation entrusted to me by the command of God our Savior,
v04: to Titus, my true child according to our common trust: Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior.
</p>

## Appoint Elders in Every Town

<p>
v05: I left you in Crete for this reason: so that you might put in order what remained and appoint elders in every town, as I directed you.
v06: An elder must be above accusation, faithful to one spouse, with believing children who are not accused of reckless living or rebellion.
v07: For an overseer, as God’s steward, must be above accusation—not arrogant, quick-tempered, addicted to wine, violent, or greedy for dishonest gain,
v08: but hospitable, loving what is good, self-controlled, righteous, holy, disciplined,
v09: holding firmly to the trustworthy word according to the teaching, so that they may be able both to encourage with sound teaching and to correct those who contradict it.
</p>

## Silence Empty Talkers

<p>
v10: For there are many rebellious people, empty talkers, and deceivers, especially those from the circumcision group.
v11: They must be silenced because they are overturning whole households by teaching what they should not for shameful gain.
v12: One of their own prophets said, “Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons.”
v13: This testimony is true. Therefore correct them sharply so that they may become sound in the trust,
v14: not paying attention to Jewish myths and commands of people who turn away from the truth.
v15: To the pure, all things are pure, but to those who are defiled and do not trust, nothing is pure; both their mind and conscience are defiled.
v16: They profess to know God, but by their works they deny him. They are detestable, disobedient, and unfit for any good work.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Paul calls himself both God’s slave and Jesus Christ’s apostle, joining humility and commission.

v01: Truth is measured by its relation to godliness; knowledge that does not produce faithful life is inadequate.

v02: God’s inability to lie grounds the promise of eternal life.

v05–09: The same local leaders are called elders in v05 and overseers in v07, suggesting overlapping leadership language rather than separate offices in this passage.

v06: “Faithful to one spouse” translates the one-woman-man idiom as relational fidelity rather than mechanically narrowing leadership to one marital history.

v09: Leadership requires both encouragement and correction, both governed by “sound” teaching.

v10: The problematic group is “especially” from the circumcision, not identical with all Jewish believers or Jewish people.

v12–13: Paul quotes a hostile ethnic stereotype from a Cretan source and calls the testimony true within this polemical context. Fluent preserves the quotation but does not generalize it as an objective description of every Cretan.

v14: “Jewish myths” refers to specific teachings in this controversy, not Jewish Scripture or Jewish identity generally.

v15–16: Purity language turns inward toward mind, conscience, profession, and works rather than external boundary rules alone.
