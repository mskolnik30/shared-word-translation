## The Holy Spirit Comes at Pentecost

<p>
v01: When the day of Pentecost had fully arrived, they were all together in one place.
v02: Suddenly a sound came from heaven like a violent rushing wind, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting.
v03: Tongues like fire appeared to them, divided and resting on each one of them.
v04: All of them were filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other languages as the Spirit enabled them to speak.
</p>

## Every Person Hears in Their Own Language

<p>
v05: Now devout Jewish people from every nation under heaven were living in Jerusalem.
v06: When this sound occurred, the crowd gathered and was bewildered, because each person heard them speaking in that person’s own language.
v07: They were amazed and astonished, saying, “Look, aren’t all these people who are speaking Galileans?
v08: Then how does each of us hear them in our own native language?
v09: Parthians, Medes, Elamites; residents of Mesopotamia, Judea, Cappadocia, Pontus, and Asia;
v10: Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt and the parts of Libya near Cyrene; visitors from Rome,
v11: both Jewish people and converts to Judaism; Cretans and Arabs—we hear them declaring God’s mighty works in our own languages!”
v12: They were all amazed and perplexed, asking one another, “What does this mean?”
v13: But others mocked them and said, “They are full of sweet wine.”
</p>

## Peter Explains What Is Happening

<p>
v14: Then Peter stood with the Eleven, raised his voice, and addressed them: “Fellow Jews and everyone living in Jerusalem, understand this and listen carefully to my words.
v15: These people are not drunk, as you suppose. It is only nine in the morning!
v16: No, this is what was spoken through the prophet Joel:
v17: ‘In the last days, God says, I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh. Your sons and your daughters will prophesy; your young men will see visions, and your elders will dream dreams.
v18: Even on my male slaves and female slaves I will pour out my Spirit in those days, and they will prophesy.
v19: I will give wonders in heaven above and signs on earth below: blood, fire, and clouds of smoke.
v20: The sun will be turned to darkness and the moon to blood before the great and glorious day of the Lord comes.
v21: Then everyone who calls on the Lord’s name will be saved.’
</p>

## Jesus Raised and Exalted

<p>
v22: People of Israel, listen to these words: Jesus the Nazarene was a man accredited to you by God through miracles, wonders, and signs that God did through him among you, as you yourselves know.
v23: This man was handed over according to God’s determined plan and foreknowledge, and you killed him by having lawless people nail him to a cross.
v24: But God raised him up, releasing him from the pains of death, because it was impossible for death to hold him.
v25: For David says about him: ‘I always saw the Lord before me. Because he is at my right hand, I will not be shaken.
v26: Therefore my heart was glad and my tongue rejoiced; moreover, my flesh will live in hope,
v27: because you will not abandon my soul to Hades or allow your Holy One to see decay.
v28: You have made the paths of life known to me; you will fill me with joy in your presence.’
v29: Brothers and sisters, I can speak openly to you about the patriarch David: he died and was buried, and his tomb is with us to this day.
v30: Because he was a prophet and knew that God had sworn an oath to him to seat one of his descendants on his throne,
v31: he foresaw and spoke about the Messiah’s resurrection: he was not abandoned to Hades, and his flesh did not see decay.
v32: God raised this Jesus, and all of us are witnesses.
v33: Exalted to God’s right hand, he received from the Father the promised Holy Spirit and poured out what you now see and hear.
v34: For David did not ascend into heaven, yet he himself says, ‘The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at my right hand
v35: until I make your enemies a footstool for your feet.’
v36: Therefore, let the whole house of Israel know with certainty that God has made this Jesus—whom you crucified—both Lord and Messiah.”
</p>

## Repent, Be Baptized, Receive the Spirit

<p>
v37: When they heard this, they were cut to the heart and asked Peter and the other apostles, “Brothers, what should we do?”
v38: Peter told them, “Repent, and each of you be baptized in the name of Jesus the Messiah for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.
v39: For the promise is for you, for your children, and for all who are far away—everyone whom the Lord our God calls.”
v40: With many other words Peter testified and kept urging them, “Be saved from this crooked generation.”
v41: So those who welcomed his word were baptized, and about three thousand people were added that day.
</p>

## The Shared Life of the Community

<p>
v42: They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching, fellowship, the breaking of bread, and prayers.
v43: Awe came upon everyone, and many wonders and signs were being done through the apostles.
v44: All the believers were together and held everything in common.
v45: They sold property and possessions and distributed the proceeds to everyone according to each person’s need.
v46: Day after day they devoted themselves together in the temple courts and broke bread from house to house. They shared food with glad and sincere hearts,
v47: praising God and enjoying favor with all the people. And each day the Lord added to their number those who were being saved.
</p>

## Notes

v04, 06, 08, 11: The same word can mean tongue or language. The crowd hears recognizable native languages declaring God’s works.

v05: The gathered diaspora worshipers are Jewish people from many nations. Pentecost begins within Israel’s story rather than replacing it.

v17–18: Joel’s promise explicitly includes sons and daughters, young and old, and male and female slaves as Spirit-empowered speakers.

v23: The verse holds God’s determined plan and foreknowledge together with the responsibility of those who handed Jesus over and killed him.

v24: ‘Pains of death’ uses the image of birth pains or binding cords. The main text retains the striking pain image.

v27, 31: Hades is the realm of the dead, not here a detailed description of final punishment.

v38: The grammar closely joins repentance, baptism in Jesus’ name, forgiveness, and the Spirit’s gift without answering every later debate about their sequence.

v44–45: The believers hold possessions in common and distribute according to need. The text describes concrete economic sharing, not only generous feelings.

## Vocabulary

v01: **πεντηκοστή (pentēkostē)**: Pentecost, the fiftieth day and Jewish Festival of Weeks.

v42: **κοινωνία (koinōnia)**: Fellowship, sharing, or participation.

v46: **ὁμοθυμαδόν (homothymadon)**: Together with one purpose or common impulse.
