## Peter and John Before the Council

<p>
v01: While Peter and John were speaking to the people, the priests, the captain of the temple, and the Sadducees came up to them.
v02: They were greatly disturbed because the apostles were teaching the people and proclaiming in Jesus the resurrection from the dead.
v03: They arrested them and put them in custody until the next day, since it was already evening.
v04: But many who had heard the word trusted, and the number of men came to about five thousand.
v05: The next day their rulers, elders, and scribes gathered in Jerusalem,
v06: along with Annas the high priest, Caiaphas, John, Alexander, and everyone from the high-priestly family.
v07: They placed Peter and John in the middle and asked, “By what power or by what name did you do this?”
v08: Then Peter, filled with the Holy Spirit, said, “Rulers of the people and elders,
v09: if we are being examined today because of a good deed done for a sick man, and you are asking how he was made well,
v10: let it be known to all of you and to all the people of Israel: this man stands before you healthy in the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, whom you crucified and whom God raised from the dead.
v11: This Jesus is
‘the stone rejected by you, the builders,
which has become the cornerstone.’
v12: Salvation is found in no one else, because there is no other name under heaven given among humanity by which we must be saved.”
</p>

## They Cannot Stop Speaking

<p>
v13: When they saw Peter and John’s boldness and realized they were uneducated, ordinary men, they were amazed and recognized that they had been with Jesus.
v14: And because they saw the healed man standing with them, they had nothing to say in reply.
v15: After ordering them to leave the council, they discussed the matter with one another:
v16: “What should we do with these men? It is obvious to everyone living in Jerusalem that a remarkable sign has happened through them, and we cannot deny it.
v17: But to keep this from spreading any further among the people, let us threaten them so they no longer speak to anyone in this name.”
v18: They called them in and ordered them not to speak or teach at all in the name of Jesus.
v19: But Peter and John answered, “Whether it is right in God’s sight to listen to you rather than to God, judge for yourselves.
v20: We cannot stop speaking about what we have seen and heard.”
v21: After threatening them further, the council released them. They could find no way to punish them because of the people, since everyone was glorifying God for what had happened.
v22: The man on whom this sign of healing had been performed was more than forty years old.
</p>

## The Community Prays for Boldness

<p>
v23: After being released, Peter and John went to their own people and reported everything the chief priests and elders had told them.
v24: When the community heard it, they raised their voices together to God: “Master, you are the one who made heaven, earth, sea, and everything in them.
v25: You said by the Holy Spirit through the mouth of our ancestor David your servant:
‘Why did the nations rage,
and the peoples plan empty things?
v26: The kings of the earth took their stand,
and the rulers gathered together
against the Lord
and against his Christ.’
v27: For truly in this city Herod and Pontius Pilate, together with the Gentiles and the peoples of Israel, gathered against your holy servant Jesus, whom you anointed,
v28: to do whatever your hand and purpose had determined beforehand would happen.
v29: And now, Lord, look at their threats and grant your servants to speak your word with all boldness,
v30: while you stretch out your hand to heal, and signs and wonders happen through the name of your holy servant Jesus.”
v31: When they had prayed, the place where they were gathered was shaken. They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke God’s word with boldness.
</p>

## No One Among Them Was in Need

<p>
v32: The whole community of those who trusted was one in heart and life. No one claimed that any possession was exclusively their own, but they held everything in common.
v33: With great power the apostles continued giving testimony to the resurrection of the Lord Jesus, and great grace was upon them all.
v34: There was not a needy person among them, because those who owned lands or houses sold them, brought the proceeds from what was sold,
v35: and laid the money at the apostles’ feet. It was distributed to each person according to need.
v36: Joseph, whom the apostles called Barnabas—which means Son of Encouragement—a Levite and native of Cyprus,
v37: owned a field, sold it, brought the money, and laid it at the apostles’ feet.
</p>

## Notes

v02: The immediate dispute concerns proclamation of resurrection “in Jesus,” not a generic philosophical claim that resurrection exists.

v08: Peter’s bold speech is explicitly framed as speech while “filled with the Holy Spirit.”

v10–12: The council is addressed as leaders within Israel. The text sharply confronts them without making their action representative of Jewish people as a whole.

v12: “No other name” is a direct salvation claim about Jesus. Fluent does not weaken or expand it.

v13: “Uneducated and ordinary” means they lacked elite recognized training and social status; it does not mean they were unintelligent or illiterate.

v19–20: Peter and John frame obedience as a conflict of hearing: God’s authority outranks human commands when the two directly conflict.

v24–30: The community prays not primarily for protection but for boldness to continue speaking while God acts.

v27: Herod, Pilate, Gentiles, and peoples of Israel are all named together in responsibility around Jesus’ death.

v28: Divine purpose and hostile human action are again placed together without philosophical resolution.

v32–35: Unity is material as well as emotional. Luke specifically says there was no needy person among them because property was shared.

v36–37: Barnabas is introduced through voluntary generosity immediately before the contrasting Ananias and Sapphira story.
