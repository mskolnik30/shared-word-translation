## Tertullus Accuses Paul Before Felix

<p>
v01: Five days later, the high priest Ananias came down with some elders and an attorney named Tertullus. They presented their case against Paul to the governor.
v02: When Paul was summoned, Tertullus began to accuse him, saying, “Because of you we enjoy lasting peace, and through your foresight reforms are being made for this nation.
v03: In every way and everywhere, Most Excellent Felix, we acknowledge this with deepest gratitude.
v04: But so as not to detain you any longer, I ask you in your kindness to hear us briefly.
v05: We have found this man to be a plague, one who stirs up riots among Jewish people throughout the world, and a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes.
v06: He even tried to desecrate the temple, so we seized him.
v08: By examining him yourself, you will be able to learn the truth about all these charges we bring against him.”
v09: The other Jewish leaders joined in the accusation, asserting that these things were so.
</p>

## Paul’s Defense

<p>
v10: When the governor motioned for him to speak, Paul answered: “Knowing that you have been a judge over this nation for many years, I gladly make my defense.
v11: You can verify that it has been no more than twelve days since I went up to Jerusalem to worship.
v12: They did not find me arguing with anyone in the temple or stirring up a crowd in the synagogues or anywhere in the city.
v13: Nor can they prove to you the charges they are now making against me.
v14: But I admit this to you: I worship the God of our ancestors according to the Way, which they call a sect. I believe everything written in the Law and in the Prophets,
v15: and I have a hope in God—which these men themselves also await—that there will be a resurrection of both the righteous and the unrighteous.
v16: For this reason I always strive to maintain a clear conscience before God and people.
v17: “After several years, I came to bring charitable gifts to my nation and to present offerings.
v18: While I was doing this, they found me purified in the temple, without a crowd and without any disturbance.
v19: But there were some Jewish people from Asia who ought to be here before you to accuse me if they had anything against me.
v20: Or let these men here say what wrongdoing they found when I stood before the council,
v21: unless it was this one statement I shouted while standing among them: ‘It is concerning the resurrection of the dead that I am on trial before you today.’”
</p>

## Felix Delays the Case

<p>
v22: Then Felix, who was well informed about the Way, adjourned the proceedings, saying, “When Commander Lysias comes down, I will decide your case.”
v23: He ordered the centurion to keep Paul in custody but give him some freedom and not prevent any of his own people from attending to his needs.
v24: Several days later, Felix came with his wife Drusilla, who was Jewish. He sent for Paul and listened to him speak about faith in Christ Jesus.
v25: But as Paul reasoned about righteousness, self-control, and the coming judgment, Felix became afraid and answered, “Go away for now. When I find an opportunity, I will send for you.”
v26: At the same time, he was hoping Paul would give him money. For that reason he sent for him frequently and talked with him.
v27: After two years had passed, Porcius Festus succeeded Felix. Wanting to gain favor with the Jewish leaders, Felix left Paul imprisoned.
</p>

## Notes

v07: Public verse 7 is absent from the pinned Greek text, so the numbering moves from 6 to 8.

v14: Paul accepts the name ‘the Way’ while rejecting its dismissal as merely a sect.

v24: Drusilla is named, identified as Felix’s wife, and identified as Jewish.

## Vocabulary

v05: **αἵρεσις (hairesis)**: Party, school, or sect.

v15: **συνείδησις (syneidēsis)**: Conscience or moral awareness.
