## Paul, Prisca, and Aquila in Corinth

<p>
v01: After this, Paul left Athens and went to Corinth.
v02: There he found a Jewish man named Aquila, a native of Pontus, who had recently come from Italy with his wife Priscilla because Claudius had ordered all Jewish people to leave Rome. Paul went to see them,
v03: and because they shared the same trade, he stayed and worked with them. They were tentmakers by trade.
v04: Every Sabbath Paul reasoned in the synagogue, trying to persuade both Jewish and Greek people.
v05: When Silas and Timothy came down from Macedonia, Paul devoted himself to the word, testifying to the Jewish people that Jesus is the Messiah.
v06: But when they opposed him and spoke abusively, he shook out his clothes and told them, “Your blood is on your own heads! I am innocent. From now on I will go to the Gentiles.”
v07: He left there and went to the house of a man named Titius Justus, a worshiper of God whose house was next door to the synagogue.
v08: Crispus, the synagogue leader, believed in the Lord along with his whole household. Many Corinthians who heard also believed and were baptized.
v09: The Lord said to Paul in a vision during the night, “Do not be afraid. Keep speaking and do not be silent,
v10: because I am with you, and no one will attack and harm you, for I have many people in this city.”
v11: So Paul stayed there for a year and six months, teaching God’s word among them.
</p>

## Paul Before Gallio

<p>
v12: While Gallio was proconsul of Achaia, some Jewish people made a united attack on Paul and brought him before the judgment seat.
v13: They said, “This man is persuading people to worship God in a way contrary to the law.”
v14: Just as Paul was about to speak, Gallio told the accusers, “If this were a matter of wrongdoing or a serious crime, I would have reason to hear you patiently.
v15: But since it involves questions about words, names, and your own law, see to it yourselves. I refuse to be a judge of these matters.”
v16: Then he drove them from the judgment seat.
v17: Then they all seized Sosthenes, the synagogue leader, and beat him in front of the judgment seat. Gallio showed no concern about any of it.
</p>

## Paul Returns Through Ephesus

<p>
v18: Paul remained for many days, then said farewell to the brothers and sisters and sailed for Syria, accompanied by Priscilla and Aquila. At Cenchreae he had his hair cut because he had taken a vow.
v19: They reached Ephesus, where Paul left Priscilla and Aquila. He himself entered the synagogue and reasoned with the Jewish people.
v20: When they asked him to stay longer, he declined.
v21: But as he said farewell, he told them, “I will return to you again, God willing.” Then he sailed from Ephesus.
v22: When he landed at Caesarea, he went up and greeted the church, then went down to Antioch.
v23: After spending some time there, Paul left and traveled from place to place through the region of Galatia and Phrygia, strengthening all the disciples.
</p>

## Prisca and Aquila Teach Apollos More Accurately

<p>
v24: Now a Jewish man named Apollos, a native of Alexandria, came to Ephesus. He was eloquent and powerful in the Scriptures.
v25: He had been instructed in the way of the Lord. Fervent in spirit, he spoke and taught accurately about Jesus, though he knew only John’s baptism.
v26: He began to speak boldly in the synagogue. When Priscilla and Aquila heard him, they took him aside and explained the way of God to him more accurately.
v27: When Apollos wanted to cross into Achaia, the brothers and sisters encouraged him and wrote to the disciples to welcome him. On arriving, he greatly helped those who had come to believe through grace,
v28: for he vigorously refuted the Jewish opponents in public, demonstrating from the Scriptures that Jesus is the Messiah.
</p>

## Notes

v02: Claudius’s expulsion order is the narrator’s historical explanation for Aquila and Priscilla’s move.

v26: The pinned text names Priscilla before Aquila while both explain the way of God to Apollos.

## Vocabulary

v03: **σκηνοποιός (skēnopoios)**: Tentmaker or leatherworker.

v28: **χριστός (christos)**: Messiah or Anointed One.
