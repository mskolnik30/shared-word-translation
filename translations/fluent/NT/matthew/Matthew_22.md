## The wedding feast

<p>
v01: Jesus spoke to them again in parables:
v02: “The kingdom of heaven is like a king who held a wedding feast for his son.
v03: He sent his slaves to call those invited to the wedding feast, but they would not come.
v04: He sent other slaves, saying, ‘Tell the invited guests: Look, I have prepared my meal. My oxen and fattened animals have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding feast.’
v05: But they paid no attention and went away, one to his own field, another to his business.
v06: The rest seized his slaves, abused them, and killed them.
v07: The king was enraged. He sent his troops, destroyed those murderers, and burned their city.
v08: Then he told his slaves, ‘The wedding feast is ready, but the invited guests were not worthy.
v09: Go out where the roads lead and invite everyone you find to the wedding feast.’
v10: Those slaves went out into the roads and gathered everyone they found, both evil and good. The wedding feast was filled with guests.
</p>

<p>
v11: When the king came in to look at the guests, he saw a man there who was not wearing wedding clothes.
v12: ‘Friend,’ he asked, ‘how did you get in here without wedding clothes?’ The man was speechless.
v13: Then the king told the attendants, ‘Bind his feet and hands and throw him into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and grinding of teeth.’
v14: For many are called, but few are chosen.”
</p>

## Tribute to Caesar

<p>
v15: Then the Pharisees went and planned how to trap him in what he said.
v16: They sent their disciples to him with the Herodians. “Teacher,” they said, “we know you are truthful and teach God’s way truthfully. You are not swayed by anyone, because you do not judge by appearances.
v17: So tell us what you think: Is it permitted to pay tribute to Caesar or not?”
v18: Jesus knew their malice. “Why are you testing me, hypocrites?” he asked.
v19: “Show me the coin used for the tribute.” They brought him a denarius.
v20: He asked them, “Whose image and inscription are these?”
v21: “Caesar’s,” they answered. Then he told them, “Give back to Caesar what belongs to Caesar, and to God what belongs to God.”
v22: When they heard this, they were amazed. They left him and went away.
</p>

## A question about resurrection

<p>
v23: That same day Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, approached him and asked,
v24: “Teacher, Moses said that if a man dies without children, his brother must marry the widow and raise offspring for his brother.
v25: There were seven brothers among us. The first married and died without offspring, leaving his wife to his brother.
v26: The same happened to the second and third, and on through all seven.
v27: After all of them, the woman died.
v28: So in the resurrection, whose wife will she be of the seven? All of them had married her.”
v29: Jesus answered them, “You are mistaken, because you know neither the Scriptures nor God’s power.
v30: In the resurrection people neither marry nor are given in marriage. They are like God’s angels in heaven.
v31: And about the resurrection of the dead, haven’t you read what God said to you:
v32: ‘I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob’? He is God of the living, not of the dead.”
v33: The crowds heard this and were astonished at his teaching.
</p>

## The greatest commandment

<p>
v34: When the Pharisees heard that he had silenced the Sadducees, they gathered together.
v35: One of them, an expert in the Law, tested him with a question:
v36: “Teacher, which commandment is greatest in the Law?”
v37: He answered, “‘Love the Lord your God with all your heart, all your soul, and all your mind.’
v38: This is the greatest and first commandment.
v39: A second is like it: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’
v40: The whole Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments.”
</p>

## David’s son and Lord

<p>
v41: While the Pharisees were gathered, Jesus asked them,
v42: “What do you think about the Messiah? Whose son is he?” “David’s,” they answered.
v43: He asked them, “How then does David, speaking in the Spirit, call him Lord? He says,
v44: ‘The Lord said to my Lord,
“Sit at my right hand
until I put your enemies beneath your feet.”’
v45: If David calls him Lord, how is he his son?”
v46: No one could answer him a word. From that day on, no one dared question him any further.
</p>

## Notes

v11–14: The story does not say that wedding clothes had been supplied to the guests. The draft does not invent that explanation for the man’s exclusion.

v21,30: The Caesar saying leaves both claims in tension. Those raised are compared to angels in the matter under discussion; the text does not say they become angels.

v37,44: The love command names heart, soul, and mind in Matthew. The psalm quotation places enemies beneath the feet; it is not mechanically conformed to another wording.

## Vocabulary

v16: **Herodians**: People associated with Herod’s cause or rule, present with the Pharisees’ disciples.

v24: **Raise offspring**: The marriage arrangement aims to continue the deceased brother’s family line.
