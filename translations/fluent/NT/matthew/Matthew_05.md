## Teaching on the mountain

<p>
v01: When Jesus saw the crowds, he went up the mountain and sat down. His disciples came to him,
v02: and he began to teach them:
</p>

## The blessed

<p>
v03: “Blessed are the poor in spirit,
for the kingdom of heaven is theirs.
v04: Blessed are those who mourn,
for they will be comforted.
v05: Blessed are the gentle,
for they will inherit the earth.
v06: Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness,
for they will be filled.
v07: Blessed are the merciful,
for they will receive mercy.
v08: Blessed are the pure in heart,
for they will see God.
v09: Blessed are the peacemakers,
for they will be called God’s children.
v10: Blessed are those persecuted for righteousness,
for the kingdom of heaven is theirs.
v11: You are blessed when people insult you, persecute you, and falsely say every kind of evil against you because of me.
v12: Rejoice and celebrate, because your reward in heaven is great. That is how they persecuted the prophets before you.
</p>

## Salt and light

<p>
v13: You are the salt of the earth. But if salt loses its saltiness, how can it be made salty again? It is good for nothing except to be thrown outside and trampled underfoot.
v14: You are the light of the world. A city set on a mountain cannot be hidden.
v15: No one lights a lamp and puts it under a grain basket. They put it on a lampstand, where it shines for everyone in the house.
v16: In the same way, let your light shine before people, so they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven.
</p>

## The Law and the Prophets

<p>
v17: Do not think I came to abolish the Law or the Prophets. I came to fulfill them, not to abolish them.
v18: Truly I tell you: until heaven and earth pass away, not one smallest letter or pen stroke will pass from the Law until everything happens.
v19: So whoever sets aside even one of these least commandments and teaches others to do the same will be called least in the kingdom of heaven. But whoever practices and teaches them will be called great in the kingdom of heaven.
v20: For I tell you, unless your righteousness exceeds that of the scribes and Pharisees, you will certainly not enter the kingdom of heaven.
</p>

## Anger and reconciliation

<p>
v21: You have heard that it was said to those of old, ‘Do not murder,’ and, ‘Whoever murders will answer to the court.’
v22: But I tell you: anyone angry with a brother or sister will answer to the court. Whoever calls a brother or sister ‘Raca’ will answer to the council, and whoever says ‘Fool!’ will answer to fiery Gehenna.
v23: So if you are bringing your gift to the altar and remember there that a brother or sister has something against you,
v24: leave your gift there before the altar. First go and be reconciled with that person; then come and offer your gift.
v25: Come to terms with your accuser quickly while you are on the way together. Otherwise your accuser may hand you to the judge, the judge to the officer, and you will be thrown into prison.
v26: Truly I tell you: you will not get out until you have paid the last quadrans.
</p>

## Adultery and divorce

<p>
v27: You have heard that it was said, ‘Do not commit adultery.’
v28: But I tell you: anyone who looks at a woman in order to desire her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.
v29: If your right eye causes you to stumble, tear it out and throw it away. It is better to lose one part of your body than for your whole body to be thrown into Gehenna.
v30: And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw it away. It is better to lose one part of your body than for your whole body to go into Gehenna.
v31: It was also said, ‘Whoever divorces his wife must give her a certificate of divorce.’
v32: But I tell you: anyone who divorces his wife, except on grounds of sexual immorality, causes her to be involved in adultery, and anyone who marries a divorced woman commits adultery.
</p>

## Oaths and truthful speech

<p>
v33: Again, you have heard that it was said to those of old, ‘Do not swear falsely, but fulfill your oaths to the Lord.’
v34: But I tell you not to swear at all: neither by heaven, because it is God’s throne,
v35: nor by earth, because it is his footstool, nor by Jerusalem, because it is the city of the great King.
v36: Do not swear by your own head either, because you cannot make a single hair white or black.
v37: Let your words be ‘Yes, yes’ or ‘No, no.’ Anything beyond this comes from evil.
</p>

## Retaliation and love for enemies

<p>
v38: You have heard that it was said, ‘An eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth.’
v39: But I tell you not to resist the wrongdoer. If someone strikes your right cheek, turn the other to him too.
v40: If someone wants to sue you for your tunic, let him have your cloak as well.
v41: If someone forces you to go one mile, go two with him.
v42: Give to the one who asks you, and do not turn away someone who wants to borrow from you.
v43: You have heard that it was said, ‘Love your neighbor and hate your enemy.’
v44: But I tell you: love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you,
v45: so you may be children of your Father in heaven. He makes his sun rise on the evil and the good, and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.
v46: If you love those who love you, what reward do you have? Do not even tax collectors do the same?
v47: And if you greet only your own brothers and sisters, what more are you doing? Do not even Gentiles do the same?
v48: So be complete, as your heavenly Father is complete.
</p>

## Notes

v22: The pinned text does not qualify anger with “without cause.” Raca is an insult; Gehenna is the named judgment image.

v29–32: The eye and hand sayings use graphic hyperbole about removing causes of sin, not medical instructions. “Sexual immorality” renders porneia, whose exact scope in the divorce saying is disputed; the passive wording about the woman is preserved.

v37,48: “Evil” may also mean “the evil one.” “Complete” renders teleios and connects the command with the preceding call to undivided love.

## Vocabulary

v03: **Blessed**: A declaration of favored standing, which can coexist with present mourning or persecution.

v22: **Gehenna**: A term associated with judgment, distinct from Hades, the realm of the dead.

v26: **Quadrans**: A small Roman copper coin; the saying presses repayment down to the last coin.
