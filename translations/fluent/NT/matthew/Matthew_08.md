## A man made clean

<p>
v01: When Jesus came down from the mountain, large crowds followed him.
v02: A man with a skin disease came up, bowed before him, and said, “Lord, if you are willing, you can make me clean.”
v03: Jesus reached out and touched him. “I am willing,” he said. “Be clean.” At once the man’s skin disease was cleansed.
v04: Then Jesus told him, “See that you tell no one. Go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift Moses commanded, as a testimony to them.”
</p>

## A centurion’s faith

<p>
v05: As Jesus entered Capernaum, a centurion approached him, pleading,
v06: “Lord, my servant is lying at home, paralyzed and suffering terribly.”
v07: Jesus said to him, “I will come and heal him.”
v08: The centurion answered, “Lord, I am not worthy to have you come under my roof. Just say the word, and my servant will be healed.
v09: I too am a man under authority, with soldiers under me. I tell one, ‘Go,’ and he goes; another, ‘Come,’ and he comes; and my slave, ‘Do this,’ and he does it.”
v10: When Jesus heard this, he was amazed. He told those following him, “Truly I tell you, I have found no one in Israel with faith like this.
v11: And I tell you, many will come from east and west and take their places at the feast with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven.
v12: But the heirs of the kingdom will be thrown into the outer darkness. There will be weeping and grinding of teeth.”
v13: Then Jesus told the centurion, “Go. Let it happen for you as you have believed.” And the servant was healed at that very hour.
</p>

## Healing in Peter’s house

<p>
v14: Jesus went into Peter’s house and saw Peter’s mother-in-law lying sick with a fever.
v15: He touched her hand, and the fever left her. She got up and began serving him.
v16: That evening, people brought him many who were possessed by demons. He drove out the spirits with a word and healed everyone who was ill.
v17: This fulfilled what was spoken through the prophet Isaiah:
“He took our weaknesses
and carried our diseases.”
</p>

## The cost of following

<p>
v18: When Jesus saw the large crowds around him, he gave orders to cross to the other side.
v19: A scribe came up and said, “Teacher, I will follow you wherever you go.”
v20: Jesus answered him, “Foxes have dens, and the birds of the sky have nests, but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay his head.”
v21: Another of the disciples said to him, “Lord, first let me go and bury my father.”
v22: But Jesus told him, “Follow me, and let the dead bury their own dead.”
</p>

## The storm stilled

<p>
v23: Jesus got into a boat, and his disciples followed him.
v24: Suddenly a violent disturbance swept over the sea, and waves were covering the boat. But Jesus was asleep.
v25: They went to him and woke him. “Lord, save us!” they said. “We’re going to die!”
v26: He said to them, “Why are you so afraid, you of little faith?” Then he got up and rebuked the winds and the sea, and a great calm came over it.
v27: The men were amazed. “What kind of man is this,” they said, “that even the winds and the sea obey him?”
</p>

## Two men among the tombs

<p>
v28: When he reached the other side, in the region of the Gadarenes, two demon-possessed men came out from the tombs to meet him. They were so violent that no one could pass along that road.
v29: They shouted, “What have you to do with us, Son of God? Have you come here to torment us before the appointed time?”
v30: A large herd of pigs was feeding some distance away.
v31: The demons begged him, “If you drive us out, send us into the herd of pigs.”
v32: “Go,” he told them. They came out and went into the pigs. Then the whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea and died in the water.
v33: The herders ran away. They went into the town and reported everything, including what had happened to the demon-possessed men.
v34: The whole town came out to meet Jesus. When they saw him, they begged him to leave their region.
</p>

## Notes

v02,06–07: The skin-disease term is broader than a modern diagnosis of leprosy. Pais can mean child or servant; servant fits this setting. Jesus’ reply in verse 7 can also be punctuated as a question: “Am I to come and heal him?”

v22,28: The doubled “dead” saying is left sharp without inventing the father’s circumstances. Matthew names two men and the Gadarenes; these are not harmonized to other accounts.

## Vocabulary

v05: **Centurion**: An officer in a military unit conventionally associated with a hundred soldiers.

v20: **Son of Man**: Jesus’ recurring self-designation; its meaning develops across suffering, authority, and coming judgment.
