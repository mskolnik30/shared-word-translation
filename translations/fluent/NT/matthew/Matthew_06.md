## Righteousness before the Father

<p>
v01: Be careful not to practice your righteousness before people in order to be seen by them. Otherwise you have no reward from your Father in heaven.
v02: So when you give to the poor, do not sound a trumpet ahead of you, as hypocrites do in synagogues and streets to win people’s praise. Truly I tell you: they have received their reward in full.
v03: But when you give to the poor, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing,
v04: so your giving may be in secret. Your Father, who sees in secret, will repay you.
</p>

## Prayer

<p>
v05: When you pray, do not be like the hypocrites. They love to stand praying in synagogues and on street corners so people will see them. Truly I tell you: they have received their reward in full.
v06: But when you pray, go into your inner room, shut the door, and pray to your Father who is in secret. Your Father, who sees in secret, will repay you.
v07: When you pray, do not babble like Gentiles. They think they will be heard because of their many words.
v08: Do not be like them. Your Father knows what you need before you ask him.
</p>

<p>
v09: Pray like this:
‘Our Father in heaven,
may your name be held holy.
v10: May your kingdom come.
May your will be done
on earth as in heaven.
v11: Give us today the bread we need.
v12: Forgive us our debts,
as we too have forgiven our debtors.
v13: Do not bring us into testing,
but rescue us from evil.’
</p>

<p>
v14: For if you forgive people their offenses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you.
v15: But if you do not forgive people, your Father will not forgive your offenses.
</p>

## Fasting

<p>
v16: When you fast, do not look gloomy like the hypocrites. They disfigure their faces so people will notice they are fasting. Truly I tell you: they have received their reward in full.
v17: But when you fast, put oil on your head and wash your face,
v18: so your fasting is seen by your Father who is in secret, not by people. Your Father, who sees in secret, will repay you.
</p>

## Treasure and masters

<p>
v19: Do not store up treasures for yourselves on earth, where moth and decay destroy and thieves break in and steal.
v20: Store up treasures for yourselves in heaven, where neither moth nor decay destroys and thieves do not break in or steal.
v21: For where your treasure is, there your heart will be too.
v22: The eye is the body’s lamp. If your eye is sound, your whole body will be full of light.
v23: But if your eye is bad, your whole body will be dark. If the light within you is darkness, how great that darkness is!
</p>

<p>
v24: No one can serve two masters. You will hate one and love the other, or be devoted to one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and wealth.
</p>

## Do not be anxious

<p>
v25: That is why I tell you: do not worry about your life, what you will eat, or about your body, what you will wear. Is life not more than food, and the body more than clothing?
v26: Look at the birds of heaven. They neither sow nor reap nor gather into storehouses, yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not worth more than they are?
v27: Which of you can add a single cubit to your lifespan by worrying?
v28: And why worry about clothing? Consider how the field’s lilies grow. They neither labor nor spin,
v29: yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not clothed like one of them.
v30: If God clothes the field’s grass this way, though it is here today and thrown into the oven tomorrow, will he not clothe you much more, you of little faith?
v31: So do not worry, asking, ‘What will we eat?’ or ‘What will we drink?’ or ‘What will we wear?’
v32: The nations seek all these things, and your heavenly Father knows you need them all.
v33: Seek first the kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well.
v34: So do not worry about tomorrow. Tomorrow will worry about itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The pinned text says righteousness, introducing the acts of giving, praying, and fasting that follow.

v11–13: The unusual bread adjective can mean daily, needed for the day, or for the coming day. The forgiveness clause says “have forgiven.” The final petition can refer to evil or the evil one; the familiar closing doxology is not printed in this source.

v22,27,33: A sound or single eye can carry a sense of generosity. The cubit saying may concern lifespan or stature; the measurement image remains. The pinned verse 33 does not add “of God” after kingdom.

## Vocabulary

v12: **Debts**: The prayer’s image for what requires forgiveness; verses 14–15 speak directly of offenses.

v24: **Mammon**: Wealth personified as a competing master.

v07: **Gentiles**: People who are not Jewish; this identifies the comparison group within Jesus’ Jewish setting.
