## The family line of Jesus the Messiah

<p>
v01: The record of the origins of Jesus the Messiah, son of David, son of Abraham.
v02: Abraham fathered Isaac; Isaac fathered Jacob; Jacob fathered Judah and his brothers.
v03: Judah fathered Perez and Zerah by Tamar; Perez fathered Hezron; Hezron fathered Ram.
v04: Ram fathered Amminadab; Amminadab fathered Nahshon; Nahshon fathered Salmon.
v05: Salmon fathered Boaz by Rahab; Boaz fathered Obed by Ruth; Obed fathered Jesse.
v06: Jesse fathered David the king. David fathered Solomon by Uriah’s wife.
v07: Solomon fathered Rehoboam; Rehoboam fathered Abijah; Abijah fathered Asaph.
v08: Asaph fathered Jehoshaphat; Jehoshaphat fathered Joram; Joram fathered Uzziah.
v09: Uzziah fathered Jotham; Jotham fathered Ahaz; Ahaz fathered Hezekiah.
v10: Hezekiah fathered Manasseh; Manasseh fathered Amos; Amos fathered Josiah.
v11: Josiah fathered Jeconiah and his brothers at the time of the deportation to Babylon.
v12: After the deportation to Babylon, Jeconiah fathered Shealtiel; Shealtiel fathered Zerubbabel.
v13: Zerubbabel fathered Abiud; Abiud fathered Eliakim; Eliakim fathered Azor.
v14: Azor fathered Zadok; Zadok fathered Achim; Achim fathered Eliud.
v15: Eliud fathered Eleazar; Eleazar fathered Matthan; Matthan fathered Jacob.
v16: Jacob fathered Joseph, Mary’s husband. From her was born Jesus, who is called the Messiah.
v17: So there were fourteen generations from Abraham to David, fourteen from David to the deportation to Babylon, and fourteen from the deportation to Babylon to the Messiah.
</p>

## The birth of Jesus

<p>
v18: This is how the birth of Jesus the Messiah came about. His mother Mary was pledged in marriage to Joseph, but before they came together she was found to be pregnant through the Holy Spirit.
v19: Her husband Joseph was righteous and did not want to expose her to public disgrace, so he decided to divorce her quietly.
v20: While he was considering this, an angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream. “Joseph, son of David,” he said, “do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife. The child conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit.
v21: She will bear a son, and you must name him Jesus, because he will save his people from their sins.”
v22: All this happened to fulfill what the Lord had spoken through the prophet:
v23: “Look, the virgin will conceive and bear a son,
and they will name him Emmanuel,”
which means “God with us.”
v24: When Joseph woke, he did what the angel of the Lord had commanded and took his wife home.
v25: But he did not have sexual relations with her until she bore a son. He named him Jesus.
</p>

## Notes

v07–10: The pinned Greek names Asaph and Amos; these are not silently changed to Asa and Amon to match other genealogies.

v16: “From whom” is feminine singular in Greek, referring to Mary. The pattern of fathers begetting sons changes at this point.

v25: “Until” states what happened before the birth; this wording alone does not specify what happened afterward.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Messiah**: The anointed one; this translates Greek Christos as a title rather than treating Christ as a surname.

v21: **Jesus**: The name is linked in the verse to saving his people from their sins.
