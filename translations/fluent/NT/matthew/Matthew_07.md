## Judging others

<p>
v01: Do not judge, so that you will not be judged.
v02: For the judgment you give will be given to you, and the measure you use will be used for you.
v03: Why do you notice the speck in your brother’s or sister’s eye but fail to consider the beam in your own?
v04: How can you say, ‘Let me remove the speck from your eye,’ when there is a beam in your own eye?
v05: Hypocrite! First remove the beam from your own eye. Then you will see clearly to remove the speck from your brother’s or sister’s eye.
v06: Do not give what is holy to dogs or throw your pearls before pigs. They may trample them underfoot, then turn and tear you apart.
</p>

## Ask, seek, knock

<p>
v07: Ask, and it will be given to you. Seek, and you will find. Knock, and the door will be opened to you.
v08: For everyone who asks receives; the one who seeks finds; and to the one who knocks, the door will be opened.
v09: Who among you would give his son a stone when he asks for bread?
v10: Or give him a snake when he asks for a fish?
v11: If you, evil as you are, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven give good things to those who ask him!
v12: So in everything, do for others what you want them to do for you. This is the Law and the Prophets.
</p>

## Two ways

<p>
v13: Enter through the narrow gate. The gate is wide and the road spacious that leads to destruction, and many enter through it.
v14: But the gate is narrow and the road constricted that leads to life, and few find it.
</p>

## Trees and fruit

<p>
v15: Watch out for false prophets. They come to you dressed as sheep, but inwardly they are ravenous wolves.
v16: You will recognize them by their fruit. Are grapes gathered from thornbushes, or figs from thistles?
v17: In the same way, every good tree produces good fruit, while a rotten tree produces bad fruit.
v18: A good tree cannot produce bad fruit, nor can a rotten tree produce good fruit.
v19: Every tree that fails to produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.
v20: So you will recognize them by their fruit.
</p>

## Words and actions

<p>
v21: Not everyone who says to me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will enter the kingdom of heaven, but the one who does my heavenly Father’s will.
v22: Many will say to me on that day, ‘Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name, drive out demons in your name, and do many mighty works in your name?’
v23: Then I will tell them plainly, ‘I never knew you. Depart from me, you workers of lawlessness!’
</p>

## Two builders

<p>
v24: So everyone who hears these words of mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on rock.
v25: The rain came down, the rivers rose, and the winds blew and beat against that house. But it did not fall, because its foundation was on rock.
v26: Everyone who hears these words of mine and does not do them will be like a foolish man who built his house on sand.
v27: The rain came down, the rivers rose, and the winds blew and struck that house. It fell, and its fall was great.”
</p>

## The crowd’s response

<p>
v28: When Jesus finished saying these things, the crowds were astonished at his teaching,
v29: because he taught them as someone with authority, unlike their scribes.
</p>

## Notes

v01–05: The judgment warning develops through reciprocal measure and the log/speck image; the imagery remains concrete.

v21–23: The contrast is between claims made in Jesus’ name and doing the Father’s will. The reported claims are not automatically endorsed by the narrator.

## Vocabulary

v06: **Holy; pearls**: Valuable things in a deliberately sharp animal comparison.

v29: **Authority**: The closing reaction concerns how Jesus teaches, in contrast to their scribes.
