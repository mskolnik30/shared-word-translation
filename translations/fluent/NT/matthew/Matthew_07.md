## Judging and Discernment

<p>
v01: “Do not judge, so that you will not be judged.
v02: For with the judgment you use, you will be judged, and with the measure you use, it will be measured to you.
v03: Why do you look at the speck in your brother's or sister's eye but do not notice the beam in your own eye?
v04: Or how can you say, ‘Let me take the speck out of your eye,’ when there is a beam in your own?
v05: Hypocrite! First take the beam out of your own eye, and then you will see clearly to take the speck out of your brother's or sister's eye.
v06: “Do not give what is holy to dogs, and do not throw your pearls before pigs, or they may trample them under their feet and turn and tear you apart.
</p>

## Ask, Seek, Knock

<p>
v07: “Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and the door will be opened to you.
v08: For everyone who asks receives, the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks the door will be opened.
v09: Or which of you, if your child asks for bread, will give a stone?
v10: Or if the child asks for a fish, will give a snake?
v11: If you, though you are evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father in heaven give good things to those who ask him!
v12: Therefore, in everything, do to others whatever you want them to do to you. For this is the Law and the Prophets.
</p>

## The Narrow Gate

<p>
v13: “Enter through the narrow gate. For the gate is wide and the road broad that leads to destruction, and many enter through it.
v14: But the gate is narrow and the road constricted that leads to life, and few find it.
</p>

## Trees and Fruit

<p>
v15: “Watch out for false prophets. They come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravenous wolves.
v16: You will recognize them by their fruits. Are grapes gathered from thornbushes, or figs from thistles?
v17: In the same way, every good tree produces good fruit, but a bad tree produces bad fruit.
v18: A good tree cannot produce bad fruit, nor can a bad tree produce good fruit.
v19: Every tree that does not produce good fruit is cut down and thrown into the fire.
v20: So then, you will recognize them by their fruits.
</p>

## Doing the Father's Will

<p>
v21: “Not everyone who says to me, ‘Lord, Lord,’ will enter the kingdom of heaven, but the one who does the will of my Father in heaven.
v22: Many will say to me on that day, ‘Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name, cast out demons in your name, and do many works of power in your name?’
v23: Then I will declare to them, ‘I never knew you. Go away from me, you who practice lawlessness.’
</p>

## The Two Builders

<p>
v24: “Therefore everyone who hears these words of mine and does them will be like a wise man who built his house on rock.
v25: The rain fell, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat against that house, but it did not fall, because its foundation had been laid on rock.
v26: And everyone who hears these words of mine and does not do them will be like a foolish man who built his house on sand.
v27: The rain fell, the floods came, and the winds blew and struck that house, and it fell—and its fall was great.”
</p>

## The Authority of Jesus

<p>
v28: When Jesus finished these words, the crowds were astonished at his teaching,
v29: because he was teaching them as one who had authority, and not like their scribes.
</p>

## Notes

v01–05: Jesus does not abolish discernment; the sequence attacks hypocritical judgment that ignores one's own condition before correcting another.

v06: The saying about holy things, dogs, pearls, and pigs is intentionally sharp and is not explained by Matthew.

v07–11: The argument moves from ordinary parental giving to trust in the goodness of the heavenly Father.

v12: Matthew explicitly identifies the positive reciprocity saying as a summary of “the Law and the Prophets.”

v13–14: Textual traditions vary in some wording around gate and road. Fluent follows the pinned source and preserves the paired images.

v21–23: Correct address and even extraordinary ministry activity do not substitute for doing the Father's will.

v24–27: The contrast is not hearing versus ignorance but hearing-and-doing versus hearing-without-doing.

v28: “When Jesus finished…” becomes a structural formula marking the close of major Matthean teaching blocks.
