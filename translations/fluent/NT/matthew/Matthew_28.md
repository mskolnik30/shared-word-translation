## The Empty Tomb

<p>
v01: After the Sabbath, as the first day of the week was dawning, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb.
v02: And look—there was a great earthquake, because an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, came, rolled away the stone, and sat on it.
v03: His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing was white as snow.
v04: The guards shook with fear of him and became like dead men.
v05: But the angel said to the women, “Do not be afraid. I know that you are looking for Jesus, who was crucified.
v06: He is not here, because he has been raised, just as he said. Come, see the place where he lay.
v07: Then go quickly and tell his disciples, ‘He has been raised from the dead, and look—he is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see him.’ Look, I have told you.”
v08: They left the tomb quickly with fear and great joy and ran to tell his disciples.
</p>

## Jesus Meets the Women

<p>
v09: And look—Jesus met them and said, “Greetings!” They came to him, took hold of his feet, and bowed before him.
v10: Then Jesus said to them, “Do not be afraid. Go tell my brothers to go to Galilee, and there they will see me.”
</p>

## The Guards' Report

<p>
v11: While the women were going, look—some of the guard went into the city and reported to the chief priests everything that had happened.
v12: After the chief priests gathered with the elders and formed a plan, they gave the soldiers a large amount of money
v13: and said, “Tell people, ‘His disciples came during the night and stole him while we were sleeping.’
v14: If this comes to the governor's attention, we will persuade him and keep you out of trouble.”
v15: The soldiers took the money and did as they were instructed. And this story has been spread among Jews to this day.
</p>

## Jesus Commissions the Disciples

<p>
v16: The eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain Jesus had directed them to.
v17: When they saw him, they bowed before him, but some doubted.
v18: Jesus came near and spoke to them: “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.
v19: Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
v20: teaching them to keep everything I commanded you. And look—I am with you always, to the completion of the age.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: Matthew narrates the women approaching to “see” the tomb, unlike Mark's explicit purpose of bringing spices to anoint Jesus.

v02: Matthew places the stone's removal within an angelic descent and earthquake. The angel sits on the stone, turning the sealed obstacle into a sign of divine action.

v06: “He has been raised” is passive, leaving God as the implied actor.

v08: Fear and great joy coexist. Unlike Mark 16:8's silence ending, Matthew's women run to tell the disciples.

v09: The women grasp Jesus' feet, underscoring embodied resurrection rather than visionary appearance alone.

v10: Jesus calls the disciples “my brothers” after their failure and flight.

v15: “Among Jews to this day” reflects Matthew's intra-Jewish polemical setting. It must not be expanded into a claim about Jewish people across later history.

v17: The Greek can mean “some doubted” or describe the disciples' hesitation collectively. Fluent retains “some doubted,” while the scene strikingly places worship and doubt together.

v18: The Gospel's authority theme reaches its climax in Jesus' claim to all authority in heaven and earth.

v19: The main imperative is “make disciples”; going, baptizing, and teaching describe the commission's movement and practice.

v19: “In the name” is singular while naming Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Fluent preserves the grammar without importing later conciliar formulations into the verse itself.

v20: “I am with you” closes the Emmanuel theme opened in 1:23. “Completion of the age” repeats Matthew's eschatological phrase from the teaching discourses.
