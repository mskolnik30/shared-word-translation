## The Empty Tomb

<p>
v01: After the Sabbath, as the first day of the week was dawning, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary came to see the tomb.
v02: Suddenly there was a great earthquake, for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, came to the tomb, rolled away the stone, and sat on it.
v03: His appearance was like lightning, and his clothing was white as snow.
v04: The guards shook with fear of him and became like dead men.
v05: The angel said to the women, “Do not be afraid. I know you are looking for Jesus, who was crucified.
v06: He is not here; he has been raised, just as he said. Come and see the place where he lay.
v07: Then go quickly and tell his disciples, ‘He has been raised from the dead and is going ahead of you into Galilee. You will see him there.’ Remember, I have told you.”
v08: They quickly left the tomb, afraid yet filled with great joy, and ran to tell his disciples.
</p>

## Jesus Meets the Women

<p>
v09: Suddenly Jesus met them. “Greetings!” he said. They came to him, took hold of his feet, and worshiped him.
v10: Then Jesus told them, “Do not be afraid. Go and tell my brothers to leave for Galilee. They will see me there.”
</p>

## The Guards' Report

<p>
v11: While the women were on their way, some of the guard went into the city and reported everything that had happened to the chief priests.
v12: The chief priests met with the elders and devised a plan. They gave the soldiers a large sum of money
v13: and told them, “Say, ‘His disciples came at night and stole him while we were asleep.’
v14: If the governor hears about this, we will persuade him and keep you out of trouble.”
v15: The soldiers took the money and did as they were instructed. This story has circulated among the Jews to this day.
</p>

## Jesus Commissions the Disciples

<p>
v16: The eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain Jesus had appointed for them.
v17: When they saw him, they worshiped him, but some hesitated.
v18: Jesus came to them and said, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.
v19: So go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
v20: and teaching them to keep everything I have commanded you. And remember, I am with you every day, to the end of the age.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: Matthew says the women came to see the tomb. The purpose is not expanded with details from another Gospel.

v06: “Has been raised” preserves the Greek passive expression.

v15: “To this day” is the narrator’s temporal perspective. The statement does not assert that every Jewish person accepted the story.

v17: The wording can refer to some disciples or describe the group’s hesitation. Distazō can be rendered “doubt” or “hesitate”; worship and uncertainty stand together in the scene.

v19: “Make disciples” is the central command. The Greek uses the singular “name” while naming Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

v20: “I am with you” recalls the Emmanuel theme in 1:23. “End of the age” is the expression also used in 24:3.

## Vocabulary

v09: **Worship**: Greek proskyneō can describe bowing down, homage, or worship; here the women grasp Jesus’ feet.

v18: **Authority**: Greek exousia: authority or the right to act.

v19: **Make disciples**: To form people as learners and followers.

v19: **Baptize**: A washing or immersion rite; here into the name of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

v20: **Keep**: To observe or carry out, not merely to remember information.
