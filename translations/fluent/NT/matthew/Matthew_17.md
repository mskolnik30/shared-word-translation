## Jesus transformed on the mountain

<p>
v01: Six days later, Jesus took Peter, James, and James’s brother John with him and led them up a high mountain by themselves.
v02: He was transformed before them. His face shone like the sun, and his clothes became white as light.
v03: Then Moses and Elijah appeared to them, speaking with him.
v04: Peter said to Jesus, “Lord, it is good for us to be here. If you wish, I will make three shelters here: one for you, one for Moses, and one for Elijah.”
v05: While he was still speaking, a bright cloud overshadowed them. A voice from the cloud said, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I delight. Listen to him.”
v06: When the disciples heard it, they fell facedown, terrified.
v07: Jesus came, touched them, and said, “Get up. Do not be afraid.”
v08: When they looked up, they saw no one except Jesus alone.
</p>

## Elijah and the Son of Man

<p>
v09: As they came down the mountain, Jesus commanded them, “Tell no one about the vision until the Son of Man has been raised from the dead.”
v10: The disciples asked him, “Why then do the scribes say Elijah must come first?”
v11: He answered, “Elijah is indeed coming and will restore everything.
v12: But I tell you, Elijah has already come. They did not recognize him and did whatever they wanted to him. In the same way, the Son of Man is going to suffer at their hands.”
v13: Then the disciples understood that he was speaking to them about John the Baptist.
</p>

## A boy healed

<p>
v14: When they came to the crowd, a man approached Jesus, knelt before him,
v15: and said, “Lord, have mercy on my son. He has seizures and suffers terribly; he often falls into fire and often into water.
v16: I brought him to your disciples, but they could not heal him.”
v17: Jesus answered, “Unbelieving and twisted generation! How long must I be with you? How long must I put up with you? Bring him here to me.”
v18: Jesus rebuked it, and the demon came out of the boy. From that moment the boy was healed.
v19: Then the disciples came to Jesus privately and asked, “Why couldn’t we drive it out?”
v20: He told them, “Because of your little faith. Truly I tell you, if you have faith like a mustard seed, you will tell this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move. Nothing will be impossible for you.”
</p>

## Handed over and raised

<p>
v22: While they were gathering in Galilee, Jesus told them, “The Son of Man is going to be handed over into human hands.
v23: They will kill him, and on the third day he will be raised.” They were deeply distressed.
</p>

## The two-drachma tax

<p>
v24: When they reached Capernaum, the collectors of the two-drachma tax approached Peter. “Doesn’t your teacher pay the two-drachma tax?” they asked.
v25: “Yes,” he said. When he entered the house, Jesus spoke first: “What do you think, Simon? From whom do earthly kings collect customs or tribute—from their sons or from others?”
v26: “From others,” he answered. Jesus told him, “Then the sons are free.
v27: But so that we do not cause them to stumble, go to the sea and cast a hook. Take the first fish you catch, and when you open its mouth you will find a stater. Take it and give it to them for me and you.”
</p>

## Notes

v15,20: The ancient term literally means moon-struck; “has seizures” describes the reported symptoms. It is not a general equation of a modern medical condition with demons. Verse 20 says little faith.

v21: The pinned Greek source has no verse 21. The established numbering is retained, so the text continues from verse 20 to verse 22.

v24–27: The two-drachma levy is distinct from the Caesar tribute discussed later. The stater supplies the payment for both Jesus and Peter; the narrative ends with instructions, not an account of catching the fish.

## Vocabulary

v04: **Shelters**: Temporary dwellings or tents, proposed for Jesus, Moses, and Elijah.

v27: **Stater**: A coin here sufficient for two two-drachma payments.
