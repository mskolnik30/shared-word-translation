## Visitors from the east

<p>
v01: After Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea, during King Herod’s reign, magi from the east arrived in Jerusalem.
v02: “Where is the one born king of the Jews?” they asked. “We saw his star at its rising and have come to bow before him.”
v03: When King Herod heard this, he was alarmed, and all Jerusalem with him.
v04: He gathered all the people’s chief priests and scribes and asked them where the Messiah was to be born.
v05: “In Bethlehem of Judea,” they told him, “for this is what was written through the prophet:
v06: ‘And you, Bethlehem in Judah’s land,
are by no means least among Judah’s rulers,
for from you will come a ruler
who will shepherd my people Israel.’”
</p>

<p>
v07: Then Herod secretly summoned the magi and determined from them exactly when the star had appeared.
v08: He sent them to Bethlehem, saying, “Go and search carefully for the child. When you find him, report back to me, so I too can go and bow before him.”
v09: After hearing the king, they went on their way. The star they had seen at its rising went ahead of them until it stopped over the place where the child was.
v10: When they saw the star, they were overwhelmed with joy.
v11: They entered the house and saw the child with Mary his mother. They fell down and bowed before him. Then they opened their treasure chests and offered him gifts: gold, frankincense, and myrrh.
v12: Warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they went home by another route.
</p>

## Escape to Egypt

<p>
v13: After they left, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream. “Get up,” he said. “Take the child and his mother and flee to Egypt. Stay there until I tell you, because Herod is about to search for the child to kill him.”
v14: Joseph got up, took the child and his mother during the night, and left for Egypt.
v15: He stayed there until Herod’s death. This fulfilled what the Lord had spoken through the prophet: “Out of Egypt I called my son.”
</p>

## Herod kills the children

<p>
v16: When Herod realized the magi had outwitted him, he became furious. He sent men to kill all the boys in Bethlehem and the surrounding district who were two years old or younger, according to the time he had learned from the magi.
v17: Then what had been spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled:
v18: “A voice was heard in Ramah,
weeping and great mourning—
Rachel weeping for her children,
refusing to be comforted,
because they are no more.”
</p>

## Return to Nazareth

<p>
v19: After Herod died, an angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt.
v20: “Get up,” he said. “Take the child and his mother and go to the land of Israel, because those who sought the child’s life are dead.”
v21: Joseph got up, took the child and his mother, and entered the land of Israel.
v22: But when he heard that Archelaus ruled Judea in place of his father Herod, he was afraid to go there. Warned in a dream, he withdrew to the region of Galilee.
v23: He settled in a town called Nazareth, fulfilling what was spoken through the prophets: “He will be called a Nazarene.”
</p>

## Notes

v01–02: The visitors are magi; the passage does not call them kings or give their number. “At its rising” can also be read “in the east.”

v16: The victims are boys aged two and under. The violence and the scope Herod orders remain explicit.

v23: Matthew speaks of the prophets in the plural and does not identify a single quotation source here.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Magi**: Learned interpreters of signs, including heavenly phenomena, from the east.

v11: **Myrrh**: An aromatic substance named with gold and frankincense among the gifts.
