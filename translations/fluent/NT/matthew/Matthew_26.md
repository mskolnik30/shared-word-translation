## The Plot Against Jesus

<p>
v01: When Jesus finished all these words, he said to his disciples,
v02: “You know that after two days the Passover comes, and the Son of Man will be handed over to be crucified.”
v03: Then the chief priests and elders of the people gathered in the courtyard of the high priest, who was named Caiaphas,
v04: and they plotted to arrest Jesus by stealth and kill him.
v05: But they said, “Not during the festival, so that there will not be a riot among the people.”
</p>

## A Woman Anoints Jesus

<p>
v06: While Jesus was in Bethany at the house of Simon the leper,
v07: a woman came to him with an alabaster jar of very expensive perfume and poured it on his head while he was reclining at the table.
v08: When the disciples saw this, they became indignant and said, “Why this waste?
v09: This could have been sold for a great deal and the money given to the poor.”
v10: But Jesus knew what they were saying and told them, “Why are you troubling the woman? She has done a beautiful work for me.
v11: You always have the poor with you, but you do not always have me.
v12: When she poured this perfume on my body, she did it to prepare me for burial.
v13: Truly I tell you, wherever this good news is proclaimed in the whole world, what she has done will also be told in memory of her.”
</p>

## Judas Agrees to Hand Jesus Over

<p>
v14: Then one of the twelve, the one called Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests
v15: and said, “What are you willing to give me if I hand him over to you?” They paid him thirty silver coins.
v16: From then on he began looking for an opportunity to hand Jesus over.
</p>

## Passover with the Disciples

<p>
v17: On the first day of Unleavened Bread, the disciples came to Jesus and asked, “Where do you want us to prepare for you to eat the Passover?”
v18: He said, “Go into the city to a certain man and tell him, ‘The Teacher says: My time is near. I will keep the Passover at your house with my disciples.’”
v19: The disciples did as Jesus directed them and prepared the Passover.
v20: When evening came, he was reclining at the table with the twelve.
v21: While they were eating, he said, “Truly I tell you, one of you will hand me over.”
v22: They became deeply grieved and began asking him one after another, “Surely not I, Lord?”
v23: He answered, “The one who dipped his hand with me in the bowl will hand me over.
v24: The Son of Man goes just as it is written about him, but woe to the person through whom the Son of Man is handed over. It would have been better for that person not to have been born.”
v25: Judas, the one handing him over, answered, “Surely not I, Rabbi?” Jesus told him, “You said it.”
</p>

## Bread and Cup

<p>
v26: While they were eating, Jesus took bread, blessed it, broke it, gave it to the disciples, and said, “Take and eat. This is my body.”
v27: Then he took a cup, gave thanks, and gave it to them, saying, “Drink from it, all of you,
v28: because this is my blood of the covenant, poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins.
v29: I tell you, I will certainly not drink again from this fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new with you in my Father's kingdom.”
v30: After singing a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.
</p>

## Peter's Denial Foretold

<p>
v31: Then Jesus said to them, “You will all fall away because of me this night, because it is written,
‘I will strike the shepherd,
and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.’
v32: But after I am raised, I will go ahead of you into Galilee.”
v33: Peter answered him, “Even if everyone else falls away because of you, I will never fall away.”
v34: Jesus told him, “Truly I tell you, this very night, before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times.”
v35: Peter said to him, “Even if I have to die with you, I will never deny you.” All the disciples said the same.
</p>

## Gethsemane

<p>
v36: Then Jesus went with them to a place called Gethsemane and told his disciples, “Sit here while I go over there and pray.”
v37: He took Peter and the two sons of Zebedee with him and began to be grieved and deeply distressed.
v38: Then he said to them, “My soul is deeply grieved, to the point of death. Stay here and watch with me.”
v39: Going a little farther, he fell facedown and prayed, “My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass from me. Yet not as I want, but as you want.”
v40: He came to the disciples and found them sleeping. He said to Peter, “So, could you not watch with me for one hour?
v41: Watch and pray so that you do not enter into testing. The spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak.”
v42: He went away a second time and prayed, “My Father, if this cannot pass unless I drink it, may your will be done.”
v43: He came again and found them sleeping, because their eyes were heavy.
v44: Leaving them, he went away again and prayed a third time, saying the same words.
v45: Then he came to the disciples and said, “Are you still sleeping and resting? Look, the hour has drawn near, and the Son of Man is being handed over into sinners' hands.
v46: Get up; let us go. Look, the one handing me over has drawn near.”
</p>

## Jesus Is Arrested

<p>
v47: While he was still speaking, look—Judas, one of the twelve, arrived. With him was a large crowd with swords and clubs, sent from the chief priests and elders of the people.
v48: The one handing Jesus over had given them a sign: “The one I kiss is he. Arrest him.”
v49: He immediately came to Jesus and said, “Greetings, Rabbi!” and kissed him.
v50: Jesus said to him, “Friend, do what you came for.” Then they came, laid hands on Jesus, and arrested him.
v51: And look—one of those with Jesus reached out his hand, drew his sword, struck the high priest's servant, and cut off his ear.
v52: Then Jesus said to him, “Put your sword back in its place, because all who take the sword will perish by the sword.
v53: Or do you think I cannot call on my Father, and he would at once provide me with more than twelve legions of angels?
v54: But then how would the Scriptures be fulfilled that say it must happen this way?”
v55: At that time Jesus said to the crowd, “Have you come out with swords and clubs to arrest me as though I were a robber? Day after day I sat in the temple teaching, and you did not arrest me.
v56: But all this has happened so that the writings of the prophets may be fulfilled.” Then all the disciples left him and fled.
</p>

## Jesus Before the Council

<p>
v57: Those who arrested Jesus led him away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and elders had gathered.
v58: Peter was following him at a distance, as far as the high priest's courtyard. He went inside and sat with the guards to see how it would end.
v59: The chief priests and the whole council were looking for false testimony against Jesus so they could put him to death,
v60: but they did not find any, though many false witnesses came forward. Finally two came forward
v61: and said, “This man said, ‘I am able to destroy God's temple and rebuild it in three days.’”
v62: The high priest stood and said to Jesus, “Do you answer nothing? What are these people testifying against you?”
v63: But Jesus remained silent. The high priest said to him, “I put you under oath by the living God: tell us if you are the Christ, God's Son.”
v64: Jesus said to him, “You said it. But I tell you, from now on you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power and coming on the clouds of heaven.”
v65: Then the high priest tore his clothes and said, “He has blasphemed! Why do we still need witnesses? Look, now you have heard the blasphemy.
v66: What do you think?” They answered, “He deserves death.”
v67: Then they spit in his face and struck him with their fists. Others slapped him
v68: and said, “Prophesy to us, Christ! Who hit you?”
</p>

## Peter Denies Jesus

<p>
v69: Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard. A servant girl came to him and said, “You also were with Jesus the Galilean.”
v70: But he denied it before everyone: “I do not know what you are talking about.”
v71: When he went out to the gateway, another servant girl saw him and said to those there, “This man was with Jesus the Nazarene.”
v72: Again he denied it with an oath: “I do not know the man.”
v73: After a little while, those standing there came to Peter and said, “Surely you also are one of them, because your accent gives you away.”
v74: Then he began to curse and swear, “I do not know the man!” Immediately a rooster crowed.
v75: Peter remembered what Jesus had said: “Before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times.” He went outside and wept bitterly.
</p>

## Notes

v02: Matthew explicitly announces crucifixion before the plot scene begins.

v15: The thirty silver coins connect Judas's action with scriptural monetary imagery later cited in 27:9–10.

v25: Judas addresses Jesus as “Rabbi,” while the other disciples in v22 say “Lord.”

v28: Matthew adds “for the forgiveness of sins” to the covenant-cup saying, echoing 1:21 and reinforcing forgiveness as a Gospel-wide theme.

v34: Matthew has one rooster crow in the prediction and fulfillment, unlike Mark's twice-crowing form.

v39–44: Matthew develops Jesus' prayer in three movements and explicitly includes “may your will be done,” echoing the Lord's Prayer.

v52: The sword saying is distinctive to Matthew and interprets violent defense in the arrest scene.

v53: “Twelve legions of angels” makes clear that Jesus' refusal of force is not due to lack of available power.

v64: Matthew's “from now on” differs from Mark's form and joins present exaltation language with future coming imagery.

v75: Matthew says Peter wept “bitterly,” intensifying the close of the denial scene.
