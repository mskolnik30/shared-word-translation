## The Plot Against Jesus

<p>
v01: When Jesus had finished saying all this, he told his disciples,
v02: “You know that Passover is two days away, and the Son of Man will be handed over to be crucified.”
v03: Then the chief priests and the elders of the people gathered in the courtyard of the high priest, Caiaphas.
v04: They plotted to arrest Jesus by deception and kill him.
v05: “But not during the festival,” they said, “or there may be a riot among the people.”
</p>

## A Woman Anoints Jesus

<p>
v06: Jesus was in Bethany, at the house of Simon the leper.
v07: While he reclined at the table, a woman came to him with an alabaster jar of very expensive perfume and poured it over his head.
v08: The disciples saw this and were indignant. “Why this waste?” they asked.
v09: “This could have been sold for a large sum and the money given to the poor.”
v10: Aware of this, Jesus said, “Why are you troubling this woman? She has done something beautiful for me.
v11: You always have the poor with you, but you do not always have me.
v12: By pouring this perfume on my body, she has prepared me for burial.
v13: I tell you the truth: wherever this good news is proclaimed throughout the world, what she has done will also be told in memory of her.”
</p>

## Judas Agrees to Hand Jesus Over

<p>
v14: Then one of the Twelve, Judas Iscariot, went to the chief priests.
v15: “What will you give me if I hand him over to you?” he asked. They weighed out thirty silver coins for him.
v16: From then on, he looked for an opportunity to hand Jesus over.
</p>

## Passover with the Disciples

<p>
v17: On the first day of Unleavened Bread, the disciples came to Jesus and asked, “Where do you want us to prepare the Passover meal for you?”
v18: He said, “Go into the city to a certain man and tell him, ‘The Teacher says: My time is near. I will celebrate Passover at your house with my disciples.’”
v19: The disciples did as Jesus directed and prepared the Passover meal.
v20: When evening came, Jesus reclined at the table with the twelve disciples.
v21: While they were eating, he said, “I tell you the truth: one of you will hand me over.”
v22: Deeply distressed, they began asking him one by one, “Surely not I, Lord?”
v23: He answered, “The one who dipped his hand in the bowl with me will hand me over.
v24: The Son of Man goes as it is written about him. But woe to the man through whom the Son of Man is handed over! It would have been better for that man never to have been born.”
v25: Judas, who was handing him over, asked, “Surely not I, Rabbi?” Jesus said to him, “You have said it.”
</p>

## Bread and Cup

<p>
v26: As they ate, Jesus took bread, spoke a blessing, and broke it. He gave it to the disciples and said, “Take, eat; this is my body.”
v27: Then he took a cup, gave thanks, and gave it to them. “Drink from it, all of you,” he said,
v28: “for this is my blood of the covenant, poured out for many for the forgiveness of sins.
v29: I tell you, from now on I will not drink this fruit of the vine until the day I drink it new with you in my Father’s kingdom.”
v30: After singing a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.
</p>

## Peter's Denial Foretold

<p>
v31: Then Jesus told them, “Tonight you will all fall away because of me, for it is written,
‘I will strike the shepherd,
and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.’
v32: But after I am raised, I will go ahead of you into Galilee.”
v33: Peter answered, “Even if everyone falls away because of you, I never will.”
v34: Jesus told him, “I tell you the truth: tonight, before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times.”
v35: Peter said, “Even if I must die with you, I will never deny you!” All the disciples said the same.
</p>

## Gethsemane

<p>
v36: Then Jesus went with them to a place called Gethsemane. He told his disciples, “Sit here while I go over there to pray.”
v37: He took Peter and the two sons of Zebedee with him and began to feel sorrow and anguish.
v38: He told them, “My soul is overwhelmed with sorrow, to the point of death. Stay here and keep watch with me.”
v39: Going a little farther, he fell facedown and prayed, “My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass from me. Yet not as I will, but as you will.”
v40: He came back to the disciples and found them sleeping. He said to Peter, “Could you not keep watch with me for even one hour?
v41: Keep watch and pray, so that you do not enter into temptation. The spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak.”
v42: He went away a second time and prayed, “My Father, if this cannot pass unless I drink it, let your will be done.”
v43: He returned and again found them sleeping, for their eyes were heavy.
v44: Leaving them, he went away again and prayed a third time, saying the same words.
v45: Then he came to the disciples and said, “Are you still sleeping and resting? Look, the hour has come. The Son of Man is being handed over into the hands of sinners.
v46: Get up; let us go. Look, the one handing me over is near.”
</p>

## Jesus Is Arrested

<p>
v47: While he was still speaking, Judas, one of the Twelve, arrived. With him was a large crowd carrying swords and clubs, sent by the chief priests and elders of the people.
v48: The man handing him over had given them a signal: “The one I kiss is the man. Arrest him.”
v49: He immediately went up to Jesus. “Greetings, Rabbi!” he said, and kissed him.
v50: Jesus said to him, “Friend, do what you came for.” Then they stepped forward, seized Jesus, and arrested him.
v51: One of those with Jesus reached for his sword, drew it, and struck the high priest’s slave, cutting off his ear.
v52: Jesus said to him, “Put your sword back where it belongs. Everyone who takes up the sword will perish by the sword.
v53: Do you think I cannot appeal to my Father, and he would at once provide me with more than twelve legions of angels?
v54: But how, then, would the Scriptures be fulfilled, which say it must happen this way?”
v55: At that moment Jesus said to the crowds, “Have you come out with swords and clubs to arrest me as though I were a bandit? Day after day I sat teaching in the temple, and you did not arrest me.
v56: But all this has happened so that the writings of the prophets may be fulfilled.” Then all the disciples abandoned him and fled.
</p>

## Jesus Before the Council

<p>
v57: Those who arrested Jesus led him to Caiaphas, the high priest, where the scribes and elders had gathered.
v58: Peter followed at a distance, as far as the high priest’s courtyard. He went inside and sat with the attendants to see how it would end.
v59: The chief priests and the whole council were looking for false testimony against Jesus so they could put him to death.
v60: Although many false witnesses came forward, they found none. At last two came forward
v61: and said, “This man said, ‘I can destroy God’s sanctuary and rebuild it in three days.’”
v62: The high priest stood up and asked him, “Have you no answer? What is this testimony against you?”
v63: But Jesus remained silent. The high priest said, “I put you under oath by the living God: tell us whether you are the Christ, the Son of God.”
v64: Jesus answered, “You have said it. But I tell you, from now on you will see the Son of Man seated at the right hand of Power and coming on the clouds of heaven.”
v65: Then the high priest tore his clothes. “He has blasphemed!” he said. “Why do we need any more witnesses? You have now heard the blasphemy.
v66: What is your judgment?” They answered, “He deserves death.”
v67: Then they spat in his face and struck him with their fists. Others slapped him,
v68: saying, “Prophesy to us, Christ! Who hit you?”
</p>

## Peter Denies Jesus

<p>
v69: Meanwhile Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard. A servant girl came up to him and said, “You too were with Jesus the Galilean.”
v70: But he denied it in front of everyone. “I do not know what you are talking about,” he said.
v71: When he went out to the gateway, another servant girl saw him and told the people there, “This man was with Jesus of Nazareth.”
v72: Again he denied it, this time with an oath: “I do not know the man.”
v73: A little later the bystanders came up to Peter. “You certainly are one of them,” they said. “Your accent gives you away.”
v74: Then he began to call down curses and swear, “I do not know the man!” Immediately a rooster crowed.
v75: Peter remembered what Jesus had said: “Before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times.” He went outside and wept bitterly.
</p>

## Notes

v20: The pinned Greek includes “disciples” after “twelve.”

v25: Judas says “Rabbi”; the other disciples say “Lord” in verse 22. “You have said it” retains Jesus’ indirect response.

v28: The pinned Greek reads “the covenant,” without “new,” and includes “for the forgiveness of sins.”

v34: Matthew’s prediction has one rooster-crowing marker. It is not changed to Mark’s twice-crowing form.

v40: Jesus addresses Peter, but “you” in the question is plural, including the others with him.

v45: The opening can also be punctuated as a statement or command: “Sleep on now and rest.” Greek manuscripts do not supply the modern punctuation that settles this choice.

v50: Jesus’ words to Judas are elliptical. “Do what you came for” is one reading; they may also be understood as asking why he has come.

v64: “Power” is a reverent designation for God. The right-hand and cloud images recall Psalm 110:1 and Daniel 7:13.

v74: The text does not specify the target of Peter’s curses.

## Vocabulary

v06: **Leper**: An ancient designation for a person with a serious skin condition; it need not identify modern Hansen’s disease.

v15: **Hand over**: Greek paradidōmi links betrayal, arrest, and delivery into another’s custody throughout the account.

v28: **Covenant**: A binding relationship or commitment; the blood language recalls Exodus 24:8.

v39: **Cup**: Here an image for the suffering Jesus faces.

v41: **Temptation**: Greek peirasmos can also mean testing or trial.

v53: **Legion**: A large Roman military unit, here applied to angels; the text gives no exact total of angels.

v61: **Sanctuary**: Greek naos refers to the temple building or sanctuary; hieron can refer more broadly to the temple precinct.
