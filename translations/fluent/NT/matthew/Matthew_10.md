## The Twelve Sent Out

<p>
v01: Jesus called his twelve disciples and gave them authority over unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal every disease and every sickness.
v02: These are the names of the twelve apostles: first, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother; James son of Zebedee and John his brother;
v03: Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James son of Alphaeus and Thaddaeus;
v04: Simon the Cananaean and Judas Iscariot, who also handed Jesus over.
</p>

<p>
v05: Jesus sent out these twelve and instructed them: “Do not go onto a road of the Gentiles, and do not enter a Samaritan town.
v06: Go instead to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.
v07: As you go, proclaim, ‘The kingdom of heaven has drawn near.’
v08: Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse those with skin diseases, and cast out demons. You received freely; give freely.
v09: Do not acquire gold, silver, or copper for your belts,
v10: no bag for the journey, no second tunic, sandals, or staff, because the worker deserves food.
v11: Whatever town or village you enter, find out who there is worthy, and stay there until you leave.
v12: When you enter a house, greet it.
v13: If the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it. But if it is not worthy, let your peace return to you.
v14: If anyone will not welcome you or listen to your words, leave that house or town and shake the dust off your feet.
v15: Truly I tell you, it will be more tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah on the day of judgment than for that town.
</p>

## Witness Under Persecution

<p>
v16: “Look, I am sending you out like sheep among wolves. Therefore be wise as snakes and innocent as doves.
v17: Be on guard against people, because they will hand you over to councils and flog you in their synagogues.
v18: You will be brought before governors and kings because of me, as a testimony to them and to the Gentiles.
v19: When they hand you over, do not be anxious about how or what you should speak, because what you are to say will be given to you in that hour.
v20: For it is not you speaking, but the Spirit of your Father speaking through you.
v21: Brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his child. Children will rise against parents and have them put to death.
v22: You will be hated by everyone because of my name. But the one who endures to the end will be saved.
v23: When they persecute you in one town, flee to the next. Truly I tell you, you will certainly not finish the towns of Israel before the Son of Man comes.
</p>

## Fear God, Not People

<p>
v24: “A disciple is not above the teacher, nor a servant above the master.
v25: It is enough for the disciple to become like the teacher and the servant like the master. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more the members of his household!
v26: Therefore do not be afraid of them, because nothing is covered that will not be revealed, and nothing hidden that will not become known.
v27: What I tell you in the dark, say in the light; and what you hear whispered in your ear, proclaim from the housetops.
v28: Do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the life. Fear instead the one who can destroy both life and body in Gehenna.
v29: Are not two sparrows sold for a small coin? Yet not one of them falls to the ground apart from your Father.
v30: Even the hairs of your head have all been counted.
v31: So do not be afraid. You are worth more than many sparrows.
</p>

## Confessing Jesus

<p>
v32: “Therefore everyone who acknowledges me before people, I also will acknowledge before my Father in heaven.
v33: But whoever denies me before people, I also will deny before my Father in heaven.
</p>

## The Cost of Allegiance

<p>
v34: “Do not think that I came to bring peace to the earth. I did not come to bring peace, but a sword.
v35: For I came to set
‘a man against his father,
a daughter against her mother,
and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;
v36: and a person's enemies will be members of their own household.’
v37: Whoever loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me, and whoever loves son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me.
v38: Whoever does not take up their cross and follow after me is not worthy of me.
v39: Whoever finds their life will lose it, and whoever loses their life because of me will find it.
</p>

## Receiving the Sent Ones

<p>
v40: “Whoever welcomes you welcomes me, and whoever welcomes me welcomes the one who sent me.
v41: Whoever welcomes a prophet because they are a prophet will receive a prophet's reward, and whoever welcomes a righteous person because they are righteous will receive a righteous person's reward.
v42: And whoever gives even a cup of cold water to one of these little ones because they are a disciple—truly I tell you, that person will certainly not lose their reward.”
</p>

## Notes

v05–06: Matthew's initial mission is explicitly focused on Israel. This does not erase the later universal commission in 28:19.

v08: SBLGNT includes “raise the dead.” Fluent follows the pinned text and does not harmonize the mission list to Mark.

v09–10: Matthew's travel instructions differ in details from Mark and Luke. Each Gospel's wording is preserved rather than blended.

v17–22: `paradidōmi`, “hand over,” becomes a mission/persecution term before it becomes a central passion term.

v23: The timing of “before the Son of Man comes” is difficult. Fluent leaves the saying intact rather than assigning it to one proposed fulfillment.

v28: `psychē` can mean life, self, or soul. “Life” keeps the contrast with body without importing a later body/soul scheme more sharply than the Greek requires.

v34–39: The “sword” is explained by the family division that follows. The passage describes the disruptive cost of allegiance rather than a command to violence.

v38: The cross appears as discipleship imagery before Jesus' own crucifixion is narrated.
