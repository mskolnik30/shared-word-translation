## The twelve sent out

<p>
v01: Jesus called his twelve disciples to him and gave them authority over unclean spirits, to drive them out and to heal every disease and every infirmity.
v02: These are the names of the twelve apostles: first, Simon, called Peter, and his brother Andrew; James son of Zebedee and his brother John;
v03: Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James son of Alphaeus and Thaddaeus;
v04: Simon the Zealot and Judas Iscariot, who also handed Jesus over.
</p>

## Instructions for the journey

<p>
v05: Jesus sent these twelve out with these instructions: “Do not go among the Gentiles or enter a Samaritan town.
v06: Go instead to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.
v07: As you go, proclaim, ‘The kingdom of heaven has come near.’
v08: Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse those with skin diseases, drive out demons. You received without paying; give without charging.
v09: Do not get gold, silver, or copper for your belts,
v10: or a travel bag, or two tunics, or sandals, or a staff. A worker deserves food.
v11: Whatever town or village you enter, find out who there is worthy, and stay with that person until you leave.
v12: As you enter the house, greet it.
v13: If the house is worthy, let your peace rest on it. If it is not worthy, let your peace return to you.
v14: If anyone will not welcome you or listen to your words, shake the dust from your feet as you leave that house or town.
v15: Truly I tell you, on the day of judgment it will be more bearable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrah than for that town.
</p>

## Opposition and endurance

<p>
v16: “Look, I am sending you out like sheep among wolves. So be shrewd as snakes and innocent as doves.
v17: Watch out for people. They will hand you over to councils and flog you in their synagogues.
v18: You will be brought before governors and kings because of me, as a testimony to them and to the Gentiles.
v19: When they hand you over, do not worry about how to speak or what to say. What you should say will be given to you at that time.
v20: For it will not be you speaking, but the Spirit of your Father speaking through you.
v21: Brother will hand brother over to death, and a father his child. Children will turn against their parents and have them put to death.
v22: Everyone will hate you because of my name. But the one who endures to the end will be saved.
v23: When they persecute you in one town, flee to another. Truly I tell you, you will not finish the towns of Israel before the Son of Man comes.
</p>

## Do not be afraid

<p>
v24: “A disciple is not above the teacher, nor a slave above the master.
v25: It is enough for the disciple to become like the teacher, and the slave like the master. If they have called the head of the house Beelzebul, how much more will they malign his household!
v26: So do not fear them. Nothing covered up will stay unrevealed, and nothing hidden will remain unknown.
v27: What I tell you in the dark, speak in the light. What you hear whispered in your ear, proclaim from the rooftops.
v28: Do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul. Fear instead the one who can destroy both soul and body in Gehenna.
v29: Aren’t two sparrows sold for an assarion? Yet not one of them falls to the ground apart from your Father.
v30: Even the hairs on your head have all been counted.
v31: So do not be afraid. You are worth more than many sparrows.
</p>

## Allegiance and division

<p>
v32: “Everyone who acknowledges me before others, I too will acknowledge before my Father in heaven.
v33: But whoever denies me before others, I too will deny before my Father in heaven.
</p>

<p>
v34: “Do not think I came to bring peace to the earth. I came to bring a sword, not peace.
v35: For I came to set a man against his father, a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law.
v36: A person’s enemies will be members of that person’s own household.
v37: Whoever loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me. Whoever loves son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me.
v38: Whoever does not take up their cross and follow me is not worthy of me.
v39: Whoever finds their life will lose it, and whoever loses their life because of me will find it.
</p>

## Welcoming the sent ones

<p>
v40: “Whoever welcomes you welcomes me, and whoever welcomes me welcomes the one who sent me.
v41: Whoever welcomes a prophet because that person is a prophet will receive a prophet’s reward. Whoever welcomes a righteous person because that person is righteous will receive a righteous person’s reward.
v42: And whoever gives even a cup of cold water to one of these little ones because that person is a disciple—truly I tell you, that giver will certainly not lose the reward.”
</p>

## Notes

v04,09–10: Simon’s designation concerns zeal, not Canaanite ancestry. The equipment restrictions follow Matthew’s wording, including the staff.

v23,28–29: The timing of the Son of Man’s coming is not resolved in translation. Soul and body remain distinct in verse 28. “Apart from your Father” does not specify whether knowledge, permission, or action is meant.

v34–39: The sword image is followed by family division and costly allegiance. The text retains the cross as an execution image.

## Vocabulary

v02: **Apostles**: Those sent out with a commission; here the twelve named disciples.

v29: **Assarion**: A small coin; the saying values the disciples above inexpensive sparrows.
