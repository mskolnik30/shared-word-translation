## The king enters Jerusalem

<p>
v01: As they approached Jerusalem and came to Bethphage on the Mount of Olives, Jesus sent two disciples ahead.
v02: He told them, “Go into the village opposite you. You will immediately find a donkey tied there, with a colt beside her. Untie them and bring them to me.
v03: If anyone says anything to you, say, ‘The Lord needs them,’ and he will send them at once.”
v04: This happened to fulfill what was spoken through the prophet:
v05: “Tell daughter Zion:
Look, your king is coming to you,
gentle and riding on a donkey,
and on a colt, the foal of a beast of burden.”
v06: The disciples went and did just as Jesus had instructed.
v07: They brought the donkey and the colt, placed their garments on them, and he sat on them.
v08: Most of the crowd spread their garments on the road. Others cut branches from the trees and spread them on the road.
v09: The crowds ahead of him and behind him kept shouting:
“Hosanna to the Son of David!
Blessed is the one who comes in the Lord’s name!
Hosanna in the highest!”
v10: When he entered Jerusalem, the whole city was shaken. “Who is this?” people asked.
v11: The crowds answered, “This is the prophet Jesus, from Nazareth in Galilee.”
</p>

## Jesus in the temple

<p>
v12: Jesus entered the temple and drove out everyone selling and buying there. He overturned the money changers’ tables and the seats of those selling doves.
v13: He told them, “It is written, ‘My house will be called a house of prayer,’ but you are making it a robbers’ den.”
v14: Blind and lame people came to him in the temple, and he healed them.
v15: The chief priests and scribes saw the wonders he did and the children shouting in the temple, “Hosanna to the Son of David!” They were indignant
v16: and asked him, “Do you hear what they are saying?” “Yes,” Jesus answered. “Have you never read, ‘From the mouths of infants and nursing babies you have prepared praise’?”
v17: He left them, went out of the city to Bethany, and spent the night there.
</p>

## The fig tree

<p>
v18: Early in the morning, as he returned to the city, he was hungry.
v19: He saw a fig tree beside the road and went to it, but found nothing on it except leaves. He said to it, “May no fruit ever come from you again.” At once the fig tree withered.
v20: The disciples saw it and were amazed. “How did the fig tree wither at once?” they asked.
v21: Jesus answered them, “Truly I tell you, if you have faith and do not doubt, you will do not only what happened to the fig tree. Even if you tell this mountain, ‘Be lifted up and thrown into the sea,’ it will happen.
v22: Whatever you ask for in prayer with faith, you will receive.”
</p>

## Authority questioned

<p>
v23: He entered the temple and was teaching when the chief priests and elders of the people approached him. “By what authority are you doing these things?” they asked. “Who gave you this authority?”
v24: Jesus answered, “I will ask you one question too. If you answer it, I will tell you by what authority I do these things.
v25: Where did John’s baptism come from—heaven or human beings?” They discussed it among themselves: “If we say, ‘Heaven,’ he will ask, ‘Why didn’t you believe him?’
v26: But if we say, ‘Human beings’—we fear the crowd, because everyone regards John as a prophet.”
v27: So they answered Jesus, “We don’t know.” He told them, “Neither will I tell you by what authority I do these things.
</p>

## Two sons

<p>
v28: “What do you think? A man had two sons. He went to the first and said, ‘Child, go work in the vineyard today.’
v29: ‘I don’t want to,’ he answered. But later he changed his mind and went.
v30: The father went to the second and said the same thing. ‘I will, sir,’ he answered, but he did not go.
v31: Which of the two did his father’s will?” “The first,” they said. Jesus told them, “Truly I tell you, tax collectors and prostitutes are going ahead of you into the kingdom of God.
v32: For John came to you in the way of righteousness, and you did not believe him. But tax collectors and prostitutes did. Even after seeing this, you did not change your minds and believe him.
</p>

## The vineyard tenants

<p>
v33: “Listen to another parable. A landowner planted a vineyard, put a fence around it, dug a winepress in it, and built a tower. He leased it to tenant farmers and went abroad.
v34: When harvest time approached, he sent his slaves to the tenants to collect his produce.
v35: The tenants seized his slaves, beat one, killed another, and stoned another.
v36: He sent other slaves, more than before, and the tenants treated them the same way.
v37: Finally he sent his son to them, saying, ‘They will respect my son.’
v38: But when the tenants saw the son, they said to one another, ‘This is the heir. Come, let’s kill him and take his inheritance.’
v39: They seized him, threw him outside the vineyard, and killed him.
v40: So when the vineyard’s owner comes, what will he do to those tenants?”
v41: They told him, “He will bring those evil men to an evil end and lease the vineyard to other tenants who will give him the produce at harvest time.”
v42: Jesus said to them, “Have you never read in the Scriptures:
‘The stone the builders rejected
has become the cornerstone.
This came from the Lord,
and it is marvelous in our eyes’?
v43: That is why I tell you: the kingdom of God will be taken from you and given to a people who produce its fruit.
v44: The one who falls on this stone will be shattered, and anyone on whom it falls will be crushed.”
v45: When the chief priests and Pharisees heard his parables, they knew he was speaking about them.
v46: They wanted to arrest him, but feared the crowds, who regarded him as a prophet.
</p>

## Notes

v05–07: Matthew names both donkey and colt. The final “them” in verse 7 most naturally refers to the garments, but the plural wording is preserved.

v16,29–31: The quoted psalm says praise in Matthew’s Greek. The first son refuses then goes in this pinned sequence, and the answer is “the first.”

v43–45: The people receiving the kingdom are described by producing its fruit, without an ethnic name supplied. The leaders recognize themselves as the parables’ targets. Verse 44 is printed in the pinned source.

## Vocabulary

v09: **Hosanna**: A cry derived from a plea for salvation, used here in acclamation.

v42: **Cornerstone**: The head-of-the-corner stone image expresses the rejected stone’s decisive new place.
