## Jesus continues teaching

<p>
v01: When Jesus finished instructing his twelve disciples, he left that place to teach and proclaim his message in their towns.
</p>

## John’s question

<p>
v02: In prison, John heard about the Messiah’s deeds. He sent word through his disciples
v03: to ask Jesus, “Are you the one who is coming, or should we expect someone else?”
v04: Jesus answered them, “Go and tell John what you hear and see:
v05: the blind receive sight, the lame walk, people with skin diseases are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised, and the poor hear good news.
v06: And blessed is anyone who does not stumble because of me.”
</p>

## Jesus speaks about John

<p>
v07: As they were leaving, Jesus began speaking to the crowds about John. “What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed swaying in the wind?
v08: What did you go out to see, then? A man dressed in soft clothes? People who wear soft clothes live in royal houses.
v09: Then what did you go out for? To see a prophet? Yes, I tell you, and more than a prophet.
v10: This is the one about whom it is written:
‘Look, I am sending my messenger ahead of you;
he will prepare your way before you.’
v11: Truly I tell you, among those born of women no one greater than John the Baptist has arisen. Yet the least in the kingdom of heaven is greater than he is.
v12: From the days of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of heaven has suffered violence, and violent people seize it.
v13: For all the Prophets and the Law prophesied until John.
v14: And if you are willing to accept it, he is Elijah who is to come.
v15: Let anyone with ears listen.
</p>

<p>
v16: “What can I compare this generation to? It is like children sitting in the marketplaces, calling to the others,
v17: ‘We played the flute for you, and you didn’t dance;
we sang a lament, and you didn’t beat your breasts.’
v18: John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, ‘He has a demon.’
v19: The Son of Man came eating and drinking, and they say, ‘Look at him—a glutton and a drunkard, a friend of tax collectors and sinners!’ Yet wisdom is vindicated by her deeds.”
</p>

## Towns that did not repent

<p>
v20: Then he began denouncing the towns where most of his mighty works had taken place, because they had not repented.
v21: “Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! If the mighty works done among you had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes.
v22: But I tell you, on the day of judgment it will be more bearable for Tyre and Sidon than for you.
v23: And you, Capernaum—will you be lifted up to heaven? You will go down to Hades. If the mighty works done in you had been done in Sodom, it would have remained until today.
v24: But I tell you, on the day of judgment it will be more bearable for the land of Sodom than for you.”
</p>

## Revelation and rest

<p>
v25: At that time Jesus said, “I praise you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because you have hidden these things from the wise and discerning and revealed them to little children.
v26: Yes, Father, for this was your good pleasure.
v27: “Everything has been handed over to me by my Father. No one knows the Son except the Father, and no one knows the Father except the Son and anyone to whom the Son chooses to reveal him.
v28: Come to me, all of you who are weary from labor and weighed down, and I will give you rest.
v29: Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, because I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.
v30: For my yoke is kind, and my burden is light.”
</p>

## Notes

v12: “Has suffered violence” is one reading of biazetai. Another is “advances forcefully.” The identity and role of the forceful people make the whole saying disputed.

v19,30: The pinned text vindicates wisdom by her deeds, not her children. “Kind” renders chrestos; “easy” or “good” are also possible descriptions of the yoke.

## Vocabulary

v23: **Hades**: The realm of the dead, used in contrast with being raised to heaven.

v29: **Yoke**: A harness image for bearing a load under guidance; it remains joined to learning and rest.
