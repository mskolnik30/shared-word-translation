## Workers in the vineyard

<p>
v01: “For the kingdom of heaven is like a landowner who went out early in the morning to hire workers for his vineyard.
v02: He agreed with the workers on a denarius for the day and sent them into his vineyard.
v03: About the third hour he went out and saw others standing in the marketplace without work.
v04: He told them, ‘You go into the vineyard too, and I will pay you whatever is right.’
v05: So they went. He went out again about the sixth and ninth hours and did the same.
v06: About the eleventh hour he went out and found others standing there. ‘Why have you stood here all day without work?’ he asked.
v07: ‘Because no one has hired us,’ they answered. He told them, ‘You go into the vineyard too.’
v08: When evening came, the vineyard owner told his manager, ‘Call the workers and pay their wages, beginning with the last and ending with the first.’
v09: Those hired about the eleventh hour came and each received a denarius.
v10: When the first came, they expected more. But they too received a denarius each.
v11: After receiving it, they grumbled against the landowner.
v12: ‘These last worked one hour,’ they said, ‘and you have made them equal to us, who bore the day’s burden and burning heat.’
v13: He answered one of them, ‘Friend, I am not wronging you. Didn’t you agree with me on a denarius?
v14: Take what is yours and go. I want to give this last worker the same as you.
v15: Am I not free to do what I want with what is mine? Or is your eye evil because I am good?’
v16: So the last will be first, and the first last.”
</p>

## The road to Jerusalem

<p>
v17: As Jesus went up to Jerusalem, he took the twelve disciples aside. On the road he told them,
v18: “Look, we are going up to Jerusalem. The Son of Man will be handed over to the chief priests and scribes, and they will condemn him to death.
v19: They will hand him over to the Gentiles to be mocked, flogged, and crucified. And on the third day he will be raised.”
</p>

## Greatness through service

<p>
v20: Then the mother of Zebedee’s sons came to him with her sons. Bowing before him, she asked him for something.
v21: “What do you want?” he asked her. She said, “Command that these two sons of mine sit beside you in your kingdom, one at your right and one at your left.”
v22: Jesus answered, “You do not know what you are asking. Can you drink the cup I am about to drink?” “We can,” they told him.
v23: He told them, “You will drink my cup. But the seats at my right and left are not mine to give; they belong to those for whom my Father has prepared them.”
v24: When the ten heard this, they were indignant with the two brothers.
</p>

<p>
v25: Jesus called them to him and said, “You know that the rulers of the Gentiles lord it over them, and their great ones wield authority over them.
v26: It will not be that way among you. Whoever wants to become great among you will be your servant,
v27: and whoever wants to be first among you will be your slave,
v28: just as the Son of Man came to serve, not to be served, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”
</p>

## Two blind men near Jericho

<p>
v29: As they left Jericho, a large crowd followed him.
v30: Two blind men were sitting beside the road. When they heard Jesus was passing by, they cried out, “Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!”
v31: The crowd rebuked them, telling them to be quiet. But they cried out all the louder, “Lord, have mercy on us, Son of David!”
v32: Jesus stopped, called them, and asked, “What do you want me to do for you?”
v33: “Lord,” they answered, “let our eyes be opened.”
v34: Moved with compassion, Jesus touched their eyes. Immediately they received sight and followed him.
</p>

## Notes

v03–06: The third, sixth, ninth, and eleventh hours count through the daylight working period. The last group says no one has hired them.

v15,22–23: An evil eye is an image of envy or grudging generosity. The cup language is retained; baptism wording is not printed in these verses of the pinned source.

## Vocabulary

v02: **Denarius**: The agreed daily wage in this story; no modern currency equivalent is imposed.

v28: **Ransom**: A release-price image for giving his life for many; the verse does not name a recipient of payment.
