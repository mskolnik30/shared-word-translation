## Into Judea

<p>
v01: When Jesus finished these words, he left Galilee and came to the region of Judea beyond the Jordan.
v02: Large crowds followed him, and he healed them there.
</p>

## A question about divorce

<p>
v03: Pharisees approached to test him. “Is a man permitted to divorce his wife for any reason?” they asked.
v04: He answered, “Haven’t you read that the Creator made them male and female from the beginning,
v05: and said, ‘For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh’?
v06: So they are no longer two, but one flesh. Therefore, what God has joined together, no human being should separate.”
v07: They asked him, “Why then did Moses command giving her a certificate of divorce and sending her away?”
v08: He told them, “Because of your hard hearts Moses permitted you to divorce your wives. But it was not that way from the beginning.
v09: I tell you, whoever divorces his wife, except on grounds of sexual immorality, and marries another woman commits adultery. And whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery.”
</p>

<p>
v10: The disciples said to him, “If that is the situation between a man and his wife, it is better not to marry.”
v11: He told them, “Not everyone can accept this saying, but only those to whom it has been given.
v12: For some eunuchs were born that way from their mother’s womb; others were made eunuchs by people; and others made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven. Let anyone who can accept this accept it.”
</p>

## Jesus welcomes children

<p>
v13: Then children were brought to him so that he could lay his hands on them and pray. But the disciples rebuked them.
v14: Jesus said, “Let the children come to me. Do not stop them, for the kingdom of heaven belongs to such as these.”
v15: He laid his hands on them and went on from there.
</p>

## Possessions and eternal life

<p>
v16: A man came up to him and asked, “Teacher, what good thing must I do to have eternal life?”
v17: He answered, “Why do you ask me about what is good? There is one who is good. If you want to enter life, keep the commandments.”
v18: “Which ones?” he asked. Jesus answered, “‘Do not murder. Do not commit adultery. Do not steal. Do not give false testimony.
v19: Honor your father and mother,’ and, ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’”
v20: The young man said to him, “I have kept all these. What do I still lack?”
v21: Jesus told him, “If you want to be complete, go, sell your possessions, and give to the poor. You will have treasure in heaven. Then come, follow me.”
v22: When the young man heard this, he went away distressed, because he had many possessions.
</p>

## Who can be saved?

<p>
v23: Jesus told his disciples, “Truly I tell you, it is hard for a rich person to enter the kingdom of heaven.
v24: Again I tell you, it is easier for a camel to pass through a needle’s hole than for a rich person to enter the kingdom of God.”
v25: When the disciples heard this, they were utterly astonished. “Who, then, can be saved?” they asked.
v26: Jesus looked at them and said, “For human beings this is impossible, but for God all things are possible.”
</p>

## The renewal and the reward

<p>
v27: Then Peter replied, “Look, we have left everything and followed you. What will there be for us?”
v28: Jesus told them, “Truly I tell you, at the renewal, when the Son of Man sits on his glorious throne, you who have followed me will also sit on twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.
v29: And everyone who has left houses, brothers, sisters, father, mother, wife, children, or fields because of my name will receive a hundredfold and inherit eternal life.
v30: But many who are first will be last, and the last first.
</p>

## Notes

v09: Both the porneia exception and the final clause about marrying a divorced woman are printed in this pinned Greek edition. The scope of porneia is disputed; the draft uses sexual immorality.

v12: The eunuch saying distinguishes birth, human action, and a kingdom-directed choice. Its final category uses stark figurative language for renouncing marriage; it is not an instruction for bodily injury.

v17,24,29: Verse 17 asks about the good rather than calling Jesus good. The camel and needle remain a striking image without an invented gate. Wife is included in the pinned verse 29.

## Vocabulary

v21: **Complete**: Teleios, also used in 5:48, conveys completeness or maturity.

v28: **Renewal**: Palingenesia, a new beginning associated here with the Son of Man’s enthronement.
