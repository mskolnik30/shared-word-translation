## Timothy Is Sent to Strengthen You

<p>
v01: So when we could bear it no longer, we decided to be left alone in Athens,
v02: and we sent Timothy, our brother and God's coworker in Christ's good news, to strengthen and encourage you in your faith,
v03: so that no one would be shaken by these afflictions. You yourselves know that we are destined for them.
v04: Even when we were with you, we kept warning you that we were going to suffer affliction, and it happened just as you know.
v05: For this reason, when I could bear it no longer, I sent to learn about your faith, fearing that somehow the tempter had tempted you and our labor had become empty.
</p>

## Timothy Brings Good News about Your Trust and Love

<p>
v06: But Timothy has just now returned to us from you and brought us good news about your faith and love. He told us that you always remember us kindly and long to see us, just as we long to see you.
v07: Therefore, brothers and sisters, in all our distress and affliction we were encouraged about you through your faith.
v08: For now we truly live, if you stand firm in the Lord.
v09: How can we repay God with enough thanks for you, for all the joy we feel because of you before our God?
v10: Night and day we pray most earnestly that we may see you face to face and supply what is lacking in your faith.
</p>

## A Prayer for Love and Holiness

<p>
v11: Now may our God and Father himself, and our Lord Jesus, direct our way to you.
v12: And may the Lord make your love increase and overflow for one another and for everyone, just as ours does for you,
v13: so that he may strengthen your hearts, blameless in holiness before our God and Father, at the coming of our Lord Jesus with all his saints.
</p>
