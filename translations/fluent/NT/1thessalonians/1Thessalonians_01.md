## Greeting and Thanksgiving

<p>
v01: Paul, Silvanus and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ: Grace and peace to you.
v02: We always thank God for all of you, mentioning you in our prayers.
v03: Before our God and Father, we continually remember your work produced by faith, your labor prompted by love and your endurance inspired by hope in our Lord Jesus Christ.
v04: Brothers and sisters loved by God, we know that he has chosen you,
v05: because our good news came to you not in word alone, but also in power, in the Holy Spirit and with deep conviction. You know what kind of people we became among you for your sake.
v06: You became imitators of us and of the Lord, receiving the word in severe affliction with joy from the Holy Spirit.
v07: As a result, you became an example to all the believers in Macedonia and Achaia.
v08: For the Lord's word rang out from you not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but your faith toward God has gone out everywhere, so that we need not say anything.
v09: They themselves report what kind of reception we had among you and how you turned to God from idols, to serve the living and true God
v10: and to wait for his Son from heaven, whom he raised from the dead—Jesus, who rescues us from the coming wrath.
</p>
