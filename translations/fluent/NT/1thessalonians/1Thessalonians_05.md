## The Day of the Lord

<p>
v01: Now concerning the times and seasons, brothers and sisters, you have no need for anything to be written to you.
v02: For you yourselves know very well that the Lord’s day comes like a thief in the night.
v03: When people are saying, “Peace and security,” then sudden destruction comes upon them like labor pains on a pregnant woman, and they will certainly not escape.
v04: But you, brothers and sisters, are not in darkness so that the day should overtake you like a thief.
v05: For you are all children of light and children of day. We do not belong to night or darkness.
v06: Therefore let us not sleep like the rest, but let us stay awake and sober.
v07: For those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who get drunk, get drunk at night.
v08: But since we belong to the day, let us be sober, putting on the breastplate of trust and love and the helmet of the hope of salvation.
v09: For God did not appoint us for wrath but for obtaining salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,
v10: who died for us so that whether we are awake or asleep, we may live together with him.
v11: Therefore encourage one another and build each other up, just as you are doing.
</p>

## Life Together in the Community

<p>
v12: We ask you, brothers and sisters, to recognize those who labor among you, lead you in the Lord, and warn you,
v13: and to regard them very highly in love because of their work. Be at peace among yourselves.
v14: We urge you, brothers and sisters: warn the disorderly, encourage the discouraged, support the weak, and be patient with everyone.
v15: See that no one repays evil for evil to anyone, but always pursue what is good for one another and for everyone.
v16: Rejoice always.
v17: Pray without ceasing.
v18: Give thanks in everything, because this is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus.
v19: Do not extinguish the Spirit.
v20: Do not despise prophecies,
v21: but test everything; hold fast to what is good.
v22: Stay away from every form of evil.
</p>

## Final Prayer and Greeting

<p>
v23: May the God of peace himself make you holy completely, and may your whole spirit, life, and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.
v24: The one who calls you is faithful; he will also do it.
v25: Brothers and sisters, pray for us too.
v26: Greet all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss.
v27: I charge you by the Lord to have this letter read to all the brothers and sisters.
v28: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.
</p>

## Notes

v01–03: Paul redirects curiosity about end-times timing into watchfulness; the day is unpredictable “like a thief.”

v03: “Peace and security” may echo imperial public rhetoric, but the verse does not identify one specific slogan or regime as its sole referent.

v04–08: Light/day language becomes ethical identity: wakefulness, sobriety, trust, love, and hope.

v08: Paul adapts armor imagery around trust, love, and hope of salvation.

v10: “Awake or asleep” likely refers to physically alive or dead in continuity with chapter 4, though it also plays on the wake/sleep imagery of this chapter.

v12: `proistēmi` can mean lead, care for, or stand before. Fluent uses “lead” without importing a later office structure.

v14: Different needs receive different responses: warning, encouragement, support, patience.

v15: Nonretaliation applies both within the community and toward “everyone.”

v19–21: Openness to the Spirit and prophecy is paired with testing rather than gullibility or suppression.

v23: `psychē` is rendered “life” rather than “soul” to avoid suggesting Paul is defining a three-part human anatomy. The prayer is for the whole person.

v24: The final ground of holiness is God’s faithfulness, not self-generated perfection.
