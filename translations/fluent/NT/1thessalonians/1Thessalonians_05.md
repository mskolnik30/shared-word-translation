## The Day of the Lord

<p>
v01: Now concerning the times and seasons, brothers and sisters, you do not need anything written to you.
v02: For you yourselves know very well that the day of the Lord comes like a thief in the night.
v03: When people say, “Peace and security,” sudden destruction will come on them like labor pains on a pregnant woman, and they will certainly not escape.
v04: But you, brothers and sisters, are not in darkness, so that the day should overtake you like a thief.
v05: For you are all children of light and children of day. We do not belong to night or darkness.
v06: So then, let us not sleep like the rest, but stay awake and sober.
v07: For those who sleep, sleep at night, and those who get drunk, get drunk at night.
v08: But since we belong to day, let us be sober, putting on faith and love as a breastplate and the hope of salvation as a helmet.
v09: For God did not appoint us to wrath but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,
v10: who died for us so that, whether we are awake or asleep, we may live together with him.
v11: Therefore, encourage one another and build each other up, just as you are doing.
</p>

## Life Together in the Community

<p>
v12: We ask you, brothers and sisters, to recognize those who labor among you, lead you in the Lord and warn you,
v13: and to regard them very highly in love because of their work. Live at peace among yourselves.
v14: We urge you, brothers and sisters: warn the disorderly, encourage the fainthearted, support the weak and be patient with everyone.
v15: See that no one repays anyone evil for evil, but always pursue what is good for one another and for everyone.
v16: Rejoice always.
v17: Pray without ceasing.
v18: Give thanks in every circumstance, for this is God's will for you in Christ Jesus.
v19: Do not extinguish the Spirit.
v20: Do not despise prophecies,
v21: but test everything; hold fast to what is good.
v22: Keep away from every kind of evil.
</p>

## Final Prayer and Greeting

<p>
v23: Now may the God of peace himself make you holy through and through, and may your whole spirit, soul and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.
v24: The one who calls you is faithful, and he will do it.
v25: Brothers and sisters, pray for us.
v26: Greet all the brothers and sisters with a holy kiss.
v27: I solemnly charge you by the Lord to have this letter read to all the brothers and sisters.
v28: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.
</p>

## Notes

v22: ‘Every kind of evil’ can also be rendered ‘every form of evil’; it does not merely mean looking evil.

## Vocabulary

v02: **ἡμέρα κυρίου (hēmera kyriou)**: Day of the Lord, pictured here as arriving like a thief at night.
