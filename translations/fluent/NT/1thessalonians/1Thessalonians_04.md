## God’s Will: Your Holiness

<p>
v01: Finally then, brothers and sisters, we ask and urge you in the Lord Jesus: just as you received from us how you must walk and please God—as indeed you are walking—do so more and more.
v02: For you know what instructions we gave you through the Lord Jesus.
v03: For this is God's will: your holiness—that you abstain from sexual immorality;
v04: that each of you know how to control your own body in holiness and honor,
v05: not with lustful passion like the nations who do not know God;
v06: that no one transgress and exploit a brother or sister in this matter, because the Lord is an avenger in all these things, as we told you before and solemnly warned you.
v07: For God did not call us to impurity but in holiness.
v08: Therefore, whoever rejects this does not reject a human being but God, who gives his Holy Spirit to you.
</p>

## Love One Another and Live Quietly

<p>
v09: Now concerning family love, you do not need us to write to you, for you yourselves are taught by God to love one another.
v10: And indeed you show this love to all the brothers and sisters throughout Macedonia. But we urge you to do so more and more,
v11: and to aspire to live quietly, attend to your own affairs and work with your own hands, just as we instructed you,
v12: so that you may walk honorably toward outsiders and depend on no one.
</p>

## The Dead in Christ and the Lord’s Coming

<p>
v13: We do not want you to be uninformed, brothers and sisters, about those who are sleeping, so that you do not grieve like the rest, who have no hope.
v14: For if we believe that Jesus died and rose, in the same way God will bring with Jesus those who have fallen asleep through him.
v15: For this we tell you by a word of the Lord: we who are alive and remain until the Lord's coming will certainly not precede those who have fallen asleep.
v16: For the Lord himself will descend from heaven with a commanding shout, with an archangel's voice and with God's trumpet, and the dead in Christ will rise first.
v17: Then we who are alive and remain will be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air, and so we will always be with the Lord.
v18: Therefore, encourage one another with these words.
</p>

## Notes

v04: The word rendered ‘body’ literally means ‘vessel’ and may instead refer to acquiring or living with a wife.

v15–17: The sequence places the dead in Christ rising before the living are caught up together with them to meet the Lord.

## Vocabulary

v17: **ἀπάντησις (apantēsis)**: A meeting or reception of an arriving person.
