## Greeting

<p>
v01: Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the chosen temporary residents of the dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia,
v02: chosen according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, by the Spirit's sanctifying work, for obedience and sprinkling with Jesus Christ's blood: May grace and peace be multiplied to you.
</p>

## A Living Hope

<p>
v03: Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ! In his great mercy he gave us new birth into a living hope through Jesus Christ's resurrection from the dead,
v04: into an inheritance that cannot decay, be defiled or fade, kept in heaven for you,
v05: who are being guarded by God's power through faith for a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.
v06: You rejoice in this, though for a little while now, if necessary, you have been grieved by various trials,
v07: so that the tested genuineness of your faith—more precious than perishable gold tested by fire—may result in praise, glory and honor when Jesus Christ is revealed.
v08: Though you have not seen him, you love him. Though you do not see him now, you believe in him and rejoice with indescribable and glorious joy,
v09: receiving the outcome of your faith: the salvation of your lives.
</p>

## The Prophets and the Good News

<p>
v10: The prophets who prophesied about the grace that would come to you searched and investigated this salvation carefully.
v11: They investigated what person or time the Spirit of Christ within them was indicating when he testified beforehand about Christ's sufferings and the glories to follow.
v12: It was revealed to them that they were serving not themselves but you in the things now announced to you through those who brought you the good news by the Holy Spirit sent from heaven—things angels long to look into.
</p>

## Called to Be Holy

<p>
v13: Therefore, with the loins of your mind girded and fully sober, set your hope completely on the grace being brought to you when Jesus Christ is revealed.
v14: As obedient children, do not be shaped by the desires you formerly had in your ignorance.
v15: Instead, just as the one who called you is holy, be holy yourselves in all your conduct,
v16: for it is written, “You will be holy, because I am holy.”
v17: If you call on as Father the one who judges each person's work impartially, live with reverent fear during the time of your temporary residence.
v18: You know that you were redeemed from the empty way of life handed down by your ancestors, not with perishable things such as silver or gold,
v19: but with Christ's precious blood, like that of an unblemished and spotless lamb.
v20: He was known beforehand before the world's foundation but revealed at the end of the times for your sake.
v21: Through him you believe in God, who raised him from the dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope are in God.
</p>

## Love One Another Earnestly

<p>
v22: Since you have purified your lives by obedience to the truth for sincere family love, love one another earnestly from the heart.
v23: You have been born anew, not from perishable seed but imperishable, through God's living and enduring word.
v24: For “all flesh is like grass, and all its glory like a grass flower. The grass withers and the flower falls,
v25: but the Lord's word endures forever.” And this is the word that was proclaimed to you as good news.
</p>

## Notes

v13: The command’s literal clothing image is ‘gird up the loins of your mind.’
