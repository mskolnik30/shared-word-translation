## Shepherd the Flock of God

<p>
v01: Therefore, I urge the elders among you, as a fellow elder, a witness of Christ's sufferings and a partner in the glory about to be revealed:
v02: Shepherd God's flock among you, exercising oversight not under compulsion but willingly, according to God; not for shameful gain but eagerly;
v03: not lording it over those entrusted to you, but becoming examples to the flock.
v04: And when the chief Shepherd appears, you will receive the unfading crown of glory.
</p>

## Humble Yourselves Under God’s Mighty Hand

<p>
v05: Likewise, younger people, submit to the elders. And all of you, clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, because “God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.”
v06: Therefore, humble yourselves under God's mighty hand, so that he may exalt you at the proper time,
v07: casting all your anxiety on him, because he cares for you.
v08: Be sober and alert. Your adversary the devil prowls around like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour.
v09: Resist him, firm in the faith, knowing that the same sufferings are being endured by your family of believers throughout the world.
v10: After you have suffered a little while, the God of all grace, who called you to his eternal glory in Christ, will himself restore, establish, strengthen and ground you.
v11: To him be power forever and ever. Amen.
</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>
v12: Through Silvanus, whom I regard as a faithful brother, I have written to you briefly, encouraging you and testifying that this is God's true grace. Stand firm in it.
v13: She who is in Babylon, chosen together with you, greets you, and so does my son Mark.
v14: Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace to all of you who are in Christ.
</p>

## Vocabulary

v01: **πρεσβύτερος (presbyteros)**: Elder; here used for community leaders and also by the writer of himself.
