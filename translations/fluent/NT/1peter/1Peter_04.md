## Living for God’s Will
<p>
v01: Therefore, since Christ suffered in the flesh, arm yourselves with the same intention, because whoever suffers in the flesh has ceased from sin,
v02: so as to live the remaining time in the flesh no longer for human desires but for God's will.
</p>

<p>
v03: For enough time has passed to carry out the desire of the nations, living in debauchery, lusts, drunkenness, orgies, drinking parties and forbidden idolatries.
v04: They are surprised that you no longer run with them into the same flood of reckless living, and they slander you.
v05: They will give an account to the one ready to judge the living and the dead.
</p>

<p>
v06: For this is why the good news was proclaimed even to the dead: so that though judged in the flesh according to human standards, they might live in the spirit according to God.
</p>

## Love, Hospitality, and Service
<p>
v07: The end of all things has drawn near. Therefore, be self-controlled and sober for prayer.
v08: Above all, maintain earnest love for one another, because love covers a multitude of sins.
v09: Offer hospitality to one another without grumbling.
</p>

<p>
v10: As each person has received a gift, use it to serve one another as good stewards of God's varied grace.
v11: If anyone speaks, let it be as God's words; if anyone serves, let it be from the strength God supplies, so that God may be glorified in everything through Jesus Christ. To him belong glory and power forever and ever. Amen.
</p>

## Suffering as a Christian
<p>
v12: Beloved, do not be surprised by the fiery ordeal among you that tests you, as though something strange were happening to you.
v13: Instead, rejoice insofar as you share Christ's sufferings, so that you may also rejoice exuberantly when his glory is revealed.
v14: If you are insulted because of Christ's name, you are blessed, because the Spirit of glory and of God rests on you.
</p>

<p>
v15: Let none of you suffer as a murderer, thief, evildoer or meddler in another's affairs.
v16: But if anyone suffers as a Christian, let that person not be ashamed but glorify God in this name.
</p>

<p>
v17: For it is time for judgment to begin with God's household; and if it begins with us, what will be the outcome for those who disobey God's good news?
v18: And “if the righteous person is saved with difficulty, what will become of the ungodly and sinner?”
</p>

<p>
v19: Therefore, let those who suffer according to God's will entrust their lives to a faithful Creator while doing good.
</p>

## Notes

v06: The ‘dead’ may be people who heard while alive and have since died, or the proclamation may be portrayed as reaching the dead; the verse is compressed.
