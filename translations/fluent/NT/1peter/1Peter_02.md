## A Living Stone and a Holy People
<p>
v01: Therefore, put away all malice, all deceit, hypocrisy, envy and every kind of slander.
v02: Like newborn babies, long for pure spiritual milk, so that by it you may grow into salvation,
v03: if indeed you have tasted that the Lord is good.
</p>

<p>
v04: As you come to him, a living stone rejected by people but chosen and honored by God,
v05: you yourselves, like living stones, are being built into a spiritual house, to be a holy priesthood offering spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.
v06: For Scripture contains this: “Look, I lay in Zion a chosen and honored cornerstone, and the one who believes in him will never be put to shame.”
</p>

<p>
v07: So honor belongs to you who believe. But for those who do not believe, “The stone the builders rejected has become the cornerstone,”
v08: and, “a stone that makes people stumble and a rock that makes them fall.” They stumble because they disobey the word, to which they were also appointed.
</p>

<p>
v09: But you are a chosen people, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God's possession, so that you may proclaim the excellencies of the one who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light.
v10: Once you were not a people, but now you are God's people; once you had not received mercy, but now you have received mercy.
</p>

## Living as Servants of God
<p>
v11: Beloved, I urge you as foreigners and temporary residents to abstain from fleshly desires that wage war against your life.
v12: Maintain honorable conduct among the nations so that, where they accuse you as evildoers, they may observe your good works and glorify God on the day he visits.
</p>

<p>
v13: For the Lord's sake, submit to every human institution, whether to the emperor as supreme
v14: or to governors sent by him to punish evildoers and praise those who do good.
v15: For this is God's will: by doing good, silence the ignorance of foolish people.
v16: Live as free people, but do not use freedom as a cover for evil; live as God's slaves.
v17: Honor everyone, love the family of believers, fear God and honor the emperor.
</p>

## The Example of Christ's Suffering
<p>
v18: Household slaves, submit with full fear to your masters—not only to the good and gentle but also to the cruel.
v19: For it is commendable if someone endures painful suffering unjustly because of consciousness of God.
v20: What credit is there if you endure beatings for sinning? But if you endure suffering for doing good, this is commendable before God.
</p>

<p>
v21: For you were called to this, because Christ also suffered for you, leaving you an example so that you might follow in his steps.
v22: He committed no sin, and no deceit was found in his mouth.
v23: When insulted, he did not insult in return; when suffering, he made no threats, but entrusted himself to the one who judges justly.
v24: He himself bore our sins in his body on the tree, so that, having died to sins, we might live for righteousness. By his wound you were healed.
v25: For you were straying like sheep, but now you have returned to the shepherd and overseer of your lives.
</p>

## Notes

v18–25: The address is to household slaves and includes suffering under unjust masters. The harm and coercive hierarchy are retained rather than moralized as harmless.

## Vocabulary

v18: **οἰκέτης (oiketēs)**: A household servant or slave.
