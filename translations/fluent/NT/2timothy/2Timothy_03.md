## Difficult Times

<p>
v01: Know this: difficult times will come in the last days.
v02: For people will be self-loving, money-loving, boastful, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to parents, ungrateful and unholy,
v03: without natural affection, unwilling to reconcile, slanderous, without self-control, brutal and hating what is good,
v04: treacherous, reckless, conceited, loving pleasure rather than loving God,
v05: holding an outward form of godliness but denying its power. Turn away from such people.
v06: For among them are those who creep into households and capture vulnerable women burdened with sins and led by various desires,
v07: always learning yet never able to come to knowledge of the truth.
v08: Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these people oppose the truth—people corrupted in mind and disqualified regarding the faith.
v09: But they will not advance much further, for their folly will be obvious to everyone, just as the folly of those two became obvious.
</p>

## Continue in What You Learned

<p>
v10: But you have followed my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, patience, love and endurance,
v11: as well as my persecutions and sufferings—what happened to me in Antioch, Iconium and Lystra, the persecutions I endured. Yet the Lord rescued me from them all.
v12: Indeed, all who want to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted.
v13: But evil people and impostors will go from bad to worse, deceiving and being deceived.
v14: But you, continue in what you learned and became convinced of, knowing from whom you learned it,
v15: and how from infancy you have known the sacred writings, which can make you wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus.
v16: Every Scripture is God-breathed and useful for teaching, rebuke, correction and training in righteousness,
v17: so that the person of God may be capable, fully equipped for every good work.
</p>

## Notes

v16: The Greek can mean ‘all Scripture is God-breathed and useful’ or ‘every God-breathed scripture is also useful.’

## Vocabulary

v16: **θεόπνευστος (theopneustos)**: God-breathed or breathed out by God.
