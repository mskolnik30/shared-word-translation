## Be Strengthened by Grace

<p>
v01: Therefore, my child, be strengthened by the grace that is in Christ Jesus.
v02: What you heard from me in the presence of many witnesses, entrust to faithful people who will also be able to teach others.
v03: Share in suffering as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.
v04: No one serving as a soldier gets entangled in the affairs of ordinary life, so that they may please the one who enlisted them.
v05: Likewise, if someone competes as an athlete, they are not crowned unless they compete according to the rules.
v06: The hardworking farmer should be the first to receive a share of the crops.
v07: Think about what I am saying, for the Lord will give you understanding in everything.
</p>

## Remember Jesus Christ

<p>
v08: Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead, descended from David, according to my good news,
v09: for which I suffer hardship, even to being chained like a criminal. But God’s word is not chained.
v10: For this reason I endure everything for the sake of the chosen ones, so that they too may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.
v11: This saying is trustworthy:
If we died with him,
we will also live with him;
v12: if we endure,
we will also reign with him;
if we deny him,
he also will deny us;
v13: if we are faithless,
he remains faithful,
because he cannot deny himself.
</p>

## A Worker Who Handles the Word of Truth Straight

<p>
v14: Remind them of these things, solemnly warning them before God not to fight about words, which is useful for nothing and ruins those who listen.
v15: Make every effort to present yourself approved to God, a worker who has no need to be ashamed, handling the word of truth straight.
v16: But avoid godless empty talk, because it will advance toward greater ungodliness,
v17: and their word will spread like gangrene. Among them are Hymenaeus and Philetus,
v18: who have departed from the truth, saying the resurrection has already happened, and they are overturning the trust of some.
v19: Nevertheless, God’s firm foundation stands, bearing this seal: “The Lord knows those who belong to him,” and, “Let everyone who names the Lord’s name turn away from unrighteousness.”
</p>

## A Vessel for Honorable Use

<p>
v20: In a large house there are not only gold and silver vessels but also wooden and clay ones, some for honorable use and some for dishonorable.
v21: Therefore, if anyone cleanses themselves from these things, they will be a vessel for honorable use, made holy, useful to the Master, prepared for every good work.
v22: Flee youthful desires, but pursue righteousness, trust, love, and peace together with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart.
v23: Reject foolish and ignorant controversies, knowing they produce fights.
v24: The Lord’s servant must not fight but must be gentle toward everyone, able to teach, patient when wronged,
v25: correcting opponents with gentleness. Perhaps God may grant them repentance leading to knowledge of the truth,
v26: and they may come to their senses and escape the devil’s trap, having been captured by him to do his will.
</p>

## Notes

v02: Ministry is deliberately reproducible: received teaching is entrusted to reliable people who can teach still others.

v03–06: Soldier, athlete, and farmer images stress focused service, disciplined faithfulness, and patient labor rather than social prestige.

v08: Resurrection and Davidic descent keep Jesus’ identity both eschatological and rooted in Israel’s messianic story.

v09: Paul is chained, but “God’s word is not chained,” making imprisonment a contrast rather than a defeat.

v11–13: The poetic trustworthy saying holds promise and warning together. Christ’s faithfulness does not erase the seriousness of denying him.

v15: `orthotomeō` literally means cut straight. “Handling the word of truth straight” preserves the directional craft image without forcing it into one interpretation.

v18: The false teaching does not deny resurrection language but claims it is already fully past, thereby overturning hope.

v19: Divine knowledge of “his own” and human turning from unrighteousness stand together as the foundation’s double inscription.

v24–25: Correcting error must be nonquarrelsome and gentle because repentance is ultimately God’s gift, not something produced by humiliation.

v26: The final pronouns are grammatically difficult: “his will” may refer to God or the devil. Fluent follows the common devil-referent reading while noting the ambiguity here.
