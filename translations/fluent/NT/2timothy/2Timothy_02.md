## Be Strengthened by Grace

<p>
v01: You then, my child, be strengthened by the grace that is in Christ Jesus.
v02: Entrust what you heard from me through many witnesses to faithful people who will be qualified to teach others also.
v03: Share in suffering as a good soldier of Christ Jesus.
v04: No one serving as a soldier gets entangled in ordinary civilian affairs, so as to please the one who enlisted him.
v05: Likewise, an athlete is not crowned unless competing according to the rules.
v06: The hardworking farmer should be the first to receive a share of the crops.
v07: Think about what I am saying, for the Lord will give you understanding in everything.
</p>

## Remember Jesus Christ

<p>
v08: Remember Jesus Christ, raised from the dead and descended from David, according to my good news.
v09: For it I suffer hardship, even chains like a criminal. But God's word is not chained.
v10: Therefore, I endure everything for the sake of the chosen, so that they too may obtain the salvation in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.
v11: This saying is trustworthy: If we died with him, we will also live with him;
v12: if we endure, we will also reign with him; if we deny him, he will also deny us;
v13: if we are unfaithful, he remains faithful, for he cannot deny himself.
</p>

## A Worker Who Handles the Word of Truth Straight

<p>
v14: Remind them of these things, solemnly warning them before the Lord not to fight over words. Such arguments do no good and ruin those who listen.
v15: Make every effort to present yourself to God as approved, a worker with no cause for shame who cuts the word of truth straight.
v16: Avoid profane empty chatter, for those who engage in it will advance into greater ungodliness,
v17: and their talk will spread like gangrene. Hymenaeus and Philetus are among them.
v18: They have missed the mark concerning the truth, saying the resurrection has already happened, and they overturn the faith of some.
v19: Nevertheless, God's firm foundation stands, bearing this seal: “The Lord knows those who are his,” and, “Let everyone who names the Lord's name turn away from unrighteousness.”
</p>

## A Vessel for Honorable Use

<p>
v20: In a large house there are not only vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and clay; some are for honorable use and some for dishonorable use.
v21: Therefore, if anyone cleanses themselves from these things, they will be a vessel for honorable use—made holy, useful to the master and prepared for every good work.
v22: Flee youthful desires and pursue righteousness, faith, love and peace, together with those who call on the Lord from a pure heart.
v23: Reject foolish and ignorant controversies, knowing that they breed quarrels.
v24: The Lord's slave must not quarrel but must be gentle toward everyone, able to teach and patient when wronged,
v25: correcting opponents with gentleness, in the hope that God may grant them repentance leading to knowledge of the truth
v26: and that they may come to their senses and escape the devil's trap, having been captured alive by him to do his will.
</p>

## Notes

v15: ‘Cutting straight’ evokes accurate handling; the precise metaphor may draw on roads, craft or plowing.
