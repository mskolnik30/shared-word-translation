## Proclaim the Word

<p>
v01: I solemnly charge you before God and Christ Jesus, who is going to judge the living and the dead, and by his appearing and his kingdom:
v02: Proclaim the word; be ready when the time is favorable or unfavorable; correct, rebuke and encourage, with complete patience and teaching.
v03: For a time will come when people will not tolerate sound teaching. Instead, with itching ears and according to their own desires, they will accumulate teachers for themselves.
v04: They will turn their ears away from the truth and turn aside to myths.
v05: But you, remain sober in everything, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelist and fulfill your ministry.
</p>

## I Have Finished the Course

<p>
v06: For I am already being poured out as a drink offering, and the time of my departure has arrived.
v07: I have fought the good fight, finished the race and kept the faith.
v08: From now on, the crown of righteousness is reserved for me. The Lord, the righteous judge, will award it to me on that day—and not only to me, but also to all who have loved his appearing.
</p>

## Personal Requests and Final Witness

<p>
v09: Make every effort to come to me soon,
v10: because Demas deserted me, having loved the present age, and went to Thessalonica. Crescens went to Galatia and Titus to Dalmatia.
v11: Only Luke is with me. Get Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me in ministry.
v12: I sent Tychicus to Ephesus.
v13: When you come, bring the cloak I left with Carpus at Troas, as well as the scrolls, especially the parchments.
v14: Alexander the metalworker did me great harm. The Lord will repay him according to his deeds.
v15: You too should watch out for him, because he strongly opposed our words.
v16: At my first defense no one came to stand with me; everyone deserted me. May it not be counted against them.
v17: But the Lord stood beside me and strengthened me, so that through me the proclamation might be fully made and all the nations might hear. And I was rescued from the lion's mouth.
v18: The Lord will rescue me from every evil deed and bring me safely into his heavenly kingdom. To him be glory forever and ever. Amen.
</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>
v19: Greet Prisca and Aquila and the household of Onesiphorus.
v20: Erastus remained in Corinth, and I left Trophimus sick in Miletus.
v21: Make every effort to come before winter. Eubulus, Pudens, Linus, Claudia and all the brothers and sisters greet you.
v22: The Lord be with your spirit. Grace be with all of you.
</p>

## Notes

v14: The prayer entrusts repayment for Alexander’s harmful acts to the Lord; it is not a command for personal retaliation.

v16: The request that abandonment not be counted against others limits the preceding judgment language.
