## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God's will, according to the promise of life in Christ Jesus,
v02: to Timothy, my beloved child: Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.
</p>

## Rekindle God’s Gift

<p>
v03: I thank God, whom I serve as my ancestors did, with a clean conscience, as I constantly remember you in my prayers night and day.
v04: Remembering your tears, I long to see you so that I may be filled with joy.
v05: I am reminded of your sincere faith, which first lived in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice and, I am convinced, lives in you as well.
v06: For this reason I remind you to fan into flame God's gift that is in you through the laying on of my hands.
v07: For God did not give us a spirit of cowardice, but of power, love and self-control.
</p>

## Do Not Be Ashamed of the Testimony

<p>
v08: So do not be ashamed of the testimony about our Lord or of me, his prisoner. Instead, share in suffering for the good news, relying on God's power.
v09: He saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works but according to his own purpose and grace, given to us in Christ Jesus before eternal times.
v10: This grace has now been revealed through the appearing of our Savior Christ Jesus, who abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the good news.
v11: For this good news I was appointed a herald, apostle and teacher.
v12: That is why I suffer these things. But I am not ashamed, because I know the one I have trusted and am convinced that he is able to guard what I entrusted to him until that day.
</p>

## Guard the Good Deposit

<p>
v13: Hold to the pattern of sound words you heard from me, in the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.
v14: Guard the good deposit through the Holy Spirit who lives in us.
v15: You know that everyone in Asia turned away from me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes.
v16: May the Lord grant mercy to the household of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain.
v17: Instead, when he was in Rome, he searched diligently for me and found me.
v18: May the Lord grant him mercy from the Lord on that day. And you know very well how much service he rendered in Ephesus.
</p>

## Notes

v07: The Greek word can denote cowardice or timidity; the contrast names power, love and self-control.
