## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through God’s will, according to the promise of life that is in Christ Jesus,
v02: to Timothy, my beloved child: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.
</p>

## Rekindle God’s Gift

<p>
v03: I thank God, whom I serve with a clear conscience as my ancestors did, as I remember you constantly in my prayers night and day.
v04: Remembering your tears, I long to see you so that I may be filled with joy.
v05: I am reminded of the sincere trust that is in you, which lived first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice and, I am convinced, lives in you too.
v06: For this reason I remind you to rekindle God’s gift that is in you through the laying on of my hands.
v07: For God did not give us a spirit of cowardice but of power, love, and self-control.
</p>

## Do Not Be Ashamed of the Testimony

<p>
v08: Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony about our Lord or of me, his prisoner. Instead, share in suffering for the good news according to God’s power,
v09: who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works but according to his own purpose and grace. This grace was given to us in Christ Jesus before eternal times,
v10: but has now been revealed through the appearing of our Savior Christ Jesus, who abolished death and brought life and immortality to light through the good news.
v11: For this good news I was appointed a herald, apostle, and teacher.
v12: This is also why I suffer these things. But I am not ashamed, because I know the one I have trusted, and I am convinced that he is able to guard what I have entrusted to him until that day.
</p>

## Guard the Good Deposit

<p>
v13: Hold to the pattern of sound words you heard from me, in the trust and love that are in Christ Jesus.
v14: Guard the good deposit entrusted to you through the Holy Spirit who lives in us.
v15: You know that everyone in Asia has turned away from me, including Phygelus and Hermogenes.
v16: May the Lord give mercy to Onesiphorus’ household, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain.
v17: Instead, when he arrived in Rome, he searched diligently for me and found me.
v18: May the Lord grant him to find mercy from the Lord on that day. And you know very well how much service he rendered in Ephesus.
</p>

## Notes

v03: Paul explicitly connects his service of God with his ancestors, framing Christian ministry in continuity with Israel’s God rather than rejection of his ancestral faith.

v05: Timothy’s trust is narrated through grandmother, mother, and Timothy, giving intergenerational faith formation unusual prominence.

v06: The gift is not merely possessed; it must be rekindled. Calling and formation remain active responsibilities.

v07: “Spirit” may refer directly to the Holy Spirit or to a Spirit-shaped disposition. Fluent keeps the lowercase grammatical sense while the theological source remains God.

v08: Timothy is called not only to defend testimony but to “share in suffering” for the good news.

v09: Calling is grounded in divine purpose and grace, explicitly “not according to our works.”

v10: Christ’s appearing is said to abolish death and illuminate life and immortality, not to deny that believers still physically die before resurrection.

v12: “What I have entrusted to him” can also mean “what he entrusted to me.” Fluent follows the personal-trust sense while the reciprocal deposit language remains intentionally resonant with v14.

v14: The entrusted deposit is guarded “through the Holy Spirit,” balancing responsibility with divine enablement.

v15: “Everyone in Asia” is obvious rhetorical overstatement because Paul immediately names faithful Onesiphorus. Fluent preserves the expression without turning it into a literal census claim.
