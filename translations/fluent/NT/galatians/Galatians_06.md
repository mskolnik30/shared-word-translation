## Bear One Another’s Burdens

<p>
v01: Brothers and sisters, even if someone is caught in some wrongdoing, you who are spiritual should restore that person in a spirit of gentleness, watching yourself so that you too are not tested.
v02: Carry one another’s burdens, and in this way fulfill the law of Christ.
v03: For if anyone thinks they are something when they are nothing, they deceive themselves.
v04: Each person should test their own work, and then they will have reason for satisfaction in themselves alone and not in comparison with someone else.
v05: For each person will carry their own load.
v06: The one who is taught the word should share all good things with the one who teaches.
</p>

## Sow to the Spirit

<p>
v07: Do not be deceived: God is not mocked. Whatever a person sows, that is what they will reap.
v08: The one who sows to their own flesh will reap decay from the flesh, but the one who sows to the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.
v09: Let us not grow weary in doing good, because at the proper time we will reap if we do not give up.
v10: So then, as we have opportunity, let us do good to everyone, and especially to those who belong to the household of faith.
</p>

## A New Creation

<p>
v11: See what large letters I am writing to you with my own hand!
v12: Those who want to make a good showing in the flesh are the ones trying to compel you to be circumcised, only so they will not be persecuted for Christ’s cross.
v13: For even those who are circumcised do not themselves keep the Law, but they want you to be circumcised so they can boast in your flesh.
v14: But may I never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world has been crucified to me and I to the world.
v15: For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision is anything; what matters is a new creation.
v16: Peace and mercy upon all who walk by this rule, and upon the Israel of God.
v17: From now on let no one cause me trouble, because I carry the marks of Jesus on my body.
v18: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers and sisters. Amen.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Restoration of a person caught in wrongdoing is assigned to the “spiritual” precisely in gentleness and self-watchfulness, not superiority.

v02,05: Paul can say both “carry one another’s burdens” and “each will carry their own load.” Mutual responsibility and personal accountability are not opposites.

v02: “Law of Christ” names the positive communal pattern of Christ-shaped love, echoing 5:13–14.

v06: Material sharing with teachers belongs to Paul’s vision of Spirit-shaped community.

v07–09: Sowing and reaping language describes moral formation and eschatological consequence; grace does not make embodied practice irrelevant.

v10: “Especially the household of faith” gives fellow believers a particular responsibility without limiting doing good to them alone.

v11: Paul draws attention to his own handwriting, likely marking the closing section as personally written.

v12–13: Paul attributes social motives to the circumcision agitators: avoiding persecution and gaining grounds for boasting.

v14: Paul relocates boasting entirely into the shameful Roman cross, turning the world’s honor system upside down.

v15: “New creation” is the climactic identity marker, replacing circumcision/uncircumcision as the decisive distinction.

v16: “Israel of God” is grammatically debated. It may refer to Jewish believers in Christ or characterize the whole community just described. Fluent does not resolve the referent by paraphrase.

v17: `stigmata` are bodily marks. Paul likely refers to scars from suffering, not mystical wounds; Fluent leaves the image open.
