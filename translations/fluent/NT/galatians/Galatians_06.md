## Bear One Another’s Burdens

<p>
v01: Brothers and sisters, even if someone is caught in some trespass, you who are spiritual should restore that person in a spirit of gentleness, watching yourself so that you too are not tempted.
v02: Carry one another's burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ.
v03: For if anyone thinks he is something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.
v04: But each person should test their own work, and then the reason for boasting will concern themselves alone and not the other person,
v05: for each person will carry their own load.
v06: The one being taught the word should share every good thing with the one who teaches.
</p>

## Sow to the Spirit

<p>
v07: Do not be deceived: God is not mocked. For whatever a person sows, that is also what the person will reap.
v08: The one who sows for their own flesh will reap decay from the flesh, but the one who sows for the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.
v09: Let us not grow weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap if we do not give up.
v10: Therefore, as we have opportunity, let us work for the good of everyone, and especially for those who belong to the household of faith.
</p>

## A New Creation

<p>
v11: See what large letters I write to you with my own hand!
v12: Those who want to make a good showing in the flesh are the ones compelling you to be circumcised, only so that they will not be persecuted for Christ's cross.
v13: For even those who are circumcised do not themselves keep the law, but they want you to be circumcised so that they may boast in your flesh.
v14: But may I never boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world has been crucified to me and I to the world.
v15: For neither circumcision nor uncircumcision is anything, but new creation is.
v16: Peace and mercy be upon all who follow this rule, and upon the Israel of God.
v17: From now on let no one cause me trouble, for I carry the marks of Jesus on my body.
v18: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brothers and sisters. Amen.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Restoration is to be done gently and with self-scrutiny, not superiority.

v02, 5: The chapter commands mutual burden-bearing and personal load-bearing. The different contexts keep solidarity and responsibility together.

v16: ‘The Israel of God’ may identify the same rule-following group or an additional group. The conjunction is retained rather than resolving the referent.

v17: The marks are bodily scars associated with belonging to Jesus; the text does not claim they are miraculous wounds.

## Vocabulary

v02: **βάρος (baros)**: A heavy burden or weight.

v17: **στίγμα (stigma)**: A mark, brand, or scar signifying ownership or experience.
