## Stand Firm in Freedom

<p>
v01: For freedom Christ set us free. Therefore stand firm and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery.
v02: Look—I, Paul, tell you that if you are circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.
v03: Again I testify to every man who is circumcised that he is obligated to keep the whole Law.
v04: You who seek to be set right by the Law have been severed from Christ; you have fallen away from grace.
v05: For through the Spirit, by trust, we eagerly wait for the hope of righteousness.
v06: In Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision has any power, but trust working through love.
</p>

## Do Not Let Freedom Become an Opportunity for the Flesh

<p>
v07: You were running well. Who cut in on you and kept you from obeying the truth?
v08: This persuasion does not come from the one who calls you.
v09: A little yeast leavens the whole batch of dough.
v10: I am confident about you in the Lord that you will think no differently. But the one troubling you will bear the judgment, whoever that person may be.
v11: Brothers and sisters, if I still preach circumcision, why am I still being persecuted? In that case the offense of the cross has been removed.
v12: I wish those who are unsettling you would go all the way and mutilate themselves!
v13: For you were called to freedom, brothers and sisters. Only do not use freedom as an opportunity for the flesh; instead, through love serve one another as slaves.
v14: For the whole Law is fulfilled in one statement: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”
v15: But if you bite and devour one another, watch out or you will be consumed by one another.
</p>

## Walk by the Spirit

<p>
v16: But I say: walk by the Spirit, and you will certainly not carry out the desire of the flesh.
v17: For the flesh desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh. These oppose each other so that you do not simply do whatever you want.
v18: But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the Law.
v19: Now the works of the flesh are obvious: sexual immorality, impurity, sensuality,
v20: idolatry, sorcery, hostilities, strife, jealousy, outbursts of anger, selfish ambition, divisions, factions,
v21: envy, drunkenness, wild parties, and things like these. I warn you, as I warned you before, that those who practice such things will not inherit God’s kingdom.
v22: But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,
v23: gentleness, and self-control. Against such things there is no law.
v24: Those who belong to Christ Jesus have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires.
v25: If we live by the Spirit, let us also keep in step with the Spirit.
v26: Let us not become conceited, provoking one another and envying one another.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Freedom is both a gift and a vocation: Christ frees, and believers must stand firm rather than reenter slavery.

v02–04: Paul is not condemning circumcision as an ethnic Jewish practice in itself. He addresses Gentile circumcision adopted as a necessary means of covenant standing and justification.

v04: `dikaioō` remains “set right.” Seeking that status “by the Law” is contrasted with grace.

v06: The letter’s positive summary is not bare faith but “trust working through love.”

v12: Paul uses intentionally shocking wordplay: those insisting on cutting Gentile flesh should “cut themselves off” entirely. Fluent retains the severity without euphemizing it.

v13: Freedom does not mean autonomy from others; Paul immediately describes love as mutual slave-service.

v14: Paul can oppose using Torah as the basis of Gentile justification while still saying the whole Law is fulfilled in love of neighbor.

v16–18: “Flesh” is not simply the physical body. It names human life organized in opposition to God’s Spirit.

v19–23: Paul contrasts plural “works” with singular “fruit,” presenting Spirit-formed character as an integrated life.

v22: `pistis` in the fruit list can mean faith or faithfulness. “Faithfulness” best fits a character-quality list.

v25: “Keep in step” translates a disciplined walking image rather than a vague inner feeling.
