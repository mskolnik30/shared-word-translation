## Stand Firm in Freedom

<p>
v01: For freedom Christ set us free. Stand firm, then, and do not be held again under a yoke of slavery.
v02: Look, I, Paul, tell you that if you are circumcised, Christ will be of no benefit to you.
v03: Again I testify to every man who is circumcised that he is obligated to do the whole law.
v04: You who seek to be justified by the law have been severed from Christ; you have fallen away from grace.
v05: For through the Spirit, by faith, we eagerly await the hope of righteousness.
v06: For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision accomplishes anything, but faith working through love does.
</p>

## Do Not Let Freedom Become an Opportunity for the Flesh

<p>
v07: You were running well. Who cut in on you and kept you from obeying the truth?
v08: That persuasion does not come from the one who calls you.
v09: A little yeast leavens the whole batch of dough.
v10: I am confident about you in the Lord that you will think no differently. But the one troubling you will bear the judgment, whoever that person may be.
v11: But as for me, brothers and sisters, if I still proclaim circumcision, why am I still persecuted? In that case, the offense of the cross has been abolished.
v12: I wish those who are unsettling you would go all the way and castrate themselves!
v13: For you were called to freedom, brothers and sisters. Only do not use freedom as an opportunity for the flesh; instead, through love serve one another as enslaved people.
v14: For the whole law is fulfilled in one word: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”
v15: But if you bite and devour one another, watch out that you are not consumed by one another.
</p>

## Walk by the Spirit

<p>
v16: But I say: walk by the Spirit, and you will certainly not carry out the desire of the flesh.
v17: For the flesh desires what is against the Spirit, and the Spirit what is against the flesh. These oppose each other, so that you do not do whatever you want.
v18: But if you are led by the Spirit, you are not under the law.
v19: Now the works of the flesh are obvious: sexual immorality, impurity, debauchery,
v20: idolatry, sorcery, hostilities, strife, jealousy, fits of anger, selfish ambitions, divisions, factions,
v21: envy, drunkenness, wild parties, and things like these. I warn you, as I warned you before, that those who practice such things will not inherit God's kingdom.
v22: But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,
v23: gentleness, and self-control. There is no law against such things.
v24: Now those who belong to Christ Jesus have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires.
v25: If we live by the Spirit, let us also keep in step with the Spirit.
v26: Let us not become conceited, provoking one another and envying one another.
</p>

## Notes

v12: Paul's wish uses bodily wordplay with circumcision and means that the agitators would castrate themselves. Its harshness is not softened.

v13: Freedom is contrasted with an opportunity for the flesh and immediately directed into loving service pictured as voluntary enslavement.

v19–23: The works are plural while the Spirit's fruit is singular. The lists concern practiced communal life, not a tool for excusing coercive judgment.

## Vocabulary

v12: **ἀποκόπτω (apokoptō)**: Cut off; here, castrate or mutilate.

v23: **ἐγκράτεια (enkrateia)**: Self-control or mastery of one's impulses.
