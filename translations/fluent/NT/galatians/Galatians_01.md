## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, an apostle—not from human beings nor through a human being, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised him from the dead—
v02: and all the brothers and sisters with me, to the churches of Galatia:
v03: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,
v04: who gave himself for our sins to rescue us from the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,
v05: to whom be glory forever and ever. Amen.
</p>

## No Other Good News

<p>
v06: I am astonished that you are so quickly deserting the one who called you by Christ's grace and turning to a different gospel—
v07: not that there is another gospel. But some people are troubling you and want to distort the gospel of Christ.
v08: But even if we or an angel from heaven should proclaim to you a gospel contrary to the one we proclaimed to you, let that one be accursed!
v09: As we have said before, so now I say again: if anyone proclaims to you a gospel contrary to what you received, let that one be accursed!
v10: Am I now seeking human approval, or God's? Or am I trying to please people? If I were still pleasing people, I would not be Christ's servant.
</p>

## Paul’s Good News Came through Revelation

<p>
v11: For I want you to know, brothers and sisters, that the gospel proclaimed by me is not of human origin.
v12: For I neither received it from a human being nor was I taught it; rather, I received it through a revelation of Jesus Christ.
v13: For you have heard about my former way of life in Judaism: I intensely persecuted God's church and tried to destroy it.
v14: I advanced in Judaism beyond many contemporaries among my people, being far more zealous for my ancestral traditions.
v15: But when God, who set me apart from my mother's womb and called me through his grace, was pleased
v16: to reveal his Son in me so that I might proclaim him among the Gentiles, I did not immediately consult flesh and blood,
v17: nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me. Instead, I went away to Arabia and then returned to Damascus.
v18: Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to get acquainted with Cephas, and I stayed with him fifteen days.
v19: I saw none of the other apostles except James, the Lord's brother.
v20: As for what I am writing to you—look, before God, I am not lying.
v21: Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.
v22: I was still unknown by sight to the churches of Judea that are in Christ.
v23: They only kept hearing, “The man who once persecuted us is now proclaiming the faith he once tried to destroy.”
v24: And they glorified God because of me.
</p>

## Notes

v08–9: The repeated curse applies even to Paul and an angel if the proclaimed message contradicts the gospel already received; it is not permission for violence.

v13–14: Paul describes his own former persecution and attempted destruction of God's church while locating that conduct within his former life in Judaism.

v18: The pinned Greek names Cephas, the Aramaic form associated with Peter; the source-form name is retained.

## Vocabulary

v08: **ἀνάθεμα (anathema)**: Accursed or devoted to destruction.

v16: **σὰρξ καὶ αἷμα (sarx kai haima)**: Flesh and blood, an idiom for human beings.
