## Rooted and Built Up in Christ

<p>
v01: I want you to know how great a struggle I have for you, for those in Laodicea and for all who have not seen my face in the flesh,
v02: so that their hearts may be encouraged, joined together in love, and reach all the riches of full assurance in understanding: the knowledge of God's mystery, Christ,
v03: in whom all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are hidden.
v04: I say this so that no one may deceive you with persuasive speech.
v05: For though I am absent in the flesh, I am with you in spirit, rejoicing to see your good order and the firmness of your faith in Christ.
v06: Therefore, just as you received Christ Jesus the Lord, continue to walk in him,
v07: rooted and being built up in him, established in the faith as you were taught, and overflowing with thanksgiving.
</p>

## Fullness in Christ

<p>
v08: Watch out that no one takes you captive through philosophy and empty deception, according to human tradition and the elemental powers of the world, and not according to Christ.
v09: For in him all the fullness of deity dwells bodily,
v10: and you have been filled in him, who is the head of every ruler and authority.
v11: In him you were also circumcised with a circumcision not performed by hands, in the stripping off of the body of flesh, in Christ's circumcision.
v12: You were buried with him in baptism, in which you were also raised with him through faith in God's power, who raised him from the dead.
v13: Though you were dead in your trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, God made you alive together with him, forgiving us all our trespasses.
v14: He erased the written record against us, with its decrees that opposed us. He took it away, nailing it to the cross.
v15: He stripped the rulers and authorities and exposed them publicly, triumphing over them in it.
</p>

## Do Not Let Anyone Disqualify You

<p>
v16: Therefore, do not let anyone judge you about food or drink, or regarding a festival, new moon or Sabbaths.
v17: These are a shadow of what was to come, but the substance belongs to Christ.
v18: Do not let anyone disqualify you by delighting in humility and worship of angels, going on about what he has seen, puffed up without cause by his fleshly mind.
v19: Such a person does not hold fast to the head, from whom the whole body, supported and held together by joints and ligaments, grows with growth from God.
</p>

## Died with Christ to the Elemental Powers

<p>
v20: If you died with Christ to the elemental powers of the world, why, as though still living in the world, do you submit to decrees:
v21: “Do not handle! Do not taste! Do not touch!”
v22: All these things perish as they are used, since they are based on human commands and teachings.
v23: Such practices have a reputation for wisdom in self-made religion, humility and harsh treatment of the body, but they are of no value against indulgence of the flesh.
</p>

## Notes

v18: The pinned text reads ‘what he has seen’; the verb and the worship-of-angels construction remain difficult.

v23: The closing phrase can describe practices that fail to restrain bodily indulgence.

## Vocabulary

v08: **στοιχεῖα (stoicheia)**: Elements, basic principles or elemental powers; the same term returns in verse 20.
