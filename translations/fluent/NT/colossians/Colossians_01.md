## Greeting and Thanksgiving

<p>
v01: Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God's will, and Timothy our brother,
v02: to the saints and faithful brothers and sisters in Christ at Colossae: Grace and peace to you from God our Father.
v03: We always thank God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, when we pray for you,
v04: because we have heard of your faith in Christ Jesus and the love you have for all the saints,
v05: because of the hope stored up for you in heaven. You heard about this hope beforehand in the word of truth, the good news
v06: that has come to you. Just as it is bearing fruit and growing throughout the whole world, so it has among you since the day you heard it and truly understood God's grace.
v07: You learned it from Epaphras, our beloved fellow slave, who is a faithful minister of Christ on our behalf.
v08: He also told us about your love in the Spirit.
</p>

## A Prayer for Knowledge and Fruitful Living

<p>
v09: For this reason, from the day we heard, we have not stopped praying for you and asking that you be filled with the knowledge of his will in all spiritual wisdom and understanding,
v10: so that you may walk worthy of the Lord and please him in every way: bearing fruit in every good work and growing in the knowledge of God,
v11: being strengthened with every kind of power according to his glorious might, for all endurance and patience, with joy
v12: giving thanks to the Father, who has enabled you to share in the inheritance of the saints in the light.
v13: He rescued us from the authority of darkness and transferred us into the kingdom of his beloved Son,
v14: in whom we have redemption, the forgiveness of sins.
</p>

## The Son before All Things

<p>
v15: He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation,
v16: because in him all things were created—in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones, dominions, rulers or authorities. All things were created through him and for him.
v17: He is before all things, and in him all things hold together.
v18: He is the head of the body, the church. He is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, so that he might be supreme in everything.
v19: For all the fullness was pleased to dwell in him
v20: and through him to reconcile all things to himself—making peace through the blood of his cross—whether things on earth or things in heaven.
</p>

## Reconciled through Christ’s Death

<p>
v21: And you, who were once alienated and hostile in mind, as shown by evil deeds—
v22: he has now reconciled in his physical body through death, to present you holy, blameless and beyond accusation before him,
v23: provided that you remain in the faith, firmly founded and steadfast, not shifting away from the hope of the good news you heard, which was proclaimed in all creation under heaven and of which I, Paul, became a minister.
</p>

## Christ in You, the Hope of Glory

<p>
v24: Now I rejoice in my sufferings for you, and in my flesh I fill up what is lacking in Christ's afflictions for the sake of his body, which is the church.
v25: I became its minister according to the stewardship God gave me for you, to bring God's word to fullness—
v26: the mystery hidden for ages and generations but now revealed to his saints.
v27: God wanted to make known to them the glorious riches of this mystery among the nations: Christ in you, the hope of glory.
v28: We proclaim him, warning every person and teaching every person with all wisdom, so that we may present every person mature in Christ.
v29: For this I labor and struggle, according to his energy powerfully at work in me.
</p>

## Notes

v14: The pinned Greek reads ‘redemption, the forgiveness of sins’ without ‘through his blood.’

v24: The difficult phrase about what is lacking in Christ’s afflictions is retained without implying a defect in Christ’s death.

## Vocabulary

v15: **πρωτότοκος (prōtotokos)**: Firstborn; a title of rank and relation, used again of resurrection in verse 18.
