## Masters, Prayer, and Wise Speech

<p>
v01: Masters, provide your slaves what is right and fair, knowing that you too have a Master in heaven.
v02: Devote yourselves to prayer, staying alert in it with thanksgiving.
v03: At the same time, pray for us too, that God may open a door for the word, so that we may speak Christ's mystery, for which I am in chains.
v04: Pray that I may make it clear, as I ought to speak.
v05: Walk wisely toward outsiders, making the most of the time.
v06: Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how to answer each person.
</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>
v07: Tychicus, our beloved brother, faithful minister and fellow slave in the Lord, will tell you everything about me.
v08: I am sending him to you for this very reason: that you may know how we are and that he may encourage your hearts.
v09: He is coming with Onesimus, our faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will tell you everything happening here.
v10: Aristarchus, my fellow prisoner, greets you, as does Mark, Barnabas's cousin. You received instructions about him: if he comes to you, welcome him.
v11: Jesus who is called Justus also greets you. These are the only circumcised coworkers for God's kingdom, and they have been a comfort to me.
v12: Epaphras, who is one of you, a slave of Christ Jesus, greets you. He is always struggling for you in his prayers, so that you may stand mature and fully assured in all God's will.
v13: For I testify that he works hard for you and for those in Laodicea and Hierapolis.
v14: Luke, the beloved physician, and Demas greet you.
v15: Greet the brothers and sisters in Laodicea, as well as Nympha and the church in her house.
v16: After this letter has been read among you, have it read in the church of the Laodiceans too; and you in turn should read the letter from Laodicea.
v17: Tell Archippus, “Pay attention to the ministry you received in the Lord, so that you fulfill it.”
v18: I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The command to masters does not dissolve slavery; it places them under a heavenly Master and requires justice and fairness.

v15: The pinned reading names Nympha and uses a feminine possessive for the church in her house.

## Vocabulary

v06: **ἅλας (halas)**: Salt; here a metaphor for speech that is fittingly seasoned.
