## Greeting and Thanksgiving

<p>
v01: Paul, Silvanus and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:
v02: Grace and peace to you from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
v03: We ought always to thank God for you, brothers and sisters, as is right, because your faith is growing abundantly and the love each one of you has for one another is increasing.
v04: Therefore, we ourselves boast about you among God's churches because of your endurance and faith in all the persecutions and afflictions you bear.
</p>

## God’s Righteous Judgment

<p>
v05: This is evidence of God's righteous judgment, so that you may be considered worthy of God's kingdom, for which you also suffer.
v06: Indeed, it is just for God to repay with affliction those who afflict you,
v07: and to give relief to you who are afflicted, along with us, when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with his powerful angels
v08: in flaming fire, bringing justice on those who do not know God and who do not obey the good news of our Lord Jesus.
v09: They will pay the penalty of eternal destruction away from the Lord's presence and from the glory of his might,
v10: when he comes on that day to be glorified among his saints and marveled at among all who have believed, because our testimony to you was believed.
</p>

## A Prayer for Worthy Calling

<p>
v11: With this in view, we always pray for you, that our God may consider you worthy of his calling and powerfully fulfill every desire for goodness and every work of faith,
v12: so that the name of our Lord Jesus may be glorified in you, and you in him, according to the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.
</p>

## Notes

v06–10: The letter voices retributive judgment in images of affliction, fire and lasting destruction; the violence is retained as the author’s claim.

v12: The Greek can be read ‘our God and Lord Jesus Christ’ or ‘our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.’
