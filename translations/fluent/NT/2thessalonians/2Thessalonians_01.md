## Greeting and Thanksgiving

<p>
v01: Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy, to the church of the Thessalonians in God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ:
v02: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
v03: We ought always to thank God for you, brothers and sisters, as is fitting, because your trust is growing greatly and the love of each one of you for one another is increasing.
v04: Therefore we ourselves boast about you among God’s churches because of your endurance and trust in all your persecutions and the afflictions you are bearing.
</p>

## God’s Righteous Judgment

<p>
v05: This is evidence of God’s righteous judgment, so that you may be considered worthy of God’s kingdom, for which you are also suffering.
v06: For it is righteous with God to repay affliction to those who afflict you,
v07: and to give relief to you who are afflicted, along with us, when the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with his powerful angels
v08: in flaming fire, bringing judgment on those who do not know God and those who do not obey the good news of our Lord Jesus.
v09: They will pay the penalty of eternal destruction away from the Lord’s presence and from the glory of his might,
v10: when he comes on that day to be glorified among his holy ones and marveled at among all who trusted—because our testimony to you was trusted.
</p>

## A Prayer for Worthy Calling

<p>
v11: For this reason we always pray for you, that our God may consider you worthy of the calling and fulfill every desire for goodness and every work of trust with power,
v12: so that the name of our Lord Jesus may be glorified in you, and you in him, according to the grace of our God and the Lord Jesus Christ.
</p>

## Notes

v03: Paul praises growth in both trust and mutual love; perseverance is communal as well as individual.

v05: Suffering does not earn the kingdom as a payment. Paul interprets endurance under suffering as evidence within God’s coming righteous judgment.

v06–07: Divine justice includes both repayment to oppressors and relief for the afflicted; believers are not instructed to execute this vengeance themselves.

v08: “Those who do not know God” and “those who do not obey the good news” are descriptions of response to God, not ethnic categories.

v09: “Eternal destruction away from the Lord’s presence” is severe judgment language. Fluent preserves it without adding a theory about the exact experience or duration beyond the adjective Paul uses.

v10: The coming of Christ is oriented toward glory and wonder among the holy ones, not fear alone.

v11: Calling and human action are held together: God fulfills desires for goodness and works of trust “with power.”

v12: Grace is the final frame even for the letter’s language of worthiness and judgment.
