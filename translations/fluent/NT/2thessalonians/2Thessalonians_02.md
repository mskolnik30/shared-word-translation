## Do Not Be Quickly Shaken

<p>
v01: Now concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering to him, we ask you, brothers and sisters,
v02: not to be quickly shaken from your senses or alarmed, whether by a spirit, a spoken message or a letter supposedly from us, claiming that the day of the Lord has already arrived.
v03: Do not let anyone deceive you in any way. That day will not come unless the rebellion comes first and the man of lawlessness is revealed, the son of destruction.
v04: He opposes and exalts himself above every so-called god or object of worship, so that he seats himself in God's temple, presenting himself as God.
v05: Do you not remember that I told you these things while I was still with you?
</p>

## The Mystery of Lawlessness

<p>
v06: And now you know what restrains him, so that he may be revealed at his own time.
v07: For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only the one now restraining it will continue until he is out of the way.
v08: Then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord Jesus will kill with the breath of his mouth and bring to nothing by the appearance of his coming.
v09: The lawless one's coming is in accordance with Satan's activity, with every kind of power, false signs and wonders,
v10: and with every wicked deception for those who are perishing, because they did not welcome the love of the truth so as to be saved.
v11: For this reason God sends them a powerful delusion, so that they believe the lie,
v12: so that all who did not believe the truth but delighted in unrighteousness may be judged.
</p>

## Stand Firm

<p>
v13: But we ought always to thank God for you, brothers and sisters loved by the Lord, because God chose you as firstfruits for salvation through the Spirit's sanctifying work and faith in the truth.
v14: He called you to this through our good news, so that you may obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.
v15: So then, brothers and sisters, stand firm and hold fast to the traditions you were taught, whether by our spoken word or by our letter.
v16: May our Lord Jesus Christ himself and God our Father, who loved us and by grace gave us eternal encouragement and good hope,
v17: encourage your hearts and strengthen you in every good work and word.
</p>

## Notes

v02: The pinned text says ‘day of the Lord.’

v03: The pinned text reads ‘man of lawlessness’; manuscripts also preserve a ‘sin’ reading.

v06–07: The text does not identify either the restraining power or the one who restrains.

v13: The pinned reading is ‘firstfruits’; another early reading can mean ‘from the beginning.’

## Vocabulary

v02: **ἐνίστημι (enistēmi)**: Be present or have arrived; the claim denied here is that the day is already present.
