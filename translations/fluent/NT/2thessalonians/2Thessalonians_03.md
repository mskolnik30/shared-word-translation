## Pray for the Word to Run

<p>
v01: Finally, brothers and sisters, pray for us, that the Lord's word may run swiftly and be honored, just as it was among you,
v02: and that we may be rescued from perverse and evil people, for not everyone has faith.
v03: But the Lord is faithful; he will strengthen you and guard you from the evil one.
v04: We are confident in the Lord about you, that you are doing and will continue to do what we command.
v05: May the Lord direct your hearts into God's love and Christ's endurance.
</p>

## Work Quietly and Do Not Grow Weary

<p>
v06: Now we command you, brothers and sisters, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to keep away from every brother or sister who lives disorderly and not according to the tradition received from us.
v07: For you yourselves know how you ought to imitate us: we were not disorderly among you,
v08: nor did we eat anyone's bread without paying. Instead, with labor and hardship we worked night and day so that we would not burden any of you.
v09: It was not because we lack the right, but so that we might offer ourselves as an example for you to imitate.
v10: Even when we were with you, we gave you this command: “If anyone is unwilling to work, that person should not eat.”
v11: For we hear that some among you live disorderly, not working at all but meddling in others' work.
v12: We command and urge such people in the Lord Jesus Christ to work quietly and eat their own bread.
v13: But you, brothers and sisters, do not grow weary in doing good.
</p>

## Correct as Family, Not as an Enemy

<p>
v14: If anyone does not obey our word in this letter, take note of that person and do not associate closely, so that the person may feel ashamed.
v15: Yet do not regard that person as an enemy, but warn them as a brother or sister.
</p>

## Final Blessing and Signature

<p>
v16: Now may the Lord of peace himself give you peace at all times in every way. The Lord be with all of you.
v17: I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. This is the sign in every letter; this is how I write.
v18: The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with all of you.
</p>

## Notes

v10: The condition concerns someone unwilling to work, not a person unable to work.

v14–15: The social boundary is expressly limited: the person is to be warned as family, not treated as an enemy.
