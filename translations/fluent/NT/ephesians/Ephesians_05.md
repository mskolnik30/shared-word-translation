## Walk in Love and Light

<p>
v01: Therefore imitate God, as dearly loved children,
v02: and walk in love, just as Christ loved us and gave himself for us as an offering and sacrifice to God, a fragrant aroma.
v03: But sexual immorality, every kind of impurity, or greed must not even be named among you, as is fitting for saints.
v04: Nor should there be shameful behavior, foolish talk, or crude joking, which are improper, but rather thanksgiving.
v05: For know this with certainty: no sexually immoral, impure, or greedy person—who is an idolater—has an inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God.
v06: Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things God's wrath comes upon the children of disobedience.
v07: Therefore do not become partners with them.
v08: For you were once darkness, but now you are light in the Lord. Walk as children of light—
v09: for the fruit of the light consists in all goodness, righteousness, and truth—
v10: testing what is pleasing to the Lord.
v11: Do not participate in the fruitless works of darkness, but instead expose them.
v12: For it is shameful even to speak of the things they do in secret.
v13: But everything exposed by the light becomes visible,
v14: for everything made visible is light. Therefore it says, “Wake up, sleeper, and rise from the dead, and Christ will shine on you.”
</p>

## Walk Wisely and Be Filled with the Spirit

<p>
v15: Therefore watch carefully how you walk—not as unwise people but as wise,
v16: buying up the opportunity, because the days are evil.
v17: Because of this, do not be foolish, but understand what the Lord's will is.
v18: Do not get drunk with wine, which leads to reckless living, but be filled by the Spirit,
v19: speaking to one another with psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, singing and making melody to the Lord with your heart,
v20: always giving thanks for everything to God the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ,
v21: submitting to one another in reverence for Christ.
</p>

## Marriage and the Mystery of Christ and the Church

<p>
v22: Wives, submit to your own husbands as to the Lord,
v23: because a husband is head of his wife as Christ is head of the church, he himself the Savior of the body.
v24: But as the church submits to Christ, so wives should submit to their husbands in everything.
v25: Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved the church and gave himself for her,
v26: so that he might make her holy, cleansing her by the washing of water with the word,
v27: so that he might present the church to himself in splendor, without spot or wrinkle or anything like that, but holy and blameless.
v28: In the same way, husbands ought to love their own wives as their own bodies. The man who loves his own wife loves himself,
v29: for no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and tenderly cares for it, just as Christ does the church,
v30: because we are members of his body.
v31: “For this reason a man will leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh.”
v32: This mystery is great, and I am speaking about Christ and the church.
v33: Nevertheless, each one of you must love his own wife as himself, and the wife should respect her husband.
</p>

## Notes

v11–14: Exposure by light concerns fruitless hidden works. The passage does not require publicizing victims' experiences or repeating shameful acts in detail.

v18–21: Being filled by the Spirit is expressed through mutual song, thanksgiving, and submission to one another; verse 21 grammatically frames the household address that follows.

v22: The pinned Greek has no explicit verb in this verse; ‘submit’ is supplied from the immediately preceding command to mutual submission.

v25–29: Husbands are commanded to love through self-giving, cleansing purpose, bodily identification, nourishment, and tender care. The image cannot justify coercion or injury.

v33: The verb can mean fear or reverence. ‘Respect’ conveys its relational force here while the husband's repeated command remains love.

## Vocabulary

v16: **ἐξαγοράζω (exagorazō)**: Buy up or make full use of an opportunity.

v21: **ὑποτάσσω (hypotassō)**: Order oneself under or submit.

v29: **θάλπω (thalpō)**: Warm, cherish, or tenderly care for.
