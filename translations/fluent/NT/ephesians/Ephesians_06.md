## Children, Parents, Slaves, and Masters

<p>
v01: Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.
v02: “Honor your father and mother”—this is the first commandment with a promise—
v03: “so that things may go well for you and you may live long on the earth.”
v04: And fathers, do not provoke your children to anger. Instead, nurture them in the Lord's discipline and instruction.
v05: Slaves, obey your earthly masters with fear and trembling and with a sincere heart, as you would Christ.
v06: Do not work only while someone is watching, trying to win human approval. Work as Christ's slaves, doing God's will from the heart.
v07: Serve with goodwill, as though serving the Lord and not people,
v08: knowing that the Lord will repay each person for whatever good they do, whether slave or free.
v09: And masters, treat them in the same way, and stop threatening them. You know that their Master and yours is in heaven, and he shows no favoritism.
</p>

## The Armor of God

<p>
v10: Finally, draw your strength from the Lord and his mighty power.
v11: Put on God's full armor so that you can stand against the devil's schemes.
v12: For our struggle is not against flesh and blood, but against rulers, against authorities, against the powers that rule this darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly realms.
v13: So take up God's full armor, so that you can resist on the evil day and, having done everything, stand.
v14: Stand, then, with truth fastened around your waist and righteousness worn as a breastplate,
v15: and with your feet fitted with the readiness that comes from the good news of peace.
v16: In every situation, take up the shield of faith. With it you will be able to put out all the evil one's flaming arrows.
v17: Receive the helmet of salvation and the sword of the Spirit, which is God's word.
v18: Pray in the Spirit at every opportunity, with every kind of prayer and petition. To that end, stay alert and persevere in prayer for all the saints.
v19: Pray for me too, that words may be given to me when I open my mouth, so that I can boldly make known the mystery of the good news.
v20: It is for this good news that I am an ambassador in chains. Pray that I may speak about it boldly, as I ought.
</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>
v21: So that you too may know how I am and what I am doing, Tychicus will tell you everything. He is a beloved brother and a faithful minister in the Lord.
v22: I am sending him to you for this very purpose: to let you know how we are and to encourage your hearts.
v23: Peace to the brothers and sisters, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
v24: Grace be with all who love our Lord Jesus Christ with an undying love.
</p>

## Notes

v04: The address is specifically to fathers. The verb rendered ‘nurture’ is also used in 5:29.

v05–09: The Greek names slaves and masters, not employees and employers. ‘Earthly masters’ is literally ‘masters according to the flesh.’

v12: ‘Flesh and blood’ refers to human beings. The repeated ‘against’ distinguishes the powers named as the opponents.

v15: ‘Readiness’ can also carry the sense of preparation or a firm foundation. The good news is explicitly characterized by peace.

v24: The final phrase literally means ‘in incorruptibility.’ It can describe undying love or the imperishable life in which the love belongs.

## Vocabulary

v06: **ὀφθαλμοδουλία (ophthalmodoulia)**: Eye-service: service performed only while watched.

v09: **προσωπολημψία (prosōpolēmpsia)**: Partiality or favoritism.

v11: **πανοπλία (panoplia)**: A full set of armor.

v20: **πρεσβεύω (presbeuō)**: Serve as an ambassador.
