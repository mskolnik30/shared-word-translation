## Made Alive by Grace

<p>
v01: And you were dead in your trespasses and sins,
v02: in which you once walked according to the age of this world, according to the ruler of the power of the air, the spirit now working in the children of disobedience.
v03: All of us also once lived among them in the desires of our flesh, doing what the flesh and our thoughts willed, and we were by nature children of wrath like the rest.
v04: But God, being rich in mercy, because of the great love with which he loved us,
v05: even when we were dead in trespasses, made us alive together with Christ—by grace you have been saved—
v06: and raised us together and seated us together in the heavenly realms in Christ Jesus,
v07: so that in the coming ages he might demonstrate the surpassing wealth of his grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.
v08: For by grace you have been saved through faith, and this is not from yourselves; it is God's gift,
v09: not from works, so that no one may boast.
v10: For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand so that we would walk in them.
</p>

## One New Humanity

<p>
v11: Therefore remember that formerly you, Gentiles in the flesh—called “uncircumcision” by what is called “circumcision,” performed in the flesh by human hands—
v12: remember that at that time you were without Christ, alienated from the citizenship of Israel and strangers to the covenants of promise, having no hope and without God in the world.
v13: But now in Christ Jesus, you who once were far away have been brought near by Christ's blood.
v14: For he himself is our peace. He made the two one and in his flesh tore down the dividing wall, the hostility.
v15: He nullified the law of commandments in decrees so that in himself he might create the two into one new humanity, making peace,
v16: and reconcile both to God in one body through the cross, killing the hostility through it.
v17: He came and proclaimed peace to you who were far away and peace to those who were near,
v18: because through him both groups have access in one Spirit to the Father.
</p>

## A Dwelling Place for God

<p>
v19: So then, you are no longer strangers and resident foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints and members of God's household.
v20: You have been built on the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ Jesus himself being the cornerstone.
v21: In him the whole building, joined together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord.
v22: And in him you also are being built together into a dwelling place for God in the Spirit.
</p>

## Notes

v01–10: The movement is from death and enslaving powers to life with Christ by grace, then to good works God prepared; works are result and vocation, not grounds for boasting.

v11–13: The passage names Gentile alienation from Israel's citizenship and covenants before describing nearness in Christ; the history is not erased.

v14–18: Christ is peace, makes the two groups one, kills hostility through the cross, and gives both access to the Father in one Spirit.

v19–22: Political belonging, household membership, building, temple, and divine dwelling overlap as images of the reconciled community.

## Vocabulary

v10: **ποίημα (poiēma)**: Workmanship, something made or crafted.

v19: **πάροικος (paroikos)**: Resident foreigner or noncitizen living alongside others.
