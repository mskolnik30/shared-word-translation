## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through God’s will, to the holy ones who are in Ephesus and faithful in Christ Jesus:
v02: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
</p>

## Every Spiritual Blessing in Christ

<p>
v03: Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly realms,
v04: just as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world to be holy and blameless before him in love.
v05: He destined us beforehand for adoption through Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,
v06: to the praise of the glory of his grace, which he freely gave us in the Beloved.
v07: In him we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of trespasses, according to the riches of his grace,
v08: which he lavished on us in all wisdom and understanding.
v09: He made known to us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure that he purposed in Christ,
v10: for the administration of the fullness of the times: to bring everything together in Christ, things in heaven and things on earth—in him.
v11: In him we also received an inheritance, having been destined beforehand according to the purpose of the one who works all things according to the counsel of his will,
v12: so that we who first hoped in Christ might be for the praise of his glory.
v13: In him you also, when you heard the word of truth, the good news of your salvation, and trusted in him, were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit,
v14: who is the down payment of our inheritance until the redemption of God’s own possession, to the praise of his glory.
</p>

## A Prayer for Wisdom and Hope

<p>
v15: For this reason, because I have heard about your trust in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the holy ones,
v16: I do not stop giving thanks for you, remembering you in my prayers,
v17: so that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give you a Spirit of wisdom and revelation in knowing him,
v18: with the eyes of your heart enlightened, so that you may know the hope of his calling, the riches of the glory of his inheritance among the holy ones,
v19: and the immeasurable greatness of his power toward us who trust, according to the working of his mighty strength.
v20: He worked this power in Christ when he raised him from the dead and seated him at his right hand in the heavenly realms,
v21: far above every ruler, authority, power, dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the age to come.
v22: He put everything under his feet and gave him as head over everything to the church,
v23: which is his body, the fullness of the one who fills everything in every way.
</p>

## Notes

v01: “In Ephesus” is bracketed in SBLGNT because important early witnesses omit the place-name. Fluent follows the pinned text while preserving the textual issue here.

v03–14: The Greek is one long sentence. Fluent breaks it into readable English sentences while preserving its cascading structure and repeated “in Christ/in him” architecture.

v05: `proorizō` is “destine beforehand.” Fluent preserves the temporal claim without using translation to settle later debates about predestination.

v07: Redemption and forgiveness are linked to Christ’s blood and to the abundance of grace.

v10: `anakephalaiōsasthai` means to sum up, bring together, or gather under a head. Fluent uses “bring everything together in Christ.”

v13–14: The Spirit is both seal and `arrabōn`, a down payment guaranteeing the inheritance to come.

v17: “Spirit of wisdom and revelation” can refer to the Holy Spirit or a God-given disposition. The capitalized main text reflects the immediate pneumatological context without excluding the experiential sense.

v22–23: Christ is head over all things “to the church,” and the church is his body. Fluent does not force `plērōma` into one later technical definition.
