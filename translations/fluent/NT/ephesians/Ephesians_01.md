## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by God's will, to the saints who are [in Ephesus] and faithful in Christ Jesus:
v02: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
</p>

## Every Spiritual Blessing in Christ

<p>
v03: Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly realms.
v04: For he chose us in Christ before the foundation of the world to be holy and blameless before him in love.
v05: He predestined us for adoption as sons through Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,
v06: to the praise of his glorious grace, which he freely bestowed on us in the Beloved.
v07: In him we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of trespasses, according to the wealth of his grace
v08: that he lavished on us, with all wisdom and understanding.
v09: He made known to us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure that he purposed in Christ,
v10: for an administration at the fullness of the times: to bring together all things in Christ, things in the heavens and things on earth—in him.
v11: In him we also received an inheritance, having been predestined according to the purpose of the one who works all things according to the counsel of his will,
v12: so that we, who were the first to hope in Christ, might live for the praise of his glory.
v13: In him you also, when you heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation, and believed in him, were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit.
v14: The Spirit is the down payment of our inheritance until the redemption of God's possession, to the praise of his glory.
</p>

## A Prayer for Wisdom and Hope

<p>
v15: For this reason, because I have heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus and your love for all the saints,
v16: I do not stop giving thanks for you as I remember you in my prayers.
v17: I pray that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in knowing him.
v18: May the eyes of your heart be enlightened so that you may know the hope of his calling, the wealth of his glorious inheritance among the saints,
v19: and the surpassing greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of his mighty strength.
v20: God exercised this power in Christ when he raised him from the dead and seated him at his right hand in the heavenly realms,
v21: far above every ruler, authority, power, and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in the age to come.
v22: He subjected everything under Christ's feet and gave him as head over everything to the church,
v23: which is his body, the fullness of the one who fills all things in every way.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The words ‘in Ephesus’ are bracketed in the pinned Greek because important early witnesses omit them. The bracket remains visible in the main text.

v03–14: The blessing unfolds as one long Greek sentence, repeatedly locating election, redemption, inheritance, and the Spirit ‘in Christ.’

v05: ‘Adoption as sons’ names full legal inheritance status in the ancient setting and is applied to the whole addressed community.

v23: The relation between the church as Christ's fullness and Christ as the one filling all things is grammatically dense; the wordplay is retained.

## Vocabulary

v10: **οἰκονομία (oikonomia)**: Administration, stewardship, or ordered plan.

v14: **ἀρραβών (arrabōn)**: Down payment, deposit, or first installment.
