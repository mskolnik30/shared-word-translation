## Walk Worthy of the Calling

<p>
v01: Therefore I, the prisoner in the Lord, urge you to walk in a manner worthy of the calling with which you were called,
v02: with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,
v03: making every effort to preserve the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace:
v04: one body and one Spirit, just as you were also called in one hope of your calling;
v05: one Lord, one faith, one baptism;
v06: one God and Father of all, who is over all, through all, and in all.
</p>

## Gifts for Building the Body

<p>
v07: But grace was given to each one of us according to the measure of Christ's gift.
v08: Therefore it says, “When he ascended on high, he took captivity captive; he gave gifts to people.”
v09: Now what does “he ascended” mean except that he also descended into the lower parts of the earth?
v10: The one who descended is also the one who ascended far above all the heavens, so that he might fill all things.
v11: And he himself gave some as apostles, some as prophets, some as evangelists, and some as pastors and teachers,
v12: to equip the saints for the work of ministry, for building up Christ's body,
v13: until all of us reach unity in the faith and in knowing the Son of God, mature humanity, the measure of the stature of Christ's fullness.
v14: Then we will no longer be children, tossed by waves and carried around by every wind of teaching, by human trickery and cunning schemes that lead into error.
v15: Instead, speaking truth in love, let us grow in every way into him who is the head—Christ.
v16: From him the whole body, joined and held together by every supporting ligament, grows and builds itself up in love as each individual part does its work in proper measure.
</p>

## Put Off the Old Humanity

<p>
v17: Therefore I say this and testify in the Lord: you must no longer walk as the Gentiles also walk, in the futility of their minds.
v18: They are darkened in understanding and alienated from God's life because of the ignorance within them, because of the hardness of their hearts.
v19: Having become callous, they gave themselves over to debauchery, to practice every kind of impurity with greed.
v20: But that is not how you learned Christ,
v21: if indeed you heard him and were taught in him, just as truth is in Jesus:
v22: you were taught to put off the old person belonging to your former way of life, which is being corrupted by deceitful desires,
v23: to be renewed in the spirit of your minds,
v24: and to put on the new person, created according to God in the righteousness and holiness that come from truth.
</p>

## Speak and Live Truthfully

<p>
v25: Therefore, putting away falsehood, “each of you speak truth with your neighbor,” because we are members of one another.
v26: “Be angry, but do not sin.” Do not let the sun set while you remain provoked,
v27: and do not give the devil an opportunity.
v28: The one who steals must steal no longer, but rather must labor, doing good work with their own hands, so that they may have something to share with anyone in need.
v29: Let no rotten word come out of your mouth, but only what is good for building up as the need requires, so that it may give grace to those who hear.
v30: And do not grieve God's Holy Spirit, by whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.
v31: Let all bitterness, rage, anger, shouting, and slander be removed from you, along with every kind of malice.
v32: Instead, be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other just as God in Christ forgave you.
</p>

## Notes

v08: The quotation literally says he ‘took captivity captive,’ an ancient victory image retained without identifying the captives.

v09: ‘The lower parts of the earth’ can mean the earth below the heavens or lower regions within the earth; the translation leaves the phrase open.

v11–13: The gifted leaders equip the saints for ministry and the building up of the body; maturity is a shared destination for ‘all of us.’

v26: The text permits anger while forbidding sin and prolonged provocation; it does not call rage or harm righteous.

v28: The reversal from stealing leads beyond private labor to sharing with a person in need.

## Vocabulary

v12: **καταρτισμός (katartismos)**: Equipping, preparing, or restoring for service.

v15: **ἀληθεύω (alētheuō)**: Speak or practice truth.
