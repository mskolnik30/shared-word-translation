## The Mystery Made Known among the Gentiles

<p>
v01: For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for the sake of you Gentiles—
v02: surely you have heard about the stewardship of God's grace that was given to me for you,
v03: that by revelation the mystery was made known to me, as I wrote briefly before.
v04: When you read this, you can understand my insight into the mystery of Christ,
v05: which was not made known to people in other generations as it has now been revealed by the Spirit to his holy apostles and prophets:
v06: the Gentiles are coheirs, members of the same body, and copartners in the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel.
v07: I became a servant of this gospel according to the gift of God's grace given to me through the working of his power.
v08: This grace was given to me, the very least of all the saints: to proclaim to the Gentiles the unsearchable wealth of Christ
v09: and to bring to light for everyone the administration of the mystery hidden for ages in God, who created all things.
v10: This was so that now, through the church, God's richly varied wisdom might be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly realms,
v11: according to the eternal purpose he accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord.
v12: In him we have boldness and confident access through his faithfulness.
v13: Therefore I ask you not to lose heart because of my afflictions for you, which are your glory.
</p>

## A Prayer to Know Christ’s Love

<p>
v14: For this reason I bend my knees before the Father,
v15: from whom every family in heaven and on earth receives its name.
v16: I pray that, according to the wealth of his glory, he may grant you to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in your inner being,
v17: so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, as you are rooted and grounded in love.
v18: May you have strength to grasp, together with all the saints, what is the width and length and height and depth,
v19: and to know Christ's love that surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.
v20: Now to the one who is able to do far beyond all that we ask or imagine, according to the power at work within us,
v21: to him be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus through all generations, forever and ever. Amen.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The sentence breaks off after Paul calls himself Christ's prisoner for the Gentiles and resumes the ‘for this reason’ language in verse 14.

v06: Three Greek compounds beginning with ‘co-’ describe Gentiles as equal heirs, one body, and equal participants in the promise.

v12: The Greek can mean faith in Christ or Christ's faithfulness. The main text uses Christ's faithfulness as the ground of confident access.

v18: Width, length, height, and depth have no explicit noun in the verse; the translation does not add one.

## Vocabulary

v06: **συγκληρονόμος (synklēronomos)**: Coheir, one sharing the same inheritance.

v10: **πολυποίκιλος (polypoikilos)**: Richly varied, many-sided, or multicolored.
