## The Mystery Made Known among the Gentiles

<p>
v01: For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for the sake of you Gentiles—
v02: surely you have heard about the stewardship of God’s grace given to me for you,
v03: how the mystery was made known to me by revelation, as I wrote briefly before.
v04: By reading this, you can understand my insight into the mystery of Christ,
v05: which was not made known to other generations as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets by the Spirit:
v06: the Gentiles are co-heirs, members of the same body, and co-participants in the promise in Christ Jesus through the good news.
v07: I became a servant of this good news according to the gift of God’s grace given to me through the working of his power.
v08: To me, the least of all the holy ones, this grace was given: to announce to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ,
v09: and to bring to light for everyone the administration of the mystery hidden for ages in God, who created all things,
v10: so that now, through the church, the many-sided wisdom of God might be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly realms,
v11: according to the eternal purpose he carried out in Christ Jesus our Lord.
v12: In him we have boldness and confident access through trust in him.
v13: Therefore I ask you not to lose heart because of my sufferings for you, which are your glory.
</p>

## A Prayer to Know Christ’s Love

<p>
v14: For this reason I bend my knees before the Father,
v15: from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named,
v16: asking that, according to the riches of his glory, he may grant you to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in your inner being,
v17: so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through trust, as you are rooted and grounded in love.
v18: May you have strength to comprehend, together with all the holy ones, what is the breadth and length and height and depth,
v19: and to know Christ’s love that surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled to all the fullness of God.
v20: Now to the one who is able to do far beyond everything we ask or imagine, according to the power at work within us,
v21: to him be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus through all generations, forever and ever. Amen.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The sentence begun here is interrupted and resumed at v14. Paul’s self-description as “prisoner of Christ” reframes Roman custody through vocation.

v02: `oikonomia` can mean stewardship, administration, or commission. Fluent uses “stewardship” for Paul’s entrusted grace-work.

v06: Paul coins a cluster of “co-” terms: Gentiles are co-heirs, same-body members, and co-participants. Equality is built into the grammar.

v08: “Least of all the holy ones” is Paul’s self-description beside an enormous Gentile mission commission.

v10: The church’s reconciled Jew-Gentile existence becomes a display of God’s wisdom even to heavenly rulers and authorities.

v12: `pistis autou` is grammatically open to “trust in him” or “his faithfulness.” Fluent uses human trust here because the immediate syntax speaks of access “in him” and the letter’s dominant response language.

v15: `patria` is a wordplay with `patēr`, Father. “Every family” preserves the broad sense better than “fatherhood” in this context.

v18–19: The four dimensions are left without an explicit object, followed by Christ’s love surpassing knowledge. Fluent does not insert “the love of Christ” into v18 itself.

v19: “Filled to all the fullness of God” is deliberately expansive and should not be reduced to moral improvement alone.
