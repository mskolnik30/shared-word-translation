## Greeting
<p>
v01: Paul, called to be an apostle of Christ Jesus by God's will, and our brother Sosthenes,
v02: to God's church in Corinth, to those made holy in Christ Jesus and called to be saints, together with all who in every place call on the name of our Lord Jesus Christ—their Lord and ours:
v03: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
</p>

## Thanksgiving
<p>
v04: I always thank my God for you because of God's grace given to you in Christ Jesus.
v05: For in him you were enriched in every way, in all speech and all knowledge,
v06: just as the testimony about Christ was confirmed among you,
v07: so that you do not lack any gift as you eagerly await the revealing of our Lord Jesus Christ.
</p>

<p>
v08: He will also strengthen you to the end, blameless on the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.
v09: God is faithful, through whom you were called into fellowship with his Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
</p>

## Divisions in the Church
<p>
v10: Now I urge you, brothers and sisters, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that all of you agree and that there be no divisions among you, but that you be restored in the same mind and the same purpose.
v11: For it has been reported to me about you, my brothers and sisters, by Chloe's people, that there are quarrels among you.
</p>

<p>
v12: What I mean is this: each of you says, “I belong to Paul,” “I to Apollos,” “I to Cephas,” or “I to Christ.”
v13: Has Christ been divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptized into Paul's name?
</p>

<p>
v14: I thank God that I baptized none of you except Crispus and Gaius,
v15: so that no one can say you were baptized into my name.
v16: I also baptized the household of Stephanas. Beyond that, I do not know whether I baptized anyone else.
</p>

<p>
v17: For Christ did not send me to baptize but to proclaim the good news—not with eloquent wisdom, so that Christ's cross would not be emptied of its power.
</p>

## Christ the Power and Wisdom of God
<p>
v18: For the message of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is God's power.
v19: For it is written: “I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and I will reject the understanding of the intelligent.”
</p>

<p>
v20: Where is the wise person? Where is the scribe? Where is the debater of this age? Has God not made the world's wisdom foolish?
v21: For since, in God's wisdom, the world did not know God through wisdom, God was pleased through the foolishness of what is proclaimed to save those who believe.
</p>

<p>
v22: For Jews ask for signs and Greeks seek wisdom,
v23: but we proclaim Christ crucified: a stumbling block to Jews and foolishness to Gentiles,
v24: yet to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ is God's power and God's wisdom.
</p>

<p>
v25: For God's foolishness is wiser than human wisdom, and God's weakness is stronger than human strength.
</p>

## God’s Choice of the Lowly
<p>
v26: Consider your calling, brothers and sisters: not many of you were wise by human standards, not many powerful, not many of noble birth.
v27: But God chose the foolish things of the world to shame the wise, and God chose the weak things of the world to shame the strong.
v28: God chose the lowborn and despised things of the world—things that are not—to bring to nothing the things that are,
v29: so that no human being may boast before God.
</p>

<p>
v30: But it is from God that you are in Christ Jesus, who became for us wisdom from God—and righteousness, holiness, and redemption—
v31: so that, as it is written, “Let the one who boasts boast in the Lord.”
</p>

## Notes

v10–13: Paul opposes leader-centered factions by asking whether Christ has been divided and whether Paul was crucified or became their baptismal name.

v18–25: ‘Foolishness,’ ‘weakness,’ wisdom, and power remain in deliberate paradox around the crucified Messiah.

v26–29: The calling language names social status and power. It does not romanticize deprivation; it denies human boasting before God.

## Vocabulary

v02: **ἅγιος (hagios)**: Holy one or saint.

v10: **σχίσμα (schisma)**: Division, split, or tear.

v30: **ἀπολύτρωσις (apolytrōsis)**: Redemption or release secured at a cost.
