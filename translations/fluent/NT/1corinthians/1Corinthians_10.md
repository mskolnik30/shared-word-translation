## Warning from Israel’s History

<p>
v01: For I do not want you to be unaware, brothers and sisters, that our ancestors were all under the cloud and all passed through the sea.
v02: They were all baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea.
v03: They all ate the same spiritual food,
v04: and all drank the same spiritual drink. For they drank from the spiritual rock that followed them, and that rock was Christ.
</p>

<p>
v05: Nevertheless, God was not pleased with most of them, and their bodies were scattered across the wilderness.
</p>

<p>
v06: Now these things became examples for us, so that we would not desire evil things as they did.
v07: Do not become idolaters as some of them did, as it is written: “The people sat down to eat and drink and rose up to play.”
</p>

<p>
v08: Let us not commit sexual immorality as some of them did, and twenty-three thousand fell in one day.
v09: Let us not test Christ as some of them did and were destroyed by snakes.
v10: And do not grumble as some of them did and were destroyed by the destroyer.
</p>

<p>
v11: These things happened to them as examples and were written as a warning for us, on whom the ends of the ages have come.
v12: So the one who thinks they stand must watch out lest they fall.
</p>

<p>
v13: No trial has overtaken you except what is common to humanity. God is faithful; he will not allow you to be tested beyond what you can bear, but with the trial he will also provide a way out so that you can endure it.
</p>

## Flee from Idolatry

<p>
v14: Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.
v15: I speak to sensible people; judge for yourselves what I say.
</p>

<p>
v16: The cup of blessing that we bless—is it not participation in Christ's blood? The bread that we break—is it not participation in Christ's body?
v17: Because there is one bread, we who are many are one body, for all of us partake of the one bread.
</p>

<p>
v18: Consider Israel according to the flesh: are not those who eat the sacrifices participants in the altar?
</p>

<p>
v19: What then am I saying? That food sacrificed to an idol is anything, or that an idol is anything?
v20: No, but what they sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons and not to God. I do not want you to become participants with demons.
</p>

<p>
v21: You cannot drink the Lord's cup and the cup of demons. You cannot partake of the Lord's table and the table of demons.
v22: Or are we provoking the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than he is?
</p>

## Do All to the Glory of God

<p>
v23: “Everything is permissible,” but not everything is beneficial. “Everything is permissible,” but not everything builds up.
v24: No one should seek one's own good but the good of the other.
</p>

<p>
v25: Eat anything sold in the meat market without raising questions for conscience's sake,
v26: for “the earth is the Lord's, and everything in it.”
</p>

<p>
v27: If an unbeliever invites you and you wish to go, eat whatever is set before you without raising questions for conscience's sake.
v28: But if someone tells you, “This was offered in sacrifice,” do not eat it, for the sake of the one who informed you and for conscience's sake—
v29: I mean the other person's conscience, not your own. For why should my freedom be judged by someone else's conscience?
</p>

<p>
v30: If I partake with gratitude, why am I denounced for something for which I give thanks?
</p>

<p>
v31: So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do everything for God's glory.
v32: Give no offense to Jews, Greeks, or God's church,
v33: just as I try to please everyone in everything, not seeking my own advantage but that of the many, so that they may be saved.
</p>

## Notes

v08: The pinned text reads twenty-three thousand, distinct from the twenty-four thousand in Numbers 25's broader account.

v09: The pinned Greek reads ‘test Christ’; some witnesses read ‘test the Lord.’

v13: The same word can mean temptation or trial. The promised ‘way out’ enables endurance; it does not deny severe circumstances.

v16–21: Participation at the Lord's table is contrasted with participation in sacrificial meals offered to demons; the argument is about embodied communal allegiance.

v23: ‘Everything is permissible’ is likely a Corinthian slogan. Paul answers with benefit and communal upbuilding.

## Vocabulary

v06: **τύπος (typos)**: Pattern, example, or type.

v13: **πειρασμός (peirasmos)**: Trial, test, or temptation.

v16: **κοινωνία (koinōnia)**: Participation, sharing, or fellowship.
