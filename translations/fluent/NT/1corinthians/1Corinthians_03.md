## Divisions in the Church

<p>
v01: And I, brothers and sisters, could not speak to you as spiritual people but as fleshly people, as infants in Christ.
v02: I fed you milk, not solid food, because you were not yet able to receive it. Even now you still are not able,
v03: for you are still fleshly. Since jealousy and quarrels are among you, are you not fleshly and walking in a merely human way?
</p>

<p>
v04: For when one says, “I belong to Paul,” and another, “I belong to Apollos,” are you not merely human?
</p>

## God Gives the Growth

<p>
v05: What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? Servants through whom you believed, as the Lord assigned to each.
v06: I planted, Apollos watered, but God caused the growth.
</p>

<p>
v07: So neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but only God who causes the growth.
v08: The one who plants and the one who waters are one, and each will receive wages according to their own labor.
</p>

<p>
v09: For we are God's coworkers; you are God's field, God's building.
</p>

## Builders on the Foundation

<p>
v10: According to God's grace given to me, I laid a foundation like a skilled master builder, and someone else is building on it. But each person must watch how they build.
v11: For no one can lay any foundation other than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ.
</p>

<p>
v12: Now if anyone builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, or straw,
v13: each person's work will become evident, for the Day will disclose it. It will be revealed with fire, and the fire will test the quality of each person's work.
</p>

<p>
v14: If the work someone built remains, that person will receive a reward.
v15: If someone's work is burned up, that person will suffer loss, though personally will be saved—but only as through fire.
</p>

## God’s Temple

<p>
v16: Do you not know that you together are God's temple and that God's Spirit dwells among you?
v17: If anyone destroys God's temple, God will destroy that person, for God's temple is holy—and you together are that temple.
</p>

## The Folly of Human Wisdom

<p>
v18: Let no one deceive oneself. If anyone among you thinks they are wise in this age, let that person become foolish so as to become wise.
v19: For this world's wisdom is foolishness with God. For it is written, “He catches the wise in their craftiness,”
v20: and again, “The Lord knows that the thoughts of the wise are futile.”
</p>

<p>
v21: So let no one boast in human leaders, for all things are yours:
v22: whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or things present or things to come—all are yours,
v23: and you belong to Christ, and Christ belongs to God.
</p>

## Notes

v01–4: Paul calls factional jealousy evidence of immaturity, not proof that embodied life itself is evil.

v09: The Greek can call Paul and Apollos ‘God's coworkers’ while the field and building belong to God; it does not make the Corinthians coworkers in this sentence.

v15: The builder may be saved ‘as through fire’ while the work is lost; the image is retained without constructing a later doctrine from it.

v16–17: The pronouns are plural: the community together is God's temple, and destroying it brings a severe warning.

## Vocabulary

v01: **σάρκινος (sarkinos)**: Made of flesh; fleshly or immature in context.

v10: **ἀρχιτέκτων (architektōn)**: Master builder or chief craftsperson.

v16: **ναός (naos)**: Temple sanctuary, the sacred dwelling.
