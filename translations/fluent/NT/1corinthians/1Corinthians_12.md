## Spiritual Gifts

<p>
v01: Now concerning spiritual gifts, brothers and sisters, I do not want you to be uninformed.
v02: You know that when you were Gentiles, you were led astray to mute idols, however you were led.
v03: Therefore, I want you to know that no one speaking by God's Spirit says, “Jesus is accursed,” and no one can say, “Jesus is Lord,” except by the Holy Spirit.
</p>

<p>
v04: There are varieties of gifts, but the same Spirit;
v05: there are varieties of ministries, but the same Lord;
v06: and there are varieties of activities, but the same God activates all of them in everyone.
v07: A manifestation of the Spirit is given to each person for the common good.
v08: To one is given through the Spirit a message of wisdom, and to another a message of knowledge according to the same Spirit;
v09: to another faith by the same Spirit, and to another gifts of healing by the one Spirit;
v10: to another workings of miracles, to another prophecy, to another discernment of spirits, to another various kinds of tongues, and to another interpretation of tongues.
v11: One and the same Spirit works all these things, distributing to each person individually just as the Spirit wills.
</p>

## One Body with Many Members

<p>
v12: For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the body's members, though many, are one body, so also is Christ.
v13: For in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body—whether Jews or Greeks, enslaved or free—and we were all given one Spirit to drink.
v14: For the body is not one member but many.
v15: If the foot says, “Because I am not a hand, I do not belong to the body,” that does not make it any less part of the body.
v16: And if the ear says, “Because I am not an eye, I do not belong to the body,” that does not make it any less part of the body.
v17: If the whole body were an eye, where would hearing be? If the whole were hearing, where would the sense of smell be?
v18: But as it is, God arranged each one of the members in the body just as he wished.
v19: If all were one member, where would the body be?
v20: But as it is, there are many members and one body.
</p>

<p>
v21: The eye cannot say to the hand, “I do not need you,” nor again the head to the feet, “I do not need you.”
v22: On the contrary, the members of the body that seem weaker are indispensable,
v23: and those members we consider less honorable we clothe with greater honor, while our unpresentable parts are treated with greater modesty.
v24: Our presentable parts have no need of this. But God has composed the body, giving greater honor to the part that lacked it,
v25: so that there may be no division in the body but the members may have the same care for one another.
v26: If one member suffers, all the members suffer together; if one member is honored, all the members rejoice together.
</p>

<p>
v27: Now you are Christ's body, and individually members of it.
v28: And God has appointed in the church first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then miracles, then gifts of healing, helping, leading, and various kinds of tongues.
v29: Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all work miracles?
v30: Do all have gifts of healing? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret?
v31: But eagerly desire the greater gifts. And now I will show you a way beyond all comparison.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The Greek says ‘spiritual things/people’; ‘gifts’ fits the following discussion but does not exhaust the term.

v07–11: Each manifestation is given for the common good, and the one Spirit distributes gifts according to the Spirit's own will.

v13: The body includes Jews and Greeks, enslaved and free. The translation retains the real social status of enslaved people rather than reducing it to employment.

v22–26: Members perceived as weaker or less honorable receive necessity, modesty, and greater honor; the body's unity requires shared suffering and joy.

v31: The command may be plural to the church: seek the gifts that most build others, as chapter 14 explains.

## Vocabulary

v07: **φανέρωσις (phanerōsis)**: Manifestation or visible expression.

v10: **διάκρισις (diakrisis)**: Discernment or distinguishing.

v28: **κυβέρνησις (kybernēsis)**: Guidance, leadership, or steering.
