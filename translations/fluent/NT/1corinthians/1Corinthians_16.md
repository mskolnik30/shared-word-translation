## The Collection for the Holy Ones

<p>
v01: Now concerning the collection for the saints: do as I directed the churches of Galatia.
v02: On the first day of every week, each of you should set something aside and save it, in keeping with whatever prosperity you have, so that collections will not be made when I come.
</p>

<p>
v03: Then when I arrive, I will send those you approve with letters to carry your gracious gift to Jerusalem.
v04: And if it is appropriate for me to go as well, they will go with me.
</p>

## Paul’s Travel Plans

<p>
v05: I will come to you after I pass through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia.
v06: Perhaps I will stay with you or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way wherever I go.
</p>

<p>
v07: For I do not want to see you now only in passing. I hope to remain with you for some time, if the Lord permits.
v08: But I will remain in Ephesus until Pentecost,
v09: for a great and effective door has opened for me, and there are many opponents.
</p>

## Final Instructions

<p>
v10: If Timothy comes, see that he has no reason to be afraid while among you, for he is doing the Lord's work, just as I am.
v11: So no one should despise him. Send him on his way in peace so that he may come to me, for I am expecting him with the brothers.
</p>

<p>
v12: Now concerning our brother Apollos: I strongly urged him to come to you with the brothers, but it was not at all his will to come now. He will come when he has an opportunity.
</p>

<p>
v13: Stay alert; stand firm in the faith; be courageous; be strong.
v14: Let everything you do be done in love.
</p>

<p>
v15: Now I urge you, brothers and sisters—you know the household of Stephanas, that they are the firstfruits of Achaia and have devoted themselves to serving the saints—
v16: submit yourselves to such people and to everyone who works and labors with them.
</p>

<p>
v17: I rejoice at the arrival of Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus, because they supplied what was lacking from you.
v18: For they refreshed my spirit and yours. Therefore, recognize such people.
</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>
v19: The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Prisca, together with the church in their house, greet you warmly in the Lord.
v20: All the brothers and sisters greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.
</p>

<p>
v21: I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand.
v22: If anyone does not love the Lord, let that person be accursed. Marana tha!
</p>

<p>
v23: The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.
v24: My love be with all of you in Christ Jesus.
</p>

## Notes

v01–4: The collection is for the saints in Jerusalem, gathered locally in advance and carried by representatives approved by the Corinthians.

v09: The open door and many opponents coexist; opportunity is not equated with lack of resistance.

v12: Paul strongly urged Apollos to visit, but Apollos did not wish to come then. His agency is not hidden.

v13: The Greek idiom is literally ‘act like men’; ‘be courageous’ carries its exhortational force without making courage male-only.

v15–16: The whole household of Stephanas devoted themselves to service, and the church is told to submit to such laboring people.

v22: Marana tha is Aramaic, commonly understood as ‘Our Lord, come!’ or ‘Our Lord has come.’ The form is retained because the word division is anciently ambiguous.

## Vocabulary

v01: **λογία (logia)**: Collection or contribution.

v09: **θύρα (thyra)**: Door, here an opportunity for effective work.

v22: **ἀνάθεμα (anathema)**: Accursed or devoted to destruction.
