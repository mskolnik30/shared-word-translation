## Sexual Immorality in the Church

<p>
v01: It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality among you, and of a kind not tolerated even among the Gentiles: a man has his father's wife.
v02: And you are puffed up! Should you not instead have mourned, so that the one who did this would be removed from among you?
</p>

<p>
v03: For though absent in body, I am present in spirit, and as though present I have already judged the one who did this.
v04: When you are assembled in the name of our Lord Jesus, and I am with you in spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus,
</p>

<p>
v05: hand this man over to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that his spirit may be saved on the day of the Lord.
</p>

## Clean Out the Old Leaven

<p>
v06: Your boasting is not good. Do you not know that a little yeast leavens the whole batch of dough?
v07: Clean out the old yeast so that you may be a new batch, as you really are unleavened. For Christ, our Passover lamb, has been sacrificed.
</p>

<p>
v08: Therefore, let us celebrate the feast not with old yeast, nor with the yeast of malice and evil, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.
</p>

## Judging Those Inside the Church

<p>
v09: I wrote to you in my letter not to associate with sexually immoral people—
v10: not at all meaning the sexually immoral people of this world, or the greedy and swindlers, or idolaters, since then you would have to leave the world.
</p>

<p>
v11: But now I am writing to you not to associate with anyone who bears the name of brother or sister yet is sexually immoral, greedy, an idolater, verbally abusive, a drunkard, or a swindler. Do not even eat with such a person.
</p>

<p>
v12: For what business is it of mine to judge outsiders? Is it not those inside whom you judge?
v13: God judges outsiders. “Remove the evil person from among you.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: ‘His father's wife’ normally identifies a stepmother; the text does not call her the man's biological mother.

v05: Handing the man to Satan ‘for destruction of the flesh’ is severe and disputed. The stated purpose is that his spirit may be saved.

v09–13: Paul explicitly limits this communal separation to a person claiming insider status; God judges outsiders.

v07–8: The Passover and unleavened-bread images shape the community's response to tolerated harm.

## Vocabulary

v01: **πορνεία (porneia)**: Sexual immorality.

v05: **ὄλεθρος (olethros)**: Destruction or ruin.

v07: **πάσχα (pascha)**: Passover, including its sacrificial lamb.
