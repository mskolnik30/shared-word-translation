## The More Excellent Way
<p>
v01: If I speak in the tongues of humans and of angels but do not have love, I have become a resounding gong or a clashing cymbal.
v02: And if I have prophecy and know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith so as to move mountains but do not have love, I am nothing.
v03: And if I give away all my possessions, and if I hand over my body so that I may boast, but do not have love, I gain nothing.
</p>

## What Love Is
<p>
v04: Love is patient; love is kind. Love does not envy, does not boast, is not puffed up.
v05: It does not behave dishonorably, does not seek its own way, is not easily provoked, and does not keep an account of wrong.
v06: It does not rejoice in injustice but rejoices together with the truth.
v07: It bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.
</p>

## What Endures
<p>
v08: Love never fails. But prophecies will be brought to an end, tongues will cease, and knowledge will be brought to an end.
v09: For we know in part and we prophesy in part,
v10: but when completeness comes, what is partial will be brought to an end.
</p>

<p>
v11: When I was a child, I spoke like a child, thought like a child, and reasoned like a child. When I became an adult, I put away childish things.
v12: For now we see in a mirror, dimly, but then face to face. Now I know in part; then I will know fully, just as I have been fully known.
</p>

<p>
v13: Now faith, hope, and love remain, these three; and the greatest of these is love.
</p>

## Notes

v01–3: Spectacular speech, knowledge, faith, generosity, and bodily surrender are nothing without love.

v03: The pinned Greek reads ‘so that I may boast’; a well-known variant reads ‘so that I may be burned.’

v05: ‘Does not keep an account of wrong’ uses a bookkeeping verb and does not require pretending harm never happened.

v07: ‘Bears, believes, hopes, and endures all things’ describes love's orientation; it is not a command to conceal abuse or ignore truth and justice.

v10: ‘Completeness’ is not identified more precisely in the verse; the main text avoids defining it as one later event or artifact.

## Vocabulary

v01: **ἀγάπη (agapē)**: Love expressed in committed regard and action.

v05: **λογίζομαι (logizomai)**: Count, reckon, or keep an account.

v12: **ἔσοπτρον (esoptron)**: Mirror, in antiquity typically polished metal.
