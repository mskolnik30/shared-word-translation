## Concerning Marriage

<p>
v01: Now concerning the matters you wrote about: “It is good for a man not to touch a woman.”
v02: But because of sexual immorality, each man should have his own wife, and each woman her own husband.
</p>

<p>
v03: The husband should give his wife what he owes her, and likewise the wife to her husband.
v04: The wife does not have authority over her own body; her husband does. Likewise, the husband does not have authority over his own body; his wife does.
</p>

<p>
v05: Do not deprive one another except perhaps by mutual agreement for a time, so that you may devote yourselves to prayer and then come together again, so Satan may not tempt you because of your lack of self-control.
</p>

<p>
v06: I say this as a concession, not as a command.
v07: I wish all people were as I am, but each has a particular gift from God, one of one kind and one of another.
</p>

## The Unmarried and Widows

<p>
v08: Now to the unmarried and widows I say: it is good for them to remain as I am.
v09: But if they cannot exercise self-control, they should marry, for it is better to marry than to burn with desire.
</p>

## Concerning Married Believers

<p>
v10: To the married I give this command—not I, but the Lord: a wife must not separate from her husband.
v11: But if she does separate, she should remain unmarried or be reconciled to her husband. And a husband must not divorce his wife.
</p>

## Concerning Mixed Marriages

<p>
v12: To the rest I say—I, not the Lord—if any brother has an unbelieving wife and she consents to live with him, he must not divorce her.
v13: And if a woman has an unbelieving husband and he consents to live with her, she must not divorce her husband.
</p>

<p>
v14: For the unbelieving husband is made holy through his wife, and the unbelieving wife is made holy through the believing husband. Otherwise your children would be unclean, but now they are holy.
</p>

<p>
v15: But if the unbelieving partner separates, let that person separate. A brother or sister is not enslaved in such cases; God has called us to peace.
v16: For how do you know, wife, whether you will save your husband? Or how do you know, husband, whether you will save your wife?
</p>

## Live as You Are Called

<p>
v17: In any case, each person should live as the Lord has assigned and as God has called. This is what I direct in all the churches.
v18: Was anyone already circumcised when called? He should not try to reverse it. Was anyone uncircumcised when called? He should not seek circumcision.
</p>

<p>
v19: Circumcision is nothing and uncircumcision is nothing; what matters is keeping God's commands.
v20: Each person should remain in the calling in which they were called.
</p>

<p>
v21: Were you enslaved when called? Do not let it trouble you. But if you can become free, make use of the opportunity.
v22: For the one who was enslaved when called in the Lord is the Lord's freed person. Likewise, the one who was free when called is Christ's slave.
</p>

<p>
v23: You were bought at a price; do not become slaves of people.
v24: Brothers and sisters, each person should remain with God in the condition in which that person was called.
</p>

## Concerning the Unmarried

<p>
v25: Now concerning virgins, I have no command from the Lord, but I give my judgment as one shown mercy by the Lord to be trustworthy.
v26: I think, then, because of the present crisis, that it is good for a person to remain as they are.
</p>

<p>
v27: Are you bound to a wife? Do not seek release. Are you released from a wife? Do not seek a wife.
v28: But if you do marry, you have not sinned, and if a virgin marries, she has not sinned. Yet such people will have distress in this life, and I am trying to spare you.
</p>

<p>
v29: This is what I mean, brothers and sisters: the time has grown short. From now on, those who have wives should live as though they had none,
v30: those who weep as though not weeping, those who rejoice as though not rejoicing, and those who buy as though not possessing,
v31: and those who use the world as though not using it fully. For this world's present form is passing away.
</p>

<p>
v32: I want you to be free from anxiety. The unmarried man is concerned about the Lord's things, how to please the Lord,
v33: but the married man is concerned about worldly things, how to please his wife,
v34: and he is divided. The unmarried woman and the virgin are concerned about the Lord's things, so that they may be holy in body and spirit. But the married woman is concerned about worldly things, how to please her husband.
</p>

<p>
v35: I say this for your own benefit, not to put a restraint around you, but to promote good order and undistracted devotion to the Lord.
</p>

<p>
v36: But if anyone thinks he is behaving improperly toward his virgin, if she is past marriageable age and it ought to be so, he should do what he wants; he is not sinning. They should marry.
v37: But the man who stands firm in his heart, under no compulsion and having control over his own will, and has decided in his heart to keep his virgin unmarried, will do well.
</p>

<p>
v38: So the one who marries his virgin does well, and the one who does not marry will do better.
</p>

<p>
v39: A wife is bound as long as her husband lives. But if her husband falls asleep in death, she is free to marry anyone she wishes, only in the Lord.
v40: Yet in my judgment she is happier if she remains as she is—and I think that I too have God's Spirit.
</p>

## Notes

v01: ‘Not to touch a woman’ is a sexual euphemism and likely repeats a claim from the Corinthians' letter before Paul qualifies it.

v03–5: Marital bodily authority and sexual obligation are stated symmetrically; temporary abstinence requires mutual agreement and is time-limited.

v15: When an unbelieving spouse leaves, the believer is ‘not enslaved’; the passage's call to peace should not be used to require coercive control.

v21: The Greek can mean ‘make use of your present condition’ or ‘take the opportunity for freedom.’ The main text chooses freedom while preserving the slavery setting.

v25–40: Paul repeatedly marks this as situational counsel during a ‘present crisis,’ not a declaration that marriage is sinful. Verses 36–38 are grammatically disputed and may concern a fiancé or a father/guardian.

## Vocabulary

v05: **συμφωνία (symphōnia)**: Agreement or consent.

v15: **δουλόω (douloō)**: Enslave or bind as a slave.

v35: **ἀπερίσπαστος (aperispastos)**: Undistracted or without being pulled away.
