## Servants of Christ

<p>
v01: This is how people should regard us: as Christ's servants and stewards of God's mysteries.
v02: In this regard, what is required of stewards is that each be found faithful.
</p>

<p>
v03: But to me it is a very small matter to be judged by you or by any human court. I do not even judge myself.
v04: For I am conscious of nothing against myself, but I am not justified by this. The Lord is the one who judges me.
</p>

<p>
v05: Therefore, judge nothing before the appointed time, until the Lord comes. He will bring to light what is hidden in darkness and reveal the purposes of hearts. Then each person's praise will come from God.
</p>

## Apostolic Example

<p>
v06: Now, brothers and sisters, I have applied these things to myself and Apollos for your sake, so that through us you may learn “not beyond what is written,” and so none of you becomes puffed up for one leader against another.
</p>

<p>
v07: For who makes you different? What do you have that you did not receive? And if you received it, why boast as though you did not?
</p>

<p>
v08: Already you are filled! Already you are rich! You have begun to reign without us! I wish you truly were reigning, so that we might reign with you.
</p>

<p>
v09: For I think God has displayed us apostles last, like people sentenced to death, because we have become a spectacle to the world, to angels, and to humans.
</p>

<p>
v10: We are fools for Christ, but you are wise in Christ! We are weak, but you are strong! You are honored, but we are dishonored!
</p>

<p>
v11: To this very hour we are hungry and thirsty, poorly clothed, beaten, and homeless.
v12: We labor, working with our own hands. When insulted, we bless; when persecuted, we endure;
v13: when slandered, we answer gently. We have become like the world's garbage, everyone's scum, to this very moment.
</p>

## A Father’s Warning

<p>
v14: I am not writing these things to shame you but to admonish you as my beloved children.
v15: For even if you have ten thousand guardians in Christ, you do not have many fathers, for in Christ Jesus I became your father through the good news.
</p>

<p>
v16: Therefore, I urge you: imitate me.
v17: For this reason I sent Timothy to you, my beloved and faithful child in the Lord. He will remind you of my ways in Christ Jesus, just as I teach everywhere in every church.
</p>

<p>
v18: Some have become puffed up, as though I were not coming to you.
v19: But I will come to you soon, if the Lord wills, and I will find out not the talk of those who are puffed up but their power.
</p>

<p>
v20: For God's kingdom is not a matter of talk but of power.
v21: What do you want? Shall I come to you with a rod, or with love and a spirit of gentleness?
</p>

## Notes

v03–5: Paul rejects premature human verdicts without claiming his own conscience proves innocence; the Lord exposes hidden motives.

v08–13: The ‘reigning’ language is irony. The apostles' hunger, exposure, beatings, displacement, and public humiliation are translated as real harm.

v15: Paul's father language describes his role through the gospel; it is not biological paternity or unlimited authority.

v21: The rod is a real disciplinary image, contrasted with love and gentleness.

## Vocabulary

v01: **οἰκονόμος (oikonomos)**: Steward or household manager entrusted with another's goods.

v09: **θέατρον (theatron)**: Spectacle or public theater.

v21: **πραΰτης (prautēs)**: Gentleness or controlled strength.
