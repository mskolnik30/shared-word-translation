## Christ’s Once-for-All Offering
<p>
v01: Since the law has only a shadow of the good things to come, not their true form, it can never perfect those who approach through the same sacrifices continually offered year after year.
v02: Otherwise, would they not have stopped being offered, since worshipers cleansed once would no longer have any consciousness of sins?
v03: But in those sacrifices there is a reminder of sins every year.
v04: For it is impossible for the blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.
</p>

<p>
v05: Therefore, when Christ comes into the world, he says, “You did not desire sacrifice and offering, but you prepared a body for me.
v06: You took no pleasure in burnt offerings and offerings for sin.
v07: Then I said, ‘Look, I have come—it is written about me in the scroll—to do your will, O God.’”
v08: First he says, “You did not desire or take pleasure in sacrifices, offerings, burnt offerings and offerings for sin,” though these are offered according to the law.
v09: Then he says, “Look, I have come to do your will.” He sets aside the first in order to establish the second.
</p>

<p>
v10: By that will we have been made holy through the offering of Jesus Christ's body once for all.
v11: Every priest stands day after day performing service and offering the same sacrifices again and again, which can never remove sins.
v12: But after Christ offered one sacrifice for sins for all time, he sat down at God's right hand,
v13: waiting from then on until his enemies are made a footstool for his feet.
v14: For by one offering he has perfected forever those who are being made holy.
</p>

<p>
v15: The Holy Spirit also testifies to us, for after saying,
v16: “This is the covenant I will make with them after those days, says the Lord: I will put my laws on their hearts and write them on their minds,”
v17: he adds, “I will never again remember their sins and lawless acts.”
v18: Now where these are forgiven, there is no longer an offering for sin.
</p>

## Draw Near, Hold Fast, Spur One Another On
<p>
v19: Therefore, brothers and sisters, since we have confidence to enter the holy place by Jesus' blood,
v20: by the new and living way he opened for us through the curtain—that is, through his flesh—
v21: and since we have a great priest over God's house,
v22: let us approach with a true heart in full assurance of faith, our hearts sprinkled clean from an evil conscience and our bodies washed with pure water.
</p>

<p>
v23: Let us hold firmly to the confession of our hope without wavering, for the one who promised is faithful.
v24: And let us consider one another so as to provoke love and good works,
v25: not abandoning our gathering together, as some habitually do, but encouraging one another—and all the more as you see the Day approaching.
</p>

## A Sobering Warning and a Strong Encouragement
<p>
v26: For if we deliberately continue sinning after receiving knowledge of the truth, no sacrifice for sins remains,
v27: but only a terrifying expectation of judgment and a fury of fire that will consume God's enemies.
v28: Anyone who rejected Moses' law died without mercy on the testimony of two or three witnesses.
v29: How much worse punishment do you think will be deserved by someone who trampled God's Son, treated as common the covenant blood by which that person was made holy and insulted the Spirit of grace?
</p>

<p>
v30: For we know the one who said, “Vengeance belongs to me; I will repay,” and again, “The Lord will judge his people.”
v31: It is terrifying to fall into the hands of the living God.
</p>

<p>
v32: But remember the earlier days when, after being enlightened, you endured a great struggle with sufferings.
v33: At times you were publicly exposed to insults and afflictions; at other times you became partners with those treated that way.
v34: For you sympathized with prisoners and accepted the seizure of your possessions with joy, knowing that you yourselves had a better and lasting possession.
</p>

<p>
v35: So do not throw away your confidence, which carries a great reward.
v36: For you need endurance, so that after doing God's will you may receive the promise.
v37: For “in just a very little while, the coming one will come and will not delay;
v38: but my righteous one will live by faith, and if he draws back, my soul takes no pleasure in him.”
v39: But we do not belong to those who draw back and are destroyed, but to those who have faith and preserve their lives.
</p>
