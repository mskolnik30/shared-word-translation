## The Great Priest Appointed by God
<p>
v01: Every high priest chosen from among human beings is appointed on their behalf in matters relating to God, to offer gifts and sacrifices for sins.
v02: He is able to deal gently with the ignorant and wandering because he himself is clothed with weakness.
v03: Because of this weakness, he is obligated to offer sacrifices for his own sins as well as for the people's.
v04: No one takes this honor for himself but receives it when called by God, just as Aaron was.
</p>

<p>
v05: In the same way, Christ did not glorify himself by becoming high priest. Instead, God said to him, “You are my Son; today I have fathered you.”
v06: As he also says elsewhere, “You are a priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.”
</p>

## The Son Learns Obedience Through Suffering
<p>
v07: During the days of his flesh, he offered prayers and pleas, with loud cries and tears, to the one able to save him from death, and he was heard because of his reverent submission.
v08: Although he was a Son, he learned obedience through what he suffered.
v09: And having been perfected, he became the source of eternal salvation for all who obey him,
v10: having been designated by God a high priest according to the order of Melchizedek.
</p>

## Press On to Maturity
<p>
v11: We have much to say about this, but it is difficult to explain because you have become sluggish in hearing.
v12: Although by this time you ought to be teachers, you again need someone to teach you the basic elements of God's sayings. You have come to need milk, not solid food.
v13: Everyone who lives on milk is inexperienced in the word of righteousness, since such a person is an infant.
v14: But solid food is for the mature, whose senses have been trained by practice to distinguish good from evil.
</p>
