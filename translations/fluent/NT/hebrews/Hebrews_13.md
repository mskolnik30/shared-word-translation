## Sacrifices Pleasing to God

<p>
v01: Let family love continue.
v02: Do not neglect hospitality, for through it some have welcomed angels without knowing it.
v03: Remember those in prison as though imprisoned with them, and those being mistreated as people who are yourselves in a body.
v04: Let marriage be honored by everyone and the marriage bed kept undefiled, for God will judge the sexually immoral and adulterers.
v05: Keep your life free from love of money, and be content with what you have, for he has said, “I will never leave you and never abandon you.”
v06: So we may say with confidence, “The Lord is my helper; I will not be afraid. What can a human being do to me?”
v07: Remember your leaders who spoke God's word to you. Consider the outcome of their way of life and imitate their faith.
v08: Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today and forever.
v09: Do not be carried away by various strange teachings. It is good for the heart to be strengthened by grace, not by foods that have not benefited those who follow such practices.
v10: We have an altar from which those who serve at the tent have no right to eat.
v11: The bodies of the animals whose blood is brought into the holy place by the high priest as an offering for sin are burned outside the camp.
v12: Therefore Jesus also suffered outside the city gate in order to make the people holy through his own blood.
v13: So let us go out to him outside the camp, bearing his disgrace.
v14: For here we have no lasting city, but we seek the one to come.
v15: Through Jesus, then, let us continually offer God a sacrifice of praise—the fruit of lips that confess his name.
v16: Do not neglect doing good and sharing, for God is pleased with such sacrifices.
</p>

## Obey Your Leaders

<p>
v17: Obey your leaders and yield to them, for they keep watch over your lives as those who will give an account. Let them do this with joy and not groaning, for that would be unprofitable for you.
v18: Pray for us, for we are convinced that we have a good conscience and desire to conduct ourselves honorably in everything.
v19: I urge you all the more to do this, so that I may be restored to you soon.
v20: Now may the God of peace, who brought back from the dead our Lord Jesus, the great shepherd of the sheep, by the blood of the eternal covenant,
v21: equip you with every good thing to do his will, working in us what is pleasing before him through Jesus Christ, to whom be glory forever. Amen.
v22: I urge you, brothers and sisters, to bear with this word of encouragement, for I have written to you briefly.
v23: You should know that our brother Timothy has been released. If he comes soon, I will see you with him.
v24: Greet all your leaders and all the saints. Those from Italy greet you.
v25: Grace be with all of you.
</p>
