## God’s Rest and Our Trust
<p>
v01: Therefore, since the promise of entering his rest remains, let us fear that any one of you may appear to have fallen short.
v02: For we too have received good news just as they did, but the message they heard did not benefit them because they were not united by faith with those who listened.
v03: For we who have believed enter the rest, just as he said, “So I swore in my anger, ‘They will never enter my rest,’” though his works were finished from the foundation of the world.
v04: For somewhere he has spoken about the seventh day this way: “And God rested on the seventh day from all his works.”
v05: And again in this passage, “They will never enter my rest.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Therefore, since it remains for some to enter it, and those who previously received the good news did not enter because of disobedience,
v07: God again appoints a certain day—“Today”—speaking through David after so long a time, as quoted above: “Today, if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts.”
v08: For if Joshua had given them rest, God would not have spoken later about another day.
v09: So a Sabbath rest remains for God's people.
v10: For whoever enters God's rest also rests from their works, just as God did from his.
v11: Therefore, let us make every effort to enter that rest, so that no one falls by following the same pattern of disobedience.
</p>

## God’s Word Exposes and Heals
<p>
v12: For God's word is living and active, sharper than any double-edged sword, penetrating to the division of soul and spirit, joints and marrow, and judging the thoughts and intentions of the heart.
v13: No creature is hidden before him; everything is naked and exposed to the eyes of the one to whom we must give account.
</p>

## Hold Fast to Our Great Priest
<p>
v14: Therefore, since we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens—Jesus, God's Son—let us hold firmly to our confession.
v15: For we do not have a high priest unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but one who has been tested in every way as we are, yet without sin.
v16: So let us approach the throne of grace with confidence, so that we may receive mercy and find grace for timely help.
</p>
