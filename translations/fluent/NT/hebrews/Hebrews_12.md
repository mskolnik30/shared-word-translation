## Jesus, Founder and Perfecter of Faith

<p>
v01: Therefore, since we too are surrounded by such a great cloud of witnesses, let us lay aside every weight and the sin that so easily entangles us, and run with endurance the race set before us,
v02: fixing our eyes on Jesus, the pioneer and perfecter of faith. For the joy set before him, he endured a cross, despising its shame, and sat down at the right hand of God's throne.
v03: Consider the one who endured such hostility from sinners against himself, so that you do not grow weary and lose heart.
v04: In struggling against sin, you have not yet resisted to the point of shedding blood.
</p>

## The Discipline of the Lord

<p>
v05: And you have forgotten the encouragement that addresses you as sons: “My son, do not make light of the Lord's discipline or give up when he corrects you,
v06: for the Lord disciplines the one he loves and flogs every son he receives.”
v07: Endure for discipline. God is treating you as sons, for what son is not disciplined by his father?
v08: But if you are without discipline, in which all sons share, then you are illegitimate children and not sons.
v09: Furthermore, we had human fathers who disciplined us, and we respected them. Should we not much more submit to the Father of spirits and live?
v10: They disciplined us for a short time as seemed best to them, but God disciplines us for our benefit, so that we may share his holiness.
v11: No discipline seems joyful at the time, but painful. Later, however, it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness to those trained by it.
</p>

## Strive for Peace and Holiness

<p>
v12: Therefore, strengthen drooping hands and weakened knees,
v13: and make straight paths for your feet, so that what is lame may not be dislocated but healed instead.
v14: Pursue peace with everyone, and holiness, without which no one will see the Lord.
v15: Watch carefully that no one falls short of God's grace and that no root of bitterness grows up to cause trouble and defile many.
v16: See that no one is sexually immoral or profane like Esau, who sold his own birthright for one meal.
v17: For you know that afterward, when he wanted to inherit the blessing, he was rejected. He found no opportunity for a change of mind, though he sought the blessing with tears.
</p>

## A Kingdom That Cannot Be Shaken

<p>
v18: For you have not come to something that can be touched, to blazing fire, darkness, gloom and storm,
v19: to a trumpet blast and a voice whose words made the hearers beg that no further message be spoken to them.
v20: For they could not bear the command, “Even if an animal touches the mountain, it must be stoned.”
v21: The sight was so terrifying that Moses said, “I am terrified and trembling.”
v22: But you have come to Mount Zion, the city of the living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, and tens of thousands of angels in joyful assembly,
v23: to the assembly of the firstborn enrolled in heaven, to God the judge of all and to the spirits of righteous people made perfect,
v24: to Jesus, mediator of a new covenant, and to sprinkled blood that speaks better than Abel's blood.
v25: See that you do not refuse the one who speaks. If they did not escape when they refused the one who warned them on earth, how much less will we if we turn away from the one who warns from heaven?
v26: At that time his voice shook the earth, but now he has promised, “Once more I will shake not only the earth but also heaven.”
v27: The words “once more” indicate the removal of what can be shaken—that is, created things—so that what cannot be shaken may remain.
v28: Therefore, since we are receiving an unshakable kingdom, let us be grateful and in this way worship God acceptably with reverence and awe,
v29: for our God is a consuming fire.
</p>
