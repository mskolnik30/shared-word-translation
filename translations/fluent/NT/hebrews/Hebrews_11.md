## The Witness of Faith

<p>
v01: Now faith is the assurance of what is hoped for, the conviction of things not seen.
v02: For by faith the elders received approval.
v03: By faith we understand that the ages were formed by God's word, so that what is seen did not come from visible things.
</p>

## Abel, Enoch, Noah

<p>
v04: By faith Abel offered God a better sacrifice than Cain did. Through it he was approved as righteous, God testifying about his gifts; and through faith, though dead, he still speaks.
v05: By faith Enoch was taken away so that he did not see death, and he could not be found because God had taken him. Before he was taken, he was approved as pleasing to God.
v06: Without faith it is impossible to please God, for anyone who approaches him must believe that he exists and that he rewards those who seek him.
</p>

<p>
v07: By faith Noah, warned about things not yet seen, responded with reverence and built an ark to save his household. By this he condemned the world and became an heir of the righteousness that comes through faith.
</p>

## Abraham and Sarah

<p>
v08: By faith Abraham obeyed when called to leave for a place he was to receive as an inheritance. He left without knowing where he was going.
v09: By faith he lived as a foreigner in the promised land as in a land belonging to others, living in tents with Isaac and Jacob, fellow heirs of the same promise.
v10: For he was waiting for the city with foundations, whose designer and builder is God.
</p>

<p>
v11: By faith Sarah herself received power to conceive, even past the proper age, because she considered the one who promised faithful.
v12: Therefore, from one man—and one already as good as dead—came descendants as numerous as the stars in heaven and as countless as the sand along the seashore.
</p>

## Strangers and Exiles

<p>
v13: All these died in faith without receiving the promises. They saw and welcomed them from far away and confessed that they were strangers and temporary residents on the earth.
v14: People who speak this way make it clear that they are seeking a homeland.
v15: If they had been remembering the land they left, they would have had opportunity to return.
v16: But now they desire a better land—a heavenly one. Therefore, God is not ashamed to be called their God, for he has prepared a city for them.
</p>

## The Binding of Isaac

<p>
v17: By faith Abraham, when tested, offered Isaac. The one who had received the promises was offering his one and only son,
v18: about whom it had been said, “Through Isaac your offspring will be named.”
v19: Abraham reasoned that God was able even to raise someone from the dead, and in a figurative sense he did receive Isaac back.
</p>

## The Patriarchs and Joseph

<p>
v20: By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau regarding things to come.
v21: By faith Jacob, as he was dying, blessed each of Joseph's sons and worshiped while leaning on the top of his staff.
v22: By faith Joseph, near the end of his life, mentioned the exodus of Israel's children and gave instructions about his bones.
</p>

## Moses

<p>
v23: By faith Moses, after he was born, was hidden for three months by his parents because they saw that the child was beautiful, and they did not fear the king's decree.
v24: By faith Moses, when he had grown up, refused to be called a son of Pharaoh's daughter.
v25: He chose to suffer mistreatment with God's people rather than enjoy sin's temporary pleasure.
v26: He considered disgrace for the Christ greater wealth than Egypt's treasures, because he was looking toward the reward.
</p>

<p>
v27: By faith he left Egypt, not fearing the king's anger, for he persevered as though seeing the invisible one.
v28: By faith he kept the Passover and the sprinkling of blood, so that the destroyer of the firstborn would not touch them.
v29: By faith they crossed the Red Sea as on dry land; when the Egyptians attempted it, they were swallowed up.
</p>

## Conquest and Beyond

<p>
v30: By faith Jericho's walls fell after being encircled for seven days.
v31: By faith Rahab the prostitute did not perish with those who disobeyed, because she welcomed the spies in peace.
</p>

<p>
v32: And what more should I say? Time would fail me if I told about Gideon, Barak, Samson, Jephthah, David, Samuel and the prophets.
v33: Through faith they conquered kingdoms, practiced justice, obtained promises and shut lions' mouths.
v34: They quenched raging fire, escaped the edge of the sword, gained strength from weakness, became mighty in war and put foreign armies to flight.
v35: Women received their dead back by resurrection. Others were tortured, refusing release so that they might obtain a better resurrection.
v36: Others experienced mockery and flogging, and even chains and imprisonment.
v37: They were stoned, sawn in two and killed by the sword. They went about in sheepskins and goatskins, destitute, afflicted and mistreated—
v38: the world was not worthy of them—wandering in deserts, mountains, caves and holes in the ground.
</p>

## The Promise Withheld for Now

<p>
v39: All these were approved through their faith, yet they did not receive the promise,
v40: because God had provided something better for us, so that they would not be perfected without us.
</p>
