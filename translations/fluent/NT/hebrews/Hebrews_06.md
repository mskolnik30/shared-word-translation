## Warning Against Falling Away

<p>
v01: Therefore, let us move beyond the elementary message about Christ and be carried on toward maturity, not laying again a foundation of repentance from dead works and faith in God,
v02: instruction about washings, laying on of hands, resurrection of the dead and eternal judgment.
v03: And we will do this, if God permits.
v04: For it is impossible to restore again to repentance those who were once enlightened, tasted the heavenly gift, became partners in the Holy Spirit,
v05: tasted God's good word and the powers of the coming age,
v06: and then fell away, since they crucify God's Son again for themselves and expose him to public shame.
v07: For land that drinks the rain falling on it again and again and produces a useful crop for those who cultivate it receives blessing from God.
v08: But land that produces thorns and thistles is worthless and near a curse; its end is burning.
</p>

## God’s Promises Are Certain

<p>
v09: But even though we speak this way, beloved, we are convinced of better things in your case—things belonging to salvation.
v10: For God is not unjust; he will not forget your work and the love you showed for his name by serving the saints, as you still do.
v11: We desire each of you to show the same diligence until the end, so as to have full assurance of hope,
v12: so that you do not become sluggish but imitate those who inherit the promises through faith and patience.
v13: When God made his promise to Abraham, since he had no one greater by whom to swear, he swore by himself,
v14: saying, “Surely I will bless you greatly and multiply you greatly.”
v15: And so, after patiently waiting, Abraham obtained the promise.
v16: For people swear by someone greater, and the oath confirms the matter and ends every dispute for them.
v17: In the same way, because God wanted to show the heirs of the promise more clearly the unchangeable nature of his purpose, he guaranteed it with an oath,
v18: so that through two unchangeable things in which it is impossible for God to lie, we who have fled for refuge might have strong encouragement to seize the hope set before us.
v19: We have this hope as a secure and firm anchor for the soul, entering the inner place behind the curtain,
v20: where Jesus entered ahead of us on our behalf, having become high priest forever according to the order of Melchizedek.
</p>
