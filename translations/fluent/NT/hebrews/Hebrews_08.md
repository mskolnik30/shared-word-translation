## The High Priest of a Better Covenant

<p>
v01: The main point of what we are saying is this: we have such a high priest, who sat down at the right hand of the Majesty's throne in heaven.
v02: He is a minister in the holy place and in the true tent, which the Lord set up, not a human being.
v03: Every high priest is appointed to offer gifts and sacrifices, so this one too must have something to offer.
v04: If he were on earth, he would not be a priest at all, since there are already priests who offer gifts according to the law.
v05: They serve a copy and shadow of the heavenly things, just as Moses was warned when he was about to complete the tent: “See that you make everything according to the pattern shown to you on the mountain.”
v06: But now Jesus has obtained a more excellent ministry, just as he is mediator of a better covenant enacted on better promises.
v07: For if that first covenant had been faultless, no place would have been sought for a second.
v08: But finding fault with them, God says, “Look, days are coming, says the Lord, when I will establish a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah.
v09: It will not be like the covenant I made with their ancestors on the day I took them by the hand to lead them out of Egypt, because they did not remain in my covenant, and I disregarded them, says the Lord.
v10: For this is the covenant I will make with the house of Israel after those days, says the Lord: I will put my laws into their minds and write them on their hearts. I will be their God, and they will be my people.
v11: No one will teach a fellow citizen or a brother or sister, saying, ‘Know the Lord,’ because they will all know me, from the least to the greatest.
v12: For I will be merciful toward their wrongdoings and will remember their sins no more.”
v13: By speaking of a “new” covenant, he has made the first obsolete; and what is becoming obsolete and growing old is near disappearance.
</p>
