## Test the Spirits

<p>
v01: Beloved, do not believe every spirit. Test the spirits to see whether they are of God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world.
v02: This is how you recognize the Spirit of God: every spirit that confesses that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh is of God,
v03: and every spirit that does not confess Jesus is not of God. This is the spirit of the antichrist, which you heard was coming and is already in the world.
v04: You are of God, little children, and you have overcome them, because the one in you is greater than the one in the world.
v05: They are of the world. That is why they speak from the world, and the world listens to them.
v06: We are of God. Whoever knows God listens to us; whoever is not of God does not listen to us. This is how we recognize the Spirit of truth and the spirit of error.
</p>

## God Is Love

<p>
v07: Beloved, let us love one another, because love is from God. Everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.
v08: Whoever does not love has not known God, because God is love.
v09: This is how God's love was revealed among us: God sent his only Son into the world so that we might live through him.
v10: Love is in this: not that we loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son as the atoning sacrifice for our sins.
v11: Beloved, if God loved us in this way, we too ought to love one another.
v12: No one has ever seen God. If we love one another, God remains in us, and his love reaches its fullness in us.
</p>

## God Remains in Us

<p>
v13: This is how we know that we remain in him and he in us: he has given us of his Spirit.
v14: We have seen and bear witness that the Father sent the Son as Savior of the world.
v15: Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God remains in that person, and that person in God.
v16: We have come to know and believe the love God has for us. God is love, and whoever remains in love remains in God, and God remains in them.
</p>

## Love and Confidence

<p>
v17: This is how love reaches its fullness with us, so that we may have confidence on the day of judgment: as he is, so are we in this world.
v18: There is no fear in love. Complete love drives out fear, because fear involves punishment. Whoever fears has not reached fullness in love.
</p>

## Loving God and One Another

<p>
v19: We love because he first loved us.
v20: If someone says, “I love God,” but hates a brother or sister, that person is a liar. For whoever does not love a brother or sister they have seen cannot love God, whom they have not seen.
v21: This is the command we have from him: whoever loves God must also love a brother or sister.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The pinned text reads simply “does not confess Jesus”; some witnesses repeat or expand the embodied confession of verse 2.

v19: The pinned text says “we love,” without an expressed object. Other witnesses add “him” or “God.”

## Vocabulary

v09: **μονογενής (monogenēs)**: Only or unique, here describing the Son.

v10: **ἱλασμός (hilasmos)**: Atoning sacrifice; the same term appears in 2:2.

v18: **κόλασις (kolasis)**: Punishment, the stated connection to fear in this passage.
