## Christ Our Advocate

<p>
v01: My little children, I write these things to you so that you will not sin. But if anyone sins, we have an advocate with the Father: Jesus Christ, the righteous one.
v02: He himself is the atoning sacrifice for our sins, and not only for ours but also for the whole world's.
</p>

## Knowing God and Keeping His Commands

<p>
v03: This is how we know that we have come to know him: we keep his commands.
v04: Anyone who says, “I know him,” but does not keep his commands is a liar, and the truth is not in that person.
v05: But in anyone who keeps his word, the love of God has truly reached its fullness. This is how we know that we are in him:
v06: whoever says they remain in him must also walk as he walked.
</p>

## An Old and New Commandment

<p>
v07: Beloved, I am not writing you a new command, but an old command you have had from the beginning. The old command is the word you heard.
v08: Yet I am writing you a new command, which is true in him and in you, because the darkness is passing away and the true light already shines.
v09: Anyone who says they are in the light but hates a brother or sister is still in darkness.
v10: Whoever loves a brother or sister remains in the light, and there is no cause for stumbling in that person.
v11: But whoever hates a brother or sister is in darkness and walks in darkness, not knowing where they are going because the darkness has blinded their eyes.
</p>

## What Remains and What Passes Away

<p>
v12: I write to you, little children,
because your sins have been forgiven on account of his name.
v13: I write to you, fathers,
because you have known the one who is from the beginning.
I write to you, young men,
because you have overcome the evil one.
v14: I have written to you, children,
because you have known the Father.
I have written to you, fathers,
because you have known the one who is from the beginning.
I have written to you, young men,
because you are strong, God's word remains in you,
and you have overcome the evil one.
</p>

<p>
v15: Do not love the world or the things in the world. If anyone loves the world, the love of the Father is not in that person.
v16: For everything in the world—the desire of the flesh, the desire of the eyes and pride in one's possessions—is from the world, not from the Father.
v17: The world and its desire are passing away, but whoever does God's will remains forever.
</p>

## Antichrists and the Anointing

<p>
v18: Children, it is the last hour. You heard that antichrist is coming, and now many antichrists have appeared. That is how we know it is the last hour.
v19: They went out from us, but they did not belong to us. If they had belonged to us, they would have remained with us. Their departure made clear that none of them belonged to us.
v20: But you have an anointing from the Holy One, and all of you know.
v21: I have written to you because you know the truth, not because you do not know it, and because no lie comes from the truth.
v22: Who is the liar but the one who denies that Jesus is the Christ? This is the antichrist: the one who denies the Father and the Son.
v23: Everyone who denies the Son is without the Father as well. Whoever acknowledges the Son has the Father too.
v24: As for you, let what you heard from the beginning remain in you. If it remains in you, you too will remain in the Son and in the Father.
v25: And this is the promise he himself made to us: eternal life.
v26: I have written these things about those who are trying to lead you astray.
v27: As for you, the anointing you received from him remains in you, and you do not need anyone to teach you. His anointing teaches you about all things; it is true and is no lie. So remain in him, just as it taught you.
</p>

## Children of God

<p>
v28: And now, little children, remain in him, so that when he is revealed we may have confidence and not shrink from him in shame at his coming.
v29: If you know that he is righteous, you know that everyone who practices righteousness has been born of him.
</p>

## Notes

v05,15: “Love of God” and “love of the Father” can refer to love from God or love for God.

v12–14: The source distinguishes “I write” and “I have written.” Its verse 14 begins with the address to children; some English numbering places that address at the end of verse 13.

v19: The final Greek clause uses not and all together; “none of them belonged to us” takes the negation as applying to each of those who left.

v20: The pinned reading is “all of you know”; another reading is “you know all things.”

## Vocabulary

v01: **παράκλητος (paraklētos)**: Advocate, helper or representative.

v02: **ἱλασμός (hilasmos)**: An atoning means or sacrifice; the term has been rendered expiation or propitiation.

v16: **βίος (bios)**: Life, livelihood or possessions; here the boast concerns the resources of worldly life.

v27: **χρῖσμα (chrisma)**: Anointing. The image is retained without replacing it with an explanatory noun.
