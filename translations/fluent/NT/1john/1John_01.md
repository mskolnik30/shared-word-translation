## The Word of Life

<p>
v01: What was from the beginning, what we have heard, what we have seen with our own eyes, what we looked upon and our hands touched, concerning the word of life—
v02: the life was revealed, and we have seen it and bear witness and announce to you the eternal life that was with the Father and was revealed to us—
v03: what we have seen and heard we announce to you too, so that you may share in fellowship with us. And our fellowship is with the Father and with his Son, Jesus Christ.
v04: We write these things so that our joy may be complete.
</p>

## Walking in the Light

<p>
v05: This is the message we have heard from him and announce to you: God is light, and there is no darkness in him at all.
v06: If we say we have fellowship with him while walking in darkness, we lie and do not practice the truth.
v07: But if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus his Son cleanses us from every sin.
v08: If we say we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.
v09: If we confess our sins, he is faithful and righteous to forgive our sins and cleanse us from all unrighteousness.
v10: If we say we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us.
</p>

## Notes

v01–03: The opening accumulates what was heard, seen and touched before returning to the act of announcement in verse 3. “Word of life” is not expanded into an explicit identification.

v04: The pinned text reads “our joy”; other witnesses read “your joy.”

## Vocabulary

v03: **κοινωνία (koinōnia)**: Fellowship, shared participation or communion.

v09: **δίκαιος (dikaios)**: Righteous or just; the same word family recurs throughout the letter.
