## Children of God

<p>
v01: See what love the Father has given us, that we should be called God's children—and that is what we are! The world does not know us because it did not know him.
v02: Beloved, we are God's children now, and what we will be has not yet been revealed. We know that when he is revealed, we will be like him, because we will see him as he is.
v03: Everyone who has this hope in him purifies themselves, just as he is pure.
</p>

## Sin and Righteousness

<p>
v04: Everyone who commits sin also commits lawlessness. Sin is lawlessness.
v05: You know that he was revealed to take away sins, and there is no sin in him.
v06: No one who remains in him sins. No one who sins has seen him or known him.
</p>

<p>
v07: Little children, do not let anyone lead you astray. Whoever practices righteousness is righteous, just as he is righteous.
v08: Whoever commits sin is of the devil, because the devil has been sinning from the beginning. The Son of God was revealed for this purpose: to destroy the devil's works.
v09: No one born of God commits sin, because God's seed remains in them. They cannot sin, because they have been born of God.
v10: This is how God's children and the devil's children are made clear: anyone who does not practice righteousness is not of God, nor is anyone who does not love a brother or sister.
</p>

## Love One Another

<p>
v11: This is the message you have heard from the beginning: we should love one another.
v12: We must not be like Cain, who was of the evil one and slaughtered his brother. And why did he slaughter him? Because his own deeds were evil and his brother's were righteous.
v13: Do not be surprised, brothers and sisters, if the world hates you.
v14: We know that we have passed from death to life because we love our brothers and sisters. Anyone who does not love remains in death.
v15: Everyone who hates a brother or sister is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life remaining in them.
</p>

## Love in Deed and Truth

<p>
v16: This is how we have come to know love: he laid down his life for us. We too ought to lay down our lives for our brothers and sisters.
v17: If someone has this world's goods, sees a brother or sister in need and shuts off compassion for them, how can the love of God remain in that person?
v18: Little children, let us love in deed and truth, not in word or speech alone.
</p>

## Confidence Before God

<p>
v19: This is how we will know that we are of the truth and reassure our hearts before him:
v20: whenever our hearts condemn us, God is greater than our hearts and knows everything.
v21: Beloved, if our hearts do not condemn us, we have confidence before God,
v22: and we receive from him whatever we ask, because we keep his commands and do what pleases him.
</p>

<p>
v23: And this is his command: that we believe in the name of his Son, Jesus Christ, and love one another, just as he commanded us.
v24: Whoever keeps his commands remains in him, and he in them. This is how we know that he remains in us: by the Spirit he has given us.
</p>

## Notes

v02: “When he is revealed” can also be understood as “when it is revealed,” referring to what the children of God will be.

v06,09: The Greek statements about not sinning are categorical. The present-tense forms have also been understood as habitual practice; this is not added to the text. Compare 1:8–2:2.

v09: “Seed” is not defined here.

v19–20: The syntax can connect the assurance to God being greater than the condemning heart; the relation of divine knowledge to assurance and judgment remains compressed.

## Vocabulary

v12: **σφάζω (sphazō)**: Slaughter or kill violently.

v17: **σπλάγχνα (splanchna)**: The inward parts, used for compassion.
