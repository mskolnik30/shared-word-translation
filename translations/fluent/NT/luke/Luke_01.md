## Preface

<p>
v01: Since many have undertaken to put together an account of the events fulfilled among us,
v02: drawing on what was handed down to us by those who were eyewitnesses from the beginning and became servants of the word.
v03: I too decided, after carefully investigating everything from the beginning, to write an orderly account for you, most excellent Theophilus,
v04: so that you may know how reliable the things you have been taught are.
</p>

## The Birth of John Foretold

<p>
v05: In the days when Herod was king of Judea, there was a priest named Zechariah from the division of Abijah. His wife, Elizabeth, was also descended from Aaron.
v06: Both were righteous before God, living blamelessly according to all the Lord's commandments and requirements.
v07: But they had no child, because Elizabeth was unable to conceive, and both were advanced in years.
v08: Once, when Zechariah's division was on duty and he was serving as priest before God,
v09: he was chosen by lot, according to priestly custom, to enter the Lord's sanctuary and burn incense.
v10: At the hour of incense, the whole gathering of people was outside praying.
</p>

<p>
v11: An angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing to the right of the incense altar.
v12: Zechariah was shaken at the sight, and fear came over him.
v13: The angel said, “Do not be afraid, Zechariah. Your prayer has been heard. Your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you are to name him John.
v14: He will bring you joy and gladness, and many will rejoice at his birth,
v15: for he will be great before the Lord. He must never drink wine or strong drink, and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit even from his mother's womb.
v16: He will turn many of the people of Israel back to the Lord their God.
v17: He will go before the Lord in Elijah's spirit and power, turning fathers' hearts toward their children and the disobedient toward the wisdom of the righteous, to make a people ready for the Lord.”
</p>

<p>
v18: Zechariah asked the angel, “How can I be sure of this? I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in years.”
v19: The angel answered, “I am Gabriel. I stand in God's presence, and I was sent to speak to you and bring you this good news.
v20: But now you will be silent, unable to speak until the day these things happen, because you did not believe my words. They will be fulfilled at their appointed time.”
v21: Meanwhile, the people were waiting for Zechariah and wondering why he stayed so long in the sanctuary.
v22: When he came out, he could not speak to them. They realized that he had seen a vision in the sanctuary. He kept making signs to them but remained unable to speak.
v23: When his period of service ended, he went home.
</p>

<p>
v24: Afterward his wife Elizabeth conceived. She kept herself out of sight for five months, saying,
v25: “This is what the Lord has done for me. He has looked on me with favor and taken away my disgrace among people.”
</p>

## The Birth of Jesus Foretold

<p>
v26: In the sixth month, God sent the angel Gabriel to Nazareth, a town in Galilee,
v27: to a virgin pledged in marriage to a man named Joseph, of David's house. The virgin's name was Mary.
v28: The angel came to her and said, “Greetings, favored one! The Lord is with you.”
v29: She was deeply troubled by his words and wondered what kind of greeting this could be.
v30: The angel told her, “Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.
v31: You will conceive and bear a son, and you are to name him Jesus.
v32: He will be great and will be called Son of the Most High. The Lord God will give him the throne of his ancestor David,
v33: and he will reign over the house of Jacob forever. His kingdom will have no end.”
</p>

<p>
v34: Mary asked the angel, “How will this happen, since I have not been with a man?”
v35: The angel answered, “The Holy Spirit will come upon you, and the power of the Most High will overshadow you. Therefore the child to be born will be called holy, the Son of God.
v36: And your relative Elizabeth has conceived a son in her old age. This is the sixth month for her, the one people called unable to conceive.
v37: For no word from God will fail.”
v38: Mary said, “Here I am, the Lord's slave. Let it happen to me according to your word.” Then the angel left her.
</p>

## Mary Visits Elizabeth

<p>
v39: Soon afterward Mary set out and hurried to a town in the hill country of Judah.
v40: She entered Zechariah's house and greeted Elizabeth.
v41: When Elizabeth heard Mary's greeting, the baby leaped in her womb, and Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit.
v42: She cried out loudly, “Blessed are you among women, and blessed is the fruit of your womb!
v43: How is it that the mother of my Lord should come to me?
v44: The moment your greeting reached my ears, the baby in my womb leaped for joy.
v45: Blessed is she who believed that what the Lord had spoken to her would be fulfilled.”
</p>

## Mary’s Song

<p>
v46: Mary said:
“My soul magnifies the Lord,
v47: and my spirit rejoices in God my Savior,
v48: for he has looked on the lowly condition of his slave.
From now on, every generation will call me blessed,
v49: for the Mighty One has done great things for me.
Holy is his name,
v50: and his mercy reaches generation after generation
for those who fear him.
v51: He has shown strength with his arm
and scattered those proud in the thoughts of their hearts.
v52: He has brought rulers down from their thrones
and raised up the lowly.
v53: He has filled the hungry with good things
and sent the rich away empty.
v54: He has come to the aid of Israel his servant,
remembering his mercy,
v55: just as he promised our ancestors,
mercy to Abraham and his descendants forever.”
</p>

<p>
v56: Mary stayed with Elizabeth about three months and then returned home.
</p>

## The Birth of John

<p>
v57: When Elizabeth's time came to give birth, she bore a son.
v58: Her neighbors and relatives heard how greatly the Lord had shown her mercy, and they rejoiced with her.
v59: On the eighth day they came to circumcise the child, and they were going to name him Zechariah after his father.
v60: But his mother spoke up: “No! He is to be named John.”
v61: They told her, “No one in your family has that name.”
v62: They made signs to his father, asking what he wanted the child to be called.
v63: He asked for a writing tablet and wrote, “His name is John.” Everyone was amazed.
v64: Immediately his mouth opened and his tongue was freed. He began to speak, praising God.
v65: Fear came over all their neighbors, and people throughout the hill country of Judea talked about everything that had happened.
v66: All who heard took it to heart and asked, “What, then, will this child become?” For the Lord's hand was with him.
</p>

## Zechariah’s Prophecy

<p>
v67: His father Zechariah was filled with the Holy Spirit and prophesied:
v68: “Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel,
for he has visited his people and brought them redemption.
v69: He has raised up a horn of salvation for us
in the house of his servant David,
v70: as he said through the mouths of his holy prophets long ago:
v71: salvation from our enemies
and from the hand of all who hate us,
v72: to show mercy to our ancestors
and remember his holy covenant,
v73: the oath he swore to Abraham our father,
to grant us
v74: rescue from the hand of our enemies,
so that we might serve him without fear,
v75: in holiness and righteousness before him
all our days.
</p>

<p>
v76: And you, child, will be called prophet of the Most High,
for you will go before the Lord to prepare his ways,
v77: to give his people knowledge of salvation
through the forgiveness of their sins,
v78: because of the tender mercy of our God,
by which the dawn from on high will visit us,
v79: to shine on those sitting in darkness and death's shadow,
to guide our feet into the way of peace.”
</p>

<p>
v80: The child grew and became strong in spirit. He lived in the wilderness until the day he appeared publicly to Israel.
</p>

## Notes

v01–04: The opening links received eyewitness testimony, the writer's careful inquiry, an orderly account, and reliable instruction. ‘Orderly’ does not by itself specify a strictly chronological arrangement.

v17: ‘Fathers' hearts’ retains the family language of the Greek. The saying links turning within families with the disobedient turning toward righteous wisdom.

v25: Elizabeth speaks of disgrace ‘among people.’ The narrator has already called both her and Zechariah righteous in verse 6.

v28: ‘Favored one’ describes someone who has received favor or grace. The text followed here does not add ‘blessed are you among women’ to this greeting; that blessing occurs in verse 42.

v35: The Greek can also be construed ‘the holy child to be born will be called Son of God.’ ‘Overshadow’ retains the source's image without explaining a mechanism.

v37: ‘No word from God will fail’ can also be rendered more broadly, ‘Nothing will be impossible with God.’ The word translated ‘word’ can also refer to a matter or event.

v38, 48: The Greek term is the feminine form of ‘slave.’ Mary uses it of herself; her spoken assent in verse 38 is also retained.

v45: The clause can be read as the content of belief, as here, or as its ground: ‘blessed is she who believed, because there will be a fulfillment.’

v69: A horn is an image of strength; ‘horn of salvation’ preserves the song's ancient image of saving power.

v78: The Greek word can mean rising or dawn. The following light imagery supports ‘dawn’ here; the verb in the text followed is future, ‘will visit.’

## Vocabulary

v04: **ἀσφάλεια (asphaleia)**: Reliability, security, or certainty.

v30: **χάρις (charis)**: Favor or grace.

v48: **ταπείνωσις (tapeinōsis)**: Lowly condition or humiliation; a person's circumstances, not necessarily a claim to humility as a virtue.

v68: **λύτρωσις (lytrōsis)**: Redemption or release.

v72: **διαθήκη (diathēkē)**: Covenant.

v77: **ἄφεσις (aphesis)**: Release or forgiveness; here release from sins.
