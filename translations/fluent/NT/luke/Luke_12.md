## Beware of Hypocrisy

<p>
v01: Meanwhile, a crowd of many thousands had gathered, so many that they were trampling one another. Jesus began speaking first to his disciples: “Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.
v02: Nothing is covered that will not be revealed, and nothing hidden that will not become known.
v03: Therefore whatever you have said in darkness will be heard in the light, and what you whispered in someone’s ear in private rooms will be proclaimed from the housetops.
v04: “I tell you, my friends, do not fear those who kill the body and after that can do nothing more.
v05: I will show you whom to fear: fear the one who, after killing, has authority to throw into Gehenna. Yes, I tell you, fear him.
v06: Are not five sparrows sold for two small coins? Yet not one of them is forgotten before God.
v07: Even the hairs of your head have all been counted. Do not be afraid. You are worth more than many sparrows.
v08: I tell you, everyone who acknowledges me before people, the Son of Man will also acknowledge before God’s angels.
v09: But whoever denies me before people will be denied before God’s angels.
v10: Everyone who speaks a word against the Son of Man will be forgiven, but whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit will not be forgiven.
v11: When they bring you before synagogues, rulers, and authorities, do not be anxious about how or what you should answer or what you should say,
v12: because the Holy Spirit will teach you in that hour what you should say.”
</p>

## The Rich Fool

<p>
v13: Someone in the crowd said to him, “Teacher, tell my brother to divide the inheritance with me.”
v14: Jesus said to him, “Man, who appointed me a judge or divider over you?”
v15: Then he said to them, “Watch out and guard yourselves from every kind of greed, because a person’s life does not consist in the abundance of possessions.”
v16: He told them a parable: “The land of a certain rich man produced abundantly.
v17: He began reasoning within himself, ‘What should I do? I have nowhere to store my crops.’
v18: Then he said, ‘I will do this: I will tear down my barns and build larger ones, and there I will store all my grain and goods.
v19: I will say to my life, “You have many goods stored up for many years. Rest, eat, drink, and enjoy yourself.”’
v20: But God said to him, ‘Fool! This very night your life is being demanded back from you. The things you prepared—whose will they be?’
v21: So it is with the one who stores up treasure for themselves and is not rich toward God.”
</p>

## Seek the Kingdom

<p>
v22: Jesus said to his disciples, “Therefore I tell you: do not be anxious about your life, what you will eat, or about your body, what you will wear.
v23: Life is more than food, and the body more than clothing.
v24: Consider the ravens. They neither sow nor reap, they have neither storeroom nor barn, yet God feeds them. How much more valuable are you than birds!
v25: Which of you by being anxious can add a single cubit to your span of life?
v26: If you cannot do even this small thing, why be anxious about the rest?
v27: Consider the lilies, how they grow. They neither labor nor spin, yet I tell you, not even Solomon in all his glory was clothed like one of these.
v28: If God clothes the grass this way—alive in the field today and tomorrow thrown into an oven—how much more will he clothe you, you of little trust!
v29: Do not keep seeking what you will eat and what you will drink, and do not be unsettled.
v30: The nations of the world seek all these things, but your Father knows that you need them.
v31: Instead, seek his kingdom, and these things will be given to you as well.
v32: Do not be afraid, little flock, because your Father is pleased to give you the kingdom.
v33: Sell your possessions and give to the poor. Make yourselves moneybags that do not wear out, an unfailing treasure in heaven, where no thief comes near and no moth destroys.
v34: For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.
</p>

## Be Ready and Faithful

<p>
v35: “Keep your belts fastened and your lamps burning.
v36: Be like people waiting for their master to return from a wedding banquet so that when he comes and knocks, they can open the door for him immediately.
v37: Blessed are those servants whom the master finds watching when he comes. Truly I tell you, he will fasten his own belt, have them recline at the table, and come serve them.
v38: If he comes in the second watch or even the third and finds them ready, blessed are they.
v39: But understand this: if the owner of the house had known at what hour the thief was coming, he would not have allowed his house to be broken into.
v40: You also must be ready, because the Son of Man is coming at an hour you do not expect.”
v41: Peter said, “Lord, are you telling this parable to us or to everyone?”
v42: The Lord said, “Who then is the faithful and wise steward whom the master will put over the household to give them their food allowance at the proper time?
v43: Blessed is that servant whom the master finds doing so when he comes.
v44: Truly I tell you, he will put that servant over all his possessions.
v45: But if that servant says in his heart, ‘My master is taking a long time to come,’ and begins beating male and female servants and eating, drinking, and getting drunk,
v46: the master of that servant will come on a day when he does not expect him and at an hour he does not know. He will cut him in two and assign him a place with the unfaithful.
v47: That servant who knew the master’s will but did not prepare or act according to it will receive many blows.
v48: But the one who did not know and did things deserving blows will receive few. From everyone who has been given much, much will be required, and from the one entrusted with much, even more will be demanded.
</p>

## Fire, Division, and Discernment

<p>
v49: “I came to cast fire on the earth, and how I wish it were already kindled!
v50: But I have a baptism to undergo, and how distressed I am until it is completed!
v51: Do you think I came to give peace on earth? No, I tell you, but rather division.
v52: From now on five in one house will be divided, three against two and two against three.
v53: They will be divided:
father against son and son against father,
mother against daughter and daughter against mother,
mother-in-law against daughter-in-law and daughter-in-law against mother-in-law.”
v54: Jesus also said to the crowds, “When you see a cloud rising in the west, you immediately say, ‘A shower is coming,’ and it happens.
v55: When the south wind blows, you say, ‘There will be scorching heat,’ and it happens.
v56: Hypocrites! You know how to interpret the appearance of earth and sky. Why do you not know how to interpret this present time?
v57: And why do you not judge for yourselves what is right?
v58: As you go with your accuser to a magistrate, make every effort to settle with them along the way, so that they do not drag you to the judge, the judge hand you over to the officer, and the officer throw you into prison.
v59: I tell you, you will certainly not get out until you have paid the last small coin.”
</p>

## Notes

v04–07: Human power to kill is real but limited; divine judgment and divine care are placed beside one another.

v10: As in Matthew, the saying about blasphemy against the Spirit occurs in a context of rejecting God’s work, not as a free-floating threat aimed at anxious consciences.

v15–21: The rich man’s speech is almost entirely self-referential. The parable’s theological critique is not wealth alone but possession imagined as self-secured life.

v20: `psychē` can mean life, self, or soul. “Life” keeps the force of God demanding back the very life the man thought he controlled.

v32: Luke explicitly describes the kingdom as the Father’s gift before commanding economic dispossession in v33.

v37: The image reverses ordinary household hierarchy: the returning master serves the watching servants.

v46: The violent judgment idiom is retained rather than softened into ordinary dismissal.

v48: Responsibility is explicitly proportioned to knowledge and entrusted resources.

v49–53: Fire, baptism, and household division describe the crisis produced by Jesus’ mission; they are not commands to create violence.

v56: Luke says “this present time,” emphasizing discernment of the moment of Jesus’ ministry.
