## Stumbling, Forgiveness, and Faith

<p>
v01: Jesus said to his disciples, “Stumbling blocks are certain to come, but woe to the person through whom they come!
v02: It would be better for him if a millstone were hung around his neck and he were thrown into the sea than for him to cause one of these little ones to stumble.
v03: Watch yourselves. If your brother sins, rebuke him; and if he repents, forgive him.
v04: Even if he sins against you seven times in a day and returns to you seven times saying, ‘I repent,’ you must forgive him.”
v05: The apostles said to the Lord, “Increase our faith!”
v06: The Lord replied, “If you had faith like a mustard seed, you could say to this mulberry tree, ‘Be uprooted and planted in the sea,’ and it would obey you.
v07: “Suppose one of you has a servant plowing or tending sheep. When he comes in from the field, will you say to him, ‘Come at once and recline at the table’?
v08: Will you not instead tell him, ‘Prepare my dinner, dress yourself to serve me, and wait on me while I eat and drink; afterward you may eat and drink’?
v09: Does the master thank the servant because he did what he was ordered to do?
v10: So you also, when you have done everything you were ordered to do, should say, ‘We are servants who deserve no special credit; we have only done what we were obligated to do.’”
</p>

## Ten Are Cleansed; One Returns

<p>
v11: On the way to Jerusalem, Jesus was passing along the border between Samaria and Galilee.
v12: As he entered a village, ten men with a skin disease met him. They stood at a distance
v13: and called out loudly, “Jesus, Master, have mercy on us!”
v14: When he saw them, he said, “Go show yourselves to the priests.” As they went, they were cleansed.
v15: When one of them saw that he was healed, he came back, glorifying God with a loud voice.
v16: He fell facedown at Jesus’ feet and thanked him. He was a Samaritan.
v17: Jesus responded, “Were not all ten cleansed? Where are the other nine?
v18: Was no one found to return and give glory to God except this foreigner?”
v19: Then he said to the man, “Get up and go. Your faith has saved you.”
</p>

## The Kingdom and the Son of Man

<p>
v20: When the Pharisees asked when the kingdom of God was coming, Jesus answered, “The kingdom of God does not come in a way that can be closely observed.
v21: People will not say, ‘Look, here it is!’ or ‘There it is!’ For look, the kingdom of God is among you.”
v22: Then he said to the disciples, “Days are coming when you will long to see one of the days of the Son of Man, but you will not see it.
v23: People will tell you, ‘Look, there!’ or ‘Look, here!’ Do not go out or chase after them.
v24: For just as lightning flashes and lights up the sky from one end to the other, so will the Son of Man be in his day.
v25: But first he must suffer many things and be rejected by this generation.
v26: Just as it was in the days of Noah, so it will also be in the days of the Son of Man.
v27: People were eating, drinking, marrying, and being given in marriage until the day Noah entered the ark. Then the flood came and destroyed them all.
v28: It was the same in the days of Lot: people were eating and drinking, buying and selling, planting and building.
v29: But on the day Lot left Sodom, fire and sulfur rained from heaven and destroyed them all.
v30: It will be the same on the day the Son of Man is revealed.
v31: On that day, anyone on the housetop whose belongings are inside the house must not go down to get them. Likewise, anyone in the field must not turn back.
v32: Remember Lot’s wife.
v33: Whoever seeks to preserve his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life will keep it alive.
v34: I tell you, on that night two people will be in one bed; one will be taken and the other left.
v35: Two women will be grinding grain together; one will be taken and the other left.”
v37: The disciples asked him, “Where, Lord?” He replied, “Where the body is, there the vultures will gather.”
</p>

## Notes

v10: ‘Servants who deserve no special credit’ renders a term that can mean unworthy, unprofitable, or without claim to reward. The saying concerns obligation, not the servants’ lack of human worth.

v11: The Greek places Jesus ‘through the middle of’ or ‘between’ Samaria and Galilee; the exact route is not specified.

v12: The traditional word ‘leprosy’ covers several ancient skin conditions and is not limited to the modern disease called Hansen’s disease.

v21: The phrase can mean ‘within you’ or ‘among you.’ Addressed to Pharisees as a group, ‘among you’ preserves the kingdom’s presence without locating it in their private interior life.

v36: Public verse 36 is absent from the pinned Greek text, so the numbering moves from 35 to 37.

v37: The bird term can mean eagles or vultures. Around a corpse, vultures makes the ominous image most immediate.

## Vocabulary

v01: **σκάνδαλον (skandalon)**: A trap, stumbling block, or cause of ruin.

v04: **ἀφίημι (aphiēmi)**: Release, forgive, or leave.

v19: **σῴζω (sōzō)**: Save, heal, rescue, or make whole.

v21: **ἐντός (entos)**: Within or among.
