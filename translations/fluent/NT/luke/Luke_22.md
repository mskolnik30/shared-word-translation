## The Plot and Judas

<p>
v01: The Festival of Unleavened Bread, called the Passover, was drawing near.
v02: The chief priests and scribes were looking for a way to kill Jesus, because they were afraid of the people.
v03: Then Satan entered Judas, the one called Iscariot, who was numbered among the Twelve.
v04: Judas went away and discussed with the chief priests and temple guard commanders how he might hand Jesus over to them.
v05: They were delighted and agreed to give him silver.
v06: He consented and began looking for an opportunity to hand Jesus over to them away from the crowd.
</p>

## Preparing the Passover

<p>
v07: Then came the day of Unleavened Bread when the Passover lamb had to be sacrificed.
v08: Jesus sent Peter and John, saying, “Go prepare the Passover for us to eat.”
v09: They asked him, “Where do you want us to prepare it?”
v10: He told them, “Look, as you enter the city, a man carrying a jar of water will meet you. Follow him into the house he enters
v11: and tell the homeowner, ‘The Teacher asks you, “Where is the guest room where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?”’
v12: He will show you a large furnished upstairs room. Prepare it there.”
v13: They went and found everything just as he had told them, and they prepared the Passover.
</p>

## The Supper

<p>
v14: When the hour came, Jesus reclined at the table, and the apostles were with him.
v15: He said to them, “I have deeply desired to eat this Passover with you before I suffer.
v16: For I tell you, I will certainly not eat it again until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God.”
v17: After taking a cup and giving thanks, he said, “Take this and share it among yourselves.
v18: For I tell you, from now on I will certainly not drink from the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes.”
v19: Then he took bread, gave thanks, broke it, and gave it to them, saying, “This is my body, [which is given for you. Do this in remembrance of me.
v20: And in the same way after supper he took the cup, saying, ‘This cup is the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you.’]
v21: But look, the hand of the one betraying me is with mine on the table.
v22: For the Son of Man is going as it has been determined, but woe to that man through whom he is betrayed!”
v23: Then they began discussing among themselves which of them could be about to do this.
</p>

## Greatness and Service

<p>
v24: A dispute also arose among them about which of them was considered to be greatest.
v25: Jesus told them, “The kings of the nations rule over them, and those who exercise authority over them are called benefactors.
v26: But you must not be like that. Instead, the greatest among you must become like the youngest, and the one who leads like one who serves.
v27: For who is greater: the one reclining at the table, or the one serving? Is it not the one reclining? But I am among you as one who serves.
v28: You are the ones who have stayed with me through my trials.
v29: And I confer a kingdom on you, just as my Father conferred one on me,
v30: so that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom and sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.
</p>

## Simon Will Be Sifted

<p>
v31: “Simon, Simon, look: Satan has demanded to sift all of you like wheat.
v32: But I have prayed for you, Simon, that your faith may not fail. And when you have turned back, strengthen your brothers.”
v33: Peter told him, “Lord, I am ready to go with you both to prison and to death.”
v34: Jesus replied, “I tell you, Peter, the rooster will not crow today until you have denied three times that you know me.”
</p>

## Purses, Bags, and Swords

<p>
v35: Then Jesus asked them, “When I sent you without a purse, bag, or sandals, did you lack anything?” They answered, “Nothing.”
v36: He told them, “But now, whoever has a purse should take it, and likewise a bag. Whoever has no sword should sell his cloak and buy one.
v37: For I tell you, this Scripture must be fulfilled in me: ‘He was counted among the lawless.’ Yes, what is written about me is reaching its fulfillment.”
v38: They said, “Lord, look, here are two swords.” He replied, “That is enough.”
</p>

## Jesus Prays and Is Arrested

<p>
v39: Jesus went out and made his customary way to the Mount of Olives, and the disciples followed him.
v40: When he reached the place, he told them, “Pray that you may not enter into testing.”
v41: He withdrew from them about a stone’s throw, knelt down, and prayed,
v42: “Father, if you are willing, take this cup away from me. Yet not my will, but yours be done.”
v43: [Then an angel from heaven appeared to him and strengthened him.
v44: In anguish, he prayed more earnestly, and his sweat became like drops of blood falling to the ground.]
v45: When Jesus rose from prayer and came to the disciples, he found them sleeping from sorrow.
v46: He said to them, “Why are you sleeping? Get up and pray so that you do not enter into testing.”
v47: While he was still speaking, a crowd appeared. The man called Judas, one of the Twelve, was leading them. He approached Jesus to kiss him.
v48: But Jesus asked him, “Judas, are you betraying the Son of Man with a kiss?”
v49: When those around Jesus saw what was about to happen, they asked, “Lord, should we strike with the sword?”
v50: Then one of them struck the high priest’s servant and cut off his right ear.
v51: But Jesus said, “No more of this!” He touched the man’s ear and healed him.
v52: Then Jesus said to the chief priests, temple guard commanders, and elders who had come against him, “Have you come out with swords and clubs as though I were a robber?
v53: Every day I was with you in the temple courts, and you did not lay hands on me. But this is your hour—and the authority of darkness.”
</p>

## Peter Denies Jesus

<p>
v54: They arrested him, led him away, and brought him into the high priest’s house. Peter followed at a distance.
v55: They kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard and sat down together, and Peter sat among them.
v56: A servant girl saw him sitting in the firelight. Looking closely at him, she said, “This man was with him too.”
v57: But Peter denied it, saying, “Woman, I do not know him.”
v58: A little later, someone else saw him and said, “You are one of them too.” But Peter replied, “Man, I am not!”
v59: About an hour later, another person insisted, “Certainly this man was with him too, because he is also a Galilean.”
v60: But Peter said, “Man, I do not know what you are talking about!” Immediately, while he was still speaking, a rooster crowed.
v61: The Lord turned and looked at Peter. Then Peter remembered the Lord’s word, how he had told him, “Before the rooster crows today, you will deny me three times.”
v62: And he went outside and wept bitterly.
</p>

## Jesus Mocked and Questioned

<p>
v63: The men holding Jesus in custody mocked and beat him.
v64: They blindfolded him and kept asking, “Prophesy! Who was it that hit you?”
v65: And they said many other blasphemous things against him.
v66: When day came, the council of the people assembled—both chief priests and scribes—and they led Jesus into their council chamber.
v67: They said, “If you are the Messiah, tell us.” He replied, “If I tell you, you will certainly not believe.
v68: And if I ask you, you will certainly not answer.
v69: But from now on the Son of Man will be seated at the right hand of the power of God.”
v70: They all asked, “Are you, then, the Son of God?” He answered them, “You say that I am.”
v71: They said, “Why do we need any more testimony? We ourselves have heard it from his own mouth.”
</p>

## Notes

v19–20: The words from ‘which is given for you’ through the cup saying are bracketed in the pinned source because of significant textual variation. They remain readable but visibly distinct from the surrounding main text.

v31–32: Satan’s demand is addressed to the disciples in the plural, while Jesus’ prayer and commission are addressed specifically to Simon. The English names Simon in verse 32 to make the shift audible.

v36–38: Jesus speaks of buying a sword in connection with being counted among the lawless. The disciples produce two swords and Jesus ends the exchange with the ambiguous ‘That is enough.’ The passage does not narrate a program of armed defense.

v43–44: The angel and sweat-like-blood verses are bracketed in the pinned source because of significant textual variation. The wording is a simile and does not require a medical diagnosis.

v50–51: A disciple cuts off the right ear of the high priest’s servant. Jesus stops the violence, touches the injured man, and heals him.

## Vocabulary

v15: **ἐπιθυμία (epithymia)**: Strong desire or longing.

v20: **διαθήκη (diathēkē)**: Covenant, a binding relationship or disposition.

v31: **σινιάζω (siniazō)**: Sift, as grain is shaken to separate it.

v40: **πειρασμός (peirasmos)**: Testing, trial, or temptation.
