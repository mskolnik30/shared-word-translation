## A Poor Widow’s Gift

<p>
v01: Jesus looked up and saw rich people putting their gifts into the temple treasury.
v02: He also saw a poor widow put in two small coins.
v03: He said, “Truly I tell you, this poor widow has put in more than all the others.
v04: For all of them gave gifts from their abundance, but she, out of her poverty, put in all she had to live on.”
</p>

## The Temple and What Is Coming

<p>
v05: When some were speaking about the temple, how it was adorned with beautiful stones and gifts dedicated to God, Jesus said,
v06: “As for these things you see, days are coming when not one stone will be left on another; every one will be torn down.”
v07: They asked him, “Teacher, when will these things happen, and what will be the sign when they are about to take place?”
v08: He replied, “Watch out that you are not led astray. Many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am he,’ and ‘The time has come near.’ Do not follow them.
v09: When you hear of wars and rebellions, do not be terrified. These things must happen first, but the end will not come immediately.”
v10: Then he said to them, “Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom.
v11: There will be great earthquakes and, in various places, famines and plagues. There will also be terrifying events and great signs from heaven.
</p>

## Persecution Becomes Testimony

<p>
v12: But before all these things, they will seize and persecute you, handing you over to synagogues and prisons and bringing you before kings and governors because of my name.
v13: This will result in an opportunity for you to testify.
v14: So resolve in your hearts not to prepare your defense beforehand,
v15: because I will give you words and wisdom that none of your opponents will be able to resist or contradict.
v16: You will be betrayed even by parents, brothers, relatives, and friends, and they will put some of you to death.
v17: Everyone will hate you because of my name.
v18: Yet not a hair of your head will perish.
v19: By your endurance you will gain your lives.
</p>

## Jerusalem Surrounded

<p>
v20: “When you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then know that its desolation has come near.
v21: Then those in Judea must flee to the mountains, those inside Jerusalem must leave, and those in the countryside must not enter it.
v22: For these are days of vengeance, when everything written will be fulfilled.
v23: How terrible it will be in those days for pregnant women and those nursing infants! For there will be great distress on the land and wrath against this people.
v24: They will fall by the edge of the sword and be taken captive into all the nations. Jerusalem will be trampled by the nations until the times of the nations are fulfilled.
</p>

## The Son of Man and Redemption

<p>
v25: “There will be signs in the sun, moon, and stars, and on earth the nations will be in anguish, bewildered by the roaring and surging sea.
v26: People will faint from fear and expectation of what is coming on the inhabited world, because the powers of the heavens will be shaken.
v27: Then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.
v28: When these things begin to happen, stand up and lift your heads, because your redemption is drawing near.”
</p>

## The Fig Tree and Watchfulness

<p>
v29: Then he told them a parable: “Look at the fig tree and all the trees.
v30: As soon as they put out leaves, you can see for yourselves and know that summer is already near.
v31: In the same way, when you see these things happening, know that the kingdom of God is near.
v32: Truly I tell you, this generation will certainly not pass away until all these things happen.
v33: Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will certainly not pass away.
v34: “Watch yourselves, or your hearts may be weighed down with dissipation, drunkenness, and the worries of daily life, and that day may come on you suddenly
v35: like a trap. For it will come upon everyone who lives on the face of the whole earth.
v36: Stay alert at all times, praying that you may have strength to escape all these things that are about to happen and to stand before the Son of Man.”
v37: Each day Jesus taught in the temple courts, and each night he went out and stayed on the hill called the Mount of Olives.
v38: Early each morning, all the people came to him in the temple courts to listen.
</p>

## Notes

v16–19: The passage holds together real betrayal and death with the promise that not a hair will perish and that endurance gains life. The translation preserves the tension rather than explaining it away.

v20–24: The warning names Jerusalem, armies, flight, killing, captivity, and trampling. Pregnant women and nursing mothers are singled out because siege and displacement intensify their vulnerability.

v32: ‘This generation’ is retained rather than replaced with an interpretation of which generation is meant.

v36: The verb can mean have strength or prevail; the prayer concerns enduring or escaping what is coming and standing before the Son of Man.

## Vocabulary

v13: **μαρτύριον (martyrion)**: Testimony or witness.

v19: **ὑπομονή (hypomonē)**: Endurance, steadfastness, or patient persistence.

v24: **καιροὶ ἐθνῶν (kairoi ethnōn)**: Times or appointed seasons of the nations.

v28: **ἀπολύτρωσις (apolytrōsis)**: Redemption, release, or liberation.
