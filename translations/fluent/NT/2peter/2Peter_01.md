## Greeting
<p>
v01: Simeon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who have received a faith equal in value to ours through the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ:
v02: May grace and peace be multiplied to you through the knowledge of God and of Jesus our Lord.
</p>

## Confirming the Calling
<p>
v03: His divine power has given us everything needed for life and godliness through knowing the one who called us by his own glory and excellence.
v04: Through these he has given us precious and very great promises, so that through them you may become partners in the divine nature, having escaped the corruption in the world caused by desire.
</p>

<p>
v05: For this very reason, make every effort to supply goodness alongside your faith, and knowledge alongside goodness,
v06: self-control alongside knowledge, endurance alongside self-control, and godliness alongside endurance,
v07: family affection alongside godliness, and love alongside family affection.
</p>

<p>
v08: For if these qualities are yours and increasing, they will keep you from being ineffective or unfruitful in knowing our Lord Jesus Christ.
v09: But anyone who lacks these is blind and shortsighted, having forgotten the cleansing of past sins.
</p>

<p>
v10: Therefore, brothers and sisters, make every effort to confirm your calling and election, for if you practice these things you will never stumble.
v11: In this way, entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ will be richly supplied to you.
</p>

## The Apostolic Witness
<p>
v12: Therefore, I will always remind you about these things, although you know them and are established in the truth you now possess.
v13: I think it is right, as long as I am in this tent, to stir you up by reminding you,
v14: because I know that I will soon put aside my tent, just as our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me.
v15: And I will make every effort so that after my departure you may always be able to remember these things.
</p>

<p>
v16: For we did not follow cleverly invented myths when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ; we were eyewitnesses of his majesty.
v17: For he received honor and glory from God the Father when a voice like this was brought to him by the Majestic Glory: “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”
v18: We ourselves heard this voice brought from heaven when we were with him on the holy mountain.
</p>

<p>
v19: We also have the prophetic word made more certain, and you do well to pay attention to it as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until day dawns and the morning star rises in your hearts.
v20: Above all, know this: no prophecy of Scripture comes from one's own interpretation,
v21: for no prophecy was ever brought by human will; rather, people spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The pinned Greek reads ‘Simeon Peter.’

v19: The ‘morning star’ rising in hearts can mark inward illumination connected to the future day.

v20: The phrase can mean that no prophecy comes from the prophet’s own interpretation or that no prophecy is a matter of private interpretation.

## Vocabulary

v05–07: **ἐπιχορηγέω (epichorēgeō)**: Supply or furnish generously; the verb frames the chain of qualities.
