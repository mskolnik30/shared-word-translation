## False Prophets and Their Destruction

<p>
v01: But false prophets also arose among the people, just as false teachers will arise among you. They will secretly introduce destructive teachings, even denying the Master who bought them, and bring swift destruction on themselves.
v02: Many will follow their shameless conduct, and because of them the way of truth will be slandered.
v03: In their greed they will exploit you with fabricated stories. Their condemnation, pronounced long ago, is not idle, and their destruction is not asleep.
v04: For if God did not spare angels when they sinned, but cast them into Tartarus and handed them over to chains of darkness, held for judgment;
v05: if he did not spare the ancient world, but protected Noah, a herald of righteousness, along with seven others, when he brought a flood on the world of the ungodly;
v06: if he condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to ruin, reducing them to ashes and making them an example of what awaits the ungodly;
v07: and if he rescued righteous Lot, worn down by the lawless people's shameless way of life—
v08: for that righteous man, living among them, tormented his righteous soul day after day with the lawless deeds he saw and heard—
v09: then the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trial and to hold the unrighteous under punishment until the day of judgment,
v10: especially those who follow the flesh in defiling desire and despise lordship. Bold and self-willed, they do not tremble at slandering glorious beings,
v11: whereas angels, greater in strength and power, do not bring a slanderous judgment against them.
v12: But these people are like unreasoning animals, creatures of instinct born to be caught and destroyed. They slander what they do not understand, and in their destruction they too will be destroyed,
v13: suffering wrong as the wages of wrongdoing. They regard daytime revelry as pleasure. They are stains and blemishes, reveling in their deceptions while they feast with you.
v14: Their eyes are full of adultery and never stop sinning. They lure unstable souls and have hearts trained in greed. They are children under a curse!
v15: They have left the straight road and gone astray, following the road of Balaam son of Bosor, who loved the wages of wrongdoing.
v16: But he was rebuked for his own lawlessness: a speechless donkey spoke with a human voice and stopped the prophet's madness.
v17: These people are springs without water and mists driven by a storm. Deep darkness is reserved for them.
v18: For with grand but empty words, they use fleshly desires and shameless conduct to lure people who are just escaping from those who live in error.
v19: They promise them freedom while they themselves are slaves of corruption. For whatever overcomes a person enslaves that person.
v20: If, after escaping the world's defilements through knowing the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, they become entangled in them again and are overcome, their last state is worse than their first.
v21: It would have been better for them never to have known the way of righteousness than to know it and turn back from the holy command handed down to them.
v22: What the true proverb says has happened to them:
“A dog returns to its own vomit,”
and,
“A washed sow returns to wallowing in mud.”
</p>

## Notes

v04: The pinned Greek reads “chains”; another reading has “pits.” Tartarus is the name used here for the place of confinement.

v10–11: “Lordship” and “glorious beings” are not identified more specifically. The pinned text of verse 11 does not include “before the Lord.”

v13: The pinned text reads “suffering wrong” and “deceptions”; other readings have “receiving” and “love feasts.”

v15: The pinned Greek spells the name Bosor; other texts have Beor.

v18: “Just escaping” can also be rendered “barely escaping.” Other texts read “truly escaped.”

## Vocabulary

v01: **αἵρεσις (hairesis)**: A party, faction or school of teaching; here rendered destructive teachings.

v14: **μεσταὶ μοιχαλίδος**: Literally “full of an adulteress,” rendered eyes full of adultery.

v19: **φθορά (phthora)**: Corruption, decay or destruction.
