## The Day of the Lord Will Come

<p>
v01: Beloved, this is now the second letter I am writing to you. In both letters I use reminders to awaken your sincere understanding,
v02: so that you remember the words spoken beforehand by the holy prophets and the command of the Lord and Savior given through your apostles.
v03: Above all, know this: in the last days scoffers will come with their scoffing, following their own desires
v04: and saying, “Where is the promise of his coming? Ever since our ancestors fell asleep, everything has continued as it was from the beginning of creation.”
v05: They deliberately overlook this: long ago the heavens existed, and the earth was formed out of water and through water by God's word.
v06: Through these, the world of that time was flooded with water and destroyed.
v07: But by the same word, the present heavens and earth are stored up for fire, kept for the day of judgment and the destruction of ungodly people.
v08: But do not overlook this one thing, beloved: with the Lord one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day.
v09: The Lord is not slow about the promise, as some understand slowness. He is patient with you, not wanting anyone to perish but everyone to come to repentance.
v10: But the day of the Lord will come like a thief. The heavens will pass away with a roar, the elements will be dissolved by fire, and the earth and the works done on it will be exposed.
v11: Since all these things are to be dissolved in this way, what sort of people ought you to be in holy conduct and godliness,
v12: waiting for and hastening the coming of God's day, because of which the heavens will be dissolved in fire and the elements will melt in the heat?
v13: But according to his promise, we await new heavens and a new earth, where righteousness makes its home.
</p>

## Final Exhortations

<p>
v14: So, beloved, as you wait for these things, make every effort to be found by him at peace, without stain or blemish.
v15: Regard our Lord's patience as salvation, just as our beloved brother Paul also wrote to you with the wisdom given to him.
v16: He speaks of these things in all his letters. Some things in them are hard to understand, and the uninstructed and unstable twist them, as they do the other Scriptures, to their own destruction.
v17: You therefore, beloved, already know this. Guard yourselves against being carried away by the error of the lawless and losing your own firm footing.
v18: Instead, grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To him be glory, both now and into the day of eternity. Amen.
</p>

## Notes

v06: “Through these” is plural in Greek; its precise relation to the preceding water and divine word is debated.

v10: The pinned text reads “will be found,” rendered “will be exposed.” Other witnesses have readings including “will be burned up” and “will not be found.”

v12: The verb can mean hasten or eagerly desire; the text does not describe how the day is hastened.

## Vocabulary

v10: **στοιχεῖα (stoicheia)**: Elements or basic constituents; the referent can include the components of the cosmos or heavenly bodies.

v18: **ἡμέρα αἰῶνος**: Literally “day of eternity,” an expression retained here.
