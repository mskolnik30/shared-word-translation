## The Temple Will Be Thrown Down

<p>
v01: As Jesus was leaving the temple, one of his disciples said to him, “Teacher, look! What magnificent stones and buildings!”
v02: Jesus said to him, “Do you see these great buildings? Not one stone will be left here on another; every one will be torn down.”
</p>

## Do Not Be Led Astray

<p>
v03: As Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter, James, John, and Andrew asked him privately,
v04: “Tell us, when will these things happen? What will be the sign that all these things are about to be completed?”
v05: Jesus began to tell them, “Watch out that no one leads you astray.
v06: Many will come in my name, saying, ‘I am he,’ and they will lead many astray.
v07: When you hear of wars and reports of wars, do not be alarmed. These things must happen, but the end is not yet.
v08: For nation will rise against nation and kingdom against kingdom. There will be earthquakes in various places; there will be famines. These are the beginning of birth pains.
</p>

<p>
v09: But watch yourselves. They will hand you over to councils, and you will be beaten in synagogues. Because of me, you will stand before governors and kings as a testimony to them.
v10: And the good news must first be proclaimed to all nations.
v11: When they lead you away and hand you over, do not worry beforehand about what to say. Say whatever is given to you in that hour, for it is not you speaking, but the Holy Spirit.
v12: Brother will hand over brother to death, and a father his child. Children will rise against their parents and put them to death.
v13: Everyone will hate you because of my name. But the one who endures to the end will be saved.
</p>

## The Desolating Abomination

<p>
v14: “When you see the abomination of desolation standing where it should not—let the reader understand—then those in Judea must flee to the mountains.
v15: Anyone on the housetop must not go down or enter the house to take anything out,
v16: and anyone in the field must not turn back to get their cloak.
v17: How terrible those days will be for women who are pregnant and for those nursing infants!
v18: Pray that it will not happen in winter,
v19: because those days will bring distress unlike any from the beginning of the creation God made until now—and never to be equaled again.
v20: If the Lord had not shortened those days, no one would survive. But for the sake of the chosen, whom he chose, he shortened the days.
</p>

<p>
v21: Then if anyone says to you, ‘Look, here is the Messiah!’ or, ‘Look, there he is!’ do not believe it.
v22: For false messiahs and false prophets will arise and perform signs and wonders to lead astray, if possible, the chosen.
v23: But you, watch out! I have told you everything ahead of time.
</p>

## The Son of Man Coming

<p>
v24: “But in those days, after that distress,
‘the sun will be darkened,
and the moon will not give its light;
v25: the stars will fall from heaven,
and the powers in the heavens will be shaken.’
v26: Then they will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory.
v27: Then he will send out the angels and gather his chosen from the four winds, from the farthest end of the earth to the farthest end of heaven.
</p>

## The Fig Tree and the Need to Stay Awake

<p>
v28: “Learn this parable from the fig tree: as soon as its branch becomes tender and puts out leaves, you know that summer is near.
v29: In the same way, when you see these things happening, you know that it is near—right at the doors.
v30: Truly I tell you, this generation will certainly not pass away until all these things happen.
v31: Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will certainly never pass away.
</p>

<p>
v32: “But no one knows about that day or hour—not the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but only the Father.
v33: Watch out! Stay alert, because you do not know when the appointed time will come.
v34: It is like a man away on a journey who left his house, gave authority to his slaves, assigned each one their work, and ordered the doorkeeper to stay awake.
v35: Therefore stay awake, because you do not know when the master of the house is coming—whether in the evening, at midnight, when the rooster crows, or at dawn.
v36: Otherwise, he may come suddenly and find you sleeping.
v37: What I say to you, I say to everyone: Stay awake!”
</p>

## Notes

v06: The claim is literally ‘I am.’ In this context it is rendered ‘I am he,’ the claim of false figures who come in Jesus’ name.

v07–08: Wars, earthquakes, and famines are not called the end. They are ‘the beginning of birth pains.’

v14: ‘Abomination of desolation’ echoes Daniel. The Greek has a masculine participle, ‘standing,’ and leaves the forbidden location indirect. ‘Let the reader understand’ interrupts the reported speech.

v20: ‘No one would survive’ is literally ‘no flesh would be saved.’ The verse repeats the related words ‘chosen’ and ‘chose.’

v29: The Greek does not state whether ‘he’ or ‘it’ is near. ‘It is near’ preserves the open referent.

v30: ‘This generation’ is retained without choosing among competing chronological and theological interpretations.

v32: The Greek explicitly includes ‘nor the Son’ among those who do not know the day or hour.

v34–37: Two related verbs urge alertness and staying awake. The four times in verse 35 correspond to successive watches of the night.

## Vocabulary

v09: **παραδίδωμι (paradidōmi)**: Hand over, deliver, or betray; the passion verb is applied here to Jesus’ followers.

v14: **βδέλυγμα τῆς ἐρημώσεως (bdelygma tēs erēmōseōs)**: Abomination of desolation, a scriptural image of desecration.

v19: **θλῖψις (thlipsis)**: Distress, affliction, or tribulation.

v20: **ἐκλεκτός (eklektos)**: Chosen or elect.

v33: **καιρός (kairos)**: The appointed or decisive time.

v34: **δοῦλος (doulos)**: Slave; here each receives authority and assigned work.
