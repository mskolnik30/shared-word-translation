## The Sealing of the 144,000

<p>
v01: After this I saw four angels standing at the four corners of the earth, holding back its four winds so that no wind would blow on the earth or sea or any tree.
v02: I saw another angel rising from the east with the seal of the living God. He cried out in a loud voice to the four angels who had been given power to harm the earth and sea,
v03: “Do not harm the earth or the sea or the trees until we have sealed the slaves of our God on their foreheads.”
v04: I heard the number of those sealed: 144,000, sealed from every tribe of the children of Israel.
v05: From the tribe of Judah, 12,000 sealed;
from the tribe of Reuben, 12,000;
from the tribe of Gad, 12,000;
v06: from the tribe of Asher, 12,000;
from the tribe of Naphtali, 12,000;
from the tribe of Manasseh, 12,000;
v07: from the tribe of Simeon, 12,000;
from the tribe of Levi, 12,000;
from the tribe of Issachar, 12,000;
v08: from the tribe of Zebulun, 12,000;
from the tribe of Joseph, 12,000;
from the tribe of Benjamin, 12,000 sealed.
</p>

## A Great Multitude from Every Nation

<p>
v09: After these things I looked, and there was a great crowd that no one could count, from every nation, tribe, people and language, standing before the throne and before the Lamb. They wore white robes and held palm branches in their hands.
v10: They cried out in a loud voice:
“Salvation belongs to our God,
who sits on the throne,
and to the Lamb!”
v11: All the angels stood around the throne, the elders and the four living creatures. They fell on their faces before the throne and worshiped God,
v12: saying:
“Amen! Blessing and glory and wisdom and thanks
and honor and power and strength
belong to our God forever and ever. Amen!”
v13: Then one of the elders asked me, “Who are these dressed in white robes, and where did they come from?”
v14: I said to him, “My lord, you know.” He said to me, “These are the ones coming out of the great suffering. They washed their robes and made them white in the Lamb's blood.
v15: That is why they are before God's throne
and serve him day and night in his temple.
The one seated on the throne will spread his tent over them.
v16: They will hunger no more and thirst no more;
the sun will not strike them,
nor any scorching heat,
v17: because the Lamb in the midst of the throne will shepherd them
and lead them to springs of the water of life,
and God will wipe every tear from their eyes.”
</p>

## Notes

v04–08: The list includes both Joseph and Manasseh, and does not name Dan or Ephraim. The names are not harmonized with other tribal lists.

v14: The Greek uses a present participle: “those coming out.” Washing robes white in blood is left as an image.

## Vocabulary

v15: **σκηνόω (skēnoō)**: Pitch a tent or dwell; here spread a tent over.
