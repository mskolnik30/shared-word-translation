## Prologue
<p>
v01: The revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave him to show his slaves what must happen soon. He made it known by sending his angel to his slave John,
v02: who bore witness to God's word and the testimony of Jesus Christ—to everything he saw.
v03: Blessed is the one who reads aloud, and blessed are those who hear the words of the prophecy and keep what is written in it, because the time is near.
</p>
## Greeting to the Seven Churches
<p>
v04: John, to the seven churches in Asia: grace and peace to you from the one who is and who was and who is coming, and from the seven spirits before his throne,
v05: and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead and the ruler of the kings of the earth. To the one who loves us and freed us from our sins by his blood—
v06: and made us a kingdom, priests to his God and Father—to him be glory and power forever and ever. Amen.
v07: Look, he is coming with the clouds,
and every eye will see him,
even those who pierced him,
and all the tribes of the earth will mourn over him.
Yes, amen.
v08: “I am the Alpha and the Omega,” says the Lord God, “the one who is and who was and who is coming, the Almighty.”
</p>
## The Vision of the Son of Man
<p>
v09: I, John, your brother and fellow participant in the suffering, kingdom and endurance in Jesus, was on the island called Patmos because of God's word and the testimony of Jesus.
v10: I was in the Spirit on the Lord's day, and I heard behind me a loud voice like a trumpet,
v11: saying, “Write what you see in a scroll and send it to the seven churches: Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia and Laodicea.”
</p>

<p>
v12: I turned to see the voice that was speaking to me. When I turned, I saw seven golden lampstands,
v13: and among the lampstands, one like a son of man, dressed in a robe reaching to his feet, with a golden sash around his chest.
v14: His head and hair were white like white wool, like snow, and his eyes were like a flame of fire.
v15: His feet were like burnished bronze, as though refined in a furnace, and his voice was like the sound of many waters.
v16: In his right hand he held seven stars. A sharp, double-edged sword came out of his mouth, and his face was like the sun shining in its strength.
</p>

<p>
v17: When I saw him, I fell at his feet as though dead. He laid his right hand on me and said, “Do not be afraid. I am the first and the last,
v18: and the living one. I was dead, and look—I am alive forever and ever! I hold the keys of Death and Hades.
v19: So write what you have seen, what is, and what is to happen after these things.
v20: As for the mystery of the seven stars you saw in my right hand and the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are seven churches.”
</p>

## Notes

v01–02,09: “Revelation of Jesus Christ” and “testimony of Jesus” can indicate what comes from Jesus or what concerns him.

v04: Asia here names the Roman province in western Asia Minor. “Seven spirits” is retained without replacing the number with an interpretation.

v05: The pinned Greek reads “freed”; another reading is “washed.”

v13: “One like a son of man” echoes the humanlike figure of Daniel 7:13.

v20: The word translated angels can also mean messengers; their precise identity is not supplied.

## Vocabulary

v15: **χαλκολίβανον (chalkolibanon)**: A bright or refined copper alloy, conventionally burnished bronze; the exact metal is uncertain.

v18: **ᾅδης (hadēs)**: Hades, the realm of the dead.
