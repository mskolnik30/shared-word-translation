## The Great Prostitute and the Beast

<p>
v01: One of the seven angels who had the seven bowls came and spoke to me: “Come, I will show you the judgment of the great prostitute who sits on many waters,
v02: with whom the kings of the earth committed sexual immorality. Those who live on the earth became drunk on the wine of her prostitution.”
v03: He carried me away in the Spirit into a wilderness. I saw a woman sitting on a scarlet beast covered with blasphemous names, with seven heads and ten horns.
v04: The woman was dressed in purple and scarlet, adorned with gold, precious stones and pearls. In her hand she held a golden cup full of abominations and the uncleanness of her prostitution.
v05: On her forehead was written a name, a mystery: “Babylon the great, the mother of prostitutes and of the earth's abominations.”
v06: I saw the woman drunk with the blood of the holy ones and the blood of Jesus' witnesses. When I saw her, I was greatly astonished.
v07: The angel said to me, “Why are you astonished? I will tell you the mystery of the woman and the beast carrying her, with its seven heads and ten horns.
v08: The beast you saw was, and is not, and is about to rise from the abyss and go to destruction. Those who live on the earth, whose names have not been written in the book of life from the foundation of the world, will marvel when they see the beast, because it was, and is not, and will be present.
v09: Here is the mind that has wisdom: the seven heads are seven mountains on which the woman sits. They are also seven kings:
v10: five have fallen, one is, and the other has not yet come. When he comes, he must remain a little while.
v11: The beast that was and is not is itself an eighth, and belongs to the seven, and goes to destruction.
v12: The ten horns you saw are ten kings who have not yet received a kingdom, but receive authority as kings for one hour with the beast.
v13: They have one purpose and give their power and authority to the beast.
v14: They will wage war against the Lamb, and the Lamb will overcome them, because he is Lord of lords and King of kings. Those with him are called and chosen and faithful.”
v15: He said to me, “The waters you saw, where the prostitute sits, are peoples, crowds, nations and languages.
v16: The ten horns you saw and the beast will hate the prostitute. They will leave her desolate and naked, eat her flesh and burn her with fire.
v17: For God has put it into their hearts to carry out his purpose, to act with one purpose and give their kingdom to the beast until God's words are fulfilled.
v18: The woman you saw is the great city that has dominion over the kings of the earth.”
</p>

## Notes

v05: “Mystery” can describe the name or be understood as part of the inscription.

v08: The pinned text ends “will be present”; other witnesses vary the last part of the temporal formula.

v09–11: The seven mountains are also seven kings. The text gives the five/one/other and eighth/from-seven relationships without naming the kings.

v16: The pinned text reads “the ten horns ... and the beast”; another reading relates the horns as being on the beast.

## Vocabulary

v01: **πόρνη (pornē)**: Prostitute; used here in the city’s personification.
