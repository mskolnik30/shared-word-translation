## The Angel and the Little Scroll

<p>
v01: I saw another mighty angel coming down from heaven, wrapped in a cloud, with a rainbow over his head. His face was like the sun, and his feet were like pillars of fire.
v02: He held an open little scroll in his hand. He set his right foot on the sea and his left on the earth,
v03: and cried out in a loud voice like a roaring lion. When he cried out, the seven thunders spoke with their own voices.
v04: When the seven thunders spoke, I was about to write, but I heard a voice from heaven say, “Seal up what the seven thunders said, and do not write it.”
v05: The angel I had seen standing on the sea and the earth raised his right hand to heaven
v06: and swore by the one who lives forever and ever, who created heaven and everything in it, earth and everything in it, and sea and everything in it: “There will be no more delay.
v07: But in the days when the seventh angel is about to sound his trumpet, God's mystery will be completed, just as he announced the good news to his slaves the prophets.”
v08: The voice I had heard from heaven spoke to me again: “Go, take the open scroll in the hand of the angel standing on the sea and the earth.”
v09: I went to the angel and asked him to give me the little scroll. He said to me, “Take it and eat it. It will make your stomach bitter, but in your mouth it will be sweet as honey.”
v10: I took the little scroll from the angel's hand and ate it. In my mouth it was sweet as honey, but when I had eaten it, my stomach became bitter.
v11: They said to me, “You must prophesy again about many peoples, nations, languages and kings.”
</p>

## Notes

v06: The Greek says “time will no longer be”; in this sequence it can mean that there will be no further delay.

v07: The Greek uses a completed form for the fulfillment set in the seventh angel’s days.

v11: The source has the plural “they say”; no speakers are identified here. “About” may also carry the sense “over” or “against.”

## Vocabulary

v02: **βιβλαρίδιον (biblaridion)**: A little scroll or book.
