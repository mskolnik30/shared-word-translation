## The Seven Seals

<p>
v01: I watched as the Lamb opened one of the seven seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures say in a voice like thunder, “Come!”
v02: I looked, and there was a white horse! Its rider had a bow, and a crown was given to him. He went out conquering and to conquer.
v03: When he opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, “Come!”
v04: Another horse came out, fiery red. Its rider was given power to take peace from the earth so that people would slaughter one another, and a great sword was given to him.
v05: When he opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, “Come!” I looked, and there was a black horse! Its rider held a balance in his hand.
v06: I heard what sounded like a voice among the four living creatures saying, “A measure of wheat for a denarius, and three measures of barley for a denarius! Do not harm the oil or the wine.”
v07: When he opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth living creature say, “Come!”
v08: I looked, and there was a pale green horse! Its rider was named Death, and Hades followed him. They were given authority over a fourth of the earth, to kill by sword, famine, death and the wild beasts of the earth.
v09: When he opened the fifth seal, I saw beneath the altar the souls of those slaughtered because of God's word and the testimony they held.
v10: They cried out in a loud voice, “How long, holy and true Master, before you judge and avenge our blood on those who live on the earth?”
v11: Each was given a white robe, and they were told to rest a little longer, until the full number of their fellow slaves, their brothers and sisters who were to be killed as they had been, was complete.
v12: I watched as he opened the sixth seal. A great earthquake struck, the sun became black like sackcloth of hair, and the whole moon became like blood.
v13: The stars of heaven fell to the earth, as a fig tree shaken by a strong wind drops its unripe figs.
v14: The sky withdrew like a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island was moved from its place.
v15: The kings of the earth, the great ones, the military commanders, the rich, the powerful, and every slave and free person hid in caves and among the rocks of the mountains.
v16: They said to the mountains and rocks, “Fall on us and hide us from the face of the one seated on the throne and from the Lamb's wrath,
v17: because the great day of their wrath has come, and who can stand?”
</p>

## Notes

v01,03,05,07: The pinned summons is simply “Come”; some witnesses add “and see.”

v08: “Death” can also denote pestilence in the list of sword, famine, death and beasts.

v11: The Greek says that the fellow slaves should be “completed”; the rendering supplies their full number. The expression can also be understood in terms of their course being completed.

v17: The pinned text reads “their wrath”; another reading has “his wrath.”

## Vocabulary

v06: **χοῖνιξ (choinix)**: A dry measure of roughly a liter, often a person’s daily grain ration.

v06: **δηνάριον (dēnarion)**: Denarius, a Roman silver coin; elsewhere a day’s wage for a laborer.
