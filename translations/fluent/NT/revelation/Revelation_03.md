## To the Church in Sardis

<p>
v01: “To the angel of the church in Sardis write: These are the words of the one who has the seven spirits of God and the seven stars: I know your works. You have a reputation for being alive, but you are dead.
v02: Wake up, and strengthen what remains and was about to die, for I have not found your works complete before my God.
v03: Remember, then, what you received and heard. Keep it and repent. If you do not wake up, I will come like a thief, and you will not know at what hour I will come upon you.
v04: Yet you have a few people in Sardis who have not stained their clothes. They will walk with me in white, because they are worthy.
v05: The one who overcomes will be clothed in white garments like these. I will never erase that person's name from the book of life, and I will acknowledge their name before my Father and his angels.
v06: Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches.
</p>

## To the Church in Philadelphia

<p>
v07: “To the angel of the church in Philadelphia write: These are the words of the holy one, the true one, who has David's key, who opens and no one will shut, and shuts and no one opens:
v08: I know your works. Look, I have set before you an open door that no one can shut. You have little strength, yet you have kept my word and have not denied my name.
v09: Look, I will give you some from the synagogue of Satan, those who say they are Jews and are not, but lie. Look, I will make them come and bow before your feet and know that I have loved you.
v10: Because you have kept my word of endurance, I too will keep you from the hour of testing that is about to come on the whole inhabited world, to test those who live on the earth.
v11: I am coming soon. Hold firmly to what you have, so that no one takes your crown.
v12: I will make the one who overcomes a pillar in the temple of my God, and that person will never leave it again. I will write on them the name of my God and the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem that comes down from heaven from my God, and my new name.
v13: Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches.
</p>

## To the Church in Laodicea

<p>
v14: “To the angel of the church in Laodicea write: These are the words of the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of God's creation:
v15: I know your works: you are neither cold nor hot. I wish you were cold or hot!
v16: So because you are lukewarm, neither hot nor cold, I am about to vomit you out of my mouth.
v17: You say, ‘I am rich, I have become wealthy, and I need nothing,’ but you do not know that you are wretched, pitiable, poor, blind and naked.
v18: I counsel you to buy from me gold refined by fire so that you may be rich, white garments to wear so that the shame of your nakedness will not be exposed, and ointment to put on your eyes so that you may see.
v19: I rebuke and discipline everyone I love. So be zealous and repent.
v20: Look, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to that person and eat with them, and they with me.
v21: I will give the one who overcomes a seat with me on my throne, just as I overcame and sat down with my Father on his throne.
v22: Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches.”
</p>

## Notes

v09: The accusation repeats the localized opponent description in 2:9.

v10: “Keep you from” does not specify whether protection occurs through the testing or by removal from it. “My word of endurance” can refer to the call to endure or the endurance associated with Jesus.

v14: “Beginning” can indicate origin or source as well as the first in a sequence.

v16: The Greek verb means vomit; the bodily image is retained.

## Vocabulary

v14: **ἀρχή (archē)**: Beginning, origin or first principle.
