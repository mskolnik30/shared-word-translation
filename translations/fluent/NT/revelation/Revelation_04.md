## The Throne in Heaven

<p>
v01: After these things I looked, and there was an open door in heaven. The first voice I had heard, speaking to me like a trumpet, said, “Come up here, and I will show you what must happen.” After these things,
v02: immediately I was in the Spirit. And there was a throne standing in heaven, with someone seated on it.
v03: The one seated there looked like jasper and carnelian, and a rainbow around the throne looked like emerald.
v04: Around the throne were twenty-four thrones, and seated on them were twenty-four elders dressed in white garments, with golden crowns on their heads.
v05: From the throne came lightning, voices and thunder. Seven fiery lamps burned before the throne; these are the seven spirits of God.
v06: Before the throne was something like a sea of glass, like crystal. In the midst of the throne and around it were four living creatures, full of eyes in front and behind.
v07: The first living creature was like a lion, the second like a calf, the third had a face like a human face, and the fourth was like a flying eagle.
v08: Each of the four living creatures had six wings and was full of eyes all around and within. Day and night they never rest, saying:
“Holy, holy, holy,
the Lord God, the Almighty,
who was and who is and who is coming.”
v09: Whenever the living creatures give glory, honor and thanks to the one seated on the throne, who lives forever and ever,
v10: the twenty-four elders fall before the one seated on the throne and worship the one who lives forever and ever. They cast their crowns before the throne, saying:
v11: “You are worthy, our Lord and God,
to receive glory and honor and power,
because you created all things;
by your will they existed and were created.”
</p>

## Notes

v01–02: The pinned edition places a sentence break before the last “after these things” in verse 1, linking it with verse 2. Other punctuation attaches it to “what must happen.”

v06: The spatial expression says “in the midst of the throne and around the throne”; it is not replaced with a reconstructed arrangement.

v11: The pinned text reads “they existed and were created”; other witnesses vary the wording and order.

## Vocabulary

v07: **μόσχος (moschos)**: A calf or young bovine.
