## To the Church in Ephesus

<p>
v01: “To the angel of the church in Ephesus write: These are the words of the one who holds the seven stars in his right hand and walks among the seven golden lampstands:
v02: I know your works, your labor and your endurance. I know that you cannot tolerate evil people, and that you tested those who call themselves apostles but are not, and found them false.
v03: You have endurance and have borne up for my name, and you have not grown weary.
v04: But I have this against you: you have abandoned the love you had at first.
v05: Remember, then, from where you have fallen. Repent and do the works you did at first. Otherwise I will come to you and remove your lampstand from its place, unless you repent.
v06: Yet you have this in your favor: you hate the works of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate.
v07: Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes, I will give food from the tree of life in God's paradise.
</p>

## To the Church in Smyrna

<p>
v08: “To the angel of the church in Smyrna write: These are the words of the first and the last, who was dead and came to life:
v09: I know your suffering and poverty—but you are rich—and the slander from those who say they are Jews and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan.
v10: Do not fear what you are about to suffer. Look, the devil is about to throw some of you into prison to test you, and you will suffer for ten days. Be faithful until death, and I will give you the crown of life.
v11: Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The one who overcomes will never be harmed by the second death.
</p>

## To the Church in Pergamum

<p>
v12: “To the angel of the church in Pergamum write: These are the words of the one who has the sharp, double-edged sword:
v13: I know where you live, where Satan's throne is. You hold firmly to my name and did not deny faith in me even in the days of Antipas, my witness, my faithful one, who was killed among you, where Satan lives.
v14: But I have a few things against you: you have some there who hold to Balaam's teaching. He taught Balak to put a stumbling block before the children of Israel, to eat food sacrificed to idols and commit sexual immorality.
v15: In the same way, you also have some who hold to the teaching of the Nicolaitans.
v16: So repent. Otherwise I will come to you soon and wage war against them with the sword of my mouth.
v17: Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches. To the one who overcomes, I will give some of the hidden manna and a white stone, with a new name written on the stone that no one knows except the one who receives it.
</p>

## To the Church in Thyatira

<p>
v18: “To the angel of the church in Thyatira write: These are the words of the Son of God, whose eyes are like a flame of fire and whose feet are like burnished bronze:
v19: I know your works, your love, faith, service and endurance, and that your later works are greater than the first.
v20: But I have this against you: you tolerate the woman Jezebel, who calls herself a prophet and teaches and leads my slaves astray to commit sexual immorality and eat food sacrificed to idols.
v21: I gave her time to repent, but she does not want to repent of her sexual immorality.
v22: Look, I am throwing her onto a bed, and those who commit adultery with her into great suffering, unless they repent of her works.
v23: I will kill her children with death. Then all the churches will know that I am the one who searches kidneys and hearts, and I will give each of you according to your works.
v24: But to the rest of you in Thyatira, who do not hold this teaching and have not known what they call the deep things of Satan, I say: I put no other burden on you.
v25: Only hold firmly to what you have until I come.
v26: To the one who overcomes and keeps my works to the end, I will give authority over the nations,
v27: and that person will shepherd them with an iron rod,
as clay vessels are shattered,
v28: just as I too have received from my Father. And I will give that person the morning star.
v29: Let anyone with an ear hear what the Spirit says to the churches.
</p>

## Notes

v09: The charge concerns the particular opponents described in this address to Smyrna; the sentence does not name Jews generally as its subject.

v13: “Faith in me” can also be rendered “my faith” or “faithfulness to me.”

v20: The pinned text has “the woman Jezebel”; another reading has “your wife Jezebel.”

v22–23: The text says “bed,” without an explicit sickbed term. “Kill with death” may suggest death by plague. Children may denote descendants or adherents.

v23: Kidneys and hearts are bodily images for the inner life.

v27–28: The iron-rod language echoes Psalm 2:9. The pinned verse 28 begins “just as I too have received from my Father.”

## Vocabulary

v23: **νεφροί (nephroi)**: Kidneys, used with hearts for inward thought and feeling.
