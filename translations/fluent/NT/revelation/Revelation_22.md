## Notes

v01–02: The syntax permits different attachments of “in the middle of the street” and “on either side of the river.” The tree of life is singular while described on both sides.

v03–04: The throne is of God and of the Lamb, followed by singular his and him; these pronouns are not replaced with explanatory names.

v06: The pinned text reads “God of the spirits of the prophets”; other witnesses vary this expression.

v14: The pinned text reads “wash their robes”; another reading has “do his commandments.”

v19: The pinned text reads “tree of life”; some later texts have “book of life.”

v21: The pinned ending is “with all.” Other witnesses expand the recipients or add Amen; no final Amen is added here.

## Vocabulary

v03: **κατάθεμα (katathema)**: A curse or something accursed.

v17: **δωρεάν (dōrean)**: Freely, as a gift, without payment.
