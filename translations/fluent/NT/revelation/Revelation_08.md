## The Seventh Seal and the Golden Censer

<p>
v01: When he opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven for about half an hour.
v02: I saw the seven angels who stand before God, and seven trumpets were given to them.
v03: Another angel came and stood at the altar with a golden incense vessel. He was given much incense to offer with the prayers of all the holy ones on the golden altar before the throne.
v04: The smoke of the incense, with the prayers of the holy ones, rose from the angel's hand before God.
v05: The angel took the incense vessel, filled it with fire from the altar and threw it to the earth. There were thunder, voices, lightning and an earthquake.
</p>

## The Seven Trumpets

<p>
v06: The seven angels with the seven trumpets prepared to sound them.
v07: The first sounded his trumpet. Hail and fire mixed with blood came and were thrown to the earth. A third of the earth burned, a third of the trees burned, and all the green grass burned.
v08: The second angel sounded his trumpet. Something like a great mountain burning with fire was thrown into the sea, and a third of the sea became blood.
v09: A third of the living creatures in the sea died, and a third of the ships were destroyed.
v10: The third angel sounded his trumpet. A great star, burning like a torch, fell from heaven onto a third of the rivers and the springs of water.
v11: The star's name is Wormwood. A third of the waters became wormwood, and many people died from the waters because they had become bitter.
v12: The fourth angel sounded his trumpet. A third of the sun, a third of the moon and a third of the stars were struck, so that a third of them became dark. A third of the day had no light, and the night likewise.
v13: I looked and heard an eagle flying high overhead, calling in a loud voice, “Woe, woe, woe to those who live on the earth, because of the remaining trumpet blasts of the three angels who are about to sound them!”
</p>

## Notes

v07: The text says a third of the earth, a third of the trees, but all the green grass; the quantities are not harmonized.

v13: The pinned text reads “eagle”; another reading has “angel.”

## Vocabulary

v11: **ἄψινθος (apsinthos)**: Wormwood, a bitter plant.
