## The Scroll and the Lamb

<p>
v01: I saw in the right hand of the one seated on the throne a scroll written on the inside and the back, sealed with seven seals.
v02: I saw a mighty angel announcing in a loud voice, “Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?”
v03: No one in heaven or on the earth or under the earth could open the scroll or look into it.
v04: I wept greatly because no one was found worthy to open the scroll or look into it.
v05: Then one of the elders said to me, “Do not weep. Look, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has overcome, so that he can open the scroll and its seven seals.”
v06: I saw, in the midst of the throne and the four living creatures and among the elders, a Lamb standing as though slaughtered. He had seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven spirits of God sent into all the earth.
v07: He came and took the scroll from the right hand of the one seated on the throne.
v08: When he took the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell before the Lamb, each holding a harp and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the holy ones.
v09: They sing a new song:
“You are worthy to take the scroll
and open its seals,
because you were slaughtered
and by your blood bought for God
people from every tribe, language, people and nation.
v10: You made them a kingdom and priests to our God,
and they reign on the earth.”
v11: Then I looked and heard the voice of many angels around the throne, the living creatures and the elders. They numbered myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands,
v12: saying in a loud voice:
“Worthy is the Lamb who was slaughtered
to receive power and riches and wisdom and strength
and honor and glory and blessing!”
v13: I heard every creature in heaven, on the earth, under the earth and on the sea—everything in them—saying:
“To the one seated on the throne and to the Lamb
be blessing and honor and glory and power
forever and ever!”
v14: The four living creatures said, “Amen,” and the elders fell down and worshiped.
</p>

## Notes

v06: The Lamb stands while appearing slaughtered; both parts of the image remain.

v09–10: The pinned text does not say “bought us.” Verse 10 reads “them,” “kingdom,” and the present “they reign.” Other witnesses vary the pronouns and use a future reign.

## Vocabulary

v06: **σφάζω (sphazō)**: Slaughter; the same violent verb describes human victims elsewhere in the vision.
