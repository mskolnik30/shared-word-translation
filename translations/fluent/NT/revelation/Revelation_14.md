## The Lamb and the 144,000

<p>
v01: I looked, and there was the Lamb standing on Mount Zion, with 144,000 who had his name and his Father's name written on their foreheads.
v02: I heard a sound from heaven like the sound of many waters and like loud thunder. The sound I heard was like harpists playing their harps.
v03: They sing what is like a new song before the throne, the four living creatures and the elders. No one could learn the song except the 144,000 who had been bought from the earth.
v04: These are the ones who have not defiled themselves with women, for they are virgins. These are the ones who follow the Lamb wherever he goes. These were bought from humanity as firstfruits for God and the Lamb,
v05: and no lie was found in their mouths; they are blameless.
</p>

## The Messages of the Three Angels

<p>
v06: I saw another angel flying high overhead, with eternal good news to announce to those living on the earth—to every nation, tribe, language and people.
v07: He said in a loud voice, “Fear God and give him glory, because the hour of his judgment has come. Worship the one who made heaven, earth, sea and springs of water.”
v08: Another angel, a second, followed, saying, “Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great, who made all the nations drink the wine of the passion of her prostitution!”
v09: Another angel, a third, followed them, saying in a loud voice, “If anyone worships the beast and its image and receives a mark on their forehead or hand,
v10: that person too will drink the wine of God's fury, poured full strength into the cup of his wrath, and will be tormented with fire and sulfur before the holy angels and before the Lamb.
v11: The smoke of their torment rises forever and ever. Those who worship the beast and its image, and whoever receives the mark of its name, have no rest day or night.”
</p>

<p>
v12: Here is the endurance of the holy ones, those who keep God's commands and the faith of Jesus.
v13: I heard a voice from heaven say, “Write: Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on.” “Yes,” says the Spirit, “they will rest from their labors, for their works follow them.”
</p>

## The Harvest of the Earth

<p>
v14: I looked, and there was a white cloud. Seated on it was one like a son of man, with a golden crown on his head and a sharp sickle in his hand.
v15: Another angel came out of the temple and cried in a loud voice to the one seated on the cloud, “Put in your sickle and reap, because the hour to reap has come: the earth's harvest is ripe.”
v16: The one seated on the cloud swung his sickle over the earth, and the earth was reaped.
v17: Another angel came out of the temple in heaven, also holding a sharp sickle.
v18: Another angel came from the altar, the one with authority over fire, and called in a loud voice to the angel with the sharp sickle, “Put in your sharp sickle and gather the clusters from the earth's vine, because its grapes are ripe.”
v19: The angel swung his sickle to the earth, gathered the earth's vine and threw it into the great winepress of God's fury.
v20: The winepress was trodden outside the city, and blood flowed from it as high as horses' bridles for 1,600 stadia.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The pinned text includes “like” before “a new song.”

v04: The text describes male virgins not defiled with women. Readings differ over literal celibacy and symbolic fidelity; the image is not replaced with either interpretation.

v08: The word rendered passion can also mean fury or wrath, giving the wine image a further possible resonance.

v12: “Faith of Jesus” may indicate faith in Jesus or Jesus’ faithfulness.

v13: “From now on” may attach to dying in the Lord or to the ensuing rest.

## Vocabulary

v20: **στάδιον (stadion)**: A distance of roughly 185 meters; 1,600 stadia is roughly 300 kilometers or 185 miles.
