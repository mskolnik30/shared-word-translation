## The Two Witnesses

<p>
v01: A reed like a measuring rod was given to me, and I was told, “Get up and measure God's temple, the altar and those worshiping there.
v02: Leave out the courtyard outside the temple; do not measure it, because it has been given to the nations. They will trample the holy city for forty-two months.
v03: I will give authority to my two witnesses, and they will prophesy for 1,260 days, dressed in sackcloth.”
v04: These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands standing before the Lord of the earth.
v05: If anyone wants to harm them, fire comes from their mouths and devours their enemies. If anyone wants to harm them, that person must be killed in this way.
v06: They have authority to shut the sky so that no rain falls during the days of their prophecy. They also have authority over the waters to turn them to blood and to strike the earth with every kind of plague as often as they wish.
v07: When they finish their testimony, the beast that rises from the abyss will wage war against them, overcome them and kill them.
v08: Their bodies will lie in the street of the great city, spiritually called Sodom and Egypt, where their Lord was also crucified.
v09: People from the peoples, tribes, languages and nations look at their bodies for three and a half days and do not allow them to be placed in a tomb.
v10: Those who live on the earth rejoice over them and celebrate, and they will send gifts to one another because these two prophets tormented those who live on the earth.
v11: After the three and a half days, the breath of life from God entered them, and they stood on their feet. Great fear fell on those watching them.
v12: They heard a loud voice from heaven saying to them, “Come up here!” They went up to heaven in the cloud, and their enemies watched them.
v13: At that hour there was a great earthquake. A tenth of the city fell, and seven thousand people were killed in the earthquake. The rest were terrified and gave glory to the God of heaven.
v14: The second woe has passed. Look, the third woe is coming soon.
</p>

## The Seventh Trumpet

<p>
v15: The seventh angel sounded his trumpet, and loud voices in heaven said:
“The kingdom of the world has become
the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ,
and he will reign forever and ever.”
v16: The twenty-four elders seated on their thrones before God fell on their faces and worshiped God,
v17: saying:
“We thank you, Lord God, the Almighty,
who is and who was,
because you have taken your great power
and begun to reign.
v18: The nations were angry,
and your wrath has come,
and the time for the dead to be judged,
to reward your slaves the prophets,
the holy ones and those who fear your name, small and great,
and to destroy those who destroy the earth.”
v19: God's temple in heaven opened, and the ark of his covenant was seen in his temple. There were lightning, voices, thunder, an earthquake and great hail.
</p>

## Notes

v03: The object of “I will give” is not expressed; authority is supplied from the context.

v08–09: The Greek alternates a collective singular “body” with plural “bodies.” The English uses bodies for the two witnesses. “Spiritually called” introduces the names Sodom and Egypt.

v12: The pinned text reads “they heard”; another reading has “I heard.”

v17: The pinned acclamation has “who is and who was,” without the “who is coming” used earlier.

## Vocabulary

v11: **πνεῦμα ζωῆς (pneuma zōēs)**: Breath or spirit of life.
