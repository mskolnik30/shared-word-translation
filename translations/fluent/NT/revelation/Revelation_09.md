## The Fifth Trumpet

<p>
v01: The fifth angel sounded his trumpet. I saw a star fallen from heaven to the earth, and the key to the shaft of the abyss was given to him.
v02: He opened the shaft of the abyss, and smoke rose from it like smoke from a great furnace. The smoke from the shaft darkened the sun and the air.
v03: Locusts came out of the smoke onto the earth. They were given power like the power of the earth's scorpions.
v04: They were told not to harm the earth's grass, any green growth or any tree, but only people who did not have God's seal on their foreheads.
v05: They were allowed to torment them for five months, but not to kill them. The torment was like that of a scorpion when it strikes a person.
v06: In those days people will seek death and will not find it. They will long to die, but death flees from them.
v07: The locusts looked like horses prepared for battle. On their heads were what looked like crowns of gold, and their faces were like human faces.
v08: They had hair like women's hair, and their teeth were like lions' teeth.
v09: They had breastplates like iron breastplates, and the sound of their wings was like the sound of chariots with many horses rushing into battle.
v10: They have tails like scorpions, with stingers, and in their tails is their power to harm people for five months.
v11: They have as king over them the angel of the abyss. His name in Hebrew is Abaddon, and in Greek he has the name Apollyon.
v12: The first woe has passed. Look, two more woes are coming after these things.
</p>

## The Sixth Trumpet

<p>
v13: The sixth angel sounded his trumpet. I heard one voice from the horns of the golden altar before God,
v14: saying to the sixth angel with the trumpet, “Release the four angels bound at the great river Euphrates.”
v15: The four angels, prepared for that hour and day and month and year, were released to kill a third of humanity.
v16: The number of the mounted troops was two hundred million; I heard their number.
v17: This is how I saw the horses and their riders in the vision: they had breastplates of fiery red, dark blue and sulfur yellow. The horses' heads were like lions' heads, and fire, smoke and sulfur came out of their mouths.
v18: A third of humanity was killed by these three plagues: the fire, smoke and sulfur that came out of their mouths.
v19: For the horses' power is in their mouths and their tails. Their tails are like snakes with heads, and with them they harm people.
v20: The rest of humanity, who were not killed by these plagues, still did not repent of the works of their hands or stop worshiping demons and idols of gold, silver, bronze, stone and wood, which cannot see or hear or walk.
v21: They did not repent of their murders, their sorceries, their sexual immorality or their thefts.
</p>

## Notes

v13: The pinned text mentions the altar’s horns without the added “four” found in some witnesses.

v16: The Greek number is twice ten thousand times ten thousand: two hundred million.

## Vocabulary

v11: **Abaddon / Apollyon**: The Hebrew name evokes destruction; the Greek name means destroyer.

v21: **φάρμακα (pharmaka)**: Potions or magical preparations, here sorceries; not a general designation for medicine.
