## The Fall of Babylon

<p>
v01: After these things I saw another angel coming down from heaven with great authority, and the earth was lit by his glory.
v02: He cried out in a mighty voice:
“Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great!
She has become a dwelling for demons,
a haunt of every unclean spirit,
a haunt of every unclean bird,
and a haunt of every unclean and hated beast.
v03: For all the nations have fallen through the wine of the passion of her prostitution. The kings of the earth committed sexual immorality with her, and the merchants of the earth grew rich from the power of her luxury.”
v04: I heard another voice from heaven say:
“Come out of her, my people,
so that you do not share in her sins
and do not receive her plagues,
v05: because her sins have piled up to heaven,
and God has remembered her wrongs.
v06: Repay her as she herself repaid;
give her double according to her works.
In the cup she mixed, mix a double portion for her.
v07: As much as she glorified herself and lived in luxury,
give her that much torment and grief.
For she says in her heart,
‘I sit as a queen; I am no widow,
and I will never see grief.’
v08: That is why her plagues will come in a single day:
death and grief and famine,
and she will be burned with fire,
for mighty is the Lord God who has judged her.
v09: The kings of the earth who committed sexual immorality and lived in luxury with her will weep and mourn over her when they see the smoke of her burning.
v10: They will stand far away in fear of her torment, saying:
‘Woe, woe, the great city,
Babylon, the mighty city!
In a single hour your judgment has come.’
v11: The merchants of the earth weep and grieve over her because no one buys their cargo anymore:
v12: cargo of gold, silver, precious stones and pearls; fine linen, purple cloth, silk and scarlet cloth; every kind of fragrant wood, every ivory object, and every object of costly wood, bronze, iron and marble;
v13: cinnamon and spice, incense, perfume and frankincense; wine, oil, fine flour and wheat; cattle and sheep; horses and carriages; and bodies—human lives.
v14: The fruit your soul longed for has gone from you. All your luxury and splendor have vanished from you, and they will never find them again.
v15: The merchants of these things, who grew rich from her, will stand far away in fear of her torment, weeping and grieving,
v16: saying:
‘Woe, woe, the great city,
clothed in fine linen, purple and scarlet,
adorned with gold, precious stones and pearls!
v17: In a single hour so much wealth has been laid waste!’ Every ship's captain, everyone traveling by sea, sailors and all who earn their living from the sea stood far away
v18: and cried out as they saw the smoke of her burning, ‘What city was like the great city?’
v19: They threw dust on their heads and cried out, weeping and grieving:
‘Woe, woe, the great city,
where everyone with ships at sea grew rich from her wealth!
In a single hour she has been laid waste.’
v20: Rejoice over her, heaven,
and you holy ones, apostles and prophets,
because God has judged your case against her.”
v21: A mighty angel picked up a stone like a great millstone and threw it into the sea, saying:
“With such violence Babylon the great city will be thrown down
and will never be found again.
v22: The sound of harpists and musicians, flute players and trumpeters
will never be heard in you again.
No worker of any craft will ever be found in you again.
The sound of a mill will never be heard in you again.
v23: The light of a lamp will never shine in you again.
The voice of bridegroom and bride will never be heard in you again,
because your merchants were the great ones of the earth,
and all the nations were led astray by your sorcery.
v24: In her was found the blood of prophets and holy ones,
and of all who have been slaughtered on the earth.”
</p>

## Notes

v02: The pinned text includes the final clause about unclean and hated beasts; some witnesses omit it.

v03: The pinned reading is “have fallen”; another widespread reading is “have drunk.”

v13: The Greek ends the cargo list with “bodies, and human souls/lives,” language that exposes human beings traded as slaves.

v14: The pinned final verb is third-person plural, “they will not find,” amid the direct address to the city.

v20: The compressed judgment phrase may mean that God has judged their case against the city or imposed on her the judgment she inflicted on them.

## Vocabulary

v12: **θύϊνος (thyinos)**: A fragrant, costly wood.

v13: **ἄμωμον (amōmon)**: An aromatic spice; its precise botanical identity is uncertain.
