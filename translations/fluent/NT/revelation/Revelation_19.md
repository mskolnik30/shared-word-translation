## Rejoicing in Heaven

<p>
v01: After these things I heard what sounded like the loud voice of a great crowd in heaven, saying:
“Hallelujah!
Salvation and glory and power belong to our God,
v02: because his judgments are true and righteous.
He has judged the great prostitute
who corrupted the earth with her prostitution,
and avenged his slaves' blood at her hand.”
v03: A second time they said, “Hallelujah! Her smoke rises forever and ever.”
v04: The twenty-four elders and the four living creatures fell down and worshiped God seated on the throne, saying, “Amen! Hallelujah!”
v05: A voice came from the throne:
“Praise our God, all his slaves,
and you who fear him, small and great.”
v06: I heard what sounded like a great crowd, like many waters and like mighty thunder, saying:
“Hallelujah!
For the Lord God, the Almighty, reigns.
v07: Let us rejoice and be glad,
and we will give him glory,
because the Lamb's wedding has come,
and his wife has prepared herself.
v08: She was given fine linen to wear, shining and clean.” The fine linen is the righteous deeds of the holy ones.
v09: He said to me, “Write: Blessed are those invited to the Lamb's wedding feast.” Then he said, “These are the true words of God.”
v10: I fell at his feet to worship him, but he said, “Do not do that! I am a fellow slave with you and your brothers and sisters who hold the testimony of Jesus. Worship God! For the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.”
v11: I saw heaven opened, and there was a white horse! Its rider is called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he judges and wages war.
v12: His eyes are a flame of fire, and on his head are many royal crowns. He has a name written that no one knows except himself.
v13: He is dressed in a robe dipped in blood, and his name is called the Word of God.
v14: The armies in heaven followed him on white horses, dressed in fine linen, white and clean.
v15: From his mouth comes a sharp sword with which to strike the nations. He will shepherd them with an iron rod, and he treads the winepress of the wine of the furious wrath of God the Almighty.
v16: On his robe and on his thigh he has a name written: “King of kings and Lord of lords.”
v17: I saw an angel standing in the sun. He cried in a loud voice to all the birds flying high overhead, “Come, gather for the great feast of God,
v18: to eat the flesh of kings, the flesh of military commanders, the flesh of the powerful, the flesh of horses and their riders, and the flesh of everyone, free and slave, small and great.”
v19: I saw the beast, the kings of the earth and their armies gathered to wage war against the rider on the horse and his army.
v20: The beast was captured, and with it the false prophet who had performed signs in its presence, deceiving those who received the beast's mark and worshiped its image. The two were thrown alive into the lake of fire burning with sulfur.
v21: The rest were killed by the sword that came from the horseman's mouth, and all the birds gorged themselves on their flesh.
</p>

## Notes

v07: The pinned text has the future “we will give”; other readings continue the exhortation “let us give.”

v10: “Testimony of Jesus” can mean testimony from Jesus or testimony about him. The relation to “spirit of prophecy” is not expanded into a definition.

v13: The pinned text reads “dipped in blood”; another reading has “sprinkled with blood.” The source of the blood is not named.

## Vocabulary

v01: **Ἁλληλουϊά (Hallēlouia)**: A Hebrew expression meaning praise the LORD.
