## Unity and Humility

<p>
v01: So if there is any encouragement in Christ, any comfort from love, any sharing in the Spirit, any tenderness and compassion,
v02: make my joy complete: think in harmony, share the same love, be united in soul and of one mind.
v03: Do nothing out of selfish ambition or empty pride. Instead, in humility regard others as more important than yourselves.
v04: Each of you should look beyond your own interests to the interests of others as well.
</p>

## The Mind of Christ

<p>
v05: Let this be your way of thinking together, the way of Christ Jesus:
</p>

<p>
v06: Though he was in the form of God,
he did not regard equality with God
as something to exploit.
v07: Instead, he emptied himself,
taking the form of a slave,
becoming like human beings.
And being found in human form,
v08: he humbled himself
and became obedient to the point of death—
even death on a cross.
</p>

<p>
v09: That is why God raised him to the highest place
and gave him the name
above every name,
v10: so that at the name of Jesus
every knee should bend—
in heaven, on earth and beneath the earth—
v11: and every tongue confess
that Jesus Christ is Lord,
to the glory of God the Father.
</p>

## Work Out What God Works In

<p>
v12: So then, my beloved, just as you have always obeyed, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling—not only when I am present, but much more now while I am absent.
v13: For God is at work in you—in your willing as well as your doing—for his good pleasure.
v14: Do everything without grumbling or arguing,
v15: so that you may be blameless and innocent, God's children without blemish in the midst of a crooked and distorted generation. Among them you shine like lights in the world,
v16: holding firmly to the word of life. Then I can boast on the day of Christ that I did not run in vain or labor in vain.
</p>

## Shared Joy in Sacrifice

<p>
v17: But even if I am being poured out as a drink offering over the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice and share my joy with all of you.
v18: You too should rejoice in the same way and share your joy with me.
</p>

## Timothy

<p>
v19: I hope in the Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you soon, so that I too may be encouraged when I learn how you are.
v20: I have no one else of his spirit who will take a sincere interest in your welfare.
v21: For all the others seek their own interests, not those of Jesus Christ.
v22: But you know Timothy's proven character. Like a child with a father, he has served with me as a slave for the good news.
v23: So I hope to send him as soon as I see how things turn out for me.
v24: And I am confident in the Lord that I myself will come soon.
</p>

## Epaphroditus

<p>
v25: Still, I thought it necessary to send you Epaphroditus—my brother, fellow worker and fellow soldier, and your messenger and minister to my needs.
v26: He has been longing for all of you and was distressed because you heard that he was ill.
v27: Indeed, he was ill and nearly died. But God had mercy on him, and not only on him but also on me, sparing me sorrow upon sorrow.
v28: So I am all the more eager to send him, so that seeing him again may bring you joy and lessen my sorrow.
v29: Welcome him in the Lord with all joy, and hold people like him in honor,
v30: because he came close to death for Christ's work, risking his life to provide the service you could not give me.
</p>

## Notes

v05: The abbreviated Greek can describe Christ’s way of thinking or the mindset that is yours in Christ.

v06: ‘Something to exploit’ can also be understood as ‘something to grasp or seize.’ The main text retains ‘form of God’ and ‘equality with God.’

v07: The text says ‘emptied himself’ without naming something he emptied himself of. The following phrases describe taking a slave’s form and becoming human.

v12–13: The commands and ‘you’ are plural, addressed to the community. ‘In you’ can also mean ‘among you.’

v16: ‘Holding firmly to’ can also mean ‘holding out.’

v25: ‘Messenger’ renders apostolos, someone sent or commissioned; here he is sent by the Philippians.

## Vocabulary

v05: **φρονέω (phroneō)**: Think, adopt a mindset or set one’s mind; a recurring verb in the letter.

v06–07: **μορφή (morphē)**: Form; used of both God and a slave.

v17: **σπένδομαι (spendomai)**: Be poured out as a drink offering.
