## Rejoice in the Lord

<p>
v01: Finally, my brothers and sisters, rejoice in the Lord. Writing the same things to you is no burden to me, and it is a safeguard for you.
</p>

## Warning Against Confidence in the Flesh

<p>
v02: Watch out for the dogs! Watch out for the evil workers! Watch out for those who mutilate the flesh!
v03: For we are the circumcision: we worship by God's Spirit, boast in Christ Jesus and put no confidence in the flesh,
v04: though I too have grounds for confidence in the flesh. If anyone else thinks they have such grounds, I have more:
</p>

<p>
v05: circumcised on the eighth day, of the people of Israel, the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew born of Hebrews; as to the law, a Pharisee;
v06: as to zeal, a persecutor of the church; as to righteousness under the law, blameless.
</p>

## Loss and Gain in Christ

<p>
v07: But whatever counted as gains for me, I have counted as loss because of Christ.
v08: More than that, I count everything as loss compared with the surpassing worth of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord. For him I have lost everything, and I count it as refuse so that I may gain Christ
v09: and be found in him, having no righteousness of my own from the law, but the righteousness that comes through Christ's faithfulness, the righteousness from God on the basis of faith.
</p>

<p>
v10: I want to know him, the power of his resurrection and the sharing of his sufferings, being shaped into the likeness of his death,
v11: if somehow I may reach the resurrection from the dead.
</p>

## Pressing On Toward the Goal

<p>
v12: Not that I have already obtained this or have already been made perfect. But I press on to take hold of it, because Christ has taken hold of me.
v13: Brothers and sisters, I do not consider that I have taken hold of it. But one thing I do: forgetting what lies behind and stretching toward what lies ahead,
v14: I press on toward the goal, for the prize of God's upward call in Christ Jesus.
</p>

<p>
v15: Let all of us who are mature think this way. And if you think differently about anything, God will reveal that to you too.
v16: Only, let us keep in step with what we have already attained.
</p>

## Our Citizenship Is in Heaven

<p>
v17: Join in following my example, brothers and sisters, and pay attention to those who live by the pattern you have in us.
v18: For many live as enemies of Christ's cross. I have often told you about them, and now I tell you even with tears.
v19: Their end is destruction, their god is their belly, and their glory is in their shame. Their minds are set on earthly things.
</p>

<p>
v20: But our citizenship is in heaven, and from there we eagerly await a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ.
v21: He will transform our lowly body to be like his glorious body, by the power that enables him to bring all things under his rule.
</p>

## Notes

v02–03: ‘Mutilation’ and ‘circumcision’ form a Greek wordplay. The text does not name the targets of the harsh warning more precisely here.

v08: The word rendered ‘refuse’ can refer to rubbish, discarded scraps or excrement. It is strongly dismissive.

v09: The Greek phrase can mean ‘Christ’s faithfulness’ or ‘faith in Christ.’ The following phrase separately names faith as the basis of righteousness from God.

v12: ‘Because Christ has taken hold of me’ can also be understood in terms of purpose: ‘for which Christ took hold of me.’ The pinned text names Christ without adding Jesus here.

v12, 15: ‘Made perfect’ and ‘mature’ translate forms of the same word-family.

v16: The pinned Greek has the shorter command without the additional phrases about a rule and a shared mindset found in some witnesses.

## Vocabulary

v07: **ζημία (zēmia)**: Loss, including an accounting loss; contrasted with gain.

v12: **καταλαμβάνω (katalambanō)**: Take hold of, grasp or attain.

v20: **πολίτευμα (politeuma)**: Citizenship or commonwealth.
