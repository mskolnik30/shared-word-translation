## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the command of God our Savior and Christ Jesus our hope,
v02: to Timothy, my true child in faith: Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.
</p>

## Charge Certain People Not to Teach Differently

<p>
v03: As I urged you when I was leaving for Macedonia, remain in Ephesus so that you may command certain people not to teach anything different
v04: or devote themselves to myths and endless genealogies. These promote speculations rather than God's stewardship, which operates by faith.
v05: The goal of this command is love from a pure heart, a good conscience and sincere faith.
v06: Some have missed these things and turned aside to empty talk.
v07: They want to be teachers of the law, though they understand neither what they say nor what they confidently assert.
</p>

## The Law Is Good When Used Lawfully

<p>
v08: But we know that the law is good if someone uses it lawfully.
v09: We know this: law is not laid down for a righteous person, but for the lawless and rebellious, the ungodly and sinners, the unholy and profane, those who kill fathers or mothers, murderers,
v10: the sexually immoral, men who have sex with men, slave traders, liars, perjurers and whatever else opposes sound teaching,
v11: in accordance with the glorious good news of the blessed God, which was entrusted to me.
</p>

## Christ Jesus Came to Save Sinners

<p>
v12: I am grateful to Christ Jesus our Lord, who strengthened me, because he considered me faithful and appointed me to service,
v13: even though I was formerly a blasphemer, persecutor and violent man. But I received mercy because I acted ignorantly in unbelief,
v14: and our Lord's grace overflowed with the faith and love that are in Christ Jesus.
v15: This saying is trustworthy and deserves full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am foremost.
v16: But I received mercy for this reason: so that in me, the foremost, Christ Jesus might display his complete patience as an example for those who would believe in him for eternal life.
v17: Now to the King of the ages, immortal, invisible, the only God, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.
</p>

## Fight the Good Fight

<p>
v18: I entrust this command to you, my child Timothy, in keeping with the prophecies previously made about you, so that by them you may fight the good fight,
v19: holding on to faith and a good conscience. Some have rejected these and shipwrecked their faith.
v20: Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I handed over to Satan so that they might be taught not to blaspheme.
</p>

## Notes

v03–07: The warning concerns speculative teaching and misuse of the law; the opponents are not named more precisely.
