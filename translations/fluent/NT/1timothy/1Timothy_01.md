## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the command of God our Savior and Christ Jesus our hope,
v02: to Timothy, my true child in the faith: Grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.
</p>

## Charge Certain People Not to Teach Differently

<p>
v03: Just as I urged you when I was leaving for Macedonia, remain in Ephesus so that you may command certain people not to teach different doctrine
v04: or devote themselves to myths and endless genealogies, which promote speculation rather than God’s stewardship that is by trust.
v05: The goal of the command is love from a pure heart, a good conscience, and sincere trust.
v06: Some have wandered away from these things and turned aside to empty talk,
v07: wanting to be teachers of the Law, though they understand neither what they are saying nor the things they confidently insist on.
</p>

## The Law Is Good When Used Lawfully

<p>
v08: But we know that the Law is good if someone uses it lawfully,
v09: knowing this: law is not laid down for a righteous person but for the lawless and rebellious, the ungodly and sinners, the unholy and profane, those who strike fathers and mothers, murderers,
v10: sexually immoral people, men who have sex with men, enslavers, liars, perjurers, and whatever else is opposed to sound teaching,
v11: according to the glorious good news of the blessed God, with which I was entrusted.
</p>

## Christ Jesus Came to Save Sinners

<p>
v12: I thank Christ Jesus our Lord, who strengthened me, because he considered me trustworthy and appointed me to service,
v13: though I formerly was a blasphemer, persecutor, and violent man. But I received mercy because I acted ignorantly in unbelief,
v14: and the grace of our Lord overflowed with the trust and love that are in Christ Jesus.
v15: This saying is trustworthy and worthy of full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am foremost.
v16: But for this reason I received mercy: so that in me, the foremost, Christ Jesus might display all his patience as an example for those who were going to trust in him for eternal life.
v17: Now to the King of the ages, immortal, invisible, the only God, be honor and glory forever and ever. Amen.
</p>

## Fight the Good Fight

<p>
v18: Timothy, my child, I entrust this command to you according to the prophecies previously made about you, so that by them you may fight the good fight,
v19: holding on to trust and a good conscience. Some have rejected these and suffered shipwreck concerning the faith,
v20: among whom are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I handed over to Satan so that they may learn not to blaspheme.
</p>

## Notes

v03: “Different doctrine” is defined by its deviation from the apostolic gospel and its fruit, not simply by novelty.

v04: The exact myths and genealogies are not identified. Fluent does not reconstruct a full false-teaching system beyond what the letter states.

v05: The command’s aim is positive: love flowing from purity, conscience, and sincere trust.

v08: Paul explicitly calls the Law good when used lawfully, preventing his critique of certain teachers from becoming rejection of Torah itself.

v10: `arsenokoitai` is a rare compound probably referring to male same-sex sexual activity and echoing Levitical wording. Fluent uses “men who have sex with men” rather than a modern identity label.

v10: `andrapodistai` refers to slave-dealers or kidnappers who enslave people. “Enslavers” preserves the violent economic reality.

v13–16: Paul’s own past violence becomes the showcase for mercy and patience, not a reason for self-exaltation.

v15: “Foremost” is Paul’s present self-description within a confessional statement, not a measurable ranking of all sinners.

v20: “Handed over to Satan” is severe disciplinary language. The stated aim is remedial—“so that they may learn”—not simple vengeance.
