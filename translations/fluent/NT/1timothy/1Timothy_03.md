## Overseers

<p>
v01: This saying is trustworthy: If anyone aspires to oversight, that person desires a good work.
v02: Therefore, an overseer must be above reproach, a one-woman man, sober, self-controlled, respectable, hospitable and able to teach;
v03: not addicted to wine, not violent, but gentle, peaceable and not loving money;
v04: managing his own household well and keeping his children submissive with complete dignity.
v05: For if someone does not know how to manage his own household, how will he care for God's church?
v06: He must not be a recent convert, or he may become conceited and fall into the devil's condemnation.
v07: He must also have a good reputation with outsiders, so that he does not fall into disgrace and the devil's trap.
</p>

## Deacons

<p>
v08: Ministers likewise must be dignified, not double-tongued, not devoted to much wine and not greedy for dishonest gain.
v09: They must hold the mystery of the faith with a clean conscience.
v10: They too must first be tested; then, if they are beyond accusation, let them serve.
v11: Women likewise must be dignified, not slanderers, sober and faithful in everything.
v12: Let ministers be one-woman men, managing their children and their own households well.
v13: For those who serve well gain a good standing for themselves and great confidence in the faith that is in Christ Jesus.
</p>

## The Mystery of Godliness

<p>
v14: I am writing these things to you, hoping to come to you soon.
v15: But if I am delayed, you will know how people ought to conduct themselves in God's household, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and foundation of the truth.
v16: And undeniably great is the mystery of godliness: He was revealed in flesh, vindicated in spirit, seen by angels, proclaimed among the nations, believed in the world and taken up in glory.
</p>

## Notes

v02, 12: The phrases literally describe a ‘one-woman man’; their implications for marital history and office qualifications require review.

## Vocabulary
