## Some Will Depart from the Faith

<p>
v01: The Spirit explicitly says that in later times some will depart from the faith, paying attention to deceiving spirits and teachings of demons,
v02: through the hypocrisy of liars whose own conscience has been seared,
v03: who forbid marriage and demand abstinence from foods God created to be received with thanksgiving by those who trust and know the truth.
v04: For everything God created is good, and nothing is to be rejected if it is received with thanksgiving,
v05: because it is made holy through God’s word and prayer.
</p>

## Train Yourself for Godliness

<p>
v06: If you place these things before the brothers and sisters, you will be a good servant of Christ Jesus, nourished by the words of the faith and the good teaching you have followed.
v07: Reject godless myths fit for old wives. Instead, train yourself for godliness.
v08: Physical training is useful for a little, but godliness is useful in every way, because it holds promise for the present life and the life to come.
v09: This saying is trustworthy and worthy of full acceptance.
v10: For this reason we labor and struggle, because we have set our hope on the living God, who is Savior of all people, especially of those who trust.
</p>

## Be an Example

<p>
v11: Command and teach these things.
v12: Let no one despise your youth. Instead, become an example for the believers in speech, conduct, love, trust, and purity.
v13: Until I come, devote yourself to the public reading of Scripture, to encouragement, and to teaching.
v14: Do not neglect the gift in you, which was given to you through prophecy with the laying on of hands by the council of elders.
v15: Practice these things; give yourself to them so that your progress may be evident to everyone.
v16: Pay close attention to yourself and to the teaching. Continue in these things, because by doing so you will save both yourself and those who hear you.
</p>

## Notes

v01: The warning concerns departure from “the faith,” not merely intellectual error; deceptive spiritual claims and teaching are intertwined.

v03–05: The false teaching rejects created goods that Paul says God made to be received with thanksgiving. Creation goodness and gratitude are the answer to ascetic prohibition.

v07: The phrase “myths fit for old wives” reflects a dismissive ancient stereotype. Fluent translates the expression but does not endorse contempt for older women.

v08: Paul does not call bodily training worthless; he says its usefulness is limited compared with godliness’ present and future promise.

v10: “Savior of all people, especially of those who trust” is intentionally broad and difficult. Fluent preserves the wording rather than narrowing “all” or redefining “especially” into “only.”

v12: Timothy’s authority is to be carried through exemplary life, not demanded on the basis of position alone.

v13: Public Scripture reading, encouragement, and teaching form a threefold ministry pattern.

v14: Gift, prophecy, and elders’ laying on of hands all appear in one commissioning memory.

v16: “Save yourself and those who hear you” uses ministerial causation without making Timothy the ultimate Savior; the letter already names God and Christ as Savior.
