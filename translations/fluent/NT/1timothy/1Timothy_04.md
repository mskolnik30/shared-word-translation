## Some Will Depart from the Faith

<p>
v01: Now the Spirit explicitly says that in later times some will abandon the faith and pay attention to deceiving spirits and teachings of demons,
v02: through the hypocrisy of liars whose own consciences have been seared.
v03: They forbid marriage and demand abstinence from foods that God created to be received with thanksgiving by those who believe and know the truth.
v04: For everything God created is good, and nothing is to be rejected if received with thanksgiving,
v05: because it is made holy through God's word and prayer.
</p>

## Train Yourself for Godliness

<p>
v06: By setting these things before the brothers and sisters, you will be a good minister of Christ Jesus, nourished by the words of faith and the good teaching you have followed.
v07: Reject profane tales fit for old women, and train yourself in godliness.
v08: For bodily training is beneficial to a limited extent, but godliness is beneficial in every way, since it holds promise for the present life and the life to come.
v09: This saying is trustworthy and deserves full acceptance.
v10: For this we labor and struggle, because we have put our hope in the living God, who is Savior of all people, especially of those who believe.
</p>

## Be an Example

<p>
v11: Command and teach these things.
v12: Let no one despise your youth. Instead, become an example for the believers in speech, conduct, love, faith and purity.
v13: Until I come, devote yourself to the public reading of Scripture, to encouragement and to teaching.
v14: Do not neglect the gift in you, which was given through prophecy with the laying on of the elders' hands.
v15: Practice these things and immerse yourself in them, so that your progress may be evident to everyone.
v16: Pay close attention to yourself and to the teaching. Persevere in them, for by doing this you will save both yourself and those who hear you.
</p>

## Notes

v10: The statement calls God Savior of all people, especially of believers; ‘especially’ is retained rather than explained.
