## Prayer for Everyone

<p>
v01: First of all, then, I urge that petitions, prayers, intercessions and thanksgivings be made for all people—
v02: for kings and all who are in high positions, so that we may lead a calm and quiet life in all godliness and dignity.
v03: This is good and acceptable before God our Savior,
v04: who desires all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.
v05: For there is one God and one mediator between God and humanity, the human Christ Jesus,
v06: who gave himself as a ransom for all, the testimony given at the proper time.
v07: For this testimony I was appointed a herald and apostle—I am telling the truth; I am not lying—a teacher of the nations in faith and truth.
</p>

## Prayer and Conduct in the Assembly

<p>
v08: Therefore, I want the men in every place to pray, lifting holy hands without anger or arguing.
v09: Likewise, women should adorn themselves in respectable clothing, with modesty and self-control, not with braided hair and gold or pearls or expensive clothes,
v10: but with good works, as is fitting for women who profess reverence for God.
v11: Let a woman learn quietly with full submission.
v12: I do not permit a woman to teach or exercise authority over a man; she is to remain quiet.
v13: For Adam was formed first, then Eve.
v14: And Adam was not deceived, but the woman, having been deceived, came into transgression.
v15: But she will be saved through childbearing, if they continue in faith, love and holiness with self-control.
</p>

## Notes

v11–12: The instructions name a woman and a man specifically. The rare verb authentein can denote exercising authority or domineering; its force and the scope of the prohibition require independent review.

v15: The claim about being saved through childbearing is difficult; the following condition is plural and names faith, love, holiness and self-control.

## Vocabulary

v12: **αὐθεντέω (authenteō)**: A rare verb meaning exercise authority, assume authority or domineer; interpretation is disputed.
