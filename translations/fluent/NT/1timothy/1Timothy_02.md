## Prayer for Everyone

<p>
v01: First of all, then, I urge that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all people,
v02: for kings and everyone in positions of authority, so that we may live a quiet and peaceful life in all godliness and dignity.
v03: This is good and acceptable before God our Savior,
v04: who wants all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.
v05: For there is one God and one mediator between God and humanity, the human Christ Jesus,
v06: who gave himself as a ransom for all, the testimony given at its proper time.
v07: For this I was appointed a herald and apostle—I am telling the truth; I am not lying—a teacher of the Gentiles in trust and truth.
</p>

## Prayer and Conduct in the Assembly

<p>
v08: Therefore I want the men in every place to pray, lifting holy hands without anger or argument.
v09: Likewise, women should adorn themselves in respectable clothing, with modesty and self-control, not with elaborate hairstyles, gold, pearls, or expensive clothing,
v10: but with good works, as is fitting for women who profess reverence for God.
v11: Let a woman learn in quietness with full receptiveness.
v12: I am not permitting a woman to teach or to exercise authority over a man; rather, she is to be in quietness.
v13: For Adam was formed first, then Eve,
v14: and Adam was not deceived, but the woman, being deceived, came into transgression.
v15: Yet she will be saved through childbearing, if they remain in trust, love, and holiness with self-control.
</p>

## Notes

v01–02: Prayer for rulers is ordered toward a peaceful social environment for faithful life, not uncritical endorsement of rulers.

v04: God’s saving desire is stated universally. Fluent preserves “all people” without narrowing the scope.

v05: Jesus is explicitly called human in the mediator formula, affirming rather than diminishing his saving role.

v06: `antilytron` is ransom or corresponding payment; Fluent retains “ransom” rather than explaining one exclusive atonement theory.

v09–10: The contrast is between status-displaying adornment and a life adorned with good works, not a blanket prohibition of every braid or piece of jewelry in all contexts.

v11: The command begins positively: “Let a woman learn.” `hēsychia` means quietness or settledness rather than absolute silence.

v12: This verse is one of the most debated in the letter. `authentein` is rare and can mean exercise authority, dominate, or act independently. Fluent uses “exercise authority” in the main text while preserving the lexical debate here. The statement is not expanded into a universal theory beyond the verse.

v13–14: Paul grounds the instruction in his reading of Genesis. Fluent preserves the argument without adding claims Genesis itself does not explicitly make.

v15: “Saved through childbearing” is highly disputed. Possibilities include preservation through childbirth, vocation in ordinary family life, or reference to the birth of the Messiah. The plural “if they remain” shifts from “she” to a broader group. Fluent does not resolve the theology by paraphrase.
