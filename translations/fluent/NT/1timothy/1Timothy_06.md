## Slaves and the Name of God

<p>
v01: All who are slaves under a yoke must regard their own masters as worthy of full honor, so that God's name and the teaching may not be slandered.
v02: Those who have believing masters must not disrespect them because they are brothers; instead, they must serve as slaves all the more, because those who benefit are believers and beloved. Teach and urge these things.
</p>

## Godliness with Contentment

<p>
v03: If anyone teaches something different and does not agree with the sound words of our Lord Jesus Christ and the teaching that accords with godliness,
v04: that person is conceited and understands nothing, but has a sick craving for controversies and verbal disputes. From these come envy, strife, slander and evil suspicions,
v05: and constant friction among people corrupted in mind and deprived of the truth, who imagine that godliness is a means of gain.
v06: Yet godliness with contentment is great gain.
v07: For we brought nothing into the world, and we can take nothing out of it.
v08: If we have food and clothing, we will be content with these.
v09: But those who want to be rich fall into temptation, a trap and many foolish and harmful desires that plunge people into ruin and destruction.
v10: For love of money is a root of all kinds of evil. By craving it, some have wandered away from the faith and pierced themselves with many pains.
</p>

## Fight the Good Fight of Trust

<p>
v11: But you, man of God, flee these things and pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance and gentleness.
v12: Fight the good fight of faith. Take hold of eternal life, to which you were called and about which you made the good confession before many witnesses.
v13: I command you before God, who gives life to all things, and Christ Jesus, who testified the good confession before Pontius Pilate,
v14: to keep the command unstained and beyond reproach until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ,
v15: which he will reveal at the proper time—the blessed and only Sovereign, the King of kings and Lord of lords,
v16: who alone has immortality and dwells in unapproachable light, whom no human has seen or can see. To him be honor and eternal power. Amen.
</p>

## Charge the Rich to Be Rich in Good Works

<p>
v17: Command those who are rich in this present age not to be arrogant or set their hope on uncertain wealth, but on God, who richly provides us with everything for enjoyment.
v18: Command them to do good, to be rich in good works, generous and ready to share,
v19: storing up for themselves a good foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of true life.
</p>

## Guard What Was Entrusted to You

<p>
v20: Timothy, guard what has been entrusted to you. Turn away from profane empty chatter and contradictions of what is falsely called knowledge.
v21: By professing it, some have missed the mark concerning the faith. Grace be with all of you.
</p>

## Notes

v01–02: The text addresses enslaved people and masters within slavery; it does not call slavery harmless or erase its coercion.

v10: The text says love of money—not money itself—is a root of all kinds of evil.
