## Treat the Community as Family

<p>
v01: Do not rebuke an older man harshly, but appeal to him as a father; treat younger men as brothers,
v02: older women as mothers and younger women as sisters, with complete purity.
</p>

## Honor Widows Who Are Truly Widows

<p>
v03: Honor widows who are truly widows.
v04: But if a widow has children or grandchildren, let them first learn to practice godliness toward their own household and repay their ancestors, for this is acceptable before God.
v05: The woman who is truly a widow and left alone has put her hope in God and continues in petitions and prayers night and day.
v06: But the widow who lives for self-indulgence is dead even while alive.
v07: Command these things as well, so that they may be above reproach.
v08: But if anyone does not provide for their own, and especially for members of the household, that person has denied the faith and is worse than an unbeliever.
</p>

## The Widows’ List

<p>
v09: A widow is to be enrolled only if she is at least sixty years old and has been a one-man woman,
v10: well attested for good works: if she raised children, welcomed strangers, washed the saints' feet, helped those in distress and devoted herself to every good work.
v11: But refuse to enroll younger widows, for when their desires draw them away from Christ, they want to marry
v12: and incur judgment because they have set aside their first commitment.
v13: At the same time, they learn to be idle, going from house to house; and not merely idle, but also gossips and busybodies, saying things they should not.
v14: So I want younger women to marry, bear children, manage their households and give the opponent no opportunity for slander.
v15: For some have already turned aside after Satan.
v16: If any believing woman has widows in her family, let her help them, so that the church is not burdened and can help those who are truly widows.
</p>

## Elders and Accountability

<p>
v17: Let elders who lead well be considered worthy of double honor, especially those who labor in speaking and teaching.
v18: For Scripture says, “Do not muzzle an ox while it is threshing,” and, “The worker deserves his wages.”
v19: Do not accept an accusation against an elder except on the testimony of two or three witnesses.
v20: Rebuke those who persist in sin before everyone, so that the rest may also have fear.
v21: I solemnly charge you before God, Christ Jesus and the chosen angels to observe these things without prejudgment, doing nothing out of partiality.
v22: Do not lay hands on anyone hastily or share in the sins of others. Keep yourself pure.
v23: Stop drinking only water, and use a little wine because of your stomach and your frequent illnesses.
v24: The sins of some people are obvious, going ahead of them to judgment, but the sins of others follow afterward.
v25: In the same way, good works are obvious, and even those that are otherwise cannot remain hidden.
</p>

## Notes

v03–16: The passage distinguishes enrolled widows, younger widows and family responsibility in its ancient household setting.

v17: ‘Double honor’ may include material support, as the following quotations suggest.
