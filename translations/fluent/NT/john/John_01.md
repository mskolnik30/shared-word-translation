## The Word

<p>
v01: In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
v02: He was in the beginning with God.
v03: Everything came into being through him, and apart from him not one thing came into being. What has come into being
v04: in him was life, and that life was the light of humanity.
v05: The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not overcome it.
</p>

<p>
v06: A man came, sent from God; his name was John.
v07: He came as a witness, to testify about the light, so that everyone might believe through him.
v08: He was not the light, but came to testify about the light.
v09: The true light, which gives light to every person, was coming into the world.
v10: He was in the world, and the world came into being through him, yet the world did not recognize him.
v11: He came to what was his own, but his own people did not receive him.
v12: But to all who did receive him—those who believe in his name—he gave the right to become children of God,
v13: who were born not from bloodlines, nor from the desire of the flesh, nor from a man’s desire, but from God.
</p>

<p>
v14: The Word became flesh and made his dwelling among us. We saw his glory, glory as of the Father’s one-of-a-kind Son, full of grace and truth.
v15: (John testifies about him and cries out, “This is the one of whom I said, ‘The one coming after me ranks ahead of me, because he existed before me.’”)
v16: For from his fullness we have all received, grace in place of grace.
v17: The Law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.
v18: No one has ever seen God. The one-of-a-kind God, who is at the Father’s side, has made him known.
</p>

## John’s Witness

<p>
v19: This is John’s testimony when the Jewish authorities sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, “Who are you?”
v20: He confessed openly and did not deny it. He confessed, “I am not the Messiah.”
v21: They asked him, “Then what? Are you Elijah?” He said, “I am not.” “Are you the Prophet?” He answered, “No.”
v22: So they said to him, “Who are you? We need to give an answer to those who sent us. What do you say about yourself?”
v23: He said, “I am a voice crying out in the wilderness, ‘Make the Lord’s way straight,’ as the prophet Isaiah said.”
v24: Those who had been sent were from the Pharisees.
v25: They asked him, “Why, then, are you baptizing if you are not the Messiah, Elijah, or the Prophet?”
v26: John answered them, “I baptize in water, but among you stands one you do not know.
v27: He is the one coming after me. I am not worthy to untie the strap of his sandal.”
v28: These things happened in Bethany across the Jordan, where John was baptizing.
</p>

## The Lamb of God

<p>
v29: The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him and said, “Look, the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!
v30: This is the one about whom I said, ‘After me comes a man who ranks ahead of me, because he existed before me.’
v31: I did not know him, but I came baptizing in water so that he might be revealed to Israel.”
v32: John testified, “I saw the Spirit come down from heaven like a dove and remain on him.
v33: I did not know him, but the one who sent me to baptize in water told me, ‘The one on whom you see the Spirit come down and remain is the one who baptizes in the Holy Spirit.’
v34: I myself have seen and have testified that this is God’s Chosen One.”
</p>

## The First Disciples

<p>
v35: The next day John was standing there again with two of his disciples.
v36: Looking intently at Jesus as he walked by, John said, “Look, the Lamb of God!”
v37: The two disciples heard him say this and followed Jesus.
v38: Jesus turned, saw them following, and asked, “What are you looking for?” They said, “Rabbi” (which means Teacher), “where are you staying?”
v39: He told them, “Come, and you will see.” So they went and saw where he was staying, and they stayed with him that day. It was about four in the afternoon.
v40: Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, was one of the two who had heard John and followed Jesus.
v41: He first found his own brother Simon and told him, “We have found the Messiah” (which is translated Christ).
v42: Andrew brought Simon to Jesus. Jesus looked intently at him and said, “You are Simon son of John. You will be called Cephas” (which is translated Peter).
v43: The next day Jesus decided to leave for Galilee. He found Philip and told him, “Follow me.”
v44: Philip was from Bethsaida, the town of Andrew and Peter.
v45: Philip found Nathanael and told him, “We have found the one Moses wrote about in the Law, and the Prophets too: Jesus son of Joseph, from Nazareth.”
v46: Nathanael asked him, “Can anything good come from Nazareth?” Philip replied, “Come and see.”
v47: Jesus saw Nathanael coming toward him and said about him, “Look, truly an Israelite in whom there is no deceit.”
v48: Nathanael asked him, “How do you know me?” Jesus answered, “Before Philip called you, while you were under the fig tree, I saw you.”
v49: Nathanael answered him, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are the King of Israel!”
v50: Jesus replied, “Do you believe because I told you I saw you under the fig tree? You will see greater things than these.”
v51: Then he said, “Truly, truly, I tell all of you: you will see heaven opened and God’s angels ascending and descending on the Son of Man.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: ‘The Word’ translates *logos*, which can mean word, message, reason, or account. John speaks of the Word as personally with God and as God.

v03–04: Ancient punctuation is not preserved in the manuscripts. The main text joins ‘what has come into being’ with ‘in him was life’; the phrase can instead close the preceding sentence.

v05: The verb can mean overcome, seize, or comprehend. ‘Did not overcome’ suits the conflict of light and darkness while the Gospel also develops the theme of failed recognition.

v14: ‘Made his dwelling’ is literally ‘pitched his tent’ or ‘tabernacled,’ evoking God’s dwelling among Israel.

v18: The pinned text reads ‘the one-of-a-kind God.’ Other ancient witnesses read ‘the one-of-a-kind Son.’

v34: The pinned text reads ‘God’s Chosen One.’ Other ancient witnesses have ‘Son of God.’

## Vocabulary

v01: **λόγος (logos)**: Word, message, reason, or account.

v14: **σάρξ (sarx)**: Flesh, embodied mortal human existence.

v17: **χάρις (charis)**: Grace, favor, or generous gift.

v29: **ἀμνὸς τοῦ θεοῦ (amnos tou theou)**: Lamb of God.
