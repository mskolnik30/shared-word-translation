## Jesus Washes the Disciples’ Feet

<p>
v01: Before the Passover festival, Jesus knew that his hour had come to leave this world and go to the Father. Having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the very end.
v02: During supper, the devil had already put it into the heart of Judas, son of Simon Iscariot, to betray Jesus.
v03: Jesus knew that the Father had given everything into his hands, and that he had come from God and was going to God.
v04: So he got up from supper, laid aside his outer garments, and tied a towel around himself.
v05: Then he poured water into a basin and began to wash the disciples’ feet and dry them with the towel tied around him.
v06: He came to Simon Peter, who said to him, “Lord, are you going to wash my feet?”
v07: Jesus answered him, “You do not understand now what I am doing, but afterward you will understand.”
v08: Peter said to him, “You will never wash my feet!” Jesus answered, “Unless I wash you, you have no share with me.”
v09: Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, then not only my feet, but also my hands and my head!”
v10: Jesus said to him, “Someone who has bathed needs only to wash his feet, but is completely clean. And you are clean—but not all of you.”
v11: For he knew who would betray him. That is why he said, “Not all of you are clean.”
</p>

## An Example of Service

<p>
v12: After he had washed their feet, put on his garments, and reclined at the table again, he said to them, “Do you understand what I have done for you?
v13: You call me ‘Teacher’ and ‘Lord,’ and rightly so, because that is what I am.
v14: So if I, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another’s feet.
v15: For I have given you an example so that you should do just as I have done for you.
v16: Truly, truly, I tell you: a slave is not greater than his master, nor is a messenger greater than the one who sent him.
v17: If you know these things, you are blessed if you do them.
</p>

## The Betrayer Identified

<p>
v18: I am not speaking about all of you. I know those I have chosen. But the Scripture must be fulfilled: ‘The one who ate my bread has lifted his heel against me.’
v19: I am telling you now, before it happens, so that when it happens you may believe that I am he.
v20: Truly, truly, I tell you: whoever receives anyone I send receives me, and whoever receives me receives the one who sent me.”
v21: After saying these things, Jesus was deeply troubled in spirit and testified, “Truly, truly, I tell you: one of you will betray me.”
v22: The disciples looked at one another, uncertain which of them he meant.
v23: One of his disciples—the one Jesus loved—was reclining beside Jesus.
v24: So Simon Peter motioned to him to ask Jesus whom he meant.
v25: Leaning back against Jesus, that disciple asked him, “Lord, who is it?”
v26: Jesus answered, “It is the one for whom I dip this piece of bread and give it to him.” Then he dipped the piece and gave it to Judas, son of Simon Iscariot.
v27: After Judas took the piece of bread, Satan entered into him. Then Jesus said to him, “What you are doing, do quickly.”
v28: None of those reclining at the table understood why Jesus said this to him.
v29: Because Judas kept the money box, some thought Jesus was telling him, “Buy what we need for the festival,” or that he should give something to the poor.
v30: After receiving the piece of bread, Judas went out immediately. And it was night.
</p>

## The New Commandment

<p>
v31: When Judas had gone out, Jesus said, “Now the Son of Man has been glorified, and God has been glorified in him.
v32: If God has been glorified in him, God will also glorify him in himself, and will glorify him at once.
v33: Little children, I am with you only a little longer. You will look for me, and just as I told the Judean leaders, I now tell you: where I am going, you cannot come.
v34: I give you a new commandment: love one another. Just as I have loved you, you also must love one another.
v35: By this everyone will know that you are my disciples: if you have love for one another.”
</p>

## Peter’s Denial Foretold

<p>
v36: Simon Peter said to him, “Lord, where are you going?” Jesus answered, “Where I am going, you cannot follow me now, but you will follow later.”
v37: Peter said to him, “Lord, why can’t I follow you now? I will lay down my life for you.”
v38: Jesus answered, “Will you lay down your life for me? Truly, truly, I tell you: the rooster will not crow until you have denied me three times.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: ‘To the very end’ can refer to love continuing through the end or love shown to the uttermost. The Greek allows both resonances.

v08: ‘No share with me’ refers to participation or belonging with Jesus, not to a material allotment.

v10: A person who has bathed is clean and needs only footwashing. The saying distinguishes the whole bath from the repeated act without explaining a later ritual system.

v18: The Scripture’s lifted heel is an image of hostile betrayal by one who shared bread.

v23: The beloved disciple is not named in this verse. His reclining position beside Jesus reflects the meal posture.

v32: The initial ‘if God has been glorified in him’ is marked as a textual variant in the pinned source but is present in its main text and therefore translated.

v34: The command is new in being measured and enacted by Jesus’ own love: ‘just as I have loved you.’

## Vocabulary

v16: **ἀπόστολος (apostolos)**: One who is sent, a messenger.

v23: **ἀγαπάω (agapaō)**: Love, value, or show devoted care.

v34: **ἐντολή (entolē)**: Command or instruction.
