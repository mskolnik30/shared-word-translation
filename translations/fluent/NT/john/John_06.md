## Jesus Feeds Five Thousand

<p>
v01: After this, Jesus crossed to the other side of the Sea of Galilee, also called the Sea of Tiberias.
v02: A large crowd followed him because they were seeing the signs he performed for those who were sick.
v03: Jesus went up the mountain and sat there with his disciples.
v04: The Passover, the Jewish festival, was near.
v05: When Jesus looked up and saw that a large crowd was coming toward him, he asked Philip, “Where can we buy bread for these people to eat?”
v06: He asked this to test Philip, for he himself knew what he was about to do.
v07: Philip answered, “Two hundred denarii worth of bread would not be enough for each person to receive even a little.”
v08: One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, said to him,
v09: “There is a boy here who has five barley loaves and two small fish. But what are these among so many people?”
v10: Jesus said, “Have the people sit down.” There was plenty of grass in that place, so they sat down—about five thousand men in number.
v11: Then Jesus took the loaves, gave thanks, and distributed them to those reclining there. He did the same with the fish, giving them as much as they wanted.
v12: When they had eaten their fill, he told his disciples, “Gather the leftover pieces so that nothing is lost.”
v13: So they gathered them and filled twelve baskets with pieces left from the five barley loaves after the people had eaten.
v14: When the people saw the sign Jesus had done, they said, “This truly is the Prophet who is coming into the world.”
v15: Jesus knew they were about to come and seize him to make him king, so he withdrew again to the mountain by himself.
</p>

## Jesus Walks on the Sea

<p>
v16: When evening came, his disciples went down to the sea,
v17: got into a boat, and started across the sea toward Capernaum. Darkness had already fallen, and Jesus had not yet come to them.
v18: A strong wind was blowing, and the sea was becoming rough.
v19: After rowing about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they saw Jesus walking on the sea and coming near the boat, and they were afraid.
v20: But he told them, “It is I. Do not be afraid.”
v21: Then they wanted to take him into the boat, and immediately the boat reached the shore toward which they were going.
</p>

## The Crowd Seeks Jesus

<p>
v22: The next day, the crowd remaining on the other side of the sea realized that only one boat had been there and that Jesus had not gotten into it with his disciples, but the disciples had left alone.
v23: Other boats from Tiberias had come near the place where the people ate the bread after the Lord had given thanks.
v24: When the crowd saw that neither Jesus nor his disciples were there, they got into the boats and went to Capernaum looking for Jesus.
v25: When they found him across the sea, they asked, “Rabbi, when did you get here?”
v26: Jesus answered them, “Truly, truly, I tell you, you are looking for me not because you saw signs, but because you ate the loaves and were filled.
v27: Do not work for food that perishes, but for food that remains for eternal life, which the Son of Man will give you. For God the Father has set his seal on him.”
v28: They asked him, “What must we do to perform the works of God?”
v29: Jesus answered, “This is God’s work: that you believe in the one he sent.”
v30: So they asked him, “What sign, then, will you perform so that we may see it and believe you? What work will you do?
v31: Our ancestors ate the manna in the wilderness, as it is written: ‘He gave them bread from heaven to eat.’”
v32: Jesus told them, “Truly, truly, I tell you, it was not Moses who gave you the bread from heaven. My Father gives you the true bread from heaven.
v33: For God’s bread is the one who comes down from heaven and gives life to the world.”
v34: They said, “Sir, always give us this bread.”
</p>

## The Bread of Life

<p>
v35: Jesus told them, “I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to me will certainly never hunger, and whoever believes in me will certainly never thirst.
v36: But as I told you, you have seen me and still do not believe.
v37: Everyone the Father gives me will come to me, and I will certainly never cast out the one who comes to me,
v38: because I have come down from heaven not to do my own will but the will of the one who sent me.
v39: This is the will of the one who sent me: that I lose none of all he has given me, but raise them up on the last day.
v40: For this is my Father’s will: that everyone who sees the Son and believes in him may have eternal life, and I will raise that person up on the last day.”
v41: Some of the Jewish hearers began grumbling about him because he said, “I am the bread that came down from heaven.”
v42: They said, “Is this not Jesus son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How can he now say, ‘I have come down from heaven’?”
v43: Jesus answered them, “Stop grumbling among yourselves.
v44: No one can come to me unless the Father who sent me draws that person, and I will raise them up on the last day.
v45: It is written in the Prophets, ‘They will all be taught by God.’ Everyone who listens to the Father and learns comes to me.
v46: Not that anyone has seen the Father except the one who is from God; he has seen the Father.
v47: Truly, truly, I tell you, whoever believes has eternal life.
v48: I am the bread of life.
v49: Your ancestors ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died.
v50: This is the bread coming down from heaven, so that anyone may eat from it and not die.
v51: I am the living bread that came down from heaven. If anyone eats this bread, they will live forever. And the bread I will give for the life of the world is my flesh.”
v52: Then the Jewish hearers began arguing with one another, “How can this man give us his flesh to eat?”
v53: Jesus told them, “Truly, truly, I tell you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you do not have life in yourselves.
v54: Whoever feeds on my flesh and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise that person up on the last day.
v55: For my flesh is true food, and my blood is true drink.
v56: Whoever feeds on my flesh and drinks my blood remains in me, and I in that person.
v57: Just as the living Father sent me and I live because of the Father, so the one who feeds on me will live because of me.
v58: This is the bread that came down from heaven—not like what the ancestors ate and then died. Whoever feeds on this bread will live forever.”
v59: Jesus said these things while teaching in a synagogue in Capernaum.
</p>

## Words of Eternal Life

<p>
v60: When many of his disciples heard this, they said, “This teaching is hard. Who can listen to it?”
v61: Knowing in himself that his disciples were grumbling about this, Jesus asked them, “Does this offend you?
v62: Then what if you see the Son of Man ascending to where he was before?
v63: The Spirit gives life; the flesh is of no help by itself. The words I have spoken to you are Spirit and life.
v64: But some of you do not believe.” For Jesus knew from the beginning who did not believe and who would betray him.
v65: He continued, “This is why I told you that no one can come to me unless it is granted by the Father.”
v66: From that time, many of his disciples turned back and no longer walked with him.
v67: So Jesus asked the Twelve, “You do not want to leave too, do you?”
v68: Simon Peter answered him, “Lord, to whom would we go? You have words of eternal life.
v69: We have come to believe and know that you are the Holy One of God.”
v70: Jesus replied, “Did I not choose you, the Twelve? Yet one of you is a devil.”
v71: He was speaking of Judas, son of Simon Iscariot, for he—one of the Twelve—was going to betray Jesus.
</p>

## Notes

v15: The crowd intends to seize Jesus and make him king. His withdrawal refuses their coercive and political framing of his sign.

v27: The Father’s seal marks the Son of Man as authorized to give the food that remains into eternal life.

v51–58: The discourse deliberately uses concrete eating, flesh, and blood language. The translation retains it without reducing the passage to one later doctrinal explanation.

v62–63: Jesus joins the Son of Man’s ascent with the Spirit’s life-giving work; ‘the flesh is no help’ does not erase the importance of his flesh in the preceding verses.

v69: The pinned Greek reads ‘the Holy One of God.’ Other ancient witnesses contain expanded titles.

## Vocabulary

v35: **ἄρτος τῆς ζωῆς (artos tēs zōēs)**: Bread of life.

v54: **τρώγω (trōgō)**: Eat; a concrete verb also used of chewing or feeding.

v63: **πνεῦμα (pneuma)**: Spirit, wind, or breath.
