## Ancient Textual Tradition: The Woman Caught in Adultery

<p>
v01: while Jesus went to the Mount of Olives.
v02: Early in the morning he came again to the temple courts. All the people came to him, and he sat down and taught them.
v03: The scribes and Pharisees brought a woman who had been caught in adultery and made her stand in the middle of the group.
v04: They said to him, testing him, “Teacher, this woman was caught in the very act of adultery.
v05: In the Law, Moses commanded us to stone women like this. So what do you say?”
v06: They said this to test him, so that they would have grounds to accuse him. But Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground with his finger, appearing not to notice them.
v07: When they kept questioning him, he straightened up and told them, “Let the one among you who is without sin be the first to throw a stone at her.”
v08: Then he bent down again and wrote on the ground.
v09: When they heard this and were convicted by their conscience, they went out one by one, beginning with the elders. Jesus was left alone with the woman standing in the middle.
v10: Jesus straightened up and, seeing no one except the woman, asked her, “Woman, where are they? Has no one condemned you?”
v11: She answered, “No one, Lord.” Jesus said, “Neither do I condemn you. Go, and from now on do not keep sinning.”⟧
</p>

## The Light of the World

<p>
v12: Jesus spoke to them again: “I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will certainly not walk in darkness but will have the light of life.”
v13: The Pharisees told him, “You are testifying about yourself; your testimony is not valid.”
v14: Jesus answered, “Even if I testify about myself, my testimony is true, because I know where I came from and where I am going. But you do not know where I come from or where I am going.
v15: You judge according to the flesh; I judge no one.
v16: Yet even if I do judge, my judgment is true, because I am not alone; I am with the Father who sent me.
v17: Even in your Law it is written that the testimony of two people is valid.
v18: I testify about myself, and the Father who sent me testifies about me.”
v19: Then they asked him, “Where is your father?” Jesus answered, “You know neither me nor my Father. If you knew me, you would know my Father also.”
v20: He spoke these words while teaching near the treasury in the temple courts. Yet no one arrested him, because his hour had not yet come.
</p>

v25: They asked him, “Who are you?” Jesus replied, “Why am I speaking to you at all?
v26: I have much to say and judge concerning you. But the one who sent me is true, and I speak to the world what I heard from him.”

v28: So Jesus said, “When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am, and that I do nothing on my own but speak these things just as the Father taught me.

v30: As he said these things, many believed in him.

## The Truth Will Set You Free

<p>

v32: You will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.”
v33: They answered him, “We are Abraham’s offspring and have never been enslaved to anyone. How can you say, ‘You will become free’?”
v34: Jesus answered them, “Truly, truly, I tell you, everyone who practices sin is a slave of sin.
v35: A slave does not remain in the household forever, but a son remains forever.
v36: So if the Son sets you free, you will truly be free.
v37: I know that you are Abraham’s offspring. Yet you are trying to kill me because my word finds no room in you.
v38: I speak what I have seen with the Father, and you do what you have heard from your father.”
v39: They answered him, “Abraham is our father.” Jesus told them, “If you were Abraham’s children, you would do Abraham’s works.
v40: But now you are trying to kill me, a man who has told you the truth I heard from God. Abraham did not do this.
v41: You are doing your father’s works.” They told him, “We were not born from sexual immorality. We have one Father—God.”
v42: Jesus told them, “If God were your Father, you would love me, because I came from God and am here. I did not come on my own; he sent me.
v43: Why do you not understand what I say? Because you cannot listen to my word.
v44: You are from your father the devil, and you want to carry out your father’s desires. He was a murderer from the beginning and did not stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own nature, because he is a liar and the father of lies.
v45: But because I tell the truth, you do not believe me.
v46: Which of you can prove me guilty of sin? If I speak truth, why do you not believe me?

## Before Abraham Was, I Am

<p>
v48: The Jewish leaders answered him, “Are we not right in saying that you are a Samaritan and have a demon?”
v49: Jesus answered, “I do not have a demon. I honor my Father, but you dishonor me.
v50: I do not seek my own glory; there is one who seeks it and judges.
v51: Truly, truly, I tell you, whoever keeps my word will certainly never see death.”
v52: The leaders told him, “Now we know that you have a demon. Abraham died, and so did the prophets, yet you say, ‘Whoever keeps my word will certainly never taste death.’
v53: Are you greater than our father Abraham, who died? The prophets died too. Who do you make yourself out to be?”
v54: Jesus answered, “If I glorify myself, my glory means nothing. My Father is the one who glorifies me—the one you say is our God.
v55: You have not known him, but I know him. If I said I do not know him, I would be a liar like you. But I know him and keep his word.
v56: Your father Abraham rejoiced that he would see my day. He saw it and was glad.”
v57: The leaders said to him, “You are not yet fifty years old, and you have seen Abraham?”
v58: Jesus told them, “Truly, truly, I tell you, before Abraham came into being, I am.”
v59: Then they picked up stones to throw at him, but Jesus hid himself and left the temple courts.
</p>

## Notes

v01–11: This passage continues the double-bracketed textual tradition begun at 7:53. Its source status is distinct from uncertainty about individual words in the main text.

v24, 28, 58: The expression ‘I am’ can function as ordinary self-identification and also echoes divine self-declaration. The translation leaves the absolute wording visible.

v31–36: Jesus speaks to people who had believed him and distinguishes initial belief from remaining in his word. Freedom here concerns slavery to sin, not a denial of Israel’s historical subjugation.

v44: The verse uses severe family and desire imagery for allegiance to the devil. It must not be generalized into an ethnic claim about Jewish people; Jesus and his first disciples are Jewish.

v56–58: Abraham’s rejoicing and Jesus’ ‘I am’ contrast Abraham’s coming into existence with Jesus’ continuing identity.

## Vocabulary

v32: **ἀλήθεια (alētheia)**: Truth or reality.

v36: **ἐλεύθερος (eleutheros)**: Free, not enslaved.

v51: **τηρέω (tēreō)**: Keep, guard, or observe.
