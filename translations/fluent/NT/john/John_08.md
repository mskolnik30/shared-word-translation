## Ancient Textual Tradition: The Woman Caught in Adultery

<p>
v01: Jesus went to the Mount of Olives.
v02: Early in the morning he came again to the temple. All the people came to him, and he sat down and taught them.
v03: The scribes and Pharisees brought a woman who had been caught in adultery and made her stand in the middle.
v04: They said, “Teacher, this woman was caught in the very act of adultery.
v05: In the Law Moses commanded us to stone such women. What do you say?”
v06: They said this to test him so they would have grounds to accuse him. But Jesus bent down and wrote on the ground with his finger.
v07: When they kept asking him, he stood up and said, “Let the one among you who is without sin be the first to throw a stone at her.”
v08: Then he bent down again and wrote on the ground.
v09: When they heard this, they went out one by one, beginning with the older ones, until Jesus was left alone with the woman standing there.
v10: Jesus stood up and said to her, “Woman, where are they? Has no one condemned you?”
v11: She said, “No one, Lord.” Jesus said, “Neither do I condemn you. Go, and from now on do not sin anymore.”
</p>

## The Light of the World

<p>
v12: Jesus spoke to them again: “I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will certainly not walk in darkness but will have the light of life.”
v13: The Pharisees said to him, “You testify about yourself. Your testimony is not valid.”
v14: Jesus answered, “Even if I testify about myself, my testimony is valid because I know where I came from and where I am going. But you do not know where I come from or where I am going.
v15: You judge according to the flesh; I judge no one.
v16: Yet even if I do judge, my judgment is true, because I am not alone; I am with the Father who sent me.
v17: Even in your Law it is written that the testimony of two people is valid.
v18: I am the one who testifies about myself, and the Father who sent me testifies about me.”
v19: They asked, “Where is your Father?” Jesus answered, “You know neither me nor my Father. If you knew me, you would know my Father also.”
v20: Jesus spoke these words in the treasury while teaching in the temple, but no one arrested him because his hour had not yet come.
</p>

## Unless You Trust That I Am

<p>
v21: Jesus said to them again, “I am going away. You will look for me, and you will die in your sin. Where I am going, you cannot come.”
v22: So some Jewish people asked, “He is not going to kill himself, is he, since he says, ‘Where I am going, you cannot come’?”
v23: Jesus told them, “You are from below; I am from above. You are from this world; I am not from this world.
v24: That is why I told you that you will die in your sins. Unless you trust that I am, you will die in your sins.”
v25: They asked him, “Who are you?” Jesus said, “What I have been telling you from the beginning.
v26: I have many things to say and judge concerning you. But the one who sent me is true, and what I heard from him I speak to the world.”
v27: They did not understand that he was speaking to them about the Father.
v28: So Jesus said, “When you lift up the Son of Man, then you will know that I am, and that I do nothing from myself. I speak just as the Father taught me.
v29: The one who sent me is with me. He has not left me alone, because I always do what pleases him.”
v30: As he was saying these things, many trusted in him.
</p>

## The Truth Will Set You Free

<p>
v31: Jesus said to the Jewish people who had trusted him, “If you remain in my word, you are truly my disciples.
v32: You will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.”
v33: They answered, “We are Abraham’s descendants and have never been enslaved to anyone. How can you say, ‘You will become free’?”
v34: Jesus answered, “Truly, truly, I tell you, everyone who practices sin is a slave of sin.
v35: A slave does not remain in the household forever; the son remains forever.
v36: So if the Son sets you free, you will truly be free.
v37: I know you are Abraham’s descendants. Yet you are seeking to kill me because my word has no place in you.
v38: I speak what I have seen with the Father, and you do what you have heard from your father.”
v39: They answered, “Abraham is our father.” Jesus said, “If you were Abraham’s children, you would do Abraham’s works.
v40: But now you are seeking to kill me, a man who has told you the truth I heard from God. Abraham did not do this.
v41: You are doing your father’s works.” They said, “We were not born from sexual immorality. We have one Father—God.”
v42: Jesus said, “If God were your Father, you would love me, because I came from God and am here. I did not come from myself; he sent me.
v43: Why do you not understand what I say? Because you cannot listen to my word.
v44: You are from your father, the devil, and you want to carry out your father’s desires. He was a murderer from the beginning and did not stand in the truth because there is no truth in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own nature, because he is a liar and the father of lies.
v45: But because I speak the truth, you do not trust me.
v46: Which one of you convicts me of sin? If I speak truth, why do you not trust me?
v47: Whoever is from God hears God’s words. This is why you do not hear: you are not from God.”
</p>

## Before Abraham Was, I Am

<p>
v48: They answered him, “Are we not right to say you are a Samaritan and have a demon?”
v49: Jesus answered, “I do not have a demon. I honor my Father, and you dishonor me.
v50: I do not seek my own glory. There is one who seeks it and judges.
v51: Truly, truly, I tell you, if anyone keeps my word, they will certainly never see death.”
v52: They said to him, “Now we know you have a demon! Abraham died, and so did the prophets, yet you say, ‘If anyone keeps my word, they will never taste death.’
v53: Are you greater than our father Abraham, who died? The prophets also died. Who do you make yourself out to be?”
v54: Jesus answered, “If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing. My Father is the one who glorifies me—the one you say is your God.
v55: You have not known him, but I know him. If I said I did not know him, I would be a liar like you. But I know him and keep his word.
v56: Your father Abraham rejoiced that he would see my day. He saw it and was glad.”
v57: They said, “You are not yet fifty years old, and you have seen Abraham?”
v58: Jesus said, “Truly, truly, I tell you, before Abraham came into being, I am.”
v59: Then they picked up stones to throw at him, but Jesus hid himself and went out of the temple.
</p>

## Notes

v01–11: This entire scene is printed by SBLGNT in double brackets as an ancient textual tradition rather than ordinary continuous critical text. Fluent includes it because of its ancient and deeply received place in the church while marking that status explicitly.

v06,08: The text never says what Jesus wrote. Fluent does not speculate.

v07: The saying does not deny the woman’s adultery or convert every legal judgment into hypocrisy; it turns the attempted trap toward the accusers’ own sin.

v12: “Light of the world” resumes John’s continuous discourse after the bracketed textual tradition.

v13–18: Testimony language is legal and relational. John holds Jesus’ self-witness together with the Father’s witness.

v24,28,58: Absolute or near-absolute `egō eimi` sayings accumulate. Fluent retains “I am” rather than supplying an interpretive predicate.

v31: The audience is explicitly Jewish people who had trusted Jesus. The ensuing conflict therefore remains an intra-Jewish discipleship controversy rather than an opposition between Jesus and Judaism as such.

v32–36: Freedom is defined in relation to remaining in Jesus’ word and being freed from slavery to sin.

v44: The accusation about “your father the devil” addresses this hostile interlocutor group within the narrative. It must not be generalized into a claim about Jewish people.

v54–55: Jesus and his interlocutors both speak of the God of Israel; the dispute is over knowing and honoring God, not over worshiping different deities.

v58: The contrast between Abraham “coming into being” and Jesus’ “I am” is intentionally stark.
