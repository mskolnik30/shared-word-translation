## Father, Glorify Your Son

<p>
v01: After Jesus said these things, he looked up to heaven and said, “Father, the hour has come. Glorify your Son so that the Son may glorify you,
v02: since you gave him authority over all humanity, so that he may give eternal life to everyone you have given him.
v03: And this is eternal life: that they know you, the only true God, and Jesus the Messiah, whom you sent.
v04: I glorified you on earth by completing the work you gave me to do.
v05: And now, Father, glorify me in your presence with the glory I had with you before the world existed.
</p>

## Jesus Prays for His Disciples

<p>
v06: I have revealed your name to the people you gave me out of the world. They were yours; you gave them to me, and they have kept your word.
v07: Now they know that everything you have given me comes from you,
v08: because I have given them the words you gave me, and they received them. They truly knew that I came from you, and they believed that you sent me.
v09: I pray for them. I am not praying for the world, but for those you have given me, because they are yours.
v10: Everything of mine is yours, and what is yours is mine, and I have been glorified in them.
v11: I am no longer in the world, but they are in the world, and I am coming to you. Holy Father, protect them in your name, which you have given me, so that they may be one just as we are.
v12: While I was with them, I protected them in your name, which you have given me. I guarded them, and not one of them was lost except the son of destruction, so that the Scripture might be fulfilled.
v13: But now I am coming to you, and I say these things in the world so that they may have my joy made complete in themselves.
v14: I have given them your word. The world hated them because they do not belong to the world, just as I do not belong to the world.
v15: I am not asking you to take them out of the world, but to protect them from the evil one.
v16: They do not belong to the world, just as I do not belong to the world.
v17: Sanctify them in the truth; your word is truth.
v18: Just as you sent me into the world, I also sent them into the world.
v19: For their sake I sanctify myself, so that they also may be sanctified in truth.
</p>

## Jesus Prays for Those Who Will Trust

<p>
v20: I ask not only for these, but also for those who will believe in me through their word,
v21: that they may all be one, just as you, Father, are in me and I am in you; may they also be in us, so that the world may believe that you sent me.
v22: The glory you have given me I have given them, so that they may be one just as we are one—
v23: I in them and you in me—so that they may be brought to complete unity, and so that the world may know that you sent me and loved them just as you loved me.
v24: Father, I desire that those you have given me may be with me where I am, so that they may see my glory, which you have given me because you loved me before the foundation of the world.
v25: Righteous Father, though the world did not know you, I knew you, and these people knew that you sent me.
v26: I made your name known to them and will continue to make it known, so that the love with which you loved me may be in them, and I may be in them.”
</p>

## Notes

v03: Eternal life is described as knowing the only true God and Jesus the Messiah whom God sent. ‘Know’ is relational and lived, not merely informational.

v05: Jesus asks for the glory he had with the Father before the world existed, explicitly locating the relationship before creation.

v11–12: Protection ‘in your name’ concerns belonging to and being kept in God’s revealed identity. ‘Son of destruction’ is an idiom for the one characterized by and destined for ruin.

v15: The adjective can be understood as evil generally or as ‘the evil one.’ The personal form fits the Gospel’s references to the world’s ruler, while the broader sense remains possible.

v17–19: To sanctify is to set apart for God’s purpose. Jesus sets himself apart for his mission so that his people may be set apart in truth.

v21–23: The prayed-for unity is patterned on Father-Son indwelling and serves public witness. It is not described as erasing the persons involved.

v24: The Father’s love for the Son is placed before the foundation of the world.

## Vocabulary

v03: **γινώσκω (ginōskō)**: Know, recognize, or come to know.

v17: **ἁγιάζω (hagiazō)**: Sanctify, consecrate, or set apart as holy.

v24: **καταβολὴ κόσμου (katabolē kosmou)**: Foundation or creation of the world.
