## Mary Magdalene Finds the Tomb Open

<p>
v01: Early on the first day of the week, while it was still dark, Mary Magdalene came to the tomb and saw that the stone had been removed from it.
v02: So she ran to Simon Peter and the other disciple, the one Jesus loved, and told them, “They have taken the Lord out of the tomb, and we do not know where they put him.”
v03: Then Peter and the other disciple set out for the tomb.
v04: The two were running together, but the other disciple ran faster than Peter and reached the tomb first.
v05: He bent down and saw the linen cloths lying there, but he did not go in.
v06: Then Simon Peter came behind him and entered the tomb. He saw the linen cloths lying there,
v07: and the cloth that had been on Jesus’ head, not lying with the linen cloths but folded up separately in one place.
v08: Then the other disciple, who had reached the tomb first, also went in. He saw and believed.
v09: For they did not yet understand the Scripture that he must rise from the dead.
v10: Then the disciples went back to where they were staying.
</p>

## Jesus Appears to Mary Magdalene

<p>
v11: But Mary stood outside near the tomb, weeping. As she wept, she bent down to look into the tomb.
v12: She saw two angels in white sitting where Jesus’ body had lain, one at the head and one at the feet.
v13: They asked her, “Woman, why are you weeping?” She answered, “They have taken away my Lord, and I do not know where they put him.”
v14: After saying this, she turned around and saw Jesus standing there, but she did not know that it was Jesus.
v15: Jesus asked her, “Woman, why are you weeping? Whom are you looking for?” Thinking he was the gardener, she said, “Sir, if you carried him away, tell me where you put him, and I will take him.”
v16: Jesus said to her, “Mary.” She turned and said to him in Hebrew, “Rabbouni!”—which means Teacher.
v17: Jesus told her, “Do not hold on to me, because I have not yet ascended to the Father. But go to my brothers and tell them, ‘I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God.’”
v18: Mary Magdalene went and announced to the disciples, “I have seen the Lord!” She also told them what he had said to her.
</p>

## Jesus Appears to the Disciples

<p>
v19: That evening, on the first day of the week, the doors where the disciples were gathered were locked because they feared the Judean leaders. Jesus came, stood among them, and said, “Peace be with you.”
v20: After saying this, he showed them his hands and his side. Then the disciples rejoiced when they saw the Lord.
v21: Jesus told them again, “Peace be with you. Just as the Father has sent me, I also send you.”
v22: After saying this, he breathed on them and said, “Receive the Holy Spirit.
v23: If you forgive anyone’s sins, they are forgiven; if you retain anyone’s sins, they are retained.”
</p>

## Thomas Sees and Trusts

<p>
v24: Thomas, one of the Twelve, called the Twin, was not with them when Jesus came.
v25: So the other disciples told him, “We have seen the Lord!” But he told them, “Unless I see the nail marks in his hands, put my finger into the nail marks, and put my hand into his side, I will never believe.”
v26: Eight days later, his disciples were inside again, and Thomas was with them. Although the doors were locked, Jesus came, stood among them, and said, “Peace be with you.”
v27: Then he told Thomas, “Put your finger here and look at my hands. Reach out your hand and put it into my side. Do not remain unbelieving, but believe.”
v28: Thomas answered him, “My Lord and my God!”
v29: Jesus told him, “Because you have seen me, you have believed. Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.”
</p>

## Why This Gospel Was Written

<p>
v30: Jesus performed many other signs in the presence of his disciples that are not written in this book.
v31: But these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Messiah, the Son of God, and by believing may have life in his name.
</p>

## Notes

v08: The verse says the beloved disciple ‘saw and believed’ without explicitly stating what he believed at that moment; the next verse says the disciples did not yet understand the Scripture about resurrection.

v17: The command can mean ‘do not keep holding on to me,’ not necessarily ‘do not touch me at all.’ Mary is immediately sent to announce Jesus’ ascent.

v19: ‘The Judean leaders’ identifies those the disciples feared in Jerusalem, not Jewish people as a whole.

v22: Jesus breathes on the disciples as he gives the Holy Spirit, evoking God’s life-giving breath without requiring the verse to be collapsed into the later Pentecost scene.

v23: The paired sayings about forgiving and retaining sins are translated directly. The verse does not itself specify a later institutional procedure.

v25: Thomas asks for embodied evidence of wounds caused by nails and the spear. His demand is not reduced to a personality label.

v31: The pinned text reads ‘so that you may believe.’ A major variant can be understood as ‘so that you may continue to believe.’

## Vocabulary

v16: **Ῥαββουνί (Rabbouni)**: My teacher or revered teacher.

v21: **πέμπω (pempō)**: Send or commission.

v29: **μακάριος (makarios)**: Blessed, flourishing under God’s favor.
