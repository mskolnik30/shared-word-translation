## Jesus and Nicodemus

<p>
v01: There was a Pharisee named Nicodemus, a leader of the Jewish people.
v02: He came to Jesus at night and said, “Rabbi, we know that you have come from God as a teacher, because no one can perform these signs you are doing unless God is with him.”
v03: Jesus answered him, “Truly, truly, I tell you, unless someone is born from above, they cannot see the kingdom of God.”
v04: Nicodemus asked, “How can a person be born when old? Surely he cannot enter his mother’s womb a second time and be born!”
v05: Jesus answered, “Truly, truly, I tell you, unless someone is born of water and Spirit, they cannot enter the kingdom of God.
v06: What is born of the flesh is flesh, and what is born of the Spirit is spirit.
v07: Do not be amazed that I told you, ‘All of you must be born from above.’
v08: The wind blows where it wants. You hear its sound but do not know where it comes from or where it is going. So it is with everyone born of the Spirit.”
v09: Nicodemus asked him, “How can these things happen?”
v10: Jesus replied, “You are the teacher of Israel, and you do not understand these things?
v11: Truly, truly, I tell you, we speak what we know and testify to what we have seen, yet all of you do not receive our testimony.
v12: If I told you about earthly things and you do not believe, how will you believe if I tell you about heavenly things?
v13: No one has ascended into heaven except the one who descended from heaven—the Son of Man.
v14: Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up,
v15: so that everyone who believes in him may have eternal life.
v16: For God loved the world in this way: he gave his one-of-a-kind Son, so that everyone who believes in him will not perish but have eternal life.
v17: For God did not send the Son into the world to condemn the world, but so that the world might be saved through him.
v18: Whoever believes in him is not condemned, but whoever does not believe has already been condemned because they have not believed in the name of God’s one-of-a-kind Son.
v19: This is the judgment: the light has come into the world, but people loved darkness rather than the light because their deeds were evil.
v20: For everyone who practices evil hates the light and does not come to the light, so that their deeds will not be exposed.
v21: But whoever does what is true comes to the light, so that it may be clearly seen that their deeds have been done in God.

## John Testifies Again

v24: for John had not yet been thrown into prison.
v25: Then a dispute about purification arose between some of John’s disciples and a Jewish man.

## Notes

v03, 07: The adverb *anōthen* can mean ‘from above’ or ‘again.’ Jesus speaks of birth from above; Nicodemus hears a second physical birth.

v05: ‘Water and Spirit’ forms one coordinated birth image. The verse does not itself specify one later ritual formula as the only interpretation.

v08: The same Greek word can mean wind or spirit. The main text uses ‘wind’ for what blows and ‘Spirit’ for the one who gives birth, allowing the wordplay to remain audible.

v14: ‘Lifted up’ gathers Jesus’ crucifixion and exaltation into one expression, in comparison with Moses lifting the snake in the wilderness.

v16: ‘In this way’ describes how God loved the world—by giving the unique Son—rather than only how intensely God loved.

v36: The contrast is between trusting the Son and refusing to obey him; the latter verb includes both disbelief and disobedience.

## Vocabulary

v03: **ἄνωθεν (anōthen)**: From above; also capable of meaning again.

v08: **πνεῦμα (pneuma)**: Spirit, wind, or breath.

v16: **μονογενής (monogenēs)**: One of a kind or unique, especially in a filial relationship.

v29: **νυμφίος (nymphios)**: Bridegroom.
