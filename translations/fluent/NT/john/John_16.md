## So That You Do Not Stumble

<p>
v01: “I have told you these things so that you will not fall away.
v02: They will expel you from the synagogues. In fact, an hour is coming when everyone who kills you will think they are offering worship to God.
v03: They will do these things because they have not known the Father or me.
v04: But I have told you these things so that when their hour comes, you may remember that I told you. I did not tell you these things from the beginning because I was with you.
</p>

## The Advocate and the World

<p>
v05: But now I am going to the one who sent me, and none of you asks me, ‘Where are you going?’
v06: Instead, because I have told you these things, sorrow has filled your hearts.
v07: Nevertheless, I tell you the truth: it is better for you that I go away. For if I do not go away, the Advocate will not come to you; but if I go, I will send him to you.
v08: And when he comes, he will expose the world concerning sin, righteousness, and judgment:
v09: concerning sin, because they do not believe in me;
v10: concerning righteousness, because I am going to the Father and you will see me no longer;
v11: and concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged.
</p>

## The Spirit of Truth

<p>

## Sorrow Turned to Joy

<p>
v16: In a little while you will no longer see me, and then a little while later you will see me.”
v17: Then some of his disciples said to one another, “What does he mean by telling us, ‘In a little while you will not see me, and then a little while later you will see me,’ and, ‘Because I am going to the Father’?
v18: So they kept asking, “What does he mean by ‘a little while’? We do not know what he is talking about.”
v19: Jesus knew that they wanted to question him, so he said to them, “Are you discussing with one another what I meant when I said, ‘In a little while you will not see me, and then a little while later you will see me’?
v20: Truly, truly, I tell you: you will weep and mourn, but the world will rejoice. You will be sorrowful, but your sorrow will turn into joy.
v21: When a woman is giving birth, she has pain because her hour has come. But when the child is born, she no longer remembers the anguish because of her joy that a human being has been born into the world.
v22: So you also have sorrow now. But I will see you again, and your hearts will rejoice, and no one will take your joy from you.
</p>

## Ask the Father in My Name

<p>

## You Will Be Scattered

<p>
v29: His disciples said, “Look, now you are speaking plainly and not using a figure of speech.
v30: Now we know that you know everything and do not need anyone to question you. Because of this, we believe that you came from God.”
v31: Jesus answered them, “Do you believe now?
v32: Look, an hour is coming—and has come—when each of you will be scattered to your own home and you will leave me alone. Yet I am not alone, because the Father is with me.
v33: I have told you these things so that in me you may have peace. In the world you have affliction. But take courage: I have overcome the world.”
</p>

## Notes

v02: The persecutors will imagine that killing Jesus’ followers offers worship or service to God. The verse exposes their self-deception; it does not authorize religious violence.

v05: Jesus says no one now asks where he is going, although questions were voiced earlier. The statement addresses the disciples’ present absorption in sorrow, not the erasure of the earlier exchange.

v08–11: The Spirit will expose or convict the world concerning sin, righteousness, and judgment; the following clauses give the Gospel’s own explanation of each term.

v13: ‘All the truth’ describes the Spirit’s faithful guidance from what he hears, not independent speech.

v21: The labor image retains a woman’s bodily pain and joy and calls the newborn a human being, emphasizing a person’s arrival into the world.

v23: Two related verbs can distinguish asking Jesus a question from making a request to the Father. English ‘ask’ covers both, so the context supplies the distinction.

v32: The disciples will leave Jesus alone, yet he denies being truly alone because the Father is with him.

## Vocabulary

v02: **λατρεία (latreia)**: Worship or sacred service.

v08: **ἐλέγχω (elenchō)**: Expose, convict, reprove, or demonstrate wrong.

v33: **θλῖψις (thlipsis)**: Affliction, pressure, or distress.
