## The Way, the Truth, and the Life

<p>
v01: “Do not let your hearts be troubled. Trust in God; trust also in me.
v02: In my Father’s house are many dwelling places. If it were not so, would I have told you that I am going to prepare a place for you?
v03: And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and take you to myself, so that where I am, you may be also.
v04: And you know the way to where I am going.”
v05: Thomas said, “Lord, we do not know where you are going. How can we know the way?”
v06: Jesus said, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.
v07: If you have known me, you will also know my Father. From now on you know him and have seen him.”
v08: Philip said, “Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us.”
v09: Jesus said, “Have I been with you so long, and you still do not know me, Philip? Whoever has seen me has seen the Father. How can you say, ‘Show us the Father’?
v10: Do you not trust that I am in the Father and the Father is in me? The words I speak to you I do not speak from myself. The Father remaining in me does his works.
v11: Trust me that I am in the Father and the Father is in me. Otherwise, trust because of the works themselves.
v12: Truly, truly, I tell you, whoever trusts in me will also do the works I do, and will do greater works than these because I am going to the Father.
v13: Whatever you ask in my name, I will do, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son.
v14: If you ask me anything in my name, I will do it.
</p>

## Another Advocate

<p>
v15: “If you love me, you will keep my commandments.
v16: And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Advocate to be with you forever:
v17: the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive because it neither sees nor knows him. You know him because he remains with you and will be in you.
v18: I will not leave you as orphans; I am coming to you.
v19: In a little while the world will no longer see me, but you will see me. Because I live, you also will live.
v20: On that day you will know that I am in my Father, you are in me, and I am in you.
v21: Whoever has my commandments and keeps them is the one who loves me. The one who loves me will be loved by my Father; I too will love that person and reveal myself to them.”
v22: Judas—not Iscariot—said, “Lord, what has happened that you are going to reveal yourself to us and not to the world?”
v23: Jesus answered, “If anyone loves me, they will keep my word. My Father will love them, and we will come to them and make our dwelling with them.
v24: Whoever does not love me does not keep my words. The word you hear is not mine but belongs to the Father who sent me.
v25: I have spoken these things to you while remaining with you.
v26: But the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you everything and remind you of everything I told you.
</p>

## My Peace I Give You

<p>
v27: “Peace I leave with you; my peace I give you. I do not give it to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled or afraid.
v28: You heard me tell you, ‘I am going away, and I am coming to you.’ If you loved me, you would rejoice that I am going to the Father, because the Father is greater than I.
v29: I have told you now before it happens so that when it happens, you may trust.
v30: I will not speak with you much longer, because the ruler of the world is coming. He has no claim on me.
v31: But this happens so that the world may know that I love the Father and do just as the Father commanded me. Get up. Let us leave here.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: The Greek can be read as two indicatives or two imperatives. Fluent uses the exhortational sense: “Trust in God; trust also in me.”

v02: `monai` means dwelling places or rooms. “Many mansions” imports a later English image not required by the word.

v03: Jesus’ promise centers on being with him, not on architectural speculation about the prepared place.

v06: “Way, truth, and life” are not three unrelated claims. Jesus identifies himself as the way to the Father precisely as truth and life.

v07: Manuscripts vary between forms equivalent to “if you have known me” and “if you had known me.” Fluent follows SBLGNT.

v10: Mutual indwelling and the Father’s works hold together Jesus’ unity with and dependence on the Father.

v12: “Greater works” are connected with Jesus going to the Father. Fluent does not define them as necessarily more spectacular miracles.

v13–14: Asking “in my name” means prayer aligned with Jesus’ person and mission, not a verbal formula guaranteeing any requested outcome.

v16: `paraklētos` has a broad range: advocate, helper, counselor, comforter. Fluent uses “Advocate” and retains the lexical range here.

v17: The Spirit “remains” with the disciples and “will be in” them, continuing John’s `menō` network.

v18: “I am coming to you” may include resurrection presence, Spirit-mediated presence, and final coming. Fluent does not reduce it to one horizon.

v23: The Father and Son make their “dwelling” with the one who loves and keeps Jesus’ word, echoing the dwelling places of v02.

v26: The Holy Spirit’s teaching and reminding ministry is tied specifically to Jesus’ words.

v28: “The Father is greater than I” is retained without using translation to solve later christological debates.

v30: “He has no claim on me” renders the idiom literally meaning he “has nothing in me.”
