## The Way, the Truth, and the Life

<p>
v01: “Do not let your hearts be troubled. Believe in God; believe also in me.
v02: In my Father’s house are many dwelling places. If it were not so, would I have told you that I am going to prepare a place for you?
v03: And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and take you to myself, so that where I am, you may be also.
v04: And you know the way to where I am going.”
v05: Thomas said to him, “Lord, we do not know where you are going. How can we know the way?”
v06: Jesus said to him, “I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.
v07: If you have known me, you will also know my Father. From now on you do know him and have seen him.”
v08: Philip said to him, “Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us.”
v09: Jesus said to him, “Have I been with you all this time, Philip, and still you do not know me? Whoever has seen me has seen the Father. How can you say, ‘Show us the Father’?
v10: Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in me? The words I say to you I do not speak on my own; the Father who remains in me does his works.
v11: Believe me that I am in the Father and the Father is in me. Otherwise, believe because of the works themselves.
v12: Truly, truly, I tell you: whoever believes in me will also do the works I do—and will do greater works than these—because I am going to the Father.
v13: Whatever you ask in my name, I will do it, so that the Father may be glorified in the Son.
v14: If you ask me for anything in my name, I will do it.
</p>

## Another Advocate

<p>
v15: If you love me, you will keep my commandments.
v16: And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Advocate to be with you forever—
v17: the Spirit of truth. The world cannot receive him because it neither sees him nor knows him. You know him because he remains with you and will be in you.
v18: I will not leave you as orphans; I am coming to you.
v19: In a little while the world will no longer see me, but you will see me. Because I live, you also will live.
v20: On that day you will know that I am in my Father, you are in me, and I am in you.
v21: Whoever has my commandments and keeps them is the one who loves me. The one who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love that person and reveal myself to them.”
v22: Judas—not Judas Iscariot—said to him, “Lord, what has happened that you are going to reveal yourself to us and not to the world?”
v23: Jesus answered him, “If anyone loves me, that person will keep my word. My Father will love them, and we will come to them and make our dwelling with them.
v24: Whoever does not love me does not keep my words. And the word you hear is not mine, but comes from the Father who sent me.
v25: I have told you these things while I remain with you.
v26: But the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you everything and remind you of everything I told you.
</p>

## My Peace I Give You

<p>
v27: Peace I leave with you; my peace I give you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled or afraid.
v28: You heard me tell you, ‘I am going away, and I am coming to you.’ If you loved me, you would rejoice that I am going to the Father, because the Father is greater than I.
v29: I have told you now, before it happens, so that when it happens you may believe.
v30: I will not speak with you much longer, for the ruler of the world is coming. He has no claim on me,
v31: but this is so the world may know that I love the Father and do exactly as the Father commanded me. Get up; let us leave this place.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: The two forms can be read as commands, ‘Believe,’ or statements, ‘You believe.’ The main text hears them as paired appeals to trust God and Jesus.

v02: ‘Dwelling places’ describes places to remain in the Father’s house. The Greek does not require the later image of separate luxurious mansions.

v06: Jesus names himself as the way, truth, and life, then speaks of access to the Father through him. The wording is retained without expanding it into a statement about people not under discussion.

v14: The pinned main text includes ‘me’: ‘If you ask me.’ The word carries textual-variant marks but is not omitted.

v16, 26: Paraclete has a range including advocate, helper, counselor, and one called alongside. ‘Advocate’ preserves a personal representative role.

v17: The Spirit now remains ‘with’ the disciples and ‘will be in’ them in the pinned wording.

v28: Jesus says the Father is greater than he. The translation leaves the relational comparison intact rather than resolving later theological questions inside the verse.

v30: ‘Has nothing in me’ is an idiom denying the ruler any claim, hold, or rightful ground in Jesus.

## Vocabulary

v02: **μονή (monē)**: Dwelling place, a place to remain.

v06: **ὁδός (hodos)**: Way, road, or path.

v16: **παράκλητος (paraklētos)**: Advocate, helper, or representative called alongside.
