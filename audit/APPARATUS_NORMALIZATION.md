# TSW Apparatus Normalization Report

## Governing standard

The stricter **Translation Rules & Conventions** control the Notes and Vocabulary apparatus. The repository philosophy document has been aligned with that standard.

### Notes

Notes are retained for significant textual variants, source-language ambiguity, public-versification differences, and interpretive possibilities that materially affect translation. They are not intended to become mini-commentaries, sermons, theological essays, defenses of TSW, or software/workflow documentation.

### Vocabulary

Vocabulary is selective and lexical. Entries identify a source-language term, transliteration, concise gloss, and only the contextual explanation needed to understand how the term functions in the verse.

## Normalization completed

- All 1,189 chapter files have exact `##Notes` and `##Vocabulary` section headings.
- Apparatus verse references were normalized to `vXX:` / `vXX–XX:` forms where applicable.
- Vocabulary presentation was normalized to source-term-first entries.
- Bullets, bold-label artifacts, footnote-label artifacts, and HTML paragraph markup were removed from apparatus presentation.
- Implementation language referring to plugins, websites, WordPress, shortcodes, GitHub mechanics, CSS, or reconciliation workflow was removed or rewritten in source-text terms.
- Commentary-heavy vocabulary language was tightened to lexical wording.
- Empty apparatus sections were retained where no note or vocabulary item was warranted; entries were not manufactured merely for symmetry.

## Final apparatus statistics

- Notes entries: 590
- Vocabulary entries: 4729
- Chapters with intentionally empty Notes: 798
- Chapters with intentionally empty Vocabulary: 19

## Final validation

No dangling note/vocabulary verse references, malformed apparatus headings, missing source-script vocabulary entries, implementation-language contamination, or structural apparatus errors remain under the final audit rules.
