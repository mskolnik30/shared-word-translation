## Book-level anchors

- Preserve **mind / mindset** language for the recurring `phroneō` network.
- Preserve **partnership / participation** for the `koinōnia` word-family.
- Preserve the civic link between **live as citizens** (1:27) and **citizenship** (3:20).
- Preserve deliberate **joy / rejoice** repetition.
- Preserve **gain / loss / account** language.
- Preserve the downward movement of Christ's self-giving in 2:6–8 and God's exaltation in 2:9–11.
- Follow the project-wide Romans 3 policy for `pistis Christou`: Christ's faithfulness as ground, human trust as response, with the grammatical alternative noted.

## Major F3 decisions

- 1:19 — `sōtēria` → **deliverance**, with salvation/vindication range in the note.
- 2:6 — `harpagmos` → **something to be exploited**, with the lexical debate preserved.
- 2:7 — **emptied himself**, without importing a theory of kenosis into the main text.
- 2:16 — **holding firmly to the word of life**, while “holding out” remains noted as possible.
- 3:9 — **the faithfulness of Christ**, aligned with Romans 3; current TSW Philippians discrepancy is flagged for later reconciliation.
- 3:20 — **our citizenship is in heaven**, without turning the verse into an escape-from-earth paraphrase.
- 4:5 — **The Lord is near**, leaving presence/coming nuance open.
- 4:12 — Vocabulary corrected to `mueō`, not `mystērion`.
- 4:13 — **I can do all things through the one who strengthens me** retained; contextual limitation belongs in the note, not the translation.
