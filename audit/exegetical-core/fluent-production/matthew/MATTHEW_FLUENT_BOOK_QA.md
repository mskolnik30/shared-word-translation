# Matthew — Fluent Book-Level QA

**Status: QA_PASSED / ready for a development PR after installation.**

Matthew contains **28 chapters and 1,071 public verse slots**. The pinned SBLGNT critical text has three intentional public-numbering gaps: **17:21, 18:11, and 23:14**. Fluent therefore contains **1,068 included verse labels**.

## Book-level anchors

- preserve Matthew's characteristic **kingdom of heaven** language rather than harmonizing it to Mark;
- preserve **fulfill / fulfillment** language and Matthew's scriptural rereading;
- preserve **righteousness**, **mercy**, **heavenly Father**, **Son of David**, and **Son of Man** networks;
- preserve **little faith / doubt** as a discipleship-perception theme;
- preserve **hand over**, **authority**, cross-bearing, service, forgiveness, and disciple-making;
- preserve the Gospel's Emmanuel inclusion: **“God with us” (1:23) → “I am with you always” (28:20)**;
- use the completed Fluent Mark as a Synoptic comparator without harmonizing Matthew's genuine differences.

## Critical-text public-numbering gaps

- Matthew 17:21
- Matthew 18:11
- Matthew 23:14

Matthew 16:2–3 is textually disputed but is present in the pinned SBLGNT main text, so those verses remain translated and are flagged rather than omitted.
