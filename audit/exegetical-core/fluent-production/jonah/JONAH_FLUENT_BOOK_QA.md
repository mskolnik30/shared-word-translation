## Book-level anchors
- Preserve repeated **great** language.
- Preserve **get up / got up** for the repeated `qûm` commission-response pattern.
- Preserve the Hebrew **ra'ah** network in apparatus while allowing natural English senses such as evil, trouble, disaster, displeasure, and discomfort.
- Preserve the sailors' movement into **fear of the LORD**.
- Preserve **appoint** for fish, plant, worm, and wind.
- Preserve **turn / relent** in Nineveh's repentance and God's response.
- Use **steadfast love** for God's `ḥesed` in 4:2.
- Keep Jonah 2:8's `ḥesed` construction explicitly disputed.
