# 1 John — Fluent Book-Level QA

**Status: QA_PASSED / ready for a development PR after installation.**

1 John contains 105 canonical verses:
- 1 John 1 — 10
- 1 John 2 — 29
- 1 John 3 — 24
- 1 John 4 — 21
- 1 John 5 — 21

## Book-level anchors

- Preserve **remain / abide** (`menō`) as a structural word-family.
- Preserve **know**, **love**, **light / darkness**, **truth / lie**, **born of God**, **testimony**, **life**, and **overcome** networks.
- Render `hilasmos` as **atoning sacrifice** in 2:2 and 4:10 without specifying a single atonement mechanism.
- Render `monogenēs` in 4:9 as **one and only**, aligned with Fluent John 1 policy.

## Major F3 decisions

- 1:4 — SBLGNT **our joy**; “your joy” remains a manuscript variant.
- 2:20 — critical text: **all of you know**; “you know all things” noted as variant.
- 2:27 — retain the no-need-of-teacher wording but do not universalize it beyond the anti-deceiver context.
- 3:6–9 — preserve the letter's strong present-tense statements about sin without explanatory weakening.
- 3:19–20 — preserve the compressed syntax; punctuation remains debated.
- 5:6 — water and blood remain uninterpreted in the main text.
- 5:7–8 — the *Comma Johanneum* is not included in the critical text.
- 5:16 — “sin leading to death” remains undefined.
- 5:18 — preserve the critical-text distinction between the one born of God and “the one who was born of God.”
- 5:20 — preserve “This one” rather than resolving its antecedent.
