# Fluent — Judges whole-book consistency review

Read all 618 English verses and the Notes/Vocabulary in all 21 chapters. Reread 265 selected Hebrew/source pairs for recurring language, names, counts, time markers, kinship, violence and textual uncertainties. This is an authoring self-check; independent scholarly review and reader testing remain pending. No publication is allowed.

Five verses were refined in chapters 5, 16 and 19:

- 5:18: made the life/death degree expression more natural while retaining poetic lineation.
- 5:28: changed the chariots’ hoofbeats to clatter, with their literal footsteps image explained in a note.
- 16:18: matched the natural full-heart-disclosure wording in verse 17, preserving both repetitions and the read tradition’s me.
- 19:8: restored stay as the father’s plural command inside his speech, rather than a narrated action.
- 19:25: identified her husband as the man who seizes his concubine, distinguishing him from the host.

The exact right-in-their-own-eyes refrain, distinct Spirit verbs, hand/grasp images, covenant and inheritance language, repeated accounts and nonmatching casualty totals remain. The review does not harmonize Judges with Joshua or turn the characters’ claims into narrator endorsement.
