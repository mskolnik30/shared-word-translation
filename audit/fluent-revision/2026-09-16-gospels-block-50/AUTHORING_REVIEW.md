# Intermediate checkpoint: Matthew 24–28 and Mark 1–3

Eight of the authorized fifty chapters are drafted and checked (366 verses). Forty-two remain: Mark 4–16, Luke 1–24, John 1–5. This is not a completed fifty-chapter block.

All 366 pinned Greek verse records were read before authoring. Matthew 24–25 involved reading prior Fluent after Greek and before final wording. Matthew 26–28 and Mark 1–3 were drafted before reading prior Fluent. TSW was consulted after drafting, as a comparator only. All new English and selective apparatus were assessed in their connected passages; 64 selected source/English/TSW passages were reread. These are drafting-assistant checks, not independent scholarly approval.

Substantive correction: Mark 3:14 formerly included “whom he also named apostles,” and its note incorrectly claimed that the pinned SBLGNT included it. The verified Greek lacks this clause. It is now excluded, with the variant noted. The old chapter review remains available in the parent Git object. Mark 1:21 also follows the pinned shorter wording rather than adding the separate entered-the-synagogue verb.

Other source-sensitive decisions include Matthew 24:36 “nor the Son”; 25:15–16 at-once placement across the verse boundary; 26:28 covenant without new; 27:16–17 Jesus Barabbas; 27:53 Jesus named to clarify whose resurrection supplies the timing; 28:17 hesitation with alternatives noted; Mark 1:41 anger; 2:26 Abiathar; 3:21 family with the associates alternative; 3:29 eternal sin. Recurring language, speakers, and ancient images are retained. No word-change quota applies.

Prior 106 ledgers and 957 chapters are byte-identical to the parent. TSW and Companion are unchanged. Prior approvals do not transfer to revised wording; Companion quotations and bindings require later reconciliation. Whole-book consistency and independent editorial reviews remain pending. Publication is not allowed.

Complete pinned Greek files for Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John are staged for continuation.
