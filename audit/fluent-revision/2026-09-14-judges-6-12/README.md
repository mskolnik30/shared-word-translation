# Fluent Judges 6–12 revision

Seven complete source-based draft chapters: 230 verses. Every Hebrew verse was read before drafting; every TSW comparator was read afterward. The ledger records exact original XML source hashes, before/after text, comparator, an individual rationale and editorial F0–F3 choice. A focused authoring reread covered 107 source/draft pairs. This is not independent scholarly review.

The reading voice keeps speakers and relationships clear while retaining biblical terms, recurring images, deliberate repetitions, ancient measures, specific gender and real harm. Notes identify consequential uncertainties without settling them through sermons. Jotham’s tree fable remains a connected story. Jephthah’s vow remains a burnt-offering vow, while its fulfillment is reported with the source’s restraint.

Normalized identical verses relative to TSW fell from 208 to 5; other verses at similarity 0.90 or higher fell from 22 to 7. All 12 remaining cases were inspected. Overlap is triage, not proof of fidelity, originality or comprehension, and no change quota applies.

See review.json for decisions and open questions; verification.json for cumulative source, structural and focused checks; focused-comparisons.json for the 107 selected pairs; overlap.json for before/after comparison; judges-verse-review.json for all 230 records. verify_batch.py reproduces the checks with locally retrieved pinned source files.
