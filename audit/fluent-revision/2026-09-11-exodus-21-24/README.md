# Fluent Exodus 21–24 revision

This batch drafts all 118 public verses of Exodus 21–24 from the pinned OSHB/WLC Hebrew. It continues the covenant instructions through the ratification scene. Fluent emphasizes readable case conditions, clear responsibility and speakers, recurring biblical terms, and selective notes that leave consequential questions open.

## Editorial work

Every Hebrew verse record was read, including written/read annotations. NET translator notes for chapters 21–22 were consulted after the Hebrew reading and before drafting; those for chapters 23–24 were consulted after drafting. All TSW comparators were read after drafting. English was composed from the Hebrew, not produced by automated synonym replacement. The assembled English received an authoring self-check, with corrections to the attack language in 21:14, consistency of peace offerings with 20:24, and the referent of teaching in 24:12.

The draft preserves enslaved people and unequal release conditions, family separation, bodily harm and death sanctions, monetary property language, legal exceptions, and all named peoples and numerical distinctions. Exodus 21:22 retains the children-coming-out expression and leaves the target of harm unspecified, with disputed readings explained briefly. The chapter 24 text retains blood on altar and people, direct seeing of God, and the leaders’ meal. No apologetic, doctrinal, or contemporary political applications were added.

The verse ledger records 58 F2 decisions, 58 F3 decisions, and two F0 retentions. The repeated injury formulas in 21:24–25 remain unchanged because their clear wording preserves the source’s literary and legal form. There is no minimum-change quota.
