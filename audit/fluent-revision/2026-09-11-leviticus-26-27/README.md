## Reading and translation decisions

The covenant discourse keeps its conditional progression and four sevenfold warnings. Agricultural abundance, peaceful rest, divine presence and upright walking remain connected with their later reversals. The draft retains metal skies and soil, yoke bars, the staff of bread, a windblown leaf, the land devouring people, explicit cannibalism, corpses of people and idols, and the uncircumcised heart. It also preserves repeated covenant remembrance and the ancestral order Jacob, Isaac, Abraham.

The final chapter separates valuation instructions into readable sentences while retaining all age bands, sex distinctions, silver amounts, the sanctuary standard, the added fifth and the seed-capacity measure. Purchased and family land have different Jubilee outcomes. The person subject to an ordinary assessed vow is not confused with the person under an irrevocable ban; the latter’s explicit death penalty is retained. Notes identify uncertainty about special vows, pronoun referents, the seller of the consecrated field, the payment date, the unclean animal and the circumstances of the ban.
