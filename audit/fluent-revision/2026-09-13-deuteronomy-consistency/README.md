# Fluent Deuteronomy consistency review

Completed a structural scan of all 34 chapters and an authoring self-check of 250 distinct source/English verse pairs. The selection consists of 94 verses containing seven specified lemma families and 160 focused comparison verses, with four overlaps. All main-text headings were read in sequence. This is not a new editorial rereading of every verse, independent scholarly review, or reader testing.

Five verses were refined: 27:20, 32:13, and 34:10–12. The changes make the repeated garment image recognizable, distinguish the agents in the song, and clarify the book’s closing comparison with Moses. Each refinement records its source hash, immediately preceding text, current text, TSW comparator, editorial choice and rationale. The existing 27–34 ledger was rebound while retaining its exact historical base, original before-text and prior decisions. Two accompanying notes were adjusted.

All 45 cumulative source-binding ledger audits pass, as do the whole-book structural scan, the focused assertions, the translation-family audit and git diff --check. All 959 Deuteronomy source records are bound exactly once. The 74 public/source numbering differences are enumerated in verification.json. Overall revised draft coverage remains 5,960 public verses in 192 chapters; no new chapters are claimed in this pass.

The source is [the pinned Open Scriptures Deuteronomy XML](https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb/blob/6a5db284c715c18b239422e57bb89684e6a19f00/wlc/Deut.xml). File SHA-256, Git blob identity and complete original XML verse hashes were verified. Written/read forms, numbering notes and other annotations remain bound to exact original bytes. TSW was read for the five refined verses after composing the refinements; it was a comparator, not their wording base.

See review.json for findings and limits; changes.json for the exact five changes; concordance.json and focused-comparisons.json for the selected Hebrew/English evidence; verify_consistency.py and verification.json for executable structural checks and results. Earlier batch reports are historical snapshots and are not silently rewritten.
