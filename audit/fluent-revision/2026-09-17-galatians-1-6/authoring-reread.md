# Galatians authoring self-reread

This is a focused bilingual drafting-assistant reread, not human or independent scholarly review. All 149 exact SBLGNT records were read before authoring, then compared verse by verse with the revised English, prior Fluent, and TSW.

The reread checked Paul's commissioning and chronology, including three years, fifteen days, and fourteen years (1:1, 18; 2:1); source-form Cephas and the named Jerusalem leaders; Paul's former persecution without generalizing it to Judaism; Titus and resisted compulsion; table-fellowship withdrawal, fear, hypocrisy, and public confrontation (2:11–14); the repeated πίστις Χριστοῦ phrases rendered as Christ's faithfulness while human believing is also stated (2:16, 20; 3:22); Abraham, promise, curse, the collective singular offspring, four hundred thirty years, angels and mediator; the guardian image; enslaved/free and male/female language; elemental powers, adoption as sons, bodily weakness without speculative diagnosis, Paul's maternal labor, and the difficult Hagar allegory.

The reread also retained castration in 5:12, freedom and voluntary service, the complete works/fruit lists, mutual burden-bearing and personal responsibility, large letters, the Israel of God without resolving its referent, and bodily marks. All decisions remain provisional.
