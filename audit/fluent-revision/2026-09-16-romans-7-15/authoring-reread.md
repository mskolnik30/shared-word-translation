Every Greek record in Romans 7:1–15:33 was read before English authoring. After the 245 independent drafts and rationales were complete, every generated verse was reread alongside its exact Greek payload, parent Fluent wording, and TSW comparator. The reread checked referents, logical connectors, verbal aspect where consequential, names, numbers, gendered terms, repeated vocabulary, quotations, source variant markers, and the continuity of Paul's argument. It did not constitute independent editorial or scholarly approval.

## Focused decisions

- Romans 7:1–6: kept the legal marriage illustration gender-specific, then preserved Paul's shift to the community's death with respect to the law and service in newness of Spirit.
- Romans 7:7–25: retained sin as the agent exploiting the commandment; preserved the unresolved identity and life-setting of the first-person “I,” the inner-person language, captivity imagery, “body of death,” and the concluding mind/flesh conflict.
- Romans 8:1–4: did not import the longer walking clause into 8:1; retained the pinned singular “you” in 8:2 and the sacrificial sense of “concerning sin” in 8:3.
- Romans 8:14–23: preserved “sons” where Roman adoption and inheritance status are explicit and “children” where the Greek changes terms; retained creation's labor pains and bodily redemption.
- Romans 8:26–30: kept prayer ignorance, inexpressible groaning, “all things work together,” and the full linked sequence foreknew–predestined–called–justified–glorified without adding a doctrinal system.
- Romans 8:31–39: retained the courtroom questions, handed-over Son, concrete affliction and lethal harm, slaughtered-sheep quotation, and the final exhaustive claim of inseparability.
- Romans 9:1–5: kept Paul's anguish for Israel, his possible self-curse, Israel's named privileges, and the compact Messiah/God doxological clause with its punctuation uncertainty noted.
- Romans 9:6–29: preserved Israel/Israel wordplay, Rebekah and the twins, love/hate, mercy/hardening, Pharaoh, potter and clay, vessels of wrath and mercy, Hosea's gendered relational imagery, remnant, and the Lord of Armies.
- Romans 9:30–10:21: maintained righteousness/faith/work repetitions, stumbling stone, `τέλος` as “culmination” with its semantic range noted, mouth/heart confession, universal “everyone,” the hearing-and-sending chain, the pinned “word of Christ,” and Israel's continuing place in the argument.
- Romans 11:1–16: kept Paul's own Israelite identity, seven thousand men, grace/remnant language, hardening, Israel's stumble not being an unrecoverable fall, jealousy, fullness, acceptance, and life from the dead.
- Romans 11:17–32: retained the wild/cultivated olive details, the root supporting Gentile believers, warnings against arrogance, Israel's own tree, partial hardening, “all Israel,” continued beloved status, irrevocable gifts and calling, and the two occurrences of “everyone” in 11:32.
- Romans 12:1–8: preserved embodied living sacrifice, transformation of mind, repeated “think” language, one body with differing functions, and the differing gifts and manners of service.
- Romans 12:9–21: kept the terse imperative rhythm, persecution, weeping, the ambiguity of “the lowly,” realistic qualification on peace, divine rather than personal vengeance, feeding an enemy, burning coals, and overcoming evil with good.
- Romans 13:1–7: translated Paul's claims about authorities, judgment, taxation, and the sword directly; did not silently universalize the descriptive claims beyond the passage or insert a modern political exception into the main text.
- Romans 13:8–14: preserved love as an ongoing debt and fulfillment of law, named prohibitions, harm to neighbor, waking, night/day movement, weapons of light, concrete vices, and putting on Christ.
- Romans 14:1–12: kept weak/strong terminology within the food-and-days dispute, mutual prohibition of contempt and judgment, shared thanksgiving, the pinned “God's judgment seat,” and individual accounting.
- Romans 14:13–23: preserved clean/unclean conscience language, real distress and destruction caused by food, kingdom priorities, mutual building, meat and wine, all three pinned harms in 14:21, private conviction, doubt, and the concluding faith/sin principle.
- Romans 15:1–13: kept the strong bearing others' weaknesses, Christ's self-denial, Scripture's encouragement, reciprocal welcome, Christ's service to the circumcised, the patriarchal promises, and the sequence of Gentile quotations.
- Romans 15:14–33: retained Paul's priestly mission metaphor, signs and wonders, Jerusalem–Illyricum geography, ambition not to build on another foundation, intended but uncompleted Spain journey, Macedonia and Achaia's gift, spiritual/material reciprocity, danger in Judea, uncertainty of acceptance in Jerusalem, and mutual refreshment.
