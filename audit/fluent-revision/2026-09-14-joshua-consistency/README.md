Read all 658 English verses and the headings, Notes and Vocabulary across Joshua 1–24. Reread 200 selected Hebrew/English pairs for recurring terms, names, numbers, speakers, harm, textual choices and remaining-land claims. This is authoring self-review, not independent scholarly approval or reader testing.
