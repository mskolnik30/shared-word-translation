## Findings retained in the draft

- Luke 9 preserves the Twelve's power and authority, every travel restriction, dust testimony, Herod's responsibility for John's death, Bethsaida, five loaves, two fish, about five thousand men, groups of about fifty, and twelve baskets. Peter says “God's Messiah.” The passion saying names elders, chief priests, and scribes; discipleship includes a cross “every day”; and Luke's form warns of losing oneself. The transfiguration occurs about eight days later during prayer, calls Jesus the Chosen One, and describes his coming *exodos* at Jerusalem. The only son's violent suffering, the disciples' failure, handing over into human hands, child beside Jesus, and outsider acting in Jesus' name remain. Jesus sets his face toward Jerusalem; the Samaritan village's stated reason, James and John's request for consuming fire, Jesus' brief rebuke without the later expanded saying, and movement to another village are retained. The three demanding call scenes keep their sharpness.
- Luke 10 preserves seventy-two messengers, pairs, lambs among wolves, all travel restrictions, “person of peace,” wages, stable lodging, offered food, healing, and kingdom nearness to both receptive and rejecting towns. Chorazin, Bethsaida, Tyre, Sidon, Capernaum, Hades, Satan's fall, snakes, scorpions, heavenly names, rejoicing in the Holy Spirit, infants, and mutual Father-Son knowledge remain. The legal expert's testing motive and self-justification frame the Samaritan account; the attacked man's stripping, beating, and near death, the priest and Levite's seeing and avoidance, the Samaritan's identity, oil, wine, animal, inn, two denarii, continuing cost, and Jesus' reversal toward becoming a neighbor are all explicit. Martha's real service and distress and Mary's protected learner posture remain; the difficult “few things—or one” wording is preserved.
- Luke 11 keeps Luke's shorter prayer: Father, holy name, kingdom, daily bread, sins, human debtors, and testing, without importing later clauses. The midnight request retains three loaves, a locked shared sleeping space, and the difficult *anaideia*. The asking sayings culminate specifically in the Holy Spirit. The mute man's speech, Beelzebul accusation, heavenly-sign demand, divided house, finger of God, stronger attacker, seven worse spirits, woman's bodily maternal blessing, and Jesus' hearing-and-keeping response remain. Jonah himself is the sign; no three-day explanation is added. The queen of the South, Ninevites, healthy/bad eye ambiguity, ceremonial washing, greed, difficult inside/alms saying, mint, rue, justice, love of God, unmarked graves, unbearable loads, murdered prophets, Wisdom of God, blood from Abel to Zechariah, key of knowledge, and final verbal trap remain unsmoothed.
