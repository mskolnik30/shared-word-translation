# Fluent Exodus 25–27 revision

This batch drafts all 98 verses of Exodus 25–27 from the pinned OSHB/WLC Hebrew. The passage describes contributions, sanctuary furnishings and construction, the altar and courtyard, and the provision of oil. Fluent uses clear instructions while preserving repeated measurements and material specifications. Every verse has an exact source binding, before/after wording, TSW comparator, editorial F0–F3 choice, and its own rationale.

## Source-based editorial work

All 98 Hebrew verse records were read before the English was drafted. Supplemental NET notes and every TSW comparator were read afterward. The assembled English was reread as a connected passage. The authoring check clarified the use of the table’s pouring vessels and removed an over-specific direction for the altar grating. The unknown taḥash material remains named and explained; the exact construction of frames, corner joints, and some vessels remains qualified.

The wording distinguishes the inner curtains from the outer tent, the dividing curtain from the entrance screen, the Holy Place from the Most Holy Place, and the gold furnishings from bronze courtyard equipment. Ark, testimony, atonement cover, cherubim, cubit, and other biblical terms remain visible with selective explanations. The difference between aron, the chest here, and the earlier tebah, the flood ark and Moses’ basket, is explained. No symbolism for every material, color, or measurement is invented.
