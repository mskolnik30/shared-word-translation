## Other selected passages

- **Genesis 1:1–5:** The English distinguishes narration from speech, reduces mechanical conjunctions and keeps the day-one refrain. Notes acknowledge alternative opening syntax and spirit/wind/breath; the capitalized Spirit is identified as an English editorial choice.
- **Exodus 3:14; Deuteronomy 6:4:** Divine naming and the confession remain compact; these are not suitable places to manufacture novelty. These brief comparisons do not settle their full exegetical questions.
- **Leviticus 19:18; Numbers 6:24–26:** Direct commands, neighbor love and the threefold blessing remain. Fluent's pronouns make the blessing easier to follow without changing the divine referent or imagery.
- **Ruth 1:16:** Shorter English clauses improve sustained reading while retaining repeated wherever and the people/God pledges.
- **Job 19:25–27:** The unnamed redeemer and “from my flesh” remain open; notes state competing understandings. Rendering kidneys with heart differs from Revelation's literal kidneys and deserves contextual consistency review, not an automatic global replacement.
- **Psalm 23:1–6:** Source superscription, shepherd/host images, first-to-second-person movement, darkness alternative and return/dwell issue remain visible. “Length of days” remains stiff and is a candidate for reader testing, not a proven source error.
- **Proverbs 8:22:** “Acquired” is qualified by the note's possess/create range; Wisdom's personified voice is not replaced by a doctrinal identification.
- **Ecclesiastes 3:1–8:** The birth/death, destruction/building, grief/joy and love/hate pairs remain. The identical 3:6–8 run is warranted parallel poetry; deliberately varying it would conflict with the repetition rule.
- **Isaiah 7:14; 9:6; 42:14; 53:4–6; 66:13:** Young woman, pregnancy, address in the naming verb, child/titles, maternal divine imagery and the servant's bodily suffering remain. The main text does not import a named later figure into these passages. This targeted finding is not approval of every messianic rendering.
- **Jeremiah 7:5–7:** The conditional clauses and then-clause were separated by paragraph markers. Joined them without changing a Scripture word.
- **Daniel 7:13:** Aramaic son-of-man comparison, clouds, Ancient of Days and presentation remain; the movement is not reversed or converted into a modern title explanation.
- **Micah 6:8:** Generic human address and justice/kindness/humble walking remain; the triad is not paraphrased as a modern ethical program.
- **John 1:1,18; 3:16:** Familiar opening retained. The explicit source reading “God” in 1:18 differs from TSW's “Son” and is noted. “One-of-a-kind” is understandable but deserves sustained voice testing across John; it is not counted as proof of superior accuracy.
- **Romans 3:22; Galatians 2:16,20; 3:28:** Faith/faithfulness remains a consequential interpretive choice. Notes may disclose the alternative under Fluent's philosophy, but an editorial heading should not repeat one choice as an unquestioned conclusion. Neutralized the Galatians heading. Jew/Greek, enslaved/free and male-and-female remain explicit.
- **Philippians 2:6–11:** Form, self-emptying, slavery, humanity, cross, exaltation, knees and tongues remain. Clause boundaries differ from TSW; simple verse-aligned similarity can understate passage-level overlap.
- **1 Timothy 2:12:** Specific woman/man language and the first-person prohibition remain. This single-verse check does not settle the disputed scope or sense of the authority verb; do not certify doctrinal neutrality from a surface wording check.
- **Revelation 2:23:** Replaced “kill her children with death” with “put her children to death.” The existing note retains the literal expression and plague possibility. Children, kidneys/hearts and recompense remain; no explanation of the children's identity is inserted.
- **Revelation 7:5–8; Joshua 12:10–23:** Read every selected source entry. Tribal/place names, twelve-thousand counts and repeated one justify the longest identical runs. These are positive fidelity cases, not plagiarism findings.
- **Revelation 13:8:** The final time phrase remains awkward, but the note accurately presents its two attachments. Refer this to sentence-level reading review; do not silently settle the ambiguity to improve an overlap score.
- **Revelation 21:7:** Recast the generic singular recipient as “that person” while preserving “my son.” The change improves referent tracking and keeps the sonship formula.
- **Revelation 22:14:** The source's robe washing and tree/city access remain; commandment-keeping is not substituted from another reading.

Additional checks read Greek transition verses for the Luke 3/6 paragraph changes and the lexical/rhetorical notes in 1 Corinthians 7 and 2 Corinthians 4. These are supplementary checks, not extra items added to the 103-comparison count. Source bindings for all edited chapters were rerun. No claim is made to have freshly read all 31,083 verses against their sources during this audit.
