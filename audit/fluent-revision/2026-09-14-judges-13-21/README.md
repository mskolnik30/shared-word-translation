# Fluent — Judges 13–21

Nine complete chapters and 243 source-based draft verses complete the Judges draft (618 verses). Cumulative coverage is 7,236 verses in 237 chapters. Every source verse was read before drafting, every TSW comparator afterward, and 125 selected Hebrew/draft pairs were reread for focused risks. Each verse has a rationale, before/after/comparator text, editorial F0–F3 choice and exact source-record hash.

The reading voice retains repeated eyes, hand, heart and inheritance language, makes changes of speaker clearer, and preserves the narrative’s violence and conflicting accounts. Notes explain consequential textual choices without sermons. Riddles and poems remain inside verse paragraphs. See review.json for decisions and open questions.

## Evidence and limits

- judges-verse-review.json: chapter bindings and all 243 verse records.
- overlap.json: before/after comparison. Normalized identical verses decrease from 217 to 2; near-identical nonidentical verses from 26 to 5. All seven high-overlap cases were inspected. Similarity is triage, not a test of fidelity, originality or comprehension.
- focused-comparisons.json: 125 second-pass authoring comparisons with exact source hashes and annotations.
- verify_batch.py and verification.json: all 51 cumulative source-binding audits, structure, selected risk assertions, provenance preservation, translation-family audit and git diff --check.
