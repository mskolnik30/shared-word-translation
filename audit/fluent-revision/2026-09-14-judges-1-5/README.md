# Fluent Judges 1–5

145 source-based draft verses across five complete chapters. Every Hebrew verse was read before drafting; all TSW comparator verses were read afterward. Eighty-eight selected source/draft pairs were reread for focused risks, including the complete song in chapter 5.

The source is the pinned Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible record in judges-verse-review.json: 618 XML records in the full book. The 145 records in this batch align one to one with the public chapter/verse references. Exact original XML substrings, including written/read notes, are hashed.

All 49 cumulative verse-ledger audits, chapter structure, focused assertions, translation-family audit and git diff --check pass. This is structural/source-binding QA and authoring self-check, not independent scholarly review. Editorial review and reader testing remain pending; publication is not allowed.

Normalized identical verses with TSW changed from 126 to 2; near-identical nonidentical verses from 19 to 12. These figures are triage only. All high-overlap cases were considered against the source; common wording remains where it serves meaning and literary form. The chapter 5 poem preserves its own images and repetitions alongside chapter 4’s narrative.

See review.json for choices and remaining questions, judges-verse-review.json for every verse, focused-comparisons.json for reread pairs, overlap.json for comparator scores, and verification.json for checks. NET translators’ notes for Judges 3 and 5 were consulted selectively after drafting; URLs are in the verse ledger. No modern translation supplied a drafting template.
