# Mark 9–12 authoring review

Status: editorial draft; independent scholarly review and reader testing remain pending. Publication is not allowed. This is the drafting assistant's source comparison and self-reread, not an independent review.

The complete pinned Mark file was verified against the full SHA-256 and Git blob identity inherited from the preceding source-bound ledger. Every Greek verse in chapters 9–10 was read before drafting those chapters; every Greek verse in chapters 11–12 was read before drafting those chapters. All 176 verse drafts and their individual rationales were written before reading the parent Fluent chapters. Prior Fluent was then read in full, including its apparatus. TSW was used afterward as a comparator. No existing English translation supplied a drafting template, and no word-change target was imposed.

The 99 references selected in config.json were displayed and reread with exact pinned Greek, the new English, and TSW. These include all provisional F3 passages plus selected names, numbers, quotation details, movement between speakers, and recurring vocabulary. The final records are bound in focused-comparisons.json. Automated assertions record selected details as regression checks; they do not establish translation quality.

The review preserved the unresolved kingdom-coming saying, the relationship between Elijah and suffering, the father's belief/unbelief, the spirit's actions without a medical diagnosis, prayer without the fasting addition, human hands, and three-day resurrection language. The argument question in 9:16 explicitly retains both parties. The wording in 9:22 was refined to preserve fire **and** water. A closing quotation mark was supplied at 9:41 before the next saying. Salt and fire remain difficult images rather than an inserted interpretation. Source omissions at 9:44 and 9:46 remain numbering gaps.

The Greek at 10:2 does not name Pharisees. Both parent Fluent and TSW name them, but the new draft follows the actual source and records the variant in a note. The source includes the joining clause at 10:7 despite variant markers; it is retained. Against her in 10:11, the woman's agency in 10:12, the unqualified saying at 10:24, mothers without fathers and with persecutions at 10:30, the distinction between servant and slave, and the compact ransom saying are preserved. Road language links the rich man's encounter, the journey, and Bartimaeus. The two requests at 10:36 and 10:51 have matching English wording.

In chapter 11 the return promise for the colt has a documented alternative construction. The named villages, two disciples, single colt, fields, Davidic acclamation, evening departure, next-day temple action, and fig-season tension are retained. The prayer saying keeps the difference between received and future possession. The 11:26 gap remains. At 11:31 the draft restores the question **What should we say?**, present in the pinned text but absent from both parent Fluent and TSW. Variant markers are not treated as instructions to remove the enclosed source words.

In chapter 12 slave renders doulos consistently with 10:44. Killing precedes the son's expulsion from the vineyard. The final tax question belongs to 12:14 in this source; parent Fluent and TSW place it under 12:15. The new public labels align it with the pinned Greek. Seven brothers, the resurrection clause, the distinction between being like angels and becoming angels, and the repeated patriarchal names remain. The scribe's three terms are not expanded into Jesus' four-term wording. The Psalm quotation says beneath the feet, without adding a footstool. The widow's two coins and the quadrans comparison are both retained; the neutral heading and the whole livelihood prevent a translation-level decision between praise and critique.

Notes and vocabulary have been shortened to selective aids. Earlier interpretive exposition has not been treated as source evidence. Old chapter reviews remain recoverable in the Git parent; the new wording does not inherit approval or Companion quotation bindings. All earlier draft coverage, earlier ledgers, TSW, Companion, and pending whole-book reviews must be preserved by verification.
