# Fluent Leviticus 1–3 revision

All 50 verses of Leviticus 1–3 have a source-based draft, selective notes and vocabulary, and a verse-specific rationale. These chapters form the opening connected instructions for burnt, grain and peace offerings. Cumulative revised coverage is Genesis 1–50, Exodus 1–40, Leviticus 1–3 and James 1–5: 2,904 verses in 98 chapters.

The draft helps readers follow the transfer between the person bringing the offering and the priests. It keeps the animal’s required sex distinct from the generally inclusive human referent, preserves slaughter and blood handling explicitly, and distinguishes splashing mammal blood from draining bird blood. The head is burned before the bird’s body is prepared. Uncertain bird and liver anatomy receives short notes.

Grain instructions retain different preparations, the memorial portion and priestly remainder, all the frankincense, the distinction between presentation and altar burning for leaven and honey, and the repeated salt requirement. Peace offerings retain both animal sexes, all three anatomical lists, the sheep’s fat tail without importing it into the goat instructions, and the explicit food image. Offering terminology remains connected to Exodus. No single theory of sacrifice is imposed on the categories.
