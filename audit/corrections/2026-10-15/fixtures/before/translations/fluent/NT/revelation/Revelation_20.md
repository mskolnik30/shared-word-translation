## The Thousand Years

<p>
v01: I saw an angel coming down from heaven with the key to the abyss and a great chain in his hand.
v02: He seized the dragon, the ancient serpent who is the Devil and Satan, and bound him for a thousand years.
v03: He threw him into the abyss, shut it and sealed it over him, so that he would no longer lead the nations astray until the thousand years were completed. After that he must be released for a little while.
v04: I saw thrones, and those seated on them were given authority to judge. I also saw the souls of those beheaded because of the testimony of Jesus and God's word, and those who had not worshiped the beast or its image or received its mark on their foreheads or hands. They came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years.
v05: The rest of the dead did not come to life until the thousand years were completed. This is the first resurrection.
v06: Blessed and holy is the one who shares in the first resurrection. The second death has no authority over them; they will be priests of God and of Christ and will reign with him for a thousand years.
</p>

## The Defeat of Satan

<p>
v07: When the thousand years are completed, Satan will be released from his prison
v08: and will go out to lead astray the nations at the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them for battle. Their number is like the sand of the sea.
v09: They spread over the breadth of the earth and surrounded the camp of the holy ones and the beloved city. Fire came down from heaven and devoured them.
v10: The devil who led them astray was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur, where the beast and the false prophet are. They will be tormented day and night forever and ever.
</p>

## The Final Judgment

<p>
v11: I saw a great white throne and the one seated on it. Earth and heaven fled from his face, and no place was found for them.
v12: I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and books were opened. Another book was opened, the book of life. The dead were judged according to their works from what was written in the books.
v13: The sea gave up its dead, and Death and Hades gave up their dead. Each was judged according to their works.
v14: Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. This is the second death: the lake of fire.
v15: Anyone not found written in the book of life was thrown into the lake of fire.
</p>

## Notes

v04: The syntax connects the beheaded witnesses with those who refused the beast; it does not explicitly settle whether two distinct groups are intended. “Came to life” and the thousand years are retained without an added chronological system.

v08: Gog and Magog are named in the vision; they are not replaced with later geographical identifications.

v14: Death and Hades remain distinct from the lake of fire into which they are thrown.

## Vocabulary

v04: **πελεκίζω (pelekizō)**: Execute by beheading, associated with an axe.
