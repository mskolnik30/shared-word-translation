## The Empty Tomb

<p>
v01: But very early on the first day of the week, the women came to the tomb carrying the spices they had prepared.
v02: They found the stone rolled away from the tomb.
v03: But when they entered, they did not find the body.
v04: While they were perplexed about this, suddenly two men stood beside them in gleaming clothes.
v05: Terrified, the women bowed their faces to the ground. The men said to them, “Why are you looking for the living one among the dead?
v06: [He is not here; he has risen.] Remember how he spoke to you while he was still in Galilee,
v07: saying that the Son of Man must be handed over into the hands of sinful people, be crucified, and rise on the third day.”
v08: Then they remembered his words.
v09: Returning from the tomb, they reported all these things to the Eleven and to everyone else.
v10: The women were Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the others with them. They kept telling these things to the apostles,
v11: but their words seemed like nonsense to the apostles, and they did not believe the women.
v12: [But Peter got up and ran to the tomb. Stooping down, he saw only the linen cloths. Then he went away, wondering to himself what had happened.]
</p>

## The Road to Emmaus

<p>
v13: Now that same day, two of them were walking to a village named Emmaus, about sixty stadia from Jerusalem.
v14: They were talking with each other about everything that had happened.
v15: As they talked and discussed these things, Jesus himself came near and began walking with them,
v16: but their eyes were kept from recognizing him.
v17: He asked them, “What are these words you are exchanging as you walk?” They stopped, looking downcast.
v18: One of them, named Cleopas, answered, “Are you the only visitor staying in Jerusalem who does not know the things that have happened there in these days?”
v19: He asked them, “What things?” They replied, “The things about Jesus the Nazarene. He was a man who became a prophet, powerful in deed and word before God and all the people,
v20: and our chief priests and rulers handed him over to be condemned to death and crucified him.
v21: But we had hoped that he was the one about to redeem Israel. Besides all this, today is the third day since these things happened.
v22: Moreover, some women from our group astonished us. They were at the tomb early this morning,
v23: and when they did not find his body, they came saying they had even seen a vision of angels, who said he was alive.
v24: Some of those with us went to the tomb and found it just as the women had said, but they did not see him.”
v25: Then Jesus said to them, “How foolish you are, and how slow in heart to believe everything the prophets spoke!
v26: Was it not necessary for the Messiah to suffer these things and enter his glory?”
v27: Beginning with Moses and all the Prophets, he interpreted for them the things about himself in all the Scriptures.
v28: As they approached the village where they were going, Jesus acted as though he were going farther.
v29: But they strongly urged him, “Stay with us, because it is nearly evening and the day is almost over.” So he went in to stay with them.
v30: When he reclined at the table with them, he took the bread, blessed it, broke it, and began giving it to them.
v31: Then their eyes were opened and they recognized him, but he vanished from their sight.
v32: They said to each other, “Were not our hearts burning within us while he spoke to us on the road and opened the Scriptures to us?”
v33: They got up that very hour and returned to Jerusalem. There they found the Eleven and those with them gathered together,
v34: who were saying, “The Lord really has risen and has appeared to Simon!”
v35: Then the two told what had happened on the road and how Jesus became known to them in the breaking of the bread.
</p>

## Jesus Appears to the Disciples

<p>
v36: While they were saying these things, Jesus himself stood among them.
v37: Startled and terrified, they thought they were seeing a spirit.
v38: He said to them, “Why are you troubled, and why do doubts arise in your hearts?
v39: Look at my hands and my feet—it is I myself. Touch me and see, because a spirit does not have flesh and bones as you see that I have.”
v40: [After saying this, he showed them his hands and feet.]
v41: While they still could not believe because of joy and amazement, he asked them, “Do you have anything here to eat?”
v42: They gave him a piece of broiled fish,
v43: and he took it and ate it in front of them.
</p>

## Scripture, Witness, and Promise

<p>
v44: Then he told them, “These are the words I spoke to you while I was still with you: everything written about me in the Law of Moses, the Prophets, and the Psalms must be fulfilled.”
v45: Then he opened their minds to understand the Scriptures.
v46: He told them, “This is what is written: the Messiah must suffer and rise from the dead on the third day,
v47: and repentance for the forgiveness of sins must be proclaimed in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem.
v48: You are witnesses of these things.
v49: And look, I am sending my Father’s promise upon you. But stay in the city until you are clothed with power from on high.”
</p>

## Jesus Blesses Them and Is Taken Up

<p>
v50: Then Jesus led them out as far as Bethany, lifted up his hands, and blessed them.
v51: While he was blessing them, he left them [and was carried up into heaven].
v52: [After worshiping him,] they returned to Jerusalem with great joy
v53: and were continually in the temple courts blessing God.
</p>

## Notes

v06: ‘He is not here; he has risen’ is bracketed in the pinned source because of textual variation.

v10–11: Luke names Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary associated with James, and other women as the first witnesses to the empty tomb report. The apostles initially dismiss their testimony.

v12: Peter’s run to the tomb is bracketed in the pinned source because of textual variation.

v40: Jesus’ act of showing his hands and feet is bracketed in the pinned source because of textual variation.

v51–52: The clauses ‘and was carried up into heaven’ and ‘after worshiping him’ are bracketed in the pinned source. The departure, return to Jerusalem, great joy, and continual temple praise remain in the surrounding text.

## Vocabulary

v26: **χριστός (christos)**: Messiah or Christ, the anointed one.

v32: **γραφή (graphē)**: Scripture or sacred writing.

v47: **ἄφεσις (aphesis)**: Forgiveness, release, or liberation from sins.
