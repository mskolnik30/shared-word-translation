# 2 John book guide

## Begin Here

Second John is a brief message from “the elder” to “the chosen lady and her children,” possibly a woman and household or a local church and its members. It holds truth and love together while warning against traveling teachers who deny Jesus Christ coming in the flesh.

## At a Glance

- **Kind of writing:** A short pastoral letter.
- **Primary setting:** A Christian household or congregation participating in networks of traveling teachers.
- **Central movement:** Walk in received truth and love; do not materially support teaching that abandons the embodied Christ.
- **Time to read aloud:** About 3 minutes.

## What Is Going On?

Hospitality could provide a teacher with housing, resources, and public endorsement. The warning not to receive a deceiver therefore concerns partnership in harmful teaching, not ordinary courtesy to every person with a different belief.

## The Book's Movement

1. **Verses 1–3:** Truth and love frame the greeting.
2. **Verses 4–6:** The old command remains: love one another by walking in God's commands.
3. **Verses 7–11:** Test teaching about the embodied Christ and do not sponsor deception.
4. **Verses 12–13:** The elder prefers face-to-face conversation and complete joy.

## Watch For

- Truth, love, command, walking, and remaining.
- The difference between welcome and endorsement.
- Embodied presence in both Christ's coming and the elder's hoped-for visit.

## A Thread to Carry

Truth without love becomes harsh, and love without truth can finance harm. Second John asks a community to practice both through faithful relationships and discerning use of its resources.
