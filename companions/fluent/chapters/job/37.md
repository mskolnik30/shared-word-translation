### Follow the Movement

Its movement through My Heart Trembles at This, He Says to the Snow, Fall on the Earth, The Clouds Turn According to His Guidance, Stop and Consider the Wonders of God, and finally Teach Us What We Should Say to Him controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 6–10 observes: Snow, rain, animal shelter, cold, and ice are all presented as responsive to divine command. Human labor pauses under weather people cannot control. The translation note at verse 13 observes: The storm may serve discipline, the needs of the land, or steadfast love. Elihu allows the same natural event to have different divine purposes rather than one simple interpretation. One vocabulary entry at verse 2 identifies רֹגֶז (rogez) “Rumbling,” “roar,” or “agitation.” Used of the sound of divine thunder.
