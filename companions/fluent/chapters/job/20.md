### Follow the Movement

The order of Job 20 matters. Its movement through Zophar: The Joy of the Wicked Is Brief, His Children Will Seek the Favor of the Poor, He Will Not Enjoy What He Took, and finally God Will Send His Burning Anger controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 12–16 observes: Wickedness is pictured as sweet food deliberately savored, which then transforms into poison inside the body. One vocabulary entry at verse 2 identifies חוּשׁ (chush) “Agitation” or “haste.” Zophar’s inward urgency to respond.
