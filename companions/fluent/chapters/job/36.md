### Follow the Movement

Its movement through Elihu: I Have More to Say for God, God Is Mighty and Does Not Reject, God Delivers the Afflicted Through Affliction, Beware That Wrath Does Not Entice You, God Is Exalted in His Power, and finally He Draws Up the Drops of Water controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 5 observes: “does not reject” is a striking claim after Job repeatedly experienced rejection. Elihu describes divine might as compatible with attention rather than indifference. The translation note at verse 8–12 observes: Elihu interprets suffering as a means through which God exposes arrogance and opens the ear to discipline. The framework remains corrective and pedagogical. One vocabulary entry at verse 3 identifies צֶדֶק (tsedeq) “Righteousness.” Elihu seeks to attribute righteousness to his Maker.
