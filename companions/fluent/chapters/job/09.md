The translation note at verse 8 observes: “heights of the sea” — The Hebrew bamot yam may mean waves, heights, or backs of the sea. The image presents God walking above the chaotic waters. One vocabulary entry at verse 2 identifies צָדַק (tsadaq) “To be right” or “be justified.” Job asks how a human can be right in a case with God.
