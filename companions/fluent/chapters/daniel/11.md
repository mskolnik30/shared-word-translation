### Follow the Movement

The order of Daniel 11 matters. Its 12 named sections move from “Kings of Persia and Greece” through “A Tax Collector and a Despised One” to “The Final Conflict of North and South.” Read that complete arc before using one detail to characterize the chapter as a whole.

The translation note at verse 2–4 observes: The sequence of Persian kings and the mighty Greek king has usually been correlated with Persian rulers culminating in Xerxes and then Alexander the Great.
