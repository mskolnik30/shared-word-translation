### Follow the Movement

The order of Daniel 8 matters. Its movement through Daniel Sees a Ram and a Goat, The Goat Strikes the Ram, A Little Horn Grows Great, How Long, Gabriel Is Sent to Explain the Vision, The Ram and Goat Are Interpreted, A Fierce King Will Arise, and finally Seal Up the Vision controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 2 observes: Daniel is transported in vision to Susa in Elam, a city that later became a major Persian royal center. The text does not require that his body was physically present there. The translation note at verse 3–4 observes: The two-horned ram is explicitly interpreted in verse 20 as Media and Persia, with the later horn becoming higher. One vocabulary entry at verse 2 identifies אוּבָל (uval) “Canal” or “river channel.” The Ulai beside which Daniel sees the vision.

For Daniel 8: Keep the prophecy's first historical horizon visible before tracing later echoes, and do not turn judgment or restoration imagery into a timetable for current events. The goal is attentive understanding, not premature resolution. Where the translation notes identify uncertainty, readers should keep that uncertainty visible.
