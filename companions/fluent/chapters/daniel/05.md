### What Needs Context

In Daniel 5, the chapter’s own structure and translation notes guide this Companion’s outline. The translation note at verse 1 observes: Belshazzar was historically a son of Nabonidus, the last Babylonian king, and acted as coregent in Babylon. Biblical Aramaic uses “father” and “son” more broadly for dynastic or ancestral relationship, and Nebuchadnezzar need not be Belshazzar’s biological father.
