The order of 2 Kings 20 matters. Its movement through Hezekiah Becomes Deathly Ill, The LORD Adds Fifteen Years, The Shadow Goes Backward, Envoys Come from Babylon, Isaiah Announces Future Exile to Babylon, and finally Hezekiah Dies controls how isolated details should be heard. His prayer is not presented as a claim of sinless perfection.
