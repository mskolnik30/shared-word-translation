# John 1

## Before You Read

### Where You Are

This opening chapter begins John’s 21-chapter movement. The chapter is organized around The Word, John’s Witness, The Lamb of God, and finally The First Disciples. Read it as one movement before separating individual lines for closer study.

### Chapter Path

- **1:1–18:** The Word.
- **1:19–28:** John’s Witness.
- **1:29–34:** The Lamb of God.
- **1:35–51:** The First Disciples.

### Watch For

### The Chapter in Brief

John 1 moves through The Word; then John’s Witness; then The Lamb of God; then The First Disciples. These headings provide the chapter’s visible architecture. The details within them show how the chapter develops its claims rather than presenting a collection of detached sayings. The ending at “The First Disciples” should therefore be heard in relation to the opening at “The Word.”

### Follow the Movement

The order of John 1 matters. Its movement through The Word, John’s Witness, The Lamb of God, and finally The First Disciples controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 3–4 observes: Ancient punctuation traditions differ over where “what has come into being” belongs. Fluent follows the common critical-text division reflected here. The translation note at verse 5 observes: katalambanō can mean grasp or comprehend as well as seize or overcome. Fluent uses “overcome” while preserving the other range here. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies λόγος (logos) “Word, speech, message.”

For John 1: Follow the narrative sequence and the responses of different characters before combining this Gospel's presentation with parallel accounts. The goal is attentive understanding, not premature resolution. Where the translation notes identify uncertainty, readers should keep that uncertainty visible.
