This final chapter completes John’s 21-chapter movement. The chapter is organized around Jesus Appears by the Sea, Jesus Restores Peter, What About This Disciple, and finally The Witness Behind the Gospel. Read it as one movement before separating individual lines for closer study.

### Follow the Movement

The order of John 21 matters. Its movement through Jesus Appears by the Sea, Jesus Restores Peter, What About This Disciple, and finally The Witness Behind the Gospel controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 6–11 observes: The catch is abundant and specific—153 large fish—yet the net is not torn. John does not explain the number symbolically. The translation note at verse 9 observes: The charcoal fire deliberately echoes the charcoal fire at Peter’s denial in 18:18, giving the restoration scene a narrative memory. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies φανερόω (phaneroō) “To reveal, make visible.”

For John 21: Follow the narrative sequence and the responses of different characters before combining this Gospel's presentation with parallel accounts. The goal is attentive understanding, not premature resolution. Where the translation notes identify uncertainty, readers should keep that uncertainty visible.
