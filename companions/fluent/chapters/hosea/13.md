### Follow the Movement

The order of Hosea 13 matters. Its 7 named sections move from “Ephraim Became Guilty through Baal” through “Where Is Your King” to “The East Wind Will Come.” Read that complete arc before using one detail to characterize the chapter as a whole.

The translation note at verse 2 observes: The Hebrew phrase translated “those who sacrifice humans kiss calves” is notoriously difficult. It may refer to human sacrificers, people offering sacrifices, or worshipers kissing calf images. The translation preserves the close connection among sacrifice, human beings, and calf-veneration without smoothing the difficulty away. The translation note at verse 5 observes: “burning heat” translates a rare wilderness term and may refer to drought, parched land, or intense thirst. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies אָשַׁם (asham) “To become guilty.” Ephraim incurs guilt through Baal worship.
