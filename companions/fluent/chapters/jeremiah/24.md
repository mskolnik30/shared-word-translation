### Follow the Movement

The order of Jeremiah 24 matters. Its movement through Two Baskets of Figs, The Good Figs, and finally The Bad Figs controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The verbs of destruction are reversed into restoration. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies דּוּדָאִים (duda'im) “Baskets.” Containers holding the two groups of figs in the vision.
