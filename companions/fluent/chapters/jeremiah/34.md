### Follow the Movement

The order of Jeremiah 34 matters. Its movement through A Word to Zedekiah During the Siege, A Covenant to Release Hebrew Servants, You Turned Back and Profaned My Name, and finally I Proclaim Release to the Sword controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 8–11 observes: The release of Hebrew servants occurs under siege pressure and is then reversed. The chapter treats the reversal not simply as social injustice but as covenant violation. The translation note at verse 14 observes: The wording reflects Israel’s instruction concerning release after six years of service. Jeremiah interprets the practice through the memory of liberation from Egypt. One vocabulary entry at verse 8 identifies דְּרוֹר (deror) “Release” or “liberty.” Freedom proclaimed to those held in servitude.
