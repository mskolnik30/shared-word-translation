The translation note at verse 4 observes: 19:4 “Take my life” expresses Elijah’s request to die. The same Hebrew word, nephesh, can mean “life,” “self,” or “person”; Elijah’s despair is bodily and personal, not merely abstract. The translation note at verse 11–12 observes: 19:11–12 The LORD is explicitly said not to be “in” the wind, earthquake, or fire. The passage does not deny God’s power over these forces; it distinguishes them from the manner in which Elijah receives God’s presence and word here. One vocabulary entry at verse 4 identifies 4 נֶפֶשׁ (nephesh) — In Elijah’s plea, the word refers to his life or very self. “Take my life.” It recurs in the chapter’s threats and fears, binding Jezebel’s vow, Elijah’s despair, and his complaint that others seek his life.
