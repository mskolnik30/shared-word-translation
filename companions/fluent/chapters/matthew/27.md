### Follow the Movement

The order of Matthew 27 matters. Its movement through Jesus Handed Over to Pilate, Judas Dies, Jesus Before Pilate, Barabbas or Jesus, Jesus Mocked, The Crucifixion, Jesus Dies, Jesus Is Buried, and finally The Guard at the Tomb controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

At verse 09–10, the source notes preserve this detail: Matthew attributes the citation to Jeremiah, though the wording strongly echoes Zechariah 11 with additional scriptural associations. Fluent does not “correct” Matthew's attribution. At verse 16–17, the source notes preserve this detail: SBLGNT reads “Jesus Barabbas,” an ancient and striking textual reading. Many manuscripts omit “Jesus” before Barabbas, likely because scribes were uncomfortable with the criminal sharing Jesus' name. One vocabulary entry at verse 03 identifies μεταμέλομαι (metamelomai) “To regret, change one's mind.”.
