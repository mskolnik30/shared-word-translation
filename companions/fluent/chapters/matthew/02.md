### Follow the Movement

The order of Matthew 2 matters. Its movement through Visitors from the East, Escape to Egypt, Herod Kills the Children of Bethlehem, and finally Return to Nazareth controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 6 observes: Matthew's citation combines scriptural language associated especially with Micah 5 and 2 Samuel 5. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies μάγος (magos) “Magi, eastern learned or priestly figure.”
