### Follow the Movement

The order of Matthew 18 matters. Its movement through Who Is Greatest, Causing Little Ones to Stumble, Do Not Despise the Little Ones, When a Brother or Sister Sins, Forgiveness Without Counting, and finally The Unforgiving Servant controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 6–9 observes: The violent self-mutilation imagery is deliberately hyperbolic and stresses the seriousness of causing stumbling; it is not an instruction for literal self-harm. The translation note at verse 10 observes: The statement about “their angels” is preserved without constructing a detailed doctrine of individual guardian angels. One vocabulary entry at verse 3 identifies στρέφω (strephō) “To turn, change direction.”
