### Follow the Movement

The order of Matthew 17 matters. Its movement through The Transfiguration, Elijah and the Son of Man, A Boy Is Healed, Jesus Again Foretells His Death, and finally The Temple Tax controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 5 observes: The heavenly voice repeats 3:17 and adds the command “Listen to him,” making Jesus' teaching central after Moses and Elijah appear. The translation note at verse 9 observes: Matthew calls the event a “vision” (horama), a term Mark does not use in this scene. One vocabulary entry at verse 2 identifies μεταμορφόω (metamorphoō) “To be transfigured, transformed in appearance.”
