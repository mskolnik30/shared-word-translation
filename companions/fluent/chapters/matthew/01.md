### Where You Are

This opening chapter begins Matthew’s 28-chapter movement. The chapter is organized around The Genealogy of Jesus the Messiah and then The Birth of Jesus. Read it as one movement before separating individual lines for closer study.

Matthew 1 moves through The Genealogy of Jesus the Messiah; then The Birth of Jesus. These headings provide the chapter’s visible architecture. ### Follow the Movement

The order of Matthew 1 matters. Its movement through The Genealogy of Jesus the Messiah and then The Birth of Jesus controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 16 observes: The repeated “was the father of” pattern changes at Jesus: Joseph is the husband of Mary, “from whom” Jesus was born. The translation note at verse 18 observes: The same genesis word-family used in verse 1 returns in the account of Jesus' birth. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies γένεσις (genesis) “Origin, birth, genealogy.”

For Matthew 1: Follow the narrative sequence and the responses of different characters before combining this Gospel's presentation with parallel accounts. The goal is attentive understanding, not premature resolution. Where the translation notes identify uncertainty, readers should keep that uncertainty visible.
