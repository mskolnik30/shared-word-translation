### Follow the Movement

The order of Matthew 21 matters. Its movement through Jesus Enters Jerusalem, Jesus in the Temple, The Fig Tree, Jesus' Authority Challenged, Two Sons, and finally The Wicked Tenants controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.
