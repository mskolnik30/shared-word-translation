### Follow the Movement

The order of Judges 18 matters. Its movement through Dan Seeks an Inheritance, The Spies Find Laish, Dan Takes Micah’s Cultic Objects, Micah Pursues Dan, and finally Dan Captures Laish controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 5–6 observes: The Danites seek divine guidance through a priest serving in an unauthorized household sanctuary. The priest’s confident blessing is not accompanied by narrative confirmation that the cult itself is legitimate. The translation note at verse 7 observes: Laish is selected precisely because its people are isolated and unsuspecting. Dan’s “inheritance” is obtained through attack on a peaceful community rather than through the earlier mandate concerning Canaanite strongholds. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies נַחֲלָה (nachalah) “Inheritance” or “allotted possession.” The tribal land Dan seeks.
