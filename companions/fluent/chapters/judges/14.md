### Follow the Movement

The order of Judges 14 matters. Its movement through Samson Sees a Woman in Timnah, Samson Kills a Lion, Samson’s Riddle, The Philistines Pressure Samson’s Wife, and finally Samson Strikes Thirty Men controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 4 observes: The narrator states that the marriage conflict would become an occasion against the Philistines while also making clear that Samson’s parents did not perceive this. Divine purpose operates through morally complicated human motives. The translation note at verse 6 observes: The Spirit’s empowerment is connected with extraordinary physical strength. Samson’s secrecy about the event contributes to the riddle that follows. One vocabulary entry at verse 3 identifies יָשַׁר בְּעֵינַי (yashar be'enai) “Right in my eyes.” An idiom for what appears desirable or correct to oneself.
