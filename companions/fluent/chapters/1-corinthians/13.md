### Follow the Movement

The order of 1 Corinthians 13 matters. Its movement through The More Excellent Way, What Love Is, and finally What Endures controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 2 observes: The threefold "all" (mysteries, knowledge, faith) names the most prized capacities of the Corinthian community. Each is rendered weightless without love. The translation note at verse 3 observes: A textual variant stands here. Some manuscripts read "so that I may be burned" (καυθήσομαι, kauthēsōmai); others read "so that I may boast" (καυχήσωμαι, kauchēsōmai). The earliest and most weighty witnesses support "boast." The translation reflects this reading; the variant is preserved as a question worth carrying. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies ἀγάπη (agapē) — "Love." A love defined by orientation toward the other rather than by feeling, attraction, or affinity. Distinct from φιλία (friendship) and ἔρως (desire).
