The repeated measurements emphasize both its steady increase and the prophet’s inability to master or contain it. The translation note at verse 8–9 observes: 47:8–9 “The sea where the waters flow out” is a difficult expression. It likely refers to the salt sea in the eastern region, but the wording keeps attention on the river’s life-giving arrival: waters that receive this flow are healed. One vocabulary entry at verse 5 identifies נַחַל (naḥal) — “river” or “wadi.” Here it names the growing, uncrossable stream flowing from the temple, in contrast to a seasonal or limited channel.
