Its movement through Malta: Hospitality, Misjudgment, and Healing, Paul Arrives in Rome, Paul Meets the Jewish Leaders in Rome, Paul Testifies from Moses and the Prophets, and finally The Kingdom Proclaimed without Hindrance controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 4–6 observes: The Maltese observers move from “murderer” to “god” based on events they misinterpret. Acts does not endorse either judgment. The translation note at verse 8 observes: Paul’s healing action includes prayer and laying on hands, keeping God rather than Paul as the implied source.
