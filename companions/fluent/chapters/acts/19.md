### Follow the Movement

The order of Acts 19 matters. Its movement through Disciples of John Receive the Spirit, The Word Spreads Throughout Asia, The Name of Jesus Is Not a Technique, Paul Sets His Direction toward Jerusalem and Rome, and finally The Riot over Artemis controls how isolated details should be heard. The move is prompted by a particular hardened opposition, not rejection of Jewish people as a whole. One vocabulary entry at verse 3 identifies βάπτισμα (baptisma) “Baptism.”

For Acts 19: Distinguish what the narrative reports from what it commands, and notice how the Spirit leads real communities through conflict and change. The goal is attentive understanding, not premature resolution. Where the translation notes identify uncertainty, readers should keep that uncertainty visible.
