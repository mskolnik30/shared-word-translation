### Follow the Movement

The order of Leviticus 17 matters. Its movement through Slaughter and the Tent of Meeting, The Life Is in the Blood, Blood from Hunted Animals, and finally Animals Found Dead or Torn controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 4 observes: “bloodguilt” — The slaughter is treated as unlawful bloodshed when the animal that belongs within sacrificial practice is not brought to the sanctuary as commanded. The translation note at verse 7 observes: “goat-demons” — The Hebrew se'irim literally means “goats” or “hairy ones” and here refers to illicit cultic beings. The unusual imagery is retained rather than generalized to “idols.” One vocabulary entry at verse 4 identifies דָּם (dam) “Blood.” Here both literal blood and the basis of bloodguilt.
