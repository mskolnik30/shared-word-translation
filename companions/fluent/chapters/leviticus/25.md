### Follow the Movement

The order of Leviticus 25 matters. Its movement through The Sabbath Year, The Year of Jubilee, Provision in the Sabbath Year, The Land Belongs to the LORD, Houses and Levitical Property, Support the Impoverished, Israelite Servants, and finally Redemption from a Resident Alien controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 10 observes: “liberty” renders דְּרוֹר (deror), release from binding economic conditions. The Jubilee joins return to family with return to ancestral property. The translation note at verse 14–17 observes: Because land returns in the Jubilee, a sale is valued according to the number of harvests remaining rather than as permanent alienation of the land. One vocabulary entry at verse 4 identifies שַׁבַּת שַׁבָּתוֹן (shabbat shabbaton) “Sabbath of complete rest.” An intensified expression for cessation from ordinary agricultural work.

The goal is attentive understanding, not premature resolution. Where the translation notes identify uncertainty, readers should keep that uncertainty visible.
