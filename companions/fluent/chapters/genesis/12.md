### What Needs Context

In Genesis 12, the chapter’s own structure and translation notes guide this Companion’s outline. The translation note at verse 1–3 observes: The call is issued before any evaluation of Abram. Command precedes promise, and promise precedes explanation. The movement of the promises expands outward—from land, to nation, to name, to blessing, to all the families of the earth—without specifying the means by which the blessing will occur.
