### Follow the Movement

The order of Deuteronomy 12 matters. Its movement through Destroy the Places of Other Worship, Seek the Place the LORD Will Choose, Do Not Offer Sacrifices in Every Place, You May Slaughter and Eat Meat in Your Towns, When the LORD Enlarges Your Territory, Bring Holy Things to the Chosen Place, and finally Do Not Imitate the Nations’ Worship controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 5 observes: “the place that the LORD will choose” is deliberately unnamed in Deuteronomy’s legislation. Later biblical history identifies Jerusalem as the central sanctuary, but the translation does not insert that later name here. The translation note at verse 8 observes: “whatever is right in his own eyes” here describes decentralized wilderness practice before settlement and sanctuary centralization; the phrase later becomes a negative refrain in Judges. One vocabulary entry at verse 2 identifies אָבַד (avad) “To destroy.” Used of eliminating rival cult sites.

For Deuteronomy 12: Keep the chapter within Israel's narrated covenant life, allowing its legal, historical, and theological tensions to remain visible. The goal is attentive understanding, not premature resolution. Where the translation notes identify uncertainty, readers should keep that uncertainty visible.
