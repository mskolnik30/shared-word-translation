This opening chapter begins Deuteronomy’s 34-chapter movement. The chapter has 7 named sections. It moves from “These Are the Words Moses Spoke” through “The Spies Are Sent from Kadesh-Barnea” to “Israel Tries to Go Up without the LORD.” Read the complete sequence before separating individual lines for closer study.
