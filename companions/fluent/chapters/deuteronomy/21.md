Its movement through Atonement for an Unsolved Murder, A Woman Captured in War, The Right of the Firstborn, A Stubborn and Rebellious Son, and finally A Body Hanged on a Tree controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 5 observes: Priests appear as authoritative witnesses to legal and ritual resolution even though local elders carry out the rite.
