Its movement through Joshua Will Cross before the People, Moses Commissions Joshua, Read This Instruction Every Seven Years, The LORD Appears at the Tent, Israel Will Turn to Other Gods, Write This Song, Joshua Is Commissioned, Put the Book beside the Ark, and finally Moses Speaks the Song controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 9 observes: Moses is explicitly said to write “this instruction,” giving written Torah a public role alongside oral teaching. One vocabulary entry at verse 2 identifies צֵאת וָבוֹא (tset va-vo) “Go out and come in.” An idiom for active leadership.
