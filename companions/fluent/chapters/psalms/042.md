The translation note at verse 2 observes: “appear before the face of God” — The expression is associated with coming into divine presence, particularly in worship. The concrete language of God’s “face” is retained. The translation note at verse 4 observes: “leading them in procession” — The Hebrew verb is rare and difficult. The context suggests movement with a festival crowd toward the house of God. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies עָרַג (arag) “To long for” or “pant after.” Intense desire expressed through the image of an animal seeking water.
