The translation note at verse 3 observes: “have given” — The land is spoken of as already given even though Israel has not yet crossed the Jordan. Divine promise and human possession are held together. The translation note at verse 7–8 observes: Joshua’s success is tied not primarily to military skill but to sustained attention to the instruction given through Moses. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies מְשָׁרֵת (mesharet) “Assistant” or “minister.” One who serves another in an official role.
