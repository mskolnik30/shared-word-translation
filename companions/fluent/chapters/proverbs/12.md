The translation note at verse 4 observes: “capable spouse” — The Hebrew literally says “a woman of strength.” The immediate reference is to a wife, though the quality being praised is capability, strength, and worthy character. The translation note at verse 9 observes: The Hebrew has a textual and interpretive difficulty concerning whether the person is “lightly regarded” or presents themselves as insignificant. The central contrast is between modest status with enough to live and impressive status without necessities. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies מוּסָר (musar) “Discipline,” “instruction,” or “correction.” Formation that includes receiving correction.
