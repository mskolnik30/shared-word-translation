# 2 Timothy 1

## Before You Read

### Where You Are

This opening chapter begins 2 Timothy’s 4-chapter movement. The chapter is organized around Greeting, Rekindle God’s Gift, Do Not Be Ashamed of the Testimony, and finally Guard the Good Deposit. Read it as one movement before separating individual lines for closer study.

### Chapter Path

- **1:1–2:** Greeting.
- **1:3–7:** Rekindle God’s Gift.
- **1:8–12:** Do Not Be Ashamed of the Testimony.
- **1:13–18:** Guard the Good Deposit.

### Watch For

In 2 Timothy 1, watch how the movement from “Greeting” toward “Guard the Good Deposit” changes the chapter's emphasis. Do not let one verse erase the sequence created by the whole chapter. The translation notes especially mark verse 3 for careful attention.

### The Chapter in Brief

2 Timothy 1 moves through Greeting; then Rekindle God’s Gift; then Do Not Be Ashamed of the Testimony; then Guard the Good Deposit. These headings provide the chapter’s visible architecture. The details within them show how the chapter develops its claims rather than presenting a collection of detached sayings. The ending at “Guard the Good Deposit” should therefore be heard in relation to the opening at “Greeting.”

### Follow the Movement

The order of 2 Timothy 1 matters. Its movement through Greeting, Rekindle God’s Gift, Do Not Be Ashamed of the Testimony, and finally Guard the Good Deposit controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 5 observes: Timothy’s trust is narrated through grandmother, mother, and Timothy, giving intergenerational faith formation unusual prominence. The translation note at verse 6 observes: The gift is not merely possessed; it must be rekindled. Calling and formation remain active responsibilities. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies ἐπαγγελία (epangelia) “Promise.”

For 2 Timothy 1: Follow the argument across the whole letter, remembering that a sentence addresses a particular community and should not be isolated from its surrounding appeal. The goal is attentive understanding, not premature resolution. Where the translation notes identify uncertainty, readers should keep that uncertainty visible.
