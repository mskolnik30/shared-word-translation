### Follow the Movement

Its movement through Jesus Teaches Prayer, Ask, Seek, Knock, The Finger of God, Hearing and Keeping God’s Word, The Sign of Jonah and Light, and finally Woes at the Table controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 8 observes: anaideia can mean shamelessness or bold persistence. The story turns on the refusal to stop asking rather than on polite social decorum. One vocabulary entry at verse 2 identifies ἁγιάζω (hagiazō) “To make holy, regard as holy.”
