The restored city is characterized by the qualities expected of the LORD and the renewed community. The translation note at verse 4–5 observes: Restoration is pictured through ordinary public life: elderly people living long enough to sit safely in the streets and children able to play there. One vocabulary entry at verse 2 identifies קָנָא (qana) “To be jealous” or “zealous.” Intense commitment directed here toward Zion.
