### Follow the Movement

The order of Zechariah 2 matters. Its movement through Third Vision: The Measuring Line, Zion Called Home, and finally The LORD Will Dwell in Zion controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 4 observes: “villages without walls” — The Hebrew describes open or unwalled settlements. The image portrays growth so extensive that ordinary city walls can no longer define Jerusalem. The translation note at verse 5 observes: “a wall of fire” — Jerusalem’s security is grounded in the LORD’s presence rather than conventional fortification. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies חֶבֶל (chevel) “Measuring line” or “cord.” A tool used for measuring land or construction.
