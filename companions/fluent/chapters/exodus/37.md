The translation note at verse 6 observes: Kapporet is rendered “cover,” as in Exodus 25. Its ritual significance later includes atonement, but the object itself is the gold cover over the ark. The translation note at verse 7–9 observes: The cherubim are formed from the same hammered gold as the cover, visually joining the ark’s cover and the throne-associated heavenly figures. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies אֲרוֹן (aron) “Ark” or sacred chest.
