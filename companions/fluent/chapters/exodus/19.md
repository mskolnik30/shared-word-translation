### Follow the Movement

The order of Exodus 19 matters. Its movement through Israel Comes to Sinai, A Kingdom of Priests and a Holy Nation, The People Prepare to Meet God, The LORD Descends on Sinai, and finally Do Not Break Through to the LORD controls how isolated details should be heard. A reading that begins with the conclusion but ignores the earlier tension may make the chapter say more—or less—than its complete shape allows.

The translation note at verse 4 observes: Eagles’ wings portray protective and powerful divine transport. Israel’s destination is ultimately not merely a land but “to myself.” The translation note at verse 5 observes: “treasured possession” does not imply that other nations lie outside the LORD’s ownership; the verse immediately says, “all the earth is mine.” One vocabulary entry at verse 5 identifies סְגֻלָּה (segullah) “Treasure” or “treasured possession.” A valued possession specially claimed by a ruler.
