### Follow the Movement

The order of Exodus 23 matters. Its movement through Justice Must Not Be Distorted, Care for an Enemy’s Animal, Do Not Deny Justice, The Sabbath Year and Sabbath Day, Three Pilgrimage Festivals, My Messenger Will Go before You, Do Not Serve Their Gods, and finally I Will Send Terror before You controls how isolated details should be heard. The translation note at verse 4–5 observes: Covenant justice reaches even into relationships of hostility. An enemy’s lost or burdened animal must still receive practical help. The translation note at verse 9 observes: Israel’s own experience as resident aliens is again made the basis for empathy: “you know the life” of the alien. One vocabulary entry at verse 1 identifies שָׁוְא (shav) “Falsehood” or “emptiness.” Used of a false report.

For Exodus 23: Keep the chapter within Israel's narrated covenant life, allowing its legal, historical, and theological tensions to remain visible. The goal is attentive understanding, not premature resolution. Where the translation notes identify uncertainty, readers should keep that uncertainty visible.
