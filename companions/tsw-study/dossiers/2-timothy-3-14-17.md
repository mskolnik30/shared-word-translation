# What Scripture Is For

## First Look

Timothy is urged to remain in what he learned from trusted teachers and from sacred writings known since childhood. God-breathed Scripture equips the person of God for every good work.

## Why This Matters

This passage bears directly on *Theopneustos* means God-breathed or breathed out by God; the verse describes Scripture's character and usefulness but does not itself define a modern theory of inerrancy, canon boundaries, or interpretation.. Translation and interpretation therefore affect not only ideas but the treatment of real people and communities. A responsible reading should identify what the text says, what readers infer, and where later institutions have made the passage do additional work.

## Look Closely

In context, 'sacred writings' first names Israel's Scriptures, which make wise for salvation through faith in Christ. Teaching, correction, and training are ordered toward mature practice, not information or domination.

Read the passage within its whole literary unit before isolating a single phrase. TSW's visible repetitions and tensions are evidence for study rather than problems to harmonize prematurely.

## More Than One Reading

Some traditions infer comprehensive inerrancy; others affirm inspiration while recognizing genre, textual history, and human authorship. Canonical readings ask how the whole church's Bible performs the listed tasks.

These positions should be compared by their linguistic evidence, historical assumptions, canonical relationships, and practical consequences. Naming several readings does not imply that every reading is equally persuasive or safe.

## Across Christian History

Patristic, medieval, Reformation, and modern churches agree on Scripture's formative authority while disagreeing about tradition, magisterium, reason, and confessional norms in interpretation.

Reception history shows what communities noticed and what they overlooked. It is evidence about interpretation, not an automatic verdict that the oldest, newest, or most powerful reading is correct.

## Power and Consequences

A leader cannot end discussion merely by labeling a private reading 'biblical.' God-breathed Scripture should equip good work; readings producing deception, coercion, or cruelty require correction by evidence, community, and the text's own ends.

Interpretation should be evaluated in the presence of people who bear its consequences. Safeguarding, truthful history, and accountable power are theological concerns rather than optional additions.
