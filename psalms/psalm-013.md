How long, O LORD?
Will you forget me forever?
How long will you hide your face from me?
How long must I bear counsel in my soul,
and have sorrow in my heart all the day?
How long shall my enemy be exalted over me?
Look upon me; answer me, O LORD my God;
give light to my eyes,
so that I do not sleep the sleep of death,
so that my enemy may not say,
“I have prevailed over him,”
so that my foes may not rejoice
because I am shaken.
But I have trusted in your steadfast love;
my heart shall rejoice in your salvation.
I will sing to the LORD,
for God has dealt bountifully with me.
