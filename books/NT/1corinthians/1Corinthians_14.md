## Prophecy and Tongues

<p>

v01: Pursue love, and earnestly desire the spiritual things, but especially that you may prophesy.

v02: For the one speaking in a tongue does not speak to men but to God; for no one understands, but in spirit he speaks mysteries.

</p>

<p>

v03: But the one who prophesies speaks to men for building up and encouragement and consolation.

v04: The one speaking in a tongue builds up himself, but the one who prophesies builds up the church.

</p>

<p>

v05: Now I wish that you all spoke in tongues, but even more that you would prophesy; and greater is the one who prophesies than the one who speaks in tongues, unless he interprets, so that the church may receive building up.

</p>

<p>

v06: But now, brothers, if I come to you speaking in tongues, what will I profit you unless I speak to you either by revelation or knowledge or prophecy or teaching?

</p>

<p>

v07: Yet even lifeless things giving sound, whether flute or harp, if they do not give a distinction in the tones, how will it be known what is played on the flute or on the harp?

v08: For if the trumpet gives an uncertain sound, who will prepare for battle?

</p>

<p>

v09: So also you, unless you give clear speech with the tongue, how will what is spoken be known? For you will be speaking into the air.

</p>

<p>

v10: There are perhaps so many kinds of languages in the world, and none is without meaning.

v11: If then I do not know the meaning of the voice, I will be a foreigner to the speaker, and the speaker a foreigner to me.

</p>

<p>

v12: So also you, since you are zealous for spiritual things, seek that you may abound for the building up of the church.

</p>

<p>

v13: Therefore let the one who speaks in a tongue pray that he may interpret.

v14: For if I pray in a tongue, my spirit prays, but my mind is unfruitful.

</p>

<p>

v15: What then? I will pray with the spirit, and I will also pray with the mind; I will sing with the spirit, and I will also sing with the mind.

v16: Otherwise, if you bless in spirit, how will the one occupying the place of the uninstructed say “Amen” at your thanksgiving, since he does not know what you are saying?

</p>

<p>

v17: For you give thanks well enough, but the other is not built up.

v18: I thank God, I speak in tongues more than all of you;

v19: but in the church I prefer to speak five words with my mind so that I may instruct others also, rather than ten thousand words in a tongue.

</p>

## Orderly Worship

<p>

v20: Brothers, do not be children in your thinking; yet in evil be infants, but in your thinking be mature.

</p>

<p>

v21: In the law it is written,

“By people of other tongues

and by the lips of strangers

I will speak to this people,

and even so they will not listen to me,” says the Lord.

</p>

<p>

v22: So then tongues are for a sign, not to believers but to unbelievers; but prophecy is not for unbelievers but for believers.

</p>

<p>

v23: Therefore if the whole church comes together and all speak in tongues, and uninstructed or unbelievers enter, will they not say that you are out of your mind?

v24: But if all prophesy, and an unbeliever or uninstructed person enters, he is convicted by all, he is called to account by all;

v25: the secrets of his heart become manifest; and so falling on his face he will worship God, declaring that God is truly among you.

</p>

<p>

v26: What then, brothers? When you come together, each has a psalm, has a teaching, has a revelation, has a tongue, has an interpretation. Let all things be done for building up.

</p>

<p>

v27: If anyone speaks in a tongue, let it be by two or at most three, and in turn, and let one interpret;

v28: but if there is no interpreter, let him keep silent in the church and speak to himself and to God.

</p>

<p>

v29: And let two or three prophets speak, and let the others discern;

v30: but if something is revealed to another sitting by, let the first keep silent.

v31: For you can all prophesy one by one, so that all may learn and all may be encouraged;

v32: and the spirits of prophets are subject to prophets.

</p>

<p>

v33: For God is not a God of disorder but of peace.

</p>

<p>

As in all the churches of the holy ones,

v34: let the women keep silent in the churches; for they are not permitted to speak, but let them be in submission, as also the law says.

v35: And if they desire to learn anything, let them ask their own husbands at home; for it is shameful for a woman to speak in church.

</p>

<p>

v36: Or did the word of God go out from you, or did it come to you only?

v37: If anyone thinks he is a prophet or spiritual, let him acknowledge that the things I write to you are a command of the Lord.

v38: But if anyone ignores this, he is ignored.

</p>

<p>

v39: Therefore, my brothers, earnestly desire to prophesy, and do not forbid speaking in tongues.

v40: But let all things be done properly and in order.

</p>

## Notes

v1–5: Prophecy is prioritized for communal edification.

v6–12: Intelligibility is essential for usefulness.

v13–19: Mind and spirit must function together.

v20–22: Tongues and prophecy serve distinct roles.

v23–25: Prophecy reveals and convicts.

v26–33: Order ensures edification and clarity.

v34–35: Instruction reflects ordered participation.

v37–40: Apostolic authority governs practice.
