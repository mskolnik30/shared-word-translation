## Sexual Immorality in the Church

<p>
v01: It is actually reported that there is sexual immorality among you, and such immorality as is not even among the Gentiles—that someone has his father’s wife.

v02: And you are arrogant! Ought you not rather to mourn, so that the one who has done this deed might be removed from among you?
</p>

<p>
v03: For I indeed, though absent in body but present in spirit, have already judged the one who has done this, as though I were present:

v04: in the name of our Lord Jesus, when you are assembled, and my spirit is present, with the power of our Lord Jesus,
</p>

<p>
v05: to deliver such a one to Satan for the destruction of the flesh, so that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord.
</p>

## Clean Out the Old Leaven

<p>
v06: Your boasting is not good. Do you not know that a little leaven leavens the whole lump?

v07: Clean out the old leaven so that you may be a new lump, just as you are unleavened. For Christ, our Passover, has been sacrificed.
</p>

<p>
v08: Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, nor with leaven of malice and evil, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.
</p>

## Judging Those Inside the Church

<p>
v09: I wrote to you in my letter not to associate with sexually immoral people—

v10: not at all meaning the sexually immoral of this world, or the greedy and swindlers, or idolaters, since then you would have to go out of the world.
</p>

<p>
v11: But now I write to you not to associate with anyone who is called a brother if he is sexually immoral or greedy or an idolater or a reviler or a drunkard or a swindler—not even to eat with such a one.
</p>

<p>
v12: For what have I to do with judging those outside? Do you not judge those inside?

v13: But God judges those outside. “Remove the evil person from among yourselves.”
</p>
## Notes
v01–02: Tolerated sin is more severe than external immorality.  
v03–05: Discipline aims at ultimate restoration.  
v06–08: Leaven metaphor emphasizes communal influence.  
v07: Christ’s sacrifice redefines identity.  
v09–11: Distinction between internal discipline and external association.  
v12–13: Judgment responsibility is limited to the community.
