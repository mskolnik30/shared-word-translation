## Imitate Christ
<p>
v01: Be imitators of me, just as I also am of Christ.
v02: Now I praise you because you remember me in everything and hold firmly to the traditions, just as I delivered them to you.
</p>

## Headship and Worship
<p>
v03: But I want you to understand that the head of every man is Christ, the head of a woman is the man, and the head of Christ is God.
v04: Every man who prays or prophesies with something on his head disgraces his head.
v05: But every woman who prays or prophesies with her head uncovered disgraces her head—it is one and the same as if her head were shaved.
v06: For if a woman does not cover her head, she might as well have her hair cut off; but if it is shameful for a woman to have her hair cut off or be shaved, let her cover her head.
</p>

<p>
v07: For a man ought not to cover his head, since he is the image and glory of God; but woman is the glory of man.
v08: For man was not made from woman, but woman from man.
v09: Neither was man created for woman, but woman for man.
v10: For this reason a woman ought to have authority over her head, because of the angels.
</p>

<p>
v11: Nevertheless, in the Lord woman is not independent of man, nor man independent of woman.
v12: For just as woman came from man, so also man is born through woman—and all things are from God.
v13: Judge for yourselves: is it proper for a woman to pray to God with her head uncovered?
</p>

<p>
v14: Does not even nature itself teach you that if a man has long hair it is a dishonor to him,
v15: but if a woman has long hair it is her glory? For her hair is given to her for a covering.
v16: But if anyone is inclined to be contentious, we have no such custom, nor do the churches of God.
</p>

## Abuses at the Lord’s Table
<p>
v17: But in giving this instruction I do not praise you, because you come together not for the better but for the worse.
v18: For in the first place, when you come together as a church, I hear that there are divisions among you; and in part I trust it,
v19: for there must also be factions among you, so that those who are approved may become evident among you.
</p>

<p>
v20: When you come together, it is not to eat the Lord’s supper,
v21: for in eating, each one goes ahead with their own meal, and one is hungry while another is drunk.
v22: What! Do you not have houses to eat and drink in? Or do you despise the church of God and shame those who have nothing? What shall I say to you? Shall I praise you? In this I do not praise you.
</p>

## The Tradition of the Supper
<p>
v23: For I received from the Lord what I also handed on to you: that the Lord Jesus, on the night when he was betrayed, took bread,
v24: and when he had given thanks, he broke it and said, “This is my body, which is for you. Do this in remembrance of me.”
v25: In the same way also the cup, after supper, saying, “This cup is the new covenant in my blood. Do this, as often as you drink it, in remembrance of me.”
</p>

<p>
v26: For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you proclaim the Lord’s death until he comes.
</p>

## Discern the Body
<p>
v27: Therefore, whoever eats the bread or drinks the cup of the Lord in an unworthy manner will be accountable for the body and blood of the Lord.
v28: Let a person examine themselves, and so eat of the bread and drink of the cup.
v29: For anyone who eats and drinks without discerning the body eats and drinks judgment on themselves.
</p>

<p>
v30: For this reason many among you are weak and ill, and some have died.
v31: But if we were judging ourselves, we would not be judged.
v32: Yet when we are judged by the Lord, we are disciplined so that we may not be condemned along with the world.
</p>

## Practical Correction
<p>
v33: So then, my sisters and brothers, when you come together to eat, wait for one another.
v34: If anyone is hungry, let them eat at home, so that when you come together it will not be for judgment. And the rest I will set in order when I come.
</p>
