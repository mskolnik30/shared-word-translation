## The Collection for the Holy Ones

<p>

v01: Now concerning the collection for the holy ones: as I directed the churches of Galatia, so also you are to do.

v02: On the first day of every week, each one of you is to put something aside, storing up as he may prosper, so that no collections be made when I come.

</p>

<p>

v03: And when I arrive, whomever you approve, I will send with letters to carry your gift to Jerusalem;

v04: and if it is fitting for me also to go, they will go with me.

</p>

## Paul’s Travel Plans

<p>

v05: But I will come to you after I pass through Macedonia, for I am passing through Macedonia;

v06: and perhaps I will stay with you or even spend the winter, so that you may send me on my way wherever I go.

</p>

<p>

v07: For I do not want to see you now just in passing, for I hope to remain with you for some time, if the Lord permits.

v08: But I will remain in Ephesus until Pentecost,

</p>

<p>

v09: for a wide door has opened for me for effective work, and there are many adversaries.

</p>

## Final Instructions

<p>

v10: Now if Timothy comes, see that he is with you without fear, for he is doing the work of the Lord, as I also am;

v11: so let no one despise him. But send him on his way in peace, so that he may come to me, for I am expecting him with the brothers.

</p>

<p>

v12: Now concerning Apollos the brother, I strongly urged him to come to you with the brothers; but it was not at all his will to come now, but he will come when he has opportunity.

</p>

<p>

v13: Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, act like men, be strong.

v14: Let all that you do be done in love.

</p>

<p>

v15: Now I urge you, brothers—you know the household of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have devoted themselves to service for the holy ones—

v16: that you also be subject to such people and to everyone who works and labors with them.

</p>

<p>

v17: And I rejoice at the coming of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus, because they have supplied what was lacking on your part.

v18: For they refreshed my spirit and yours. Therefore acknowledge such men.

</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>

v19: The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Prisca greet you warmly in the Lord, together with the church in their house.

v20: All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.

</p>

<p>

v21: The greeting is in my own hand—Paul.

v22: If anyone does not love the Lord, let him be accursed. Our Lord, come!

</p>

<p>

v23: The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you.

v24: My love be with you all in Christ Jesus. Amen.

</p>

## Notes

v01–04: Structured giving reflects communal responsibility.

v05–09: Mission planning is flexible under divine guidance.

v09: Opportunity and opposition coexist.

v10–12: Leadership cooperation is affirmed.

v13–14: Final exhortations summarize ethical posture.

v15–18: Recognition of devoted service reinforces community structure.

v19–20: Network of churches emphasizes unity.

v21–24: Closing combines warning, hope, grace, and love.
