## Spiritual Gifts

<p>
v01: Now concerning spiritual matters, brothers and sisters, I do not want you to be uninformed.

v02: You know that when you were Gentiles, you were led astray to mute idols, however you were led.

v03: Therefore I make known to you that no one speaking in the Spirit of God says, “Jesus is accursed,” and no one can say, “Jesus is Lord,” except in the Holy Spirit.
</p>

<p>
v04: Now there are varieties of gifts, but the same Spirit;

v05: and there are varieties of service, but the same Lord;

v06: and there are varieties of workings, but the same God who works all things in all.

v07: To each is given the manifestation of the Spirit for the common good.

v08: For to one is given through the Spirit a word of wisdom, and to another a word of knowledge according to the same Spirit,

v09: to another faith in the same Spirit, to another gifts of healings in the one Spirit,

v10: to another workings of powers, to another prophecy, to another discernments of spirits, to another kinds of tongues, to another interpretation of tongues.

v11: All these are worked by the one and the same Spirit, distributing to each one individually just as he wills.
</p>

## One Body with Many Members

<p>
v12: For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, though many, are one body, so also Christ.

v13: For in one Spirit we were all baptized into one body—whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free—and all were made to drink of one Spirit.

v14: For the body does not consist of one member but of many.

v15: If the foot should say, “Because I am not a hand, I do not belong to the body,” that does not make it any less a part of the body.

v16: And if the ear should say, “Because I am not an eye, I do not belong to the body,” that does not make it any less a part of the body.

v17: If the whole body were an eye, where would be the hearing? If the whole were hearing, where would be the sense of smell?

v18: But as it is, God arranged the members in the body, each one of them, as he chose.

v19: If all were a single member, where would the body be?

v20: As it is, there are many members, yet one body.
</p>

<p>
v21: The eye cannot say to the hand, “I have no need of you,” nor again the head to the feet, “I have no need of you.”

v22: On the contrary, the members of the body that seem to be weaker are necessary,

v23: and those parts of the body that we think less honorable we bestow greater honor, and our unpresentable parts are treated with greater modesty,

v24: while our presentable parts have no need. But God has so composed the body, giving greater honor to the part that lacked it,

v25: so that there may be no division in the body, but that the members may have the same care for one another.

v26: If one member suffers, all suffer together; if one member is honored, all rejoice together.
</p>

<p>
v27: Now you are the body of Christ and individually members of it.

v28: And God has appointed in the church first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then works of power, then gifts of healings, helps, administrations, various kinds of tongues.

v29: Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all teachers? Do all work powers?

v30: Do all have gifts of healings? Do all speak in tongues? Do all interpret?

v31: But earnestly desire the greater gifts. And yet I show you a still more excellent way.
</p>

##Vocabulary

v01: πνευματικός (pneumatikos)
“Spiritual.” Relating to the Spirit, not merely internal or immaterial.

v02: ἄγω (agō)
“To lead.” Often implies being guided or carried along.

v07: φανέρωσις (phanerōsis)
“Manifestation.” Visible expression of the Spirit’s presence.

v08: σοφία (sophia)
“Wisdom.” Insight into divine truth or application.
