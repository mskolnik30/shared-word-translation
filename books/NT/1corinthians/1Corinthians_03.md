## Divisions in the Church

<p>
v01: And I, brothers, was not able to speak to you as to spiritual people, but as to fleshly, as to infants in Christ.

v02: I gave you milk to drink, not solid food, for you were not yet able; but even now you are still not able,

v03: for you are still fleshly. For since there is jealousy and strife among you, are you not fleshly and walking according to man?
</p>

<p>
v04: For when one says, “I am of Paul,” and another, “I am of Apollos,” are you not merely human?
</p>

## God Gives the Growth

<p>
v05: What then is Apollos? And what is Paul? Servants through whom you believed, as the Lord assigned to each.

v06: I planted, Apollos watered, but God was causing the growth.
</p>

<p>
v07: So then neither the one who plants nor the one who waters is anything, but God who causes the growth.

v08: Now the one who plants and the one who waters are one, but each will receive his own reward according to his own labor.
</p>

<p>
v09: For we are God’s fellow workers; you are God’s field, God’s building.
</p>

## Builders on the Foundation

<p>
v10: According to the grace of God given to me, as a wise master builder I laid a foundation, and another builds upon it; but let each one take care how he builds upon it.

v11: For no one is able to lay another foundation besides the one that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.
</p>

<p>
v12: Now if anyone builds upon the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw,

v13: each one’s work will become evident; for the day will disclose it, because it is revealed in fire, and the fire itself will test what sort of work each one has done.
</p>

<p>
v14: If anyone’s work which he built remains, he will receive a reward;

v15: if anyone’s work is burned up, he will suffer loss, but he himself will be saved, yet so as through fire.
</p>

## God’s Temple

<p>
v16: Do you not know that you are God’s temple and that the Spirit of God dwells in you?

v17: If anyone destroys God’s temple, God will destroy him; for the temple of God is holy, which you are.
</p>

## The Folly of Human Wisdom

<p>
v18: Let no one deceive himself. If anyone among you thinks that he is wise in this age, let him become foolish so that he may become wise.

v19: For the wisdom of this world is foolishness with God. For it is written, “He catches the wise in their craftiness,”

v20: and again, “The Lord knows the thoughts of the wise, that they are futile.”
</p>

<p>
v21: So then let no one boast in men. For all things are yours,

v22: whether Paul or Apollos or Cephas or the world or life or death or things present or things to come—all are yours,

v23: and you are Christ’s, and Christ is God’s.
</p>
## Notes
v1–3: Spiritual immaturity is evidenced by division.  
v5–9: Ministers are instruments; God is the source of growth.  
v10–11: Christ alone is the foundation.  
v12–15: Works are tested and evaluated.  
v16–17: Community identity as God’s temple carries responsibility.  
v18–20: Human wisdom is inverted under divine evaluation.  
v21–23: Believers belong to Christ, transcending factionalism.
