## The Illness of Lazarus
<p>
v01: Now a certain man was ill—Lazarus of Bethany, the village of Mary and her sister Martha.
v02: Mary was the one who anointed the Lord with ointment and wiped his feet with her hair—her brother Lazarus was the one who was ill.
v03: So the sisters sent to Jesus, saying, “Lord, the one you love is ill.”
v04: But when Jesus heard it, he said, “This illness does not lead to death; rather, it is for the glory of God, so that the Son of God may be glorified through it.”
v05: Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus.
v06: So when he heard that Lazarus was ill, he stayed two days longer in the place where he was.
</p>

<p>
v07: Then after this he said to the disciples, “Let us go to Judea again.”
v08: The disciples said to him, “Rabbi, the Judeans were just now seeking to stone you, and are you going there again?”
v09: Jesus answered, “Are there not twelve hours in the day? If anyone walks in the day, they do not stumble, because they see the light of this world.
v10: But if anyone walks in the night, they stumble, because the light is not in them.”
</p>

<p>
v11: After saying this, Jesus said to them, “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep; but I go to awaken him.”
v12: The disciples said to him, “Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will be saved.”
v13: Jesus had spoken about his death, but they thought he meant taking rest in sleep.
v14: Then Jesus told them plainly, “Lazarus has died.
v15: And for your sake I am glad I was not there, so that you may come to trust. But let us go to him.”
v16: So Thomas, who was called the Twin, said to his fellow disciples, “Let us also go, that we may die with him.”
</p>

## “I Am the Resurrection and the Life”
<p>
v17: When Jesus came, he found that Lazarus had already been in the tomb four days.
v18: Now Bethany was near Jerusalem, about two miles away.
v19: And many of the Judeans had come to Martha and Mary to console them about their brother.
v20: So when Martha heard that Jesus was coming, she went and met him; but Mary remained seated in the house.
</p>

<p>
v21: Martha said to Jesus, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.
v22: Yet even now I know that whatever you ask from God, God will give you.”
v23: Jesus said to her, “Your brother will rise again.”
v24: Martha said to him, “I know that he will rise again in the resurrection on the last day.”
</p>

<p>
v25: Jesus said to her, “I am the resurrection and the life. Those who trust in me, even if they die, will live,
v26: and everyone who lives and trusts in me will never die. Do you trust this?”
v27: She said to him, “Yes, Lord; I trust that you are the Christ, the Son of God, the one coming into the world.”
</p>

<p>
v28: When she had said this, she went and called her sister Mary, saying privately, “The Teacher is here and is calling for you.”
v29: And when Mary heard it, she rose quickly and went to him.
v30: Now Jesus had not yet come into the village, but was still in the place where Martha had met him.
v31: So when the Judeans who were with Mary in the house, consoling her, saw Mary rise quickly and go out, they followed her, supposing that she was going to the tomb to weep there.
</p>

<p>
v32: Now when Mary came to where Jesus was and saw him, she fell at his feet, saying to him, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died.”
v33: When Jesus saw her weeping, and the Judeans who came with her also weeping, he was deeply moved in spirit and troubled.
v34: And Jesus said, “Where have you laid him?” They said to him, “Lord, come and see.”
v35: Jesus wept.
v36: So the Judeans said, “See how he loved him!”
v37: But some of them said, “Could not the one who opened the eyes of the blind man have kept this man from dying?”
</p>

## Lazarus Raised from the Dead
<p>
v38: Then Jesus, deeply moved again, came to the tomb. It was a cave, and a stone lay against it.
v39: Jesus said, “Take away the stone.” Martha, the sister of the one who had died, said to him, “Lord, by this time there will be a stench, for he has been dead four days.”
v40: Jesus said to her, “Did I not tell you that if you trust, you will see the glory of God?”
</p>

<p>
v41: So they took away the stone. And Jesus lifted up his eyes and said, “Father, I thank you that you have heard me.
v42: I knew that you always hear me, but I said this for the sake of the crowd standing here, that they may trust that you sent me.”
v43: When Jesus had said these things, he cried out with a loud voice, “Lazarus, come out!”
v44: The one who had died came out—his hands and feet bound with strips of cloth, and his face wrapped with a cloth. Jesus said to them, “Unbind him, and let him go.”
</p>

## The Plot to Kill Jesus
<p>
v45: Many of the Judeans therefore, who had come with Mary and had seen what Jesus did, trusted in him.
v46: But some of them went to the Pharisees and told them what Jesus had done.
v47: So the chief priests and the Pharisees gathered the council and said, “What are we doing? For this man performs many signs.
v48: If we let him go on like this, everyone will trust in him, and the Romans will come and take away both our place and our nation.”
</p>

<p>
v49: But one of them, Caiaphas, who was high priest that year, said to them, “You know nothing at all.
v50: Nor do you consider that it is better for you that one man should die for the people, than that the whole nation should perish.”
v51: He did not say this of his own accord, but being high priest that year he prophesied that Jesus was about to die for the nation—
v52: and not for the nation only, but also to gather into one the children of God who are scattered abroad.
v53: So from that day on they made plans to put him to death.
</p>

<p>
v54: Therefore Jesus no longer walked openly among the Judeans, but went from there to the region near the wilderness, to a town called Ephraim, and there he stayed with the disciples.
</p>

## Before Passover
<p>
v55: Now the Passover of the Judeans was near, and many went up to Jerusalem from the countryside before the Passover to purify themselves.
v56: They were looking for Jesus and saying to one another as they stood in the temple, “What do you think—that he will not come to the festival at all?”
v57: Now the chief priests and the Pharisees had given orders that if anyone knew where Jesus was, they should report it, so that they might arrest him.
</p>
