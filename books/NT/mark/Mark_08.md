## Feeding the Four Thousand

<p>

v01: In those days, when again a great crowd was present and they had nothing to eat, he called his disciples and said to them,

v02: “I have compassion on the crowd, because they have remained with me now three days and have nothing to eat.

v03: And if I send them away hungry to their homes, they will faint on the way, and some of them have come from far away.”

v04: And his disciples answered him, “From where will anyone be able to satisfy these people with bread here in a desolate place?”

v05: And he asked them, “How many loaves do you have?” And they said, “Seven.”

</p>

<p>

v06: And he commanded the crowd to recline on the ground, and taking the seven loaves, giving thanks, he broke them and gave them to his disciples to set before them, and they set them before the crowd.

v07: And they had a few small fish, and blessing them, he said to set these also before them.

v08: And they ate and were satisfied, and they took up what remained of broken pieces—seven baskets.

v09: And there were about four thousand, and he sent them away.

</p>

## The Pharisees Demand a Sign

<p>

v11: And the Pharisees came and began to argue with him, seeking from him a sign from heaven, testing him.

v12: And sighing deeply in his spirit, he said, “Why does this generation seek a sign? Truly I say to you, no sign will be given to this generation.”

</p>

## The Leaven of the Pharisees

<p>

v14: And they had forgotten to take bread, and they had no more than one loaf with them in the boat.

v15: And he was warning them, saying, “Watch out; beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and the leaven of Herod.”

v16: And they were reasoning with one another that it is because they have no bread.

</p>

<p>

v17: And knowing, he said to them, “Why do you reason that you have no bread? Do you not yet perceive or understand? Are your hearts hardened?

v18: Having eyes, do you not see? And having ears, do you not hear? And do you not remember?

v19: When I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many baskets full of broken pieces did you take up?” They said to him, “Twelve.”

v20: “And the seven for the four thousand, how many baskets of broken pieces did you take up?” And they said, “Seven.”

v21: And he said to them, “Do you not yet understand?”

</p>

## Peter’s Confession

<p>

v27: And Jesus went out with his disciples to the villages of Caesarea Philippi, and on the way he was asking his disciples, saying to them, “Who do people say that I am?”

v28: And they told him, saying, “John the Baptist; and others, Elijah; and others, one of the prophets.”

v29: And he asked them, “But you—who do you say that I am?” Peter answered and said to him, “You are the Christ.”

v30: And he strictly ordered them that they should tell no one about him.

</p>

## Notes

v02: Compassion is grounded in sustained presence (“three days”).

v12: Refusal of a sign resists coercive proof.

v15: “Leaven” functions as a metaphor for influence.

v17: Failure to understand is framed as perceptual and moral.

v29: “Christ” is confessed without immediate clarification.

## Vocabulary

v02: σπλαγχνίζομαι (splagchnizomai) — to have compassion.

v12: σημεῖον (sēmeion) — sign.

v15: ζύμη (zymē) — leaven.

v17: πωρόω (pōroō) — to harden.

v29: χριστός (christos) — anointed one.
