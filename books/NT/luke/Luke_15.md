## Setting and Complaint
 v01 Now all the tax collectors and sinners were coming near to listen to him.
 v02 And the Pharisees and the scribes were grumbling and saying, “This man welcomes sinners and eats with them.”

## The Parable of the Lost Sheep
 v03 So he told them this parable:
 v04 “Which one of you, having a hundred sheep and losing one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the wilderness and go after the one that is lost until he finds it?
 v05 And when he has found it, he lays it on his shoulders, rejoicing.
 v06 And when he comes home, he calls together his friends and neighbors, saying to them, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep that was lost.’
 v07 Just so, I tell you, there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous persons who have no need of repentance.

## The Parable of the Lost Coin
 v08 “Or what woman, having ten silver coins, if she loses one coin, does not light a lamp, sweep the house, and search carefully until she finds it?
 v09 And when she has found it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, ‘Rejoice with me, for I have found the coin that I had lost.’
 v10 Just so, I tell you, there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner who repents.”

## The Parable of the Prodigals’ Father
 v11 Then Jesus said, “There was a man who had two sons.
 v12 The younger of them said to his father, ‘Father, give me the share of the property that belongs to me.’ So he divided his livelihood between them.

 v13 Not many days later, the younger son gathered everything together and traveled to a distant country, and there he squandered his property in disordered living.
 v14 When he had spent everything, a severe famine came upon that country, and he began to be in need.

 v15 So he went and hired himself out to one of the citizens of that country, who sent him into the fields to feed pigs.
 v16 He longed to fill his stomach with the pods that the pigs were eating, and no one gave him anything.

 v17 But when he came to himself, he said, ‘How many of my father’s hired workers have bread in abundance, and here I am perishing with hunger!
 v18 I will get up and go to my father, and I will say to him, “Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you;
 v19 I am no longer worthy to be called your son; treat me like one of your hired workers.”’

 v20 So he got up and went to his father.

 v21 While he was still far off, his father saw him and was moved with compassion; he ran and embraced him and kissed him.

 v22 Then the son said to him, ‘Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you; I am no longer worthy to be called your son.’
 
 v23 But the father said to his servants, ‘Quickly, bring out the best robe and put it on him; put a ring on his hand and sandals on his feet.
 v24 And bring the fattened calf and kill it, and let us eat and celebrate;
 v25 for this son of mine was dead and has come to life again; he was lost and has been found.’ And they began to celebrate.

 v26 Now his elder son was in the field; and when he came and drew near to the house, he heard music and dancing.
 v27 He called one of the servants and asked what these things meant.

 v28 He said to him, ‘Your brother has come, and your father has killed the fattened calf, because he has received him back safe and sound.’
 v29 Then he became angry and refused to go in. His father came out and began to plead with him.

 v30 But he answered his father, ‘Listen! For all these years I have been serving you, and I have never disobeyed your command; yet you have never given me even a young goat, so that I might celebrate with my friends.
 v31 But when this son of yours came, who has devoured your property with prostitutes, you killed the fattened calf for him!’

 v32 Then the father said to him, ‘Child, you are always with me, and all that is mine is yours.
 v33 But we had to celebrate and rejoice, because this brother of yours was dead and has come to life; he was lost and has been found.’”
