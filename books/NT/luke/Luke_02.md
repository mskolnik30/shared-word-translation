## The Birth of Jesus
<p>
v01: In those days a decree went out from Caesar Augustus that all the inhabited world should be registered.
v02: This was the first registration, taken while Quirinius was governing Syria.
v03: All went to be registered, each to their own city.
</p>

<p>
v04: Joseph also went up from Galilee, from the city of Nazareth, to Judea, to the city of David called Bethlehem, because he was descended from the house and family of David.
v05: He went to be registered with Mary, to whom he was pledged, and who was expecting a child.
</p>

<p>
v06: While they were there, the time came for her to give birth.
v07: She gave birth to her firstborn child, wrapped him in bands of cloth, and laid him in a feeding trough, because there was no place for them in the guest room.
</p>

## Good News for All People
<p>
v08: In that region there were shepherds living in the fields, keeping watch over their flock by night.
</p>

<p>
v09: Then an angel of the Lord stood before them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them, and they were filled with great fear.
v10: The angel said to them, “Do not be afraid; for see—I am bringing you good news of great joy for all the people.
v11: To you is born this day, in the city of David, a Savior, who is the Messiah, the Lord.
v12: This will be a sign for you: you will find a child wrapped in bands of cloth and lying in a feeding trough.”
</p>

<p>
v13: And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of the heavenly host, praising God and saying,
v14: “Glory to God in the highest heaven,
and on earth peace among those whom God favors.”
</p>

<p>
v15: When the angels had left them and gone into heaven, the shepherds said to one another, “Let us go now to Bethlehem and see this thing that has taken place, which the Lord has made known to us.”
v16: So they went with haste and found Mary and Joseph, and the child lying in the feeding trough.
</p>

<p>
v17: When they saw this, they made known what had been told them about this child.
v18: And all who heard it were amazed at what the shepherds told them.
v19: But Mary treasured all these words and pondered them in her heart.
v20: The shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all they had heard and seen, just as it had been told them.
</p>

## Offering Jesus to God
<p>
v21: When eight days were completed for his circumcision, he was named Jesus—the name given by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.
v22: When the days of their purification according to the law of Moses were fulfilled, they brought him up to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord
v23: (as it is written in the law of the Lord, “Every firstborn male shall be designated as holy to the Lord”),
v24: and they offered a sacrifice according to what is stated in the law of the Lord, “a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons.”
</p>

## Simeon Sees God's Salvation
<p>
v25: Now there was a man in Jerusalem whose name was Simeon. This man was righteous and devout, looking forward to the consolation of Israel, and the Holy Spirit rested upon him.
v26: It had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he would not see death before he had seen the Lord’s Messiah.
</p>

<p>
v27: Guided by the Spirit, Simeon came into the temple; and when the parents brought in the child Jesus, to do for him what was customary under the law,
v28: Simeon took him in his arms and praised God, saying,
</p>

<p>
v29: “Now, Sovereign Lord, you are dismissing your servant in peace,
according to your word;
v30: for my eyes have seen your salvation,
v31: which you have prepared in the presence of all peoples,
v32: a light for revelation to the nations
and for glory to your people Israel.”
</p>

<p>
v33: And the child’s father and mother were amazed at what was being said about him.
</p>

<p>
v34: Then Simeon blessed them and said to Mary, his mother, “This child is destined for the falling and the rising of many in Israel, and to be a sign that will be opposed,
v35: so that the inner thoughts of many will be revealed—
and a sword will pierce your own soul also.”
</p>

## Anna Bears Witness
<p>
v36: There was also a prophet, Anna daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Asher. She was advanced in years, having lived with her husband seven years after her marriage,
v37: and then as a widow to the age of eighty-four. She never left the temple, but worshiped there with fasting and prayer night and day.
</p>

<p>
v38: At that very moment she came, and began to praise God and to speak about the child to all who were looking for the redemption of Jerusalem.
</p>

## The Maturing Jesus
<p>
v39: When they had finished everything required by the law of the Lord, they returned to Galilee, to their own town of Nazareth.
v40: The child grew and became strong, filled with wisdom; and the favor of God was upon him.
</p>

<p>
v41: Now his parents went to Jerusalem every year for the festival of the Passover.
v42: And when he was twelve years old, they went up as usual for the festival.
v43: When the festival was over and they were returning, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem, but his parents did not know it.
</p>

<p>
v44: Assuming that he was in the group of travelers, they went a day’s journey. Then they began to look for him among their relatives and friends.
v45: When they did not find him, they returned to Jerusalem to search for him.
</p>

<p>
v46: After three days they found him in the temple, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions.
v47: And all who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.
</p>
