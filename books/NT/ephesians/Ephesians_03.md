## The Mystery Revealed

<p>
v01: For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for the sake of you Gentiles—

v02: if indeed you have heard of the stewardship of the grace of God that was given to me for you,

v03: that by revelation the mystery was made known to me, as I wrote briefly before,
</p>

<p>
v04: by which, when you read, you can perceive my understanding in the mystery of Christ,

v05: which in other generations was not made known to the sons of humanity as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets by the Spirit:

v06: that the Gentiles are fellow heirs, members of the same body, and sharers in the promise in Christ Jesus through the good news.
</p>

<p>
v07: Of this I became a servant according to the gift of the grace of God given to me by the working of his power.

v08: To me, the very least of all the holy ones, this grace was given: to proclaim to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ,

v09: and to bring to light for all what is the administration of the mystery hidden for ages in God who created all things,
</p>

<p>
v10: so that through the church the manifold wisdom of God might now be made known to the rulers and authorities in the heavenly places,

v11: according to the eternal purpose that he accomplished in Christ Jesus our Lord,

v12: in whom we have boldness and access with confidence through faith in him.
</p>

<p>
v13: Therefore I ask you not to lose heart over my afflictions for you, which are your glory.
</p>

## Prayer for Strength and Comprehension

<p>
v14: For this reason I bow my knees before the Father,

v15: from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named,

v16: that according to the riches of his glory he may grant you to be strengthened with power through his Spirit in the inner person,
</p>

<p>
v17: so that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith—being rooted and grounded in love—

v18: that you may have strength to comprehend with all the holy ones what is the breadth and length and height and depth,

v19: and to know the love of Christ that surpasses knowledge, so that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.
</p>

<p>
v20: Now to the one who is able to do beyond all things exceedingly more than we ask or think, according to the power that is at work in us,

v21: to him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus to all generations, forever and ever. Amen.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v02: οἰκονομία (oikonomia)
“Stewardship” or “administration.” Management of what is entrusted.

v03: ἀποκάλυψις (apokalypsis)
“Revelation.” Disclosure of what was hidden.

v06: συγκληρονόμος (synklēronomos)
“Fellow heir.” One who shares inheritance.

v07: διακονία (diakonia)
“Service” or “ministry.” Active participation in a task.
