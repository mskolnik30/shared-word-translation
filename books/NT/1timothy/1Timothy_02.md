## Prayer for All People

<p>
v01: First of all, then, I urge that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all people,

v02: for kings and all who are in high positions, so that we may lead a peaceful and quiet life in all godliness and dignity.
</p>

<p>
v03: This is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Savior,

v04: who desires all people to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth.
</p>

<p>
v05: For there is one God, and one mediator between God and human beings, the man Christ Jesus,

v06: who gave himself as a ransom for all, the testimony given at the proper time,

v07: for which I was appointed a herald and an apostle—I am telling the truth, I am not lying—a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth.
</p>

## Conduct in Worship

<p>
v08: Therefore I want the men in every place to pray, lifting up holy hands without anger or dispute;
</p>

<p>
v09: likewise also that women should adorn themselves in respectable apparel, with modesty and self-control, not with braided hair and gold or pearls or costly clothing,

v10: but with what is fitting for women who profess reverence for God—with good works.
</p>

<p>
v11: Let a woman learn in quietness with all submission.

v12: I do not permit a woman to teach or to exercise authority over a man; rather, she is to remain quiet.
</p>

<p>
v13: For Adam was formed first, then Eve;

v14: and Adam was not deceived, but the woman, having been deceived, became a transgressor.
</p>

<p>
v15: Yet she will be saved through childbearing—if they continue in faith and love and holiness with self-control.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: δέησις (deēsis)
“Petition.” Specific request.

v01: ἔντευξις (enteuxis)
“Intercession.” Appeal on behalf of another.

v04: ἐπίγνωσις (epignōsis)
“Full knowledge.” Deep understanding.

v05: μεσίτης (mesitēs)
“Mediator.” One who stands between.
