## Seven Chosen to Serve

<p>
v01: Now in these days, as the disciples were increasing in number, there arose a complaint of the Hellenists against the Hebrews, because their widows were being overlooked in the daily distribution.
</p>

<p>
v02: And the twelve, summoning the multitude of the disciples, said, “It is not pleasing for us to neglect the word of God to serve tables.

v03: Therefore, brothers, select from among you seven men of good reputation, full of the Spirit and wisdom, whom we will appoint over this need.
</p>

<p>
v04: But we will devote ourselves to prayer and to the ministry of the word.”
</p>

<p>
v05: And the word pleased the whole multitude, and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and the Holy Spirit, and Philip, and Prochorus, and Nicanor, and Timon, and Parmenas, and Nicolaus, a proselyte of Antioch.

v06: These they set before the apostles, and having prayed, they laid hands on them.
</p>

<p>
v07: And the word of God was increasing, and the number of the disciples in Jerusalem was multiplying greatly, and a great many of the priests were becoming obedient to the faith.
</p>

## Stephen Seized

<p>
v08: And Stephen, full of grace and power, was doing great wonders and signs among the people.

v09: But some of those from the synagogue called that of the Freedmen, and of the Cyrenians and Alexandrians, and of those from Cilicia and Asia, rose up disputing with Stephen.
</p>

<p>
v10: And they were not able to resist the wisdom and the Spirit with which he was speaking.

v11: Then they secretly instigated men to say, “We have heard him speaking blasphemous words against Moses and God.”
</p>

<p>
v12: And they stirred up the people and the elders and the scribes, and coming upon him, they seized him and brought him to the council,

v13: and they set up false witnesses who said, “This man does not cease speaking words against this holy place and the Law;

v14: for we have heard him saying that this Jesus the Nazarene will destroy this place and will change the customs that Moses handed down to us.”
</p>

<p>
v15: And all those sitting in the council, looking intently at him, saw his face like the face of an angel.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Internal tension arises from cultural-linguistic divisions within the community.  

v02–04: Apostolic priorities distinguish between word ministry and logistical service.  

v03: Qualification emphasizes character and Spirit-empowerment, not merely skill.  

v05–06: Communal selection and apostolic commissioning function together.  

v07: Structural adjustment results in continued growth.  

v08: Stephen’s ministry combines proclamation and signs.  

v09–10: Opposition fails at the level of argument.  

v11–14: Accusation escalates through false testimony and distortion.  

v15: Stephen’s appearance signals divine presence and vindication.
