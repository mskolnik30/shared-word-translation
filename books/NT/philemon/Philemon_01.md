## Greeting

<p>
v01: Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother, to Philemon our beloved fellow worker,

v02: and Apphia our sister, and Archippus our fellow soldier, and the church in your house:

v03: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
</p>

## Thanksgiving and Prayer

<p>
v04: I give thanks to my God always when I remember you in my prayers,

v05: because I hear of your love and of the faith that you have toward the Lord Jesus and for all the saints,

v06: and I pray that the sharing of your faith may become effective in the knowledge of every good thing that is in us for the sake of Christ.

v07: For I have derived much joy and comfort from your love, my brother, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you.
</p>

## Paul’s Appeal for Onesimus

<p>
v08: Accordingly, though I am bold enough in Christ to command you to do what is required,

v09: yet for love’s sake I prefer to appeal to you—I, Paul, an old man and now a prisoner also for Christ Jesus—

v10: I appeal to you for my child Onesimus, whose father I became in my imprisonment.

v11: Formerly he was useless to you, but now he is indeed useful to you and to me.

v12: I am sending him back to you, sending my very heart.

v13: I would have been glad to keep him with me, in order that he might serve me on your behalf during my imprisonment for the gospel,

v14: but I preferred to do nothing without your consent, in order that your goodness might not be by compulsion but of your own accord.

v15: For this perhaps is why he was parted from you for a while, that you might have him back forever,

v16: no longer as a slave but more than a slave, as a beloved brother—especially to me, but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord.

v17: So if you consider me your partner, receive him as you would receive me.

v18: If he has wronged you at all, or owes you anything, charge that to my account.

v19: I, Paul, write this with my own hand: I will repay it—to say nothing of your owing me even your own self.

v20: Yes, brother, I want some benefit from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in Christ.

v21: Confident of your obedience, I write to you, knowing that you will do even more than I say.

v22: At the same time, prepare a guest room for me, for I am hoping that through your prayers I will be graciously given to you.
</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>
v23: Epaphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends greetings to you,

v24: and so do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers.

v25: The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: δέσμιος (desmios)
“Prisoner.” One in bonds.

v01: συνεργός (synergos)
“Fellow worker.” One who labors together.

v02: στρατιώτης (stratiōtēs)
“Soldier.” One engaged in service.

v25: χάρις (charis)
“Grace.” Favor given freely.
