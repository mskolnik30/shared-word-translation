## The Seven Angels with Seven Plagues

<p>
v01: Then I saw another sign in heaven, great and marvelous: seven angels having seven plagues, which are the last, for with them the wrath of God is completed.
</p>

## The Song of Moses and of the Lamb

<p>
v02: And I saw what appeared to be a sea of glass mixed with fire, and those who had conquered the beast and its image and the number of its name, standing beside the sea of glass, holding harps of God.

v03: And they sing the song of Moses, the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying,

“Great and marvelous are your works,

Lord God Almighty.

Just and true are your ways,

O King of the nations.

v04: Who will not fear, O Lord,

and glorify your name?

For you alone are holy.

All nations will come

and worship before you,

for your righteous acts have been revealed.”
</p>

## The Seven Bowls of Wrath

<p>
v05: After this I looked, and the sanctuary of the tent of witness in heaven was opened,

v06: and out of the sanctuary came the seven angels with the seven plagues, clothed in pure, bright linen, and with golden sashes around their chests.

v07: And one of the four living creatures gave to the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God, who lives forever and ever,

v08: and the sanctuary was filled with smoke from the glory of God and from his power, and no one could enter the sanctuary until the seven plagues of the seven angels were finished.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: πληγή (plēgē)
“Plague.” A blow or strike, here as judgment.

v02: θάλασσα ὑαλίνη (thalassa hyalinē)
“Sea of glass.” A stable, reflective expanse.

v03: ᾠδή (ōdē)
“Song.” A formal expression of praise.

v08: καπνός (kapnos)
“Smoke.” Manifestation of presence or glory.
