## The Thousand Years

<p>
v01: Then I saw an angel coming down from heaven, holding in his hand the key to the abyss and a great chain.

v02: And he seized the dragon, that ancient serpent, who is the devil and Satan, and bound him for a thousand years,

v03: and threw him into the abyss, and shut it and sealed it over him, so that he might not deceive the nations any longer, until the thousand years were ended. After that he must be released for a little while.

v04: Then I saw thrones, and seated on them were those to whom authority to judge was given. Also I saw the souls of those who had been beheaded for the testimony of Jesus and for the word of God, and those who had not worshiped the beast or its image and had not received its mark on their foreheads or their hands. They came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years.

v05: The rest of the dead did not come to life until the thousand years were ended. This is the first resurrection.

v06: Blessed and holy is the one who shares in the first resurrection. Over such the second death has no authority, but they will be priests of God and of Christ, and they will reign with him for a thousand years.
</p>

## The Defeat of Satan

<p>
v07: And when the thousand years are ended, Satan will be released from his prison

v08: and will come out to deceive the nations that are at the four corners of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them for battle; their number is like the sand of the sea.

v09: And they marched up over the broad plain of the earth and surrounded the camp of the saints and the beloved city, but fire came down from heaven and consumed them,

v10: and the devil who had deceived them was thrown into the lake of fire and sulfur where the beast and the false prophet were, and they will be tormented day and night forever and ever.
</p>

## The Final Judgment

<p>
v11: Then I saw a great white throne and the one seated on it. From his presence earth and sky fled away, and no place was found for them.

v12: And I saw the dead, great and small, standing before the throne, and books were opened. Then another book was opened, which is the book of life. And the dead were judged by what was written in the books, according to their works.

v13: And the sea gave up the dead who were in it, Death and Hades gave up the dead who were in them, and they were judged, each one of them, according to their works.

v14: Then Death and Hades were thrown into the lake of fire. This is the second death, the lake of fire.

v15: And if anyone’s name was not found written in the book of life, he was thrown into the lake of fire.
</p>
## Notes
v1: The angel acts with given authority; restraint precedes release.
v2: Multiple identifiers are retained without harmonization.
v3: Deception is limited temporally, not eliminated.
v4: Authority to judge is given; identity of those seated is not defined.
v4: Resurrection and reign are stated without mechanism.
v5–6: The distinction between first and second is asserted without explanation.
v6: Blessing is tied to participation rather than condition.
v7–8: Release leads immediately to deception; pattern repeats.
v8: “Gog and Magog” are invoked without clarification.
v9: Conflict is resolved abruptly; no battle description is given.
v10: Final judgment of the devil parallels prior figures.
v11: Creation itself recedes; no place remains.
v12–13: Judgment is individualized and based on recorded works.
v14: Death and Hades are treated as entities subject to judgment.
v15: The book of life functions as final distinction without elaboration.

“To seal.” To close securely.

“Throne.” Seat of authority.

“Resurrection.” Rising to life.

v6: δεύτερος θάνατος (deuteros thanatos)

“Second death.” Final state of judgment.

v8: πλανάω (planaō)

“To deceive.” To lead astray.

v10: βασανίζω (basanizō)

“To torment.” Ongoing suffering.

v11: τόπος (topos)

“Place.” Location or space.

v12: βιβλίον (biblion)

“Book.” Record of deeds.

v14: λίμνη τοῦ πυρός (limnē tou pyros)

“Lake of fire.” Final place of judgment.
