## Greeting

<p>
v01: The elder to the elect lady and her children, whom I love in truth, and not only I, but also all who know the truth,

v02: because of the truth that abides in us and will be with us forever:

v03: Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us, from God the Father and from Jesus Christ the Father’s Son, in truth and love.
</p>

## Walking in Truth and Love

<p>
v04: I rejoiced greatly to find some of your children walking in the truth, just as we were commanded by the Father.

v05: And now I ask you, dear lady—not as though I were writing you a new commandment, but the one we have had from the beginning—that we love one another.

v06: And this is love, that we walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, just as you have heard from the beginning, so that you should walk in it.
</p>

## Watch Yourselves

<p>
v07: For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who do not confess the coming of Jesus Christ in the flesh. Such a one is the deceiver and the antichrist.

v08: Watch yourselves, so that you may not lose what we have worked for, but may receive a full reward.

v09: Everyone who goes ahead and does not abide in the teaching of Christ does not have God. Whoever abides in the teaching has both the Father and the Son.

v10: If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into your house or give him any greeting,

v11: for whoever greets him takes part in his wicked works.
</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>
v12: Though I have much to write to you, I would rather not use paper and ink. Instead I hope to come to you and speak face to face, so that our joy may be complete.

v13: The children of your elect sister greet you.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: ἐκλεκτός (eklektos)
“Chosen” or “elect.” One set apart.

v01: ἀλήθεια (alētheia)
“Truth.” Reality expressed and lived.

v02: μένω (menō)
“To abide.” To remain or continue.

v03: χάρις (charis)
“Grace.” Favor given freely.
