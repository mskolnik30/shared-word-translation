## Warning Against Worldliness

<p>

v01: What causes quarrels and what causes fights among you? Is it not this, that your passions are at war within you?

v02: You desire and do not have, so you murder. You covet and cannot obtain, so you fight and quarrel. You do not have because you do not ask.

v03: You ask and do not receive, because you ask wrongly, to spend it on your passions.

v04: You adulterous people! Do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with God? Therefore whoever wishes to be a friend of the world makes himself an enemy of God.

v05: Or do you suppose it is to no purpose that the Scripture says, “He yearns jealously over the spirit that he has made to dwell in us”?

v06: But he gives more grace. Therefore it says, “God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.”

v07: Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

v08: Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded.

v09: Be wretched and mourn and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning and your joy to gloom.

v10: Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will exalt you.

</p>

## Do Not Speak Evil Against One Another

<p>

v11: Do not speak evil against one another, brothers. The one who speaks against a brother or judges his brother, speaks evil against the law and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law but a judge.

v12: There is one lawgiver and judge, he who is able to save and to destroy. But who are you to judge your neighbor?

</p>

## Boasting About Tomorrow

<p>

v13: Come now, you who say, “Today or tomorrow we will go into such and such a town and spend a year there and trade and make a profit”—

v14: yet you do not know what tomorrow will bring. What is your life? For you are a mist that appears for a little time and then vanishes.

v15: Instead you ought to say, “If the Lord wills, we will live and do this or that.”

v16: As it is, you boast in your arrogance. All such boasting is evil.

v17: So whoever knows the right thing to do and fails to do it, for him it is sin.

</p>

## Notes

v1: Conflict is traced to internal desire, not external cause.

v2–3: Lack is linked to both action and intention.

v4: Worldliness is framed relationally, not structurally.

v5: The citation is introduced without clear source.

v6: Grace is set in opposition to pride.

v7–8: Movement toward God is reciprocal.

v8: External and internal cleansing are paired.

v9: Emotional reversal is commanded, not explained.

v10: Exaltation follows humility without specified timing.

v11–12: Judgment is limited to God; human judgment is constrained.

v13–14: Planning assumes control that is not possessed.

v14: Life is described as transient.

v15: Dependence is expressed verbally.

v16: Boasting is identified with arrogance.

v17: Knowledge increases responsibility.

## Vocabulary

v1: ἡδονή (hēdonē)

“Passion.” Desire or pleasure.

v2: φονεύω (phoneuō)

“To murder.” To take life.

v4: φιλία (philia)

“Friendship.” Close association.

v4: ἔχθρα (echthra)

“Enmity.” Hostility.

v6: ταπεινός (tapeinos)

“Humble.” Lowly in status or attitude.

v7: ὑποτάσσω (hypotassō)

“To submit.” To place under authority.

v8: καθαρίζω (katharizō)

“To cleanse.” To make clean.

v8: δίψυχος (dipsychos)

“Double-minded.” Divided in allegiance.

v11: καταλαλέω (katalaleō)

“To speak against.” To slander.

v14: ἀτμίς (atmis)

“Mist.” Vapor or breath.

v16: καύχησις (kauchēsis)

“Boasting.” Expression of pride.

v17: ἁμαρτία (hamartia)

“Sin.” Missing the mark or wrongdoing.
