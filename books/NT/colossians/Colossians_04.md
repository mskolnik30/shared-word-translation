## Instructions for Households and Prayer

<p>
v01: Masters, grant to your servants what is just and fair, knowing that you also have a Master in heaven.
</p>

<p>
v02: Continue steadfastly in prayer, being watchful in it with thanksgiving,

v03: at the same time praying also for us, that God may open to us a door for the word, to speak the mystery of Christ, on account of which I am in chains,

v04: so that I may make it clear, as I ought to speak.
</p>

<p>
v05: Walk in wisdom toward those outside, making the most of the time.

v06: Let your speech always be with grace, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how you ought to answer each person.
</p>

## Final Greetings

<p>
v07: Tychicus will make known to you all my affairs; he is a beloved brother and faithful minister and fellow servant in the Lord.

v08: I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know our circumstances and that he may encourage your hearts,
</p>

<p>
v09: together with Onesimus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They will make known to you everything that has taken place here.
</p>

<p>
v10: Aristarchus my fellow prisoner greets you, and Mark the cousin of Barnabas (about whom you received instructions—if he comes to you, welcome him),

v11: and Jesus who is called Justus. These are the only ones of the circumcision among my fellow workers for the kingdom of God, and they have been a comfort to me.
</p>

<p>
v12: Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ Jesus, greets you, always striving on your behalf in his prayers, that you may stand mature and fully assured in all the will of God.

v13: For I bear him witness that he has labored much for you and for those in Laodicea and in Hierapolis.
</p>

<p>
v14: Luke the beloved physician greets you, as does Demas.

v15: Greet the brothers and sisters in Laodicea, and Nympha and the church in her house.
</p>

<p>
v16: And when this letter has been read among you, have it also read in the church of the Laodiceans; and you, in turn, read the letter from Laodicea.

v17: And say to Archippus, “See that you fulfill the ministry that you received in the Lord.”
</p>

<p>
v18: I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Grace be with you.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: ἴσος (isos)
“Fair” or “equal.” Justice measured in right relation.

v02: προσκαρτερέω (proskartereō)
“To continue steadfastly.” Persistent devotion.

v03: θύρα (thyra)
“Door.” Opportunity for action.

v05: καιρός (kairos)
“Time.” Opportune or decisive moment.
