## Please Others, Not Yourselves

<p>
v01: Now we who are strong ought to bear the weaknesses of those who are without strength, and not to please ourselves.

v02: Let each one of us please his neighbor for good, toward building up.
</p>

<p>
v03: For even Christ did not please himself, but as it is written, “The reproaches of those who reproached you fell upon me.”

v04: For whatever was written before was written for our instruction, so that through endurance and through the encouragement of the Scriptures we might have hope.
</p>

<p>
v05: Now may the God of endurance and encouragement grant you to think the same thing among one another according to Christ Jesus,

v06: so that with one accord, with one mouth, you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
</p>

<p>
v07: Therefore receive one another, just as Christ also received you, to the glory of God.
</p>

## Christ the Hope of Jews and Gentiles

<p>
v08: For I say that Christ has become a servant of the circumcision on behalf of the truth of God, to confirm the promises to the fathers,

v09: and that the Gentiles might glorify God for mercy, as it is written,

“Therefore I will confess you among the Gentiles,

and sing to your name.”
</p>

<p>
v10: And again he says,

“Rejoice, O Gentiles, with his people.”
</p>

<p>
v11: And again,

“Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles,

and let all the peoples praise him.”
</p>

<p>
v12: And again Isaiah says,

“There shall be the root of Jesse,

and the one who rises to rule the Gentiles;

in him the Gentiles will hope.”
</p>

<p>
v13: Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that you may abound in hope by the power of the Holy Spirit.
</p>

## Paul the Minister to the Gentiles

<p>
v14: And I myself am convinced about you, my brothers, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish one another.
</p>

<p>
v15: But I have written more boldly to you in some measure, as reminding you, because of the grace that was given to me from God,

v16: to be a minister of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, serving as a priest of the gospel of God, so that the offering of the Gentiles may become acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.
</p>

<p>
v17: Therefore I have boasting in Christ Jesus in the things pertaining to God.

v18: For I will not dare to speak of anything except what Christ has accomplished through me for the obedience of the Gentiles, by word and deed,
</p>

<p>
v19: in the power of signs and wonders, in the power of the Spirit, so that from Jerusalem and all the way around to Illyricum I have fulfilled the gospel of Christ.
</p>

<p>
v20: And thus I make it my ambition to proclaim the gospel, not where Christ has already been named, so that I might not build upon another’s foundation,

v21: but as it is written,

“Those who were not told about him will see,

and those who have not heard will understand.”
</p>

## Paul’s Plan to Visit Rome

<p>
v22: Therefore I was often hindered from coming to you;

v23: but now, having no more place in these regions, and having a longing for many years to come to you,

v24: whenever I go to Spain—for I hope to see you as I pass through and to be helped on my way there by you, if first I may enjoy your company for a while—
</p>

<p>
v25: but now I am going to Jerusalem serving the holy ones.

v26: For Macedonia and Achaia have been pleased to make a contribution for the poor among the holy ones in Jerusalem.
</p>

<p>
v27: For they were pleased to do it, and they are indebted to them; for if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual things, they are obligated also to minister to them in material things.
</p>

<p>
v28: Therefore, when I have completed this and have sealed to them this fruit, I will go by way of you to Spain.

v29: And I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the blessing of Christ.
</p>

<p>
v30: Now I urge you, brothers, through our Lord Jesus Christ and through the love of the Spirit, to strive together with me in prayers to God for me,

v31: that I may be delivered from those who are disobedient in Judea, and that my service for Jerusalem may be acceptable to the holy ones,

v32: so that I may come to you in joy by the will of God and be refreshed together with you.
</p>

<p>
v33: Now the God of peace be with you all. Amen.
</p>
