## God’s Righteous Judgment

<p>
v01: Therefore you are without excuse, O man, every one who judges; for in that which you judge another, you condemn yourself, for you who judge practice the same things.

v02: And we know that the judgment of God is according to truth against those who practice such things.
</p>

<p>
v03: But do you suppose this, O man, who judges those who practice such things and yet does them yourself, that you will escape the judgment of God?

v04: Or do you despise the riches of his kindness and forbearance and patience, not knowing that the kindness of God leads you to repentance?
</p>

<p>
v05: But according to your hardness and unrepentant heart you are storing up for yourself wrath in the day of wrath and revelation of the righteous judgment of God,

v06: who will render to each one according to his works:
</p>

<p>
v07: to those who by perseverance in good work seek glory and honor and incorruption, eternal life;

v08: but to those who are self-seeking and do not obey the truth but obey unrighteousness, wrath and fury.
</p>

<p>
v09: There will be tribulation and distress for every soul of man who does evil, of the Jew first and also of the Greek,

v10: but glory and honor and peace for everyone who does good, for the Jew first and also for the Greek.
</p>

<p>
v11: For there is no partiality with God.
</p>

<p>
v12: For as many as sinned without the law will also perish without the law, and as many as sinned under the law will be judged by the law;

v13: for not the hearers of the law are righteous before God, but the doers of the law will be justified.
</p>

<p>
v14: For when Gentiles, who do not have the law, do by nature the things of the law, these, though not having the law, are a law to themselves,

v15: who show the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience bearing witness and their thoughts accusing or even defending them,

v16: on the day when God judges the secrets of men through Jesus Christ, according to my gospel.
</p>

## The Jews and the Law

<p>
v17: But if you call yourself a Jew and rely upon the law and boast in God,

v18: and know his will and approve the things that are excellent, being instructed out of the law,

v19: and are confident that you yourself are a guide to the blind, a light to those in darkness,

v20: an instructor of the foolish, a teacher of infants, having the form of knowledge and of the truth in the law—
</p>

<p>
v21: you therefore who teach another, do you not teach yourself? You who proclaim that one must not steal, do you steal?

v22: You who say not to commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples?
</p>

<p>
v23: You who boast in the law, through your transgression of the law do you dishonor God?

v24: For “the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you,” just as it is written.
</p>

## True Circumcision

<p>
v25: For circumcision indeed profits if you practice the law; but if you are a transgressor of the law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.

v26: Therefore if the uncircumcised keeps the righteous requirements of the law, will not his uncircumcision be counted as circumcision?
</p>

<p>
v27: And the one who is physically uncircumcised, if he fulfills the law, will judge you who through the letter and circumcision are a transgressor of the law.
</p>

<p>
v28: For he is not a Jew who is one outwardly, nor is circumcision outward in the flesh;

v29: but he is a Jew who is one inwardly, and circumcision is of the heart, in spirit, not in letter, whose praise is not from men but from God.
</p>
