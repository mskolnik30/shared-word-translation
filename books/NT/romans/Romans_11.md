## The Remnant of Israel

<p>
v01: I say then, has God rejected his people? May it never be! For I also am an Israelite, from the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.

v02: God has not rejected his people whom he foreknew. Or do you not know what the Scripture says in Elijah, how he pleads with God against Israel?
</p>

<p>
v03: “Lord, they have killed your prophets, they have torn down your altars, and I alone am left, and they seek my life.”

v04: But what does the divine response say to him? “I have left for myself seven thousand men who have not bowed the knee to Baal.”
</p>

<p>
v05: So then also in the present time there has come to be a remnant according to the election of grace.

v06: But if by grace, it is no longer from works; otherwise grace is no longer grace.
</p>

## Israel’s Rejection Is Not Final

<p>
v07: What then? Israel did not obtain what it seeks, but the elect obtained it, and the rest were hardened,

v08: just as it is written,

“God gave them a spirit of stupor,

eyes not to see and ears not to hear,

to this very day.”
</p>

<p>
v09: And David says,

“Let their table become a snare and a trap

and a stumbling block and a recompense to them;

v10: let their eyes be darkened so that they do not see,

and bend their backs continually.”
</p>

## The Salvation of the Gentiles

<p>
v11: I say then, did they stumble so that they might fall? May it never be! But by their transgression salvation has come to the Gentiles, to provoke them to jealousy.

v12: Now if their transgression is riches for the world and their failure riches for the Gentiles, how much more their fullness!
</p>

<p>
v13: But I am speaking to you Gentiles. Inasmuch then as I am an apostle of Gentiles, I magnify my ministry,

v14: if somehow I may provoke to jealousy those who are my flesh and save some of them.
</p>

<p>
v15: For if their rejection is the reconciliation of the world, what will their acceptance be but life from the dead?
</p>

## The Olive Tree

<p>
v16: And if the firstfruit is holy, so also the whole; and if the root is holy, so also the branches.
</p>

<p>
v17: But if some of the branches were broken off, and you, being a wild olive shoot, were grafted in among them and became a fellow-partaker of the root of the rich olive tree,

v18: do not boast against the branches; but if you boast, remember that you do not support the root, but the root supports you.
</p>

<p>
v19: You will say then, “Branches were broken off so that I might be grafted in.”

v20: True; they were broken off because of unbelief, but you stand by faith. Do not be arrogant, but fear;

v21: for if God did not spare the natural branches, neither will he spare you.
</p>

<p>
v22: Behold then the kindness and severity of God: toward those who have fallen, severity; but toward you, kindness, if you continue in that kindness; otherwise you also will be cut off.
</p>

<p>
v23: And they also, if they do not continue in unbelief, will be grafted in; for God is able to graft them in again.

v24: For if you were cut from what is by nature a wild olive tree and were grafted contrary to nature into a cultivated olive tree, how much more will these, the natural branches, be grafted into their own olive tree?
</p>

## The Mystery of Israel’s Salvation

<p>
v25: For I do not want you to be unaware of this mystery, brothers, lest you be wise in yourselves: that a partial hardening has come upon Israel until the fullness of the Gentiles has come in,

v26: and so all Israel will be saved, just as it is written,

“The Deliverer will come from Zion,

he will remove ungodliness from Jacob;

v27: and this is my covenant with them,

when I take away their sins.”
</p>

<p>
v28: According to the gospel they are enemies for your sake, but according to election they are beloved for the sake of the fathers;

v29: for the gifts and the calling of God are irrevocable.
</p>

<p>
v30: For just as you once were disobedient to God but now have received mercy because of their disobedience,

v31: so also these now have been disobedient, so that by the mercy shown to you they also may now receive mercy.

v32: For God has shut up all into disobedience so that he may show mercy to all.
</p>

## Doxology

<p>
v33: Oh, the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are his judgments and untraceable his ways!

v34: For “Who has known the mind of the Lord, or who has become his counselor?”

v35: “Or who has given to him first, and it will be repaid to him?”
</p>

<p>
v36: For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be the glory forever. Amen.
</p>
## Notes
v01–02: God’s covenantal faithfulness remains intact.
v05–06: Grace excludes works as a basis.
v07–10: Hardening fulfills Scripture.
v11–15: Israel’s rejection serves Gentile inclusion and future restoration.
v16–24: Olive tree imagery explains inclusion and warning.
v25–27: Mystery involves partial hardening and future salvation.
v28–29: Election and promise remain operative.
v32: Universal disobedience sets stage for universal mercy.
v33–36: Theology culminates in doxology.
