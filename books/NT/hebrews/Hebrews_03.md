## Jesus Greater Than Moses

<p>
v01: Therefore, holy brothers, sharers in a heavenly calling, consider Jesus, the apostle and high priest of our confession,

v02: who was faithful to him who appointed him, just as Moses also was faithful in all God’s house.

v03: For Jesus has been counted worthy of more glory than Moses—as much more glory as the builder of a house has more honor than the house itself.

v04: For every house is built by someone, but the builder of all things is God.

v05: Now Moses was faithful in all God’s house as a servant, to testify to the things that were to be spoken later,

v06: but Christ is faithful over God’s house as a son. And we are his house, if indeed we hold fast our confidence and our boasting in hope.
</p>

## A Rest for the People of God

<p>
v07: Therefore, as the Holy Spirit says,

“Today, if you hear his voice,

v08: do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion,

on the day of testing in the wilderness,

v09: where your fathers put me to the test

and saw my works for forty years.

v10: Therefore I was provoked with that generation,

and said, ‘They always go astray in their heart;

they have not known my ways.’

v11: As I swore in my wrath,

‘They shall not enter my rest.’”

v12: Take care, brothers, lest there be in any of you an evil, unbelieving heart, leading you to fall away from the living God.

v13: But exhort one another every day, as long as it is called “today,” that none of you may be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.

v14: For we have come to share in Christ, if indeed we hold our original confidence firm to the end.

v15: As it is said,

“Today, if you hear his voice,

do not harden your hearts as in the rebellion.”

v16: For who were those who heard and yet rebelled? Was it not all those who left Egypt led by Moses?

v17: And with whom was he provoked for forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose bodies fell in the wilderness?

v18: And to whom did he swear that they would not enter his rest, but to those who were disobedient?

v19: So we see that they were unable to enter because of unbelief.
</p>
