## The Beloved Comes to the Garden
<p>
v01: I have come into my garden,
my sister, my bride.
I have gathered my myrrh with my spice.
I have eaten my honeycomb with my honey.
I have drunk my wine with my milk.
Eat, friends;
drink,
and be drunk with love.
</p>

## The Bride's Dream
<p>
v02: I slept,
but my heart was awake.
The sound of my beloved!
He is knocking:
“Open to me,
my sister,
my love,
my dove,
my perfect one,
for my head is wet with dew,
my locks with the drops of the night.”
</p>

<p>
v03: I had taken off my robe;
must I put it on again?
I had washed my feet;
must I soil them again?
v04: My beloved put his hand
to the latch,
and my heart was stirred within me.
v05: I arose to open to my beloved,
and my hands dripped with myrrh,
my fingers with flowing myrrh,
upon the handles of the bolt.
</p>

<p>
v06: I opened to my beloved,
but my beloved had turned and gone.
My soul failed me when he spoke.
I sought him,
but I did not find him.
I called him,
but he gave no answer.
</p>

<p>
v07: The watchmen found me
as they went about in the city.
They struck me;
they wounded me.
The keepers of the walls
took away my veil from me.
</p>

<p>
v08: I adjure you,
O daughters of Jerusalem,
if you find my beloved,
what will you tell him?
Tell him that I am sick with love.
</p>

## The Daughters of Jerusalem Respond
<p>
v09: What is your beloved
more than another beloved,
O most beautiful among women?
What is your beloved
more than another beloved,
that you so adjure us?
</p>

## The Bride Praises Her Beloved
<p>
v10: My beloved is radiant and ruddy,
distinguished among ten thousand.
v11: His head is finest gold;
his locks are wavy,
black as a raven.
</p>

<p>
v12: His eyes are like doves
beside streams of water,
washed with milk,
set beside fullness.
v13: His cheeks are like beds of spices,
mounds of sweet-scented herbs.
His lips are lilies,
dripping flowing myrrh.
</p>

<p>
v14: His arms are rods of gold,
set with jewels.
His body is polished ivory,
adorned with sapphires.
v15: His legs are pillars of marble,
set upon bases of fine gold.
His appearance is like Lebanon,
choice as the cedars.
</p>

<p>
v16: His mouth is most sweet,
and he is altogether desirable.
This is my beloved,
and this is my friend,
O daughters of Jerusalem.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: כַּלָּה (kallâ)
“Bride.” A woman joined in covenant marriage.

v01: דּוֹדִים (dōḏîm)
“Love.” Expressions of affection, delight, and intimacy.

v02: תָּם (tām)
“Perfect” or “whole.” Complete, blameless, and cherished.

v04: מֵעֶה (mēʿeh)
“Inner being.” The seat of deep emotions and affections.
