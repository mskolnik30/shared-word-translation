## The Beloved Praises the Bride
<p>
v01: Behold, you are beautiful, my love;
behold, you are beautiful.
Your eyes are doves
behind your veil.
Your hair is like a flock of goats
streaming down the slopes of Gilead.
</p>

<p>
v02: Your teeth are like a flock of shorn ewes
that have come up from the washing,
all of them bearing twins,
and none among them has lost its young.
v03: Your lips are like a scarlet thread,
and your mouth is lovely.
Your cheeks are like halves of a pomegranate
behind your veil.
</p>

<p>
v04: Your neck is like the tower of David,
built in rows of stone.
Upon it hang a thousand shields,
all of them shields of warriors.
v05: Your two breasts are like two fawns,
twins of a gazelle,
that pasture among the lilies.
</p>

<p>
v06: Until the day breathes
and the shadows flee,
I will go to the mountain of myrrh
and to the hill of frankincense.
v07: You are altogether beautiful, my love;
there is no blemish in you.
</p>

## Come with Me from Lebanon
<p>
v08: Come with me from Lebanon, my bride;
come with me from Lebanon.
Depart from the summit of Amana,
from the summit of Senir and Hermon,
from the dens of lions,
from the mountains of leopards.
</p>

<p>
v09: You have captured my heart, my sister, my bride;
you have captured my heart
with one glance of your eyes,
with one jewel of your necklace.
v10: How beautiful is your love,
my sister, my bride!
How much better is your love than wine,
and the fragrance of your oils
than any spice.
</p>

<p>
v11: Your lips drip nectar, my bride;
honey and milk are under your tongue,
and the fragrance of your garments
is like the fragrance of Lebanon.
</p>

## A Garden Locked
<p>
v12: A garden locked is my sister, my bride,
a spring locked,
a fountain sealed.
v13: Your shoots are an orchard of pomegranates
with choice fruits,
henna with nard,
v14: nard and saffron,
calamus and cinnamon,
with every kind of frankincense tree,
myrrh and aloes,
with all the finest spices.
</p>

<p>
v15: A garden fountain,
a well of living water,
and flowing streams from Lebanon.
</p>

## The Bride's Invitation
<p>
v16: Awake, north wind,
and come, south wind.
Blow upon my garden,
that its spices may flow out.
Let my beloved come into his garden
and eat its choicest fruits.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: צַמָּה (ṣammâ)
“Veil.” A covering worn by a woman.

v01: גִּלְעָד (Gilʿāḏ)
“Gilead.” A fertile region east of the Jordan River.

v03: רִמּוֹן (rimmôn)
“Pomegranate.” A fruit often associated with beauty and fruitfulness.

v08: כַּלָּה (kallâ)
“Bride.” A woman joined in covenant marriage.
