## Will the Faultfinder Contend with the Almighty?

<p>
v01: The LORD answered Job and said:

v02: “Shall the one who contends with the Almighty correct him?
Let the one who argues with God answer it.”
</p>

## Job Answers Briefly

<p>
v03: Then Job answered the LORD and said:

v04: “Look, I am small. What can I answer you?
I put my hand over my mouth.

v05: Once I have spoken, and I will not answer;
twice, and I will add no more.”
</p>

## The LORD Answers Again from the Storm

<p>
v06: Then the LORD answered Job from the storm and said:

v07: “Gird up your waist like a man;
I will question you,
and you make me know.

v08: Will you really annul my justice?
Will you condemn me
so that you may be righteous?

v09: Do you have an arm like God?
Can you thunder with a voice like his?
</p>

## Clothe Yourself with Majesty

<p>
v10: Adorn yourself now with majesty and exaltation;
clothe yourself with glory and splendor.

v11: Pour out the overflowing of your anger;
see everyone proud and bring him low.

v12: See everyone proud and humble him;
tread down the wicked where they stand.

v13: Hide them together in the dust;
bind their faces in the hidden place.

v14: Then even I will praise you,
that your own right hand can save you.
</p>

## Look at Behemoth

<p>
v15: Look now at Behemoth,
which I made with you.
It eats grass like an ox.

v16: Look at its strength in its loins
and its power in the muscles of its belly.

v17: It makes its tail stiff like a cedar;
the sinews of its thighs are knit together.

v18: Its bones are tubes of bronze;
its limbs like bars of iron.

v19: It is the first of the ways of God;
its Maker alone can bring near his sword.

v20: For the mountains yield food for it,
where all the wild animals play.

v21: Under lotus plants it lies,
in the shelter of reeds and marsh.

v22: Lotus plants cover it with their shade;
willows of the wadi surround it.

v23: Look, if the river surges, it does not panic;
it is confident though the Jordan rushes against its mouth.

v24: Can one take it by its eyes
or pierce its nose with snares?
</p>
##Notes

v24: In the Masoretic Text, the material corresponding to English Job 41:1–8 continues as Job 40:25–32.

##Vocabulary

v02: יִסּוֹר (yissor)
“To correct” or “reprove.” The question asks whether Job will correct the Almighty.

v08: מִשְׁפָּט (mishpat)
“Justice” or “judgment.” Job is asked whether he will annul God’s justice.

v09: זְרוֹעַ (zeroa)
“Arm.” A symbol of effective strength and power.

v15: בְּהֵמוֹת (Behemot)
“Behemoth.” A great creature whose name is related to the Hebrew word for beasts or cattle.
