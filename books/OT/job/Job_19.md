## How Long Will You Crush Me with Words?

<p>
v01: Then Job answered and said:

v02: “How long will you torment my soul
and crush me with words?

v03: These ten times you have humiliated me;
you are not ashamed to treat me as a stranger.

v04: And even if I have truly erred,
my error remains with me.

v05: If indeed you magnify yourselves against me
and argue my disgrace against me,

v06: know then that God has bent me crooked
and has closed his net around me.
</p>

## He Has Fenced Up My Way

<p>
v07: Look, I cry, ‘Violence!’ but I am not answered;
I cry for help, but there is no justice.

v08: He has fenced up my way so that I cannot pass,
and he has set darkness upon my paths.

v09: He has stripped my glory from me
and removed the crown from my head.

v10: He breaks me down on every side, and I am gone;
he uproots my hope like a tree.

v11: He kindles his anger against me
and counts me as one of his adversaries.

v12: His troops come together,
build up their siege ramp against me,
and camp around my tent.
</p>

## Those I Loved Have Turned Against Me

<p>
v13: He has put my brothers far from me,
and those who know me have become complete strangers.

v14: My relatives have ceased to come,
and my close companions have forgotten me.

v15: Those staying in my house and my female servants count me as a stranger;
I have become a foreigner in their eyes.

v16: I call to my servant, but he does not answer;
with my own mouth I must beg him.

v17: My breath is offensive to my wife,
and I am repulsive to the children of my own body.

v18: Even little children despise me;
when I rise, they speak against me.

v19: All the men of my counsel abhor me,
and those I loved have turned against me.

v20: My bones cling to my skin and flesh,
and I have escaped only by the skin of my teeth.
</p>

## Have Mercy on Me, My Friends

<p>
v21: Have mercy on me, have mercy on me, my friends,
for the hand of God has touched me.

v22: Why do you pursue me as God does
and never become satisfied with my flesh?
</p>

## I Know That My Redeemer Lives

<p>
v23: If only my words were written!
If only they were inscribed in a scroll!

v24: With an iron stylus and lead,
engraved in rock forever!

v25: But I know that my redeemer lives,
and afterward he will rise upon the dust.

v26: And after my skin has been destroyed—this!—
yet from my flesh I shall see God,

v27: whom I myself shall see,
and my eyes shall behold, and not a stranger.
My inward parts are consumed within me.
</p>

## Fear the Sword

<p>
v28: If you say, ‘How shall we pursue him?’
and, ‘The root of the matter is found in him,’

v29: fear the sword yourselves,
for wrath brings the punishments of the sword,
so that you may know there is judgment.”
</p>
##Notes

v20: “skin of my teeth” is an unusual Hebrew expression preserved nearly literally in English. Its precise anatomical or idiomatic sense is uncertain, but it conveys the narrowness of Job’s survival.

##Vocabulary

v02: דָּכָא (daka)
“To crush.” Job says the friends crush him with words.

v07: חָמָס (chamas)
“Violence.” Job’s unanswered cry for justice.

v10: תִּקְוָה (tiqvah)
“Hope.” God has uprooted Job’s hope like a tree.

v21: חָנַן (chanan)
“To have mercy” or “show favor.” Job’s plea to his friends.
