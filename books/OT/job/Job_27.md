## I Will Not Put Away My Integrity

<p>
v01: Job again took up his discourse and said:

v02: “As God lives, who has taken away my justice,
and the Almighty, who has made my soul bitter,

v03: as long as my breath is in me
and the spirit of God is in my nostrils,

v04: my lips will surely not speak injustice,
and my tongue will not utter deceit.

v05: Far be it from me to declare you righteous!
Until I expire, I will not put away my integrity from me.

v06: I hold fast my righteousness and will not let it go;
my heart does not reproach me for any of my days.
</p>

## Let My Enemy Be Like the Wicked

<p>
v07: Let my enemy be like the wicked,
and the one who rises against me like the unjust.

v08: For what is the hope of the godless when God cuts him off,
when God takes away his life?

v09: Will God hear his cry
when distress comes upon him?

v10: Will he delight himself in the Almighty?
Will he call upon God at all times?
</p>

## I Will Teach You About the Hand of God

<p>
v11: I will teach you about the hand of God;
what is with the Almighty I will not conceal.

v12: Look, all of you have seen it.
Why then do you become completely empty?
</p>

## This Is the Portion of a Wicked Man

<p>
v13: This is the portion of a wicked man with God,
and the inheritance ruthless people receive from the Almighty:

v14: If his children multiply, it is for the sword,
and his offspring will not be satisfied with bread.

v15: Those who survive him will be buried by death,
and his widows will not weep.

v16: Though he heaps up silver like dust
and prepares clothing like clay,

v17: he may prepare it, but the righteous will wear it,
and the innocent will divide the silver.

v18: He builds his house like a moth,
like a shelter a watchman makes.

v19: He lies down rich, but he will not do so again;
he opens his eyes, and it is gone.

v20: Terrors overtake him like waters;
at night a whirlwind steals him away.

v21: The east wind carries him away, and he is gone;
it sweeps him from his place.

v22: It hurls itself against him and does not spare;
he desperately flees from its hand.

v23: It claps its hands at him
and hisses him out of his place.”
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v02: מִשְׁפָּט (mishpat)
“Justice” or “case.” Job says God has taken away his justice.

v02: מָרַר (marar)
“To make bitter.” The Almighty has embittered Job’s life.

v05: תֻּמָּה (tummah)
“Integrity.” Job refuses to surrender his claim to integrity.

v06: צְדָקָה (tsedaqah)
“Righteousness.” Job holds fast to his righteous standing.
