## Eliphaz Begins to Speak

<p>
v01: Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said:

v02: “If someone ventures a word with you,
will you become impatient?
But who can hold back words?

v03: Look, you have instructed many,
and you strengthened weak hands.

v04: Your words raised up one who was stumbling,
and you strengthened knees that were bending.

v05: But now it comes to you, and you are impatient;
it touches you, and you are terrified.

v06: Is not your fear of God your confidence,
and the integrity of your ways your hope?
</p>

## Who Ever Perished Being Innocent?

<p>
v07: Remember now: who, being innocent, ever perished?
And where were the upright ever cut off?

v08: As I have seen,
those who plow trouble
and sow harm reap it.

v09: By the breath of God they perish,
and by the wind of his anger they are consumed.

v10: The roar of the lion,
the voice of the fierce lion,
and the teeth of the young lions are broken.

v11: The strong lion perishes for lack of prey,
and the cubs of the lioness are scattered.
</p>

## A Word Came Secretly to Me

<p>
v12: Now a word came secretly to me,
and my ear received a whisper of it,

v13: amid troubling thoughts from visions of the night,
when deep sleep falls upon people.

v14: Dread came upon me, and trembling,
and made all my bones shake.

v15: A spirit passed before my face;
the hair of my flesh stood up.

v16: It stood still,
but I could not recognize its appearance.
A form was before my eyes;
there was silence, then I heard a voice:
</p>

## Can a Human Be More Righteous Than God?

<p>
v17: ‘Can a mortal be righteous before God?
Can a man be pure before his Maker?

v18: Look, he does not trust his servants,
and he charges his messengers with error.

v19: How much more those who dwell in houses of clay,
whose foundation is in the dust,
who are crushed before a moth.

v20: Between morning and evening they are broken;
without anyone noticing, they perish forever.

v21: Is not their tent-cord pulled up within them?
They die, and not in wisdom.’”
</p>
##Notes

v18: “messengers” renders Hebrew *malakhim*, which can mean angels.

##Vocabulary

v06: תִּקְוָה (tiqvah)
“Hope” or “expectation.” Eliphaz identifies Job’s integrity as the supposed basis of hope.

v08: אָוֶן (aven)
“Trouble,” “harm,” or “iniquity.” Wrongdoing pictured as seed that produces a harvest.

v09: נְשָׁמָה (neshamah)
“Breath.” The breath of God as an image of destructive power.

v15: רוּחַ (ruach)
“Spirit,” “wind,” or “breath.” The mysterious presence passing before Eliphaz.
