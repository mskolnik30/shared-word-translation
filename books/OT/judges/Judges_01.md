## Judah Goes Up First

<p>
v01: After the death of Joshua, the children of Israel asked the LORD, “Who shall go up first for us against the Canaanites, to fight against them?”

v02: The LORD said, “Judah shall go up. Look, I have given the land into his hand.”

v03: Then Judah said to Simeon his brother, “Come up with me into my allotted land, and let us fight against the Canaanites. Then I also will go with you into your allotted land.” So Simeon went with him.

v04: Judah went up, and the LORD gave the Canaanites and the Perizzites into their hand, and they struck down ten thousand of them at Bezek.

v05: They found Adoni-Bezek at Bezek and fought against him, and they struck down the Canaanites and the Perizzites.

v06: Adoni-Bezek fled, but they pursued him and seized him, and they cut off his thumbs and his big toes.

v07: Adoni-Bezek said, “Seventy kings with their thumbs and big toes cut off gathered scraps under my table. As I have done, so God has repaid me.” They brought him to Jerusalem, and he died there.
</p>

## Jerusalem and Hebron

<p>
v08: The children of Judah fought against Jerusalem and captured it. They struck it with the edge of the sword and set the city on fire.

v09: Afterward the children of Judah went down to fight against the Canaanites who lived in the hill country, the Negev, and the foothills.

v10: Judah went against the Canaanites who lived in Hebron—Hebron was formerly named Kiriath-Arba—and they struck Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.

v11: From there they went against the inhabitants of Debir. The name of Debir was formerly Kiriath-Sepher.

v12: Caleb said, “Whoever strikes Kiriath-Sepher and captures it, I will give Achsah my daughter to him as wife.”

v13: Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother, captured it, and Caleb gave him Achsah his daughter as wife.

v14: When she came to him, she urged him to ask her father for a field. She got down from her donkey, and Caleb said to her, “What do you want?”

v15: She said to him, “Give me a blessing. Since you have given me land in the Negev, give me springs of water also.” So Caleb gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.
</p>

## Judah and Simeon

<p>
v16: The descendants of the Kenite, Moses’ father-in-law, went up with the children of Judah from the City of Palms into the wilderness of Judah, which is in the Negev near Arad. They went and lived with the people.

v17: Judah went with Simeon his brother, and they struck the Canaanites who inhabited Zephath and devoted it to destruction. The name of the city was called Hormah.

v18: Judah captured Gaza with its territory, Ashkelon with its territory, and Ekron with its territory.

v19: The LORD was with Judah, and Judah took possession of the hill country, but they could not drive out the inhabitants of the plain because they had iron chariots.

v20: Hebron was given to Caleb, as Moses had said, and he drove out from there the three sons of Anak.

v21: But the children of Benjamin did not drive out the Jebusites who inhabited Jerusalem. So the Jebusites have lived with the children of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this day.
</p>

## The House of Joseph Takes Bethel

<p>
v22: The house of Joseph also went up against Bethel, and the LORD was with them.

v23: The house of Joseph sent men to spy out Bethel. The name of the city was formerly Luz.

v24: The spies saw a man coming out of the city and said to him, “Please show us the entrance to the city, and we will show you steadfast love.”

v25: He showed them the entrance to the city. They struck the city with the edge of the sword, but they let the man and all his family go.

v26: The man went to the land of the Hittites and built a city, and he called its name Luz. That is its name to this day.
</p>

## Israel Does Not Drive Them Out

<p>
v27: Manasseh did not drive out the inhabitants of Beth-Shean and its villages, or Taanach and its villages, or the inhabitants of Dor and its villages, or the inhabitants of Ibleam and its villages, or the inhabitants of Megiddo and its villages. The Canaanites persisted in living in that land.

v28: When Israel became strong, they put the Canaanites to forced labor, but did not drive them out completely.

v29: Ephraim did not drive out the Canaanites who lived in Gezer, so the Canaanites lived among them in Gezer.

v30: Zebulun did not drive out the inhabitants of Kitron or the inhabitants of Nahalol. The Canaanites lived among them and became forced labor.

v31: Asher did not drive out the inhabitants of Acco or the inhabitants of Sidon, Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphik, or Rehob.

v32: So the Asherites lived among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land, because they did not drive them out.

v33: Naphtali did not drive out the inhabitants of Beth-Shemesh or the inhabitants of Beth-Anath, but lived among the Canaanites, the inhabitants of the land. Yet the inhabitants of Beth-Shemesh and Beth-Anath became forced labor for them.

v34: The Amorites pressed the children of Dan into the hill country and did not allow them to come down into the plain.

v35: The Amorites persisted in living in Mount Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim, but the hand of the house of Joseph became heavy upon them, and they became forced labor.

v36: The border of the Amorites was from the ascent of Akrabbim, from Sela and upward.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: יָרַשׁ (yarash)
“To possess,” “inherit,” or “dispossess.” A central term for taking possession of the land.

v02: נָתַן (natan)
“To give.” The LORD declares the land given even while Israel must still enter and fight.

v07: שָׁלַם (shalam)
“To repay” or “make complete.” Used by Adoni-Bezek for corresponding recompense.

v17: חָרַם (charam)
“To devote to destruction.” To place something under irrevocable ban.

v24: חֶסֶד (ḥesed)
“Steadfast love,” “loyalty,” or “kindness.” Faithful action within a relationship or agreement.
