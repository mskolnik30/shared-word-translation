## Micah’s Silver and Image

<p>
v01: There was a man from the hill country of Ephraim whose name was Micah.

v02: He said to his mother, “The eleven hundred pieces of silver that were taken from you, concerning which you uttered a curse and also spoke it in my hearing—look, the silver is with me. I took it.” His mother said, “Blessed be my son by the LORD.”

v03: He returned the eleven hundred pieces of silver to his mother. His mother said, “I solemnly consecrate the silver to the LORD from my hand for my son, to make a carved image and a cast image. Now therefore I will return it to you.”

v04: When he returned the silver to his mother, his mother took two hundred pieces of silver and gave them to the silversmith. He made from it a carved image and a cast image, and it was in the house of Micah.

v05: This man Micah had a house of gods. He made an ephod and household gods and ordained one of his sons, who became his priest.

v06: In those days there was no king in Israel. Everyone did what was right in their own eyes.
</p>

## A Levite Comes to Micah’s House

<p>
v07: There was a young man from Bethlehem in Judah, from the clan of Judah. He was a Levite, and he was staying there.

v08: The man departed from the city, from Bethlehem in Judah, to stay wherever he might find a place. As he traveled, he came to the hill country of Ephraim, to Micah’s house.

v09: Micah said to him, “Where do you come from?” He said to him, “I am a Levite from Bethlehem in Judah, and I am going to stay wherever I may find a place.”

v10: Micah said to him, “Stay with me and be to me a father and a priest. I will give you ten pieces of silver a year, a set of garments, and your food.” So the Levite went in.

v11: The Levite agreed to stay with the man, and the young man became to him like one of his sons.

v12: Micah ordained the Levite, and the young man became his priest and was in Micah’s house.

v13: Then Micah said, “Now I know that the LORD will do good to me, because I have a Levite as priest.”
</p>
##Notes

v06: “Everyone did what was right in their own eyes” becomes the interpretive refrain for the final chapters of Judges.

##Vocabulary

v03: קָדַשׁ (qadash)
“To consecrate” or “make holy.” To set something apart for sacred use.

v03: פֶּסֶל (pesel)
“Carved image.” An image fashioned by carving.

v03: מַסֵּכָה (massekhah)
“Cast image.” An image formed from molten metal.

v05: מִלֵּא יָד (mille yad)
“To fill the hand,” rendered “ordain.” An idiom for installing someone in priestly service.
