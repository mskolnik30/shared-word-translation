## Twenty-Three Years of Warning

<p>
v01: The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah—that was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon—

v02: which Jeremiah the prophet spoke to all the people of Judah and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying:

v03: “From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon, king of Judah, until this day—these twenty-three years—the word of the LORD has come to me, and I have spoken to you, rising early and speaking, but you have not listened.

v04: The LORD has sent to you all his servants the prophets, rising early and sending them, but you have not listened or inclined your ear to hear.

v05: They said, ‘Turn now, each of you, from your evil way and from the evil of your deeds, and dwell upon the land that the LORD gave to you and your ancestors from long ago and forever.

v06: Do not go after other gods to serve them and bow down to them, and do not provoke me to anger with the work of your hands; then I will not bring harm upon you.’

v07: But you did not listen to me,” says the LORD, “so that you provoked me to anger with the work of your hands, to your own harm.”
</p>

## Seventy Years for Babylon

<p>
v08: Therefore thus says the LORD of Hosts: “Because you have not listened to my words,

v09: look, I am sending and taking all the clans of the north,” says the LORD, “and Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, my servant, and I will bring them against this land, against its inhabitants, and against all these surrounding nations. I will devote them to destruction and make them a horror, a hissing, and everlasting ruins.

v10: I will destroy from them the sound of joy and the sound of gladness, the voice of bridegroom and the voice of bride, the sound of millstones and the light of the lamp.

v11: This whole land will become a ruin and a horror, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon seventy years.

v12: Then, when seventy years are completed, I will attend to the king of Babylon and that nation for their guilt,” says the LORD, “and to the land of the Chaldeans, and I will make it everlasting desolations.

v13: I will bring upon that land all my words that I have spoken against it, all that is written in this book, which Jeremiah prophesied against all the nations.

v14: For many nations and great kings will make slaves even of them, and I will repay them according to their deeds and according to the work of their hands.”
</p>

## The Cup of the Wine of Wrath

<p>
v15: For thus said the LORD, the God of Israel, to me: “Take this cup of the wine of wrath from my hand and make all the nations to whom I send you drink it.

v16: They will drink and stagger and act madly because of the sword that I am sending among them.”

v17: So I took the cup from the hand of the LORD and made all the nations to whom the LORD sent me drink:

v18: Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, its kings and its officials, to make them a ruin, a horror, a hissing, and a curse, as they are this day;

v19: Pharaoh king of Egypt, his servants, his officials, and all his people;

v20: all the mixed people; all the kings of the land of Uz; all the kings of the land of the Philistines—Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod;

v21: Edom, Moab, and the children of Ammon;

v22: all the kings of Tyre, all the kings of Sidon, and the kings of the coastland beyond the sea;

v23: Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who clip the corners of their hair;

v24: all the kings of Arabia and all the kings of the mixed people who dwell in the wilderness;

v25: all the kings of Zimri, all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of Media;

v26: all the kings of the north, near and far, one after another, and all the kingdoms of the earth that are on the face of the ground. And the king of Sheshach shall drink after them.
</p>

## You Shall Surely Drink

<p>
v27: “Then you shall say to them: Thus says the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel: Drink, become drunk, vomit, fall, and rise no more because of the sword that I am sending among you.

v28: And if they refuse to take the cup from your hand to drink, then you shall say to them: Thus says the LORD of Hosts: You shall surely drink!

v29: For look, I am beginning to bring harm upon the city that is called by my name, and should you go entirely unpunished? You shall not go unpunished, for I am calling for a sword against all the inhabitants of the earth,” says the LORD of Hosts.
</p>

## The LORD Has a Controversy with the Nations

<p>
v30: “And you, prophesy against them all these words and say to them:

The LORD will roar from on high
and from his holy dwelling give his voice.
He will roar mightily against his pasture;
he will shout like those who tread grapes
against all the inhabitants of the earth.

v31: The tumult will reach
to the ends of the earth,
for the LORD has a controversy
with the nations.
He enters into judgment
with all flesh;
the wicked he gives to the sword,”
says the LORD.

v32: Thus says the LORD of Hosts:

“Look, disaster is going out
from nation to nation,
and a great storm is stirring
from the farthest parts of the earth.

v33: Those slain by the LORD on that day will be from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth. They will not be lamented, gathered, or buried. They will become manure on the face of the ground.
</p>

## Wail, Shepherds

<p>
v34: Wail, shepherds, and cry out!
Roll yourselves in ashes, leaders of the flock!
For your days for slaughter are fulfilled,
and I will scatter you,
and you will fall like a precious vessel.

v35: Flight will perish from the shepherds,
and escape from the leaders of the flock.

v36: A sound—the cry of the shepherds,
the wailing of the leaders of the flock!
For the LORD is devastating their pasture.

v37: The peaceful pastures are silenced
because of the burning anger of the LORD.

v38: Like a young lion he has left his thicket,
for their land has become a horror
because of the fierce oppressor
and because of his burning anger.
</p>
## Notes
v01: The fourth year of Jehoiakim corresponds to the first year of Nebuchadnezzar in Jeremiah’s reckoning and marks a major transition in the book’s geopolitical setting.
v03: Jeremiah frames his ministry as twenty-three years of persistent speech met by persistent refusal to listen.
v09: Nebuchadnezzar is called “my servant,” a striking designation for a foreign king used as an instrument of the LORD’s judgment.
v11–12: “seventy years” expresses the appointed period of Babylonian domination and becomes an important chronological promise in Jeremiah 29.
v13: In the Masoretic arrangement, Jeremiah’s oracles against the nations appear later in chapters 46–51. The Greek tradition places much of that material after 25:13 and in a different order.
v15–29: The cup of wrath is a prophetic image of judgment that moves from Judah outward to the nations.
v26: “Sheshach” is generally understood as a cryptographic name for Babylon using the Hebrew *atbash* letter substitution system.
v30–31: The LORD’s judgment is described through both lion imagery and legal language: roaring and entering into a controversy with the nations.

##Vocabulary

v03: הַשְׁכֵּם (hashkem)
“To rise early.” Part of Jeremiah’s repeated idiom for persistent divine sending and prophetic warning.

v09: חֵרֶם (cherem)
“Devoted destruction” or “ban.” Used for comprehensive judgment.

v11: שִׁבְעִים שָׁנָה (shiv'im shanah)
“Seventy years.” The appointed span of Babylonian service.

v15: כּוֹס (kos)
“Cup.” A vessel used metaphorically for an allotted experience of judgment.

v26: שֵׁשַׁךְ (Sheshakh)
“Sheshach.” A coded designation for Babylon.
