## A Word to Baruch

<p>
v01: The word that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch son of Neriah when he wrote these words in a book from the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying:

v02: “Thus says the LORD, the God of Israel, concerning you, Baruch:

v03: You said,
‘Woe to me!
For the LORD has added grief to my pain.
I am weary with my groaning,
and I find no rest.’
</p>

## Do Not Seek Great Things for Yourself

<p>
v04: “Thus you shall say to him: Thus says the LORD:

Look, what I built
I am tearing down,
and what I planted
I am uprooting—
that is, this whole land.

v05: And you—do you seek great things for yourself?
Do not seek them.
For look, I am bringing disaster
upon all flesh,” says the LORD,
“but I will give you your life as plunder
in every place where you go.”
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v03: יָגוֹן (yagon)
“Grief” or “sorrow.” Deep emotional pain.

v03: אֲנָחָה (anachah)
“Groaning” or “sighing.” Audible expression of distress.

v04: בָּנָה (banah)
“To build.” Here the LORD tears down what had been built.

v04: נָטַע (nata)
“To plant.” Here paired with uprooting.
