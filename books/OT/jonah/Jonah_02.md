## Jonah’s Prayer from the Fish
<p>
v01: Then Jonah prayed to the LORD his God from the belly of the fish,
v02: saying,
“I called out to the LORD from my distress,
and he answered me.
From the belly of Sheol I cried for help;
you heard my voice.
</p>

<p>
v03: For you cast me into the deep,
into the heart of the seas,
and the current surrounded me.
All your breakers and your waves
passed over me.
v04: Then I said,
‘I am driven away from before your eyes;
yet I shall again look
upon your holy temple.’
</p>

<p>
v05: The waters closed in over me to take my life;
the deep surrounded me;
seaweed was wrapped around my head
v06: at the roots of the mountains.
I went down to the land
whose bars closed upon me forever;
yet you brought up my life from the pit,
O LORD my God.
</p>

<p>
v07: When my life was fainting away,
I remembered the LORD,
and my prayer came to you,
into your holy temple.
v08: Those who cling to worthless idols
forsake their covenant loyalty.
v09: But I, with the voice of thanksgiving,
will sacrifice to you.
What I have vowed I will pay.
Salvation belongs to the LORD.”
</p>

## Jonah Delivered
<p>
v10: Then the LORD spoke to the fish, and it vomited Jonah out upon the dry land.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v02: שְׁאוֹל (Šĕʾôl)
“Sheol.” The realm of the dead, often associated with death and destruction.

v04: הֵיכַל קָדְשֶׁךָ (hêḵal qodšeḵā)
“Your holy temple.” The sanctuary toward which the prayer is directed.

v08: חֶסֶד (ḥeseḏ)
“Covenant loyalty” or “steadfast love.” Faithful devotion within a covenant relationship.

v09: יְשׁוּעָה (yĕšûʿâ)
“Salvation” or “deliverance.” Rescue that comes from God.
