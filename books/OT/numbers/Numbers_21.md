## The Bronze Serpent

## Israel Defeats Arad

<p>
v01: When the Canaanite king of Arad, who lived in the Negev, heard that Israel was coming by the way of Atharim, he fought against Israel and took some of them captive.

v02: Israel vowed a vow to the LORD and said, “If you surely give this people into my hand, then I will devote their cities to destruction.”

v03: The LORD listened to the voice of Israel and gave the Canaanites into their hand. They devoted them and their cities to destruction, and the name of the place was called Hormah.
</p>

<p>
v04: And they set out from Mount Hor by the way to the Sea of Reeds, to go around the land of Edom, and the soul of the people grew short on the way.

v05: And the people spoke against God and against Moses, “Why have you brought us up out of Egypt to die in the wilderness? For there is no bread and no water, and our soul loathes this worthless bread.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Then the LORD sent among the people the fiery serpents, and they bit the people, and many people of Israel died.

v07: And the people came to Moses and said, “We have sinned, for we have spoken against the LORD and against you. Pray to the LORD that he take away the serpents from us.” So Moses prayed for the people.
</p>

<p>
v08: And the LORD said to Moses, “Make for yourself a fiery serpent and set it on a pole, and it shall be that everyone who is bitten, when he looks at it, shall live.”

v09: So Moses made a serpent of bronze and set it on the pole. And if a serpent bit anyone, and he looked at the bronze serpent, he lived.
</p>

## The Journey toward Moab

<p>
v10: The sons of Israel set out and camped at Oboth.

v11: They set out from Oboth and camped at Iye-Abarim, in the wilderness that is before Moab toward the sunrise.

v12: From there they set out and camped in the Wadi Zered.

v13: From there they set out and camped beyond the Arnon, which is in the wilderness extending from the territory of the Amorites, for the Arnon is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites.

v14: Therefore it is said in the Book of the Wars of the LORD:

“Waheb in Suphah,
and the wadis of the Arnon,

v15: and the slope of the wadis
that extends toward the dwelling of Ar
and leans upon the border of Moab.”
</p>

<p>
v16: From there they went to Beer. This is the well of which the LORD said to Moses, “Gather the people, and I will give them water.”

v17: Then Israel sang this song:

“Spring up, O well! Sing to it—

v18: the well that the rulers dug,
that the nobles of the people excavated,
with the scepter, with their staffs.”

And from the wilderness they went to Mattanah,

v19: and from Mattanah to Nahaliel, and from Nahaliel to Bamoth,

v20: and from Bamoth to the valley that is in the field of Moab, to the top of Pisgah, which overlooks Jeshimon.
</p>

## Israel Defeats Sihon

<p>
v21: Israel sent messengers to Sihon king of the Amorites, saying,

v22: “Let me pass through your land. We will not turn aside into field or vineyard. We will not drink water from a well. We will go by the King’s Highway until we have passed through your territory.”

v23: But Sihon did not allow Israel to pass through his territory. Sihon gathered all his people and went out against Israel into the wilderness. He came to Jahaz and fought against Israel.

v24: Israel struck him with the edge of the sword and possessed his land from the Arnon to the Jabbok, as far as the children of Ammon, for the border of the children of Ammon was strong.

v25: Israel took all these cities, and Israel lived in all the cities of the Amorites, in Heshbon and in all its towns.

v26: For Heshbon was the city of Sihon king of the Amorites, who had fought against the former king of Moab and had taken all his land from his hand as far as the Arnon.
</p>

<p>
v27: Therefore those who speak in proverbs say:

“Come to Heshbon;
let the city of Sihon be built and established.

v28: For fire went out from Heshbon,
a flame from the city of Sihon;
it consumed Ar of Moab,
the lords of the heights of the Arnon.

v29: Woe to you, Moab!
You are undone, people of Chemosh!
He has given his sons as fugitives
and his daughters into captivity
to Sihon king of the Amorites.

v30: We shot them down;
Heshbon has perished as far as Dibon;
we laid waste as far as Nophah,
which reaches to Medeba.”
</p>

<p>
v31: So Israel lived in the land of the Amorites.

v32: Moses sent to spy out Jazer, and they captured its towns and dispossessed the Amorites who were there.
</p>

## Israel Defeats Og

<p>
v33: Then they turned and went up by the way of Bashan. Og king of Bashan came out against them, he and all his people, for battle at Edrei.

v34: The LORD said to Moses, “Do not fear him, for I have given him and all his people and his land into your hand. You shall do to him as you did to Sihon king of the Amorites, who lived at Heshbon.”

v35: So they struck him and his sons and all his people until no survivor remained to him, and they possessed his land.
</p>

## Notes

v04: “The soul grew short” signals impatience and emotional exhaustion—weariness becomes theological complaint.

v05: Speech turns accusatory; divine provision (“this bread”) is reinterpreted as contemptible.

v06: The serpents function as both judgment and consequence—death enters through what bites.

v07: Confession is direct and verbal (“we have sinned”), restoring relationship through acknowledgment and intercession.

v08: Healing is mediated through paradox—what afflicts becomes the instrument of restoration.

v09: The act required is simple yet interpretive: to “look” is to trust. Life comes through directed attention.

## Vocabulary

v04: קָצַר נֶפֶשׁ (qāṣar nepeš)  

“To grow short of soul.” To become impatient or discouraged.

v05: דָּבַר (dāḇar)  

“To speak.” Here, to speak against.

v05: קָלֹקֵל (qālōqēl)  

“Worthless/light.” Despised or insubstantial.

v06: שָׂרָף (śārāf)  

“Fiery serpent.” Burning, possibly venomous creature.

v06: נָשַׁךְ (nāšaḵ)  

“To bite.” To strike with harm.

v07: חָטָא (ḥāṭāʾ)  

“To sin.” To miss the mark or act wrongly.

v07: פָּלַל (pālal)  

“To pray/intercede.” To intervene before God.

v08: נֵס (nēs)  

“Pole/standard.” Raised sign or signal.

v08: רָאָה (rāʾāh)  

“To see/look.” To direct attention with intent.

v09: נְחֹשֶׁת (neḥōšeṯ)  

“Bronze.” Metal associated with durability.

v09: חָיָה (ḥāyāh)  

“To live.” To remain alive or be restored.
