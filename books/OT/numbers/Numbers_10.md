## The Silver Trumpets

<p>
v01: The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,
v02: “Make two silver trumpets for yourself; make them of hammered work. Use them for summoning the congregation and for setting the camps in motion.
v03: When both are blown, the whole congregation shall gather to you at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.
v04: But if only one is blown, the leaders, the heads of Israel’s thousands, shall gather to you.
v05: When you sound a signal blast, the camps on the east shall set out.
v06: When you sound a second signal blast, the camps on the south shall set out. A signal blast shall be sounded for their setting out.
v07: But when the assembly is gathered, you shall blow the trumpets without sounding the signal blast.
</p>

<p>
v08: The sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow the trumpets. This shall be a perpetual statute for you throughout your generations.
v09: When you go to war in your land against an adversary who oppresses you, you shall sound the signal with the trumpets, and you shall be remembered before the LORD your God and be saved from your enemies.
v10: Also on your days of rejoicing, your appointed festivals, and the beginnings of your months, you shall blow the trumpets over your burnt offerings and your sacrifices of well-being. They shall be for you as a remembrance before your God. I am the LORD your God.”
</p>

## Israel Leaves Sinai

<p>
v11: In the second year, in the second month, on the twentieth day of the month, the cloud lifted from over the tabernacle of the testimony.
v12: Then the Israelites set out from the wilderness of Sinai by stages, and the cloud settled in the wilderness of Paran.
v13: They set out for the first time according to the command of the LORD through Moses.
</p>

<p>
v14: The standard of the camp of the Judahites set out first by their divisions, and over its division was Nahshon son of Amminadab.
v15: Over the division of the tribe of Issachar was Nethanel son of Zuar.
v16: Over the division of the tribe of Zebulun was Eliab son of Helon.
v17: Then the tabernacle was taken down, and the Gershonites and Merarites, who carried the tabernacle, set out.
</p>

<p>
v18: Then the standard of the camp of Reuben set out by their divisions, and over its division was Elizur son of Shedeur.
v19: Over the division of the tribe of Simeon was Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai.
v20: Over the division of the tribe of Gad was Eliasaph son of Deuel.
v21: Then the Kohathites set out, carrying the holy things. The others would set up the tabernacle before they arrived.
</p>

<p>
v22: Then the standard of the camp of the Ephraimites set out by their divisions, and over its division was Elishama son of Ammihud.
v23: Over the division of the tribe of Manasseh was Gamaliel son of Pedahzur.
v24: Over the division of the tribe of Benjamin was Abidan son of Gideoni.
</p>

<p>
v25: Then the standard of the camp of the Danites set out as the rear guard for all the camps, by their divisions, and over its division was Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai.
v26: Over the division of the tribe of Asher was Pagiel son of Ochran.
v27: Over the division of the tribe of Naphtali was Ahira son of Enan.
v28: This was the order of setting out for the Israelites by their divisions whenever they set out.
</p>

## Moses Invites Hobab

<p>
v29: Moses said to Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses’ father-in-law, “We are setting out for the place of which the LORD said, ‘I will give it to you.’ Come with us, and we will do good to you, for the LORD has spoken good concerning Israel.”
v30: But he said to him, “I will not go; rather, I will go to my own land and my own kindred.”
v31: Moses said, “Please do not leave us, for you know where we should camp in the wilderness, and you can be eyes for us.
v32: If you come with us, whatever good the LORD does for us, we will do for you.”
</p>

## The Ark Goes Before Them

<p>
v33: So they set out from the mountain of the LORD on a journey of three days, and the ark of the covenant of the LORD went before them for those three days to seek out a resting place for them.
v34: The cloud of the LORD was over them by day when they set out from the camp.
</p>

<p>
v35: Whenever the ark set out, Moses said:
“Rise up, O LORD,
and let your enemies be scattered;
let those who hate you flee before you.”

v36: And whenever it came to rest, he said:
“Return, O LORD,
to the countless thousands of Israel.”
</p>
## Notes
v02–10: The trumpets coordinate assembly, movement, warfare, and worship. The same instrument gathers the community and marks its dependence upon the LORD.
v11: Israel leaves Sinai nearly one year after arriving there, moving from the extended period of instruction toward the wilderness journey.
v21: The sanctuary’s structure travels ahead of the Kohathites so that it can be erected before the holy furnishings arrive.
v29: The Hebrew relationship in “Hobab son of Reuel the Midianite, Moses’ father-in-law” is grammatically compressed. Other passages identify Reuel/Jethro as Moses’ father-in-law, and the precise kinship designation of Hobab is debated.
v31: Hobab’s practical knowledge of the wilderness does not replace the guiding cloud. Human experience and divine guidance are placed beside one another rather than treated as rivals.
v35–36: These short sayings frame the ark’s movement and rest as the movement and rest of the LORD among Israel.

## Vocabulary

v02: חֲצוֹצְרָה (chatsotserah)
“Trumpet.” A long silver instrument used for signals and sacred occasions.

v05: תְּרוּעָה (teruah)
“Signal blast,” “shout,” or “alarm.” A distinct trumpet sound associated with movement or battle.

v10: זִכָּרוֹן (zikkaron)
“Remembrance.” A ritual act or sign that brings a person or people before God in covenantal memory.

v25: מְאַסֵּף (me'assef)
“Rear guard.” The force that gathers or protects the rear of a moving body.

v33: מְנוּחָה (menuchah)
“Resting place.” A place of settled rest sought for the traveling people.
