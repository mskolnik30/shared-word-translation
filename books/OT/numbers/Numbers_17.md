## Aaron’s Staff Buds

<p>
v01: The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,
v02: “Speak to the Israelites and take from them one staff from each ancestral house, from all their leaders according to their ancestral houses, twelve staffs. Write each man’s name on his staff.
v03: Write Aaron’s name on the staff of Levi, for there shall be one staff for the head of each ancestral house.
v04: Place them in the Tent of Meeting before the testimony, where I meet with you.
v05: The staff of the man whom I choose shall sprout. In this way I will bring to an end before me the grumblings of the Israelites that they grumble against you.”
</p>

<p>
v06: Moses spoke to the Israelites, and all their leaders gave him staffs, one for each leader according to their ancestral houses, twelve staffs, and Aaron’s staff was among their staffs.
v07: Moses placed the staffs before the LORD in the Tent of the Testimony.
v08: On the next day Moses entered the Tent of the Testimony, and look—Aaron’s staff for the house of Levi had sprouted. It had brought forth buds, produced blossoms, and borne ripe almonds.
v09: Moses brought all the staffs out from before the LORD to all the Israelites. They looked, and each man took his staff.
</p>

## A Sign Against Rebellion

<p>
v10: The LORD said to Moses, “Put Aaron’s staff back before the testimony to be kept as a sign for the rebellious, so that you may bring their grumblings against me to an end and they may not die.”
v11: Moses did so. As the LORD commanded him, so he did.
</p>

<p>
v12: The Israelites said to Moses, “Look, we are perishing! We are lost! We are all lost!
v13: Everyone who comes near, who comes near to the LORD’s tabernacle, dies. Are we all to perish?”
</p>

##Notes
v02: The same Hebrew word, *matteh*, can mean both “staff” and “tribe.” The sign therefore represents both an individual leader and the ancestral house he leads.
v08: The staff displays every stage of fruitfulness at once—sprouting, buds, blossoms, and ripe almonds—making the sign unmistakably extraordinary.
v10: The staff is retained not as a trophy but as a continuing sign directed toward repeated grumbling and the danger of unauthorized approach.
v12–13: The people’s response shows that the preceding judgments have created fear of the sanctuary. Chapter 18 answers that fear by clarifying priestly and Levitical responsibility.

##Vocabulary
v02: מַטֶּה (matteh)
“Staff” or “tribe.” The double meaning gives the sign both personal and tribal force.
v05: פָּרַח (parach)
“To sprout” or “blossom.” Sudden visible growth from Aaron’s staff.
v08: שָׁקֵד (shaqed)
“Almond.” The fruit produced by Aaron’s staff.
v10: אוֹת (ot)
“Sign.” A visible marker that bears continuing meaning.
v10: מְרִי (meri)
“Rebellion.” Persistent resistance against the LORD’s ordering.
