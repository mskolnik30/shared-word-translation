## The Camps Around the Tent of Meeting

<p>
v01: The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,  
v02: “The Israelites shall camp, each by their own standard, with the banners of their ancestral houses. They shall camp around the Tent of Meeting at a distance.  
</p>

## The Camp of Judah

<p>
v03: Those camping on the east side, toward the sunrise, shall be under the standard of the camp of Judah by their divisions. The leader of the Judahites shall be Nahshon son of Amminadab,  
v04: and his division, those counted, seventy-four thousand six hundred.  
v05: Those camping next to him shall be the tribe of Issachar. The leader of the Issacharites shall be Nethanel son of Zuar,  
v06: and his division, those counted, fifty-four thousand four hundred.  
v07: Then the tribe of Zebulun. The leader of the Zebulunites shall be Eliab son of Helon,  
v08: and his division, those counted, fifty-seven thousand four hundred.  
v09: All those counted of the camp of Judah were one hundred eighty-six thousand four hundred by their divisions. They shall set out first.  
</p>

## The Camp of Reuben

<p>
v10: On the south side shall be the standard of the camp of Reuben by their divisions. The leader of the Reubenites shall be Elizur son of Shedeur,  
v11: and his division, those counted, forty-six thousand five hundred.  
v12: Those camping next to him shall be the tribe of Simeon. The leader of the Simeonites shall be Shelumiel son of Zurishaddai,  
v13: and his division, those counted, fifty-nine thousand three hundred.  
v14: Then the tribe of Gad. The leader of the Gadites shall be Eliasaph son of Reuel,  
v15: and his division, those counted, forty-five thousand six hundred fifty.  
v16: All those counted of the camp of Reuben were one hundred fifty-one thousand four hundred fifty by their divisions. They shall set out second.  
</p>

## The Tent of Meeting in the Center

<p>
v17: Then the Tent of Meeting shall set out, with the camp of the Levites in the midst of the camps. As they camp, so they shall set out, each in position by their standards.  
</p>

## The Camp of Ephraim

<p>
v18: On the west side shall be the standard of the camp of Ephraim by their divisions. The leader of the Ephraimites shall be Elishama son of Ammihud,  
v19: and his division, those counted, forty thousand five hundred.  
v20: Next to him shall be the tribe of Manasseh. The leader of the Manassites shall be Gamaliel son of Pedahzur,  
v21: and his division, those counted, thirty-two thousand two hundred.  
v22: Then the tribe of Benjamin. The leader of the Benjaminites shall be Abidan son of Gideoni,  
v23: and his division, those counted, thirty-five thousand four hundred.  
v24: All those counted of the camp of Ephraim were one hundred eight thousand one hundred by their divisions. They shall set out third.  
</p>

## The Camp of Dan

<p>
v25: On the north side shall be the standard of the camp of Dan by their divisions. The leader of the Danites shall be Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai,  
v26: and his division, those counted, sixty-two thousand seven hundred.  
v27: Those camping next to him shall be the tribe of Asher. The leader of the Asherites shall be Pagiel son of Ochran,  
v28: and his division, those counted, forty-one thousand five hundred.  
v29: Then the tribe of Naphtali. The leader of the Naphtalites shall be Ahira son of Enan,  
v30: and his division, those counted, fifty-three thousand four hundred.  
v31: All those counted of the camp of Dan were one hundred fifty-seven thousand six hundred. They shall set out last by their standards.  
</p>

## Israel Encamps as Commanded

<p>
v32: These are those counted from the Israelites by their ancestral houses. All those counted in the camps by their divisions were six hundred three thousand five hundred fifty.  
v33: But the Levites were not counted among the Israelites, as the LORD commanded Moses.  
v34: Thus the Israelites did. According to all that the LORD commanded Moses, so they camped by their standards, and so they set out, each by clan and ancestral house.  
</p>

## Notes
v2: The camp is organized around the Tent of Meeting. Israel’s military and tribal arrangement therefore has the divine dwelling, rather than a king or army commander, at its center.  
v3: “toward the sunrise” — The east is named through the movement of the sun, preserving the Hebrew spatial expression.  
v17: The Levites and sanctuary move at the center of the marching order just as they occupy the center of the encamped community.  
v34: The repeated formula “according to all that the LORD commanded” presents the ordered camp as an act of obedience rather than merely administration.  

## Vocabulary
v2: דֶּגֶל (degel)  
“Standard.” A tribal marker around which a camp or military division assembled.  
v2: אֹת (ot)  
“Sign” or “banner.” Here a marker associated with an ancestral house.  
v3: מַחֲנֶה (machaneh)  
“Camp.” An organized encampment, often with military associations.  
v17: מוֹעֵד (moed)  
“Meeting” or “appointed time.” In “Tent of Meeting,” the place appointed for encounter with God.
