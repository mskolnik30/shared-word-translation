<p>
v01: The word of the LORD came to me, saying,
</p>
<p>
v02: "And you, son of man, will you judge? Will you judge the bloody city? Then make her know all her abominations.
v03: You shall say: Thus says the LORD GOD: A city shedding blood in her midst so that her time will come, and making idols that defile her.
v04: You have become guilty by the blood you have shed and defiled by your idols you have made. You have brought your days near and come to your years. Therefore I have made you a reproach to the nations and a mockery to all lands.
v05: Those who are near and those who are far from you will mock you: you who are defiled of name, full of tumult.
v06: Behold, the princes of Israel in you, every one according to his power, were bent on shedding blood.
v07: Father and mother are treated lightly in you; the resident alien they have oppressed within you; the orphan and widow they have mistreated.
v08: You have despised my holy things and profaned my sabbaths.
v09: Men who slander are in you to shed blood, and in you they eat at the mountains. Within you they commit lewdness.
v10: They uncover their fathers' nakedness in you; they violate women during their impurity.
v11: One commits abominations with his neighbor's wife, another defiles his daughter-in-law with lewdness; another violates his sister, his father's daughter, within you.
v12: They accept bribes in you to shed blood. You take interest and profit and you extort your neighbors for gain, but me you have forgotten, declares the LORD GOD.
</p>
<p>
v13: "Behold, I strike my hand at your dishonest gain which you have made, and at the bloodshed which has been in your midst.
v14: Can your heart endure, or can your hands be strong in the days that I deal with you? I, the LORD, have spoken and will act.
v15: I will scatter you among the nations and disperse you through the countries, and I will consume your uncleanness from you.
v16: You will be profaned in yourself before the eyes of the nations, and you shall know that I am the LORD."
</p>
<p>
v17: The word of the LORD came to me, saying,
v18: "Son of man, the house of Israel has become dross to me. All of them are bronze and tin and iron and lead in the midst of a furnace; they are the dross of silver.
v19: Therefore thus says the LORD GOD: Because all of you have become dross, therefore behold, I will gather you into the midst of Jerusalem.
v20: Like the gathering of silver and bronze and iron and lead and tin into the midst of a furnace, to blow the fire upon it to melt it, so I will gather you in my anger and in my wrath, and I will lay you there and melt you.
v21: I will gather you and blow upon you with the fire of my wrath, and you shall be melted in its midst.
v22: As silver is melted in the furnace, so you shall be melted in its midst, and you shall know that I, the LORD, have poured out my wrath upon you."
</p>
<p>
v23: The word of the LORD came to me, saying,
v24: "Son of man, say to her: You are a land not cleansed, not rained upon in the day of indignation.
v25: A conspiracy of her prophets is within her, like a roaring lion tearing prey; they have devoured souls, taken treasure and precious things; they have made many widows within her.
v26: Her priests have done violence to my law and have profaned my holy things; they have made no distinction between the holy and the common, nor have they taught the difference between the unclean and the clean; they have hidden their eyes from my sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.
v27: Her officials within her are like wolves tearing prey, shedding blood, destroying lives to get dishonest gain.
v28: Her prophets have smeared whitewash for them, seeing false visions and divining lies for them, saying, 'Thus says the LORD GOD,' when the LORD has not spoken.
v29: The people of the land have oppressed and robbed, and have wronged the poor and needy; they have oppressed the foreigner without justice.
</p>
<p>
v30: And I sought for a man among them who would build up the wall and stand in the breach before me for the land, that I might not destroy it, but I found none.
v31: Therefore I have poured out my indignation upon them. I have consumed them with the fire of my wrath. I have returned their way upon their heads, declares the LORD GOD."
</p>
## Notes
v2: - **v2**: The "bloody city" refers to Jerusalem, accused of bloodshed and idolatry.  
v18–22: - **v18-22**: The metaphor of dross and melting reflects Israel's impurities and judgment.  
v26: - **v26**: The corruption of priests highlights the failure of religious leaders to maintain holiness.
