<p>
v01: Now it came to pass in the eleventh year, on the first of the month, that the word of the LORD came to me, saying,
</p>
<p>
v02: "Son of man, because Tyre has said concerning Jerusalem, 'Aha! The gateway of the peoples is broken; it has turned to me; I shall be filled, she that is laid waste,'
</p>
<p>
v03: therefore thus says the LORD GOD: Behold, I am against you, O Tyre, and I will bring up many nations against you, as the sea causes its waves to come up.
</p>
<p>
v04: They will destroy the walls of Tyre and break down her towers; I will also scrape her debris from her and make her a bare rock.
</p>
<p>
v05: She will be a place for spreading nets in the midst of the sea, for I have spoken, declares the LORD GOD, and she shall become spoil for the nations.
</p>
<p>
v06: Also her daughters who are in the field will be slain by the sword, and they will know that I am the LORD."
</p>
<p>
v07: For thus says the LORD GOD: "Behold, I will bring against Tyre Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the north, king of kings, with horses and chariots, with horsemen and a vast company and many people.
</p>
<p>
v08: He will slay your daughters on the field with the sword, and he will set siege works against you, build a ramp against you, and raise a protective shield against you.
</p>
<p>
v09: He will direct the thrust of battering rams against your walls, and with his weapons he will break down your towers.
</p>
<p>
v10: From the abundance of his horses, their dust will cover you; your walls will shake at the noise of the cavalry, wagons, and chariots, when he enters your gates as men enter a breached city.
</p>
<p>
v11: With the hooves of his horses he will trample all your streets; he will kill your people with the sword, and your mighty pillars will fall to the ground.
</p>
<p>
v12: They will plunder your riches and seize your merchandise; they will break down your walls and destroy your pleasant houses; your stones, wood, and debris they will throw into the water.
</p>
<p>
v13: I will cause the noise of your songs to cease, and the sound of your harps will no longer be heard.
</p>
<p>
v14: I will make you a bare rock; you will be a place for spreading nets. You will never be rebuilt, for I, the LORD, have spoken, declares the LORD GOD.
</p>
<p>
v15: Thus says the LORD GOD to Tyre: Shall not the coastlands tremble at the sound of your downfall, when the wounded groan, and the massacre occurs in your midst?
</p>
<p>
v16: Then all the princes of the sea will step down from their thrones, lay aside their robes, and strip off their embroidered garments. They will put on trembling; they will sit on the ground, tremble every moment, and be appalled at you.
</p>
<p>
v17: They will take up a lamentation for you and say to you, 'How you have perished, inhabited from the seas, O city renowned, who was strong on the sea, she and her inhabitants, who caused their terror to be on all her inhabitants!
</p>
<p>
v18: Now the coastlands will tremble on the day of your fall; yes, the coastlands that are in the sea will be alarmed at your departure.'"
</p>
<p>
v19: For thus says the LORD GOD: "When I make you a desolated city, like cities that are not inhabited, when I bring up the deep over you and the great waters cover you,
</p>
<p>
v20: then I will bring you down with those who go down to the pit, to the people of old, and I will make you dwell in the lower parts of the earth, like the ancient ruins, with those who go down to the pit, so you will not be inhabited; but I will set glory in the land of the living.
</p>
<p>
v21: I will make you a terror, and you shall be no more; you will be sought, but you will never be found again, declares the LORD GOD."
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v03: שָׁחַת (shachat)
“Destroy; Corrupt.” Here it signals comprehensive dismantling—applying both physical chaos and moral judgment upon Tyre’s structures.

v10: פָּרָשׁ (parash)
“Horseman; cavalry.” The term belongs to the description of the invading force.
