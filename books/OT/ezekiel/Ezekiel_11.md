v01: Then the Spirit lifted me up and brought me to the east gate of the house of Yahweh that faces east. There, at the entrance of the gate, were twenty-five men. And among them I saw Jaazaniah the son of Azzur, and Pelatiah the son of Benaiah, leaders of the people.

v02: And he said to me, "Son of man, these are the men who devise iniquity and give wicked counsel in this city."

v03: They say, 'It is not near; let us build houses. This city is the pot, and we are the meat.'

v04: Therefore prophesy against them. Prophesy, son of man."

<p>
v05: Then the Spirit of Yahweh fell upon me, and he said to me, "Say this: Thus says Yahweh, so you have spoken, house of Israel, and I know the thoughts that come into your mind.
v06: You have multiplied your slain in this city and filled its streets with the dead.

v07: Therefore thus says the Lord Yahweh: Your slain whom you have laid in the midst of it, they are the meat, and this city is the pot, but you shall be brought out of its midst.
v08: You have feared the sword, and I will bring the sword upon you," declares the Lord Yahweh.
v09: "And I will bring you out of its midst and deliver you into the hands of foreigners and execute judgments upon you.

v10: By the sword you shall fall. I will judge you at the border of Israel, and you will know that I am Yahweh.
v11: This city will not be a pot for you, nor shall you be meat in its midst. I will judge you at the border of Israel,
v12: and you will know that I am Yahweh. For you have not walked in my statutes nor executed my judgments, but you have acted according to the rules of the nations around you."
</p>

<p>
v13: As I prophesied, Pelatiah the son of Benaiah died. Then I fell on my face and cried with a loud voice, "Ah, Lord Yahweh! Will you make a full end of the remnant of Israel?"
</p>

<p>
v14: And the word of Yahweh came to me:
v15: "Son of man, your brothers, your brothers, the men of your redemption, and the whole house of Israel— they are those to whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, ‘Go far from Yahweh; this land is given to us for a possession.’
</p>

<p>
v16: Therefore say this: Thus says the Lord Yahweh: Though I removed them far away among the nations, and though I scattered them among the countries, yet I have been a sanctuary to them for a little while in the countries where they have gone.

v17: Therefore say this: Thus says the Lord Yahweh: I will gather you from the peoples and assemble you out of the countries where you have been scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel.
</p>

<p>
v18: When they come there, they will remove all its detestable things and all its abominations from it.
v19: And I will give them one heart, and I will put a new spirit within them. I will remove the heart of stone from their flesh and give them a heart of flesh,
v20: so that they may walk in my statutes and keep my judgments and do them. And they shall be my people, and I will be their God.
</p>

<p>
v21: But as for those whose hearts go after their detestable things and their abominations, I will bring their deeds upon their own heads," declares the Lord Yahweh.
</p>

<p>
v22: Then the cherubim lifted their wings, with the wheels beside them, and the glory of the God of Israel was above them.

v23: The glory of Yahweh went up from the midst of the city and stood on the mountain east of the city.

v24: And the Spirit lifted me up and brought me in the vision by the Spirit of God into Chaldea, to the exiles. Then the vision that I had seen went up from me.

v25: And I told the exiles all the things that Yahweh had shown me.
</p>

## Notes

- **v1**: Twenty-five men are described, drawing attention to their role and numbers, which may symbolize representative leadership or governance within the city.
- **v5**: The Spirit falling upon Ezekiel underscores divine inspiration, indicating the prophetic message's authority.
- **v13**: Pelatiah's death is an immediate fulfillment of the prophetic word, heightening the urgency and reality of divine judgment.
- **v19-20**: The promise of a new heart and spirit highlights the transformative work of God, emphasizing inner renewal necessary for true obedience.

## Vocabulary

- **v1 רוּחַ (ruach)**: Spirit, referring to God's presence and activity, indicating divine initiative in moving Ezekiel to see and understand God's will.
- **v19 לֵב (lev)**: Heart, symbolizing the center of will and emotion, highlighting the profound change God promises for his people.
