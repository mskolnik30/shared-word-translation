Thus says the Lord Yahweh:

## Notes

- **30:3 "a day of clouds"** - The imagery of clouds signifies a time of divine judgment and chaos, typically associated with the presence of God.
  
- **30:6 "Migdol to Syene"** - These cities denote the northernmost and southernmost boundaries of Egypt, indicating total destruction throughout the land.
  
- **30:21 "the arm of Pharaoh"** - In ancient imagery, the arm symbolizes power and strength. God's breaking of Pharaoh's arm implies the complete weakening of Egypt's military might.

## Vocabulary

- **30:10 נְבוּכַדְרֶאצַּ֥ר (ne.vu.khad.nets.tsar)**: "Nebuchadnezzar" - The Babylonian king, representative of God's chosen instrument for judgment against Egypt.

- **30:16 סִין (sin)**: "Pelusium" - A fortress and stronghold in northeastern Egypt, symbolizing the defenses of the nation. 

- **30:21 פַּרְעֹה (par.oh)**: "Pharaoh" - The title for the king of Egypt, here referring to the decisive defeat of Egyptian leadership by divine intervention.
