## Fourth Vision: Joshua the High Priest

<p>
v01: Then he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the LORD, and the accuser standing at his right hand to accuse him.

v02: And the LORD said to the accuser, “The LORD rebuke you, O accuser! The LORD who has chosen Jerusalem rebuke you! Is this not a burning brand plucked from the fire?”

v03: Now Joshua was clothed in filthy garments as he stood before the angel.

v04: And the angel answered and said to those standing before him, “Remove the filthy garments from him.”

Then he said to Joshua, “See, I have taken your guilt away from you, and I will clothe you in festal garments.”

v05: And I said, “Let them put a clean turban on his head.”

So they put the clean turban on his head and clothed him with garments, while the angel of the LORD stood by.
</p>

## The Charge to Joshua

<p>
v06: Then the angel of the LORD solemnly charged Joshua:

v07: “Thus says the LORD of Hosts: If you walk in my ways and if you keep my charge, then you shall govern my house and keep my courts, and I will give you access among these who are standing here.
</p>

## The Branch Is Coming

<p>
v08: Hear now, Joshua the high priest, you and your companions who sit before you, for they are people who are a sign: Look, I am bringing my servant, the Branch.

v09: For look at the stone that I have set before Joshua. On this one stone are seven eyes. Look, I will engrave its inscription, says the LORD of Hosts, and I will remove the guilt of this land in a single day.

v10: On that day, says the LORD of Hosts, each of you shall invite your neighbor to sit under the vine and under the fig tree.”
</p>
##Notes

v07: “access among these who are standing here” — The Hebrew expression is unusual and can refer to places or paths of access among the heavenly attendants.

v08: “the Branch” — The Hebrew term can mean a shoot or sprout and became an important royal and restoration image.

##Vocabulary

v01: שָׂטָן (satan)
“Accuser” or “adversary.” One who opposes or brings accusation against another.

v02: אוּד (ud)
“Burning brand” or “firebrand.” A piece of wood pulled from a fire before being consumed.

v04: עָוֹן (avon)
“Guilt,” “iniquity,” or “wrong.” The term can describe wrongdoing together with the burden or consequence attached to it.

v07: מִשְׁמֶרֶת (mishmeret)
“Charge,” “duty,” or “responsibility.” Something entrusted to another for faithful keeping.
