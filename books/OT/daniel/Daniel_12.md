## Michael Will Arise

<p>
v01: “At that time Michael will arise, the great prince who stands over the children of your people. There will be a time of distress such as has not happened since there was a nation until that time. But at that time your people will escape, everyone who is found written in the book.
</p>

## Many Who Sleep in the Dust Will Awake

<p>
v02: “Many of those who sleep in the dust of the earth will awake, these to everlasting life and these to reproach and everlasting contempt.

v03: Those who have insight will shine like the brightness of the expanse, and those who make the many righteous like the stars forever and ever.
</p>

## Seal the Book until the Time of the End

<p>
v04: “But you, Daniel, shut up the words and seal the book until the time of the end. Many will go back and forth, and knowledge will increase.”
</p>

## How Long until the End?

<p>
v05: Then I, Daniel, looked, and behold, two others were standing, one on this bank of the river and one on the other bank of the river.

v06: One said to the man clothed in linen, who was above the waters of the river, “How long until the end of these wonders?”

v07: I heard the man clothed in linen, who was above the waters of the river, and he raised his right hand and his left hand toward the heavens and swore by the one who lives forever that it would be for a time, times, and half a time. And when the shattering of the power of the holy people is completed, all these things will be completed.
</p>

## The Words Are Sealed until the Time of the End

<p>
v08: I heard, but I did not understand. Then I said, “My lord, what will be the outcome of these things?”

v09: He said, “Go, Daniel, for the words are shut up and sealed until the time of the end.

v10: Many will purify themselves, make themselves white, and be refined, but the wicked will act wickedly. None of the wicked will understand, but those with insight will understand.
</p>

## The 1,290 and 1,335 Days

<p>
v11: “From the time the continual offering is removed and the detestable thing that makes desolate is set up, there will be one thousand two hundred ninety days.

v12: Happy is the one who waits and reaches one thousand three hundred thirty-five days.
</p>

## You Will Stand for Your Lot at the End

<p>
v13: “But you, go your way until the end. You will rest, and you will stand for your lot at the end of the days.”
</p>
## Notes
v01: Michael, introduced in Daniel 10 as “your prince,” is now called the great prince standing over Daniel’s people.
v01: “written in the book” uses heavenly registry imagery for those preserved through the crisis. The book itself is not further described.
v02: This is one of the clearest Old Testament statements of resurrection involving both everlasting life and everlasting disgrace.
v02: “many” need not mean only a fraction in contrast with “all”; biblical Hebrew can use “many” for a great multitude. The verse itself does not settle later questions about the scope of resurrection.
v03: “make the many righteous” can mean leading many toward righteousness, causing many to act rightly, or vindicating them through wise instruction. TSW retains the active righteousness language.
v04: Sealing the book protects and preserves the revelation until its appointed time; it does not mean the message is permanently unintelligible.
v04: “many will go back and forth” can describe searching through the book, restless movement, or broader activity in the time of the end. The connection to increased knowledge is suggestive but not fully explained.
v07: The messenger swears with both hands raised, intensifying the solemnity of the oath.
v07: “time, times, and half a time” repeats the limited period from Daniel 7:25.
v07: The end is linked to the completion of the shattering of the holy people’s power, a difficult statement in which apparent defeat becomes the boundary before fulfillment.
v08: Daniel himself does not understand everything he hears. The book presents faithful reception without total interpretive mastery.
v10: Understanding is moral as well as intellectual: the wicked do not understand, while those with insight are refined and understand.
v11: The 1,290 days differ from the 2,300 evenings-mornings of Daniel 8 and the time-times-half-time formula. TSW preserves the distinct numbers rather than harmonizing them.
v12: The additional 1,335-day blessing extends beyond the 1,290-day marker without explaining the significance of the extra forty-five days.
v13: Daniel is promised rest and a future standing “for your lot,” combining death, inheritance, and resurrection hope at the close of the Hebrew book.
v13: The Hebrew/Aramaic canonical form of Daniel ends here. Greek forms include additional stories and prayers—Susanna, Bel and the Dragon, and the Prayer of Azariah/Song of the Three—which are not incorporated into the TSW main text because the project treats the Masoretic Text as the primary Old Testament source.

## Vocabulary

v01: עֵת צָרָה (et tsarah)
“Time of distress.” An unparalleled period of crisis.

v01: סֵפֶר (sefer)
“Book.” The heavenly record containing names.

v02: יָשֵׁן (yashen)
“To sleep.” A metaphor for death.

v02: חַיֵּי עוֹלָם (chayye olam)
“Everlasting life.”

v02: דֵּרָאוֹן (dera'on)
“Contempt” or “abhorrence.” Enduring disgrace.

v03: מַשְׂכִּילִים (maskilim)
“Those with insight.” The wise who understand and teach.

v03: זֹהַר (zohar)
“Brightness.” Radiance of the heavenly expanse.

v04: חָתַם (chatam)
“To seal.” To secure and preserve a document.

v07: מוֹעֵד (moed)
“Appointed time.” The end occurs according to fixed divine timing.

v10: לָבַן (lavan)
“To make white.” Purification imagery.

v11: תָּמִיד (tamid)
“Continual offering.” The regular sanctuary sacrifice that is removed.

v11: שִׁקּוּץ שֹׁמֵם (shiquts shomem)
“Detestable thing that makes desolate.”

v12: אַשְׁרֵי (ashrei)
“Happy” or “blessed.” The one who waits and reaches the appointed day.

v13: גּוֹרָל (goral)
“Lot” or “allotted portion.” Daniel’s promised future inheritance.
