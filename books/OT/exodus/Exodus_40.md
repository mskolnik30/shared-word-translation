## Set Up the Dwelling

<p>
v01: The LORD spoke to Moses, saying,

v02: “On the first day of the first month you shall set up the dwelling, the tent of meeting.

v03: You shall put there the ark of the testimony and screen the ark with the veil.

v04: You shall bring in the table and arrange what belongs on it. You shall bring in the lampstand and set up its lamps.

v05: You shall put the gold altar for incense before the ark of the testimony and set up the screen at the entrance of the dwelling.

v06: You shall put the altar of burnt offering before the entrance of the dwelling, the tent of meeting.

v07: You shall put the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar and put water in it.

v08: You shall set up the court all around and hang the screen at the gate of the court.
</p>

## Anoint and Consecrate the Sanctuary

<p>
v09: “You shall take the anointing oil and anoint the dwelling and all that is in it. You shall consecrate it and all its furnishings, and it shall be holy.

v10: You shall anoint the altar of burnt offering and all its utensils and consecrate the altar, and the altar shall be most holy.

v11: You shall anoint the basin and its stand and consecrate it.

v12: Then you shall bring Aaron and his sons near to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.

v13: You shall clothe Aaron with the holy garments, anoint him, and consecrate him so that he may serve as priest to me.

v14: You shall bring his sons near and clothe them with tunics.

v15: You shall anoint them as you anointed their father, so that they may serve as priests to me. Their anointing shall be for them a perpetual priesthood throughout their generations.”
</p>

## Moses Sets Up the Dwelling

<p>
v16: Moses did according to all that the LORD had commanded him; so he did.

v17: In the first month of the second year, on the first day of the month, the dwelling was set up.

v18: Moses set up the dwelling, placed its bases, set up its frames, put in its crossbars, and set up its pillars.

v19: He spread the tent over the dwelling and put the covering of the tent over it from above, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

v20: He took the testimony and put it into the ark, put the poles on the ark, and put the cover on the ark from above.

v21: He brought the ark into the dwelling, set up the screening veil, and screened the ark of the testimony, as the LORD had commanded Moses.
</p>

## The Furnishings Are Put in Place

<p>
v22: He put the table in the tent of meeting, on the north side of the dwelling, outside the veil,

v23: and arranged the bread upon it before the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

v24: He put the lampstand in the tent of meeting opposite the table, on the south side of the dwelling,

v25: and set up the lamps before the LORD, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

v26: He put the gold altar in the tent of meeting before the veil

v27: and burned fragrant incense upon it, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

v28: He set up the screen at the entrance of the dwelling.
</p>

## The Altar, Basin, and Court

<p>
v29: He put the altar of burnt offering at the entrance of the dwelling, the tent of meeting, and offered upon it the burnt offering and the grain offering, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

v30: He put the basin between the tent of meeting and the altar and put water in it for washing,

v31: and Moses, Aaron, and his sons washed their hands and feet from it.

v32: When they entered the tent of meeting and when they approached the altar, they washed, as the LORD had commanded Moses.

v33: He set up the court around the dwelling and the altar and hung the screen at the gate of the court.

So Moses completed the work.
</p>

## The Glory of the LORD Fills the Dwelling

<p>
v34: Then the cloud covered the tent of meeting, and the glory of the LORD filled the dwelling.

v35: Moses was not able to enter the tent of meeting because the cloud settled upon it and the glory of the LORD filled the dwelling.

v36: Whenever the cloud was taken up from over the dwelling, the children of Israel would set out in all their journeys.

v37: But if the cloud was not taken up, then they did not set out until the day it was taken up.

v38: For the cloud of the LORD was upon the dwelling by day, and fire was in it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel throughout all their journeys.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v02: הֵקִים (heqim)
“To set up” or “erect.” Used of raising the dwelling.

v09: מָשַׁח (mashach)
“To anoint.” To apply sacred oil for consecration.

v10: קֹדֶשׁ קָדָשִׁים (qodesh qodashim)
“Most holy.” Intensified sacred status.

v20: עֵדוּת (edut)
“Testimony.” The covenant tablets placed in the ark.

v34: כָּבוֹד יְהוָה (kavod YHWH)
“Glory of the LORD.” The manifest weight and presence of the LORD.
