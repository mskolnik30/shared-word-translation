## Restitution for Theft

<p>
v01: “If a man steals an ox or a sheep and slaughters it or sells it, he shall make restitution of five oxen for the ox and four sheep for the sheep.

v02: If a thief is found breaking in and is struck so that he dies, there shall be no bloodguilt for him.

v03: But if the sun has risen upon him, there shall be bloodguilt for him. The thief shall surely make restitution. If he has nothing, then he shall be sold for his theft.

v04: If the stolen animal is found alive in his hand, whether ox, donkey, or sheep, he shall make double restitution.
</p>

## Damage to Fields and Property

<p>
v05: “If a man causes a field or vineyard to be grazed over and lets his livestock loose so that it grazes in another’s field, he shall make restitution from the best of his own field and the best of his own vineyard.

v06: If fire breaks out and catches in thorns so that stacked grain, standing grain, or a field is consumed, the one who started the fire shall surely make restitution.
</p>

## Property Entrusted to Another

<p>
v07: “If a man gives his neighbor silver or goods to keep and it is stolen from the man’s house, if the thief is found, he shall make double restitution.

v08: If the thief is not found, then the owner of the house shall be brought before God to determine whether he has stretched out his hand against his neighbor’s property.

v09: For every matter of transgression concerning an ox, donkey, sheep, garment, or any lost thing about which one says, ‘This is it,’ the case of both parties shall come before God. The one whom God declares guilty shall make double restitution to his neighbor.

v10: If a man gives his neighbor a donkey, ox, sheep, or any animal to keep and it dies, is injured, or is driven away when no one sees,

v11: an oath before the LORD shall be between them to determine whether he stretched out his hand against his neighbor’s property. Its owner shall accept this, and the other shall not make restitution.

v12: But if it was truly stolen from him, he shall make restitution to its owner.

v13: If it was torn apart, he shall bring it as evidence. He shall not make restitution for what was torn.
</p>

## Borrowed Animals

<p>
v14: “If a man borrows an animal from his neighbor and it is injured or dies while its owner is not with it, he shall surely make restitution.

v15: If its owner is with it, he shall not make restitution. If it was hired, the loss is covered by its hire.
</p>

## Seduction and Other Covenant Laws

<p>
v16: “If a man seduces a virgin who is not betrothed and lies with her, he shall surely pay the bride-price for her to become his wife.

v17: If her father absolutely refuses to give her to him, he shall weigh out silver according to the bride-price for virgins.

v18: You shall not allow a sorceress to live.

v19: Whoever lies with an animal shall surely be put to death.

v20: Whoever sacrifices to a god other than the LORD alone shall be devoted to destruction.
</p>

## Do Not Oppress the Vulnerable

<p>
v21: “You shall not wrong a resident alien or oppress him, for you were resident aliens in the land of Egypt.

v22: You shall not afflict any widow or orphan.

v23: If you do afflict them and they cry out to me, I will surely hear their cry.

v24: My anger will burn, and I will kill you with the sword, and your wives will become widows and your children orphans.
</p>

## Lending to the Poor

<p>
v25: “If you lend silver to my people, to the poor among you, you shall not be to him like a creditor. You shall not impose interest upon him.

v26: If you take your neighbor’s garment as a pledge, you shall return it to him before the sun goes down,

v27: for that is his only covering; it is his garment for his body. In what will he sleep? And when he cries to me, I will hear, for I am gracious.
</p>

## Honor God and Give the Firstborn

<p>
v28: “You shall not revile God, and you shall not curse a leader among your people.

v29: You shall not delay to offer from the fullness of your harvest and from the flow of your presses. The firstborn of your sons you shall give to me.

v30: You shall do likewise with your oxen and sheep. Seven days it shall be with its mother; on the eighth day you shall give it to me.

v31: You shall be holy people to me. Therefore you shall not eat flesh torn by animals in the field; you shall throw it to the dogs.
</p>
##Notes

v01: This verse is numbered 21:37 in the Masoretic chapter division.

v28: Hebrew *elohim* can mean God or, in some legal contexts, judges.

##Vocabulary

v01: שָׁלַם (shalam)
“To make restitution.” To compensate for theft or damage.

v02: מַחְתֶּרֶת (machteret)
“Breaking in.” Burglary by digging or forcing entry.

v25: נֶשֶׁךְ (neshekh)
“Interest.” Literally something that bites; profit charged on a loan.

v31: קֹדֶשׁ (qodesh)
“Holy.” Set apart as belonging to the LORD.
