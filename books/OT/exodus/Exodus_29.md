## Consecrating Aaron and His Sons

<p>
v01: “And this is what you shall do to them to consecrate them, so that they may serve as priests to me: take one young bull and two rams without blemish,

v02: unleavened bread, unleavened cakes mixed with oil, and unleavened wafers spread with oil. You shall make them of fine wheat flour.

v03: You shall put them in one basket and bring them in the basket, along with the bull and the two rams.

v04: You shall bring Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting and wash them with water.

v05: Then you shall take the garments and clothe Aaron with the tunic, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastpiece, and bind the ephod upon him with its woven band.

v06: You shall set the turban on his head and put the holy crown upon the turban.

v07: You shall take the anointing oil, pour it on his head, and anoint him.

v08: Then you shall bring his sons and clothe them with tunics.

v09: You shall gird them with sashes, Aaron and his sons, and bind head coverings on them. The priesthood shall be theirs by a perpetual statute. Thus you shall fill the hands of Aaron and his sons.
</p>

## The Bull for the Sin Offering

<p>
v10: “You shall bring the bull before the tent of meeting, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the bull.

v11: You shall slaughter the bull before the LORD at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

v12: You shall take some of the blood of the bull and put it on the horns of the altar with your finger, and pour all the rest of the blood at the base of the altar.

v13: You shall take all the fat that covers the entrails, the appendage of the liver, and the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, and burn them on the altar.

v14: But the flesh of the bull, its skin, and its dung you shall burn with fire outside the camp. It is a sin offering.
</p>

## The First Ram

<p>
v15: “Then you shall take one ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.

v16: You shall slaughter the ram, take its blood, and throw it against the altar on all sides.

v17: You shall cut the ram into its pieces, wash its entrails and legs, and put them with its pieces and its head.

v18: You shall burn the whole ram on the altar. It is a burnt offering to the LORD, a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.
</p>

## The Ram of Ordination

<p>
v19: “Then you shall take the second ram, and Aaron and his sons shall lay their hands on the head of the ram.

v20: You shall slaughter the ram, take some of its blood, and put it on the lobe of Aaron’s right ear, on the lobes of his sons’ right ears, on the thumbs of their right hands, and on the big toes of their right feet. Then throw the rest of the blood against the altar on all sides.

v21: You shall take some of the blood that is on the altar and some of the anointing oil and sprinkle it on Aaron and his garments, and on his sons and the garments of his sons with him. Then he and his garments shall be holy, and his sons and their garments with him.
</p>

## The Portion Placed in Their Hands

<p>
v22: “You shall take from the ram the fat, the fat tail, the fat that covers the entrails, the appendage of the liver, the two kidneys with the fat that is on them, and the right thigh, for it is a ram of ordination,

v23: and one loaf of bread, one cake of bread with oil, and one wafer from the basket of unleavened bread that is before the LORD.

v24: You shall put all these in the palms of Aaron and in the palms of his sons, and wave them as a wave offering before the LORD.

v25: Then you shall take them from their hands and burn them on the altar with the burnt offering as a pleasing aroma before the LORD. It is an offering by fire to the LORD.

v26: You shall take the breast from the ram of Aaron’s ordination and wave it as a wave offering before the LORD, and it shall be your portion.

v27: You shall consecrate the breast of the wave offering and the thigh of the contribution that was waved and lifted up from the ram of ordination, from that which belongs to Aaron and his sons.

v28: It shall belong to Aaron and his sons as a perpetual portion from the children of Israel, for it is a contribution. It shall be a contribution from the children of Israel from their offerings of well-being, their contribution to the LORD.
</p>

## The Priestly Garments Continue through the Generations

<p>
v29: “The holy garments of Aaron shall belong to his sons after him, to be anointed in them and to have their hands filled in them.

v30: Seven days the son who becomes priest in his place shall wear them when he enters the tent of meeting to minister in the Holy Place.
</p>

## Eating the Ram of Ordination

<p>
v31: “You shall take the ram of ordination and boil its flesh in a holy place.

v32: Aaron and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram and the bread that is in the basket at the entrance of the tent of meeting.

v33: They shall eat those things by which atonement was made to fill their hands and consecrate them, but an outsider shall not eat them, because they are holy.

v34: If any of the flesh of the ordination offering or any of the bread remains until morning, you shall burn the remainder with fire. It shall not be eaten, because it is holy.
</p>

## Seven Days of Ordination

<p>
v35: “Thus you shall do to Aaron and his sons according to all that I have commanded you. Seven days you shall fill their hands.

v36: Each day you shall offer a bull as a sin offering for atonement. You shall purify the altar when you make atonement for it, and you shall anoint it to consecrate it.

v37: Seven days you shall make atonement for the altar and consecrate it. Then the altar shall be most holy; whatever touches the altar shall become holy.
</p>

## The Continual Burnt Offering

<p>
v38: “And this is what you shall offer upon the altar: two year-old lambs each day, continually.

v39: The one lamb you shall offer in the morning, and the second lamb you shall offer at twilight.

v40: With the first lamb shall be a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a quarter hin of beaten oil and a quarter hin of wine as a drink offering.

v41: The second lamb you shall offer at twilight. You shall offer with it a grain offering and drink offering like those of the morning, as a pleasing aroma, an offering by fire to the LORD.

v42: It shall be a continual burnt offering throughout your generations at the entrance of the tent of meeting before the LORD, where I will meet with you to speak to you there.
</p>

## I Will Dwell among the Children of Israel

<p>
v43: “There I will meet with the children of Israel, and the place will be consecrated by my glory.

v44: I will consecrate the tent of meeting and the altar. Aaron and his sons I will consecrate to serve as priests to me.

v45: I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel and will be their God.

v46: They will know that I am the LORD their God, who brought them out from the land of Egypt so that I might dwell in their midst. I am the LORD their God.
</p>
## Notes
v01: Priesthood is established through washing, clothing, anointing, sacrifice, blood, food, and a seven-day rite. No single action alone constitutes the whole consecration.
v09: “fill the hands” is the Hebrew idiom for ordination. The priest’s hands are literally filled with sacred portions later in the ritual.

v10, v15, v19: Laying hands on the animal identifies Aaron and his sons with the sacrifice in distinct rites of purification, burnt offering, and ordination.
v20: Blood is placed on right ear, thumb, and big toe, symbolically marking hearing, action, and walking within priestly service.
v21: Blood from the altar and anointing oil are applied to priests and garments together, joining sacrificial and anointing holiness.
v22: The “fat tail” refers to the large fatty tail of ancient Near Eastern sheep breeds.
v24: “wave offering” describes a ritual presentation before the LORD. The exact physical motion is not fully described by the text.
v33: The priests eat the food “by which atonement was made,” joining sacred meal and ordination. The meal is restricted because the food is holy.
v37: “whatever touches the altar shall become holy” expresses the altar’s intensified sacred status and the danger of casual contact with holy space.
v38–42: The daily lambs establish a continual rhythm of worship morning and evening, independent of special festivals.
v46: The exodus reaches its theological purpose in divine dwelling: Israel is brought out of Egypt so that the LORD may dwell in their midst.

##Vocabulary

v01: קָדַשׁ (qadash)
“To consecrate.” To set apart for sacred service.

v07: שֶׁמֶן הַמִּשְׁחָה (shemen ha-mishchah)
“Anointing oil.” Oil used to install and consecrate priests.

v09: מִלֵּא יָד (mille yad)
“To fill the hand.” The Hebrew idiom for ordination.

v14: חַטָּאת (chatta't)
“Sin offering” or “purification offering.” A sacrifice addressing impurity and guilt.

v18: רֵיחַ נִיחוֹחַ (reach nichoach)
“Pleasing aroma.” Formula describing an offering accepted before the LORD.

v22: אֵיל מִלֻּאִים (eil millu'im)
“Ram of ordination.” The ram used in filling the priests’ hands.

v24: תְּנוּפָה (tenufah)
“Wave offering.” A sacred portion presented before the LORD.

v28: שְׁלָמִים (shelamim)
“Offerings of well-being.” Sacrifices associated with peace, wholeness, and shared eating.

v36: כִּפֶּר (kipper)
“To make atonement.” To address guilt or impurity and restore sacred order.

v38: תָּמִיד (tamid)
“Continually.” Regular, ongoing sanctuary service.
