## The Covenant in Moab

<p>
v01: These are the words of the covenant that the LORD commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab, besides the covenant that he made with them at Horeb.
</p>

## You Have Seen What the LORD Did in Egypt

<p>
v02: Moses called to all Israel and said to them:

“You yourselves have seen all that the LORD did before your eyes in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh, to all his servants, and to all his land—

v03: the great trials that your eyes saw, the signs, and those great wonders.

v04: Yet the LORD has not given you a heart to know, eyes to see, or ears to hear, to this day.

v05: I led you forty years in the wilderness. Your garments did not wear out upon you, and your sandal did not wear out upon your foot.

v06: You did not eat bread and did not drink wine or strong drink, so that you might know that I am the LORD your God.

v07: When you came to this place, Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out to meet us for battle, and we struck them.

v08: We took their land and gave it as an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half-tribe of Manasseh.

v09: So keep the words of this covenant and do them, so that you may act wisely in all that you do.
</p>

## You Stand Today before the LORD

<p>
v10: “You are standing today, all of you, before the LORD your God: your heads, your tribes, your elders, your officers, every man of Israel,

v11: your little children, your wives, and the resident alien who is in the midst of your camp, from the one who cuts your wood to the one who draws your water,

v12: so that you may enter into the covenant of the LORD your God and into his oath that the LORD your God is making with you today,

v13: in order to establish you today as his people and that he may be your God, as he spoke to you and as he swore to your ancestors, to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

v14: And not with you alone am I making this covenant and this oath,

v15: but with whoever is standing here with us today before the LORD our God and with whoever is not here with us today.
</p>

## Do Not Turn to the Gods of the Nations

<p>
v16: “For you yourselves know how we lived in the land of Egypt and how we passed through the midst of the nations through which you passed.

v17: You saw their detestable things and their idols of wood and stone, silver and gold, that were among them.

v18: Lest there be among you a man or woman, clan or tribe, whose heart turns away today from the LORD our God to go and serve the gods of those nations; lest there be among you a root bearing poisonous and bitter fruit;

v19: and when he hears the words of this oath, he blesses himself in his heart, saying, ‘I will have peace, though I walk in the stubbornness of my heart,’ so as to sweep away the watered with the thirsty.

v20: The LORD will not be willing to forgive him, but then the anger of the LORD and his jealousy will smoke against that man, and all the oath written in this book will lie upon him, and the LORD will wipe out his name from under the heavens.

v21: The LORD will separate him for harm from all the tribes of Israel, according to all the oaths of the covenant written in this book of instruction.
</p>

## The Land Will Become Sulfur and Salt

<p>
v22: “The generation to come, your children who rise after you, and the foreigner who comes from a distant land will say, when they see the plagues of that land and the sicknesses with which the LORD has afflicted it—

v23: all its land sulfur and salt, a burning waste, not sown, not sprouting, and no plant rising in it, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboiim, which the LORD overthrew in his anger and fury—

v24: all the nations will say, ‘Why did the LORD do this to this land? What is the heat of this great anger?’

v25: Then they will say, ‘Because they abandoned the covenant of the LORD, the God of their ancestors, which he made with them when he brought them out from the land of Egypt.

v26: They went and served other gods and bowed down to them, gods whom they had not known and whom he had not allotted to them.

v27: Then the anger of the LORD burned against that land, to bring upon it every curse written in this book.

v28: The LORD uprooted them from their ground in anger, fury, and great wrath and threw them into another land, as it is this day.’
</p>

## The Hidden Things Belong to the LORD

<p>
v29: “The hidden things belong to the LORD our God, but the revealed things belong to us and to our children forever, so that we may do all the words of this instruction.
</p>

## Notes

v01: In the Masoretic chapter division this verse is Deuteronomy 28:69. Standard English Bibles place it at Deuteronomy 29:1. TSW follows public English numbering for website and shortcode compatibility.

v01: This verse is numbered 28:69 in the Masoretic chapter division.

v04: The statement that the LORD has not yet given a heart to know, eyes to see, or ears to hear holds divine gift and human perception together. Deuteronomy later promises inward transformation.

v05–06: Wilderness preservation is interpreted as direct formation in knowing the LORD. The absence of ordinary agricultural food and drink emphasizes dependence upon divine provision.

v10–15: The covenant assembly includes leaders, men, women, children, resident aliens, and laborers, then extends beyond those physically present to future generations.

v12: “enter into the covenant” literally carries the image of passing into or through covenant and oath.

v18: “root bearing poisonous and bitter fruit” portrays apostasy as something hidden underground that eventually produces communal harm.

v19: The difficult phrase “to sweep away the watered with the thirsty” has several interpretations, including indiscriminate destruction of saturated and dry alike. TSW preserves the concrete wording.

v20: The language of smoking jealousy is intentionally intense and bodily, describing covenant judgment against deliberate self-deception.

v22–28: Future generations and foreigners interpret devastated land through covenant history. Environmental ruin becomes public testimony to broken allegiance.

v29: The closing saying distinguishes what belongs to divine hidden knowledge from revealed instruction given for obedience. It warns against allowing speculation to replace what has been made known.

## Vocabulary

v01: בְּרִית (berit)
“Covenant.” The Moab covenant renews and extends the Horeb relationship.

v04: לֵב לָדַעַת (lev lada'at)
“Heart to know.” Inward capacity for covenant understanding.

v09: שָׂכַל (sakhal)
“To act wisely” or “prosper through insight.”

v12: אָלָה (alah)
“Oath” or “covenant curse.” The sworn sanction accompanying the covenant.

v18: רֹאשׁ (rosh)
“Poison” or bitter plant. Used in the image of a toxic root.

v19: שְׁרִרוּת לֵב (sherirut lev)
“Stubbornness of heart.” Self-directed inward obstinacy.

v20: קִנְאָה (qin'ah)
“Jealousy” or “zeal.” Exclusive covenant passion.

v28: נָתַשׁ (natash)
“To uproot.” Israel removed from the land.

v29: נִסְתָּרוֹת (nistarot)
“Hidden things.” Matters belonging to the LORD.

v29: נִגְלֹת (niglot)
“Revealed things.” What has been made known for obedience.
