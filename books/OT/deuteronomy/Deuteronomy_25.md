## Limits on Corporal Punishment

<p>
v01: “If there is a dispute between men and they come to judgment, and the judges judge them, acquitting the righteous and condemning the wicked,

v02: then if the wicked person deserves beating, the judge shall make him lie down and be beaten before him according to the measure of his wickedness, by number.

v03: Forty blows he may give him, but no more, lest he continue to beat him beyond these with many blows and your brother be degraded in your eyes.
</p>

## Do Not Muzzle the Ox

<p>
v04: “You shall not muzzle an ox while it is threshing.
</p>

## The Duty of a Brother-in-Law

<p>
v05: “If brothers live together and one of them dies and has no son, the wife of the dead man shall not marry outside the family to a foreign man. Her husband’s brother shall go in to her, take her to himself as wife, and perform the duty of a husband’s brother for her.

v06: The firstborn whom she bears shall stand in the name of his dead brother, so that his name is not wiped out from Israel.

v07: But if the man does not desire to take his brother’s wife, then his brother’s wife shall go up to the gate to the elders and say, ‘My husband’s brother refuses to raise up for his brother a name in Israel; he is not willing to perform the duty of a husband’s brother for me.’

v08: Then the elders of his city shall call him and speak to him. If he stands and says, ‘I do not desire to take her,’

v09: then his brother’s wife shall approach him before the eyes of the elders, pull his sandal from his foot, spit before his face, and answer and say, ‘Thus shall it be done to the man who will not build up his brother’s house.’

v10: And his name shall be called in Israel, ‘The house of the one whose sandal was removed.’
</p>

## An Improper Intervention in a Fight

<p>
v11: “If men fight together, a man and his brother, and the wife of one comes near to rescue her husband from the hand of the one striking him and stretches out her hand and seizes his genitals,

v12: then you shall cut off her hand. Your eye shall not pity.
</p>

## Honest Weights and Measures

<p>
v13: “You shall not have in your bag differing weights, a large and a small.

v14: You shall not have in your house differing measures, a large and a small.

v15: A full and righteous weight you shall have; a full and righteous measure you shall have, so that your days may be prolonged upon the ground that the LORD your God is giving you.

v16: For everyone who does these things, everyone who does injustice, is detestable to the LORD your God.
</p>

## Remember Amalek

<p>
v17: “Remember what Amalek did to you on the way when you came out from Egypt,

v18: how he met you on the way and cut off all the stragglers behind you when you were faint and weary, and he did not fear God.

v19: Therefore when the LORD your God gives you rest from all your enemies around you in the land that the LORD your God is giving you as an inheritance to possess, you shall wipe out the memory of Amalek from under the heavens. You shall not forget.
</p>
## Notes
v01–03: Corporal punishment is limited by number. The explicit rationale is protection of the offender’s dignity as “your brother,” even after conviction.
v03: Forty is the maximum, not the required number. The judge must match the punishment to the offense and stop before degrading excess.
v04: The threshing ox must be allowed access to the grain while working, embedding animal welfare within agricultural labor.
v05–10: The levirate law seeks to preserve the dead brother’s name and household line. The widow’s public action at the gate provides a remedy when the brother refuses.
v06: “stand in the name” means the firstborn child carries forward the deceased brother’s household identity and inheritance.
v09: Sandal removal and spitting create a public ritual of shame, but the refusing brother is not forced into the marriage.
v11–12: The bodily penalty is exceptional within Deuteronomy’s case law. The verse addresses deliberate seizure of male reproductive organs during a fight; TSW does not broaden it beyond the stated case.
v13–16: Commercial fraud is treated as a covenantal abomination. Economic honesty is tied directly to life in the land.
v17–19: Amalek is condemned for attacking the exhausted and vulnerable at Israel’s rear. The command to erase Amalek belongs to Israel’s ancient warfare memory and should not be reapplied to modern peoples.

##Vocabulary

v01: צָדַק (tsadaq)
“To declare righteous” or “acquit.”

v01: רָשַׁע (rasha)
“To declare wicked” or “condemn.”

v13: אֶבֶן וָאָבֶן (even va-even)
“Weight and weight.” An idiom for dishonest differing weights.

v15: צֶדֶק (tsedeq)
“Righteous” or “honest.” Applied to weights and measures.
