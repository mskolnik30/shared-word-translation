## Blessings for Listening to the LORD

<p>
v01: “And it will be, if you diligently listen to the voice of the LORD your God, being careful to do all his commandments that I command you today, then the LORD your God will set you high above all the nations of the earth.

v02: All these blessings will come upon you and overtake you if you listen to the voice of the LORD your God.

v03: Blessed shall you be in the city,
and blessed shall you be in the field.

v04: Blessed shall be the fruit of your womb,
the fruit of your ground,
and the fruit of your livestock,
the offspring of your cattle
and the increase of your flock.

v05: Blessed shall be your basket
and your kneading bowl.

v06: Blessed shall you be when you come in,
and blessed shall you be when you go out.
</p>

## The LORD Will Defeat Your Enemies

<p>
v07: “The LORD will cause your enemies who rise against you to be struck down before you. They will come out against you one way and flee before you seven ways.

v08: The LORD will command the blessing upon you in your barns and in all to which you put your hand, and he will bless you in the land that the LORD your God is giving you.

v09: The LORD will establish you as a holy people for himself, as he swore to you, if you keep the commandments of the LORD your God and walk in his ways.

v10: Then all the peoples of the earth will see that the name of the LORD is called upon you, and they will fear you.

v11: The LORD will make you abound in good—in the fruit of your womb, the fruit of your livestock, and the fruit of your ground—upon the ground that the LORD swore to your ancestors to give you.

v12: The LORD will open for you his good treasury, the heavens, to give rain to your land in its season and to bless all the work of your hand. You will lend to many nations, but you will not borrow.

v13: The LORD will make you the head and not the tail, and you will be only above and not below, if you listen to the commandments of the LORD your God that I command you today, being careful to do them,

v14: and do not turn aside from any of the words that I command you today, to the right or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.
</p>

## Curses for Refusing to Listen

<p>
v15: “But it will be, if you do not listen to the voice of the LORD your God, being careful to do all his commandments and statutes that I command you today, then all these curses will come upon you and overtake you.

v16: Cursed shall you be in the city,
and cursed shall you be in the field.

v17: Cursed shall be your basket
and your kneading bowl.

v18: Cursed shall be the fruit of your womb,
the fruit of your ground,
the offspring of your cattle
and the increase of your flock.

v19: Cursed shall you be when you come in,
and cursed shall you be when you go out.
</p>

## Confusion, Disease, and Drought

<p>
v20: “The LORD will send upon you curse, confusion, and rebuke in all to which you put your hand to do, until you are destroyed and perish quickly because of the evil of your deeds, because you have abandoned me.

v21: The LORD will make plague cling to you until he has finished you from the ground that you are entering to possess.

v22: The LORD will strike you with wasting disease, fever, inflammation, burning heat, drought, blight, and mildew, and they will pursue you until you perish.

v23: The heavens over your head will be bronze, and the earth beneath you iron.

v24: The LORD will make the rain of your land powder and dust; from the heavens it will come down upon you until you are destroyed.
</p>

## Defeat before Enemies

<p>
v25: “The LORD will cause you to be struck down before your enemies. You will go out against them one way and flee before them seven ways, and you will become a horror to all the kingdoms of the earth.

v26: Your dead body will become food for every bird of the heavens and animal of the earth, and there will be no one to frighten them away.

v27: The LORD will strike you with the boils of Egypt, tumors, scabs, and itching, from which you cannot be healed.

v28: The LORD will strike you with madness, blindness, and confusion of heart.

v29: You will grope at noon as the blind person gropes in darkness, and you will not make your ways prosper. You will only be oppressed and robbed all the days, with no one to save you.
</p>

## The Fruit of Your Labor Will Go to Others

<p>
v30: “You will betroth a woman, but another man will lie with her. You will build a house, but you will not live in it. You will plant a vineyard, but you will not begin to enjoy its fruit.

v31: Your ox will be slaughtered before your eyes, but you will not eat from it. Your donkey will be robbed from before you and will not return to you. Your flock will be given to your enemies, and there will be no one to save you.

v32: Your sons and daughters will be given to another people, and your eyes will look and fail for them all day long, but there will be no power in your hand.

v33: A people whom you have not known will eat the fruit of your ground and all your labor, and you will only be oppressed and crushed all the days.

v34: You will become mad because of the sight that your eyes see.

v35: The LORD will strike you on the knees and legs with severe boils from which you cannot be healed, from the sole of your foot to the crown of your head.
</p>

## You Will Go into Exile

<p>
v36: “The LORD will bring you and the king whom you set over you to a nation that you and your ancestors have not known, and there you will serve other gods of wood and stone.

v37: You will become a horror, a proverb, and a taunt among all the peoples where the LORD leads you.

v38: Much seed you will carry out to the field, but little you will gather, for the locust will consume it.

v39: You will plant vineyards and cultivate them, but you will not drink wine or gather grapes, for the worm will eat them.

v40: Olive trees you will have throughout all your territory, but you will not anoint yourself with oil, for your olives will drop off.

v41: Sons and daughters you will father, but they will not be yours, for they will go into captivity.

v42: The cricket will possess all your trees and the fruit of your ground.

v43: The resident alien who is in your midst will rise above you higher and higher, while you will descend lower and lower.

v44: He will lend to you, but you will not lend to him. He will be the head, and you will be the tail.
</p>

## Because You Did Not Serve the LORD with Joy

<p>
v45: “All these curses will come upon you, pursue you, and overtake you until you are destroyed, because you did not listen to the voice of the LORD your God, to keep his commandments and statutes that he commanded you.

v46: They will be upon you as a sign and a wonder, and upon your offspring forever,

v47: because you did not serve the LORD your God with joy and gladness of heart because of the abundance of everything.

v48: Therefore you will serve your enemies whom the LORD will send against you, in hunger, thirst, nakedness, and lack of everything. He will put an iron yoke upon your neck until he has destroyed you.
</p>

## A Nation from Far Away

<p>
v49: “The LORD will raise against you a nation from far away, from the end of the earth, as an eagle swoops down, a nation whose language you do not understand,

v50: a hard-faced nation that will not respect the old or show favor to the young.

v51: It will eat the fruit of your livestock and the fruit of your ground until you are destroyed. It will leave you no grain, new wine, or oil, no offspring of your cattle or increase of your flock, until it has caused you to perish.

v52: It will besiege you within all your gates until your high and fortified walls in which you trust fall throughout all your land. It will besiege you within all your gates throughout all your land that the LORD your God has given you.
</p>

## The Horror of Siege

<p>
v53: “You will eat the fruit of your own womb, the flesh of your sons and daughters whom the LORD your God has given you, in the siege and distress with which your enemy will distress you.

v54: The man among you who is tender and very delicate—his eye will be evil toward his brother, toward the wife of his embrace, and toward the rest of his children whom he leaves,

v55: so that he does not give to any of them from the flesh of his children that he is eating, because nothing remains to him in the siege and distress with which your enemy will distress you within all your gates.

v56: The tender and delicate woman among you, who would not venture to set the sole of her foot upon the ground because of delicateness and tenderness—her eye will be evil toward the husband of her embrace, toward her son and daughter,

v57: toward her afterbirth that comes out from between her feet and toward the children whom she bears, for she will secretly eat them for lack of everything in the siege and distress with which your enemy will distress you within your gates.
</p>

## If You Do Not Fear This Glorious Name

<p>
v58: “If you are not careful to do all the words of this instruction that are written in this book, to fear this glorious and fearsome name, the LORD your God,

v59: then the LORD will make extraordinary your plagues and the plagues of your offspring, great and lasting plagues, and severe and lasting sicknesses.

v60: He will bring back upon you all the diseases of Egypt that you feared, and they will cling to you.

v61: Also every sickness and every plague that is not written in the book of this instruction, the LORD will bring upon you until you are destroyed.

v62: You will be left few in number, though you were as numerous as the stars of the heavens, because you did not listen to the voice of the LORD your God.

v63: And it will be, just as the LORD rejoiced over you to do you good and multiply you, so the LORD will cause you to perish and destroy you, and you will be torn from the ground that you are entering to possess.
</p>

## Scattered among All Peoples

<p>
v64: “The LORD will scatter you among all the peoples, from one end of the earth to the other end of the earth. There you will serve other gods whom you and your ancestors have not known, wood and stone.

v65: Among those nations you will find no rest, and there will be no resting place for the sole of your foot. The LORD will give you there a trembling heart, failing eyes, and despairing life.

v66: Your life will hang in suspense before you. You will fear night and day and will have no assurance of your life.

v67: In the morning you will say, ‘If only it were evening!’ and in the evening you will say, ‘If only it were morning!’ because of the fear that your heart fears and because of the sight that your eyes see.

v68: The LORD will bring you back to Egypt in ships, by a way of which I said to you, ‘You shall never see it again.’ There you will offer yourselves for sale to your enemies as male and female servants, but there will be no buyer.”
</p>
## Notes
v01–14: The blessing section reverses many conditions later named in the curses: city and field, fertility, food, battle, rain, lending, and national status.
v02: Blessings “overtake” the obedient just as curses overtake the disobedient in verse 15, creating deliberate structural symmetry.
v09: Israel’s holiness remains tied to keeping commandments and walking in the LORD’s ways.
v12: Heaven is imagined as the LORD’s “good treasury” from which seasonal rain is given.
v15–68: The curse section is much longer than the blessing section, creating an intentionally overwhelming warning about covenant abandonment.
v20: The Hebrew shifts unexpectedly into first person—“because you have abandoned me”—within words otherwise spoken about the LORD in third person. TSW preserves the personal divine claim.
v22: Several disease and agricultural terms are rare and difficult to identify precisely. TSW uses conventional equivalents while retaining the piling-up effect.
v25: “a horror” can also mean an object of trembling or terror among kingdoms.
v30–34: The curses attack the ordinary expectations of settled life: marriage, home, vineyard, livestock, children, labor, and sanity.
v36: Exile includes king and people together and leads ironically to service of the very kind of gods Israel was forbidden to worship.
v42: The insect term translated “cricket” is uncertain and may refer to another consuming insect. TSW retains a conventional rendering.
v47: The stated failure is not only disobedience but failure to serve the LORD with joy amid abundance.
v49: The eagle image emphasizes speed and predatory power. The unnamed nation is characterized by distance, foreign language, and ruthlessness rather than explicitly named in the verse.
v53–57: The siege imagery is deliberately horrifying and culminates in cannibalism under starvation. TSW preserves the severity without graphic expansion beyond the Hebrew.
v57: The verse’s bodily language includes the afterbirth and newborn child. It portrays the destruction of ordinary maternal tenderness under siege famine.
v58: Covenant obedience is connected to fear of “this glorious and fearsome name,” making the divine name itself central to the book of instruction.
v63: The verse’s language of the LORD causing Israel to perish is severe and balanced against earlier divine joy in doing good. Deuteronomy uses relational language without softening covenant judgment.
v68: Return to Egypt reverses the exodus. The final humiliation is so complete that even attempted self-sale into slavery finds no buyer.

##Vocabulary

v02: נָשַׂג (nasag)
“To overtake.” Blessings or curses catch up with the covenant community.

v08: אֲסָם (asam)
“Barn” or “storehouse.” A place of agricultural abundance.

v12: אוֹצָר (otsar)
“Treasury.” Heaven as the LORD’s storehouse of rain.

v42: צְלָצַל (tselatsal)
“Cricket” or another consuming insect. The exact species is uncertain.

v68: מָכַר (makhar)
“To sell.” The final curse imagines return to Egyptian servitude.
