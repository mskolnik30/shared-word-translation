## Bless the LORD in the Night

<p>
v01: Look, bless the LORD,
all you servants of the LORD
who stand in the house of the LORD by night.
v02: Lift up your hands toward the holy place
and bless the LORD.
v03: May the LORD bless you from Zion,
the maker of heaven and earth.
</p>
