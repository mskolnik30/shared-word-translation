## For the Director of Music. According to “The Doe of the Dawn.” A Psalm of David.

<p>
v01: My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?
  Why are you so far from saving me,
  from the words of my groaning?

v02: O my God, I cry by day, but you do not answer,
  and by night, but I find no rest.

v03: Yet you are holy,
  enthroned on the praises of Israel.

v04: In you our ancestors trusted;
  they trusted, and you delivered them.

v05: To you they cried and were rescued;
  in you they trusted and were not put to shame.

v06: But I am a worm and not human,
  scorned by mortals and despised by the people.

v07: All who see me mock me;
  they make mouths at me; they shake their heads:

v08: “Trust in the LORD—let the LORD deliver him!
  Let the LORD rescue him, since the LORD delights in him!”

v09: Yet you are the one who took me from the womb;
  you made me trust at my mother’s breasts.

v10: On you I was cast from my birth,
  and from my mother’s womb you have been my God.

v11: Do not be far from me,
  for trouble is near,
  and there is no one to help.

v12: Many bulls surround me;
  strong bulls of Bashan encircle me.

v13: They open wide their mouths at me,
  like a ravening and roaring lion.

v14: I am poured out like water,
  and all my bones are out of joint.
  My heart is like wax;
  it is melted within my chest.

v15: My strength is dried up like a potsherd,
  and my tongue sticks to my jaws;
  you lay me in the dust of death.

v16: For dogs surround me;
  a company of evildoers encircles me.
  They have pierced my hands and my feet—

v17: I can count all my bones—
  they stare and gloat over me.

v18: They divide my garments among them,
  and for my clothing they cast lots.

v19: But you, O LORD, do not be far away!
  O my strength, hurry to help me!

v20: Deliver my life from the sword,
  my only life from the power of the dog.

v21: Save me from the mouth of the lion!
  From the horns of the wild oxen you have answered me.
</p>
## Praise After Deliverance

<p>
v22: I will declare your name to my sisters and brothers;
  in the midst of the assembly I will praise you.

v23: You who fear the LORD, praise him!
  All offspring of Jacob, honor him!
  Stand in awe of him, all offspring of Israel!

v24: For he has not despised or abhorred
  the affliction of the afflicted;
  he has not hidden his face from him,
  but has heard when he cried to him.

v25: From you comes my praise in the great assembly;
  I will pay my vows before those who fear him.

v26: The afflicted shall eat and be satisfied;
  those who seek the LORD shall praise him.
  May your hearts live forever!

v27: All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn to the LORD;
  and all the families of the nations shall worship before you.

v28: For kingship belongs to the LORD,
  and the LORD rules over the nations.

v29: All the prosperous of the earth eat and worship;
  before the LORD shall bow all who go down to the dust,
  even those who cannot keep their life.

v30: Posterity will serve the LORD;
  future generations will be told of the Lord.

v31: They will come and proclaim the LORD’s righteousness
  to a people yet unborn,
  that the LORD has done it.
</p>
