<p>
v01: For the leader. To the tune of "The Silent Dove of Distant Places." A Miktam of David, when the Philistines seized him in Gath.
</p>

<p>
v02: Be gracious to me, O God, for people trample on me; all day long an attacker oppresses me.
v03: My enemies trample upon me all day long, for many are fighting against me proudly.
v04: When I am afraid, I will trust in you.
v05: In God, whose word I praise, in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can flesh do to me?
v06: All day long they twist my words; all their thoughts are against me for evil.
v07: They attack, they lurk, they watch my steps, as they wait for my life.
v08: Because of wickedness, cast them out; in anger bring down the peoples, O God.
v09: You have kept count of my wanderings; put my tears in your bottle. Are they not in your book?
</p>

v10: Then my enemies will turn back on the day when I call. This I know, that God is for me.
v11: In God, whose word I praise, in the LORD, whose word I praise,
v12: in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can humans do to me?
v13: O God, I must perform my vows to you; I will render thank offerings to you.
v14: For you have delivered my soul from death, indeed my feet from falling, so that I may walk before God in the light of life.

## Notes

- **v01:** "Miktam" - A term whose precise meaning is uncertain, possibly a literary or musical notation associated with Davidic psalms.
- **v01:** "The Silent Dove of Distant Places" - Likely a tune name or musical reference, situating the psalm within a traditional setting for worship or reflection.
- **v09:** "Tears in your bottle" - A metaphor reflecting God's attentiveness and care, suggesting that God preserves the psalmist's sorrows as one would store precious liquid.

## Vocabulary

- **v05:** דָּבָר (da.var) - "Word" in this psalm emphasizes the reliability and trustworthiness of God's promises, worthy of trust and praise.
- **v14:** אוֹר (or) - "Light," used here to symbolize life and divine presence, signifying hope and guidance on the psalmist's path.
