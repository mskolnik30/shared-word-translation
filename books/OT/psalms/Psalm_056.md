<p>
For the leader. To the tune of "The Silent Dove of Distant Places." A Miktam of David, when the Philistines seized him in Gath.
</p>

<p>
v01: Be gracious to me, O God, for people trample on me; all day long an attacker oppresses me.
v02: My enemies trample upon me all day long, for many are fighting against me proudly.
v03: When I am afraid, I will trust in you.
v04: In God, whose word I praise, in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can flesh do to me?
v05: All day long they twist my words; all their thoughts are against me for evil.
v06: They attack, they lurk, they watch my steps, as they wait for my life.
v07: Because of wickedness, cast them out; in anger bring down the peoples, O God.
v08: You have kept count of my wanderings; put my tears in your bottle. Are they not in your book?
</p>

<p>
v09: Then my enemies will turn back on the day when I call. This I know, that God is for me.
v10: In God, whose word I praise, in the LORD, whose word I praise,
v11: in God I trust; I will not be afraid. What can humans do to me?
v12: O God, I must perform my vows to you; I will render thank offerings to you.
v13: For you have delivered my soul from death, indeed my feet from falling, so that I may walk before God in the light of life.
</p>

## Notes

Superscription: "Miktam" - A term whose precise meaning is uncertain, possibly a literary or musical notation associated with Davidic psalms.

Superscription: "The Silent Dove of Distant Places" - Likely a tune name or musical reference, situating the psalm within a traditional setting for worship or reflection.

## Vocabulary

v04: דָּבָר (davar)
“Word" in this psalm marks the reliability and trustworthiness of God's promises, worthy of trust and praise.

v13: אוֹר (or)
“Light," used here to symbolize life and divine presence, denoting hope and guidance on the psalmist's path.
