For the director. A psalm of David.

<p>
v01: LORD, the king rejoices in your strength,
    and in your salvation how greatly he exults!
</p>
<p>
v02: The desire of his heart you have given him,
    and the request of his lips you have not withheld. Selah.
</p>
<p>
v03: You have welcomed him with the blessings of goodness,
    you set a crown of fine gold on his head.
</p>
<p>
v04: He asked you for life, and you gave it to him,
    length of days forever and ever.
</p>
<p>
v05: His glory is great through your salvation;
    majesty and splendor you bestow upon him.
</p>
<p>
v06: For you make him most blessed forever;
    you make him joyful with gladness in your presence.
</p>
<p>
v07: Because the king trusts in LORD,
    and through the steadfast love of the Most High, he will not be shaken.
</p>
<p>
v08: Your hand will find all your enemies;
    your right hand will find those who hate you.
</p>
<p>
v09: You will make them like a fiery furnace
    at the time of your appearance.
LORD, in his anger, will swallow them up,
    and fire will devour them.
</p>
<p>
v10: You will destroy their offspring from the earth,
    their descendants from among humanity.
</p>
<p>
v11: Though they plot evil against you,
    devising schemes, they will not succeed.
</p>
<p>
v12: For you will put them to flight;
    you will aim your arrows at their faces.
</p>
<p>
v13: Be exalted, LORD, in your strength;
    we will sing and praise your power.
</p>
## Notes
v02: "Desire of his heart" and "request of his lips" emphasize a harmonious alignment between the king's expressed wishes and God's gracious response.
v05: "Glory," "majesty," and "splendor" contribute to the elevated portrayal of the king, underscoring divine favor as the source of true kingly authority.
v06: "Most blessed forever" indicates divine favor and the enduring legacy conferred by divine presence and blessing.

##Vocabulary

v01: יְהוָ֗ה (Yahweh)
“LORD.” The divine name, represented in English by small-capital LORD.

v05: כְּבוֹד (κάwod)
“Glory.” Speaks to honor, respect, and the king's reputation, significantly enhanced by divine salvation.

v07: חֶ֫סֶד (chesed)
“Steadfast love" embodies God's faithful, covenantal loyalty, crucial for understanding divine-human relationships and the king's security.
