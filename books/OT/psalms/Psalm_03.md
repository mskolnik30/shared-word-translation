<p>
v01: A psalm of David, when he fled from Absalom, his son.
</p>
<p>
v02: Yahweh, how many are my foes!  
    Many rise up against me!
v03: Many are saying to my soul,  
    "There is no salvation for him in God." <br> Selah
</p>
<p>
v04: But you, Yahweh, are a shield around me,  
    my glory, and the lifter of my head.
</p>
<p>
v05: I cry out with my voice to Yahweh,  
    and he answers me from his holy hill. <br> Selah
</p>
<p>
v06: I lie down and sleep;  
    I awake, for Yahweh sustains me.
</p>
<p>
v07: I will not fear the ten thousands of people  
    who have set themselves against me all around.
</p>
<p>
v08: Arise, Yahweh! Save me, my God!  
    For you strike all my enemies on the cheek;  
    you break the teeth of the wicked.
</p>
<p>
v09: Salvation belongs to Yahweh;  
    Your blessing be upon your people. <br> Selah
</p>
## Notes

- **v1**: The superscription provides historical context, situating the psalm in the period of David's flight from Absalom. The narrative is found in 2 Samuel.
- **v2-3**: The repetition of "many" emphasizes the overwhelming nature of the adversaries. "There is no salvation..." voices doubt about divine deliverance.
- **v4**: "But you" shifts the focus from threat to trust. The imagery of God as a shield and the one who "lifts the head" symbolizes protection and restoration of dignity.
- **v5**: The holy hill refers to the location of God's presence. "Selah" likely indicates a pause or reflection.
- **v6**: The simple daily actions of lying down, sleeping, and waking testify to trust in God's sustaining presence.
- **v7**: "I will not fear..." expresses confidence in God's protection despite numerically superior opposition.
- **v8**: The imperative "Arise" echoes a battle cry, calling on God for rescue. The vivid imagery of striking and breaking underscores divine intervention against the wicked.
- **v9**: The final verse ascribes deliverance to Yahweh and wishes God's blessing upon the community—a communal concern reflected in individual prayer.

## Vocabulary

- **v02, v04, v09, "Yahweh" (יְהוָה, ye.ho.vah)**: The covenant name of God, emphasizing His personal relationship with Israel.
- **v03, v08, "Salvation" (יְשׁוּעָה, ye.shu.ah)**: A key theological term indicating deliverance and rescue, central to the Psalm's plea and trust in divine intervention.
- **v08, "Enemies" (אֹיֵב, o.yeyv)**: Signifies adversaries or foes, highlighting the conflict backdrop of the Psalm.
- **v09, "Blessing" (בְּרָכָה, be.ra.khah)**: Conveys God's favor and benevolence, often resulting in tangible well-being and prosperity.
