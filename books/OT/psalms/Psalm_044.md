<p>
For the director. Of the sons of Korah. A Maskil.
</p>

## What Our Ancestors Told Us

<p>
v01: O God, with our ears we have heard;
our ancestors have told us
the work you worked in their days,
in days of old.

v02: With your hand you drove out nations,
but you planted our people;
you afflicted peoples,
but you spread our people out.

v03: For not by their sword
did they possess the land,
and their arm did not save them;
but your right hand and your arm,
and the light of your face,
because you delighted in them.
</p>

## You Are My King

<p>
v04: You are my King, O God;
command saving help for Jacob.

v05: Through you we push back our foes;
through your name we tread down
those who rise against us.

v06: For I do not trust in my bow,
and my sword does not save me.

v07: But you have saved us from our foes
and put to shame those who hate us.

v08: In God we boast all day long,
and we praise your name forever.
Selah.
</p>

## Yet You Have Rejected Us

<p>
v09: Yet you have rejected us
and put us to shame,
and you do not go out
with our armies.

v10: You make us turn back from the foe,
and those who hate us
plunder for themselves.

v11: You give us like sheep for food,
and you scatter us among the nations.

v12: You sell your people for no gain;
you gain nothing from their price.
</p>

<p>
v13: You make us a reproach to our neighbors,
a scorn and derision
to those around us.

v14: You make us a proverb among the nations,
a shaking of the head among the peoples.

v15: All day my disgrace is before me,
and the shame of my face covers me,

v16: at the voice of the one who taunts and reviles,
before the enemy and the avenger.
</p>

## We Have Not Forgotten You

<p>
v17: All this has come upon us,
yet we have not forgotten you,
and we have not dealt falsely
with your covenant.

v18: Our heart has not turned back,
and our steps have not departed
from your path,

v19: though you crushed us
in a place of jackals
and covered us
with the shadow of death.
</p>

<p>
v20: If we had forgotten
the name of our God
or spread out our hands
to a foreign god,

v21: would not God discover this?
For God knows
the secrets of the heart.

v22: Yet for your sake
we are killed all day long;
we are counted
as sheep for slaughter.
</p>

## Rise Up and Help Us

<p>
v23: Awake!
Why do you sleep, Lord?
Rouse yourself;
do not reject us forever.

v24: Why do you hide your face?
Why do you forget
our affliction and oppression?

v25: For our soul is bowed down to the dust;
our belly clings to the earth.

v26: Rise up;
be our help.
Redeem us
for the sake of your steadfast love.
</p>
##Notes

Superscription: “Maskil” — The precise meaning of the designation remains uncertain. It may indicate a literary, musical, or instructional category and is therefore retained rather than translated.

##Vocabulary

v02: נָטַע (nata)
“To plant.” Used metaphorically for establishing a people securely in a land.

v04: יְשׁוּעָה (yeshuah)
“Saving help,” “deliverance,” or “salvation.” The Hebrew appears here in a plural form with a sense of acts or manifestations of deliverance.

v17: בְּרִית (berit)
“Covenant.” A binding relationship characterized by obligation, promise, and faithfulness.

v26: חֶסֶד (ḥesed)
“Steadfast love.” Faithful, enduring love that becomes the final ground of the community’s appeal for redemption.
