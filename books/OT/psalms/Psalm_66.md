<p>
v01: To the leader. A song, a psalm.
Shout joyfully to God, all the earth;
v02: sing the glory of his name; make his praise glorious.
v03: Say to God, "How awe-inspiring are your works!
Because of the greatness of your power, your enemies cower before you.
v04: All the earth will bow down to you, and they will sing praises to you;
they will sing praises to your name." Selah
</p>

<p>
v05: Come and see the works of God, the awe-inspiring deeds he has done among the children of humanity.
v06: He turned the sea into dry land; they passed through the river on foot.
There we rejoiced in him,
v07: who rules by his might forever, whose eyes watch the nations—
let not the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah
</p>

<p>
v08: Bless our God, peoples, and make the sound of his praise heard,
v09: who holds our soul in life and does not allow our feet to stumble.
v10: For you, God, have tested us; you have refined us as silver is refined.
v11: You brought us into the net; you laid affliction on our backs.
v12: You made men ride over our heads; we went through fire and water,
but you brought us to a place of abundance.
</p>

<p>
v13: I will enter your house with burnt offerings; I will fulfill my vows to you,
v14: which my lips uttered and my mouth spoke when I was in trouble.
v15: I will offer you burnt sacrifices of fat animals,
with the aromatic smoke of rams; I will make an offering of bulls with goats. Selah
</p>

<p>
v16: Come and hear, all you who fear God, and I will declare what he has done for my soul.
v17: I cried out to him with my mouth, and he was exalted with my tongue.
v18: If I had regarded wickedness in my heart, the Lord would not have listened.
v19: But God has surely listened; he has attended to the voice of my prayer.
v20: Blessed be God, who has not rejected my prayer,
or turned his loving kindness from me.
</p>

## Notes

v1: "To the leader. A song, a psalm" - This superscription is a common introduction suggesting the liturgical context for the psalm's use.  
v3: "How awe-inspiring are your works!" - This exclamation invites consideration of God's mighty acts both in creation and in the history of Israel.  
v6: "He turned the sea into dry land ... There we rejoiced in him" - Reference to the Exodus event, highlighting God's power to deliver.  
v10–12: A vivid metaphor of refining through fiery trials, reflecting both communal and individual experiences of divine testing.  
v16–19: A testimony section centering on personal deliverance, showcasing the spiritual confidence that arises from God's attentive response.  

## Vocabulary

v1: לַמְנַצֵּחַ (la-m'na-tse-ach) — "To the leader" indicates a direction, possibly to a choir director or liturgical leader.  
v10: בְחַנְתָּנוּ (be-chan-ta-nu) — "You have tested us" uses imagery of refining metals to express spiritual purification.  
v15: עוֹלָה (o-lah) — "burnt offering" refers to sacrifices wholly consumed by fire, symbolizing complete devotion to God.  
v20: תְּפִלָּה (te-phil-lah) — "prayer" highlights the intimate communication between the psalmist and God.
