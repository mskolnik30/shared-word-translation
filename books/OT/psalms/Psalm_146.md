v01: Hallelujah! Praise Yahweh, O my soul.
v02: I will praise Yahweh while I live;
    I will sing praises to my God while I have being.
v03: Do not trust in princes,
    in a human being who cannot save.
v04: His spirit departs, he returns to the earth;
    in that very day, his plans perish.
v05: Blessed is he whose help is the God of Jacob,
    whose hope is in Yahweh his God,
v06: who made heaven and earth,
    the sea and all that is in them;
    who keeps faith forever;
v07: who executes justice for the oppressed;
    who gives food to the hungry.
    Yahweh frees the prisoners;
v08: Yahweh opens the eyes of the blind;
    Yahweh lifts up those who are bowed down;
    Yahweh loves the righteous;
v09: Yahweh watches over the strangers;
    He upholds the orphan and widow,
    but the way of the wicked He brings to ruin.
v10: Yahweh will reign forever,
    your God, O Zion, to all generations.
    Hallelujah!

## Notes

- **Hallelujah (v1, v10)**: This term is a command to praise Yahweh, frequently appearing in psalms to invite communal worship.
- **Do not trust in princes (v3)**: The psalm contrasts the transience of human power and plans with the eternal reliability of God.
- **Blessed (v5)**: This term suggests divine favor, emphasizing the security of those who trust in Yahweh.
- **Keeps faith forever (v6)**: Yahweh's faithfulness endures, providing a foundation for confidence in His justice and benevolence outlined in the following verses.
- **Reign forever (v10)**: Signals the enduring sovereignty of Yahweh, contrasting the fleeting nature of human reign emphasized earlier.

## Vocabulary

- **יְהוָ֣ה (Yahweh) (v1, v10)**: The personal name of Israel's God, emphasizing a covenantal relationship throughout the psalm.
- **נֶ֫פֶשׁ (nephesh) (v1)**: Signifying the whole self or life; here, it represents an individual's entire being committed to praising Yahweh.
- **שֵׂ֫בֶר (sever) (v5)**: Hope or expectation; highlights the trust and reliance on God as an ongoing act in the worshiper's life.
