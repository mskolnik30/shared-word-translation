v01: A psalm. Sing to Yahweh a new song, for he has done marvelous things; his right hand and his holy arm have worked salvation for him.
v02: Yahweh has made known his salvation; he has revealed his righteousness in the sight of the nations.
v03: He has remembered his steadfast love and faithfulness to the house of Israel; all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.
v04: Make a joyful noise to Yahweh, all the earth; break forth into joyous song and sing praises.
v05: Sing praises to Yahweh with the lyre, with the lyre and the sound of melody.
v06: With trumpets and sound of the horn make a joyful noise before the King, Yahweh.
v07: Let the sea roar, and all that fills it, the world and those who dwell in it!
v08: Let the rivers clap their hands; let the hills sing for joy together
v09: before Yahweh, for he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness, and the peoples with equity.

## Notes

- **v1 "new song"**: A motif often associated with God’s acts of deliverance and faithfulness, encouraging fresh praise due to fresh acts of God.
- **v1 "right hand" and "holy arm"**: Metaphors for God’s power and might, emphasizing divine intervention.
- **v3 "steadfast love" and "faithfulness"**: Core attributes of God’s covenantal commitment to Israel, ensuring the fulfillment of promises.
- **v4 "joyful noise"**: A call for enthusiastic and exuberant worship, encompassing all creation.
- **v9 "judge the earth"**: Reflects the theme of divine justice, where God is portrayed as ruling with righteousness and fairness for all peoples.

## Vocabulary

- **v02 יְשׁוּעָה (ye.shu.ah)**: Translated as "salvation," this term carries the sense of deliverance and rescue, often associated with God’s saving acts.
- **v03 חֶ֫סֶד (che.sed)**: Often rendered as "steadfast love," it denotes God's loyal, covenantal love and mercy towards his people.
- **v09 מֵישָׁרִים (me.shar)**: Translated as "equity," this term describes fairness and uprightness, particularly in the context of justice.
