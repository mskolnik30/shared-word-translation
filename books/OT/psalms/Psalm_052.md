<p>
v01: For the director, a maskil of David.  
</p>
<p>
v02: When Doeg the Edomite came and reported to Saul,  
saying, "David has come to the house of Ahimelech."  
</p>
<p>
v03: Why do you boast of evil, mighty man?  
The steadfast love of God endures all the day.  
v04: Your tongue plots destruction,  
like a sharp razor, you worker of deceit.  
v05: You love evil more than good,  
falsehood more than speaking righteousness.  
Selah  
v06: You love all words that devour,  
O deceitful tongue.  
</p>
<p>
v07: But God will break you down forever;  
he will snatch and tear you from your tent,  
he will uproot you from the land of the living.  
Selah  
</p>
<p>
v08: The righteous shall see and fear;  
they shall laugh at him, saying,  
v09: "See the man who would not make God his refuge,  
but trusted in the abundance of his riches  
and strengthened himself in his destruction."  
</p>
<p>
v10: But I am like a green olive tree  
in the house of God.  
I trust in the steadfast love of God  
forever and ever.  
v11: I will thank you forever,  
because you have done it.  
I will wait for your name,  
for it is good, in the presence of your faithful ones.  
</p>
## Notes

## Vocabulary

v3: חֶסֶד (chesed) - "steadfast love." This term evokes a covenantal loyalty or mercy, often emphasizing God's enduring faithfulness and compassion toward his people.
