## I Will Extol You, My God and King
<p>
v01: I will extol you, my God and King,
and bless your name forever and ever.
v02: Every day I will bless you
and praise your name forever and ever.
</p>

<p>
v03: Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised,
and his greatness is unsearchable.
</p>

## Generational Praise
<p>
v04: One generation shall commend your works to another,
and shall declare your mighty acts.
v05: On the glorious splendor of your majesty,
and on your wondrous works, I will meditate.
</p>

<p>
v06: They shall speak of the might of your awesome deeds,
and I will declare your greatness.
v07: They shall pour forth the fame of your abundant goodness
and shall sing aloud of your righteousness.
</p>

## The LORD’s Character
<p>
v08: The LORD is gracious and merciful,
slow to anger and abounding in steadfast love.
v09: The LORD is good to all,
and his mercy is over all that he has made.
</p>

## Universal Praise
<p>
v10: All your works shall give thanks to you, O LORD,
and all your faithful ones shall bless you!
v11: They shall speak of the glory of your kingdom
and tell of your power,
</p>

<p>
v12: to make known to the children of humanity your mighty deeds,
and the glorious splendor of your kingdom.
</p>

<p>
v13: Your kingdom is an everlasting kingdom,
and your dominion endures throughout all generations.
</p>

## God’s Care for Creation
<p>
v14: The LORD upholds all who are falling
and raises up all who are bowed down.
v15: The eyes of all look to you,
and you give them their food in due season.
</p>

<p>
v16: You open your hand;
you satisfy the desire of every living thing.
</p>

## The LORD’s Righteousness and Nearness
<p>
v17: The LORD is righteous in all his ways
and kind in all his works.
v18: The LORD is near to all who call on him,
to all who call on him in truth.
</p>

<p>
v19: He fulfills the desire of those who fear him;
he also hears their cry and saves them.
v20: The LORD preserves all who love him,
but all the wicked he will destroy.
</p>

## Closing Praise
<p>
v21: My mouth will speak the praise of the LORD,
and let all flesh bless his holy name forever and ever.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: רוֹמֵם (romem)
“To exalt.” Lift up.

v01: בָּרַךְ (barakh)
“To bless.” Praise or honor.

v03: גָּדוֹל (gadol)
“Great.” Magnified beyond measure.

v13: מַלְכוּת (malkut)
“Kingdom.” Rule or reign.
