## Sing to the LORD a New Song
<p>
v01: Praise the LORD!
Sing to the LORD a new song,
his praise in the assembly of the faithful!
</p>

<p>
v02: Let Israel be glad in his Maker;
let the children of Zion rejoice in their King!
v03: Let them praise his name with dancing,
making melody to him with tambourine and lyre!
</p>

## The LORD Delights in His People
<p>
v04: For the LORD takes pleasure in his people;
he adorns the humble with salvation.
v05: Let the faithful exult in glory;
let them sing for joy on their beds.
</p>

## Praise and Judgment
<p>
v06: Let the high praises of God be in their throats
and two-edged swords in their hands,
v07: to execute vengeance on the nations
and punishments on the peoples,
</p>

<p>
v08: to bind their kings with chains
and their nobles with fetters of iron,
v09: to execute on them the judgment written.
This is honor for all his faithful ones.
Praise the LORD!
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: הָלַל (halal)
“To praise.” Celebrate.

v01: שִׁיר (shir)
“To sing.” Express in song.

v04: יְשׁוּעָה (yeshuah)
“Salvation.” Deliverance.

v09: מִשְׁפָּט (mishpat)
“Judgment.” Justice or decree.
