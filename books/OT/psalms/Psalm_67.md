v01: For the director of music. With stringed instruments. A psalm. A song.
</p>
v02: May God be gracious to us and bless us,
     make his face shine upon us, Selah
v03: that your way may be known on earth,
     your salvation among all nations.
v04: May the peoples praise you, O God;
     may all the peoples praise you.
v05: May the nations be glad and sing for joy,
     for you judge the peoples with equity
     and guide the nations of the earth. Selah
v06: May the peoples praise you, O God;
     may all the peoples praise you.
v07: The earth has yielded its produce;
     God, our God, blesses us.
v08: May God bless us,
     and all the ends of the earth fear him.

## Notes

**v02** "make his face shine upon us" — This expression echoes the priestly blessing in Numbers 6:25, invoking God's favorable and illuminating presence.

**v03** "your way" — Referring to God's path, teachings, or character being revealed and understood globally.

**v05** "judge the peoples with equity" — God's just and fair governance brings joy and singing among nations, emphasizing divine just rule.

**v07** "The earth has yielded its produce" — Indicates both literal agricultural blessing and a metaphor for God's provision and faithfulness to his people.

## Vocabulary

**v02** אֱלֹהִים (e.lo.him) — Refers to God, used throughout the psalm expressing the divine benefactor of blessings.

**v03** יְשׁוּעָה (ye.shu.ah) — Salvation, emphasizing deliverance or rescue, a central theme extended to all nations in this text.

**v05** מִישׁוֹר (mi.shor) — "Equity" or "level path," underscoring the fairness and justice of divine judgment and guidance.
