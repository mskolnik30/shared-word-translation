## Give Ear to My Words
<p>
v01: Give ear to my words, O LORD;
consider my groaning.
v02: Give attention to the sound of my cry,
my King and my God,
for to you do I pray.
</p>

<p>
v03: O LORD, in the morning you hear my voice;
in the morning I prepare a sacrifice for you and watch.
</p>

## God’s Holiness and Justice
<p>
v04: For you are not a God who delights in wickedness;
evil may not dwell with you.
v05: The boastful shall not stand before your eyes;
you hate all evildoers.
</p>

<p>
v06: You destroy those who speak lies;
the LORD abhors the bloodthirsty and deceitful man.
</p>

## Approach to God
<p>
v07: But I, through the abundance of your steadfast love,
will enter your house.
I will bow down toward your holy temple
in the fear of you.
</p>

<p>
v08: Lead me, O LORD, in your righteousness
because of my enemies;
make your way straight before me.
</p>

## Description of the Wicked
<p>
v09: For there is no truth in their mouth;
their inner being is destruction;
their throat is an open grave;
they flatter with their tongue.
</p>

<p>
v10: Make them bear their guilt, O God;
let them fall by their own counsels;
because of the abundance of their transgressions cast them out,
for they have rebelled against you.
</p>

## Joy of the Righteous
<p>
v11: But let all who take refuge in you rejoice;
let them ever sing for joy,
and spread your protection over them,
that those who love your name may exult in you.
</p>

<p>
v12: For you bless the righteous, O LORD;
you cover him with favor as with a shield.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: אֹזֶן (ozen)
“Ear.” Attention.

v01: הָגִיג (hagig)
“Groaning.” Murmur or meditation.

v03: עָרַךְ (arak)
“To prepare.” Arrange (offering/prayer).

v07: חֶסֶד (chesed)
“Steadfast love.” Covenant loyalty.
