<p>
For the director. A psalm of David.
</p>

## The LORD Sustains the Weak

<p>
v01: Blessed is the one who pays attention to the weak;
in the day of trouble
the LORD will deliver them.

v02: The LORD will guard them
and preserve their life;
they will be called blessed in the land.
You will not give them over
to the desire of their enemies.

v03: The LORD sustains them
on the bed of illness;
you turn their whole bed
in their sickness.
</p>

## Be Gracious to Me

<p>
v04: As for me, I said,
“O LORD, be gracious to me;
heal my soul,
for I have sinned against you.”

v05: My enemies speak evil against me:
“When will he die
and his name perish?”

v06: If someone comes to see me,
they speak emptiness;
their heart gathers trouble.
When they go outside,
they tell it.
</p>

## Even My Close Friend

<p>
v07: All who hate me
whisper together against me;
against me they devise harm:

v08: “A deadly thing
has been poured upon him;
now that he lies down,
he will not rise again.”

v09: Even my close friend,
in whom I trusted,
the one who ate my bread,
has lifted the heel against me.
</p>

## Raise Me Up

<p>
v10: But you, O LORD,
be gracious to me and raise me up,
so that I may repay them.

v11: By this I know
that you delight in me:
my enemy does not shout
in triumph over me.

v12: As for me,
you uphold me in my integrity;
you set me before your face forever.
</p>

## Blessing to the LORD

<p>
v13: Blessed be the LORD,
the God of Israel,
from everlasting to everlasting.
Amen and Amen.
</p>
##Notes

Superscription: “A psalm of David” — The superscription belongs to the Hebrew text and is not assigned a public verse number.

v08: “A deadly thing has been poured upon him” — The Hebrew expression is difficult and literally involves a destructive or worthless “thing” being poured or fixed upon the sufferer.

##Vocabulary

v01: דַּל (dal)
“Weak,” “poor,” or “lowly.” Someone diminished in strength, resources, or standing.

v01: מַשְׂכִּיל (maskil)
“One who acts wisely” or “one who pays attention.” The verbal form here describes thoughtful attention directed toward the weak.

v04: חָנַן (chanan)
“To be gracious” or “show favor.” An appeal for freely given mercy.

v12: תֹּם (tom)
“Integrity,” “wholeness,” or “completeness.” A condition of undivided or blameless faithfulness.
