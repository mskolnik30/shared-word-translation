## Who May Dwell with the LORD?
<p>
v01: O LORD, who may dwell in your tent? Who may live on your holy mountain?
v02: Those who walk with integrity, do what is right, and speak truth from the heart;
v03: who do not slander with their tongue, do no harm to others, and do not heap shame on their neighbors;
v04: in whose eyes the contemptible are rejected, but who honor those who fear the LORD; who keep their word even when it costs them;
v05: who do not lend money at interest, and do not take a bribe against the innocent. Those who do these things shall never be shaken.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: שָׁכַן (shākan)
“To dwell.” The verb describes settled presence or residence.

v02: אֱמֶת (ʾĕmet)
“Truth; faithfulness.” Reliability expressed in speech and action.

v04: יָרֵא (yārēʾ)
“To fear; revere.” Here the term describes reverence toward the LORD.

v05: שֹׁחַד (šōḥad)
“Bribe.” A payment that corrupts or distorts judgment.
