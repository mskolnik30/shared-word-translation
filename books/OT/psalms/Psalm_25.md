<p>
v01: To you, O Yahweh, I lift up my soul.
</p>
<p>
v02: O my God, in you I trust. Do not let me be put to shame;  
<span class="indent">do not let my enemies exult over me.</span>
</p>
<p>
v03: Indeed, none who wait for you will be put to shame;  
<span class="indent">they will be ashamed who deal treacherously without cause.</span>
</p>
<p>
v04: Make known to me your ways, O Yahweh;  
<span class="indent">teach me your paths.</span>
</p>
<p>
v05: Lead me in your truth and teach me,  
<span class="indent">for you are the God of my salvation;</span>  
I wait for you all the day long.
</p>
<p>
v06: Remember your compassionate acts, O Yahweh, and your constant love,  
for they have been from of old.
</p>
<p>
v07: Do not remember the sins of my youth or my transgressions;  
<span class="indent">according to your love, remember me,</span>  
<span class="indent">for the sake of your goodness, O Yahweh.</span>
</p>
<p>
v08: Good and upright is Yahweh;  
<span class="indent">therefore, he instructs sinners in the way.</span>
</p>
<p>
v09: He leads the humble in what is right,  
<span class="indent">and teaches the humble his way.</span>
</p>
<p>
v10: All the paths of Yahweh are steadfast love and faithfulness,  
<span class="indent">to those who keep his covenant and his decrees.</span>
</p>
<p>
v11: For your name's sake, O Yahweh,  
<span class="indent">pardon my iniquity, for it is great.</span>
</p>
<p>
v12: Who is the one who fears Yahweh?  
<span class="indent">He will instruct him in the way he should choose.</span>
</p>
<p>
v13: His soul will abide in well-being,  
<span class="indent">and his offspring will inherit the land.</span>
</p>
<p>
v14: The counsel of Yahweh is for those who fear him,  
<span class="indent">and he makes them know his covenant.</span>
</p>
<p>
v15: My eyes are ever toward Yahweh,  
<span class="indent">for he will pluck my feet out of the net.</span>
</p>
<p>
v16: Turn to me and be gracious to me,  
<span class="indent">for I am lonely and afflicted.</span>
</p>
<p>
v17: The troubles of my heart have grown;  
<span class="indent">bring me out of my distresses.</span>
</p>
<p>
v18: Look upon my affliction and my trouble,  
<span class="indent">and take away all my sins.</span>
</p>
<p>
v19: Consider my enemies, for they are many,  
<span class="indent">and they hate me with violent hatred.</span>
</p>
<p>
v20: Guard my soul and deliver me;  
<span class="indent">do not let me be put to shame, for I take refuge in you.</span>
</p>
<p>
v21: Let integrity and uprightness preserve me,  
<span class="indent">for I wait for you.</span>
</p>
<p>
v22: Redeem Israel, O God,  
<span class="indent">from all his troubles.</span>
</p>

## Notes

v1: "I lift up my soul" — An expression of trust and surrender, implying seeking guidance and protection from Yahweh.

v6: "your compassionate acts" — Emphasizes God's longstanding mercy and steadfast love and contrasts with human forgetfulness.

v7: "sins of my youth" — A plea acknowledging past mistakes, seeking forgiveness based on God's graciousness rather than past wrongs.

v8: "Good and upright" — Attributes of God's character, implying that divine guidance leads to righteousness.

## Vocabulary

v10: יְהוָ֑ה (Yahweh) — The personal name of God, emphasizing an intimate relationship with Israel.

v10: חֶ֣סֶד (chesed) — Often translated as "steadfast love," this term conveys loyal love and faithfulness grounded in covenant commitment.

v2: אֱֽלֹהַ֗י (Elohim) — A title of divinity used here for God, highlighting majesty and power in the context of personal trust.
