v01: Of Solomon. O God, give the king your justice and your righteousness to the king's son.  
v02: May he judge your people with righteousness, and your poor with justice.  
v03: Let the mountains bring peace to the people, and the hills, in righteousness.  
v04: May he judge the poor of the people, save the children of the needy, and crush the oppressor.  
v05: May they fear you as long as the sun endures, and as long as the moon, through all generations.  
v06: May he come down like rain upon mown grass, like showers that water the earth.  
v07: In his days may the righteous flourish and abundance of peace until the moon is no more.  
v08: May he have dominion from sea to sea and from the River to the ends of the earth.  
v09: Before him let desert tribes bow down and his enemies lick the dust.  
v10: May the kings of Tarshish and of the islands bring tribute; may the kings of Sheba and Seba offer gifts.  
v11: Let all kings bow down before him; all nations serve him.  
v12: For he will deliver the needy when they cry for help, the poor also, and him who has no helper.  
v13: He will have compassion on the poor and needy, and the souls of the needy he will save.  
v14: From oppression and violence he will redeem their soul; precious will their blood be in his sight.  
v15: May he live long; may gold of Sheba be given to him. May prayer also be made for him continually; may he be blessed all day long.  
v16: Let grain abound in the land, atop the mountains; let its fruit wave like Lebanon, and let those of the city flourish like the grass of the earth.  
v17: May his name endure forever; as long as the sun, may his name be continued. May all nations be blessed in him, and may they call him blessed.  
v18: Blessed be Yahweh, God, the God of Israel, who alone performs wonders.  
v19: And blessed be his glorious name forever; may the whole earth be filled with his glory. Amen and Amen.  
v20: The prayers of David, son of Jesse, are ended.

## Notes

**72:1** The psalm's ascription "Of Solomon" could imply authorship by Solomon or be in dedication to him, reflecting royal responsibilities.

**72:8** "From sea to sea" and "from the River to the ends of the earth" describe extensive dominion, echoing the ideal of a far-reaching kingly rule.

**72:17** "May his name endure forever" speaks to the lasting impact and remembrance of the king's legacy, associated with the enduring presence of the sun.

**72:20** The concluding note suggests this psalm's compilation within a collection attributed to David, though involving Solomon here.

## Vocabulary

**72:2** צֶ֫דֶק (tse.deq) — Translated "righteousness," referring here to moral correctness and justice applied in rulership and society.

**72:12** אֶבְיוֹן (ev.yon) — Translated "needy," emphasizing those lacking basic resources, whom the righteous king aids.

**72:18** יְהוָ֣ה (ye.ho.vah) — Rendered "Yahweh," the personal covenant name of Israel's God, the doer of mighty acts.
