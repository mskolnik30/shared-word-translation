<p>
v01: As the leader: A psalm of David. When Nathan the prophet came to him, after he went to Bathsheba.
</p>

<p>
v02: Be gracious to me, O God, according to your lovingkindness;
according to the multitude of your tender mercies, wipe away my transgressions.
v03: Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and purify me from my sin.
v04: For I know my transgressions, and my sin is ever before me.
v05: Against you, you alone, have I sinned,
    and what is evil in your sight I have done,
so that you are justified in your sentence
    and blameless when you judge.
v06: Behold, in iniquity I was brought forth,
    and in sin my mother conceived me.
v07: Behold, you desire truth in the inward being,
    and in the secret heart you teach me wisdom.
</p>

<p>
v08: Purify me with hyssop, and I shall be clean;
    wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.
v09: Let me hear joy and gladness;
    let the bones you have crushed rejoice.
v10: Hide your face from my sins,
    and blot out all my iniquities.
</p>

<p>
v11: Create in me a clean heart, O God,
    and renew a right spirit within me.
v12: Cast me not away from your presence,
    and do not take your holy spirit from me.
v13: Restore to me the joy of your salvation,
    and sustain me with a willing spirit.
v14: Then I will teach transgressors your ways,
    and sinners will return to you.
</p>

<p>
v15: Deliver me from the guilt of bloodshed, O God,
    O God of my salvation,
    and my tongue will sing aloud of your righteousness.
v16: Lord, open my lips,
    and my mouth will declare your praise.
</p>

<p>
v17: For you do not desire sacrifice, or I would give it;
    you are not pleased with burnt offering.
v18: The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit;
    a broken and contrite heart, O God, you will not despise.
</p>

<p>
v19: Do good to Zion in your good pleasure;
    build the walls of Jerusalem.
v20: Then you will delight in righteous sacrifices,
    in burnt offerings and whole burnt offerings;
    then they will offer bulls on your altar.
</p>

## Notes

v2 For the leader: The psalm is designated for liturgical instruction or leading worship, suggesting it has a community and didactic purpose.

v5 In sin my mother conceived me: This verse points to a sense of deep-rooted sinfulness, possibly hinting at original sin—a point of theological reflection on the human condition.

v17 You do not desire sacrifice: The psalmist's insight here emphasizes internal integrity over a cultic ritual, conveying that what God truly desires is a transformed heart.

## Vocabulary

v3 חֶ֫סֶד (chesed): This term, often translated as "lovingkindness" or "steadfast love," conveys a loyal, covenantal love central to Israel's understanding of God's nature.

v12 רוּחַ (ruach): Meaning "spirit," this word denotes the presence and sustaining power of God. In this context, it implies both an inner renewal and divine empowerment for living faithfully.

v18 נִשְׁבָּר (nishbar): Meaning "broken," this term conveys a state of repentance and vulnerability, describing the openness required for true reconciliation with God.
