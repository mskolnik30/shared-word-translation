<p>
A Psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judah.
</p>
<p>
v01: O God, you are my God; I earnestly seek you.
  My soul thirsts for you;
  my flesh faints for you,
  in a dry and weary land without water.
v02: Thus I have beheld you in the sanctuary,
  seeing your power and your glory.
v03: Because your steadfast love is better than life,
  my lips will praise you.
v04: So I will bless you as long as I live;
  in your name I will lift up my hands.
v05: My soul will be satisfied as with the richest of foods,
  and my mouth will praise you with joyful lips
v06: when I remember you upon my bed,
  and meditate on you in the night watches.
v07: For you have been my help,
  and in the shadow of your wings I will sing for joy.
v08: My soul clings to you;
  your right hand upholds me.
</p>
<p>
v09: But those who seek to destroy my life
  shall go down into the depths of the earth;
v10: they shall be given over to the power of the sword;
  they shall be the portion of jackals.
</p>
<p>
v11: But the king shall rejoice in God;
  all who swear by him shall exult,
  for the mouths of liars shall be stopped.
</p>
## Notes
v01: "my soul thirsts" — Using the metaphor of thirst, the psalmist conveys deep yearning for communion with God.
v03: "steadfast love" — A term often translated from the Hebrew *chesed*, referring to God's covenantal loyalty and love.
v04: "richest of foods" — Symbolizes abundance and satisfaction found in God's presence, contrasting with the physical thirst of v01.
v08: "your right hand upholds me" — Indicates strength and support, a common biblical image for divine assistance.

##Vocabulary

v03: חַסְדְּךָ (chasdecha)
“steadfast love" — Reflects God's enduring loyalty and kindness, foundational to the psalmist's praise.

v07: צֵל (tsel)
“shadow" — Implies protection and security, as one would find refuge under the shadow of God's wings.
