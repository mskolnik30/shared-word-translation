<p>
v01: A Psalm of David.
Ascribe to Yahweh, O sons of God,
  ascribe to Yahweh glory and strength.
v02: Ascribe to Yahweh the glory of his name;
  worship Yahweh in the splendor of holiness.
</p>
<p>
v03: The voice of Yahweh is over the waters;
  the God of glory thunders,
  Yahweh is over the mighty waters.
v04: The voice of Yahweh is powerful,
  the voice of Yahweh is full of majesty.
v05: The voice of Yahweh breaks the cedars;
  Yahweh breaks the cedars of Lebanon.
v06: He makes Lebanon skip like a calf,
  and Sirion like a young wild ox.
</p>
<p>
v07: The voice of Yahweh strikes with flashes of fire.
v08: The voice of Yahweh shakes the wilderness;
  Yahweh shakes the wilderness of Kadesh.
v09: The voice of Yahweh causes the deer to give birth
  and strips the forests bare,
  and in his temple all cry, "Glory!"
</p>
<p>
v10: Yahweh sits enthroned over the flood;
  Yahweh is enthroned as king forever.
v11: May Yahweh give strength to his people!
  May Yahweh bless his people with peace!
</p>

## Notes

- v1: "sons of God" could also be understood as a reference to heavenly beings or divine beings, emphasizing the celestial praise of Yahweh.
- v3-9: The repetition of "The voice of Yahweh" signifies the power and authority of God's word in creation and nature. Each mention is connected to an aspect of nature, underscoring God's sovereignty over the natural world.
- v10: "sits enthroned over the flood" points to Yahweh's rule over chaotic waters, reminiscent of the Flood narrative, highlighting God's ultimate sovereignty and control.
- v11: The psalm concludes with a prayer for Yahweh's power to be manifest as strength and peace among his people, contrasting the tumultuous imagery earlier in the psalm.

## Vocabulary

- v1: מִזְמוֹר (miz.mor) - A term for "psalm" or "song," indicating a musical composition intended for worship.
- v2: הַדְרַת קֹֽדֶשׁ (ha.da.rah qo.desh) - "splendor of holiness," emphasizing the beauty and majesty of holiness in worship.
- v3: קוֹל (qol) - "voice," referring to the powerful, commanding presence of God's word, manifest in natural phenomena.
- v10: מַבּוּל (mab.bul) - "flood," drawing a connection to the Flood narrative, symbolizing chaos that God masters.
- v11: שָׁלוֹם (sha.lom) - "peace," a state of harmony and well-being, reflecting God's blessing and provision for his people.
