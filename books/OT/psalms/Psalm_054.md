<p>
For the director with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David.
</p>
<p>
When the Ziphites came and said to Saul, "Is not David hiding among us?"
</p>
<p>
v01: O God, by your name save me, and by your might vindicate me.
v02: O God, hear my prayer; give ear to the words of my mouth.
</p>
<p>
v03: For strangers rise against me,
and ruthless men seek my life.
They have not set God before them. Selah
</p>
<p>
v04: Behold, God is my helper;
the Lord is the sustainer of my life.
v05: He will repay the evil to my foes;
in your faithfulness, destroy them.
</p>
<p>
v06: With a freewill offering I will sacrifice to you;
I will praise your name, O Lord, for it is good.
v07: For he has delivered me from every trouble,
and my eye has looked in triumph on my enemies.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: אֱלֹהִים (elohim)
“God.” The common Hebrew designation for God.

v05: אֱמֶת (emet)
“Faithfulness.” Marks the reliability and trustworthiness of God in dealing with the psalmist's enemies.

v06: יְהוָה (yehovah)
“LORD.” The divine name, represented in English by small-capital LORD.
