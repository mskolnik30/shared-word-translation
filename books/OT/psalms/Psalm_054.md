<p>  
v01: For the director with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David.  
</p>  
<p>  
v02: When the Ziphites came and said to Saul, "Is not David hiding among us?"  
</p>  
<p>  
v03: O God, by your name save me, and by your might vindicate me.  
v04: O God, hear my prayer; give ear to the words of my mouth.  
</p>  
<p>  
v05: For strangers rise against me,  
and ruthless men seek my life.  
They have not set God before them. Selah  
</p>  
<p>  
v06: Behold, God is my helper;  
the Lord is the sustainer of my life.  
v07: He will repay the evil to my foes;  
in your faithfulness, destroy them.  
</p>  
<p>  
v08: With a freewill offering I will sacrifice to you;  
I will praise your name, O Lord, for it is good.  
v09: For he has delivered me from every trouble,  
and my eye has looked in triumph on my enemies.  
</p>  

## Notes

v2: "Ziphites" - This refers to the people from Ziph, who informed King Saul about David's location, seeking favor from the king.  
v3: "by your name" - This invocation appeals to God's character and authority for salvation.  
v5: "strangers" and "ruthless men" - These may refer to enemies who are both external and internal, emphasizing the breadth of opposition faced.  
v9: "my eye has looked" - A poetic expression for having seen or experienced deliverance, indicating confidence in God's protection.  

## Vocabulary

v3: אֱלֹהִים (e.lo.him) - Here, the term emphasizes God's power and authority, which the psalmist calls upon for salvation and justice.  
v7: אֱמֶת (e.met) - Translated as "faithfulness," this term underscores the reliability and trustworthiness of God in dealing with the psalmist's enemies.  
v8: יְהוָה (ye.ho.vah) - The personal name for God, often representing the covenantal relationship He has with His people.
