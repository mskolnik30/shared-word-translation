<p>
For the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David.
</p>
<p>
v01: Hear my voice, O God, in my complaint;
    preserve my life from the terror of the enemy.
v02: Hide me from the secret counsel of evildoers,
    from the tumult of workers of iniquity,
v03: who sharpen their tongue like a sword
    and aim their arrows—bitter words
v04: to shoot in secret at the blameless;
    suddenly they shoot at him and do not fear.
v05: They hold fast to their evil purpose;
    they talk of laying snares secretly; they say, “Who will see them?”
v06: They devise injustices, saying, “We have perfected a secret plan.”
    Both the inner thought and the heart of a person are deep.
v07: But God will shoot at them with an arrow;
    suddenly they will be wounded.
v08: They will be made to stumble, their own tongue against them;
    all who see them will shake their heads.
v09: Then all people will fear;
    they will declare the work of God,
    and will consider what He has done.
v10: The righteous will rejoice in LORD and take refuge in him;
    all the upright in heart will glory.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: מִזְמוֹר (mizmor)
“A technical term for a Psalm, often indicating a sung or chanted piece of music.”

v07: פִּתְאֹם (pitom)
“Suddenly.” Conveys the abruptness and surprising nature of God’s response.

v09: יָרֵא (yare)
“Fear" in this context encompasses reverence, awe, and recognition of God’s power and justice.

v10: צַדִּיק (tsaddiq)
“Righteous; just.” One characterized by right conduct or standing.
