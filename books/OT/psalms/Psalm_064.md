<p>
v01: For the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David.
</p>
<p>
v02: Hear my voice, O God, in my complaint;
    preserve my life from the terror of the enemy.
v03: Hide me from the secret counsel of evildoers,
    from the tumult of workers of iniquity,
v04: who sharpen their tongue like a sword
    and aim their arrows—bitter words
v05: to shoot in secret at the blameless;
    suddenly they shoot at him and do not fear.
v06: They hold fast to their evil purpose;
    they talk of laying snares secretly; they say, “Who will see them?”
v07: They devise injustices, saying, “We have perfected a secret plan.”
    Both the inner thought and the heart of a person are deep.
v08: But God will shoot at them with an arrow;
    suddenly they will be wounded.
v09: They will be made to stumble, their own tongue against them;
    all who see them will shake their heads.
v10: Then all people will fear;
    they will declare the work of God,
    and will consider what He has done.
v11: The righteous will rejoice in Yahweh and take refuge in Him;
    all the upright in heart will glory.
</p>

## Notes

- **v02 "complaint"**: This term implies not just a personal grievance but a deeply felt lament or appeal to God for justice.
- **v04 "bitter words"**: Indicates words intended to harm or cause distress, likened to deadly weapons.
- **v07 "Both the inner thought and the heart of a person are deep"**: Suggests the depth of human deceit and the complexity of malicious intent, yet acknowledges divine understanding.
- **v08 "suddenly they will be wounded"**: Emphasizes the unexpected nature of God’s intervention against those who plot evil.
- **v10 "all people will fear"**: Envisions a collective awe and recognition of God’s justice, leading to a declaration of His deeds.

## Vocabulary

- **v01 מִזְמוֹר (miz.mor)**: A technical term for a Psalm, often indicating a sung or chanted piece of music.
- **v08 פִּתְאֹם (pit.om)**: Translated as "suddenly," this word conveys the abruptness and surprising nature of God’s response.
- **v10 יָרֵא (ya.re)**: "Fear" in this context encompasses reverence, awe, and recognition of God’s power and justice.
- **v11 צַדִּיק (tsad.diq)**: Refers to one who is righteous or just, living in harmony with God’s commandments.
