## A Song of Ascents
<p>
v01: Out of the depths I cry to you, O LORD.

v02: Lord, hear my voice!
  Let your ears be attentive
  to the sound of my pleas for mercy.

v03: If you, O LORD, should mark iniquities,
  Lord, who could stand?

v04: But with you there is forgiveness,
  so that you may be held in awe.

v05: I wait for the LORD; my whole being waits,
  and in the LORD’s word I hope.

v06: My whole being waits for the Lord
  more than those who watch for the morning,
  more than those who watch for the morning.

v07: O Israel, hope in the LORD!
  For with the LORD there is steadfast love,
  and with the LORD is plentiful redemption.

v08: The LORD will redeem Israel
  from all its iniquities.
</p>

“Iniquity; guilt; twisted wrongdoing.”

v07: חֶסֶד (ḥesed)
“Steadfast love; covenant loyalty.”

v07: פְּדוּת (pedut)
