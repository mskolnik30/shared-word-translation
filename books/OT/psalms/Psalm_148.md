## Praise the LORD from the Heavens

<p>
v01: Praise the LORD!
Praise the LORD from the heavens;
praise God in the heights!
v02: Praise God, all God’s angels;
praise God, all God’s hosts!
v03: Praise God, sun and moon;
praise God, all shining stars!
v04: Praise God, highest heavens,
and waters above the heavens!
v05: Let them praise the name of the LORD,
for God commanded, and they were created.
v06: God established them forever and ever
and gave a decree that shall not pass away.
</p>

## Praise the LORD from the Earth

<p>
v07: Praise the LORD from the earth,
you sea monsters and all the deeps,
v08: fire and hail, snow and smoke,
storm wind fulfilling God’s word,
v09: mountains and all hills,
fruit trees and all cedars,
v10: wild animals and all livestock,
creeping things and winged birds,
v11: kings of the earth and all peoples,
princes and all judges of the earth,
v12: young men and young women together,
the old with the young.
</p>

## The Name of the LORD Alone Is Exalted

<p>
v13: Let them praise the name of the LORD,
for God’s name alone is exalted;
God’s splendor is over earth and heaven.
v14: God has raised up a horn for the people,
praise for all the faithful ones,
for the children of Israel, a people near to God.
Praise the LORD!
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v07: תַּנִּינִים (tanninim)
“Sea monsters” or great sea creatures. The term retains the ancient scale and imaginative force of creatures associated with the deep.

v14: קֶרֶן (qeren)
“Horn,” a poetic symbol of strength, vitality, and honor.
