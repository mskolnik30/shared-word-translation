v01: לַמְנַצֵּחַ עַל-מָחֲלַת מַשְׂכִּיל לְדָוִד

<p>
v01: For the director; upon Mahalath. A Maskil of David.
</p>
<p>
אֱלוֹהִים הִשְׁקִיף מִשָּׁמַיִם עַל־בְּנֵי אָדָם לִרְאוֹת הֲיֵשׁ מַשְׂכִּיל דֹּרֵשׁ אֱלוֹהִים
</p>
<p>
v03: God looked down from heaven upon the children of humanity
to see if there is anyone who acts wisely,
any who seek after God.
</p>
<p>
כֻּלּוֹ סָג יַחְדָּו נֶאֱלָחוּ אֵין עֹשֵׂה טוֹב אֵין גַּם אֶחָד
</p>
<p>
v04: All have turned aside; together they have become corrupt.
There is none who does good,
not even one.
</p>
<p>
הֲלֹא יָדְעוּ פֹּעֲלֵי אָוֶן אֹכְלֵי עַמִּי אָכְלוּ לֶחֶם אֱלוֹהִים לֹא קָרָאוּ
</p>
<p>
v05: Do they not know, all evildoers,
who eat up my people as they eat bread,
and do not call upon God?
</p>
<p>
שָׁם פָּחֲדוּ פַחֲד לֹא הָיָה כִּי אֱלוֹהִים פִּזַּר עַצְמוֹת חֹנֵיךְ הֱבִשֹׁתָה כִּי אֱלוֹהִים מְאָסָם
</p>
<p>
v06: There they were in great fear where no fear was.
For God scattered the bones of those who encamped against you;
you put them to shame, for God rejected them.
</p>
<p>
מִי יִתֵּן מִצִּיּוֹן יְשׁוּעֹת יִשְׂרָאֵל בְּשׁוּב אֱלוֹהִים שְׁבֻת עַמּוֹ יָגֵל יַעֲקֹב יָשִׂמַּח יִשְׂרָאֵל
</p>
<p>
v07: Oh, that salvation for Israel would come out of Zion!
When God restores the fortunes of his people,
Jacob will rejoice; Israel will be glad.
</p>

## Notes

v1: "Mahalath" — The exact meaning and significance are uncertain, possibly a musical term or tune.

v4: "not even one" — Emphasizes the universal scope of human corruption in the psalmist's view.

v6: "no fear was" — The lack of external cause for fear highlights an internal or divine source of terror.

v7: "will be glad" — Expressions of communal joy and restoration often occur in the Psalms, anticipating divine deliverance.

## Vocabulary

v3: אֱלֹהִים (e.lo.him) — A plural form often used with singular verbs, indicating majesty, emphasizes divine omnipresence and involvement.
