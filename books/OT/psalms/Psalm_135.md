## Praise the Name of the LORD

<p>
v01: Praise the LORD!
Praise the name of the LORD;
give praise, servants of the LORD,
v02: you who stand in the house of the LORD,
in the courts of the house of our God.
v03: Praise the LORD, for the LORD is good;
make music to God’s name, for it is pleasant.
v04: For the LORD has chosen Jacob for himself,
Israel as God’s treasured possession.
</p>

## The LORD Does Whatever God Pleases

<p>
v05: For I know that the LORD is great,
and our Lord is above all gods.
v06: Whatever the LORD pleases, God does,
in the heavens and on the earth,
in the seas and all the depths.
v07: God raises clouds from the ends of the earth,
makes lightning for the rain,
and brings out the wind from storehouses.
</p>

## God Gave Their Land as a Heritage

<p>
v08: God struck the firstborn of Egypt,
from human being to animal.
v09: God sent signs and wonders into your midst, O Egypt,
against Pharaoh and all his servants.
v10: God struck many nations
and killed mighty kings:
v11: Sihon king of the Amorites,
Og king of Bashan,
and all the kingdoms of Canaan.
v12: God gave their land as a heritage,
a heritage to Israel, God’s people.
v13: O LORD, your name is forever;
O LORD, your remembrance is from generation to generation.
v14: For the LORD will vindicate the people
and have compassion on the servants.
</p>

## The Idols of the Nations

<p>
v15: The idols of the nations are silver and gold,
the work of human hands.
v16: They have mouths but do not speak;
they have eyes but do not see.
v17: They have ears but do not hear;
there is no breath in their mouths.
v18: Those who make them become like them,
as do all who trust in them.
</p>

## Bless the LORD from Zion

<p>
v19: House of Israel, bless the LORD!
House of Aaron, bless the LORD!
v20: House of Levi, bless the LORD!
You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD!
v21: Blessed be the LORD from Zion,
the One who dwells in Jerusalem.
Praise the LORD!
</p>
## Notes
v14: The Hebrew verb can mean “judge” and, in this context, “vindicate” or defend the cause of God’s people. The following line of compassion clarifies the direction of the judgment.

##Vocabulary

v04: סְגֻלָּה (segullah)
“Treasured possession,” a specially valued possession belonging to a sovereign; covenant language for Israel’s belonging to the LORD.

v14: נָחַם (nacham)
Here, “have compassion” or “relent,” describing God’s responsive mercy toward the servants.

v14: נָחַם (nacham)
“To relent; have compassion.” The verb can describe a change in announced action or a movement of compassion.
