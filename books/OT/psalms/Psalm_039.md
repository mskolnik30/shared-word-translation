<p>
For the director, for Jeduthun. A psalm of David.
</p>

<p>
v01: I said, “I will keep watch over my ways,
so that I do not sin with my tongue;
I will keep a muzzle over my mouth
while the wicked are before me.”
v02: I was mute in silence;
I kept silent, even from good,
and my pain was stirred up.
v03: My heart grew hot within me;
as I meditated, fire burned.
Then I spoke with my tongue:
v04: “Make known to me, LORD, my end
and the measure of my days—what it is;
let me know how fleeting I am.
v05: Look, you have made my days handbreadths,
and my lifetime is as nothing before you.
Surely every human being, standing firm, is only breath. Selah
v06: Surely a person walks about as an image;
surely they bustle about for breath.
One heaps up,
but does not know who will gather it.
v07: And now, Lord, what do I wait for?
My hope—it is in you.
v08: Deliver me from all my transgressions;
do not make me the reproach of a fool.
v09: I am mute; I do not open my mouth,
for you have done it.
v10: Remove your blow from me;
I am spent by the assault of your hand.
v11: With rebukes for iniquity you discipline a person,
and like a moth you melt away what is precious to them.
Surely every human being is breath. Selah
v12: Hear my prayer, LORD,
and give ear to my cry;
do not be silent at my tears.
For I am a sojourner with you,
a resident alien, like all my ancestors.
v13: Look away from me, that I may brighten,
before I go and am no more.”
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: מַחְסוֹם (maḥsôm)
“Muzzle” is a concrete restraint placed over the mouth. The word intensifies the speaker’s determination not merely to speak carefully but to prevent speech altogether.

v05: הֶבֶל (hevel)
“Breath” names what is vapor-like, fleeting, and unsubstantial. Its repeated use in this psalm frames human life and striving as radically transient.

v06: צֶלֶם (tselem)
“Image” can evoke a likeness, form, or shadowy appearance. Here it describes a person’s passing movement through life without defining precisely what that appearance can denote.

v12: גֵּר (ger)
“Sojourner” identifies one living away from an inherited home and dependent on the protection of others. In this prayer, the term expresses the speaker’s unsettled life before God.
