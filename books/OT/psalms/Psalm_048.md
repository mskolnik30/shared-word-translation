<p>
A song. A psalm of the sons of Korah.
</p>

## The City of the Great King

<p>
v01: Great is the LORD and greatly to be praised
in the city of our God, on the holy mountain.
v02: Beautiful in height, the joy of all the earth,
is Mount Zion, in the far north,
the city of the great King.
v03: God is in her citadels;
God has made known a stronghold.
v04: For look, the kings assembled;
they passed by together.
v05: They saw it and were astonished;
they panicked; they fled.
v06: Trembling seized them there,
anguish like a woman in labor.
v07: With an east wind
you shatter the ships of Tarshish.
v08: As we have heard, so we have seen
in the city of the LORD of Hosts,
in the city of our God.
God will establish her forever.
Selah.
</p>

## Steadfast Love within the Temple

<p>
v09: We have pondered your steadfast love, O God,
within your temple.
v10: As your name, O God,
so your praise reaches to the ends of the earth.
Your right hand is filled with righteousness.
v11: Let Mount Zion rejoice;
let the daughters of Judah be glad
because of your judgments.
</p>

## Tell the Next Generation

<p>
v12: Walk around Zion; go all around her;
count her towers.
v13: Set your hearts on her ramparts;
pass through her citadels,
so that you may tell the next generation:
v14: This is God, our God forever and ever;
God will guide us until death.
</p>
## Notes

v14: The final Hebrew expression can be read “until death.” Its form is unusual, and ancient and modern witnesses have understood it in more than one way; the main text preserves the Masoretic wording without expanding it.

## Vocabulary

v08: סֶלָה (selah)
“A liturgical or musical term of uncertain meaning. It is retained rather than interpreted.”

v14: עַל־מוּת (ʿal-muth)
“Literally, “upon/to death.” The phrase is textually and interpretively difficult.”
