<p>
v01: For the leader. A psalm of David.
</p>
<p>
v01: Yahweh, in your strength the king rejoices,  
and in your salvation how greatly he exults!  
v02: You have granted him the desire of his heart  
and have not withheld the request of his lips. Selah
</p>
<p>
v03: For you have met him with rich blessings;  
you placed on his head a crown of fine gold.  
v04: He asked life of you, and you gave it to him—  
length of days forever and ever.  
v05: His glory is great through your salvation;  
splendor and majesty you bestow upon him.  
</p>
<p>
v06: For you make him most blessed forever;  
you gladden him with the joy of your presence.  
v07: For the king trusts in Yahweh;  
through the steadfast love of the Most High he shall not be moved.
</p>
<p>
v08: Your hand will find out all your enemies;  
your right hand will find out those who hate you.  
v09: You will make them like a fiery furnace  
    when you appear.  
Yahweh will swallow them up in his wrath,  
    and fire will consume them.  
v10: You will destroy their offspring from the earth,  
    and their descendants from among humankind.  
v11: Though they plot evil against you,  
    though they devise mischief,  
    they will not succeed.  
</p>
<p>
v12: For you will put them to flight,  
    aim your arrows at their faces.  
v13: Be exalted, Yahweh, in your strength!  
    We will sing and praise your power.
</p>

## Notes

1. **v1: Yahweh, in your strength the king rejoices** — This verse sets the tone of royal thanksgiving and celebration of divine support and protection. The shift from second-person address to third-person acknowledgment of God's action emphasizes personal relationship and covenant faithfulness.
   
2. **v5: crown of fine gold** — The image of a royal crown signifies honor and divine favor, celebrating God's provision and establishment of leadership.

3. **v7: steadfast love of the Most High** — This expression is a theme in Psalms that highlights God's covenantal faithfulness and assurance that strengthens the king's trust.

4. **v9: A fiery furnace** — The imagery of judgment invokes earthly experience with divine intervention, highlighting the seriousness of opposing God's chosen king.

5. **v11: They will not succeed** — Despite human intentions, divine sovereignty governs the outcome, ensuring God's plans prevail.

## Vocabulary

1. **v1: יְהֹוָה (ye.ho.vah)** — Refers to the divine name of God, invoking God’s covenantal presence and authority.

2. **v5: עֹז (oz)** — Strength, might, or power, often associated with God's protective and salvific acts for the king.

3. **v7: חֶסֶד (che.sed)** — Steadfast love, a covenant term implying loyalty, kindness, and faithful love, integral to the relationship with God.

4. **v9: אַף (aph)** — Wrath or anger, describing God's response to evil and opposition, reflecting justice and righteousness.
