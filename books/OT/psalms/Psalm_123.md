## Our Eyes Look to the LORD

<p>
v01: To you I lift up my eyes,
O you who are enthroned in the heavens.
v02: Look, as the eyes of servants
look to the hand of their master,
as the eyes of a female servant
look to the hand of her mistress,
so our eyes look to the LORD our God
until God is gracious to us.
v03: Be gracious to us, O LORD, be gracious to us,
for we have had more than enough contempt.
v04: Our soul has had more than enough
of the scorn of those who are at ease,
of the contempt of the proud.
</p>

## Notes

## Vocabulary

v03: חָנַן (chanan)
“Be gracious,” to show favor freely toward one who is dependent or in need.
