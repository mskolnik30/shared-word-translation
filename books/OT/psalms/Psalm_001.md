## The Way of the Righteous and the Wicked

<p>
v01: Blessed is the man

who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked,

nor stand in the path of sinners,

nor sit in the seat of scoffers;

v02: but his delight is in the law of the LORD,

and on his law he meditates day and night.
</p>

<p>
v03: He is like a tree

planted by streams of water,

which yields its fruit in its season,

and its leaf does not wither—

in all that he does, he prospers.
</p>

<p>
v04: Not so the wicked!

They are like chaff

that the wind drives away.

v05: Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment,

nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous;

v06: for the LORD knows the way of the righteous,

but the way of the wicked will perish.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: אַשְׁרֵי (ʾašrê)
“Blessed.” Flourishing, well-ordered life.

v01: רָשָׁע (rāšāʿ)
“Wicked.” One opposed to covenantal order.

v01: לֵץ (lēṣ)
“Scoffer.” One who mocks and resists correction.

v02: תּוֹרָה (tôrāh)
“Law/instruction.” Guiding teaching.
