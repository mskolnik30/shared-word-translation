<p>
For the leader. A psalm. Of David. A song.
</p>
<p>
v01: Silence and praise await you, God, in Zion;
  and to you vows shall be fulfilled.
</p>
<p>
v02: O you who hear prayer,
  to you all flesh will come.
</p>
<p>
v03: Words of iniquities prevail against me;
  our transgressions—you atone for them.
</p>
<p>
v04: Happy are those whom you choose and bring near,
  to dwell in your courts!
  We shall be satisfied with the goodness of your house,
  the holiness of your temple.
</p>
<p>
v05: With awe-inspiring deeds of righteousness you answer us,
  O God of our salvation,
  the hope of all the ends of the earth
  and of the distant seas.
</p>
<p>
v06: You set the mountains in place by your strength,
  being girded with might;
</p>
<p>
v07: Who calms the roaring of the seas,
  the roaring of their waves,
  and the tumult of the peoples.
</p>
<p>
v08: Those who dwell at the farthest ends are in awe of your signs;
  you make the dawn and the sunset shout for joy.
</p>
<p>
v09: You visit the earth and water it,
  you greatly enrich it;
  the river of God is full of water;
  you prepare their grain,
  for so you provide for it.
</p>
<p>
v10: You drench its furrows, smooth its ridges,
  you soften it with showers, and bless its growth.
</p>
<p>
v11: You crown the year with your bounty;
  your carts overflow with abundance.
</p>
<p>
v12: The pastures of the wilderness overflow,
  and the hills gird themselves with joy;
</p>
<p>
v13: The meadows clothe themselves with flocks,
  the valleys deck themselves with grain;
  they shout and sing together for joy.
</p>
## Notes
v01: "Silence and praise await you" — The dual notion of silence and praise in one phrase suggests a reverent anticipation for God's presence.
v05: "O God of our salvation" — This title emphasizes God's saving power and his protection over the people.
v09: "The river of God" — This metaphor conveys God's abundant provision and care for the earth.
v11: "You crown the year with your bounty" — Indicating God's provision and blessing throughout the agricultural year.

## Vocabulary

v04: עָוֺנֹת (a.van) — Translated as "iniquities", it denotes acts or conditions contrary to God's order, which here rise up against the psalmist.

v07: כּוּן (kun) — Rendered as "set", indicates God's active establishment of the mountains through his strength.

v09: מוֹצָאֵי (mo.tsa) — Refers to "dawn" and "sunset", marking the daily renewal and God's continuous order. 

v12: עָטַר (a.tar) — "Crown", used metaphorically here to describe God adorning or enriching the year.
