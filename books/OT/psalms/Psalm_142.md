<p>
A maskil of David, when he was in the cave. A prayer.
</p>

## I Pour Out My Complaint before the LORD

<p>
v01: With my voice I cry to the LORD;
with my voice I plead to the LORD for mercy.
v02: I pour out my complaint before God;
I tell my trouble before God.
v03: When my spirit grows faint within me,
you know my path.
On the way where I walk
they have hidden a trap for me.
v04: Look to my right and see:
there is no one who recognizes me.
Refuge has vanished from me;
no one cares for my life.
</p>

## You Are My Refuge

<p>
v05: I cry to you, O LORD;
I say, “You are my refuge,
my portion in the land of the living.”
v06: Attend to my cry,
for I have been brought very low.
Deliver me from my persecutors,
for they are too strong for me.
v07: Bring my life out of prison,
so that I may give thanks to your name.
The righteous will gather around me
because you deal bountifully with me.
</p>

## Notes

Superscription: “Maskil” is a literary or musical designation of uncertain meaning.

## Vocabulary

v05: חֵלֶק (cheleq)
“Portion,” one’s allotted share or sustaining possession. God is named as the speaker’s true portion while still “in the land of the living.”
