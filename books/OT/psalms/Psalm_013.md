<p>
For the director. A psalm of David.
</p>

## Asking for Relief

<p>
v01: How long, O LORD?
Will you forget me forever?
How long will you hide your face from me?

v02: How long must I carry counsel in my soul,
sorrow in my heart day after day?
How long shall my enemy be lifted up over me?
</p>

## Plea for Answer and Help

<p>
v03: Look upon me; answer me, O LORD my God.
Give light to my eyes,
so that I do not sleep the sleep of death,

v04: so that my enemy will not say, “I have prevailed,”
and my foes do not rejoice because I am shaken.
</p>

## Trust and Praise During Sorrow

<p>
v05: But I have trusted in your steadfast love;
my heart shall rejoice in your saving help.

v06: I will sing to the LORD,
because the LORD has dealt bountifully with me.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: פָּנִים (panim)
“Face” or “presence.” In the expression “hide your face,” the term communicates the felt withdrawal of divine attention or presence.

v02: יָגוֹן (yagon)
“Sorrow” or “grief.” Deep inward distress, here located in the heart.

v05: חֶסֶד (ḥesed)
“Steadfast love.” Faithful and enduring love expressed within relationship and covenant.

v05: יְשׁוּעָה (yeshuah)
“Saving help,” “deliverance,” or “salvation.” Rescue or deliverance brought by God.
