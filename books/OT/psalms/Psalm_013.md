## Asking for Relief, Now
v01 How long, O LORD?
v02 Will you forget me forever?
v03 How long will you hide your face from me?
v04 How long must I carry counsel in my soul,
v05 sorrow in my heart day after day?
v06 How long shall my enemy be lifted up over me?

## Plea for Answer and Help
v07 Look upon me; answer me, O LORD my God.
v08 Give light to my eyes,
v09 so that I do not sleep the sleep of death,
v10 so that my enemy will not say, “I have prevailed,”
v11 so that my foes do not rejoice because I am shaken.

## Trust and Praise During Sorrow
v12 But I have trusted in your steadfast love;
v13 my heart shall rejoice in your saving help.
v14 I will sing to the LORD,
v15 because the LORD has dealt bountifully with me.
