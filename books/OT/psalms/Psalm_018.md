## I Love You, O LORD
<p>
v01: I love you, O LORD, my strength.
v02: The LORD is my rock and my fortress and my deliverer,
my God, my rock, in whom I take refuge,
my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold.
</p>

<p>
v03: I call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised,
and I am saved from my enemies.
</p>

## Distress and Deliverance
<p>
v04: The cords of death encompassed me;
the torrents of destruction assailed me;
v05: the cords of Sheol entangled me;
the snares of death confronted me.
</p>

<p>
v06: In my distress I called upon the LORD;
to my God I cried for help.
From his temple he heard my voice,
and my cry to him reached his ears.
</p>

## The LORD’s Powerful Intervention
<p>
v07: Then the earth reeled and rocked;
the foundations also of the mountains trembled
and quaked, because he was angry.
v08: Smoke went up from his nostrils,
and devouring fire from his mouth;
glowing coals flamed forth from him.
</p>

<p>
v09: He bowed the heavens and came down;
thick darkness was under his feet.
v10: He rode on a cherub and flew;
he came swiftly on the wings of the wind.
</p>

<p>
v11: He made darkness his covering, his canopy around him,
thick clouds dark with water.
v12: Out of the brightness before him
hailstones and coals of fire broke through his clouds.
</p>

<p>
v13: The LORD also thundered in the heavens,
and the Most High uttered his voice,
hailstones and coals of fire.
v14: And he sent out his arrows and scattered them;
he flashed forth lightnings and routed them.
</p>

<p>
v15: Then the channels of the sea were seen,
and the foundations of the world were laid bare
at your rebuke, O LORD,
at the blast of the breath of your nostrils.
</p>

## Rescue and Restoration
<p>
v16: He sent from on high, he took me;
he drew me out of many waters.
v17: He rescued me from my strong enemy
and from those who hated me,
for they were too mighty for me.
</p>

<p>
v18: They confronted me in the day of my calamity,
but the LORD was my support.
v19: He brought me out into a broad place;
he rescued me, because he delighted in me.
</p>

## Righteousness and Reward
<p>
v20: The LORD dealt with me according to my righteousness;
according to the cleanness of my hands he rewarded me.
v21: For I have kept the ways of the LORD,
and have not wickedly departed from my God.
</p>

<p>
v22: For all his judgments were before me,
and his statutes I did not put away from me.
v23: I was blameless before him,
and I kept myself from my guilt.
</p>

<p>
v24: So the LORD has rewarded me according to my righteousness,
according to the cleanness of my hands in his sight.
</p>

## God’s Character
<p>
v25: With the merciful you show yourself merciful;
with the blameless man you show yourself blameless;
v26: with the pure you show yourself pure;
and with the crooked you make yourself seem tortuous.
</p>

<p>
v27: For you save a humble people,
but the haughty eyes you bring down.
v28: For it is you who light my lamp;
the LORD my God lightens my darkness.
</p>

## Strength for Battle
<p>
v29: For by you I can run against a troop,
and by my God I can leap over a wall.
v30: This God—his way is perfect;
the word of the LORD proves true;
he is a shield for all those who take refuge in him.
</p>

<p>
v31: For who is God, but the LORD?
And who is a rock, except our God?
v32: the God who equipped me with strength
and made my way blameless.
</p>

<p>
v33: He made my feet like the feet of a deer
and set me secure on the heights.
v34: He trains my hands for war,
so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze.
</p>

<p>
v35: You have given me the shield of your salvation,
and your right hand supported me,
and your gentleness made me great.
v36: You gave a wide place for my steps under me,
and my feet did not slip.
</p>

<p>
v37: I pursued my enemies and overtook them,
and did not turn back till they were consumed.
v38: I thrust them through, so that they were not able to rise;
they fell under my feet.
</p>

<p>
v39: For you equipped me with strength for the battle;
you made those who rise against me sink under me.
v40: You made my enemies turn their backs to me,
and those who hated me I destroyed.
</p>

<p>
v41: They cried for help, but there was none to save;
they cried to the LORD, but he did not answer them.
v42: I beat them fine as dust before the wind;
I cast them out like the mire of the streets.
</p>

## Dominion and Praise
<p>
v43: You delivered me from strife with the people;
you made me the head of the nations;
people whom I had not known served me.
v44: As soon as they heard of me they obeyed me;
foreigners came cringing to me.
</p>

<p>
v45: Foreigners lost heart
and came trembling out of their fortresses.
</p>

<p>
v46: The LORD lives, and blessed be my rock,
and exalted be the God of my salvation—
v47: the God who gave me vengeance
and subdued peoples under me,
</p>

<p>
v48: who rescued me from my enemies;
yes, you exalted me above those who rose against me;
you delivered me from the man of violence.
</p>

<p>
v49: For this I will praise you, O LORD, among the nations,
and sing to your name.
v50: Great salvation he brings to his king,
and shows steadfast love to his anointed,
to David and his offspring forever.
</p>

## Notes

v01–03: Opening cluster of metaphors—God as refuge, strength, and deliverer.

v04–06: Distress is overwhelming and total; prayer initiates the turning point.

v07–15: Theophany imagery—cosmic upheaval signals divine intervention.

v16–19: Rescue is personal and deliberate; rooted in divine delight.

v20–24: Righteousness language reflects covenant fidelity, not perfectionism.

v25–26: God’s dealings mirror human orientation.

v27–30: God reverses status—humble lifted, proud brought low.

v31–42: Empowerment for battle—God acts through the psalmist.

v43–45: Expansion beyond survival to dominion.

v46–50: Concluding praise—personal deliverance becomes public testimony.
