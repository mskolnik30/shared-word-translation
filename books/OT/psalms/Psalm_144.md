<p>
Of David.
</p>

## Blessed Be the LORD, My Rock

<p>
v01: Blessed be the LORD, my rock,
who trains my hands for battle
and my fingers for war,
v02: my steadfast love and my fortress,
my stronghold and my deliverer,
my shield and the One in whom I take refuge,
who subdues peoples under me.
v03: O LORD, what is a human being, that you know them,
a mortal, that you think of them?
v04: A human being is like a breath;
one’s days are like a passing shadow.
</p>

## Bow Your Heavens and Come Down

<p>
v05: O LORD, bow your heavens and come down;
touch the mountains so that they smoke.
v06: Flash lightning and scatter them;
send out your arrows and rout them.
v07: Stretch out your hand from on high;
rescue me and deliver me from mighty waters,
from the hand of foreigners,
v08: whose mouths speak falsehood
and whose right hand is a right hand of lies.
</p>

## I Will Sing a New Song to You

<p>
v09: O God, I will sing a new song to you;
on a ten-stringed harp I will make music to you,
v10: the One who gives victory to kings,
who rescues David, the servant, from the cruel sword.
v11: Rescue me and deliver me from the hand of foreigners,
whose mouths speak falsehood
and whose right hand is a right hand of lies.
</p>

## Happy Are the People Whose God Is the LORD

<p>
v12: May our sons in their youth
be like plants grown to full stature,
and our daughters like corner pillars,
carved for the structure of a palace.
v13: May our storehouses be full,
providing every kind of produce.
May our sheep multiply by thousands,
by tens of thousands in our fields.
v14: May our cattle bear without mishap,
with no breach and no going out,
and no cry of distress in our streets.
v15: Happy are the people for whom it is so;
happy are the people whose God is the LORD.
</p>
##Notes

v02: The final verb can be read “subdues my people under me” or, with ancient witnesses and a different vocalization, “subdues peoples under me.” The plural “peoples” is used here while the textual question is acknowledged.

v14: The Hebrew livestock line is compressed and uncertain. “Bear without mishap” represents flourishing cattle without forcing the several possible senses of the underlying participle and following phrases.

##Vocabulary

v04: הֶבֶל (hevel)
“Breath,” “vapor,” or what is fleeting.

v10: תְּשׁוּעָה (teshuʿah)
“Victory,” “deliverance,” or saving help, especially rescue that changes the outcome of danger.
