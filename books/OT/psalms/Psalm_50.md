v01: A psalm of Asaph. God, Elohim, the LORD, has spoken and called the earth from the rising of the sun to its setting.

v02: From Zion, perfect in beauty, God has shone forth.

v03: Our God comes and does not keep silent; a fire devours before him, and around him it is very tempestuous.

v04: He calls to the heavens above and to the earth, that he may judge his people:

v05: "Gather to me my faithful ones, those who have made a covenant with me by sacrifice."

v06: The heavens declare his righteousness, for God himself is judge. Selah

<p>

v07: "Hear, O my people, and I will speak; O Israel, I will testify against you. I am God, your God.

v08: Not concerning your sacrifices will I rebuke you, nor your burnt offerings, which are ever before me.

v09: I will not take a bull from your house or goats from your folds.

v10: For every animal of the forest is mine, and the cattle on a thousand hills.

v11: I know every bird in the mountains, and everything that moves in the field is mine.

v12: If I were hungry, I would not tell you, for the world and its fullness are mine.

v13: Do I eat the flesh of bulls or drink the blood of goats?

v14: Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving, and fulfill your vows to the Most High.

v15: And call upon me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you, and you will honor me."

</p>

<p>

v16: But to the wicked God says, "What right have you to recite my statutes or take my covenant on your lips,

v17: when you hate discipline and cast my words behind you?

v18: If you see a thief, you are pleased with him, and you keep company with adulterers.

v19: You open your mouth for evil, and your tongue frames deceit.

v20: You sit and speak against your brother; you slander your own mother's son.

v21: These things you have done, and I kept silent; you thought I was one like yourself. But now I reprove you and lay the charge before you.

v22: Mark this, you who forget God, lest I tear you apart, and there be none to deliver!

v23: Whoever offers a sacrifice of thanksgiving honors me, and to the one who orders his way rightly, I will show the salvation of God."

</p>

## Notes

v1: "Elohim" (אֱלֹהִים, e.lo.him) — This name for God emphasizes his majesty and plurality of attributes. The names used in this verse (God, Elohim, the LORD) reflect the psalm's theological depth, underscoring God's transcendence and relational covenant.

v10: "Cattle on a thousand hills" — This poetic phrase communicates God's unfathomable ownership of all creation. It serves to remind worshipers that God’s need for human offerings is not due to solitude necessity or divine hunger but rather commands true worship and devotion from his people.

v14: "Thanksgiving" — In the context of Israelite worship, thanksgiving sacrifices express gratitude and acknowledgment of God's provision and faithfulness. It stresses the importance of gratitude as integral worship rather than merely ritualistic sacrifices.

## Vocabulary

v1: מִזְמוֹר (miz.mor) — Translated here as "psalm," this term refers to a song or hymn, typically one meant for public worship or liturgical use, especially in the context of the Hebrew Bible's Psalter.

v5: חָסִיד (cha.sid) — Often rendered as "faithful" or "saint," this term describes those bound by special covenant fidelity to God, illustrating a close, reciprocal relationship defined by steadfast love and truth.
