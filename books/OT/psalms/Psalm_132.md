<p>
A song of ascents.
</p>

## Remember David and All His Affliction

<p>
v01: O LORD, remember David
and all his affliction,
v02: how he swore to the LORD
and vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob:
v03: “I will not enter the tent of my house
or go up onto the couch of my bed;
v04: I will not give sleep to my eyes
or slumber to my eyelids
v05: until I find a place for the LORD,
a dwelling place for the Mighty One of Jacob.”
v06: Look, we heard of it in Ephrathah;
we found it in the fields of Jaar.
v07: “Let us go to God’s dwelling place;
let us bow down at God’s footstool.”
v08: Rise, O LORD, to your resting place,
you and the ark of your strength.
v09: Let your priests be clothed with righteousness,
and let your faithful ones shout for joy.
v10: For the sake of David your servant,
do not turn away the face of your anointed.
</p>

## The LORD Has Chosen Zion

<p>
v11: The LORD swore to David in truth
and will not turn from it:
“One of the fruit of your body
I will set upon your throne.
v12: If your children keep my covenant
and my testimony that I teach them,
their children also, forever,
shall sit upon your throne.”
v13: For the LORD has chosen Zion;
God has desired it as a dwelling place:
v14: “This is my resting place forever;
here I will dwell, for I have desired it.
v15: I will abundantly bless her provisions;
I will satisfy her poor with bread.
v16: Her priests I will clothe with salvation,
and her faithful ones will shout aloud for joy.
v17: There I will cause a horn to sprout for David;
I have prepared a lamp for my anointed.
v18: His enemies I will clothe with shame,
but upon him his crown will flourish.”
</p>
## Notes
v06: “Ephrathah” and “the fields of Jaar” locate the remembered search for the ark in poetic geography. The exact historical referents are debated, so the place-names are preserved rather than harmonized with another narrative.
v17: “Horn” and “lamp” are royal images: strength that rises and a dynastic light preserved. The metaphors are allowed to stand together without explanation in the main text.

##Vocabulary

v08: אֲרוֹן (ʾaron)
“Ark,” the sacred chest associated with the covenant and with the LORD’s enthroned presence among Israel.

v10: מָשִׁיחַ (mashiach)
“Anointed one,” here the Davidic king for whom the community asks divine regard.
