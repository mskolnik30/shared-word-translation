<p>
v01: For the director, on the lilies, of David.  
v02: Save me, God, for the waters have come up to my neck.  
v03: I have sunk in deep mire, where there is no foothold; I have come into deep waters, and the flood sweeps over me.  
v04: I am weary with my crying; my throat is parched. My eyes fail, with hoping for my God.  
</p>
<p>
v05: More than the hairs of my head are those who hate me without cause; mighty are those who would destroy me, those who attack me with lies. What I did not steal must I now restore?  
v06: God, you know my folly, and my guilt is not hidden from you.  
v07: Do not let those who hope in you be shamed because of me, O Lord God of hosts; do not let those who seek you be dishonored because of me, O God of Israel.  
v08: For it is for your sake that I have borne reproach, that shame has covered my face.  
</p>
<p>
v09: I have become a stranger to my brothers, a foreigner to my mother's sons.  
v10: For the zeal for your house has consumed me, and the reproaches of those who reproach you have fallen on me.  
v11: When I wept and humbled my soul with fasting, it became my reproach.  
v12: When I made sackcloth my clothing, I became a byword to them.  
</p>
<p>
v13: Those who sit at the gate talk about me, and I am the song of drunkards.  
v14: But as for me, my prayer is to you, O LORD, at an acceptable time, O God, in the abundance of your steadfast love answer me in your saving faithfulness.  
v15: Rescue me from the mire; do not let me sink; deliver me from my foes and from the depths of the waters.  
</p>
<p>
v16: Do not let the flood sweep over me, or the deep swallow me up, or the pit close its mouth over me.  
v17: Answer me, O LORD, for your steadfast love is good; according to your abundant mercy, turn to me.  
v18: Do not hide your face from your servant; for I am in trouble, answer me quickly.  
v19: Draw near to my soul, redeem it; ransom me because of my enemies.  
</p>
<p>
v20: You know my reproach, my shame, and my dishonor; my adversaries are all known to you.  
v21: Reproach has broken my heart, and I am in despair. I looked for pity, but there was none, and for comforters, but I found none.  
v22: They gave me poison for food, and for my thirst, they gave me vinegar to drink.  
</p>
<p>
v23: Let their table before them become a snare; when they are at peace, let it become a trap.  
v24: Let their eyes be darkened, so they cannot see, and make their loins tremble continually.  
v25: Pour out your anger upon them, and let your burning wrath overtake them.  
</p>
<p>
v26: May their camp be desolate; let none dwell in their tents.  
v27: For they persecute those you have struck down, and they recount the pain of those you have wounded.  
v28: Add iniquity to their iniquity, and let them not come into your righteousness.  
v29: Let them be blotted out of the book of the living; let them not be enrolled among the righteous.  
</p>
<p>
v30: But I am afflicted and in pain; let your salvation, O God, set me on high.  
v31: I will praise the name of God with a song; I will magnify him with thanksgiving.  
v32: This will please the LORD more than an ox or a bull with horns and hoofs.  
v33: The humble shall see this and be glad; you who seek God, let your hearts revive.  
</p>
<p>
v34: For the LORD hears the needy and does not despise his own people who are prisoners.  
v35: Let heaven and earth praise him, the seas and everything that moves in them.  
v36: For God will save Zion and build the cities of Judah; they shall dwell there and possess it.  
v37: The children of his servants shall inherit it, and those who love his name shall dwell in it.  
</p>
## Notes

v1: "on the lilies" — Likely a musical or literary term indicating the theme or tune of the psalm.  

v10: "zeal for your house has consumed me" — This phrase emphasizes the intensity of devotion to God's temple, which becomes a source of personal suffering.  

v22: "They gave me poison for food" — The metaphorical language here expresses profound betrayal and abandonment.  

v23–29: These verses contain imprecations or curses against the psalmist's enemies, reflecting a cry for justice. They may be uncomfortable but are an honest expression of human frustration and pain in prayer.  

## Vocabulary

v10: קִנְאָה (qin.ah) — "zeal" in this context refers to a fervent devotion to God's house, which results in personal suffering.  

v15: נָצַל (na.tsal) — "rescue" signifies a deliverance from dire straits, emphasizing dependence on God's intervention.  

v27: מַכְאוֹב (makh.ov) — "pain" describes the suffering or wounds experienced, specifically pointing to divine afflictions.  

v29: מָחָה (ma.chah) — "blotted out" indicates removal or obliteration, often associated with memory or record. Here, it applies to the book of life, signaling exclusion from blessing or covenant.
