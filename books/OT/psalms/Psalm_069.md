## Save Me, O God

<p>

v01: Save me, O God!

For the waters have come up to my neck.

v02: I sink in deep mire,

where there is no foothold;

I have come into deep waters,

and the flood sweeps over me.

</p>

<p>

v03: I am weary with my crying out;

my throat is parched;

my eyes grow dim

with waiting for my God.

</p>

## Reproach and Isolation

<p>

v04: More in number than the hairs of my head

are those who hate me without cause;

mighty are those who would destroy me,

great are my enemies without cause.

I am forced to restore

what I did not steal.


<p>

v05: O God, you know my folly;

the wrongs I have done are not hidden from you.

v06: Let not those who hope in you be put to shame through me,

O Lord GOD of hosts;

let not those who seek you be brought to dishonor through me,

O God of Israel.

</p>

<p>

v07: For it is for your sake that I have borne reproach,

that shame has covered my face.

v08: I have become a stranger to my brothers,

an alien to my mother’s sons.

</p>

<p>

v09: For zeal for your house has consumed me,

and the reproaches of those who reproach you have fallen on me.

v10: When I wept and humbled my soul with fasting,

it became my reproach.

</p>

<p>

v11: When I made sackcloth my clothing,

I became a byword to them.

v12: I am the talk of those who sit in the gate,

and the drunkards make songs about me.

</p>

## Plea for Deliverance

<p>

v13: But as for me, my prayer is to you, O LORD.

At an acceptable time, O God,

in the abundance of your steadfast love answer me

in your saving faithfulness.

</p>

<p>

v14: Deliver me from sinking in the mire;

let me be delivered from my enemies

and from the deep waters.

v15: Let not the flood sweep over me,

or the deep swallow me up,

or the pit close its mouth over me.

</p>

<p>

v16: Answer me, O LORD, for your steadfast love is good;

according to your abundant mercy, turn to me.

v17: Hide not your face from your servant,

for I am in distress; make haste to answer me.

</p>

<p>

v18: Draw near to my soul, redeem me;

ransom me because of my enemies!

</p>

## Deep Reproach

<p>

v19: You know my reproach,

and my shame and my dishonor;

my foes are all known to you.

v20: Reproaches have broken my heart,

so that I am in despair.

I looked for pity, but there was none,

and for comforters, but I found none.

</p>

<p>

v21: They gave me poison for food,

and for my thirst they gave me sour wine to drink.

</p>

## Imprecation Against Enemies

<p>

v22: Let their table become a snare before them,

and when they are at peace, let it become a trap.

v23: Let their eyes be darkened, so that they cannot see,

and make their loins tremble continually.

</p>

<p>

v24: Pour out your indignation upon them,

and let your burning anger overtake them.

v25: May their camp be a desolation;

let no one dwell in their tents.

</p>

<p>

v26: For they persecute him whom you have struck down,

and they recount the pain of those you have wounded.

</p>

<p>

v27: Add to them punishment upon punishment;

may they have no acquittal from you.

v28: Let them be blotted out of the book of the living;

let them not be enrolled among the righteous.

</p>

## Turning Toward Praise

<p>

v29: But I am afflicted and in pain;

let your salvation, O God, set me on high!

</p>

<p>

v30: I will praise the name of God with a song;

I will magnify him with thanksgiving.

v31: This will please the LORD more than an ox

or a bull with horns and hoofs.

</p>

<p>

v32: When the humble see it they will be glad;

you who seek God, let your hearts revive.

v33: For the LORD hears the needy

and does not despise his own people who are prisoners.

</p>

<p>

v34: Let heaven and earth praise him,

the seas and everything that moves in them.

v35: For God will save Zion

and build up the cities of Judah,

and people shall dwell there and possess it;

</p>

<p>

v36: the offspring of his servants shall inherit it,

and those who love his name shall dwell in it.

</p>

## Notes

v5–6: The psalmist acknowledges personal fault while still appealing for communal protection.

v7–12: Reproach is linked to fidelity to God, not merely personal failure.

v9: Zeal aligns the psalmist with God’s honor, intensifying opposition.

v13–18: The plea is grounded in God’s character (steadfast love, mercy).

v19–21: Isolation reaches its peak—no comforters, only hostility.

v21: The imagery becomes specific and visceral, later echoed in Gospel narratives.

v22–28: Imprecation reflects desire for justice, not neutral resignation.

v29–31: The turning point—praise emerges before circumstances change.

v32–33: Personal deliverance becomes communal encouragement.

v34–36: The psalm expands outward—cosmic praise and restoration of Zion.

## Vocabulary

v05: אִוֶּלֶת (ivvelet)  

“Folly.” Moral failure.

v06: בּוֹשׁ (bosh)  

“To be ashamed.” Experience disgrace.

v08: נָכְרִי (nokhri)  

“Stranger.” Outsider.

v09: קִנְאָה (qin’ah)  

“Zeal.” Consuming passion.

v13: עֵת רָצוֹן (et ratson)  

“Acceptable time.” Favorable moment.

v14: טִיט (tit)  

“Mire.” Mud, instability.

v18: גָּאַל (ga’al)  

“To redeem.” Rescue.

v20: שָׁבַר (shavar)  

“To break.” Crush emotionally.

v21: רֹאשׁ (rosh)  

“Poison.” Bitter substance.

v22: פַּח (pach)  

“Snare.” Trap.

v28: סֵפֶר חַיִּים (sefer chayyim)  

“Book of life.” Record of the living.

v29: שָׂגַב (sagav)  

“To set on high.” Lift to safety.

v30: גָּדַל (gadal)  

“To magnify.” Exalt.

v33: אֶבְיוֹן (evyon)  

“Needy.” Poor and dependent.

v35: יָשַׁע (yasha)  

“To save.” Deliver.
