## God Is King over All the Earth

<p>
v01: Clap your hands, all peoples;
shout to God with a voice of joy.

v02: For the LORD Most High is fearsome,
a great king over all the earth.

v03: He subdued peoples under us,
and nations under our feet.

v04: He chose our inheritance for us,
the pride of Jacob whom he loves.
</p>

<p>
v05: God has gone up with a shout,
the LORD with the sound of a trumpet.

v06: Sing praises to God, sing praises;
sing praises to our king, sing praises.

v07: For God is king of all the earth;
sing praises with understanding.

v08: God reigns over the nations;
God sits on his holy throne.
</p>

<p>
v09: The princes of the peoples gather
as the people of the God of Abraham.

For the shields of the earth belong to God;
he is highly exalted.
</p>

“To clap” or “to strike.” Often associated with public or communal expression.

v02: עֶלְיוֹן (ʿelyon)
“Most High.” A title with a sense of exalted status and supremacy.

v04: נַחֲלָה (naḥalah)
“Inheritance.” Often tied to land, promise, and covenant identity.

v07: מַשְׂכִּיל (maskil)
“With understanding” or “skillfully.” Can refer to insight, instruction, or ordered performance.
