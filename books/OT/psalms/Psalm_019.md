## The Glory of God in Creation and Law

<p>
v01: The heavens declare the glory of God,

and the firmament proclaims the work of his hands.

v02: Day to day pours forth speech,

and night to night reveals knowledge.

v03: There is no speech, nor are there words;

their voice is not heard—

v04: yet their line goes out through all the earth,

and their words to the end of the world.

In them he has set a tent for the sun,

v05: which comes out like a bridegroom from his chamber,

and rejoices like a strong man to run his course.

v06: Its rising is from the end of the heavens,

and its circuit to the other end of them,

and nothing is hidden from its heat.
</p>

<p>
v07: The law of the LORD is perfect, restoring the soul;

the testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple;

v08: the precepts of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart;

the commandment of the LORD is pure, enlightening the eyes;

v09: the fear of the LORD is clean, enduring forever;

the judgments of the LORD are true, righteous altogether.

v10: More to be desired are they than gold, even much fine gold;

sweeter also than honey and drippings of the honeycomb.

v11: Moreover, by them is your servant warned;

in keeping them there is great reward.
</p>

<p>
v12: Who can discern errors?

Declare me innocent from hidden faults.

v13: Keep back your servant also from presumptuous sins;

let them not rule over me.

Then I shall be blameless

and innocent of great transgression.

v14: Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart

be acceptable in your sight,

O LORD, my rock and my redeemer.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: כָּבוֹד (kāḇôḏ)
“Glory.” Weight, significance, honor.

v07: תּוֹרָה (tôrāh)
“Law/instruction.” Guiding teaching.

v07: עֵדוּת (ʿēḏûṯ)
“Testimony.” Witness, attested truth.

v12: שְׁגִיאָה (šĕgîʾāh)
“Error.” Unintentional sin.
