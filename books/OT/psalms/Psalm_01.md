<p>
v01: Blessed is the one  
who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked,  
nor stand in the way of sinners,  
nor sit in the seat of scoffers.
</p>
<p>
v02: Rather, the Lord's instruction is his delight,  
and on his law, he meditates day and night.
</p>
<p>
v03: He is like a tree planted by streams of water,  
which yields its fruit in its season,  
and its leaf does not wither.  
In all he does, he prospers.
</p>
<p>
v04: Not so the wicked!  
They are like chaff that the wind drives away.
</p>
<p>
v05: Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment,  
nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous.
</p>
<p>
v06: For the Lord knows the way of the righteous,  
but the way of the wicked will perish.
</p>

## Notes

v1: "Blessed" — The Hebrew word here (אַשְׁרֵי, esher) conveys a state of happiness or well-being that results from a right relationship with God.

v2: "Instruction" — Translated from the Hebrew "Torah" (תוֹרָה, torah), a term often associated with God's law or instruction and integral to covenantal faith.

v3: "Prospers" — The imagery of a flourishing tree emphasizes stability and fruitful growth, mirroring the life choices aligned with divine guidance.

v6: "Knows" — This signifies a profound, relational awareness by God of the righteous, contrasting the ultimate fate of the wicked.

## Vocabulary

v1: רְשָׁעִים (rasha) — Often translated as "wicked," this term refers to those who actively oppose God's ways in thought and action.

v2: תּוֹרָה (torah) — Commonly rendered as "law" or "instruction," it encompasses the broader teachings and commandments given by God.

v3: צַדִּיקִים (tsaddiq) — Translates to "righteous," highlighting those living in faithful response to God's covenant.
