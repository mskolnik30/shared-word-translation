<p>
A psalm of David.
</p>

## Do Not Enter into Judgment with Your Servant

<p>
v01: O LORD, hear my prayer;
give ear to my pleas for mercy.
In your faithfulness answer me,
in your righteousness.
v02: Do not enter into judgment with your servant,
for no one living is righteous before you.
v03: For the enemy has pursued my life;
he has crushed my life to the ground.
He has made me dwell in darkness
like those long dead.
v04: My spirit grows faint within me;
my heart within me is appalled.
v05: I remember the days of old;
I meditate on all you have done;
I ponder the work of your hands.
v06: I spread out my hands to you;
my soul thirsts for you like a parched land.
Selah.
</p>

## Teach Me the Way I Should Go

<p>
v07: Answer me quickly, O LORD;
my spirit fails.
Do not hide your face from me,
or I will be like those who go down to the Pit.
v08: Let me hear of your steadfast love in the morning,
for in you I trust.
Make known to me the way I should go,
for to you I lift up my soul.
v09: Deliver me from my enemies, O LORD;
to you I have fled for refuge.
v10: Teach me to do your will,
for you are my God.
Let your good Spirit lead me
on level ground.
</p>

## Preserve My Life for Your Name’s Sake

<p>
v11: For your name’s sake, O LORD, preserve my life;
in your righteousness bring my soul out of trouble.
v12: In your steadfast love cut off my enemies
and destroy all who afflict my life,
for I am your servant.
</p>
## Notes
v09: The final Hebrew verb is textually difficult. The consonants support the sense of covering or hiding; “I have fled for refuge” expresses the idiomatic force of seeking shelter in God without resolving the underlying form more narrowly.

## Vocabulary

v02: צָדַק (tsadaq)
“Be righteous” or “be in the right.” The verse denies that any living person can stand as righteous before God in judicial comparison.

v10: רוּחֲךָ טוֹבָה (ruchakha tovah)
“Your good Spirit,” God’s own life-giving and guiding Spirit, invoked here for moral direction.
