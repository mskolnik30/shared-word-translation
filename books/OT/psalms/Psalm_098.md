<p>
v01: A psalm.

Sing to the LORD a new song,
for he has done wondrous things.
His right hand and his holy arm
have won salvation for him.
v02: The LORD has made known his salvation;
before the eyes of the nations
he has revealed his righteousness.
v03: He has remembered his steadfast love and his faithfulness
to the house of Israel.
All the ends of the earth have seen
the salvation of our God.
</p>

<p>
v04: Raise a shout to the LORD, all the earth;
break forth, cry out for joy, and sing praise!
v05: Sing praise to the LORD with the lyre,
with the lyre and the sound of song;
v06: with trumpets and the sound of the ram’s horn,
raise a shout before the King, the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v07: Let the sea roar, and all that fills it,
the world and those who live in it.
v08: Let the rivers clap their hands;
together let the mountains sing for joy
v09: before the LORD, for he comes
to judge the earth.
He will judge the world with righteousness
and the peoples with equity.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v02: צְדָקָה (tsedaqah)
“righteousness” is revealed before the nations as God’s public, saving faithfulness and right ordering of the world.

v03: חֶסֶד (chesed)
“steadfast love” names the LORD’s enduring covenant loyalty toward the house of Israel.

v03: אֱמוּנָה (’emunah)
“faithfulness” marks God’s dependability, paired here with steadfast love.

v09: שָׁפַט (shaphat)
“judge” denotes active rule as well as judicial decision. The LORD’s coming judgment is defined by righteousness and equity.
