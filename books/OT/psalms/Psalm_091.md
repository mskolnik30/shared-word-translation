## A Psalm of Refuge
<p>
v01: The one who sits in the shelter of the Most High,
  who lodges in the shadow of the Almighty—

v02: I say of the LORD, “My refuge and my stronghold,
  my God, in whom I trust.”

v03: For he will deliver you from the snare of the fowler,
  from the deadly pestilence;

v04: he will cover you with his pinions,
  and under his wings you will find refuge;
  his faithfulness is shield and rampart.

v05: You will not fear the terror of the night,
  nor the arrow that flies by day,

v06: nor the pestilence that walks in darkness,
  nor the destruction that lays waste at noon.

v07: A thousand may fall at your side,
  and ten thousand at your right hand;
  it will not come near you.

v08: You will only look with your eyes
  and see the recompense of the wicked.

v09: Because you have made the LORD your refuge,
  the Most High your dwelling,

v10: no harm will befall you,
  no plague will come near your tent.

v11: For he will command his angels concerning you,
  to guard you in all your ways;

v12: on their hands they will bear you up,
  lest you strike your foot against a stone.

v13: You will tread on the lion and the cobra;
  the young lion and the serpent you will trample underfoot.

v14: “Because he holds fast to me in love, I will deliver him;
  I will set him on high, because he knows my name.

v15: When he calls to me, I will answer him;
  I will be with him in trouble;
  I will deliver him and honor him.

v16: With long life I will satisfy him
  and show him my salvation.”
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: סֵתֶר (sēter)
“A hidden place, a covering, the inside of a shelter where one cannot be seen.”

v01: עֶלְיוֹן (ʿElyôn)
“Most High.” A divine title formed from the adjective “high, uppermost.”

v01: שַׁדַּי (Šadday)
“Almighty; Shaddai.” A traditional divine title whose precise etymology remains uncertain.

v16: יְשׁוּעָה (yəšûʿâ)
“Salvation, deliverance. The term names rescue rather than a state of being saved.”
