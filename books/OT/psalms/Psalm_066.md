## Make a Joyful Shout to God

<p>
v01: Make a joyful shout to God,
all the earth.

v02: Sing the glory of his name;
give glory to his praise.

v03: Say to God,
“How fearsome are your works.

Because of the greatness of your strength,
your enemies cringe before you.

v04: All the earth bows down to you
and sings to you;

they sing praise to your name.”
</p>

## Come and See What God Has Done

<p>
v05: Come and see the works of God;
fearsome is his doing toward the children of humankind.

v06: He turned the sea into dry ground;
through the river they passed on foot.

There we rejoiced in him,

v07: who rules by his strength forever,
whose eyes keep watch on the nations.

Let the rebellious not exalt themselves.
</p>

## God Has Kept Us Among the Living

<p>
v08: Bless our God, O peoples;
let the sound of his praise be heard,

v09: who has kept our soul among the living
and has not let our foot slip.

v10: For you, O God, have tested us;
you have refined us as silver is refined.

v11: You brought us into the net;
you laid a crushing burden on our backs.

v12: You let mortals ride over our heads;
we went through fire and through water,

yet you brought us out
to a place of abundance.
</p>

## I Will Come into Your House

<p>
v13: I will come into your house with burnt offerings;
I will pay you my vows,

v14: which my lips uttered
and my mouth spoke when I was in distress.

v15: I will offer to you burnt offerings of fatlings,
with the smoke of rams;

I will make an offering of bulls
with goats.
</p>

## Come and Hear

<p>
v16: Come and hear, all you who fear God,
and I will tell what he has done for my soul.

v17: I cried to him with my mouth,
and praise was under my tongue.

v18: If I had regarded iniquity in my heart,
the Lord would not have listened.

v19: But truly God has listened;
he has attended to the voice of my prayer.

v20: Blessed be God,
who has not turned away my prayer

or his steadfast love from me.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v09: נֶפֶשׁ (nephesh)
“Soul” or “life.” The word refers to the living self, not an immaterial part separated from bodily existence.

v16: יִרְאֵי אֱלֹהִים (yirʾei ʾelohim)
“Those who fear God.” The phrase describes reverent worshipers whose awe leads to attentive hearing and faithful response.

v18: אָוֶן (aven)
“Iniquity.” The term can carry the sense of wrongdoing, trouble, or moral distortion held within the heart.

v20: חַסְדּוֹ (ḥasdo)
“His steadfast love.” The word denotes covenant loyalty, mercy, and faithful commitment rather than sentiment alone.
