v01: Of David.  
<p>  
Yahweh is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?  
Yahweh is the stronghold of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?  
</p>  
v02:  
<p>  
When evildoers approached me  
to devour my flesh,  
my adversaries and foes,  
it is they who stumbled and fell.  
</p>  
v03:  
<p>  
If an army encamps against me,  
my heart will not fear.  
If war rises against me,  
even then I will be confident.  
</p>  
v04:  
<p>  
One thing I have asked of Yahweh,  
that I will seek:  
to dwell in the house of Yahweh  
all the days of my life,  
to gaze upon the beauty of Yahweh,  
and to inquire in his temple.  
</p>  
v05:  
<p>  
For he will hide me in his shelter  
in the day of trouble;  
he will conceal me under  
the cover of his tent;  
he will lift me high upon a rock.  
</p>  
v06:  
<p>  
And now my head shall be lifted up  
above my enemies all around me,  
and I will offer sacrifices in his tent  
with shouts of joy;  
I will sing and make melody to Yahweh.  
</p>  
v07:  
<p>  
Hear, Yahweh, when I cry aloud;  
be gracious to me and answer me.  
</p>  
v08:  
<p>  
To you my heart says, “Seek his face.”  
Your face, Yahweh, I will seek.  
</p>  
v09:  
<p>  
Hide not your face from me.  
Do not turn your servant away in anger,  
you who have been my help.  
Do not cast me off;  
do not forsake me,  
O God of my salvation!  
</p>  
v10:  
<p>  
For my father and my mother have forsaken me,  
but Yahweh will gather me in.  
</p>  
v11:  
<p>  
Teach me your way, Yahweh,  
and lead me on a level path  
because of my enemies.  
</p>  
v12:  
<p>  
Do not give me up to the will of my adversaries,  
for false witnesses have risen against me,  
and they breathe out violence.  
</p>  
v13:  
<p>  
I believe I shall see the goodness of Yahweh  
in the land of the living!  
</p>  
v14:  
<p>  
Wait for Yahweh;  
be strong, and let your heart take courage;  
wait for Yahweh!  
</p>  

## Notes

### Psalm 27:1
**Yahweh is my light and my salvation**—This introductory declaration sets the emotional and theological tone of the psalm. It reinforces confidence in God's protective presence, signifying guidance and deliverance.

### Psalm 27:4
**to dwell in the house of Yahweh**—The psalmist expresses a deep longing for proximity to God's presence. In ancient Israelite worship, the temple represented God's dwelling place among the people, making it a focal point for prayer, safety, and divine encounter.

### Psalm 27:10
**my father and my mother have forsaken me**—This verse uses hyperbolic language to express abandonment, accentuating Yahweh's faithful presence even when human relationships fail.

### Psalm 27:13
**I believe I shall see the goodness of Yahweh in the land of the living**—The phrase expresses hope and trust in experiencing God's goodness within one's lifetime, despite despairing circumstances.

### Psalm 27:14
**Wait for Yahweh**—The theme of waiting reflects a posture of trust and patience, encouraging perseverance in faith with the assurance that God will eventually act.

## Vocabulary

### 27:1
**אוֹרִי (or)**—"Light" in this context symbolizes guidance, clarity, and presence from God, dispelling darkness and uncertainty in the psalmist's life.

### 27:1
**יִשְׁעִי (yesha')**—Translated as "salvation," carrying implications of rescue and safety provided by God, offering reassurance against fears.
