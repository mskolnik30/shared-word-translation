## The LORD Is in His Holy Temple
<p>
v01: In the LORD I take refuge;
how can you say to my soul,
“Flee like a bird to your mountain,
v02: for behold, the wicked bend the bow;
they have fitted their arrow to the string
to shoot in the dark at the upright in heart;
</p>

<p>
v03: if the foundations are destroyed,
what can the righteous do?”
</p>

## The LORD’s Sovereign Vision
<p>
v04: The LORD is in his holy temple;
the LORD’s throne is in heaven;
his eyes see, his eyelids test the children of humanity.
</p>

<p>
v05: The LORD tests the righteous,
but his soul hates the wicked
and the one who loves violence.
</p>

## The LORD’s Judgment
<p>
v06: Let him rain coals on the wicked;
fire and sulfur and a scorching wind
shall be the portion of their cup.
</p>

<p>
v07: For the LORD is righteous;
he loves righteous deeds;
the upright shall behold his face.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: חָסָה (chasah)
“To take refuge.” Seek shelter.

v01: נוּד (nud)
“To flee.” Wander or escape.

v02: דָּרַךְ (darakh)
“To bend.” Prepare a bow.

v02: יָרָה (yarah)
“To shoot.” Cast or throw.
