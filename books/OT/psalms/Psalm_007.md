## In You I Take Refuge
<p>
v01: O LORD my God, in you I take refuge;
save me from all my pursuers and deliver me,
v02: lest like a lion they tear my soul apart,
rending it in pieces, with none to rescue.
</p>

## Claim of Innocence
<p>
v03: O LORD my God, if I have done this,
if there is wrong in my hands,
v04: if I have repaid my friend with evil
or plundered my enemy without cause,
</p>

<p>
v05: let the enemy pursue my soul and overtake it,
and let him trample my life to the ground
and lay my glory in the dust. Selah.
</p>

## Appeal for Divine Judgment
<p>
v06: Arise, O LORD, in your anger;
lift yourself up against the fury of my enemies;
awake for me—you have appointed a judgment.
</p>

<p>
v07: Let the assembly of the peoples be gathered about you;
over it return on high.
v08: The LORD judges the peoples;
judge me, O LORD, according to my righteousness
and according to the integrity that is in me.
</p>

<p>
v09: Oh, let the evil of the wicked come to an end,
and may you establish the righteous—
you who test the minds and hearts,
O righteous God!
</p>

## God the Righteous Judge
<p>
v10: My shield is with God,
who saves the upright in heart.
v11: God is a righteous judge,
and a God who feels indignation every day.
</p>

<p>
v12: If one does not repent, God will sharpen his sword;
he has bent and readied his bow;
v13: he has prepared for him his deadly weapons,
making his arrows fiery shafts.
</p>

## The Self-Destruction of the Wicked
<p>
v14: Behold, the wicked conceives evil
and is pregnant with mischief
and gives birth to lies.
v15: He makes a pit, digging it out,
and falls into the hole that he has made.
</p>

<p>
v16: His mischief returns upon his own head,
and on his own skull his violence descends.
</p>

## Final Praise
<p>
v17: I will give thanks to the LORD according to his righteousness,
and I will sing praise to the name of the LORD Most High.
</p>

## Notes

v01–02: Refuge language frames the psalm—threat is immediate and violent.  

v03–05: The psalmist invites judgment if guilty—confidence rests on integrity.  

v06–09: Appeal shifts from personal defense to divine justice over all peoples.  

v09: God is described as examiner of “minds and hearts,” internal as well as external.  

v10–13: Divine judgment is active and prepared, not passive.  

v14–16: Evil is self-generating and self-destructive—violence returns to its source.  

v17: The psalm ends in praise grounded in God’s righteousness, not circumstances.  

## Vocabulary

v01: חָסָה (chasah)  
“To take refuge.” Seek protection.  

v02: טָרַף (taraph)  
“To tear.” Devour violently.  

v03: עָוֹן (‘avon)  
“Iniquity.” Moral wrong.  

v04: גָּמַל (gamal)  
“To repay.” Deal with.  

v05: רָדַף (radaph)  
“To pursue.” Chase down.  

v06: קוּם (qum)  
“To arise.” Take action.  

v08: שָׁפַט (shaphat)  
“To judge.” Govern justly.  

v09: בָּחַן (bachan)  
“To test.” Examine.  

v10: יָשָׁר (yashar)  
“Upright.” Straight in heart.  

v12: חָרַט (charat)  
“To sharpen.” Prepare weapon.  

v14: הָרָה (harah)  
“To conceive.” Begin process.  

v15: בּוֹר (bor)  
“Pit.” Trap.  

v16: חָמָס (chamas)  
“Violence.” Destructive force.  

v17: עֶלְיוֹן (‘elyon)  
“Most High.” Supreme God.
