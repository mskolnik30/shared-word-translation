<p>
v01: Of David. Judge me, Yahweh,  
    for I have walked in my integrity;  
    and I have trusted in Yahweh,  
    not wavering.  
v02: Examine me, Yahweh, and test me;  
    refine my heart and my mind.  
v03: For your steadfast love is before my eyes,  
    and I have walked in your truth.  
</p>

<p>
v04: I have not sat with deceitful people,  
    nor do I associate with hypocrites.  
v05: I hate the assembly of evildoers,  
    and I will not sit with the wicked.  
v06: I wash my hands in innocence,  
    and go around your altar, Yahweh,  
v07: proclaiming thanksgiving aloud,  
    and telling all your wondrous deeds.  
</p>

<p>
v08: Yahweh, I love the house where you dwell,  
    the place where your glory resides.  
v09: Do not sweep my life away with sinners,  
    nor my life with bloodthirsty men,  
v10: whose hands are sinful deeds,  
    whose right hands are full of bribes.  
</p>

<p>
v11: But as for me, I will walk in my integrity;  
    redeem me, and be gracious to me.  
v12: My foot stands on level ground;  
    in the congregations I will bless Yahweh.  
</p>

## Notes

v1: **Judge me, Yahweh** — The psalmist invites divine judgment, claiming integrity and trust in Yahweh's justice. This reflects a confident plea for vindication.  
v6: **I wash my hands in innocence** — An expression of ritual purity potentially signifying sincerity of heart, distancing oneself from any association with wrongdoing.  

## Vocabulary

v1: **תֹּם (tom)** — **integrity** is a theme of the psalm, emphasizing moral completeness and commitment to ethical conduct.  
v2: **בָּחַן (bachan)** — **examine** denotes scrutinizing one's inner being, inviting God to test the psalmist's motivations and thoughts.  
v3: **חֶסֶד (chesed)** — **steadfast love** describes Yahweh's covenant loyalty and faithfulness, central to the psalmist's assurance and walking in truth.
