<p>
v01: For the director with strings. A Maskil of David.  
</p>

<p>
v02: Give ear, O God, to my prayer,  
and do not hide yourself from my supplication.  
v03: Attend to me and answer me.  
I am restless in my complaint and moan,  
v04: because of the voice of the enemy,  
because of the oppression of the wicked.  
For they bring down trouble on me,  
and in anger they bear a grudge against me.  
</p>

<p>
v05: My heart is in anguish within me,  
and the terrors of death have fallen upon me.  
v06: Fear and trembling come upon me,  
and horror envelopes me.  
v07: And I say, "Oh, that I had wings like a dove!  
I would fly away and be at rest;  
</p>

<p>
v08: behold, I would wander far away;  
I would lodge in the wilderness. Selah  
</p>

<p>
v09: I would hurry to find a shelter  
from the raging wind and tempest."  
</p>

<p>
v10: Confuse, O Lord, divide their tongues,  
for I see violence and strife in the city.  
v11: Day and night they go around its walls,  
and iniquity and trouble are within it.  
v12: Ruin is in its midst;  
oppression and deceit do not depart from its square.  
</p>

<p>
v13: For not an enemy insults me—then I could bear it.  
It is not one who hates me—who has raised himself against me;  
then I could hide from him.  
v14: But you—a person of my equal,  
my companion and familiar friend.  
</p>

<p>
v15: We used to take sweet counsel together;  
within God's house we walked in the throng.  
</p>

<p>
v16: Let death seize them;  
let them go down to Sheol alive.  
For evil is in their homes and within them.  
</p>

<p>
v17: But I call to God,  
and the Lord will save me.  
v18: Evening and morning and at noon,  
I utter my complaint and moan,  
and he hears my voice.  
</p>

<p>
v19: He redeems my soul in safety  
from the battle against me,  
for many are against me.  
</p>

<p>
v20: God will hear and answer them—  
he who is enthroned from of old—Selah  
because they do not change  
and do not fear God.  
v21: My companion stretched out his hand against his friends;  
he has violated his covenant.  
v22: His speech was smooth as butter,  
yet war was in his heart.  
His words were softer than oil,  
yet they were drawn swords.  
</p>

<p>
v23: Cast your burden on the Lord,  
and he will sustain you.  
He will never permit  
the righteous to be moved.  
</p>

<p>
v24: But you, O God, will bring them down  
to the pit of destruction.  
Men of blood and deceit  
will not live out half their days.  
But I will trust in you.  
</p>

## Notes

v1: "A Maskil of David" — The term "Maskil" may denote a contemplative or didactic poem, inviting reflection.  

v10: "Confuse, O Lord, divide their tongues" — This echoes the story of Babel, where language confusion scatters humanity, symbolizing division among evildoers.  

v13–14: "not an enemy... you—a person of my equal" — The psalmist highlights the deep betrayal by a close friend, contrasting it with the pain of a usual adversary.  

v16–17: "Let death seize them; let them go down to Sheol alive" — A strong petition for divine justice against those who persistently work evil.  

v23: "Cast your burden on the Lord" — An invitation to trust in God as sustainer, who provides stability amidst life's uncertainties.  

## Vocabulary

v11: יוֹמָם וָלַ֗יְלָה (yo.mam va.la'yla) — "Day and night" captures the continual unrest and vigilance surrounding the city.  

v18: עֶ֤רֶב וָבֹ֣קֶר וְצָהֳרַיִם (e.rev va.vo.qer va.tso.ho.rayim) — "Evening and morning and at noon" signifies all times of prayer, emphasizing a life committed to persistent prayer.  

v23: יְהָבְךָ֮ (ye.hav'kha) — "your burden" refers to the anxiety, troubles, or challenges an individual might carry, which are to be entrusted to God.
