<p>
For the director with strings. A Maskil of David.
</p>

<p>
v01: Give ear, O God, to my prayer,
and do not hide yourself from my supplication.
v02: Attend to me and answer me.
I am restless in my complaint and moan,
v03: because of the voice of the enemy,
because of the oppression of the wicked.
For they bring down trouble on me,
and in anger they bear a grudge against me.
</p>

<p>
v04: My heart is in anguish within me,
and the terrors of death have fallen upon me.
v05: Fear and trembling come upon me,
and horror envelopes me.
v06: And I say, "Oh, that I had wings like a dove!
I would fly away and be at rest;
</p>

<p>
v07: behold, I would wander far away;
I would lodge in the wilderness. Selah
</p>

<p>
v08: I would hurry to find a shelter
from the raging wind and tempest."
</p>

<p>
v09: Confuse, O Lord, divide their tongues,
for I see violence and strife in the city.
v10: Day and night they go around its walls,
and iniquity and trouble are within it.
v11: Ruin is in its midst;
oppression and deceit do not depart from its square.
</p>

<p>
v12: For not an enemy insults me—then I could bear it.
It is not one who hates me—who has raised himself against me;
then I could hide from him.
v13: But you—a person of my equal,
my companion and familiar friend.
</p>

<p>
v14: We used to take sweet counsel together;
within God's house we walked in the throng.
</p>

<p>
v15: Let death seize them;
let them go down to Sheol alive.
For evil is in their homes and within them.
</p>

<p>
v16: But I call to God,
and the Lord will save me.
v17: Evening and morning and at noon,
I utter my complaint and moan,
and he hears my voice.
</p>

<p>
v18: He redeems my soul in safety
from the battle against me,
for many are against me.
</p>

<p>
v19: God will hear and answer them—
he who is enthroned from of old—Selah
because they do not change
and do not fear God.
v20: My companion stretched out his hand against his friends;
he has violated his covenant.
v21: His speech was smooth as butter,
yet war was in his heart.
His words were softer than oil,
yet they were drawn swords.
</p>

<p>
v22: Cast your burden on the Lord,
and he will sustain you.
He will never permit
the righteous to be moved.
</p>

<p>
v23: But you, O God, will bring them down
to the pit of destruction.
Men of blood and deceit
will not live out half their days.
But I will trust in you.
</p>
## Notes
v09: "Confuse, O Lord, divide their tongues" — This echoes the story of Babel, where language confusion scatters humanity, symbolizing division among evildoers.
v12–13: "not an enemy... you—a person of my equal" — The psalmist highlights the deep betrayal by a close friend, contrasting it with the pain of a usual adversary.
v15–16: "Let death seize them; let them go down to Sheol alive" — A strong petition for divine justice against those who persistently work evil.
v22: "Cast your burden on the Lord" — An invitation to trust in God as sustainer, who provides stability amidst life's uncertainties.
