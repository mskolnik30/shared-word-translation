## A Psalm for the Sabbath Day

<p>
v01: It is good to give thanks to the LORD,
to sing praise to your name, Most High;

v02: to declare your steadfast love in the morning,
and your faithfulness by night,

v03: with the ten-stringed instrument and the harp,
with the melody of the lyre.
</p>

<p>
v04: For you, O LORD, have made me glad by your work;
at the works of your hands I sing for joy.

v05: How great are your works, O LORD!
your thoughts are very deep.
</p>

<p>
v06: A dull person does not know,
and a fool does not understand this:

v07: that though the wicked sprout like grass,
and all evildoers flourish,
they are doomed to destruction forever;

v08: but you, O LORD, are on high forever.
</p>

<p>
v09: For behold, your enemies, O LORD,
for behold, your enemies shall perish;
all evildoers shall be scattered.

v10: But you have exalted my horn like that of a wild ox;
you have poured over me fresh oil.

v11: My eyes have seen the downfall of my enemies;
my ears have heard the doom of the evildoers who rise against me.
</p>

<p>
v12: The righteous flourish like the palm tree,
and grow like a cedar in Lebanon.

v13: They are planted in the house of the LORD;
they flourish in the courts of our God.

v14: In old age they still bear fruit;
they are full of sap and green,

v15: to declare that the LORD is upright;
he is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in him.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: טוֹב (tov)
“Good.” Not merely pleasant, but fitting or right within God’s order.

v02: חֶסֶד (chesed)
“Steadfast love.” Covenantal loyalty expressed in enduring relationship.

v02: אֱמוּנָה (emunah)
“Faithfulness.” Reliability or steadiness over time.

v12: צַדִּיק (tsaddiq)
“Righteous.” One aligned with God’s order and ways.
