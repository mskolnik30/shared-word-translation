## A Prayer of Integrity

<p>
v01: Vindicate me, O LORD,

for I have walked in my integrity,

and I have trusted in the LORD without wavering.

v02: Prove me, O LORD, and try me;

test my heart and my mind.

v03: For your steadfast love is before my eyes,

and I walk in your truth.
</p>

<p>
v04: I do not sit with men of falsehood,

nor do I go with those who hide what they are.

v05: I hate the assembly of evildoers,

and I will not sit with the wicked.
</p>

<p>
v06: I wash my hands in innocence

and go around your altar, O LORD,

v07: proclaiming thanksgiving aloud

and telling all your wondrous deeds.

v08: O LORD, I love the dwelling of your house

and the place where your glory dwells.
</p>

<p>
v09: Do not sweep away my soul with sinners,

nor my life with men of blood,

v10: in whose hands are schemes,

and their right hand is full of bribes.
</p>

<p>
v11: But as for me, I will walk in my integrity;

redeem me and be gracious to me.

v12: My foot stands on level ground;

in the assembly I will bless the LORD.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: בָּטַח (bāṭaḥ)
“To trust.” To rely securely.

v03: חֶסֶד (ḥeseḏ)
“Steadfast love.” Loyal covenant love.

v03: אֱמֶת (ʾĕmeṯ)
“Truth.” Reliability, faithfulness.

v08: כָּבוֹד (kāḇôḏ)
“Glory.” Weight, presence.
