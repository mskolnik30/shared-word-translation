v01: A psalm of David.  
<p>  
v01: The Lord is my shepherd;  
          I will not lack.  
v02: In green pastures he makes me lie down;  
          beside still waters he leads me.  
v03: He restores my soul;  
          he guides me in paths of righteousness for his name's sake.  
</p>  
<p>  
v04: Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death,  
          I will fear no evil,  
          for you are with me;  
          your rod and your staff, they comfort me.  
v05: You prepare a table before me  
          in the presence of my enemies;  
          you anoint my head with oil;  
          my cup overflows.  
v06: Surely goodness and mercy will pursue me  
          all the days of my life,  
          and I will dwell in the house of the Lord forever.  
</p>  

## Notes

v1: The metaphor of the Lord as a shepherd emphasizes provision, guidance, and care. "I will not lack" conveys trust in God's sufficiency.  
v4: "Valley of the shadow of death" evokes life-threatening or deeply fearful circumstances. The psalmist's confidence is rooted in God's presence and protection.  
v5: "Prepare a table" implies God's provision and honor in the midst of adversity. The anointing and overflowing cup signify abundance and blessing.

## Vocabulary

v1: רֹעִי (ra.ah) — "shepherd": Evokes the image of guidance and care, associating God with the role of protector and provider in a pastoral setting.  
v4: צַלְמָוֶת (tsal.ma.vet) — "shadow of death": Suggests the deepest darkness or extreme danger, highlighting the psalmist's trust in God amidst peril.  
v6: חֶסֶד (che.sed) — "mercy": Indicates God’s steadfast love and covenantal faithfulness, pursuing the psalmist throughout life.
