## The Allotment of Judah

<p>
v01: The lot for the tribe of the children of Judah according to their clans extended toward the border of Edom, to the wilderness of Zin southward, at the southernmost end.

v02: Their southern border was from the end of the Salt Sea, from the bay that faces south.

v03: It went out south of the ascent of Akrabbim, crossed to Zin, went up south of Kadesh-Barnea, crossed to Hezron, went up to Addar, turned toward Karka,

v04: crossed to Azmon, went out to the Wadi of Egypt, and the border ended at the sea. This shall be your southern border.

v05: The eastern border was the Salt Sea as far as the mouth of the Jordan. The border on the north side began at the bay of the sea at the mouth of the Jordan.

v06: The border went up to Beth-Hoglah and passed north of Beth-Arabah. The border went up to the Stone of Bohan son of Reuben.

v07: The border went up to Debir from the Valley of Achor, and northward turned toward Gilgal, which is opposite the ascent of Adummim, which is south of the valley. The border passed to the waters of En-Shemesh and ended at En-Rogel.

v08: The border went up by the Valley of Ben-Hinnom to the south shoulder of the Jebusite—that is, Jerusalem. The border went up to the top of the mountain that is before the Valley of Hinnom toward the west, which is at the northern end of the Valley of Rephaim.

v09: From the top of the mountain the border curved to the spring of the waters of Nephtoah, went out to the cities of Mount Ephron, and curved to Baalah—that is, Kiriath-Jearim.

v10: The border turned from Baalah westward to Mount Seir, crossed to the north shoulder of Mount Jearim—that is, Chesalon—went down to Beth-Shemesh, and crossed to Timnah.

v11: The border went out to the north shoulder of Ekron, curved to Shikkeron, crossed to Mount Baalah, went out to Jabneel, and the border ended at the sea.

v12: The western border was the Great Sea and its coast. This is the border around the children of Judah according to their clans.
</p>

## Caleb Takes Hebron and Debir

<p>
v13: To Caleb son of Jephunneh Joshua gave a portion among the children of Judah, according to the command of the LORD to Joshua: Kiriath-Arba, father of Anak—that is, Hebron.

v14: Caleb drove out from there the three sons of Anak: Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai, descendants of Anak.

v15: From there he went up against the inhabitants of Debir. The name of Debir formerly was Kiriath-Sepher.

v16: Caleb said, “Whoever strikes Kiriath-Sepher and captures it, I will give Achsah my daughter to him as wife.”

v17: Othniel son of Kenaz, the brother of Caleb, captured it, and Caleb gave him Achsah his daughter as wife.

v18: When she came to him, she urged him to ask her father for a field. She got down from her donkey, and Caleb said to her, “What do you want?”

v19: She said, “Give me a blessing. Since you have given me land in the Negev, give me springs of water also.” So he gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.
</p>

## Notes
v01–12: Judah’s inheritance is defined by detailed geographic boundaries. The accumulation of place names reflects land as concrete inheritance rather than abstract territory.
v13–19: Caleb’s taking of Hebron and the account of Achsah and Othniel repeat material also preserved in Judges 1, linking the land allotment with stories of actual possession.
v19: Achsah’s request recognizes that land in the Negev requires water to be viable. The “blessing” she requests is therefore materially specific.
v32: The stated total of twenty-nine cities does not correspond simply to the number of names preserved in the received list. TSW retains both the names and the traditional total without harmonization.
v63: The chapter closes by acknowledging incomplete possession in Jerusalem, anticipating later narratives in Judges and Samuel.

##Vocabulary

v01: גּוֹרָל (goral)
“Lot” or “allotment.” The means by which Judah’s territorial portion is assigned.

v04: נַחַל מִצְרַיִם (Nachal Mitsrayim)
“Wadi of Egypt.” A southwestern boundary marker.

v08: גֵּיא בֶן־הִנֹּם (Ge Ben-Hinnom)
“Valley of Ben-Hinnom.” A valley associated with Jerusalem’s southern boundary.

v19: בְּרָכָה (berakhah)
“Blessing.” Achsah uses the term for the practical gift of water sources.

v20: נַחֲלָה (nachalah)
“Inheritance.” Judah’s allotted territorial possession.

v63: יָרַשׁ (yarash)
“To dispossess” or “possess.” Judah is unable to drive out the Jebusites.
