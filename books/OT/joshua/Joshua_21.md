## The Levites Ask for Their Cities

<p>
v01: Then the heads of the ancestral houses of the Levites approached Eleazar the priest, Joshua son of Nun, and the heads of the ancestral houses of the tribes of the children of Israel.

v02: They spoke to them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, “The LORD commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities to live in, with their pasturelands for our livestock.”

v03: So the children of Israel gave to the Levites from their inheritance, according to the command of the LORD, these cities with their pasturelands.
</p>

## Cities for the Kohathites

<p>
v04: The lot came out for the clans of the Kohathites. The children of Aaron the priest, from the Levites, received by lot thirteen cities from the tribe of Judah, from the tribe of Simeon, and from the tribe of Benjamin.

v05: The rest of the children of Kohath received by lot ten cities from the clans of the tribe of Ephraim, from the tribe of Dan, and from the half-tribe of Manasseh.
</p>

## Cities for the Gershonites and Merarites

<p>
v06: The children of Gershon received by lot thirteen cities from the clans of the tribe of Issachar, from the tribe of Asher, from the tribe of Naphtali, and from the half-tribe of Manasseh in Bashan.

v07: The children of Merari according to their clans received twelve cities from the tribe of Reuben, from the tribe of Gad, and from the tribe of Zebulun.

v08: The children of Israel gave these cities with their pasturelands by lot to the Levites, as the LORD had commanded by the hand of Moses.
</p>

## Priestly Cities in Judah, Simeon, and Benjamin

<p>
v09: From the tribe of the children of Judah and from the tribe of the children of Simeon they gave these cities named here:

v10: they were for the children of Aaron from the clans of the Kohathites, from the children of Levi, for theirs was the first lot.

v11: They gave them Kiriath-Arba—Arba was father of Anak—that is, Hebron, in the hill country of Judah, with its pasturelands around it.

v12: But the fields of the city and its villages they gave to Caleb son of Jephunneh as his possession.

v13: To the children of Aaron the priest they gave Hebron, the city of refuge for the manslayer, with its pasturelands; Libnah with its pasturelands;

v14: Jattir with its pasturelands; Eshtemoa with its pasturelands;

v15: Holon with its pasturelands; Debir with its pasturelands;

v16: Ain with its pasturelands; Juttah with its pasturelands; and Beth-Shemesh with its pasturelands: nine cities from these two tribes.

v17: From the tribe of Benjamin: Gibeon with its pasturelands; Geba with its pasturelands;

v18: Anathoth with its pasturelands; and Almon with its pasturelands: four cities.

v19: All the cities of the priests, the children of Aaron, were thirteen cities with their pasturelands.
</p>

## Cities for the Rest of Kohath

<p>
v20: The clans of the children of Kohath, the Levites who remained from the children of Kohath, received the cities of their lot from the tribe of Ephraim.

v21: They gave them Shechem, the city of refuge for the manslayer, with its pasturelands in the hill country of Ephraim; Gezer with its pasturelands;

v22: Kibzaim with its pasturelands; and Beth-Horon with its pasturelands: four cities.

v23: From the tribe of Dan: Elteke with its pasturelands; Gibbethon with its pasturelands;

v24: Aijalon with its pasturelands; and Gath-Rimmon with its pasturelands: four cities.

v25: From the half-tribe of Manasseh: Taanach with its pasturelands and Gath-Rimmon with its pasturelands: two cities.

v26: All the cities for the clans of the remaining children of Kohath were ten, with their pasturelands.
</p>

## Cities for the Gershonites

<p>
v27: To the children of Gershon, from the clans of the Levites, from the half-tribe of Manasseh they gave Golan in Bashan, the city of refuge for the manslayer, with its pasturelands, and Be-Eshterah with its pasturelands: two cities.

v28: From the tribe of Issachar: Kishion with its pasturelands; Daberath with its pasturelands;

v29: Jarmuth with its pasturelands; and En-Gannim with its pasturelands: four cities.

v30: From the tribe of Asher: Mishal with its pasturelands; Abdon with its pasturelands;

v31: Helkath with its pasturelands; and Rehob with its pasturelands: four cities.

v32: From the tribe of Naphtali: Kedesh in Galilee, the city of refuge for the manslayer, with its pasturelands; Hammoth-Dor with its pasturelands; and Kartan with its pasturelands: three cities.

v33: All the cities of the Gershonites according to their clans were thirteen cities with their pasturelands.
</p>

## Cities for the Merarites

<p>
v34: To the clans of the children of Merari, the remaining Levites, from the tribe of Zebulun: Jokneam with its pasturelands; Kartah with its pasturelands;

v35: Dimnah with its pasturelands; and Nahalal with its pasturelands: four cities.

v36: From the tribe of Reuben: Bezer with its pasturelands; Jahaz with its pasturelands;

v37: Kedemoth with its pasturelands; and Mephaath with its pasturelands: four cities.

v38: From the tribe of Gad: Ramoth in Gilead, the city of refuge for the manslayer, with its pasturelands; Mahanaim with its pasturelands;

v39: Heshbon with its pasturelands; and Jazer with its pasturelands: four cities.

v40: All the cities for the children of Merari according to their clans, the rest of the clans of the Levites, were twelve cities by their lot.
</p>

## Forty-Eight Levitical Cities

<p>
v41: All the cities of the Levites in the midst of the possession of the children of Israel were forty-eight cities with their pasturelands.

v42: Each of these cities had its pasturelands around it; so it was with all these cities.
</p>

## The LORD Gives Israel Rest

<p>
v43: Thus the LORD gave to Israel all the land that he had sworn to give to their ancestors. They possessed it and lived in it.

v44: The LORD gave them rest on every side, according to all that he had sworn to their ancestors. Not one of all their enemies stood before them; the LORD gave all their enemies into their hand.

v45: Not one word failed from all the good words that the LORD had spoken to the house of Israel. All came to pass.
</p>
## Notes
v01–03: The Levites receive cities within tribal territories rather than one continuous land allotment. Their presence is therefore distributed throughout Israel.

v13, v21, v27, v32, v38: Several Levitical cities are also cities of refuge, joining priestly presence with the legal protection established in chapter 20.
v25: The place-name Gath-Rimmon is textually difficult here and may reflect a variant tradition concerning Ibleam or another Manassite city. TSW retains the received form.
v36–37: Some Hebrew manuscript traditions omit these verses, while ancient versions and many textual traditions include the Reubenite cities. TSW includes them as part of the standard public verse numbering.
v36–37: Some Hebrew manuscript traditions omit these verses, while ancient versions and many textual traditions include the Reubenite cities. The verses are retained in the standard public numbering.
v43–45: The chapter closes with a strong fulfillment summary. Later narratives still describe remaining peoples and conflicts, so “all the land” functions as covenantal fulfillment language rather than a denial of every unfinished local struggle.

##Vocabulary

v02: מִגְרָשׁ (migrash)
“Pastureland.” Open land surrounding a city for livestock and related use.

v04: גּוֹרָל (goral)
“Lot.” The method by which Levitical cities are assigned.

v13: עִיר מִקְלָט (ir miqlat)
“City of refuge.” A designated place of protection for the unintentional manslayer.

v41: אֲחֻזָּה (achuzzah)
“Possession.” Tribal land within which Levitical cities are located.

v44: נוּחַ (nuach)
“To give rest.” To provide settled security from surrounding enemies.
