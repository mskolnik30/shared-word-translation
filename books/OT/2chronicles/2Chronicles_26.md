# 2 Chronicles 26

## Uzziah Becomes King

<p>
v01: All the people of Judah took Uzziah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah.

v02: He built Eloth and restored it to Judah after the king lay down with his ancestors.

v03: Uzziah was sixteen years old when he became king, and he reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Jecoliah from Jerusalem.

v04: He did what was right in the eyes of the LORD, according to all that Amaziah his father had done.

v05: He set himself to seek God in the days of Zechariah, who instructed him in the fear of God. And in the days that he sought the LORD, God caused him to prosper.
</p>

## Uzziah’s Military Success

<p>
v06: He went out and fought against the Philistines and broke down the wall of Gath, the wall of Jabneh, and the wall of Ashdod. He built cities in the territory of Ashdod and among the Philistines.

v07: God helped him against the Philistines, against the Arabians who lived in Gur-Baal, and against the Meunites.

v08: The Ammonites gave tribute to Uzziah, and his fame went as far as the entrance of Egypt, for he became exceedingly strong.

v09: Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the Corner Gate, at the Valley Gate, and at the Angle, and fortified them.

v10: He built towers in the wilderness and dug many cisterns, for he had much livestock in the foothills and on the plain. He had farmers and vineyard workers in the mountains and in the fertile lands, for he loved the soil.
</p>

## Uzziah’s Army and Inventions

<p>
v11: Uzziah had an army trained for war, going out to battle by divisions according to the number in their enrollment by Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the officer, under the hand of Hananiah, one of the king’s commanders.

v12: The whole number of heads of ancestral houses of the mighty warriors was two thousand six hundred.

v13: Under their hand was an army of three hundred seven thousand five hundred who made war with mighty power to help the king against the enemy.

v14: Uzziah prepared for them, for all the army, shields, spears, helmets, armor, bows, and sling stones.

v15: In Jerusalem he made devices invented by skilled men to be upon the towers and the corners, to shoot arrows and great stones. His fame went out far, for he was marvelously helped until he became strong.
</p>

## Uzziah Becomes Proud

<p>
v16: But when he became strong, his heart became high, to his destruction. He acted unfaithfully against the LORD his God and entered the temple of the LORD to burn incense upon the altar of incense.

v17: Azariah the priest went in after him, and with him eighty priests of the LORD, courageous men.

v18: They stood against King Uzziah and said to him, “It is not for you, Uzziah, to burn incense to the LORD, but for the priests, the sons of Aaron, who are consecrated to burn incense. Go out from the sanctuary, for you have acted unfaithfully, and it will not be for your honor from the LORD God.”
</p>

## Uzziah Is Struck with Skin Disease

<p>
v19: Then Uzziah became enraged, and in his hand was a censer for burning incense. While he was enraged with the priests, skin disease broke out on his forehead before the priests in the house of the LORD, beside the incense altar.

v20: Azariah the chief priest and all the priests turned toward him, and behold, he had skin disease on his forehead. They hurried him out from there, and he himself hurried to go out, for the LORD had struck him.

v21: King Uzziah had the skin disease until the day of his death and lived in a separate house, being diseased, for he was cut off from the house of the LORD. Jotham his son was over the king’s house, judging the people of the land.

v22: Now the rest of the acts of Uzziah, first and last, Isaiah son of Amoz the prophet wrote.

v23: Uzziah lay down with his ancestors, and they buried him with his ancestors in the burial field that belonged to the kings, for they said, “He has a skin disease.” Jotham his son became king in his place.
</p>
## Notes
v05: Uzziah’s prosperity is explicitly tied to seeking the LORD.
v10: “He loved the soil” is a rare personal detail presenting the king as invested in agriculture.
v15: The “devices” are described broadly enough that their exact mechanical form is uncertain; they are defensive military inventions.
v16: Strength becomes the setting for pride, repeating a major Chronicler pattern.
v21: Uzziah’s exclusion from the temple is both ritual and royal humiliation.
