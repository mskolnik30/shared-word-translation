# 2 Chronicles 17

## Jehoshaphat Strengthens Judah

<p>
v01: Jehoshaphat his son became king in his place and strengthened himself against Israel.

v02: He placed forces in all the fortified cities of Judah and put garrisons in the land of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that Asa his father had captured.

v03: The LORD was with Jehoshaphat because he walked in the earlier ways of David his ancestor and did not seek the Baals,

v04: but sought the God of his father and walked in his commandments and not according to the acts of Israel.

v05: So the LORD established the kingdom in his hand, and all Judah brought tribute to Jehoshaphat. He had riches and honor in abundance.

v06: His heart was courageous in the ways of the LORD, and he further removed the high places and the Asherah poles from Judah.
</p>

## Teachers Carry the Instruction through Judah

<p>
v07: In the third year of his reign he sent his officials Ben-Hail, Obadiah, Zechariah, Nethanel, and Micaiah to teach in the cities of Judah.

v08: With them were Levites: Shemaiah, Nethaniah, Zebadiah, Asahel, Shemiramoth, Jehonathan, Adonijah, Tobijah, and Tob-Adonijah; and with these Levites were Elishama and Jehoram the priests.

v09: They taught in Judah, having with them the book of the instruction of the LORD. They went around throughout all the cities of Judah and taught among the people.
</p>

## The Fear of the LORD Falls on the Nations

<p>
v10: The fear of the LORD fell upon all the kingdoms of the lands that were around Judah, and they did not make war against Jehoshaphat.

v11: Some of the Philistines brought Jehoshaphat gifts and silver as tribute, and the Arabians also brought him flocks: seven thousand seven hundred rams and seven thousand seven hundred male goats.

v12: Jehoshaphat grew greater and greater. He built fortresses and storage cities in Judah.

v13: He had much work in the cities of Judah, and men of war, mighty warriors, in Jerusalem.
</p>

## Jehoshaphat’s Military Forces

<p>
v14: This was their enrollment according to their ancestral houses: from Judah, commanders of thousands—Adnah the commander, and with him three hundred thousand mighty warriors;

v15: next to him Jehohanan the commander, and with him two hundred eighty thousand;

v16: next to him Amasiah son of Zichri, who willingly offered himself to the LORD, and with him two hundred thousand mighty warriors.

v17: From Benjamin: Eliada, a mighty warrior, and with him two hundred thousand armed with bow and shield;

v18: next to him Jehozabad, and with him one hundred eighty thousand equipped for war.

v19: These served the king besides those whom the king had put in the fortified cities throughout all Judah.
</p>

##Notes

v03: “Earlier ways of David” may distinguish David’s faithful period from later failures or may reflect a textual variant.

v06: “His heart was courageous” describes inward boldness in pursuing the LORD’s ways.

v09: The royal teaching mission centers on the written “book of the instruction of the LORD.”

v10: Military security is attributed to the “fear of the LORD” falling upon surrounding kingdoms.

##Vocabulary

v03: בַּעַל (Baal)
“Baal.” A Canaanite deity.

v04: דָּרַשׁ (darash)
“To seek.”

v06: גָּבַהּ לֵב (gavah lev)
“To lift/courage the heart.” Here positive boldness in the LORD’s ways.

v09: סֵפֶר תּוֹרַת יְהוָה (sefer torat YHWH)
“Book of the instruction of the LORD.”

v10: פַּחַד יְהוָה (pachad YHWH)
“Fear of the LORD.”

v16: נָדַב (nadav)
“To offer willingly.”
