# 2 Chronicles 19

## Jehu Rebukes Jehoshaphat

<p>
v01: Jehoshaphat king of Judah returned to his house in peace at Jerusalem.

v02: Jehu son of Hanani the seer went out to meet him and said to King Jehoshaphat, “Should you help the wicked and love those who hate the LORD? Because of this, wrath has come upon you from before the LORD.

v03: Nevertheless, good things are found in you, for you have removed the Asherah poles from the land and have set your heart to seek God.”
</p>

## Jehoshaphat Appoints Judges

<p>
v04: Jehoshaphat lived in Jerusalem, and he went out again among the people from Beersheba to the hill country of Ephraim and brought them back to the LORD, the God of their ancestors.

v05: He appointed judges in the land, in all the fortified cities of Judah, city by city.

v06: He said to the judges, “Consider what you are doing, for you do not judge for humanity but for the LORD, and he is with you in the matter of judgment.

v07: And now, let the fear of the LORD be upon you. Guard yourselves and act, for with the LORD our God there is no injustice, partiality, or taking of a bribe.”
</p>

## Priests and Levites Judge in Jerusalem

<p>
v08: In Jerusalem also Jehoshaphat appointed some of the Levites, priests, and heads of the ancestral houses of Israel for the judgment of the LORD and for disputes. They returned to Jerusalem.

v09: He commanded them, saying, “Thus you shall act in the fear of the LORD, with faithfulness and with a whole heart.

v10: Whenever any dispute comes to you from your brothers who live in their cities—between blood and blood, between instruction and commandment, statutes and judgments—you shall warn them so that they do not become guilty before the LORD and wrath come upon you and your brothers. Thus you shall act, and you will not incur guilt.

v11: And look, Amariah the chief priest is over you in every matter of the LORD, and Zebadiah son of Ishmael, ruler of the house of Judah, in every matter of the king. The Levites shall be officers before you. Be strong and act, and may the LORD be with the good.”
</p>
## Notes
v02: Jehoshaphat is rebuked not for diplomacy in general but for giving active help to Ahab’s wicked regime.
v03: Chronicles again distinguishes a king’s failures from genuine reforms rather than flattening him into a single moral category.
v06–07: Judges are told that their work is performed before the LORD, making impartial justice a theological obligation.
v10: “Between blood and blood” is a legal idiom for different categories of homicide or bloodguilt.

## Vocabulary

v02: עָזַר (azar)
“To help.” Jehoshaphat’s alliance with the wicked is condemned.

v03: כּוּן לֵב (kun lev)
“To set the heart.” A deliberate orientation toward seeking God.

v06: מִשְׁפָּט (mishpat)
“Judgment” or justice.

v07: מַשָּׂא פָנִים (massa panim)
“Partiality,” literally “lifting a face.”

v09: אֱמוּנָה (emunah)
“Faithfulness.”

v11: שֹׁטֵר (shoter)
“Officer.” An official assisting judicial order.
