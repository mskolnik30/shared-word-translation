## A Man with a Bodily Discharge

<p>
v01: The LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying,
v02: “Speak to the Israelites and say to them: When any man has a discharge from his body, his discharge makes him unclean.
v03: This shall be his uncleanness because of the discharge: whether his body continues to flow with the discharge or his body is stopped from the discharge, it is his uncleanness.
</p>

<p>
v04: Every bed on which the man with the discharge lies shall be unclean, and everything on which he sits shall be unclean.
v05: Whoever touches his bed shall wash their clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
v06: Whoever sits on anything on which the man with the discharge sat shall wash their clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
v07: Whoever touches the body of the man with the discharge shall wash their clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
v08: If the man with the discharge spits on someone who is clean, that person shall wash their clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
v09: Any saddle on which the man with the discharge rides shall be unclean.
v10: Whoever touches anything that was under him shall be unclean until evening, and whoever carries such things shall wash their clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
v11: Anyone whom the man with the discharge touches without first rinsing his hands in water shall wash their clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
v12: An earthen vessel touched by the man with the discharge shall be broken, and every wooden vessel shall be rinsed in water.
</p>

## Cleansing after a Discharge

<p>
v13: When the man with a discharge is cleansed from his discharge, he shall count seven days for his cleansing, wash his clothes, and bathe his body in fresh water; then he shall be clean.
v14: On the eighth day he shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and come before the LORD to the entrance of the Tent of Meeting and give them to the priest.
v15: The priest shall offer them, one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering. The priest shall make atonement for him before the LORD concerning his discharge.
</p>

## Seminal Emissions

<p>
v16: If a man has an emission of semen, he shall bathe his entire body in water and be unclean until evening.
v17: Every garment and every leather article on which semen comes shall be washed with water and be unclean until evening.
v18: If a man lies with a woman and there is an emission of semen, both shall bathe in water and be unclean until evening.
</p>

## Menstrual Uncleanness

<p>
v19: When a woman has a discharge, and the discharge from her body is blood, she shall be in her menstrual uncleanness seven days. Whoever touches her shall be unclean until evening.
v20: Everything on which she lies during her menstrual uncleanness shall be unclean, and everything on which she sits shall be unclean.
v21: Whoever touches her bed shall wash their clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
v22: Whoever touches anything on which she sat shall wash their clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
v23: Whether it is on the bed or on anything on which she sat, whoever touches it shall be unclean until evening.
v24: If a man lies with her and her menstrual flow comes upon him, he shall be unclean seven days, and every bed on which he lies shall be unclean.
</p>

## An Abnormal Discharge of Blood

<p>
v25: If a woman has a discharge of blood for many days outside the time of her menstrual period, or if the discharge continues beyond her period, all the days of the discharge of her uncleanness shall be like the days of her menstrual uncleanness; she is unclean.
v26: Every bed on which she lies during all the days of her discharge shall be to her like the bed of her menstrual period, and everything on which she sits shall be unclean like the uncleanness of her menstrual period.
v27: Whoever touches these things shall be unclean and shall wash their clothes, bathe in water, and be unclean until evening.
</p>

## Cleansing after the Discharge

<p>
v28: But if she is cleansed from her discharge, she shall count seven days, and afterward she shall be clean.
v29: On the eighth day she shall take two turtledoves or two young pigeons and bring them to the priest at the entrance of the Tent of Meeting.
v30: The priest shall offer one as a sin offering and the other as a burnt offering, and the priest shall make atonement for her before the LORD concerning the uncleanness of her discharge.
</p>

## Protecting the Sanctuary from Uncleanness

<p>
v31: Thus you shall keep the Israelites separate from their uncleanness, so that they do not die in their uncleanness by defiling my tabernacle that is in their midst.
v32: This is the law concerning the man who has a discharge, and the man who has an emission of semen and becomes unclean by it,
v33: and concerning the woman during her menstrual uncleanness, and anyone, male or female, who has a discharge, and the man who lies with a woman who is unclean.
</p>
## Notes
v02–15: The chapter distinguishes an ongoing abnormal male discharge from ordinary seminal emission in vv16–18.
v16–18: Sexual intercourse within itself is not called sinful here; the resulting bodily emission creates temporary ritual uncleanness.
v19–30: Menstrual and abnormal uterine bleeding are treated as ritual states with different durations and procedures. The text does not describe menstruation as moral failure.
v31: The concern is ultimately sanctuary-centered: unmanaged uncleanness threatens the holy dwelling of the LORD among the people.

## Vocabulary

v02: זוֹב (zov)
“Discharge.” A bodily flow that can create ritual uncleanness.

v13: מַיִם חַיִּים (mayim chayyim)
“Fresh water,” literally “living water.” Flowing water used in cleansing.

v16: שִׁכְבַת־זֶרַע (shikhvat zera)
“Emission of semen.” Literally “lying of seed.”

v19: נִדָּה (niddah)
“Menstrual uncleanness.” A ritual state associated with menstrual bleeding.

v31: נָזַר (nazar)
“To separate” or “keep apart.” To maintain distinction from uncleanness.
