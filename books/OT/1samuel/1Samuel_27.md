# 1 Samuel 27

## David Flees to the Philistines

<p>
v01: David said in his heart, “Now I will perish one day by the hand of Saul. There is nothing better for me than that I escape, escape to the land of the Philistines. Then Saul will despair of seeking me any more throughout all the territory of Israel, and I will escape from his hand.”

v02: So David arose and crossed over, he and six hundred men who were with him, to Achish son of Maoch, king of Gath.

v03: David lived with Achish at Gath, he and his men, each man with his household, and David with his two wives, Ahinoam the Jezreelite and Abigail wife of Nabal the Carmelite.

v04: It was told to Saul that David had fled to Gath, and he did not continue to seek him.
</p>

## Achish Gives David Ziklag

<p>
v05: David said to Achish, “If now I have found favor in your eyes, let them give me a place in one of the cities of the countryside, and I will live there. Why should your servant live in the royal city with you?”

v06: So Achish gave him Ziklag on that day. Therefore Ziklag has belonged to the kings of Judah to this day.

v07: The number of days that David lived in the territory of the Philistines was a year and four months.
</p>

## David Raids Southern Peoples

<p>
v08: David and his men went up and raided the Geshurites, the Girzites, and the Amalekites, for those peoples had inhabited the land from ancient times as far as Shur and as far as the land of Egypt.

v09: David struck the land and did not keep alive man or woman. He took sheep, cattle, donkeys, camels, and garments and returned and came to Achish.

v10: Achish would say, “Where have you raided today?” David would say, “Against the Negev of Judah,” or “against the Negev of the Jerahmeelites,” or “against the Negev of the Kenites.”

v11: David did not keep alive man or woman to bring to Gath, saying, “Lest they tell concerning us, saying, ‘Thus David did.’” And such was his practice all the days that he lived in the territory of the Philistines.

v12: Achish trusted David, saying, “He has surely made himself odious among his people Israel, and he shall be my servant forever.”
</p>

## Notes

v01: Despite Saul’s promise in chapter 26, David concludes inwardly that remaining within Saul’s reach will eventually kill him.

v02: David returns to Achish of Gath, but unlike chapter 21 he now arrives as leader of six hundred men and their households.

v06: The narrator’s “to this day” reflects a later period when Ziklag remained associated with Judah’s monarchy.

v08: The precise identification of the Girzites is uncertain, and some manuscripts/readings differ. TSW follows the Masoretic form.

v09–11: David’s raids include total killing of local populations specifically to prevent witnesses from exposing his deception. The narrative reports this severe violence without explicitly commending it.

v10: David tells Achish that his raids were against groups associated with Judah, creating the appearance that he has permanently alienated Israel.

v12: Achish’s trust rests on a false conclusion produced by David’s deception.

## Vocabulary

v01: נוֹאָשׁ (no'ash)
“To despair.” David expects Saul to give up the search.

v02: עָבַר (avar)
“To cross over.” David moves into Philistine territory.

v05: חֵן (chen)
“Favor.”

v06: צִקְלַג (Tsiqlag)
“Ziklag.” The town given to David.

v08: פָּשַׁט (pashat)
“To raid.”

v09: חָיָה (chayah)
“To keep alive.”

v12: בָּאַשׁ (ba'ash)
“To become odious,” literally “stink.”

v12: בָּטַח (batach)
“To trust.”
