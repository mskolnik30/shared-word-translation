## Israel Is a Spreading Vine

<p>
v01: Israel is a spreading vine;
he produces fruit for himself.
As his fruit increased,
he multiplied altars;
as his land prospered,
they made beautiful pillars.

v02: Their heart is divided;
now they will bear their guilt.
He will break down their altars;
he will destroy their pillars.
</p>

## We Have No King

<p>
v03: For now they will say,
“We have no king,
for we do not fear the LORD.
And the king—
what can he do for us?”

v04: They speak words,
swearing empty oaths,
making covenants;
so judgment springs up
like poisonous weeds
in the furrows of a field.
</p>

## The Calf of Beth-Aven

<p>
v05: The inhabitants of Samaria tremble
for the calf of Beth-Aven.
Its people mourn for it,
and its idolatrous priests cry out over it,
over its glory,
for it has departed from it.

v06: It also will be carried to Assyria
as tribute to the great king.
Ephraim will receive shame,
and Israel will be ashamed
of his counsel.

v07: Samaria’s king is cut off
like foam
on the face of water.

v08: The high places of Aven,
the sin of Israel,
will be destroyed.
Thorn and thistle
will grow over their altars.
Then they will say to the mountains,
“Cover us!”
and to the hills,
“Fall upon us!”
</p>

## From the Days of Gibeah

<p>
v09: From the days of Gibeah
you have sinned, Israel.
There they have stood.
Will not war against the children of injustice
overtake them in Gibeah?

v10: When I desire,
I will discipline them,
and peoples will be gathered against them
when they are bound
for their twofold guilt.
</p>

## Ephraim Was a Trained Heifer

<p>
v11: Ephraim was a trained heifer
that loved to thresh,
and I passed over
the beauty of her neck.
I will make Ephraim carry a rider;
Judah will plow;
Jacob will harrow for himself.

v12: Sow for yourselves righteousness;
reap according to steadfast love.
Break up for yourselves fallow ground,
for it is time to seek the LORD
until he comes
and rains righteousness upon you.
</p>

## You Have Plowed Wickedness

<p>
v13: You have plowed wickedness;
you have reaped injustice;
you have eaten the fruit of falsehood.
Because you trusted in your way,
in the multitude of your warriors,

v14: tumult will rise among your people,
and all your fortresses will be devastated,
as Shalman devastated Beth-Arbel
on the day of battle,
when mother was shattered
upon children.

v15: Thus he will do to you, Bethel,
because of your great evil.
At dawn
the king of Israel
will be utterly cut off.
</p>

## Notes

v01: Israel’s fruitfulness becomes fuel for multiplying altars rather than gratitude. Prosperity intensifies misplaced worship.

v02: “divided” can also carry the sense of smooth, deceitful, or slippery. The heart’s lack of integrity is followed by the dismantling of cultic structures.

v04: Empty covenant-making produces “judgment” like toxic growth in cultivated land. Legal and agricultural imagery are deliberately joined.

v05: “idolatrous priests” translates *kemarim*, a term used contemptuously for non-YHWH cultic priests.

v07: The Hebrew image may mean foam, chip, or twig on water. The point is the fragility and disappearance of Samaria’s king.

v08: The cry to mountains and hills expresses terror before judgment and the desire to disappear beneath creation itself.

v09: Gibeah again functions as a memory of entrenched violence. Hosea sees continuity between that earlier corruption and present Israel.

v10: “twofold guilt” is a difficult expression and may refer to two transgressions, double bondage, or another paired offense. TSW preserves the general sense without specifying the pair.

v11: Threshing was easier than plowing and allowed the animal to eat while working. Ephraim’s former preference for easier service gives way to imposed labor imagery.

v12: Agricultural repentance imagery reverses verse 13: righteousness is sown, steadfast love harvested, and hard ground broken open.

v14: Shalman and Beth-Arbel are historically uncertain. The verse assumes a remembered atrocity without identifying it further.

## Vocabulary

v01: גֶּפֶן (gefen)
“Vine.” Israel is compared with a luxuriant or spreading vine.

v02: חָלַק (chalaq)
“To divide” or “be smooth/deceitful.” Used of Israel’s heart.

v05: כְּמָרִים (kemarim)
“Idolatrous priests.” A contemptuous designation for cultic personnel.

v08: בָּמָה (bamah)
“High place.” A cultic site associated here with Israel’s sin.

v11: עֶגְלָה (eglah)
“Heifer.” Ephraim is compared with a trained young cow.

v12: נִיר (nir)
“Fallow ground.” Unplowed soil that must be broken up.

v12: צְדָקָה (tsedaqah)
“Righteousness.” What is to be sown and what the LORD will rain down.

v13: כָּזָב (kazav)
“Falsehood” or “lie.” The fruit Israel has consumed.
