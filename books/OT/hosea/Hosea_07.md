## When I Would Heal Israel

<p>
v01: When I would heal Israel,
the guilt of Ephraim is uncovered
and the evil deeds of Samaria,
for they practice falsehood.
A thief enters,
and a band raids outside.

v02: But they do not say in their hearts
that I remember all their evil.
Now their deeds surround them;
they are before my face.
</p>

## They Make the King Glad with Evil

<p>
v03: With their evil they make the king glad,
and with their lies the officials.

v04: All of them are adulterers;
they are like an oven heated by a baker
who stops stirring the fire
from kneading the dough
until it is leavened.

v05: On the day of our king
the officials became sick
with the heat of wine;
he stretched out his hand
with scoffers.

v06: For they approach with their heart
like an oven in their plotting.
All night their anger sleeps;
in the morning it burns
like a flaming fire.

v07: All of them are hot like an oven,
and they devour their rulers.
All their kings have fallen;
none of them calls to me.
</p>

## Ephraim Is a Cake Not Turned

<p>
v08: Ephraim mixes himself
among the peoples.
Ephraim is a cake
not turned.

v09: Strangers devour his strength,
but he does not know it.
Gray hairs are also sprinkled on him,
but he does not know it.

v10: The pride of Israel
testifies to his face,
yet they do not return
to the LORD their God
or seek him
for all this.
</p>

## Like a Dove without Sense

<p>
v11: Ephraim has become
like a dove, simple and without heart:
they call to Egypt;
they go to Assyria.

v12: As they go,
I will spread my net over them;
I will bring them down
like birds of the heavens.
I will discipline them
according to the report to their congregation.
</p>

## They Flee from Me

<p>
v13: Woe to them,
for they have fled from me!
Destruction to them,
for they have rebelled against me!
I would redeem them,
but they speak lies against me.

v14: They do not cry out to me
from their heart,
but they wail upon their beds.
For grain and new wine
they gather themselves;
they turn against me.

v15: Though I trained
and strengthened their arms,
they devise evil against me.

v16: They return,
but not upward.
They are like a deceptive bow.
Their officials will fall by the sword
because of the rage of their tongue.
This will be their mockery
in the land of Egypt.
</p>
##Notes

v16: “not upward” is a compressed Hebrew expression. It may mean they return, but not to the Most High, or that their turning is unreliable.

##Vocabulary

v01: רָפָא (rapha)
“To heal.” Divine restoration that also exposes sin.

v04: תַּנּוּר (tannur)
“Oven.” The repeated metaphor for burning desire and conspiracy.

v08: בָּלַל (balal)
“To mix.” Ephraim mixes himself among the peoples.

v13: פָּדָה (padah)
“To redeem.” To reclaim or rescue from bondage.
