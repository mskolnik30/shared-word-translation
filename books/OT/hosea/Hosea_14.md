## Return, Israel, to the LORD

<p>
v01: Return, Israel,
to the LORD your God,
for you have stumbled
because of your guilt.

v02: Take words with you
and return to the LORD.
Say to him:

“Take away all guilt;
receive what is good,
and we will repay
the fruit of our lips.

v03: Assyria will not save us;
we will not ride on horses.
We will no longer say, ‘Our God,’
to the work of our hands,
for in you
the orphan finds compassion.”
</p>

## I Will Heal Their Turning Away

<p>
v04: “I will heal their turning away;
I will love them freely,
for my anger
has turned away from him.

v05: I will be like dew to Israel;
he will blossom like the lily
and send out his roots
like Lebanon.

v06: His shoots will spread;
his beauty will be like the olive tree,
and his fragrance
like Lebanon.

v07: Those who dwell in his shadow
will return;
they will revive like grain
and blossom like the vine.
His remembrance will be
like the wine of Lebanon.
</p>

## What More Have I to Do with Idols?

<p>
v08: Ephraim will say,
“What more have I to do with idols?”
I myself answer him
and watch over him.
I am like a flourishing cypress;
from me
your fruit is found.
</p>

## Who Is Wise?

<p>
v09: Who is wise
and understands these things?
Who is discerning
and knows them?

For the ways of the LORD are right;
the righteous walk in them,
but transgressors
stumble in them.
</p>
##Notes

v02: “fruit of our lips” follows the Masoretic reading.

##Vocabulary

v01: שׁוּב (shuv)
“To return.” Hosea’s central verb for covenantal turning back.

v02: קָח (qach)
“Take.” Israel is told to bring words rather than empty ritual.

v02: פְּרִי שְׂפָתֵינוּ (peri sefatenu)
“Fruit of our lips.” Verbal praise, confession, or thanksgiving offered to God.

v03: רָחַם (racham)
“To have compassion.” The orphan finds mercy in the LORD.
