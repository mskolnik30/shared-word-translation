## David Sings to the LORD

<p>
v01: David spoke to the LORD the words of this song on the day the LORD rescued him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.

v02: He said:

The LORD is my rock,
my stronghold,
and my rescuer.

v03: My God is my rock;
in him I take refuge—
my shield,
the horn of my salvation,
my high fortress
and my refuge,
my savior;
you save me from violence.

v04: I call upon the LORD,
who is worthy of praise,
and I am saved
from my enemies.
</p>

## The Waves of Death Surrounded Me

<p>
v05: For the waves of death surrounded me;
torrents of destruction
terrified me.

v06: The cords of Sheol surrounded me;
the snares of death
confronted me.

v07: In my distress I called upon the LORD,
and to my God I called.
From his temple he heard my voice,
and my cry
came into his ears.
</p>

## The LORD Came Down

<p>
v08: Then the earth shook and trembled;
the foundations of the heavens quaked
and shook
because he was angry.

v09: Smoke rose from his nostrils,
and consuming fire from his mouth;
coals burned
from it.

v10: He bent the heavens
and came down,
and thick darkness
was under his feet.

v11: He rode upon a cherub
and flew;
he was seen
upon the wings of the wind.

v12: He made darkness
booths around him,
a gathering of waters,
thick clouds of the skies.

v13: From the brightness before him
coals of fire
burned.

v14: The LORD thundered
from the heavens,
and the Most High
gave his voice.

v15: He sent arrows
and scattered them,
lightning
and confused them.

v16: Then the channels of the sea appeared;
the foundations of the world
were uncovered
at the rebuke of the LORD,
at the blast
of the breath of his nostrils.
</p>

## He Drew Me Out of Great Waters

<p>
v17: He sent from on high;
he took me.
He drew me out
of great waters.

v18: He rescued me
from my strong enemy,
from those who hated me,
for they were too mighty for me.

v19: They confronted me
on the day of my disaster,
but the LORD
was my support.

v20: He brought me out
into a broad place;
he rescued me
because he delighted in me.
</p>

## The LORD Rewarded Me according to My Righteousness

<p>
v21: The LORD dealt with me
according to my righteousness;
according to the cleanness of my hands
he returned to me.

v22: For I have kept
the ways of the LORD
and have not acted wickedly
away from my God.

v23: For all his judgments
were before me,
and as for his statutes,
I did not turn away from them.

v24: I was blameless toward him,
and I guarded myself
from my guilt.

v25: So the LORD returned to me
according to my righteousness,
according to my cleanness
before his eyes.
</p>

## With the Faithful You Show Yourself Faithful

<p>
v26: With the faithful
you show yourself faithful;
with the blameless mighty one
you show yourself blameless.

v27: With the pure
you show yourself pure,
but with the twisted
you show yourself shrewd.

v28: You save
an afflicted people,
but your eyes are upon the proud
to bring them low.

v29: For you are my lamp, O LORD,
and the LORD illumines
my darkness.

v30: For by you
I run against a troop;
by my God
I leap over a wall.
</p>

## The Word of the LORD Is Refined

<p>
v31: As for God,
his way is blameless.
The word of the LORD
is refined.
He is a shield
to all who take refuge in him.

v32: For who is God
besides the LORD?
And who is a rock
besides our God?

v33: God is my strong fortress,
and he makes my way
blameless.

v34: He makes my feet
like the feet of deer
and makes me stand
upon my heights.

v35: He trains my hands for battle,
so that my arms
bend a bow of bronze.

v36: You have given me
the shield of your salvation,
and your answering care
has made me great.

v37: You make a broad place
for my steps beneath me,
and my ankles
do not slip.
</p>

## I Pursued My Enemies

<p>
v38: I pursued my enemies
and destroyed them,
and I did not turn back
until they were consumed.

v39: I consumed them
and shattered them
so that they did not rise;
they fell
under my feet.

v40: You girded me with strength
for battle;
you made those who rose against me
bow beneath me.

v41: You made my enemies
turn their backs to me,
and those who hated me
I destroyed.

v42: They looked,
but there was no one to save;
to the LORD,
but he did not answer them.

v43: I beat them fine
like the dust of the earth;
like mud of the streets
I crushed them
and stamped them down.
</p>

## You Have Kept Me as Head of Nations

<p>
v44: You rescued me
from the disputes of my people;
you kept me
as head of nations.
A people I had not known
served me.

v45: Foreigners came cringing to me;
as soon as their ear heard,
they obeyed me.

v46: Foreigners faded
and came trembling
from their strongholds.
</p>

## The LORD Lives

<p>
v47: The LORD lives!
Blessed be my rock!
Exalted be God,
the rock of my salvation—

v48: the God who gives
vengeance for me
and brings peoples
down beneath me,

v49: who brings me out
from my enemies.
You exalted me
above those who rose against me;
from the man of violence
you rescued me.
</p>

## I Will Praise Your Name among the Nations

<p>
v50: Therefore I will give thanks to you, O LORD,
among the nations,
and to your name
I will sing.

v51: He is a tower of salvation
for his king
and shows steadfast love
to his anointed,
to David and to his offspring
forever.
</p>
##Notes

v01: This song closely parallels Psalm 18 but contains numerous small textual and stylistic differences.

v17: “drew me out” sounds like Moses’ name in Hebrew, though the text does not explicitly make the wordplay.

##Vocabulary

v03: קֶרֶן יִשְׁעִי (qeren yishi)
“Horn of my salvation.” Strength pictured as an animal’s horn.

v06: שְׁאוֹל (Sheol)
“Sheol.” The realm of the dead.

v26: חָסִיד (chasid)
“Faithful” or “loyal.” One characterized by steadfast covenant devotion.

v51: מָשִׁיחַ (mashiach)
“Anointed one.” The king set apart by God.

v51: חֶסֶד (ḥesed)
“Steadfast love.” The enduring covenant commitment to David’s line.
