## David Sings to the LORD

<p>
v01: David spoke to the LORD the words of this song on the day the LORD rescued him from the hand of all his enemies and from the hand of Saul.

v02: He said:

The LORD is my rock,  
my stronghold,  
and my rescuer.

v03: My God is my rock;  
in him I take refuge—  
my shield,  
the horn of my salvation,  
my high fortress  
and my refuge,  
my savior;  
you save me from violence.

v04: I call upon the LORD,  
who is worthy of praise,  
and I am saved  
from my enemies.
</p>

## The Waves of Death Surrounded Me

<p>
v05: For the waves of death surrounded me;  
torrents of destruction  
terrified me.

v06: The cords of Sheol surrounded me;  
the snares of death  
confronted me.

v07: In my distress I called upon the LORD,  
and to my God I called.  
From his temple he heard my voice,  
and my cry  
came into his ears.
</p>

## The LORD Came Down

<p>
v08: Then the earth shook and trembled;  
the foundations of the heavens quaked  
and shook  
because he was angry.

v09: Smoke rose from his nostrils,  
and consuming fire from his mouth;  
coals burned  
from it.

v10: He bent the heavens  
and came down,  
and thick darkness  
was under his feet.

v11: He rode upon a cherub  
and flew;  
he was seen  
upon the wings of the wind.

v12: He made darkness  
booths around him,  
a gathering of waters,  
thick clouds of the skies.

v13: From the brightness before him  
coals of fire  
burned.

v14: The LORD thundered  
from the heavens,  
and the Most High  
gave his voice.

v15: He sent arrows  
and scattered them,  
lightning  
and confused them.

v16: Then the channels of the sea appeared;  
the foundations of the world  
were uncovered  
at the rebuke of the LORD,  
at the blast  
of the breath of his nostrils.
</p>

## He Drew Me Out of Great Waters

<p>
v17: He sent from on high;  
he took me.  
He drew me out  
of great waters.

v18: He rescued me  
from my strong enemy,  
from those who hated me,  
for they were too mighty for me.

v19: They confronted me  
on the day of my disaster,  
but the LORD  
was my support.

v20: He brought me out  
into a broad place;  
he rescued me  
because he delighted in me.
</p>

## The LORD Rewarded Me according to My Righteousness

<p>
v21: The LORD dealt with me  
according to my righteousness;  
according to the cleanness of my hands  
he returned to me.

v22: For I have kept  
the ways of the LORD  
and have not acted wickedly  
away from my God.

v23: For all his judgments  
were before me,  
and as for his statutes,  
I did not turn away from them.

v24: I was blameless toward him,  
and I guarded myself  
from my guilt.

v25: So the LORD returned to me  
according to my righteousness,  
according to my cleanness  
before his eyes.
</p>

## With the Faithful You Show Yourself Faithful

<p>
v26: With the faithful  
you show yourself faithful;  
with the blameless mighty one  
you show yourself blameless.

v27: With the pure  
you show yourself pure,  
but with the twisted  
you show yourself shrewd.

v28: You save  
an afflicted people,  
but your eyes are upon the proud  
to bring them low.

v29: For you are my lamp, O LORD,  
and the LORD illumines  
my darkness.

v30: For by you  
I run against a troop;  
by my God  
I leap over a wall.
</p>

## The Word of the LORD Is Refined

<p>
v31: As for God,  
his way is blameless.  
The word of the LORD  
is refined.  
He is a shield  
to all who take refuge in him.

v32: For who is God  
besides the LORD?  
And who is a rock  
besides our God?

v33: God is my strong fortress,  
and he makes my way  
blameless.

v34: He makes my feet  
like the feet of deer  
and makes me stand  
upon my heights.

v35: He trains my hands for battle,  
so that my arms  
bend a bow of bronze.

v36: You have given me  
the shield of your salvation,  
and your answering care  
has made me great.

v37: You make a broad place  
for my steps beneath me,  
and my ankles  
do not slip.
</p>

## I Pursued My Enemies

<p>
v38: I pursued my enemies  
and destroyed them,  
and I did not turn back  
until they were consumed.

v39: I consumed them  
and shattered them  
so that they did not rise;  
they fell  
under my feet.

v40: You girded me with strength  
for battle;  
you made those who rose against me  
bow beneath me.

v41: You made my enemies  
turn their backs to me,  
and those who hated me  
I destroyed.

v42: They looked,  
but there was no one to save;  
to the LORD,  
but he did not answer them.

v43: I beat them fine  
like the dust of the earth;  
like mud of the streets  
I crushed them  
and stamped them down.
</p>

## You Have Kept Me as Head of Nations

<p>
v44: You rescued me  
from the disputes of my people;  
you kept me  
as head of nations.  
A people I had not known  
served me.

v45: Foreigners came cringing to me;  
as soon as their ear heard,  
they obeyed me.

v46: Foreigners faded  
and came trembling  
from their strongholds.
</p>

## The LORD Lives

<p>
v47: The LORD lives!  
Blessed be my rock!  
Exalted be God,  
the rock of my salvation—

v48: the God who gives  
vengeance for me  
and brings peoples  
down beneath me,

v49: who brings me out  
from my enemies.  
You exalted me  
above those who rose against me;  
from the man of violence  
you rescued me.
</p>

## I Will Praise Your Name among the Nations

<p>
v50: Therefore I will give thanks to you, O LORD,  
among the nations,  
and to your name  
I will sing.

v51: He is a tower of salvation  
for his king  
and shows steadfast love  
to his anointed,  
to David and to his offspring  
forever.
</p>

##Notes

v01: This song closely parallels Psalm 18 but contains numerous small textual and stylistic differences. TSW translates 2 Samuel’s received form rather than importing the Psalm wording.

v02–03: Rock, stronghold, shield, horn, fortress, refuge, and savior accumulate military and topographic images of protection.

v05–06: Death and Sheol are personified as waters, cords, and traps enclosing David.

v08–16: The divine appearance uses storm-theophany imagery: earthquake, smoke, fire, darkness, cherub, wind, thunder, lightning, and exposed sea channels.

v11: The LORD rides on a cherub, imagery associated elsewhere with the divine throne and sanctuary.

v12: “booths around him” is a distinctive Samuel reading where Psalm 18 has darkness as a covering. The Hebrew wording is difficult, and TSW preserves the shelter image.

v17: “drew me out” sounds like Moses’ name in Hebrew, though the text does not explicitly make the wordplay.

v21–25: David speaks of righteousness and clean hands in the song’s poetic perspective. Read within Samuel’s larger narrative, these claims cannot mean moral perfection; they describe covenant integrity in the conflicts from which he was delivered.

v26–27: The repeated reflexive pattern describes God responding correspondingly to human character, while the final line portrays divine shrewdness toward the twisted.

v29: The LORD as David’s “lamp” resonates with David himself being called Israel’s lamp in 21:17.

v31: “refined” pictures metal tested and purified by fire, applied to the LORD’s word.

v36: The Hebrew phrase translated “your answering care” is difficult and differs from Psalm 18’s “your gentleness.” TSW follows the Samuel consonants in a relational sense without claiming certainty.

v42: Enemies cry even to the LORD without answer, emphasizing that invoking God is not a guarantee of rescue apart from right relation.

v44: David’s deliverance extends from internal disputes to international rule, joining national kingship and imperial reach.

v48: The song celebrates divine vengeance in the context of David’s enemies. TSW preserves the royal-warrior theology without making personal vengeance a general command.

v51: The song closes by linking David’s deliverance to the enduring covenant with his offspring from chapter 7.

##Vocabulary

v02: מְצוּדָה (metsudah)
“Stronghold.” A fortified place of refuge.

v03: קֶרֶן יִשְׁעִי (qeren yishi)
“Horn of my salvation.” Strength pictured as an animal’s horn.

v05: מִשְׁבְּרֵי מָוֶת (mishbere mavet)
“Waves of death.” Overwhelming mortal danger.

v06: שְׁאוֹל (Sheol)
“Sheol.” The realm of the dead.

v11: כְּרוּב (keruv)
“Cherub.” A heavenly throne-being.

v17: מָשָׁה (mashah)
“To draw out.” David is drawn from great waters.

v26: חָסִיד (chasid)
“Faithful” or “loyal.” One characterized by steadfast covenant devotion.

v31: צָרַף (tsaraf)
“To refine.” To test and purify like metal.

v34: אַיָּלָה (ayyalah)
“Deer” or “doe.” An image of sure-footed movement.

v47: צוּר (tsur)
“Rock.” A recurring divine title.

v51: מָשִׁיחַ (mashiach)
“Anointed one.” The king set apart by God.

v51: חֶסֶד (ḥesed)
“Steadfast love.” The enduring covenant commitment to David’s line.
