## The Day of the LORD
<p>
v01: “For behold, the day is coming,
burning like a furnace,
when all the arrogant
and all evildoers
will be stubble.
The day that is coming shall set them ablaze,”
says the LORD of Hosts,
“so that it will leave them
neither root nor branch.
</p>

<p>
v02: But for you who fear my name,
the sun of righteousness shall rise
with healing in its wings.
And you shall go out
and leap like calves from the stall.
v03: You shall tread down the wicked,
for they shall be ashes
under the soles of your feet
on the day when I act,”
says the LORD of Hosts.
</p>

## Remember the Law
<p>
v04: “Remember the law of Moses my servant,
the statutes and judgments
that I commanded him at Horeb
for all Israel.
</p>

## Elijah Will Come
<p>
v05: Behold, I am sending to you Elijah the prophet
before the coming of the great and fearful day of the LORD.
v06: And he will turn the hearts of fathers to their children
and the hearts of children to their fathers,
lest I come
and strike the land
with a decree of destruction.”
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v02: שֶׁמֶשׁ צְדָקָה (šemeš ṣĕḏāqâ)
“Sun of righteousness.” An image of God's righteous and healing deliverance.

v04: תּוֹרָה (tôrâ)
“Law” or “instruction.” God's covenant teaching given through Moses.

v04: חֹק וּמִשְׁפָּט (ḥōq ûmišpāṭ)
“Statute and judgment.” Covenant commands and legal rulings.

v05: אֵלִיָּהוּ (ʾĒliyyāhû)
“Elijah.” The prophet associated with covenant faithfulness and repentance.
