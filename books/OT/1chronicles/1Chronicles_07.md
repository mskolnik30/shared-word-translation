<p>
v01: The sons of Issachar: Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron—four.
</p>
<p>
v02: The sons of Tola: Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Samuel, heads of their ancestral houses of Tola, warriors of strength. Their number in the days of David, by their generations, was twenty-two thousand six hundred.

v03: The son of Uzzi: Izrahiah. The sons of Izrahiah: Michael, Obadiah, Joel, and Isshiah—five, all of them heads.

v04: Along with them, by their generations and their ancestral houses, were divisions of an army for war: thirty-six thousand, for they had many wives and children.

v05: Their kindred among all the clans of Issachar were warriors of strength: eighty-seven thousand, enrolled in all.

v08: The sons of Becher: Zemirah, Joash, Eliezer, Elioenai, Omri, Jeremoth, Abijah, Anathoth, and Alemeth. All these were sons of Becher.

v09: Their genealogical enrollment, by their generations, listed twenty thousand two hundred heads of ancestral houses, warriors of strength.
</p>

v10: The sons of Jediael: Bilhan. The sons of Bilhan: Jeush, Benjamin, Ehud, Kenaanah, Zethan, Tarshish, and Ahishahar.

v11: All these were sons of Jediael, heads of ancestral houses, warriors of strength—seventeen thousand two hundred able to go out to war.
</p>
<p>
v12: Shuppim and Huppim were sons of Ir; Hushim was the son of Aher.
</p>
<p>
v13: The sons of Naphtali: Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, and Shallum, sons of Bilhah.
</p>

v15: Machir took a wife for Huppim and Shuppim; the name of his sister was Maacah. The name of the second was Zelophehad, and Zelophehad had daughters.

v17: The son of Ulam: Bedan. These were the sons of Gilead, son of Machir, son of Manasseh.

v18: His sister Hammoleketh bore Ishhod, Abiezer, and Mahlah.

v19: The sons of Shemida were Ahian, Shechem, Likhi, and Aniam.
</p>

v21: Zabad his son, Shuthelah his son, and Ezer and Elead. The men of Gath who were born in the land killed them, because they went down to take their livestock.

v22: Ephraim their father mourned many days, and his kindred came to comfort him.

v23: Then he went in to his wife, and she conceived and bore a son. He named him Beriah, because calamity had come upon his house.

v24: His daughter was Sheerah. She built Lower Beth-horon and Upper Beth-horon, and Uzzen-sheerah.

v26: Ladan his son, Ammihud his son, Elishama his son,

##Notes

v01: The written form of Jashub’s name differs from its traditional reading. The translation follows the reading indicated by the received Hebrew tradition.

##Vocabulary

v02: תּוֹלְדוֹת (toledot)
“generations” or “family records.” Here it organizes Tola’s descendants as a recognized ancestral line and provides the framework for the military count.

v02: גִּבּוֹרֵי חַיִל (gibbore chayil)
“warriors of strength.” The expression denotes capable, valiant fighting men and recurs throughout the chapter as an honorific for tribal households.

v05: הִתְיַחֵשׂ (hityaches)
“to enroll by genealogy.” The term describes formal registration according to family descent, linking belonging within Israel to a recorded lineage.

v28: אֲחֻזָּה (achuzzah)
“possession” or “holding.” It refers to land held as an inherited territorial claim, not simply a place of residence.

v40: בְּרוּרִים (berurim)
“chosen” or “selected.” The Asherites are presented as distinguished warriors and leading household heads, set apart within the tribal register.
