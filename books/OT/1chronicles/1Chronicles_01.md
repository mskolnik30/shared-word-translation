## From Adam to Noah

<p>
v01: Adam, Seth, Enosh,

v02: Kenan, Mahalalel, Jared,

v03: Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

v04: Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

</p>

## The Descendants of Japheth

<p>
v05: The sons of Japheth: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

v06: The sons of Gomer: Ashkenaz, Diphath, and Togarmah.

v07: The sons of Javan: Elishah, Tarshish, Kittim, and Rodanim.

</p>

## The Descendants of Ham

<p>
v08: The sons of Ham: Cush, Egypt, Put, and Canaan.

v09: The sons of Cush: Seba, Havilah, Sabta, Raama, and Sabteca. The sons of Raama: Sheba and Dedan.

v10: Cush fathered Nimrod. He was the first to become mighty upon the earth.

</p>
<p>
v11: Egypt fathered Ludim, Anamim, Lehabim, Naphtuhim,

v12: Pathrusim, Casluhim—from whom the Philistines came—and Caphtorim.

</p>
<p>
v13: Canaan fathered Sidon his firstborn, and Heth,

v14: the Jebusite, the Amorite, the Girgashite,

v15: the Hivite, the Arkite, the Sinite,

v16: the Arvadite, the Zemarite, and the Hamathite.

</p>

## The Descendants of Shem

<p>
v17: The sons of Shem: Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech.

v18: Arpachshad fathered Shelah, and Shelah fathered Eber.

v19: Two sons were born to Eber. The name of the one was Peleg, for in his days the earth was divided; and his brother’s name was Joktan.

</p>
<p>
v20: Joktan fathered Almodad, Sheleph, Hazarmaveth, Jerah,

v21: Hadoram, Uzal, Diklah,

v22: Ebal, Abimael, Sheba,

v23: Ophir, Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

</p>
<p>
v24: Shem, Arpachshad, Shelah,

v25: Eber, Peleg, Reu,

v26: Serug, Nahor, Terah,

v27: Abram, that is, Abraham.

</p>

The Descendants of Abraham

<p>
v28: The sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ishmael.
</p>

## Ishmael

<p>
v29: These are their generations: Nebaioth, the firstborn of Ishmael, then Kedar, Adbeel, Mibsam,

v30: Mishma, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema,

v31: Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These were the sons of Ishmael.

</p>

## Keturah

<p>
v32: The sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bore Zimran, Jokshan, Medan, Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan: Sheba and Dedan.

v33: The sons of Midian: Ephah, Epher, Hanoch, Abida, and Eldaah. All these were the sons of Keturah.

</p>
<p>
v34: Abraham fathered Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel.
</p>

## The Descendants of Esau

<p>
v35: The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Reuel, Jeush, Jalam, and Korah.

v36: The sons of Eliphaz: Teman, Omar, Zephi, Gatam, Kenaz, Timna, and Amalek.

v37: The sons of Reuel: Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

</p>

## The Descendants of Seir

<p>
v38: The sons of Seir: Lotan, Shobal, Zibeon, Anah, Dishon, Ezer, and Dishan.

v39: The sons of Lotan: Hori and Homam. Lotan’s sister was Timna.

v40: The sons of Shobal: Alian, Manahath, Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. The sons of Zibeon: Aiah and Anah.

v41: The sons of Anah: Dishon. The sons of Dishon: Hamran, Eshban, Ithran, and Cheran.

v42: The sons of Ezer: Bilhan, Zaavan, and Akan. The sons of Dishan: Uz and Aran.

</p>

## The Kings of Edom

<p>
v43: These are the kings who reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the Israelites: Bela son of Beor, and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

v44: Bela died, and Jobab son of Zerah from Bozrah reigned in his place.

v45: Jobab died, and Husham from the land of the Temanites reigned in his place.

v46: Husham died, and Hadad son of Bedad reigned in his place. He struck down Midian in the field of Moab, and the name of his city was Avith.

v47: Hadad died, and Samlah from Masrekah reigned in his place.

v48: Samlah died, and Shaul from Rehoboth by the River reigned in his place.

v49: Shaul died, and Baal-hanan son of Achbor reigned in his place.

v50: Baal-hanan died, and Hadad reigned in his place. The name of his city was Pai, and his wife’s name was Mehetabel daughter of Matred, daughter of Me-zahab.

</p>

## The Chiefs of Edom

<p>
v51: Hadad died. The chiefs of Edom were chief Timna, chief Aliah, chief Jetheth,

v52: chief Oholibamah, chief Elah, chief Pinon,

v53: chief Kenaz, chief Teman, chief Mibzar,

v54: chief Magdiel, and chief Iram. These were the chiefs of Edom.

</p>

## Notes
v01–04: The genealogy begins with Adam and moves directly to Noah. Israel’s story is placed within the story of all humanity before it narrows to a particular family.

v05–23: The descendants of Noah are organized by family lines and peoples. The chapter presents the nations as related branches of a common human ancestry.

v10: Nimrod receives a brief narrative note in the midst of the genealogy. His prominence is remembered, but the Chronicler resists expanding the story.

v19: Peleg’s name is linked to a division of the earth. The text does not explain the nature of the division, allowing the ambiguity to remain.

v24–27: The genealogy accelerates from Shem to Abraham. The movement narrows from the nations to the covenant family.

v28–33: Ishmael and Keturah’s descendants are remembered alongside Isaac’s line. The Chronicler preserves the wider family history before focusing on Israel.

v34: Jacob is identified by the name Israel. The covenant identity becomes the organizing thread for the generations that follow.

v35–42: Esau’s descendants are listed in detail. Edom remains closely connected to Israel through shared ancestry.

v43–50: The kings of Edom are remembered before Israel’s monarchy existed. The note highlights Edom’s early political development without assigning superiority.

v51–54: The chapter closes with clan chiefs rather than kings. Leadership in Edom is portrayed through both royal and tribal structures.

## Vocabulary

v01: אָדָם (adam)

“Adam” or “human.” The name stands at the head of both humanity’s story and Israel’s genealogy.

v10: גִּבּוֹר (gibbor)

“Mighty one.” A term describing strength, prominence, or exceptional power.

v18: עֵבֶר (ever)

“Eber.” The ancestor from whom the designation “Hebrew” is traditionally connected.

v19: פֶּלֶג (peleg)

“Peleg.” The name is associated with division or splitting, reflecting the explanation given in the text.

v27: אַבְרָהָם (avraham)

“Abraham.” The covenant patriarch through whom the genealogy narrows toward Israel.

v34: יִשְׂרָאֵל (yisra’el)

“Israel.” Jacob’s covenant name, which becomes the name of the people descended from him.

v43: מֶלֶךְ (melekh)

“King.” A ruler exercising recognized authority over a people or territory.

v51: אַלּוּף (alluph)

“Chief.” A clan leader or tribal head, especially characteristic of Edom.
