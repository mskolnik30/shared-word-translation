## Whoever Loves Discipline Loves Knowledge

<p>
v01: Whoever loves discipline
loves knowledge,
but whoever hates correction
is senseless.

v02: A good person obtains favor
from the LORD,
but one who devises evil
the LORD condemns.

v03: No one is established by wickedness,
but the root of the righteous
will not be moved.

v04: A capable spouse is a crown
to their partner,
but one who brings shame
is like decay in the bones.
</p>

<p>
v05: The thoughts of the righteous are just,
but the guidance of the wicked
is deceitful.

v06: The words of the wicked
lie in wait for blood,
but the mouth of the upright
delivers them.

v07: The wicked are overthrown
and are no more,
but the house of the righteous
will stand.

v08: A person is praised
according to their insight,
but one with a twisted heart
is despised.
</p>

## Diligence and Care

<p>
v09: Better to be lightly regarded
and have a servant
than to honor oneself
and lack bread.

v10: The righteous know
the life of their animals,
but the compassion of the wicked
is cruel.

v11: Whoever works their land
will have plenty of bread,
but whoever pursues empty things
lacks sense.

v12: The wicked desire
the catch of evil people,
but the root of the righteous
produces fruit.
</p>

<p>
v13: An evil person is trapped
by the transgression of their lips,
but the righteous escape trouble.

v14: From the fruit of the mouth
a person is filled with good,
and the work of a person’s hands
returns to them.

v15: The way of a fool
is right in their own eyes,
but the wise listen to counsel.

v16: A fool’s anger is known at once,
but the prudent overlook an insult.
</p>

## Truthful Speech

<p>
v17: Whoever speaks truth
declares what is right,
but a false witness
speaks deceit.

v18: There is one whose reckless speech
is like the thrusts of a sword,
but the tongue of the wise
brings healing.

v19: Truthful lips endure forever,
but a lying tongue
only for a moment.

v20: Deceit is in the heart
of those who devise evil,
but those who counsel peace
have joy.
</p>

<p>
v21: No harm overtakes the righteous,
but the wicked are filled
with trouble.

v22: Lying lips
are an abomination to the LORD,
but those who act faithfully
are the LORD’s delight.

v23: A prudent person conceals knowledge,
but the heart of fools
proclaims folly.

v24: The hand of the diligent will rule,
but the slack hand
will be put to forced labor.
</p>

## Anxiety and Encouragement

<p>
v25: Anxiety in a person’s heart
weighs it down,
but a good word
makes it glad.

v26: The righteous guide their neighbor carefully,
but the way of the wicked
leads them astray.

v27: A lazy person does not roast
what they have hunted,
but diligence is
a precious possession.

v28: In the path of righteousness
is life,
and along its pathway
there is no death.
</p>
##Notes

v04: “capable spouse” — The Hebrew literally says “a woman of strength.” The immediate reference is to a wife, though the quality being praised is capability, strength, and worthy character.

v09: The Hebrew has a textual and interpretive difficulty concerning whether the person is “lightly regarded” or presents themselves as insignificant.

v11: “empty things” — The Hebrew can refer to worthless pursuits or people.

##Vocabulary

v01: מוּסָר (musar)
“Discipline,” “instruction,” or “correction.” Formation that includes receiving correction.

v01: בַּעַר (baar)
“To be senseless” or “brutish.” To act without the discernment expected of a rational person.

v20: שָׁלוֹם (shalom)
“Peace,” “wholeness,” or “well-being.” The goal intentionally pursued by wise counselors.

v28: צְדָקָה (tsedaqah)
“Righteousness.” Right conduct and relationship, pictured here as a path leading to life.
