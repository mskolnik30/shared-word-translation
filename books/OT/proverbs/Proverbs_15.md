## A Gentle Answer

<p>
v01: A gentle answer turns away wrath,
but a harsh word
stirs up anger.

v02: The tongue of the wise
makes knowledge good,
but the mouth of fools
pours out folly.

v03: The eyes of the LORD
are in every place,
watching the evil
and the good.

v04: A healing tongue
is a tree of life,
but perversity in it
breaks the spirit.

v05: A fool despises
a father’s discipline,
but whoever heeds correction
is prudent.
</p>

## The LORD Knows the Heart

<p>
v06: In the house of the righteous
there is great treasure,
but the income of the wicked
brings trouble.

v07: The lips of the wise
scatter knowledge,
but the hearts of fools
do not.

v08: The sacrifice of the wicked
is an abomination to the LORD,
but the prayer of the upright
is the LORD’s delight.

v09: The way of the wicked
is an abomination to the LORD,
but the LORD loves
the one who pursues righteousness.

v10: Severe discipline awaits
the one who abandons the path;
whoever hates correction
will die.
</p>

<p>
v11: Sheol and Abaddon
lie open before the LORD;
how much more
the hearts of human beings!

v12: A scoffer does not love
the one who corrects them;
they will not go
to the wise.

v13: A joyful heart
makes the face glad,
but sorrow of heart
crushes the spirit.

v14: The heart of the discerning
seeks knowledge,
but the mouth of fools
feeds on folly.

v15: All the days of the afflicted are difficult,
but a good heart
has a continual feast.
</p>

## Better with the Fear of the LORD

<p>
v16: Better is a little
with the fear of the LORD
than great treasure
with turmoil.

v17: Better is a meal of vegetables
where there is love
than a fattened ox
with hatred.

v18: A hot-tempered person
stirs up conflict,
but one slow to anger
calms a quarrel.

v19: The way of the lazy
is like a hedge of thorns,
but the path of the upright
is a level road.

v20: A wise child makes a father glad,
but a foolish person
despises their mother.
</p>

## The Path of Life

<p>
v21: Folly is joy
to one lacking sense,
but a person of understanding
walks straight ahead.

v22: Plans fail
where there is no counsel,
but with many advisers
they are established.

v23: A person has joy
in an appropriate answer,
and a word at the right time—
how good it is!

v24: The path of life
leads upward for the prudent,
turning them away
from Sheol below.

v25: The LORD tears down
the house of the proud
but establishes
the boundary of the widow.
</p>

<p>
v26: Evil plans
are an abomination to the LORD,
but gracious words
are pure.

v27: Whoever is greedy for unjust gain
troubles their own household,
but whoever hates bribes
will live.

v28: The heart of the righteous
considers how to answer,
but the mouth of the wicked
pours out evil.

v29: The LORD is far from the wicked,
but hears
the prayer of the righteous.

v30: The light of the eyes
makes the heart glad,
and good news
strengthens the bones.

v31: The ear that listens
to life-giving correction
will dwell among the wise.

v32: Whoever rejects discipline
despises their own life,
but whoever listens to correction
gains understanding.

v33: The fear of the LORD
is instruction in wisdom,
and before honor
comes humility.
</p>
## Notes
v01: The proverb does not merely commend being quiet. It contrasts speech capable of de-escalating anger with speech that intensifies it.  
v03: Divine awareness encompasses both evil and good. Nothing in the moral life lies outside the LORD’s attention.  
v08: Religious sacrifice cannot substitute for upright life. The prayer of the upright is preferred to worship offered from wickedness.  
v11: “Abaddon” means destruction and is paired with Sheol as a poetic designation of the realm of death.  
v16–17: Both sayings relativize material abundance. Reverence and love can make little preferable to wealth accompanied by fear or hatred.  
v24: Wisdom is again presented as directional movement: life goes upward, away from the realm of death below.  
v25: The LORD’s opposition to pride is paired with protection of a widow’s land boundary, a concrete matter of economic vulnerability.  
v30: Bodily imagery describes the effect of encouragement. Good news reaches beyond thought into the whole person.  

## Vocabulary

v01: רַךְ (rakh)  
“Gentle” or “soft.” Here describing speech capable of turning aside wrath.  

v04: מַרְפֵּא (marpe)  
“Healing.” What restores health or wholeness.  

v11: אֲבַדּוֹן (Abaddon)
“Destruction.” A poetic term associated with death and the place of the dead.

v16: מְהוּמָה (mehumah)
“Turmoil” or “confusion.” Disturbance that can accompany abundance without peace.

v22: סוֹד (sod)  
“Counsel” or “confidential deliberation.” Shared planning among trusted advisers.  

v24: אֹרַח חַיִּים (orach chayyim)  
“Path of life.” A way of living that moves away from death.  

v27: בֶּצַע (betsa)  
“Unjust gain.” Profit obtained through exploitation or wrongful advantage.  

v33: עֲנָוָה (anavah)  
“Humility.” Lowliness or modesty that precedes true honor.
