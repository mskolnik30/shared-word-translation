## The LORD Establishes the Way

<p>
v01: The plans of the heart  
belong to a person,  
but the answer of the tongue  
comes from the LORD.

v02: All a person’s ways  
are pure in their own eyes,  
but the LORD weighs  
the spirits.

v03: Commit your works to the LORD,  
and your plans  
will be established.

v04: The LORD has made everything  
for its purpose,  
even the wicked  
for a day of trouble.

v05: Everyone proud in heart  
is an abomination to the LORD;  
be assured,  
they will not go unpunished.
</p>

<p>
v06: Through steadfast love and faithfulness  
guilt is covered,  
and through the fear of the LORD  
one turns away from evil.

v07: When a person’s ways  
please the LORD,  
even their enemies  
are made to be at peace with them.

v08: Better is a little  
with righteousness  
than abundant income  
without justice.

v09: A person’s heart plans their way,  
but the LORD  
establishes their steps.

v10: An oracle is on the lips of a king;  
in judgment  
his mouth must not betray justice.
</p>

## Honest Measures and Wise Rule

<p>
v11: Honest balances and scales  
belong to the LORD;  
all the weights in the bag  
are the LORD’s concern.

v12: Doing wickedness  
is an abomination to kings,  
for a throne is established  
through righteousness.

v13: Righteous lips  
are a king’s delight,  
and the king loves  
one who speaks what is upright.

v14: A king’s wrath  
is a messenger of death,  
but a wise person  
will appease it.

v15: In the light of a king’s face  
there is life,  
and the king’s favor  
is like a cloud of spring rain.
</p>

## Wisdom Is Better Than Gold

<p>
v16: How much better  
to acquire wisdom than gold!  
To acquire understanding  
is preferable to silver.

v17: The highway of the upright  
turns away from evil;  
whoever guards their way  
protects their life.

v18: Pride comes before destruction,  
and a haughty spirit  
before a fall.

v19: Better to be lowly in spirit  
with the poor  
than to divide plunder  
with the proud.

v20: Whoever pays attention to a matter  
will find good,  
and blessed is the one  
who trusts in the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v21: The wise in heart  
are called discerning,  
and sweetness of speech  
increases persuasiveness.

v22: Understanding is a fountain of life  
to the one who possesses it,  
but the discipline of fools  
is folly.

v23: The heart of the wise  
makes their mouth discerning  
and adds persuasiveness  
to their lips.

v24: Pleasant words  
are a honeycomb,  
sweet to the soul  
and healing to the body.

v25: There is a way  
that seems right to a person,  
but its end  
is the way of death.
</p>

## The Power of Speech and Self-Control

<p>
v26: A worker’s appetite  
works for them,  
for their mouth  
urges them onward.

v27: A worthless person  
digs up evil,  
and their speech  
is like scorching fire.

v28: A perverse person  
sows conflict,  
and a gossip  
separates close friends.

v29: A violent person  
entices a neighbor  
and leads them  
into a way that is not good.

v30: One who closes their eyes  
is planning perversity;  
one who purses the lips  
brings evil to pass.
</p>

<p>
v31: Gray hair is a crown of glory  
when it is found  
in the way of righteousness.

v32: One slow to anger  
is better than a warrior,  
and one who rules their spirit  
than one who captures a city.

v33: The lot is cast into the lap,  
but every decision from it  
is from the LORD.
</p>

## Notes

v01–03: Human planning and divine sovereignty are placed beside one another without eliminating either. People plan and work; the LORD remains decisive.

v04: The proverb affirms divine ordering even in relation to the wicked without saying that wickedness itself is morally created or commanded by God.

v06: “steadfast love and faithfulness” can describe faithful covenantal conduct. The verse connects restored relationship with a life that turns from evil.

v10: The king’s authority creates heightened responsibility for justice. “Oracle” does not make every royal statement divinely inspired.

v11: Honest commerce belongs to the LORD. Even ordinary weights used in trade fall within divine moral concern.

v18: The familiar saying places collapse after self-exaltation. Pride distorts judgment before destruction becomes visible.

v32: Mastery of one’s own anger is valued above military conquest.

v33: Even a practice that appears random remains, in the proverb’s theological vision, under the LORD’s sovereignty.

## Vocabulary

v01: מַעֲרָךְ (maarakh)
“Plan” or “arrangement.” An ordering formed within the heart.

v02: תָּכַן (takhan)
“To weigh” or “measure.” To evaluate accurately, here used of the LORD examining spirits.

v03: גָּלַל (galal)
“To roll.” In the idiom “commit,” one rolls one’s work onto the LORD.

v06: אֱמֶת (emet)
“Faithfulness” or “truth.” Reliability expressed in relationship and conduct.

v11: מֹאזְנַיִם (moznayim)
“Balances” or “scales.” Instruments of trade whose honesty is a matter of justice.

v18: גָּאוֹן (gaon)
“Pride” or “exaltation.” Self-elevation preceding destruction.

v24: מַרְפֵּא (marpe)
“Healing.” Restoration or health.

v32: מָשַׁל (mashal)
“To rule.” Here used of governing one’s own spirit.
