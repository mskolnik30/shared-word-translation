## Wisdom Has Built Her House

<p>
v01: Wisdom has built her house;
she has hewn out her seven pillars.

v02: She has slaughtered her animals;
she has mixed her wine;
she has also set her table.

v03: She has sent out her young women;
she calls from the highest places of the city:

v04: “Whoever is simple,
turn in here!”

To the one lacking sense she says,

v05: “Come, eat of my bread
and drink of the wine I have mixed.

v06: Leave behind simplicity and live;
walk in the way of understanding.”
</p>

## The Wise Receive Correction

<p>
v07: Whoever corrects a scoffer
receives abuse,
and whoever rebukes the wicked
receives injury.

v08: Do not rebuke a scoffer,
or the scoffer will hate you;
rebuke the wise,
and they will love you.

v09: Give instruction to the wise,
and they will become still wiser;
teach the righteous,
and they will increase in learning.
</p>

## The Beginning of Wisdom

<p>
v10: The fear of the LORD
is the beginning of wisdom,
and knowledge of the Holy One
is understanding.

v11: For through me your days will be multiplied,
and years will be added to your life.

v12: If you are wise,
you are wise for yourself;
and if you scoff,
you alone will bear it.
</p>

## Folly Calls from Her Door

<p>
v13: Woman Folly is loud;
she is simple
and knows nothing.

v14: She sits at the door of her house,
on a seat at the highest places of the city,

v15: calling to those who pass by,
who are going straight on their ways:

v16: “Whoever is simple,
turn in here!”

And to the one lacking sense she says,

v17: “Stolen water is sweet,
and bread eaten in secret is pleasant.”

v18: But they do not know
that the dead are there,
that her guests
are in the depths of Sheol.
</p>
##Notes

v04: “lacking sense” — The Hebrew literally says “lacking heart.” Because the heart includes judgment and discernment, the expression describes someone deficient in practical understanding.

v10: “the Holy One” — The Hebrew form is plural in appearance and has been interpreted in more than one way.

##Vocabulary

v01: חָכְמוֹת (chokhmot)
“Wisdom.” A plural-form expression used for personified Wisdom.

v04: חֲסַר־לֵב (chasar lev)
“Lacking sense.” Literally “lacking heart,” referring to deficient judgment or discernment.

v07: לֵץ (lets)
“Scoffer.” One who treats correction, wisdom, or what is right with contempt.

v10: יִרְאַת יְהוָה (yirat YHWH)
“Fear of the LORD.” Reverent recognition of the LORD that forms the foundation of wisdom.
