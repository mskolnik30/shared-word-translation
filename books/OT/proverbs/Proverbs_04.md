## A Father's Instruction

<p>
v01: Hear, children, the discipline of a father,
and pay attention
so that you may know understanding.

v02: For I give you good teaching;
do not abandon my instruction.

v03: When I was a child with my father,
tender and the only one
before my mother,

v04: he taught me and said to me:

“Let your heart hold fast to my words;
keep my commandments and live.

v05: Acquire wisdom;
acquire understanding.
Do not forget,
and do not turn away
from the words of my mouth.
</p>

<p>
v06: Do not abandon wisdom,
and she will guard you;
love her,
and she will keep you.

v07: The beginning of wisdom is this:
Acquire wisdom,
and with all you acquire,
acquire understanding.

v08: Prize her,
and she will exalt you;
she will honor you
when you embrace her.

v09: She will place a graceful wreath
upon your head;
she will give you
a beautiful crown.”
</p>

## The Path of Wisdom

<p>
v10: Hear, my child,
and receive my words,
and the years of your life
will be many.

v11: I have instructed you
in the way of wisdom;
I have led you
in upright paths.

v12: When you walk,
your steps will not be restricted,
and if you run,
you will not stumble.

v13: Hold fast to discipline;
do not let go.
Guard her,
for she is your life.
</p>

## Do Not Enter the Path of the Wicked

<p>
v14: Do not enter the path of the wicked,
and do not walk
in the way of evildoers.

v15: Avoid it;
do not pass along it.
Turn away from it
and pass on.

v16: For they cannot sleep
unless they have done evil;
their sleep is taken away
unless they make someone stumble.

v17: For they eat the bread of wickedness
and drink the wine of violence.
</p>

<p>
v18: But the path of the righteous
is like the light of dawn,
shining brighter and brighter
until the full day.

v19: The way of the wicked
is like deep darkness;
they do not know
what makes them stumble.
</p>

## Guard Your Heart

<p>
v20: My child,
pay attention to my words;
incline your ear
to my sayings.

v21: Do not let them depart from your eyes;
keep them within your heart.

v22: For they are life
to those who find them
and healing
to their whole body.

v23: Above all that you guard,
guard your heart,
for from it flow
the springs of life.
</p>

<p>
v24: Put crooked speech away from you,
and keep deceptive lips far from you.

v25: Let your eyes look straight ahead,
and let your gaze be fixed
directly before you.

v26: Make level the path of your feet,
and let all your ways be established.

v27: Do not turn to the right or to the left;
turn your foot away from evil.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: מוּסָר (musar)
“Discipline,” “instruction,” or “correction.” Formation that teaches and corrects toward wisdom.

v05: חָכְמָה (chokmah)
“Wisdom.” Skill in discerning and living well within the order of life before God.

v08: סָלַל (salal)
“To exalt” or “raise up.” Here wisdom raises the one who values her.

v11: דֶּרֶךְ (derekh)
“Way” or “path.” A physical road and, frequently in wisdom literature, a pattern or direction of life.
