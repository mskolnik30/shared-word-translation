## The Words of King Lemuel

<p>
v01: The words of King Lemuel,
an oracle
that his mother taught him:

v02: What, my son?
What, son of my womb?
What, son of my vows?

v03: Do not give your strength
to women,
or your ways
to those who destroy kings.

v04: It is not for kings, O Lemuel,
it is not for kings to drink wine,
or for rulers
to desire strong drink,

v05: lest they drink
and forget what is decreed
and distort the rights
of all the afflicted.
</p>

## Speak for Those Without a Voice

<p>
v06: Give strong drink
to the one who is perishing
and wine
to those bitter in life.

v07: Let them drink
and forget their poverty
and remember their misery
no more.

v08: Open your mouth
for those without a voice,
for the rights
of all who are passing away.

v09: Open your mouth;
judge with righteousness;
defend the rights
of the poor and needy.
</p>

## A Woman of Strength

<p>
v10: A woman of strength—
who can find?
Her worth
is far beyond jewels.

v11: The heart of her husband
trusts in her,
and he will not lack
gain.

v12: She does him good
and not harm
all the days
of her life.

v13: She seeks wool and flax
and works
with willing hands.

v14: She is like
merchant ships;
she brings her food
from far away.
</p>

<p>
v15: She rises
while it is still night
and gives food
to her household
and portions
to her servants.

v16: She considers a field
and buys it;
from the fruit of her hands
she plants a vineyard.

v17: She girds herself
with strength
and makes
her arms strong.

v18: She perceives
that her merchandise is good;
her lamp does not go out
at night.
</p>

<p>
v19: She reaches her hands
to the distaff,
and her palms
hold the spindle.

v20: She opens her palm
to the poor
and reaches out her hands
to the needy.

v21: She does not fear snow
for her household,
for all her household
are clothed in scarlet.

v22: She makes coverings
for herself;
fine linen and purple
are her clothing.
</p>

## Strength and Dignity

<p>
v23: Her husband is known
at the city gates
when he sits
with the elders of the land.

v24: She makes linen garments
and sells them
and supplies belts
to the merchant.

v25: Strength and dignity
are her clothing,
and she laughs
at the days to come.

v26: She opens her mouth
with wisdom,
and the instruction
of steadfast love
is on her tongue.

v27: She watches carefully
over the ways of her household
and does not eat
the bread of idleness.
</p>

## A Woman Who Fears the LORD

<p>
v28: Her children rise
and call her blessed;
her husband also,
and he praises her:

v29: “Many daughters
have acted with strength,
but you surpass
them all.”

v30: Charm is deceptive,
and beauty is fleeting,
but a woman who fears the LORD
is to be praised.

v31: Give her
from the fruit of her hands,
and let her works praise her
at the gates.
</p>
##Notes

v10: The Hebrew *eshet chayil* literally describes a “woman of strength,” capacity, valor, or excellence.

v10–31: These verses form an alphabetic acrostic in Hebrew, each verse beginning with the successive letters of the Hebrew alphabet.

##Vocabulary

v01: מַשָּׂא (massa)
“Oracle” or “weighty saying.” The designation given to Lemuel’s received instruction.

v11: בָּטַח (batach)
“To trust.” The husband places confident reliance in her.

v26: תּוֹרַת־חֶסֶד (torat ḥesed)
“Instruction of steadfast love.” Teaching characterized by faithful kindness.

v30: יִרְאַת יְהוָה (yirat YHWH)
“Fear of the LORD.” Reverent relationship with the LORD, identified throughout Proverbs as foundational to wisdom.
