## Wisdom Receives Discipline

<p>
v01: A wise child receives a father’s discipline,
but a scoffer does not listen
to rebuke.

v02: From the fruit of the mouth
a person eats what is good,
but the appetite of the treacherous
is for violence.

v03: Whoever guards their mouth
guards their life,
but whoever opens their lips wide
comes to ruin.

v04: The appetite of the lazy person desires
and has nothing,
but the appetite of the diligent
is richly satisfied.
</p>

<p>
v05: The righteous hate falsehood,
but the wicked bring shame
and disgrace.

v06: Righteousness guards
the one whose way has integrity,
but wickedness overturns
the sinner.

v07: One pretends to be rich
yet has nothing;
another pretends to be poor
yet has great wealth.

v08: The ransom of a person’s life
may be their wealth,
but the poor hear no threat.
</p>

## The Light of the Righteous

<p>
v09: The light of the righteous rejoices,
but the lamp of the wicked
will be extinguished.

v10: Pride produces only conflict,
but wisdom is with those
who receive counsel.

v11: Wealth gained too quickly diminishes,
but whoever gathers little by little
makes it increase.

v12: Hope deferred
makes the heart sick,
but desire fulfilled
is a tree of life.
</p>

<p>
v13: Whoever despises a word
brings destruction upon themselves,
but whoever fears the commandment
will be rewarded.

v14: The instruction of the wise
is a fountain of life,
turning a person away
from the snares of death.

v15: Good insight brings favor,
but the way of the treacherous
is hard.

v16: Everyone prudent acts with knowledge,
but a fool displays folly.
</p>

## Faithful Messengers and Wise Companions

<p>
v17: A wicked messenger falls into trouble,
but a faithful envoy
brings healing.

v18: Poverty and disgrace come
to the one who ignores discipline,
but whoever heeds correction
will be honored.

v19: Desire fulfilled
is sweet to the soul,
but turning from evil
is an abomination to fools.

v20: Whoever walks with the wise
becomes wise,
but a companion of fools
will suffer harm.
</p>

## A Good Inheritance

<p>
v21: Trouble pursues sinners,
but good repays the righteous.

v22: A good person leaves an inheritance
to children’s children,
but the wealth of the sinner
is stored up for the righteous.

v23: The field of the poor
may produce abundant food,
but it can be swept away
through injustice.

v24: Whoever withholds the rod
hates their child,
but whoever loves their child
is diligent in discipline.

v25: The righteous eat
until their appetite is satisfied,
but the belly of the wicked
goes hungry.
</p>
##Notes

v11: The Hebrew expression translated “gained too quickly” literally refers to wealth gained from vapor or emptiness and may imply dishonest or insubstantial acquisition.

##Vocabulary

v01: גְּעָרָה (gearah)
“Rebuke” or “correction.” A direct verbal challenge intended to turn someone from wrong conduct.

v23: מִשְׁפָּט (mishpat)
“Justice” or “judgment.” Right ordering and judgment; its absence can contribute directly to deprivation.

v24: שֵׁבֶט (shevet)
“Rod” or “staff.” An instrument of authority and correction, used metaphorically and concretely in wisdom literature.

v24: מוּסָר (musar)
“Discipline,” “instruction,” or “correction.” Intentional formation toward wisdom.
