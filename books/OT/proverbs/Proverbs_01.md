## The Purpose of Proverbs

<p>
v01: The proverbs of Solomon son of David,
king of Israel:

v02: To know wisdom and discipline,
to understand words of insight,

v03: to receive discipline in wise conduct,
in righteousness, justice, and equity;

v04: to give prudence to the simple,
knowledge and discretion to the young—

v05: let the wise hear and increase in learning,
and let the discerning acquire guidance,

v06: to understand a proverb and a saying,
the words of the wise and their riddles.
</p>

## The Beginning of Knowledge

<p>
v07: The fear of the LORD
is the beginning of knowledge;
fools despise wisdom and discipline.
</p>

## Do Not Join Those Who Shed Blood

<p>
v08: Hear, my child,
the discipline of your father,
and do not abandon
the instruction of your mother,

v09: for they are a graceful wreath for your head
and ornaments for your neck.
</p>

<p>
v10: My child,
if sinners entice you,
do not consent.

v11: If they say,
“Come with us;
let us lie in wait for blood;
let us ambush the innocent without cause.

v12: Let us swallow them alive like Sheol,
whole, like those who go down to the pit.

v13: We will find all kinds of precious wealth;
we will fill our houses with plunder.

v14: Cast your lot among us;
we will all have one purse”—

v15: my child,
do not walk in the way with them;
keep your foot from their path,

v16: for their feet run toward evil,
and they hurry to shed blood.
</p>

<p>
v17: For a net is spread in vain
before the eyes of any bird,

v18: but these lie in wait for their own blood;
they ambush their own lives.

v19: Such are the paths
of everyone greedy for unjust gain;
it takes away the life
of the one who possesses it.
</p>

## Wisdom Calls in the Streets

<p>
v20: Wisdom cries aloud in the street;
in the public squares she raises her voice.

v21: At the head of the noisy streets she calls;
at the entrances of the city gates
she speaks her words:

v22: “How long, O simple ones,
will you love being simple?
How long will scoffers delight in scoffing,
and fools hate knowledge?

v23: Turn at my correction.
Look, I will pour out my spirit to you;
I will make my words known to you.
</p>

<p>
v24: Because I called
and you refused,
I stretched out my hand
and no one paid attention;

v25: because you ignored all my counsel
and would have none of my correction,

v26: I also will laugh at your disaster;
I will mock when your dread comes,

v27: when your dread comes like a storm
and your disaster comes like a whirlwind,
when distress and anguish come upon you.
</p>

<p>
v28: Then they will call to me,
but I will not answer;
they will seek me diligently,
but they will not find me,

v29: because they hated knowledge
and did not choose the fear of the LORD.

v30: They would have none of my counsel;
they despised all my correction.

v31: Therefore they will eat
the fruit of their way
and be filled
with their own schemes.

v32: For the turning away of the simple will kill them,
and the complacency of fools will destroy them.

v33: But whoever listens to me
will dwell securely
and will be at ease,
without dread of harm.”
</p>
##Notes

v01: “proverbs” — The Hebrew *mashal* can refer to a proverb, comparison, saying, or figurative teaching.

v08: “my child” — The Hebrew literally says “my son.” Within the instructional setting, the address identifies the learner in a parent-child relationship.

##Vocabulary

v01: מָשָׁל (mashal)
“Proverb,” “saying,” or “comparison.” A compact form of wisdom speech that can include analogy, observation, or instruction.

v02: חָכְמָה (chokmah)
“Wisdom.” Skill in discerning and living well within the order of life before God.

v02: מוּסָר (musar)
“Discipline,” “instruction,” or “correction.” Formation that includes teaching as well as the correction necessary for growth.

v03: צֶדֶק (tsedeq)
“Righteousness” or “right conduct.” What conforms to what is right within relationships and communal life.
