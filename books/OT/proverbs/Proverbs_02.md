## Seek Wisdom as Hidden Treasure

<p>
v01: My child,
if you receive my words
and treasure my commandments within you,

v02: making your ear attentive to wisdom
and inclining your heart to understanding;

v03: if indeed you call out for insight
and raise your voice for understanding;

v04: if you seek it like silver
and search for it like hidden treasures,

v05: then you will understand the fear of the LORD
and find the knowledge of God.
</p>

## The LORD Gives Wisdom

<p>
v06: For the LORD gives wisdom;
from the LORD’s mouth
come knowledge and understanding.

v07: The LORD stores up sound wisdom for the upright,
a shield to those who walk in integrity,

v08: guarding the paths of justice
and keeping the way of the LORD’s faithful ones.

v09: Then you will understand
righteousness and justice and equity—
every good path.
</p>

## Wisdom Will Guard You

<p>
v10: For wisdom will enter your heart,
and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul.

v11: Discretion will watch over you;
understanding will guard you,

v12: rescuing you from the way of evil,
from those who speak perverse things,

v13: who abandon upright paths
to walk in ways of darkness,

v14: who rejoice in doing evil
and delight in the perversity of evil,

v15: whose paths are crooked
and whose ways are twisted.
</p>

## Rescue from the Strange Woman

<p>
v16: Wisdom will rescue you from the strange woman,
from the foreign woman
who makes her words smooth,

v17: who abandons the companion of her youth
and forgets the covenant of her God.

v18: For her house sinks down toward death,
and her paths toward the dead.

v19: None who go to her return
or reach again the paths of life.
</p>

## Walk in the Way of the Good

<p>
v20: Therefore walk in the way of the good
and keep to the paths of the righteous.

v21: For the upright will dwell in the land,
and those of integrity will remain in it,

v22: but the wicked will be cut off from the land,
and the treacherous will be torn from it.
</p>
##Notes

v07: “sound wisdom” — The Hebrew term is uncommon and can refer to effective wisdom, resourcefulness, or enduring success.

##Vocabulary

v05: דַּעַת אֱלֹהִים (daat Elohim)
“Knowledge of God.” Knowing that includes recognition, relationship, and faithful response.

v07: תּוּשִׁיָּה (tushiyyah)
“Sound wisdom,” “effective wisdom,” or “resourcefulness.” Wisdom capable of producing stability and faithful action.

v08: חָסִיד (chasid)
“Faithful one” or “loyal one.” A person characterized by covenant loyalty or steadfast faithfulness.

v17: בְּרִית (berit)
“Covenant.” A binding relationship established through commitment and obligation.
