## Pay Attention to Wisdom

<p>
v01: My child,
pay attention to my wisdom;
incline your ear
to my understanding,

v02: so that you may preserve discretion
and your lips may guard knowledge.
</p>

## The Strange Woman

<p>
v03: For the lips of the strange woman
drip honey,
and her mouth
is smoother than oil,

v04: but in the end she is bitter as wormwood,
sharp as a two-edged sword.

v05: Her feet go down to death;
her steps take hold of Sheol.

v06: She does not consider
the path of life;
her ways wander,
and she does not know it.
</p>

## Keep Far from Her

<p>
v07: And now, children, listen to me;
do not turn away
from the words of my mouth.

v08: Keep your way far from her,
and do not go near
the door of her house,

v09: lest you give your honor to others
and your years to one who is cruel,

v10: lest strangers be filled with your strength
and your labor go
to the house of a foreigner,

v11: and at the end of your life you groan,
when your flesh and body are consumed,

v12: and you say,
“How I hated discipline!
How my heart despised correction!

v13: I did not listen
to the voice of my teachers
or incline my ear
to those who instructed me.

v14: I was almost in complete ruin
in the midst of the assembly
and congregation.”
</p>

## Rejoice in the Love of Your Marriage

<p>
v15: Drink water from your own cistern
and flowing water
from your own well.

v16: Should your springs be scattered outside,
your streams of water
in the streets?

v17: Let them be yours alone,
and not shared
with strangers.

v18: Let your fountain be blessed,
and rejoice
in the wife of your youth,

v19: a lovely deer,
a graceful doe.
Let her breasts satisfy you at all times;
be continually intoxicated
with her love.
</p>

<p>
v20: Why, my child,
should you be intoxicated
with a strange woman
or embrace the body
of a foreign woman?
</p>

## The LORD Sees Every Path

<p>
v21: For the ways of a person
are before the eyes of the LORD,
and the LORD examines
all their paths.

v22: The wicked are caught
by their own iniquities,
and held fast
by the cords of their sin.

v23: They die for lack of discipline,
and because of their great folly
they wander away.
</p>
##Notes

v16: The Hebrew may be read either as a statement or as a rhetorical question.

##Vocabulary

v01: חָכְמָה (chokmah)
“Wisdom.” Skill in discerning and living faithfully.

v02: מְזִמָּה (mezimmah)
“Discretion” or “purposeful planning.” The capacity to consider consequences and choose wisely.

v05: שְׁאוֹל (Sheol)
“Sheol.” The realm of the dead in Hebrew poetic and wisdom language.

v22: עָוֹן (avon)
“Iniquity,” “wrong,” or “guilt.” Wrongdoing that can also carry its own consequence.
