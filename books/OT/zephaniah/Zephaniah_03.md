<p>
v01: Woe to the rebellious and defiled,
the oppressing city!
</p>

<p>
v02: She has not listened to the voice;
she has not accepted discipline.
She has not trusted in the LORD;
she has not drawn near to her God.
</p>

<p>
v03: Her officials within her are roaring lions;
her judges are wolves of the evening—
they leave no bones until morning.
</p>

<p>
v04: Her prophets are reckless, treacherous people;
her priests have profaned what is holy;
they have violated the law.
</p>

<p>
v05: The LORD is righteous within her;
he does no wrong.
Morning by morning he brings his justice to light;
it does not fail.
Yet the unjust know no shame.
</p>

<p>
v06: “I have cut off nations;
their strongholds are desolate.
I have laid waste their streets,
with no one passing through.
Their cities are ruined,
without people, without an inhabitant.
</p>

<p>
v07: I said, ‘Surely you will fear me;
you will accept discipline.’
Then her dwelling would not be cut off—
all that I had decreed against her.
But they rose early
to corrupt all their deeds.
</p>

<p>
v08: Therefore wait for me,” declares the LORD,
“for the day when I rise up for plunder.
For my judgment is to gather nations,
to assemble kingdoms,
to pour out on them my indignation,
all the burning of my anger;
for all the earth will be consumed
by the fire of my jealousy.
</p>

<p>
v09: “For then I will turn the peoples
to a pure speech,
that all of them may call on the name of the LORD,
to serve him shoulder to shoulder.
</p>

<p>
v10: From beyond the rivers of Cush,
my worshipers—the daughter of my scattered ones—
will bring my offering.
</p>

<p>
v11: On that day you will not be ashamed
of all your deeds by which you rebelled against me,
for then I will remove from your midst
those who exult in your pride,
and you will no longer be haughty
on my holy mountain.
</p>

<p>
v12: But I will leave in your midst
a people poor and lowly,
and they will take refuge
in the name of the LORD.
</p>

<p>
v13: The remnant of Israel will do no wrong
and speak no lie,
nor will a deceitful tongue
be found in their mouths.
For they will pasture and lie down,
with no one to make them afraid.
</p>

<p>
v14: Sing for joy, Daughter Zion!
Shout, Israel!
Rejoice and exult with all your heart,
Daughter Jerusalem!
</p>

<p>
v15: The LORD has removed your judgments;
he has turned away your enemy.
The King of Israel, the LORD, is in your midst;
you will fear disaster no more.
</p>

<p>
v16: On that day it will be said to Jerusalem:
“Do not fear, Zion;
do not let your hands grow slack.
</p>

<p>
v17: The LORD your God is in your midst,
a mighty warrior who saves.
He will rejoice over you with gladness;
he will be silent in his love;
he will exult over you with singing.”
</p>

<p>
v18: “I have gathered those grieving over the appointed festival;
they were from you.
Reproach was a burden upon her.
</p>

<p>
v19: Look, at that time I will deal
with all who oppress you.
I will save the one who limps
and gather the outcast;
and I will make them praise and renown
in every land where they were put to shame.
</p>

<p>
v20: At that time I will bring you in,
at the time when I gather you.
For I will make you renowned and praised
among all the peoples of the earth,
when I restore your fortunes before your eyes,”
says the LORD.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v01: יוֹנָה (yonah)
“Oppressing; violent.” The adjective characterizes the city’s conduct.

v04: תּוֹרָה (torah)
“Law.” Denotes divine instruction. The priests’ violation of it joins corrupt worship to injustice in communal life.

v09: שָׂפָה (safah)
“Lip.” Its purification enables the peoples’ united invocation of the LORD’s name.

v13: שְׁאֵרִית (she’erit)
“Remnant” names those who remain after judgment. In this passage, their identity is marked by truthfulness, justice, and freedom from fear.
