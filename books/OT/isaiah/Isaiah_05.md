<p>
v01: Let me sing for my beloved my beloved’s song concerning his vineyard:
My beloved had a vineyard on a fertile hill.
v02: He dug it and cleared it of stones,
and planted it with choice vines.
He built a tower in the midst of it,
and also carved out a wine vat in it.
He waited for it to yield grapes, but it produced wild grapes.
</p>
<p>
v03: So now, inhabitants of Jerusalem
and people of Judah,
judge between me and my vineyard.
v04: What more was there to do for my vineyard
that I have not done in it?
Why, when I waited for grapes,
did it yield wild grapes?
</p>
<p>
v05: Now I will tell you
what I will do to my vineyard.
I will remove its hedge, and it will become waste;
I will break down its wall, and it shall be trampled.
v06: I will make it a desolation;
it will not be pruned nor hoed,
and briars and thorns shall grow;
I will also command the clouds
that they rain no rain upon it.
</p>
<p>
v07: For the vineyard of the LORD of Hosts
is the house of Israel,
and the people of Judah
are his pleasant planting;
he waited for justice,
but behold, bloodshed;
for righteousness,
but behold, an outcry!
</p>
<p>
v08: Woe to those who join house to house,
who add field to field,
until there is no place left,
and you are left alone in the land.
v09: In my hearing, the LORD of Hosts said:
Surely many houses shall be desolate,
large and beautiful, without inhabitant.
v10: For ten acres of vineyard shall produce but one bath,
and a homer of seed shall yield but an ephah.
</p>
<p>
v11: Woe to those who rise early in the morning
that they may run after strong drink,
who tarry late into the evening
as wine inflames them!
v12: They have lyre and harp,
tambourine and flute, and wine at their feasts,
but they do not regard
the deeds of the LORD,
or see the work
of his hands.
</p>
<p>
v13: Therefore my people go into exile
for lack of knowledge;
their honored men go hungry,
and their multitude parched with thirst.
v14: Therefore Sheol enlarges its appetite
and opens its mouth beyond measure;
and down go her nobles,
and her masses, her revelers, and those who exult in her.
</p>
<p>
v15: The people are bowed down,
everyone brought low;
and the eyes of the haughty are humbled.
v16: But the LORD of Hosts is exalted in justice,
and the Holy God shows himself holy in righteousness.
v17: Then shall the lambs graze
as in their pasture,
and nomads shall eat among
the ruins of the rich.
</p>
<p>
v18: Woe to those who draw iniquity with cords of falsehood,
who draw sin as with cart ropes,
v19: who say, “Let him make haste,
let him speed his work
that we may see it;
let the purpose of the Holy One of Israel draw near
and come, that we may know it!”
</p>
<p>
v20: Woe to those who call evil good
and good evil,
who put darkness for light
and light for darkness,
who put bitter for sweet
and sweet for bitter!
</p>
<p>
v21: Woe to those who are wise
in their own eyes,
and shrewd in their own sight!
v22: Woe to those who are heroes at drinking wine,
and valiant men in mixing strong drink,
v23: who acquit the guilty for a bribe,
and deprive the innocent of his right!
</p>
<p>
v24: Therefore, as the tongue of fire devours stubble,
and as dry grass sinks down in flame,
so their root will be as rottenness,
and their blossom go up like dust;
for they have rejected the law of the LORD of Hosts,
and have despised the word of the Holy One of Israel.
</p>
<p>
v25: Therefore the anger of the LORD was kindled against his people,
and he stretched out his hand against them and struck them,
and the mountains quaked;
and their corpses were as refuse in the streets.
For all this his anger has not turned away,
and his hand is stretched out still.
</p>
<p>
v26: He will raise a signal for nations far away,
and whistle for them from the end of the earth;
And behold, they quickly come,
v27: none is weary, none stumbles,
none slumbers or sleeps,
not a belt is loose,
nor a sandal strap broken;
v28: their arrows are sharp,
all their bows bent,
Their horses' hooves seem like flint,
And their wheels like the whirlwind.
</p>
<p>
v29: Their roaring is like a lioness;
like young lions they roar,
they growl and seize their prey;
they carry it off, and none can rescue.
v30: They will growl over it on that day,
like the roaring of the sea.
And if one looks to the land—
behold, darkness and distress;
and the light is darkened by its clouds.
</p>
## Notes
v01: **5:1**: "my beloved's song concerning his vineyard": This introduces a parabolic narrative where the vineyard symbolizes the house of Israel and God is the beloved caring for it.  
v04: **5:4**: "What more was there to do for my vineyard": A rhetorical question emphasizing the owner's (God's) exhaustive efforts and the vineyard's (Israel's) failure to produce.  
v07: **5:7**: "justice...bloodshed; righteousness...outcry": A wordplay in Hebrew between "mishpat" (justice) and "mishpach" (bloodshed), "tsedaqah" (righteousness) and "tse‘aqah" (outcry), emphasizing the people's moral failure.  
v07: The Hebrew uses wordplay between *mishpat* (“justice”) and *mispach* (“bloodshed”), and between *tsedaqah* (“righteousness”) and *tseaqah* (“outcry”).
v20: **5:20**: "Woe to those who call evil good": This verse addresses the reversal of moral values, showing the people's deep corruption.  
v25: “For all this his anger has not turned away, and his hand is stretched out still” functions as a recurring refrain in Isaiah, extending the announcement of judgment beyond a single act of punishment.

## Vocabulary

v07: **5:7**: יְהוָה (YHWH, Yahweh) — This name of God refers to the eternal, self-existent one. It is personal and relational, as shown by God's expectation of justice and righteousness from Israel.  

v16: **5:16**: צְבָאוֹת (Tseva'oth, Hosts) — Often translated as "Almighty," this term depicts God as a military leader, commanding the armies of heaven.  

v16: צְבָאוֹת (Tseva'oth)
“Hosts; armies.” In the title “LORD of Hosts,” the noun evokes ordered heavenly or earthly forces under divine command.

v19: **5:19**: קְדוֹשׁ (Kadosh, Holy One) — This title for God emphasizes his distinctness and moral purity, underscoring his rightful expectation of obedience and reverence.  

v19: קְדוֹשׁ (Kadosh)
“Holy One.” A title with a sense of holiness or sacred distinctness.

v07: יְהוָה (YHWH)
“LORD.” The divine name, represented in English by small-capital LORD.
