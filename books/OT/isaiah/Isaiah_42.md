## The Servant and a New Song

<p>
v01: Look, my servant, whom I uphold, my chosen one in whom my soul delights. I have put my Spirit upon him;  
he will bring forth justice to the nations.
v02: He will not cry out or raise his voice or make it heard in the street.
v03: A bruised reed he will not break, and a dimly burning wick he will not extinguish;  
faithfully he will bring forth justice.
v04: He will not fail nor be discouraged, until he has set justice in the earth, and the islands will wait for his law."
</p>

<p>
v05: Thus says God the LORD, the one who created the heavens and stretched them out, the one who spread out the earth and that which comes out of it, the one who gives breath to its people and spirit to those who walk in it.
v06: "I, the LORD, have called you in righteousness, and will hold your hand, and will keep you, and make you a covenant for the people, as a light for the nations;
v07: to open the blind eyes, to bring the prisoners out of the dungeon, and those who sit in darkness out of the prison.
v08: "I am the LORD. That is my name. I will not give my glory to another, nor my praise to engraved images.
</p>

<p>
v09: Look, the former things have happened, and I declare new things. I tell you about them before they come up."
v10: Sing to the LORD a new song, and his praise from the end of the earth, you who go down to the sea, and all that is in it, the islands and their inhabitants.
v11: Let the wilderness and its cities raise their voices, with the villages that Kedar inhabits. Let the inhabitants of Sela sing. Let them shout from the top of the mountains!
v12: Let them give glory to the LORD, and declare his praise in the islands.
</p>

<p>
v13: The LORD will go out like a mighty man. He will stir up zeal like a man of war. He will raise a war cry. Yes, he will shout aloud. He will triumph over his enemies.
v14: "I have been silent a long time. I have been quiet and restrained myself. Now I will cry out like a laboring woman. I will both gasp and pant.
v15: I will destroy mountains and hills, and dry up all their herbs. I will make the rivers islands, and will dry up the pools.
v16: I will bring the blind by a way that they do not know. I will lead them in paths that they do not know. I will make darkness light before them, and crooked places straight. I will do these things, and I will not forsake them.
</p>

<p>
v17: "Those who trust in engraved images, who tell molten images, 'You are our gods' will be turned back. They will be utterly disappointed.
v18: "Hear, you deaf, and look, you blind, that you may see.
v19: Who is blind, but my servant? Or who is as deaf as my messenger whom I send? Who is as blind as the one who is at peace, and as blind as the LORD's servant?
v20: You see many things, but do not observe. His ears are open, but he does not listen.
</p>

<p>
v21: It pleased the LORD, for his righteousness' sake, to magnify the law, and make it honorable.
v22: But this is a robbed and plundered people. All of them are snared in holes, and they are hidden in prisons. They have become a prey, and no one delivers;  
and a spoil, and no one says, 'Restore them!'
v23: Who is there among you who will give ear to this? Who will listen and hear for the time to come?
v24: Who gave Jacob as plunder, and Israel to the robbers? Didn't the LORD, he against whom we have sinned? For they would not walk in his ways, and they disobeyed his law.
</p>

<p>
v25: Therefore he poured the fierceness of his anger on him, and the strength of battle;  
and it set him on fire all around, but he did not know;  
and it burned him, but he did not take it to heart."
</p>

##Notes

v06: The servant is appointed as “a covenant for the people” and “a light for the nations,” joining Israel’s restoration with a wider vocation.

v01: “servant” — Isaiah uses servant language in several contexts. TSW preserves the immediate wording rather than forcing every occurrence into a single later identification.

v06: “covenant” — The term names a binding relationship shaped by promise and obligation. Isaiah uses covenant language in both judgment and restoration.

v01: “nations” — The Hebrew *goyim* refers to peoples beyond Israel and can carry political, geographic, and theological force depending on context.

##Vocabulary

v03: קָנֶה רָצוּץ (qaneh ratsuts)
“Bruised reed.” An image of fragility the servant does not crush.

v01: עֶבֶד (eved)
“Servant.” One who serves another; used in Isaiah for several figures and for Israel.

v06: בְּרִית (berit)
“Covenant.” A binding relationship shaped by promise and obligation.

v01: גּוֹיִם (goyim)
“Nations” or “peoples.” Peoples beyond Israel.
