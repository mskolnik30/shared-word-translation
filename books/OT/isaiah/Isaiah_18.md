## An Oracle Concerning Cush

<p>
v01: Ah, the land of the rustling of wings, which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia;
v02: that sends ambassadors by the sea, even in vessels of papyrus on the waters, saying, "Go, you swift messengers, to a nation tall and smooth, to a people awesome from their beginning onward, a nation that measures out and treads down, whose land the rivers divide!"
v03: All you inhabitants of the world, and you dwellers on the earth, when a banner is lifted up on the mountains, look! When the trumpet is blown, listen!
v04: For the LORD said to me, "I will be still, and I will see in my dwelling place, like clear heat in sunshine, like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest."
</p>

<p>
v05: For before the harvest, when the blossom is over, and the flower becomes a ripening grape, he will cut off the sprigs with pruning hooks, and he will cut down and take away the spreading branches.
v06: They will be left together for the ravenous birds of the mountains, and for the animals of the earth. The ravenous birds will summer on them, and all the animals of the earth will winter on them.
v07: In that time, a present will be brought to the LORD of Hosts from a people tall and smooth, even from a people awesome from their beginning onward, a nation that measures out and treads down, whose land the rivers divide, to the place of the name of the LORD of Hosts, Mount Zion.
</p>
## Notes
v01: The identity of the land beyond the rivers of Cush is described poetically; the oracle resists easy reduction to one modern geography.  
v07: “LORD of Hosts” — The title presents the LORD as sovereign over heavenly and earthly hosts and is especially prominent in Isaiah.  
v07: “Zion” — The name can refer to Jerusalem, its people, or the city personified. Isaiah often allows those senses to overlap.  

## Vocabulary

v07: יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת (YHWH tseva'ot)  
“LORD of Hosts.” A divine title emphasizing sovereign command.  

v07: צִיּוֹן (tsiyyon)  
“Zion.” Jerusalem and, by extension, the city’s people and theological identity.  

v01: דָּבָר (davar)  
“Word,” “matter,” or “thing.” A common Hebrew term for speech that also names the matter spoken.  

v07: יְהוָה צְבָאוֹת (YHWH tseva'ot)
“LORD of Hosts.” A title joining the divine name to “hosts/armies.”

v07: צִיּוֹן (tsiyyon)
“Zion.” A name for Jerusalem, especially the city associated with the temple and Davidic rule.
