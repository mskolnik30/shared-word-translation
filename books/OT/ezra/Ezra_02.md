## Notes

- **v01. Nebuchadnezzar**: The Babylonian king who exiled the people of Judah to Babylon. His actions set the backdrop for the Jewish return to their homeland.
- **v62. Excluded from the priesthood**: This exclusion highlights the importance of genealogical records for establishing priestly legitimacy.

## Vocabulary

- **v02. Zerubbabel** (זְרֻבָּבֶל, ze.rub.ba.vel): A leader of the first return to Jerusalem; grandson of Jehoiachin, the captive king.
- **v63. Urim and Thummim** (אוּרִים וְתֻמִּים, u.rim u.tum.mim): Objects used in biblical times for divination, revealing God's will.
