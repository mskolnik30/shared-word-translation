<p>v1 When the seventh month came, and the Israelites were in the towns, the people gathered together as one person to Jerusalem.  
v02: Then Jeshua son of Jozadak with his fellow priests, and Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel with his companions, arose and built the altar of the God of Israel, to offer burnt offerings upon it, as it is written in the Law of Moses, the man of God.  
v03: They set the altar in its place, because fear was upon them due to the surrounding peoples, and they offered burnt offerings on it to Yahweh, burnt offerings morning and evening.  
v04: They kept the Feast of Booths as it is written, and offered the daily burnt offerings by number according to the ordinance, each day’s requirement.  
v05: Afterward, they offered the regular burnt offering, and offerings for the new moons and for all the appointed feasts of Yahweh that were consecrated, and for everyone who willingly offered a freewill offering to Yahweh.  
v06: From the first day of the seventh month, they began to offer burnt offerings to Yahweh, but the foundation of the temple of Yahweh was not yet laid.  
v07: So they gave money to the masons and carpenters, and food, drink, and oil to the Sidonians and the Tyrians to bring cedar trees from Lebanon to the sea at Joppa, according to the authorization granted by Cyrus king of Persia.</p>

<p>v8 In the second year after their coming to the house of God at Jerusalem, in the second month, Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel, Jeshua son of Jozadak, and the rest of their associates, the priests and the Levites, and all who had returned from the captivity to Jerusalem, began the work. They appointed the Levites, from twenty years old and upward, to oversee the work on the house of Yahweh.  
v09: Jeshua with his sons and brothers, and Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of Judah, took their places together to supervise those working on the house of God. The sons of Henadad, with their sons and brothers, the Levites, joined in supervising those working on the house of God.</p>

<p>v10 When the builders laid the foundation of the temple of Yahweh, the priests in their apparel, with trumpets, and the Levites, the sons of Asaph, with cymbals, stood to praise Yahweh, according to the instructions of David king of Israel.  
v11: They sang responsively, praising and giving thanks to Yahweh, saying, “For he is good, for his steadfast love toward Israel endures forever.” And all the people shouted with a great shout when they praised Yahweh, because the foundation of the house of Yahweh was laid.</p>

<p>v12 But many of the priests, the Levites, and the heads of fathers' houses, old men who had seen the first house, wept with a loud voice when they saw this house’s foundation being laid, though many shouted aloud for joy,  
v13: so that the people could not distinguish the sound of the joyful shout from the sound of the people's weeping. For the people shouted with a loud shout, and the sound was heard far away.</p>

## Notes

- **v2: Jeshua son of Jozadak… Zerubbabel son of Shealtiel**: These leaders, representing the priestly and royal lines, initiated the reconstruction of the altar as a central act of religious and communal life.  
- **v4: Feast of Booths as it is written**: The returnees kept the festival in full alignment with the pre-exilic tradition, as a reaffirmation of their identity after exile.  
- **v7: cedar trees from Lebanon**: The use of cedar for temple building connects this construction with Israel’s past, reminiscent of the materials used in Solomon’s temple.  
- **v11: Yahweh… his steadfast love… endures forever**: The community praises Yahweh using a common refrain of Israel’s hymnic tradition, emphasizing divine faithfulness.

## Vocabulary

- **v11: חֶסֶד (chesed)**: This term, often translated as "steadfast love" or "lovingkindness," captures Yahweh's enduring covenant loyalty to Israel, a recurring theme in their worship and scripture.
