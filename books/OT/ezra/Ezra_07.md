<p>
v01: After these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, Ezra the son of Seraiah, son of Azariah, son of Hilkiah,
v02: son of Shallum, son of Zadok, son of Ahitub,
v03: son of Amariah, son of Azariah, son of Meraioth,
v04: son of Zerahiah, son of Uzzi, son of Bukki,
v05: son of Abishua, son of Phinehas, son of Eleazar, son of Aaron the chief priest—
v06: this Ezra went up from Babylon. He was a skilled scribe in the Law of Moses, which the LORD, the God of Israel, had given. The king granted him all his requests, according to the hand of the LORD his God upon him.
</p>
<p>
v07: Some of the Israelites, along with priests and Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and temple servants, went up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of King Artaxerxes.
v08: He came to Jerusalem in the fifth month, which was in the seventh year of the king.
v09: On the first day of the first month, he began his ascent from Babylon, and on the first day of the fifth month, he arrived in Jerusalem, according to the good hand of his God upon him.
v10: Ezra had prepared his heart to seek the Law of the LORD, to do it, and to teach Israel the statutes and judgments.
</p>
<p>
v11: Now this is the copy of the decree that King Artaxerxes gave to Ezra the priest, the scribe, a scribe of the law of the commands of the LORD and his statutes regarding Israel:
v12: "Artaxerxes, king of kings, to Ezra the priest, the scribe of the Law of the God of heaven, perfect peace. And now,
v13: I issue a decree that all those in my kingdom who are of the people of Israel and their priests and Levites, and who wish to go to Jerusalem, may go with you.
v14: Since you are sent by the king and his seven counselors to make inquiries about Judah and Jerusalem according to the Law of your God which is in your hand,
v15: and to carry the silver and gold which the king and his counselors have freely offered to the God of Israel, whose dwelling is in Jerusalem,
v16: and all the silver and gold that you find in the province of Babylon, along with the freewill offerings of the people and the priests, made willingly for the house of their God which is in Jerusalem—
v17: with this silver, therefore, you shall diligently buy bulls, rams, lambs, with their grain offerings and their drink offerings, and sacrifice them on the altar in the house of your God in Jerusalem.
v18: And whatever else you and your brethren decide to do with the rest of the gold and silver, do according to the will of your God.
v19: Also, the articles that are given to you for the service of the house of your God, deliver in full before the God of Jerusalem.
</p>
<p>
v20: And whatever else is needed for the house of your God that you may have occasion to provide, you may pay for out of the king’s treasury.
v21: I, even I, King Artaxerxes, issue a decree to all the treasurers in the region beyond the River: whatever Ezra the priest, the scribe of the Law of the God of heaven, may ask of you, it is to be done diligently,
v22: up to one hundred talents of silver, one hundred cors of wheat, one hundred baths of wine, one hundred baths of oil, and salt without limit.
v23: Whatever is commanded by the God of heaven, let it be diligently done for the house of the God of heaven. Why should there be wrath against the kingdom of the king and his sons?
</p>
<p>
v24: Moreover, we make known to you that it is not lawful to impose tribute, custom, or toll on any of the priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, temple servants, or other workers of this house of God.
v25: And you, Ezra, according to the wisdom of your God that is in your hand, appoint magistrates and judges who may judge all the people beyond the River, all those who know the laws of your God; and those who do not know them, you shall teach.
v26: Whoever will not observe the law of your God and the law of the king, let judgment be executed speedily upon him, whether it be death, banishment, confiscation of goods, or imprisonment."
</p>
<p>
v27: Blessed be the LORD, the God of our ancestors, who has put such a thing as this into the king's heart, to beautify the house of the LORD which is in Jerusalem,
v28: and who has extended loving-kindness to me before the king and his counselors, and all the mighty princes of the king. I took courage, for the hand of the LORD my God was upon me, and I gathered leaders from Israel to go up with me.
</p>

## Notes

v1: "After these things" positions the narrative transitionally within the larger context of Persian rule, suggesting a new chapter in Israel's restoration story.

v6: "The hand of the LORD his God" implies divine favor and providence regarding Ezra's mission, underscoring God's active role in the narrative as seen in previous scriptures.

v10: "Prepared his heart" indicates a deliberate and proactive orientation toward learning, obeying, and teaching God's law, reflecting Ezra’s character as a devout leader and teacher in the community.

v27: "Blessed be the LORD" echoes traditional formulas of praise, acknowledging divine sovereignty and the fulfillment of God's promises through historical events.

## Vocabulary

v6: סֹפֵר (so.pher) - "scribe," a term signifying Ezra's role as a scholar and interpreter of divine law, skilled in transcribing and teaching the Law of Moses. In this passage, it highlights his authority and expertise.

v10: תּוֹרַת (to.rah) - "Law," referring to the instruction given by God. It suggests not only legal statutes but guidance for life, central to Ezra's mission in Israel's spiritual renewal.

v21: טַעַם (ta.am) - "decree" or "command," used here to convey the authority of King Artaxerxes in supporting the mission of Ezra with governmental resources, reflecting the political backdrop in which Ezra's journey and work unfold.
