## Esther Reveals Her Petition

<p>
v01: So the king and Haman came to drink with Queen Esther.

v02: On the second day, while they were drinking wine at the feast, the king said again to Esther, “What is your petition, Queen Esther? It shall be given to you. And what is your request? Even to half the kingdom, it shall be done.”

v03: Then Queen Esther answered and said, “If I have found favor in your eyes, O king, and if it is good to the king, let my life be given to me at my petition, and my people at my request.

v04: For we have been sold, I and my people, to be destroyed, killed, and annihilated. If we had merely been sold as male and female servants, I would have remained silent, for the distress would not have been worth troubling the king.”
</p>

## Esther Names the Enemy

<p>
v05: Then King Ahasuerus said to Queen Esther, “Who is he, and where is he, whose heart has filled him to do this?”

v06: Esther said, “An adversary and enemy—this wicked Haman!” Then Haman was terrified before the king and queen.
</p>

## The King’s Anger against Haman

<p>
v07: The king arose in his fury from the wine feast and went into the palace garden, but Haman remained to seek his life from Queen Esther, for he saw that disaster had been determined against him by the king.

v08: When the king returned from the palace garden to the house of the wine feast, Haman had fallen upon the couch where Esther was. Then the king said, “Will he even assault the queen with me in the house?” As the word went out from the king’s mouth, they covered Haman’s face.
</p>

## Haman Is Hanged on His Own Tree

<p>
v09: Then Harbona, one of the eunuchs before the king, said, “Look, the tree that Haman made for Mordecai, who spoke good for the king, is standing at Haman’s house, fifty cubits high.” The king said, “Hang him on it.”

v10: So they hanged Haman on the tree that he had prepared for Mordecai. Then the king’s fury subsided.
</p>
##Notes

##Vocabulary

v03: נֶפֶשׁ (nefesh)
“Life” or “self.” Esther asks that her life be given back to her.

v04: מָכַר (makhar)
“To sell.” Esther describes herself and her people as sold into destruction.

v05: מָלֵא לֵב (male lev)
“To fill the heart.” An idiom for becoming inwardly resolved or daring to act.

v06: צַר וְאוֹיֵב (tsar ve-oyev)
“Adversary and enemy.” Esther’s direct designation of Haman.
